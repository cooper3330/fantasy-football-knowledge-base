# Keon Coleman

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-20 - Matt Waldman]]): called Coleman "very physical" and "great at the catch point" per film, smarter than his media portrayal, from what Waldman's heard via Bills radio voice Stevie Johnson. The open question for Waldman is whether Coleman can become a full route-runner with enough explosiveness for what Buffalo wants from an outside receiver — and whether front-office politics (reported disagreement among personnel staff that led to an ousting) affects his opportunity. Framed as a late-round dart throw, going around wide receiver 88 per ADP discussed on the show.
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): dismissed Coleman as a dark-horse pick to lead the Bills/division in production — "no thanks... that would be like a big shock to me." Discussed mainly as competition [[Skyler Bell]] could beat out for the WR3 role.
