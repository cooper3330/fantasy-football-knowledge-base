# Malik Nabers

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-20 - Matt Waldman]]): recovering from ACL surgery/cleanup (surgery dated to late February), and Waldman is playing it cautious on draft price — guessed Nabers could miss roughly half the season, though he said he hopes to be wrong. Current evidence is limited (surgery timeline, a video of him moving without full fluidity on a softball field), not a broader track record yet. Once healthy, Waldman does expect him to return to form. Reported ADP context discussed on the show: wide receiver 14 / third round.

- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27 - Matt Waldman]]): slightly more optimistic since Nabers avoided the PUP list, but still cautious given past false-alarm cases of similar early optimism. Specific projections given: if he plays ~10 games, ~82 targets/50 catches/620 yards/3 TD; if he plays a full 15-17 games, 800-900 yards/6-8 TD. Says that outcome is worth a 4th-5th round pick, not the 2nd round some are paying. Explicitly says he hopes to be proven wrong.

- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): notes NY beat reporters have a history of overhyping Nabers news, so he's discounting the recent optimistic coverage — but avoiding the PUP list is the most positive signal of the "forking paths" available. Hasn't moved his rank yet, wants to see him in full team practice first, but sees real upward mobility pending more information.
