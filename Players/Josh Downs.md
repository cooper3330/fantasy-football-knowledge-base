# Josh Downs

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27 - Matt Waldman]]): sees WR48 as more of a floor than a ceiling with Michael Pittman Jr. now gone — projects ~114 targets, ~80 catches, ~850 yards, and a team-leading 7 TDs, with expanded outside/inside alignment flexibility. A path to true WR2 value exists (if [[Alec Pierce]] is slow to return healthy, or [[Tyler Warren]] draws enough defensive attention to open things up), but Waldman says it takes "a lot of ifs" to get there.

- 2026-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-30 - Matt Harmon]]): bullish on reports Downs will play significantly more outside after running ~77% of his snaps in the slot across his first three seasons — backed by coach Shane Steichen's own comments about getting him the ball "any way we can." Harmon's case: Downs' three-year man-coverage success rate (77.5%/76.7%/75.3%) and career-best 82.4% vs. press (albeit a small sample) suggest he can hold up on the perimeter, and the Colts' outside depth behind him is thin enough that a competent play-caller has little reason not to play him extensively. Expects a big payday for Downs (a pending free agent) if the season goes well.
