# Garrett Wilson

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): entering year 5 with 1,100/1,000/1,100-yard seasons already, despite mediocre-to-poor QB play (Geno Smith ranked 39th of 45 qualifying QBs in adjusted net yards/attempt, 33rd in dropback success rate last season). Harmon's 2024-season player-comp tool returned elite names (Ja'Marr Chase, Antonio Brown, Stefon Diggs, DeAndre Hopkins) but Harmon stops short of calling him a true tier-one/elite receiver yet — "tier two," a "couple little things" short of graduating, though "no question he could be a top-10 receiver." Picked Wilson over [[DJ Moore]] as his choice for most-productive AFC East WR in 2026, citing higher target-upside than Moore in a run-heavy Buffalo offense.
