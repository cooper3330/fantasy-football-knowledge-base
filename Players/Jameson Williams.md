# Jameson Williams

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28 - Chris Harris]]): more bullish on him than ever, ranking him WR16 (standard) / WR18 (PPR) — notably higher than guest Jeff Erickson's WR25. Usage got "much more interesting" in the second half of last season once Dan Campbell took over play-calling; Harris pointed to him finally running a dig route as evidence of real route-tree growth, not just a pure speed/deep threat anymore. Real volatility risk flagged: two zero-catch games last season, including a 29-route, zero-target game vs. Tampa Bay. Harris explicitly hedges on attribution for the 2nd-half breakout — open to it being driven by [[Sam LaPorta]]'s absence changing defensive attention rather than the coaching change itself, and said he'd reconsider his ranking if that's the real explanation.
