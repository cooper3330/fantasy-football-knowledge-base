# Romeo Doubs

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): his pick as the AFC East's most likely "dark horse" pass-catcher, over [[Kyle Williams]] and other Patriots/Dolphins names — coming off what Harmon called clearly the best season of that group. Backed by charting: 69.2% success rate vs. man, 69% vs. press, 75% vs. zone (54th/59th/24th percentile). Was primarily an X-receiver last season (84.6% of snaps outside, ~82% on the line), and Harmon is skeptical the team needs to move him into the slot — he thinks [[AJ Brown]], even in decline, is still more of a dynamic mover than Doubs, a more technical X-type. Was proactively targeted/paid by New England, which Harmon thinks makes his profile less boom-bust than his volatile Green Bay tenure suggested; expects "some good weeks" as Drake Maye's clear No. 2 and "a good season overall."
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-28 - Matt Harmon]]): reporting (Evan Lazar) has Doubs stepping into a "big slot" role replacing the departed Stefon Diggs (85 catches/1,000 yards/4 TD last year), with [[AJ Brown]] staying mostly outside X — a plan Harmon called curious and possibly suboptimal for Brown, but which does make him more bullish on Doubs specifically: his 2025 profile is X-receiver-coded but with strong breaking-route ability that Harmon thinks could translate well to a Z/slot role. Floated real upside — "could end up going in the deep forties at wide receiver in fantasy" and finish as a top-30 fantasy WR — and noted a real (if not the most likely) scenario where Doubs out-catches Brown this season if usage plays out this way. Co-host projection discussed on air: ~80 catches, 900 yards, 4-5 TDs.
