# Dontayvion Wicks

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-28 - Matt Harmon]]): pushed back on head coach Nick Sirianni's public comparison of Wicks to Keenan Allen — "I don't think he'll be the next Keenan Allen." Wicks' rookie season (with Green Bay) was legitimately promising, including a 72.7% success rate vs. press (69th percentile), which does support the release-quickness part of the Allen comp. But Harmon's own charting shows Wicks took a real step back in year two — success rate vs. man, zone, and press all declined — and thinks he was somewhat overrated/overstated for his separation ability at the time (Harmon's team even contributed to the hype with a highly-viewed YouTube video). Expects him to be Philadelphia's third receiver, likely deployed on slot out-breakers and sail-concept routes off play action — a role he handled well in Green Bay. Overall take: a solid, useful "good number three receiver," a strong outcome for a 5th-round pick, but not a star.
