# DJ Moore

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): called Moore the "wild card" of the AFC East WR group — genuinely unsure what to expect entering his third team, since receivers rarely jump a level that late in their career. Backed this with Reception Perception charting: 2025 success rate was 63% vs. man / 77% vs. zone (both up slightly from 2024) but still well down from his Carolina peak (~71-72% vs. man from 2020-2022). Doesn't think he's declined physically. Key argument for a bounce-back: Buffalo's Joe Brady-offense X-receiver role (heavy on slant/curl/9-routes) fits Moore's charted strengths (2025: 88.2% success on curls, 80% on slants, 62% on the 9-route) much better than the dig/out-heavy role he played under Ben Johnson in Chicago. Ranks him 3rd among AFC East WRs in pure talent (behind [[AJ Brown]] and [[Garrett Wilson]]) but sees him more as a No. 2 than a true No. 1 at this stage, with a "good solid 2026" plausible.
