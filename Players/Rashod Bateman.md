# Rashod Bateman

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-20 - Matt Waldman]]) [Best Ball]: called Bateman the Ravens' "only hope" at WR this year among the options discussed (vs. Elijah Surratt, Jacoby Lane, Devontez Walker), and said he's "good enough" when healthy — the issue has been health/consistency, not talent. Referenced a 2024 career-high of 756 yards and 9 TDs as the target if he gets back to that level. With Bateman going roughly wide receiver 92 (~269th overall) per ADP discussed on the show, Waldman recommended him as a free-square play in best ball drafts specifically.

- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): still a self-described Bateman "holdout" fan, but frames this as realistically his last chance to lock down the Ravens' WR2 role before rookies [[Ja'Kobi Lane]] and [[Elijah Sarratt]] push him out of it — notes the team has drafted multiple direct threats to his role two years running. Reiterates he'd like to see Bateman play less at X, believing it doesn't suit his skill set as well as more off-ball usage would.
