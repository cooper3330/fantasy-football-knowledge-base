# Omar Cooper Jr.

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): a real first-round talent who's currently "almost completely undiscussed" — likely the cheapest first-round-caliber WR you can draft in fantasy this year, in part because a crowded Jets receiver room ([[Garrett Wilson]] as the clear WR1) probably limits him to a smaller role in year one. If re-drafting for 2027 instead, Harmon said Cooper is "probably the right answer," but he's willing to be wrong on the limited-role call for this season.
