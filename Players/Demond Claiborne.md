# Demond Claiborne

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28 - Chris Harris]]): explicitly warns against the training-camp hype building around him, comparing his size/archetype (5'10", 188 lbs, a converted-speed-receiver-type back) to a long list of historical busts in that mold (Dri Archer, Chris Rainey, Naheem Hines, Leon Washington, Garrett Wolfe, Chad Morton), plus his own bust comp for Claiborne in this year's Almanac (Breshard Smith, last year's version of the same hype cycle). Won't be fantasy-useful unless he becomes a true every-down feature back, which Harris doesn't expect — he'd rather bet on [[Jordan Mason]] if the backfield situation improves.
