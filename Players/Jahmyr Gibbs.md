# Jahmyr Gibbs

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28 - Chris Harris]]): his No. 1 overall fantasy pick in most formats. Cutback ability graded as "historically elite" — 6 runs of 40+ yards last season, most in the league. Volatile week to week: 97+ scrimmage yards in half his 2025 games, but under 70 six times, and mostly absent in the fantasy playoffs. With David Montgomery gone (Isaiah Pacheco now the backup), Harris expects Gibbs to be the clear choice inside the 5-yard line once the Lions' O-line gets banged up, as happened late last season — though he's not the primary reason for the RB1 ranking, since bigger backs (Bijan Robinson, Jonathan Taylor) have a stronger goal-line claim generally. The real reason for ranking him first: Harris considers him the best "week winner" in a league where production is increasingly spread out — nobody else at the position matches his explosive-play rate.

- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): noted Gibbs is holding in for a new contract at Lions camp, alongside Bijan Robinson doing the same in Atlanta — not concerned about either situation, expects both deals to get done soon.
