# AJ Brown

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): has Brown ranked above [[Garrett Wilson]] in his own fantasy rankings, but closer than market ADP has it — deliberately baking in decline risk. Even in a lesser-version-of-himself scenario, Harmon still gives him the production edge over Wilson given the Patriots' improved surrounding pieces (offensive line, run game) and pairing with Drake Maye. Notably, when removing team situation entirely and just ranking talent, Harmon said he currently has Garrett Wilson ahead of Brown.
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-28 - Matt Harmon]]): thinks Brown, at this career stage, should be used more off-ball/in the slot — instead reports point to New England using him mostly as an outside X (with [[Romeo Doubs]] taking the vacated slot role), which Harmon called a plan he doesn't love for Brown individually. Noted 2025 decline signs (some of it possibly a hamstring injury playing through it); 3 of his top 4 statistical player comps from last season (Odell Beckham 2019, DK Metcalf 2024, Terry McLaurin 2024) were more X-receiver-role seasons, not clearly "still elite" comps. Still expects him to be a good starting receiver for New England even in a static X role, just not maximized.
