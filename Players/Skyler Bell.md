# Skyler Bell

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-20 - Matt Waldman]]): worth a late redraft selection if you've missed out on [[Rashod Bateman]] or [[Malik Nabers]] — a big-play, multi-position option who could squeeze into the WR3 role with a strong camp or an injury to [[Keon Coleman]]/Josh Palmer/Khalil Shakir. Called it high-upside but boom-bust even at the tail end of drafts.
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): thinks Bell "went in the right range for his skill set" as a Day 3 pick, projects him as a potential speed-Z option, and could open the season as Buffalo's WR3 if he beats out [[Keon Coleman]] or Josh Palmer in camp — a bar Harmon called low to clear. Framed that outcome as "a big win for Skyler Bell."
