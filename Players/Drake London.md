# Drake London

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-30 - Matt Harmon]]): called London possibly his favorite receiver in the league right now. Directly disputes the pre-draft "big contested-catch guy who can't separate" scouting narrative — charting shows career-best 75.7% success vs. man (83rd percentile) and 83.8% vs. zone (88th percentile) last season. Evolution note: his first two years (under Arthur Smith) were pure X-receiver usage (86%/85% on-the-line rates, only 15-17% of snaps off-ball); the last two years under Zach Robinson flipped that to 52.7%/53.3% snaps off-ball as a "big movement Z receiver" (not a true slot player — his actual slot rate dropped from 38.9% to 33.1% even as his off-ball rate rose, meaning the movement is more pre-snap motion than a slot alignment shift). Exceptional on the big breaking routes that come with that role: 89.5% success on posts, 84% on digs, 89.2% on outs. Harmon considers him the model archetype for a modern true WR1 (outside of true tier-one talents like Ja'Marr Chase/Justin Jefferson) specifically because he can credibly play X while also unlocking as a mover. With OC Zach Robinson gone to Tampa Bay, new play-caller Kevin Stefanski (with Tanner Engstrand, ex-Lions passing coordinator under Ben Johnson, calling the passing game) is a scheme Harmon is cautiously optimistic fits London well — Stefanski has a track record of adapting to personnel rather than forcing a fixed system. Real risk flagged: Michael Penix Jr.'s health (a back tweak, not yet doing full team drills) and a newly-signed Kirk Cousins as competition muddy the QB picture. Harmon still expects/wants London to remain "mostly a Z receiver" in 2026.
