# Jauan Jennings

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Jeff Erickson ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28 - Chris Harris]]): both describe him as a tough, high-floor possession receiver ("if you need seven yards, I'll get you eight"), willing to go over the middle and take hits, sure-handed — but lower fantasy ceiling than [[Jordan Addison]] since he lacks a big-play deep-threat element. Erickson noted he'll have standout games against defenses that lack quality cornerback depth.
