# Jonathan Brooks

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27 - Matt Waldman]]): fully "aboard the hype train" — rates his football smarts, footwork, contact balance, pass-blocking, and receiving among the better instincts in his draft class, placing his movement skill between Bijan Robinson and Jonathan Taylor (not as explosive as either, but in that company for feel/vision). Projects a lead role over [[Chuba Hubbard]]: ~200-210 carries to Hubbard's ~120-125, ~900 rushing yards to Hubbard's ~500, and roughly a 2:1 rushing-TD edge — plus expects Brooks to out-produce Hubbard as a receiver too, albeit by a closer margin. Noted rising ADP (recently RB40 to RB34) against Hubbard's falling one as a real market inefficiency worth exploiting.
