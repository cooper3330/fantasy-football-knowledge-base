# Pat Bryant

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): his pick to be Denver's third receiver / primary big-slot option, consistent with Harmon's own pre-draft prospect charting on him.
