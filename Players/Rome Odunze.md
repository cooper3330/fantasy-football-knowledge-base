# Rome Odunze

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28 - Chris Harris]]) and guest Jeff Erickson: recovering from a foot stress fracture; Odunze's own "new normal" comment about the foot worried both hosts more than it should have, in their view — Erickson plans to watch practice participation rather than trust reporting. Harris (WR21) and Erickson (WR24) both rank him ahead of [[Luther Burden]], with a similar gap between them. Harris sees real upward mobility — into the Zay Flowers/Ladd McConkey range (~WR19) — if he has a fully clean camp with no missed practices, but clear downward mobility if he starts sitting out reps. Both were skeptical that a DJ Moore trade automatically means a target windfall for Odunze/Burden, given Ben Johnson's run-heavy tendencies and a new pass-catching tight end added via the draft.

- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): positive update — Odunze arrived at camp saying his foot "feels good," contrary to months of "his foot healed weird" chatter. Consistent with Harris's stated framework from the prior show: a clean camp supports the case for moving him up.
