# Bo Nix

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): sees real mechanical questions to work through — arm strength is "a little average," and his most-charted deep route (the post, ~11% of his throws) graded poorly (33.3% success rate). Co-host James Koh flagged Nix's checkdown/flat rate (~29% combined) as needing to drop significantly for the passing game to open up; Harmon agreed but expects it to improve with [[Jaylen Waddle]] added. Also flagged as things to monitor: a lingering ankle issue from a second cleanup surgery (bone spurs) after the injury that ended his prior season before the AFC Championship, and a new play-caller (Davis Webb) taking over duties from noted control-freak Sean Payton for the first time in Payton's career — an arrangement of uncertain durability past the season's first month.
