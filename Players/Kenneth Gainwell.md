# Kenneth Gainwell

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27 - Matt Waldman]]): "absolutely feeling" him overtaking [[Bucky Irving]] as the Bucs' lead back, given real doubts about Irving's size/durability (played most of college around 178-185 lbs before bulking up in the NFL). Draws a long-developer comp to Tiki Barber and Priest Holmes — both smart, versatile backs without elite timed speed who each took ~5 years to have a breakout season. Backs it with real 2025 production: from Week 11 on, 13.3 carries/85 yards/17.8 fantasy points per game, a top-6 scoring pace among all RBs over that stretch. Season projection if he wins the job clearly: 1,000-1,200 total yards, double-digit TDs, top-10/15 RB finish. Calls him a safe RB3/4 pick capable of returning RB2 value.
