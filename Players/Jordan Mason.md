# Jordan Mason

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28 - Chris Harris]]): pushes back on the fantasy community writing Mason off for last year's poor Vikings offensive environment — notes (with guest Jeff Erickson agreeing) his underlying production was essentially the same as his prior 49ers usage, just less efficient behind a worse offensive line; not a receiving or third-down back, which won't change. Currently ranked RB41 (behind Aaron Jones' RB38), but Harris said if the Vikings situation turns around, Mason — not rookie [[Demond Claiborne]] — is the one he'd actually trust to capitalize as the power/goal-line option.
