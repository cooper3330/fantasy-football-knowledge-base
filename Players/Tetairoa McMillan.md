# Tetairoa McMillan

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-28 - Matt Harmon]]): buys the report that McMillan will play more in the slot in 2026 (was charted at just a 15.2% slot rate last season — 101st of 117 qualifying WRs, squarely in the same X-only bucket as Courtland Sutton/Nico Collins/DK Metcalf), though he's more skeptical of the specific "1,500 yards" ceiling some are floating, since that requires a broader passing-offense change too. His best statistical games last year (10-99 vs. Buffalo, 8-130-2TD vs. Atlanta) came in the few contests where his slot rate approached 20%. Harmon's development comp for him is Drake London, who went from X-only usage to 30%+ slot snaps over his first two-to-four seasons and took a real leap as a result — Harmon isn't projecting McMillan to become a true slot-heavy player like Chris Godwin/Cooper Kupp, just a meaningfully bigger slot share than his rookie year. Considers him a legitimate No. 1 receiver already, aided by rookie Chris Brazzell's arrival freeing up more flexible usage of the room (with Jalen Coker also pushing for snaps). Notes McMillan himself has expressed wanting more off-ball usage in a direct conversation with Harmon.

- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27 - Matt Waldman]]): reversed his prior skepticism ("all last year I was like, fuck that") on McMillan's slot-usage reports now that Carolina's own staff has signaled the plan. Identifies McMillan's real 2025 weakness as struggling to beat press coverage at the line — last year only Jalen Coker could reliably win press reps on that roster; rookie Chris Brazzell adds a second option, freeing McMillan from press-heavy X reps. Compares the likely outcome to Drake London's situation rather than a pure contested-catch/T.J.-Higgins-style profile — ceiling could reach London-like target volume (top-5 overall) if Carolina's QB play improves, but "not happening this year." For 2026 specifically, calls him "more of a low-end wide receiver one at best."
