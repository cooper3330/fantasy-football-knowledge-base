# Courtland Sutton

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): the clear, static X-receiver of Denver's offense under Sean Payton — snap rates outside/on-the-line have stayed high and consistent (86%/79% in 2024, similar in 2025), never dropping below 73% on-ball in any charted season since Payton arrived. Described as a limited separator overall (65.2% success rate on the out route was cited as evidence there's no real speed threat on the roster without [[Jaylen Waddle]]), but solid in press/contested situations and a respectable dig-route runner relative to how often Bo Nix throws it.
