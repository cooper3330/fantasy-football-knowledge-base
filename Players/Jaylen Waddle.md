# Jaylen Waddle

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-23 - Matt Harmon]]): one of his biggest "flag plants" of the offseason — has Waddle ranked as a top-12/13 fantasy WR while ADP has him going in the 20s at the position, calling that gap "kind of crazy." Core thesis: in Miami, Waddle was mostly an on-ball/outside X-receiver (77-84% outside, similarly high on-line-of-scrimmage rates 2022-2025) because [[Tyreek Hill]] did the move-around work; in Denver, Harmon projects a role flip toward off-ball/Z-receiver/motion usage, drawing a direct historical comp to 2016 Brandon Cooks in Sean Payton's Saints offense (Cooks' on-line rate dropped from ~54% to 31% that year). Charting support: Waddle posted an ~87% success rate on slants and ~84% on the 9-route in his final Miami season — routes [[Bo Nix]] specifically struggled with mechanically (arm strength) but succeeds against on a rate basis, and routes the Broncos otherwise lack a reliable target for. Two acknowledged risks Harmon doesn't dismiss: Waddle's injury history (missed chunks of multiple seasons), though he waves off the "Sean Payton always rotates receivers" concern given the 1st + 3rd round draft capital Denver spent to acquire him.
