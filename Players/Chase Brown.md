# Chase Brown

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): an early flag-player pick two years ago whose price has now caught up — no longer great value at ADP. Real injury/workload concern given his smaller frame and lack of a proven complementary back (Samaje Perine isn't a real answer); Harris would actually welcome the Bengals adding a competent second back, since it would push Brown's ADP down without necessarily hurting his per-game upside if he stays the lead. According to [[Matt Harmon]] (same episode): "really into Chase Brown this year," expects the Bengals passing attack (and offense generally) to take a step forward with a healthy Joe Burrow, which lifts Brown's receiving-game value too.
