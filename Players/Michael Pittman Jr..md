# Michael Pittman Jr.

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-30 - Matt Harmon]]): traded from Indianapolis to Pittsburgh; charted as fairly versatile (65% snaps outside, 58.8% off-ball in 2024), enough that Harmon expects him to soak up some X-receiver snaps alongside [[DK Metcalf]] rather than being purely a Z/slot piece.

- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): both praise him as a durable, physical, professional receiver ("a contact magnet" who "takes so much punishment," per Harris) and rank him essentially even with [[DK Metcalf]] (see Metcalf's note for exact numbers). Harmon: likes him as a safer, lower-variance option than a boom/bust rookie like Luther Burden when a roster just needs reliable receiver production. [[Jeremy Bernard]] is the name both are watching as the waiver-wire replacement if Pittman gets hurt.
