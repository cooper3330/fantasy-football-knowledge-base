# DK Metcalf

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-30 - Matt Harmon]]): skeptical of a dramatic "Drake London-style" role transformation now that Arthur Smith is gone (replaced by Mike McCarthy) — unlike London, Metcalf doesn't profile as a credible big-slot option; he's been a near-pure outside X (84.5% outside-snap rate last season, 17th-highest among 200+-route WRs, alongside similarly static players like Courtland Sutton and Adonai Mitchell). Does expect some movement increase given McCarthy's own comments about moving him around more, since he's the only receiver on the roster who profiles as a true vertical press-beater — teammates Michael Pittman and Jeremy Bernard can only share that load a little. Flags a specific charted weakness from his 2024 tape as unusually poor for a player of his caliber: 47% success rate on curl routes and 66% on comebacks.

- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]) and [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): ranked essentially even with [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] (Harris: standard WR35 Metcalf/WR37 Pittman, PPR WR33 Pittman/WR38 Metcalf) — the two are close enough that roster construction, not a talent gap, should decide which one you'd rather own. Harmon reiterated skepticism that camp "he'll move around more" reports will change much, given how structurally static Pittsburgh's personnel groupings are likely to stay under Mike McCarthy/Aaron Rodgers.
