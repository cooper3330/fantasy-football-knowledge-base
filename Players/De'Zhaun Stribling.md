# De'Zhaun Stribling

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-30 - Matt Harmon]]): projects him to open the year as the 49ers' WR3 at worst, with a real chance to lock that down further given [[Christian Kirk]]'s calf strain. Profile: 6'2"/207 lbs, 91st-percentile 40 time, 82nd-percentile broad jump; charted 68.4% success vs. man and 67.9% vs. press as a rookie — solid, not spectacular — with real strength on in-breakers (83% success on digs, ~81% on slants). Harmon calls him a high-effort, physical "glue guy" type (good blocker, strong hands, plays bigger than his college billing suggested) who profiles as more than a straight X — capable of playing some off-ball/slot reps too. Notably skeptical of the broader fan/aggregator hype cycle around Stribling, which he attributes to a "reach" narrative from draft weekend more than anything Stribling has actually done since.

- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): raised his rank given the [[Ricky Pearsall]] injury uncertainty, though only into "late-round draftable territory" — pushed back on any 9th-round-or-earlier valuation as overreaching for an unproven Day 3 rookie ("an absolute cipher... nobody knows if he can play").
