# Cyrus Allen

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27 - Matt Waldman]]): explicitly fades him for 2026 redraft value ("unless there's injuries across the board, fuck that") despite liking his long-term/dynasty upside, comparing his playmaking profile to a Tank Dell type. Cites a crowded target picture (Jalen Royals, Tyquan Thornton, Xavier Worthy, and Travis Kelce all ahead of him in the pecking order) as the reason he'd need a lot of injury luck to be startable this year.

- 2026-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-30 - Matt Harmon]]): getting notable first-team reps early in camp, per beat reporting. Harmon's case for taking it seriously: Kansas City's receivers as a group struggle badly against man coverage, while Allen graded a strong 75% success rate vs. man in his rookie-year charting — a real skill-set fit for a specific team need. Self-aware about his own pattern of over-drafting undersized separators (jokingly cites his own "Matt Harmon fell for it again" history), and notes he's the analyst furthest above market consensus on Allen in his own dynasty rankings tool — but still frames this as an early, low-confidence signal to file away on a 5th-round rookie, not a redraft-changing development.
