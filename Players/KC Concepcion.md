# KC Concepcion

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): a real fan, says he was "ahead of consensus" on him as a prospect. Acknowledges catch-point issues/drops but rates his separation ability and man-coverage-beating skill as legitimate; floats a Zay-Flowers-style usage plan. Thinks he's a better player than incumbent [[Jerry Jeudy]] and has a real path to passing him on the depth chart — could climb into the mid-40s among WRs (from ~130ish overall currently) if Jeudy is released, higher still if Shedeur Sanders (viewed as a plus-accuracy, quick-trigger passer by Harmon) wins the Browns' QB job, which Harmon expects.
