# Tahj Brooks

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-20 - Matt Waldman]]): droppable in most redraft leagues right now — expects a special-teams role fitting his draft slot, worth monitoring from afar rather than rostering. Said he "could make lemonade for somebody else" if there's an injury elsewhere, but isn't worth holding as a lottery ticket.

- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31 - Chris Harris]]): still a "black box" behind [[Chase Brown]] in Cincinnati — didn't force the coaching staff's hand last year, and neither host has enough camp signal yet to say whether that changes. Harmon floated that the Bengals are a team that could realistically trade for a complementary back (mentioning James Connor as a hypothetical target) rather than lean on Brooks, which would cap his outlook if it happens.
