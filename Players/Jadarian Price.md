# Jadarian Price

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-20 - Matt Waldman]]): skeptical of drafting Price as the presumptive Seattle lead back — he still has to prove he can pass protect, pass catch, and handle the load in live games. Waldman called the range of outcomes wide: on the good end Price is "the lead back, they get what they paid for," but nothing is certain given competition from Zach Charbonnet, George Holani, and Emmanuel Wilson. Given the uncertainty, Waldman said he'd rather draft this backfield's later picks than its earlier ones. His going ADP was noted as running back 26 in the conversation.
