# Matthew Golden

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28 - Chris Harris]]), agreeing with guest Jeff Erickson: both explicitly refuse to chase the training-camp hype narrative around Golden (built partly on Green Bay not having drafted a first-round WR in decades) — Harris called his rookie-season tape "awful" and specifically cited a woefully underthrown deep ball as a low point. Harris has him at WR54 (a "14th round pick, total flyer" at best, won't pay more than that); Erickson independently landed at WR50, calling it a close match.
