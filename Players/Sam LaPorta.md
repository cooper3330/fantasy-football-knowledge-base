# Sam LaPorta

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28 - Chris Harris]]), agreeing with guest Jeff Erickson: his back injury (ruptured discs) wasn't the most severe type but is "not unserious," and he's reportedly in better shape now. Both agreed his 2025 absence changed the Lions' offense considerably — Erickson specifically noted there wasn't much depth behind him when he was out. Flagged as one of the health situations worth extra attention heading into camp.

- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27 - Matt Waldman]]): now more comfortable on the health question after Bob Harris made the case the back issue is less concerning than feared. Waldman's real concern is target competition, not health or talent: projects only ~70-75 targets for LaPorta given [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] (~80-90 targets), Amon-Ra St. Brown (130-150), and [[Jameson Williams]] (~130) all likely drawing more volume — a ceiling of 90-100 targets only materializes if one of those three gets hurt or Detroit leans more run-heavy.
