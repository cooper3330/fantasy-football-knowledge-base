# DeMario Douglas

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-30 - Matt Harmon]]): despite being "pretty buried" last season (25.9% of snaps), was specifically weaponized against man coverage — 3.2 yards per route run vs. man, 6th-most among 200+-route WRs, trailing only names like George Pickens/Nico Collins. Harmon's charting shows he's actually a better man-coverage receiver than a zone one, part of why he's never become a true full-time slot player. Still projects New England's top 3 to be [[AJ Brown]], [[Kayshon Boutte]], and [[Romeo Doubs]], with Douglas as a situational 4th piece — though co-host Bob Harris pushed back, arguing Douglas's 50/50 inside-outside, man-coverage-beating skill set is actually the better statistical match for the vacated Stefon Diggs role (52.3% slot snaps last year) than Boutte, who profiles more as a pure outside X/Z split.
