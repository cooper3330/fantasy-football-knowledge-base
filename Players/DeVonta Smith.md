# DeVonta Smith

## Expert Takes
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-28 - Matt Harmon]]): emphatically bullish on Smith stepping into the Eagles' true WR1/X role now that [[AJ Brown]] is gone. Unlike some "unproven in the role" prospects, Harmon says there's already a 3-year proof of concept: Smith's rookie season and first two years playing alongside Brown, he was primarily the X-receiver (86%/81%/73% snaps outside those years) and averaged 63.6 yards/game even sharing the field with an elite receiver. 2025 charting: 76% success rate vs. man (85th percentile), 85% vs. zone (90th percentile, a career best), 77.6% vs. press (84th percentile) — was #2 in the league behind only Brown in success vs. man the prior year. Standout routes: 93% success on outs (10% of his routes), 94.5% on curls (19% of his routes), 85% on digs, ~79% on posts. Two acknowledged limitations: below-average success on 9/go routes (Harmon calls it not damning given league-wide go-route rates are declining anyway, though new starter [[Jalen Hurts]] specifically favors the go route, a potential mismatch), and limited after-the-catch production (in space on only 38.5% of catches, goes down at first contact 70% of the time) — Harmon expects [[McKay Lemon]] to be the offense's YAC/"layup" option instead. Overall verdict: "an overdue promotion to the number one receiver role... his talent is commensurate with it."
- 2026-07-28 — Risk factor flagged in the same episode: co-host James Koh raised that Philadelphia's receiver room got small and fast this offseason (Smith, [[McKay Lemon]], [[Dontayvion Wicks]] all under 205 lbs; only Wicks over 6'0"), which may not fit Jalen Hurts' downfield-accuracy profile. Harmon called it a fair concern worth tracking rather than dismissing it, while still expecting the room's talent to complement each other.
