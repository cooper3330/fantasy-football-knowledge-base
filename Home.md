# Fantasy Football Knowledge Base

An LLM-maintained wiki of fantasy football advice, built from podcast and media
transcripts of select experts. See [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for how this wiki is
maintained.

## How it works
Drop a transcript — or a link/summary of one — into a conversation with Claude
Code, and it will log the source, extract attributed player- and concept-level
takes, and weave them into the wiki: creating new notes and updating existing
ones as needed.
