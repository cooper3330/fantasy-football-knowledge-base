# Marquise Brown

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-13]]), floated by co-host James Koh as a hypothetical Kansas City free-agent fit at outside receiver: Harmon rejects the idea on archetype grounds. "That just doesn't do it for me. I don't need a speed guy here... I like Hollywood Brown for the player he is, but he's another zone-beating type." His explicit read of what Kansas City actually needs is a receiver who can beat man coverage and win contested-catch situations — "Hollywood Brown is a nice player, but he's neither of those things." See [[Zone vs Man Route Running]].

- 2024-03-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-05]]): "I kind of tend to think he's not back in Arizona" — expects Brown to leave in free agency, which would leave the Cardinals with "a gaping hole" at wide receiver even with him still nominally on the roster; sorted Arizona into the "critical need" wide receiver bucket regardless of how his free agency resolves.

- 2024-03-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-07]]): full RP
  charting on a career-worst 2023 (101 targets, 51/571/1, career-low 50.5%
  catch rate) that Harmon partly excuses as an injury year (a heel/foot
  injury, worse in the back half of the season) but not entirely — career-low
  success rates vs. man (59%) and zone (78.8%, 51st percentile). He's "never
  even crossed 65%" success rate vs. press in his career and is not a
  high-quality press-coverage beater at his size, yet Arizona deployed him on
  the line as an X receiver on 77.4% of snaps (only 16.2% from the slot) — a
  scheme mismatch Harmon says the Cardinals only got "50 right": correctly
  targeting him on in-breaking routes vs. zone, but wrong to line him up
  outside on the line so often given a sub-75% slant success rate (down from
  84-87% in 2020/2022) and almost no out-breaking route usage. Also poor after
  the catch and in contested catches due to size — broken down on first
  contact on 82.4% of in-space attempts, a number trending worse year over
  year. Harmon's read: a complementary piece best used at flanker/slot on
  intermediate crossers and dig/post/out routes in a play-action offense, not
  a true WR1, but still enters his age-27 season with "a little something
  something" if he stays healthy. See [[Reception Perception Methodology]] and
  [[Scheme vs Talent]].

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: reacting to his signing with Kansas City — "he actually has a quarterback" (Patrick Mahomes) and a scheme for the first time, having only ever had one or the other in Baltimore/Arizona. Grades him "75 to 80% Tyreek Hill" — lacking Hill's physicality/contact balance but with comparable speed and better hands than his drop reputation suggests, "definitely a high upgrade" over Marquez Valdes-Scantling. Explicitly not calling him a great receiver merely unlocked by system, but "a damn good receiver" who fits the vacated role well.
