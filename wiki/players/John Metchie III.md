# John Metchie III

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]): "showed some flashes" filling in during Houston's Wild Card win over Cleveland after [[Noah Brown]]'s injury, playing outside on 76.9% of his routes (3 catches, 44 yards) — floated as Houston's likely flanker/X option opposite [[Nico Collins]] for the Divisional Round at Baltimore, with anytime-touchdown odds of +600. No settled long-term role projection given beyond the immediate Baltimore game.
- 2024-01-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-23]]): a real downgrade from the Wild Card flashes — after the Divisional Round loss to Baltimore, Harmon says flatly he "has proven nothing to this point in his career," grouped with Robert Woods (not a tracked page) as replacement-level depth rather than a real answer opposite [[Nico Collins]]. Used as the explicit case for why Houston needs to add outside receiver help even though the top of the depth chart is set.
- 2024-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-29]]): Metchie was reportedly having a strong camp and a nice preseason while Houston explored trading him, and Harmon expects he could be on the move. He thought the second-round selection was a bit of a reach at the time and does not expect Metchie to become a great player, but sees a functional NFL receiver who could be somebody's slot — more interesting on another team than buried behind [[Nico Collins]], [[Tank Dell]] and [[Stefon Diggs]]. He credits the context: Metchie missed his entire first year battling leukemia, so simply returning to the field in 2023 was a significant achievement.
- 2024-11-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-12]]): a sell on Metchie as a rest-of-season productive player despite six targets, five catches, 71 yards and a touchdown against Detroit — his best game to date. Harmon respects the comeback story and thinks Metchie can be a solid role player, but says he has only shown hidden spurts and doubts he becomes an integral part of the passing game; he graded Metchie a slot-only player coming out of college and wonders whether [[Xavier Hutchinson]] offers more of a power-slot dimension. This is a rare split with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert), who is a tentative buy purely on role: with [[Stefon Diggs]] out there is a vacant slot job, [[Tank Dell]] is a natural flanker and [[Nico Collins]] belongs outside, and Houston has no answers for pressure — the quick-hitting inside role [[Stefon Diggs]] filled is exactly what they need. Metchie ran a 51 percent slot rate in the game but fewer routes than both Dell and Hutchinson.
- 2025-11-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman does not believe Metchie has become the Jets' true WR1 just because he's playing that role with [[Garrett Wilson]] out -- charting his targets, most came on two-man route combinations where he was schemed open or worked underneath, and when the offense needed him to win a contested single-coverage rep on 4th-and-2, he couldn't get open. Waldman said he'd rank three or four other receivers ahead of Metchie as a deep-desperation flex, though he's usable in that range. He and [[Adonai Mitchell]] were effectively the Jets' top-two receivers on the field constantly this game, giving both a good extended look down the stretch.
- 2025-11-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-25]]): caught six passes for 65 yards against Baltimore off a Breece Hall screen that set up the score, a legitimately good line. Still, Harris says 'at this point in the season, it's really hard...to chase Jets receivers' and lists Metchie only as an honorable-mention waiver option -- 'it was good, but I don't want a lot of Jets.'
- 2025-12-02 — According to Matt Harmon ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-02]]) [Redraft]: Harmon called Metchie 'found money' for the Jets and 'absolutely a usable NFL player' in the slot, though not a starting-caliber option Harmon would pencil in without competition. Metchie had two drops against Atlanta but Harmon said he's happy to see him recovering his career after missing his first season while battling leukemia.
