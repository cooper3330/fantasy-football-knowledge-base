# John Metchie III

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]): "showed some flashes" filling in during Houston's Wild Card win over Cleveland after [[Noah Brown]]'s injury, playing outside on 76.9% of his routes (3 catches, 44 yards) — floated as Houston's likely flanker/X option opposite [[Nico Collins]] for the Divisional Round at Baltimore, with anytime-touchdown odds of +600. No settled long-term role projection given beyond the immediate Baltimore game.
- 2024-01-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-23]]): a real downgrade from the Wild Card flashes — after the Divisional Round loss to Baltimore, Harmon says flatly he "has proven nothing to this point in his career," grouped with Robert Woods (not a tracked page) as replacement-level depth rather than a real answer opposite [[Nico Collins]]. Used as the explicit case for why Houston needs to add outside receiver help even though the top of the depth chart is set.
- 2024-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-29]]): Metchie was reportedly having a strong camp and a nice preseason while Houston explored trading him, and Harmon expects he could be on the move. He thought the second-round selection was a bit of a reach at the time and does not expect Metchie to become a great player, but sees a functional NFL receiver who could be somebody's slot — more interesting on another team than buried behind [[Nico Collins]], [[Tank Dell]] and [[Stefon Diggs]]. He credits the context: Metchie missed his entire first year battling leukemia, so simply returning to the field in 2023 was a significant achievement.
