## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-15 — Guest **Daniel Harms** (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-15]] [Dynasty]: names him his most overrated 2024 running back prospect, pushing directly back on public "RB1 of the class" chatter. His case: heavy college volume was largely a product of Wisconsin having no other real receiving/rushing options after an offensive overhaul, not evidence of an elite skill set; he grades as a poor pass protector who doesn't yet get his hands or feet in the right spots, a limited lateral athlete without the change-of-direction to make defenders miss in tight spaces, and lacking a true third-gear breakaway burst. Ranks him behind Bucky Irving, Trey Benson, and [[Blake Corum]] in the class — all backs he credits with real lateral suddenness that Allen lacks — and would not have him in his personal top five at the position.
