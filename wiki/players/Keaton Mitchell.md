# Keaton Mitchell

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-08-22 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)] [Dynasty]: Mitchell makes Harris's super-deep sleeper list for the third consecutive year — first named in 2023 before [[Derrick Henry]] was a Raven, when he was a very fast, smaller-framed back who got hurt in his first camp, debuted halfway through his rookie year, went off in a blowout against the Seahawks, worked as a satellite back, then tore his ACL that December. Harris put him on the 2024 list hoping he would become the passing-down alternative to Henry, but Mitchell only got back to game-level fitness around Week 17. He is healthy again and was talked up in early August as a big-play threat and 'jitterbug' guy, but Harris no longer expects him to displace [[Justice Hill]], because Mitchell has not proved himself in pass protection the way Hill has — he may be more of a special-teams player. Touches are hard to project; it would probably take an injury to Henry or Hill for him to matter. The bull case: Baltimore has no other truly quick or truly fast back (Henry is very fast once going, not at the start), and the offense could be good enough to support two fantasy backs. Explicitly not a draft recommendation in normal leagues.
