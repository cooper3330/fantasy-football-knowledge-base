# Jaylen Wright

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: named one of four backs Angelo grades as early-round talents regardless of actual draft capital, alongside [[Jonathan Brooks]], [[Blake Corum]], and [[Dylan Laube]]. Comps him to a rawer [[Travis Etienne]] (not tracked) coming out of Clemson — real high-ceiling flash including brilliant spin-move plays, undercut by the same flaw Etienne had early: turning through contact too often and absorbing hits he didn't need to take. Same episode, according to [[Matt Waldman]]: rates him lower than Angelo and lower than most public boards — in the same range as [[Blake Watson]] rather than a top-5 back — specifically on ball security and contact-balance concerns against certain shot types that graded a little weaker on tape than expected. Explicitly conditional: if those two things improve, Waldman puts him back in the "reliable committee back" conversation; until then, "gotta prove these two things." Both hosts flag him as an elite high-school-caliber accelerator — Waldman notes a 55-meter time at a listed weight near 210 lbs that both call an excellent mark for his size — whose NFL translation depends on learning to play slower the way Etienne eventually did: weaponizing that acceleration through contact rather than trying to bounce or spin away from it.
- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast [Dynasty]: his most detailed positive take yet — despite a Tevin Coleman-type (not tracked) archetype that historically struggles with it, Wright shows real skill setting up lead blockers and moving linebackers into blocks, giving him "a little more saving grace" than Coleman had at Indiana. Calls his movement "one of the most appealing facets of his game": strong stop-start skills, efficient hip mobility, and footwork that sets up creases and forces missed tackles in space. Specifically flags his high-knee "kicking of his heels to his backside" to extricate his legs from wraps as "one of the most Alvin Kamara-like aspects of his game."
- 2024-04-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-29]]): addressed Miami adding a fourth notable back into an already crowded room (De'Von Achane, Raheem Mostert, Christopher Brooks, Salvon Ahmed) by framing Wright as Mostert's eventual replacement as the home-run/change-of-pace threat, given Mostert's age. He said he's 'feeling it' as a football evaluation — having pre-draft-comped Wright's aspiration directly to Mostert — but conceded it's 'not great for fantasy managers' given the added competition for touches. He guessed Christopher Brooks and Salvon Ahmed are likeliest to lose roster spots if Miami prioritizes speed, with Jeff Wilson also at risk due to age, and floated that special-teams value (not just backfield role) explains why some late-round backs land where they do.
- 2024-05-17 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-17]]) [Dynasty]: Wright is a running back Angelo is willing to reach for because Miami's backfield is aging out around him — he flags that [[Raheem Mostert]] will be 32 by the end of next season and likely playing his last usable year, comparable to [[James Connor]] turning 30 in Arizona, while the team wants to keep [[De'Von Achane]] fresh through efficient rather than heavy usage. Angelo projects Wright could be the Dolphins' touch leader in a good offense by the end of next season.
- 2024-08-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-12]]): Wright is his lead example of preseason clip inflation. A reversal-of-field run that Brian Baldinger reviewed drew thousands of likes and retweets, but Waldman notes it is exactly the kind of play Wright made at Tennessee, so it is not new information — it only confirms he is comfortable enough to play to his tape. He says there are real reasons to be excited about Wright, but the thing to evaluate is judgment on a play-to-play basis, not whether he can reverse field and show speed once. He adds that many of the analysts amplifying these clips are seeing the player for the first time and are also chasing clicks.
- 2025-08-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-14]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Wright is the number 10 flag player for 2025 and the lowest-ranked name on the list, going in the 15th round and 'absolutely free' in drafts. Harris liked but did not love the rookie tape — straight-line fast, not an electric cutter or jitterbug like [[De'Von Achane]], but possibly better vision, with carries where Wright appeared to see what was coming, hit the pause button and restart. He stresses the case is not a prediction that Achane cedes enough work to make a teammate immediately playable; it is price plus Achane being 'indisputably tiny boned,' making alternatives valuable. He notes Jamie Eisenberg (not tracked) doubted Wright as a touchdown vulture, possibly citing his roughly 170-pound frame, and that Wright was stuffed three straight times near the goal line in an exhibition. With [[Alexander Mattison]] out for the year, Harris will roster him a few weeks to see how Miami deploys the backfield.
- 2025-08-21 — According to Jake Ciely of The Athletic (guest, NOT a tracked expert) on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-21]] [Redraft (Standard)]: Wright continues to fall on his face every week and simply has not looked good on tape, beyond the nine preseason carries and a goal-line series where he got stuffed — Ciely stressed he is not focused on the yards-per-carry noise, just how Wright looks. He would still take a flyer on Wright over [[Ollie Gordon II]] right now, but sees the job as genuinely available. [[Chris Harris]] said he does not watch the preseason, still likes Wright as a 15th-round flag player, and noted no beat reporter is describing Wright as clamoring for the number one job — which he would prefer, since an undeniable Wright would make the situation cleaner.
- 2025-08-22 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Wright limped off the practice field on Thursday with Miami beat reporters saying it did not look minor — he walked off on his own but limped heavily. Harris had Wright as the last of his 'flag players' by draft location, going around the 15th round, and says that if Wright misses significant time he is no longer a particularly good flag player even at that price. The knock-on effect is that [[Ollie Gordon II]] would stop being a super-deep sleeper and become a genuinely draftable back in standard-sized leagues, since he and Mike Boone would be all that is left behind [[De'Von Achane]], who himself has a calf injury. Harris adds the caveat that a serious enough injury might compel the Dolphins to sign a veteran such as [[Brian Robinson Jr.]], which would push Gordon back down. Framed alongside the [[Joe Mixon]] news as the flag-player list taking another knock before the season starts.
- 2025-08-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-25]]): after Wright limped off in a joint practice against Jacksonville, Miami is now calling him week to week — not many weeks before games start counting — so Harris lowered him and raised [[Ollie Gordon II]] to sit essentially side by side in the deep-flyer band with [[Isaac Guerendo]]. That drops Wright off the flag-player list. Harris says there are probably other players he would take ahead of Wright in the 14th or 15th round at this exact moment, though he hedges 'but maybe not' and tells listeners to check the live ranks. He stresses it is not too late for Wright to get healthy and contribute, just bad timing.
- 2025-08-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-28]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Wright will not play in Week 1, and Miami's response was to bring Jeff Wilson back after the 49ers cut him. Harris says there is a chance Wilson is active for the opener but nobody is starting him, and the only value in tracking the situation is seeing who actually gets the work behind [[De'Von Achane]]. In his Almanac update he refers to 'poor dumb Jaylen Wright' as one of the profiles that changed most in red text — a clear downgrade from where Wright was valued earlier in the summer.
- 2025-09-19 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-19]]): Wright was active on Thursday night at Buffalo and did not play a single snap. Harris says his bad training camp and his injuries have definitely cost him, and that there is 'no reason at all' to roster him until and unless [[De'Von Achane]] gets hurt — with [[Ollie Gordon II]] having taken the short-yardage and goal-line work in the meantime.
- 2025-09-23 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-23]]): 'I don't know what Jaylen Wright's role is — it doesn't seem like he has one.' Harris volunteered that he was 'all over Jaylen Wright this summer before he got hurt, before he had a bad camp, and that just turned out to be absolutely wrong'; Wright was available for Thursday night and did not play a single offensive snap. Guest SCOTT FISH (Fantasy Cares, NOT tracked) agreed the return does not threaten [[Ollie Gordon II]], because Wright is the backup to De'Von Achane rather than the power back.
