# Antonio Gibson

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-20]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman called Antonio Gibson a worthwhile late handcuff, pointing to how Alex Van Pelt's Cleveland offense got fantasy viability out of two backs and noting New England runs a good amount of gap-scheme runs that fit Gibson's traits as a scheme-dependent athlete. But he was blunt that "if Antonio Gibson hasn't done it in four years," it's hard to project him becoming a poor man's Kareem Hunt behind Rhamondre Stevenson — his rushing efficiency lags his reputation, which is inflated by receiving numbers built in a zone-heavy Memphis college scheme rather than a true NFL slot role. His framing: Gibson is a late lottery ticket to take and flip if he pops, not someone to plan on starting.
- 2025-09-22 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Gibson got the series after [[Rhamondre Stevenson]]'s second fumble and looked fast and powerful on two straight carries — then fumbled the second one away at 10:13 of the third quarter and never touched the ball again, playing only three more offensive snaps. He had earlier drawn the play-action fake that sprang Hunter Henry's touchdown. Harris's takeaway is the same as with Stevenson: New England's veterans fumbled themselves out of the game and handed it to [[TreVeyon Henderson]].
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-06]]): Gibson briefly took over as New England's lead back after [[Rhamondre Stevenson]]'s two fumbles and 'was running pretty good,' but the Patriots were still using him to return kicks at 3-3 — a decision Harris questioned given the state of the backfield — and he took a big shot on his knee, had to be helped off, and was crying on the sideline in the second half. Harris's read: 'one imagines Antonio Gibson is probably out for a good long while.'
