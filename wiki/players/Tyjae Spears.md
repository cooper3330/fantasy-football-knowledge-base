# Tyjae Spears

> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded

> Spears"; normalized here to his real name, Tyjae Spears.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-05]] [Redraft (Standard)]: stock is rising regardless, on the strength of his receiving work out of the backfield — he "played pretty well even with that crappy offensive line performance" as a rookie. Two separate tailwinds stack on top of that baseline: new offensive line coach Bill Callahan (father of HC Brian Callahan) arriving to fix one of the league's worst lines, and a probable [[Derrick Henry]] departure in free agency clearing the workload. Waldman's explicit value comp is Cleveland's Jerome Ford, who delivered real fantasy production behind an injury-depleted line — with the caveat "I think Tyjae Spears is more talented than Jerome Ford." Ceiling case if the Titans also address the line (free agency, trade, or draft): a "top-24 back" floor even before accounting for further growth; downside case if Henry somehow stays: muddier but still rising given the O-line investment alone.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: [[Tony Pollard]]'s signing is "short-term fuck it" for Spears's redraft value, which he expects to keep dropping — but calls Spears a buy-low, "Bob Harris value special" for later in drafts, since the Pollard deal reads as insurance rather than a full commitment away from Spears if he outplays Pollard.

- 2024-04-15 — According to **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) and [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]] [Best Ball]: with new Titans OC Nick Holz calling Spears and [[Tony Pollard]] "interchangeable, 1A/1B," Harris takes Spears "as the bargain" — better pass protector, equal-or-better receiver, slightly better inside runner — while Waldman holds few shares of either back at current prices and prefers other value at the same cost.
- 2024-07-12 — According to JJ Zachariason (not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-12]]): Spears will be 'one of my highest rostered running backs this year,' still favoring him over free-agent addition Tony Pollard. He cited Spears' 14.8% target share per game last year as the 6th-best rookie RB target share since 2011 (behind only CMC, Saquon Barkley, Alvin Kamara, Bijan Robinson and Tarik Cohen, who was RB15 in his second year), plus a trend that RBs in the RB25-42 range past year five of their career hit at a worse rate than younger backs -- Pollard enters year six. New OC Brian Callahan has called Spears and Pollard 'interchangeable,' running a straight timeshare rather than divided roles, which JJ likes because it lets the better player win the job. Waldman separately noted the Titans should be more pass-heavy under Callahan with Calvin Ridley added and Will Levis pushing the ball downfield ('no governor'), boosting both backs' value, and relayed that Spears drew heavy Week 1 rookie-year pass-protection reps against blitzing NFL defenders and handled them one-on-one -- evidence a defensive-minded, former pass-rusher head coach in Mike Vrabel trusted him early, which Waldman treats as a strong signal of real talent.
