# Dylan Gabriel

- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-10-27]]) [Redraft]: threw two interceptions against New England's pass rush, including one thrown five yards behind David Njoku and another moon-ball thrown into double coverage. Harris says whatever stats Gabriel, [[Quinshon Judkins]] and Dylan Sampson posted came almost entirely in fourth-quarter garbage time and shouldn't be trusted going forward.
- 2025-12-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-04]]): both hosts said Gabriel looked overwhelmed and 'shouldn't even be a backup,' calling him 'light years' worse than [[Shedeur Sanders]] -- the key difference being Gabriel can't threaten the third level of the defense at all, stripping any play-action threat from the offense. Waldman jokingly comped Gabriel to 'a left-handed Teddy Bridgewater, maybe,' then walked it back as unfair to Bridgewater.
- 2026-01-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-26]]) [Dynasty]: fuck it -- Gabriel projects as, at best, a hopeful backup or a team's number three. Waldman says he does not throw with the level of anticipation required to start, and that the limits of his arm talent were plainly on display over the season. He does not believe Gabriel has the game to ever be a real NFL starter.
- 2026-04-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-28]]): expects Gabriel to eventually be phased out of Cleveland's crowded quarterback room, leaving [[Deshaun Watson]], [[Shedeur Sanders]] and [[Taylen Green]] as the operative options once Watson's grip on the job loosens.
