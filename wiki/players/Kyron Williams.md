- 2026-08-18 — According to Dave Kluge (Football Guys, not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Kluge's #2 ADP surprise -- Williams going in the late 3rd round is too low for a player he grades RB13-15, in the same tier as Josh Jacobs and Javonte Williams. His hesitation: the Rams' second-half 2025 tendency to rotate drives ('one drive on, one drive off'), and concern that [[Blake Corum]] is pushing for a bigger workload. Harris strongly disagrees, saying he'd take Williams in the second round 'without blinking' -- he's finished RB4/RB6/RB8 the last three seasons, gets the high-value work (two-minute drill, goal line), and Harris notes three straight years of the market assuming someone would take his job with nobody ever actually doing it. Both agree Williams is currently the healthiest back in a shallow elite tier that also includes an injured Josh Jacobs and Breece Hall.
