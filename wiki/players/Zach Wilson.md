# Zach Wilson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]]: with the Jets shopping him for a trade, says a Baker-Mayfield-style redemption path is realistically in range if Wilson can sit for a few years, genuinely work at his craft, and take accountability for what he's contributed to his own struggles — "the baseline skills are there." Notes he graded Wilson pre-draft as having enough ability to be a low-end NFL starter, but post-draft tape showed him making mistakes that were also present at BYU and went overlooked at the time; without maturity and real self-correction, thinks he could be out of the league within a couple of years.
- 2024-04-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-22]]): no interest in an NFL team trading for Wilson, arguing it's not worth spending draft capital when a team could get quality special-teams value instead or just sign him off waivers after a cut; per his scouting, Wilson still needs more development time with an uncertain outcome even then.
