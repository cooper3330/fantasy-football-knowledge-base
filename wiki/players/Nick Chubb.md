# Nick Chubb

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023, during the season Chubb lost to a severe knee
> injury. Team/role are as of that date and are almost certainly stale — treat
> as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21 (Pacing and Control)]]): Chubb is the named exception to Waldman's own rule that big backs cannot change direction efficiently in tight space. He is "as big as [[Saquon Barkley]]," still relies on jump-cut-class movement, "and has gotten away with it and been efficient." Waldman walks through the rep that convinced him — a goal-line toss where Chubb hurdles a fallen body and then cuts *outside* around a defensive back waiting at the five, "his outside foot literally turned to the boundary when he lands," pivoting as he lands in extremely tight quarters. He says Chubb has done it more than once and has a similar play against the Eagles.
- 2023-12-21 — [[Brandon Angelo]], same episode, supplying the mechanism: "how he prepares for the ground is super important — he doesn't prepare to catch himself, he prepares to re-accelerate. He's one of the best ever at re-accelerating post-contact." Both hosts call him badly underappreciated; Waldman: "I'd argue he's a Hall of Fame caliber talent — we'll see if he gets the opportunity from the media." Both flag the 2023 knee injury as the live question over what remains of the career.

**Note:** this is the one case in the episode that cuts *against* the
size-and-movement framework rather than for it — recorded as such on
[[Running Back Size and Movement Skills]].
