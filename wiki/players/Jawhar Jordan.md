- 2025-12-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Nick Chubb]] out and [[Woody Marks]] leaving the game early, undrafted Texans back Jordan popped for 15 carries and 101 yards, and Waldman is 'totally feeling' him as the Texans' lead back for the rest of the season if needed, calling him a good playoffs streaming target. He tied it to a broader pattern -- Houston has a recurring history of unheralded backs emerging from nowhere (citing Dominic Davis, Steve Slaton and Arian Foster) -- while cautioning it's only a single game and he's not putting Jordan in that historical company yet, and he was noticeably less excited about Jordan's outlook beyond this season.
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]), with waiver input from guest Dave Kluge of FootballGuys.com (untracked): [[Jawhar Jordan]], a 26-year-old 2024 draftee getting his first NFL regular-season action, took over after [[Woody Marks]]'s ankle injury and looked quick with long speed against a poor Cardinals defense, finishing 15 carries for 101 yards plus 2 catches for 17. Houston plays the Raiders next week. Both hosts flagged it as a very narrow path to relevance -- Nick Chubb could return and Marks' injury is reportedly being downplayed -- so neither is aggressively adding him, but Kluge said if the Marks injury proves worse than expected and Chubb can't return, Jordan would warrant RB3 consideration, maybe higher. Kluge ranked him his No. 5 waiver add; Harris called him only an honorable mention.
- 2025-12-23 — According to Jake Seeley (The Athletic, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-23]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Jordan is his #4 championship waiver add (8% rostered) -- he outplayed [[Nick Chubb]] for lead work against the Raiders (28 snaps to 20) and Seeley says he can be productive on roughly 15 touches, but he ranks him modestly because he expects [[Woody Marks]] to reclaim the lead role in week 17 against the Chargers, with Chubb remaining 'more of a thorn in his side' than a real threat regardless. Seeley notes he actually had Jordan ranked ahead of Chubb even before this game.
- 2026-01-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-19]]) [Dynasty]: [[Jawhar Jordan]] 'rose up out of nowhere' with a big game before getting beat up, with [[Joe Mixon]]'s career likely over. Waldman currently slots him in the same tier as [[Jalen Wright]] -- Houston has [[Woody Marks]] as the lead back and will probably cut Mixon, leaving Jordan a path to complementary touches since Marks lacks power for short-yardage/goal-line work, but Waldman expects the Texans to draft or sign a bigger complementary back over him. If Houston re-signs Chuba Hubbard-type insurance or otherwise adds a back after round five with nothing else at the position, Jordan moves up into the lower end of the [[Ray Davis]] tier -- a deep-roster (25-40 man) waiver watch rather than a hold.
- 2026-07-21 — According to James Koh (untracked co-host) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-21]]) [Best Ball]: bullish on Jawhar Jordan as a rising sleeper, saying he has a firm hold on Houston's RB3 role and predicting he'll make a 'sleeper king' appearance at some point this season. Koh cited Jordan's 101 rushing yards against Arizona in Week 15 last year, the most by any Texans back in a game across the last two seasons (topping figures posted by [[Woody Marks]], [[Dameon Pierce]] and [[Joe Mixon]]), plus a 74-yard game against Buffalo. [[Matt Harmon]] agreed Jordan has real sleeper appeal, while cautioning it's still July and a lot can change before the season.
