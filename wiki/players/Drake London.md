# Drake London

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: "feeling that possibility" on top-15 wide receiver value for 2024, contingent on Atlanta hiring a real head coach and solving its quarterback situation after moving on from Arthur Smith. Attributes Smith's firing mainly to poor quarterback play and media friction rather than misuse of his skill talent, and expects the new staff to make sure London (along with [[Bijan Robinson]] and Kyle Pitts) actually gets used at the level of his talent once a competent passer is in place. Flags that London's ADP will likely still carry a premium, since the industry consensus already expects an Atlanta breakout.
- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: closes out a running "please don't trade London for [[Zay Flowers]]" debate from earlier in the season — "no question to me that London had the more impressive rookie year," with London also roughly six months younger than Flowers. Says the market on the two receivers is close, maybe even slightly favoring Flowers, and calls that a mispricing now that the full season is in.
- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]): one of only five wide receivers to ever earn a tier-one grade ("worth picking in the top 10... a ready-made star") on Harmon's cross-class Reception Perception "stacked board," alongside [[Ja'Marr Chase]], Chris Olave, DeVonta Smith and Garrett Wilson (Olave/Smith/Wilson not tracked) — a durable pedigree marker distinct from Waldman's more recent redraft-value read on him. See [[Reception Perception Methodology]].
- 2024-01-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-30]]): direct scheme-fit optimism tied to Atlanta's new OC hire (Zach Robinson, from the Rams) — Harmon says he'd "love to see" London used on the same big in-breaking routes the Rams ran for Puka Nacua and Cooper Kupp, and calls the incoming offense "well set up to be in line with the league's meta right now" for a talented X-type receiver like London.

- 2024-03-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-12]]: reacting to the finalized [[Kirk Cousins]] signing — expects an uptick in both volume and target quality. Says London's talent profile has no real limitations ("there's nothing where I see... there's a limitation on this guy") and groups him with [[Terry McLaurin]], [[D.J. Moore]] and Allen Robinson (not tracked) as talented X-receivers stuck on bad-quarterback carousels — arguing London is in that same star caliber and projects as a true No. 1 once paired with functional QB play. Calls Cousins especially well-suited to unlock him: Cousins trusts receivers on contested, tight-window throws and loves working the middle of the field on the exact route types (digs, crossers, slants, big posts) where London wins most. Cites Atlanta's WR-starved 2023 offense (209 pass attempts to wide receivers, fewest in the NFL) as the depressed baseline Cousins should lift, and separately flags the receiver depth behind London (Austin Mack, Chris Blair, Josh Ali — none tracked, no meaningful stats) as a real need Atlanta should still address. See [[Weak Quarterback Play and Receiver Value]].
