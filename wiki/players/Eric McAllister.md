# Eric McAllister

- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: McAllister is physical and agile after the catch with strong hip flip and lateral cuts for a tall receiver, keeps his feet moving through direct hits, spins through leg wraps and wards off tacklers with a stiff arm. Waldman says if his route skills develop to match his already-strong catch-point ability, 'look out.'
- 2026-04-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-02]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman flagged TCU's Eric McAllister (6'4', 194) as a fun name worth knowing -- physical, smooth, and strong at the catch point -- who projects as a solid future number-two receiver once he improves his route tree and technique.
- 2026-04-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-21]]) [Dynasty]: Eric McAllister (TCU, 6'4', 194 lbs, 4.56 forty at his pro day) posted a huge 2025 season -- 72 catches, nearly 1,200 yards, 10 touchdowns -- but Harmon flags major red flags alongside real traits. Off the field: a 2024 felony aggravated assault arrest for allegedly threatening a man with a gun, pled down to a misdemeanor terroristic-threat charge with two years probation, a subsequent bond violation, and a failed drug test. Physically, he suffered a Jones fracture at his own pro day. On tape Harmon likes his late hands, ball-tracking, leverage recognition (breaking outside or inside off how he's played) and after-catch/contested ability -- he broke multiple tackles on 24% of in-space attempts -- comping him to 'a poor man's [[Quentin Johnston]] with better hands.' He's also a skewed-alignment receiver (76.8% of snaps at right outside, just 0.8% left). His separation marks are weak: 64% success vs. man, 57% vs. press (both well off Harmon's 70% bar), 77.7% vs. zone. Dane Brugler (The Athletic, untracked) told Harmon he isn't sure McAllister gets drafted at all. Setting the off-field issues aside, Harmon's own grade purely on tape and testing would be an early Round 4 pick; factoring in the character and injury concerns, he thinks McAllister could fall out of the draft entirely as a priority free agent, though he cautioned it only takes one aggressive team (citing the Jermaine Burton precedent, who fell to Round 3 despite bigger red flags and is now out of the league).
