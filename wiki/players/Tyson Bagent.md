# Tyson Bagent

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2024-08-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-19]]): Bagent has a real NFL future even with [[Caleb Williams]] in Chicago — you could see it in 2023, holding his own from a small-school program in a Bears offense with far less weaponry than it has now. Waldman describes him as a very good athlete, good in and out of the pocket, with a very strong arm, and would not be surprised if he is a Raider or elsewhere next year as a starter or on a year-long audition; he expects the Bears eventually to deal him for a nice haul. If Williams gets hurt, Waldman would not be shocked to see Bagent post starter numbers, which makes him a high name on your quarterback waiver-wire watch list.
- 2024-11-14 — According to Adam Harstad (NOT a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-14 (Age Cliffs and Dented Cans)]] [Dynasty]: Bagent is the quarterback version of his institutional-buy-in archetype — an undrafted passer who was not great as a rookie but has held the backup job in back-to-back years, which Harstad reads as a real organizational signal. He is explicit about the format boundary: in 1QB leagues this does not matter, because you need a top-six quarterback to move your bottom line, but in superflex or deep IDP leagues where you already have depth and a luxury roster spot, Bagent is free or close to free and worth the stash. [[Matt Waldman]] said he has already done exactly that in a couple of leagues.
- 2024-11-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-22]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman has added and kept Bagent on good rosters because he looked good enough in 2023 and teams inquired about him over the offseason knowing the Bears were taking [[Caleb Williams]]. Untracked co-host Adam Harstad framed him as the quarterback exception to his own rule — Bagent is quintessentially not a high draft pick, he went undrafted, but he passes the organizational-support test that Harstad usually applies to running backs: the Bears' actions say they like him and want to be in the Bagent business. Harstad's caveat is that the future may simply be a Chase Daniel career holding a clipboard for 17 years, and his standing advice is to assume every waiver add will do nothing so you are never disappointed.
- 2026-02-27 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (untracked co-host) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-27]]): both hosts were baffled by multiple teams' reported trade interest in Chicago backup Tyson Bagent, with Harmon saying he 'moved the needle zero percent' on tape and that the appeal is mostly Ben Johnson's personal affinity for him -- praised repeatedly in press availabilities -- plus Atlanta GM Ian Cunningham's Bears front-office ties and Atlanta's need for a bridge/competition option behind a possible [[Michael Penix Jr.]] reset. Harmon predicted Bagent will eventually become more of a Ben Johnson QB-coach type than a real starter, and both hosts framed the trade buzz as one of several thin-market 'sell jobs' teams are running given a shallow 2026 starting-QB market.
