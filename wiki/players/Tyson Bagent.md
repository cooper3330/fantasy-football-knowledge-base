# Tyson Bagent

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2024-08-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-19]]): Bagent has a real NFL future even with [[Caleb Williams]] in Chicago — you could see it in 2023, holding his own from a small-school program in a Bears offense with far less weaponry than it has now. Waldman describes him as a very good athlete, good in and out of the pocket, with a very strong arm, and would not be surprised if he is a Raider or elsewhere next year as a starter or on a year-long audition; he expects the Bears eventually to deal him for a nice haul. If Williams gets hurt, Waldman would not be shocked to see Bagent post starter numbers, which makes him a high name on your quarterback waiver-wire watch list.
