- 2024-07-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-04]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said Washington built rapport with [[Trevor Lawrence]] late last year (after Christian Kirk's injury) and is his top target of the young receivers discussed, calling him more ready to play right now than [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] and comparing his role to a 'Walmart version' of Robert Woods -- an extension of the run game who wins zone coverage and can become an off-script security blanket for Lawrence. Both hosts said Washington is a contested-catch monster who can win at multiple levels and could realistically be Jacksonville's second- or third-leading target-getter behind [[Christian Kirk]] and [[Evan Engram]], with Waldman floating a long-term Hines Ward-type comp if he keeps developing. - 2024-08-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-05]]): Washington is a reasonable late-round dart throw in 20-round formats and belongs high on a waiver-monitor list through late camp. Waldman's timing argument is the substance: early camp produces highlight athletic plays that inflate everybody, while the later weeks are when offenses test whether receivers can adjust to what quarterbacks want against more complex defenses and game-planned looks — the period when [[Aaron Rodgers]] publicly warned [[Romeo Doubs]] and the 2022 Packers receivers that they would not play if they could not adjust with him. That is when Washington's ability to sync with [[Trevor Lawrence]] should show, and where an injury ahead of him could make him a pre-season waiver add. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) said he holds shares, thought Washington acquitted himself well when thrown into the fray as a rookie, and joked his [Dynasty] shares will still be there in 20 years.
- 2024-10-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-28]]): with [[Christian Kirk]] done for the year, it could be Washington's season. Waldman conceded it is partly process of elimination — Devin Duvernay out, [[Gabe Davis]] looking out, [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] with a chest injury — and expects more two-tight-end sets with [[Brenton Strange]] and [[Evan Engram]] while Kirk is out. Bob Harris (not tracked) said he traded Washington away roughly a week earlier and is not thrilled by the Jaguars' passing attack.
