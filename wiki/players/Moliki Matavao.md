# Moliki Matavao

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Matavao is already a technically sound route runner for his stage of development, and Waldman thinks he could grow into a really skilled zone receiver who manipulates off-coverage defenders before settling into open space or working across the field. The disconnect is after the catch: coming off the bus and working downfield he looks like an imposing runner, but he goes down easily — even when smaller defenders wrap him high, when he ought to be able to carry them for a long ride. Waldman calls his ball-carrying disappointing for his size unless he is playing through an injury nobody knows about.
- 2025-07-30 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-07-30]]): Matavao out of UCLA is his sleeper tight end call for the Saints — he says the kid can play and that New Orleans got a steal with a late-round pick on him. Koh expects the Saints to run some two-tight-end sets, which would give Matavao a path to snaps behind returning veteran [[Jonnu Smith]], and flags him as a name to watch in a room where the overall pass-catching depth beyond [[Chris Olave]] and [[Rashid Shaheed]] is thin. [[Matt Harmon]] did not offer his own evaluation of Matavao in this segment.
