# Elijah Badger

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-05-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-05-09]]): one of four UDFAs he charted this year and the one whose going undrafted surprised him most alongside [[Andrew Armstrong]]. Harmon goes as far as saying Badger might be the best X receiver candidate on the Kansas City roster — a room he and James Koh (not tracked) agree lacks a big ball-winning X behind [[Xavier Worthy]], [[Marquise Brown]] and [[Rashee Rice]] — while stressing he is not predicting a starting job. The profile: 6-1, 200, lined up outside on 85% of his sample snaps and on the line of scrimmage on 87.5%, a whopping 20 yards per reception for Florida in 2024, roughly 70-71% success rate versus man, and good marks on big-boy vertical routes (nine, post, out). He can get jammed up by press and needs work there. Harmon also points to 8.4 yards after catch per reception at Arizona State in 2023 as evidence of after-catch ability that Florida's vertical role hid, and says he would have loved to spend a day-three flier on him.
