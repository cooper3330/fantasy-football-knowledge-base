# Diontae Johnson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his name as
> "Deontay Johnson"; normalized here to the real name of the Steelers
> receiver, Diontae Johnson.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-01]]): cited as part of the charted evidence for how badly Matt Canada's route concepts under-featured the middle of the field in Pittsburgh — just 9.4% of Johnson's routes were dig routes in the full 2022 Canada offense. Not given an explicit new projection, but grouped with [[George Pickens]] as a receiver who stands to benefit from Arthur Smith's incoming route-concept shift toward the middle of the field. Separately referenced as part of the outgoing receiver corps alongside Allen Robinson (not expected back) and Calvin Austin (not yet shown as "a big-time target getter").
- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]): with trade rumors surfacing and Arizona reported as the team showing "the most interest," grades him as capable of "at least low-end wide receiver two," with a ceiling "somewhere just outside the top 15" at the position. Reads Pittsburgh's willingness to move him as a function of liking their depth — pointing to Calvin Austin (not a tracked page), the short, speedy Memphis product they drafted two years earlier — and the strength of the incoming receiver draft class.
- 2024-03-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-14]]): actually
  traded, Pittsburgh to Carolina, for only a Day 3 pick swap plus CB Dante
  Jackson — both hosts "stunned," calling it a fleecing, especially since
  Carolina is "not a well-managed team." Harmon's charted case for why the
  return was so light relative to Johnson's quality: 2019-2022 success rate
  vs. man coverage of 75.0/77.7/76.9/76.1%, No. 1 in success rate vs. zone in
  2020 and No. 2 in 2021 (behind only [[Cooper Kupp]]'s historic year), and
  above the 80th percentile vs. press in multiple seasons — full-field
  separation at all three levels, with a 2022 out-route success rate of
  89.7% and dig-route success rate of 92.3%, despite being underused on
  those routes (9.3% dig, 17.4% slant) in Matt Canada's route-limited
  Pittsburgh offense. Ranked 7th in success rate vs. man coverage in 2022
  (behind Stefon Diggs, Justin Jefferson, A.J. Brown, Davante Adams, Brandon
  Aiyuk and Tyler Lockett) and one of only five players top-7 in both man
  *and* zone success rate that year — company otherwise limited to Diggs,
  Jefferson and Adams. On the drop reputation: calls it overblown by
  negativity bias — PFF drop rate 6.7% (2021), 8.5% (2022), just 3.5% (2023)
  — driven partly by high target volume in inefficient, hit-prone areas of
  the field and shaky Pittsburgh QB play, not a "crippling" issue. Projects
  him as Carolina's clear focal point for [[Bryce Young]] over incumbents
  like [[Adam Thielen]] (short-area only), [[Jonathan Mingo]] (can't win
  outside) and DJ Chark (not a tracked page): "if he doesn't catch 100
  receptions in 2024, something bad happened."


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: skeptical Johnson himself can be the alpha [[Bryce Young]] needs to lean on — "he's an on-and-off player, he's a light switch player," the type who needs a strong veteran presence around him rather than being expected to provide it himself. Worries Carolina's front office (owner David Tepper) will be too quick to "rip the duct tape off" and micromanage if things don't click immediately. Frames [[Bryce Young]] as needing to be that stabilizing "duct tape" himself but isn't sure he's there yet.

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): the swing
  addition that lifts Carolina's receiver room out of the "disaster" tier and
  into merely "fragile" — asked directly whether Carolina would be in the
  disaster group without him, both hosts agree "oh yeah... they're in
  disaster." Still, when stacked against the other three "fragile"-tier WR1s
  (Amon-Ra St. Brown, [[Zay Flowers]], [[Drake London]]), Harmon ranks Johnson
  fourth of the four.

- 2024-04-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]] [Best Ball]: with Johnson going around WR40 in early best balls, calls it a value price alongside [[Adam Thielen]] (going around a WR5-6-tier price) — the logic being that if you think [[Bryce Young]] isn't worth drafting, then his receivers aren't either, and that's "discount time" if you're willing to bet on Young's 2024 improvement.
