- 2024-07-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-29 (Feel It or F-It)]]): close to pulling the plug on Hooker, who is at best a backup to [[Jared Goff]] and now appears to be pushed by Nate Sudfeld. Waldman jokes Hooker has a great 1970s quarterback name but is unsure it translates to the 2020s game, expects him to end up on another team at some point, and projects a Josh Dobbs-style career; he did not think Hooker was a good pick and says the excitement around him was mostly about athletic ability. He allows Hooker could still develop into a reserve of some quality. Bob Harris (not tracked) admits to holding a share in a superflex deep dynasty league and is indifferent to the outcome.
