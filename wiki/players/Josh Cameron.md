# Josh Cameron

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-08]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman compared Baylor prospect Josh Cameron (listed roughly 6'1', 224) to [[Dontayvion Wicks]] -- strong after the catch, capable of winning targets in the air, and a good route runner on inside-breaking routes. Cameron has one of the largest release-move vocabularies of any non-top prospect Waldman has scouted in a decade, but struggles to actually separate using them, and Waldman flagged a ball-security issue -- he needs to cinch the ball tighter to his body rather than carrying it loosely. Waldman called Cameron a boom-bust evaluation: if he adds nuance to his releases he could become a really good primary receiver quickly, but the bust outcome is a long-term depth contributor with occasional big weeks depending on usage.
- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Cameron has essentially every release-footwork and hand-counter technique on Waldman's press-coverage checklist, but hasn't shown he can turn that technique into actual separation against tight man coverage -- he wins against zone and off coverage, and the craft underscoring his technique 'just isn't there yet' in terms of translating to production.
