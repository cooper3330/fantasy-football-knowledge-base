# Blake Corum

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Going Deep - Draft Prospects)]]) [Dynasty]: both hosts have him in their top five running backs in the class, and Angelo calls him possibly "the safest prospect in this class" at running back. The case is built entirely on [[Pace Control and Movement Intellect]] rather than measurables — feel for closing space in tight and open areas, not size, speed, or explosiveness. Comped to [[Kyren Williams]] and [[Devin Singletary]] as the modern template for a back who wins on processing rather than traits, with a more grounded aspirational comp to Ray Rice (small, unspectacular long speed, but excellent between the tackles and in the open field) rather than JK Dobbins (raised as a more explosive but probably unrealistic ceiling comp). Waldman notes he personally wasn't as high on [[Kyren Williams]]'s talent level as the market ended up being (treating [[Devin Singletary]] as more the "exception to the rule" back then), which is part of why he's willing to rate Corum — whom he sees as more explosive than Williams — this highly. Both expect a Day 2 pick, not the first running back off the board.
