# Odell Beckham Jr.

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, while he was an unsigned free agent —
> treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-26 — According to **James Koh** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-26]]: surprised the Jets didn't push harder to sign Beckham instead of (or alongside) [[Mike Williams]] to pair with [[Garrett Wilson]], and says he's personally more intrigued by an OBJ/Wilson pairing than a Williams/Wilson one — while admitting real uncertainty about what Beckham has left after his usage decline in Baltimore.
- 2024-03-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]], same episode: pushes back hard on "most overrated receiver" hot takes circulating about Beckham, citing his own Reception Perception charting showing Beckham was "literally dominant" across his first three NFL seasons. Calls his 2023 Baltimore usage "confusing" given how thin the Ravens' receiver room otherwise was — snap share fell as low as ~35% late in the season, he was 5th on the team in routes but 1st in yards for a stretch mid-year, then barely played in either of Baltimore's two playoff games, a decline Harmon can't fully explain (trust, health, or otherwise). Credits him as critical to the Rams' Super Bowl LVI run, providing the boundary/X dimension neither Robert Woods nor Allen Robinson (not tracked pages) could supply, and says he "was good in Cleveland too, in isolation," with the Baker Mayfield partnership souring more from the two "not seeing the game the same way" than from a talent gap. Given his lower-body injury history, doesn't expect him to be a full-time player again. Notes: entering his age-32 season; posted 35 receptions/565 yards/3 TDs with Baltimore in 2023; last played before that in 2021 split between Cleveland and the Rams (44 receptions, 537 yards in 14 games, capped by a Super Bowl ring) after missing all of 2022 with a Super Bowl ACL tear. As of recording, Miami has confirmed a contract offer; Baltimore has instead re-signed Nelson Agholor (not a tracked page) and still values [[Rashad Bateman]] as its starting outside receiver.

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) on *Reception Perception: The Show* ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-11]]), buy/sell on Beckham's remaining free agency: both buy him to Buffalo (if the Bills don't otherwise add an X receiver) but sell Miami — Koh doubts he'd accept being a clear WR3 there, while Harmon thinks he could still be a quality (if not high-volume) target earner in Miami's scheme, freeing up [[Jalen Waddle]] from a stationary-X role, but is skeptical of the overall fit given the Dolphins' cap situation and Beckham's public "not so fast" reaction to Miami rumors.
- 2024-05-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-06]]) [Best Ball]: Waldman calls fantasy value on Odell Beckham Jr. a 'fuck it' -- he still loves watching Beckham when healthy and on the field, but compares him to 'an old band on a 50-years-later tour': flashes of the old magic, not worth the price of admission. He relays a similar read from an SI Dolphins beat writer, expecting only occasional big moments that are essentially unpredictable to time. Waldman's actionable takeaway is a last-round best-ball flyer only, nothing more.
- 2024-07-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harmon does not think 2024 Odell Beckham Jr. can play the boundary X role at the level 'Rams OBJ' once did, but notes he was productive against man coverage on a yards-per-route-run basis last season and has the experience to fill that backside role in spot usage -- primarily to eat snaps and occasionally get schemed touches rather than command a real target share (well short of a 60-70 target role). He called Miami's addition of Beckham alongside [[Malik Washington]] 'very smart, really crafty' team-building because it adds lineup flexibility without taking snaps, targets or air yards away from [[Tyreek Hill]] and [[Jaylen Waddle]].
