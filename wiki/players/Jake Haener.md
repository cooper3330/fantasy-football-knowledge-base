- 2024-10-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-28]]): asked whether Haener is better than [[Spencer Rattler]], Waldman said yes, and thought he showed it in the game. Haener may not have the arm or the mobility people covet, but he has just enough of both and enough of what matters between the ears to be a good backup who is startable at times. The specific tell Waldman liked: when he entered, he targeted the best player on the field — [[Chris Olave]] — relentlessly rather than messing with ancillary pieces, and did it without getting Olave killed.
