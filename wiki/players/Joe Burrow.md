# Joe Burrow

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]) [Best Ball]: calls himself "Goldilocks" on Burrow's QB7 best ball ADP — a top-3 skilled quarterback when healthy, and anything below QB5 is a comfortable price for him. QB7 specifically is "a slight value," since most drafters gravitate to a price just shy of the true premium rather than paying it outright.

- 2024-04-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]] [Best Ball]: "feeling" him finishing as a top-5 fantasy quarterback in 2024 without needing to draft him like one — values him around QB7 on Underdog ADP, calling it "a slight value" since most drafters pay just short of the true premium rather than for it outright (roughly a 3-to-5-round gap between QB5 and QB7 in typical drafts). The outstanding risk, if he's finally fully healthy: three straight years without a full training camp (rookie-year ACL tear, an appendectomy, a calf strain). Says he'd "take a discount" on Burrow in a trade given the strength of his supporting cast ([[Ja'Marr Chase]], plus [[Tee Higgins]] or an in-class equivalent).
- 2024-09-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-12]]): Harmon says Burrow was 'really quick to pass up throws down the field and check it down' in a 10-point Week 1 debacle against New England, and that it will not cut it. He waves off the viral Gatorade-bottle wrist speculation as people hyper-analyzing how someone picks up a bottle. The more serious point is a year-long trend: outside of the Week 8 49ers game — which Harmon calls by far his best in roughly two seasons — and the Bills game after it (134.8 and 108.9 passer ratings), Burrow has mostly played below his reputation, starting 2023 poorly with the calf injury and living under seven yards per attempt. Harmon's stance is show-me: 'I need to see him put together a consistent stretch of games,' and he compares the industry's habit of imagining the old version to how people still search for the old [[Deshaun Watson]]. He believes Burrow can get back; he will not assume it.
- 2024-10-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-07]]): asked whether the five-touchdown performance against Baltimore is the Burrow to expect on the regular, Waldman answered 'absolutely' and called him one of the three best passing talents in the world. He throws with great anticipation and knows how to layer throws; he lacks the [[Josh Allen]] cannon but paints the corners — Waldman's comp is Greg Maddux. Cited evidence: the throw to Mike Gesicki from the opposite hash to the far sideline in the red zone with pressure hitting him in the chest, placed where only Gesicki could get it; and two underrated touchdowns to [[Tee Higgins]] dropped between defenders. Waldman calls him unstoppable on sail routes and elite at moving within the pocket — the ceiling of the spectrum [[Brock Purdy]] is climbing. Caveat raised by both hosts: Burrow has never hit the ground running healthy (ACL, COVID, appendectomy, wrist, calf).
- 2024-11-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-11]]): he 'feels it' on Burrow being the best quarterback in the NFL right now, while validating that arguments for others are worthwhile. The case is placement, pocket movement, work outside the pocket, throwing on the run with placement, and anticipation from the pocket to get people open — Waldman says Burrow sees the field as well as anybody in the league. He concedes Burrow is not as athletically inclined as [[Josh Allen]] and does not make the otherworldly athletic throws [[Patrick Mahomes]] does, but on intellect, anticipation, toughness and versatility he would take him first: if he held the fourth or fifth pick in a franchise-building draft and Burrow slid there, he would be 'dancing' to take him. Bob Harris (not tracked) adds that Burrow is 'cool like a cucumber.'
- 2024-11-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-18]]): Burrow is the clearest proof that quarterback wins are a team stat — 28-of-50, 356 yards and three touchdowns in a loss that dropped Cincinnati to seven defeats, having been called possibly the best quarterback in the league on the prior week's show. Waldman singled out the play Chris Collinsworth flagged: reading [[Derwin James]]'s blitz early and understanding he had to climb and run *earlier* than coaching orthodoxy dictates, because James was too fast and agile to wait on — most quarterbacks are taught to wait until pressure is close before climbing. That escape ended in the throw to [[Chase Brown]], and Waldman noted he also put the ball on [[Tee Higgins]]'s hands on a Hail Mary he had to release early. Waldman cited Collinsworth's stat that Burrow is statistically the best in the league throwing on the run this season, and said he hopes Cincinnati keeps the offense intact.
- 2024-12-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-16]]): Burrow may be the best mobile passer in the NFL — with the distinction that mobile means creating throwing chances by moving, not running. He explicitly contrasts [[Kyler Murray]], who he calls a running quarterback labeled mobile who 'occasionally throws yolo balls.' Burrow's ability to move and maintain or create a chance to throw is as good or better than anyone; [[Matthew Stafford]], [[Brock Purdy]], [[Josh Allen]] and [[Lamar Jackson]] are in that mix, but Burrow finds the quick opportunities the labeled-mobile guys miss, takes fewer risks, will stand tough in the pocket, and can buy far more time than expected. Noted in the segment: six straight games with three-plus touchdown passes, a streak only Tom Brady, Andrew Luck and Peyton Manning have bettered.
- 2025-08-20 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Burrow is his round-four safe pick and the only top-six quarterback he ever ends up rostering, because the market discounts him for not running. Harris concedes a rushing quarterback like [[Jalen Hurts]] has an out Burrow does not — bad through three quarters, rush for a score — but argues Burrow is probably the second-best quarterback in the league and you are simply going to get a great passing season, even if the touchdown total dips. The setup is the point: elite talent around him, extremely concentrated targets in [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Tee Higgins]] and [[Chase Brown]], and a defense that looks bad again with [[Trey Hendrickson]] possibly being traded, making Cincinnati a 'carnival team' that produces weekly pinball games. Guest Scott Pianowski (Yahoo, not tracked) agrees using his golf frame — he hits three woods and five woods, not driver — and says Burrow is the only vanity quarterback he would take this year.
- 2025-09-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Burrow is on three of his teams, drafted in the fifth round twice plus kept in one auction, and is his QB3 for fantasy this season. He mocks the standard objection sarcastically — everyone leads with the fact that Burrow does not derive many fantasy points from rushing — then points out that while not racking up points with his legs last year Burrow finished as the QB3 anyway, so the rushing complaint is not the argument people think it is.
