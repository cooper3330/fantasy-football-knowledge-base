## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]) [Best Ball]: calls himself "Goldilocks" on Burrow's QB7 best ball ADP — a top-3 skilled quarterback when healthy, and anything below QB5 is a comfortable price for him. QB7 specifically is "a slight value," since most drafters gravitate to a price just shy of the true premium rather than paying it outright.

- 2024-04-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]] [Best Ball]: "feeling" him finishing as a top-5 fantasy quarterback in 2024 without needing to draft him like one — values him around QB7 on Underdog ADP, calling it "a slight value" since most drafters pay just short of the true premium rather than for it outright (roughly a 3-to-5-round gap between QB5 and QB7 in typical drafts). The outstanding risk, if he's finally fully healthy: three straight years without a full training camp (rookie-year ACL tear, an appendectomy, a calf strain). Says he'd "take a discount" on Burrow in a trade given the strength of his supporting cast ([[Ja'Marr Chase]], plus [[Tee Higgins]] or an in-class equivalent).
