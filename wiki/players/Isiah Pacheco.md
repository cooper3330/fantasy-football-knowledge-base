# Isiah Pacheco

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-21 — **Brandon Angelo** (co-host of *Going Deep* — **not** a tracked expert in this wiki) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21 (Pacing and Control)]]: Pacheco is the episode's named negative exemplar for pace control — "the polar opposite" of [[Lamar Jackson]]. "He's only running 100 miles an hour. You get zero or a hundred." The contrast is with backs who vary their gears play to play to set up blocks and defender angles ([[Christian McCaffrey]], [[Jahmyr Gibbs]], [[James Cook]]); Angelo's argument is that a single-speed runner is easier to corral in space because a defender only has one tempo to solve. Delivered in passing as an illustration, not as a ranking or start/sit call. Framework: [[Pace Control and Movement Intellect]].
