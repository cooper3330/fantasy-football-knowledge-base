# Casey Concepcion

## Expert Takes

- 2026-05-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-18]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman expects rookie Concepcion to be the run-game complement OC Todd Monken wants alongside [[Deshaun Watson]], extending what a healthy run game gave Watson previously in Houston. In the separate stride-mechanics discussion around [[Denzel Boston]], a movement coach cited Concepcion as having excellent footwork, in contrast to backs flagged for inefficient stride patterns.
- 2026-06-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-22 (Feel It or Fk It)]]) [Dynasty]: grouped with [[Denzel Boston]] and [[Isaiah Bond]] as the Cleveland youth trio Waldman expects to push [[Jerry Jeudy]] out of the starting mix; Waldman suggests Concepcion is closest to a direct route-tree replacement for what Jeudy currently offers.
- 2026-07-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-16]]): mapping quarterback charting to Cleveland's rookie receivers, Harmon is high on Concepcion, projecting he'll be used in a Zay Flowers-ish designed/vertical Z role under new OC Todd Monken and predicting he'll get most of the screen usage since [[Shedeur Sanders]] leans heavily on screens (16% of his attempts, second-highest charted in 2025 behind only Aaron Rodgers). Harmon rated Concepcion the best zone beater of Cleveland's rookie trio (83.2% success rate vs. zone as a college prospect, third in his class behind Jordan Tyson and Elijah Surratt) and thinks he could even be better than Flowers eventually. He expects Concepcion could take some X-receiver snaps despite size concerns, and that his rookie-year success is tied to fit with Monken's scheme and Monken surviving as head coach long-term.
- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31]]) [Dynasty] [Redraft (Standard)]: Harmon says he was ahead of consensus on Casey Concepcion as a prospect and remains a big fan -- he has catch-point issues and some drops, but real separation ability and knows how to beat man coverage, comping his likely usage plan to [[Zay Flowers]]. Harmon expects him to get 'designer' schemed touches and thinks he could produce spike PPR weeks even with [[Shedeur Sanders]] at quarterback because of his explosiveness. Currently ranked around 130 overall by Harmon, with a path to the mid-40s among receivers (or possibly cracking the top 100) if [[Jerry Jeudy]] is released or traded, since that would clear the target competition in a receiver room Harmon says otherwise has real talent shortage.
- 2026-08-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-13]]): Casey Concepcion is his preferred Cleveland rookie sleeper over teammate [[Denzel Boston]] for fantasy purposes this year, going 131st overall on ADP. Harmon calls him the best separator of the rookie duo and someone who 'can win with the football in their hands,' getting plenty of easy, schemed touches regardless of Cleveland's messy quarterback situation -- he thinks Concepcion is the more insulated of the two from QB chaos and fits better if [[Shedeur Sanders]] starts than with [[Deshaun Watson]]. He and co-host James Koh (untracked) project Concepcion into a Zay-Flowers-style Z-slot role, and Koh notes even the Cleveland market's better beat reporters have given conflicting reports on the Sanders/Watson competition, making current camp noise hard to trust in either direction. Harmon expects Concepcion to go around the 10th-12th round and calls him a candidate to be a genuine late-round home run.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]): Waldman still leans Concepcion over the more buzzed-about [[Denzel Boston]] for now, pointing to OC Todd Monken's tendency (seen back at Georgia) to funnel the highest target share and the most schematically accessible looks to the slot receiver, which is Concepcion's role in Cleveland's offense. He called it close and worth revisiting as camp usage becomes clearer.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]) [Dynasty]: Concepcion has staked a claim to Cleveland's slot job and is still outside Waldman's top-100 PPR receivers but is draftable in the double digits. If forced to pick one Browns rookie receiver, Waldman takes Concepcion, because offensive coordinator Todd Monken's passing game has historically run through slot-archetype receivers -- [[Zay Flowers]] last year and [[Ladd McConkey]] at Georgia -- and Concepcion fits that mold better than [[Denzel Boston]], who Waldman thinks will face tougher outside-receiver reps once defenses start disguising coverage between pre- and post-snap. Waldman calls Concepcion the 'safe play' and the 'bargain' if Boston's camp buzz keeps his own ADP artificially higher.
- 2026-08-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: as part of his 'what if one of the Browns quarterbacks is even just passable' thesis, Harmon says Concepcion could be a 'superstar potentially' in that scenario, and notes he has been getting real buzz over the course of the offseason.
- 2026-08-20 — According to [[Mark Sessler]] and [[Dan Hanses]] (not tracked, Heed The Call) and [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-20]]): Casey Concepcion was Harmon's WR2 in this rookie class, and Harmon says early preseason usage (as a runner, receiver in multiple alignments, and punt returner) already has the pick 'aging well' -- he compares Concepcion to a souped-up Zay Flowers, calling him harder to bring down in the open field and a more disciplined route runner than Flowers was as a rookie. Head coach Todd Monken reportedly said after one preseason game they need to 'get the ball in his hands as many times as possible.' The shared caveat: Monken's job security is shaky given Cleveland's unresolved QB situation, so the scheme fit making Concepcion exciting now may not be Monken's offense 12 months from now.
- 2026-08-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-25]]): Harmon expects heavy 'gadgety,' designer short-area usage for rookie first-rounder Concepcion under [[Deshaun Watson]], likening it to how the Ravens deployed a rookie Zay Flowers before he proved he could run a full route tree. He notes Monken is 'gassed up' on Concepcion and expects plenty of early work even if the touches are schemed rather than downfield.
