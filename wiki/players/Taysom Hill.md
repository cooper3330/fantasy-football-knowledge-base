# Taysom Hill

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2024-07-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-08]]) [Best Ball]: In leagues where he is TE-eligible, Waldman said he could make 'a really strong argument' for [[Taysom Hill]] over a lot of TE2 options, expecting the Saints' tight-end usage to expand under new OC Clint Kubiak, who arrives from the McVay/Shanahan coaching tree. He projects Hill in a Garrett Graham/Owen Daniels-style role out of the backfield, getting 'meaty' red-zone and short-yardage opportunities including some snaps under center.
- 2024-08-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-22]]): Hill is his bargain of the draft past round 12 and a potential league-winning tight end. The argument is system: New Orleans has moved to a Kubiak-style offense with lots of rollouts, a six-gap run game and multiple tight ends — the scheme that fed Owen Daniels with Matt Schaub — and Waldman calls [[Derek Carr]] the closest thing to Matt Schaub in the league today, just with a bigger arm. Everyone knows Hill was a red-zone monster in Pete Carmichael's offense, but defenses now must re-scout how a completely different staff disguises and targets him, which Waldman thinks takes six to eight weeks; after that he could be TE2 or TE3. [[Brandon Angelo]] adds that Hill and [[Alvin Kamara]] have had roughly the same red-zone touches (about 30 apiece) over recent years, notes Hill finished TE9 and TE12 the past two seasons largely on red-zone work, and that with [[Kendre Miller]] out of favor and Jamaal Williams diminished, Hill might lead the team in touchdowns. Waldman's math: add 35-45 catches and 250-400 yards to that red-zone floor and he is an eight-to-ten touchdown tight end.
- 2025-10-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-03]]): Hill is eligible to return from PUP after last year's devastating knee injury and has been limited in practice this week, with no clarity on whether he plays in Week 5 or later. Harris has no idea whether the knee will allow the kind of punishment Hill used to absorb, would have 'a very hard time' starting him this week even if active, and would only roster him in a very deep league if you need a tight end — while adding it would be nice if 'the guy who absolutely is not a tight end' once again lays waste to fantasy's dumbest position. [[Juwan Johnson]] is questionable for the same game.
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: nine offensive snaps in his return — six at quarterback, one in the slot, one at tight end, one at running back — including a stuffed red-zone jet handoff, a converted third-and-two wildcat carry, a throw to [[Juwan Johnson]] to set up a field goal, and a wildcat run stuffed short on third-and-one. Harris' verdict: 'obviously not enough to use him yet in a fantasy league, but just as obviously they immediately wanted to have him touching the ball. So who knows? Maybe the role grows.'
- 2025-11-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-10]]): 'we are definitely getting more goon, more Taysom Hill' — a season-high seven touches, all carries including one at the goal line (no score), on 25 offensive snaps, more than he has played lately. Harris's conclusion: you cannot start him yet on this basis, and he would want the Saints offense to be a lot better first, but in a very deep league with a bench spot and no tight end, Hill is a legitimate add because 'Taysom Hill's not a tight end and he can help you.'
- 2025-11-24 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Hill kept his heavy red-zone and goal-line role for New Orleans in a game where the Saints ran 29 offensive plays to Atlanta's 8 in the second half yet were outscored 8-3, largely because of repeated failures inside the five (including a fourth-down wildcat throw batted down and a botched shotgun snap that led to intentional grounding). With Kamara out, Harris raised the possibility of 'more Taysom Hill as a fill-in tight end' in fantasy lineups going forward.
- 2025-11-25 — According to Scott Fish (guest on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-25]], not a tracked expert) [Redraft (Standard)]: ranked Hill (1% rostered) as his #5 Week 13 waiver add purely as a Wildcat/goal-line touchdown play, calling it 'commit to the bit' for desperate deep-league tight end situations. Against Atlanta the Saints tried repeatedly to punch Hill in from inside the two-yard line and failed every time, including a botched shotgun snap that went over his head and drew what Chris Harris said was the only intentional-grounding call on a tight end all year. Fish notes Hill was a league-winner in this exact role in December of last year despite how 'stupid' the strategy looks on paper.
- 2025-12-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris walked back the case he'd made for Hill on last Wednesday's show (where he'd argued co-host Andy was too low on him), admitting 'I eat poop' after New Orleans did not go back to that well following the Falcons game -- Hill played just 12 offensive snaps against Miami (three at tight end, one carry at quarterback for two yards) for two total touches and seven yards. Harris's blunt conclusion: 'I'm a jackass, don't use Taysom Hill.'
- 2025-12-23 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-23]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: in what may have been Hill's final home game for New Orleans (an 'emeritus' send-off usage), he lost a fumble on his first carry, then played 13 snaps at running back for six carries and three targets, and threw a garbage-time touchdown to [[Chris Olave]] -- a passing/rushing/receiving/quarterback line Harris jokes he and Jake Seeley discuss every single year. Harris ranks Hill his #1 championship waiver add regardless of role clarity, reasoning that if Hill does even one of the three (rush/catch/throw) he's TE-relevant, and if he does all three he's a top-10 option; Harris personally has him rostered ahead of Colby Parkinson or Mark Andrews in his own semifinal lineup. Jake Seeley (The Athletic, not tracked) also lists Hill but not quite as high, and cautions this could have been a pure farewell gesture that doesn't repeat against Tennessee in week 17 -- the contingency being whether it was a one-off tribute or Hill keeps the touches.
