- 2025-10-16 — According to guest Dave Kluge of FootballGuys.com (not a tracked expert), disagreeing with [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-16 (Week 7 Flex Decisions)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with head coach Brian Callahan out of the picture, Kluge likes Tajay Spears as a Week 7 flex, saying he 'has a little bit more juice' than [[Tony Pollard]] right now, is 'breaking tackles,' generating yards after contact, and finally looks healthy after injuries dating back to Tulane limited his opportunities. Kluge ranked both backs as RB3s back-to-back but argued Spears' ceiling is higher for anyone needing to 'swing for the fences' as an underdog. Harris pushed back, saying he'd 'need to see it first' -- an actual change in usage -- noting Spears' snaps last week came mostly on competitive-game second/third downs rather than a clear third-down carve-out, so it's too soon to call a shift.
- 2025-10-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-28]]) [waiver wire, Harris's #5, guest Scott Fish's #5-6]: Fish says Spears has 'out-snapped, out-touched and out-produced' [[Tony Pollard]] in two of Tennessee's last three games, calls him a better pass-catcher and more explosive, and thinks he could take over the 1A role -- especially with trade rumors swirling around Pollard ahead of the deadline. Harris is less convinced given the small sample came almost entirely in a blowout game script, and notes Spears's actual Week 8 line (a garbage-time wildcat touchdown at the very end of a 40-3 game) doesn't make a statistical case on its own -- but still calls him 'a good player' worth a nice speculative add. Both agree the appeal is contingent on a Pollard trade or injury, since 'starting him feels disgusting' in the current offense.
- 2026-07-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Dave Kluge (footballguys.com, untracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-10]]): Harris has long liked Tajay Spears's tape -- 'a fun little player,' jitterbug movement, blazing quick -- though he notes former coach Brian Callahan never bought in. Kluge explains Spears's limited role traces to a lengthy ACL injury history (multiple tears in college, plus a Year 2 stretch where his touches spiked near 20 in one game before he got hurt twice more), which he thinks scared off the old staff more than talent did. With a new coaching staff on an expiring contract and no long-term stake in protecting him, both see a real chance Spears gets expanded work if he stays healthy and the staff prefers him to [[Tony Pollard]] in camp -- Harris says he'd be 'the audience' for that vibe shift -- though [[Nicholas Singleton]]'s health also complicates the picture for all three backs.
