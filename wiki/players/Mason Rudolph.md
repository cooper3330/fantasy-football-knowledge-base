- 2025-11-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-25]]): started for Pittsburgh with Aaron Rodgers out. His first throw was an aggressive shot down the left sideline that turned into an insane one-handed interception, and after that the Steelers ran what Harris called the 'screens and stops program' -- short, conservative passing regardless of down and distance. Harris says 'even Aaron Rodgers is more aggressive' than what Rudolph did in this game, framing Pittsburgh's passing offense as having almost no margin for error under him.
