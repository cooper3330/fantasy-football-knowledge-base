# Desmond Reed

## Expert Takes

- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman called Reed a dynamic open-field runner and good pass catcher who's better between the tackles than his size would suggest, though that same size could limit his touch share. His flagged issue is footwork on pressure cuts at top speed -- Waldman has seen him fall because his plant foot isn't stabilized, or slide to the ground trying to bounce outside when his weight lands outside the plant step.
- 2026-04-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-13]]) [Best Ball]: at 5'6', 174 lbs and very explosive, Reed draws Darren Sproles comparisons that Waldman flatly rejects -- 'nope, we're going to fuck it.' Offers Tarik Cohen as the better comp instead, noting Cohen had genuinely good moments with the Bears despite similar size skepticism. Sees Reed as a compelling player in the right situational role, capable of contributing as a scat-back and return specialist.
