# Mark Andrews

- 2024-09-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: 'completely fuck that' to the idea that Andrews is washed. Waldman says he went to multiple scouts and athletic trainers about the term after hearing it applied to running backs and receivers, and was told the claim that observers can see a player is washed is one of the most ridiculous assessments out there — after 21 years of scouting he says it's hard enough to judge speed and explosiveness at a player's peak, let alone judge loss. He cites past wrong calls on [[David Montgomery]] and [[Nick Chubb]]. On the tape, Andrews clicked his heels on an out route and fell, but was otherwise open on intermediate routes against zone; he has always been a zone player rather than a man-beater, and Kansas City has clamped him for five straight games (about 20 yards a game, no touchdowns) — that is not new. Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) added that Andrews looked gassed after limited practice following his car accident and warned against broad conclusions from a single data point.
