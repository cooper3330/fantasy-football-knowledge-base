# Devin Culp

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: small (Shannon Sharpe-range size, under 235 lbs). Can high-point and extend well outside his frame, and attacks the ball well at its earliest point when the target arrives in the direction of his break — but is a worse high-pointer and a "clap catcher" with lapses attacking the ball side-by-side with his hands, described as catching "like a kid learning to catch an oversized ball that's bouncy so it won't hurt them."
