# Davon Booth

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-02-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-09]]) [Dynasty]: an under-the-radar name Waldman likes -- 'nobody's talking about this guy at all.' He comps Booth to [[Kyle Monangai]] with a bit more burst: a smart, patient runner who plays with power. A graduate player in his fifth college season, Waldman sees a path for Booth as an undrafted or late-round back who could stick on a roster if he's resilient about bouncing around the league to find the right fit.
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman's first impression was 'a more explosive Kyle Monangai without Monangai's relentless power,' but he refined that after more tape: Booth's low center of gravity, footwork and curvilinear speed recalled Dalvin Cook and Khalil Herbert -- backs who can flip the field between the tackles -- and after watching him catch the ball, Waldman called him 'more reliable than Herbert and more dynamic than Cook' as a pure mover, though not overall more dynamic than Cook. His summary comp: combine the best of Monangai, Herbert and Cook and 'you get back the caliber of J.K. Dobbins before the Achilles tear and knee surgeries' -- though Waldman said Booth isn't on Dobbins' level as a creator or after-contact runner. He also praised Booth's ability to absorb contact while adjusting to inaccurate targets, giving him legitimate passing-down potential Herbert lacked.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]) [Dynasty]: With [[Quinshon Judkins]] getting glowing camp reports and a dominant share of the run, Waldman said Booth is worth stashing as a handcuff. He pointed to Booth's college tape and the fact Cleveland cut T.J. Harden while giving Booth real preseason carries as signs the team wants a closer look with an eye toward a roster spot. Waldman described him as shifty and quick with good hands but without great power, though he shows competent contact balance for a role player; he projects Booth could grow into a committee role if called upon, and would be a legitimate fantasy commodity if pressed into an injury-replacement role.
