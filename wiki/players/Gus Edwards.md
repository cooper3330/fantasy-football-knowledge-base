# Gus Edwards

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]] [Best Ball]: pushes back on best-ball ADP treating Edwards as a fringe RB4 — flags his history with new Chargers OC Greg Roman as underrated, citing four years of shared tape in which Edwards averaged over 5 yards per carry (career mark 4.91, with last season the one year he dipped below 5). Expects Edwards to "figure prominently" in the backfield given that history plus the departure of Keenan Allen and Mike Williams shifting the offense's identity, likely in a committee with either [[Braelon Allen]] or [[Jaylen Wright]] depending on how the Chargers want to balance the room; also reminds listeners he scored 13 touchdowns last season.
- 2024-08-01 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-01]] [Dynasty]: Koh admits he has Edwards on a suspicious number of dynasty rosters and needs him healthy, then immediately lists why that is a bad bet — 29 years old, a rough-and-tumble running style, and an offseason surgery Jim Harbaugh would not acknowledge despite it being widely reported. [[Matt Harmon]] joins in on the Harbaugh information blackout point, comparing it to Pete Carroll and saying Harbaugh will not tell you who is healthy. Neither host is worried about rookie [[Kimani Vidal]] as a threat to the backfield — Harmon says he is 'zero percent' concerned about the fourth back in camp.
- 2024-08-05 — According to Bob Harris (co-host, not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-05]]: he holds more shares of Edwards than of [[J.K. Dobbins]] at an RB37 price, because people who cover the Chargers daily expect Edwards to be the lead back and, as [[Matt Waldman]] added, the red-zone back. Harris tempered it by doubting the 13 rushing touchdowns from the prior season are a repeatable standard. Both hosts framed Edwards as the perceived-safer of the two Chargers backs — the pick for a roster that already swung for upside early — while Dobbins carries the higher ceiling if the Achilles holds.
- 2024-09-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Edwards investors shouldn't hit the panic button yet, but the Chargers have been signalling the change for a while — from Edwards-as-bell-cow when he signed to an explicit hot-hand split, and [[J.K. Dobbins]] out-snapped him on third downs and obvious passing situations in week one. Waldman expects Dobbins to have the hot hand often, while Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked), who owns both, notes that doesn't stop Edwards from keeping goal-line and short-yardage work.
