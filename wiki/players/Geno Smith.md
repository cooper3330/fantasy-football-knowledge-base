# Geno Smith

> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]]: in a competition framing against newly acquired [[Sam Howell]], says that when everybody's playing their best, "Geno Smith wins this in a cakewalk" — but argues Smith is perceived as shakier under pressure than he actually is, since he's genuinely good at moving around and finding open men; contrasts that with Howell being credited as tougher against pressure mainly because he's willing to throw into it, arguing the two quarterbacks would draw identical praise/criticism if you "switched jerseys."

- 2024-04-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]]: "f\*\*k it" on calling him a clear frontline NFL starter right now — two years ago he looked to be trending that way, but last season's dip was worsened by an early offensive-line injury and reported front-office/scheme turmoil between Pete Carroll and the offensive coordinator. Reads new OC Ryan Grubb's arrival and the [[Sam Howell]] acquisition as real, unresolved competition rather than a lock for Smith, a shift down from his 2024-03-25 "wins in a cakewalk when healthy" framing.
