# Geno Smith

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]]: in a competition framing against newly acquired [[Sam Howell]], says that when everybody's playing their best, "Geno Smith wins this in a cakewalk" — but argues Smith is perceived as shakier under pressure than he actually is, since he's genuinely good at moving around and finding open men; contrasts that with Howell being credited as tougher against pressure mainly because he's willing to throw into it, arguing the two quarterbacks would draw identical praise/criticism if you "switched jerseys."

- 2024-04-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]]: "f\*\*k it" on calling him a clear frontline NFL starter right now — two years ago he looked to be trending that way, but last season's dip was worsened by an early offensive-line injury and reported front-office/scheme turmoil between Pete Carroll and the offensive coordinator. Reads new OC Ryan Grubb's arrival and the [[Sam Howell]] acquisition as real, unresolved competition rather than a lock for Smith, a shift down from his 2024-03-25 "wins in a cakewalk when healthy" framing.
- 2024-06-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: both hosts are 'totally feeling' Smith as a rebound/value play, attributing his down 2023 to a Seattle offensive line ravaged by tackle injuries rather than a talent decline, with the surrounding skill talent still intact. New OC Ryan Grubb is noted for a pass-heavy scheme in his prior stop in Washington. Waldman likes Smith as a QB2 in start-one leagues or a high-upside Superflex piece for managers punting the position early, and laid out Underdog's QB13-23 tier — Brock Purdy, Tua Tagovailoa, Trevor Lawrence, Jared Goff, Justin Herbert, Kirk Cousins, Matthew Stafford, Aaron Rodgers, Deshaun Watson, Baker Mayfield, then Smith at QB23 — saying he'd be comfortable with any of them if he waits at the position.
- 2024-06-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-18]]): Waldman expects a rebound season for Geno Smith under new OC Ryan Grubb, arguing much of last year's struggle traced to losing both starting tackles in Week 1 with no adequate depth behind them - a common problem across the league that badly hurt a Pete Carroll-style play-action, drop-back scheme. Waldman says Smith has always been a 'heady' processor whose post-snap coverage recognition should improve with better protection and weapons, comparing his prior spot to Mac Jones' situation in New England rather than a genuinely limited passer. With Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Tyler Lockett and DK Metcalf all viable, Waldman projects Seattle to support a clear fantasy WR1 or two strong WR2s plus a good WR3, which could make Smith a top-15 and possibly top-10 fantasy QB in 2024.
- 2024-08-27 — According to guest Derek Klassen (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-27]]): Smith is his pick for the quarterback most likely to surprise. Klassen says 30-plus touchdowns is absolutely on the table if Seattle's offensive line is anything but bottom five — even 18th in pass protection would do — and calls a ranking like Mike Sando's QB tiers putting Smith around 23rd alongside [[Deshaun Watson]] 'completely bonkers' and 'aggressively crazy'. His supporting argument: [[DK Metcalf]] is still good even where he frustrates Klassen as a player, [[Tyler Lockett]] still has something left, and [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] has become underrated. The stated risk is the coordinator — Klassen and host James Koh (not tracked) both question how much of Washington's college offense first-time NFL play caller Ryan Grubb actually authored.
