# Justin Jefferson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: **feeling it** with Nick Mullens at quarterback. Calls Jefferson "one of the best three route runners in the NFL" and an acrobatic, high-flying finisher, and says he "put on an absolute clinic on even just three routes" the prior week. The practical ruling: bump him one tier down from his absolute WR1-overall ceiling because of the quarterback, but he stays a clear start. The reliability of the route running is exactly what lets a backup quarterback fit balls into tight windows — see [[Weak Quarterback Play and Receiver Value]].

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]): says he's "loved Justin Jefferson's game since LSU," and while he'd take [[Ja'Marr Chase]] "ever so slightly" ahead of him right now in a dynasty startup — purely on Chase's better quarterback certainty — he'd "run to the podium" to grab Jefferson if someone took Chase first. Frames the two as effectively 1-2 with a negligible gap. Notes Jefferson made backup Nick Mullens (not tracked) look serviceable when healthy last season, reinforcing that the QB-agnostic talent read still holds even as the situational edge favors Chase.

- 2024-03-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-12]]: with [[Kirk Cousins]] signing in Atlanta and Minnesota moving to a bridge-veteran-plus-likely-rookie quarterback plan, says simply "I think Justin Jefferson will be fine" regardless of who ends up throwing him the ball — contrasted directly with [[Jordan Addison]], who he expects to suffer more from the downgrade at quarterback.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: admits some irritation that Jefferson won't get "the most ideal of outcomes" now that [[Kirk Cousins]] has officially left Minnesota for Atlanta, though he notes that doesn't necessarily mean bad things for a player this good.
- 2024-04-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-16]]) [Dynasty]: calls [[Justin Jefferson]] deserving of a market-altering extension (likely ~$30-35M/year) given his historic per-game production (98 yds/game, 68% career catch rate, 31 TDs in 60 games), and pegs the holdup as a negotiation over guaranteed money rather than any doubt about his value.
- 2024-04-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-22]]): notes Jefferson ranked 3rd in fantasy points per game over Weeks 14-18 last season despite a QB carousel of Josh Dobbs/Jaren Hall/Nick Mullens, and expects him to remain highly productive whether Minnesota starts [[Sam Darnold]] or a rookie.
- 2024-06-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: on the day Jefferson signed the NFL's richest receiver contract (~$35M/year, making him the league's highest-paid WR), Waldman argued the market is overstating concern about rookie QB J.J. McCarthy as a limiting factor. He cited Amari Cooper's 100+ catch, WR18 finish on the NFL's worst-scoring offense with Bryce Young, and Davante Adams' WR10 finish over age 30, as proof elite receivers survive shaky QB play; he called the QB worry 'more a mental thing than an actual thing.' Co-host Bob Harris (not tracked) countered that Jefferson was WR3 in per-week scoring over his final month last year playing with backups Josh Dobbs, Jaren Hall and Nick Mullens — real evidence the concern isn't purely psychological. Both agreed Jefferson going anywhere WR1-4 is reasonable, with CeeDee Lamb and Ja'Marr Chase also in that tier discussion; Waldman personally has Lamb as his WR1.
- 2024-07-22 — According to Bob Harris (co-host, NOT a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-22]]: Jefferson is the least of his worries in a Minnesota offense with quarterback uncertainty — after returning from the hamstring injury late last season he was third in the league in points per game across five games thrown by a combination of Josh Dobbs, Jaren Hall and Nick Mullens. [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-22]]) agrees he likes the offense and the coaching, and expects whoever plays quarterback — [[Sam Darnold]] or eventually [[J.J. McCarthy]] — to be coached up enough to get it done, adding that a young quarterback with that level of surrounding talent does not have to overthink much.
- 2024-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-30]]): asked whether Jefferson is the best receiver in the league, Harmon says 'hell yeah' without qualification and adds he has been for a while, maybe the best route runner and best contested-catch receiver in the league. He calls 2023 Jefferson's worst Reception Perception season and it was still 76.6% success rate vs man; he attributes the dip partly to sampling the first four and last four games, with the return games coming straight off injury before he was on a full snap load. Harmon's headline projection is volume: with Minnesota's depth chart 'falling off a cliff' after [[Jordan Addison]], [[T.J. Hockenson]] hurt and either [[Sam Darnold]] or a rookie under center, he thinks Jefferson has a real shot to lead the NFL in target share and reach a 200-target season. He would take the over on 40.6% first-read target share — the figure [[Davante Adams]] led the league with in 2023 per Fantasy Points — against Jefferson's own 33.2%, and floats 45%. His reasoning: a rookie or journeyman QB makes 18 the first read maybe 50% of the time and the first read on the concept 80-85% of the time. He points at the Nick Mullens stretch as proof the targets come regardless of quarterback quality — 'not good football, but enthralling football'. Koh does the math: 12 targets a game over 17 games is 204, and given this depth chart that does not sound crazy.
