# Sean Tucker

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2024-09-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-05 (Going Deep)]]): Tucker belongs on the waiver-wire watch list near the top of the running back queue. As Tampa Bay incorporates more zone concepts, Waldman argues the cutback is the key skill in the scheme and Tucker is the most natural cutback runner of the three, ahead of [[Bucky Irving]] (whom he calls only okay at it) and clearly ahead of [[Rachaad White]], whose strength it is not. He also credits Tucker with the best combination of size, speed and traits in the room and calls him the backfield's best big-play threat. [[Brandon Angelo]] agrees emphatically. The caveat is opportunity, not talent — the coaching staff had signalled a hot-hand rotation between White and Irving.
- 2024-09-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-05 (Film and Theory)]]) [Dynasty]: he rosters Tucker as a third-string back in multiple deep dynasty leagues and expects to rotate him on and off those rosters several times during the season. He likes the idea of what Tucker could do if given a real opportunity, but says explicitly that he will not hold him at the expense of players who can help him win now — Tucker is a practice-squad-style hold rather than a roster commitment.
- 2024-10-14 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-14]]): Tucker leapt onto the waiver-wire radar with 136 yards and a touchdown on 14 carries plus three catches for 56 yards and a second score with [[Rachaad White]] out. Waldman pushes back on Dwain McFarland's (not a tracked expert) framing of it as garbage time — he calls them 'closeout carries,' where everyone knows the run is coming and the question is whether you can still do it, and Tampa Bay wore the Saints defense out. He has said for over a year that the best back on the roster might be the No. 3 guy: Tucker has the best long speed and best acceleration of the three backs and runs well between the tackles, and Tampa paid a record UDFA bonus after a heart condition scared teams off. If the Bucks let competition play out rather than staying loyal to the draft pick, Waldman is 'totally feeling' Tucker cutting into White's work.
- 2024-10-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-28]]): fold up the Tucker dreams for now. The touches are going to [[Rachaad White]] and [[Bucky Irving]], and White is making enough of his to make clear he will not lose the job — Waldman called the Tucker hype a classic over-reaction to a box score people do not like. He is blunt about White: not a great back, but good enough that he keeps the job barring an injury that hands Tucker enough volume to explode. Waldman also used Tampa as his example of a team that has not got the message that running back by committee is over, though he conceded it will not change while Irving is playing as well as he is.
- 2024-11-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-22]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said Tucker has shown he can play and the only question is whether the opportunity comes in Tampa Bay or somewhere else down the line. He listed him among the backs he would definitely keep an eye on, in the same watch-list bucket as [[George Holani]] and [[Dillon Johnson]] — see where he lands, then listen for the team saying some version of 'we didn't realize what we just picked up'.
- 2025-03-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-10]]): 'totally feeling the idea' that Tucker moves ahead of [[Rachaad White]] as Tampa's second-best back, at an RB106 ADP that co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) labelled a free square. Waldman's case is a skill neither [[Bucky Irving]] nor White provides: high-end game-breaking speed, the 60-to-70-yard touchdown element, plus good hands as a pass receiver. He believes Tucker showed enough in 2024 that the team will give him a real chance to compete for that job. [Best Ball]
- 2025-06-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-30]]): Tucker is the most underpriced Tampa Bay back and the best value available at a reasonable cost. Nominally the third back behind [[Bucky Irving]] and [[Rachaad White]], Waldman says he is fast enough and big enough that he might wind up the second back before the season is out, and if Irving does not follow up his rookie year, Tucker could be the guy heading into the following camp while everyone asks whether Irving can reclaim his rookie glory — a pattern Waldman says we have seen repeatedly. Co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) said Tucker is a player he has been drafting on late-round fliers, while noting he has not forgotten Rachaad White exists, he just does not know what White's role will be.
- 2025-07-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-07]]) [Best Ball]: 'if there's a running back I don't want to leave a draft without, it's this dude.' At RB82, one pick after [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] and deep into the 20th round, Waldman says Tucker has the best size-speed combination in the Tampa room, is a good receiver and a smart between-the-tackles runner, is both faster and bigger than [[Bucky Irving]], and is a better cutback runner and smoother than [[Rachaad White]]. He cites Todd Bowles saying at the combine they need to get Tucker the ball more and GM Jason Licht calling him a starter-caliber talent; Tucker went undrafted only because of the heart issue and got the largest running back signing bonus that year, effectively redshirting before flashing on closeout carries against New Orleans. Waldman thinks White could be cut or leapfrogged, and that Irving is not a high-touch back and will need a complement.
- 2025-07-10 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-10]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Tucker offers legitimate home-run ability as a runner and, but for the health concerns coming out of Syracuse, Angelo believes he would have gone in the second or third round; he places him in the same linear, downhill archetype as [[Nick Chubb]], a much lesser version but the same type, capable of an 85-yard touchdown. Usage has been sparse because of [[Bucky Irving]]'s ascension, and Angelo expects Tucker to stick in the league and produce in the right system, possibly after Tampa. [[Matt Waldman]] says the drumbeats are real — GM Jason Licht called him legitimate NFL starter talent at the combine, Todd Bowles said they need him on the field more, and [[Rachaad White]] has posted that this is his last year in Tampa. Waldman calls Tucker a good cutback runner, decent receiver with great home-run ability, the best blend of the backfield's traits, and a must-have late-round player in his drafts.
- 2025-08-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-11]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman floats Tucker as a top-15-rounds pick with [[Rachaad White]] banged up and says he has him around the 15th or 16th, calling it splitting hairs — a good player on a good team. Guest Dave Kluge (Footballguys, not tracked) agrees and argues contingent-upside backs are falling too far this year: Tucker has multiple paths, since he would step in whether [[Rachaad White]] or [[Bucky Irving]] were hurt, and last year showed he can handle a big workload as a three-down back who rips off chunk plays and catches. Kluge is unsure how serious White's groin injury is; if White returns within days, Tucker is still a stash. Both note that as long as Irving and White are healthy their complementary skill sets keep them ahead of him. Waldman jokes the show is on 'full-on Sean Tucker idiocy'.
- 2025-08-22 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)] [Dynasty]: Tucker returns to the super-deep sleeper list for a second straight year. Harris was intrigued by him pre-draft at Syracuse, where he was a genuine prospect who fell entirely out of the draft over a heart condition found at the combine. Harris connected the dots last summer thinking [[Rachaad White]] was beatable as a runner, then did not see [[Bucky Irving]] coming; Tucker still got 50 carries in year two, mostly when White missed the Week 6 Saints game, where he went 14 for 136 and became a co-lead with Irving — and Harris stresses it was not one long run, he was carrying it while Tampa trailed in the second half of a game they went on to win comfortably. Profile: the prototypical 'rolling ball of butcher knives', a legitimately starting-level look, good speed, not a hard cutter but good body control, squat and short and hard to tackle, smashes hard, physical. The blocker is opportunity — Irving is a first- or second-round pick and White is going surprisingly late but should still be drafted in all leagues, so 'there's nothing for him there until one of those guys gets hurt' — which Harris notes very much could happen.
- 2025-08-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-25]]): Harris calls Tucker awesome and names him a super deep sleeper for a second straight year. Guest JJ Zachariason (not a tracked expert) agrees Tucker is the true wild card in the Tampa Bay backfield — with [[Rachaad White]] a little banged up, there may be an opportunity for Tucker to showcase more behind [[Bucky Irving]].
