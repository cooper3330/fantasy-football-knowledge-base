# Jahmyr Gibbs

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]): takes an explicit victory lap — "everything I said about Jahmyr Gibbs this summer is 100% correct," noting he "took a lot of abuse" for it. His 2023 argument was a consistency argument about pricing, not a knock on [[Bijan Robinson]]: if draft capital and situation justified Robinson as a fantasy first-rounder, Gibbs deserved a third-round pick or better on the same logic. Gibbs finished the segment as roughly RB9, ahead of Robinson at RB11.
- 2023-12-18 — **Matt Waldman**, same episode [Best Ball]: immediately turns the call around for 2024 — because the market will now pay a premium for Gibbs, he expects the value to sit with Robinson instead. This is a valuation call; he does not walk back the talent evaluation.
- 2023-12-18 — Risk flagged by both hosts, same episode: Ben Johnson was a hot head-coaching candidate (Carolina interest noted), so Gibbs's 2024 offense and play-caller were not settled.
- 2023-12-21 — [[Brandon Angelo]] (co-host of *Going Deep*) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21 (Pacing and Control)]] (recorded before the *Film and Theory* episode below, which cites it): two things, both new. First, the maturity marker — watching Gibbs "slipping and sliding out of tackles," Angelo's note is that when the run isn't there, "he protected himself and went down, and he's okay doing that. Young players a lot of times are not okay making that step. They want to break the big run every time." The named counter-case is [[Breece Hall]]. Second, the scheme leverage: Detroit is "much more successful with Gibbs on the field — not because Gibbs is so much better a player than [[David Montgomery]], but because defenses have to think a lot more when Gibbs is out there." Montgomery "very rarely will break a 25-plus-yard run"; Gibbs widens the range of outcomes a defense has to cover. Angelo also names Gibbs's **re-acceleration** as the specific thing a linebacker dreads. Framework: [[Pace Control and Movement Intellect]].
- 2023-12-21 — **Angelo**, same episode, on fit: names Gibbs ("listen to him speak") among the players Detroit drafted to a cultural mold — coachable, unselfish — alongside [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], [[David Montgomery]] and Jared Goff. See [[Player Development and Coachability]].
- 2023-12-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21]]): lists Gibbs among the backs defined by change of direction in tight space at speed ([[Christian McCaffrey]], [[James Cook]], [[Austin Ekeler]], [[Lamar Jackson]]) — the trait Waldman argues cannot survive being scaled up to 210-plus pounds. Corroborates the existing evaluation rather than moving it. See [[Running Back Size and Movement Skills]].
