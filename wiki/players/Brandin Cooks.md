# Brandin Cooks

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-07-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-04]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: both hosts called Cooks a perennial afterthought who quietly produces close to 1,000 yards nearly every season, and expect him to push for 100-plus targets in 2024. Their reasoning: Dallas 'didn't draft another playmaker' beyond [[CeeDee Lamb]], with only [[Jake Ferguson]] and a hoped-for step forward from [[Jalen Tolbert]] behind him, no backfield beyond [[Tony Pollard]]/Zeke Elliott reliable enough to lean on, and a [[Dak Prescott]]-led offense that will be pass-first largely out of necessity. Waldman framed him as a 'circle every single year' target-share bet rather than a true deep sleeper.
- 2024-07-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-22]]): asked whether Cooks is a fine WR5, Waldman is 'feeling that' and goes further — he would take him as a WR4 and expects to get to play him as a WR3 in some weeks. Cooks is going as WR59 in ADP, round 11. The case: a good outside receiver Dallas moves into the slot in key situations to create matchups for either him or [[CeeDee Lamb]], very good against zone, works his way open, strong after the catch, though not a contested-catch fiend — a clean complement to Lamb. Waldman calls him 'one of the most underrated receivers over the past 10 years' because he has moved teams so often yet produced everywhere. Bob Harris (not tracked) adds reporting from Jon Machota of The Athletic, who told him directly that the [[Dak Prescott]]–Cooks connection was there in OTAs.
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-13]]): [[Brandin Cooks]] is done in Dallas and close to done generally — Harmon does not expect him back and says he is 'probably closer to done than anything else'. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) put it at 90% that Cooks, an unrestricted free agent still counting against the Cowboys cap, does not return.
- 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24 (Feel It or Not with Bob Harris)]]): asked whether Cooks's days as a viable fantasy producer are in the rearview after signing back with New Orleans alongside [[Chris Olave]] and [[Rashid Shaheed]], Waldman says with a different quarterback he would 'feel it', but with [[Derek Carr]] he has real concerns. He would still take Cooks as a late-round flier on the grounds that the risk is low, so why not. Waldman is careful to distinguish him from [[Brandon Cooks]], calling the confusion the same problem as the Daniel Jones name mixups.
- 2025-03-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-26]]): Cooks returning to New Orleans is sensible depth, nothing more — Harmon says he does not think Cooks has much left and pegs him as a WR3 at best, valuable mainly as a veteran presence so the Saints are not left with nothing if [[Chris Olave]] and [[Rashid Shaheed]] miss time again. He calls the Saints the weirdest team in the league, with [[Derek Carr]] reportedly still preferring a trade and Mickey Loomis holding unusual power, and notes a non-zero chance they draft a quarterback. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) highlights six 1,000-yard seasons across 11 years — more than [[Cooper Kupp]] — calling Cooks a low-key reliable producer who was never a needle-mover.
- 2025-11-06 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-06]]): Cooks is the likeliest beneficiary of [[Rashid Shaheed]]'s departure in volume terms and Harmon 'would not be surprised' if he sees a significant bump, crediting him with more inside-outside versatility than [[Devaughn Vele]] and more vertical ability, though he says Cooks cannot really get vertical either at this stage — 'he is going vertical and then nothing's happening.' The strong caveat: Cooks has been targeted on under 10% of his routes and is 'a very sacrificial player in the truest sense of the word,' frequently starting in the slot, motioning out wide and running a clear-out to open deep hooks, crosses and digs for [[Chris Olave]]. Harmon doubts he can become more than that at this point in his career. Volume context: New Orleans runs 11 personnel 71.5% of the time, third-highest in the league against a ~59% average, and five non-running backs run routes on over 23% of dropbacks with slot rates all bunched between 38% and 43%. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) says the younger [[Devaughn Vele]] is the more interesting name.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-11]]): Cooks drew zero targets on 22 routes in Week 10 despite playing 49.3% of snaps, which Harmon calls being the 'love of the game' player and formally welcomes him to [[Sacrificial X Receiver]] territory — the boundary role that occupies coverage and clears space without earning looks. He ran only 24% of his routes from the slot, the lowest of the Saints' top pass catchers, while [[Devaughn Vele]] took over as the room's snap and route leader after the Rashid Shaheed trade and [[Chris Olave]] absorbed the downfield work from [[Tyler Shough]]. The implication is that Cooks is not a startable fantasy asset in this offense regardless of playing time.
