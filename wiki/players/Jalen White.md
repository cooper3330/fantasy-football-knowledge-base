## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast: works effectively into tight, short-yardage gap creases but needs more conviction attacking with his pads down, and struggles when the hole is tighter than shoulder-width. Correctly avoids bouncing plays outside when leverage doesn't support it on interior runs, but on edge-penetration plays designed to go outside, lapses into bouncing when he shouldn't and then compounds the mistake by spinning back inside rather than reading leverage correctly. Reading the field and decision-making is "an issue," which matters more given he otherwise profiles as "a straight-line runner."
