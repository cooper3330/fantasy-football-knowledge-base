- 2024-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-20]]) [Dynasty]: on undrafted rookie Justin Strong, who is getting a tryout with Indianapolis, Waldman flagged burst, quick-twitch athleticism and efficient, decisive footwork in the limited tape he'd seen, but hadn't yet seen him handle the difficult runs that require advanced judgment. He framed Strong and Evan Hull as pieces that could collectively "recreate [[Jahmyr Gibbs|Jonathan Taylor]] in the aggregate" if Taylor were to miss time, and said that if Strong beats [[Tyler Goodson]] in camp there could be real RB3 value — but stressed this is an August deep-league free-agency stash, not a player worth a startup draft pick now.
