# Darius Slayton

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): a modest
  but real bar-setter for the Giants' otherwise disaster-level receiver room —
  "he's a professional wide receiver... he's not God's gift or anything, but
  he's a pro." His known floor (alongside [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] and [[Jalen Hyatt]])
  is part of why the hosts rank the Giants' room ahead of the Chargers',
  Patriots' and Cardinals' among the league's worst. Separately floated as a
  fit to move to flanker if the Giants draft [[Rome Odunze]] or [[Malik Nabers]]
  to play X/slot respectively.
- 2024-07-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-16]]): Slayton remains, in Harmon's words, 'an above-average receiver' and 'a professional wide receiver' -- competent but unlikely to wow -- with only one RP chart on file (2019 rookie season: 66.5% success rate vs. man). The Giants restructured his contract into a reduced $6.2 million deal with incentives rather than extending him, which Harmon reads as a signal his role could shrink behind [[Malik Nabers]] and a healthier [[Jalen Hyatt]]. Both Harmon and co-host James Koh (a self-described long-time Slayton fan, not a tracked expert) noted his real on-field chemistry with [[Daniel Jones]] as a mitigating factor, and Harmon projects him best-suited as a flanker taking occasional deep shots rather than a true X receiver. [Redraft (Standard)]
- 2024-08-08 — According to co-host James Koh, not a tracked expert ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-08]]): Koh, calling himself 'the lone Darius Slayton truther,' reports via the Athletic's Dan Duggan that [[Jalen Hyatt]] is running ahead of Slayton, putting his roster spot in real trouble. [[Matt Harmon]] adds the Giants brought back Isaiah Hodgins for inside-outside flexibility and signed Myles Boykin as an outside/special-teams body, and that Slayton has dealt with offseason injuries even though the team adjusted his contract for a small raise. Harmon still would not bet against him: Slayton is 'a little cockroachy at the wide receiver position — he just survives,' and could re-emerge if Hyatt does not hold the outside job, which Harmon says has been the story of Slayton's whole career.
