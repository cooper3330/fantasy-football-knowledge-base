# Darius Slayton

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): a modest
  but real bar-setter for the Giants' otherwise disaster-level receiver room —
  "he's a professional wide receiver... he's not God's gift or anything, but
  he's a pro." His known floor (alongside [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] and [[Jalen Hyatt]])
  is part of why the hosts rank the Giants' room ahead of the Chargers',
  Patriots' and Cardinals' among the league's worst. Separately floated as a
  fit to move to flanker if the Giants draft [[Rome Odunze]] or [[Malik Nabers]]
  to play X/slot respectively.
- 2024-07-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-16]]): Slayton remains, in Harmon's words, 'an above-average receiver' and 'a professional wide receiver' -- competent but unlikely to wow -- with only one RP chart on file (2019 rookie season: 66.5% success rate vs. man). The Giants restructured his contract into a reduced $6.2 million deal with incentives rather than extending him, which Harmon reads as a signal his role could shrink behind [[Malik Nabers]] and a healthier [[Jalen Hyatt]]. Both Harmon and co-host James Koh (a self-described long-time Slayton fan, not a tracked expert) noted his real on-field chemistry with [[Daniel Jones]] as a mitigating factor, and Harmon projects him best-suited as a flanker taking occasional deep shots rather than a true X receiver. [Redraft (Standard)]
- 2024-08-08 — According to co-host James Koh, not a tracked expert ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-08]]): Koh, calling himself 'the lone Darius Slayton truther,' reports via the Athletic's Dan Duggan that [[Jalen Hyatt]] is running ahead of Slayton, putting his roster spot in real trouble. [[Matt Harmon]] adds the Giants brought back Isaiah Hodgins for inside-outside flexibility and signed Myles Boykin as an outside/special-teams body, and that Slayton has dealt with offseason injuries even though the team adjusted his contract for a small raise. Harmon still would not bet against him: Slayton is 'a little cockroachy at the wide receiver position — he just survives,' and could re-emerge if Hyatt does not hold the outside job, which Harmon says has been the story of Slayton's whole career.
- 2024-10-17 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-17]]): 'all roads lead back to Darius Slayton' — Harmon joked with Nate Tice that he is the wide receiver cockroach, survivor of trade attempts, salary cuts and the drafting of [[Jalen Hyatt]], whom he beat out in camp despite his own contract beef. Harmon's framing: Slayton is the barometer for outside receiver play, the receiver equivalent of the old Dalton scale — worse than Slayton and you should be off the field, better than Slayton and you are a clear-cut starter. He is a good, useful outside receiver who can stretch the field but will drop passes and make mistakes. With [[Malik Nabers]] out the last two weeks Harmon says Slayton is a player the Giants should keep finding ways to involve. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) argued the Nabers/Slayton/[[Wan'Dale Robinson]] trio is genuinely solid with defined roles, better than Cleveland, Las Vegas, New Orleans, and better than Dallas without [[Brandin Cooks]] — Harmon agreed.
- 2024-10-22 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-22]]): Slayton is Harmon's standing example of quarterback chemistry outrunning the metrics — [[Daniel Jones]] simply likes throwing him the football, and on the field Jones isn't thinking about success rate versus man or yards per route run, he's thinking that Slayton is where he needs to be. Harmon ties it to the pair coming into the league together in the 2019 draft class. He adds, half-complaining, that he wishes Jones would stop throwing at [[Wan'Dale Robinson]], who has the second-most targets in the NFL.
- 2024-11-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-20]]): Koh criticized the Giants for not trading Slayton at the deadline when plenty of teams could have used a 'fully functional X receiver,' and called him completely irrelevant from a functional fantasy standpoint now that New York is starting Tommy DeVito and building for the future. Harmon's counter was that the team that always needs a Darius Slayton is the Giants themselves — they have shown no willingness to play [[Jalen Hyatt]], whom Harmon called a swing and a miss unlikely to make an NFL contribution, though he noted Hyatt was only a third-round pick and went lower than expected, so it is no big deal.
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-13]]): [[Darius Slayton]] hits free agency and both Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) expect him to move on, but Harmon repeats the show's long-running joke that the Giants are a team that always finds their way back to him — they do not want to play Slayton and keep trying to replace him with [[Jalen Hyatt]], and he just beats the replacement out and stays on the field. Harmon leaves it open ('we'll see about the Slayton stuff') rather than calling the departure a certainty.
- 2025-03-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-13]]) on Slayton re-signing with the Giants for three years, $36M: Harmon says he was not outraged by the money in the abstract — he likes Slayton as 'a low-level X starting receiver in a league where there is a huge dearth of X receivers' — but is outraged that he went back to New York after finally getting a clean break. He describes the pair as a toxic on-again relationship: every year the Giants try to make him the fourth receiver, he was a widely assumed cut candidate last year, and every year they come back to each other. He predicts a cut roughly 365 days from now. Koh calls it a bad deal for New York and uses it as a market marker: [[Saquon Barkley]] signed for three years, $37.8M and [[Derrick Henry]] is making 'Darius Slayton money', which he says shows how out of whack running back pay is against low-end receivers.
- 2025-08-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-08]]): Harmon says Slayton still has genuine nine-route and out-route juice in his charted seasons and will catch roughly four good deep balls from [[Russell Wilson]] over the year — with the caveat that predicting which weeks is hopeless, and that Wilson may not last the season with [[Jaxson Dart]] behind him. James Koh (not tracked) expresses mild disbelief that the Giants re-signed him at all.
