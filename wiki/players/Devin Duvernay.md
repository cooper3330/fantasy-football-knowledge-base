# Devin Duvernay

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-06-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said 'fuck it' on Devin Duvernay becoming a real offensive asset in Arizona this year, while agreeing with the underlying idea that Duvernay is capable of more than his current role. A five-star recruit at Texas who was used there both as a deep threat and as a runner/receiver out of the backfield, Duvernay has mostly been limited to a Pro Bowl-caliber kick-return role in Baltimore; Waldman said the Ravens had a real chance to deploy him more creatively out of the backfield and never did, and compared the missed opportunity to what a Danny Woodhead-style role elsewhere could unlock. He doesn't expect Arizona to give Duvernay that workload unless [[Jeremiah Love]], [[Trey Benson]], [[James Conner]] and [[Tyler Allgeier]] were all out simultaneously, calling the 28-year-old Duvernay worth mentioning but 'doubtful' to matter for fantasy.
