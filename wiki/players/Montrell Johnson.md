# Montrell Johnson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast [Dynasty]: reads leverage well as a gap runner, makes multiple defenders miss with efficient movement and timing, and anticipates angles well with a good feel for cutbacks on stretch plays. Leaves clean third-level lanes on the table by bouncing toward unblocked linebackers rather than anticipating the blocks in front of him — a fixable gap that leaves Waldman with an "oh, what could have been" read on his overall prospect grade.
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): asked whether being 'boring' at 216 pounds with a 4.45 forty will limit Johnson in the NFL, Waldman said 'f**k it' — it will limit the imagination of fantasy players and maybe his draft capital, but not his football. He frames Johnson as what people expected LaMichael James to be: good at everything, great at nothing. Where James in Waldman's view was only good enough to make a roster, Johnson is good enough to help if a starter goes down mid-game, and with development could be a productive outside zone runner in a Terrell Davis mold, or at minimum give good carry-to-carry production early in his career. Waldman calls the speed underrated and predicts he is the sixth- or seventh-round pick fantasy players grab off waivers after the draft while wondering how he was taken ahead of more-hyped names — a Chester Taylor or Justin Forsett type when called upon.
