- 2024-05-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-06]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman credits Trey Palmer with a real developmental jump in 2023 -- a drop-prone, unpolished pass-catcher at Nebraska/LSU who cleaned that up significantly as a rookie. But he reads the Buccaneers spending far better draft capital on [[Jalen McMillan]] this offseason than they spent on Palmer last year as a signal the front office doesn't see Palmer as their long-term slot/WR3 answer, or at best sees him fitting a narrower role. Palmer is still worth monitoring, along with Rakim Jarrett, as a camp performer who could push back ahead of McMillan for at least one more season with a strong summer.
