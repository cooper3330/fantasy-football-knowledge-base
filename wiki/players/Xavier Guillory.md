# Xavier Guillory

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): the Arizona State receiver is off most radars after an injury-hit final season, but Waldman likes him as a smaller version of a [[Mack Hollins]]-type roster asset — an excellent special-teamer and excellent blocker with skills after the catch, decent route running and good hands. What stood out to Waldman was less the receiving profile than the toughness and physicality; Guillory is coached by Hines Ward and there is a Hines Ward quality to his game. He would not be surprised if Guillory makes an NFL roster and grows into a name fantasy players become aware of as a bye-week waiver gem. Bob Harris (co-host, not a tracked expert) joked the real Hollins test is whether he shows up to games barefoot and in costume.
