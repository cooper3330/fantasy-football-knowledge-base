# JJ McCarthy

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman flags that McCarthy has reportedly had accuracy problems early in camp, which is part of why a real QB competition with [[Kyler Murray]] persists per Ian Rapoport's reporting that it will run through week one, though Waldman still expects Murray to hold the starting job.
- 2026-08-06 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-06]]): Harmon relayed reports that McCarthy has 'looked terrible at times' in Vikings training camp, but still thinks it wouldn't be surprising if McCarthy starts a game this season given how tight the competition with Kyler Murray appears. He characterized the battle as neither quarterback clearly separating -- 'the better of two not-great options' -- rather than one player emerging as an obvious CJ Stroud-style takeover, and floated that head coach Kevin O'Connell could be on the hot seat if the offense struggles either way.
- 2026-09-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-09-29]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: McCarthy was traded from Minnesota to the Giants for a fifth-round pick after being, in Harmon's words, 'one of the worst starting quarterbacks in the NFL in 2025' and losing a backup competition to [[Carson Wentz]] behind [[Kyler Murray]] in camp. Harmon called the trade odd for New York given reports frame McCarthy as just a long-term backup plan behind [[Jaxson Dart]], despite the real draft-capital cost, and views it as a sign the Giants are 'kind of flailing' at the position.
- 2026-10-26 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-26]]): in his first extended look as the Giants' starter (promoted after Jaxson Dart's injury, with Jameis Winston deemed unusable), McCarthy 'played great' in an overtime win over Houston. Harris pointed to several 'absolute 10 bells throws,' including a laser into a tight window to [[Malik Nabers]] on a third-down conversion right before halftime delivered under an unblocked blitzer, and deep outs to [[Malachi Fields]] thrown in rhythm before the receiver even turned his head (broadcaster Drew Brees singled out the anticipation). McCarthy finished with no turnover-worthy throws despite Houston bringing pressure on multiple third downs, 'took his medicine' on sacks rather than forcing anything, and set up Nabers (7-111) and Fields (4-83) for big days. Harris: 'it was everything the Vikings thought they were drafting,' though he cautioned against overreacting to one game and noted McCarthy still has to rebuild trust after the trade.
- 2026-10-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-10-29]]): Harmon, who has defended JJ McCarthy's rookie-year struggles in Minnesota as more scheme-driven than talent-driven, pointed to McCarthy now 'ripping throws down the field' for the Giants as supporting evidence -- he credits the turnaround largely to New York's use of combination routes pairing [[Malik Nabers]] and [[Isaiah Likely]] that give McCarthy one clear read, something Kevin O'Connell's Minnesota offense never provided. Harmon noted that in Minnesota, McCarthy would wait on routes to develop because he wasn't seeing the field well, then overcompensate by throwing too hard; he still shows a touch of that in New York but is clearly more comfortable overall.
