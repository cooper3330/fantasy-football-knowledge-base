- 2025-10-20 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Wentz had a rough start against Philadelphia -- an early pick-six off a blitz he never saw, a panicked backwards screen thrown out of bounds, and another pick on a covered deep shot -- then failed to hit a wide-open Hockenson on a red-zone play, settling for a field goal via intentional grounding instead. But Harris credited him as 'gritty' and 'gutty' in the second half: once play-action started working with Wentz able to turn his back to the defense under center, Minnesota moved the ball consistently, repeatedly attacking whichever side [[Jordan Addison]] ran his out route against man coverage. Harris noted Wentz is already announced to start Week 8 against the Chargers despite the bad first-half mistakes.
