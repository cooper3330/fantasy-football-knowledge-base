# Carson Wentz

- 2025-10-20 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Wentz had a rough start against Philadelphia -- an early pick-six off a blitz he never saw, a panicked backwards screen thrown out of bounds, and another pick on a covered deep shot -- then failed to hit a wide-open Hockenson on a red-zone play, settling for a field goal via intentional grounding instead. But Harris credited him as 'gritty' and 'gutty' in the second half: once play-action started working with Wentz able to turn his back to the defense under center, Minnesota moved the ball consistently, repeatedly attacking whichever side [[Jordan Addison]] ran his out route against man coverage. Harris noted Wentz is already announced to start Week 8 against the Chargers despite the bad first-half mistakes.
- 2026-09-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Sam Gold (untracked guest) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-14]]): Wentz threw three touchdowns filling in for the injured [[J.J. McCarthy]] in a blowout win over Green Bay, but both hosts resist reading the box score at face value -- Gold says most of the good field position and stops came from the Vikings defense, and while Wentz made a couple of good throws, 'maybe he didn't look as good as what that looked like' once you actually watch the tape.
- 2026-09-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-09-15 (Feel It or Fk It)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: after Kyler Murray's awful decision to throw the ball up for grabs, Wentz is Waldman's 'feel it' as the option best suited to Kevin O'Connell's offense right now. Waldman said it's less about pure talent -- Wentz is 'probably the least talented' of Minnesota's three quarterbacks -- and more about fit: Wentz has played in enough systems to know his role, understands his own strengths and weaknesses, and reads defenses well, so 'you kind of know what you're going to get from him' -- solid against bad defenses, limited against good ones. Waldman flagged the existing rapport with [[Justin Jefferson]] as the key fantasy asset, and expects Wentz to start again this week if Murray isn't ready to go.
