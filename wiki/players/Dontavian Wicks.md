- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-28]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: reacting to Nick Sirianni comparing newly acquired Wicks to Keenan Allen, Harmon calls it 'not the worst comparison in the world' archetypally -- Wicks's best trait is his release work (72.7% success rate vs. press as a rookie, a 69th-percentile mark) and his ability to win breaking routes at the top of the stem -- but stresses Allen's peak press-win rate was 'a lot higher.' Harmon flags real regression in year two: Wicks's success rates vs. man, zone and press all dropped per Reception Perception charting, meaning he never took the expected next step as a separator, and his year-two player comps (including Keelan Cole) were notably weaker than his promising rookie-year comps. Harmon expects Wicks to be Philadelphia's third receiver, kicked into the slot to run outbreaking routes off play action -- where he excelled in Green Bay -- competing with rookie Makai Lemon for slot snaps, and predicts flatly he 'won't be the next Keenan Allen,' projecting him at worst as a solid role player, which he calls 'a hell of a good outcome for a fifth-round pick.'
