# Noah Brown

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: **absolutely feeling it** — "I didn't even say the f\*\*k because I knew we were going to feel it." His evaluation is conditional but strong: Brown "is probably the best receiver on this roster right now" whenever Nico Collins is unavailable, which makes him a viable weekly start in exactly those weeks. Context: eight catches for 89 yards and a touchdown with Case Keenum starting, with Collins out for the season. Bob Harris noted C.J. Stroud was expected back, which both hosts read as adding to the case.
- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]): placed on IR, out for the rest of Houston's playoff run — removes him from the Divisional Round equation entirely, leaving [[Nico Collins]] as the only proven weapon in an already-thin receiver room.
- 2024-01-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-23]]): confirms the loss was real, not just a name removed from availability — Brown "was actually impactful" for the Houston offense, part of why the Divisional Round receiver room behind [[Nico Collins]] looked so thin against Baltimore.
- 2024-07-04 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-04]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Angelo said if he's drafting in the 18th-20th round and Brown is still on the board he takes him without hesitation, citing the rapport Brown showed with [[C.J. Stroud]] last year and the multiple roles he can play. He argued Brown profiles as a reliable reserve who becomes immediately relevant if any two of [[Stefon Diggs]], [[Tank Dell]], or [[Nico Collins]] miss time on a very pass-heavy Texans offense with a now-more-experienced Stroud. Waldman added that a friend nicknamed Brown 'fake CD' because his size, build, and jersey number are similar enough to Collins' that it's hard to tell them apart on tape, and joked Brown could start on most other NFL rosters given his all-around solidity.
- 2024-07-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-08]]) [Best Ball]: Waldman is 'easily' taking [[Noah Brown]] at his current WR103/final-pick-range price as a pure contingent-value play, noting he's proven twice over he can produce big games when given volume. He said the Texans' receiving corps -- a 'hyper-twitched-up' Tank Dell, big-bodied Nico Collins, and an aging [[Stefon Diggs]] -- gives Brown three separate paths to real playing time if any of them miss time, plus an existing rapport with [[C.J. Stroud]].
- 2024-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-29]]): Brown was a surprise cut by Houston and signed with Washington during the recording. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) calls the release surprising because Brown knows the system and played at a decent level when injuries forced him forward in 2023. Harmon's read is that the cut was about roster crowding at the bottom rather than performance — Robert Woods, Steven Sims, [[John Metchie III]] and [[Xavier Hutchinson]] all sit behind an elite trio of [[Nico Collins]], [[Tank Dell]] and [[Stefon Diggs]], and it was Woods, Metchie and Hutchinson who forced Brown off. In Washington he joins a group both hosts consider one of the worst in the league behind [[Terry McLaurin]].
