# Spencer Rattler

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-29]]), discussed alongside [[Xavier Legette]] as his South Carolina target: liked what he saw at South Carolina better than his earlier Oklahoma tape, and now considers him draftable — possibly even developable — moving better in the pocket than expected. A real split noted in his coverage-beating ability: strong against one of man/zone coverage and poor against the other (Waldman was not certain on air which was which), which he summarizes as a prospect who currently has "half a game" and needs to develop the other half.
