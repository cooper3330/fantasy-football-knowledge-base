# Greg Dortch

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): flagged as
  the most promising name in one of the league's worst receiver rooms behind
  [[Michael Wilson]] — a projected 2024 Reception Perception charting subject
  based on a high targets-per-route-run and yards-per-route-run rate whenever
  he's on the field. Preferred over [[Rondale Moore]] in the slot role Arizona
  previously earmarked for Moore. Still framed as a projection, not a proven
  role.
- 2024-08-06 — According to James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-06]]: Dortch is not going to break anybody off in man coverage, but working out of the slot against zone he can get the job done, and Koh counts him alongside [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] and [[Michael Wilson]] as a third Cardinals receiver who can genuinely create separation. [[Matt Harmon]] agrees the room is underrated and says he is really excited about it. Both frame Arizona as a sleeper offense given [[Kyler Murray]]'s late-2023 form, though they also note the Cardinals ran a minus-6.5% pass rate over expected, second lowest in the NFL.
- 2024-09-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-12]]): per Fantasy Points data Dortch drew eight targets in Week 1, six of them first-read targets, a 28.6% share of Arizona's first reads, turning into six catches for 47 yards. Harmon's verdict is 'way too much Dortch' — he likes the player but argues those first-read designs belong to [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], and reads [[Kyler Murray]]'s deflection about target distribution as an implicit admission that the concepts need reordering around the rookie.
- 2025-02-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-26]]): Dortch is 'fine' but strictly limited — Harmon described him as a pure slot only, used on a lot of designer stuff rather than as a real high-volume inside-out slot receiver, and said Arizona could use a genuine slot in the [[Jakobi Meyers]] mold instead, while conceding that is a luxury rather than a need behind [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] and [[Michael Wilson]]. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) noted Dortch is an unrestricted free agent, so there is no guarantee he returns to Arizona at all.
