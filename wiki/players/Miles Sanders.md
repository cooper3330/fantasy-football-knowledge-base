# Miles Sanders

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-04-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-22]]): not feeling Sanders at his RB60 ADP, describing him as a stagnated player who was clearly outplayed by [[Chuba Hubbard]] from game one of last season.
- 2024-05-02 — According to Brandon Angelo ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-02]]): Sanders, whom Angelo noted carries roughly the fifth-highest AAV among running backs, hasn't been good in Carolina and Angelo doesn't expect that to change — he argued the jump from Philadelphia's infrastructure to Carolina's, under ownership he says doesn't understand what it takes to support a player, explains the decline more than any loss of talent.
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Sanders had an almost-50-yard run in the third quarter but Harris was unimpressed — 'he didn't look all that fast to me,' the seas parted around the left side and linebacker Zack Baun ran him down from directly behind. Then on the same drive, in the red zone right before the thunderstorm, Sanders fumbled and never touched the ball again after the break. Harris' read is that whatever committee threat Sanders posed to [[Javonte Williams]] is likely a one-week story.
