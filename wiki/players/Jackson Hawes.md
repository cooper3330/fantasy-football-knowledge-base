# Jackson Hawes

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]): Hawes, at Georgia Tech by way of Yale, may sit near the bottom of the RSP board — which weights pass catchers who can block ahead of blockers who can catch — but Waldman calls him the best inline blocker in the class and does not think he is alone in that. Against man-over assignments he is a skilled striker with good footwork and leverage who pushes defensive ends off their spots and controls interactions long enough for plays to work as designed, and Waldman stresses this is ACC tape, not Ivy League tape. He uses a one-arm technique efficiently to counter defenders trying to redirect him and is hard to shake once he locks a hand on, thanks to his center of gravity, strength and stance. As a ball carrier he spins off contact with tight motion, gets his feet through wraps and kicks his heels toward his backside the way good running backs do, needing only a couple of steps to transition downhill from a perimeter approach. Waldman calls him one of the most intriguing skill players in the class from a scouting perspective — implicitly a football, not fantasy, endorsement.
- 2026-01-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-26]]) [Dynasty]: a hold and an early stash worth targeting, particularly in leagues that reward TE depth or use 1.5 PPR scoring. Waldman is a big fan, noting Hawes is already Buffalo's best blocking tight end as a rookie -- ahead of Dawson Knox, who is 'not a slouch' in that department -- which shows he's forced his way onto the field through smart, high-leverage usage that should only expand. He frames playing alongside Josh Allen, plus the coaching change, as tailwinds that could 'float' several of Buffalo's tight end boats, though he separately flags [[Dalton Kincaid]]'s durability/usage as a lingering question given the team limited his snaps even when healthy.
- 2026-02-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-16]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is starting to 'feel it' on Hawes given rumblings that [[Dawson Knox]] could be released, calling Hawes possibly already the best blocker in Buffalo's tight end room (a real bar, since Waldman also rates Knox highly as a blocker) and someone who's flashed enough with his hands to keep developing as a receiver. Waldman says he's already added Hawes 'quietly' as a late-round luxury stash in leagues with 1.5-per-catch PPR and tight end premium scoring, viewing him as the cheaper option Buffalo is likely to lean on if Knox is moved.
- 2026-06-16 — According to Matt Bove ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-06-16]]): the Bills have really liked Jackson Hawes as a run-game asset, calling him 'almost like having an extra offensive lineman on the field' -- 'he has been that good.' The offseason talk has centered on finding ways to get him on the field even more, and Bove floated the possibility of Hawes-and-Kincaid two-tight-end sets replacing the traditional Dawson Knox-and-Kincaid pairing.
