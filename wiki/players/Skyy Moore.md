# Skyy Moore

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-13]]), in the Chiefs' offseason-outlook segment: a direct scheme-fit/evaluation criticism rather than a talent grade — "I don't think Skyy Moore ever should have been an outside receiver. I think that was a mis-evaluation of that player." Doesn't expect him in Kansas City's future plans at all, and would rather see him traded to another team to restart his role than continue as a "complete blank slate" outside option for the Chiefs' 2024 receiver rebuild.
- 2024-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-29]]): Moore surprisingly survived cuts, to the visible disgust of the fan base. Harmon's view is that Moore was never likely to be special, but he should not have finished as one of the least efficient receivers in the league — that outcome was a deployment failure, since Kansas City pushed him into outside snaps he could not handle rather than using him as a short-area slot player. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) is harsher, calling it 'textbook 101 how not to treat a young wide receiver,' saying Moore got eaten alive outside and that the Chiefs killed his confidence, while noting he looked good as a rookie in the slot filling in for [[JuJu Smith-Schuster]]. Harmon adds he had expected Moore and [[Rashee Rice]] to occupy each other's roles, but concedes Rice is far more dangerous with the ball in his hands.
