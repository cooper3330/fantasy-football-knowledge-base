# Javonte Williams

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]): feels Williams as a real value right now, theorizing last year's underwhelming return from his catastrophic multi-ligament knee injury reflected him never getting back to full speed rather than a talent decline — the same injury-not-skill framing he applies to [[Tony Pollard]], [[Austin Ekeler]] and [[Travis Kelce]] elsewhere in this episode. Floats a possible one-two punch with backfield-mate [[Samaje Perine]] in Denver's backfield, though the specific complementary-role upside for Perine is left underexplored. Explicit caveat: multi-ligament tears like his can take a full extra year to shake off, so a slow or unconvincing summer would signal he may never quite be the same player.
- 2024-05-17 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-17]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo says it 'smells like' Javonte Williams isn't the fit Sean Payton wants in Denver's backfield — he can't 'see the picture' the way [[Blake Watson]] or [[Audric Estime]] might, and Payton's scheme leans on a timing-based decisiveness that Williams' downhill instincts reportedly haven't shown.
