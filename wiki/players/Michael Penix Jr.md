- 2026-10-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]], with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) raising the counterpoint ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-10-08]]): the Falcons offense has flipped 'night and day' since Penix replaced Cooper Rush — Harmon's own offensive efficiency metric (success rate, EPA, explosive-play rate, points/drive) had Atlanta ranked 26th and 32nd in weeks 1-2, then 2nd and 1st the two weeks since. Koh's skepticism: both were nationally-televised 'island games' against the Packers and Saints, so it may be matchup-driven small-sample noise rather than real. Harmon calls it 'pretty real' regardless, crediting Bill Callahan's O-line (even after losing Jake Matthews), passing-game coordinator Tanner Angstrand's scheme, and a run game split roughly 65% inside/35% outside zone pairing Bijan Robinson and Brian Robinson. Notably, Penix is 10-of-14 for 152 yards and a TD on under-center dropbacks in these two starts, versus just six total under-center attempts across his first two NFL seasons combined under the prior coaching staff.
