# Javon Baker

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-29]]), on Senior Bowl prospects: a transfer from Alabama to UCF that Waldman says the industry's better draft writers are already onto. Good size, "really excellent catch-point skills," and a strong release package off the line — which he calls a very important part of playing wide receiver. Ceiling comp is a "2/1b"-type number-one-adjacent receiver rather than a true alpha — "could he be a number one receiver? I don't know if he's that good" — but calls him underrated overall, with "a little bust quality to his game, but not a ton" if he can refine his game and get more consistent.
- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): needs tighter transitions after the catch to fully cash in real open-field skills he already has — a tight spin he can execute immediately after a high-point catch, and the ability to drop his weight for sudden stops/direction changes using only two steps to turn 90 degrees, which Waldman calls "starter quality stuff." Identifies the depth of pursuit well and times his moves to beat multiple defenders, presses unblocked defenders before choosing a cutback or bounce, sets up blockers, and anticipates unanticipated obstacles — an intriguing player after the catch.

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-28 (Going Deep - If We Were NFL GMs)]]): his "plant my flag" player of the episode — his fourth-highest-graded receiver of the cycle, higher than any grade he gave last cycle. Praises a true three-level skill set at X that's rare in this class: separates at all phases of the route, including late with full extension, and is a "roller" at the catch point — rotating away from the ball on contested extension catches to survive both the ground and the defender's hit. Not overly fast (21.3 mph top speed) but a good accelerator, competitive after the catch, and best on intermediate in-breaking routes, comped in stem-setup savvy to [[Brandon Aiyuk]] coming out of Arizona State. Projects him an immediate X/movement starter in the third or fourth round, the class's third true outside impact prospect behind [[Malik Nabers]] and [[Rome Odunze]]. [[Matt Waldman]], same episode: had him lower but calls the gap to Angelo's grade marginal — flags a low margin for error as an athlete who is good, not elite, by starter standards at X, with a specific mechanical flaw (widens his hands just before the catch, causing drops/fights with the ball) that needs fixing; comps his ceiling to a "one-b" flanker with enough WR1 traits to be a compelling 1A alternative.


- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-11 (Going Deep - Fave Evals, Coach-Killer Candidates, and High-End Career Outcomes)]]): names Baker his favorite write-up of the cycle — found more nuance to his outside-receiver game than the public "Bama transfer who'll improve at a smaller school" narrative expected. Praises a genuine three-level game, physicality and activity after the catch, and specifically his limb dexterity/body control tracking the ball in the air to manage getting in bounds, securing the catch, and surviving the ground and the defender simultaneously — calls it unique for a player who hasn't played a ton of football.
- 2024-04-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-23]]) [Dynasty]: Baker (UCF, 6'1"/202, 4.54 40, 37" vertical, 10'1" broad) tested poorly but Harmon called the tape elite, saying it's 'all gas, no brakes' for him. Flagged red flag: 70.7% of snaps came lined up on the outside left, matching a historical bust pattern in Harmon's charting data (Kevin White, Corey Coleman, Josh Doctson, Laquan Treadwell, Quentin Johnston, Cedric Tillman) versus few outside-left hits (DJ Moore, Rasheed Rice, JuJu Smith-Schuster) — though Baker didn't align this way in 2022, muddying the signal. Elsewhere the profile was excellent: 78.3% success vs. press (88th percentile, third-best charted behind Marvin Harrison Jr. and Rome Odunze), 70.3% vs. man, 80.2% vs. zone. He ran the nine route on a class-high 35.6% of routes (more than double No. 2 Jermaine Burton's 21.7%) and still posted 61.5% success on it, plus 93.3% on the post and 87.5% on the corner — 45% of his routes were nine/post/corner combined. Contested catch rate approached 70% on largely difficult looks. Steve Smith Sr. comped him to Brandon Lloyd; Harmon comped him to Michael Gallup — a vertical X who could develop into an underrated intermediate technician — and separately floated a 'better version of Deonte Harty (Deami Brown)' upside case. Weaknesses: no comeback/flat route reps charted (NA), and dig-route success (62%) needs work to become a true No. 1 target earner. Co-host James Koh (untracked) ranks Baker ahead of both Adonai Mitchell and [[Troy Franklin]] but a clear step below Brian Thomas Jr.; both hosts want him landing on a receiver-needy team (Bills, Chiefs, or Cowboys named) as an immediate vertical X rather than in a slow-developing WR3 role.
- 2024-04-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-25]]) [Dynasty]: one of three non-top-three players singled out as elite contested-catch winners (with [[Xavier Legette]] and [[Keon Coleman]]), ranking Baker ahead of Coleman for his ball tracking and body control against tight coverage despite a lower raw number -- 68.2% contested-catch rate -- which Harmon attributes partly to inaccurate UCF quarterback play inflating the difficulty of his contested opportunities rather than to a flaw in Baker's own game. Also considered him for the "deep-ball specialist" superlative given heavy go-route usage, but felt there was more nuance to his all-around game to justify that label. Harmon calls him "a straight-up artist" adjusting to the ball with a defender draped on him.
