# Isaiah Likely

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-05-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-09]]) [Dynasty]: Discussing Baltimore's receiving room, Harmon said the Ravens 'gotta try to find some way to get Isaiah Likely on the field because he's just too good' to keep stashed behind [[Mark Andrews]], flagging his 16.1 yards-per-reception average last year (boosted by several big plays) on only 64 targets as evidence of untapped efficiency. He suggested Baltimore, with two traditional in-line tight ends who both offer real target-earning ability when split out wide, is one of the few teams that could credibly lean on two-tight-end sets more heavily this year, even though most teams that talk up 12-personnel don't actually follow through on usage.
- 2024-07-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-08]]) [Best Ball]: Waldman is skeptical of [[Isaiah Likely]] having real standalone value at his current TE23 price, since the Ravens ran 12-personnel only 11% of the time last year -- far below the Todd Monken-led Georgia offenses that led the nation in two-TE sets -- though he noted that could shift with [[Derrick Henry]] now in the backfield, whose extra gap-blocking presence could open more 12-personnel looks. He cited Likely's 40 targets/5 TDs last year and expanded reps in the slot and backfield as reasons for some optimism, and said the price is fair as a [[Mark Andrews]] handcuff/insurance play without paying a 'handcuff premium' on top of it. Co-host Bob Harris (not tracked) floated speculation that heavy Likely chatter could be setup for a trade of Likely, Charlie Kolar, or even Andrews, which Waldman said he wasn't necessarily buying but found worth raising.
- 2024-07-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-22]]): Likely is a good value at a TE21 price because of two distinct paths, which Waldman puts explicit odds on. If Mark Andrews (who says he is 100% after last year's injury) gets hurt, Likely taking over as a fantasy starter is an 85–90% proposition. The second path — Likely moving around the formation into receiver roles and earning real volume — Waldman puts at only about 15%, because [[Rashad Bateman]] and [[Zay Flowers]] are better matchup options on the outside, so Likely gets scheme plays rather than sustained targets. Bob Harris (not tracked) is more bullish on that second path, citing Todd Monken's long history of heavy two-tight-end usage including at Georgia. Both hosts note they are heavily invested in Andrews continuing to dominate.
- 2024-09-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Likely is a must-add. Kansas City devoted anywhere from two to four defenders to [[Mark Andrews]], which vacated zones for Likely, and he still got hurt reaching for a difficult pass, returned, and nearly won the game. The usage change is the signal: Baltimore ran 53 percent two-tight-end sets in this game, up from roughly 10 percent last year, after an offseason of talk about getting Likely involved that never showed up in preseason — no reason to reveal the plan early. Waldman had flagged him in his fantasy notebook about two weeks before the season, and says if anything happens to Andrews he goes to the moon. He is explicit, though, that Likely's emergence does not mean Andrews is finished.
