# Brandon Cooks

## Expert Takes

- 2024-07-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-16]]): Harmon projects Cooks, entering his age-31 season, as 'a pretty good three' right now -- effective when targeted but not earning enough volume, with most of his usage still downfield -- and pegs him for roughly 86.25 targets behind [[CeeDee Lamb]]'s 180. Both hosts flagged real decline risk: Harmon cited age-driven concern about eroding explosiveness, while James Koh (not tracked) wondered aloud if 'father time' is catching up given inconsistent flashes in 2023. [Redraft (Standard)]
- 2025-11-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-20]]): New Orleans surprisingly released Cooks even though he'd been on the field a ton -- 283 routes run this season, third-most among Saints receivers behind [[Chris Olave]] and Rashid Shaheed. Despite that snap volume, Cooks was targeted on just 8.8% of his routes, the lowest rate of any receiver league-wide with 250-plus routes, and averaged only 0.58 yards per route run -- Harmon calls him 'the most sacrificial player you could possibly imagine.' He ran 37.6% of his snaps from the slot but wasn't a true X; his real job was running 'for the love of the game' routes at full speed to clear space and leverage for Olave's underneath work. Harmon says Devaughn Vele can't replicate that specific role and expects UDFA [[Mason Tipton]] to inherit it instead.
- 2025-11-27 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-27]]): Brandon Cooks signed with Buffalo in a corresponding move that ended the [[Elijah Moore]] era there, and Harmon expects Cooks to inherit Moore's 'on the field, don't get the football' role rather than provide juice. As of November 19 (pre-Week 12), Cooks was the only qualifying WR with 250-plus routes below 15% targets per route run at just 8.8%, the lowest by a wide margin, alongside [[Andrei Iosivas]] (10.6%), [[Xavier Hutchinson]] (12.1%), [[Darius Slayton]] (13.4%) and [[Jalen Nailor]] (13.8%); his 0.58 yards per route run was also the group's worst. Harmon notes Cooks has mostly run 'for the love of the game' decoy routes in New Orleans -- full-speed motion verticals clearing space for [[Chris Olave]] and [[Rashid Shaheed]] -- and questions who he'd be doing that for in Buffalo. Both hosts are skeptical the signing moves the needle; James Coe called it 'literally just to say we did something,' and Harmon says Cooks hasn't been a true man-beating speed threat in three years.
- 2025-12-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-30]]): Cooks, at 32, put up 101 yards on 6 targets/4 catches against Philadelphia, playing 85% of his snaps outside and finishing 4th among Bills WRs in routes run despite not being fully unleashed -- the first Buffalo outside WR to crack 100 yards since Keon Coleman's Week 1 game (co-host James Koh, untracked, noted Tyrell Shavers had 90 yards in Week 11). Harmon remains skeptical this means anything: Tom Brady had flagged on the broadcast that Cooks had more drops than catches with Buffalo, and Cooks had gone three straight weeks (14-16) without a catch before this game after struggling to crack a useful rotation in New Orleans pre-release. Harmon calls Buffalo's WR room 'one of the worst pass-catching groups in the NFL' with zero margin for error unless Josh Allen plays like Superman, and puts roughly 90% of the blame on personnel over OC Joe Brady's scheme (mesh-heavy concepts because none of these receivers win one-on-one against man coverage), though Koh partially defends Brady, arguing the staff hasn't designed enough touches to get these players the ball.
- 2026-01-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-15]]): Cooks was expected to be just a rotational piece for Buffalo but has been genuinely impressive in small doses -- Harmon said whenever he's called on 'he's making those damn plays,' running a route on 54% of dropbacks in the most recent game and becoming the offense's most important outside receiver, even though he was a waiver-wire pickup and neither host expected him to become a difference-maker.
- 2026-01-20 — According to James Koh (untracked co-host) and [[Matt Harmon]] on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-20]]: the disputed Brandon Cooks fourth-quarter/overtime catch that helped sink the Bills' Divisional Round loss was, in both hosts' opinion, correctly ruled not a catch by the letter of the rule -- Koh noted Cooks himself got up without selling it, which he reads as evidence Cooks knew it wasn't a catch, though both hosts also joked that if Cooks had sold it with an emphatic gesture the call likely flips given NFL officiating's inconsistency. Separately, both credited Cooks for being far more productive and clutch for Buffalo across the season than either expected entering the year -- Koh called it 'not a nothing-burger' -- while noting he left some plays on the field in this specific game, which is now typical of him at this stage of his career.
