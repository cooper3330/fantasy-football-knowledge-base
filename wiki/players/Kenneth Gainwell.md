# Kenneth Gainwell

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-09-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-08]]): the Steelers backfield was the truly unexpected result of the week and shattered Harris's mind palace, with Mike Tomlin apparently having told the truth on his depth chart. Gainwell was clearly the starter in the first quarter, out-snapping [[Jaylen Warren]] seven to four, and was in for the two-minute drill at the end of the half, where he got crushed and nearly fumbled. He also made arguably the biggest play of the game, forcing a fumble on a kickoff after a Pittsburgh touchdown. Harris is nonetheless not that excited about adding him and does not think he is a particularly impressive player, so the news value here is mostly what it does to [[Kaleb Johnson]] and to Warren's workload rather than Gainwell as a fantasy asset.
- 2025-09-09 — According to guest Jake Ciely of The Athletic (not a tracked expert) on [[Chris Harris]]'s show ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-09]]): Gainwell is his number four add and the only running back on his list, at 1% rostered. Pittsburgh drafted [[Kaleb Johnson]] to be the new [[Najee Harris]], but Johnson is not a factor right now and might not be all year; meanwhile [[Jaylen Warren]] is the same Warren he has always been despite the new contract, and Gainwell stepped in for roughly a 50-50 split. Ciely likes the touch volume as an RB3 in an offense he expects to be more run-heavy than in Week 1 without [[Aaron Rodgers]] lighting it up; he would not go crazy with FAAB. Harris pushes back harder — he knows what Gainwell is 'down to a fair thee well', calls him diminishing returns, notes the Eagles kept trying him and then thought better of it, and expects Pittsburgh to do the same. Both agree there are no great running back answers on waivers right now.
- 2025-09-29 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-29]]): with [[Jaylen Warren]] a surprise inactive, this was a Gainwell game — and Harris pointedly notes that inside the Vikings five it was the journeyman sub-200-pound back in the game rather than the 225-pounder. Gainwell got three carries inside the five: he converted the first at the end of the first quarter after a third-and-short deep ball to [[DK Metcalf]], converted a second in the third quarter, and was stuffed on a fourth-down call with the Steelers up 10 and four minutes left, which helped launch a Minnesota comeback. Harris will not call him amazing, says he played okay, and expects Warren back after the Week 5 bye — so he does not see Gainwell as rosterable in most leagues going forward. He is similarly dismissive of [[Kaleb Johnson]], who barely out-snapped Warren.
- 2025-10-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-02]]): Gainwell's big yardage game against Minnesota (25 fantasy points, more than [[Bijan Robinson]] or [[Tyler Allgeier]] managed on that defense) came with huge creases, but the cutbacks and setups were 'a perfect game from a run standpoint' and teaching tape for what good running back play looks like behind good blocking. His profile: essentially Tiki Barber with Devin Singletary's athleticism minus the short-area quickness — smart, technically and conceptually sound, does everything well enough, but without the athletic ability to be more. Waldman recalled hoping Gainwell's pre-draft training year would add weight and twitch from 190 to 205-210, and the combine showed he had not gained much. [[Brandon Angelo]] agreed, calling him a better receiver than he was credited for, a back who does not give you negative plays: [[Jaylen Warren]] hits home runs, Gainwell hits singles and keeps an offense on schedule. Angelo would not be surprised by a closer-to-50/50 split after the bye and expects a strong second quarter of the season; Waldman capped him at roughly 600-800 yards this year and warned against long-term over-excitement — 'he's not the next Priest Holmes' — while both agreed he is a valuable Chester Taylor/Jerome Harrison type who would have real fantasy value if Warren missed time.
