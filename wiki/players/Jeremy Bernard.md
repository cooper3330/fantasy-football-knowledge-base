# Jeremy Bernard

## Expert Takes

- 2026-06-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-29]]) [Dynasty]: Feels Aaron Rodgers individually working with rookie Jeremy Bernard will matter long-term but downplays the short-term significance, comparing it to a past example of a scout overselling individualized veteran-QB reps with an undrafted running back as more important than it actually was. Projects Bernard to clearly beat out [[Roman Wilson]] for the third-receiver role -- 'not going to be even close' -- forecasting close to 75 targets, about 600 yards and 4 touchdowns. Compares the opportunity to what people hoped [[Calvin Austin III]] would be in this offense, a hope that ultimately disappointed, but notes Bernard now has more established weapons drawing attention elsewhere on the roster.
- 2026-10-01 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-10-01]]) [Dynasty]: Jeremy Bernard is Angelo's top breakout pick among rookies -- he out-snapped Roman Wilson 15-to-5 after a Wilson drop turned into an interception, and offers run-game value Pittsburgh's other receivers can't match, including a 40-50-yard lead block on a Darnell Washington run and clean interior blocks walling off a linebacker for Jalen Warren. Angelo calls him one of the best post-catch players in his draft class dating back to his Washington tape, and says he's positioned to take over volume because DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. 'don't look good' and appear to be playing hurt, and Wilson is making too many mistakes, while none of those three offer anything after the catch the way Bernard does.
