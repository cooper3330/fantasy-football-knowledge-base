# Deshaun Watson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]): "open to the idea" that Watson can be a real difference-maker again after the Browns acquire [[Jerry Jeudy]] — cites "good quality play from him last year in spurts" that "got better as the season went along" as the basis for optimism, without a firm projection attached.

- 2024-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-21]]): flags Watson as the swing factor for the whole receiver-room gamble Cleveland has made — "quite frankly, I don't know if this is a team that has that kind of time" to develop [[Jerry Jeudy]]/[[Elijah Moore]]/[[Cedric Tillman]] into reliable options, framing Cleveland's timeline as needing to win in 2024, not a two-to-three-year plan, and calling him along with the receiver room's reliability the central swing question for the offense ("just don't set my watch to it" applied to the team overall, and to Watson specifically). Notes losing [[Nick Chubb]]'s complementary run-game impact removes another stabilizer, leaving the offense's identity resting on [[Amari Cooper]] and [[David Njoku]] regardless of Watson's play.
