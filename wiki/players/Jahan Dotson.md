## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]), asked to evaluate Dotson after a confounding down 2023 (Dotson himself cited multiple explanations without full clarity, per Waldman): grades him "a very skilled wide receiver three who can give you wide receiver two numbers in the right offense," but is unsure incoming OC Kliff Kingsbury's scheme (not a tracked entity — coach) will be that offense — output will hinge heavily on whichever quarterback situation Washington settles into. Credits him as a good route runner capable of functioning as a matchup WR2. Notes Kingsbury has a track record of running his favored receiver out of the same spot on nearly every snap — a dig at his Arizona usage of Rondale Moore and Andy Isabella (neither tracked) — and while Waldman thinks Dotson is a better player than either of those two, he's not convinced that guarantees the scheme actually unlocks him. Discussed in the same breath as [[Terry McLaurin]], whose forced X-only usage is partly attributed to Washington lacking another true X-caliber receiver — Dotson included.
- 2024-05-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-13]]) [Dynasty]: Dotson is more of an unknown than [[Terry McLaurin]] — Waldman wants to see him build on the flashes shown two seasons ago, stay healthy, and find usage in Washington's offense under [[Jayden Daniels]]. Despite the uncertainty, his explicit recommendation for anyone who traded for or drafted Dotson is to hold, not sell, calling it too early to have full information and joking he'll still be futilely holding Dotson well into the 2030s if it doesn't pan out.
