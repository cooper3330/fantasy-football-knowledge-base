- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]), asked to evaluate Dotson after a confounding down 2023 (Dotson himself cited multiple explanations without full clarity, per Waldman): grades him "a very skilled wide receiver three who can give you wide receiver two numbers in the right offense," but is unsure incoming OC Kliff Kingsbury's scheme (not a tracked entity — coach) will be that offense — output will hinge heavily on whichever quarterback situation Washington settles into. Notes Kingsbury has a track record of running his favored receiver out of the same spot on nearly every snap — a dig at his Arizona usage of Rondale Moore and Andy Isabella (neither tracked) — and while Waldman thinks Dotson is a better player than either of those two, he's not convinced that guarantees the scheme actually unlocks him. Discussed in the same breath as [[Terry McLaurin]], whose forced X-only usage is partly attributed to Washington lacking another true X-caliber receiver — Dotson included.
