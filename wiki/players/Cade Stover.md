# Cade Stover

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his
> name as "Kate Stover"; normalized here to the real name, Cade Stover.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: a former linebacker/basketball player with "the budding skills to become what Hunter Henry would like to be physically as a receiving tight end" — Henry still has the edge as a blocker and on the conceptual side of the passing game, but Waldman doesn't think that edge holds for long given Stover's development rate. Calls him one of his favorite tight ends in the class to watch — excellent technical development for an inexperienced pass catcher, with a habit of attacking targets at the earliest available point and optimal hand use. Named, alongside [[Brock Bowers]], as one of the class's two most pro-ready prospects if used as a receiver/H-back rather than an in-line blocker.

- 2024-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on *Feel It or F@#k It* ([[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-01]]): "he could start right now" for a lot of teams in a [[Sam LaPorta]]-type move-tight-end role, though not as good a route runner as LaPorta — "looks more like a really good linebacker who's figured out how to become a tight end." Where he shines is catching the ball: textbook technique attacking targets from a variety of angles in tight coverage or the open field, like he's "been playing tight end for about 15 years." A bruiser who's still nimble in the open field. Projects as a sleeper, including in end-of-redraft formats, with a good landing spot.
- 2024-04-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-29]]) [Dynasty]: called Stover's landing spot in Houston the best of the three rookie TE spots discussed (ahead of Ja'Tavion Sanders and Ben Sinnott), noting the Texans just re-signed Dalton Schultz but that deal likely expires within two years, while Tank Dell is the only receiver under contract past 2025. He projected Stover as the better intermediate-route pass-catcher relative to Schultz's underneath-heavy role, and cited reports that C.J. Stroud specifically wanted Stover drafted. Waldman flagged this as bad news for Brevin Jordan's target share, and noted this take actually contradicted his own post-draft rankings ('contrarian Monday').
