# Tyrod Taylor

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-10-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-21]]): With [[Justin Fields]] benched/injured, Matt Harmon expects Tyrod Taylor (36) to take over in New York, and said that while Taylor is not dynamic and not a top-20 NFL quarterback, he provides professionalism, preparation and steadiness that lets a staff build a simplified, realistic game plan around him -- comping it to how Daniel Jones stabilizes Indianapolis relative to the higher-ceiling but unsteady Anthony Richardson. Harmon doesn't think the Jets go winless with Taylor starting and expects them to 'steal a win every now and again,' especially once [[Garrett Wilson]] is back, though he stressed Taylor caps the offense's ceiling.
- 2025-10-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-21 (Week 8 Waivers & W7 Reviews)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Taylor took over for [[Justin Fields]] and, while Harris credited him as a steady veteran ('the trains run on time, the clock in his head functions'), he wasn't much better statistically -- he should have thrown a deep touchdown to [[Josh Reynolds]] on back-to-back plays but overthrew one incomplete and had the second intercepted, and he was sacked three times just like Fields had been against a Panthers pass rush that recorded six sacks in the game after totaling fewer than six all season entering it. Harris explicitly said not to crown him 'the king of getting the ball out on time.'
- 2025-11-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-25]]): took over at quarterback for the Jets against Baltimore and should have thrown a pick-six on his very first attempt (the defender dropped it), then threw a meaningless garbage-time interception late. In between he played conservatively but Harris says Taylor 'gives everything a little more life' than Justin Fields had, even though the offense still managed only seven points into the fourth quarter.
- 2025-12-09 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Taylor left the Dolphins game with a groin injury in the first quarter, forcing rookie Brady Cook into action and sinking the entire Jets passing offense (21-0 loss, only score a punt-return TD). Harris says no Jets receiver, including Breece Hall or Adonai Mitchell, is usable in Week 15 vs. Jacksonville if anyone other than Taylor starts at quarterback.
