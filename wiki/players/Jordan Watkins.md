# Jordan Watkins

## Expert Takes

- 2025-06-04 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) with [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-06-04]]): the 49ers' 2025 fourth-round pick out of Ole Miss is an inside-outside flanker type at 5-11, 196 with a 4.37 forty, but a 36.5-inch vertical and a 9-11 broad jump — under ten feet, which Koh calls a problem — and limited lateral quickness. Koh warns the college production is concentrated: 49 catches for 906 yards and nine touchdowns, but five touchdowns came in one game against Arkansas and two more against Duke, so seven of nine touchdowns and 48 percent of his yards came in two games. Harmon adds context: over 18 yards per reception, only 17.4 percent slot snaps in 2024 though more slot work in 2022-23, and three straight years above six yards after catch per reception, which fits what this offense prioritizes. Both see him as the most plausible fill-in for the [[Ricky Pearsall]] role, with Koh liking his engagement as a run blocker in a Shanahan offense.
- 2025-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception - 2025-07-23]]): Watkins is an interesting fourth-round rookie out of Ole Miss, mostly because of opportunity in a thinned-out 49ers room. Harmon saw things on his table pre-draft, thinks he can play along the boundaries and is pretty good on deeper routes, but stops short of a projection. The caveat is that San Francisco still lacks a receiver who breaks inside and delivers deep in-breakers other than [[Jauan Jennings]], and Harmon's read of the team's July activity — signing Equanimeous St. Brown on July 22 and running a batch of veteran tryouts — is a sign the front office is not pleased with the room as constructed.
