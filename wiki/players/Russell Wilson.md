# Russell Wilson

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]): a hedged, not-fully-resolved read. Waldman expects Wilson to open 2024 as a Week 1 starter somewhere, but is doubtful he's still a starter by the time that season (or his career) is over, and says plainly he hasn't watched enough of Wilson's 2023 Denver tape to form a fully confident opinion either way. Relays a harder-line outside view from **Dwain McFarland** (not a tracked expert): that the league-wide shift toward Cover-2-high shells, which took away the deep game Wilson's whole career was built on, effectively ended his run as a viable NFL starter. Waldman pushes back on pinning 2023's struggles entirely on Wilson himself — Denver's receiving corps ([[Jerry Jeudy]], [[Courtland Sutton]]) isn't strong enough to support a downfield-attacking passer regardless of arm talent, and the receiver room got worse in-season (Tim Patrick's injury, K.J. Hamler's retirement). Explicitly "not all out on" Wilson. Floats Pittsburgh as a landing spot he's "reading between the lines" on and would like to see, or alternatively a Cleveland fit if the Browns move on from Deshaun Watson (not a tracked page).
