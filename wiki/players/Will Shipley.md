## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: flagged as underrated in the class's early-round-talent-caliber conversation, not getting enough attention given his receiving pedigree and speed — Angelo cites a 6.4-second 55-meter time coming out of high school as a genuinely good mark. Both expect the combine to be the real swing event for his draft stock: a low-4.3/high-4.4 40 keeps him off the Day 3 range some public boards currently have him in, in the same way Chase Brown's testing moved his own 2023 stock. Waldman's fuller scouting case: very good movement skills, a good tight-crease runner, technically sound across scheme fits, and a real receiving asset — but projects his likely NFL role as complementary (a Dion Lewis/James White mold, neither tracked) rather than as a lead-back successor to a bell-cow role like [[Travis Etienne]]'s (not tracked).
