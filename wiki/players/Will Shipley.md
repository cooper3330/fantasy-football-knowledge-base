# Will Shipley

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: flagged as underrated in the class's early-round-talent-caliber conversation, not getting enough attention given his receiving pedigree and speed — Angelo cites a 6.4-second 55-meter time coming out of high school as a genuinely good mark. Both expect the combine to be the real swing event for his draft stock: a low-4.3/high-4.4 40 keeps him off the Day 3 range some public boards currently have him in, in the same way Chase Brown's testing moved his own 2023 stock. Waldman's fuller scouting case: very good movement skills, a good tight-crease runner, technically sound across scheme fits, and a real receiving asset — but projects his likely NFL role as complementary (a Dion Lewis/James White mold, neither tracked) rather than as a lead-back successor to a bell-cow role like [[Travis Etienne]]'s (not tracked).
- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast [Dynasty]: reaffirms receiving as where Shipley makes an early impact, but pushes back on scouting reports that undersell his interior rushing — calls him a "notably quick processor" at the line who sets up and splits double teams well, and a light-footed, well-integrated cutback runner who could hold up between the tackles better than many reports suggest despite modest power/contact balance. Reaching that ceiling depends on adding muscle to his frame without losing quickness or agility.
- 2025-07-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-07]]) [Best Ball]: Shipley's buzz is too much. The upside is real — a very good receiver behind a great offensive line — but Waldman sees recency bias in 'who was on the depth chart last year,' and argues that if [[Saquon Barkley]] goes down, the between-the-tackles and closing-out-games work goes to [[A.J. Dillon]], not the 212-pound Shipley. He also thinks Dillon can cut into Shipley's time toward a 50-50 split even with Barkley healthy. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) disclosed he has been accruing late Shipley shares — he used to get him in the 20th round in early best balls — but says the price has risen past his interest, and Shipley now goes ahead of [[Blake Corum]], [[Jarquez Hunter]], [[Brashard Smith]], [[Raheem Mostert]] and [[Jaleel McLaughlin]].
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Shipley rotated in on the very first series, which briefly suggested he was the clear handcuff, and 'got free for a good one' in the first half — but he left the game with a rib injury, after which [[A.J. Dillon]] took the third-quarter rotation snaps. The split ended up three carries each. Harris' conclusion is that we are 'probably right where we started' with these backups: [[Saquon Barkley]] likely doesn't have a handcuff worth rostering right now, and if Barkley ever does get hurt there will be a mad scramble in which one or both backups get added. Pending clarity on the rib injury, he isn't naming a winner.
