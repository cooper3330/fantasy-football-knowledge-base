# Tez Johnson

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-05 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-05 (Going Deep)]]): the Oregon slot receiver he kept circling on 2023 tape ('who is 15?') ahead of [[Bo Nix]], [[Troy Franklin]] and [[Bucky Irving]], and whom he calls the best receiver on that offense — roughly 86 catches, about 1,100 yards and 10 touchdowns, with 93%-plus of snaps in the slot. He is listed around 5-10 and 160-170 pounds. Angelo pushes back on the reflexive [[Tank Dell]] comp: Dell can play inside-out and separates vertically, which Johnson does not, but the one part of Dell's Houston tape he does share is elite low-red-zone separation and being a quarterback-friendly option in high-leverage situations, where Angelo rates him among the best in the nation. Chain-mover with after-catch creation and some downfield speed. Projects him as a day-two pick.
