# Dylan Laube

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his
> name as "Dylan Lobby"; normalized here to the real name of the New
> Hampshire running back, Dylan Laube.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): the episode's headline small-school riser — a small-school (New Hampshire) player who, in Senior Bowl one-on-ones, "was running with the receivers and torching defensive backs" as a pass catcher and also beat linebackers. Angelo's read: this is exactly the kind of confirmation Senior Bowl week exists to provide — "maybe the NFL might be higher on this kid than we might have thought" — since receiving work rarely shows up on his college tape at the volume needed to grade it confidently otherwise.
- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]), relaying secondhand Senior Bowl reports rather than his own tape: corroborates Laube as a name he's specifically hearing about and needs to revisit.

- 2024-02-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-12]]) [Dynasty]: his own tape-based follow-up, no longer just relaying secondhand Senior Bowl buzz. Confirms the small-school-riser framing directly — All-Star Games exist precisely to test whether a small-school player (5'10", ~210 lbs, faced FCS-level competition) "can really hold up with big-time football," and Laube's Senior Bowl week was "validation that he holds up pretty well": quick, efficient, a skillful pass catcher who understands how to use his pads, not powerful but capable enough to handle between-the-tackles work. Names him a genuine [[Austin Ekeler]] archetype candidate — "that doesn't mean he's exactly Austin Ekeler, it just means he has some traits that could put him in a role similar to that." Explicitly the best 2024-fantasy-relevance bet of a trio of underrated Waldman-liked backs this episode (with Georgia's Daijun Edwards and Boise State's George Holani) — "Dylan Laube is probably your best shot" — while cautioning this is not a strong running back class at the top and none of the three should be top-12-rookie-round picks.
