# Carson Ryan

- 2026-03-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-02]]) [Dynasty]: the highest PFF-graded tight end of the 2025 season, but he was not invited to the combine, which left Waldman audibly stunned ('fuck yes' he's the best skill player not invited this year). At 6'4/250-ish, Waldman believes he may be the best blocker in a deep tight end class -- not quite pound-for-pound the single best, but able to hold up against defensive ends on the front side, with strong technique (punch, angles) and excellent stalk blocking since teams will use him outside a fair bit. Called him patient, someone who forces defenders to approach rather than tipping his position. He can also catch downfield, especially off play action, with decent quickness. Waldman said Ryan may land in his top five tight ends this year, possibly top three, and projects him as a potential eventual starter in the right offense.
- 2026-03-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-05]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said he is 'much higher' on BYU tight end [[Carson Ryan]] (formerly at Utah and UCLA) than industry consensus and was stunned Ryan was not invited to the 2026 Combine, calling him a likely fifth-ranked tight end on his board who could become his overall TE1 if the 12/13-personnel, blocking-tight-end shift discussed earlier in the episode (Max Toscano's theory) holds. At roughly 6'4', 250 lbs, Waldman praised Ryan's technical blocking -- angle-setting, patience, hand placement, hip-into-the-punch mechanics -- as more advanced than bigger tight ends in this class, capable of pulling, lead-blocking, or holding up in pass protection against non-elite pass rushers. He also praised Ryan as a functional, not explosive, receiver who consistently uncovers late on routes, comparing one rep to [[Ladd McConkey]] working open for [[Justin Herbert]] in the red zone, and noted PFF independently graded Ryan the nation's top-rated tight end for the season. Waldman's overall player comp is [[Jake Ferguson]]: not elite at any single trait, but good enough across blocking and receiving to be hard to keep off the field -- whether that profile becomes fantasy-relevant depends heavily on landing spot.
- 2026-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-11]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman calls BYU's Ryan (also played at Utah and UCLA) the best blocker in the 2026 tight end class pound-for-pound despite being a modest 250 pounds -- praising his patience setting up blocks (baiting the defender's angle rather than rushing the point of attack), his hand-strike technique (getting inside to the chest plate, uppercutting and rolling his hips through the punch to stand defenders up), and his versatility as a wing blocker, perimeter sealer, lead/stalk/combo blocker and in pass protection against college defensive ends, including reps against Miami. He doesn't expect Ryan to handle NFL edge rushers like Micah Parsons or Myles Garrett one-on-one -- Waldman says only a Rob Gronkowski type has really done that -- but expects him to give a quarterback a fighting chance more often than tight ends of his size typically would. He also flags an underrated vertical game: Ryan uncovers late on deep targets, wins jump balls and trust throws, and can subtly cut off a trailing defender to make a clean catch. Waldman says he thinks Ryan could be viable in either a spread or in-line ('dystopian') tight end future, has no idea why he wasn't invited to the combine, and flags this as a take he could end up dead wrong about.
- 2026-05-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-04]]) [Dynasty]: was pre-draft high on BYU tight end Carson Ryan (Cleveland's 248th overall pick) but flags a workout weight of 235 pounds as a real concern versus his college playing weight of roughly 250-255 -- at 235 he looks more like a fullback and not a fantasy factor, but a legit 250-255 would make him arguably the best blocker in the class behind [[Sam Roush]], maybe pound-for-pound better. Waldman likes his catching ability, route-running and after-the-catch skill and comps him as a possible 'middle class,' more well-rounded version of [[David Njoku]] -- less of a straight-line-routes-only player. He's a name Waldman is keeping an eye on as a post-draft dynasty free-agent add behind [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] on Cleveland's depth chart.
