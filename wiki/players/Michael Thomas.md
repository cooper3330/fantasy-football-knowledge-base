# Michael Thomas

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, ahead of a signing. Team/role are as of
> that date and are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast -
  2024-03-19]]: floats a move to Miami as a "great fit" if he can finally stay
  healthy. Acknowledges fantasy managers have soured on him given his
  extensive injury history ("I'm totally off him now" is the expected market
  read), but says he'd still take a late, cheap flier — "when he's healthy,
  he produces."
- 2024-07-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-04]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the hosts noted Thomas remains unsigned and has played just 20 games over the past four years, catching 90 balls for 1,000 yards and 4 touchdowns in that span -- solid on a per-game basis but proof of how injury-limited he's been. They expect him to sign somewhere by midseason with a contender needing a trusted veteran option, floating the Jets (if Mike Williams gets hurt and Rodgers needs a target), the Bengals (if [[Tee Higgins]] is traded), the Steelers, or the Falcons. On Pittsburgh specifically, Waldman floated a 'wish-cast' scenario pairing Thomas as an inside/middle-of-field threat with [[Calvin Austin III]] motioned outside and [[George Pickens]] freed up vertically, giving [[Russell Wilson]] a true middle-of-field option he hasn't had since Seattle; Angelo agreed Pittsburgh adding a bigger-bodied inside receiver would make life easier for Pickens, who is still developing as a route-runner at the first/second level.
