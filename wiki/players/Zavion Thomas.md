# Zavion Thomas

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman slots Thomas between Devin Duvernay and Dee Eskridge as a return/vertical-game weapon with real YAC ability, though he thinks Thomas isn't as accomplished a runner as Duvernay was and expects draft-capital limits to cap his opportunity. His route setups, hands and open-field skills are good enough for an immediate situational role if a team needs it, and if he sharpens his routes Waldman's outcomes range from early-career Duvernay to a long-term [[Curtis Samuel]]-type.
- 2026-05-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-05-05]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon hadn't pre-draft charted Thomas (LSU, listed 5'9'-5'10', 190 lbs) but charted three games after Chicago's pick and came away liking his tools -- a 98th-percentile 4.28 40, strong post/nine/dig-route success, and real catch-point ability (26% contested target rate, 80% contested catch rate). He struggled versus press and takes himself out of short/intermediate routes (slants, curls) with unrefined releases, but flashed good hip sync when he applied it. Harmon frames Ben Johnson's 'biblical greed' for offensive skill talent as the reason Chicago spent a day-two pick here despite defensive-line need, projecting Thomas as the long-term replacement for Kalief Raymond's old Lions role: a leverage-creating, occasional-explosive-play third receiver who won't meaningfully cut into [[Rome Odunze]] or [[Luther Burden III]]'s target shares. Harmon called the pick aggressive relative to consensus rankings but said the traits are there for the staff to develop.
