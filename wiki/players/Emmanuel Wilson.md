# Emmanuel Wilson

- 2025-11-18 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-11-18]]): with [[Josh Jacobs]] banged up, Wilson is the clear next-man-up in Green Bay -- Harris notes it was Chris Brooks who was seen as the early-season handcuff but that shifted to Wilson over the past five-to-six weeks. Wilson scored a short touchdown against the Giants and, in an offense that likes to run behind coordinator Matt LaFleur, projects to mid-to-low RB2 territory as a lead back if Jacobs sits. Harris says Wilson lacks Jacobs's signature shiftiness in the tackle box ('nobody at that size moves like that') but is a solid power runner, and ranks him his No. 2 overall waiver add at just 9% rostered. Guest Jake Seeley (The Athletic, not tracked) independently ranks Wilson his No. 3 add and says he'd rather have Wilson than [[Kenneth Gainwell]] or [[Sean Tucker]] for the rest of the fantasy season if Jacobs is out multiple weeks, citing Wilson's clearer path to a true bell-cow role.
- 2025-12-05 — According to Cousin Josh (untracked guest) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Josh ranks Wilson second on his handcuff priority list (Harris has him fourth) as the primary backup to Josh Jacobs, expressing shock that Wilson is only 34% rostered given the role. Both flag him as a must-add for any Josh Jacobs owner.
- 2025-12-09 — According to Jeff Bell (Football Guys, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-09]]): Bell gives the same handcuff recommendation for Wilson as for Brian Robinson Jr. -- worth adding over an unlikely-to-start bench player heading into the fantasy playoffs.
- 2025-12-26 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-26]]): With [[Josh Jacobs]]'s workload reduced late in the season, Harris said managers should expect 'an uncomfortable amount' of Emmanuel Wilson involvement in the Packers backfield, though he still favors starting Jacobs over Wilson.
- 2026-05-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-04]]) [Dynasty]: believes Emmanuel Wilson will be a fantasy factor in Seattle, at least early in the season and likely throughout, even with [[Jadarian Price]] expected to lead the backfield. Waldman frames Green Bay's decision to keep [[Chris Brooks]] over Wilson as close to a coin flip ('tomato-tomato') that may have come down to cost rather than a real talent gap, and expects Seattle's backfield to run as a committee where Wilson still earns enough touches to matter for fantasy.
- 2026-05-11 — According to Matt Waldman ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-11]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman expects Emmanuel Wilson, alongside [[Jadarian Price]], to handle the bulk of Seattle's early-season work while [[Zach Charbonnet]] is out, calling it 'the Wilson and Price show' likely into late October. He noted Wilson is also a special-teams contributor which has probably suppressed his profile, and said Wilson is someone he'd be picking up late in leagues where he's still available, though he's seeing him drafted more than teammate [[George Holani]] right now.
- 2026-07-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman noted [[Emmanuel Wilson]] (RB66 ADP) is currently going ahead of [[George Holani]] (RB67) in Seattle's crowded backfield picture, and said that could change by the end of training camp; he's a name to know if [[Jadarian Price]] proves unable to handle a full workload.
- 2026-07-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-21]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Wilson, the cheap free-agent signing added to Seattle's backfield, is ranked in the 50s among running backs by Harris -- arguable as either an exciting late-round (13th-14th round) dart throw or a non-athletic profile without much upside. Guest Ben Gretsch (Stealing Signals newsletter, not a tracked expert) noted Wilson did a credible job filling in for Josh Jacobs in Green Bay at times, but flagged that if camp reports on him stay quieter than expected while the noise centers on Jadarian Price, that would reinforce Price as the clear early-down option in this backfield.
- 2026-08-27 — According to Jake Seeley of The Athletic (not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-27]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Seeley has Wilson well ahead of [[George Holani]] in the Seahawks backfield and considers him 'quote-unquote draftable' with a very late pick (comparable to how he views [[Kalen Black]]), though he expects plenty of drafts where Wilson goes undrafted entirely. [[Chris Harris]] agreed Wilson is likely the safer bet as the contrarian pick behind rookie Jadarian Price, but said beat-reporter sentiment is genuinely split between Wilson and Holani, calling Seattle's backfield a potential 'mind palace shatterer.'
- 2026-09-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-10]]): Wilson played just 3 snaps in Seattle's Week 1 win, including one red-zone snap that was nullified by a Drew Lock delay-of-game penalty -- a clear third option behind [[Jadarian Price]] and [[George Holani]] for now.
