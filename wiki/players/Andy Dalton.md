# Andy Dalton

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-25]]): Koh jokes Dalton is the most productive quarterback in the league on a per-game basis after replacing [[Bryce Young]], and says the gap is shocking given it is the exact same team and the Raiders defense is not an excuse. Harmon's serious point is archetypal: with defensive coordinators currently ahead of offenses and two-high shells everywhere, the offenses working are well-designed ones with veteran quarterbacks who have seen it all — he lists Detroit with [[Jared Goff]], Seattle with [[Geno Smith]] and Tampa Bay with [[Baker Mayfield]] — because a veteran can get to the line, identify the blitz, slide the protection and then push the ball over the middle. Dalton, roughly 36 and about 15 years in, fits that archetype and can run a really good offense if the unit is well coached, which Harmon thinks Carolina is. He is careful not to overclaim: this does not make Carolina a playoff team. Koh notes Dalton has been in the league long enough that cover two was dominant, then ridiculed, then back in vogue within his career.
