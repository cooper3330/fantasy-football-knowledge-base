# Oronde Gadsden II

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Gadsden, the son of former Dolphins receiver Oronde Gadsden Sr., is a converted wide receiver whose route repertoire is small — the moves, techniques and concepts he owns are not expansive, which restricts him to being effective mostly against zone coverage. Waldman is blunt that from what he has seen Gadsden has 'zero craft' against man-to-man coverage and must develop it. At the catch point he wins the ball against tight coverage and attacks it early, and he can take hits to earn the football, though he has lapses where he clap-attacks the ball when high-pointing. Waldman's summary is that he is mostly going to have use in space.
- 2025-07-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-28]]): 'fuck it' on Gadsden having immediate fantasy value in the Chargers offense, but with an unusually explicit caveat — if the camp buzz translates, Gadsden is the one rookie Waldman says he completely whiffed on, and the first player he would go back and re-evaluate against his tape. His concerns from Syracuse are route running above all: he did not think Gadsden won consistently man-to-man, which Waldman says top tight ends must do, and he saw issues in how Gadsden attacked the ball even though he could still win it. Waldman loved the father's game and Syracuse's willingness to let him do what he did best. Bob Harris (untracked) says he is not yet chasing Gadsden in the 19th or 20th round of best ball but sees more people doing it, and calls it a defensible play while cautioning that early buzz is just early buzz.
