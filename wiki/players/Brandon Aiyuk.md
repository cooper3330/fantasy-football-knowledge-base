# Brandon Aiyuk

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]) [Best Ball]: asked whether he'd bet on Aiyuk as a true WR1 — "oh hell yeah, I'm feeling that." His case is route running: Aiyuk is the best route runner on the 49ers and has developed into one of the top route runners in the league. Context at the time: past 1,000 yards and WR14 on the season. Waldman expects the Brock Purdy connection to keep improving and says "his career is only moving up" — and adds the market angle, that he's getting Aiyuk at a bargain price anyway, so the bet costs little.
- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]) [Best Ball]: a strong Divisional Round matchup case against Green Bay — averages 3.7 yards per route run against single-high coverage and 3.54 against Cover 3, both looks the Packers are likely to return to after playing heavier Cover 4/two-high against Dallas. Notes Green Bay allowed the 4th-worst completion percentage and 5th-most yards per game to outside receivers between Weeks 10-17, and that CB Jaire Alexander was banged up late in the Wild Card game.
