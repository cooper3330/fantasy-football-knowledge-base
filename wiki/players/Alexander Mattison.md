## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast -

  quarterback instability collapsing his red-zone efficiency rather than a

  Calls him a solid running back who can catch the ball, giving Las Vegas a
  capable number two.
