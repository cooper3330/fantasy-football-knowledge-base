# Dylan Johnson

## Expert Takes

- 2024-05-02 — According to Matt Waldman, agreed with by Brandon Angelo ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-02]]): Dylan Johnson was Waldman's other favorite UDFA, landing behind [[Tony Pollard]] and Tyjae Spears (a similar pairing to what he already offers) ahead of Hassan Haskins ('no juice') and Julius Chestnut, who Waldman said lacks the processing level teams want. Waldman likes Johnson as a pass protector and specifically as a low-red-zone banger role the Titans' room currently lacks, comparing the role to what Jamaal Williams provided Detroit; Angelo agreed, noting neither Pollard nor Spears profiles as a low-red-zone option under Tennessee's current staff. Waldman called it a low-odds bet on a multi-year path if Pollard doesn't work out, with Johnson possibly earning real touches within two to three years.
- 2024-08-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-22]]): Johnson is his monitor-from-afar back — cut after the preseason, but Waldman says the game was a showcase and he would be shocked if no team adds him and he works his way onto a roster. The comp is this era's Spencer Ware: stuck behind Marshawn Lynch, passed by Robert Turbin, a DUI, a cut, a cup of coffee with the Giants, then a climb from the Chiefs' practice squad into a role he would likely have split with Kareem Hunt for longer had he stayed healthy. Waldman names Cleveland and Detroit as systems that could use a back like this — even acknowledging interest in [[Sione Vaki]] — and New Orleans if the Saints tire of [[Kendre Miller]], whose coach publicly called him out for not staying healthy, giving [[Jordan Mason]]-style competition for the job.
