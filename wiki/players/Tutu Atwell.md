# Tutu Atwell

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-10-17 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-17]]): Harmon says some of what the Rams have done with Atwell this year is 'pretty cool' — designed shot plays out of heavy personnel with two tight ends on the field, using the run-look to take vertical shots. Atwell is averaging 16.5 yards per reception and has caught 68% of his targets, and he ranks fifth in the NFL in team offensive success rate when targeted, behind [[Jordan Whittington]] (first), [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Chris Godwin]] and [[Chris Olave]]. Harmon wants those shot plays kept in the offense even after [[Cooper Kupp]] and [[Puka Nacua]] return, framing Atwell as a designed-usage complement rather than a volume receiver. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) was cooler on the Rams depth pieces overall, saying he does not love any of them apart from Whittington.
- 2025-04-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-04-08]]): reacting to Sean McVay saying he had not done a good job utilizing Atwell, Harmon grants the tactical value — motion, stressing defenses, opening things up — and notes the Rams' biggest need late last season was speed, with [[Puka Nacua]] an intermediate/YAC threat rather than a stacker, [[Cooper Kupp]] slow at this stage and [[Kyren Williams]] a slow back. But he calls Atwell hyper-singular and would like to see something else at the Rams' third receiver spot, since a $10 million-a-year deal is simply the going rate for playable receivers ([[Dyami Brown]] and [[Josh Palmer]] also at $10M, [[Curtis Samuel]] around $8M). Koh (not tracked) would normally clown the contract on cap grounds given how many rookies could fill the role for pennies, but defends it on the complexity of McVay's offense and McVay's distrust of rookie receivers — [[Van Jefferson]] did nothing as a rookie and even Kupp took seasons to ramp, with Nacua the gigantic exception. On production, Koh cites weeks three to seven with Nacua and Kupp out: 71 yards per game, 14.2 yards per reception, 66.7% catch rate, zero touchdowns — productive rather than effective. Also noted: 165 pounds now versus 155 at the combine.
- 2025-07-23 — According to James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception - 2025-07-23]], with [[Matt Harmon]] concurring: Atwell is a limited player who will not line up at X but will move around the formation, and his real job is what Harmon calls the sacrificial role — get downfield, occupy the safety and loosen coverage so [[Davante Adams]] and [[Puka Nacua]] can work deep in-breakers underneath. Koh refines Harmon's label to 'sacrificial slot'. The upside case is that when a defense leaves him in single coverage with no safety over the top, [[Matthew Stafford]] will still look for him, as happened at times last season. Harmon separately notes Atwell's $10 million salary as the market comparison making [[Jauan Jennings]]'s $7.8 million look light.
- 2026-03-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host, untracked) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-03-19]]): flagged simply as a name on Miami's now-thin post-Waddle-trade receiver depth chart, with Koh joking that most fans didn't realize Atwell was even on the Dolphins roster. No evaluative take beyond his presence on the depth chart.
- 2026-06-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: asked which Dolphins receiver he'd draft behind [[Malik Washington]], Waldman named Tutu Atwell as his pick inside the 15th round, ahead of [[Jalen Tolbert]], [[Caleb Douglas]] and [[Chris Bell]].
- 2026-07-17 — According to Patrick Doherty (Rotoworld, not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-17]]): Doherty dismisses Atwell as part of the crowded, unresolved Dolphins receiver room, saying flatly he's 'never had a moment in his life' and shouldn't be part of any breakout-watch conversation there.
- 2026-08-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-28]]): the Rams traded to reacquire Atwell from the Dolphins, which Harris reads as a signal the Rams weren't fully thrilled with their receiver depth chart. He guesses the return may be more about special teams than offense but says he isn't sure.
