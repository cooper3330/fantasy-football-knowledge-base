## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): the episode's most detailed single breakdown, and Waldman's headline developmental pick. A tall, listed 6'7"/270 lbs prospect (looked "a little thinner" in person) who played linebacker and other positions in high school and "looked like he'd barely played tight end" as a blocker in 2022 — "one step late all the time," clowned by defenders, drop issues, but flashed real power and elusiveness after the catch when he did secure a target. By late in the 2023 season (specifically cited: a Purdue game), the technique had visibly clicked — competent releases, correct shoulder placement and foot width in blocking reps, successful cut blocks on linebackers pulled across the formation, and visible in-game excitement at his own improvement. Waldman's framing: "before it was like he was drowning out there, and now it's like someone taught him a couple of strokes" — a real, trackable athletic-development arc rather than settled talent. Projected outcome: a practice-squad-to-depth-tight-end NFL path, contingent on the technique continuing to hold under pressure. See [[Player Development and Coachability]].
