# Isaiah Davis

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast [Dynasty]: unexpectedly strong route-running and receiving chops against man coverage; well built, good hand use, and NFL-caliber speed to contribute in the league. Needs to become more decisive as a runner — "plays slow right now because too often he's not confident in what he sees."
- 2024-12-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-09]]): with [[Breece Hall]] out, the expected heavy [[Braelon Allen]] workload turned into a true timeshare with Davis, who had roughly six snaps the week prior. Waldman thinks Davis has a chance to be a journeyman starter by the end of his rookie contract: he runs gap schemes really well, is decisive and hits creases hard, has the power you want and pretty good burst — but the separator is the receiving work, which is what stood out to Waldman in his pre-draft study, specifically how well he ran routes and caught the ball in tight coverage. Caveat: not a player to rush out and acquire unless you are in something like a 45-man league, and even there you would be rotating him out of lineups. [Dynasty]
