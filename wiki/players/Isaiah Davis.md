# Isaiah Davis

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast [Dynasty]: unexpectedly strong route-running and receiving chops against man coverage; well built, good hand use, and NFL-caliber speed to contribute in the league. Needs to become more decisive as a runner — "plays slow right now because too often he's not confident in what he sees."
- 2024-12-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-09]]): with [[Breece Hall]] out, the expected heavy [[Braelon Allen]] workload turned into a true timeshare with Davis, who had roughly six snaps the week prior. Waldman thinks Davis has a chance to be a journeyman starter by the end of his rookie contract: he runs gap schemes really well, is decisive and hits creases hard, has the power you want and pretty good burst — but the separator is the receiving work, which is what stood out to Waldman in his pre-draft study, specifically how well he ran routes and caught the ball in tight coverage. Caveat: not a player to rush out and acquire unless you are in something like a 45-man league, and even there you would be rotating him out of lineups. [Dynasty]
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said [[Isaiah Davis]]'s development as a tough runner and a skilled receiver/route runner out of the backfield is a real reason the Jets might be willing to trade [[Breece Hall]], envisioning New York running a committee approach behind Davis and Braelon Allen once Allen returns healthy.
- 2025-12-05 — According to Cousin Josh (untracked guest) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Josh's deepest, most speculative handcuff pick -- he's rostered Davis for six or seven weeks purely because a Week 16 game at New Orleans looks favorable if Breece Hall were to sit, projecting Davis would get meaningful carries and be a useful desperation waiver pickup in that scenario. Explicitly framed as a deep-league, low-probability speculative stash, not a must-add.
- 2026-01-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-19]]) [Dynasty]: unlike backfield-mate [[Braelon Allen]], Waldman says [[Isaiah Davis]] 'is going to be a factor' -- he rates Davis as a better receiver and a smarter, better-timed runner than Allen at times, making him worth tracking as the Jets backfield sorts itself out post-Sean McDermott (note: McDermott was fired in Buffalo, not the Jets, but is cited as part of the wider offseason coaching churn discussed).
