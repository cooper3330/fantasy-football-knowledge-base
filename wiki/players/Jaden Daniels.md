# Jaden Daniels

## Expert Takes

- 2026-08-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-27]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris noted beat reporters were unanimous that Washington's offense was 'dominated' in joint practice against Baltimore, with the offensive line missing Laremy Tunsil as well as the starting center (calf) and right guard (illness), and neither top-two RB used -- one Commanders beat writer called it 'the worst Commanders offense in years' with an 'inoperable' offensive line. Harris already had Daniels ranked QB7, below market, and said this report reinforces rather than changes that: he's not dropping Daniels further, but confirmed that if it's close between Daniels and another QB on his board, he'd now lean the other way, calling the joint-practice sentiment unanimous enough to use as a real tiebreaker rather than a reason to bench Daniels outright.
- 2026-09-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-09-01]]): says he wanted to buy back into Daniels as a productive quarterback this year, but the offensive line implosion, a first-time OC in David Blough installing a new scheme, and internet pile-on over Daniels' 'control what you can control' comments have him saying the vibes are as low as they get -- he jokes 'it can always get worse.'
- 2026-09-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Sam Gold (untracked guest) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-14]]): Daniels was under pressure on 40% of his snaps (per Gold's stat check), but Gold argues much of that was self-created by holding the ball to scramble because Philadelphia's defense simply contained him and no receiver besides [[Terry McLaurin]] could get open -- 'a big theme of all of last year.' His completion percentage cratered to roughly 52% because he threw the ball away a dozen times with nowhere to go, behind an offensive line both hosts flatly call 'bad.' Harris agrees some pressure is fairly attributed to Daniels's own tendency to hold and scramble but says if you put 'truth serum' in Washington's new offensive staff, they'd wish he threw it away faster. At one point in the third quarter Daniels was 8-for-21 for 60 yards, which Harris calls 'not a functional offense.'
