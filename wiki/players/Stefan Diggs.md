# Stefan Diggs

## Expert Takes

- 2026-08-06 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-06]]): Diggs signed with the Commanders on a deal worth 'up to $12 million' (i.e., not fully guaranteed) after finishing 2025 as WR24 in standard/WR17 in PPR with the Patriots, topping 1,000 yards -- one of only 19 receivers league-wide to do so. Harris kept Diggs at WR45 standard/WR43 PPR, saying he'd already priced in the long-rumored Commanders move even before it was official. He credits Diggs as a vocal leader who was very good against zone coverage with reliable hands during New England's Super Bowl run, but says good playoff defenses (Chargers, Texans, Broncos, Seahawks) mauled him at the line because no one else in the Patriots' receiving corps could beat man coverage, and that Diggs no longer has the quickness to punish press coverage the way he once did -- he's easier to erase than earlier in his career and 'shouldn't be your NFL team's best receiver.' Harris does not view the signing as a downgrade for [[Terry McLaurin]], who stays the clear WR1 in DC if healthy; Diggs projects as a complementary 5-6 target/game piece whose fantasy ceiling depends on the Commanders offense improving under [[Jayden Daniels]] after a Marcus Mariota-limited 2025.
- 2026-08-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-11]]), with co-host James Koh (not tracked) [Dynasty]: Diggs signed a one-year deal with Washington after a productive 2025 in New England — 85 receptions, 1,013 yards, 4 TDs on just 55% of snaps (15th in yards, 9th in catches league-wide despite the part-time role). His reception-perception charting held up remarkably: 70.5% success rate vs. man, 79.2% vs. zone (a tick up from 78.8% in his 2024 Houston season), 70.6% vs. press — almost identical to his 2024 marks despite an ACL tear in between. Harmon calls him a 'capital E elite technician,' with a case as the best route runner of the reception perception era (2017: #1 success rate vs. man in the league; 2020 with Buffalo: still the #1 all-time single-season mark vs. man in the RP database; multiple seasons over the 93rd percentile vs. zone and 96th vs. press). He still wins primarily on outbreaking/dig routes and has run more than 40% of his snaps from the slot the last two years (54.3% for New England in 2025) after being a 50/50 X/Z earlier in his career. Caveats: this is his fourth offense in four years and he turns 32 this season, so further decline is possible, though Harmon isn't worried about him picking up a new scheme given his football IQ.
