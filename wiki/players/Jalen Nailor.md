# Jalen Nailor

## Expert Takes

- 2024-08-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-19]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: not draftable — 'fuck no, but watch.' Nailor is a speedster who has shown up well in camp for two straight years, with injuries the recurring problem. With [[Jordan Addison]] possibly missing time and [[K.J. Osborne]] gone, he is close to a lock for the number three role. Waldman concedes the quarterback play is a downgrade with [[J.J. McCarthy]] out for the year and [[Sam Darnold]] starting, but sees a path to nice looks and high-efficiency big plays. The point of the segment is the watch list: compile one now so that when the Week 1-4 waiver frenzy hits over a one-run wonder, you already know which names are real.
- 2024-09-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-30]]): asked whether Nailor is no longer a fantasy value with Jordan Addison back, Waldman said he feels it only slightly — a moderate drop, not an elimination. He argued Nailor is still someone to buy low off the waiver wire because Sam Darnold is playing proficiently and prolifically enough to support three receivers until T.J. Hockenson returns, and even after that Nailor and Hockenson may alternate productive weeks. He closed with a nod to Adam Harstad (not a tracked expert): if you follow Harstad's approach of not wanting players you have to think too much about before starting them, the answer is 'fuck it' — and Waldman conceded he had to think about this one.
- 2026-02-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-16]]) [Dynasty]: Bob Harris raised the idea that Minnesota benched Nailor in favor of [[Tai Felton]], but Waldman doesn't see a strong second season from Felton as enough to push the Vikings off [[Jordan Addison]].
- 2026-03-17 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-03-17]]) [Dynasty]: [[Jalen Nailor]] signed a surprising 3-year, $35M deal with Las Vegas ($23M guaranteed) off a career-high of just 29 catches/444 yards and a 60% catch rate; Koh was incredulous a 'healthy market' existed for those numbers, while Harmon defended the profile. Harmon's charting: 68% success rate vs. man (47th percentile, roughly average), 73% vs. zone (18th percentile, below average), 70% on the slant route (below the ~80% good-route bar) and 64% on the curl route (also weak), but strong on out-breaking and flat routes with good ball-tracking and a high contested-catch rate; he played all three receiver alignments capably and profiles as a sub-6-foot, sub-200-pound slot/motion piece who blocks well. Harmon likes the fit with new Raiders rookie QB Fernando Mendoza, who favors out-cut route trees, and pegs Nailor as a useful complementary piece rather than a star -- roughly the going rate ($10-11M/year) for a receiver who 'can see the field and not embarrass you' in today's market.
- 2026-07-24 — According to Chris Harris and Andy Behrens (untracked co-host) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris has Nailor inside his top-80 overall in written profiles but is skeptical of the media narrative that the Raiders 'really wanted' him, noting he was buried behind two established receivers in Minnesota and that the Vikings spending a first-round pick on Jordan Addison undercuts the idea he was ever going to break out there. Harris rewatched his film and concluded 'I don't think he's fast,' crediting a couple of nice plays in the end zone but framing him as a player who already had -- and missed -- his window at 27. Both hosts say no camp report about a 'Kirk Cousins-Jalen Nailor connection' would move their opinion; they'd need him to become genuinely 'undeniable' in camp, which they consider unlikely.
- 2026-08-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-03]]): ranked behind [[Tre Tucker]] among the muddled Raiders WR3 competition (with Jack Bech also outside the top 60). Harris notes Nailor 'has a knack for getting open' from his time in Minnesota, but discounts it somewhat since that came as Minnesota's clear number-three option. He expects Clint Kubiak to funnel targets to Brock Bowers over any of these receivers, framing the whole group as a name to remember as a late-round flex if Bowers ever misses time, since extra volume would flow to whoever survives.
- 2026-08-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (untracked co-host) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-25]]): Harmon says he'd take [[Tre Tucker]] over Nailor as Las Vegas's outside receiver, arguing Nailor has 'primarily been a Z-slot guy' throughout his career and doesn't reliably win outside. Nailor was actually better vs. man coverage than Tucker last year (68% success, 47th percentile, vs. Tucker's 59.6%, 19th percentile), and his best route was the out, but he was held out of the same preseason game as Tucker, which Harmon read as notable. Harmon's final projection has Tucker taking the most snaps as primary Z, with Nailor sliding into some X-receiver work despite the fit concerns, and [[Jack Bech]] as the 11-personnel big slot.
- 2026-08-31 — According to Scott Pianowski (guest, not tracked; [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-31]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Pianowski flags that live ADP has corrected so that Nailor now goes behind Tre Tucker, calling that the right outcome -- he says he never saw what generated excitement about Nailor's Minnesota tape and considers the earlier ADP gap (with Nailor going ahead of Tucker) a market mistake that's since been ironed out. Harris did not dispute the read.
