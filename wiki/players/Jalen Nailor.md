- 2024-08-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-19]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: not draftable — 'fuck no, but watch.' Nailor is a speedster who has shown up well in camp for two straight years, with injuries the recurring problem. With [[Jordan Addison]] possibly missing time and [[K.J. Osborne]] gone, he is close to a lock for the number three role. Waldman concedes the quarterback play is a downgrade with [[J.J. McCarthy]] out for the year and [[Sam Darnold]] starting, but sees a path to nice looks and high-efficiency big plays. The point of the segment is the watch list: compile one now so that when the Week 1-4 waiver frenzy hits over a one-run wonder, you already know which names are real.
- 2024-09-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-30]]): asked whether Nailor is no longer a fantasy value with Jordan Addison back, Waldman said he feels it only slightly — a moderate drop, not an elimination. He argued Nailor is still someone to buy low off the waiver wire because Sam Darnold is playing proficiently and prolifically enough to support three receivers until T.J. Hockenson returns, and even after that Nailor and Hockenson may alternate productive weeks. He closed with a nod to Adam Harstad (not a tracked expert): if you follow Harstad's approach of not wanting players you have to think too much about before starting them, the answer is 'fuck it' — and Waldman conceded he had to think about this one.
