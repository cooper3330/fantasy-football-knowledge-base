# Zamir White

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: calls White the hottest sleeper name in the industry right now — fielding near-unanimous "Zamir White" answers on a live radio Q&A — but flags the overhype risk explicitly, self-aware that a bad outcome could get him "run out of the industry" for the call. Conditions the outlook on new head coach Antonio Pierce, who Waldman expects to keep his job (and keep favoring White) because Pierce "wants to keep that job and is smart enough to know how to keep it," building around physical run-and-defense identity with Josh Jacobs expected to leave in free agency.
- 2024-01-01 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert), same episode, raises Adam Harstad's "chaos theory" as the counter-scenario: this could be the second time White "burns overzealous fantasy analysts" who bought in too early, as happened once before. Waldman concedes the caution but maintains there are better reasons to buy in now than the last time.

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: directly compared to [[Josh Jacobs]] as a free agent, Waldman still takes Jacobs as the better back. His 2024 read on White: a running back two if Las Vegas keeps Antonio Pierce and things stay stable, versus a likely top-five running back for Jacobs if he leaves for a good situation elsewhere.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: calls [[Josh Jacobs]]'s departure to Green Bay "the ideal move" for both Jacobs and White, who inherits the Las Vegas backfield. Reads free-agent addition [[Alexander Mattison]] as depth, not a real threat to White's workload — expects a "buff," hard, straight-line runner with a little wiggle, a promising pass protector but not a real receiving threat, who can still get the job done as Las Vegas's lead back after a heavy-usage 2023. Notes the Raiders, under new HC Antonio Pierce and new OC Luke Getsy (with [[Gardner Minshew]] at quarterback), project as run-heavy regardless of Getsy's public statements, and flags a mid-round rookie back as real competition to watch for behind White/Madison.
- 2024-07-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman thinks [[Zamir White]] is underpriced based on anticipated volume, pointing to last year's stretch once [[Josh Jacobs]] got hurt when White (already on pace for 80%+ of snaps) averaged 114 scrimmage yards per game down the stretch, including a big performance against the Chiefs. He questioned whether new OC Luke Getsy (from a run-heavy Bears offense) and Antonio Pierce's known run-first preference cap the ceiling, and said White still has to prove himself as a receiver and pass protector before locking down every-down work, with [[Dylan Laube]] as the likely beneficiary if he doesn't.
- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman laid out why Zamir White is underrated: last year Josh Jacobs was on pace for roughly 80% of Raiders backfield touches before his injury, then White stepped in and averaged 114 scrimmage yards per game down the stretch (boosted by a big Christmas Day game vs. Kansas City), and head coach Antonio Pierce -- an old-school, loyal, 'goes with his guys' coach -- appears to view White as one of his players, similar to how he's stuck with Aidan O'Connell. The knock is receiving: White hasn't shown much as a pass-catcher, and Waldman noted Alexander Mattison is a genuinely competent, versatile complementary back while Dylan Laube could take over the receiving-back role early, both capping White's ceiling. Net, Waldman called White reasonably priced right now as an RB3 bordering on RB2 given his path to a massive workload share if the depth chart holds.
- 2024-07-29 — According to Bob Harris (co-host, not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-29 (Feel It or F-It)]]: relaying Marcus Mosher of Raiders Wire, Harris says a little-known fact from late last season is that [[Josh Jacobs]] could have returned for the final two games and the Raiders declined because they felt they had a better player in White. Antonio Pierce loves him and the organization believes White adds more juice to the backfield than Jacobs did — Jacobs having been the RB3 in fantasy a couple of years earlier. [[Matt Waldman]] agrees, while cautioning that [[Dylan Laube]] would need an injury to matter and that late swings on rookie backs do not all hit.
- 2024-09-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman conceded he took a resounding beating from the utilization community on White in week one and will bench him for a while until he figures things out — but he isn't dropping him on one game, and still isn't sure he won't be more right than they are on the White/[[Alexander Mattison]] split by season's end. His counter-detail: the touchdown Mattison scored came on a first-and-ten reception, which cuts against the clean 'White is the early-down back' framing. He points to [[Josh Jacobs]] in the 2022 Hall of Fame game — declared finished on preseason usage, then led the league in rushing and finished RB3 — as the reason not to settle this on one week of data.
