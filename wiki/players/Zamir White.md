## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: calls White the hottest sleeper name in the industry right now — fielding near-unanimous "Zamir White" answers on a live radio Q&A — but flags the overhype risk explicitly, self-aware that a bad outcome could get him "run out of the industry" for the call. Conditions the outlook on new head coach Antonio Pierce, who Waldman expects to keep his job (and keep favoring White) because Pierce "wants to keep that job and is smart enough to know how to keep it," building around physical run-and-defense identity with Josh Jacobs expected to leave in free agency.
- 2024-01-01 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert), same episode, raises Adam Harstad's "chaos theory" as the counter-scenario: this could be the second time White "burns overzealous fantasy analysts" who bought in too early, as happened once before. Waldman concedes the caution but maintains there are better reasons to buy in now than the last time.

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: directly compared to [[Josh Jacobs]] as a free agent, Waldman still takes Jacobs as the better back. His 2024 read on White: a running back two if Las Vegas keeps Antonio Pierce and things stay stable, versus a likely top-five running back for Jacobs if he leaves for a good situation elsewhere.
