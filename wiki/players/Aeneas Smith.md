- 2024-05-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-30]]): Smith, a 2024 Eagles draft pick out of Texas A&M, posted a 74.4% man coverage success rate and 77.8% zone success rate in his prospect Reception Perception profile, and ran a 4.47 40 at his pro day plus 21 reps on the bench press at 190 pounds. Harmon liked the pick, comparing Smith to [[Curtis Samuel]] given a shared background as a part-time slot/gadget weapon with real explosiveness — Smith had a season as A&M's primary running back (49 carries in 10 games, 2020) plus 43 catches and return-man experience, and Harmon argued "there's more to get out of" that Curtis Samuel-type archetype than teams have typically used. With veteran [[Devante Parker]] retired and Parker/Paris Campbell battling for slot reps unclear, Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) project Smith as Philadelphia's likely slot receiver behind boundary starters [[A.J. Brown]] and [[DeVonta Smith]], noting his 66.7% slant success rate looked weak in isolation but his broader explosive/gadget skill set gives new OC Kellen Moore something extra to scheme with. Not framed as a fantasy sleeper — explicitly discussed as a real-life role evaluation.
