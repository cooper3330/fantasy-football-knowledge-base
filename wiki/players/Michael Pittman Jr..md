# Michael Pittman Jr.

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024 — treat as historical context until
> superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-04]]) [Best Ball]: calls him the top free-agent wide receiver value if he remains a Colt, currently going in the late third round of early best ball drafts at roughly the same price as [[Mike Evans]], "maybe a little before." Argues that could actually be better value than Evans because of [[Anthony Richardson]]'s breakout upside — "if he's healthy enough, [Richardson] could very well make a big leap next year," which would lift Pittman's per-target value. Says watching Pittman play through 2023 "was phenomenal" even with Gardner Minshew at quarterback, and expects the Colts' offensive future — tied to Richardson's development — to be bright.

- 2024-03-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-05]]): sorts the Colts into the "substantial need" wide receiver bucket, arguing they still need a receiver who can win on the outside against press coverage — something neither Pittman ("a big receiver" but "not really a downfield burner") nor Alec Pierce (not a tracked page) has consistently provided. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) disagreed, arguing the Colts' receiver room is fine "in totality" given the group's youth and developmental trajectory.

- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]) [Best Ball]: with his new deal finalized (three years, reported $71.5M with $46M guaranteed), grades him "probably a low-end wide receiver one" playing with [[Anthony Richardson]] — a contested-catch, strong over-the-middle receiver who gives Richardson "just enough dimensions" to target often, with the added note that Richardson's own superstar breakout "could happen as early as this year."

- 2024-03-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-12]]: reacting to his finalized three-year, ~$70M deal ($46M guaranteed) — calls it a team-friendly "good deal" for the Colts, pricing him in the [[Terry McLaurin]]/[[Deebo Samuel]]/DK Metcalf (not tracked) contract tier rather than the A.J. Brown/Cooper Kupp/Stefon Diggs tier. States plainly "I do think Michael Pittman is a true one" — a legitimate No. 1 receiver and "the most underrated receiver in the NFL" the last few years — comparing his usage profile to a "Bud Light version" of peak Michael Thomas (not tracked) with the Saints: 61.4% of his 2023 charted routes were slants/curls/digs, with a 74.3%/74.2%/74.8% success rate vs. man coverage across three straight seasons and over 80% vs. both zone and press in each of those years. Attributes his lack of explosive/downfield plays to a QB carousel — Matt Ryan, Carson Wentz, Gardner Minshew, [[Anthony Richardson]], a different starting quarterback every year of his career — rather than a talent limitation, and says "don't be surprised" if Pittman, entering his age-27 season, has a "banner year" in 2024 that puts him in the top-10 wide receiver conversation. See [[Reception Perception Methodology]].
- 2024-06-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-25]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harmon cites Pittman as the clearest example of coaching deployment being the tiebreaker for a receiver's season -- Shane Steichen maximized him in Indianapolis even through inconsistent quarterback play from Gardner Minshew and Anthony Richardson, illustrating how a well-designed role can outweigh volatile QB circumstances.
