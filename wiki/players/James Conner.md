# James Conner

## Expert Takes

- 2024-07-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman called [[James Conner]] (RB27, round 7) 'ridiculously well-priced' relative to [[Trey Benson]] (RB31, round 8), arguing that gap should be much wider in real life given Conner is the locked-in starter per beat reporter Johnny Venerable. He noted Conner has played 13+ games in each of his last three healthy seasons, has averaged 10 rushing TDs (no worse than 7 in the past three years), gives quality receiving production, and has finished no worse than a mid-range RB2 since arriving in Arizona. Listing alternatives at that draft-price range -- DeAndre Hopkins, Jordan Addison, Diontae Johnson, Njoku, Tony Pollard -- Waldman said he'd still take Conner over all of them.
- 2024-11-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-04]]): total agreement with Eric Edholm's line that Conner is the heartbeat of the Cardinals and is not slowing down as he approaches 30 — 170 yards on 18 carries in the win, and Waldman says he has been all in on him. He revisits the Benny Snell episode years earlier in Pittsburgh, when everyone predicted Conner's demise off one 100-yard Snell game, and his own video response that Conner could still play — quick, agile, savvy, able to catch the ball and still powerful — and that the real problem was the Steelers offensive line. Waldman credits the longevity to supreme care of his body: nutrition, recovery and conditioning are why he has extended his career, and the fantasy production in Arizona has followed.
- 2025-08-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-04]]): Waldman still has Conner leading the Arizona backfield, but expects something closer to a two-thirds / one-third split after Conner himself endorsed a 1A/1B arrangement — a take Waldman credits to Conner's unusual perspective as an older back who has taken care of himself and who survived a life-threatening illness in college, so he understands the sun is setting on his career and wants to be a good teammate to [[Trey Benson]]. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) supplies the baseline: 236 carries to 63 last year, a career-best 1,094 rushing yards and an RB11 finish, a new deal this offseason, and an RB19 ADP that he argues is roughly where Conner should go — Harris says the market may have been drafting Conner correctly all along and simply been too low on Benson, and he will happily keep taking Conner if he slides.
- 2025-09-22 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Conner suffered an ankle injury on the first Cardinals drive of the second half that Harris believes is a break, ending his season — he was carted off after a lengthy delay. Harris called it 'a crusher' that will hurt fantasy teams more than [[Najee Harris]]'s Achilles, since Conner had been very good for two and a half games, converting a fourth-and-one on the opening series and nearly scoring on a scramble-drill throw from Kyler Murray. He also flagged that Conner was hip-dropped earlier in the game with no flag, part of his running complaint about officiating.
