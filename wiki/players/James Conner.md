# James Conner

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2024-07-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman called [[James Conner]] (RB27, round 7) 'ridiculously well-priced' relative to [[Trey Benson]] (RB31, round 8), arguing that gap should be much wider in real life given Conner is the locked-in starter per beat reporter Johnny Venerable. He noted Conner has played 13+ games in each of his last three healthy seasons, has averaged 10 rushing TDs (no worse than 7 in the past three years), gives quality receiving production, and has finished no worse than a mid-range RB2 since arriving in Arizona. Listing alternatives at that draft-price range -- DeAndre Hopkins, Jordan Addison, Diontae Johnson, Njoku, Tony Pollard -- Waldman said he'd still take Conner over all of them.
- 2024-11-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-04]]): total agreement with Eric Edholm's line that Conner is the heartbeat of the Cardinals and is not slowing down as he approaches 30 — 170 yards on 18 carries in the win, and Waldman says he has been all in on him. He revisits the Benny Snell episode years earlier in Pittsburgh, when everyone predicted Conner's demise off one 100-yard Snell game, and his own video response that Conner could still play — quick, agile, savvy, able to catch the ball and still powerful — and that the real problem was the Steelers offensive line. Waldman credits the longevity to supreme care of his body: nutrition, recovery and conditioning are why he has extended his career, and the fantasy production in Arizona has followed.
- 2025-08-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-04]]): Waldman still has Conner leading the Arizona backfield, but expects something closer to a two-thirds / one-third split after Conner himself endorsed a 1A/1B arrangement — a take Waldman credits to Conner's unusual perspective as an older back who has taken care of himself and who survived a life-threatening illness in college, so he understands the sun is setting on his career and wants to be a good teammate to [[Trey Benson]]. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) supplies the baseline: 236 carries to 63 last year, a career-best 1,094 rushing yards and an RB11 finish, a new deal this offseason, and an RB19 ADP that he argues is roughly where Conner should go — Harris says the market may have been drafting Conner correctly all along and simply been too low on Benson, and he will happily keep taking Conner if he slides.
- 2025-09-22 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Conner suffered an ankle injury on the first Cardinals drive of the second half that Harris believes is a break, ending his season — he was carted off after a lengthy delay. Harris called it 'a crusher' that will hurt fantasy teams more than [[Najee Harris]]'s Achilles, since Conner had been very good for two and a half games, converting a fourth-and-one on the opening series and nearly scoring on a scramble-drill throw from Kyler Murray. He also flagged that Conner was hip-dropped earlier in the game with no flag, part of his running complaint about officiating.
- 2026-03-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Andy Behrens (not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-03-05]]): Both hosts still rate Conner as a good player undermined by durability, and both expect/want Arizona to bring him back given his standing as a team leader. Harris says the best fantasy outcome is staying in Arizona (paired with Trey Benson insurance), while the best 'winning environment' outcome would be Detroit in a David Montgomery-style role -- helped by the fact his former Arizona offensive coordinator, Petzing, is now Detroit's OC.
- 2026-03-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: after Conner re-signed with Arizona, Waldman feels he'll still be decent but likely a committee piece rather than a clear starter -- his game has always leaned on quickness, contact balance, vision and decision-making rather than high-end speed, and Waldman expects a real timeshare with [[Trey Benson]] that 'coexist reasonably well' but isn't promising for fantasy unless one of them gets hurt. Bob Harris (not tracked) agreed Conner can still play but questioned whether he's a true offensive centerpiece, and flagged that re-signing a presumed cap casualty suggests real uncertainty about Benson's health. Both noted Arizona should be a better situational offense now that Kyler Murray-era down-and-distance issues change, with Waldman adding a Malik Willis-style QB could help further.
- 2026-03-12 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Dave Kluge (Footballguys.com, untracked guest) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-03-12]]) [Dynasty]: Conner returns to a crowded Arizona backfield alongside [[Tyler Allgeier]] and [[Trey Benson]]. Both hosts see him bringing 'proven track record' to what they expect to be a genuine four-way split with no clear favorite; their broad advice is to only invest in any of the Cardinals backs around the 10th round or later, since 'consensus is always wrong' about which one breaks out.
- 2026-07-21 — According to Ben Gretsch (Stealing Signals newsletter, not a tracked expert), discussed with [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-21]]): Conner was restructured this offseason even as the Cardinals paid [[Tyler Allgeier]] and drafted [[Jeremiah Love]], and Gretsch views him as the most likely of Arizona's four backs to be moved given his respected-veteran standing around the league; before his 2025 injury he had actually been earning more work than [[Trey Benson]]. Both flagged that if Conner is traded, it could meaningfully change how they evaluate both his eventual new backfield and the one he leaves behind.
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman says he has always liked Conner's game and thinks he can still be a factor in an early-season split, but reads OC Nathaniel Hackett naming Conner as 'in the mix' with rookie [[Jeremiah Love]] as motivational rhetoric aimed at Love rather than a real vote of confidence in Conner as the lead back long-term.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Conner (ankle) and [[Trey Benson]] have both been out for the Cardinals, and there is speculation Conner's ankle is 'nowhere close' and he might be done for an extended stretch -- worth monitoring closely as camp progresses.
