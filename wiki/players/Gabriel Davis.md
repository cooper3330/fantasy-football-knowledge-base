# Gabriel Davis

## Expert Takes

- 2024-07-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-04]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the hosts contrasted Davis with [[Parker Washington]] as near-opposite archetypes at the same target spot -- Davis wins on scheme-dependent third-level shots when a defender climbs too far forward or a quarterback escapes pressure, but Waldman said dropped passes and needing to be schemed open will remain persistent issues, and Angelo added he expects Davis' inconsistency to follow him throughout his career.
- 2024-09-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-12]]): Davis had a good Week 1 but 'was Gabriel Davis' — he benefited from working open zones, and Waldman's read is that Jacksonville knows exactly who he is and targets him on plays where the scheme does the bulk of the work to get him open. He only needs to run to the right spot, recognize it, sit down or work across it, catch, and break tackles, which Waldman says he does very well all day long. Waldman did credit one good man-coverage route at the boundary where Davis won real separation. The framing is explicitly comparative: Davis is scheme-dependent production, whereas [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] was doing the nuanced route work that projects to growth and to eventually taking over as the team's primary target.
