- 2026-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-30]]) [Dynasty]: Jaden Nixon is a 'deep sleeper' at 5'9'/190 who Waldman thinks should add 10-15 pounds, though he's unsure the frame allows it. Waldman expects Nixon makes a roster purely as a return specialist, but also credits him as a smart between-the-tackles runner for his size with a genuinely good pass-protection skill set -- solid hand placement and positioning. Waldman frames UCF's backfield as one of the most underrated running back rooms in this draft class, with Nixon part of that depth.
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman noted Nixon backed up [[Ashton Jeanty]] in high school -- a reminder, in Waldman's framing (comparing it to a 188-pound freshman Saquon Barkley), that Jeanty's rise doesn't diminish Nixon's own outlook. He called Nixon explosive with elite speed and acceleration, strong field vision in space, traffic and between the tackles, good at mitigating penetration and managing risk by game script -- a UDFA-type prospect worth watching.
