- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: his favorite 2026 receiver, though he is careful to say favorite rather than best. Waldman likes the feel as a route runner, how well he finds openings, his consistency as a pass catcher, and how tough and physical he plays for his skill set, and thinks the move to a better situation helps. His framing comp is oppositional: Concepcion is 'the anti-[[Nyck Harbor]]' in every capacity, making everything look easy where Harbor makes everything look difficult. [[Brandon Angelo]] agrees enthusiastically, calling him an absolute gamer who was the entire NC State offense as a freshman and projecting a day-two draft grade.
