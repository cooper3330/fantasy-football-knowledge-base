# Noah Fant

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-07-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-21]]): after his release by Seattle, Fant offers real value to an NFL team but only borderline fantasy value, and Waldman is 'not feeling' him. He is a terrific 'spark athlete' of the kind John Schneider is drawn to, and if a team schemes him open — sneaking behind a defense, matched on a linebacker up the seam — he can produce big plays. But he drops his share of balls, does not gain good position, and is not a good timing route runner, which is the gap between a locked-in top-12 tight end with top-3-to-5 upside and what Fant actually is: a low-end version of [[David Njoku]]. Waldman argues his moment passed in Denver, where he never grew into 'that guy,' and that the elite athleticism never converted into great football play — not every great athlete is a great football player. If the Chargers signed him he would likely be committee filler; Waldman rates Tyler Conklin a better route runner and [[Will Dissly]] a wiser zone route runner. He stays open to circumstance changing things.
- 2025-08-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-04]]): asked whether Fant will be a fantasy factor in Cincinnati, Waldman says 'fuck it' and calls him 'an aspiring [[Gabriel Davis]] of tight ends' — a first-round athlete Denver tried to force into being their next Travis Kelce despite him not positioning himself well to the ball, not running man-to-man routes well, fighting the football at Iowa and needing to be schemed open. He is a good runner after the catch with decent blocking. Waldman reads Seattle cutting him in late July, before even seeing him in camp, as a statement that they saw nothing positive left, and thinks Cincinnati signed an athlete who most likely replaces Erick All as a fullback-type blocker rather than a receiving weapon. He adds that Seattle's remaining room — [[A.J. Barner]] as a good blocker and short-range zone receiver plus rookie [[Elijah Arroyo]], who has promise but uncertain development — is not a great scenario either. Verdict: poor fantasy outlook, with Bob Harris '100% in on that.'
- 2025-09-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-08]]): he now feels Fant in Cincinnati and says he was wrong before. The reason is the surrounding cast rather than Fant: [[Ja'Marr Chase]] can win against anyone, [[Tee Higgins]] is a matchup player within his role, plus [[Chase Brown]], [[Jermaine Burton]] and [[Andrei Iosivas]]. That means Fant only has to be a schemed-up player and be himself. Not a weekly huge producer, but Waldman expects him to be the biggest beneficiary of the talent around him. Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked) is 'not feeling nor fucking' it yet given the Bengals' underwhelming Week 1 output despite a full August together.
