# Samaje Perine

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his name as "Samajay
> Piran"; normalized here to the real name, Samaje Perine.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]), confirming Perine remains under contract for 2024: calls him a legitimate weapon in the passing game — by Waldman's recollection, among the league's leaders in yards-after-catch per touch at the position last season — and a tough, physical between-the-tackles runner. Expects him to be a real complicating factor ("monkey wrench") for Denver's backfield touch distribution, cutting into any potential [[Javonte Williams]] breakout given the Broncos' three-back trio.
- 2024-09-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Clyde Edwards-Helaire]] on the non-football injury list for four games, Perine's arrival in Kansas City matters more. Waldman called him effective both between the tackles and as a receiver, and specifically the 'Darrell Williams guy' in Andy Reid's system who can deliver usable fantasy weeks — as opposed to [[Carson Steele]], who might only get isolated looks. Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris agreed Perine and [[Isiah Pacheco]] would split the meaningful touches, which is why Waldman thinks Perine irritates Pacheco managers without actually tanking Pacheco's value.
- 2024-09-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-05 (Going Deep)]]): Perine is his headline late addition, a plus version of Darrel Williams for Andy Reid — a fine receiver, a good pass protector, and 'literally a tractor-trailer' as a power back once he is downhill. Waldman argues the burst was always there at Oklahoma and that, like David Montgomery or Jordan Howard, backs labelled slow early are often just acclimating to reads; Perine has gotten better and more confident with age. [[Brandon Angelo]] connects the signing to [[Clyde Edwards-Helaire]] being away from the team, arguing Kansas City needed a veteran who could pick up the system immediately and handle third down and two-minute work to keep [[Patrick Mahomes]] upright in January. Both call it a strong signing.
- 2024-09-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-16]]): of the two Chiefs backs left after [[Isiah Pacheco]]'s fractured fibula, Waldman prefers Perine — 'he's the one that I think gives them the best combination of the skills' — and it is his experience more than his talent that puts him a step ahead of [[Carson Steele]]. He floats a Darrell Williams-type role as the realistic upside, i.e. a complementary receiving-and-situational back rather than a lead. Waldman still expects Kansas City to add a body (Keaontay Ingram from the practice squad if confident, a veteran if not), so he is not treating Perine as a settled workhorse.
- 2024-09-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-19]]): with [[Isiah Pacheco]] hurt, Waldman is recommending Perine over [[Carson Steele]] among the Chiefs' backs because Perine is so good in the passing game and is a very strong red-zone runner, even though the team appears to want to give Steele a chance. Co-host Adam Harstad (not a tracked expert) agreed on the floor but took the other side on upside: Perine 'deserves to be in the NFL' and is the kind of veteran teams are glad to have when a Pacheco injury hits, but 'he's going to be who he is' and will give only low-end starting numbers — probably getting the job because he is easier to trust. Harstad grabbed Steele instead precisely because if either evaluation is wrong, it is more likely to be wrong on Steele. Harstad added that if the split skews Perine's way early, it may flip by the fantasy playoffs, since rookies and young unproven players are the only fantasy players who average more points per game in the second half.
- 2025-10-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-16 (Week 7 Flex Decisions)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris called Samaje Perine 'coaches' catnip' -- a reliable pass blocker who has unexpectedly carved out roughly half of the Bengals' backfield work behind 40-plus-year-old Joe Flacco, cutting into [[Chase Brown]]'s touches. Harris said bluntly 'he stinks, he's bad at football' as a runner, but the role keeps going to him for pass protection, which is annoying for anyone rostering Brown.
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-10-27]]): Harris asks whether it's fair to question why Perine was out there so much -- he played 24 of the RB snaps to [[Chase Brown]]'s 31, and only 9 of his 24 snaps were third down, meaning he was mixed in beyond just passing downs. He scored on a short goal-line series (Flacco punched the actual TD in after Perine set it up at the half-yard line).
- 2025-10-30 — According to Jeff Bell (Football Guys, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-30]]): Perine has begun absorbing more of the between-the-tackles, contact-heavy running back work for Cincinnati, which Bell credits with opening space for [[Chase Brown]] to produce more explosive runs.
- 2025-11-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-03]]): Perine is banged up — Waldman described his ankle bending back horrifically on a kick return, after which Perine brushed past the trainers and ran off the field before being shown writhing in pain on the bench. Waldman used him as evidence that 'old dudes rule' at a position that ages badly, and said he is actually starting Perine in two of his roughly 30 leagues. He noted the injury opens the door for [[Tahj Brooks]] behind [[Chase Brown]].
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-08]]): Harris called this 'probably the best I ever saw Samaje Perine play,' picking up third- and fourth-down work behind [[James Cook]] against Cincinnati - but was clear Buffalo fantasy managers should still be using Cook, not Perine.
- 2025-12-23 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-23]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Perine scored a touchdown in the two-minute drill even with [[Chase Brown]] otherwise dominant, and Harris names him his #5 championship waiver add given how many managers are chasing running backs right now. His case: even in that game, Perine still got roughly a third of the touches and, crucially, still gets goal-line carries -- Brown's touchdowns that game came from outside the red zone. Harris compares Perine's ceiling to 'kind of not quite David Montgomery from 2024' but around 80% of that value, and likes the matchup against a Cardinals defense he hopes turns into a shootout with Jacoby Brissett's Arizona offense.
