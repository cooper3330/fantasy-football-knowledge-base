# Tyler Goodson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]): a viable one-week fill-in with Zack Moss hurt, but the real point is long-term — Waldman thinks Goodson "has more long-term value for this organization" than [[Trey Sermon]] does. Evaluation: an explosive athlete whose college tape undersold him, because a scout told Waldman that Iowa eliminates the cutback as an option on outside zone, and cutting back is precisely what Goodson is naturally strong at. Also a genuinely good route runner as a receiver — Waldman's comp is "an aspiring [[Austin Ekeler]]" in skill set. Looked good in two Green Bay preseasons but was a roster-math casualty there. Expects the Colts to keep him and wouldn't be surprised to see him paired with Jonathan Taylor as the third-down back in 2024, or to out-produce Sermon in the immediate term given he plays big enough between the creases.
- 2023-12-18 — Matchup caveat from **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert), same episode: Jonathan Taylor wasn't ruled out for the following week, and Atlanta had allowed the second-fewest fantasy points to running backs on the season — so the fill-in call came with a bad draw.
- 2024-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-20]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman still likes Tyler Goodson as a competing RB3 behind Jonathan Taylor and Evan Hull, citing his receiving ability and flashes as a runner last year, but flagged a costly late-season fumble that hurt his standing; he sees Goodson's roster spot now contested by undrafted rookie [[Justin Strong]] in camp.
- 2024-09-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-30]]): with [[Jonathan Taylor]] hurt, Waldman called Goodson a low-end stopgap whose appeal is big plays rather than volume — the type of player he planned to profile in his Football Guys 'Replacements' column as a first-come, first-serve waiver add for cheap points. He drew the comparison to recommending Cordarrelle Patterson before Patterson got hurt: a back who was starting to get rolling against the Colts before it ended.
- 2024-11-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-22]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said he will probably be getting off the Goodson train. He thought there was a possibility there and still sees potential blips, and the versatility continues to fascinate him, but he does not think it is going to happen in Indianapolis. He kept him on the see-where-he-lands list rather than dropping the evaluation itself.
