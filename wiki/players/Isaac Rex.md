## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: good feel adjusting to his quarterback on over routes/crossers — stops or slides back inside when a defender is undercutting, with solid back-shoulder timing rapport. But has lapses on crossing routes where he runs too far past the spot the quarterback expects him to settle. If he cleans that up, Waldman thinks he could become a nice zone player.
