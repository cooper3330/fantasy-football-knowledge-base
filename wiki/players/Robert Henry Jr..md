# Robert Henry Jr.

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-02-13 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-13]]) [Dynasty]: Henry was Waldman's early standout of the 2026 running back class -- he sent RSP newsletter subscribers a full scouting report on Henry by early October after Henry torched Texas A&M for 177 yards and 2 touchdowns on 16 carries, part of a stretch where he averaged 156 yards and 9.5 yards per carry with 9 touchdowns over UTSA's first four games (production fell off afterward, which both hosts attributed partly to the risk of mid-major offensive lines losing depth to injury against tougher fronts). Waldman praised his quickness-power combination at the second level and his decision-making working out of a mediocre interior offensive line ('ability to get out of the mud'); pass-catching hands were strong despite low usage there (a scheme limitation, not an ability limitation), and blocking 'needs a little work.' Waldman comped him to a James Cook-type runner who can work inside zone, gap, and outside, and called him a likely priority value pick given a probable Day 3 draft slot relative to his tape. Angelo independently rated Henry above fellow 2026 back Le'Veon Moss after watching Henry's tape, and both hosts called him one of the better backs in what they consider a weak overall running back class.
