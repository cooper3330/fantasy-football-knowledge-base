# Xavier Weaver

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes. Transferred to Colorado from South

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): a "[[Jordan Addison]] starter kit" with strong catch-point skills for a smaller receiver in contested scenarios. Waldman thinks his route game is the foundation of a starting-caliber receiver, and he's better at contact balance than his size would suggest.
- 2024-08-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-19]]): a name to plant, not to act on — 'you don't need to know him now.' Weaver has impressed in camp with big plays beyond the return game and the staff likes his route running, and Waldman believes he would have been drafted in almost any receiver class other than this one or the Mike Evans / Odell Beckham class of 2014. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) adds the only near-term case is Scott Fish Bowl, where return men may matter and everyone will be chasing them in Week 1. Waldman: he may have a future eventually, but it is not right now.
