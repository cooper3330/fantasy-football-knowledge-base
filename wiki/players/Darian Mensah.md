- 2024-09-11 — According to guest co-host Felix Sharpe of Campus to Canton (NOT a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman]]'s show ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-11]]) [Dynasty]: Mensah, who replaced Michael Pratt at Tulane, is one of the best passers in the country and Sharpe's deep-dive name of the episode. He is a drop-back passer who needs protection, but he throws aggressively downfield without fear and has excellent ball placement — Sharpe cites red-zone throws to a covered 6-4 tight end with a safety over the top that most group-of-five quarterbacks would not even attempt. He slots Mensah into the same bucket of lower-level-competition passers he has liked before, naming [[Cam Ward]], [[Jake Haener]] and Carson Strong, and predicts LSU or Alabama calls and that Mensah is playing power-four football the following season rather than staying at Tulane.
