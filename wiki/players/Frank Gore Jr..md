# Frank Gore Jr.

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast [Dynasty]: a smart cutback runner with quick lateral movement and the same kind of pre-collision "micro movements" to mitigate contact that Ezekiel Elliott and Gore's own father, Frank Gore Sr. (neither tracked), were known for. A low center of gravity helps him break more tackles than his size would suggest — but like [[Devin Singletary]], Waldman says he'll have to prove on an NFL field that he can transcend his size limitations.
