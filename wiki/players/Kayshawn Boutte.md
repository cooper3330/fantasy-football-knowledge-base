- 2026-02-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harmon liked the over on Boutte's longest-reception prop (17.5 yards), identifying Seattle corner Tariq Woolen as a talented but volatile outside cover man who 'can get got' on occasion, and predicting a moment where Drake May connects deep with Boutte against Woolen. Harmon framed this matchup as one of the few clear individual advantages he saw for New England's passing game against an otherwise top-two NFL defense.
- 2026-02-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-12]]): Harmon puts Boutte in the same bucket as [[Mack Hollins]] -- not a consistent separator, which is why he doesn't get 8-10 targets a game, but capable of making the catch when the ball does come his way, part of why New England may still chase outside receiver help such as [[AJ Brown]] this offseason.
- 2026-03-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-03-12]]) [Dynasty]: If [[Romeo Doubs]] takes over the X receiver spot in New England as Harmon expects, Boutte could shift to a Z/slot role that Harmon says 'better suits his skill set' rather than the 'sacrificial X' vertical work he was often stuck with when he saw the field last year -- a move Harmon frames as making two spots on the roster better in a single transaction.
- 2026-06-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-08]]) [Dynasty]: despite trade rumors and Boutte skipping voluntary Patriots workouts, Waldman is fully bought into using Kayshawn Boutte as a slot receiver, arguing that claims he's no longer suited for the slot post-foot-injury don't hold up given the acceleration and contested-catch skills he flashed there and at X at LSU. He sees Boutte as a possible upgrade over Demario Douglas as a big slot who could exploit defenses occupied outside by A.J. Brown -- who Waldman noted was also a college slot receiver -- and argued the Patriots front office, historically unable to draft anything but slot receivers under Belichick, might be giving up on a player who could be better than newly-acquired Romeo Doubs. Waldman flagged that Boutte has since said publicly he wants to be a 'Patriot for his career,' and that he has him unranked/unprojected for now while he watches the situation resolve; current ADP has Boutte at WR69 (184 overall) behind Doubs (WR49).
- 2026-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-30]]) [Dynasty]: Per a Boston Herald report, the Drake May-to-[[A.J. Brown]] connection has reportedly been shaky in camp while May-to-Kayshawn Boutte has been a 'highlight machine' -- Harmon says he can't scroll five posts without seeing Boutte praise. Harmon argues the Patriots should keep their top three as Brown, Boutte and Romeo Doubs, with Boutte staying in his 2025 role as a 'pretty pure X' and 'sacrificial X' for the offense; he notes Boutte's pre-draft prospect profile actually projected more of a slot/Z role, so there's a chance he does more than that this year, though Harmon is skeptical much slot work is truly available for him on this roster.
- 2026-08-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-06]]) [Dynasty]: Boutte has been the best receiver on the field in Patriots camp, not particularly close, per beat reports (Ben Volin) showing him making the biggest plays even while [[DeMario Douglas]] gets most of the reps. Waldman frames this as a front-office 'yes men' problem -- New England drafted Boutte late as a slot/flanker at LSU, tried to remake him into an outside X, and now has brought in [[A.J. Brown]] and [[Romeo Odunze]], making a trade of Boutte likely regardless of on-field performance because reversing the plan would look bad. Both agree Boutte is a three-level option who is better than his reputation and projects to an ascending career if he lands somewhere that needs a true WR3 -- both floated the [[Kansas City Chiefs]] (uncertain [[Rashee Rice]] health, aging [[Travis Kelce]]) and the [[Los Angeles Rams]] as plausible landing spots where he'd start day one.
