# Joe Mixon

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]): expects Cincinnati to move on from Mixon this offseason (per Pro Football Network reporting), noting the same reports circulated last year before Mixon apparently took a pay cut to stay — uncertain whether he'd do that again. Names Dallas as his ideal landing spot on pure skill-set grounds: Mixon offers the explosiveness, power and receiving ability that fans hoped [[Tony Pollard]] would provide but hasn't fully delivered, to the point Waldman jokes that combining Rico Dowdle (not a tracked page) with Pollard and "a little fairy dust" roughly produces a Joe Mixon. Calls a Dallas/Mixon pairing "the elixir" for both team and player over the next couple of seasons.
