# Alexander Madison

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: defends Madison's Las Vegas signing against fantasy-community criticism of his down 2023 season — notes he was one of only two backs in recent history (with Melvin Gordon) to log 180+ carries with zero rushing touchdowns, but attributes that specifically to the Raiders' quarterback instability depressing red-zone efficiency, not to Madison's own play. Calls him "a solid running back who can catch the ball," projecting him as a real, useful RB2 behind [[Zamir White]] rather than a name to write off.
