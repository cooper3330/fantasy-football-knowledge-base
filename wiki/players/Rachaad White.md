# Rachaad White

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his name as "Rashad
> White"; normalized here to the real name of the Buccaneers running back,
> Rachaad White.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]) [Best Ball]: with Tampa Bay's skill-position pieces ([[Baker Mayfield]], [[Mike Evans]]) all back in place, expects White to duplicate his 2023 wide-receiver-one-level fantasy production for a running back — driven mainly by volume and receiving work rather than efficient rushing ("wasn't a very efficient player, certainly not an efficient runner"). Notes he's grown as a between-the-tackles runner since his rookie year, when he showed "little man syndrome" — trying to play like a scat back despite his size instead of dropping his pads and finishing runs; now shows the willingness to run through a defender rather than try to make him miss. Verdict: "he may not be the most efficient guy, but I'm totally buying into him."
- 2024-03-11 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert), same episode: drafted White as his RB1 in the third round of a best ball draft live during the recording, calling it "great value."
- 2024-05-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-13]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman is buying into White's falling ADP (RB14, roughly picks 48-52), noting he was tied for the second-most carries and had the second-most touches in the NFL last year behind Christian McCaffrey despite inefficient rushing on a Tampa Bay line that doesn't run-block well. He dismisses the notion that rookie [[Bucky Irving]] threatens White's role, arguing Irving lacks the two traits Waldman says can't be coached — size and speed — though Irving does show creative, Devin Singletary-esque movement skill that could let him squeeze through tight interior creases. Waldman notes White actually played smarter on tape in 2023 than 2022 and says he'll keep buying White as his price drops, unless Tampa Bay's offensive line looks worse than expected.
- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman called Rachaad White a 'middle-class [[Christian McCaffrey]]' -- both accumulate huge touch totals (White tied McCaffrey's 272 carries last year and totaled roughly 336 touches, fourth in the NFL in scrimmage yards at 1,539), but where McCaffrey creates yardage on his own and sets up his blockers, White is still developing that skill. He attributed White's modest 3.6 YPC more to scheme than talent: Tampa Bay runs a lot of duo (two inside double-teams), which produces tight creases rather than the big gap/outside-zone holes McCaffrey runs through, so White absorbs more first/second-level contact and reaches the secondary less often. Waldman said not to worry about White's efficiency given his workload security and improved decision-making, and dismissed [[Bucky Irving]] as more of a camp story than a real threat to the touches -- unless White stops 'hitting the hole.'
- 2024-09-05 — According to Adam Harstad (Footballguys co-host, not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-05 (Film and Theory)]] [Dynasty]: he traded for White this offseason purely on price — he considers White too discounted — while stating plainly that he is not a big Rachaad White fan and does not think a top-12 fantasy finish is especially likely. He used the trade to make a point about emotional investment: because White has not produced for him yet, he holds him without attachment, but if White finished as an RB1 Harstad would become a huge fan and talk about how right he was. He also drafted White's backup on the back of hype he had heard, hedging so that the bust case still pays off.
- 2024-09-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-12]]): the Buccaneers are finally using White to his strengths rather than throwing him out there to see what works. Waldman's mechanism: duo is two pairs of double teams up front, which creates narrow creases and asks the back to make a fast multiple-choice read — great for a back with elite vision and footwork, bad for a bigger back like White who has good footwork but not that anticipatory feel in tight creases. Adding zone plays gives him wider creases and a simpler multiple choice, which fits him — and also fits [[Sean Tucker]] and [[Bucky Irving]] — while retaining the gap game (power, counter) for versatility. Waldman's view is that this makes the run game better overall and helps [[Baker Mayfield]] too. Harstad (NOT tracked) raised the usage change as one of his Week 1 notes.
- 2024-09-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-23]]): responding to Kyle Brandt's claim that White's run as a starter is near its end, Waldman gave the idea credence but stopped short of endorsing it. Bob Harris (untracked) supplied [[Bucky Irving]]'s share of running back rushes by week: 37.5, 41.2, then 56.3 percent. Waldman's counterweights are that White has been banged up, which is part of why the split moved, and that Tampa Bay will still need a complement to Irving. His verdict: White may never get bell-cow status back, if he ever really had it, but he does not think White loses the job completely at this stage — he remains the receiving asset, and investors should not abandon ship because it is not going to be that bad.
- 2024-09-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-30]]): against the framing that [[Bucky Irving]] has already taken the job, Waldman said White still has value because Irving is not an every-down back and Tampa Bay will run a split. Co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) noted White 'put up a fight,' equaling Irving's ground production, while questioning whether either of them winning the job is a good outcome.
- 2024-10-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-07]]): the White-versus-[[Bucky Irving]] 'referendum' on who is more talented is 'just dumb' — the right question is which play designs are working. Watching the Atlanta game, Waldman saw a number of White carries where the blocking simply sucked on Tampa Bay's gap plays and his best runs came in spite of the line and scheme, whereas some of Irving's best runs came through big holes White would have handled just as well. He cites a red-zone sequence where the staff got every assignment backwards: the downhill power carry went to the smaller back, and the quick perimeter pass into a one-on-one with a corner went to White instead of the slipperier Irving. Verdict: both are flex plays capable of RB2 production any Sunday and RB1 weeks with touchdown luck.
- 2024-10-14 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-14]]): White missed Week 6 with a foot injury that online speculation pegged as a possible midfoot sprain, which Harris notes is unlikely to be a short-term absence either way. In his place [[Sean Tucker]] went for 136 yards and a touchdown on 14 carries plus a receiving score, and [[Bucky Irving]] also played well. Waldman's view is that Tucker has the best long speed and acceleration in the room and that Tampa Bay may have had a realization this week; his caveat is loyalty to the higher draft pick, but if the staff lets competition decide it he expects Tucker to cut into White's playing time.
- 2025-07-10 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-10]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: White is not a great rusher and does not elevate a run game, but he may be the best pass-protecting back in the league and is certainly top three, and he can catch — which Angelo says will keep him employed for a very long time even as [[Sean Tucker]] and [[Bucky Irving]] out-explode him. [[Matt Waldman]] notes White has publicly signalled this is his last year in Tampa, and defends his 2024: White absorbed the 'we-know-it's-coming-to-you' carries that [[Nick Chubb]] used to get, so his efficiency looks worse than a back getting touches when defenses do not expect them, and he was not as bad as the fanbase rooting for Irving made him out to be. Waldman recalls fans making the identical argument against [[Leonard Fournette]] in White's favor three or four years earlier.
- 2025-08-22 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris says he has never really liked White as a runner, but notes he is going surprisingly late in drafts this year and should still be drafted in all leagues, standing ahead of [[Sean Tucker]] in the pecking order behind [[Bucky Irving]]. He also revisits Cousin Josh's 2024 Least Valuable Player call on White (Josh is a recurring guest, not a tracked expert), calling it a great one: nobody thought Irving was a thing, White was a clear pure fantasy starter, and by the end of the year White 'absolutely tortured you' if you met him in the fantasy playoffs.
- 2025-08-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-25]]): Harris says he ends up with White in every single draft and is frustrated about it, asking his guest directly whether he is being an idiot — he has told listeners for years that White is bad at football. Guest JJ Zachariason (not a tracked expert) says no: he sees White as a better [[Best Ball]] player than redraft player because the production is not very predictable, but he is at least a handcuff to [[Bucky Irving]] and nobody knows how big Irving's workload will be. JJ adds that whatever you think of White as a runner, he is not a bad pass catcher and Tampa Bay uses him that way, so the profile works in PPR. Both flag [[Sean Tucker]] as the wild card who could take that opportunity, especially with White banged up.
- 2025-09-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris finds it strange that White was ignored enough that he routinely took him in the 13th round, yet both hosts now have him as roughly a top-40 running back for Week 1 with White back at practice. Guest Andy Behrens (not tracked) had him RB41 in pre-draft ranks and noted that per FantasyPros that was still ten spots above consensus, which he called nuts for a back who finished as the overall RB24 last season. Behrens attributed the fade to the summer-long groin issue and to how little White saw the field in Tampa's late-season must-win games and the playoff game, though he noted [[Bucky Irving]] was dinged then too. Harris recalled their long-running disagreement, saying Behrens came out very pro-White and was smacked around for it, and restated his own position: 'he's not that good, but he's not this bad.'
- 2025-09-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-16]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: White kind of stole [[Bucky Irving]]'s thunder by scoring the winning touchdown on the goal line, but Harris is careful with the sample — that was literally the only snap Tampa has had inside an opponent's five-yard line all season, and on third down needing two yards or fewer the split is 8-1 for Irving. He is not denigrating White, who had three straight touches late in the third quarter where he mashed and made a difference, but the overall snap count was 53-21 for Irving and Harris does not think the goal-line preference is the plan when Tampa is not literally on the goal line.
- 2025-10-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris raised White to RB22 for Week 5 on the [[Bucky Irving]] foot injury and was tempted to go higher, though he does not feel great about it — he described the typical White game as three quarters of bashing into defenders he somehow does not see, followed by a two-yard checkdown on third-and-nine that he houses. With [[Josh Jacobs]] on bye, Harris said he is in 'bye-week hell' and will have White in several lineups, contingent on Irving not being cleared late in the week.
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-06]]): with [[Bucky Irving]]'s long-term prognosis unknown, White at least didn't have to share with [[Sean Tucker]] in this one. Guest **Sam Gold** (NOT a tracked expert) splits his answer explicitly between evaluation and fantasy: as a talent evaluator he is 'not really a fan' — no juice for breakaways, doesn't consistently break tackles, a broken-tackle rate of only 14% he calls awful, and he can't consistently make the next guy miss. But on volume — 14 of Tampa's 18 back carries plus four targets — 'I'm going to start him and be completely okay with it,' as a flex or straight RB2. Harris offers the embodying play: early third quarter, good inside-zone lane up the gut, a safety arrives and White 'is never ever going to be able to change directions to make that guy miss,' getting absolutely drilled.
- 2025-10-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-13]]): White is better than fantasy players think and has been victimized by his priors. Waldman argued the early Tampa offense with [[Leonard Fournette]] was a tight-crease scheme that made yardage hard to come by, expectations were set too high, and people wrote him off; White has actually gotten better and better. Watching the Rams game, the box score looked poor but the effort to earn what he got was skilled, physical and quick enough, and Waldman told a colleague who called him no good to go back and watch. He also defended the hole-hitting complaint: sometimes White's patience served him better than an immediate hit. Waldman said that as an NFL evaluator he would consider White an upgrade to some starter or co-starter situations next year. Bob Harris (not tracked) recalled White as the overall RB4 two years ago on 272 touches at 3.6 yards per carry, and both agreed that from a fantasy view it is 'feel him for now, fuck him later' once [[Bucky Irving]] returns.
- 2025-10-13 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-13]]): Harris admitted he's 'not a huge Rachaad White fan' but said White 'showed me a little something' in Tampa's win, breaking a tackle and slashing for a first down on a play where the running lane wasn't initially there, then getting consecutive short-yardage carries to close out a drive. He contrasted that with backup Sean Tucker, who took over when White tired and scored on an edge run behind a collapsed defensive front -- Harris said Tucker 'has power' and 'can scoot straight ahead' but is 'not impressed at all' by his lateral agility, adding he'll 'see about Bucky Irving' as the incumbent recovers.
- 2025-10-16 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-16]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Angelo said White has genuinely improved since his rookie year in three areas -- down-and-distance management, pass protection (among the best in the NFL), and reliability as a pass-catcher -- making him at worst a steady bridge starter, even though he lacks a true contact-breaking or lateral 'out' tool the way David Montgomery, or even a lesser version like Tyler Allgeier, has; Angelo called him a '40-degree day' back who simply doesn't make mistakes. He pushed back on the media narrative crowning Bucky Irving 'the future,' arguing White's consistency still has real roster value, and said if he were a Bears fan he'd rather have Sean Tucker than White as a stopgap while Kyle Monangai develops. Waldman agreed White has real value as a bridge option and third-down piece amid Tampa's other injuries.
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-10-27]]) [Redraft]: broke a signature 20-yard screen ('I ain't done nothing but tick you off' type play, per Harris), but was stuffed on three separate goal-line carries across two separate Saints stands. Harris reiterates his running critique -- White 'is not a convincing person in a power situation,' too upright and prone to evading rather than blasting through contact.
- 2026-03-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Andy Behrens (not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-03-05]]): Both hosts acknowledge Behrens was once a big believer in White but has 'long since completely abandoned' that enthusiasm. Harris says Tampa needs a complementary back who can absorb the physical work Bucky Irving can't, but argues White 'was never that guy' -- he has the frame to be physical but 'refuses,' producing his best plays only in low-leverage garbage time. Behrens speculates he could land somewhere like Jacksonville and get interesting again, but neither host is currently excited about him as a fantasy asset.
- 2026-06-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-22 (Feel It or Fk It)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: projects White as a top-10 PPR back this year in Washington's backfield alongside [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]], driven almost entirely by receiving-game usage -- Waldman calls White 'the best receiving back/between-the-tackles runner combination' on the roster and expects him to dominate touches even though Croskey-Merritt should see more passing-game work than last year. Waldman frames the likely split as a Nick Chubb/Kareem Hunt dynamic if White takes over, contingent on the offensive line staying healthy so Washington can run-and-play-action with Jayden Daniels (who the team reportedly wants to limit as a runner). Bob Harris (untracked co-host) agrees on the top-10 range, citing White's proven high-level performance versus Croskey-Merritt's more inconsistent weekly production (only two 20-point games last year) despite preseason hype. Waldman adds White's rapport with Daniels on option/leak routes and screens -- passes he calls 'underrated in difficulty' -- as a modest tailwind.
