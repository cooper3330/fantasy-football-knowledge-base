# Kyler Murray

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21 (Pacing and Control)]]): used as the foil for [[Lamar Jackson]]'s pocket comfort, and referred to as a standing view ("we've talked about Kyler Murray"). Waldman's charge is that Murray's internal clock is set by proximity rather than by the play: "when somebody gets within three yards of him, he runs as if a bomb has blown up in the pocket." The contrast is Jackson, who Waldman says has always been able to operate "in the tightest of pockets" and would "dodge the shrapnel and still throw the ball downfield." Offered as an illustration in the pocket-poise discussion rather than as a ranking or start/sit call.

**Note:** the one live evaluative claim here is negative and narrow — pocket
discipline under near pressure. Nothing in this episode addresses Murray's
2023 ACL return, role, or fantasy price.
