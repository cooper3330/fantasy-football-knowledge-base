# Keenan Allen

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]): **feeling it** as a top-5 wide receiver. The general principle first — "you don't always have to have blazing speed to be a top wide receiver; it's about how they fit in their offense" and about route-running skill. What separates Allen (and [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]]) from receivers who are only zone route runners is that he wins against both man and zone, and is used inside and outside — versatility is the trait doing the work. See [[Zone vs Man Route Running]].
- 2023-12-18 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert), same episode [Best Ball]: notes 2023 was "a change in offense, no change in outcomes" for Allen — if anything he was better. His drafting rule for the January best-ball market: keep betting on Allen and St. Brown until you get more information, while flagging that Allen's offense could change again in 2024.
- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: "irresistible" as a fourth-round pick, assuming Los Angeles keeps him (cap questions are live but Waldman is hoping they work something out). Base case: over the games Justin Herbert played healthy in 2023 (roughly Weeks 1–12), Allen was fantasy's WR2. New HC Jim Harbaugh and OC Greg Roman have a run-heavy reputation, but Waldman argues that history (see [[Justin Herbert]]) points to more efficient, "dink and dunk" quarterback play under this staff rather than fewer opportunities for Allen specifically — a scheme that should suit Allen's game more than a pure vertical receiver like Mike Williams (not a tracked page).

- 2024-03-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-05]]): the Chargers' cap situation (the NFL's highest wide receiver spend, $76M, entering 2024) is flagged as a real risk to Allen's roster spot, alongside Mike Williams (not a tracked page) — notes Allen "is not a young man" either. If he leaves, the Chargers would be left with a room of just Josh Palmer (not a tracked page) and [[Quentin Johnston]], pushing the team from "substantial" into "critical" wide receiver need.

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: reacting to his trade to Chicago. Notes 2023 was arguably Allen's best season (108 catches, 1,243 yards, a Chargers franchise record, WR2-or-better over games 2–13) and that he "seems to be at his peak right now for an aging player." Optimistic he sustains it with [[Caleb Williams]] and opposite [[D.J. Moore]] — expects good early rapport — while flagging the standard aging-veteran caveat: the cliff "comes fast and comes suddenly" and could be triggered by a new system/QB forcing him to think twice physically. See [[Aging Curves and Career Longevity]].
