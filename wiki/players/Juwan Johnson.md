# Juwan Johnson

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-09-09 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-09]]): Johnson is his number four waiver add and a sneaky potential tight end one with no [[Taysom Hill]] for most of this season. In an extremely conservative Saints game of screens and checkdowns he still accumulated volume, and he nearly won it — [[Spencer Rattler]] threw a really great ball in the end zone with four seconds left that Johnson had in his hands before a hit from Budda Baker dislodged it. Guest Jake Ciely of The Athletic (not a tracked expert) also picked him at number four, noting he listed on Twitter that every player on the field for 100% of the dropbacks was a wide receiver plus Juwan Johnson — a converted college receiver playing 'the stupid position'. Ciely picked Johnson over [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] but conceded it could be wrong. Both hosts were unsure they would rank him above [[Mark Andrews]] in Week 2; Harris said probably not, Ciely said it would be close.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not tracked) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-11]]): Johnson was a big-play beneficiary of [[Tyler Shough]]'s debut stretch, hauling in an on-time throw off a pocket climb that Koh singled out as the game's best rep — the ball arrived in rhythm so Johnson never slowed down and could add yards after catch. He posted a 100% catchable target rate in the game and was second among Saints pass catchers in slot snap rate at 38%, behind [[Chris Olave]]. Harmon notes Johnson and Shough were college teammates at Oregon, a genuine oddity since Johnson is already on his second NFL contract, though he is skeptical of what Adam Levitan calls the shower narrative and doubts chemistry from that long ago carries over.
- 2026-04-30 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-30]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris noted [[Juwan Johnson]] finished third among tight ends in receiving yards last year but only 12th in scoring because he scored just three touchdowns, and raised the idea that Johnson functions more like a 'converted wide receiver' in the passing game than a traditional inline tight end -- a role that could get diluted now that New Orleans drafted a receiver in round one, though Harris noted that pick could also mean more reliable mouths to feed across the offense rather than fewer for Johnson specifically.
