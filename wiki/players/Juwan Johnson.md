# Juwan Johnson

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-09-09 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-09]]): Johnson is his number four waiver add and a sneaky potential tight end one with no [[Taysom Hill]] for most of this season. In an extremely conservative Saints game of screens and checkdowns he still accumulated volume, and he nearly won it — [[Spencer Rattler]] threw a really great ball in the end zone with four seconds left that Johnson had in his hands before a hit from Budda Baker dislodged it. Guest Jake Ciely of The Athletic (not a tracked expert) also picked him at number four, noting he listed on Twitter that every player on the field for 100% of the dropbacks was a wide receiver plus Juwan Johnson — a converted college receiver playing 'the stupid position'. Ciely picked Johnson over [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] but conceded it could be wrong. Both hosts were unsure they would rank him above [[Mark Andrews]] in Week 2; Harris said probably not, Ciely said it would be close.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not tracked) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-11]]): Johnson was a big-play beneficiary of [[Tyler Shough]]'s debut stretch, hauling in an on-time throw off a pocket climb that Koh singled out as the game's best rep — the ball arrived in rhythm so Johnson never slowed down and could add yards after catch. He posted a 100% catchable target rate in the game and was second among Saints pass catchers in slot snap rate at 38%, behind [[Chris Olave]]. Harmon notes Johnson and Shough were college teammates at Oregon, a genuine oddity since Johnson is already on his second NFL contract, though he is skeptical of what Adam Levitan calls the shower narrative and doubts chemistry from that long ago carries over.
- 2026-04-30 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-30]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris noted [[Juwan Johnson]] finished third among tight ends in receiving yards last year but only 12th in scoring because he scored just three touchdowns, and raised the idea that Johnson functions more like a 'converted wide receiver' in the passing game than a traditional inline tight end -- a role that could get diluted now that New Orleans drafted a receiver in round one, though Harris noted that pick could also mean more reliable mouths to feed across the offense rather than fewer for Johnson specifically.
- 2026-08-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: keep bumping Johnson up fantasy boards -- he's an underrated TE who projects as New Orleans's No. 2 passing-game option behind [[Jordyn Tyson]] with Tyler Shough at QB, just as he was last year, and directly benefits from Tyson's hamstring/knee injury history keeping him off the field.
- 2026-08-28 — According to cousin Josh (Chris Harris's untracked co-host) and [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-28]]): Josh's #3 Warp Zone pick, a tight end -- Josh has [[Brenton Strange]] 8th and Johnson 9th at the position and calls this his version of a wait-at-TE argument, since Johnson goes around pick 135, roughly 40 picks after Travis Kelce and Dalton Kincaid, with little real gap in expected production. Johnson caught 77 passes for 889 yards last year, 5th in receptions and 3rd in yards among TEs, but Josh says nobody talks about it because he scored only 3 TDs -- if it were 6 he'd be a top-5 name. Johnson is part of a Kellen Moore-called Saints offense that's been tight-end friendly, and with Chris Olave's target bump coming as another receiver is sidelined, Josh argues some of that added volume should still flow to Johnson too; he also played college ball with Tyler Shough at Oregon. Harris agrees fully, says he'd be happy getting Johnson in Round 12, and adds a bump for 0.5-PPR kids' leagues.
- 2026-08-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-31]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris's number one late-round pick is Johnson, pointing to 'proof of concept' from last season -- a new contract, 77 catches, 889 yards (fewer TDs than he'd like) -- plus a Kellen Moore-led, fast-paced offense and rapport with QB Tyler Shough dating back to their time together at Oregon. Harris ranks him TE11-12, well ahead of a market ADP in the high teens, and calls him a fit for any tight-end draft strategy, including as a bridge starter for someone who drafted a rookie tight end who needs time to develop. Pianowski (guest, untracked) ranks Johnson even higher, TE9, ahead of his own TE8 Brenton Strange, and both say they'd happily start Johnson in Week 1 -- 'there's not a big difference among the options there.'
- 2026-09-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-10]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: named alongside [[Brenton Strange]] as a Week 1 streaming tight end option Harris would be comfortable using over a difficult [[George Kittle]] matchup.
