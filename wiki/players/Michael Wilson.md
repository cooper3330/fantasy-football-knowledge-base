# Michael Wilson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Dynasty]: was "pretty keen on him heading in," describing him as a different kind of piece in the Arizona receiver room, and thinks that reading held up — rookie-year role limits and health issues (a recurring problem going back to Stanford) kept him from rising further, nearly hitting 100 yards in the season finale. Calls him a genuinely good route runner in the mold of an "aspiring Michael Thomas," and puts top-24 wide receiver value in play for 2024 if he can stay on the field the full season — the biggest swing factor after health being whether [[Kyler Murray]] is the full-season starting quarterback.
- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Adam Harstad** (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: Harstad's [[Post-Rookie-Year Receiver Model]] scores him higher than his draft slot and limited pre-season profile would suggest, with among the highest usage rates in the class; the comp pool below him (Keelan Cole, Donnie Avery) skews weaker than the pool above (Jarvis Landry, D.J. Moore, Brandon Cooks) — several of which are unqualified NFL-starter hits, not just complicated ones. Waldman assigns him an archetype comp of Allen Hurns and, to a lesser degree, Robert Woods — a high-volume possession profile with a real chance at a multi-year strong-volume stretch.

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): named the
  most promising player in one of the league's worst receiver rooms — good
  rookie film supports him as a "non-embarrassing" starting outside receiver
  and a good number-two next to a rookie-drafted [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], who
  the hosts pair with Arizona at #4 in a top-10 mock scenario. Also flagged as
  a better fit at true X than [[Marquise Brown]], who the previous Arizona
  staff mis-deployed running Marvin Harrison-style in-breaking/slant routes.
- 2024-08-06 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-06]]): Wilson was far more solid as a rookie than people realize, and his profile carries real similarities to [[Michael Pittman Jr.]]'s rookie-year RP profile. He was used early as a flanker and near power-slot, a role Harmon expects Arizona to keep exploiting as a big slot, but the important data point is the final three games after Hollywood Brown went down, all with [[Kyler Murray]]: Wilson took 81.8% of sampled snaps outside and 87.3% on the line of scrimmage, and Murray was willing to throw him the ball in tight coverage as the X, with varying success. Harmon says that matters because a functional part-time X lets Arizona move [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] around the formation. He also names a step forward from Wilson as one condition that could make Arizona the division's best receiver room.
