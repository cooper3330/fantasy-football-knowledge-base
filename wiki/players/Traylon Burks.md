# Traylon Burks

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-12-02 — According to Matt Harmon ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-02]]): Burks made what Harmon called 'one of the catches of the year' -- a reaching, one-handed sideline grab (shades of Odell Beckham Jr.) against Denver, made while playing through a recent finger surgery -- in a game Harmon wasn't especially high on him for entering. Harmon flagged Washington QB Marcus Mariota trying to force-feed Burks tight-window, back-shoulder sideline throws afterward as overplaying the moment ('you're not prime Aaron Rodgers, this isn't prime Jordy Nelson'), and said he expects Burks' role to shrink once [[Terry McLaurin]] is fully back.
- 2026-10-20 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: The Titans castoff, now in his fifth NFL year, 'played really well' against San Francisco -- a deep out that got Washington's third-quarter comeback going, and a 30-yard catch on an awesome fourth-quarter throw from [[Jayden Daniels]]. He had the potential game-tying touchdown in his hands on the goal line (thrown low by design to protect him, per Aikman) but couldn't hang on, and got hurt on the play, missing the final series -- Harris notes it might have been a concussion. Harris initially called Burks the more valuable commodity over rookie [[Antonio Williams]], but says a miss on Burks (a known fifth-year player) stings less than a miss on the rookie; Washington is on bye Week 7 regardless.
