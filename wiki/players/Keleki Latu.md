# Keleki Latu

- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]): Latu is a good striker as a blocker who earns optimal position inline, and Waldman thinks he looks like he could add another 15 to 20 pounds of muscle to his frame — if he does, there is potential for him to develop into a contributor in short-yardage scenarios. As a receiver he has hand strength and can attack the ball with an overhand technique away from his frame against tight coverage in his back, and he handles defenders swatting at the ball after the initial catch; Waldman says it is hard to knock the ball loose from his hands.
