# Mike Washington

## Expert Takes

- 2026-03-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-02]]) [Dynasty]: had one of the buzziest combine workouts at running back -- 4.33 40 (led all RBs, tied for eighth overall among all positions), 39-inch vertical, 10-foot-8 broad jump (second at the position). Waldman thinks 'he can live up to his combine buzz when he takes the field,' but the swing factor is ball security: his college fumble rate is 'atrocious,' recurring every season relative to his carry volume, not a one-year blip. Per Waldman's own RSP research, fewer than 10% of running backs with a bad college fumble rate ever produce an RB1 fantasy season, so talent alone doesn't guarantee outcome -- 'talent is also being reliable.' He compared the risk profile to Sonny Michel, who corrected the issue, versus David Wilson and Stevan Ridley, uber-talented backs whose fumbling 'got in their heads' and derailed their careers; called Washington the class's coin-flip, 'Targaryen' of running backs who could either rule or 'go mad' depending on whether it's fixed. Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) projected him as a likely Round 2-3 pick.
- 2026-04-09 — According to Paul Pertichese, guest analyst on the [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-09]] (not a tracked expert) [Dynasty]: Arkansas's Mike Washington is the consensus RB3, with size, straight-line speed, burst and power as his calling card, but inconsistent vision/patience, ball security issues and pass-protection problems that could keep him off the field on third down despite functional receiving hands. Pertichese put his range as anywhere from a Latavius Murray to a DeMarco Murray outcome, and noted tracked expert [[Matt Waldman]] has Washington at RB14 in his Rookie Scouting Portfolio -- not calling RB3 wrong, but seeing 'a whiff of Carlos Hyde,' a workout-warrior who never translated gym-shorts explosiveness into consistent tape. Pertichese personally prefers [[Kaytron Allen]]'s vision and patience to Washington's tools, calling the gap between Washington and [[Emmett Johnson]] 'almost a coin flip.'
- 2026-05-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-05-14]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon noted Washington's new profile just went live on the site and said dynasty rookie drafters 'needed him to go somewhere else' -- landing behind [[Ashton Jeanty]] in Las Vegas gives him only a little backfield pop and depth rather than a real path to touches.
- 2026-07-09 — According to James Koh (co-host, not tracked) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-09]]): Washington projects as a 'straight-line' change-of-pace back behind [[Ashton Jeanty]], comparable in role to Zach Charbonnet in Seattle -- someone who could see perimeter/crack-toss packages and short-yardage or goal-line work rather than true competition for lead-back touches.
