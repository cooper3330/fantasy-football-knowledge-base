# Dallin Holker

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: his game's strength is catching the football — an effective ball tracker with awareness and productive execution at the boundary, and enough speed to hold off DBs who bite on play fakes. A long-striding (not sudden) seam runner who forces defenders to chase him deep. Needs refinement on timing routes — breaks and break steps aren't flat, long, or violent enough, and weight drop into hard breaks isn't deep enough — but has the mobility to improve; works well with his quarterback when a target doesn't arrive on the first break. Waldman's timeline: maybe two to three years before he sees the field.
