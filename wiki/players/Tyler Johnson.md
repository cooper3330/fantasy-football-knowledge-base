# Tyler Johnson

## Expert Takes

- 2024-09-10 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-10]]): Johnson was the surprise of the Rams' Week 1, ripping off a roughly 60-yard catch and run against Detroit. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) says he liked Johnson a little in Tampa Bay, went back to his Minnesota tape, and found an in-breaking route winner who is tough to bring down after the catch — 'a beautiful fit' in Los Angeles — and thinks his inside/outside versatility (35% of snaps outside) could make him the WR3 that emerges over [[Jordan Whittington]]. Harmon is more skeptical: he doesn't have a ton on Johnson, notes he's bounced around without catching on anywhere, and — crucially — that there were zero beat-writer press clippings on him all offseason, which makes Harmon think this 'could just be kind of a one-week flash in the pan.' Harmon also doubts Johnson is the rugged physical type the McVay offense wants outside.
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-27]]) [Redraft]: Waldman said if you need a Jets receiver for a PPR lineup right now, [[Tyler Johnson]] is the pick over [[Arian Smith]] because of his work over the middle and his ability to adjust to the football on scripted fade concepts, though he called the overall payoff 'probably not' worthwhile on a true week-to-week basis for either receiver.
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-10-27]]) [Redraft]: caught a 34-yard deep play-action dig that Harris calls the moment [[Justin Fields]] found his rhythm, and later a fade-route touchdown Harris calls 'undefendable,' a 'Jordan Love special.'
