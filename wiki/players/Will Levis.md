# Will Levis

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-14]]): reacting
  to Tennessee signing [[Calvin Ridley]] to throw to Levis. Harmon's framing:
  on a rookie quarterback contract, a team should "spend money with reckless
  abandon" to get a real evaluation of the player before committing further —
  which is why he's fine with the Titans overpaying at receiver here rather
  than running Levis out with a bare cupboard (previously just [[DeAndre
  Hopkins]] and [[Treylon Burks]], who "is not going to work out"). Skeptical
  of the idea Tennessee should instead trade up for J.J. McCarthy and bench
  Levis: "how much of an upgrade is J.J. McCarthy over Will Levis? I'm not
  that sure." Both hosts agree it would be a very Tennessee outcome to trade
  up for McCarthy only to have him bust.
