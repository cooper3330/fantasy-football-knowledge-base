# Kyle Monangai

## Expert Takes

- 2024-08-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-22]]) [Dynasty]: the Rutgers back does not strike him as extremely explosive, but has a decent enough initial burst, good contact balance, is a powerful runner and a pretty good blocker, and plays with high effort. Waldman sees potential to develop along those lines and enjoyed watching him — a developmental profile note from his summer college viewing rather than a ranking.
- 2024-09-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-05 (Going Deep)]]): comps him to Devonta Freeman — about 5-9, 210, versatile enough to run zone, gap and duo, sets up blocks well, and a determined runner who gets his pads down and works through multiple points of contact. The headline number is ball security: 410 career touches in three years with zero fumbles. Waldman ties that to his RSP grading work showing only about 3% of backs who reached elite fantasy production graded below an adequate ball-security tier, and only about 10% of RB1/RB2 finishers did. He also credits toughness — doing it on a Rutgers team that is not good, against solid Big Ten defenses. The caveat: not the fastest, and Waldman files him with the players he expects to have a longer NFL career than a big fantasy career.
