# Payne Durham

## Expert Takes

- 2025-09-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-01]]): Waldman is 'feeling it' that Durham is the Tampa Bay tight end to have if [[Cade Otton]] cannot play. He allows Otton might give more yardage, but says Durham is the red-zone guy — with maybe the best hands of any tight end he has seen, an excellent tight-coverage receiver in the short area, very quick for his size though not fast — and someone who has endeared himself to [[Baker Mayfield]] in those situations, as he did at Buford High School in Suwanee and at Purdue. He tags him a sneaky desperation add. Bob Harris (not tracked) recalled Otton being that same play last year with [[Mike Evans]] and [[Chris Godwin]] out, finishing as a top-two or top-four tight end over a stretch on roughly a 28% target share, and noted Mayfield is not afraid to use the tight end.
