# Kaytron Allen

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-08-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-15]]) [Dynasty]: on the 2026 class, Waldman acknowledges a lot of people like Allen a little more than teammate [[Nicholas Singleton]], and both he and guest Dan Hatman (Scouting Academy, NOT tracked) declined to pick a winner, saying they are definitely different players and you can see why Penn State wanted to use both. Allen is never going to reach Singleton's speeds; he plays a different game inside. Hatman's point in his favour is that coaches have always responded to backs who can go get tough yards in the A and B gaps, so someone is going to find a use for that.
- 2026-02-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-09]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman prefers teammate [[Nicholas Singleton]] over Allen despite a co-host's Nick Chubb comparison (relayed from analyst Dave Kluge) that Waldman only partially endorses. His own comp is Javonte Williams -- good footwork and power but not special explosion -- versus Chubb's 'elite explosion... elite contact balance and power and vision.' Waldman: 'I don't see the explosion in his game that really elevates a guy to being an instant starter,' projecting Allen as a solid rotational contributor rather than a starter-caliber prospect.
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman praised Allen's mobile hips for efficient 90-degree cuts, his stride/pace adjustments to set up blockers, and his ability to string together moves in the open field, but flagged speed and acceleration as clear weaknesses -- closer to the minimum for an NFL contributor than a starter, though Waldman noted most reserve-tier backs in today's talent-glutted league are actually closer to starter-caliber athletically than combine followers assume.
- 2026-04-09 — According to Paul Pertichese, guest analyst on the [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-09]] (not a tracked expert) [Dynasty]: Kaytron Allen is the one player in this class Pertichese is notably lower on than consensus -- most industry rankings have him RB4-RB6, but Pertichese has him well down his own top 10, calling him just 'average,' with real doubt about whether his contact-balance/power style translates unless he lands behind a strong offensive line running mostly interior. He compared Allen unfavorably to teammate [[Nicholas Singleton]] over the years (Penn State kept trusting Allen with touches over the more explosive Singleton), and rates [[Seth McGowan]] slightly ahead of him on tape with more athletic upside, even though Allen will likely be drafted with more capital. Pertichese said he'd be largely out on Allen in dynasty rookie drafts absent an ideal landing spot.
- 2026-05-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-18]]) [Best Ball]: Waldman similarly flagged Allen as a late-round RB flyer in Washington worth stashing for contingent upside.
- 2026-05-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-05-21]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon called Allen (5'11', 216 lbs, self-nicknamed 'fat man') a genuinely productive Penn State back -- nearly 1,400 scrimmage yards, 15 total touchdowns, 6.2 yards per carry, and Penn State's all-time leading rusher -- who two ESPN evaluators (Mel Kiper and Matt Miller) reportedly had as a top-five running back prospect before he fell to Washington in the sixth round. Charting-wise, Allen posted a 74% outside success rate (one of the best marks in the 2025 class) and a 60.4% zone success rate, but by far the lowest inside-run success rate (33.96%) of any back Harmon has ever charted -- comparable to Woody Marks -- because Allen is 'obsessed' with bouncing runs outside even on plays designed to hit inside. Harmon pushed back on typecasting Allen as a between-the-tackles grinder given that profile; he actually looked best 'in chaos' when plays broke down. Allen landed in a cheaply-paid, crowded Washington committee alongside explosive change-of-pace back Jacory Croskey-Merritt, passing-down back Rashad White, and free agent Jerome Ford. Harmon said Allen projects well if new OC David Blau imports Ben Johnson-style zone/outside run concepts from Chicago, but poorly if Washington leans power, with Allen's untested athleticism (he skipped all combine and pro-day testing) the swing variable for whether he can be a starting-caliber value pick.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]): Allen posted 85 yards on 23 carries and a 1-yard touchdown in the exhibition opener. Waldman said Allen lacks [[Robert Henry]]'s burst but profiles as a serviceable short-yardage/pass-protection option if Washington wants a bigger, grinding back; he still expects Henry to pass Allen on the depth chart by the end of the preseason.
