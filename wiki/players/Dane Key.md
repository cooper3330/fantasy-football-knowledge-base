# Dane Key

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-01-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-26]]) [Dynasty]: feel it -- an underrated prospect from his film study. Waldman describes Key (6'3', 210) as being in Alec Pierce's body-type range but not nearly as fast, projecting instead as a possession receiver who is quick and sudden for his size, can make people miss in the open field, breaks tackles, and is very good at the catch point on contested targets. He thinks Key has good enough route breaks to develop into a real number-two receiver within a couple of years, or show signs of it by year three or four as a good fantasy-viable number three in a dynamic offense. His closest comp is Michael Wilson -- not as refined a route runner as Wilson was coming out, but sharing the hands, after-the-catch ability, and quickness -- and Waldman jokes he'd like to earn the unrestricted free-agent money Alec Pierce is about to make.
- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Key sets up routes effectively into his breaks and can execute flat speed turns, but needs more consistency and precise execution at every range of the field. Waldman calls him a good pass catcher and says he's interested to see if he lands as a late-round pick.
