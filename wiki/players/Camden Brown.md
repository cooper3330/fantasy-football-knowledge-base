- 2026-04-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-13]]) [Dynasty]: he 'absolutely' feels Brown, a Georgia Southern receiver who started at Auburn, as a developmental sleeper -- well-built with speed. Frames Auburn as a program that recruits elite athletes who don't always develop into polished college football players, which colors how he weighs Brown's tape. Says Brown is very good in contested catches with some route-running ability, and comps him directly to former Ravens receiver Marlon Brown (also out of Georgia) -- a player with real flashes and one big touchdown-heavy year who never developed into a full-time starter -- hoping Camden Brown gets more luck turning similar flashes into something stable.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]): Brown made a huge contested-catch touchdown against Seattle, and Waldman feels he could make the Cowboys' roster despite a fairly set WR rotation. He described Brown (an Auburn transfer who finished at Georgia Southern) as a strong, tall athlete who tracks the ball well but whose hand placement sometimes leads to drops -- the tape shows both. Dallas's staff reportedly told him what he showed in the game is what he does in practice every day, urging him to stay humble. Waldman expects an acclimation year, hoping Brown develops the way [[Ryan Flournoy]] did, with a chance to contribute or even start only in the event of an injury -- calling him roughly a year away from being fantasy-relevant.
