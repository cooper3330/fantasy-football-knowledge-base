# Jacob Cowing

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-01-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-01-07]]): Harmon liked Cowing coming out of school as a really interesting slot receiver but, in his opinion, he is a slot-only player, and that is why he cannot get on the field in San Francisco — [[Jauan Jennings]], [[Ricky Pearsall]] and [[Deebo Samuel]] all need slot reps, and even removing Samuel does not clear enough room. The deeper objection is scheme fit: Harmon says Kyle Shanahan has never used a pure slot-only player, preferring receivers he can move around the formation, so he is curious why the team drafted Cowing at all beyond possible return-specialist value. He pushed back on dynasty managers who reply to any Pearsall tweet insisting Cowing will be the better player, saying so far that is not looking great.
- 2025-06-04 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-06-04]]): Cowing is making some OTA noise and is reportedly getting some of the old [[Deebo Samuel]] jet-sweep snaps, but Harmon calls his rookie year disappointing — just 67 routes despite plenty of injuries around him, behind Chris Conley and Ronnie Bell, and mostly a special-teams role. He measured 5-8 and under 170 pounds with a 4.38 forty but only 35th-percentile broad jump and 37th-percentile three-cone. Harmon declines to write off a rookie who did not play in a complex offense with veterans ahead of him, and notes San Francisco may simply want a small speed player and a returner, citing Kyle Shanahan's line that they drafted a kicker in the third round because it was the only open starting spot. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) describes him as a traditional slot receiver, contrasted with the bigger [[Jordan Watkins]].
