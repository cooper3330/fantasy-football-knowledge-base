# A.T. Perry

## Expert Takes

- 2024-05-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-09]]) [Dynasty]: Co-host James Koh (untracked) called A.T. Perry 'his guy'; Harmon stopped short of that but said he has real affinity for him as a pure X receiver coming from Wake Forest's unconventional college offense into a limited rookie role. He floated a sleeper comp to Nico Collins in Houston's Bobby Slowik offense, noting new OC Clint Kubiak also comes from the 49ers coaching tree where the X-receiver role has mattered a lot -- meaning Perry could end up a bigger part of the offense than his rookie usage suggested, depending on how New Orleans splits 2-vs-3-receiver personnel with Olave and Shaheed.
- 2024-06-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-18]]): [[A.T. Perry]] is floated as a candidate for what Harmon jokingly calls a 'sacrificial X receiver' role in New Orleans -- a high-snap, low-target backside X (in the mold of Alec Pierce or Romeo Doubs) freeing up [[Chris Olave]] and [[Rashad Bateman]] for more motion/slot usage. Harmon notes Dennis Allen has been consistently negative about Perry publicly, reportedly answering 'everything' when asked what Perry needs to improve, and it's unclear he's in line for a real role.
- 2024-06-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-25]]) [Dynasty]: With Michael Thomas gone and the Saints declining to draft a receiver early, A.T. Perry (full name Atorian Perry) is set up for an expanded role after playing a full complement of snaps from Week 10 on as a rookie (12 catches, 246 yards, 4 TDs). Harmon's pre-draft Reception Perception profile on Perry graded him as a true X receiver at Wake Forest -- 93.3% of sampled snaps outside, 97.2% on the line, 68.7% success rate versus man and 70.7% versus press, though only 5th percentile versus zone. Harmon thought Perry profiled more like a third-round talent and was surprised he fell to the sixth round. He's not the next Nico Collins or Brandon Aiyuk, but Clint Kubiak's Shanahan-tree scheme specifically needs a press-man-beating X receiver to unlock more motion usage for Chris Olave and Rashid Shaheed, so Perry emerging as a starter is a key swing factor for the whole passing game.
- 2024-07-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-25]]): Perry was a late-round-three talent who fell to round six amid whispered personality concerns — a pure X receiver charted outside on 93.3% and on the line on 97.2% of sampled prospect snaps, 45th percentile vs man and an impressive 67th vs press, but needing work vs zone. Harmon's comps are a very poor man's [[George Pickens]] or a healthy pre-injury [[D.J. Chark]], with a statistical best case of [[Van Jefferson]]'s 2021 (89 targets, 802 yards, six touchdowns, 16.0 yards per catch as a low-usage, high-yield third option) — though Harmon believes Perry's ceiling is higher than Jefferson's. Koh praises his downfield ball tracking and contested-catch strength, noting three of his four rookie touchdowns on 18 targets were heavily contested. Both like that a true X frees [[Chris Olave]] to move around, but call the third-receiver job an open competition.
