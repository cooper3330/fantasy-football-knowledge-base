# Kirk Cousins

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-12]]: both "feel it" on Cousins staying in Minnesota — the Vikings "need him desperately," and while they may still draft a quarterback, Waldman expects Cousins to "give them another year or two" first rather than being displaced immediately. Flags real free-agency uncertainty regardless: light talk of a hometown Atlanta fit if the Falcons miss on Justin Fields, with Atlanta's Fields pursuit explicitly named as the swing variable. Bob Harris's redraft framing: coming off a torn Achilles that limited him to four-of-eight-plus games in 2023, "we always are drafting him in double-digit rounds" — a standing value gap Waldman says he'll continue to exploit wherever Cousins lands.

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]): repeats the hope that Minnesota looks closely at re-signing Cousins, since [[Justin Jefferson]] has said he'd like to keep playing with him. Also notes Atlanta remains mentioned as a possible Cousins destination alongside [[Justin Fields]], with most quarterback-seeking teams (Atlanta included) likely to weigh both options.

- 2024-02-29 — According to **Kevin Knight** (Falcons reporter, SB Nation's Falcoholic — guest, not a tracked expert), same episode ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-29]]): says Cousins "makes a ton of sense" schematically for Atlanta's incoming Zach-Robinson-run, Rams-style offense, but that the front office's stated preference to "go young" at quarterback favors a Justin Fields trade over signing Cousins.

- 2024-03-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-04]]): on Atlanta, calls Cousins the pick that "makes the most sense" for immediate contention — "basically get Matt Ryan 2.0 injected immediately" into a roster with a decent defense, strong run game and good offensive line — rating him above [[Justin Fields]] and [[Baker Mayfield]] there for near-term ceiling, while still preferring Fields for longer-term development upside. On Minnesota, expects the Vikings to let him test free agency to see if he'll take a discount to stay with [[Justin Jefferson]], rather than re-signing him outright before he hits the market.

- 2024-03-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-12]]: reacting to the finalized deal (4 years, $180M, $45M/year, $100M guaranteed) — dubs him the "bag king" but flags real risk: turns 36 this season coming off a late-October Achilles tear, with genuine doubt he's ready for Week 1. Expects a "grown-up offense" boost for [[Drake London]] once he's on the field, tied to new OC Zach Robinson's Rams-schemed system and Cousins's familiarity with new HC Raheem Morris from their Washington days — calls Cousins "the only guy on the market" who had shown, over multiple seasons, above-replacement-level QB play, making this Atlanta's only real option at the position this offseason.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: still "feeling" the finalized move to Atlanta — argues Cousins "cures all the foul" on that offense given the good offensive line and strong ground game already in place (headlined by "future All-Pro" [[Bijan Robinson]]), and that Cousins can finally give the Falcons a quarterback who reads the full field, delivers on time and manipulates coverage. Downplays the Achilles/age worry after seeing his offseason workout videos ("looked pretty good"), noting recovery outcomes from that injury have generally improved with the technology. Points to his own *Fantasy Notebook* research (Footballguys) that 27%+ of Cousins's career targets have gone to the tight end and that he's "carried some tight ends to great heights" as the basis for expecting a real [[Kyle Pitts]] boost.
- 2024-04-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-29]]): addressed Cousins' public 'shocked and stunned' reaction to Atlanta drafting Michael Penix Jr. eighth overall, saying the reaction undercut his standing as a locker-room leader even though the football logic behind the pick is sound. Waldman argued Cousins wasn't established as a good quarterback until age 27 — comparable to Drew Brees at 27 and Steve Young at 31 — and that any-good quarterbacks typically play another 8-12 years (16-20 if elite), so drafting insurance now isn't as combative as fans assumed. The real driver of the hedge, per Waldman, is that Cousins turns 36 in August and is still limited to running only in a straight line this offseason coming off an Achilles tear. He still expects Cousins to 'earn' his contract and lead Atlanta to some success if healthy, while noting Vikings fans would point to a pattern of playoff disappointment.
- 2024-05-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-06]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman says 'fuck it' on Kirk Cousins being ready for Week 1 -- expecting him on the field but not fully healthy, using a 'Tony Pollard-ready-but-not-really' framing. Citing tracked expert [[Brandon Angelo]]'s analysis from the prior week's Going Deep episode, he notes Cousins' injured leg is specifically the one he needs to torque when releasing the ball, and that if he still can't break the pocket or curl around defenders during May minicamp, his accuracy and velocity could be more compromised early in the season than public expectation accounts for -- potentially making Cousins a player not worth drafting near his current cost.
- 2024-05-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-13]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman's central concern with Cousins entering 2024 is not whether his surgically repaired Achilles allows him to be on the field, but whether it allows him to plant, turn and generate torque off his front foot for accurate ball-striking — the same 'healthy enough to play vs. healthy enough to perform' distinction he applies to [[Tony Pollard]]. He puts real odds on Cousins being replaced by [[Michael Penix Jr.]] around Week 7-8 if that mechanical issue shows up on tape.
- 2024-05-17 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-17]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo says Cousins, turning 36 coming off an Achilles rupture, likely has at most two more years as a starter — the underlying rationale for reaching for [[Michael Penix Jr.]] as insurance in Superflex and 6-point passing-TD leagues.
- 2024-06-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-20]]): expects new OC Zach Robinson's Atlanta offense to borrow from the Jared Goff/Mike Zimmer-era McVay playbook that Cousins thrived in — stretch-zone runs to set up play-action, with either shots over the top when safeties bite or checkdowns to the back when they hold — rather than the Matthew Stafford-era empty/drop-back version. Notes Cousins performed well in play-action under Zimmer in Minnesota. Harmon: not a top-five QB, but 'a professional quarterback' who is 'better than the average' starter and comfortable operating from shotgun as well.
- 2024-07-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-29 (Feel It or F-It)]]): Cousins not playing at all in the preseason sounds precautionary and Waldman is happy to draft him at his current price and thinks he will be fine — but he restates the concern [[Brandon Angelo]] raised on their Going Deep podcast this spring, that a 36-year-old coming off an Achilles tear may not be able to drive off the foot to generate the velocity needed on the routes Atlanta will ask him to throw. Waldman adds an ego dimension: Cousins has a penchant for playing the tough quarterback-savior, and if he struggles while trying to play through it, the replacement will not be Taylor Heinicke. Bob Harris (not tracked) points to Cousins's NFL Network conversation with Steve Smith, whose own repaired calf remains visibly smaller than the healthy one years later, as evidence the healing process is lengthier than assumed.
- 2024-08-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-22]]): Atlanta resting [[Michael Penix Jr.]] to evaluate a third quarterback reads as the team being a little less bullish on Cousins than the public is. Angelo's medical framing: with Achilles injuries, in his experience confidence returns around the 18-to-24-month mark and Cousins is not even at 12 months — he points to [[Aaron Rodgers]] practice clips still showing a favored left leg. The fantasy-relevant question is whether being short of 100 percent at game speed degrades drop-backs, progressions, reads and time to throw. [[Matt Waldman]], whom Angelo says he has been quoting all year on the Cousins foot question, agrees nobody has seen yet what Cousins can or cannot do, and both frame Atlanta's quarterback room as a hedge in case they need their draft pick.
- 2024-09-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Cousins's job is not secure. The quarterback who took more snaps under center than anyone in the league last year took exactly one snap from center in week one — it was all pistol, which guts the play-action game this Sean McVay-style offense needs, evidently because Atlanta is protecting his front leg. When he had to throw intermediate to the boundary with velocity he couldn't: the ball was behind the receiver and wobbly, and the pass distribution chart showed nothing else requiring that velocity, so Waldman reads the Falcons as hiding him. With Philadelphia, New Orleans and Tampa Bay ahead, he expects Cousins's job to be insecure by the end of September and [[Michael Penix Jr.]] to play if Atlanta starts 0-4. Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) pushed back, granting a 'T.J. Watt pass' the way Waldman granted [[Lamar Jackson]] a 'Chris Jones pass', and said relax.
- 2024-09-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-12]]): Cousins' Atlanta debut was 16-of-26, 59.9 rating, one touchdown and two interceptions, and Harmon calls the scheme fit broken rather than merely rusty. Per Next Gen Stats the Falcons used pistol on 55% of plays when Cousins had never taken more than 10 pistol snaps in any game of his career; they ran zero play-action snaps, took one snap under center, ran on 81% of pistol plays and called zero runs from shotgun. Harmon notes Cousins took 31% of dropbacks on play-action in 2023, mostly under center, and that the whole McVay-tree rationale for the signing was familiarity. He suspects the Achilles is the reason: 'Kirk is not moving,' he cannot get onto the plant/back foot, which let T.J. Watt tee off and turned a late downfield throw into the interception. He explicitly retracts grouping Cousins with [[Aaron Rodgers]] as 'Achilles quarterbacks' — different timelines, and Cousins tore the plant foot.
- 2024-10-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-07]]): after the 500-yard, four-touchdown Thursday-night win over Tampa Bay, Waldman says the September Achilles worries can be dropped. Atlanta slowly expanded his confidence in the injured foot and is now asking for more downfield throws and throws that require velocity. He may never be all the way back as a mover — he compares Cousins' current mobility to Philip Rivers — but throwing downfield with confidence over the past two to three weeks means the offense has fewer weak spots for defenses to attack. The caveat Waldman keeps: a team will eventually figure something out and Cousins will throw a clunker or melt down, because he still reaches for 'hero ball' throws — a term Waldman argues is a pejorative describing hubris and trying to write a check your technique cannot cash.
- 2024-12-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-02]]): a fading asset whose job is under real threat. Co-host Bob Harris (not tracked) noted six interceptions and zero touchdowns over the last three games, four picks coming in the most recent one, and that Steve Smith on NFL Network voiced similar benching chatter. Waldman says Cousins struggles to throw vertically with zip — better than early season, when Atlanta had to protect him wholeheartedly, but still limited — that timing routes are harder this year because of his injury recovery, and that he keeps forcing hubris plays, which has always been the nature of his game. He ties Cousins to the broader thread of players returning from serious injury needing longer than expected, alongside [[Aaron Rodgers]] and [[Tony Pollard]].
- 2024-12-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-12-03]]): 'real' on the Kirk Cousins is cooked question. In the 17-13 loss to the Chargers all four interceptions came on throws targeting slot receivers per Next Gen Stats, and Harmon called them all earned — Cousins was late on nearly everything against a Jesse Minter zone defense that communicates well on the back end, so windows that were open when the receiver settled were closed by the time the ball arrived. Over his last three games, all losses, Cousins has six interceptions and zero touchdowns. Harmon: 'if you are this limited as a physical player you also cannot be late.' He also flagged Cousins checking down to [[Bijan Robinson]] behind the line of scrimmage on fourth-and-three. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) suspected a health issue, saying the throws looked like 'push passes' and 'paper airplanes' with no drive off the leg, and noted Cousins had played well through the middle of the season. Both wondered whether Atlanta turns to [[Michael Penix Jr.]].
- 2024-12-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-09]]): after a brutal four-game stretch with more interceptions in Minnesota and Raheem Morris publicly standing by him, Waldman says 'fuck yeah, he's cooked' — with a hedge. He believes Cousins is probably playing hurt to an unknown extent, and the optimistic case is that a full offseason of rest brings him back next year. The pessimistic case, which Waldman leans toward, is that he is playing through something that five years ago he would have handled and simply cannot recover fast enough at his age — which would confirm cooked next season. On why Atlanta sticks with him: the optics of benching a $100M-guaranteed Arthur Blank signing mid-playoff-race, and not wanting to tell the fan base the season is over.
- 2024-12-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-12-19]]): the Atlanta benching for [[Michael Penix Jr.]] 'had to be done' — unlike Cleveland, the Falcons were still in the NFC South race, and the play was not good enough. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) supplied the numbers: 10 TD to 15 INT, sub-80 passer rating, 5.5 net yards per attempt, and since Week 11 a league-worst seven interceptions to the intermediate area (10-19 air yards), six of them to the intermediate middle, a 40% completion rate there (second worst) and minus 20.6 total EPA, dead last by a wide margin. Eight of his touchdowns and one interception came in two games against Tampa Bay's zone. Harmon: the ball arrives late, windows close, and he can no longer get to the top of the drop and drive it between zones — the same bucket where [[Tommy DeVito]] and [[Drew Lock]] live. Deep-ball numbers were still fine. Harmon expects a trade, not a mentor role, given how he took the Penix pick.
- 2024-12-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-19 (NFL QB Lunacy)]]): Cousins has been benched for [[Michael Penix Jr.]] after eight interceptions and zero touchdowns over four weeks — per ESPN the last passer to do that was Brett Favre, who at least had a stronger arm and could move. Waldman says [[Brandon Angelo]] flagged the exact failure mode before the season on their Wednesday show: watch whether Cousins can rotate on the repaired left foot and generate velocity, because if he can't it would be a disaster. By week four it was clear the velocity wasn't there consistently, defenses read the limitation and adjusted, and the playbook shrank because he couldn't throw boundary timing routes. Waldman also criticizes Cousins for telling media he was 'stunned' by the Penix pick and that the capital should have been spent elsewhere while in the same interview admitting he could only run in a straight line — a 36-year-old coming off an Achilles has no equity to play GM, and Atlanta should have pre-briefed players before the draft. Harstad (not tracked) counters that at $50M Cousins was still a better bet than Derek Carr at $37.5M or Baker Mayfield at $33.3M.
- 2024-12-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-23]]): Cousins's arm velocity in 2024 was 'by far the lowest of any quarterback in the NFL this year,' which Waldman frames as the structural reason Atlanta's passing game could not stretch the field. Comparing the box scores of Cousins and rookie [[Michael Penix Jr.]] would suggest little difference between them, but on film Waldman saw Penix attacking downfield and completing throws that required velocity Cousins no longer has — the deficiency that made the benching sensible rather than reactionary.
- 2025-01-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-01-27]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: 'fuck it' on a Cousins rebound. At 36 going on 37 with a bad Achilles he did not look good, and Waldman's concern is less pocket mobility than the ability to drive the football. He argues Atlanta's supporting cast should have won that division handily, and that Cousins played well enough in a limited offense for a while before it all fell apart. He explicitly separates Cousins from [[Aaron Rodgers]] — 'I wouldn't even put those guys in the same tier' as athletes — so he is not counting on a recovery. Bob Harris (not tracked) is entirely landing-spot dependent, reserving judgment and warning himself that he will probably be overly optimistic and overdraft Cousins if the landing spot looks good. The episode's RSP giveaway contest was to email the best nickname for Cousins.
- 2025-06-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-02]]): the losing side of the Rodgers-versus-Cousins Steelers question, and Waldman is pointed about why. He grants Cousins may be everything the audience wants narratively as a person, but says his arm was trashed last year because he was still injured, and that his leadership reputation is unearned — Cousins went on air after Atlanta drafted [[Michael Penix Jr.]] and effectively subverted his new team by criticizing the pick and saying they needed another position, then in the same interview admitted he could not run anything more than a straight line at full speed. Waldman calls trashing the team that just paid you 'rule book 101' and questions the self-awareness, adding that Atlanta missed the playoffs on a tiebreaker and without Penix would have no quarterback right now.
- 2025-08-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-18]]): asked whether Cousins is 'the bologna sandwich of the NFL,' Waldman upgraded him — reports from practice say he has returned to form and if the velocity is back because the Achilles is another year healthy, he buys it, noting how often it takes an older player a full extra year to fully recover. On a hypothetical Rams trade of [[Jimmy Garoppolo]] for Cousins if [[Matthew Stafford]]'s back is a real problem, Waldman said that would probably be fine given the very similar offense: he does not believe Cousins is a good leader and thinks people are fooled because he fits the stereotype of one, but as an on-field executor he is a step up from Garoppolo because he can make big plays in key moments.
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-27]]) [Redraft]: Waldman defended [[Kirk Cousins]]'s Week 8 spot start for the injured Michael Penix Jr., saying he 'had no chance' behind a leaky offensive line that couldn't establish the run early, and that Atlanta's overall offensive struggles -- also missing Drake London -- were not Cousins's fault. He still delivered a well-placed 15-yard throw to [[Kyle Pitts]] where he correctly read a dropping safety. Waldman's verdict: the real problem was Atlanta's blocking and lack of weapons, not Cousins, despite last season's running joke that Cousins was a 'bologna sandwich.'
- 2025-11-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-17]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with Michael Penix Jr. banged up, Cousins is back in the bridge-starter conversation for Atlanta, but Waldman was dismissive of his current level, joking he's gone from a 'fried bologna sandwich' to 'deli-counter' quality -- still able to make some throws but unable to consistently challenge a defense when pushed. Not viewed as a real fantasy asset even in a starting role.
- 2025-11-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-18]]): with [[Michael Penix Jr.]] likely lost for the season, Cousins is back in as Atlanta's starter, and Harmon called his play this year 'borderline completely unacceptable.' He argued Cousins has lost significant arm strength coming off his major injury and never had much mobility to begin with, making him 'a complete liability' behind a banged-up offensive line, especially with [[Drake London]] out and no receiving depth to lean on.
- 2025-11-18 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-11-18]]): Cousins takes over for the injured [[Michael Penix Jr.]] for the rest of the season. Harris watched the back half of the Falcons-Browns game with Cousins under center (with [[Drake London]] still in for most of it) and came away unimpressed -- 'not great,' mostly checkdowns with little downfield production. Harris does note Cousins has a track record of dragging receivers into fantasy relevance, which is why he still gives [[Darnell Mooney]] a chance to be usable with London out, but is not chasing ancillary Falcons pass-catchers like [[Kyle Pitts]] off this offense.
- 2025-11-21 — According to Chris Harris on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-21]]: Cousins will start for the Falcons with [[Michael Penix Jr.]] out for the season.
- 2025-11-24 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Without Michael Penix Jr. or Drake London, Cousins led Atlanta past New Orleans, throwing a 50-yard touchdown bomb to Darnell Mooney to seal it. Harris defended Cousins on Atlanta's pick-six, noting he correctly hit 'the second window' but the ball hit Kyle Pitts's hands and popped up for the interception. Harris's conclusion: 'this doesn't mean that you use Kirk Cousins, but it is nice to know he's not utterly done.'
- 2025-12-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Cousins led the Falcons past the Jets without Drake London or a productive [[Darnell Mooney]], hitting David Sills and checking the ball down to [[Kyle Pitts]] repeatedly. Harris isn't starting Cousins but pointed to four or five difficult throws he nailed in the game and said he isn't 'completely washed' -- adding he doubts Michael Penix Jr. would have made those same throws. Harris said if London can return, Cousins can plausibly keep London's fantasy stock rolling.
- 2025-12-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-04]]): used as the same 'system operator' archetype comp applied to [[Shedeur Sanders]] -- Waldman said he's described Cousins for years as a quarterback who, on any third-and-long, you can watch from the pocket and see him telling himself he's about to make a hero throw 'just like Brett Favre,' and it rarely works out. They framed Sanders as a 'Kirk Cousins starter kit': let him struggle for a few years across a couple of teams, mature, and land with a strong roster before he becomes a good quarterback.
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-08]]): Harris pushed back hard on the idea that Cousins is 'sinking' Atlanta's offense, arguing anyone actually watching the Falcons-Seahawks game wouldn't make that claim. Cousins threw two interceptions on the box score, but both were tipped at the line - one off [[Kyle Pitts]]'s hands, one off David Sills' - and he also threw what would have been a perfect deep touchdown to [[Darnell Mooney]] that was nullified when Mooney was shoved out of his route without a flag. Harris's overall read: Cousins 'is not that bad,' and if [[Drake London]] is healthy for the fantasy playoffs Harris would feel comfortable using this passing game again.
- 2025-12-18 — According to Matt Harmon and James Koh ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-18]]): Kirk Cousins' career-long dominance of Tampa Bay was flagged as one of the stranger stat lines in the league -- across 6 career games against the Bucs he's averaging 331 passing yards with 18 total touchdowns to just 2 interceptions (including a 500-yard, 4-touchdown game last year), a pace that projects to 5,627 yards and 51 touchdowns over a full 17-game season. Over his last two seasons specifically it's 11 touchdowns to 1 interception, a 125.2 rating and 10.18 adjusted yards per attempt against Tampa Bay, versus 13 touchdowns to 18 interceptions and a 78.5 rating against everyone else as a Falcon. Harmon attributed it partly to Tampa Bay's blitz-heavy scheme colliding badly with injuries across the Bucs' pass rush, giving Cousins easy answers with [[Kyle Pitts]] and [[Drake London]] as vacancy options.
- 2026-01-15 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Ben Gretsch (untracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-01-15]]): Cousins has $10M guaranteed in the functional final year of his deal, and with little cap savings available from a cut, both agree Atlanta is far more likely to trade him than release him outright -- he proved in December he's still useful as at worst a strong backup/spot starter. His trade value may hinge partly on how the Falcons view the incoming rookie QB class in April's draft.
- 2026-02-27 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (untracked co-host) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-27]]): Atlanta is expected to cut Kirk Cousins purely for cap reasons -- Koh believes new HC Kevin Stefanski, who coached Cousins in Minnesota, 'would be more than happy to reunite' with him if money weren't an issue, and floated a non-zero chance the team cuts and later re-signs him given their history together.
- 2026-04-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-06]]) [Dynasty]: with Kirk Cousins signing with the Raiders under Clint Kubiak's Shanahan-lineage scheme, Waldman feels it that [[Fernando Mendoza]] needs a redshirt year and expects Mendoza to sit at least half to three-quarters of the season if Cousins stays healthy. Waldman says he isn't a fan of Cousins as a starter or of how Cousins presents himself publicly (calls it disingenuous 'Andy Griffith'-esque wholesomeness), but is 'an absolute raving fan' of the mentorship fit: Cousins learned the hard way as a young Washington QB making rookie-level mistakes before developing into a Pro Bowl-caliber passer, and reportedly worked well with Michael Penix Jr. in Atlanta after being benched there. Waldman expects Cousins to be a good journeyman teacher for Mendoza, who he believes is smart enough to take what he needs and discard the rest. He also notes Cousins has earned roughly $333 million in fully guaranteed career contracts without ever playing the franchise-tag game.
- 2026-07-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman expects [[Kirk Cousins]] to start roughly half to three-quarters of the season for the Raiders before [[Fernando Mendoza]] takes over once the team is out of AFC West contention -- Waldman joked Las Vegas isn't competing 'unless Jerry Glanville is driving the team planes' for its rivals. He said he's personally hoping Cousins stays on the field as long as possible given his career-long affinity for targeting tight ends, which would directly benefit [[Brock Bowers]].
- 2026-07-24 — According to Chris Harris and Andy Behrens (untracked co-host) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris is highly skeptical of the Raiders' messaging that Cousins will start the whole season, calling it 'state media lies' comparable to the Falcons' insistence Cousins would keep his job all of 2024, and says the only real question is the timing of a [[Fernando Mendoza]] takeover, not whether it happens. Both hosts warn against building a redraft or superflex strategy around a Raiders quarterback regardless of who starts. They note 'late-career Kirk Cousins' is not nothing -- he can still make a receiver fantasy-relevant, citing his Kyle Pitts track record -- but both would rather see Mendoza win the job for the offense's longer-term outlook.
- 2026-08-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-24 (Feel It or Fk It)]]): Waldman feels it that Kirk Cousins will start more than 12 games this season, arguing Cousins remains 'a very good signal caller, a very good tactician' but that his tactical execution and pocket mobility have declined post-Achilles. He noted the coaching staff has stopped ruling out starting rookie [[Fernando Mendoza]] this year, but he still expects Cousins to hold the job through at least the week 13 bye unless the team is meaningfully under .500 by mid-October, at which point Mendoza would get a look for the final four to six games. Co-host Bob Harris needled Waldman for what he framed as dislike of Cousins; Waldman maintained his read is about declining tactical/mobility skills tied to the Achilles recovery, not personal bias. He predicts Cousins can probably stay just good enough to fend off a switch to [[Fernando Mendoza]] for about a month before the rookie takes over.
