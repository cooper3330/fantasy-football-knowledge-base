## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: a South Carolina-to-Oklahoma transfer (by way of Oklahoma twice) who projects as a move tight end in spread schemes — lead blocker, wham blocker, or wall-off option in double teams, catches with competent technique and takes contact well. Best against zone coverage; still has to develop timing routes to beat man coverage, though his hip mobility as a runner suggests he can get there. Physical enough to become a competent reserve who contributes in multiple-tight-end sets.
