# Bucky Irving

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: named as a player Angelo has mixed feelings about relative to outside hype. Direct comp to [[Devin Singletary]]'s pre-draft profile — extreme range from "best player on the field" flashes (breaking multiple tackles on the same play, real explosive-gain ability Singletary himself never had) to plays where he goes off schedule for no reason on a run that was blocked to work as designed. Angelo's explicit ceiling case: if Irving finds the same "on schedule" discipline Singletary eventually found in the NFL (tempering dramatic movement into more controlled, contact-balance-based play), he projects as a "Singletary-plus" player because Irving already has more explosiveness in space than Singletary ever did. [[Matt Waldman]]'s same-conversation caveat: doesn't think Irving currently has Singletary's decision-making/vision consistency — Singletary's game was built almost entirely on compensating for a lack of NFL-average speed and power with elite vision and footwork, whereas Irving (who is faster than Singletary) still needs to *add* skills rather than simply calibrate ones he already has. Both agree he has a real NFL role; they disagree on how close he already is to unlocking it. See [[Pace Control and Movement Intellect]] for the "on schedule vs. off schedule" framework this take draws on.
