## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-01]]): named alongside [[Najee Harris]] as an intriguing piece of the backfield under incoming OC Arthur Smith's run-heavy, volume-funneling scheme, but explicitly tempered — "if you were just waiting for a full-on Jalen Warren revival, I don't think we're going to get that." No specific 2024 role projection given beyond that caution.
- 2024-05-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-06]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman floats Jaylen Warren as a 'Tyler Allgeier mini' -- a smaller-scale change-of-pace comp -- with the caveat that if new OC Arthur Smith leans on a similar role to what Allgeier had in Atlanta, it could complicate touch shares for the whole Steelers backfield, [[Najee Harris]] included.
- 2024-06-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-10]]): projects Warren for roughly 1,000 total yards and 6-7 touchdowns as the price gap to [[Najee Harris]] (RB22 vs. Warren's RB25) narrows, but still prefers Harris, expecting Arthur Smith's downhill run-game scheme to favor Harris's style and widen the usage gap again. Bob Harris noted he'd lean toward the higher-upside option (implicitly Warren) if he already had solid volume elsewhere on a roster, but at current prices sided with Harris regardless.
