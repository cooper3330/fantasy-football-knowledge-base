# Hunter Dekkers

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-31 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-31]]) [Dynasty]: Dekkers is Waldman's unofficial 13th quarterback — watched but not charted, so no RSP scouting report pre-draft, though he may appear in the post-draft guide. Roughly 6-foot-3 and 215 to 225 pounds, a lefty who succeeded [[Brock Purdy]] at Iowa State before a gambling issue ended his Division I eligibility after his first year as a starter; he played at Iowa Western this year and Waldman thought he showed well at the Hula Bowl. Waldman watched all of his interceptions, big-play throws and runs plus his work against pressure and progressions: mobile with good open-field speed, able to move the chains and occasionally flip the field, and notably confident splitting tight coverage in the middle of the field with daring, well-placed throws he is not afraid to pull the trigger on. The flaw is not seeing dropping defenders, trail coverage or defenders over the top and misreading leverage, which produced a fair number of interceptions in a single starting season. Waldman eyeballs him around QB7 to QB10 on his board, possibly higher had the accuracy shown up stronger, with a viable grade to become a starter by a second contract if a team takes a chance — though making a roster on low draft capital is difficult.
- 2025-04-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-07]]) [Dynasty]: Dekkers is NOT in the 2025 Rookie Scouting Portfolio, but Waldman watched a spread of his tape the Friday before publication after hearing him come up repeatedly. The profile: a lefty with good size, one season as a starter succeeding [[Brock Purdy]] at Iowa State with 19 touchdowns, 15 interceptions and over 3,000 yards. He makes high-end NFL-caliber throws into tight windows with good timing and anticipation, but also throws where you wonder what he saw — a one-step-forward, two-steps-back player. Grading his interceptions, touchdowns, big plays and pressure reps, Waldman estimates he would have landed roughly QB7–QB12, probably seven through ten, on his board of twelve. Same camp as [[Will Howard]]; the single year of major-level football is a concern.
