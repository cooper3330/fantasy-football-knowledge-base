# Ulysses Bentley

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-08-10 — According to Bob Harris and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-10]]) [Dynasty]: both hosts believe UDFA Bentley (SMU, previously Ole Miss) beats out [[DJ Giddens]] for the Colts' RB3 job. Waldman's case is film-based: Bentley catches and blocks well, sets up his blockers, has good short-area quickness, breaks tackles and shows creativity in the open field, while Giddens 'has a lot of those tools' but something is consistently missing on a play-to-play basis -- lapses in finish or execution. Waldman thought Bentley might have been a late-round pick and isn't surprised he's carving out a roster spot as UDFA. He compares Bentley's ceiling to an 'aspiring DeVonta Freeman type' if [[Jonathan Taylor]] were to go down, and sees him as the passing-down/third-down/two-minute option behind power runner [[Seth McGowan]]. Local beat reporting (James Boyd, The Athletic) instead has McGowan looking best-suited for between-the-tackles work, with both Giddens and McGowan otherwise unimpressive in camp so far, leaving the RB3 job wide open.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]): If [[Jonathan Taylor]] misses extended time, Waldman projects Bentley as the scat-back/passing-down complement to [[Seth McGowan]] in an Indianapolis committee.
