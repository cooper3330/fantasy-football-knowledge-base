- 2026-10-01 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Danny Carter (Rotoworld, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Both agreed Braylon Allen 'has to play over' [[TreVeyon Henderson]] this week -- Harris called it 'not close' given Henderson's limited route-running role in a Patriots offense that keeps passing even in neutral situations.
- 2026-10-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-05]]): in a Jets offense that ran only 32 plays, Allen was 'perfectly solid... nothing spectacular, nothing egregious' and the only running back to touch the ball for New York. Harmon says in a more normal-script game he'd still feel comfortable starting Allen as a low-end flex/RB3, noting he ran well after contact even in a game with almost no opportunities.
- 2026-10-13 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-13]]): Harris keeps comparing Allen's style to James Connor -- 'big, tough dude, doesn't run fancy' -- though he notes Allen 'doesn't catch it like Connor probably.' Allen fumbled into three Browns defenders on the Jets' first third-quarter possession, but atoned later by breaking through a corner's tackle to set up a drive, and ultimately delivered the winning score: on third-and-nine, with Cleveland not expecting a run, Allen broke contain on the left edge for the game-winning touchdown -- the first rushing touchdown Cleveland had allowed all season. Harris frames him as a legitimate bruising lead-role back whenever he gets rolling. [Redraft]
- 2026-10-19 — According to Patrick Doherty (Rotoworld, untracked guest) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-19]]): Doherty highlighted Allen trucking Kevin Byard on one run as proof of real physical ability, saying Allen 'can't completely go away' even once [[Breece Hall]] is fully healthy -- a complementary, change-of-pace role rather than a true threat to Hall's workload.
- 2026-10-23 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-23]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Breece Hall]] trending toward a return from his quad injury, Harris says Allen won't be rendered useless in a likely rotation but will go lower in the rankings.
