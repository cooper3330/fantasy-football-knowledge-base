- 2024-04-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-22]]): considers Waddle 'frankly a better wide receiver' than [[DeVonta Smith]] — better at the catch point, more physical, better after the catch — but still gives Smith the slight overall value edge because of Philadelphia's stronger surrounding context.
- 2024-05-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-29]]), discussing Miami's addition of rookie [[Malik Washington]]: Waddle, used almost purely as an outside receiver in Mike McDaniel's offense, was frequently in and out of the lineup last season with nagging absences; Harmon floats that free-agent addition [[Odell Beckham Jr.]] could function as informal "injury insurance" for him, while cautioning the availability pattern could be random/fluky rather than a real durability concern.
- 2024-06-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: at WR17 ADP, Waldman called Waddle 'a great price' — he'd take Waddle as a top-15 receiver in almost any year, even coming off last year's WR32 finish, on talent and role alone, projecting 109 targets, 75 catches, just over 1,000 yards and 5 touchdowns. He sees real upside if Tyreek Hill's career winds down in the next year or two, at which point Waddle could post a 1,500-yard season. Co-host Bob Harris (not tracked) said he'd personally have more shares of [[Mike Evans]] and named [[DeVonta Smith]] and (with some hesitation) [[DJ Moore]] as players he prefers at a similar price. Waldman countered he'd rank Waddle above every comp Harris named except Moore and Evans on pure player evaluation, even if his actual draft shares skew toward those other names given the crowded Miami role with Hill and [[Odell Beckham Jr.]] still ahead of him.
