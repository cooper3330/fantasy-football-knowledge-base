# Ollie Gordon

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-10-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-28]]) [waiver wire, #3/#2 on the two hosts' lists, 11% rostered]: Gordon got 10-plus touches against Atlanta including multiple good goal-line/red-zone carries and a misdirection touchdown reception off run-game design, part of a roughly 43-20 snap split with [[De'Von Achane]]. Harris likens the archetype -- if Miami keeps using him this way -- to what Achane himself used to be schemed as a few years back, though he doesn't expect Gordon's per-touch upside to match Achane's ceiling. Both hosts call one game 'not enough' to call Gordon a flex, but view him as a worthwhile speculative add given his 6'3' size, goal-line role, and Miami's improved run-game function; Scott Fish (Fantasy Cares, not tracked) noted Gordon has been getting worked in as more than just a backup over recent weeks.
- 2026-10-27 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-27]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Gordon was mostly bottled up against the Jets but converted a short-yardage touchdown after Miami got a first down inside the one, and was 'physical and good' late when the team needed first downs as its offense sputtered. Harris: Gordon 'deserves his stranglehold on that job for as long as he stays healthy,' while cautioning not to read this close win as a reason to invest heavily in Miami's offense more broadly.
