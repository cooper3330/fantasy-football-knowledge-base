## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]: calls Carter "a pretty damn good back" who hasn't gotten the opportunities he deserves behind [[James Connor]], expecting him to cut into Connor's 2024 workload — though both hosts agree probably not as much as some expect.
- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], same episode: notes Carter shared a college backfield with Javonte Williams at North Carolina, where both were 1,000-yard rushers, and argues Carter's receiving and route-running skills specifically have not been leveraged at the level they deserve in Arizona. Expects a real timeshare with Connor in 2024, with standalone fantasy value possible even in a Connor-healthy scenario.
