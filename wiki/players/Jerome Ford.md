# Jerome Ford

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-11-22 — According to Adam Harstad, untracked co-host ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-22]]) [Dynasty]: Harstad said he has been holding a candle for Ford for a while but he is reaching the probably-not-going-to-happen point. He grouped Ford last year with [[Jaylen Warren]] and [[Kyren Williams]] as the same archetype — later-round backs the organization loves who probably will not do anything but might. [[Matt Waldman]] said he has always liked Ford and is with Harstad on him. A watch-list name whose window is closing rather than an active add.
- 2024-12-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-16]]): with [[Nick Chubb]] out for the season with a broken foot, Ford is the lead piece the rest of the way after an 80-plus-yard day that included a 60-yard touchdown run. On 2025, Waldman says 'f**k it — the picture's a little too cloudy': he expects Chubb to be re-signed and a genuine split, plus the possibility Cleveland drafts a back. Still, he rates Ford as good enough to start in the NFL — a very good pass catcher with big-play ability who is 'a tougher runner than people realize' — so there is a real chance he holds a meaningful role. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) framed the four-way question as Ford vs. Chubb vs. a draft pick.
- 2025-07-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-21]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: 'totally feeling it' on Ford as a cheap post-news beneficiary. He had risen 33 ADP spots in a week but was still around 150th overall after previously going as RB57 — near [[Kareem Hunt]] — which Bob Harris (not tracked) called basically a free square, noting he had already been drafting Ford around pick 186 before the [[Quinshon Judkins]] news broke. Waldman's case: Ford is 'going nowhere' and staying in Cleveland, he has speed, he can catch the football, and he finishes runs hard; with a healthier offensive line he expects markedly more efficient play. He would not be surprised if Ford is the lead back all year, or at minimum until Judkins is eased in should the legal situation resolve. Local reporting even before the arrest had Ford as the likely early-season starter. Bob Harris noted this outcome is bad news for [[Dylan Sampson]] backers.
- 2025-09-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-08]]): Ford is the Cleveland back with the most to lose when [[Quinshon Judkins]] returns, alongside Rocket Sanders. Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked) put it more bluntly — he already sees the writing on the wall, 'the train is coming and I don't know that Ford's going to be a passenger' — while both expect [[Dylan Sampson]] to keep a role.
