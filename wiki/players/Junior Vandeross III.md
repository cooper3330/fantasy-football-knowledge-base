# Junior Vandeross III

- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Vandeross is quick with promising routes, hands and after-catch skill, and Waldman says he offers more intermediate/vertical upside than a similarly-sized [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] did at Kentucky. He stacks coverage early, uses peak and inset pace variations to bait defenders (uncommon for a young prospect), and his breaks and speed turns are precise with real snap -- efficient, technically sound work that Waldman contrasts with 'playground' improvisers like Braxton Miller's college-QB scrambling. Probably won't get drafted, but Waldman thinks he could stick on a roster given the opportunity.
- 2026-04-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-02]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said the 5'8', 182-pound Junior Vandeross III would compare favorably to Wan'Dale Robinson if Vandeross had played at a Power 5 school with Robinson's draft-capital pedigree instead of a smaller program -- Waldman thinks his catch-point ability and downfield timing-route skill are actually a bit better than Robinson's. He put Vandeross's outcome range at a Greg Dortch floor with real bust risk given the lack of draft capital, comparing the group dynamic to when he ranked Wan'Dale Robinson, Calvin Austin III and Kalil Pimpleton together and Robinson ended up the outlier who got real NFL opportunity. Waldman's advice: an add-for-nothing dynasty stash to monitor in training camp.
