# Tua Tagovailoa

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and guest **Daniel Harms** (not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: agreement that he can't get Miami to the Super Bowl as currently built, though both stop well short of calling him bad. Harms's case: he's a genuine anticipation-and-timing thrower who can hit tight windows between the numbers when the scheme (speed everywhere, heavy play-action) creates them for him, but he's not a strong post-snap reader of coverage and can be fooled — take away the scheme-manufactured windows and force him to go through progressions or throw outside the numbers, and he's unreliable. Structural problem layered on top: as a cold-weather-disadvantaged offense in the AFC East, Miami has to travel to Buffalo, New York and New England every year on the way to a title. Waldman agrees the defense needs to improve for Miami to get further, but both land on "good football player, not an elite quarterback" and don't see him as the long-term answer to get Miami to February.
- 2024-01-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-16]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the sharpest negative take on Tua yet — "straight up bad," not just "bad for him," in a Wild Card loss at Kansas City (20/39, 199 yards, 0 TD, 1 INT in extreme cold). Cites Derrick Klassen's Reception Perception charting: Tua posted the 5th-lowest success-rate-against-pressure figure among 2022 starters (alongside Kyler Murray, [[Jared Goff]], Aaron Rodgers, [[Lamar Jackson]], Jalen Hurts) — but unlike the mobile passers on that list, he has no scrambling ability to bail him out. Diagnosis: not a plus athlete, no answer for second-reaction situations once the play breaks down, and a weaker arm than Jared Goff's. Harmon pegs his ceiling "just a touch under" Goff's — outside a top-12-to-15 quarterback conversation. See [[Reception Perception Methodology]].
- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]): a more optimistic redraft-relevant read than Harmon's January critique — reframes 2023 (4,624 yards, 29 TD, 69% completion, QB9) as not yet his statistical peak, expecting his next two-to-three seasons to land in low-end fantasy QB1 range as he continues developing. Diagnoses the market's skepticism as an archetype-recognition problem rather than a talent problem: evaluators discount "limited" throwers who lack elite arm strength or mobility, the same dynamic Waldman says has kept [[Kirk Cousins]] and [[Jared Goff]] perennially undervalued.
