## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and guest **Daniel Harms** (not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: agreement that he can't get Miami to the Super Bowl as currently built, though both stop well short of calling him bad. Harms's case: he's a genuine anticipation-and-timing thrower who can hit tight windows between the numbers when the scheme (speed everywhere, heavy play-action) creates them for him, but he's not a strong post-snap reader of coverage and can be fooled — take away the scheme-manufactured windows and force him to go through progressions or throw outside the numbers, and he's unreliable. Structural problem layered on top: as a cold-weather-disadvantaged offense in the AFC East, Miami has to travel to Buffalo, New York and New England every year on the way to a title. Waldman agrees the defense needs to improve for Miami to get further, but both land on "good football player, not an elite quarterback" and don't see him as the long-term answer to get Miami to February.
