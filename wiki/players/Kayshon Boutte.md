# Kayshon Boutte

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-04-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-22]]): calls Boutte one of the more intriguing speculative stash adds on expanded offseason rosters given New England's thin receiver room behind an ACL-recovering [[Kendrick Bourne]], comparing the upside path to a Rhamondre Stevenson-style late breakout if Boutte proves to be a worker — though Waldman isn't yet sure that he is.
- 2024-10-14 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-14]]): Waldman has 'always liked' Boutte and 'feels it' on him as a future contributor after three catches for 59 yards and a touchdown with [[Drake Maye]], with whom he has been building chemistry away from the starters. He comps him to a mix of Robert Woods and Jarvis Landry, and points to the staff giving him more snaps over the past three weeks — including beating Derion Stingley one-on-one on a deep shot, which Waldman thought looked legitimate rather than a coverage bust at the end of the half. The caveat is New England's history of throwing receivers at the wall to see what sticks: he notes he could list about 15 non-slot receivers there who never did anything, and that plenty of people liked [[Ja'Lynn Polk]], a healthy scratch in this game.
- 2024-10-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-15]]): Boutte's involvement is interesting — only three targets but a 19-yard average depth of target, and stylistically he is the vertical X type New England lacks amid all the flanker/slot bodies. Harmon did not like the LSU film in Boutte's final season, calling him undisciplined and not technically sound, with a strange college arc where his freshman year was his best and he got worse from there, then nothing as a rookie. Koh (not tracked) walks through the athletic testing that dropped him to the sixth round in 2023: expected to run in the 4.4s at 5-11, 195, he ran a flat 4.5 with a sub-30-inch vertical and a sub-10-foot broad jump, numbers both hosts call bad for his size and inconsistent with how athletic he looks on film. Verdict: monitor whether the role holds; still a long shot alongside [[Javon Baker]].
- 2024-10-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-21 (Feel It or Fk It 10.21.24)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: asked whether Boutte is New England's best big-play threat, Waldman said 'feel it' — partly by default, because [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] 'can't hold onto the ball right now.' On Boutte's own merits: two big plays in the past two games, and while 'he's never been known as a huge speedster, he does have enough acceleration to get past one-on-one coverage and win the ball and track it down.' The caveat is snap count — 'it may not be as quick of an emergence as we'd like to see, but it is a steady emergence as a factor in that passing game,' and Waldman expects that role to keep growing.
- 2024-10-31 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-31]]): the pair disagree on him. Angelo says Jerod Mayo is doing a poor job managing the receiver room and that New England should not be starting [[K.J. Osborne]] and Boutte — he would rather they start [[Javon Baker]]. Waldman defends Boutte partially: he has been the Patriots' best receiver, which is more an indictment of the room than praise, and Waldman thinks Boutte would make a good number three now and could have a long career in that role; he also credits Boutte with two big plays late to set up the winning touchdown. Angelo concedes the number-three projection. Both are amazed [[Tyquan Thornton]] is still getting snaps, and both say [[Drake Maye]] needs far more help than he is getting.
- 2024-11-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-20]]): Boutte has run a route on 96.3% of New England's dropbacks over the last four weeks, by far the most on the team, and has become their X and vertical guy — remarkable for a player who was a roster-bubble candidate in camp, as Koh pointed out. Harmon is unmoved: 'I was never that big on Kayshon Boutte. Maybe I end up being wrong, but I don't really think there's anything there.' He notes Boutte is not being targeted at a rate matching the routes and reads the whole thing as evidence the Patriots are in discovery mode, likely to scrap the room and start over around [[Drake Maye]] next offseason.
- 2025-01-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-01-13]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon gives Boutte credit for real improvement — he was a genuine roster-bubble player who began the year behind Tyquan Thornton, who is no longer on the roster at all, and he built a little something with [[Drake Maye]] running vertical routes as the team's X. Harmon still calls him an inconsistent player, as he was as a prospect, and says he would hope Boutte does not enter next season as New England's starting X receiver. The realistic outcome he sees is a future hanging onto the roster as a depth piece. [[Matt Waldman]] frames the question as whether the production was desperation or evidence of something more, noting the character concerns and the slot-flanker profile.
- 2025-01-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-01-23]]) [Dynasty]: Boutte is the New England receiver Waldman would install as the starter, saying he thinks Boutte gives you more than [[Demario Douglas]] if he has caught on to what is going on — and noting Boutte is nominally a slot who can play flanker, yet was still beating people when the Patriots lined him up at X. [[Brandon Angelo]] agrees Boutte looks better and better and can become a secondary option in an offense: four years ago he was a top-three dynasty devy asset before multiple surgeries and legal issues pushed him down, and he has fought through it and is turning into a pro. The caveat both give is that New England has roughly four primary slot receivers on the roster and keeps miscasting them.
- 2025-08-06 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-06]]): Harmon considers Boutte locked into a roster spot and, if he had to bet, would have him open the season as the Patriots' X receiver on the back of a reportedly good camp — ahead of [[Kyle Williams]] and well ahead of [[Ja'Lynn Polk]]. He frames this as an open competition at X that [[Terry McLaurin]] would win instantly if traded to New England, so Boutte holds the job by default rather than by conviction.
- 2025-08-07 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-07]]): New England is committing malpractice with Boutte — a player they nearly ran out of town after year one who then played X and played well enough there, but whom Angelo wants on another team as a slot or flanker. [[Matt Waldman]] agreed and put a number on it: freed from his current role, Boutte is a player who could give you 800 to 1,100 yards, and he compared him to Jarvis Landry with a little more speed. Waldman's extended joke — 'a turtle in a rabbit costume with an outboard motor' — was about Boutte finding ways to win without top-end speed. Both floated Buffalo and Carolina as better fits, Carolina as a successor to [[Adam Thielen]] [Dynasty].
- 2025-09-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-01]]): Waldman is 'absolutely feeling' Tom Curran's (NBC Sports) prediction that Boutte outproduces [[Stefon Diggs]]. He liked Boutte coming out of school as a very good flanker/slot type who wins in the middle of the field after the catch, adjusts well to the ball and runs decent routes — a gambling issue, not the tape, submarined his draft stock into the late rounds. Waldman notes that after a quiet rookie year New England then played him at split end, the worst of the three roles for him given he is not unbelievably fast, and he still played well. Curran reports Boutte looks better than Diggs, is winning contested catches, has improved hands and works well with [[Drake Maye]] in the red zone and two-minute drill. Waldman has moved him up his rankings and added him in late drafts; Bob Harris (not tracked) cited beat writer Tom King placing Boutte neck-and-neck with Diggs for target share behind [[Hunter Henry]].
- 2025-09-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-08]]) [Dynasty]: 'fuck yes' he remains a weekly start — Waldman traded [[Stefon Diggs]] and [[Tank Bigsby]] for Boutte and a second-round pick in the Footballguys Dynasty IDP League and does not regret it, though he suspects he could have gotten more back. Boutte is playing exactly as Waldman charted him at LSU: a [[Robert Woods]] / Jarvis Landry-type who wins back-shoulder throws, runs good routes against zone, is rugged, wins contested balls and takes hits — all on display against the Raiders. Beat writers say the two receivers [[Drake Maye]] is most comfortable with are Hunter Henry and Boutte. Waldman adds he may still be lined up at X, his worst position, and could still be New England's best receiver. Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked) cautions Diggs and [[Demario Douglas]] are not going away.
