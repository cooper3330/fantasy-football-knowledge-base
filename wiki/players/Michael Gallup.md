# Michael Gallup

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-15 — Guest **Daniel Harms** (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-15]] [Redraft (Standard)]: firmly "f\*\*k it" on a 2024 rebound. Says he doesn't create enough separation to hold down an outside/X role, doubts he's even still in Dallas next season given the team's uncertain quarterback situation, and projects a "good NFL player, not a fantasy-relevant one" career arc comparable to Chris Conley's — a long career without ever being a real fantasy factor.

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) on *Reception Perception: The Show* ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-11]]): both sell his reported link to the Steelers — a redundant X-receiver archetype fit behind [[George Pickens]] on a team that doesn't want to throw much anyway. Harmon instead pegs Detroit (replacing departed [[Josh Reynolds]]) as a good fit — rounding out the room alongside [[D.J. Chark]] (unresolved), [[Jameson Williams]] and [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] without being fully dependent on either. Believes at his peak Gallup was a better player than Reynolds and would take the reclamation-project upside.
- 2024-07-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-25]]): Gallup was placed on the reserve/retired list by the Raiders the night before recording. Harmon calls it a genuinely sad arc — before tearing his ACL in 2020 he was off to a really nice start as a high-end number two, a vertical X who was really good against man and press coverage and could win jump balls, and he was never the same afterward. His point is broader than Gallup: people 'yada yada' injuries and assume that being back on the field means being back to 100%, when anyone embedded with the Cowboys would say Gallup never got his confidence back. He pairs it with [[Rashad Bateman]] saying post-season that he did not think he would play at all in 2023, and uses both as the risk case he hopes does not apply to [[Chris Godwin]].
- 2025-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-21]]): on Gallup un-retiring to sign with Washington, Harmon concedes this could easily be nothing — he signed with the Raiders last offseason, never played a snap and retired before camp — but says Gallup was a Reception Perception favorite early in his career and a genuinely underrated true perimeter X: 88.6% of sample snaps outside and 81.4% on the line of scrimmage in 2019, 95% outside and just 5% slot in 2020, with 71% success rate vs man in 2019 and 72% in 2020, though he was never a zone beater. Harmon argues that archetype is actually what the Commanders lack — [[Terry McLaurin]] can play X but is better moved around, [[Deebo Samuel]] wants the middle of the field, and they lost [[Dyami Brown]], so with [[Jayden Daniels]] loving deep throws outside the numbers there is a hypothetical role winning perimeter jump balls. James Koh (co-host, not tracked) is more skeptical, questioning what shape Gallup is in after the injuries that drove him to retire and whether he even makes the roster, and jokes that Terry McLaurin moving around requires Kliff Kingsbury to cooperate, which 2024 did not show.
- 2025-07-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-07-09]]): Harmon revisits Gallup's 2020 season with Dallas as the historical baseline for a productive Cowboys X receiver — 72% versus man, 70.6% versus zone, 75.5% versus press — and says he was a big Gallup fan in his prime. He raises it to argue that [[George Pickens]], whose 2024 marks beat Gallup's across the board, should clear that bar as a more fluid version of the same role.
