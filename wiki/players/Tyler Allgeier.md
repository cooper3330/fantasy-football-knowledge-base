# Tyler Allgeier

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Dynasty]: expects [[Bijan Robinson]] to get the bulk of Atlanta's new-coaching-staff workload increase in 2024, but flags Allgeier as a real complementary piece worth "a dash of" draft capital in his own right — he "plays really well when in the system they put him in."
- 2024-01-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-30]]): with Atlanta's new OC hire (Zach Robinson, from the Rams) bringing a man-heavy run scheme, Harmon expects [[Bijan Robinson]] to be the clear lead, but still expects Allgeier to "get involved" as a complementary piece rather than being phased out entirely.
- 2024-11-22 — According to Adam Harstad, untracked co-host ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-22]]) [Dynasty]: Harstad likes watching the Falcons scale back Allgeier's snaps as [[Bijan Robinson]]'s role grows — not because it hurts Allgeier's outlook but because he hopes leaguemates read the shrinking workload as a reason to move on. In his view Allgeier has clearly passed the organizational-commitment test and has looked good when given opportunities. He doubts Allgeier ever reaches waivers outright, but flags him as a buy at a discount around next year's rookie draft when roster crunch pushes managers to clear room for shiny new toys — the classic later-round back with future opportunity whom the organization loves.
- 2025-09-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-08]]): Allgeier played only 18 snaps and Harris cannot understand how he still got 10 carries out of it, calling it come-on-man usage that should belong to [[Bijan Robinson]]. He is not mad at the 10 for 24 line given Tampa's good run defense, but the split reflects Atlanta being too clever with alignments rather than any Allgeier merit.
- 2025-09-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-16]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: guest John Daigle (Establish The Run, not tracked) made Allgeier his number-two Week 3 waiver add at 30% rostered — the add you make if you need an immediate RB3 or flex rather than a no-standalone-value stash like [[Blake Corum]], [[Woody Marks]] or [[Chris Rodriguez Jr.]] — because he sits behind [[Bijan Robinson]] in a friendly spot against a Carolina team that let [[Travis Etienne Jr.]] run for a career-high 143 yards in Week 1, with [[Bryce Young]] struggling and Atlanta's defense playing well; Daigle projects 10-15 carries. Harris flags his usual objection to projecting game scripts, but agrees the outcome range is real: 10 and 16 carries in the two wins, unproductive against Tampa (Harris kept wanting Bijan back in) but effective in heavy garbage time against Minnesota. Harris expects him ranked around RB35 and among the highest of the backup backs.
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris' No. 5 and guest Brandon Funston's No. 4 Week 6 waiver target, 31% rostered. Harris' pitch is deliberately modest: 'if you find yourself in a bad situation, I think he's startable... it isn't great and you're not going to be happy, but as your flex, it can be Tyler Allgeier.' Funston adds the volume case — a couple of games with 16 carries, though completely out of the passing game — and rates him near the top of any next-man-up handcuff list, finding the end zone even in a shared role with Bijan Robinson. Both attribute the 31% availability to managers dropping handcuffs to survive bye weeks. Harris groups him with [[Kendre Miller]] as a good power back who would be useful, not a superstar, in a pure starting role.
- 2025-10-23 — According to Scott Pianowski (untracked Yahoo analyst) and [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-23]]): despite [[Bijan Robinson]] being one of the best backs in the NFL, Allgeier has posted double-digit carries in four games this season with three touchdowns, and the Falcons consistently keep a package for him even when Robinson is playing well (barring total blowouts). He's dinged up heading into a home matchup with a Miami run defense that 'has offered little resistance,' logging 15-20 snaps in a normal week. Pianowski would start him proactively if Friday's injury report is favorable, ranking him ~RB28 in standard leagues; both hosts pointed to a goal-line pass-interference call setting up Atlanta at the one-yard line as evidence Allgeier still gets scoring-range work regardless of Robinson's workload. Roughly half-rostered as a handcuff play.
- 2025-11-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-10]]): Allgeier scored twice, but Harris reads the usage as closer/fresher-back rotation rather than a goal-line takeover. The first came after a run from the 22 down to the one with no personnel change — a good run, stay in, get another run. The second sequence started near midfield with two minutes left: Robinson got a carry, came out, and Allgeier stayed in for a pass to [[Kyle Pitts]] plus three straight runs from the 19. Harris's month-long counts show Allgeier trailing [[Bijan Robinson]] 13-23 on red zone snaps; both of Allgeier's inside-the-five snaps came Sunday. He concedes you are allowed to be freaked out that both backs worked, but calls the 'Allgeier has all the goal line work' claim false.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-11]]): Allgeier was the number one Week 11 waiver add for both Harris and guest **Brandon Funston** (The Athletic, not tracked), at 33 percent rostered. Harris pushes back on the goal-line narrative - the Germany score against the Colts was the only carry Atlanta has had inside the three-yard line in the past month, so it is thin evidence of a defined role. The real question he poses is whether you can start him with [[Bijan Robinson]] healthy: 'Is he [[David Montgomery]]? No', but both agreed he is a flex play, with six touchdowns, about a third of the rushing carries, and Carolina at home giving up around five yards per carry. Funston adds context that [[Michael Penix Jr.]] has been struggling and Raheem Morris looks to be limiting his passing exposure with a conservative, run-heavy approach. Harris: 'is he going to be the second-highest second-banana running back in everybody's ranks? Yes.'
- 2025-11-21 — According to Chris Harris on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-21]] [Dynasty]: Harris's number-one pick, framed as the inverse of his [[Zach Charbonnet]] pick -- Allgeier is a pending UFA this summer whom Harris called a genuinely efficient, good back who ran for 1,000 yards as a rookie a few years ago. Harris's bet is that Allgeier leaves Atlanta for a bigger role and payday (naming Dallas, Washington, or Arizona as plausible fits) rather than re-signing to keep splitting touches; on a team giving him 16-18 touches a week, Harris called him 'an easy RB2 every single week.' He noted FantasyPros currently lists Allgeier as RB48/dynasty and 168th overall, which both hosts flatly called wrong given he's already effectively an every-week RB2-caliber producer splitting with one of the NFL's best backs. Josh, who considered Allgeier for his own list, agreed there's little practical difference between Allgeier's and Charbonnet's skill sets -- both are strong, contact-absorbing, goal-line-capable backs who lack much wiggle or explosiveness -- and floated that Atlanta may not even match a modest $5M offer to retain him.
- 2025-12-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Allgeier again vultured a goal-line touchdown from [[Bijan Robinson]] right before halftime against the Jets after Robinson was stuffed from the one -- Harris joked he could 'hear the angry emails' from fans he'd told this wouldn't keep happening.
- 2025-12-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris ranks Allgeier his number-one handcuff/speculative add, noting he's already rostered in 41% of Yahoo leagues and is startable as a flex play in his own right even before considering a Bijan Robinson injury scenario, though he cautions Atlanta's offensive environment isn't as lucrative as some other backfields on the list.
- 2026-02-24 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-24]]): as a speculative free-agent idea, Harmon floats Allgeier reuniting in Baltimore with his former Falcons run-game coordinator Dwayne Ledford (now hired by the Ravens) as a complementary/backup piece behind Derrick Henry.
- 2026-03-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Andy Behrens (not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-03-05]]): Harris comps Allgeier's likely deal to David Montgomery's and wouldn't be shocked if it comes in a bit bigger, arguing Allgeier is a much better player than the 'bruiser' label suggests -- he floats the idea that if the market clears he could 'squint and look like a number one' alongside a lesser complement, calling him a guy with real ceiling nobody got to see in Atlanta's committee. Reported to have signed a three-year, $27M-guaranteed deal in Denver, pairing with RJ Harvey similarly to how J.K. Dobbins and Harvey worked together, which both hosts view as a strong fantasy landing spot given Seattle and Denver both had backfield voids.
- 2026-03-12 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Dave Kluge (Footballguys.com, untracked guest) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-03-12]]) [Dynasty]: Allgeier leaves Atlanta for Arizona, joining a crowded backfield with [[James Conner]], [[Trey Benson]] and Bam Knight. Harris compares him to a 'David Montgomery-mode' honest, safe runner and expects him to be the safest bet of the group price-wise, but both hosts expect a genuine four-way muddle where the 'consensus' pick to win the job is usually wrong. Harris says he'll likely fade Allgeier once he gets drafted up into the 5th-7th round range, preferring to see where he falls.
- 2026-07-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-21]]): Allgeier received real guaranteed money from Arizona this offseason even after the team drafted [[Jeremiah Love]], and will still handle some special teams work while retaining an involved role in the backfield mix, though the exact shape of that role remains unclear behind Love, [[James Conner]], and [[Trey Benson]].
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]): Waldman noted Allgeier is listed as the starter into camp over rookie [[Jeremiah Love]] because the team trusts him as a pass protector, receiver, and processor of quick adjustments that they aren't yet sure Love can handle.
- 2026-08-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-31]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris's number one overall pick is Allgeier, whom he expects to be the Cardinals' Week 1 starter and possibly the outright lead back, arguing the market hasn't priced in that Jeremiah Love's high-ankle sprain is worse than advertised, that James Conner opens the year on IR (out at least four games, did nothing in camp, with no guarantee he returns at all), and that Trey Benson is out for the season. Harris calls Allgeier easy to acquire at no real cost and takes him 'a thousand times over' Jordan Tyson at similar ADP. Pianowski (guest, untracked) agrees, comparing Allgeier to David Montgomery/Chris Carson/Chris Rodriguez as 'the same kind of player' along an explosiveness axis, and both note Allgeier got a real veteran contract (not a minimum deal), signaling organizational trust; Pianowski calls his 5'10', 225 frame the prototype for a 15-20 touch NFL back.
- 2026-09-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-10]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: floated by a listener as the Week 1 flex fallback if [[Jeremiah Love]] can't go; Harris said he'd lean Allgeier over [[DJ Moore]] in that scenario, but noted it sounds like Love is expected to play, making it a low-probability contingency.
- 2026-09-11 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-11]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris confirmed [[Tyler Allgeier]] will also play in Week 1, noting that's not a disqualifier for fantasy relevance since most teams roll with two backs anyway.
