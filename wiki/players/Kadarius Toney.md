# Kadarius Toney

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-05-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-06]]): Waldman says Kadarius Toney's run as an impactful NFL starter is effectively over -- roughly 80-90% likely done, in his estimate -- after the Giants' GM publicly framed him as a potential WR1 last year and he fell far short. He compares Toney to Michael Westbrook: a boom/bust talent capable of an 80-yard house call one week and a teammate altercation the next, pointing to Toney's 189-yard, 10-catch game against Dallas followed by an in-game ejection for punching an opponent as the whole arc of his career in miniature. Waldman frames it as a focus-and-maturity problem rather than a talent-ceiling problem, leaving only a small window for a turnaround if Toney has a genuine 'coming to the light' moment.
- 2024-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-29]]): Toney was cut by Kansas City and, as of recording, unsigned — an obvious move to Harmon, who says Toney 'never showed the traits of being a real wide receiver' and could never simply line up and win with route running, a position he has held consistently from Toney's college profile onward: 'I don't care who's told you otherwise. I don't know what film they're watching.' On the yards-per-route-run defence, Harmon replies that gaining yards without running a real route 'can make you look like something that you're not.' He argues Toney became redundant the moment KC drafted [[Xavier Worthy]], because the roster only has so much capacity for designed-touch players who need role catering. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) calls the decline cataclysmic rather than steady, notes EPA numbers support Toney being a net negative for the Chiefs passing game and for [[Patrick Mahomes]] in 2023, and cites drops that led to pick-sixes plus a social media outburst.
