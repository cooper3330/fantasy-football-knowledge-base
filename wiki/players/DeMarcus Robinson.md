## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]): feels Robinson's strong close to 2023 (money in DFS from roughly Weeks 13-17) as buildable, given the Rams run three-receiver sets on roughly 95% of snaps, but cautions to temper expectations to that late-season level rather than assume a touchdown every week. Notes he passed since-concussed Tutu Atwell (not given a page this episode) in target competition down the stretch. Values him most as a high-upside 4th-receiver option in dynasty formats that start more than three wide receivers, citing his rapport with [[Matthew Stafford]] — whom Waldman comps to a less-mobile, slightly more mistake-prone "Mahomes 1.0" for accuracy into tight throwing windows, the exact trait that lets a complementary piece like Robinson profit off-script. At his Underdog best ball ADP of WR99 (the literal last pick of the draft), Waldman calls him a "free square" he'll keep taking at that price. [Dynasty] [Best Ball]
