# DeMarcus Robinson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]): feels Robinson's strong close to 2023 (money in DFS from roughly Weeks 13-17) as buildable, given the Rams run three-receiver sets on roughly 95% of snaps, but cautions to temper expectations to that late-season level rather than assume a touchdown every week. Notes he passed since-concussed Tutu Atwell (not given a page this episode) in target competition down the stretch. Values him most as a high-upside 4th-receiver option in dynasty formats that start more than three wide receivers, citing his rapport with [[Matthew Stafford]] — whom Waldman comps to a less-mobile, slightly more mistake-prone "Mahomes 1.0" for accuracy into tight throwing windows, the exact trait that lets a complementary piece like Robinson profit off-script. At his Underdog best ball ADP of WR99 (the literal last pick of the draft), Waldman calls him a "free square" he'll keep taking at that price. [Dynasty] [Best Ball]
- 2024-04-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-22]]) [Best Ball]: still rostering Robinson in some dynasty leagues off his strong finish last season, and recommends him as a round-20 best-ball flyer.
- 2024-06-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-10]]): named Robinson his top wide receiver pick in Football Guys' over/undervalued 'deep sleeper' series, citing his phenomenal stretch run in 2023 and projecting roughly 80 targets, low-50s catches, 750 yards and 8 touchdowns -- calling that touchdown total high relative to last year's pace but plausible given his role. Bob Harris fully co-signed, calling Robinson 'a free-square player' he can't resist and noting he'll be a close third in production behind [[Puka Nacua]] and [[Cooper Kupp]].
- 2024-07-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-29 (Feel It or F-It)]]) [Best Ball]: loves Robinson all day long as a late-round best-ball flyer, on the back of a late-season run in which he scored nearly every game as an integral piece of the Rams offense with [[Matthew Stafford]] clearly looking his way and two other receivers occupying defensive attention. He highlights [[Puka Nacua]] describing Robinson as the quintessential X who can win on an island and run the full route tree — a teammate endorsement that also leaks schematic intent — and Stafford's fearlessness in letting receivers go up and win the ball, a habit formed with Calvin Johnson. Caveats: Waldman still has Robinson third in the pecking order and thinks it takes an injury for the upside to land, and he is unhappy to hear camp reports of Tutu Atwell having a great day.
- 2024-09-10 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-10]]): with [[Puka Nacua]] on short-term IR, Robinson is the Rams' best remaining vertical option — he was the only Ram with an average depth of target north of eight in Week 1 and played about 75% of his snaps lined up outside, versus 25% for [[Cooper Kupp]] and 35% for [[Tyler Johnson]]. Harmon says he has 'shown some things' as their X receiver and could have had a bigger night but for a pass interference call in the end zone. The caveat Harmon attaches is that Robinson is a pure vertical player, not the rugged, physical press-beating route runner the McVay offense needs from its outside spot, which is what Nacua provided — so Robinson alone probably does not solve the Rams' problem.
