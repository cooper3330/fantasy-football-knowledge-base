# Breece Hall

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-21 — [[Brandon Angelo]] (co-host of *Going Deep*) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21 (Pacing and Control)]]: uses Hall as the counter-example to the episode's pace-control thread. Hall "tries to hit the home run every time — either you're getting a home run or you're striking out." His comp: "that old baseball player who's hitting .198 but he has 20 home runs. It's great when it works, but a lot of times you have to control the tempo of your own run and get what's blocked." He attributes the Jets giving Dalvin Cook more work partly to this, citing on-air comments from Robert Saleh.
- 2023-12-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], same episode, the fuller and more sympathetic version — and an explicit bullish forecast attached to it. "With a better line, even without the skills you're mentioning, he's going to be a big-time yardage producer. It's going to be scary." The knock is specific: "he averts one disaster at a time." Hall processes what is immediately in front of him extremely fast, but "he's not maybe seeing two steps ahead, he's not seeing it in the full scope" the way [[Christian McCaffrey]], [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] and [[James Cook]] do. Waldman's framing is that this makes him *more* impressive, not less — "the fact that he's literally just going, okay, what do I do next, what do I do next, and he can get eight yards on that play is incredible" — "but it's not optimal for what he could be."

**Read:** both hosts are describing a ceiling-raiser, not a bust flag — the
projection is that improved processing (or an improved offensive line) unlocks a
volume producer. The near-term risk is the workload split that the boom/bust
style invites.
