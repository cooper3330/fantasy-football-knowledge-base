# Chris Rodriguez Jr.

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-08-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-25]]): there is legitimate short-term value — early in the season or on an injury to anyone in Washington's trio he can be a low-end RB2 or flex — but the long-term picture with everyone healthy is 'the [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] show' as the lead back, [[Austin Ekeler]] used situationally, and Rodriguez limited to blocking, some short yardage split with Croskey-Merritt, and special teams. Waldman's comp: if Spencer Ware were a band, Rodriguez would be the weekend cover band your rib joint hires once or twice a week — competent, gets it done, but not worth arena money. Bob Harris (Footballguys, NOT tracked) accepted the framing, adding that it is the Croskey-Merritt show until it is not, and when it is not, it becomes the Rodriguez show.
- 2025-09-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Rodriguez, 7% rostered, is Harris's number two waiver name and was on guest BRANDON FUNSTON's short list without quite making it. Harris says he wrote this in an Almanac update: there were stretches last year, mostly in blowouts either way, where Rodriguez would get a couple of carries and look okay, even interesting. He wants to see how much Rodriguez works in against the Giants, and frames the open question as whether the Washington backfield picture is about [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]], about [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] being a pill over his contract, or about the team genuinely seeing a three-headed monster. He is not necessarily adding him, but wants him on the list.
- 2025-09-04 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-04 (Going Deep Rookie Receivers and RB Depth Charts)]]): Angelo expects Rodriguez to get the first crack at short yardage and low-red-zone work in Washington behind [[Austin Ekeler]], having earned it with an efficient preseason and an efficient finish to his rookie year — 'they're going to want to give him his pie.' Waldman comped him to a 'middle-class Spencer Ware' without the special skills that could elevate Ware, and framed the edge as executional: he does the offense the way Cliff Kingsbury wants it done, and most offensive coaches will take reliable execution over higher-end athletic ability as a tiebreaker. Waldman still expects [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] to be able to leapfrog him if [[Austin Ekeler]] goes down.
- 2025-09-23 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-23]]): with [[Austin Ekeler]] out for the year on a torn Achilles, Rodriguez started and took the first two full series — four straight carries to open the game before [[Marcus Mariota]]'s sneak touchdown — finishing with 21 snaps, level with [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] and ahead of Jeremy McNichols's 14. Harris made him his number one Week 4 waiver add at five percent rostered: 'if there's a dude who I can imagine developing into a lead on his team, which might not be such a bad team, it would be Rodriguez,' while conceding he would not want to start any back on this week's list. Guest SCOTT FISH (Fantasy Cares, NOT tracked) had him fourth for the same reasons — the between-the-tackles guy who knows the offense, got the goal line, more snaps, touches and carries — but 'I don't love it,' since Croskey-Merritt is going to be annoying.
- 2025-11-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-03]]): Washington turned back to Rodriguez late in the first half after [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] got nowhere against Seattle, and he was also used to run out the clock late and scored what Harris calls 'an absolute basura touchdown'. Harris says he has argued multiple times on the show that Rodriguez should be part of the mix on the small-back/big-back split, while conceding 'they don't ask me' — and that with [[Jayden Daniels]] out the whole backfield is hard to trust week to week.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-11]]): Rodriguez was the actual starter against Detroit, rewarded after being the only injection of energy in the Seattle blowout - he took the first two carries and the first-half snap count while the game was competitive went 17 Rodriguez, 6 [[Jeremy McNichols]], 4 [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]], and he scored easily from the one after [[Deebo Samuel]] was stuffed there. He appears to have hurt his shoulder covering a punt late in the third quarter. Guest **Brandon Funston** (The Athletic, not tracked) made him his number four Week 11 waiver add at 6 percent rostered pending the shoulder news, with the Commanders playing Miami in Spain. Harris will not tell you who to use in that backfield - 'someone in Washington's backfield could produce, I don't currently have the means to tell you who' - and would be wise not to chase.
