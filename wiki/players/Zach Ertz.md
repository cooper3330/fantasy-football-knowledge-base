# Zach Ertz

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-06-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-25]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harmon is skeptical of Ertz's outlook, recapping how the Cardinals placed him on IR in October 2023 then waived him at his own request in November, after which he went unsigned until January 22 with the Lions' practice squad and produced nothing for Detroit despite insider hype that a contender would sign him. Harmon reads Ertz as a placeholder in Washington while the team hopes rookie Ben Sinnott can be more of an immediate-impact 'Sam LaPorta' than a typical slow-developing rookie tight end, while acknowledging that bet is always risky.
- 2024-07-16 — According to James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-16]]): Koh was blunt that he doesn't know 'why you go get ancient Zach Ertz,' saying he was 'atrophied last year, and that's putting it nicely,' with little left in the tank. [[Matt Harmon]] didn't fully rebut the age concern but noted new OC Kliff Kingsbury has a track record of playing multiple tight ends heavily -- including Ertz himself alongside Trey McBride in Kingsbury's final Arizona season -- so Ertz could still see real snaps in a 12-personnel-heavy Washington offense alongside rookie [[Ben Sinnott]]. [Redraft (Standard)]
- 2025-01-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-01-28]]): Ertz played better than Harmon and probably Washington expected this year and can reasonably be brought back, but he is one of the veteran-rental decisions the team has to make. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) frames the ceiling problem bluntly: he is '90 percent convinced' you lose a game in which the 34-year-old Ertz sees 16 targets, because you need production from someone more explosive. Harmon agrees — Vic Fangio would happily concede that distribution, since the whole idea is refusing anything downfield to [[Terry McLaurin]]. The counterweight is developmental: Washington wants reps for [[Ben Sinnott]], so keeping veteran competency risks stalling progress.
- 2025-03-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-10]]): Ertz re-signing in Washington after a 2024 season with 11 touchdowns could support a TE1 finish again, though Waldman framed the current TE26 ADP as likely to climb now that there is certainty. His scheme note is blunt: Ertz cannot go much more than about 10 yards from the line of scrimmage, and that fits Washington's offense perfectly. He also observed that [[Jayden Daniels]] seems to have an affinity for his elders, citing Bobby Wagner. Waldman treated the signing as either a signal that the team is not sold on [[Ben Sinnott]] or simply base-covering, and said it was speculation rather than fact.
