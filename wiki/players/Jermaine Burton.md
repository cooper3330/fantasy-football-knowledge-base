# Jermaine Burton

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): has all the tools of an effective NFL route runner, and Waldman thinks his best outcome is at flanker or in the slot, where he can get defenders on their back hip and excels as a pass catcher — attacks the ball early, has the focus to win with a safety coming downhill, and wins contested targets while defenders use him "as a ping-pong ball." A tough, consistent, smart player.

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-28 (Going Deep - If We Were NFL GMs)]]): a high-floor pick — candidate for "better NFL career than college career" since his college box score undersold his upside, buried behind Georgia/Alabama depth. Comps him to Robert Woods as a versatile X/flanker/slot piece, though Angelo thinks he's best deployed as a flanker who can also work the slot (a Rashid Shaheed-style deep/win-me role) rather than forced outside as a pure X. [[Matt Waldman]], same episode, agrees, adding that he was graded just a few points behind [[Ja'Marr Chase]] as a Georgia freshman before being stuck in wide-receiver-purgatory buried on the depth chart; calls him a day-one starter in a role, with real after-the-catch value thanks to short-area quickness, patience and suddenness. Both hosts peg him as a high-floor guy who in a weaker class would go round two/three but may fall to round three or four here.
