## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): has all the tools of an effective NFL route runner, and Waldman thinks his best outcome is at flanker or in the slot, where he can get defenders on their back hip and excels as a pass catcher — attacks the ball early, has the focus to win with a safety coming downhill, and wins contested targets while defenders use him "as a ping-pong ball." A tough, consistent, smart player.
