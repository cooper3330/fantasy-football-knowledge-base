# Tyrell Shavers

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-11-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-20]]): with Curtis Samuel banged up, Shavers posted a 53% snap share tied for the team lead with Samuel and [[Khalil Shakir]]. Harmon charted him for the site: 15.6 yards per reception on a small sample of 11 catches, a 65% catch rate, a balanced route tree (no route type run more than twice), and solid work as a blocker/insert player in the run game, plus downfield ability despite being a 50/50 inside-outside player. His technique and route-running timing 'is really not there yet' -- Harmon pointed to wasted motion at the top of a route that cost him extra yards after the catch. His touchdown came on a broken play off what Harmon called an 'unbelievable' throw from [[Josh Allen]], and Harmon noted that getting open on broken plays with Allen is 'a requirement, not a luxury.' Not a finished product, but Harmon wants to keep tracking him given Buffalo's receiver injuries.
- 2026-01-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-15]]): Shavers tore his ACL in the first quarter of Buffalo's regular-season finale but played through it for 33 snaps, ending his season. Harmon called him an aggressive, willing and tenacious run-game blocker who was earning real playing time and had emerged as a genuinely useful rotational piece -- not a star, but a guy who was going to hang onto a roster spot -- which makes the injury 'a real stinker' for his outlook.
