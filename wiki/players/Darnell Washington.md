# Darnell Washington

- 2024-10-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-21 (Feel It or Fk It 10.21.24)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Washington should 'continue to grow as a fantasy value' specifically because of [[Russell Wilson]]. Wilson's ability to change plays and his conceptual savvy let Pittsburgh live in two-tight-end sets, which Waldman calls 'very difficult on defenses — you can put stress on them.' Getting Washington into open space functions 'kind of like an additional running back on some of these rollouts,' helping the run game and organizing the short passing game. The ceiling he set is deliberately modest: Washington 'may be at least a reasonable bye-week PPR option on a more consistent basis' in favorable matchups, not a weekly starter.
