- 2026-03-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-02]]) [Dynasty]: the other SMU tight end and son of former Miami Hurricanes defensive tackle Russell Maryland -- Waldman thinks he might be one of the best pure pass-catchers among tight ends in this class, calling him natural at winning the ball in the air, able to take hard contact and stay smooth, comping his hands to 'Michael Irvin's kid.' At 240 pounds and fast, blocking 'is not his jam' and needs real development. Waldman's framing: if the rest of his game catches up to his hands, he could be a star, but he's 'not counting on that.'
- 2026-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-11]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman ranks SMU's Maryland (son of former NFL DT Russell Maryland) as having the second-best hands in the 2026 tight end class behind [[Justin Joly]], praising how smoothly he catches above his frame, extends for the ball, takes contact and makes acrobatic sideline adjustments -- 'catches the ball like a wide receiver,' with 4.5 speed at 6'4' that has Waldman thinking he might even profile better at outside receiver. His concern is how Maryland bends at the waist running man routes, which Waldman compares to 'a geriatric man before he got a hip replacement' -- if that's a real physical limitation rather than a fixable technique issue, it caps him as a zone/seam receiver, and his blocking is currently too limited for him to hold up as a true tight end. Waldman's comp for his overall risk/reward is [[Chase Claypool]], and he pushes back on draft media hyping Maryland as the class's best tight end purely off pass-catching highlight tape.
