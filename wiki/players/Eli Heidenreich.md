# Eli Heidenreich

## Expert Takes

- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Heidenreich looks like a gadget/running-back type given Navy's offense, but Waldman says he's closer to a real receiver than his usage suggests -- he's already developed release, setup and break technique and mainly needs to extend the precision of his execution beyond the shallow parts of the field he's mostly asked to work.
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman sees Heidenreich as a special-teams/return specialist first, contributing as a fourth or fifth receiver and a utility back who could earn as many touches as a receiver as he does as a runner. He said Heidenreich's ball-carrying skill shows on the perimeter and in open space, and that he's closer to being a good receiver than a between-the-tackles runner given how he was used in college.
- 2026-04-09 — According to Paul Pertichese, guest analyst on the [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-09]] (not a tracked expert) [Dynasty]: Navy's Eli Heidenreich is a name Pertichese flags purely as a stash -- an interesting, dual-threat quickness/agility/stop-start player who profiles as a possible slot or hybrid option rather than a pure running back. He projects him going around round five or early six, and is genuinely uncertain what role an NFL team envisions given Navy's unconventional option-based offense; recommends stashing him late on a dynasty taxi squad as a lottery ticket.
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman agrees Heidenreich has a long way to go to make the Steelers' final roster, arguing he was never really an NFL-style running back at Navy (mostly draw plays and jet sweeps) and profiles better as a developmental wide receiver. He attributes some of the media hype around Heidenreich to a longstanding bias toward players from military academies, comparing it to the old Roger Staubach-era fetishization of that background, and says his realistic path is special teams/return work with a shot at eventually being a depth receiver over a few years -- not an immediate contributor.
