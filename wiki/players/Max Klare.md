# Max Klare

## Expert Takes

- 2026-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-11]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman thinks Ohio State's Klare (a Purdue transfer) was actually showcased better at Purdue, where more of his route running and after-the-catch skill was on display. He has solid hands and growth potential as a route runner, projecting as a one-cut runner with more juice than [[Matthew Hibner]]. If a team commits to 11 personnel, Waldman sees a path to a starting role, but groups him with [[Jack Endries]] as a player who could 'get lost in the NFL's trends' toward condensed, in-line-heavy tight end usage and wind up only a situational contributor instead.
- 2026-04-16 — According to Dalton Wasserman (guest draft analyst, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-16]]) [Dynasty]: unlike fellow converted-quarterback tight ends [[Eli Stowers]] and [[Kenyon Sadiq]], [[Max Klare]] can already block, which Wasserman thinks gets him on the field for every down sooner than the other two. His receiving numbers dipped in his final season at Ohio State after transferring from Purdue, but Wasserman attributes that to scheme rather than a talent decline -- and thinks the added blocking reps make him a legitimate three-down tight end long term, since he still has the athleticism to flex into the slot. He projects Klare going in the top 75 picks with starting-tight-end potential sooner than people expect, comping the archetype (a non-superstar who stays on the field because he can block) to names like [[Hunter Henry]], [[Dallas Goedert]] and a pre-injury [[Tucker Kraft]].
- 2026-04-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman leans 'fuck it' on Klare landing with the Rams as an exception to his general fade of rookie tight ends this year. He likes Klare and thinks he has the best shot in the class of becoming a real move tight end, but flags uncertainty over whether incoming QB Ty Simpson (expected in 2027) will favor the tight end, plus the likelihood the Rams' receiving corps keeps changing around him. Waldman would only value Klare in rookie drafts if he falls well below his real ADP.
