# Josh Allen

## Expert Takes

- 2024-04-04 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-04]]), reflecting on the [[Stefon Diggs]] trade to Houston: Allen "cementing himself as an elite quarterback" coincided directly with Diggs's 2020 arrival in Buffalo, after showing early stabilization signs in 2019 (his first year with John Brown and Cole Beasley). Harmon relays that Allen has told him personally he doesn't care about route depth or play design when throwing to Diggs — "I'm cool with him just improvising and getting open, I will find him" — and frames Allen as a fearless, willing-to-play-off-script quarterback who aggressively locks onto Diggs downfield, a dynamic that elevated both players' reputations simultaneously ("they were perfectly timed for each other"). Notes the two had a publicly emotional relationship (a since-referenced clip of Allen thanking Diggs) that makes the trade "sad" given how untenable the situation had become behind the scenes by the time it happened.


- 2024-04-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-08]]) [Best Ball]: going late second/early third round in early best balls, but "I'm not touching Josh Allen. I'm not going anywhere near him" — would only reconsider if he fell to round five or six, which he doesn't expect to happen. Prefers a receiver, running back, or top tight end at that price instead. Would reconsider a share if Buffalo landed a high-value rookie receiver early and it pushed Allen's price down. Separately floats [[Dalton Kincaid]] as effectively already Buffalo's wide receiver one, and discusses hypothetical Bills draft-fit archetypes for a rookie WR1 ([[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[Keon Coleman]], [[Xavier Legette]], [[Johnny Wilson]], [[Javon Baker]]) to pair with Allen. See [[Stefon Diggs]] for Waldman's read on why Allen and Diggs's relationship soured.

- 2024-04-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]]: in the [[C.J. Stroud]] dynasty-QB1 debate, cites Allen's "consistent level of production" even with [[Stefon Diggs]] already gone for most of last season as a reason he still can't be displaced from the top of dynasty quarterback rankings.
- 2024-04-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-22]]): has a low-level worry about Josh Allen's makeup and commitment level, speculating he could be an early-to-mid-career version of Ben Roethlisberger in terms of wearing on coaches and teammates (not criminal behavior, but decision-making); still expects the on-field production to hold up and would bet on him remaining a stable fantasy option.
- 2024-07-01 — According to Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Redraft]: Bob notes Allen has finished QB1 or QB2 three of the last four years, largely on rushing equity, and his ADP has fallen from the second round in prior years to late third/early fourth this year -- asking whether he's still worth his QB1-overall ceiling with Stefon Diggs traded to Houston. Waldman is not fully bought in as QB1 overall, calling the field 'so close between him and probably four or five other quarterbacks' -- he'd want at least two of Khalil Shakir, Curtis Samuel, Keon Coleman and Dalton Kincaid to play up to potential for Allen to justify that tag, and thinks Diggs will actually play better in Houston away from Allen, calling that 'a little bit of a knock' on him. They cite a counter-argument from Eric at NFL.com that Allen benefits from not force-feeding targets to Diggs or to Gabe Davis on plays he knew would be incomplete. Waldman floats a theory that Diggs wanted a more mentally/conceptually sharp quarterback (contrasted with C.J. Stroud) rather than one who leans on physical talent.
