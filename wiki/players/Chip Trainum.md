- 2026-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-30]]): a former Ohio State linebacker converted to running back who transferred to Kentucky and then Toledo, Chip Trainum has gotten sharper with each stop -- faster processing, better use of pad level for his size -- even as program 'profile' declined and opportunity grew. Waldman rates him close to [[Adam Randall]] in ceiling, slightly behind him overall, but says Trainum could surpass Randall if he 'maximizes what he needs to learn.' Remaining issues are patience and setting up runs, plus some tentative behavior. Bob Harris (untracked co-host), after watching highlights, notes Trainum flashed as a big hitter and athletic special-teams player, which both agree could be his path onto a roster if he doesn't make the RB depth chart -- special teams, practice squad, or a futures deal.
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: A former linebacker turned running back, Trainum has made enough gains between last season and this that Waldman sees room for more -- he's become a more decisive gap runner and is beginning to press creases as a zone runner, even if the depth of those presses isn't NFL-ready yet. Waldman flagged a processing tell: Trainum hops during his gait while assessing creases well before setting up a path, unlike Le'Veon Bell's hop (a deliberate ploy to set up defenders) -- Waldman said this often precedes more tentative behavior from him.
