# Tank Dell

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]]): keeps Dell just outside his top-five 2023 rookie wide receivers, behind [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], [[Jordan Addison]], [[Puka Nacua]] and [[Jayden Reed]] — a strong route runner who "plays bigger than his size" and was excellent when on the field, but undersized, still inconsistent attacking the ball at the catch point, and untested by a heavier workload. Says he could move into Waldman's top three if he "passes that test" once leaned on more.
- 2024-01-04 — **Adam Harstad** (co-host — not a tracked expert), same episode, the competing read: applies a "do the harder thing" heuristic (see [[Role Difficulty and Replaceability]]) to argue Dell's and [[Jordan Addison]]'s production is inflated by a teammate drawing the tougher coverage — in Dell's case, [[Nico Collins]]. His comps: Chris Godwin benefiting from Mike Evans, Torry Holt-era Isaac Bruce, and Anquan Boldin-era Larry Fitzgerald. His conclusion is that Collins is harder to replace than Dell, not that Dell isn't good.
- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Adam Harstad** (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: lands in the [[Post-Rookie-Year Receiver Model]]'s "fantastic rookie season" bucket alongside Mike Evans, Chris Olave, Terry McLaurin, A.J. Green, Keenan Allen and Julio Jones — a historical range with essentially zero outright busts. Harstad calls the result "pretty much bulletproof." Waldman still ranks Dell below several others in that bucket on the same concerns raised previously — size/durability and [[Nico Collins]] doing more of the harder work in Houston's offense (see [[Role Difficulty and Replaceability]]) — but doesn't dispute the historical read.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: grouped with [[Nico Collins]] as quality-but-not-true-No.-1 options for Houston — thinks the team could end up "overpaying" for what the two of them provide relative to adding a genuine top receiver, while still projecting both as startable in 2024.

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-19]]): same pushback as applied to [[Nico Collins]] — Houston's reported interest in free agent [[Keenan Allen]] isn't read as a stock-down signal for Dell, since Allen's slot-specific role wouldn't have overlapped much with Dell's; expects Houston could still add a receiver on Day 2 of the draft without it meaningfully changing Dell's outlook.

- 2024-04-04 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-04]]), reacting to Houston trading for [[Stefon Diggs]]: flags real durability risk before assuming Dell is a lock for target volume — he's "170 pounds" and coming off a serious injury, having absorbed several massive hits in his rookie year; Harmon says "injury optimism generally is the worst bet you can make" and specifically distrusts offseason Instagram workout-video reassurances. Argues Houston likely wouldn't have made the Diggs trade at all if the team were fully confident Dell would return as a set-it-and-forget-it top receiver. Still calls all three of Dell/[[Nico Collins]]/Diggs great players and says target distribution among them "could go any which way" in 2024.


- 2024-04-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-08]]) [Best Ball]: with Dell going late third/early fourth round in early best balls, calls him the best value and cheapest of the Houston WR trio (with [[Nico Collins]] and [[Stefon Diggs]]) at that price — the "Bob Harris method" pick of the three.
- 2024-05-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-21]]): charting Dell's rookie season (11 games, 47/75 targets, 709 yards, 7 TD, 62.7% catch rate, 15.1 YPR; 75.8% of sampled snaps outside, only 19% in the slot) shows a player who is 'awesome' despite being sub-180 lbs at the combine. He posted a 68.2% success rate vs. press (above average, echoing an 83rd-percentile college mark on limited 8.3% press volume) and 72.8% vs. man (74th percentile), but a below-expectation 77.6% vs. zone (40th percentile) and 71% on the slant route -- surprising given his size/twitchiness. He was elite on outbreaking routes as a rookie: corner 81.3%, out 80.6%, comeback 80%, flat 81.8%, and an 84.4% success rate on dig routes that Harmon puts in the conversation with Tyreek Hill, Tyler Lockett, and DK Metcalf. He also caught 77.8% of contested targets (25.7% of his targets were contested), which Harmon compares to Steve Smith Sr.-level catch-point ability. Harmon calls Dell the rookie-year receiver he thinks most resembles Tyreek Hill, though Hill was significantly better vs. press as a rookie and ran more gadget/close-to-line touches, while Dell was a true downfield route winner. C.J. Stroud reportedly told Texans brass to draft Dell specifically and remains vocally high on him.
- 2024-06-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman projects Dell for the most targets of Houston's three-man WR group (120), but with the lowest catch percentage because he draws the deepest targets on the offense — still clearing 60% catch rate, which he called good, and roughly 7 touchdowns. He argued Dell is 'very capable of equaling or exceeding' both [[Nico Collins]] and [[Stefon Diggs]] production if he stays fully healthy for the whole season, given his big-play, after-the-catch skill set, though his health has been the recurring question mark.
- 2024-07-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-11]]) [Best Ball]: Dell posted a 72% success rate vs. man as a rookie while running out, dig, post and corner routes -- his four highest-usage route types relative to NFL average -- which Harmon calls 'big boy receiver stuff' for a rookie working primarily on the outside. He views Dell as a great player in his own right within a Houston room he considers top-two-or-three in the NFL, though the group's ceiling depends partly on Dell returning healthy and on form after his injury.
- 2024-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-23]]): Harmon flagged the camp-highlight caveat first — invoking the late Chris Wesseling's point that padless OTAs and minicamps are exactly when small, speedy receivers light it up (the 'Parris Campbell honorary award') — and noted the two receivers filling his feed with clips were Dell and [[Xavier Worthy]], both sub-180 pounds. He is explicit that he is not suggesting either is a Campbell-style mirage: Dell was fantastic last year, and seeing him running full speed after his leg injury and the offseason shooting is a genuinely good takeaway. Harmon separately ranked Dell 12th on his Yahoo top-15 receivers 25 and under, ahead of [[Tee Higgins]] at 13 — a placement James Koh (not tracked) pushed back on, calling himself a big-time sizeist and asking how much of Dell's separation is a function of the attention [[Nico Collins]] draws.
- 2024-09-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-05]]): Fantasy Points' new average separation score rates Dell the number one receiver in the league, and Harmon's guess at why is the out-breaking route work he has highlighted before — high-difficulty comebacks, outs and corners were his college strength and he posted 80% or better success rates on all of those out-breaking routes in year one, which he suspects is doing the heavy lifting in that metric. He stresses Dell is not a tiny slot receiver despite his size. Projecting the Houston deployment, Harmon expects Dell as the off-ball, pre-snap-motion player — Houston did a good bit to get him away from press coverage last year — working front-side combinations with [[Nico Collins]] while [[Stefon Diggs]] handles backside ISO work.
- 2024-09-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-12]]): Dell was not a part-time player in Week 1 — he ran 80% of the routes with a 16.3 average depth of target, which Harmon treats as confirmation that Houston is an 11-personnel team with three full-time receivers (they telegraphed it by releasing fullback Andrew Beck). His role is the vertical and outside-breaking route runner, screaming down the field from the slot, complementary to [[Nico Collins]] on go routes and deep overs and [[Stefon Diggs]] underneath. The only Week 1 surprise for Harmon was how much Houston ran the ball, which he found impressive but did not treat as a threat to the receivers: 'wheels up for all three of these guys.'
- 2024-09-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-19]]): buying Dell long term with no concerns, but urging patience after one catch for minus three yards in Week 2. Harmon notes Dell dropped a would-be long touchdown from [[C.J. Stroud]] that would have changed the conversation, and stresses two things the market forgets: he is coming back from a serious rookie-year injury, comparable to the [[Tony Pollard]] situation where Pollard told Harmon at the Super Bowl that he did not feel like himself until Week 11 — and separately that Dell was shot in the offseason. Harmon's framing is that players are human beings, not numbers on a spreadsheet or video game sprites. He also never expected Dell to be the best receiver on the roster because [[Nico Collins]] is that far ahead as a top-10 NFL alpha, with [[Stefon Diggs]] also being worked in. He would not be shocked if Dell piles up numbers late in the year.
- 2024-09-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-26]]): a downgrade on last week's provisional read. Harmon charted Dell's routes in Weeks 2 and 3 and says he has not looked as good as in his rookie season — Week 2 in particular 'didn't look like the same explosive player,' with Week 3 more explosive but still short of the separation he generated as a rookie, especially on deeper routes against zone. Harmon attributes it to Dell coming off the leg injury and the offseason shooting. Compounding it is role: Dell's rookie-year specialty was outside-breaking routes against man coverage, and those are the throws Houston is calling least now that [[Stefon Diggs]] is the easy underneath layup and [[Nico Collins]] is the alpha over the middle.
- 2024-10-10 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-10]]): Dell's charting through the first few weeks — up on the Reception Perception site — shows he has not played to the level he did last season, which Harmon attributes to the injury and the shooting and everything around it. That matters now because Houston saw Dell kick to X when [[Nico Collins]] missed time in 2023 and it was not the best use of him given his size, and Harmon is unsure they can expect him to be the vertical replacement this time either. He is still 'a very good player' with a good rookie RP profile who 'can get right at any moment' — Harmon bet Houston is hoping that moment is Week 6 — but the ramp-up timing is genuinely unknown, and that uncertainty is a big part of why Harmon thinks Houston's downfield dimension is simply missing.
- 2024-10-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-15]]): Harmon watched the Week 6 film after saying publicly that Dell is not playing to last year's ability, and stands by it despite Dell's early touchdown and the pushback he got — Dell is averaging under 10 yards per catch and is 'not even close' to the same explosive player yet. He frames it as the standard post-injury pattern: people want a returning player to immediately be who he was, but it can take a full year, exactly as it did with [[Chris Godwin]] after his ACL. Dell can still produce for a fantasy team but is no longer the vertical element Houston lost with [[Nico Collins]] out, and Harmon now ranks [[Stefon Diggs]] ahead of him. As a rookie, Dell's Reception Perception profile made him one of the best outbreaking route runners in the league — a bar Harmon uses to measure others against.
- 2024-10-31 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-31]]): Dell 'has not looked like himself' all year and has not been as healthy, though Harmon allowed he may be rounding into form and praised his touchdown catch the previous week as picture-perfect technique. The key evidence is deployment: Dell's average depth of target over the last three weeks is 8.76 yards, which Harmon reads as Houston not yet counting on him for the deep outs and corner routes he thrived on in 2023 — they do not believe he can sting teams deep right now, so he is not being used as a high-volume player. With Stefon Diggs lost for the season to a knee injury, Harmon expects Dell to move to X with [[Xavier Hutchinson]] as flanker. He called it a shame that the projected Collins-Diggs-Dell trio never played together, and both hosts lamented that the room billed as the NFL's best entering 2024 is down to 'Tank Dell and basically no one' until [[Nico Collins]] returns.
- 2024-11-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-21]]): Harmon calls Dell really disappointing on film and attributes it mostly to injury rather than technique — he simply does not look like the same player physically. Early-season separation data from Reception Perception charting was not good; recent games represent improvement and he is getting open again, but the explosive separation, the ability to throttle down and create miles of space on out routes, corner routes and comebacks that defined his rookie profile is not on this year's film. Average air yards per target has fallen to 10.19 from 14.3 last year, and he has already dropped five passes after dropping four all of last season in a similar workload. Harmon thinks he can get it back but says he would be guessing. Ranked third-most disappointing. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) adds that Dell was shot in the offseason, is on pace for 762 yards, 66 receptions and a 62% catch rate, and stresses the lost explosiveness after the catch has hurt the Houston offense alongside a disappointing [[C.J. Stroud]] season.
- 2024-12-24 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-12-24]]): Dell suffered a dislocated kneecap with a possible torn ACL and additional ligament damage against Kansas City — his second major leg injury in two years after breaking a leg bone as a rookie — and it came in what was probably his best game of 2024. Harmon says it is fair to wonder when, and even whether, we see the rookie-year version of Dell again. He called that rookie film fantastic: at or above an 80% Reception Perception success rate on all four out-breaking routes (flat, comeback, out, corner), 87% on curls and 84.4% on digs, which almost no rookie and few high-tier veterans post. If Harmon assigned bonus points for separation created, Dell would have ranked near the top. He had already been raising alarm bells earlier in 2024 that the in-season charting was nowhere close to the rookie year, and calls most of 2024 a lost year for Dell as an individual player. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) notes Dell wins with quickness, deception and technique at 165 pounds, not physicality, which makes the injury history scarier.
- 2025-01-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-01-21]]): you are not counting on Dell next year — he has to be looked at as 'gravy', so anything you get from him is great, but Houston needs the 'mashed potatoes on the plate' first and must add real pass-catching talent regardless of him.
- 2025-02-04 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-04]]): the outlook is bleak. Harmon says the in-season Reception Perception charting data on Dell in 2024 was 'not kind', especially early, that it came together somewhat late, but that we never saw the rookie-year player again — and 'we might never see that guy again' after back-to-back devastating lower-body injuries. He would not be surprised if Dell does not play at all in 2025, and says if Houston gets anything from him it should be treated as gravy rather than planned around. Koh (not tracked) agrees, expecting a PUP start and asking what version even returns. Harmon separately defends the Texans against criticism for the rookie-year play he was hurt on: a starting receiver has to block, and he simply got rolled up on.
- 2025-03-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-07]]): Dell 'went from an outlier to a cautionary tale very quickly'. Koh details the injury — torn ACL, MCL, LCL and meniscus requiring multiple surgeries, the MCL/LCL/meniscus repaired first and the ACL not until March 5 — and says he is expected to miss all of 2025 and will not be part of the Texans offense. Harmon stresses that in his rookie season Dell was the best out-breaking route runner in the NFL, over 80% success rate on the corner, out, comeback and flat, creating remarkable space at 165 pounds; he was hurt on a routine blocking play at the goal line, not a coaching error. Harmon's point is that the 'this is why you don't draft small guys' verdict arrives fast and unfairly, but concedes Dell may end up a what-if story. He also notes Dell was [[C.J. Stroud]]'s best friend on the roster, which drove target share.
- 2025-03-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-10]]): co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) said Dell is a year away — the ACL repair was the last of multiple injuries, since teams wait for the other injuries to heal before starting ACL rehab. Waldman, comparing him to new arrival [[Christian Kirk]], acknowledged Dell is far more explosive but said Kirk is the all-around upgrade because he can usually play an entire season, and wished Dell well in his recovery.
- 2026-01-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-20]] [Dynasty]: Tank Dell's absence has been deeply missed -- his out-breaking-route pairing with Nico Collins' in-breakers during Stroud's rookie year was one of the league's most effective combinations, and Houston hasn't replicated anything close to it since (now three years ago). Harmon is uncertain what Dell can still contribute coming back from injury, floating that he 'might just be too small to be a full-time player,' and Houston's current in-house answer, Xavier Hutchinson as a nominal number-two receiver, is a clear downgrade from what Dell provided.
- 2026-05-11 — According to Matt Waldman ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-11]]) [Dynasty]: asked whether Tank Dell is worth a late-round pick, Waldman said fuck it outside of a super-deep taxi-squad rebuild league -- between the knee dislocation and the ACL tear, he thinks Dell may never get his quick-twitch change-of-direction burst back, the exact trait [[Brandon Angelo]] has repeatedly flagged as the foundation of Dell's game, calling that 'ship sailed.' Waldman believes [[Jaylin Noel]] is now Dell's long-term positional replacement in Houston and doesn't see Dell reclaiming the role even with a full recovery. Waldman noted Houston's own draft behavior last year signaled the team already planned around not needing Dell back.
- 2026-05-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-05-14]]): Harmon called Dell's rookie season one of the best out-breaking-route seasons by any NFL receiver that year, rookie or otherwise (elite corner/out/comeback/flat success rates), but said his second season 'was not the same' before it ended in a serious injury. Dell is expected to try to return in 2026; Harmon said he hopes he does but won't dismiss concerns given the major injury history and smaller frame, and thinks [[Jaylin Noel]] can replicate some of what made Dell so effective as an out-breaking weapon for [[C.J. Stroud]].
- 2026-06-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman said the Texans are managing Tank Dell's workload carefully after he sat out all of last season with a devastating multi-ligament knee injury (ACL, MCL, LCL and a dislocated kneecap); he's made significant progress -- running and cutting -- but isn't yet at full speed, with the hope of ramping up in training camp. Asked if Dell is worth a last-round dynasty/redraft flyer, both hosts agreed -- 'just about anybody's worth a last-round flyer' -- but Waldman said he's more likely to spend those chances elsewhere given how serious the injury is, noting Dell would need to look like pre-injury [[Malik Nabers]] given Houston's crowded receiving talent for the pick to hit.
- 2026-07-10 — According to guest Dave Kluge (footballguys.com, untracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-10]]): Kluge says he isn't factoring Tank Dell into his projections at all given the severity of his knee injury -- Dell was still not 100% and mostly watching from the sidelines during OTAs. He notes teammates consider Dell the alpha/leader of the Texans' WR room, which could cause beat reporters to over-hype any camp progress, but doesn't think that hype would be enough to get Dell fantasy-drafted or push [[Jayden Higgins]] down draft boards -- only real on-field health evidence in preseason would change his evaluation.
