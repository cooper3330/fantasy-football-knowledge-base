# Rico Dowdle

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: on whether drafting a Cowboys back is a waste of time, Waldman said Dowdle's cost has been low enough that you can give him a shot. Dowdle is 'the guy that they want things to work out with,' and Waldman believes he has the talent to surprise. The caveat is that both Dowdle and [[Ezekiel Elliott]] are expendable — if Dowdle does not work out, Dallas can fall back on an Elliott-plus-[[Dalvin Cook]] combination. Waldman was more confident about the Dallas passing game than the backfield.
- 2024-09-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-23]]): Dowdle still looks pretty good to Waldman on tape, but the situation caps him — the Dallas offensive line does not look strong and the defense has real questions. Bob Harris (untracked) cited JJ Zachariason's data showing Dowdle's rush share climbing from 42 percent in Week 1 to 62 percent in Week 2, with 22 more snaps than [[Ezekiel Elliott]] in Week 3, possibly game-script related. Waldman's verdict on Dowdle being the better play: 'he's the better play of bad plays,' and he still expects Elliott to take the goal-line work either way. Separately, both agreed the Eagles and Cowboys defenses are weekly matchups to target with a strong ground game — both are playing nickel base and five-man fronts and still cannot stop the run.
- 2024-10-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-10 (Regression Candidates and Exuberance-Pessimism)]]): Dowdle is one of the few backs he expects to grow rather than regress. He is averaging 4.1 yards per carry and ranks 27th in PPR, and Waldman thinks the market is down on him mainly because the profile is unexciting — but he is emerging as the back who will take the most touches in that backfield and has become the more reliable option in the short term. Waldman would take a cheap bet on him and lean on him going forward. He explicitly prefers Dowdle to [[Tank Bigsby]], who ranks 37th but is the hotter name, because Bigsby's eight yards per carry is fool's gold built on a handful of long runs while Dowdle's production reflects a stabilizing role.
- 2024-12-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-16]]): after becoming the first undrafted player with three straight 100-plus-yard rushing games since Arian Foster in 2014, Dowdle has a chance to open 2025 as Dallas's starter — he will probably head into camp as the starter — but the incoming running back class is rich and he will get competition. Waldman expects fantasy analysts to spend the offseason touting whichever back Dallas drafts in rounds one through four, and frames the open question as whether it becomes a [[Kyren Williams]]/[[Blake Corum]]-style situation where the incumbent holds, or a [[Travis Etienne Jr.]]/[[Tank Bigsby]]-style committee. He has long compared Dowdle to Arian Foster and preached patience — 'I didn't think we'd have to be this patient' — and says Dowdle should be one of Dallas's top two backs next year if he stays on the roster. Bob Harris (not tracked) noted Dallas kept him through the injuries, which says they knew what they had.
