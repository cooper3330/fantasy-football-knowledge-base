# Coleman Bennett

- 2026-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-30]]): Coleman Bennett, a Kennesaw State (via Bucknell) running back, has already visited Seattle, which Waldman calls a good sign given the Seahawks' backfield need after trading [[Kenneth Walker III]] to Kansas City. Waldman likes Bennett's special-teams value, an emerging receiving skill set, and developing patience/between-the-tackles work, and thinks Seattle -- comfortable with [[Zach Charbonnet]] and still evaluating [[George Holani]] -- could carve out an RB3 role for him built on special teams. Waldman frames him as a classic multi-year RSP developmental follow who might not surface as a real contributor for two to three years.
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman called Bennett a decisive, tight-crease runner when he reads the defense correctly, but said his decision-making 'can go to extremes' with worse outcomes at the NFL level when the crease is clogged. His saving grace is accounting for game script and field position, which Waldman said makes him a promising red/black-zone game manager and an intriguing UDFA option.
