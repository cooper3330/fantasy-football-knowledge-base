## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-05]] [Redraft (Standard)]: both hosts unreservedly "feel it" on a 2024 rebound now that Atlanta has hired Zach Robinson (ex-Rams) as offensive coordinator under new HC Raheem Morris — "totally feel it... Pitts is too good," "good talent wins out." Waldman's diagnosis of the down 2023 season is scheme/QB-driven rather than a talent decline: he traces Atlanta's passing-game struggles to Desmond Ridder being unable to read the field dynamically between zones, which forced the offense into simpler, static routes that "break back to the quarterback and sit there" rather than working combinations across the field — a constraint on the whole passing game, not specific to Pitts. See [[Scheme vs Talent]] for the full Ridder/Arthur-Smith-era Atlanta case.
