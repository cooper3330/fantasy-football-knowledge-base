# Kyle Pitts

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-05]] [Redraft (Standard)]: both hosts unreservedly "feel it" on a 2024 rebound now that Atlanta has hired Zach Robinson (ex-Rams) as offensive coordinator under new HC Raheem Morris — "totally feel it... Pitts is too good," "good talent wins out." Waldman's diagnosis of the down 2023 season is scheme/QB-driven rather than a talent decline: he traces Atlanta's passing-game struggles to Desmond Ridder being unable to read the field dynamically between zones, which forced the offense into simpler, static routes that "break back to the quarterback and sit there" rather than working combinations across the field — a constraint on the whole passing game, not specific to Pitts. See [[Scheme vs Talent]] for the full Ridder/Arthur-Smith-era Atlanta case.

- 2024-02-29 — According to **Kevin Knight** (Falcons reporter, SB Nation's Falcoholic — guest, not a tracked expert) on *Reception Perception: The Show*, with [[Matt Harmon]] absent ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-29]]): says Pitts "was never really healthy last year" and could take real pressure off a thin receiver room in 2024 if he's finally healthy and paired with a quarterback able to get him the ball.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: flags his own *Fantasy Notebook* (Footballguys) research on [[Kirk Cousins]] — over 27% of Cousins's career targets have gone to the tight end, and he's "carried some tight ends to great heights" — as the case for a real Pitts rebound now that the signing is official; still "feeling" the move as a plus for Pitts.

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]]: restates that Pitts's rookie season ranked 4th all-time among rookie tight ends in PPR scoring, despite Atlanta trading Julio Jones and Calvin Ridley taking a leave of absence during that season — the lack of surrounding talent limited Pitts to one touchdown but didn't stop him from being the first rookie tight end since Mike Ditka to gain 1,000 yards.
- 2024-05-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-09]]) [Dynasty]: In discussing Atlanta's receiver-need decisions, Harmon floated provocatively that 'who cares about Kyle Pitts at this point' if [[Drake London]] and a hypothetical second true X receiver were getting 130 targets each -- arguing the offense could realistically be built around London first with Bijan Robinson mop-up work, leaving Pitts a lower priority. Co-host James Koh (untracked) strongly disagreed and defended Pitts's role, and Harmon ultimately softened the framing rather than press it, joking he didn't want to invite 'tight end truthers' into his mentions. The exchange reflects real uncertainty about where Pitts ranks in Atlanta's post-draft pecking order behind London -- an unresolved disagreement between the two hosts, not a settled take.
