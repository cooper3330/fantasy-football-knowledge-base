# Oscar Delt

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-03-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-02]]) [Dynasty]: was invited to the combine but a physical revealed a hairline foot fracture, so he did not participate in drills. Waldman was impressed on tape and thinks he'll have draft-day value -- good route runner, real skill after the catch, a smooth athlete -- but the flaw that caps his grade is ball security on contested catches: on multiple significant hits he saw on tape, Delt could not hold on. At roughly 6'5/245-255, Waldman called that more a mindset/focus/physicality issue than a size issue, said he's physical enough to be an NFL contributor and maybe even a starter, but wasn't confident he'll actually elevate his game to match his ceiling.
- 2026-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-11]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman (pronouncing it 'Delp') flags Georgia's Delt as one of the more intriguing under-the-radar tight ends in the 2026 class -- a smooth route runner with good eye discipline who attacks seams and defenders all the way up the stem, wins in the red zone and other compressed timing windows, and would suit savvy quarterbacks who throw into tight windows more than slower-processing quarterbacks who need wide-open targets. At about 245 pounds he's already a good blocker for his size and could sneak into a decent-starter ceiling with another 10-15 pounds if he keeps his current explosion, though Waldman isn't sure added muscle alone gets him to elite blocker. The catch is a small sample against heavy contact -- he backed up [[Brock Bowers]] and Ben Urosek (untracked mention) at Georgia -- so Waldman hasn't seen enough contested-catch-versus-physical-coverage reps to know if that holds up, and says if it does he could be 'very underrated' relative to his current buzz.
- 2026-04-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-13]]) [Dynasty]: most outlets have Delt outside the top-10 tight ends in the class and Waldman has him similarly outside the top tier, but says the gap between that ranking and being a top-three tight end is thin. The swing factor is his hands -- he catches fine in space but struggles winning contested catches through a substantial hit; if he shows more toughness at the catch point than Waldman has seen, he'd vault inside the top five. At 6'5', 245 lbs he isn't heavy enough to be a highly effective in-line blocker yet, but Waldman thinks adding roughly 10 pounds makes that more feasible -- he's already a technically sound, smart blocker who runs good routes and catches reasonably well at his current level. Waldman estimates eight to ten prospects in this tight end class are similarly bunched on talent, separated mainly by which situation they land in.
