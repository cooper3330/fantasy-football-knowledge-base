# DJ Giddens

## Expert Takes

- 2025-08-21 — According to Jake Ciely of The Athletic (guest, NOT a tracked expert) on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-21]] [Redraft (Standard)]: Giddens was his No. 4 super-deep sleeper — the back stepping in right behind [[Jonathan Taylor]], who has been hurt in each of the last three seasons, with [[Tyler Goodson]] now banged up and clearing the path further. He comped Giddens to [[David Montgomery]] with a bit more passing-game upside given the one-cut style, has him RB55, a full tier behind the [[Tyler Allgeier]] / [[Braelon Allen]] / [[Jordan Mason]] group, and would take him in the 14th or 15th round as a final pick given top-20 upside if Taylor misses time — arguing you should take him over drafting Kenneth Gainwell. [[Chris Harris]] thought the 14th round was a stretch but agreed he is a genuine handcuff for Taylor drafters in a standard league, unlike [[Tahj Brooks]]. Harris added he was a no-star recruit who walked into Kansas State, that the scouting knock was hesitation and pitter-patter footwork that will not work in the NFL, and offered Joseph Addai as a size/speed comp both agreed would be a lofty outcome.
