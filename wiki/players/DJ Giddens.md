# DJ Giddens

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-08-21 — According to Jake Ciely of The Athletic (guest, NOT a tracked expert) on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-21]] [Redraft (Standard)]: Giddens was his No. 4 super-deep sleeper — the back stepping in right behind [[Jonathan Taylor]], who has been hurt in each of the last three seasons, with [[Tyler Goodson]] now banged up and clearing the path further. He comped Giddens to [[David Montgomery]] with a bit more passing-game upside given the one-cut style, has him RB55, a full tier behind the [[Tyler Allgeier]] / [[Braelon Allen]] / [[Jordan Mason]] group, and would take him in the 14th or 15th round as a final pick given top-20 upside if Taylor misses time — arguing you should take him over drafting Kenneth Gainwell. [[Chris Harris]] thought the 14th round was a stretch but agreed he is a genuine handcuff for Taylor drafters in a standard league, unlike [[Tahj Brooks]]. Harris added he was a no-star recruit who walked into Kansas State, that the scouting knock was hesitation and pitter-patter footwork that will not work in the NFL, and offered Joseph Addai as a size/speed comp both agreed would be a lofty outcome.
- 2026-01-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-26]]) [Dynasty]: give him one more year, but with a quick hook if needed. Waldman says he actually had Tyler Goodson ranked ahead of Giddens entering the rankings cycle, despite Indianapolis trying to 'ramrod' Giddens into the number-two role, because Giddens flashes excellent ability but his technique and execution consistency have 'always been all over the place' -- on any given snap something is missing, and it's a different something each time. Waldman reads that as a guy who hasn't yet developed the work ethic and attention to detail to be consistent at the level required, even though the underlying skills are there.
- 2026-07-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-13]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman says [[Seth McGowan]] looks like the Colts' RB2 over Giddens, arguing scouting often overrates a runner's physical talent while missing conceptual and diagnostic deficiencies. He says Giddens shows all the physical traits but lacked refined technique and footwork on tape, with 'always something lacking' on a given play that projects to repeated mental mistakes at the NFL level, and hasn't looked savvy or experienced enough as a runner. Bob Harris (untracked co-host) added that per Stephen Holder (ESPN), Giddens has been 'underwhelming' in camp and hasn't developed as quickly as hoped, which Waldman said corroborates his read from tape.
- 2026-08-10 — According to Bob Harris and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-10]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman prefers UDFA [[Ulysses Bentley]] over Giddens for the Colts' RB3 job, saying Giddens has the athletic tools but something is consistently missing on a play-to-play basis -- a lapse in finish or execution -- while Bentley wins with catching, blocking, tackle-breaking and creativity. Local beat reporting (James Boyd, The Athletic) separately describes Giddens as 'unimpressive' in camp so far.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]): Waldman feels for Giddens but says last year's tape showed a talented player where 'every play, there's something that needs to be fixed' -- he never ties his game together even though he should be able to, which is why Waldman isn't shocked that [[Seth McGowan]] has a real shot at overtaking him for the backup job in Indianapolis.
