# Noah Whittington

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-08-15 — According to guest Dan Hatman of the Scouting Academy — a former Giants and Eagles scout who is NOT a tracked expert — speaking with [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-15]]) [Dynasty]: Whittington is a name he wanted to raise from the 2026 class, a transfer who has dealt with injuries and been stuck behind a couple of really good players at Oregon. Hatman has been interested in him since watching [[Bucky Irving]] a couple of years back, and says Whittington has flashed in different scenarios to the point where he wanted to see the kid get real volume before another injury intervened. He notes the Tulane transfer at Oregon is the back everyone else has their eye on, but he is more interested in watching Whittington develop this year. Framing his broader preference: Hatman has not yet found a scheme-agnostic, plug-and-play back in this class, and his flavour is multi-cut backs with problem-solving ability in the body plus patience and reads, a blend that usually comes in a smaller profile.
