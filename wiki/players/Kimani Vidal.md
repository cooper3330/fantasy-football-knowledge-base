## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: named as a mid-to-late-pick who could become a viable committee contributor if he survives the perception attached to his likely Day 3 draft capital, avoids mistakes in camp, and earns real opportunity. Profile: 5'7", ~215 lbs, a powerful runner with a low center of gravity and real movement skill, plus decent decision-making on tape. A standout in Senior Bowl pass-protection reps specifically — both hosts flag it as a real "earned his stripes" moment. [[Matt Waldman]], same episode: agrees the low center of gravity and movement skills are genuine translatable traits, and floats a fourth-round outcome as plausible given how he could be viewed once teams see the tape.

- 2024-03-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-04]]) [Dynasty]: reaffirms he has Vidal "as a top 10 running back" in the 2024 class, while noting he was still surprised by Vidal's 4.46 combine 40 time given that ranking.
