- 2024-11-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception - 2024-11-14]]): Dissly has quietly become a real part of the Chargers passing game, ranking third on the team in first-read share at 16.8% behind [[Ladd McConkey]] at 25.8% and [[Quentin Johnston]] at 22.6% — and tied with Johnston on raw first-read looks despite playing fewer games. Since the week-five bye, Fantasy Points data has Dissly and McConkey tied for the team lead in targets, and Dissly leads the team in designed targets. Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) treat this as a Greg Roman play-calling complaint rather than praise: Koh objects to a game coming out of the bye with five designed targets to Dissly and zero to McConkey. Harmon's counterpoint is that designed targets skew toward screens, and the Chargers are still designing shot plays for McConkey.
