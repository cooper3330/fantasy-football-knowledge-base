# James Connor

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-01 — Both hosts on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-01]] [Redraft (Standard)]: hanging on to Connor as a 2024 fantasy starter rather than writing him off, citing his play over the final month of 2023 as proof he "can still play football" — against a backdrop of Steelers/Cardinals-fan skepticism that Waldman says had already declared him done "multiple years ago." Both hope his ADP stays cheap given that skepticism.
- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: contextualizes the "toast" skepticism as a second-contract finance story rather than a talent decline — Pittsburgh's run game, arm-weary quarterback play, and roster needs elsewhere made paying Connor a bad allocation, "but that doesn't mean he was bad." Three years into Arizona he's been "strong for most of those three years with some injuries, but nothing devastating," and Waldman expects [[Michael Carter]] to cut into — but not replace — his 2024 workload.
