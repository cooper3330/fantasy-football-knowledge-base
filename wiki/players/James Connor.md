# James Connor

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-01 — Both hosts on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-01]] [Redraft (Standard)]: hanging on to Connor as a 2024 fantasy starter rather than writing him off, citing his play over the final month of 2023 as proof he "can still play football" — against a backdrop of Steelers/Cardinals-fan skepticism that Waldman says had already declared him done "multiple years ago." Both hope his ADP stays cheap given that skepticism.
- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: contextualizes the "toast" skepticism as a second-contract finance story rather than a talent decline — Pittsburgh's run game, arm-weary quarterback play, and roster needs elsewhere made paying Connor a bad allocation, "but that doesn't mean he was bad." Three years into Arizona he's been "strong for most of those three years with some injuries, but nothing devastating," and Waldman expects [[Michael Carter]] to cut into — but not replace — his 2024 workload.
- 2024-05-17 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-17]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo notes Connor is turning 30 and is a pending free agent after 2024, expecting Arizona to move on barring a cheap deal and hand the reins to [[Trey Benson]], with Connor's main value this year being as a mentor Benson can learn the pro game behind.
- 2024-05-20 — According to guest Jagger May (Football Guys, untracked) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-20]]) [Dynasty]: he cautioned against letting James Connor's name drive dynasty roster decisions — Connor "was good a long time" but is now old and was never durable even in his younger years, so his 2024 role is really to hold the seat and mentor rookie [[Trey Benson]] for a short runway rather than to be a building block himself.
- 2026-01-15 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Ben Gretsch (untracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-01-15]]): Connor turns 31 in May with a $6.3M base and no guaranteed money left -- cutting him saves the Cardinals big money against only a $2M cap hit. Gretsch notes Connor posted his career-best missed-tackles-forced and yards-after-contact numbers in 2023, topped them again in 2024 with a career high in total yards, but played only three games in 2025 before another injury (this time a broken foot rather than a major ligament tear). Both think Trey Benson is presumed to be next in line, but note Benson has his own health/workhorse-usage questions dating back to college -- which Gretsch argues actually makes it MORE likely Arizona moves on from Connor, since a new coaching staff won't want to enter 2026 relying on two backs who can't stay on the field (as happened in 2025 with Michael Carter and Bam Knight forced into lead roles).
- 2026-05-28 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-28 (Going Deep)]]) [Dynasty]: if [[Josh Jacobs]] misses significant time, Angelo said Connor is 'the perfect fit' as a veteran presence to bring in -- over 30 but still a good player who keeps the offense on schedule, freeing Green Bay to lean on [[Christopher Brooks]] or a hot [[Marshawn Lloyd]] for explosiveness while [[Jordan Love]] and the passing game carry more of the load.
