- 2024-09-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman said Elliott is worth a shot as long as you drafted him low enough, and that he is better than people realize 'in certain ways — but maybe not for fantasy.'
