## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast: finds cutbacks well when a play design breaks down — works inside-out to set up alleys and pullers when early penetration foils the front-side design. But trips over his own blockers by getting too tight too soon on gap plays, or veers too wide and runs into a teammate's feet in the next gap over. Contrasted unfavorably with [[Dylan McDuffie]], who Waldman says shows more control over maximizing space; Bailey needs to develop better control, timing, and overall vision.
