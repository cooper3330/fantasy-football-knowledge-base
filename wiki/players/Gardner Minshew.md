# Gardner Minshew

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-21 — [[Brandon Angelo]] (co-host of *Going Deep*) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21 (Pacing and Control)]]: offers Minshew as the model of the earned-stripes backup, in contrast to [[Drew Lock]]. "Gardner Minshew is a freaking rock star in my life — he can back up my quarterback any day of the week, because he's someone I know can go in there, execute schematically, not make mistakes, just give me a chance to win." His stated league context: "there's a lot of bad quarterbacks right now in the NFL," so a quarterback who simply doesn't lose you the game has real value.
- 2023-12-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], same episode: the self-knowledge point — Minshew "knows exactly who he is. He has no illusions about being a starter" and probably never did, but committed to doing everything he could with what he had. Waldman notes he entered football expecting to become a coach, and (approvingly, as a signal of how he treats the career's fragility) that he still keeps the mattress he found in a campus dumpster: "he knows that waterfall may stop on any play."

**Read:** this is a character/role evaluation, not a fantasy start recommendation
— neither host makes a starting-lineup call on Minshew.
