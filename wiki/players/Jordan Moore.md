# Jordan Moore

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): asked whether the Duke receiver is a [[Khalil Shakir]] clone, Waldman said yes with a twist — Moore is what people wrongly thought Shakir was. Shakir, he argues, is a rare case of a player cast in an overly negative light, labelled as unable to catch the football when in fact he catches it very well. Moore genuinely fits that mistaken description: a very talented player after the catch who runs decent routes and helps in the return game, but whose hands need work and who has to secure the ball more efficiently to stop the careless drops. If he fixes that, Waldman sees a slot receiver who could emerge over a few years and earn an active game-day role.
