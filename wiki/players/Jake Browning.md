# Jake Browning

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: **feeling it** for the next two weeks (Pittsburgh, then Kansas City) — a low-end QB1 you play on matchup, even though he was outproducing that. What Browning does well on film: throws the ball well in the middle of the field, strong on timing routes, will stand in against the blitz. What he can't do: the Joe Burrow off-platform game — rolling either direction and dropping a ball 25–40 yards downfield on the move — which is why [[Tee Higgins]] becomes boom/bust with him.
- 2023-12-18 — **Matt Waldman**, same episode, on the matchups: Pittsburgh is the tougher of the two because Minkah Fitzpatrick was likely to return, and Waldman didn't expect a repeat of Browning's first outing against them. Kansas City had been softer than its reputation. Pushes back on the pure-schedule explanation for Browning's run: yes, Jacksonville "made Jake Browning look like the best quarterback of all time," but he also faced mid-tier defenses in Indianapolis and Minnesota. The surrounding talent — [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Tee Higgins]], Tyler Boyd, Joe Mixon — is a real part of the case.
- 2023-12-18 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert), same episode: agrees, "I'm feeling Jake Browning for the next two weeks." Says he'd stayed skeptical despite 50 fantasy points over the prior two games, but was convinced by Browning looking unfazed against Brian Flores's blitz-heavy Minnesota defense. Caveat: don't expect a top-5 week every week.
- 2023-12-18 — Framing from the same episode: Browning is both hosts' evidence that "journeyman quarterbacks are a thing" — see [[Weak Quarterback Play and Receiver Value]].
