# Charlie Kolar

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-10-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-07]]) [Dynasty]: asked whether Kolar is anything besides an impediment to the Ravens tight ends fantasy managers already roster, Waldman said Kolar can succeed [[Mark Andrews]] in the Andrews role. Of the two tight ends Baltimore drafted together, Waldman remembers Kolar as the earlier pick and as the one most like Andrews — very good against zone coverage, able to make tough catches and take contact, a better blocker than [[Isaiah Likely]] though not on [[Eric All]]'s level, and a useful move tight end. Most likely outcome is a career as a team's second tight end, but if he lands elsewhere on a second contract he could start and give you a plus version of Hunter Henry.
- 2024-10-10 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-10]]) [Dynasty]: a starting NFL tight end stuck behind two starting-caliber tight ends, and a name he is now adding to every one of his dynasty rosters after seeing him get real action. He hated the landing spot on draft day but liked the player out of Iowa State, and thinks a 2025 Baltimore room of Kolar plus [[Isaiah Likely]] would make him happy, since [[Mark Andrews]] looks finished as a 1,000-yard player. Angelo rates Kolar the better blocker of the two and says he is closer to [[Sam LaPorta]] than people think. [[Matt Waldman]] agreed, saying that when Kolar was drafted the comp he saw was Andrews himself and he expected Kolar, not Likely, to be TE2 — Likely was the athlete, but Kolar was the better all-around player against zone, tougher at the catch point, a genuine three-down tight end. Waldman qualifies the LaPorta comp: comparable over the middle, not as dynamic as a perimeter receiver, but a rugged starter you can throw into a tight window.
- 2024-11-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-22]]): Kolar is the only tight end Waldman really wants as a stash, and only in a 1.5 PPR format where managers hoard backup options off the wire. He thinks Kolar has shown a little something and that the team likes him; the problem is purely that he is stuck behind two really good tight ends. Waldman contrasted him with [[Adam Trautman]], on whom he thinks the ship has sailed after too many chances — a good backup and nothing more. Untracked co-host Adam Harstad said he never has trouble finding tight ends he likes and cycles through names like [[Darnell Washington]], [[Brenton Strange]], [[Noah Gray]] and [[Tucker Kraft]], but warned that the typical successful outcome is a TE8 you cannot trade, which is why he tries to fight the urge to roster them.
