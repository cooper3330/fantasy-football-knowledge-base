- 2026-04-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-13]]) [Dynasty]: he views Black as underrated, comping him to a 'plus-size Justin Forsett' or a Chester Taylor type -- backs in the 5'10', 205-215-lb range who run hard between the tackles, break tackles, finish strong and attack contact, with just enough burst to stick on an NFL roster. Notes he catches the ball well out of the backfield on screens and wide routes and plays with intelligence and reliability. Projects a possible 10-year complementary-back career, potentially producing a 1,200-1,300-yard season if leaned on for an extended stretch due to injury elsewhere, but doesn't see him as an every-year starter over a more physically compelling option.
