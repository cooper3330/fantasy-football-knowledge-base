- 2026-08-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-06]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is buying the hype cautiously -- good speed out of Oregon/FSU, can catch, works over the middle well. He flags that teammates describing Benson as playing 'like a veteran' is the kind of camp comment he pays attention to, drawing a parallel to how teammates said the same of [[Ladd McConkey]] before his breakout (citing specific examples of McConkey 'uncovering' in the red zone). He hasn't heard that specific type of detailed praise about Benson yet, but calls him a 'free square' worth monitoring as something real may be developing.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is fully in on Benson for Dynasty ('absolutely') though he says Redraft managers can still largely ignore him barring injuries elsewhere on their roster. Benson is in a three-way, dead-heat competition with second-year players [[Jack Bech]] and [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]] for a role; Waldman reads that dead heat as bullish for Benson specifically, since neither incumbent has separated himself in year two while the rookie is already even with them. Teammates reportedly say he prepares and carries himself like a veteran pro, and while coaches' 'stay humble, keep working' language signals he's still making mistakes and hasn't fully grasped what's needed to be a high-end contributor, Waldman still sees the talent and physical tools. He projects Benson as a late-round dynasty add or practice-squad stash, not someone to roster in redraft unless multiple starters ahead of him get hurt.
