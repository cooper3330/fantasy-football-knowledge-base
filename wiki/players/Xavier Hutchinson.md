# Xavier Hutchinson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]): a name to watch as a potential long-term Houston WR3 behind [[Nico Collins]], but faint praise — "really good at doing the dirty work," a good blocker, comped to Allen Lazard as "a fine player to have around" rather than a real weapon, explicitly not "a fine player to pay $44 million in free agency" for. Was barely used even after [[Noah Brown]]'s Wild Card injury, and neither host is confident Houston has a real plan to get him more involved for the Divisional Round game at Baltimore.
- 2024-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-29]]): Hutchinson graded as a jack-of-all-trades, master of none in his Reception Perception profile — Harmon thought he was a nice day-three pick but was not as high on him as others were. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) is more positive, calling him a pretty good outside receiver who is decent in contested-catch situations, liking his limited 2023 opportunities and his college contested-catch profile, and arguing that in a league short on outside receivers who win contested balls he would have a role somewhere else. Both agree he is buried behind [[Nico Collins]], [[Tank Dell]] and [[Stefon Diggs]] in what Koh calls arguably the best receiver room in the NFL, with Harmon saying he would take Houston's room over Chicago's.
- 2024-10-10 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-10]]): Hutchinson played heavily after [[Nico Collins]] went down, running over 50% of the routes and finishing fourth on the team behind [[Stefon Diggs]], [[Tank Dell]] and [[Dalton Schultz]]. Harmon's pre-draft Reception Perception report was not high on him and gave him an [[Allen Lazard]] comp — a big slot who wins on quick-hitting stuff, slants and flats, not a perimeter player; his college profile showed little green downfield on the corner or post and his best routes were the slant and comeback, with a weak 42.9% dig success rate. Harmon says he is 'definitely not' the downfield X Houston's system needs, though he could be a useful NFL player in the [[Noah Brown]] dirty-work auxiliary role. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) noted Hutchinson is 6'2-6'3, 205+ and lined up outside on 72% of Week 5 snaps and ~65% in 2023, and argued Houston should want his size blocking on the perimeter given their poor run game. Harmon floated kicking Diggs outside so Hutchinson could play big slot, but immediately said he doesn't think it will happen.
- 2024-10-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-10]]): a dark-horse free-agent add while [[Nico Collins]] is out, worth a couple of FAAB dollars but not worth cutting consistent production for. Watching him against Buffalo, Waldman saw the same player he charted at Iowa State and in preseason — good route runner who works back to the ball, very good off structure at finding open spots, good after the catch, and strong acrobatic ability to earn contested balls at the intermediate boundary. The limitation is separation: he is not explosive and will not consistently uncover, which Waldman likens to [[JuJu Smith-Schuster]] early in his career, back at USC when JuJu still had some athletic electricity. [[Brandon Angelo]] framed the role differently — he sees Hutchinson as more of a possession piece, with [[Tank Dell]] taking over the level-three double-move work once healthy, and thinks that possession role matters because Houston will need more ball-control football behind a strong defense.
- 2024-10-31 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-31]]): with Stefon Diggs out for the season with a knee injury, Hutchinson becomes Houston's number two receiver by default. Harmon's projection for the Thursday night game was [[Tank Dell]] at X and Hutchinson as the flanker/power slot, comparing him to an [[Allen Lazard]] type from a deployment standpoint. He was already top-five in routes for Houston across the three weeks without [[Nico Collins]], alongside Dell, Diggs, [[Dalton Schultz]] and [[Cade Stover]]. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) supplied the bear case: last season Hutchinson played six games above a 50 percent snap share and never had more than two catches or 31 receiving yards in any of them, so the 'next man up' billing is not backed by production. Harmon also expects Houston to lean into more 12 personnel rather than the 11 personnel spread look they wanted.
- 2025-02-04 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-04]]): Hutchinson is a player Houston can slot as the third receiver and 'get away with it' — Harmon is deliberate that getting away with it is the operative phrase. He profiles Hutchinson as more of a dirty-work blocking, power-slot type than a field-stretcher, which is why Harmon insists the Texans still need another true outside receiver and does not think the answer is in free agency.
- 2025-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-29]]): Hutchinson is playing ahead of [[Jayden Higgins]], and while Harmon never liked him as a high-ceiling prospect, his write-up pegged him as a good dirty-work player who could do some power slot work, comped to [[Allen Lazard]] — a fellow Iowa State receiver who became a big slot and even took move-tight-end reps in Green Bay. That fits Nick Caley's incoming Rams-style system, which uses receiver motion and wants receivers active in the run game: [[Christian Kirk]] is a slot but not a dirty-work slot, and Houston wants to limit the punishment on [[Nico Collins]]'s hamstrings. Harmon's projected opener alignment is Collins at X, Hutchinson at flanker, Kirk in the slot.
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-05]]): if [[Christian Kirk]] cannot go in Week 1, Hutchinson may play a lot more than people think — he has experience with the team even in a new offense and is a useful blocker. Harmon repeated his pre-NFL comp of [[Allen Lazard]] and said that is roughly the player Hutchinson is, adding that Lazard is only a punchline because the Jets overpaid him at [[Aaron Rodgers]]'s urging, which is not the player's fault. He characterized Hutchinson as the 'dirty work guy' in the room, contrasted against [[Jaylin Noel]]'s vertical juice and [[Jayden Higgins]]'s size — useful for snaps, not the one likely to inherit Kirk's target volume.
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-06]]): asked whether Hutchinson emerges as a weekly fantasy play, Waldman said he does not think it happens. He liked a fade route with a nice release and acknowledged the profile — a very good pass catcher who can take contact and is quicker than fast, so compressed-space and red-zone work is the ideal role if Houston decides they have something there in timing and route running. But he explicitly rejected the [[Nick Westbrook-Ikhine]]-style touchdown-fueled outcome as a baseline expectation, saying it is worth monitoring for a week or two to see whether a trend forms rather than starting him. Bob Harris (not tracked) noted he was forced to start him, and that he rosters him on two teams without starting him in either.
- 2025-10-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception - 2025-10-09]]): Hutchinson is not simply ceding snaps to the rookies — he played the exact same snap total as [[Nico Collins]] in this game and scored his second NFL touchdown, which Harmon thinks keeps him involved. Since [[Christian Kirk]]'s Week 3 return, Hutchinson has run a route on 72% of dropbacks, but Harmon called his 0.65 yards per route run over that stretch 'real sick' in the sarcastic sense — the playing time is not producing. He still expects Houston to rotate Hutchinson, Kirk, [[Jayden Higgins]] and [[Jaylin Noel]] rather than settle on a clear third receiver.
