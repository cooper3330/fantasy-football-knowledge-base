## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]): a name to watch as a potential long-term Houston WR3 behind [[Nico Collins]], but faint praise — "really good at doing the dirty work," a good blocker, comped to Allen Lazard as "a fine player to have around" rather than a real weapon, explicitly not "a fine player to pay $44 million in free agency" for. Was barely used even after [[Noah Brown]]'s Wild Card injury, and neither host is confident Houston has a real plan to get him more involved for the Divisional Round game at Baltimore.
