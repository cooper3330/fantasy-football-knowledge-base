# JK Dobbins

## Expert Takes

- 2026-01-22 — According to Matt Harmon ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-22]]): Denver is activating Dobbins' practice window ahead of the AFC Championship; Harmon wasn't certain he'll play but said getting him back would help as a steadier early-down complement to [[RJ Harvey]]'s boom-or-bust profile.
- 2026-08-03 — According to Ian Hartitz (untracked co-host) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-03 (Feel It or Fk It with Ian Hartitz)]]): he has Dobbins as his Denver RB1 and wants to 'just take JK Dobbins and find somebody else' rather than chase the crowded backfield, noting Dobbins outproduced RJ Harvey down the stretch last season (5.0 yards per carry to Harvey's 3.7) once Harvey had no competition for the role, and posted a faster tracked ball-carrier speed than TreVeyon Henderson and Chase Brown last year, pushing back on any 'washed' narrative; he credited Dobbins for reinventing his game after injuries derailed his once five-star, Ohio State-caliber athletic profile.
- 2026-09-01 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-01]]): he prefers JK Dobbins to [[RJ Harvey]] as a late bench/flex bet in the muddled Broncos backfield, while acknowledging the risk that [[Jonah Coleman]] could vulture all the touchdowns and make both veterans irrelevant. Guest Ben Gretsch (not a tracked expert) agreed Dobbins 'was really, really good' last year before he got hurt, reinforcing the case for him over Coleman, though both hosts stress this backfield could evolve significantly once the season starts.
- 2026-09-28 — According to guest Jamie Eisenberg (CBS Fantasy, not tracked) with [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-28]]): with RJ Harvey banged up during the game, Eisenberg noted [[JK Dobbins]] -- 'usually the most injury-prone running back in the NFL' -- could end up the 'last man standing' in Denver's backfield. Both hosts want to see what a fully featured role would look like for him but doubt the team fully commits given his injury history; Harris flagged Denver's overall inability to run the ball as 'a big problem' through three weeks.
- 2026-10-15 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Dave Kluge (Football Guys, guest, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-15]]): after a month of the Broncos backfield trending toward [[RJ Harvey]], Dobbins was clearly the preferred back last week, coinciding with Sean Payton taking over play-calling. Neither host is sure if that's the new baseline or a one-week bump tied to Dobbins's history with the Chargers (his old team). Harris ranks Dobbins RB27 this week, ahead of Harvey (RB30), with [[Marshawn Lloyd]] and [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] sandwiched in between.
