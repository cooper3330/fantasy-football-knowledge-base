# Isaiah Pacheco

## Expert Takes

- 2025-09-12 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-12]]): Pacheco is his number one, and it is a downer. He had Pacheco RB21 to start the year and has him 34 this week, with the message: look for a reason not to use Isaiah Pacheco, especially against Philadelphia. The volume is what alarms him because it reflects a coaching decision — Pacheco played more snaps than [[Kareem Hunt]] but not by much, and got zero third downs and zero goal line. Everyone told themselves the awful December and January was the broken leg and a full summer would fix it, and nobody says he is not healthy, so why would usage look like this unless it is the decision? Harris thinks Pacheco is the better player than Hunt, who he thought had no juice in this game in an offense desperate for juice, and would love to say water seeks its level, but he does not know. He reads it as a signal to go get [[Brashard Smith]] for nothing, since Kansas City may want another player involved. Guest cousin Josh (untracked) agrees, saying he was floored by the stat line, had expected an RB2 season with Hunt dusted, and does not see this as a buy-low — it is how they want to use those backs.
- 2025-09-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-16]]): 'he is not the same guy, period.' What made Pacheco was being a hard north-south runner with sub-4.4 speed, and Harmon promises he does not have sub-4.4 speed anymore — the injuries have sacked him of a lot of athleticism and he is not playing as powerfully. He calls the Kansas City plan of running back a straight committee between 'the ghost of [[Kareem Hunt]]' and 'the shell of Isaiah Pacheco' a symptom of a roster that has atrophied from a talent perspective, made worse by letting the offensive line continuity go when [[Patrick Mahomes]]'s contract is now a bargain relative to the market.
- 2025-09-18 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris ranks Pacheco RB34 for week 3 and explicitly said he is not telling anyone to start him — 'don't play your Giants or Chiefs running backs if you can help it', while acknowledging many managers will be forced to use one at flex. He grouped the Kansas City and New York backfields together as the week's least exciting fantasy situations, and the segment's affectionate listener mixtape characterized Pacheco as having 'typewriter feet', running hard with short, frantic steps into the line rather than creating with elusiveness.
- 2025-09-19 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-19]]): the Chiefs backfield is guest Josh Fisch's (not a tracked expert) number-two mind-blower — he calls it arrogant for Kansas City to bring back [[Kareem Hunt]] ('I'm amazed he's still in the NFL'), assume a healthy Pacheco is enough, and add nothing at the position in a league where the run game came back into fashion; he would rather see what [[Brashard Smith]] has, because he feels he already knows what Pacheco is. Harris is less certain and records the disagreement: he expected Pacheco to come back and be the dude, does not know that he is not, thinks Kansas City probably believed the same but 'maybe they don't like what they see,' and stresses the schedule caveat — the Chargers and Eagles are two good run defenses, so he is not sure he has seen enough of these backs to judge.
- 2025-09-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-22]]): Pacheco 'has looked no better than he did late last season coming off the injury' — the expectation set by training camp reporting does not match what is on the field. Waldman flagged a pass play where Pacheco turned the wrong way on a route and the resulting throw went down as a fumble, the kind of error he said a back should know better than to make. He still expects the Kansas City backfield to stay mostly Pacheco and [[Kareem Hunt]], whose best days are clearly behind him, with only spot work for [[Brashard Smith]], and argued the bigger constraint on all of them is the offensive line rather than the backs themselves.
- 2025-10-13 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-13]]): after weeks of an even committee, Pacheco finally got clear separation from Kareem Hunt on Sunday night, playing 48 snaps to Hunt's 18, though Harris felt the NBC broadcast overhyped it beforehand. Pacheco touched the ball four times on the opening drive before Hunt took over on the ensuing series including work in close, and Patrick Mahomes ultimately vultured the goal-line touchdown himself rather than either back scoring. Harris called the snap split legitimate for the first time and said he expects to finally start ranking Pacheco meaningfully ahead of Hunt in weekly RB rankings for Week 7, given the talent gap he sees between the two.
- 2025-10-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-16]]): after opening the season poorly (10 carries for 22 yards after Week 2 vs Philadelphia), [[Isaiah Pacheco]] has run over 4.6 yards per carry with increased playing time over the last three-to-four games and is showing more 'angry downhill running' as Kansas City has committed to getting under center and pounding the ball. Harmon still called him 'annoying for fantasy' because Kareem Hunt vultures goal-line and short-yardage work and Brashard Smith is mixed in as a receiving-only change of pace, capping Pacheco's ceiling even as his per-carry efficiency has clearly improved.
- 2025-10-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-28]]): Pacheco took a scary hit on his knee on a play that was called back for holding, and in slow motion it looked like a serious injury, but he was inspected by trainers, got up on his own and asked back in -- Harris called leaving him in a 28-7 blowout with four minutes left 'really dumb' by the coaching staff regardless of the outcome. The Chiefs backfield remains a full committee: all three running backs got carries on the opening drive and [[Kareem Hunt]] is still the choice at the one-yard line. Harris says none of the Chiefs backs is being used like a true RB2, but as long as Pacheco is healthy 'you can do worse at flex.'
- 2025-10-30 — According to Jeff Bell (Football Guys, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-30]]): Pacheco has been labeled week-to-week entering Week 9, which is driving increased projected volume for [[Kareem Hunt]] and opportunity for [[Brashard Smith]] in the Chiefs backfield.
- 2025-10-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-31]]): Pacheco hadn't practiced as of Thursday, and Harris assumes he won't play against the Bills.
- 2025-11-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-14]]): Pacheco has not practiced this week, and Andy Reid telling a reporter he'd 'need a little more time' signals he probably won't play against Denver. Harris notes the irony that Pacheco originally got hurt in a blowout where Reid still had starters in deep into the fourth quarter.
- 2025-11-21 — According to Chris Harris on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-21]]: Pacheco was limited Wednesday and Thursday in a chunky knee brace, probably questionable but with a chance to play against the Colts. Harris said he 'wouldn't feel any need at all to rush out and use him,' and that Pacheco's main fantasy impact would be making [[Kareem Hunt]] less usable as an emergency streamer.
- 2025-12-01 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-01]]): Isaiah Pacheco returned from injury against Dallas but was clearly being worked back in slowly -- 15 snaps and 5 touches to [[Kareem Hunt]]'s 40 snaps and 16 touches. Harris said it was 'pretty clear which one has any juice,' calling the backfield 'certainly until you see different, not an attractive' one. He noted the bigger story from that Chiefs loss was three offensive line starters going down to injury, including a tackle he believes broke a bone and won't be back soon.
- 2026-03-12 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Dave Kluge (Footballguys.com, untracked guest) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-03-12]]) [Dynasty]: Pacheco signs with Detroit as a David Montgomery-style complement to [[Jahmyr Gibbs]]. Harris disagrees with 'spreadsheet' analysts claiming this doesn't affect Gibbs at all, arguing Montgomery himself wasn't a real drain but there's always going to be a second back who takes some volume -- it just shouldn't change anyone's evaluation of Gibbs as a top-two overall running back. Kluge, after digging into last year's usage, actually thinks Montgomery had been a bigger threat to Gibbs than Pacheco will be, since Dan Campbell shifted red-zone work heavily to Gibbs even with Montgomery active. Both see Pacheco as a reasonable ~8th-round pick with real contingent upside rather than a real threat to Gibbs's role.
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28]]): Pacheco joins Detroit as Jahmyr Gibbs's backup/insurance following David Montgomery's departure. Harris notes Pacheco was routinely pulled for Kareem Hunt in goal-line situations late last season with his prior team, which is part of why Harris expects Gibbs -- not Pacheco -- to inherit the Lions' clear goal-line role, though Harris still stops short of calling Gibbs a true touchdown-equity play, noting bigger backs like Bijan Robinson and Jonathan Taylor retain a stronger claim to that kind of usage leaguewide.
