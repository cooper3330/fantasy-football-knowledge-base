# Cole Kmet

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-06-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-23]]) [Dynasty]: reacting to reports Kmet is nearing the end in Chicago in the final year of his deal, Waldman says he is worth keeping only in deep formats — 40 to 50 roster spots, maybe still at 30 to 35, where he could be a low-end TE1. The core limitation is that Waldman does not anticipate any team viewing Kmet as a one-on-one matchup threat, and that is what separates reliably fantasy-caliber tight ends from guys who post an occasional good year when their quarterback is forced to check down and throw to the open man against zone. Kmet is very good at exactly that, and is a good blocker, so he can start for an NFL team — but Waldman groups him with [[Will Dissly]] and Drew Sample as starters who will never be a top-five fantasy talent. Holding him expecting a bargain high-end starter elsewhere is holding the wrong guy.
- 2026-10-23 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-23]]): Kmet took a hard hit to the knee and was carted off during Chicago's win over New England. No further update on severity was given, but Harris notes the absence opens up more immediate target share for [[Colston Loveland]].
- 2026-10-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-10-26 (Feel It or Fk It)]]): Cole Kmet suffered a season-ending knee injury on Thursday Night Football. Waldman expects the Bears' run game and passing attack to absorb the loss reasonably well -- backup Sam Roush was 'the best blocking tight end in this class' even with rookie drops, Chicago can lean on practice-squad blocker Steven Carlson and more jumbo six-lineman sets, and the receivers (Khalif Raymond, Colston Loveland, Luther Burden) block well enough that it shouldn't hurt the ground game much beyond possible situational red-zone wrinkles once Kyle Monangai returns.
