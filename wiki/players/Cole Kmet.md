# Cole Kmet

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-06-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-23]]) [Dynasty]: reacting to reports Kmet is nearing the end in Chicago in the final year of his deal, Waldman says he is worth keeping only in deep formats — 40 to 50 roster spots, maybe still at 30 to 35, where he could be a low-end TE1. The core limitation is that Waldman does not anticipate any team viewing Kmet as a one-on-one matchup threat, and that is what separates reliably fantasy-caliber tight ends from guys who post an occasional good year when their quarterback is forced to check down and throw to the open man against zone. Kmet is very good at exactly that, and is a good blocker, so he can start for an NFL team — but Waldman groups him with [[Will Dissly]] and Drew Sample as starters who will never be a top-five fantasy talent. Holding him expecting a bargain high-end starter elsewhere is holding the wrong guy.
