# Baker Mayfield

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]): **feeling it** on Mayfield being Tampa Bay's starting quarterback in 2024. Reasoning: the Buccaneers are likely to re-sign him based on how well he held the offense up behind an offensive line Waldman describes as not good. Raises a Geno Smith-style year-over-year leap as a live possibility. Waldman's own framing is measured rather than enthusiastic — "I don't love Baker, but I like what he's doing this year within his capabilities."
- 2024-01-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the tone shifts from measured to genuinely bullish. Both hosts now expect Tampa Bay to re-sign Mayfield "assuming a reasonable enough price tag"; Harris grades the season "Pro Bowl level Jeff Garcia," crediting a more mature package around the same traits (gritty, downfield arm, offense command) that read as flaws during his Cleveland rookie year. [[Mike Evans]] has publicly said he hopes to stay in Tampa too, which both hosts read as the offense wanting to keep "the band together."
- 2024-01-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-23]]): a more skeptical, "hard to pin down" read than Waldman's — calls his career "streaky," citing genuinely bad stretches (the worst quarterback in the league during his Carolina run) alongside real high points (the Raiders/Broncos wins). Explicit ceiling: "always be somewhere lower than the 20th-best quarterback in the league, but still" a viable starter. On contract: floats something in a 2-year, $50M range as fair value, doubts he'd actually sign for that little, but strongly recommends he stay in Tampa Bay rather than face what would be his 7th head coach (including interims) if he changed teams again — especially with [[Mike Evans]] and OC Dave Canales likely also staying.
- 2024-01-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-30]]): the "likely also staying" Canales assumption from the January 23 episode is now overtaken — **Dave Canales is leaving Tampa Bay to become Carolina's head coach.** Harmon credits Canales's scheme design directly for getting "the best out of Baker Mayfield that they possibly could" in 2023 (leaning into air-raid tendencies while keeping him disciplined within Todd Bowles's run-heavy first-and-10 approach), which makes the OC departure a fresh, ungraded 2024 variable on Mayfield's outlook rather than a settled Tampa re-sign story.
