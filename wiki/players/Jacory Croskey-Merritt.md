# Jacory Croskey-Merritt

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-08-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-22]]) [Dynasty]: Croskey-Merritt reminds him a little of [[Aaron Jones]] in style of play and quickness. Around 205-210 pounds, with excellent lateral movement, explosiveness in the short area, the ability to flip his hips and set up blocks reasonably well, and creativity — he alters his body in ways that do not look efficient but let him get skinny and slide away to make do with what is available. Waldman's caveat is that other backs 'run the lemonade stand' better, turning genuinely bad situations into good ones, but says Croskey-Merritt's is not bad. Waldman traced the path from Alabama State to New Mexico, a record-breaking season there, a stop at Ole Miss and now Arizona.
