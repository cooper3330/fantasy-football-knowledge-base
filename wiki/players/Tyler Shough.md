# Tyler Shough

## Expert Takes

- 2025-02-27 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-27]]): [[Tyler Shough]] is his off-consensus pick to be a starter in the NFL, ranked above roughly two-thirds of the class. What he likes most is Shough's ability to get rid of the ball accurately with imminent contact or through contact in a 'money pocket', a relatively unique trait to build on. The concern is decision-making — not knowing where to go with the ball but, like [[Kyle McCord]], believing he can fit it in when NFL windows close far faster. Angelo pegs the outcome as a mid-tier or bridge starter, 'Gardner Minshew plus'; [[Matt Waldman]] counters that the arm leans more toward [[Matthew Stafford]] than [[Gardner Minshew]], with a Jay Cutler comp as the upside case. Waldman adds his own worry: a specific weakness against blindside pressure (playside pressure is fine) and immature decision-making in the black zone backed up in his own territory, where Shough tries to create instead of throwing it away. Angelo wants him sitting behind an end-of-career veteran like [[Geno Smith]] — explicitly not [[Sam Howell]].
- 2025-03-28 — According to Cory Kinnan (quarterback charter, not a tracked expert) with [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-28]]): Shough has a genuinely big arm, the biggest in the tier Kinnan dislikes, but the profile is unworkable for him on age and availability — 2024 was the first year in seven that he played more than seven games in a college season, he is 26 years old, and he spent two years as Justin Herbert's backup in college. Kinnan lumps him with [[Jackson Dart]] and [[Will Howard]] as 'what you see is what you get' quarterbacks and would rather take the upside shot on [[Jalen Milroe]] outside round one.
- 2025-03-31 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-31]]) [Dynasty]: Shough's highlights will excite people — runner's speed, impressive throws from varied arm slots on the move, and if you squint, some of what made early-career Jay Cutler compelling — but Waldman calls that potential a projection more than a reality. His vertical and deep accuracy is catchable far more often than pinpoint, and the varied arm slots themselves contribute to the dip; asked to put air under a deep ball from an off-balance platform he fails to lead the target away from the deep safety. The bigger issue is decision-making. He must improve recognition of early opportunities the defense shows, which are superior to the predetermined decisions he often makes, and he is frequently a step or two late executing a viable decision he identified on time because he lacks the confidence to trust it with pressure arriving — leading to off-platform middle-of-field throws to targets no longer open, and to avoidable sacks, including in opponent territory late in the third quarter down multiple scores. Waldman compares the slow processor to what limited Alex Smith. He credits some effective reading skills, wisdom to take a sack at times, and nice mechanics, but flags a concern he thinks is hidden from those touting Shough: his pocket management, detailed in the RSP.
- 2025-04-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-07]]) [Dynasty]: asked by Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) whether missing pocket presence could derail Shough's shot at a starting role, Waldman says it could. There are good elements of pocket presence in his game, but against edge pressure his pocket clock fails him or he does not see the rush at all, taking unnecessary hits and sacks he should have anticipated pre-snap or early post-snap — and NFL defenses are good at identifying whether interior or edge pressure is what beats a given quarterback. Compounding it, he is inconsistent throwing on the move: some good plays, but when he extends he is often not throwing from a balanced position, and pressure exacerbates his bad off-script decisions.
- 2025-04-18 — According to guest Cory Kinnan (Reception Perception QB charter, not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Harmon]]'s show ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-04-18]]): the first-round buzz is unwarranted supply-and-demand noise, comparable to the 2022 hype that produced Desmond Ridder and [[Malik Willis]]. Kinnan's bigger worry is not the injury history itself but that Shough seems aware of it — he throws the ball away, fades away, and refuses to step up into gaping pockets, a pattern Kinnan compares to offensive linemen who play timid after a rookie-year injury. Charting over three games: 69% on nines, 92% on curls, 75% on comebacks, but 33% on digs and 71% on slants, with sub-80% on-target in the short game — a low floor Kinnan calls a giant red flag. Harmon adds that the common [[Jared Goff]] and [[Ryan Tannehill]] comps make no sense because both stand in and drive digs, and Shough is not an anticipatory thrower. Koh notes he turns 26 as a seven-year college player.
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): Waldman concedes a coach could fall in love with Shough on the bullet points — 6-5, 220, arm strength, some off-structure moments, an enormous college sample, a [[Bo Nix]]-style long career (he was [[Justin Herbert]]'s backup at Oregon) — and if he does, the ceiling comp is Jay Cutler, or with leaps-and-bounds improvement a [[Matthew Stafford]] type. Watch the film, though, and Waldman is worried: Shough shows what he calls the pocket deafness of [[Daniel Jones]] on edge plays, either holding the ball too long or short-circuiting under edge pressure. Like [[Kenny Pickett]], he handles it acceptably when he knows it is coming but does not seem to know it is coming as often as he should, and edge pressure is the most common pressure a pocket sees. Waldman's floor comp is Ryan Lindley — the size and the arm analysts love, with the soul of a good pocket quarterback missing.
- 2025-04-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-28 (Feel It or F-It Post-Draft)]]) [Dynasty]: think Jay Cutler circa Chicago — it could be really good or really bad. Shough went 40th, the highest New Orleans has taken a quarterback in decades, so the interest is real, and he has a big arm, big body, mobility, weapons around him and a coordinator in Kellen Moore who seems to like him; Waldman grants he could be a big-play quarterback. But he sees more chance of [[Jameis Winston]] — good and bad — than of Matthew Stafford, and the pocket work concerns him. Practical advice: treat him as a short-term option, and if he puts together a strong half-season, look to deal him upward and sell high; the long-term profile is middle-to-upper-middle-class Stafford at best. Bob Harris (not tracked) drafted Shough in the second round of a pre-draft superflex rookie draft after missing on [[Jalen Milroe]], and is thrilled with the landing spot.
- 2025-05-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-12]]): asked whether Shough is ready to start after [[Derek Carr]]'s retirement, Waldman said 'fuck it' — he is not ready, though he added he is not sure any of the three Saints options is. He rated Jake Haener conceptually the best quarterback of the three and [[Spencer Rattler]] as the blend of the two given his 2024 starting experience, but called Shough the one with the most physical talent and the team's actual hope. His comp was a 'remedial Jay Cutler' without the pocket skills: big arm, can throw on the move and to his left, genuinely impressive when accurate, the type Greg Cosell loves. The caveat is pressure — if Shough identifies it late or misreads the source he gets rattled and stops processing, so expect big ups and downs.
- 2025-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-07-30]]): a mini-sample Reception Perception quarterback charting profile on Shough shows a 69% success rate on the nine route and 75% on the corner, but a 33.3% success rate on the dig — among the worst in the class, and Harmon's stated floor is that you never want to be under 50% on any route — plus 20% on the post. That is a poor structural match for [[Chris Olave]] and [[Rashid Shaheed]], whose best work comes on digs and posts splitting the safeties, and Harmon frames it as an adjacent concern to the one they wrongly yada-yada'd away with [[Derek Carr]], whose own charting flagged poor downfield accuracy. In a cover-two league you need a quarterback who will sit in the pocket and rip the deep in-cut, and Harmon says Shough is not a guy who wants to take that hit. The positive: a 91.7% curl success rate pairs with Olave's 86.4% on curls. Camp reports are mixed and Harmon is not certain Shough starts Week 1.
- 2025-08-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-07]]): Shough is the biggest rookie disappointment so far. Waldman stressed the scale of the opportunity — as wide-open a quarterback job as he has seen a rookie walk into, and not a day-three flier but a pick who could legitimately win a starting job — and yet the reports are that he is not doing well, with the job apparently [[Spencer Rattler]]'s to lose, which Waldman said he does not consider a good sign. [[Brandon Angelo]] agreed it is the clearest miss, noting that at least Cleveland has multiple options while New Orleans does not. Both projected the Saints as a bottom-five scoring offense [Redraft (Standard)].
- 2025-08-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-27]]): the second-round rookie 'only had to overcome [[Spencer Rattler]] to win the starting job' in New Orleans and did not win it. Harris frames this as damning for the draft pick — Shough is a year older than Rattler and turns 26 next month, so it is hard to believe things get appreciably better for him going forward if he cannot beat Rattler now. He allows the alternative reading that Rattler may simply be about to be good under Kellen Moore.
- 2025-10-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-13]]): agreeing with co-host Bob Harris (not tracked) that the Saints made a total biff investing that kind of draft capital in Shough, Waldman said Shough is 'the Davis Webb of picks this week' — either [[Spencer Rattler]] is doing everything possible to keep him off the field, or Shough is truly horrible and will never see it.
- 2025-10-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-20]]): Waldman expects the Saints to keep giving Spencer Rattler the reps for now, but noted the team still has to find out what it has in Shough given his draft slot and second-round rookie contract with a $10 million guarantee -- Waldman himself owns Shough in at least one league.
- 2025-10-20 — According to Matt Harmon and [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: both were skeptical of Shough continuing to sit behind [[Spencer Rattler]] -- Harmon said flatly he 'has to be terrible' to stay benched, citing poor preseason play and bad offseason practice reports, and framed New Orleans's likely paths as either Rattler establishing himself as a mildly viable starter or the team drafting another quarterback next year.
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said 'fuck it' on [[Tyler Shough]] after an unimpressive debut, calling his pocket work weak and comparing him to an 'aspiring Jay Cutler' and to Davis Webb -- he can throw on the move some but struggles from a clean pocket. Waldman noted the surrounding circumstances (guaranteed money, draft capital invested, a new coaching staff that chose him) all pointed to New Orleans wanting to start him much sooner, and the fact they waited until midway through a blowout loss to a division rival 'tells you everything you need to know' about their real evaluation of his readiness.
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-10-27]]): took over at quarterback after [[Spencer Rattler]] was benched. Harris isn't confident Shough unlocks New Orleans's skill players any better than Rattler did, but is 'willing to see' now that the change has happened -- his only real action came in garbage time, and he was even credited with an interception on a ball that bounced off Chris Olave's hands to Antoine Winfield.
- 2025-11-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-10]]): the rookie's day was mostly conservative dribs and drabs, but with real off-script flashes. He wriggled free of what everyone assumed was a completed sack on third-and-long, found [[Juwan Johnson]] standing alone because the coverage had stopped playing, and hit him for 50; he later scrambled and threw off-platform again to Johnson for a touchdown. He also nearly took a safety pinned inside his five, saved only by an emergency Aaron Rodgers-style throw at his running back's feet, and took a sack on fourth-and-four around the Panthers' 40. A bomb to [[Chris Olave]] against Jaycee Horn went 62 yards for the score.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not tracked) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-11]]): both admit low expectations given Shough's college profile and his failure to win the job out of camp — reportedly not a close competition — and both came away impressed. He led all Week 10 quarterbacks in adjusted yards per attempt. Koh credits a certifiable grade-A NFL arm with more control than [[Spencer Rattler]]'s wilder version, plus on-time, in-rhythm processing that puts the ball where receivers expect it, and says New Orleans is in better shape with Shough than Rattler. Harmon's biggest takeaway was pocket climbing, previously a knock on him — he stepped up rather than bailing or turtling. Both caveat it came against a beatable Carolina secondary with no pass rush and a bad game from Jaycee Horn, and want a longer sample.
- 2025-11-24 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Shough was stuck on 39 passing yards for most of the Falcons game before finishing at 243, which Harris described as having to 'Bortle his way' to a respectable-looking total - he was ineffective in tight quarters throughout and threw a meaningless late interception, reinforcing Harris's read that New Orleans's passing offense remains thin.
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-08]]): Shough was the surprise hero of New Orleans' upset over Tampa Bay, rushing for two touchdowns including a broken-play score where he was sacked, escaped, and took 'the great circle route' to the end zone. He also threw a bad interception, misreading [[Chris Olave]]'s route as a stop when Olave actually broke it off deep. Harris said he 'could' see using Shough in an emergency superflex spot given his legitimate rushing ability and that he's hard to tackle, but flagged the turnover risk as real.
- 2025-12-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-15]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is 'feeling it' on Shough as the Saints' 2026 starter, crediting Kellen Moore's scheme for letting him attack downfield and play smart football. He pushed back on age-based skepticism of Shough (drafted at 26), calling the standard cherry-picked 'older QBs who didn't make it' argument short-sighted -- the right evaluation is individual, not an age cutoff, and a QB you believe in is worth developing for six to nine years regardless of starting at 26 or 27. He contrasted this with the Titans and Browns, who've combined for roughly 55-60 years without a good quarterback (Bernie Kosar for Cleveland in the early '90s, Steve McNair for Tennessee about 25 years ago) because neither organization stuck with a developmental starter.
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]): [[Tyler Shough]] steadied New Orleans in the win over Carolina and Harris called him 'okay at the very end,' though he was popped by a late personal-foul hit on a QB draw (helping set up the game-winning field goal) and went to the blue tent -- Harris believes only because he sold the injury hard, with Spencer Rattler subbing for the final snaps before Shough returned. Harris expects Shough to be fine for Week 16.
- 2025-12-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-30]]): Shough is now the betting favorite for Offensive Rookie of the Year and possibly to start the whole season. Over the last two weeks he ranks 7th among all QBs in adjusted yards per attempt, behind only Malik Willis, Joe Burrow, Drake Maye, Matthew Stafford, Brock Purdy and Dak Prescott -- Harmon notes those two weeks came against bad defenses (Jets, Titans) but says good QBs are supposed to demolish cupcake matchups, which Shough did. Harmon's specific praise: Shough stands calm in the pocket and will drop back from the far hash to launch deep outside-the-numbers throws, connecting with [[Chris Olave]] on touchdowns; he's kept the offense on schedule the way Spencer Rattler did while also adding the explosive plays Rattler's tenure lacked. Harmon flags remaining room to grow -- some drops/mistimed throws on his tape weren't his fault -- but with the Saints reportedly wanting to extend him and Shough saying he wants to stay, Harmon says you can 'verifiably walk in with a straight face into 2026' and call him the starter.
- 2026-01-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-05]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is open-minded and leaning positive on Shough, saying he's played well with a steadily diminishing supporting cast, including last week. Waldman is encouraged that Shough isn't 'trying to kill' [[Chris Olave]] with bad decisions -- he played low-mistake football for a rookie thrown into a midseason role and was aggressive downfield, comping him to 'good Jay Cutler' while noting he'd been worried the floor case looked like 'bad Jay Cutler.' Waldman says he had written the day after the draft that Shough, not [[Cam Ward]], was the rookie QB most likely to start 50% of his team's games -- which came true -- and he'd be comfortable rostering Shough as a dynasty QB2 or QB3, and already holds him on multiple teams.
- 2026-01-22 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-01-22]]): named Shough his #2 level-jumper, saying his pocket confidence -- standing in and pushing throws hash-to-sideline on deeper outside-the-numbers passes -- was not what his pre-draft profile suggested, and it directly enabled bigger games for a heavily undervalued [[Chris Olave]]. Harmon called his arm talent and decision-making solid and floated a [[Trevor Lawrence]] comp (both are 26), arguing Shough could add rushing production if he leans into athleticism he flashed at the combine but barely used, potentially producing a Baker Mayfield-type scrambling outing. [[Chris Harris]] said co-host Andy has argued Shough is too old (drafted at 25) to have a high ceiling and benefited from a soft schedule against teams playing out the string; Harris disagreed, arguing a 25-26 year old can develop just as a younger prospect can and that most QBs peak around 32, so even a six-year Shough starter run would be a win for New Orleans. Both agreed the 'is he getting replaced next year' conversation is already over -- he's the Saints' future.
- 2026-02-27 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-27]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon is 'pretty high' on Tyler Shough, not as a future top-five QB but as a strong fit for [[Kellen Moore]]'s offense, capable of 'real big boy quarterback stuff' -- sitting in the gun and throwing from the far hash to the boundary -- that backup Spencer Rattler (a solid backup/low-end starter, per Harmon) couldn't provide even though Rattler kept the offense efficient by success rate. Harmon says the offense visibly opened up once Shough took over, and both hosts praised his chemistry with [[Chris Olave]], specifically his ball placement on boundary throws against the Titans and Jets. Co-host James Koh (untracked) cautioned that Shough's rookie success came largely against weak competition and that New Orleans needs to add better skill talent around him to guard against a sophomore regression, unlike Washington's 'run it back' approach after Jayden Daniels' rookie year.
- 2026-04-23 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-23]]) [Dynasty]: Shough made Harris's redraft list at #17 (Behrens, untracked, left him off entirely), with Harris admitting he wouldn't have predicted this level of enthusiasm entering last season. Harris said he expects to like Shough's receivers and offense, called him 'pretty frisky' with real rushing juice, and pushed back on the in-house argument (from Behrens on the Yacht Club) to fade him for being 25 -- Harris called age-based skepticism 'a horrible reason' given how many years a quarterback can sustain this level of play, and predicted Shough will probably be a starter in the league.
- 2026-06-25 — According to RP quarterback charter Alfredo Brown, with co-host James Koh (neither a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-06-25]]): Brown called Shough's rookie tape a revelation -- 66% success vs. man (within shouting distance of Bo Nix and Josh Allen) despite Chris Olave being his only reliable man-beater, and over 80% vs. zone, which Brown termed 'elite' territory alongside Josh Allen and class-leader Jared Goff. He was pressured on 16.2% of dropbacks (2nd-highest in class) yet posted a 62.5% success rate under pressure (2nd-best), stayed composed with his eyes downfield when plays broke down, and posted an 84% success rate throwing to the intermediate middle -- an area Brown called essential to modern NFL offenses. Brown credited HC Kellen Moore's structure (calling him underrated relative to Sean McVay/Kyle Shanahan/Kevin O'Connell) and praised Moore for correctly benching Spencer Rattler once it wasn't working. Headline take: 'I think Tyler Shough might actually be the best quarterback out of this group by the end of next year,' with Brown floating him as a potential top-10/12 NFL QB if the Saints' improved weapons (Olave, Jordan Tyson, Devaughn Vele, Oscar Delp, Travis Etienne Jr., Alvin Kamara) stay healthy.
- 2026-07-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: In his new almanac profile, Harris pushes back on 'too old' criticism of Shough turning 27 in September, noting his rookie wins came against a beaten-up slate (Panthers twice, Buccaneers, Jets, Titans) for a team that started 1-7. Harris highlights Shough's escapability as the most interesting trait of his rookie tape -- wriggling free from sacks for off-platform completions -- and argues that mobility alone can translate to roughly 400 rush yards and 6 rushing touchdowns, boosting his superflex appeal. But Harris flags real risk: Shough looked like an 'unnecessary checkdown king' at times and will need to handle the rest of the quarterbacking job well so a game without a rushing floor doesn't sink him. Harris cites [[Chris Olave]]'s fantasy-playoff surge (352 yards, 4 TDs) with Shough at QB as a positive signal. Almanac film grades: arm strength B+, accuracy B, vision B, running B, situation C+.
- 2026-08-06 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-06]]): Shough, New Orleans's quarterback change from Spencer Rattler, developed strong chemistry with [[Chris Olave]] specifically over the middle of the field -- Olave posted 78.3% success on digs and 78.4% on posts working with Shough, an area where Rattler (strong instead on quick out-breaking routes) 'wasn't his best.' Harmon expects that connection to continue into 2026.
