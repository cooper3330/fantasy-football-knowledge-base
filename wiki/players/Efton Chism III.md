# Efton Chism III

## Expert Takes

- 2025-05-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-26]]): Patriots UDFA [[Efton Chism III]] — signed with more guarantees than any of New England's seventh-round picks — drew a Phil Perry report of a stop-and-go move that had Perry reminiscing about Julian Edelman. Waldman's verdict is 'hype'. He grants the underlying skill is real: Chism is genuinely good at stop-start moves and short-area quickness, and that is his game on film. But that is precisely why the report is uninformative — in pads and shorts, against off coverage and two-way goes, beating people with stop-start movement is his bag and is exactly what you would expect to see. Waldman wants tougher coverage in real game action before believing it, calling the report 'more fever dream from Phil Perry', while noting Perry is fairly reporting what he saw and it is simply early.
- 2025-08-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-21]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Chism was his second super-deep sleeper, an undrafted rookie who has made real noise in Patriots camp — the one team Harris says he has any pipeline into, and they really love him. He cited play strength for a little guy, breaking away from tackles, and good joint-practice performance, while disliking the boring shared argument that a Josh McDaniels offense automatically elevates slot receivers (the [[Demario Douglas]] case) — the player still has to be able to play. Risks: Chism is no guarantee to make the roster, could spend a year on the practice squad and never be heard from again, and is a slot-only player with Douglas in the way. Ciely liked the pick, noting the depth chart around [[Stefon Diggs]], [[Kyle Williams]] and [[Kayshon Boutte]] is soft enough that Chism could land in the top four or five, and praised his work on option routes where he has freedom to choose.
