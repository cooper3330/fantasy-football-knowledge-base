# Deneric Prince

## Expert Takes

- 2024-07-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-29 (Feel It or F-It)]]): no on Prince for now despite reports he has been running ahead of [[Clyde Edwards-Helaire]], who missed time early in camp and may simply be easing back. Waldman uses him as the example for a general camp warning, crediting Ed Valentine of Big Blue View: coaches give first-team reps because they want to see a player in certain situations, and that is not an automatic endorsement of a depth-chart jump — especially not in July, where some of those players are cut soon after. He grants Prince has good speed and some intriguing traits and says to keep an eye on him, but expects he may be walking the plank in Kansas City.
- 2024-08-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-19]]): unconvinced Prince has actually moved ahead of [[Clyde Edwards-Helaire]]. Waldman's caution is structural — the player leading the preseason box score at a position is often the one who ends up cut, because those reps function as a showcase, the same pattern he flags with [[Dillon Johnson]] and [[Kimani Vidal]]. Bob Harris (not tracked) notes Edwards-Helaire was beat up early in camp and Kansas City may simply be preserving him, or Keaontay Ingram, or someone else; Waldman thinks Prince probably makes the team over Ingram, and notes Brett Veach's back-in-May 'don't forget about him' comment is the kind of pep talk that comes with that time of year. Neither is digging deep into that backfield.
