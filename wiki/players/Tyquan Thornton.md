- 2024-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-29]]): reports have Thornton coming off a strong camp and opening as New England's starting X receiver, which co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) says shocks him — he does not see it and asks whether he is taking crazy pills. - 2024-09-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-03]]): Thornton nominally starting at X for New England has to be a training camp story and cannot hold for many weeks. Harmon has not been a fan since charting him in his rookie-season in-season report, has seen no evidence of leaps in his development, and questions whether a thinner player can hold up at X at all; he profiles him as a design-touches receiver rather than an outside starter. He acknowledges the counter-evidence — several Patriots beat writers reported a good camp and sharp route running — but wants [[Javon Baker]] on the field instead.
