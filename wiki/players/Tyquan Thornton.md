# Tyquan Thornton

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-29]]): reports have Thornton coming off a strong camp and opening as New England's starting X receiver, which co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) says shocks him — he does not see it and asks whether he is taking crazy pills. Harmon says everything about the Patriots should be caveated 'for now,' since the team had not even settled on Jacoby Brissett versus [[Drake Maye]] at quarterback. His view is that Thornton is not a long-term starter at X, and that [[Javon Baker]] likely only lost the job by failing to haul in preseason catches an old-school staff would fixate on — he would be shocked if Baker were not playing over Thornton by roughly Week 10 or 11.
- 2024-09-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-03]]): Thornton nominally starting at X for New England has to be a training camp story and cannot hold for many weeks. Harmon has not been a fan since charting him in his rookie-season in-season report, has seen no evidence of leaps in his development, and questions whether a thinner player can hold up at X at all; he profiles him as a design-touches receiver rather than an outside starter. He acknowledges the counter-evidence — several Patriots beat writers reported a good camp and sharp route running — but wants [[Javon Baker]] on the field instead. See [[Training Camp Report Skepticism]].
- 2025-10-02 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-02]]): Harmon is buying Thornton as 'something real' — not a massive season, but a genuinely useful third or fourth receiver whose speed helps the whole offense structurally. His comp is 'a more useful [[Justin Watson]]'. Thornton ran 36.6% of Kansas City routes in Week 4 and scored a touchdown; Harmon would like him rotating with [[Marquise Brown]], since a vertical threat opposite [[Xavier Worthy]] makes it easier to funnel targets to Worthy on quick crossers and out-breakers and to [[Rashee Rice]] short-to-intermediate against zone. The wider point is speed: Harmon listed Kansas City's 2024 route leaders — [[Travis Kelce]], Worthy, Watson, [[Noah Gray]], [[JuJu Smith-Schuster]], [[DeAndre Hopkins]] — and called it a slow group with no reinforcements coming, whereas Thornton, Worthy and a healthy Rice give the offense real speed.
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: a hold-your-nose waiver add. Guest Brandon Funston of The Athletic (not a tracked expert) ranked Thornton his No. 5 Week 6 waiver target — four straight games of helping, a touchdown in three of the last four, 90 yards Monday night, with [[Xavier Worthy]] banged up and a likely shootout against Detroit, though the window closes when [[Rashee Rice]] returns from suspension in Week 7 and moves to the top of what Funston calls 'a pile of receivers' in Kansas City. Harris ranked him higher still at No. 3 and the only receiver on his list, while flatly disagreeing on the talent: 'I don't think he's any good at all... he kind of stinks,' citing a deep target Monday he simply didn't go get. Both framed it as one week being worth something.
