# Theo Johnson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: a fast pre-draft riser off a strong workout — elite speed, acceleration, good change of direction and near-prototypical size. But the greatest weakness is translating that athleticism to the field: against tight man coverage he "looks like a slower, lumbering option," largely because his releases and footwork aren't refined enough yet. In zone coverage his speed shows up fully — "gliding, explosive, big man." Waldman's framing: he'll have NFL impact even without fixing the man-coverage issues, but "if he can deliver on those things, look out." One of the class's names who could handle extended in-line work, at some cost to receiving upside.
- 2024-06-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-10]]): fades both Giants tight ends for fantasy, projecting only about 44 (or as low as 36) combined catches and 350 yards between Johnson and Daniel Bellinger. He compared Johnson to Albert Okwuegbunam -- a zone-route winner who doesn't beat man coverage and needs the offense schemed for him -- and estimated Johnson could hit 11-12 yards/catch but only if the Giants' run game is respected enough for play-action to keep working, which he doubts given a shaky backfield and Daniel Jones facing pressure. He called Johnson someone with 'a nice future,' just not in 2024.
- 2025-07-07 — According to Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-07]] [Best Ball]: Johnson at TE30 is one of two late tight ends he really likes — a big, physical, really athletic player who had injury issues as a rookie — and he pairs him with [[Ja'Tavion Sanders]] as the cabooses he grabs if he misses the tight end train, particularly in tight end premium formats where people draft tight ends late and take dart tosses. Harris was explicit about which late tight ends he does not want: [[Darren Waller]] at TE28 and Jonnu-adjacent names rising up the board. [[Matt Waldman]] agreed with the Johnson-and-Sanders pairing.
- 2025-08-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-08]]): Harmon offers Johnson as the deep sleeper in the Giants offense and thinks he could finish second on the team in receptions given how thin the receiver room is behind [[Malik Nabers]], while noting he still has developmental ground to make up. James Koh (not tracked) highlights the athletic profile — 6-6, 259, with a 91st-percentile forty, 96th-percentile vertical and 93rd-percentile broad jump at the 2023 combine. Harmon adds a caution: he rates Daniel Bellinger a genuinely good and more refined tight end, so the snaps are not uncontested.
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: two touchdowns and a big statistical day — an easy play-action score from the one and a high-degree-of-difficulty grab for the second — and nearly a third before the ball was thrown short. Harris says you can chase it, it's okay, with two honest caveats: a couple of the catches came on a meaningless drive in the final minute, and Daniel Bellinger plays a lot too, including a catch down to the one. His tiebreaker is archetype and position scarcity: Johnson is a huge, relatively athletic-looking player and it's tight end, 'so I'm fine with it.'
- 2025-11-04 — According to Jeff Erickson of RotoWire, a guest and not a tracked expert ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-04]]): Johnson is his number four waiver add at 33% rostered — a 60-yards-and-a-touchdown type, with the ability to break tackles, earn targets, and a role the Giants like using in the red zone. [[Chris Harris]] had him only on his honorable mention list and has no qualms with the ranking: the 15-yard first-drive touchdown where Johnson broke a tackle was a good play, but he balances it against a third-down drop near midfield with a minute left in the half that Harris calls a crusher at the moment the Giants still felt in the game.
- 2025-11-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-13]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Jameis Winston]] starting, Harmon names Johnson the most interesting pass catcher on the Giants right now — a view Koh agrees with despite calling himself a longtime Daniel Bellinger guy. The framing is comparative rather than glowing: Harmon is unexcited by the Winston 'pump and dump' precisely because [[Darius Slayton]] and [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] do not appeal to him, and Johnson is the one target in the room he would rather chase. No charting numbers were offered on him in this episode.
