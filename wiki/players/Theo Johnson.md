# Theo Johnson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: a fast pre-draft riser off a strong workout — elite speed, acceleration, good change of direction and near-prototypical size. But the greatest weakness is translating that athleticism to the field: against tight man coverage he "looks like a slower, lumbering option," largely because his releases and footwork aren't refined enough yet. In zone coverage his speed shows up fully — "gliding, explosive, big man." Waldman's framing: he'll have NFL impact even without fixing the man-coverage issues, but "if he can deliver on those things, look out." One of the class's names who could handle extended in-line work, at some cost to receiving upside.
