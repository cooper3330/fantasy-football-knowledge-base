# Rondale Moore

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-21]]): traded from Arizona to Atlanta straight-up for [[Desmond Ridder]]. Harmon is unenthusiastic about him as a receiver — "I don't think he's a real receiver, I think he's a gadget player" — but sees value in the explosive athleticism he adds to Atlanta's offense (sub-4.3 40, reported ~41" vertical at 5'7/160) and floats him as a candidate for full-speed pre-snap motion usage, comparing the archetype to what the Rams got from Tutu Atwell, though he's less sure Moore fills that role as well as [[Darnell Mooney]] might. Groups him with [[Drake London]] and Mooney as part of Atlanta's offseason speed infusion, but still thinks the Falcons need one more true X-caliber receiver since neither Mooney nor Moore profiles as one.
- 2024-07-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-25]]): Moore is a pure gadget player and Harmon says he does not know where Atlanta will line him up. Koh is blunter — Moore has never cracked 500 receiving yards and, in his words, 'he's not even a real receiver,' strictly a gadget option. Both use him as evidence that the Falcons' offseason additions addressed the room without adding anyone who changes its outlook, leaving [[Drake London]] as effectively the only outside receiver and the room ranked last in the division and among the ten worst in the NFL.
