# Tyler Lockett

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-01]]): still credited as part of "one of the best three-person wide receiver trios in the NFL" alongside DK Metcalf and [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], but his roster spot is flagged as a real 2024 question under new HC Mike McDonald — a notable cap hit makes him a plausible cap-casualty/trade candidate, floated specifically as a fit to reunite with outgoing Seattle OC Shane Waldron in Chicago. Explicit career-stage read: "on the back nine of his career" but "probably can still play a little bit."
- 2024-06-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-18]]) [Best Ball]: Waldman doesn't think Lockett, entering his age-31 season, has lost much and expects at least one more strong year, maybe two, even as Jaxon Smith-Njigba threatens to pass him as Seattle's top receiver. Waldman has them virtually even in his own rankings (Lockett WR35, JSN WR36) even though current Underdog ADP has JSN going notably ahead (WR44 vs WR51 for Lockett).
- 2024-06-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-20]]): calls himself 'the biggest Lockett guy in the world' but says plainly Lockett is 'definitely a declining player.' Notes the team restructured his contract to keep him under new coach Mike Macdonald's staff, calling that a positive signal for his role, but expects Jaxon Smith-Njigba to pass him on the depth chart this year as Lockett's heavy flat/screen-route usage (12.4% flat routes last year, above NFL average) doesn't fit new OC Ryan Grubb's vertical scheme as well as JSN's intermediate/downfield profile.
- 2024-08-06 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-06]]): Lockett — a receiver Harmon says he has pounded the table for his whole career — is still a quality NFL receiver but no longer in the superstar tier he occupied through 2022. Harmon argues 2022 may have been Lockett's best season ever, with his career-best man success rate, second-best zone and roughly third-best press score, which makes the 2023 step back the harder lesson: not enormous, but enough to raise an eyebrow, and with older receivers the trend rarely zigzags back up. It is either a steady decline or the cliff. Harmon reads the Seahawks' restructure as loyalty to a franchise lifer and a new staff signalling they reward their guys, and suspects Seattle is internally prepared for him not to be the same player. James Koh (not tracked) frames [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] as the likeliest beneficiary of the target share.
- 2024-09-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-03]]): Lockett is a declined version of himself but nowhere near washed — as Seattle's third receiver he is better than most third receivers in the NFL, and Harmon thinks the new staff can deploy him creatively, particularly with more slot reps if [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] moves around. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) agrees, saying he went back and watched a lot of Lockett tape: not as explosive downfield as he was, but he can still play.
