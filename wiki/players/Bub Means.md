# Bub Means

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes. (Full name Gerard "Bub" Means.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): a well-built outside receiver with open-field skills who could develop into a competent flanker, and has enough speed, leaping ability, size and physicality to maybe become a starting split end one day. Has a good feel for setting up cornerbacks with his releases at the line of scrimmage, with enough of a repertoire to do a pretty good job as he acclimates to the speed and savvy of professional corners.
- 2024-06-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-25]]) [Dynasty]: Means (6'1', 212 lbs, 4.4 40, 39.5' vertical) is an athletic fifth-round rookie Harmon likes in contested-catch situations, but Harmon notes he went in the fifth round rather than the first three, unlike A.T. Perry who Harmon is more bullish on for the Saints' vacant X-receiver role. No Reception Perception profile exists on him yet, though Harmon flags him as a name to watch.
