# Bub Means

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes. (Full name Gerard "Bub" Means.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): a well-built outside receiver with open-field skills who could develop into a competent flanker, and has enough speed, leaping ability, size and physicality to maybe become a starting split end one day. Has a good feel for setting up cornerbacks with his releases at the line of scrimmage, with enough of a repertoire to do a pretty good job as he acclimates to the speed and savvy of professional corners.
- 2024-06-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-25]]) [Dynasty]: Means (6'1', 212 lbs, 4.4 40, 39.5' vertical) is an athletic fifth-round rookie Harmon likes in contested-catch situations, but Harmon notes he went in the fifth round rather than the first three, unlike A.T. Perry who Harmon is more bullish on for the Saints' vacant X-receiver role. No Reception Perception profile exists on him yet, though Harmon flags him as a name to watch.
- 2024-07-25 — According to James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-25]]: Means is a fantastic athlete at 6-foot-1, 212 pounds with a 4.43 forty and a 39.5-inch vertical, but the tape shows poor start-stop ability — he can go, he just cannot change direction. Koh reads him as better against zone coverage than man, which he notes is the mirror image of [[A.T. Perry]], giving New Orleans room to mix and match depth pieces by coverage matchup. He promises Means will make a couple of spectacular highlight catches in 2024 on athleticism alone. [[Matt Harmon]] does not push back, treating him as a depth addition alongside Cedric Wilson rather than a rotation threat.
- 2024-10-14 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-14]]): Means, who stepped in after [[Chris Olave]] left with a concussion, has a long-term future as a contributor but probably not this year once Olave returns. What he has always had, going back to Tennessee and Pitt, is the ability to win after the catch and to be physical at the catch point. Waldman was most impressed by how well Means attacked the football — clean, sound hands technique, at a moment when Greg Olsen was on the broadcast describing an epidemic of receivers catching the ball close to their chest without a good attack, which Waldman notes has been his own hobby horse for about five years. He is also getting open. Ceiling comp: a [[Josh Palmer]]-type contributor.
- 2026-01-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-19]]) [Dynasty]: [[Bub Means]], who got hurt this year, is 'one of those talented guys' who -- like most rookie receivers -- has just enough skill to intrigue but not enough proven production to rely on yet. Waldman says he'll need a few years to develop and is only worth an expanded-offseason-roster add, one you should be willing to cut by September if he doesn't show real growth in camp.
