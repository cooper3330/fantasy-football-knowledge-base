# Kenny Pickett

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024 — treat as historical context until
> superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-04]]), *Feel It or F**k It*: diagnoses a pressure-processing issue — "he's in the pocket when he knows that pressure's coming, he's fine," but "short circuits" when pressure catches him by surprise, particularly in significant game situations. Says he "can develop into a Kirk Cousins-like player" if he corrects that, but doubts the Steelers organization has shown the patience needed to let that development play out, with [[Justin Fields]] viewed as the preferred veteran alternative at the position this offseason.
- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]): with [[Russell Wilson]] now signed in Pittsburgh, still "feeling it" that Pickett has a slight chance to win the starting job — mainly if Wilson "is toast." Expects the Steelers to stage a real-looking competition regardless, reading their public "he can be salvaged" language as protecting Pickett's trade value rather than praising him as a starter (praising him outright would undercut what they could get back in a trade). Restates the pressure-processing diagnosis from March 4, and reiterates a good-backup/journeyman ceiling with some chance for more.

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not tracked) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: reacting to his trade to Philadelphia as [[Jalen Hurts]]'s backup. Harris relays Pittsburgh beat-reporter framing (Jerry Dulac, not tracked) that Pickett didn't want to dress for a game last year post-ankle-surgery and didn't want to compete once [[Russell Wilson]] arrived. Waldman: the Steelers "got rid of guys who really could use a little more maturity and professionalism," grouping Pickett with new Panthers WR [[Diontae Johnson]] as players who need to not be "the man" to succeed.
