# Kenny Pickett

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024 — treat as historical context until
> superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-04]]), *Feel It or F**k It*: diagnoses a pressure-processing issue — "he's in the pocket when he knows that pressure's coming, he's fine," but "short circuits" when pressure catches him by surprise, particularly in significant game situations. Says he "can develop into a Kirk Cousins-like player" if he corrects that, but doubts the Steelers organization has shown the patience needed to let that development play out, with [[Justin Fields]] viewed as the preferred veteran alternative at the position this offseason.
- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]): with [[Russell Wilson]] now signed in Pittsburgh, still "feeling it" that Pickett has a slight chance to win the starting job — mainly if Wilson "is toast." Expects the Steelers to stage a real-looking competition regardless, reading their public "he can be salvaged" language as protecting Pickett's trade value rather than praising him as a starter (praising him outright would undercut what they could get back in a trade). Restates the pressure-processing diagnosis from March 4, and reiterates a good-backup/journeyman ceiling with some chance for more.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: Pickett is dealt to Philadelphia, where he "enters the backup phase of his career" as a locked-in number two behind Jalen Hurts. Reads Pittsburgh's move as consistent with the Mike Tomlin "volunteers, not hostages" line — per local reporting (Jerry Dulac) that Pickett didn't want to dress last year following ankle surgery and didn't want to compete once [[Russell Wilson]] arrived.
- 2024-12-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-30]]): he pushes back on the 'massive hit' framing of the play that knocked Pickett out, saying it was a shoulder-to-shoulder hit and perfectly reasonable, not even a tough one. His read is that Pickett entered the game with a broken rib, took a painkilling injection before the game and another at halftime, and simply wore out as those wore off. Waldman contrasts it with the hit [[Jayden Daniels]] took near the goal line, which he did consider massive and thought Daniels should avoid putting himself in position for. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) agrees and frames the whole thing as a generational difference in what counts as a big hit.
- 2025-04-14 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-14]]): he feels it that Pickett wins the Browns' Week 1 starting job despite the [[Joe Flacco]] signing, but 'fucks it' on him holding the job all year. Waldman pushes back on the hand-size narrative and on the framing that Pickett fled Philadelphia at the first sign of competition — on tape he sees real traits: mobility, the ability to buy time inside and outside the pocket, adequate field-reading and enough arm talent to be a good NFL starter. The disqualifier is that certain pressure types derail him quickly, and whether he matures past that is the whole question. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) says nobody should draft a Browns quarterback in fantasy given the queue of Pickett, Flacco and a likely rookie.
