# Robert Woods

## Expert Takes

- 2025-05-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-05-07]]): Woods signed a one-year, $2M deal with Pittsburgh, barely more than [[Diontae Johnson]]'s minimum, and is no lock for the roster. Harmon's read is that the value is cultural rather than statistical — Woods may quite literally be signing to be the adult in the room alongside what he calls one of the most volatile receiver duos in the NFL in [[DK Metcalf]] and [[George Pickens]]. He credits Woods as a tone setter and strong blocker, and argues the run-blocking effort from Houston's young receivers, [[Nico Collins]] included, traces back to Woods setting that standard. Koh (not a tracked expert) supplied the production context: entering his age-33 season after 15 games with Houston in 2024 for 20 receptions and 203 receiving yards, and said he doubts Woods makes the roster at all. Neither host offered a fantasy case for him.
- 2026-02-24 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-24]]), reflecting on Woods' retirement and new hire as LA Rams assistant WR coach: his best fantasy stretch was four seasons with Sean McVay, averaging 120 targets, 80 catches, 1,127 scrimmage yards and 5.8 TDs per season on a 67% catch rate, after doing little in Buffalo. His 2018 season (in Harmon's historic RP database) was his career-high slot rate at 49.6% -- boosted because Cooper Kupp tore his ACL that year -- and he posted a 70% success rate vs. man coverage and 84% vs. zone, one of the better zone-beating marks Harmon tracks. He and Kupp were the 'original duo' of McVay's blurred slot/outside 11-personnel scheme. Harmon notes Woods was not an elite athlete (a solid ~4.5-range runner, not a burner and not a big frame) but excelled through scheme fit, run blocking and toughness over the middle -- exactly the profile Harmon says makes for a natural player-to-coach conversion. James Koh (untracked co-host) agreed the hire makes obvious sense given that resume.
