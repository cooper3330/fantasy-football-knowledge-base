# Dawson Knox

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-08]]): Knox played more snaps than [[Dalton Kincaid]] against Cincinnati because he's used more as a blocker, but ran fewer routes (13 to Kincaid's 16) - Harris currently prefers Kincaid for fantasy purposes between Buffalo's two tight ends.
- 2025-12-15 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-15]]): Knox scored two touchdowns against the Patriots (one wiped out by a Josh Allen holding penalty) despite running roughly the same number of routes and drawing the same target/catch line (4 targets, 3 catches, similar yardage) as [[Dalton Kincaid]] -- it just broke Knox's way in the red zone. Harris had him ranked #25 among tight ends for the week and says he's 'working his way higher' since Knox plays far more snaps than Kincaid as the run-blocking tight end in Buffalo's offense. He cautions it's hard to get ultra-aggressive with either given the overall volume isn't massive, but Knox is now the one he trusts more of the two heading into a matchup with Cleveland.
- 2026-02-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-16]]) [Dynasty]: There is 'rumbling' per the hosts that Buffalo could release Knox despite Waldman rating him as a very good blocker, with the team reportedly viewing [[Jackson Hawes]] as the less expensive option who can still develop as a receiver.
- 2026-06-16 — According to Matt Bove ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-06-16]]): Dawson Knox remains one of the pass-catchers Josh Allen trusts most, 'maybe more than anybody.' Bove expects Knox to keep a role in expected 12-personnel groupings alongside Dalton Kincaid, though he noted Jackson Hawes' elite run-blocking has the staff looking for ways to get Hawes on the field more, which could mean some Hawes/Kincaid two-tight-end sets cutting into Knox's snaps.
