# Xavier Thomas

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-04-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman notes Thomas, a late-round Bears pick, runs a legitimate 4.48 40 and can catch the ball, comping his archetype loosely to Henry Ruggs -- pure speed plus special-teams value -- but says he's a better pass-catcher than a 'Bayless Jones' type of speed-only prospect. He frames Thomas as a fringe dynasty dart-throw rather than a core rookie-draft target.
- 2026-05-11 — According to Matt Waldman ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-11]]) [Dynasty]: on reports the Steelers like Xavier Thomas as a between-the-tackles runner, Waldman said he needs to study more tape before committing to that projection, but floated that the team may be eyeing a 'Tyrone Tracy-esque' fast-track conversion, which is why they may have drafted him earlier than his profile alone justified -- he thinks Thomas is being overdrafted at his current price. He'd consider Thomas in the fifth or sixth round as a rebuilding running-back dart throw, but would be surprised if he beats out DeAndre Swift for the lead role, and doesn't think he beats out the other back Pittsburgh's staff favors either.
- 2026-06-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-29]]) [Dynasty]: Says pay attention to Xavier Thomas's legitimate 4.28 speed and open-field big-play ability in a gadget/third-role capacity, but is skeptical of the offseason hype cycle -- head coach Ben Johnson's actual comments were generic 'if he learns the offense and does the things he's supposed to do' coach-speak, the same language used about [[Luther Burden III]] last year before it took half a season to matter, and Waldman rates Burden the better talent anyway. Expects Thomas mainly on jet sweeps and flare routes rather than real backfield touches, behind DeAndre Swift and Kyle Monangai as pass-catching backs, with any starting outside-WR opportunity going through other receivers on the depth chart first. Views him as a waiver-wire watch, not a roster add yet.
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28]]): Harris views Thomas strictly as a gadget-package piece for the Bears, not even a real WR3 competitor, and doesn't expect that assessment to change based on anything shown in training camp.
