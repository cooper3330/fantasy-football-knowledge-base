# Trey Lance

> ⚠️ Take below is from April 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-04-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]]: unchanged from his original scouting grade — one of the top two-to-three quarterbacks in his draft class and a player who could become a good NFL starter; says people calling him a bust are "overstating and over-dramatizing" a situation where he barely got an opportunity in San Francisco. Reads Dallas as a good landing spot, since he'll play in an offense that lets him create rather than forcing him to be a strict "unstructured grammarian" the way Kyle Shanahan's system demanded. Ties his outlook to [[CeeDee Lamb]]'s: if Lamb stays in Dallas long-term, Lance has a lead receiver to grow with. Separately reasons the Cowboys wouldn't have let Dak Prescott's contract situation reach a $55M dead-cap crossroads without believing they already have a plan at quarterback in hand.
- 2024-08-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-26]]) [Dynasty]: 'feel it' on Lance eventually developing into a viable starter, mostly as an argument about patience — Waldman says almost everyone in the world is far too impatient with young quarterbacks. He grants the negatives: Dallas has emphatically signaled he is a backup, he has made bad throws again, and his confidence has been shaken. But he was 19 when he came out, offenses are more sophisticated now, and the skills are there. Waldman's thought experiment: if [[Aaron Rodgers]] were six years younger and [[Jordan Love]] had gotten a couple of bad starts, looked poor in preseason and then been shipped elsewhere to sit behind another starter, the same people would call him the biggest bust ever. Practical advice: you do not need to roster him, because nobody else values him — you can pick him up and drop him repeatedly until it is time. He calls Lance the most talented of the quarterbacks in this segment.
