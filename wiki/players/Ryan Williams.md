- 2024-09-11 — According to guest co-host Felix Sharpe of Campus to Canton (NOT a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman]]'s show ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-11]]) [Dynasty]: Williams is a 17-year-old true freshman who reclassified and should still be a senior in high school — Sharpe says his birthday is around February 9 and he will not turn 18 until the following year. He is described as the most productive receiver in Alabama high school history and is already leading Kalen DeBoer's Alabama team in receiving through two games, and not close: roughly 270 yards to the next man's 70. Sharpe's core scouting note is that he keeps getting behind SEC defenses. He pairs Williams with [[Jeremiah Smith]] as the two true freshmen who will sit atop draft boards three years out, though he admitted he would not have offered the same trade package for Williams that he offered for Smith. Waldman said he acquired Williams shares in his own devy league partly on Sharpe's recommendation.
