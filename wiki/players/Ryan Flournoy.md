# Ryan Flournoy

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): "there's no doubt" he's an NFL-caliber athlete with the potential to develop into a contributor, but how well he actually develops depends on his releases, breaks and catch-point consistency — which will determine whether he's a reserve who gets passed over in two to three years by younger players, or a real contributor.
- 2025-12-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Flournoy was involved even before Lamb's injury, drawing a deep target (incomplete) in the first quarter plus four other first-half looks, then saw expanded second-half usage once Lamb went out and Pickens struggled. Harris pushes back on the broadcast narrative that Pickens' poor game was mostly about being 'double covered,' calling it overstated cover-two shading rather than true double coverage.
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-08]]) [Dynasty]: Flournoy's 47-yard touchdown (with [[George Pickens]] out of the game after CeeDee Lamb's injury reshuffled routes) featured one of the better double moves Waldman has seen -- selling an in-breaking route with his hands on the defender without pulling or pushing him, then working back downfield -- reason enough to 'keep an eye on this kid.' But Waldman cautioned most of Flournoy's other production came on underneath zone routes that even KaVontae Turpin or Jalen Tolbert could have made, and that he lacks the explosion in and out of routes that Lamb or Pickens have. He's a 'big dude' who wins in specific, limited ways right now rather than an every-down threat.
- 2025-12-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-11]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon admits he'd been dismissing Flournoy despite a producer and Nate Tice pushing him, but after charting the all-22 of his 13-target, 9-catch, 115-yard, one-touchdown game against Detroit (with [[CeeDee Lamb]] banged up), he thinks 'he's really got something.' Flournoy is 26 in his second NFL season with an unusual path -- redshirted at Central Missouri in 2018, partially tore his ACL in 2019, lost his 2020 season to COVID, transferred to Iowa Western CC, then landed only one FCS offer at Southeast Missouri State. He tests as a legit athlete (89th percentile vertical, 94th percentile broad jump, 83rd percentile bench, 4.44 forty at 6'1/202). On tape Harmon likes his release package and ability to win vertical/breaking routes and create separation vs. man, though he settles less well against zone; he plays flanker and slot since Lamb occupies both and George Pickens is the X. Harmon comps him to a Dontayvion Wicks/Jalen Coker-type fan-favorite WR3 worth a full charting profile in the offseason.
- 2025-12-18 — According to Matt Harmon and James Koh ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-18]]): Ryan Flournoy was named alongside Jalen Coker as one of Harmon's two favorite fourth-round-or-later players from the 2024 class, someone who 'looks really good in isolation' and 'can win on his routes,' though Harmon questioned how much real upside remains for a 26-year-old, second-year role player, especially with George Pickens now in Dallas ahead of him -- projecting Flournoy more as a solid number-three option.
- 2026-01-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-19]]) [Dynasty]: an overlooked hold worth adding this summer in 25-to-35-man leagues, or using as the small throw-in that evens out an otherwise-fair trade. Waldman says [[Ryan Flournoy]] has shown growing confidence and improved route running -- not 'unbelievably explosive,' but capable of real work -- while cautioning that Dallas as an organization is unpredictable ('a reality show'), so his opportunity could shift with the front office's next move.
- 2026-02-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-12]]): Flournoy, making just $1M in 2026, had modest full-season totals (475 yards, 4 touchdowns) but flashed real splash ability with back-to-back 114- and 115-yard games -- roughly half his season yardage in those two outings. Harmon likes him as Dallas' clear WR3 given his inside-outside versatility, which pairs well with [[CeeDee Lamb]] moving around to Z and slot, and expects the Cowboys' receiver depth to stay largely as-is beyond retaining [[George Pickens]].
- 2026-06-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-29]]) [Dynasty] [Best Ball]: Doesn't see room for a third 1,000-yard receiver in Dallas behind [[CeeDee Lamb]] and [[George Pickens]], ranking Ryan Flournoy only the fourth target-getter on the roster -- behind Lamb, Pickens and [[Jake Ferguson]], just ahead of [[Javonte Williams]]. Frames him as a bye-week/matchup streamer worth 500+ yards and 3-5 touchdowns, but with real contingent upside: if Lamb or Pickens gets hurt, Flournoy 'suddenly becomes a low-end starter' in Brian Schottenheimer's prolific offense behind an improved offensive line brought in from the Arizona staff. ADP: WR72, 200th overall -- cheap enough, per Waldman, that it 'won't cost you anything to find out.'
- 2026-08-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-18]]) [Dynasty]: both Harmon and guest Jeff Bell (FantasyPros' top-accuracy WR ranker, not tracked) are fully aligned on Flournoy locking into the Cowboys' WR3 role. Bell notes reports the team isn't thrilled with [[Jake Ferguson]]'s blocking involvement, which could open room for Flournoy if they ease off Ferguson, and has Flournoy 19 spots ahead of current dynasty ADP, framing him as a talented player attached to a great ecosystem. Harmon adds that [[CeeDee Lamb]] took a career-low slot-snap rate last year behind a weak WR3 (Kavontae Turpin) and that Flournoy's versatility should let Lamb move around the formation more; he notes Flournoy was 'mossing' incumbent Jalen Tolbert in camp for the starting job. Both call him tough to make work in [Redraft (Standard)] given Lamb and the other Dallas pass catchers ahead of him, but a clear buy in [Dynasty].
