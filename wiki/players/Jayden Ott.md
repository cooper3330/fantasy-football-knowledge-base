# Jayden Ott

## Expert Takes

- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said Ott is patient and reads leverage well against tight boxes, taking what the defense gives him, but against heavier boxes he makes ill-advised cutbacks and bounces -- a tell, in Waldman's view, that he's more of a space player than a true between-the-tackles runner.
- 2026-04-09 — According to Paul Pertichese, guest analyst on the [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-09]] (not a tracked expert) [Dynasty]: Jayden Ott (Oklahoma, formerly Cal) once looked like a top-50 pick two years ago on size, speed, athleticism and receiving ability, but his transfer to Oklahoma 'has been a disaster' amid injuries, and he wasn't invited to the combine or even the Senior Bowl, only the Shrine Bowl. Pertichese still flags him as a name to stash on a dynasty taxi squad given the underlying physical traits, viewing him as a legitimate 'diamond in the rough' Day 3 sleeper.
- 2026-05-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-04]]) [Dynasty]: is not high on Jayden Ott's fantasy outlook in Kansas City despite the Chiefs' running backs coach (Ott's position coach at Cal) vouching for him. Waldman evaluated Ott mostly from his best Cal season and came away unimpressed -- a good shallow-box runner against light fronts (5-6 defenders) who faltered against heavier boxes (7-8) and struggled in traffic despite being a good open-field player. He expects Ott to sit third on the depth chart at best, likely stick around for special-teams value as a kick returner, and poses no real threat to [[Emmitt Johnson]] for touches.
