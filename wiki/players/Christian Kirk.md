# Christian Kirk

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-14]]): says he's "been a guy that has been a fan of Christian
  Kirk" and wasn't critical of Jacksonville's earlier free-agent deal for him
  even though it was a similar-scale overpay to the [[Calvin Ridley]] deal —
  "who gives a shit, you need players." Currently the best receiver on
  Jacksonville's roster. Role projection: not a true slot-only player, but
  "definitely a slot-mostly receiver." Now grouped with newly-signed
  [[Gabe Davis]] (limited to outside/vertical routes) and TE Evan Ingram (not
  a tracked page — "can't have him run block... more of a split-out guy") in
  what Harmon calls a "hyper-specific" group of skill-position pieces that,
  despite the money spent, leaves Jacksonville's receiver room in the
  conversation for one of the worst — arguably bottom-five — in the league.
