# Christian Kirk

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-14]]): says he's "been a guy that has been a fan of Christian
  Kirk" and wasn't critical of Jacksonville's earlier free-agent deal for him
  even though it was a similar-scale overpay to the [[Calvin Ridley]] deal —
  "who gives a shit, you need players." Currently the best receiver on
  Jacksonville's roster. Role projection: not a true slot-only player, but
  "definitely a slot-mostly receiver." Now grouped with newly-signed
  [[Gabe Davis]] (limited to outside/vertical routes) and TE Evan Ingram (not
  a tracked page — "can't have him run block... more of a split-out guy") in
  what Harmon calls a "hyper-specific" group of skill-position pieces that,
  despite the money spent, leaves Jacksonville's receiver room in the
  conversation for one of the worst — arguably bottom-five — in the league.

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): ranked
  between [[George Pickens]] and [[Courtland Sutton]] in the "disaster-adjacent"
  tier, but flagged as the shakiest team situation of the three — "it is much
  more difficult to run your offense through the slot," and Jacksonville's
  outside options ([[Zay Jones]], [[Gabe Davis]]) are "passable" but not enough
  to lift the passing game, so the offense is effectively forced through Kirk.
  Notes 2023's Kirk-vs-[[Calvin Ridley]] target competition as illustrative:
  when Ridley was healthy Kirk had big primetime games while Ridley
  disappeared, which Harmon reads as "this offense sucks," not evidence Kirk
  is the alpha of Jacksonville's passing game. Flags Jones's age as a real risk
  to Kirk's supporting cast.
- 2024-07-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (untracked co-host) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-11]]): Kirk is 'a good player' with established chemistry with [[Trevor Lawrence]] and will lead the Jacksonville WR room's targets early in 2024, before [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] is expected to take over as the passing-game focal point roughly after week eight. Harmon does not view him as 'a needle-moving slot receiver.'
- 2024-10-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-29]]): Kirk suffered a broken collarbone in Week 8 and is out for the season, which also ends any chance of the trade-deadline move Harmon says Jacksonville appeared to be considering. Harmon frames the loss as a structural problem for the Jaguars rather than just a body count: Kirk is a 'vertical slot only' player, so even when healthy he could not have slid outside to cover for [[Brian Thomas Jr.]], and the roster is built around 'hyper specific' pieces ([[Evan Engram]] as a de facto slot receiver, receiving-only backup tight ends) that cannot absorb an injury. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) called the team 'absolutely not built for injuries.'
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-13]]): if Jacksonville cuts [[Christian Kirk]], Harmon would not rule out a Washington reunion with Cliff Kingsbury — there is familiarity and Kirk can play inside and out, which is what Harmon wants for [[Jayden Daniels]]. His framing of Kirk is that he is a vertical slot receiver who was never actually used as one in the Arizona offense under Kingsbury, the same archetype he applies to [[Jahan Dotson]]. He also wants Washington's next receiver to help in run blocking, since the run game is meant to be the base of the offense and wilted late in the year.
- 2025-02-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-19]]): Kirk and [[Evan Engram]] are Jacksonville's two highest cap charges and Harmon expects one or both to be gone, though he flags a real chance Kirk stays on an adjusted contract — new head coach Liam Coen's introduction video shows him connecting Kirk to Spencer Whipple, now the Jaguars' QB coach, who was co-pass game coordinator and assistant WR coach in Arizona during Kirk's time there. Harmon's caveat: beyond [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] the depth is troubling, and while he has faith in [[Parker Washington]] and notes [[Gabe Davis]] cannot be cut yet, the room would be thin enough to force a high draft pick at receiver. James Koh (not tracked) floated a Washington reunion with Kliff Kingsbury, his Arizona head coach. Both noted the oddity that Jacksonville has no GM with the Combine a week away, and joked Coen is effectively running the team.
- 2025-03-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-07]]): Kirk was set to be released by Jacksonville — no surprise given the contract, most likely a post-June-1 cut for maximum cap savings, off a $24M 2025 number and more than $16M saved. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) adds in an intro recorded after the session that the Kirk trade went down almost immediately after they finished recording. Harmon suggests Houston as a landing spot that would make sense — a team needing an interior 'dirty work' target next to [[Nico Collins]] with [[Tank Dell]] out for the year — saying Kirk 'is a player that could fit in here and do some work for the Houston Texans'.
- 2025-03-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-10]]): a clear 'feel it' that Kirk, traded to Houston, can again approach his 2022 Jacksonville career highs (84 catches, roughly 1,100 yards, eight touchdowns) — those numbers are exactly what a high-end possession receiver produces, and that is what Kirk is, with a little downfield ability on top. The caveat is Houston getting its offensive line together. Waldman does not think Kirk suppresses [[Nico Collins]], who he still sees delivering high-end WR1 production, and he called Kirk an all-around upgrade on the rehabbing [[Tank Dell]] — Dell is far more explosive, but Kirk can usually play an entire season.
- 2025-03-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-13]]) on Houston trading a 2026 seventh-round pick for Kirk: he likes the move — the pick is meaningless and they get a decent, younger player at a lower price than the veteran market. Kirk fills the vertical-slot role Houston maybe hoped [[Stefon Diggs]] would, and though he is not the same type of player as Diggs or [[Tank Dell]], he makes a nice number two. Harmon has not charted his 2024 season yet and plans to before camp. Asked if Kirk is good enough to be on the field in two-receiver sets, Harmon says he is more of a slot than a true flanker — there have been stretches where he is a solid man beater and playing him outside is 'not embarrassing', but ideally he is inside. He flags a possible fit problem if Houston leans into 12 personnel, and says Houston still needs another outside receiver so [[Nico Collins]] can move inside.
- 2025-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-21]]): Harmon calls Kirk the picture-perfect player you would rather have as your number three but could get away with as your number two, which is why Houston still belongs in the bucket of teams that must add a receiver in the first three rounds. James Koh (co-host, not tracked) says Kirk at this point in his career is best just sticking in the slot, leaving the Texans needing a genuine flanker to replicate what [[Tank Dell]] gave them. Harmon's stated nightmare for [[C.J. Stroud]] is reaching the end of the season with [[Nico Collins]] and Kirk both out and nobody left, and he notes that charting the Diggs film reminded him how atrocious the Houston offense and offensive line were — Stroud was pressured before out-breaking routes ever came open.
- 2025-06-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-02]]): Kirk 'may not be a fantasy favorite but he's a very good NFL starting wide receiver' capable of low-end WR1 production when genuinely relied upon, comped to Keenan McCardell or [[Robert Woods]] as a player who can work slot and outside. In Houston he benefits from defenses having to account for [[Nico Collins]] on one side and [[Jayden Higgins]] on the other, plus a [[Joe Mixon]] run game that keeps the offense on schedule. With Kirk being drafted around WR60 — well into the double-digit rounds, roughly the 13th — Waldman calls him a great WR4/WR5 pick and a clear value off the radar. He adds the contingency that if Kirk goes down, [[Jaylin Noel]] is good enough to be an interesting waiver-wire play or a late pick in deeper leagues.
- 2025-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-29]]): Harmon expects Kirk to open the season as Houston's slot in three-receiver sets and thinks he has more left than his co-host does — he was really good in 2023 and last season ended on an upper-body injury rather than anything structural like an ACL, so a solid starting slot season is reasonable; he is a more vertical slot, and therefore quite similar to rookie [[Jaylin Noel]]. James Koh (not tracked) disagreed, liking the signing in principle and as depth for a seventh-round trade cost but asking how much Kirk has left and whether they are still thinking about the 2023 version. Both agreed Kirk offers veteran run-blocking that a Houston offense flirting with fullback usage may value.
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-05]]): Kirk did not practice Wednesday or Thursday of Week 1 prep with a hamstring injury and looked likely to miss the opener, which Harmon treated as the trigger for sorting out Houston's receiver pecking order. He noted Kirk is not old but has been in the league since the 2018 draft class and that his 2024 season ended with injuries, so this is worth monitoring rather than dismissing. Harmon framed Kirk's role as primarily an in-breaking route player, distinct from [[Jaylin Noel]]'s vertical outside-the-numbers work and [[Jayden Higgins]]'s big-body profile, and said an absence could open real Week 1 opportunity for the rookies and for [[Xavier Hutchinson]] — with [[Dalton Schultz]] and [[Cade Stover]] letting Houston lean on 12 personnel instead.
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Kirk will miss Week 1 with a pulled hamstring and could be out longer than that. Harris advises against chasing the replacements — 'don't go chasing the rookies in Houston or [[Xavier Hutchinson]] until we see how they all deploy' — and folds the absence into his broader fear about the dismantled Texans offense.
