# Sione Vaki

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-08-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-19]]) [Dynasty]: not a draftable fantasy option in 2024 — 'no, not this year, of course not' — but absolutely one of the week's waiver-wire watch-list names. He is a good receiver out of the backfield, and Detroit has said all camp that while he clearly has not played the position much, he shows a lot of natural skill for it: he runs with power and has good movement. What he must still develop is game management and consistently good decision-making between the tackles. Waldman notes some people think Vaki is the guy to replace [[David Montgomery]] when Montgomery's time is up, and says he is not against that idea.
- 2024-12-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-16]]): with [[David Montgomery]] reportedly out indefinitely with an MCL injury and [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] the obvious add, Vaki is the cheap puncher's-chance stash behind him — a Utah safety/running back convert who catches the ball well and whom Detroit liked in camp. Waldman's framing is roster-construction: if everyone is bidding up Craig Reynolds, take Reynolds if you need the likely snaps, but if you have a luxury bench spot Vaki can be had cheap and could very well factor.
- 2026-11-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]], with guest Jeff Erickson of RotoWire (not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-11-02]]) [Dynasty]: Vaki got real extended run behind [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] for the first time this season — 11 carries that 'actually looked pretty good,' plus first-series snaps alongside Gibbs rather than only mop-up work. Harris calls him a handcuff 'you'd want' in that offense: 'he's not going to be Jameer Gibbs, but it's a good' running back to roster behind Gibbs given Detroit's evident intent to manage Gibbs's workload, whether through more Vaki usage or potentially a trade for outside help.
