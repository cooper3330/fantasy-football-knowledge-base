# Le'Veon Moss

## Expert Takes

- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said what he loves about Moss is the urgency with which he attacks creases and sets up blocks on perimeter runs, taking what defenses give him in the green zone. He's also a rare accelerate-into-collision tackle breaker who wins against linebackers and safeties, but Waldman flagged a bad habit of cutting back on gap plays like counter instead of showing patience with the design.
- 2026-04-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-06]]) [Dynasty]: on Texas A&M RB Le'Veon Moss, a media/fan favorite Waldman doesn't think will have a strong NFL draft day because he lacks the top-end speed teams covet (he has burst, not speed). Waldman comps his power running style to Zach Moss out of Utah, but says Le'Veon has more efficiency and decision-making wisdom, and is also a solid pass-catcher. His projection: Moss becomes an 'oh, we actually have something here' story a year or two into his career, similar to how [[Ray Davis]] developed -- but likely gets labeled a committee/backup piece early, probably behind an established starter, with real opportunity only opening up if that starter's situation changes.
