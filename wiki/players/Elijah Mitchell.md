# Elijah Mitchell

> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Dynasty]: acknowledges Mitchell got more of the workload than [[Jordan Mason]] down the stretch of 2023 (after a since-corrected report suggested he wasn't practicing), but still prefers Mason as the primary [[Christian McCaffrey]] injury-contingency stash — Mitchell figures to cost more on waivers/dynasty rosters for what Waldman sees as a similar or lesser outcome.
- 2025-08-18 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: reading the 2025 Almanac profile, Harris jokes that the best career move Mitchell makes every year is not playing football — he was RB53 in last year's first edition before wrecking a hamstring in camp, then went on IR without a return designation in late August, so there is no 2024 tape at all. Mitchell has touched a regular-season football no more than 81 times since his 2020 rookie year, after hamstring, knee, shoulder, rib, head, groin and finger injuries. Five years ago he was fast and physical in the Shanahan-junior system, and he is now attached to [[Patrick Mahomes]] and Andy Reid with [[Isiah Pacheco]] coming off a broken leg, a 30-year-old [[Kareem Hunt]] who looked slow, and seventh-round converted wideout [[Brashard Smith]] in the mix. Harris ranked Mitchell highest of that group in the book but now says that is unclear and Smith may deserve the spot, with a possible change coming.
