# Jack Plummer

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): "I dig a lot about his game, but the accuracy is just not quite there" — a specific, bounded ceiling claim: "if he had the accuracy, he would probably be a top-five quarterback in this class." Named as one of a handful of quarterbacks Waldman wants to revisit in more depth.
- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]): floated as a real-deep-league dynasty stash (explicitly framed as a last-picks-in-the-draft-still-not-enough-players type of league) — "there's something about that kid" — with new context on why: HC Jeff Brohm has a track record of developing quarterbacks. Waldman's caveat is mobility, not arm or pocket feel — Plummer "can't move like Jake" — but grades his throwing skills and pocket management as good enough to be worth a very late-round pick or a free-agent stash.

- 2024-03-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-08]]), solo cast on the full QB class: moves efficiently in the pocket once he identifies pressure, but lacks strong awareness of edge pressure and closing off escape routes. Sustained pressure through a game notably slows his processing speed, and underneath defenders dropping into passing lanes trip him up at this point in his career. Technical room exists to gain more velocity as a thrower; if his situational game management improves, Waldman sees potential for him to develop into an NFL reserve.
