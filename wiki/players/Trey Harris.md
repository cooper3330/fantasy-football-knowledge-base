# Trey Harris

## Expert Takes

- 2025-12-23 — According to Matt Harmon ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-23]]): the Chargers are effectively benching Keenan Allen for Trey Harris -- near-identical snap rates (46.8% Allen, 45.2% Harris) and route participation (52% each) this game, with Harris drawing 5 targets for 54 yards. Harmon cites offensive coordinator Greg Roman's quote that 'this is not a Burger King offense, you have to be able to play three receiver positions' as evidence the staff values Harris' versatility (X, flanker, slot) and blocking -- a shift from the X-only prospect profile Harmon originally had on him. Harmon expects real fantasy buzz on Harris entering the 2026 offseason in what remains a crowded receiver room (Quentin Johnston, KeAndre Lambert-Smith, Ladd McConkey), and thinks it's more likely than not that Allen isn't back with the Chargers next season given his snaps have been ceded to Harris.
- 2026-03-10 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-03-10]]): Harmon said the previous Chargers staff (including Jim Harbaugh, who remains) 'really, really liked' [[Trey Harris]], the team's 2025 second-round pick, specifically his ability to play multiple receiver spots -- flanker and slot -- which surprised Harmon given his X-receiver profile coming out. With [[Quentin Johnston]] occupying the X spot, Harris was pushed inside; if Johnston is traded, Harmon floated Harris as a candidate to take over the on-ball X role, freeing [[Ladd McConkey]] to move around the formation under new OC Mike McDaniel.
- 2026-04-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-07]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon recalled projecting Harris as a three-route, largely sacrificial X receiver last summer, assuming he'd inherit the outside role after Mike Williams' retirement -- instead the Chargers are using him as a three-position player, working him at Z and in a condensed slot because they value his blocking. Both see this as a real signal the team 'loves' Harris, but it may mean a longer runway to a true starting role than the direct X-receiver path they originally projected, since he now has to develop three spots' worth of nuance rather than one.
- 2026-05-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-05-28]]) charting Harris's rookie season: 63% success rate versus man, 75% versus zone -- numbers similar to [[Pat Bryant]]'s. Alignment was highly movable: 61% outside, 32% slot, 6% backfield, split almost evenly on/off the line (51%/49%). Route breakdown showed a classic X-receiver tree: 74% success on digs, 55% on nines (at heavy 23% route participation), 80% on curls, 76.5% on slants. His run blocking stood out enough that the previous staff (Greg Roman) played him over the top of the depth chart in heavy 12/13/21/22 personnel packages -- he led the Chargers' WR room with 169 snaps there, ahead of [[Quentin Johnston]]'s 119. Stat line was modest (32 catches, 344 yards, 1 TD) for a good-not-great old-Miss prospect; comps in Harmon's tool are unusual -- Tim Patrick, Jamison Williams' 2024 season, and Sammy Watkins' 2019 season. James Koh (not tracked) floated Harris pushing [[Quentin Johnston]] down the pecking order; Harmon pushed back, calling that premature and Harris's ultimate role still TBD entering year two under new OC Mike McDaniel.
- 2026-06-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman flagged Trey Harris as a name drawing high praise for his development this summer in the Chargers' receiver room behind [[Ladd McConkey]] and [[Quentin Johnston]], saying that at a viable draft price, Harris would be 'absolutely fantastic' value for him.
- 2026-08-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: cited alongside [[Quentin Johnston]] as banged up in Chargers training camp, one more piece of evidence Harmon points to as a warning sign the Chargers' skill-position rooms are messier than the ADP suggests.
