# Rivaldo Fairweather

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]): Fairweather, at Auburn by way of FIU, is a good athlete with the speed to stretch zones and beat man coverage, and a good pass tracker with a lot of tools to win against tight coverage. To become a legitimate man-coverage threat he needs artistry in his footwork and pacing when releasing from the line and setting up during his stems. The bigger issue is his hands: Waldman wants improved grip strength, focus and retraction of the football, and notes lapses where he fails to attack with uniform hand position or pick the ideal attack type for the ball's trajectory. Clean that up and Waldman thinks he could be a dynamic pass catcher.
