# Jacoby Meyers

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-10-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-23]]): Meyers is the veteran most likely to be traded before the deadline -- he's made clear he wants out and wants an extension he won't get from a Raiders staff Harmon isn't sure survives past this season, calling the situation 'a full reset' for Las Vegas. Harmon pushed back on fan speculation that a contending team would surrender a young receiver like [[Jalen Waddle]] or [[Chris Olave]] to acquire him, arguing both are better players in their mid-20s and teams don't trade primes like that for a receiver who'll turn 30 next year. He'd rather see the Raiders evaluate their own young receivers -- [[Jack Bech]] and [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]] haven't looked great so far, but [[Tre Tucker]] has been good, and Harmon is intrigued by him as a power slot who has also blocked well.
- 2025-11-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-17]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman thinks Meyers is more than a band-aid in Jacksonville and could be a short-term (next two to three years) cornerstone piece. Meyers reportedly told the team he was excited about the trade, viewing Jacksonville as a team on the come-up. Waldman tied this to a scheme shift he credits [[Brandon Angelo]] with flagging on the Going Deep podcast a couple weeks earlier -- that Brian Thomas Jr. might not be long for the team. Because Trevor Lawrence hasn't been accurate downfield and the two haven't been on the same page, Jacksonville is adjusting to use Meyers and Parker Washington on shorter, inside throws that better fit Lawrence right now, which could set up a future Brian Thomas Jr. trade.
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-08]]): Meyers scored a touchdown and saw increased usage when the game against Indianapolis was still close, and Harmon said his new role - handling the underneath, zone-beating 'dirty work' as the F receiver - is what now allows [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] to stay pinned outside as a true X receiver running vertical routes, a development Harmon views as a net positive for Jacksonville's passing game.
- 2025-12-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (untracked co-host) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Meyers is now clearly Jacksonville's 'lead dog' in the passing game, drawing 10 targets vs Indianapolis (targeted on 33% of his routes) -- no other Jaguars receiver had more than six. Harmon said Jacksonville brought Meyers in specifically to run the move-around role originally intended for Travis Hunter before his injury, and that the offense runs through Meyers ahead of Brian Thomas Jr. right now.
- 2026-01-13 — According to Matt Harmon ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-13]]): a good player who profiled well as Jacksonville's WR2 this season, but Harmon cautioned against projecting him as a true No. 1 receiver -- he turns 29 this offseason and showed limitations when forced to be the centralizing force in the passing game, which is part of why [[Parker Washington]] took off alongside him.
- 2026-02-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-16]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman relays that Jacksonville GM James Gladstone explicitly said, when re-signing/extending Meyers, that he has 'a superpower -- catching the football,' and that not all the team's other receivers have that same trait. Waldman's read: Meyers 'is going nowhere fast.'
