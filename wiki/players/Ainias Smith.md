## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Going Deep - Draft Prospects)]]) [Dynasty]: the episode's title subject ("the sleeper miscast as a gadget"), and both hosts are unusually high on him. Waldman calls him "my sleeper in this class" and expects him to outplay his current public perception once he gets an NFL camp. The evaluation rests on fine catch-point technique that a true gadget/gimmick player typically lacks — turning the body away from contact before hitting the ground, tracking the ball on difficult across-the-middle and back-shoulder throws, hands technique on contested catches — plus efficient (not just dynamic) movement in the open field, an explicit contrast with Kadarius Toney, whom Angelo describes as dynamic but inefficient, "moving in circles around himself." Both flag the same risk regardless of talent: getting typecast early as a gadget/gimmick piece (comps: Lynn Bowden Jr., LaViska Shenault) and never escaping a marginal role — the preferred career shape instead compared to Danny Woodhead, whose value was largely unlocked outside his original drafting team. Both expect a Day 2-or-3 pick, discounted further by a lingering injury, and call him a diamond in the rough at that price. See [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]].
