# Jam Miller

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said Miller's speed and acceleration to the second level are the key to his projection -- on 2024 tape he split linebackers and safeties for field-flipping gains, but he played hurt in 2025 and that burst wasn't there. Waldman is uncertain whether he retains enough second-level acceleration to be dangerous at the next level.
- 2026-04-09 — According to Paul Pertichese, guest analyst on the [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-09]] (not a tracked expert) [Dynasty]: Alabama's Jam Miller offers quickness and stop-start ability and can handle a bit more workload than a smaller change-of-pace back like [[DeMond Claiborne]], projecting as a functional Day 3 (round 4-5) back with real fantasy viability if given opportunity. But Pertichese cautions the whole back half of this class -- Miller included -- lacks a standout trait combination, so even a solid rookie year is likely to be short-lived once a team upgrades the position via free agency or a later draft, the way Kansas City added Kenneth Walker III or New Orleans added a veteran outside piece.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]): Waldman said Miller would be the likely beneficiary if Rhamondre Stevenson gets hurt, describing him as more of a grind-it-out back who can pick up 2-4 yards a carry without much negative variance, versus [[Lan Larison]], who profiles as the more explosive, higher-ceiling option if TreVeyon Henderson goes down instead.
