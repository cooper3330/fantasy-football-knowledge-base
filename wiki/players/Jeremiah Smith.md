# Jeremiah Smith

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-11 — According to guest co-host Felix Sharpe of Campus to Canton (NOT a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman]]'s show ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-11]]) [Dynasty]: Smith is the highest-rated wide receiver recruit of all time — Julio Jones is number three on that list and Percy Harvin number five. At 6-3, 215 and 18 years old, the preseason question was not how good he is but whether he would even start in a deep Ohio State receiver room; instead he is leading the team in receiving at over 100 yards per game through two games in a Chip Kelly offense that wants to run. Sharpe cites end-zone body control on a fade and a 70-yard catch-and-run on a five-yard hitch against Western Michigan. He rates Smith a top-of-the-board prospect three years out and says he tried to trade [[Keon Coleman]] plus [[Jonathan Brooks]] for the 1.01 in a Campus to Canton league to get him, and was turned down.
- 2025-02-04 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-04]]: at 18 years old Smith is 'out here just balling', and part of what you see on tape is scheme — watching Ohio State you wonder how he keeps seeing single coverage on the boundary, and the answer is that Chip Kelly threatened defenses on the ground and in the seams first, then designed shot plays that at minimum give his boundary receiver a 50-50 ball down the sideline. Koh's point is that in the NFL a 50-50 ball deep down the sideline is all you want. [[Matt Harmon]] agrees Kelly did a good job clearing out space for Smith while [[Emeka Egbuka]] handled the grimy underneath work. This is a comment on Kelly's design as much as on Smith.
- 2026-10-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-10-22 (Going Deep Eagles & 2027 QB Class)]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman says if he's an NFL front office, Smith is his guy over Oklahoma State's [[Drew Mestemaker]] for the top overall pick, calling him a 'pre-All-Pro' even as a non-quarterback. Grading receivers since 2019, Waldman puts Smith at the top of his all-time board alongside [[Ja'Marr Chase]] and [[DeVonta Smith]] -- higher than he had [[Justin Jefferson]] or [[Travis Hunter]] (whom he graded as a pure receiver, ignoring the defensive-back tape) coming out of college. Comparing Smith to a pre-injury [[Malik Nabers]], Waldman gives Smith a slight edge for giving quarterbacks a more 'indefensible frame' -- a bit bigger than Chase, not as fast but a touch faster than Nabers, a strong 50-50 ball winner, good tracker, good after the catch, and a 'walking target magnet' he expects to be a Pro Bowler as a rookie. He flags 2027 as a thin quarterback class beyond Mestemaker and [[Trinidad Chambliss]], with Smith as the one clear alpha-dog weapon in the group.
