## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-08]]): accurate with boundary routes and back-shoulder plays, with a quick release stroke capable of velocity and pinpoint accuracy at times. Waldman's read: NFL-caliber arm talent, but lacking the conceptual acumen to earn a long-term backend roster spot without more playing experience elsewhere first.
