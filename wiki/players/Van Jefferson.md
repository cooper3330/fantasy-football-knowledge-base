- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman pushed back on a report suggesting Van Jefferson's prior yardage/reception totals point to starter upside in Pittsburgh, saying 'potential' at this stage of his career just means it hasn't happened yet -- his catching remains inconsistent and his speed isn't good enough to threaten the third level without slow-developing plays, which can fit Arthur Smith's scheme in theory but doesn't make him more than a cheap, replaceable stopgap. He expects Pittsburgh to keep looking for a better WR2 answer until [[Roman Wilson]] proves he's ready to contribute consistently.
- 2025-10-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-14]]): Jefferson -- the player [[Elic Ayomanor]] beat out for Tennessee's starting X job -- unexpectedly emerged as the Titans' leading receiver in Week 6 after [[Calvin Ridley]] left with an injury, running a route on 79% of Tennessee's dropbacks. Harmon presents this as circumstantial, a byproduct of Ridley's early exit and the Titans' overall receiver-room weakness, rather than a sign Jefferson has taken over the role, and cautions the offense remains one of the worst in the league regardless of who is leading it in targets.
