# Travis Kelce

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: his days as a first-round fantasy pick are over. Flags that a nagging injury from the start of the 2023 season may have quietly affected him all year — "he never seemed quite right" — and is non-committal on buying back in even at a discount: "he may not drop far enough" to be worth it.
- 2024-01-01 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert), same episode, floats a self-described "tinfoil hat" theory that Kelce's heavy media/marketing visibility late in the season is a deliberate cash-grab ahead of a decline he can see coming. Waldman's only response is a wry "I think we're all in that same boat" — neither endorsing nor rejecting it.
- 2024-01-15 — Guest **Daniel Harms** (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-15]] [Redraft (Standard)]: pushes back hard on the January 1 decline read, and it's recorded here as a live disagreement rather than resolved. His case: Kelce played through two significant injuries this season rather than declining on talent — a Week 1 hyperextension/bone bruise he returned from after a single week rather than the usual 3-4, and an ankle injury in Week 4 vs. Minnesota — and was still the only Chiefs offensive player defenses feared all season, evidenced by opponents finally dedicating a true double-team after Kansas City's blowout of the Chargers. Predicts "at least two more seasons" of real production once he's fully healthy and Kansas City gets any secondary receiving threat to pull attention away — directly contradicting Waldman's January 1 "his days as a first-round pick are over."
