# Jalen McMillan

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): has enough skill to become a weekly contributor who can do extensive work in a starting lineup early in his career, if he lands the right scheme and depth-chart fit. A smooth operator with releases and route setups, he creates after the catch with or without blockers — makes the first man miss, sometimes the second — and sets up his lead blocker well in space. The flaw: in higher-traffic situations with multiple blockers, he doesn't always recognize when he has a wide-open lane and stay patient behind his blocks, and Waldman has seen him veer into unblocked defenders when he had a clear path to the end zone (still scored anyway, just made it harder on himself). Washington teammate of [[Ja'Lynn Polk]].
- 2024-04-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-23]]) [Dynasty]: McMillan tested very similarly to teammate [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] (6'1"/197, 4.47 40 — actually faster than Polk — 37" vertical, 10'7" broad) but Harmon felt he played slower on tape. To avoid another 'Rasheed Rice situation,' Harmon sampled early healthy 2023 games plus 2022 tape rather than McMillan's injury-affected midseason 2023 stretch. Alignment was almost exclusively slot (83.1% slot snaps, 77.5% off-line, only 4.8% backfield), and downfield numbers were poor: 48% success on the nine, 47% on the post (run on 12% of routes) — which co-host James Koh (untracked) called 'bonkers bad,' calling the profile a 'non-starter' as a slot-only player; Harmon pushed back on that framing but shared the concern. Zone success was 74.1%, well below Harmon's ~80% threshold for slot viability, though man coverage success (69.2%) and slant/dig marks (85%+/83%) were strong. Harmon compared his projected role to Hunter Renfrow — a player Harmon has long felt is overrated by the public. Production dropped from a strong healthy 2022 (79-1,096-9) to an injury-hampered 2023 (560 yards, 5 TD); Harmon attributed the flip partly to Polk developing and passing him on the depth chart rather than pure health, and currently projects McMillan as a Day 3 pick, well behind Polk's Day 2/priority-Round-2 grade.
- 2024-05-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-06]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman expects Jalen McMillan to push immediately for the Buccaneers' WR3 role, and says he graded McMillan slightly ahead of [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] in this WR class, projecting a Chris Godwin-style long-term ceiling -- capable of working inside with real outside utility too. He points to draft capital as the tell: Tampa Bay spent significantly better draft capital on McMillan this year than they spent on [[Trey Palmer]] last year, signaling the front office doesn't view Palmer as their long-term slot answer even after Palmer's improved 2023 receiving season. Waldman flags Palmer and Rakim Jarrett as camp names who could still push past McMillan for a year if either has a strong camp, but McMillan remains his preferred long-term dynasty asset of the group.
- 2024-07-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-25]]): McMillan charts as a slot-majority player — 83.1% of sampled snaps from the slot, 4.8% in the backfield, 77.5% aligned behind the line of scrimmage — but his coverage data cuts the other way, at the 49th percentile vs man and only the 19th vs zone. Harmon says that split is exactly why Tampa seems intent on moving him outside rather than into the slot, since [[Chris Godwin]] is going back inside and [[Mike Evans]] owns the X; McMillan could take flanker snaps. He expects him to win on a small handful of routes — slants, digs, over-the-middle work — and compares the odd profile to [[Demario Douglas]], a man-beating slot whose chart looks slot-ish but whose coverage data does not. Koh notes the third-round capital, 6-foot-1, 200 pounds, a 4.47 and a 37-inch vertical, and that a 2023 knee injury muddied his final college year.
- 2024-08-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-22]]): McMillan is his long-term monitor — in three to five years, maybe one of the better number-two receivers in the NFL, comped to a 'Darius Slayton plus' type, which Angelo means as a compliment. He expects McMillan to start immediately in three-receiver sets alongside [[Chris Godwin]] and [[Mike Evans]], with Godwin bumping into the slot and Evans on one side at X, leaving McMillan to move around at Z — in Angelo's opinion his most natural position. The caveat is distribution: it only works if [[Baker Mayfield]] can feed all three. Angelo's pitch is a player who grows into a larger role while getting real game experience from day one.
