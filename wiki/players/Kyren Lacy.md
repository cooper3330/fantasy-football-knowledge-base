# Kyren Lacy

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24]]): the LSU receiver's game is almost there as a route runner and pass catcher — a capable receiver who, if he works on his positioning before the catch point, can box out defenders before attacking the ball. Waldman's ceiling comp if Lacy is dedicated to his craft is a [[Noah Brown]] type: a possession receiver doing the dirty work over the middle and against zone. Lacy may have more athletic explosion than Brown, but Waldman saw no evidence of enough to make him a future starter on the outside unless his entire game becomes razor sharp.
