# Jaxson Dart

## Expert Takes

- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]): Waldman remains skeptical of Dart, comparing him to Will Levis and to [[Kyler Murray]] in his tendency to bounce plays outside rather than stay in structure -- except, unlike Murray, Waldman doesn't think Dart can throw accurately more than 25 yards downfield. Waldman expects the Giants to shut down the scrambling approach that worked last year and for [[Jameis Winston]] to take over, which is part of why he likes [[Malachi Fields]] as a post-QB-change value add.
- 2026-08-21 — According to cousin Josh (not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-21]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Dart is Josh's #4 frag player. His case leans on the 14-game 2025 sample where Dart ran for 9 rushing touchdowns and 487 rushing yards but topped 200 passing yards in only 6 games; Josh remains unsure Dart is a good enough passer, pegging him QB16 against a QB9-ish ADP (end of round 7, picks in the 80s). He flags a hidden injury red flag: Dart was in the medical tent in nearly 40% of his starts once preseason is included, playing under OC Matt Nagy, whom Josh calls 'an absolute nightmare' from his Chicago stint. The weapons are unproven too -- [[Malik Nabers]]'s exact role is unclear, Colston Loveland is recovering from injury. Josh dismisses the Lamar Jackson rushing-QB comp, noting Jackson's rushing TDs typically run only 3-4 a year; he'd rather draft the running backs and receivers going after Dart in ADP (Blake Corum, Chris Godwin, George Kittle, Jordan Mason) or a cheaper veteran QB (Stafford, Goff, Purdy). Chris Harris fully agrees, also has Dart at QB16, and separately notes his own scare during a preseason game where Dart appeared woozy after a hit, though it reportedly wasn't ruled a concussion.
