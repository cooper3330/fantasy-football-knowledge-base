# Hassan Haskins

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the No. 2 Week 6 waiver add behind [[Kimani Vidal]] for both Harris and guest Brandon Funston of The Athletic (not tracked), with [[Omarion Hampton]] on IR for at least four weeks. Funston's argument: Haskins has history with Jim Harbaugh and a trust factor, and Vidal isn't known for pass protection with the Chargers' tackles out — but Haskins is 'kind of a one-note player, just a big physical guy' unlikely to feature in the passing game. Harris' counter-angle is special teams: Haskins is a core Chargers special teamer, so why would they pull him from that role, which makes Vidal the likelier surprise as the 'Austin Ekeler type body type' little dude with power. Both admit they are guessing — 'we can talk until we're blue in the face about how this is probably a timeshare, and then the Cardinals absolutely wasn't this past week' — and both would place a contingency bid, Haskins a dollar or two under Vidal.
- 2025-10-13 — According to [[Chris Harris]] with Chris Raybon (guest, not a tracked expert; [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-13]]): an NFL Network report pegged Haskins as the 'expected lead back' for the Chargers, and Harris said he believed it -- 'they love him, they love him all camp.' Instead Kimani Vidal played 43 snaps to Haskins' 19 and had an 'Ekeler-esque' performance with roughly 100 rushing yards, which Harris called a big miss on his part. Raybon agreed the Chargers' backfield plan flipped, and both noted the Dolphins' run defense (among the league's worst by rush EPA entering the week) makes the sample hard to fully trust going forward.
- 2025-10-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Dave Kluge of FootballGuys.com (not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-16 (Week 7 Flex Decisions)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris ranks Hassan Haskins outside his top 40 running backs and calls him 'slow,' explicitly not advocating starting him even though reports had suggested he could see Chargers early-down work. Kluge floated that a tougher Week 7 matchup against the Colts, where the Chargers may lean more on power/kick-out schemes away from their weak offensive tackles, could tilt some volume toward Haskins over [[Kimani Vidal]], but called that only a possibility, not an expectation.
