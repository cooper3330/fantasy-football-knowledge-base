# Luke Musgrave

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]]): had Musgrave ranked 35th overall pre-draft (7th tight end) — higher than most of the industry, on athletic ability — but in hindsight calls him "slightly overrated" relative to teammate [[Tucker Kraft]], whom he had in the same tier 15 spots lower on his board. Grades Musgrave's speed as an asset but sees him as less refined than Kraft in the areas where tight ends get most of their targets.

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Dynasty]: reiterates that his athletic ability and speed are real, well-liked traits, but treats the comparison to [[Tucker Kraft]] as "more of a price thing than anything else" — Kraft offers more in the middle of the field and the shallow areas at a lower cost.
- 2024-07-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-12]]): his prospect model liked Musgrave more than Michael Mayer coming out of college, and he expects Musgrave to reclaim the '1A' receiving tight end role in Green Bay this year over Tucker Kraft. He pushed back on the narrative that Kraft passed Musgrave down the stretch last season, noting Musgrave was playing at less than 100% off injury during that late-season stretch, and that Musgrave's per-route and target-share numbers pointed to him as the lead tight end when healthy. Waldman also likes Musgrave's 'climbable depth chart': Green Bay's WR room (Dontayvion Wicks, Romeo Doubs, Jayden Reed, Christian Watson) is talented but no one is a locked-in 20%+ target-share player, leaving room for a tight end to exceed projection -- he put a 15% target share, which he said would make Musgrave a TE1, well within Musgrave's range.
