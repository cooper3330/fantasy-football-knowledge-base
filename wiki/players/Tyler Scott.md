# Tyler Scott

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: scores in the bottom tier of Adam Harstad's [[Post-Rookie-Year Receiver Model]]. Waldman says he was "overrated by a lot in the draft community" on speed and athleticism rather than substance, and that Chicago used him mostly as a one-dimensional RPO/deep-shot option rather than developing the more complete route tree some hoped he'd offer — comped to what the Bears originally envisioned from Darnell Mooney before Mooney became a more well-rounded receiver. Sees a path to becoming that kind of all-around piece over time, but not yet.
