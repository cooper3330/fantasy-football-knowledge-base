# Mario Anderson

> with moderate confidence, to Mario Anderson, the Memphis running back who

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): flagged while discussing Memphis's running back pipeline (Tony Pollard, Darrell Henderson, Kenneth Gainwell) as the program's next name to know — a Newberry College transfer with a South Carolina prep background who "knows how to hit a hole," is "shifty," and draws a size/balance comp to [[Ray Davis]]. Not blazing fast, but Waldman is explicit: "I couldn't stop watching him... I'm a fan of that dude."
- 2025-04-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-03]]) [Dynasty]: the Memphis back is another player Waldman thinks he is too bearish on, purely because no combine invite meant no confirmation of his speed. At South Carolina he watched Anderson outrun SEC cornerbacks and safeties who had angles on him in the open field, and the miles-per-hour figures he measured were promising enough that a combine time would likely have closed the circle. Lacking it, Waldman deliberately played it safe and ranked him 15th on his running back board — still high relative to consensus, but below where he suspects Anderson belongs.
- 2025-04-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-28 (Feel It or F-It Post-Draft)]]): a genuine slipped-through-the-cracks UDFA who signed with the Rams. Anderson (Newberry, then South Carolina, then Memphis) has better speed than people realize, runs tough and physical after contact, moves well, catches the ball, and is very reliable holding onto the football. The comp is a short, low-center-of-gravity build — Waldman says with [[Ashton Jeanty]]-level speed he would be a D'Angelo Williams type, and calls him a bit better than the previous bowling-ball Anderson the Rams had. He fits Sean McVay's stated type, the same build as [[Kyren Williams]] and [[Blake Corum]], and the Rams have hit on late-round backs before. Caveat: purely a deep-roster or practice-squad-stash monitor in case of injury, and Waldman thinks in a weaker RB class he would have been a fifth or sixth rounder.
