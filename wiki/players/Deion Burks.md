# Deion Burks

## Expert Takes

- 2026-03-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-03-03]]): Deion Burks, a player Harmon hadn't yet charted (about 3-4 games logged across roughly 14 prospects so far), stood out physically at the combine -- 26 bench press reps at a listed 180 lbs (Harmon called it 'outrageous,' noting DK Metcalf's combine bench record is 27), a 4.34 40, and a 42.5-inch vertical at 5'10, 180 lbs. Harmon flagged that Jordan Tyson's widely-circulated bench reps looked technically dubious (hips visibly off the bench) and wasn't sure if Burks' reps held to the same standard, but called him 'jacked' and 'freaking cut' regardless. Daniel Jeremiah is reportedly a fan. Harmon has not watched Burks' college film yet and stressed this is pure athletic testing, not a scouting evaluation, so the film review is still pending.
- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Burks stacks defenders once he earns early separation and paces his stems and setups well, which Waldman calls the 'invisible thread' that turns technically sound routes into ones that actually compel defenders out of position. His main weakness is his breaks -- he needs flatter turns so defenders can't undercut them -- and he's had communication breakdowns with his quarterback over when to settle versus continue across the field.
- 2026-04-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-07]]) [Dynasty]: a personal 'keep a candle lit' day-two/day-three flier despite a production profile that 'stinks out loud' and a small frame (5'9', 180 lbs) that runs counter to where the league is trending. Harmon likes his toughness and how well he settles into the ball on contested targets, and jokingly flags himself as a repeat victim of the 'small separator' bias. His pitch to dynasty drafters who lean on production models: Burks is cheap enough (fourth round of a startup) to speculate on with minimal downside if he never hits.
- 2026-04-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-14]]): Burks 'absolutely crushed' the combine at 5'10', 180 lbs -- a 4.3-flat 40, 42.5' vertical, ~10'11' broad, and 26 bench reps -- but Harmon flags a disturbing production profile, citing JJ Zachariason's 'ZAP' model note that Burks posted a 1.35 career yards-per-route-run rate, third-worst in the class since 2020; among the 24 sub-1.5 YPRR receivers the model has tracked entering the league, the best-case outcome was Josh Palmer, an acceptable but unspectacular WR3. In Harmon's RP sample, Burks ran sub-71% success vs. man and 81.6% vs. zone, lined up on one side of the field 70.3% of the time (a red flag for alignment diversity), and had short arms (12th-percentile wingspan) that Harmon says showed up on tape -- he never saw Burks climb the ladder for a contested catch. Strengths: elite short-area burst/twitch, toughness fighting through tacklers bigger than him, and excellent in-breaking route success (88% on slants, 88%+ on posts, 80.7% on curls). Harmon comps his athletic profile to Jalen Noel but ranks Noel several tiers higher as a prospect; co-host James Koh floats the Rams (with a Rams WR need behind Puka Nacua/Davante Adams) and a Marvin Mims-type role as a plausible fit given Sean McVay's motion-heavy scheme.
