# David Moore

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-11-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-25]]): Moore's game against the Chiefs was the best Waldman has ever seen from him, and he 'literally looked like a starter.' Known in Seattle purely as a deep-ball winner — Waldman's comp was a Breshad Perriman type who just wins deep targets — Moore showed far more here: slants, work over the middle, toughness, tight catches, and zone route-running good enough for [[Bryce Young]] to favor him. Waldman floated a [[Kendrick Bourne]] career arc as the optimistic path. The heavy caveat is what it says about Carolina: Bob Harris (not tracked) framed Moore being one of the three best receivers on the roster as proof the Panthers must go back to the drawing board and find Young a primary target. For now, a bench player you start when you need him.
- 2024-11-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-26]]): Moore led Carolina in targets, routes run and receiving yards (10 targets, six catches, 81 yards, the only [[Bryce Young]] touchdown, and nearly a second) at 12.5 air yards per target, but Harmon reads the day as a reflection of Young rather than of Moore. He notes Moore was the player Carolina slid into [[Diontae Johnson]]'s vacated X spot after the trade, which speaks to Dave Canales' trust and Moore's familiarity with the scheme from Seattle and Tampa. Coming out of the bye, [[Xavier Legette]] moved to X and Moore played flanker with [[Adam Thielen]] in the slot and [[Jalen Coker]] hurt. Harmon expects him to fall back to an auxiliary fourth-receiver or depth role once Coker returns; Koh is more curious whether the role sticks.
