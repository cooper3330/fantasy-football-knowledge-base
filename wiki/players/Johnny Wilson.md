# Johnny Wilson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): 6'6"/231 lbs, not only a quick accelerator but someone who eats a lot of cushion fast, with violent hands to counter defenders at the line or at the top of the stem, and patient-but-sudden footwork to set those hands up. Doesn't have a full library of release techniques yet — Waldman's comp is a pitcher with a great fastball, a strong curve and a decent slider who just needs more experience to add breaking pitches — but thinks that's already enough to "strike out a lot of batters," i.e. earn separation against man-to-man coverage at his size.
- 2024-04-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-25]]) [Dynasty]: called FSU's Johnny Wilson his most intriguing evaluation precisely because he has no confident projection -- a 6'6"/231 X receiver with a shockingly poor 10-inch-hand profile despite the frame, comping him to [[Devin Funchess]] and, athletically (4.52 forty, 37" vertical at that size), to size/speed outliers like the late Vincent Jackson or Malcolm Floyd if the hands come around. Ran a true X-receiver alignment (72.8% on the line, 48.1% outside-right, only 10% slot) with solid man/press success rates (71.4%/74%), good release moves and strong short-route sink-and-drive ability, but bad hands and poor contested-catch performance. Harmon is wary teams will rush him to tight end -- arguing he shows zero tight-end usage on tape -- and says he'd try him at receiver first for two to three years before considering a position change.
- 2024-05-17 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-17]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo names Wilson as a player he wasn't high on pre-draft but is now more willing to project positively given his landing spot — Philadelphia drafted him for traits ('we just draft freaks'), and there's an open third-receiver role behind the set trio of [[A.J. Brown]], [[DeVonta Smith]] and [[Ainias Smith]] that no one has claimed yet.
- 2024-06-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-25]]) [Dynasty]: Wilson is a developmental big-bodied X-receiver project for Philadelphia -- Harmon notes his Reception Perception rookie profile showed no tight end usage in college despite some speculation he could be a hybrid piece, projecting him as a pure outside X-receiver depth option behind [[A.J. Brown]] and [[DeVonta Smith]].
- 2024-08-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-08]]): Harmon says he is 'obsessed with the Johnny Wilson storyline' and disagrees with co-host James Koh (not tracked) that Wilson projects as a big slot — his rookie-roundup charting had Wilson outside on 88.9% of sampled snaps and on the line of scrimmage 72.9%, a true college X. Harmon charted a 71.4% success rate versus man and 74.1% versus press and was surprised how well the 6-6, 230-pound receiver got open against man. Via Derrick Brown of FantasyPros: Wilson's career yards per route run was 2.52 (best season 3.36) against [[Keon Coleman]]'s 1.87 (best 2.07) at Florida State. The caveat is some of the worst contested-catch hands Harmon has seen from a player that size. He dismisses the move-to-tight-end idea and comps him to Malcolm Floyd, arguing a real sacrificial X lets Philadelphia slot [[DeVonta Smith]] and move [[A.J. Brown]] around.
