## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): 6'6"/231 lbs, not only a quick accelerator but someone who eats a lot of cushion fast, with violent hands to counter defenders at the line or at the top of the stem, and patient-but-sudden footwork to set those hands up. Doesn't have a full library of release techniques yet — Waldman's comp is a pitcher with a great fastball, a strong curve and a decent slider who just needs more experience to add breaking pitches — but thinks that's already enough to "strike out a lot of batters," i.e. earn separation against man-to-man coverage at his size.
