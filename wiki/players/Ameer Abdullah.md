# Ameer Abdullah

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-12-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-30]]): Abdullah is his example of a player carrying an inaccurate label. Waldman has always liked him — an early-round Detroit pick given a starting job he fumbled away, then a journeyman through Minnesota, Carolina and now Las Vegas — and credits him with versatility, good speed and excellent contact balance for his size, plus more burst than [[Alexander Mattison]]. His point is that once a player becomes a journeyman, coaches and scouts label him by what he last did: 'good on special teams, good third-down option.' Waldman does not expect a 30-or-31-year-old Abdullah to become a startable fantasy back, but warns that 'we know who he is' often means teams *think* they know, citing Cedric Peerman as a special-teams-labeled back who produced when finally given carries. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) admits he talked himself out of Abdullah and into Mattison in a semifinal loss, despite two straight 19-point games, because of that receiving-back label.
