# Germie Bernard

## Expert Takes

- 2026-02-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-09]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is not buying the buzz on Bernard as a nuanced route runner, saying he lacks the ability to contrast patience and suddenness within a release or to set up breaks -- tools he has physically but hasn't consistently shown on tape. He compares Bernard to last year's [[Tre Harris]], a good athlete who 'got away with a lot' in the SEC and needed two-to-three years before emerging as a starter-caliber talent, rather than a plug-and-play early contributor.
- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Bernard's route running won't be confused with prime [[Robert Woods]], but Waldman likes his library of release maneuvers and the patience/suddenness contrast he executes them with. His setups are the weak link -- not enough variety in his portfolio -- so even though his breaks are reasonably clean, he'll often face tight coverage at the catch point if targeted, since he won't have earned separation with setups the way he did against inferior college athletes. Waldman calls this a hidden trap door: if Bernard is rushed into a big role early, those technical lapses could be a real obstacle, projecting him more as a schemed-open WR2 than a true primary option for now.
- 2026-04-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-07]]) [Dynasty]: in a rapid-fire comparison, both preferred [[Antonio Williams]] over Bernard, with Waldman noting Bernard lets the ball into his frame too often and has catch-technique issues, comping him to a 'generic Cola version of Robert Woods' with Van Jefferson-style journeyman bust risk if he doesn't develop further. Harmon pushed back slightly, arguing Bernard's work on dig routes over the middle fits the current NFL meta and some teams will value him purely as a glue guy. Both project him as a likely NFL WR3.
- 2026-05-07 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-07 (Going Deep with Brandon Angelo)]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo rates Bernard well above [[Matt Waldman]]'s early-third-round grade (Bernard's rookie ADP sits around pick 20), calling him the Colts' 'easy-button' receiver. His case: Mike McCarthy's slot receivers over the last decade -- [[CeeDee Lamb]] and Randall Cobb -- have averaged 136 targets a year (Cobb's low was 109, in 2017), and Bernard profiles as a Robert Woods-type slot/motion piece who extends the run game. Angelo argues [[DK Metcalf]] is what he is at this stage and [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] hasn't been the same since his back injury, leaving a real target vacancy; Indy spending a day-two pick on Bernard, rather than trading up for Makai Lemon the way a rival team did, signals the archetype -- tough, combative, run-after-catch over the middle -- they wanted. Waldman was lower, citing an unsettled quarterback picture (a possibly late-arriving Aaron Rodgers, or a Will Howard/Mason Rudolph rotation) as a risk to Bernard actually hitting that target share early.
- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon likes Germie Bernard as a high-floor 'glue guy' type prospect who should get an instant role, calling him a body-type match for [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] -- a big Z receiver who can kick inside. Harmon frames the value here as long-term/dynasty and as a waiver-wire speed-dial watch: a good training camp could put Bernard on the radar as the direct beneficiary if Pittman or [[DK Metcalf]] gets hurt, since Harmon doesn't expect Metcalf to suddenly develop short-area technician skills even if given more slot snaps. Harris agrees this is worth tracking specifically for long-term planning purposes rather than an immediate 2026 fantasy relevance.
- 2026-09-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-09-01]]): 'as good as advertised' in the preseason -- a slot/movement Z who should work in tandem with [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] as a power-slot option. Harmon says the narrative that [[Roman Wilson]] would be the Steelers' third receiver is dead, and that job should now belong to Bernard, especially with [[DK Metcalf]] dealing with a mysterious camp injury and Pittman himself banged up -- making Bernard a potential fantasy waiver-wire speed dial. Harmon calls him pro-ready and possibly a better real-life player than fantasy asset, but says he'll play a real role in a Pittsburgh offense expected to run heavy 11-personnel spread sets.
