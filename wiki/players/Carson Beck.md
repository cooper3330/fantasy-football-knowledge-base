# Carson Beck

## Expert Takes

- 2024-09-25 — According to guest Felix Sharpe of Campus2Canton (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman]]'s show ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-25 (Campus2Canton with Felix Sharpe)]]) [Dynasty]: Beck is 'boring and good' — he throws receivers open, likes the back shoulder and the middle of the field, and is aggressive attacking the intermediate and deep areas. A real pocket passer who will not impress with rushing ability but can move and avoid pressure. Now in his second year as a starter with full command of the offense after out-lasting fellow elite recruit Brock Vandagriff, and for Sharpe's money he is the first quarterback off the board in his draft class.
- 2024-10-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-23]]) [Dynasty]: after Beck's three-interception, 56%-completion game against Texas (eight interceptions on the season), Waldman says he is actively trying to TRADE FOR him and remains very high — he had Beck top-15 when that was considered crazy, Beck finished as his QB1 last year, and Waldman believes he still has him as his No. 1 quarterback. His read on the three picks: one forced over the middle on an in-breaking route with a zone defender present, one a tipped ball he could have placed better to TE Oscar Delp, and the highlight one a back-shoulder throw the receiver ran as a conventional hitch (the corner caught it outside the receiver). The core argument: Beck is the same player as last year without the same talent around him, having lost [[Brock Bowers]] and [[Ladd McConkey]] with no replacement, while Georgia now leans on him to throw far more than it traditionally has. He remains an aggressive downfield passer who improvises within the pocket by stepping up to the second or third read, and — like [[Matthew Stafford]] — an interception does not faze him or turn him into 'check down Charlie.' Co-host Felix Sharpe (not tracked) notes the consensus has Beck falling behind [[Cam Ward]] and Shedeur Sanders, both of whom have arguably the two best receiving corps in the country; Sharpe also credits Georgia's defense with carrying that game.
- 2026-02-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-09]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman expects Beck to go on day two, likely round 2-3, because teams will lean on his prior first-day-pick pedigree and the 'we can develop an older QB' precedent set by Tyler Shough succeeding under Kellen Moore. Beck offers prototype size, technically sound mechanics, play-action/under-center and pistol reps, and enough legs to move the chains for 5-7 yards (occasionally 15-20 vs. soft coverage). But Waldman sees a processing ceiling he may never clear: Beck reads coverage accurately only when the read is simple (two defenders in the throw window) and throws 'a lot of hospital balls' and contested/defended passes once a third defender or an adjacent-zone receiver complicates the picture -- a problem that compounds against NFL-level disguised coverage. Waldman: 'he's basically got the physical talent of a pro starter... but the diagnostic, processing, management talent is not there yet.'
- 2026-04-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-09]]) [Dynasty]: lower than public consensus in the opposite direction -- Waldman thinks Carson Beck could actually go in the first or early second round given his physical prototype (arm strength, size, experience at two winning programs) and that he processes some reads well, but flags a persistent, unresolved flaw: Beck doesn't account for the 'ancillary' third defender in a passing triangle, repeatedly throwing into windows a peripheral defender can undercut, producing what Waldman called 'hospital balls.' He noted this pattern has drawn public criticism from [[Michael Thomas]], who Waldman said is having flashbacks to his frustrations with [[Derek Carr]]. Waldman compared it to a narrower, more fixable version of the same issue in [[Garrett Nussmeier]], who only misses the ancillary defender on outside/intermediate-to-vertical throws versus Cover 2 -- whereas Beck shows the blind spot everywhere he throws.
- 2026-04-16 — According to Dalton Wasserman (guest draft analyst, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-16]]) [Dynasty]: of the three faded former blue-chips ([[Cade Klubnick]], [[Drew Allar]], [[Carson Beck]]), Wasserman calls Beck 'the classic backup-type quarterback' -- a Jared Goff-style pocket passer without Goff's arm talent, who sees the field and gets the ball out quickly. He floated Beck as a plausible long-term NFL backup, joking about the 'your backup should mirror your starter' theory pointing him toward Detroit given the Goff comp.
- 2026-07-21 — According to Ben Gretsch (Stealing Signals newsletter, not a tracked expert), discussed with [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-21]]): Gretsch wants to hear preseason reports on rookie Beck, since young quarterbacks sometimes reveal a lot in exhibition action; he expects that if Beck plays a significant amount, it would probably be a negative for the Cardinals' overall passing volume relative to [[Jacoby Brissett]], since young QBs tend to hold the ball longer, take more sacks, and scramble more instead of consistently getting to pass attempts.
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman fully feels Beck has the edge over [[Gardner Minshew]] to be [[Jacoby Brissett]]'s backup, based purely on draft capital -- Arizona wants Beck game-ready as a potential starter if Brissett can't keep them competitive through midway-to-three-quarters of the season. His expected hierarchy: if Brissett struggles and the team is out of contention, Beck gets the QB2/spot-start opportunities; for a single-game injury replacement need, Minshew (whom Waldman calls capable as a 'QB15'-level fill-in) plays first, with Beck stepping in if the injury extends beyond one week. Bob Harris (not tracked) agrees with this read.
- 2026-08-10 — According to Bob Harris and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-10]]) [Dynasty]: a strong preseason outing puts Beck in play to start earlier than expected if Arizona struggles early, since Harris expects the front office to press the issue even before it's merit-based. A veteran scout source told Harris that Beck made nice throws to the middle of the field and handled a two-high-style defensive shell reasonably well in the preseason game. Waldman is skeptical that one game against a lighter defensive plan proves Beck is ready for NFL disguise coverages, and says he'd still rather see Jacoby Brissett start as long as possible -- partly, he admits, because he wants to see Michael Wilson thrive. Waldman's long-term comp for Beck is a 'young Derek Carr,' with hope he eventually surpasses Carr's career peak.
