# Carson Beck

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-25 — According to guest Felix Sharpe of Campus2Canton (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman]]'s show ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-25 (Campus2Canton with Felix Sharpe)]]) [Dynasty]: Beck is 'boring and good' — he throws receivers open, likes the back shoulder and the middle of the field, and is aggressive attacking the intermediate and deep areas. A real pocket passer who will not impress with rushing ability but can move and avoid pressure. Now in his second year as a starter with full command of the offense after out-lasting fellow elite recruit Brock Vandagriff, and for Sharpe's money he is the first quarterback off the board in his draft class.
- 2024-10-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-23]]) [Dynasty]: after Beck's three-interception, 56%-completion game against Texas (eight interceptions on the season), Waldman says he is actively trying to TRADE FOR him and remains very high — he had Beck top-15 when that was considered crazy, Beck finished as his QB1 last year, and Waldman believes he still has him as his No. 1 quarterback. His read on the three picks: one forced over the middle on an in-breaking route with a zone defender present, one a tipped ball he could have placed better to TE Oscar Delp, and the highlight one a back-shoulder throw the receiver ran as a conventional hitch (the corner caught it outside the receiver). The core argument: Beck is the same player as last year without the same talent around him, having lost [[Brock Bowers]] and [[Ladd McConkey]] with no replacement, while Georgia now leans on him to throw far more than it traditionally has. He remains an aggressive downfield passer who improvises within the pocket by stepping up to the second or third read, and — like [[Matthew Stafford]] — an interception does not faze him or turn him into 'check down Charlie.' Co-host Felix Sharpe (not tracked) notes the consensus has Beck falling behind [[Cam Ward]] and Shedeur Sanders, both of whom have arguably the two best receiving corps in the country; Sharpe also credits Georgia's defense with carrying that game.
