# Drake Maye

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his
> name as "Drake May"; normalized here to his real name, Drake Maye.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-15 — Guest **Daniel Harms** (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-15]] [Dynasty]: strongly feeling it — explicitly rejects a "Mitchell Trubisky 2.0" comparison some had floated for a fellow North Carolina passer. His case rests on lower-body mechanics rather than arm talent (which he also grades highly): Maye has what he calls a "spidey sense" for pressure — the same trait he credits to Tom Brady, Patrick Mahomes, and Josh Allen to a degree — that lets him make small in-pocket adjustments to extend plays and avoid sacks, unlike a prospect such as Will Levis, whose lower body he calls "almost completely detached" from his reads. Flags one specific mechanical fix needed: his back foot comes up early on some outside-the-hash throws, causing accuracy misses. [[Matt Waldman]] agrees the feet are "the window into what a quarterback sees" as a general evaluation principle, and reframes some of Maye's sack/pressure numbers as more a product of surrounding talent and offensive circumstances than a pocket-processing problem.
- 2024-01-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-22]]) [Dynasty]: his own first detailed grade, delivered more skeptically than guest Daniel Harms's bullish 2024-01-15 take — a live disagreement, not a resolution of it. Flags Maye as a "high-end accuracy in the box score, but not on the film" case, comparable to Baker Mayfield's college profile: pocket movement grades well, but decision-making and footwork-driven accuracy grade below what the box score suggests. Explicitly floats him as "my Desmond Ritter of this class" — a player who may be overhyped relative to what the tape shows — while flagging more film left to re-watch before finalizing a grade over the following six weeks.
- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]) [Dynasty]: the promised re-watch, and the skepticism holds. Admits he'd worried he "sleepwalked" through the first evaluation and had avoided saying much publicly until rewatching — but the rewatch "didn't change my feeling." Specific diagnosis: can extend plays and take hits, but his ability to diagnose coverage and know what to do with it "consistently at a higher level" is missing. New comp, replacing the earlier Baker Mayfield reference: Jake Locker — a quarterback with all the physical tools (size, arm) but accuracy that was never there — with a stated rule of thumb that a starting-caliber quarterback needs to solve roughly 3-5 of a game's 5-7 "defense has you cornered" moments, and Maye "strikes me as that player" who can't yet do it that consistently. Flags a systemic risk on top of the individual one: the archetype of quarterback North Carolina's system produces (Mitch Trubisky, Sam Howell, Drake Maye — described as "not too far apart on the same wavelength") has not translated to sustained NFL success. Grade if it holds: "my Will Levis, Desmond Ritter" of the class — an explicit downgrade in company from the January 22 Desmond-Ritter-only comp.
