# Malik Willis

## Expert Takes

- 2024-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-20]]), with guest Jagger May noting Willis is rumored to be cut or on the roster bubble in Tennessee: Waldman's ceiling comp for Malik Willis is Tyler Huntley at best. He described Willis as always having been a slower processor of the middle of the field, with the arm talent and movement teams covet but neither the accuracy/placement nor processing speed needed to start. Waldman was skeptical Willis has fixed those flaws behind the scenes and expects him to either wash out, catch on elsewhere as a fringe backup, or be asked to switch positions the way Terrelle Pryor moved from QB to WR and had a genuinely productive year doing it.
- 2024-09-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-19]]): Willis came up twice. Waldman listed him alongside Andy Dalton and [[Skylar Thompson]] as one of three quarterbacks stepping in for injured or benched starters in Week 3, and separately noted Willis was part of the 2022 draft class in which Waldman graded Thompson highest. Co-host Adam Harstad (not a tracked expert) praised Willis' character — asked whether he held a grudge against the Titans, Willis said they paid him, gave him his shot and did right by him — but then said, half-joking, that this is also why Willis will never be great: 'I'm moving him down my fantasy football rankings after that answer,' contrasting him with the Brady, Rodgers and Jordan archetype where 'you've got to be a little bit on the hinge to achieve that level of greatness.' Waldman agreed Willis comes across as a genuinely wholesome, well-adjusted person.
- 2024-09-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-23]]): not a legit NFL starter yet — the crucial qualifier being that no defense has scouted him as a Packer and built a game plan against him. Waldman noted you could have plugged [[Will Levis]], Marcus Mariota, Paxton Lynch, [[Drew Lock]] or Josh Dobbs into the same early-season narrative, and that Tennessee's knowledge of Willis does not transfer because they do not know what Green Bay is doing with him. He gave Willis real credit: low-mistake football, runs the ball very well, a cannon arm, and genuinely good when set up to make quick, accurate decisions — which is exactly what Matt LaFleur, whom Waldman does call a legit NFL coach, has been doing. The risk is when defenses find a way to make him overthink. Waldman allows a path to becoming a real starter; his point is that it was never going to happen immediately, and never on a team that handed him a bad offensive line and asked him to be the savior.
- 2024-11-14 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-14]]): Willis is a live example of a cast-off quarterback resetting his career. A third-stringer in Tennessee, he started for Green Bay after Jordan Love went down and, in Angelo's telling, admitted in post-trade interviews that he had no idea what he was doing in Tennessee and was not equipped to start at that time. Angelo says if you polled GMs now on Willis versus [[Will Levis]], most would take Willis — a reversal he treats as evidence that inventory of information and experience, not traits, is what separates young quarterbacks. He puts Willis alongside [[Anthony Richardson]], Geno Smith and [[Drew Lock]] in the group who have seen the career divide and responded.
- 2024-11-22 — According to Adam Harstad, untracked co-host ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-22]]) [Dynasty]: Willis is a highly drafted backup quarterback Harstad sees sitting on waivers in a lot of leagues and likes as a free lottery ticket. He is explicit that he is not predicting anything — probably Willis is nothing — but there is a really good chance he gets another starting shot and a non-trivial chance of a Brad Johnson or Rich Gannon style late-blooming career. [[Matt Waldman]] added the developmental angle: Willis has admitted he did not know what he was doing in Tennessee, and that admission is itself a positive signal that he has figured out how to work at it. Harstad noted culture and fit matter, but tempered it with the Mitch Trubisky-in-Buffalo warning that team praise often amounts to nothing.
- 2025-02-27 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-27]]): [[Malik Willis]] is the cautionary tale for the 2025 class — Angelo says that if you had not traded him to Matt LaFleur's Green Bay he would probably be out of the league, and that playing a not-ready rookie in year one can sink him permanently. He uses Willis explicitly as the risk case for [[Jalen Milroe]]. [[Matt Waldman]] adds that Willis genuinely needed more development than [[Patrick Mahomes]] did, but what he showed in relief in 2024 'looked more like an NFL quarterback and a guy who wasn't so lost'. Both also recall that the pre-draft market badly missed on Willis's draft slot — expectations of Detroit at two, then a wait until the third round — as evidence that a prospect like [[Shedeur Sanders]] could still slide.
