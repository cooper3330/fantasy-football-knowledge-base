# Anthony Gould

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): "there are few doubts at all" he's capable of developing into an offensive contributor by the end of his first contract, and he could deliver as a rookie in offensive sub-packages — has the skills to challenge off and zone coverage on short, intermediate and vertical routes, and catches the ball well enough to be a competent receiver. Whether he develops into a starter is a bigger question, because he's small for the role: he has the fundamental release/stem/setup/break knowledge, but lacks the craft and detail to execute productively against true press man and even some off-man looks.
