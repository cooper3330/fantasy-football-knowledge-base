# Jacoby Brissett

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-12]]: names him the best available veteran bridge-quarterback option for Minnesota after [[Kirk Cousins]]'s departure to Atlanta — "I think Brissett would be perfect," pairing him with [[Justin Jefferson]] and [[Jordan Addison]] in a competitive-rebuild year — and ranks him ahead of [[Joe Flacco]], [[Gardner Minshew]], Jameis Winston (not tracked) and [[Ryan Tannehill]] among the available veterans. Cites his 2023 Washington tape (showed "very well") and an earlier Cleveland stretch running a top-12 passing offense by EPA/dropback before [[Deshaun Watson]] took the job over and it collapsed, plus an encouraging brief relief stint for Sam Howell (not tracked) last season. Notes he's entering his age-32 season and isn't a long-term answer, just a well-regarded bridge option.

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]]: on Brissett as New England's presumptive bridge/backup — "a competent guy" who's "proven it time and time again" without the ceiling teams look for, but who offers professionalism and locker-room leadership that helps a young quarterback learn how to work at the game; New England would need "a monster team" for him to compete deep into the playoffs, but he can keep games close and competitive as a bridge option.
- 2024-09-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-19]]): New England is an oil-and-water problem — Brissett's strength is standing statuesque in the pocket, eating hits and firing deep shots, but he is under pressure on 44.3 percent of dropbacks per PFF and averaging 4.7 yards per attempt under pressure. Harmon says you cannot run a functional pass game under pressure on nearly half your snaps, and on play action Brissett shows only a 5.4 average depth of target with a 3.6 second time to throw. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) argues the line can give two and a half seconds, maybe 2.7, and that deep passing needs three. Harmon still rates Brissett a good caretaker and one of the better bridge quarterbacks in the league, but not a boot-game or downfield play-action operator.
- 2025-10-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-21 (Week 8 Waivers & W7 Reviews)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Brissett threw a rope on a free play to [[Zay Jones]] and generally played well against Green Bay -- Harris said 'it's hard to argue [he] hasn't been better as a thrower' than [[Kyler Murray]] this season, despite lacking Murray's rushing upside. He did have a sack-fumble playing hero ball in the pocket against a dominant Micah Parsons and was stopped on a fourth-and-short QB sneak that set up Green Bay's winning drive. John Daigle (Establish the Run, not tracked) agreed the offense 'is actually functioning better' with Brissett at QB and both hosts said it's genuinely uncertain who starts for Arizona coming out of their Week 8 bye.
- 2025-11-06 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-06]]: Koh says Brissett is a better fit for this particular Arizona scheme than [[Kyler Murray]] — carefully distinguishing fit from being the better player — and that the fit has helped activate [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]. Harmon agrees and supplies the numbers: Brissett has been under center on 46% of dropbacks versus under 21% for Murray, with 7.5 yards per attempt against sub-seven, which he calls two different offenses. His reasoning is that the league's best offenses now operate under center and throw over the middle of the field, and Brissett simply lets Arizona do that. Gannon announced Brissett would start this week and said he would have made that call anyway because he likes where the offense is. Harmon's caveat: the vindicating performance came against a Cowboys defense that 'stinks out loud.'
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-11]]): treat the 258-yard, two-touchdown line as noise - Brissett put up two fumble-sixes on Arizona's first three drives, it was 28-0 Seattle two plays into the second quarter, and 'literally everything that happened thereafter is basically useless, highly suspect', including the garbage-time touchdown to [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] that saved an 11-target two-catch day. Harris pushes back hard on the people saying Brissett is better than [[Kyler Murray]]: it is fair that Brissett should have taken over full-time, 'but that's because Kyler Murray played bad, not because Brissett is good', and he has personally had Brissett as his own team's starting quarterback twice and 'it's not great'. His message is that this game, not the Cowboys game, is the version of the Brissett Cardinals you should expect.
- 2025-11-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-18]]): Brissett broke the NFL record for completions in a game (47) against San Francisco, completing 82% of his passes with zero sacks taken, in a game the Cardinals otherwise lost badly. Harmon noted the irony that Brissett unlocked a far more productive passing attack -- including [[Michael Wilson]]'s career game -- than [[Kyler Murray]] has managed with the same weapons this season.
- 2025-11-18 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-11-18]]): Brissett finished with 452 yards but Harris says the box score is misleading -- he was 0 touchdowns and 2 interceptions through three quarters with the game at 35-10, and only compiled his two garbage-time touchdowns and bulk yardage once the outcome was decided. Harris's verdict: 'he was not good, in fact, mostly because he's Jacoby Brissett,' while noting the garbage-time volume is exactly what makes him usable purely for fantasy stat-padding in blowouts.
- 2025-11-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-25]]): threw roughly 50 passes against Jacksonville, took a huge hit on the sideline after a scramble that forced him out for part of a series, and was sacked six times as the Jaguars blitzed heavily and played man coverage on the back end. Harris calls him 'clearly a starting quality player' when he can get through his reads and release from his back foot, but says his complete lack of mobility let Jacksonville's pressure package take over the game as it wore on.
- 2025-11-27 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-27]]): Brissett has Arizona leading the NFL in passing yards per game since taking over, and Harmon credits him with 'ripping throws a lot of young guys are not willing to' and understanding the offense conceptually -- a sharp contrast to his poor play with the Patriots last year, underscoring it's about scheme fit rather than talent alone. He notes Arizona is just 1-0 in Brissett's starts so it hasn't translated to wins, but the passing game is 'so much more alive.' Harmon groups Brissett with veterans like Sam Darnold, [[Baker Mayfield]], Daniel Jones and Mac Jones as proof teams should sign steady, tools-plus-experience veterans rather than gamble a premium pick on an unproven young QB in a 2026 draft class he considers 'good but not great.'
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-08]]): Filling in against the Rams, Brissett threw a third-quarter interception deep in his own territory and looked 'completely helpless' any time Arizona sent pressure, extending a career-long pattern. Harris cautioned against reading the early ease of the offense as evidence Brissett solves Arizona's quarterback problem - the team will still be looking for answers at the position next year.
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]): filling in for Arizona, [[Jacoby Brissett]] was efficient in a 30-7 loss to Houston, throwing accurately (66 of his first ~66 yards went to [[Trey McBride]]) despite the deficit largely stemming from an early fumbled kickoff return and a poor first Cardinals series, not Brissett's own play. He nearly hit [[Michael Wilson]] for a second touchdown late in the third quarter but the pass was thrown too far out front and intercepted.
- 2025-12-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-22]]): Waldman expects Brissett to keep the Cardinals' starting job for at least half to three-quarters of the 2026 season even after the draft. His reasoning: he hasn't dug into the incoming quarterback class yet, but early hype around a shifting set of top names is usually a sign the class 'isn't really worthwhile' -- and even a good rookie prospect would likely sit and develop behind Brissett for a year rather than start immediately.
- 2026-02-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-02]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said 'feel it' on Brissett as a viable NFL bridge starter in Arizona, acknowledging the team didn't win many games with him but crediting him with keeping the offense 'on schedule' and giving hope the unit could function the way it's supposed to -- more than [[Kyler Murray]] provided, in his view.
- 2026-02-27 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (untracked co-host) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-27]]): Harmon hopes financial pressure forces Arizona to cut Jacoby Brissett, who is set to make $9 million against just a $1.75 million dead cap hit -- 'at least a cut candidate,' though not certain to actually be released. Both hosts floated Malik Willis as an under-the-radar fit in Atlanta if Brissett becomes available, with Harmon saying Willis would 'thrive' paired with new HC Kevin Stefanski.
- 2026-03-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-03-05]]): Harmon says Brissett could competently execute Arizona's new middle-of-field-heavy scheme for a full season if needed -- he's comfortable throwing over the middle and smart enough to handle pre-snap operations -- but notes persistent trade rumors given his old head coach Frank Reich is now Jets OC; some have floated Brissett starting a tanking 2026 Jets season, which Harmon says would be a boon for Garrett Wilson if it happens.
- 2026-07-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Ben Gretsch (Stealing Signals newsletter, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-21]]): Gretsch flagged Brissett's offseason contract dispute as a real camp storyline worth tracking -- whether he gets the new deal he's wanted, and whether he could hold out into or delay reporting to camp. Both said Brissett's specific style (big arm, stands in the pocket, gets the ball out quickly with relatively few sacks or scrambles) is a major reason Arizona's passing volume was so high in 2025, funneling targets to [[Michael Wilson]] and a huge target share to Trey McBride. Gretsch said if Brissett is clearly the Week 1 starter over rookie [[Carson Beck]], that's a positive for the passing game's overall volume relative to the more conservative offense a young quarterback might produce.
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman stresses Brissett won't get nearly the rope he got last year -- he explicitly expects Brissett will not still be taking all the 11-personnel starter reps if Arizona is at the same underwhelming record by week 13 that led last year's hook, with [[Carson Beck]] or [[Gardner Minshew]] ready to take over depending on the game situation.
