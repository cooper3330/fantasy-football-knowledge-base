## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]]: credits real physical talent as a thrower and fearlessness fitting the ball into tight windows, but doesn't think his 2023 rookie tape proved he's a long-term starter — still has real things to work on in how he sees the field and what he can/can't do in the pocket. Reads the Raiders' signing of [[Gardner Minshew]] as a hedge, not a vote of no-confidence, in O'Connell specifically, with Antonio Pierce's public support framed as standard "opportunity, not job guarantee" coach-speak; expects Las Vegas still drafts a quarterback to develop.
