# Aidan O'Connell

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]]: credits real physical talent as a thrower and fearlessness fitting the ball into tight windows, but doesn't think his 2023 rookie tape proved he's a long-term starter — still has real things to work on in how he sees the field and what he can/can't do in the pocket. Reads the Raiders' signing of [[Gardner Minshew]] as a hedge, not a vote of no-confidence, in O'Connell specifically, with Antonio Pierce's public support framed as standard "opportunity, not job guarantee" coach-speak; expects Las Vegas still drafts a quarterback to develop.

- 2024-04-15 — According to **Hondo Carpenter** (Raiders reporter, SI.com — not a tracked expert), relayed by [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]]: is convinced Antonio Pierce means it when he says O'Connell gets the first shot to start in 2024. Waldman separately notes his own best-ball rooms are already drafting [[Gardner Minshew]] ahead of O'Connell, suggesting the market isn't fully convinced.
- 2024-05-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-13]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman says it's reasonable that new HC Antonio Pierce is 'blowing sunshine' up O'Connell's ass early in camp by publicly naming him the leader over Gardner Minshew — a coach-motivation tactic, since Minshew (proven, well-paid, no longer needing motivation) doesn't need the same treatment while O'Connell, in his first real chance to be 'the guy,' does. On tape, Waldman sees O'Connell flash toughness and arm talent as a pocket passer on middle-of-field throws, but the swing factor is whether he avoids the late, reckless downfield decisions that dogged him at Purdue; if he cleans that up, Waldman thinks he can be 'a very good pocket quarterback' for the Raiders over the next couple years.
- 2024-05-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-17]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman says new Raiders coach Antonio Pierce has made clear this is O'Connell's job to win, not [[Gardner Minshew]]'s, because O'Connell performed solidly as a rookie in a difficult situation last year and the team wants to see whether he's the long-term answer before committing to a bridge veteran.
- 2024-08-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-01]]): Harmon would start O'Connell for the Raiders and see what happens, keeping Gardner Minshew as a break-glass option, unless O'Connell is non-functional in camp — which he does not expect. He grants the Raiders do not have a good quarterback situation and projects them last in the NFL in pass attempts as a result: hide the quarterback, run the football, lean on a passable defense, run few plays. He also notes O'Connell's redeeming trait per the [[Davante Adams]] discussion — he will stand in the barrel, eat a hit and push the ball downfield without getting frenetic, which is why Adams preferred him to Jimmy Garoppolo.
- 2024-08-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-05]]): Waldman expects O'Connell to open the season as the Raiders starter because Antonio Pierce has set the expectation that [[Gardner Minshew]] has to actually win the job, and the culture-first logic of a former-player head coach means the incumbent keeps it if the competition is close — the staff will let O'Connell prove he does not deserve to be on the field rather than hand it over. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) said the reporting has been roughly 50-50, that Kirk Morrison expects O'Connell, and that Hondo Carpenter of SI.com will fight anyone who suggests Pierce was not being straight about the open competition.
- 2024-08-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-19]]): O'Connell makes the team but loses the starting job to [[Gardner Minshew]], and Waldman thinks that is the right call for his development. Bob Harris notes the spring buzz was that the job would not be handed to Minshew and that O'Connell had to be beaten out — but O'Connell handed it over with a fourth-quarter pick-six against Dallas that likely settled the decision. Waldman does not read it as a politically motivated ploy: you do not take a job away without a competition. His development argument is that starting a young QB, letting him fail publicly and then benching him at 0-5 flushes him down the toilet of quarterback potential, while sitting behind a veteran who is effectively a coach on the field lets him work on his weaknesses with intermittent playing time. O'Connell went 5-5 as a starter in 2023.
