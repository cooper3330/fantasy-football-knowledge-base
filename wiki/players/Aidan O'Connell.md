# Aidan O'Connell

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]]: credits real physical talent as a thrower and fearlessness fitting the ball into tight windows, but doesn't think his 2023 rookie tape proved he's a long-term starter — still has real things to work on in how he sees the field and what he can/can't do in the pocket. Reads the Raiders' signing of [[Gardner Minshew]] as a hedge, not a vote of no-confidence, in O'Connell specifically, with Antonio Pierce's public support framed as standard "opportunity, not job guarantee" coach-speak; expects Las Vegas still drafts a quarterback to develop.

- 2024-04-15 — According to **Hondo Carpenter** (Raiders reporter, SI.com — not a tracked expert), relayed by [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]]: is convinced Antonio Pierce means it when he says O'Connell gets the first shot to start in 2024. Waldman separately notes his own best-ball rooms are already drafting [[Gardner Minshew]] ahead of O'Connell, suggesting the market isn't fully convinced.
- 2024-05-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-13]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman says it's reasonable that new HC Antonio Pierce is 'blowing sunshine' up O'Connell's ass early in camp by publicly naming him the leader over Gardner Minshew — a coach-motivation tactic, since Minshew (proven, well-paid, no longer needing motivation) doesn't need the same treatment while O'Connell, in his first real chance to be 'the guy,' does. On tape, Waldman sees O'Connell flash toughness and arm talent as a pocket passer on middle-of-field throws, but the swing factor is whether he avoids the late, reckless downfield decisions that dogged him at Purdue; if he cleans that up, Waldman thinks he can be 'a very good pocket quarterback' for the Raiders over the next couple years.
- 2024-05-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-17]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman says new Raiders coach Antonio Pierce has made clear this is O'Connell's job to win, not [[Gardner Minshew]]'s, because O'Connell performed solidly as a rookie in a difficult situation last year and the team wants to see whether he's the long-term answer before committing to a bridge veteran.
