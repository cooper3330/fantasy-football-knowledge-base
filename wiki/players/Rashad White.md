# Rashad White

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-10-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-21 (Week 8 Waivers & W7 Reviews)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: White (referred to on-air as the 'gerbil') opened the Bucs-Lions game with a strong 5-yard run where he lowered his shoulder and didn't bounce it outside, but was largely phased out of the game plan from there in a game Tampa Bay trailed most of the way. Separately, guest John Daigle (Establish the Run, not tracked) discussed acquiring White in a trade for Davante Smith in his own stacked league specifically as handcuff insurance for [[Bucky Irving]], calling Irving's foot injury 'the second most mysterious foot injury in the league because Joe Mixon exists' -- both hosts agreed White may end up starting the rest of the year if Irving's absence extends past Tampa Bay's Week 9 bye, though last night's performance specifically was 'not very good.'
- 2025-11-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-17]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman said White still got plenty of work and scored on some plays that were actually set up by defenses over-keying on [[Sean Tucker]]'s outside action, but views Tucker as the more explosive, cutback-oriented back of the two and expects Tucker's role to grow if usage evens out.
- 2025-11-17 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-17 (Week 11 Game Film Review)]]): in Tampa's wild 33-31 loss to Buffalo, White and [[Sean Tucker]] split touches roughly evenly (about 33 combined for the pair by the fourth quarter), but every touchdown went to Tucker -- White was 'a little unlucky' not to score, getting stopped inside the 1-yard line on a red-zone carry right before Tucker punched it in on the very next series. Harris reiterated he's never rated White as a particularly good player and called White's situation effectively settled since he's not under contract for 2026; he stressed 'you can't legislate usage' and doesn't yet know how touches split once Bucky Irving returns from injury.
- 2025-11-21 — According to Chris Harris on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-21]]: With [[Bucky Irving]] reportedly out (per Tampa radio announcer Ronde Barber) for the Rams game, White would split the backfield with [[Sean Tucker]] if Irving sits.
- 2025-11-24 — According to guest Sam Gold (Sam's Film Room, not a tracked expert) and Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: White out-snapped Sean Tucker against the Rams even in a lopsided score, which Gold attributed to Tampa trusting White in pass protection - a role Tucker doesn't yet have - capping Tucker's upside even when the Bucs are forced to throw. Bucky Irving's return timeline remains uncertain, muddying the backfield further.
- 2025-12-12 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-12]]): Harris pushed back on broadcaster Kirk Herbstreit calling White's season 'a tremendous year' on the Thursday night broadcast, saying flatly that White is not actually having a tremendous year and 'you do not need to roster him' -- an example of Sunday-morning-style insider praise not matching the actual tape. [Redraft (Standard)]
- 2026-03-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-09]]) [Dynasty]: asked whether White could replace [[Kenneth Gainwell]] in Jacksonville given his history with OC Liam Cohen, Waldman said White has comparable or greater physical ability -- more explosive, and like Gainwell a solid between-the-tackles runner and receiver out of the backfield -- but called him a 'second choice' behind Gainwell because he lacks the same veteran locker-room leadership value teams weigh in that kind of role.
- 2026-07-14 — According to Jake Seeley and [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-14]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: both currently have White ahead of [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] in a muddled Commanders backfield that also includes rookie Kaytron Allen, citing White's three-year history of top-three-in-receptions-among-RBs efficiency as a reason new OC David Blough could lean on him for near-every-down work; Seeley notes White is 'pretty kind of inefficient at times' as a runner, which is the case against him. Both stress they're not preemptively moving anyone into legit RB2 range even if beat reporters signal a lead back emerging in camp - Seeley currently has the group in the fringe RB3 conversation and says a camp 'winner' would move a back only into roughly the RB28ish/late-RB3 range, not startable RB2 territory, until it's proven over actual games.
- 2026-08-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-03]]): none of Washington's backs are in his top 30 running backs, but if forced to own one it would be White in PPR - though he says flatly 'I don't love Rashad White either.' Guest Dave Richard (CBS Fantasy, not a tracked expert) is more bullish, calling White a potential 'zero RB target' who could average roughly 12 PPR points a week (with some weeks near 20 and others near 9) on high-value touches, and predicts that if there's no positive [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] buzz by September, 'Rashad White's the guy.' Harris counters he'd be 'shocked' if this ends up being Croskey-Merritt's backfield outright but says he's open to being wrong if beat reports firm up.
