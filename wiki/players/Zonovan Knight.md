# Zonovan Knight

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-11-04 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-04]]): on the Monday night film Knight started and out-snapped [[Emari Demercado]] 37 to 26, and owned the work inside the five 3 to 0 — including two congested sweep plays that produced first downs but not touchdowns — plus the entire two-minute drill at the end of the first half. But Demercado led on third downs 8 to 5 and in touches 15 to 11, and Harris could see no rhyme or reason to the rotation. Jeff Erickson of RotoWire (not a tracked expert) thinks Demercado is the better player. With Arizona travelling to Seattle and one of the league's best run defenses, Harris says none of these Cardinals backs will look startable in his Week 10 ranks.
- 2025-11-06 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-06]]): Harris gives Knight (referred to as 'Bam' Knight) the slight nod over [[Emari Demercado]] for Week 10 but stresses he has no real conviction, having re-watched the Arizona possessions specifically to find a pattern and failing. What tipped him was the close-in work: Knight handled the two-minute drill before halftime and got three carries to Demercado's zero inside the five. Guest Ben Gretch (Stealing Signals, not tracked) describes Knight as the physical runner used in the 'green zone' inside the 10 — he broke tackles down to the one before [[Jacoby Brissett]]'s QB sneak, so his lack of a rushing TD was not ineffectiveness — while calling Demercado the more explosive of two similarly sized, broadly comparable backs. Both cite Seattle's strong run defense as a problem.
- 2025-11-20 — According to Dave Kluge (Football Guys, not a tracked expert) and Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with Emari Demercado out, Kluge grouped Knight with Michael Carter as a 'gross' committee he still wants exposure to given Arizona's improved offense under Jacoby Brissett, expecting Knight to see slightly more work than Carter due to his burst and pop versus Carter's space-based game, but ranked both only as low-end RB4s (Knight RB37) since neither can be trusted for volume. Harris was more dismissive, saying he has no Cardinals running back ranked above RB34 and prefers Tony Pollard over both. Both flagged that a Trey Benson return from injury would upend the equation and that a likely 50-50 split means hoping whichever back happens to punch in the touchdown.
