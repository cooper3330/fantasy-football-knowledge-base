# Jarquez Hunter

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-13]]) [Dynasty]: his second underrated back of the class. At 5'10 and 210 out of Auburn, Waldman says he can be a cannonball when he needs to be, has contact balance, and is somewhat limited by his scheme. The framing is of a multi-year player who still looks like he is feeling his way to learning the position, similar in that respect to [[Brashard Smith]] — Waldman compares him to a naturally gifted wrestler with preternatural balance and quickness who has not yet learned the techniques. He describes moments that are breathtaking, plays where you laugh and wonder how he got through, and believes there is more still to learn and therefore more to come.
- 2025-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-20]]) [Dynasty]: guest JJ Zachariason (not a tracked expert) said Hunter was his favorite day-three sleeper running back pre-draft and rates him a better prospect than [[Blake Corum]], or at least in the same vicinity. The case is fit plus explosion: [[Kyren Williams]], Corum and Hunter all have similar size profiles, McVay has shown he will hand a roughly 200-205 pound back a big workload and rotates by drive rather than by down and distance, and Hunter brings burst neither of the others does — better testing and a better explosive run rate in college. He tempers expectations for 2025, but notes Kyren Williams is a free agent next year, so if the Rams see 'a more explosive Kyren Williams,' Hunter is at minimum in a tandem with Corum in 2026. Waldman called him a fine prospect and joked he was saved by [[Mario Anderson]] leaving for Saskatchewan.
- 2025-07-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-07]]) [Best Ball]: expect the camp hype to be all about Hunter — the speed, the contact balance, the promise — but Waldman cautioned that Auburn ran him on a lot of gap scheme, so he may be less well-versed in the outside zone work [[Blake Corum]] has an extra year of reps in. Waldman's broader point is that West Coast offensive coaches have learned practice tells you less than in-game running back performance, so he does not consider the Rams' backfield behind [[Kyren Williams]] settled until roughly week three or four and would stay flexible; Hunter could open as the RB2 and be overtaken by Corum inside a month, the way Cam Akers and Darrell Henderson traded places. Both backs are 16th-round picks (Corum RB63, Hunter RB64), so the market is split; Bob Harris (not tracked) said he would still take the Corum chance.
