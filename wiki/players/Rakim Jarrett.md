# Rakim Jarrett

## Expert Takes

- 2024-10-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-28]]): 'kind of feeling it' on Jarrett as the answer among Tampa Bay's receivers. He liked Jarrett coming out of Maryland — 'a [[Stefon Diggs]] starter kit with some of the pieces missing' — physical, a good special-teams player, wins the ball in the air and is good after the catch. He made three big, technically unpretty but effective catches in the game, and Waldman noted the Buccaneers cannot seem to keep him off the field when he is healthy, which he has not been this year. Caveat: he views it with the same wary eye as [[Jalen Coker]] — do not drop anyone for him, just monitor and you will know when to add. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) tied it to the mining rule: if you are digging deep, do it on a high-scoring offense, and Tampa Bay qualifies with [[Baker Mayfield]] playing through pain.
