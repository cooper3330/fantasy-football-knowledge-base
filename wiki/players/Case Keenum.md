# Case Keenum

## Expert Takes

- 2026-09-29 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-29]]): Keenum won outright as a 38-year-old third-stringer against Philadelphia, but the real story was [[Ben Johnson]]'s system more than Keenum himself -- a conservative ball-control offense (Chicago held the ball almost the entire first quarter) mixed with aggression when it mattered. Harris does not view Keenum as a true starting-level NFL QB and expects diminishing returns given a career of flaming out across multiple teams; the short-term hope is only that Chicago's skill players stay fantasy-viable through scheme (not quarterback quality) until [[Caleb Williams]] returns. Keenum is expected to start again in Week 4 against a theoretically easier Jets matchup, which should rescue some Bears weapons from obscurity in the short term, even though Harris doesn't buy it as sustainable.
- 2026-10-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-10-01]]): at 38 years old and not having played in three years, Keenum came in cold and threw for three total touchdowns against Philadelphia in Week 3 — his first three-touchdown game since 2019 — already matching Justin Herbert and C.J. Stroud's season touchdown total and tripling Drake Maye's. Harmon credits both Ben Johnson's scheme (well-designed open throws) and Keenum's own 'gamer' instincts and moxie, including back-foot fade-away throws under pressure that Harmon says echo his old scouting notes on Keenum as an undrafted Texans rookie. Harmon expects Keenum to keep starting for now given the performance, with [[Kalif Raymond]] a major beneficiary of the offense he's running.
