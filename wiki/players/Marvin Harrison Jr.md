- 2026-02-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the Cardinals hiring Mike LaFleur as offensive coordinator is a huge pickup for [[Marvin Harrison Jr]]. Harmon said he hoped one of LaFleur's first calls after getting the job was to Harrison personally, noting there was 'something always weird and off' between the outgoing Drew Petzing coaching staff and Harrison despite his talent, and that getting him 'bought into the process' could unlock a big season. Co-host James Koh (untracked) went further, predicting a 'banner year' in 2026, arguing LaFleur's scheme will feature a ton of in-breaking, intermediate routes that specifically suit Harrison -- the route depth Koh said Harmon has argued for 'ages' that Harrison needs more of. Both agreed the hire also signals [[Kyler Murray]] will not return as Arizona's starter, since Murray 'cannot do' what LaFleur wants to install offensively; the incoming starting QB was left undetermined, to be addressed via draft or free agency.
- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Discussing 2026 prospect [[Bryce Lance]]'s ball-tracking issues, Waldman notes he was one of the few pre-draft evaluators flagging the same flaw in Harrison's game, and says it has shown up as expected -- the tracking-and-attacking-the-ball problems present at Ohio State have persisted in Arizona. Waldman's framing: technique flaws like this get magnified, not masked, once a prospect reaches the NFL level.
