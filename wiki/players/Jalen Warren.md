- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-10-27]]) [Redraft]: 'that guy's really good, clearly a fantasy starter' -- Warren had roughly 10 carries for about 50 yards in a strong first half, but his workload dried up in the second half (3 carries, 12 yards, 1 catch) even while Pittsburgh was up nine points. Harris says Pittsburgh should have run more with him in that spot; backup Kenneth Gainwell (untracked-relevant) fumbled late with Pittsburgh's last flicker of hope alive.
- 2025-11-17 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-17 (Week 11 Game Film Review)]]): Warren broke off a 35-yard run on Pittsburgh's opening drive against Cincinnati but left in the third quarter with an apparent ankle injury after getting 'wishboned' on a screen reception, and did not return to the locker room. Harris said Mike Tomlin's postgame comments didn't sound overly alarmed, but that in the moment it 'looked bad, looked like it might be season-ending, and who knows.' He flagged that potential Warren replacements would be covered on Tuesday's waiver-wire segment, with Pittsburgh facing the Bears in Week 12.
- 2025-11-21 — According to Chris Harris on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-21]] [Redraft (Standard)]: Warren was back at practice on a limited basis Thursday after missing Wednesday with a sprained ankle suffered against Cincinnati in Week 11, opening the door for him to return to Harris's ranks. Harris said if Warren gets another practice Friday, even limited, [[Kenneth Gainwell]] 'no longer looks like anything other than a Hail Mary,' and Warren would probably be back in fantasy lineups even with a sore ankle.
- 2025-11-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-25]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: split touches with Kenneth Gainwell (34 combined, more than Christian McCaffrey's total by himself) against Chicago. Warren appeared to be protected early given a bad ankle -- Gainwell got the game's first carry -- but Warren punched in the eventual touchdown after Gainwell was ruled down at the one on what was initially called a 50-yard score.
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]): in the Steelers' Monday night win over Miami, [[Jalen Warren]] was questionable pregame with an illness. He got two carries at the goal line but was stuffed both times by a Dolphins defense that hadn't allowed a rushing TD in five weeks, so Connor Heyward scored on a tush-push instead. Warren also had a long run wiped out by a holding penalty. Harris cautioned the box score makes it look like [[Kenneth Gainwell]] dominated the backfield, but that's mostly a single uncalled-back 38-yard Gainwell run once Miami stopped resisting late; success rates between the two were about the same, and Warren did the tougher close-quarters work. Ranking Pittsburgh's backs for a Week 16 game against Detroit where the Steelers will need every point, Harris said he's considering moving them closer together but is still ranking Warren above Gainwell.
- 2026-01-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-05]]) [Dynasty]: noted as having signed a two-year contract extension in September, giving Pittsburgh a locked-in, assignment-sound complementary back that Waldman says makes it harder for [[Caleb Johnson]] to leapfrog into a lead role.
- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31]]) [Best Ball] [Redraft (Standard)]: both hosts view Jalen Warren and [[Rico Dowdle]] as effectively the same-tier late RB2/handcuff option -- 'the last call at RB2,' as Harris puts it, in builds where you've punted the position (e.g. Hero-RB). Early camp reports of the two backs splitting first-team reps don't tell them anything new; Harmon expects that split to hold and predicts Warren will keep catching the Kenny Gainwell-style checkdowns off [[Aaron Rodgers]]'s contact-averse play style, calling that 'kind of what I think is going to happen anyway.' Neither expects the Steelers to publicize real role information in August -- Harris notes nobody knew Gainwell's target share last year until the games counted.
- 2026-08-06 — According to JJ Zachariason (not a tracked expert; guest) [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-06]]: Zachariason named Warren his #1 film room hero, calling him a 'fringe top-10 running back' with elite contact balance ('bounces off defenders like a pinball'), a patient running style, and strong receiving and pass-protection skills, saying he's frustrated the Steelers coaching staff won't give him a bigger workload. He ranks Warren RB27 PPR/RB29 standard and expects the market to favor teammate Rico Dowdle amid a 'Mike McCarthy prefers Dowdle' training-camp narrative, but believes Warren is clearly the better all-around player. Chris Harris agreed emphatically, projecting Warren as a flex play and noting a long show history of being high on Warren as a sleeper who never quite breaks out under Mike Tomlin, with Najee Harris having previously blocked his path to more volume.
- 2026-08-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-25]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris names Warren his #1 safe player, taking him around the fifth-to-sixth round (live ADP 64 overall) despite Pittsburgh again pairing him with a bigger back, now Rico Dowdle, instead of a true bell-cow role. He says Warren is capable of handling a bigger workload but even in a timeshare is 'a very safe flex,' arguing the Steelers' overall lack of buzz around Aaron Rodgers's return depresses Warren's price the same way it depresses [[DK Metcalf]]'s, even though both are solid weekly building blocks.

Harris says the show was early on Warren's size/skill combo relative to most, but cautions that Steeler coordinators have historically not stuck with a single-back model even when it's working, so he's wary of assuming this usage level holds going forward.
- 2026-10-01 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Previewing Steelers-Browns on Thursday Night Football, Harris called Warren 'maybe the only must-start' player in the game after he posted over 170 scrimmage yards last week and could have gone over 200 if not for a called-back long touchdown. Harris said Warren has 'always been a favorite around here.'
- 2026-10-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-02]]): Warren was 'simply and obviously the best thing going on' the Steelers offense Thursday night, posting 126 scrimmage yards, breaking tackles and making 'really really good' close-quarters cuts despite unremarkable acceleration. Al Michaels compared him on-air to Bijan Robinson; Harris pushes back on the comp, saying Warren 'does not run anything like Bijan Robinson' but still makes something out of nothing consistently -- calling Michaels' take basic pattern recognition rather than an accurate stylistic match.
