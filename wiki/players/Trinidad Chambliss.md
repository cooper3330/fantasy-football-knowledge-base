# Trinidad Chambliss

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-10-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-10-22 (Going Deep Eagles & 2027 QB Class)]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo calls Chambliss, alongside [[Drew Mestemaker]], his favorite watch in the 2027 class and the best quarterback in college football right now -- a mature operator who excels with cadence, tempo, and getting in and out of plays, with legitimate run-game and low-red-zone value. He thinks Chambliss is a top-five talent likely to be underdrafted into the Ty Simpson range because of his age (24, close to Tyler Shough's draft age) and size. Waldman separately says Chambliss reminds him a lot of [[Brock Purdy]] -- with a better arm than Purdy but not quite Purdy's processing and timing feel -- and names him and Mestemaker his two favorite quarterbacks in the cycle. Both think a team could fall in love and take him inside the top ten, which they'd consider the right call given his talent level.
