# Tyler Harrell

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take, and did not receive a 2024 Combine invite
> — treat as historical scouting context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]) [Dynasty]: a deep-league sleeper call — "a wild shot" for best-ball formats with very deep rosters. A journeyman college prospect (Louisville, then Alabama, then Miami) who Waldman says has logged more plays with the ball in his hands above 21 mph than any receiver he's charted in the past five or ten years, with a rumored (and Waldman-doubted) hand-timed 40 in the 4.08–4.24 range. His verdict: "as fast on any given day as [[Xavier Worthy]]," 30 pounds heavier, a capable vertical route runner and catcher. The concern is health and off-field context — he "couldn't stay healthy" at either Alabama or Miami despite being "promising" at Louisville and winning one-on-ones on tape, and Waldman says he wonders (while "rooting for" him) whether something beyond injury explains the lost playing time.
