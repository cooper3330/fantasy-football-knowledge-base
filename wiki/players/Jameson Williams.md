# Jameson Williams

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-29]]): a real but explicitly hedged optimism after a big NFC Championship showing — "I'm optimistic for him" — paired immediately with a sharp warning against overreacting to a single game: "how many people are going to overdraft Jamison Williams based on this game? The Gabe Davis factor will be in effect, people. Be careful of it." Not given a specific 2024 ranking or role projection beyond that caution.
- 2024-01-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-30]]), independent light corroboration: acknowledges the pre-draft trade-up to select him "hasn't worked out" so far as a value proposition, but immediately tempers that criticism given the two-touchdown NFC Championship performance — "I don't want to slam him too hard right now."
- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-01]]): the most technically critical read on Williams yet, delivered alongside the news that OC Ben Johnson is *staying* in Detroit (resolving the scheme-change risk this bullet would otherwise flag). Harmon's specific complaint isn't just "not yet refined" route running — it's "the shaky hands" and weak ball-tracking, calling him explicitly "not a go-up-and-get-it guy." His projection: a career "splash-play dude" rather than a full-route-tree receiver, with an added dependency flag — many of his highlight plays are specifically Ben-Johnson-designed structure plays, so his ceiling is somewhat tied to Johnson staying as play-caller (which, as of this same episode, he has).
