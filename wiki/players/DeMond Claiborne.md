# DeMond Claiborne

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-08-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-15]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman calls Claiborne the obvious name in his early 2026 look and says he is fun. Good feet, mobile hips, presses creases well, a good windback runner, catches the ball reasonably well, and decent contact balance for his size. He is not unbelievably fast and not a breakaway guy, but he can extend against safeties and force them to chase another 15 to 20 yards once he reaches the secondary, so he can flip the field. Waldman shows more fluidity and better lower-body answers than [[Bucky Irving]] did, and Hatman (Scouting Academy, NOT tracked) expects people to place him on the [[RJ Harvey]] and [[De'Von Achane]] continuum minus the peak speed — the smaller body type the NFL has found a way to use, so size knocks should carry less weight now. Waldman's caveat is weight: a spring verification in the low 190s, and if he can get to a true 210 to 212 he sees a neighbourhood a few miles down the road from LeSean McCoy's.
