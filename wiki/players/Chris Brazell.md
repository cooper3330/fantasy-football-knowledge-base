# Chris Brazell

## Expert Takes

- 2026-03-31 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-03-31]]): Brazell (Tennessee, 6'4'/~200 lbs, 92nd-percentile arm length, 62/1,000/9 in 2025) ran a shocking 4.37 forty that neither host saw reflected on tape -- Harmon notes he gets a lot of cushion from off-coverage so he rarely gets to 'truly stride it out,' and his 9-route success rate is only 50% despite heavy volume there. His biggest red flag is alignment: 95.7% of his RP sample snaps came at right outside WR (one snap on the left all season), one of the most extreme 'skewed alignment' profiles Harmon has charted -- a red flag he ties to a study of 35 such prospects since 2015 in which only four (JuJu Smith-Schuster, DJ Moore, Drake London, Rasheed Rice) became high-impact early starters, with busts like Javon Baker, Corey Coleman and Laquan Treadwell as cautionary comps. Brazell is weak vs. zone (73%, wants 80%+) but strong on dig/out routes (24% of his tree, 80-81% success), which Harmon says separates him from fellow Tennessee alum [[Cedric Tillman]]'s more rudimentary 2023 tree. Harmon models him as a developmental 'sacrificial X' in the mold of [[Alec Pierce]] -- a guy who can eat snaps early on a limited route tree and grow into the role -- but warns he won't rank this alignment archetype in the top 60 picks, projecting a third-to-fifth round pick. His father's nine years of pro (NFL/CFL) experience is flagged as a positive makeup signal.
- 2026-04-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman rejects the hyperbolic framing that Brazell 'saves' [[Tetairoa McMillan]]'s career in Carolina -- McMillan's career doesn't need saving -- but agrees Brazell's arrival augments it. His reasoning: McMillan produced last year mostly against zone or off coverage and struggled versus tight man-to-man from linebackers/safeties, a matchup Carolina had to manufacture around him. Waldman thinks Brazell, a true press-man X, lets Carolina bump [[Jalen Coker]] (the team's best press-man receiver currently) out to flanker and move McMillan inside into a Drake London-like power slot role, where Waldman is comfortable calling McMillan a legitimate fantasy WR1 -- stressing that's a slot projection, not an X projection, which he says has been his position on McMillan all along.
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-28]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon reads Carolina's decision to draft Brazell as a 'declaration of intent' to move [[Tetairoa McMillan]] off the line more, since Brazell -- a big-bodied perimeter/vertical receiver -- gives them a credible X-alignment body. Head coach Dave Canales called Brazell a 'space clearer,' which Harmon translates as an admission Brazell is being deployed as a sacrificial X early on; Harmon calls him raw and incomplete but says he 'liked him better coming out of college' than his testing suggests, and hopes he follows Alec Pierce's path -- a sacrificial X as a rookie who develops into more, as Pierce did by his fourth Colts season.
