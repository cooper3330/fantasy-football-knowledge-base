# Daijun Edwards

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his
> name as "Dajon Edwards"; normalized here to the real name of the Georgia
> running back, Daijun Edwards.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-12]]) [Dynasty]: one of three underrated backs Waldman flags this episode (with [[Dylan Laube]] and [[George Holani]]), and the one he expects to land on the most fantasy rosters purely on Georgia name recognition and 2023 production. Played through a preseason MCL injury (visible large brace) and still moved well — very quick and shifty, a good pass catcher with real blocking value, plays bigger than his 200-lb frame. Floor comp: a Jalen Richard-style Raiders-esque change-of-pace role. Ceiling comp: "a little more agile, dynamic version of James White" if he lands in the right system. Explicit caveat: this is not viewed as a strong running back class at the top, so none of the three names this episode are top-12-rookie-round bets.
- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: independent corroboration of the Waldman read above, delivered as one of Angelo's own late-round/reserve-projection picks. Surprised by more pop and physicality on tape than expected; a good mover and good receiver, roughly equally competent in gap and zone schemes, with strong, largely mistake-free decision-making and a reliable, competent pass-blocking floor. Grades him not as a long-term speedster but another James White/Dion Lewis-type contributor capable of finding his way into an NFL lineup, and expects him to stick in a league running back room for a while on the strength of understanding how to play the position.

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-11 (Going Deep - Fave Evals, Coach-Killer Candidates, and High-End Career Outcomes)]]): rated him 9th at the position; testing numbers were poor for the size (a 4.7-second 40, 7.23 three-cone, 30" vertical at 5'9"/207 lbs) but "processes much faster than his timed movements," with strong vision and good footwork. Comps him to Jalen Richard, and floats a "maybe even a James White type" ceiling if he shows he's faster than he timed and stays consistent in the right fit. [[Brandon Angelo]], same episode: independently rates him the back he'd trust most in a crowded, talented Georgia running back room — not necessarily the most talented back there, but the one an OC could count on. Both hosts separately note that pairing Edwards's processing and approach with [[Kendall Milton]]'s physical tools would produce a top-five prospect in the class.
