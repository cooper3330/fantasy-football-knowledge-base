# Bryson Nesbitt

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]): Nesbitt plays faster than his NFL Combine workout suggests and is a nimble athlete with the ball in his hands, technically skilled with releases against coverage, with potential value as a man-to-man receiver in a big slot role or matched against less athletic defenders on the perimeter. Waldman's problem with him is the absence of an elevating trait: he is athletic enough to be on a roster but lacks a skill that lifts him to a starting role, and he is neither big nor fast enough to thrive as a primary weapon at tight end. Waldman adds a role-competition caveat — many teams would rather have one of their own wide receivers play the big slot, because a receiver offers as much there and more on the outside.
