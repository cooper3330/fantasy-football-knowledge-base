- 2024-12-12 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-12]]) [Dynasty]: named twice — first as the kind of day-one-impact rookie weapon Carolina should get for [[Bryce Young]], and then as effectively the only receiver in the 2025 class Angelo can name off the top of his head with the size and physicality to be a genuine NFL ball-winner. - 2025-01-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-01-23]]): McMillan is the 2025 receiver he thinks is overhyped — not a bust call, since his score is good enough that combine cross-checking could push him into Waldman's top five to ten, but right now he is not there. Waldman likes [[Savion Williams]] more, says McMillan clearly catches well but that his route running is between tiers, and sees him as more of a specialist than an every-down primary starter — a [[Mike Williams]] type with a niche. [[Brandon Angelo]] names McMillan as his own too-much-buzz pick and agrees on the routes: good at the stem sometimes, but too much drifting, not as clean and concise as an NFL starter needs. Angelo's real worry is fit — he expects a top-10 pick in a thin receiver class, which means being asked to be the WR1 against number-one corners from day one; his realistic ceiling is a [[Tee Higgins]]-type secondary receiver, which is fine but easy to overvalue at that cost.
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-13]]): Harmon has charted only two games of [[Tetairoa McMillan]] and explicitly does not have a firm take yet, but says he is an impressive player and he likes what he has seen — he started with McMillan rather than [[Emeka Egbuka]] ahead of his first combine so he would not go in uninformed on the top five or six receivers. - 2025-03-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-03]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is materially below consensus on the Arizona receiver, joking that relative to the buzz his answer is 'f**k it.' If he cannot separate against man coverage he is a zone player winning contested targets — real value, but not a top-20 or top-30 receiver to draft. Waldman's realistic ceiling comp is a Mike Williams, or the actual (rather than imagined) version of a Tee Higgins. - 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24]]): McMillan's releases need more craft — he has a lot of maneuvers in the toolbox but almost none carry the contrast between patience and suddenness that beats NFL press man. Waldman also wants a better pre-snap plan and alternatives to it: when a defender plays unexpected leverage McMillan stays too goal-focused on the bad plan and gets pinned at the boundary. The sharpest line is conditional praise — if he did not have lapses with clap attacks he would earn an elite pass-catching score and rank among the best pure receivers in the class. He shows great highs at the catch point, but extends his hands in directions requiring significant adjustment while accounting for a defender. Fix that and Waldman thinks he could be 'pretty unstoppable' at the catch point. See [[Catch Technique and Ball Tracking]].

- 2025-03-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-26]]) [Dynasty]: McMillan (6'4″, sub-220, pro-day 40 reported anywhere from 4.44 to 4.55) is a 'really good tier-two prospect', not tier one — a round-one player he would take top-10 only because the class lacks blue chips. Charted: 70.4% vs man, 81.0% vs zone, 73.1% vs press (80th percentile), 82.5% on digs at 14.1% dig usage, 88.9% on comebacks, but only 48.7% on nines and merely okay on slants/curls. - 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21 (Film & Data with Dwain McFarland)]]): McMillan is only his WR5 because he doesn't grade as a timing-route separator on par with [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Justin Jefferson]], [[Luther Burden III]] or [[Malik Nabers]] — a boundary winner and 'bully' who is very strong on tight-coverage targets and good after the catch, but whose college separation may not project cleanly against NFL press and timing work.
