# Caleb Johnson

## Expert Takes

- 2026-01-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-05]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman thinks a year-two reboot is possible -- 'the talent is there' -- but it hinges heavily on the Steelers' offseason decisions on [[Kenneth Gainwell]] (one-year deal, needs re-signing) and [[Jalen Warren]] (signed a two-year deal in September). If Gainwell returns, Waldman thinks Johnson is buried behind two backs who aren't great but are extremely assignment-sound and experienced, forcing Johnson to have an outstanding summer/fall just to leapfrog either of them -- 'a high bar for just about anybody.' If Gainwell leaves and Pittsburgh doesn't add competition early in the draft or via free agency, Waldman says the door opens for Johnson. He notes Johnson put himself deep in the doghouse as a rookie (compared to [[Roman Wilson]]'s similar fall from grace) but believes Mike Tomlin is generally willing to give a player a second chance, just not usually a third.
- 2026-01-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-26]]) [Dynasty]: still feel it, despite a disappointing rookie year (fumble, a kickoff return touchdown allowed in Week 2, inactive/benched Week 3). Waldman offers a reframed explanation he says he wishes he'd considered during pre-draft analysis: with Aaron Rodgers at quarterback, a rookie running back had to be either a savant at adjusting to line-of-scrimmage changes while still learning the playbook, or an elite pass protector, because Rodgers' staff would not risk him behind a rookie prone to blown assignments -- so Johnson's limited role was likely more about the Rodgers fit than his own talent, compounded by rookie mistakes when he did get chances. Waldman still likes Johnson as a long-striding, powerful outside-zone runner: if new head coach Mike McCarthy's staff runs a similarly outside-zone-oriented scheme, Johnson has a real shot; if the scheme is 'vastly different,' he could get buried and end up fighting for a second contract elsewhere. Whether Rodgers or Mike Tomlin's doghouse dynamics persist is also in play now that both may be gone.
- 2026-04-23 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-23]]) [Dynasty]: Johnson also missed both hosts' top-20 redraft entirely, which Harris called 'just a brutal whiff.' He admitted he'd bought into the system-fit argument for Johnson (Iowa-to-a-run-scheme optics) over actual player traits, and that Johnson simply 'played his way off this list' -- not good on special teams (an unusual use of him), not good in his limited opportunities, and a costly fumble that landed him in the coaching staff's doghouse. Harris noted the coaching staff instead trusted Kenneth Gainwell, who 'parlayed into a magical role' elsewhere, as further evidence Johnson never earned trust.
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman notes the Steelers' new coaching staff appears to like Johnson at least as much as the previous staff did, in the context of him competing with [[Eli Heidenreich]] for a depth backfield/return role.
- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: both hosts say they will not believe any positive Caleb Johnson training-camp buzz over the next month, given how poor his rookie year was (including fumbling on kickoffs). Harmon says he'd need to see something like [[Rico Dowdle]] in danger of losing his roster spot before taking Johnson seriously as a threat to the current backfield pecking order, and notes Johnson is a previous coaching staff's draft pick even though that same staff iced him out. Harris frames this as a category-one-vs-category-two-knowledge distinction: skepticism of camp reports doesn't mean Johnson can never be good, just that he has to prove it in games that count before either host adjusts. If something happens to Warren or Dowdle, Johnson would become a waiver-wire speed-dial name, with Dowdle's banger-back role seen as the more replaceable of the two if Johnson ever does emerge.
