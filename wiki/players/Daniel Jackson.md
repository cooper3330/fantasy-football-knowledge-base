# Daniel Jackson

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18 (Feel It or F-It Free Agency Winners and Losers)]]) [Dynasty]: asked whether the Minnesota receiver, not highly rated by the draft world, could be a modern Wayne Chrebet, Waldman feels it. He calls Jackson 'the grittiest receiver at the catch point in this class' and illustrates with an extended hypothetical of Jackson running mesh, taking a hospital ball into a middle linebacker, popping up and signaling first down. Beyond toughness over the middle he credits Jackson as a good route runner with excellent quickness. His projection: better in the slot than as a flanker, though capable of both, and he thinks Jackson may end up the best slot receiver to come out of this class — a late-round dynasty rookie target given the market's low opinion.
- 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24]]): the Minnesota receiver has the elements of a tough slot who is just fast enough to earn time as a flanker — a strong route runner with short-area explosion who separates inside and outside against both man and zone. After the catch he has good feel for pursuit and keeps a downfield mentality while routinely making the first man miss with efficient moves. Waldman's comp: a modern-day Wayne Chrebet with hands manufactured in Hoover's top-secret laboratory.
- 2025-04-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-07]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman says yes, the Minnesota receiver is a better slot prospect than [[Xavier Restrepo]]. Jackson is very quick rather than fast, with strong short-area change of direction, is a good route runner, and can give you a little on the outside in multiple-receiver sets. His signature trait is toughness at the catch point — Waldman calls him the grittiest pass catcher in the class, the guy who takes hard shots and does not even blink.
