## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]) [Best Ball]: coming off a stretch-run 2023 as effectively "tight end two" alongside Amari Cooper (Njoku's own page) as Cleveland's top two targets, Waldman doesn't think the [[Jerry Jeudy]] trade knocks Njoku down the pecking order. Still grades him a value at his current TE8 best-ball ADP, and expects "a similar rise" by mid-season — possibly earlier, once the Browns try Jeudy in a role and find he's still probably their third option behind [[Amari Cooper]] and Njoku. Cites a strong [[Deshaun Watson]] connection as the reason Njoku holds his role, calling Jeudy more of a "scheme player" who needs a settled, established passing structure to produce.

- 2024-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-21]]): names Njoku (with [[Amari Cooper]]) as the settled identity of Cleveland's offense — "the identity can be Amari Cooper and David Njoku" — with the rest of the receiver room ([[Jerry Jeudy]], [[Elijah Moore]], [[Cedric Tillman]]) cast as supporting characters rather than reliable pieces in their own right.
