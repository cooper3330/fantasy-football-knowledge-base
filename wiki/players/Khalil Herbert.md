## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]): feels Herbert as the better best ball value versus backfield-mate [[Roschon Johnson]] at their current Underdog ADPs (Johnson RB35, Herbert RB44) — calls Herbert the smarter, more explosive runner of the two, and pushes back hard on the "bad receiver" label some have put on him: not just "not as bad as people think," but underrated as a receiver outright, projecting as a legitimate screen/checkdown/leak-out option, which he argues is "good enough" even without being an Austin Ekeler-level receiving back. Expects a real timeshare in Chicago given the incoming rookie-QB uncertainty, and says he'll take Herbert over Johnson more often than not — ideally getting both backs two rounds cheaper than their current price as a value tandem. - 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]] [Best Ball]: Chicago's addition of [[DeAndre Swift]] actually *bolstered* his best-ball shares rather than hurt them — Swift is a part-time complementary piece, not a bell cow, so Herbert (going around running back 37, outside the top-36) becomes an even cheaper, still-productive fade. Floats Herbert, not [[Roschon Johnson]], as the more likely trade chip if Chicago still likes Johnson enough to consolidate the backfield.
