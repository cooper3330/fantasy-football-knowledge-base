# Khalil Herbert

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]): feels Herbert as the better best ball value versus backfield-mate [[Roschon Johnson]] at their current Underdog ADPs (Johnson RB35, Herbert RB44) — calls Herbert the smarter, more explosive runner of the two, and pushes back hard on the "bad receiver" label some have put on him: not just "not as bad as people think," but underrated as a receiver outright, projecting as a legitimate screen/checkdown/leak-out option, which he argues is "good enough" even without being an Austin Ekeler-level receiving back. Expects a real timeshare in Chicago given the incoming rookie-QB uncertainty, and says he'll take Herbert over Johnson more often than not — ideally getting both backs two rounds cheaper than their current price as a value tandem. [Best Ball]


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]] [Best Ball]: Chicago's addition of [[DeAndre Swift]] actually *bolstered* his best-ball shares rather than hurt them — Swift is a part-time complementary piece, not a bell cow, so Herbert (going around running back 37, outside the top-36) becomes an even cheaper, still-productive fade. Floats Herbert, not [[Roschon Johnson]], as the more likely trade chip if Chicago still likes Johnson enough to consolidate the backfield.
- 2024-06-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-10]]): called Herbert the Bears' best inside runner with the best game-breaking ability of the three-man committee, but said he's not a powerful runner and isn't a strong receiver -- of the trio (with [[DeAndre Swift]] and [[Roschon Johnson]]), Herbert is projected closest to 700 rushing yards. Waldman noted the backfield could shrink to two heads if Herbert can't stay healthy or gets traded to Dallas, a scenario Bob Harris said 'could be really nice too' for whoever remains.
- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: With reporting suggesting Khalil Herbert may not stick in Chicago, Waldman said he should be one of the top-five names on any redraft waiver-wire watch list -- if he lands with a team facing a running back injury or need, he's a good enough runner to provide real emergency fantasy value, and Waldman said to add him within a week or two of any signing that projects early work.
- 2024-11-14 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-14 (Age Cliffs and Dented Cans)]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is 'a little iffy' but likes Herbert's skills, and discounts Chicago giving up on him because in his view the Bears 'don't know their head from their ass' as an organization and have had so much regime turnover that Herbert was never really any administration's guy — so he never got a chance. Herbert is now in Cincinnati and Waldman can see him moving again in a year or two and actually starring somewhere, because he is a good inside runner with legitimate speed; the limitation is that he is not a good receiver. Waldman's framing is not lead-back-on-a-multi-year-deal but insurance: until you find out he is broken down, he is the kind of back who can be inserted in any season and give you 1,100-1,200 rushing yards.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]): With San Francisco's other backs hurt, Herbert is the healthy 'camp body' -- a smart interior runner who'll run things effectively but isn't a good passing-down back; Waldman also notes Herbert himself 'constantly gets hurt.'
