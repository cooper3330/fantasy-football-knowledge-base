# Jordan Lyle

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-08-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-22]]) [Dynasty]: Lyle, a Miami freshman running back out of St. Thomas Aquinas, is a devy prospect who over the next three years has a chance to be a late-first/early-second round NFL pick if everything pans out, on a lightning-in-a-bottle skill set. Angelo's method is to watch high-school tape and separate the players who dominated weak competition from those who looked otherworldly against peers of their own caliber — the latter group is where he placed [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] years earlier, and where he places Lyle now. He cites movement skill, abruptness and the ability to stay on his feet through hard contact, with the caveat that Lyle is not a big frame at roughly 180-185 pounds.
