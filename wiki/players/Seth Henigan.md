# Seth Henigan

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-31 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-31]]) [Dynasty]: Henigan has genuine arm range — Waldman charted a 46-yard pinpoint fade to the near-side boundary from his pitch point, and a 53-to-55-yard throw to the near-side flat with catchable accuracy while taking a hit during the release, enough to profile as a big-play vertical threat. He handles pressure well for a small-school prospect: late climbs away from blindside edge rushers, reducing the shoulder as the edge defender reaches, pulling through the reach, sidestepping early to access a passing lane, and good spin-and-roll to either side with good timing on the delivery. The negatives are placement under duress and leverage awareness — he misses intermediate targets by throwing slightly behind receivers facing tight trail coverage, putting the defender in position to knock it away, and he has lapses leading receivers into coverage where he cannot protect them from the oncoming hit. He also tries to fit balls into tight zones that exceed his placement prowess under pressure, particularly when throwing over an edge defender's rush.
