# Josh Jacobs

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: names Jacobs, as a free agent, "easily" the best current candidate to become "the next James Connor" — a good back who becomes a bargain the moment his name value drops — and would be a strong fit for the Cowboys or Ravens. Directly compares him to [[Zamir White]]: still the better back of the two, and would be a top-five running back landing almost anywhere with a real opportunity, versus a low-end RB2 ceiling for White if Jacobs leaves Las Vegas.
- 2024-01-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: floats Baltimore as a landing spot alongside [[Derrick Henry]] — either back joining [[Lamar Jackson]]'s Ravens offense would be "massive." Separately, discussing Houston's backfield need, names Jacobs (with Henry) as a plausible fit if he doesn't land in Baltimore; also name-checks Saquon Barkley as the closest thing to a [[Christian McCaffrey]]-style free-agent fit for that Houston offense, while doubting Barkley actually leaves the Giants.
- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]): grouped with [[Derrick Henry]] as the two free-agent backs Waldman is most confident land real starting jobs somewhere this offseason — "they're getting starting gigs somewhere, and if they don't, somebody's stupid."

- 2024-03-01 — **Adam Harstad** (co-host on *RSP Film and Theory* — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-01]] [Dynasty]: names Jacobs his top bet of the "Big Four" 2024 free-agent backs (ahead of [[Austin Ekeler]], [[Tony Pollard]] and [[Derrick Henry]]) — "I'm a big Josh Jacobs fan," crediting him for doing things "casual fans" undervalue but that NFL franchises pay for. Argues Jacobs over the last three years is about as good at Henry's core skills as Henry himself was over that same span, with more receiving upside and youth on top of it, making true decline less likely.
- 2024-03-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], same episode [Dynasty]: agrees Jacobs is the best bet of the four. Adds a skills note beyond the numbers: thinks Jacobs is likely a better receiver/route runner than Pollard and comparable to Ekeler if he lands somewhere that uses him that way — he "was fantastic at Alabama" and a good route runner there, but the Raiders' play-action-heavy, Jacobs-as-focal-point scheme never called on him to run a full route tree. Groups him with Henry in the "Column B" bell-cow tier of the free-agent class, versus the receiving-back "Column A" of Ekeler and Pollard.

- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: predicts Jacobs re-signs and stays with the Las Vegas Raiders — "feeling that," calling it "a good" outcome — while noting the Cowboys would be another strong fit given his all-around skill set, and that a [[Derrick Henry]] arrival in Vegas would push his preference toward wanting Jacobs to land with the Ravens instead.

- 2024-03-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-12]]: on his move to Green Bay — likes it for the Packers given Jacobs's relative youth (turning 26) versus the since-released [[Aaron Jones]] (turning 30), while flagging his 2023 workload/production collapse (340 attempts/1,650 yards in 2022 down to 233 attempts/805 yards in 2023) as largely a product of the Raiders' non-competitive, post-McDaniels context rather than a talent decline. Says it's still unclear whether he'll be a good scheme fit in Matt LaFleur's system.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: both fully "feel" the finalized move to Green Bay, calling it one of the best fits of this free-agent cycle — helped further by the Packers releasing [[Aaron Jones]] and bringing back only complementary A.J. Dillon (not a tracked page). Waldman: Jacobs is "a pro's pro," a do-it-all back who can run gap or zone and is a much better receiver than his box-score totals suggest to anyone who "never watched Alabama tape" — expects the receiving numbers to climb now that he isn't sharing touches with Jones.
