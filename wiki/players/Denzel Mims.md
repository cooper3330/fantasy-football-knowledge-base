- 2024-06-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-10]]): defended Mims's disappointing Jets tenure, saying he bought the report that Mims lost over 20 pounds to food poisoning in year two under a coaching regime change and became a forgotten man before a strong preseason flash led him to Detroit and then Pittsburgh. He likes Mims's fit running the in-breaking/dig routes Arthur Smith favors and projects him as a Corey Davis-type complementary big receiver opposite George Pickens, with a real chance to outproduce Pickens in a strong year -- calling it 'a lot of ifs there' but a worthwhile sleeper bet for the Steelers' open WR2 job over Van Jefferson (whom Waldman called a probable WR4 talent due to a high drop rate) and Scotty Miller.
