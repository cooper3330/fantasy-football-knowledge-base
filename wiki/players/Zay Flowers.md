# Zay Flowers

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]]): belongs in his top tier of the 2023 rookie wide receiver class, but a little more boom/bust within it than [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], [[Jordan Addison]], [[Puka Nacua]] and [[Jayden Reed]] — the swing factor is how broadly [[Lamar Jackson]]'s offense will trust and target him, not his own play, which Waldman expects to keep improving as the two build familiarity.
- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: resolves an open question from earlier in the season — a running "please don't trade [[Drake London]] for Flowers" plea — decisively in London's favor now that the full rookie season is in: "no question to me that London had the more impressive rookie year," and London is roughly six months younger. Not a downgrade on Flowers, whose downside risk Waldman says has "largely been reduced" (Adam Harstad's [[Post-Rookie-Year Receiver Model]] puts him in an ~80%-hit-rate score range), but his efficiency component is only slightly above average — his score is propped up mostly by usage/target volume rather than per-route quality, tempering the "elite" framing a bit.
