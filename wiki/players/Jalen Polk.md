# Jalen Polk

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-08-04 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-04]]): Jalen Polk unexpectedly retired from the Saints, which Harmon called shocking given reports just a month or two earlier described him as highly motivated to repay New Orleans's faith in him. Polk finishes his career with 33 targets, 12 receptions, 87 yards and 2 touchdowns as a former 2nd-round Patriots pick. Harmon called it a prospect miss but partly attributed it to poor role fit -- New England played him as a vertical X receiver as a rookie despite his college profile being a big Z who could kick inside, more zone-beater than man-beater -- while also noting Polk didn't help himself, struggling with drops and contested catches and a shaky blocking reputation. Harmon said the Saints have enough depth to be fine without him: [[Chris Olave]] (fresh off a career year and contract extension), Jordan Tyson, and Devaughn Vele as a big slot option, plus Day 3 rookie Bryce Lance as a developmental vertical X.
