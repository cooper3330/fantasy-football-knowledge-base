## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): graded as having "solidified himself" as a late Day 2/Day 3 pick through Senior Bowl week — "I think he's pretty good," without a more specific ceiling/role projection given.
- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): one of his best skills is getting his feet above defenders' low shots as a ball carrier, which compensates for average-for-a-starter speed and power; works well behind his blocks, and avoiding trash/low hits makes him a better runner than his overall profile suggests. Waldman doesn't think he'll reach his father Jerry Rice's career heights, but calls him a legitimate prospect who can create a name for himself.
