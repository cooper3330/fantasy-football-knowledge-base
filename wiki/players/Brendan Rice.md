# Brendan Rice

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): graded as having "solidified himself" as a late Day 2/Day 3 pick through Senior Bowl week — "I think he's pretty good," without a more specific ceiling/role projection given.
- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): one of his best skills is getting his feet above defenders' low shots as a ball carrier, which compensates for average-for-a-starter speed and power; works well behind his blocks, and avoiding trash/low hits makes him a better runner than his overall profile suggests. Waldman doesn't think he'll reach his father Jerry Rice's career heights, but calls him a legitimate prospect who can create a name for himself.
- 2024-05-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-17]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman adds Rice to his sizzle-then-produce list, saying he lacks confidence in any Rams receiver behind [[Ladd McConkey]] and compares Josh Palmer's likely career arc to journeyman Chris Conley. He likens Rice's game after the catch to early-career Juju Smith-Schuster: not yet as polished, with some drop concerns, but with a feel for breaking tackles and making defenders miss that could let him rise to the occasion if given the opportunity.
- 2024-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-20]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said he's been accumulating cheap dynasty shares of Brendan Rice late, seeing some of the same catch-technique issues ("clap attacker") as [[Quentin Johnston]] but to a lesser degree, while praising his after-the-catch ability; his outcome comp is an early-career Juju Smith-Schuster. He tied this to the Chargers also adding depth piece Cornelius Johnson (not a notable prospect but a possible Marquez Valdes-Scantling-type contributor), reading new coach Jim Harbaugh's moves as bringing in inexpensive, no-strings-attached options rather than fully committing to Johnston long-term.
- 2024-06-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-13]]), evaluating the RP rookie mini sample (3 games): Brendan Rice, a 7th-round USC pick at 6'2'/210 with a 4.5-second 40 but poor athletic testing otherwise (36.5' vertical, 9'11' broad jump, both weak), was hammered by his most-used route -- nine routes were 35.4% of his charted sample and he won only 39% of the time on them, dragging his overall success rates to 52.2% vs. man, 75.8% vs. zone and 64.3% vs. press. He was also poor on post routes (28.6%) and is not a good ball tracker down the field despite the Jerry Rice bloodline. But he flashed real ability in-breaking: 83.3% success rate on slants, 76% on curls, and both hosts liked what they saw on the dig route on tape. Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) comp him to former Panthers/Bears possession receiver Muhsin Muhammad -- a throwback outside/flanker X-type, not a big slot -- and note the Chargers' WR depth chart behind Ladd McConkey is wide open (Quentin Johnston, Josh Palmer, D.J. Chark), giving Rice a real chance to stick on the roster and even contribute early despite his round-7 draft slot. [Dynasty]
