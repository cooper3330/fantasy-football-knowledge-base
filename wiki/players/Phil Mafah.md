# Phil Mafah

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-13]]) [Dynasty]: a hope-against-hope profile. At 6'1 230 out of Clemson he looks like he could be fast and Waldman wants him to be [[Leonard Fournette]] or Stephen Davis, but says it is not there and he does not know if it will be — good enough to be on the field and do a number of things, not good enough to project beyond that. His fear is that Mafah becomes Wayne Gallman, the fourth-round Clemson back who spent time with the Giants and the 49ers without ever getting better and ended up a career backup who occasionally gets in a game. Waldman and [[Brandon Angelo]] noted the coincidence that Gallman and Mafah are both Clemson products from the same hometown of Loganville, Georgia.
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): Mafah is genuinely overlooked by draft media, and Waldman 'feels it' on that proposition — the media is hyper-focused on fast scat backs who flip the field and on high-end production, while Mafah is built like a Stephen Davis or [[Leonard Fournette]] type. Waldman also suspects logo scouting is at work: teams have soured on recent Clemson backs on the theory that the system leaves them underdeveloped as decision-makers, and he concedes he can see some of that. His own read is a good between-the-tackles banger with some speed and burst who catches the football, and he would not be shocked if Mafah went in the fifth-to-seventh round, made a roster, and turned into at worst a [[Gus Edwards]] type. Bob Harris (co-host, not a tracked expert) read the Edwards comp as shade at [[Travis Etienne Jr.]]. Waldman groups him with [[LeQuint Allen]] as similar RSP scores with different floor/ceiling shapes.
- 2026-05-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-18]]) [Dynasty]: grouped with [[Jayden Blue]] as a rookie who hasn't yet earned expected offseason buzz behind emerging [[Malik Davis]] in Dallas -- Waldman said fuck it on both as must-hold dynasty stashes right now, calling them 'wait and see' pieces you can cut loose if needed.
- 2026-06-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-22 (Feel It or Fk It)]]) [Dynasty]: profiles Mafah as a 'dirty work guy' comparable to Isaiah Davis -- big and strong enough to carry a load if needed, but without standout top-end speed or explosiveness as it shows up on tape, behind starter [[Javonte Williams]] and more explosive backup [[Jaydon Blue]].
