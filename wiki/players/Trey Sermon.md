# Trey Sermon

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: a split verdict — **feel it short-term, f\*\*k it long-term**. With Zack Moss hurt, Sermon is a viable one-week fill-in and has a chance to be the closeout back against Atlanta. But the film is unexciting: "competent," no decision-making mistakes, some ability to bounce outside and outrun penetration, some power and pad level, and nothing special beyond that. Waldman's comp is a mix of Jordan Howard and Jamaal Williams "with a dash of maybe David Montgomery" if you want to hope for more athletic upside. He expects [[Tyler Goodson]] to hold more long-term value for the Colts and possibly to out-produce Sermon even in the short run.
- 2024-09-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-30]]): with [[Jonathan Taylor]] dealing with a high ankle sprain against Jacksonville, Waldman graded Sermon a low-end but real stopgap — 'Sermon can certainly get the volume.' If the Colts can get ahead with Joe Flacco starting, which Waldman said is the likely case, Sermon is the type of back whose volume could produce a 100-yard day. He framed both Colts backs as cheap points rather than every-week starters.
