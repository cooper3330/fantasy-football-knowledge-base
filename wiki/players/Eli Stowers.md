- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: his favorite 2026 tight end — a 6-4, 235 former quarterback (likely 240-245 by next season) who moved to tight end after a knee injury and has not played the position long. Waldman's notes: good suddenness in his movements suggesting the craft of releases can develop; he can beat high safeties at the near-side boundary with acceleration; he has the weight drop into whip routes and should improve his pop-and-drop breaks; good flat-drive and line steps produce sharp inside and outside breaks; he is a friendly target who attacks the ball, digs out low throws, high-points and has the hand-eye coordination for one-handed catches. After the catch he runs like the quarterback-runner he was — feet high, works over low shots, decisive between the tackles on screens, hits with authority but stays patient, and manages risk well as a ball carrier.
