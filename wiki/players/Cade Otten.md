# Cade Otten

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-10-24 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-24]]): Harmon said he was confused by the fantasy discourse arguing over which Tampa Bay wide receiver inherits [[Chris Godwin]]'s targets, because the likeliest volume winner is Otten — 'I think that guy is going to actually catch a ton of passes going forward'. He grouped Otten with [[Rachaad White]] as the pass catchers who realistically absorb work with Godwin out for the year and [[Mike Evans]] hurt, ahead of [[Jalen McMillan]] and [[Trey Palmer]]. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) agreed, adding that White should see close to ten targets a game.
- 2024-10-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-28]]): asked to choose between Otton (then TE6) and [[Kyle Pitts]] (then TE4) for the rest of the season, Waldman took Otton, for four reasons — opportunity created by the weaknesses of Tampa Bay's receiving corps; a true rapport with [[Baker Mayfield]]; a scheme that actively works him open in ways Atlanta is not doing for Pitts; and greater versatility, since he blocks more often, which sets up play-action and more chances to get open for big plays. He is the more consistent player right now and, while not the athlete Pitts is after the catch, is flexible and maneuverable with the ball in the air and makes tight-coverage catches. Waldman said Otton has the higher upside going forward; Bob Harris (not tracked) agreed and expected fewer ups and downs and more even production. (Note: this page uses the spelling already in the vault; the player is Cade Otton.)
- 2025-02-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-17]]) [Best Ball]: 'fuck it' at Underdog TE19 relative to [[Kyle Pitts]], though Waldman said he likes Otten. Untracked co-host Bob Harris supplied the key fact: Otten's target share evaporated the moment [[Mike Evans]] returned to the lineup, so his upside was totally dependent on Evans being out — and if Tampa Bay retains [[Chris Godwin]], a three-man rotation with [[Jalen McMillan]] squeezes him further. Harris's projection is a long career as a good tight end who sneaks into the top 12 occasionally, which is why he prefers the super-upside swing at that point in a draft.
- 2025-08-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-28]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Otten has a leg injury and will be out for a little bit, with no clarity on whether that means missed practices or missed regular-season games. Harris's advice is blunt — hopefully you are not relying too heavily on Cade Otten.
- 2025-09-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris is dismissive of the Otton case, calling him 'not an exciting option, the fabric of our lives' and mocking that the only thing the shouty stat-quoters ever say about him is that he had three high-volume, productive games last year when all the Tampa receivers were hurt. He allows that with [[Chris Godwin]] and [[Jalen McMillan]] unavailable some accidental volume might find Otton again, but Otton suffered a leg injury a couple of weeks earlier and Harris says you should not start him until you know he is healthy — start your starter and see from there.
