## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: weaves through traffic, finds open space, patiently sets up defenders on blocks and exploits their leverage to cut away, and is aware/effective at the goal line. Waldman's blunt bottom line: "let's just be real, this dude's a running back" who has outgrown the tight end position — a decisive gap runner with YAC skills who needs a different role than tight end. Projects as a special-teamer and maybe a gadget second tight end rather than a starter.
- 2024-05-13 — According to Bob Harris (untracked, RSP Cast co-host) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-13]]): Bell, a seventh-round fullback pick, has no fantasy relevance, and Harris said he doesn't understand why anyone would roster him outside of special teams value. [[Matt Waldman]] offered the lone caveat that if New England tries to use Bell in a Chris Cooley-style old-school fullback/H-back role or an Aaron Hernandez-type checkdown role there's a sliver of upside, but he'd still rather see those targets go to Rhamondre Stevenson out of the backfield. Both hosts agree Bell is not a fantasy option in any format.
