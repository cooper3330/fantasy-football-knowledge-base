# Pat Freiermuth

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: a real usage-risk flag off the Arthur Smith-to-Pittsburgh hire (see [[Scheme vs Talent]]), not a talent knock. Pittsburgh now has three tight ends who can play (Freiermuth, rookie Darnell Washington, and Connor Heyward, a former Michigan State fullback), and Waldman's read is "two of those three guys are probably going to be okay, and it's probably going to be the more athletic two" — i.e., not necessarily Freiermuth. His explicit worry is a repeat of what happened to Kyle Pitts under Smith in Atlanta last year, tempered by one mitigating factor Pitts didn't have: Freiermuth was playing through a real injury for part of that stretch, which muddies how much to blame the scheme itself.
- 2024-06-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-10]]): called Freiermuth, currently going as roughly TE16, a cheap gamble with TE1 upside and possibly the most accomplished receiver, period, on the Steelers -- a good route runner who gets open and is 'kind of what Michael Mayer wants to be.' He argued the market is overlooking him because of how badly OC Arthur Smith's Falcons misused Kyle Pitts, but attributed that to being stuck with Desmond Ridder running a simplified, static-route offense rather than a real read on Smith's tight-end usage; with better quarterback play in Pittsburgh, Freiermuth should benefit. Waldman expects the fantasy community to stay skeptical anyway, keeping his price low.
- 2025-06-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: he is emphatically 'feeling' that [[Aaron Rodgers]] unlocks Freiermuth's full fantasy potential. Waldman has liked him on film since his rookie year with Ben Roethlisberger, especially in the red area, and believes Rodgers will trust him because — other than an out-of-prime [[Robert Woods]] — he is the one Steeler in his prime who will be where he is supposed to be and on the same page reading defenses. Freiermuth is also an excellent contested-catch player. Waldman's comp: Heath Miller, who he thinks would have been a 10-12 touchdown tight end in Green Bay instead of a check-down option; he sees a real chance at double-digit touchdowns on roughly 600-700 yards and a return to the top 12 at the position. Co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) said Freiermuth is one of his most-rostered best ball players at his TE19 price, drafting him around picks 12-13 as a second tight end, because he always expected the Rodgers signing.
- 2025-06-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-30]]): the [[Jonnu Smith]] trade dents but does not ruin Freiermuth, whom Waldman had just drafted as his third tight end in the tight-end-heavy Scott Fish Bowl. Using Arthur Smith's 2023 Atlanta split ([[Kyle Pitts]] and Jonnu Smith combining for 158 targets and about 1,245 yards), he projects Freiermuth at 60-70 targets, roughly 600-700 yards and three to four touchdowns, with Smith at 70-80 targets — Freiermuth as the second tight end rather than the clear lead. He expects [[DK Metcalf]], Freiermuth and Smith to be the top three target earners, with [[Aaron Rodgers]] having meaningful input on the scheme. Waldman said he would be adjusting his rankings and projections for Freiermuth as a result.
- 2025-07-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Freiermuth is not as doomed by the [[Jonnu Smith]] trade as people think. Waldman calls him the best contested-catch player on the roster, period — not [[DK Metcalf]], not [[Calvin Austin III]], not [[Roman Wilson]] or [[Robert Woods]], definitely not [[Darnell Washington]] or Smith. He is slow ('Slowkowski'), but works the seam, is very good in the red zone and end zone, and gets thrown open, which matters in a place where Ben Roethlisberger, Russell Wilson (five touchdowns to him) and [[Justin Fields]] all fed the tight end. Waldman projects roughly 50 catches, 500-600 yards and 8-10 touchdowns — fantasy starter value, just not a 70-catch season. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert), who drafted him the day before the trade, said both tight ends are less valuable than the day before but the tight end premium numbers may still work.
- 2025-08-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-04]]) [Best Ball]: Waldman says most of the market is way too low on Freiermuth because it overrates the [[Jonnu Smith]] addition. His read on Smith is that in Arthur Smith's offense he produces low-end RB / TE1-or-high-TE2 value on flat routes, dump-offs and some red-zone work, but is not good at tracking the football one-on-one or winning against man coverage up the seam. Freiermuth is the opposite: great at tracking the ball, good one-on-one in the red zone, an excellent contested-catch player with power. Waldman predicts Freiermuth leads the Steelers in touchdowns — over [[DK Metcalf]], [[Calvin Austin III]] and [[Jaylen Warren]] — with double-digit scores and 600–700 receiving yards, arguing [[Aaron Rodgers]] is the first quarterback capable of targeting him one-on-one properly and that the reported connection is strong. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) cites a longtime beat writer calling Freiermuth the heartbeat of the offense, and says he is buying everywhere at TE24 / pick 195, a price he thinks should be roughly halved.
- 2025-09-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-04 (Film & Theory Sequences, Signal, Steelers)]]): [[Pat Freiermuth]] is the only Steelers pass catcher Waldman considers a sure bet to be conceptually advanced enough for what [[Aaron Rodgers]] will demand pre-snap. He framed Freiermuth's career as quarterback-dependent — just old enough to catch the end of Ben Roethlisberger, then a run of what he jokingly called illegitimate starters, before a taste of competence with Russell Wilson — and cited the split as evidence: five of Freiermuth's seven touchdowns last year came with Wilson, and the last time he reached seven touchdowns was with Roethlisberger. The implication for 2025 is that Rodgers represents another quarterback upgrade for a tight end whose production has tracked quarterback quality closely.
- 2025-09-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-15]]): Waldman is 'feeling' more targets for Freiermuth, while openly flagging that he may be confirming his own bias. His evidence: [[Aaron Rodgers]] tried to hit him in the end zone before [[Calvin Austin III]] made the wrong route adjustment into traffic and caused the interception, plus a beautiful high-point catch on an out-and-up down the sideline. Waldman argues Freiermuth can be for Pittsburgh what [[Tucker Kraft]] is for Green Bay — a scheme-created-space tight end — and that Pittsburgh could scheme him open the same way but does not. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) noted Freiermuth was second in targets and cited Steelers beat writer Mark Kaboly's summer take that Freiermuth could end up the heartbeat of the offense, calling it maybe an overstatement but directionally right, especially near the goal line.
- 2025-10-17 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-17]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Freiermuth caught two touchdowns against Cincinnati, the second a 68-yard catch-and-run off a busted coverage after [[Aaron Rodgers]] escaped the pocket on second-and-20. Harris says it's hard to chase for fantasy purposes because Pittsburgh's tight end scoring is split three ways with [[Jonnu Smith]] and [[Darnell Washington]], making none of them a reliable weekly starter despite the box score.
- 2025-10-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman said 'fuck it' on Freiermuth despite a big touchdown game, crediting most of the production to [[Aaron Rodgers]] rather than a role change -- the first score came off Rodgers correctly reading a Cover 2 look with wide linebacker splits and Freiermuth working underneath, and the second off Rodgers spinning away from pressure and finding a wide-open Freiermuth against a split Cover 3/Cover 2 shell. Waldman likes how Pittsburgh uses him as an extension of the run game and thinks they'd be wise to keep leaning on him that way, especially with Darnell Washington used as a blocker and Jonnu Smith preferred in the flats. But he expects targets to shrink further once Calvin Austin returns, since Freiermuth is currently playing extra snaps largely because [[Roman Wilson]] hasn't produced ('can't do fuck,' in Waldman's words, despite beat-writer hype calling him the offense's heartbeat). Waldman still holds dynasty shares he's hoping recover, calling this outlier game a hopeful but unproven signal.
- 2025-11-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-03]]): Waldman said it is unjustified to give [[Darnell Washington]] run at Freiermuth's expense — the snaps should come off [[Jonnu Smith]] instead. In this offense he considers Freiermuth the better blocker, the better contested-catch receiver, and the tight end who can run a wider route tree; he is the best in the room at adjusting to the ball, which showed on his work with [[Aaron Rodgers]] this week, and he carries downfield big-play potential as a good route runner. He told Arthur Smith to stop protesting and keep Freiermuth on the field, calling Washington plus Freiermuth the best of both worlds.
- 2025-12-09 — According to Jeff Bell (Football Guys, not tracked) and Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Bell's #5 Week 15 waiver add (11% rostered) -- with Darnell Washington dealing with a concussion, Bell expects Freiermuth to step into a heavy role in a Steelers offense that has funneled a lot through tight end, especially amid reported friction between Jonnu Smith and Aaron Rodgers. Harris agrees Freiermuth is a viable streaming option (roughly TE11-15) for managers desperate at the position, though the Monday night game complicates lineup timing around Washington's status.
- 2026-01-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-15]]): Freiermuth 'completely disappeared' from the Steelers offense in 2025, grouped alongside [[Jonnu Smith]] as pass-catchers Arthur Smith's scheme failed to feature.
- 2026-05-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-18]]) [Redraft]: with Arthur Smith gone as Pittsburgh's OC, Waldman noted new play-caller Mike McCarthy has a track record of getting good value out of tight ends, which could help Freiermuth after beat reporters wrongly pegged him as Smith's most valuable offensive weapon last year -- a projection Waldman called 'a big miss' and 'a flat line' that never really materialized, though he conceded the Bengals did give up production to Freiermuth in a couple of games late in the season.
- 2026-06-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said he is 'starting to feel it' on Pat Freiermuth after being a year early on the same call in 2025. Unlike last year, when outside reporters (not Freiermuth himself) hyped him as the offense's 'heart and soul,' this year Freiermuth is saying it himself -- that the role is his to claim. Waldman doesn't currently see him as a leading Steelers receiver but could see him becoming the red-zone touchdown leader, projecting a path to 500-600 yards with 8-10 touchdowns if that happens, which would make him the fantasy factor many expected last year. Waldman also noted [[Darnell Washington]] has trimmed down from over 300 pounds to around 270, which the team reportedly views as a positive for his role.
