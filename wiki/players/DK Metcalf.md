# DK Metcalf

## Expert Takes

- 2024-06-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-18]]) [Best Ball]: Waldman calls DK Metcalf, who per Next Gen Stats was the fastest player in the NFL last season, currently underpriced at roughly WR22 on Underdog ADP (a third-round pick), and expects a pass-happier offense under new OC Ryan Grubb (drawing on Grubb's University of Washington system) to boost his opportunity. Waldman says he likes all three Seattle receivers (Metcalf, Lockett, Smith-Njigba) and views the whole group as underpriced relative to the passing volume he expects this season.
- 2024-06-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-20]]): new OC Ryan Grubb's Washington Huskies offense was an extreme, downfield-heavy vertical passing attack, and Harmon views that as close to a perfect scheme fit for Metcalf, who 'hasn't had one of his most productive seasons in a minute.' Caveats that the fit depends heavily on pass protection holding up, and that quarterback play (whether Geno Smith can push the ball downfield effectively) matters as much as scheme.
- 2024-07-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Redraft]: Waldman says Metcalf's WR20 ADP is correctly priced and expects him to outperform it, projecting him closer to WR1 territory than WR3 territory. The driver is new OC Ryan Grubb running a far more pass-heavy offense than Seattle has used previously; Waldman calls 'production' -- not talent or scheme fit -- the swing factor, i.e. the plus/minus on where Metcalf actually lands.
