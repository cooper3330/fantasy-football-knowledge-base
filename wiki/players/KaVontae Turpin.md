# KaVontae Turpin

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-23]]): a worthwhile temporary add if you are desperate at receiver or rotating the back of your bench — but a deep-roster play only, not a 15-man-roster play. He liked Turpin's inside zone-beating routes, his ability to win against man coverage on some reps, his reliability as a pass catcher and what he does after the catch, and noted [[Dak Prescott]] trusted him enough to produce two touchdowns or at least two big plays. Bob Harris (untracked) cautioned that Turpin played only 18 snaps against 71 for [[CeeDee Lamb]], 64 for Cooks and 60 for [[Jalen Tolbert]], but agreed on deep rosters — his reasoning being that Dallas is not going to be a run-heavy team and will throw a lot this year.
- 2024-12-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-02]]): feeling him as a break-glass-in-case-of-emergency playoff add. Waldman notes a chunk play in essentially every game over the past three or four weeks — not only on returns but in the short passing game — plus built-up trust with Cooper Rush from second-team reps together, and success finding openings out of the slot. He expects usage to keep increasing and compares the archetype to Marcel Reece with the Raiders: a limited-workload difference-maker who can win you a week outright. Co-host Bob Harris (not tracked) added the Next Gen Stats hook that Turpin posted the fastest speed in the NFL this season at 22.36 mph on a 64-yard reception, and that he has had games with as many as seven targets, though not since mid-season.
- 2025-05-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-12]]): with [[George Pickens]] pulling coverage downfield, Turpin has looked good in the slot and profiles as a big-play option who could be a waiver-wire addition worth monitoring rather than a draft-day target. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) noted his Next Gen Stats mark of 22.36 mph last season, the fastest speed recorded in the NFL, while joking that speed alone is not the point.
