# Alvin Kamara

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]): flags real 2024 uncertainty driven by New Orleans's dire salary-cap situation rather than by anything on tape — notes he could be "on thin ice" there, though Waldman expects the Saints to restructure their way out of the crunch rather than move on from core pieces, pushing the cap problem further down the road as usual. On performance: over the weeks he actually played (roughly Weeks 4-17), Kamara finished close to a top-4 fantasy running back. States his workload "is not over" if he stays in New Orleans, and specifically flags a Klint Kubiak/Shanahan-tree-style scheme as a good fit that could extend his effective range as a receiving-heavy back.
