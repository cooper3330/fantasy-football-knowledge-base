# Nyck Harbor

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: firmly on his do-not-draft list for 2026, alongside [[Evan Stewart]]. From what he has seen, Harbor makes everything look difficult — he uses him as the negative pole against [[KC Concepcion]] and is skeptical of the mocks placing him high. [[Brandon Angelo]] agrees: watching [[LaNorris Sellers]] he saw Harbor, South Carolina's number one receiver for most of the year, make everything look hard. Angelo's constructive suggestion is that Harbor move to tight end and add 15-20 pounds — a 10.11-second 100-meter track athlete with that linear profile could be 'what [[Kyle Pitts]] should have been' at the position, but he is emphatically not an outside receiver.
