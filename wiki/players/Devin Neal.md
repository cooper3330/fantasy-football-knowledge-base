# Devin Neal

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-13]]) [Dynasty]: the most underrated back in the class by the public. Angelo says that in the 2024 running back class Neal would have been battling [[Jonathan Brooks]] to be his first or second back. The signature trait is a left-foot lead plant in the open field that lets him re-accelerate, bait defenders toward his outside shoulder and explode upfield — a mechanism that reminds him of [[DeAndre Swift]] coming out of Georgia, but under far better control. Angelo's point is that Neal marries function and dynamism, which he considers what it takes to play the position at a high level, and he can do whatever an offense needs; he is simply not a mega athlete. His expectation is that the depth of this class causes Neal to fall, at which point people will wonder how.
- 2025-06-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-16]]) [Dynasty]: Neal is an unknown because of how he was used in college — a gap scheme where, in Waldman's own admittedly hyperbolic framing, he mostly got weird toss plays and rarely ran NFL-style runs between the tackles, so there is much less evidence than the production suggests. Waldman still thinks Neal has the potential to become a good NFL back, but the likelier outcome is a Bilal Powell-type utility player who can do a little of everything and get the job done if forced to start — not the man you want replacing Alvin Kamara long term. He rates [[Kendre Miller]] ahead of him on talent, and reads the Cam Akers signing as the team hedging against Neal not working out.
- 2025-10-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-28]]): Neal is a Saints honorable-mention add, but Harris cautions against 'chasing the box score' -- Neal didn't touch the ball at all in New Orleans' most recent game because the team simply threw it constantly, not because [[Alvin Kamara]] is fading. Guest Scott Fish (not tracked) believes Neal will ramp up as Kamara's role shrinks; Harris disagrees, arguing the whole offense, not just the backfield, is the problem, and calling Kamara 'the mayor of New Orleans' in a disastrous unit, implying entrenched touches regardless of efficiency.
- 2025-11-24 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: With Alvin Kamara out after a knee injury, Neal became the primary Saints back (alongside a heavy Taysom Hill red-zone role) and had a couple of nice runs. Harris was cautious - 'I'm not telling you that Devin Neal looks like a sure bet' - but expects him to be a notable waiver-wire add this week given Kamara's uncertain status.
- 2025-11-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-25]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: ranked as Harris's #1 (and Scott Fish's #2) Week 13 waiver add with Alvin Kamara out. Neal played nearly 90% of snaps and ran a heavy route tree once Kamara left the game, but Harris flags that it may partly reflect Audric Estime being a healthy scratch that week (Estime is expected active in Week 13), so the workload 'isn't guaranteed volume' in a poor offense. Still, with a great Week 13 matchup against Miami, Harris compares his outlook to Tony Pollard's workload and says he 'could save you in a flex,' potentially playing like a low-end starting running back.
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-08]]): Neal punched in a short touchdown and broke a 21-yard carry to the outside during the worst of the rain against Tampa Bay, handling a 'man's workload' of 20 touches in the upset win, which Harris said could work him into flex-type territory. But he cautioned nothing about the tape looked extraordinary, Neal ceded some short-yardage work to Audric Estime, and the prior week Neal managed just 14 carries for 47 yards - so the workload swing 'will always be on the table.'
- 2025-12-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-15]]) [Dynasty]: both Waldman and Bob Harris said 'fuck it' on Neal being a 2026 Saints starter -- neither believes it yet, though Waldman wants to see New Orleans 'hit on all three' of its skill-position bets (also referencing [[Tyler Shough]] and an anticipated receiver addition) for the rebuild to take shape.
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]): [[Devin Neal]] scored New Orleans' first touchdown on a 12-minute, penalty-extended second-quarter drive against Carolina but hurt his hamstring on the play and left the game. His Week 16 availability is unresolved; if he's out, Harris expects [[Audric Estime]] and [[Evan Hull]] to split work, with Estime having 'injected some life' in relief with two clutch screen catches late.
- 2026-07-07 — According to untracked guest Jim McCormick ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: McCormick argues that if [[Alvin Kamara]] is moved off the Saints roster, Devin Neal -- not the knee-injured Kendre Miller -- should be the preferred backup behind [[Travis Etienne Jr.]], noting Neal finished last season well before an in-game celebration knee injury (jumping after a touchdown) ended his year; the team never confirmed the mechanism, but McCormick says he watched the film and is confident that's how it happened.
- 2026-08-20 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Neal returned from a prolonged absence in the same Saints joint practice where [[Alvin Kamara]] got hurt, putting him in the mix behind Kamara. Harris says it's unclear whether Neal or [[Kendre Miller]] would be the number-two option, and it may end up split between the two.
