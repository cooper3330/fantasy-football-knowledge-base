# Jonathan Brooks

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-29]]), a higher-profile name than the other Shrine Game backs discussed the same day: good size, and a real standout as a pass protector — "the guy can pass protect really well." Wants to see more before a full grade, but calls him a real three-down-back candidate in the league, with an explicit note that pass protection is an increasingly valuable skill for backs given rising quarterback contract costs across the league. Expects his athletic testing to grade well given Texas's recruiting level.
- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: named one of the class's clear early-round talents regardless of actual draft slot — Angelo grades him a round-two prospect, and Waldman independently ranks him third overall in the class (after [[Blake Corum]] and [[Dylan Laube]]) among these early-round names. Style comp: "smooth," with a Cadillac Williams-esque feel and a game that looks slowed down for him — Angelo's specific stylistic comp is a smaller version of Rhamondre Stevenson (not a tracked page). Grades him as one of the best, or one of the closest to best, pass protectors in the entire class. The torn ACL is the swing variable on draft slot and rookie-year availability — could realistically fall to Day 3 depending on medical evaluations despite the talent grade, with both hosts floating that his real impact season may not come until 2025.
- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast [Dynasty]: his clearest statement yet — absent the ACL tear, Brooks "would easily, easily be the top running back on the board" for NFL teams. "Moves like a veteran in the prime of his NFL career" and grades as the most efficient mover of the entire 2024 class, using every ounce of his 216 lbs to run through multiple points of contact on a given carry while "often looking effortless."

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-28 (Going Deep - If We Were NFL GMs)]]): names him his RB1 (Angelo has [[Blake Corum]] one spot ahead, a gap both call small), and the best pass protector in the class from the running back position. Frames his game through the "problem solvers" lens the episode built around Corum and [[Caleb Williams]] — best players dictate the chain of movements they want rather than react to them, and have a quick counter when something disrupts that chain; Brooks has that, plus enough gliding speed, acceleration and scheme versatility to mitigate direct angles of attack.

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-11 (Going Deep - Fave Evals, Coach-Killer Candidates, and High-End Career Outcomes)]]), both hosts' clearest statement of their affection for Brooks yet: Waldman credits his handle on **sequential information** — understanding information in sequence "that he is the creator of," a trait he says [[DeAndre Swift]], Miles Sanders and [[Marshawn Lloyd]] (not tracked comps) all lack. Angelo frames it as an "attack first" mentality — dictating the movement chain and forcing the defender to react, rather than absorbing the first hit — and calls his calm processing when plays get muddy in the backfield a rare trait for a young back. Both invoke down-to-down control as the shared elite-back trait, naming [[Christian McCaffrey]] (exceptional, "the best running back in the league" in their view) and [[Nick Chubb]] (fascinatingly good) as the standard, with [[Saquon Barkley]] cited as a cautionary comp — athletically never fully the same since his rookie-year ACL, but still a top-10 back because his game evolved around it. The open question for Brooks post-ACL is whether his deceleration/re-acceleration and contact integrity return to pre-injury form (usually a year-two-post development), but "if everything were across the board normal, Brooks is the absolutely number one back in this class hands down." Projects an early-second-round selection, floats Dallas as a likely landing spot, and calls him a Pro Bowl-caliber player if he returns to full health.
- 2024-05-02 — According to Brandon Angelo ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-02]]): Angelo said he feels bad for Brooks landing in Carolina's infrastructure, which he called poor even with Dave Canales now at the helm — he expects it will take years to improve and might never fully recover given ownership he described as dysfunctional (citing owner David Tepper's reported involvement calling plays as an example).
- 2024-06-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-18]]): Waldman says it's no surprise Jonathan Brooks won't be ready for the start of training camp after his ACL tear, and expects him to open the season on the PUP list. He compares the likely trajectory to Breece Hall's 2023 return - a fast start followed by stretches of caution and soreness management - and expects Brooks to finish 2024 stronger than he starts, which could add value if his current ADP undervalues the back half of the season. Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) calls warm 'ready for camp' buzz a predictable pre-draft-season trap that shouldn't change the plan.
