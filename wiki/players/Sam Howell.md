# Sam Howell

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast -
  2024-03-19]]: on Howell's trade to Seattle to compete with incumbent Geno
  Smith (not a tracked page) — grades Smith as having more shown-on-tape
  upside, but says Howell "gives you the mystery of what could be" since the
  league hasn't seen the full range of his game yet. Comps him to Drew Lock —
  a talented but inconsistent player who could still rehab his career with
  the right fit. Predicts Seattle stages a real-looking competition (with
  Mike Macdonald's staff avoiding committing publicly either way) but expects
  Smith to keep the job, with Howell pushing him "at some point... by
  mid-season."

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]]: says the Howell trade "throws a little cold water" on Seattle being an ideal [[Michael Penix Jr.]] landing spot despite new OC Ryan Grubb's Washington ties. On the live Howell-vs-[[Geno Smith]] competition: when everybody's playing their best, Smith "wins this in a cakewalk," but Howell being hot is enough to make it a compelling competition; argues the two are perceived very differently for similar underlying traits — Smith seen as shakier under pressure though better at moving and finding open men, Howell seen as tougher mainly because he's more willing to throw into pressure — "if you switched jerseys... you would probably have the same things to say about both of them."
