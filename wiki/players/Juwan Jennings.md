# Juwan Jennings

## Expert Takes

- 2026-01-20 — According to James Koh (untracked co-host) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-20]] [Dynasty]: Juwan Jennings is a pending free agent both hosts expect to move on from San Francisco, leaving the 49ers' receiver room 'light' behind Ricky Pearsall and largely unproven depth heading into what Koh called a needed wide receiver rebuild.
- 2026-03-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-03-12]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harmon said Jennings 'just was not good' last year working as San Francisco's on-ball outside receiver in Brandon Aiyuk's absence -- he doesn't separate at a high enough level to play X -- and wasn't healthy all season despite scoring some touchdowns. Signing [[Mike Evans]] should reduce Jennings's exposure to that miscast role.
- 2026-05-11 — According to Matt Waldman ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-11]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman likes the theoretical fit for Juwan Jennings in Minnesota better than the reported Miami interest that didn't materialize -- Jennings isn't a burner or a complete route runner, but the Vikings' outside speed at Jefferson and Addison should occupy safeties and let Jennings win underneath and inside in a way Miami's speed-first personnel wouldn't have. The swing factor is the quarterback: Waldman wants [[J.J. McCarthy]] to win the job because he distributes the ball across the offense rather than locking onto one target, whereas if [[Kyler Murray]] plays like he did in Arizona, Waldman said Jennings becomes 'Michael Wilson, a worse version of Michael Wilson' production-wise.
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Jeff Erickson (Rotowire, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28]]): Jennings, newly added to Minnesota, is described by Harris as a longtime 'fantasy killer' possession receiver -- 'if you need 10 yards, I'll get you 8' -- sure-handed and willing to take hits over the middle, with a history of some high-volume, high-touchdown games against teams lacking a third corner. Both hosts rank him behind Jordan Addison for fantasy purposes since he lacks Addison's big-play speed, but note there's a real role for him in this offense and it's not something training camp joint practices will meaningfully clarify.
- 2026-10-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-10-15]]): Of 83 qualifying wide receivers, Jennings is targeted on just 4.2% of his routes -- dead last by a wide margin (next-worst is Bryce Lance at ~9%, then [[Marvin Harrison Jr]] at 9.2%) -- averaging only 0.08 yards per route run despite a 58% slot-snap rate. Harmon's read: Jennings isn't getting targeted because he 'was toast last year,' not primarily a coaching-staff grudge, though he understands reported frustration with the Minnesota staff and rumors Jennings wants out. Harmon and Koh both found a reported 49ers reunion (via a 7th-round pick) confusing given San Francisco chose not to re-sign Jennings in the first place -- 'that sounds like it makes absolutely no sense' -- though they noted the Niners' receiver room has changed enough to make it at least plausible.
