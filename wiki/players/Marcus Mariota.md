# Marcus Mariota

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-10-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-21 (Feel It or Fk It 10.21.24)]]): asked whether Mariota's spot start was good scheme, good matchup or good player, Waldman crossed off good player 'by starter standards' — he had good play, but he is 'kind of like [[Gardner Minshew]] in this, though I think Minshew's a little headier and better.' Not a good NFL starter, but obviously a good scheme and, against Carolina, 'a great matchup without a doubt — the Panthers' defense just is bad.' The provocative extension Waldman drew: if Mariota can walk in and deliver that game, how much of Washington's offense is [[Jayden Daniels]] and how much is the scheme? He expects that question to get sharper as schemes get found out and game-planned for.
- 2024-12-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-09]]): Mariota is the low-grade version of the fantasy black hole quarterback. Waldman's evidence is his Atlanta tenure — [[Mack Hollins]] produced while [[Drake London]] and [[Kyle Pitts]] were largely shut out of the offense. He frames Mariota as a good example of a bad NFL starter: limited in what he can do to actually help a team win, but capable of putting up starter-level fantasy points even when none of the pass catchers he is supposed to support do anything.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-11]]): on film against Detroit, Mariota was not tormented by the pass rush so much as unable to move the offense - the Lions spied him and took away the run, 'and he's just not all that good a passer'. Despite that, Mariota is Harris's Week 11 streaming quarterback against Miami, on the 20-to-30-yard rushing floor: in four starts his worst finish is QB18, mostly middle of the pack, with one top-10 finish, and Miami has around two interceptions on the year. 'Yeah, he stinks, but' - guest **Brandon Funston** (The Athletic, not tracked) disagreed and would stream [[Geno Smith]] against the Cowboys instead, predicting a five-touchdown game if Smith proves healthy.
