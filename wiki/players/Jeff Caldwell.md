# Jeff Caldwell

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-04-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-23]]): Caldwell, [[Cyrus Allen]]'s Cincinnati teammate, is a 'freak show level' athlete -- 6'5', 216 lbs, 4.31 40, 42-inch vertical, 11'2' broad jump -- with a mockdraftable.com spider chart Harmon says fills out almost entirely (96th percentile height, 96th broad jump, 97th vertical, 96th 40, 95th 10-yard split), and a closest athletic comp of [[Christian Watson]]. But Harmon says the tape does not match the testing: he 'wasn't a great separator' and his development as a route runner 'was not good either,' leading Harmon to comp his on-field play to [[Dante Thornton]] rather than his measurables. Harmon explicitly warns that he hopes 'some team' doesn't draft Caldwell in the fourth round and pencil him in as a day-one starting X receiver the way a team did with Thornton -- he needs real development first, though the ceiling from special-teams value and physical tools alone is real if a receivers coach can clean up his release and hip movement.
