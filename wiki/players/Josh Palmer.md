# Josh Palmer

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): called "a
  non-embarrassing number three receiver" who now, after the [[Keenan Allen]]
  trade, might have to be the Chargers' de facto number one — a big reason the
  hosts name the Chargers' receiver room (Palmer plus [[Quentin Johnston]]) the
  single worst in the NFL. Acknowledges "Josh Palmer bros" expecting a 2024
  breakout given the added opportunity, but is noncommittal ("who knows?").
- 2024-05-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-06]]): Waldman calls Josh Palmer a proven, capable contributor in a complementary rather than lead role for the Chargers, with his hold on snaps this year dependent on efficiency given Jim Harbaugh/Greg Roman's expected run-heavy offense.
- 2024-06-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-13]]): Harmon floats Josh Palmer as the Chargers' likely 'sacrificial' X receiver -- not a great scheme fit for the role, but capable of a professional, C-plus-level job there similar to how Romeo Doubs (untracked comp) is used as Green Bay's functional WR3, which would let the offense deploy more athletically interesting players like Quentin Johnston and Ladd McConkey around him. [Dynasty]
- 2024-07-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-18]]): Harmon described Palmer as a 'non-needle-mover' receiver but a solid, reliable piece in a Chargers passing game both hosts expect to lean heavily on play-action within a run-first, 60/40 split under Jim Harbaugh and Greg Roman, given guest OL analyst Brandon Thorn's (not tracked) 7th-ranked offensive line grade for LA, anchored by LT Rashawn Slater and rookie RT Joe Alt.
- 2024-07-29 — According to Bob Harris (co-host, not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-29 (Feel It or F-It)]]: he expects to invest more in Palmer than in [[Ladd McConkey]] purely on price, calling himself a cheapskate — Palmer is available around WR50-something and it is 'not out of the range of possibilities' that he leads the Chargers in receptions. He stresses this is not a disagreement with [[Matt Waldman]]'s view that McConkey is the team's WR1; Waldman's counter is the Porsche-vs-Toyota analogy — at similar prices, take the better car.
- 2024-08-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-01]]): Palmer is a player you can get by with as a No. 2 but is an ideal No. 3. Asked by co-host James Koh (not tracked) whether a pass offense can function with Palmer at 110-120 targets, Harmon answers it can be functional and even efficient, but probably not high volume. He also argues Palmer, not [[Ladd McConkey]], is the better fit for the run-game-adjacent slot snaps under Greg Roman because he has more size to set the edge as a blocker. Koh is mixed on Palmer overall but concedes he could lead the Chargers in yards on more downfield usage, and floats a 900-yard season if the staff misuses McConkey as a low-aDOT slot. Harmon notes he still owes subscribers a Palmer profile before the August 26 last call.
- 2024-09-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: on the claim Palmer will lead the Chargers in targets, Waldman said he feels it only if it is confined to outside receivers — he expects Palmer to lead Chargers outside receivers in targets while [[Ladd McConkey]] still leads all Chargers receivers, a position Waldman said he staked out in May and is 'ready to die on.' He also pushed back on the market fear that Los Angeles simply will not throw: they will run a lot, but the defense and the AFC West schedule mean they will also throw plenty — he conceded you can lean on running backs against the Raiders and Broncos, but not against the Chiefs or the rest of the league. Untracked co-host Bob Harris agreed Palmer will be busy.
- 2024-09-10 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-10]]): Palmer led the Chargers in routes run with 26 but Harmon calls him, without meaning disrespect, 'a fine NFL number three wide receiver' and 'just a guy.' His specific criticism is route detail on downfield throws from [[Justin Herbert]] — a couple of attempts looked like bad quarterback decisions when the actual issue was Palmer being slightly off his landmark or running a route through when he should have settled. Harmon's conclusion is that those snaps would be better spent on [[Ladd McConkey]], who he thinks can handle downfield work.
