## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: a former Texas A&M four-star recruit who suffered a spiral tibia/fibula fracture requiring surgery on eight separate breaks plus a dislocated ankle — "a wicked injury." Still moves smoothly as a route runner post-injury, with top speed on film flirting with 20 mph. Needs to develop more craft with releases and a better route-setup repertoire; has good weight drop and hip mobility but must prove he can deliver them at higher speeds and on deeper routes for NFL defenders to respect him as more than a short-area/zone threat.
