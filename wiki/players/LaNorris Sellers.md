- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: his 2026 QB1 and a player with 'future stardom written all over him'. Angelo credits the nerve Sellers showed as a first-year starter going to Death Valley and playing Alabama and Clemson, and rates the off-script ability as [[Ben Roethlisberger]]-caliber sack avoidance. He is also an extension of the run game — in Angelo's view the best read-option quarterback in college football — has hit 21 miles per hour, and measures roughly 6-3, 240 with growing left to do. Angelo thinks he would be a top-five pick if he declared next year but stresses he has not played enough football and would want him to sit a year. [[Matt Waldman]] had not yet watched him and declined to offer a view.
