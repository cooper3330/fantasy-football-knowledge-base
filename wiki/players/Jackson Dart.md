- 2025-02-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-24]]) [Dynasty]: asked whether Dart is draft media's overhyped poster boy, Waldman gives a conditional answer — he is feeling it only if Dart lands in a short passing game with play action, rollouts and a real runway, or in a Carson Wentz-style setup that limits the footwork demands and keeps his deep attempts in the 25-30 yard range. - 2025-02-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-27]]): [[Jackson Dart]] is this class's 'doughy-faced guy with upside' that the NFL will talk itself into, and Waldman comps him to [[Drew Lock]] coming out of Missouri in reverse — Lock was erratic from zero to 15 yards but fine beyond, Dart is the opposite. Inside 20 yards, on play action, moving the pocket, throwbacks and quick game, Waldman thinks he can be really good, and his ball placement on stop routes and comebacks is 'pretty damn accurate'. Beyond 25 yards he calls the accuracy an 'utter disaster'; when someone on X posted two Penn State throws as a rebuttal he said find the other 23 plays and he is at 50 percent. [[Brandon Angelo]] agrees, drawing the line at 15 yards, and complains the Ole Miss passing game was 'stop route after stop route' that wasted [[Tre Harris]]. Angelo gives Lock a slightly better arm and says it is hard to start in the NFL when your level-three accuracy is that poor.
- 2025-03-28 — According to Cory Kinnan (quarterback charter, not a tracked expert) with [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-28]]): Kinnan cannot find the selling point and says the first-round buzz is 'driving me insane.' He grades a plus-average arm at best, no big-time athleticism, and a system he calls maybe 29 plays that makes life easy yet still leaves Dart late after three years in it. Dart charted 51% on deep balls, second-best of the five charted behind Will Howard, but Kinnan discounts it because 'all he wants to do is throw nine balls anyway.' His comp band is the third-round lottery-ticket group — Davis Mills, Kellen Mond, Malik Willis, Desmond Ridder — even though he expects Dart to go inside the top 32. - 2025-03-31 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-31]]) [Dynasty]: Dart's accuracy wanes somewhere between 25 and 30 yards, and beyond that it is scattershot — overthrows, underthrows, behind and ahead of wide-open targets. What worries Waldman is that Dart's setup, stance and release lack significant flaws, so there is no obvious fix; the visible symptom is that his feet get too wide when he maneuvers from pressure, when they need to be undisturbed and narrower to deliver at 30 to 45 yards. Developing a precise pocket climb is the path to overcoming it. Waldman, who notes he is not a quarterback coach, comps the mechanics-versus-accuracy mismatch to Jake Locker. The positives are real: he works two to three reads sideline to sideline before pressure arrives, identifies pre-snap interior and edge pressure, handles multiple simultaneous rushers, protects receivers by placing throws away from the nearest defender, throws the ball away when pinned to the boundary, and eats it in the red zone to preserve field goal position. Ideal landing spot is a West Coast scheme with pistol, shotgun, moving pockets off play action and misdirection throwbacks; starting early would be inadvisable unless the surrounding talent keeps him on schedule.
