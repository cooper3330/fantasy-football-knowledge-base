# Jalin Conyers

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Conyers, at Texas Tech by way of Arizona State, sits near the bottom of the third tier of Waldman's tight end rankings but has the most realistic path of any third-tier tight end to climb into the second tier. Four areas need cleaning up: release footwork; developing hard breaks with good weight drop against man coverage so he separates quickly; limiting lapses attacking the football, since his hand positioning leads to drops; and curtailing overextension as a blocker, because an overextended blocker cannot see what he is hitting, is off balance and hands the chain of events to the defender. Waldman's conditional: fix two or three of those and Conyers has similar promise to the second-tier tight ends and could have a meaningful career with legitimate receiving production; fix all four and he could become a starter with weekly box-score value and be a genuine fantasy factor.
