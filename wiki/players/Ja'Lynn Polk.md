## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-29 — According to guest **Eric Froton** (NBC Sports college football analyst — not a tracked expert) on *Reception Perception: The Show*, hosted solo by James Koh with [[Matt Harmon]] absent ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-29]]) [Dynasty]: grades him NFL-caliber alongside Washington teammates [[Rome Odunze]] and Jalen McMillan (not otherwise profiled on this wiki), praising his tenacity as a route runner and competitiveness at all three levels of the field — "he's attacking the ball like he just wants it."
- 2024-02-29 — According to **Kevin Knight** (Falcons reporter, SB Nation's Falcoholic — guest, not a tracked expert), same episode ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-29]]): names him a realistic Atlanta second-round target if the team doesn't land [[Malik Nabers]] or [[Rome Odunze]] at 8th overall — a receiver who "checks all the boxes" and would complement Atlanta's existing pieces well.
- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): the strength of his game is pass catching and positioning — high-points and pulls the ball away from a defender's reach into his chest, is adept working the boundary, and has the awareness and technique to get in bounds multiple ways. Sometimes allows targets into his chest with underhand hand position when overhand at the earliest point would be optimal, but shows on tape that he's capable of attacking at the earliest point when he squares the target — he just doesn't do it enough yet.
