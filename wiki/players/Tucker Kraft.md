# Tucker Kraft

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]]): had Kraft in the same pre-draft tier as teammate [[Luke Musgrave]], 15 spots lower on his overall board — meaning Kraft was available at a discount relative to his grade. Calls him "a more rugged all-around player" with more remaining growth room as an intermediate/vertical route option than the more athletically-hyped Musgrave, and says if forced to bet on only one Packers rookie tight end at a discount, Kraft is the one he'd take.

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Dynasty]: reframes the Kraft-vs-Musgrave question as "more of a price thing than anything else" — Kraft is a little more rugged, makes more plays in the middle of the field and the shallow areas, and may be a better blocker in some of Green Bay's scheme concepts, which Waldman summarizes as making Kraft "just a little more cost-efficient" than [[Luke Musgrave]].
- 2024-07-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-12]]): he 'liked [Kraft] as a prospect' and Kraft showed up in the 2023 stretch run, but Waldman cautions that Kraft's late-season snap/route edge over Luke Musgrave was largely a product of Musgrave playing hurt rather than a real changing of the pecking order, and he expects Musgrave to be the lead receiving tight end again in 2024.
