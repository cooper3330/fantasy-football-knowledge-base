# J. Michael Sturdivant

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): a UCLA receiver (transferred from Cal), 6'3"/205 lbs, "can move." Graded as a high-end trait prospect who "just didn't really emerge at the highest level" of production relative to that traits profile. Noted as having declared for the draft, with Waldman flagging real interest in following his process further rather than offering a settled grade yet.
- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman slots Sturdivant between two poles -- if he'd come out with the polish of a young [[Michael Wilson]] at Stanford he'd look more accomplished, but he's not as raw as former Auburn receiver Seth Williams either. His best route-running tape actually came at Cal, and Waldman thinks both UCLA and Florida schemed him too simply relative to what he's capable of. Big, fast, can win the ball -- an interesting, underrated prospect worth continued tracking rather than a finished product.
- 2026-07-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-07]]) [Dynasty]: flagged UDFA J. Michael Sturdivant (Florida/UCLA, 6'3, 207 lbs, 4.4-second forty, 39-inch vertical, 10'11 broad jump) as a name to monitor, noting he didn't have a clean enough combined Florida/UCLA sample to chart him fully. The signal Harmon cares about is that Green Bay aggressively pursued him after the draft and gave him guaranteed money, which is rare for a UDFA -- a sign of real front-office conviction. Harmon jokingly floated the scenario where Sturdivant and Savion Williams end up on the field together in two-receiver sets over Matthew Golden and Jayden Reed.
