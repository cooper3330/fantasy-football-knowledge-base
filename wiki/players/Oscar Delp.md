# Oscar Delp

## Expert Takes

- 2026-04-16 — According to Dalton Wasserman (guest draft analyst, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-16]]) [Dynasty]: [[Oscar Delp]] carries a lot of pre-draft hype and is a genuinely good athlete who can play in-line for Georgia, but Wasserman flagged the most concerning part of his profile as never producing at a high level in college -- a day 3-5 dynasty flier at best.
- 2026-04-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-20]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is 'totally feeling it' on Georgia TE Oscar Delp (6'5', 245) as a potential top-three producer in what he called a compressed, wide-open 2026 tight end class. He needs to add muscle to sustain the blocking level he showed in the SEC, and Waldman flagged a catch-point toughness issue -- Delp didn't show enough reps absorbing hard contact and holding on, and on the few instances tested that way, he dropped the ball; outside of hard contact he's a reliable catcher. Waldman comped him to [[Tucker Kraft]] as a strong open-field runner who could get easy dump-offs and screens and turn into 'a bulldozer with some speed.' If Delp proves he can secure the ball through contact, Waldman said 'everything else is there for him.'
- 2026-04-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman leans 'fuck it' on Delp being an exception to his fade-rookie-tight-ends stance in New Orleans, viewing it as a two-year plan -- Noah Fant is a journeyman bridge starter even on a two-year deal and Juwan Johnson remains the lead in-line producer, so Delp likely plays a third-tight-end role in sets this season. Waldman says the skills are there for Delp to prove something if Fant or Johnson gets hurt, and he'd want him as a dynasty stash for that scenario.
- 2026-04-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-28]]): New Orleans added Delp as an athletic bet at inline tight end, a position group that had zero true inline blocker on the roster last year, despite modest college production. Harmon likes the swing-for-upside profile (hoping for an eventual George Kittle-type payoff) as the Saints dip a toe into heavier personnel while remaining one of the league's most spread, motion-heavy offenses.
- 2026-05-25 — Asked if Oscar Delp is worth a two-year dynasty stash amid talk of a slow rise, [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-25]]) [Dynasty] said he's 'feeling that.' He framed the Saints' TE room as [[Noah Fant]] being 'at the one-year rental stage' -- a decent blocker who can catch and run in space but never became the special prospect Denver drafted, still fighting hands/tracking issues, a linear-but-high-end athlete comparable to (but not as good as) [[David Njoku]] -- with [[Jonnu Smith]] getting older and never a blocking specialist. By contrast, Waldman called Delp a high-level blocker already, with the ceiling to become an NFL-caliber one if he's acclimated to reading an NFL offense and gets comfortable with his quarterback; he projected Delp could be the Saints' TE1 within two years and said his current roughly-fourth-round dynasty ADP 'is a good spot for him for sure.' Both hosts noted [[Taysom Hill]] and Foster Moreau appear finished in New Orleans, which Bob Harris said could also help [[Jonah Coleman]].
