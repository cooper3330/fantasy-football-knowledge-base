# Dylan McDuffie

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast: a willing tight-crease runner (Buffalo and Georgia Tech transfer before Kansas) who knows his limitations as a cutback athlete — stays tight to his inside down blocks when working behind pullers on gap plays, which maximizes the space between himself and front-side pursuit and buys room into the secondary. Direct comp to [[Raheem Mostert]], who Waldman says does this same thing but can separate in the secondary "in the blink of an eye" once he gets there; McDuffie can only extend an early lead gradually, not explosively.
