# Robert Henry

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-02-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-16]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman calls Henry underrated, comping him to [[James Cook]] -- explosive with a feel for setting up defenders between the tackles and in the open field, plus a Chris Johnson-lite juke element (not as extreme as prime Johnson, but 'pretty close'). He can catch the ball and has room to grow as a route runner and pass protector. Waldman noted co-host [[Brandon Angelo]] independently named Henry as one of the class's biggest surprises on a recent 'Going Deep' episode -- Waldman had the same reaction after giving RSP subscribers an early scouting report on him back in September/October. Henry got banged up late in the season and barely played in the all-star game, which likely muted his stock, but Waldman still expects a team to end up 'very happy with him.'
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman highlighted Henry's receiving polish as his carrying tool into the NFL -- he tracks the ball well, high-points it, uses late hands so he doesn't tip coverage, and attacks the catch point with overhand technique rather than letting the ball into his frame. Waldman said he has the tools to develop into a route runner who matches up against most backfield coverage assignments and even some safeties, given his ability to decelerate after building speed downhill, and that it's reasonable to project him into a plus NFL receiver at the position. His one flagged lapse: turning downfield before securing a catch, which has cost him a drop.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]) [Dynasty]: Henry scored on a 22-yard run showing 'juice' (per John Keim) in the exhibition opener. Waldman called him the clear best pure runner of the two Commanders rookie backs -- citing his ability to create in space, set up blocks, absorb contact despite being undersized (comping him to an Aaron Jones/James Cook type), break big runs, and catch downfield at a high level -- versus [[Kaytron Allen]], who managed 85 yards on 23 carries and a 1-yard score but lacks Henry's burst. Waldman referenced a post-draft conversation with 'Bloom' predicting that once the team sees Henry's burst live, it will be over for Allen on the depth chart by the end of preseason, though he noted Allen hasn't played poorly and remains the high-volume back for now. Waldman does not think we've seen the last of Henry even if Washington leans early on Allen, Rashad White, or Jacory Croskey-Merritt.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]) [Dynasty]: Henry went undrafted and signed a UDFA deal with Washington, but Waldman says he's actually the best rookie running back the Commanders brought in, doing 'a pretty mean [[Aaron Jones]] imitation' himself -- worth a waiver-wire watch.
- 2026-08-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-20]]) [Dynasty]: both hosts like Henry and have all last year -- he flashed again in the preseason and is seen as the closest thing Washington has to a Jacory Croskey-Merritt-style explosive complement, which they'd prefer over [[Kaytron Allen]] for that role. But the room is crowded (Croskey-Merritt, Allen, Rashad White brought in for third-down/pass-pro work, plus others), and Waldman thinks Henry could genuinely get cut to the practice squad despite the talent -- he notes Henry would be a much bigger story on a team with an obvious opening (e.g., Chicago) than buried on Washington's depth chart behind a locked-in Croskey-Merritt. Dynasty cost of acquisition is basically nothing, making him a fine speculative stash, but both are skeptical the coaching staff will actually lean on him over Allen if an injury opens the door -- see the [[Kaytron Allen]] page for the counter-read.
