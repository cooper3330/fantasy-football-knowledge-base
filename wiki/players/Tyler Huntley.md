- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-10-27]]) [Redraft, Superflex]: started in place of [[Lamar Jackson]] and 'played quite well' -- only one incompletion in the first half, an accurate and layered throw to [[Zay Flowers]], mostly conservative and didn't push the ball deep. - 2026-05-11 — According to Matt Waldman ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-11]]) [Dynasty]: with [[Skylar Thompson]] now also in Baltimore's QB room behind [[Lamar Jackson]], Waldman characterized Tyler Huntley as a known, capped quantity -- 'not gonna embarrass us as a starter but not gonna win big games for us either' -- but the takeaway for superflex managers was not to cut him as a QB3 just yet; the depth chart situation was called 'a little fluid' and worth monitoring rather than an immediate reason to move off Huntley.
- 2026-10-12 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-12]]): Huntley kept the Ravens close but 'pretty close doesn't mean pretty' -- Harris says Huntley 'can't force himself to pull the trigger' downfield, a problem 'all these years in the league,' comparing his reluctance to Lamar Jackson's occasional hesitancy 'multiplied by five.' Still, down eight points with no choice left, Huntley marched Baltimore down the field on the game-tying drive and sneaked in the overtime-forcing score from the one, which Harris calls a good, if 'rope-a-dope,' win.
