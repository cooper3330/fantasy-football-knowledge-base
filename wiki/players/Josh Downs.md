## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]]): flags Downs as "certainly going to be worthwhile" specifically on the strength of [[Anthony Richardson]]'s return at quarterback, tying his outlook directly to Richardson's health and development rather than to anything new about Downs's own game.
- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Adam Harstad** (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: [[Post-Rookie-Year Receiver Model]] score comes back almost identical to Sterling Shepard's rookie year, and every individual component (efficiency, touchdown rate, usage) lands within a tight band just above 100 — a flat, unremarkable profile with no standout trait. Read as "fair value" rather than a buy-low or sell-high: not a market inefficiency either direction independent of the [[Anthony Richardson]] health question already driving his outlook.
- 2024-05-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-23]]): Downs' rookie Reception Perception profile is outstanding despite playing hurt and catching passes from Gardner Minshew, whom Harmon calls a 'net negative' hard ceiling on any offense -- Downs still went for almost 800 yards. He posted a 93rd-percentile success rate vs. man coverage (77.5%), 82.5% vs. zone, and a 69th-percentile mark vs. press (72.7%), with green results across nearly the whole route tree except the nine route (only 5.4% of his routes, so a non-issue for a player who isn't a vertical threat). He lined up in the slot on 71.8% of snaps and off the line 78.8% of the time, but ran 25% of snaps outside and caught both of his 2023 touchdowns from an outside alignment, leading Harmon to argue he could be deployed as a flanker rather than a slot-only player. He carried an elite 72.7% contested-catch rate as a rookie (dropping only one pass all year) after posting a historic 93.8% contested-catch rate as a prospect. Per a stat from Jacob Gibbs (Fantasy Points Suite) that Harmon endorses, 331 of Downs' 771 receiving yards (43%) came on plays where he was not the first read or a designed target -- the highest rate in the NFL, comparable in target/yard share only to [[CeeDee Lamb]] and [[Puka Nacua]] -- which Harmon says projects well alongside [[Anthony Richardson]]'s tendency to work through progressions and off-script. Harmon expects [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] to remain the first-read/X option with Downs as the reliable off-script/second option. Harmon's pre-draft and current comp for Downs is Sterling Shepherd -- a good, productive player when healthy -- and he cautions dynasty managers against overreacting if Downs' box score lands around 800 yards again, noting the fantasy community over-hyped an unsustainable weeks 5-8 stretch (5.8 rec/78.8 yds per game, both TDs, an 98-catch/1,339-yard full-season pace) that faded down the stretch.

He expects [[Anthony Richardson]] to lean on Downs as a reliable short-to-intermediate target and projects him as a slot-dominant player who can also line up as a flanker in two-receiver sets.
