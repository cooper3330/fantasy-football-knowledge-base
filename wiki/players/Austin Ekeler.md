# Austin Ekeler

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: sees a possible 2024 rebound after a down year but is explicit he wouldn't personally invest in it — "I'm not sure I want to invest in it" — with price being the deciding factor either way. Notes Ekeler himself has publicly acknowledged (on his own show, alongside Matt Harmon) that his window is closing, which Waldman reads as a sign he may be closer to the end of his relevance than the industry fully appreciates.
- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]): floated as a potential 2024 free agent, cited (with a joking aside about asking [[Matt Harmon]] for a raise) as the running back at the very top of the list of backs who understand how the league is undervaluing the position — logged here as market/leverage commentary rather than a fresh skill grade.

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]): grouped with [[Tony Pollard]] and [[Travis Kelce]] — reads the trio's down 2023 as more likely injury-related than skill-related, though for Ekeler (and Kelce) specifically flags it may also be age-related, given the cumulative toll of NFL workload, rather than purely a scheme or opportunity issue.

- 2024-03-01 — **Adam Harstad** (co-host on *RSP Film and Theory* — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-01]] [Dynasty]: ranks Ekeler second of the "Big Four" 2024 free-agent backs (behind [[Josh Jacobs]]), reasoning that receiving work is so disproportionately valuable in fantasy that "he doesn't need to be" 2022 Ekeler to still be valuable, both for an NFL offense and for fantasy purposes.
- 2024-03-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], same episode [Dynasty]: groups Ekeler with [[Tony Pollard]] as "Column A" — the receiving-back tier of the free-agent class, as distinct from the bell-cow "Column B" of [[Josh Jacobs]] and [[Derrick Henry]] — and says an ideal roster build takes one back from each column.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: "hates" Ekeler's move to Washington from a fantasy standpoint given how good [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] looked in 2023 — reads Ekeler's cheap two-year, $11M deal ($5M year one, up to $1.5M in likely-unreachable incentives) as a market signal he's viewed as a complementary piece (a McKissick-type role) rather than a true lead back, and expects the split to be "disappointing for both players unless one gets hurt."
- 2024-07-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Redraft]: Waldman takes Ekeler over [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] in a feel-it-or-f-it pairing despite Ekeler's price having 'plummeted' to RB40 -- earlier in the best ball season he was going well ahead of Robinson -- arguing he hasn't fully declined and may never have recovered from an early ankle injury that masked his real level last year. Bob disagrees, taking Robinson instead, calling this the pairing he 'feels like I'm making the wrong pick on' even as he sticks with Ekeler.
