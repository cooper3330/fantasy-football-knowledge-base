# Bam Knight

## Expert Takes

- 2025-10-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-14 (Week 7 Waiver Wire & More W6 Film)]]): Knight scored a 1-yard TD after [[Emari Demercado]] left with an ankle injury, and per Harris's film review took over the bulk of early-down work in the second half once the backfield split from a three-way timeshare to essentially a 50-50 split with [[Michael Carter]] -- Knight is clearly the Cardinals' inside-the-five short-yardage option. Guest Jake Seeley (The Athletic, not a tracked expert) ranked Knight his #3 Week 7 waiver add, likening the role to Kenneth Gainwell's but with more guaranteed weekly touches, and put him just ahead of Carter given the goal-line role, while cautioning that with [[Trey Benson]] possibly returning around Week 11-12 this is a short-term-only add.
- 2025-10-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-21 (Week 8 Waivers & W7 Reviews)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Knight was 'absolutely clear' as the lead back against Green Bay -- 4 carries on the opening drive alone, a 33-to-25 snap edge over [[Michael Carter]], and Harris said watching the tape 'it actually felt heavier in Knight's favor' than the raw numbers suggest, though he briefly left with a possible concussion (short trip to the tent, returned). Harris flagged that Arizona has 'a big enough painfully slow guy and the other guy is fast but painfully small' -- not an ideal committee, but functional. Guest John Daigle (Establish the Run, not tracked) named Knight his #1 waiver add for those not needing an immediate Week 8 starter (he's on bye), citing his clear lead role and third-down/passing usage over Carter, calling him a long-term flex option, especially with the Brissett offense humming; Harris separately said he 'doesn't love' Knight as a talent but doesn't dispute the role.
- 2025-10-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-28]]) [waiver wire, both hosts' #1]: with Michael Carter released and [[Trey Benson]]'s return still indefinite, Knight is the clear lead back in Arizona, getting 12-15+ touches over recent weeks with startable-caliber volume; he's a capable if unspectacular receiving option and gets goal-line work. Both hosts caution against spending big FAAB given Benson could retake the job within a few weeks, but call Knight a must-add given the clean path to volume, with practice-squad back Emari Demercado as a lesser complication.
- 2025-11-21 — According to Chris Harris on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-21]] [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris said if [[Trey Benson]] is active against Jacksonville after his practice window opened, that ends any enthusiasm for using Knight.
- 2025-11-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-25]]): got the goal-line work over Michael Carter in overtime, scoring on his second of two carries from the doorstep, but possibly injured himself later in overtime. Harris says it's probably best not to chase any of Arizona's running backs given the uncertainty.
- 2025-12-02 — According to guest John Daigle of Establish the Run (not tracked) and [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Trey Benson]] out, Daigle and Harris both had Knight rostered highly on their waiver lists (Daigle #4, Harris #2), citing 28 touches to [[Michael Carter]]'s 18 over the last two games with two touchdowns for Knight in that span, and RB16/RB14 fantasy finishes those weeks. Harris cited a specific run from Monday's film review where Knight made a play 'entirely by himself... like Jahmyr Gibbs couldn't have done it better' before fumbling on the next carry -- a sign, per Harris, of a player who's not quite a finished product but flashing real traits as backup running backs get more exposure. Both recommend plugging Knight in as an emergency RB if Benson remains out; Carter is also earning run and finished this past game 'freaking dealing,' per Harris.
- 2026-10-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-10-19]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman is 'feel it' on Knight as a rosterable handcuff, tying the case directly to injury risk for the Cardinals' lead back, [[James Conner]] -- 'once you see a dent in the fender, then you're going to start to see maybe the axle fall off.' Knight catches the ball well even if underused that way, has good contact balance, and has earned real production across two different coaching regimes in Arizona despite lacking the top-end physical traits teams want from a starter -- a 'good backup player' who's stuck around as an outlier through regime change. Both hosts flagged him as worth rostering given upcoming byes and in-season attrition; Bob separately noted the Cardinals' weird game script undercut [[Tyler Allgeier]]'s expected workload the same week.
- 2026-10-19 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-19]]): Knight broke off a 30-yard run on a power sweep untouched and earned the entire fourth-quarter drive to himself, including staying in for red-zone work, over [[Tyler Allgeier]] -- evidence, per Harris, that Arizona's coaches saw more juice in Knight even if some of the discrepancy was blocking luck. Knight came out late clutching his hand, so Harris flagged a possible injury to monitor heading into Week 7 against Denver, with the overriding question still being whether [[Jeremiah Love]] returns.
- 2026-10-26 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-26]]): Knight flashed more burst than [[Tyler Allgeier]] and drew a personal-foul penalty against the Broncos that set up a short field, but then dropped a wide-open touchdown pass in the end zone, costing Arizona points -- a frustrating game for anyone who started him.
- 2026-10-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-10-26 (Feel It or Fk It)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman says he's 'feeling it' on Bam Knight, calling him a 'more compact Tyler Allgeier with a little more versatility,' built about 10-15 pounds heavier than his listed weight and requiring real wrap-up tackles, with good feel for hitting creases and standout receiving ability out of the backfield. Where Waldman always pegged Allgeier as a 'career backup,' he thinks Knight has a real chance to be a 'plus backup' given his route running and pass-catching range. Waldman says he recommended Knight last week as a [[Jeremiah Love]] injury fill-in and has now ranked him ahead of Tyler Allgeier based mostly on coaching-staff comments during the week suggesting Allgeier remains 'a complimentary piece' -- not what Allgeier had in mind, Waldman jokes, but apparently what Knight has earned. With Love's return timeline unclear, Waldman views Knight as the better buy-low option of the two right now.
