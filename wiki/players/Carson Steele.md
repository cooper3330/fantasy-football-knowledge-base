# Carson Steele

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: asked 'feel it or f**k it' on whether Steele is for real, Waldman answered 'f**k it' — real enough to make the roster, not real enough to matter. He was skeptical of Andy Reid's camp praise that nobody wanted to tackle Steele, reading it as code for poor preseason tackling rather than evidence about the back. Waldman still credited genuine traits: Steele can catch, has good vision and 'pretty good footwork for a big man,' and may surprise reserves who missed tackles on him at UCLA. But he expects [[Samaje Perine]] to be the Darrell Williams-type who actually produces fantasy weeks while Steele gets isolated looks. Untracked co-host Bob Harris agreed Perine and [[Isiah Pacheco]] take the meaningful touches.
- 2024-09-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-16]]): with [[Isiah Pacheco]] on IR, Steele has 'played reasonably well' and Kansas City showed confidence in him by going right back to him after a fumble in Week 2. Waldman still ranks [[Samaje Perine]] ahead of him for now, specifically on experience — Perine gives the best combination of skills across the offense's facets, and it is the experience gap rather than raw ability that separates them at this stage. He expects the Chiefs to shop for either a more explosive back or a trusted three-down type rather than hand the job to Steele outright, with Keaontay Ingram the cheap in-house option off the practice squad.
- 2024-09-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-19]]): the hosts split on Steele versus [[Samaje Perine]] in the Chiefs' backfield while [[Isiah Pacheco]] is out. Co-host Adam Harstad (not a tracked expert) claimed Steele over Perine on waivers, reasoning that Perine's outcome is essentially known and capped, so if either projection is wrong it is far more likely to be Steele — and adding that any early Perine-leaning split could flip toward the younger back by the fantasy playoff weeks, which are the games he actually wants to optimize for. Waldman is the more cautious of the two here: he expects Steele to be, at best, a change-of-pace back who might pick up some goal-line work, and he is recommending Perine to listeners even though he acknowledges the team seems to want to give Steele the chance. Kareem Hunt was framed by both as break-glass depth only.
- 2024-09-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-30]]): asked whether the Carson Steele era just ended, Waldman said Steele 'fumbled an opportunity away' and went from first up to essentially third up in the Kansas City rotation. The reasons are that [[Kareem Hunt]] looked really good and fresh, and that Steele is worse on passing downs — he dropped a pass and is not as sharp in pass protection, where Samaje Perine can take those duties instead. Waldman contrasted the market: people paid up for Steele in the offseason while he took cheap Hunt shares. He still expects Andy Reid to use a three-back backfield, so Steele is not off the roster, but he is behind two players for meaningful work.
- 2024-11-22 — According to Adam Harstad, untracked co-host ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-22]]): Harstad saw Steele on waivers in a few leagues and is interested purely on organizational signal. He acknowledged the profile is odd — Steele is effectively a fullback playing a style not usually associated with strong fantasy production, and there are plenty of reasons not to like him — but his stated position is that there are plenty of reasons not to like any waiver player, and what separates them is whether the team is saying good things. Kansas City is, and Harstad credits the Chiefs with knowing a thing or two about developing and deploying players.
