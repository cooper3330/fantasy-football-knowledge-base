# Aaron Rodgers

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-12]]: both "feel it" on a return to form after his Week 1 2023 Achilles tear. Waldman's case is intangible/effort-based rather than tape-based at this point — "too obsessed with the game," still "one of the best quarterbacks that's ever strapped it on" — and explicitly separates the player evaluation from off-field opinions of Rodgers as a person. Flagged as the Jets' best realistic path out of a season of off-field/organizational drama (friction between the team and certain media outlets) rather than a specific scheme or supporting-cast argument.
- 2024-06-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-18]]): Waldman projects top-10, possibly top-5, fantasy quarterback numbers for a healthy Aaron Rodgers returning from his Achilles injury, tied closely to his [[Garrett Wilson]] outlook. Hosts also discussed Rodgers' unexcused absence from mandatory June minicamp, framing it as the Jets mishandling the PR on what was really just a two-day absence rather than a genuine concern about Rodgers' commitment.
- 2024-07-09 — According to James Coe (co-host, not tracked) and [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Both hosts cautioned against assuming Aaron Rodgers returns to his prior peak form given his age and the Achilles injury that ended his 2023 debut after four snaps, with Harmon calling the assumption of a fully-recovered Rodgers 'a bad assumption.' Still, Harmon said even a diminished Rodgers is 'light years better' than what the Jets got from Zach Wilson, and Coe noted Rodgers looked genuinely 'loose' and invested on Hard Knocks before the injury. The team is otherwise running back the same core with [[Mike Williams]], Tyron Smith and first-round tackle Olu Fashanu added to protect him.
