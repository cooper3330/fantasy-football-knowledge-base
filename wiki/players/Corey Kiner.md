- 2026-10-22 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: [[Corey Kiner]] is an undrafted Patriots back who Harris says embodies the organization's DNA under Mike Vrabel -- a 'we picked this guy up off the street, he worked so hard' lunch-pail archetype who gets opportunity (including a late-game series after a TreVeyon Henderson fumble) almost regardless of performance. Guest Jeff Bell says he may be 'too high' on Kiner in his own ranks. Neither views him as a reliable enough lead option over Rhamondre Stevenson, but both acknowledge the touch distribution has been genuinely unpredictable week to week, and Harris says if forced into an emergency flex choice between Kiner and the Bears' Roschon Johnson, he'd take Johnson because that backfield split looks more intentional.
