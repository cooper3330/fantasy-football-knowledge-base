# Chris Brooks

## Expert Takes

- 2026-06-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman firmly feels it that Chris Brooks can be an effective starter in Green Bay if needed, pushing back on the notion that his burst is lacking -- he called Brooks' change of direction, decision-making and blocking/receiving all strong. He projects Brooks as the clear #2 back behind an unsettled [[Marshawn Lloyd]] (who isn't well-loved internally and needs more proof of concept) and a [[Josh Jacobs]] situation complicated by a domestic-violence charge that was filed and released, still under investigation. If Jacobs plays a full season, Waldman still sees Brooks capable of 150 carries and 600 rushing yards (near 800 total yards from scrimmage); if Brooks became the lead back, Waldman doesn't expect a true bell-cow workload but projects 240-260 carries for 1,200-1,300 yards if the offensive line holds up -- drawing a name-recognition comp to how [[Emmanuel Wilson]] (whom Green Bay released) once broke out unexpectedly.
- 2026-08-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-10]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman currently prefers Brooks over the more physically talented [[Marshawn Lloyd]] for Green Bay's early-down work because Brooks 'has his game together,' though he acknowledges the roster's desired outcome is still Lloyd taking over once he proves he can stay healthy and hold onto the ball.
- 2026-08-31 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and Bob Harris ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-31]]) [Dynasty] [Redraft (Standard)], with Josh Jacobs on the commissioner's exempt list, Brooks is the presumptive complement to Marshawn Lloyd in the Raiders backfield. Waldman calls Brooks 'a good doubles hitter' between the tackles and projects him for a third to almost half of the team's touches depending on how well Lloyd performs, since the coaching staff wants more between-the-tackles production than Lloyd's speed-reliant style naturally provides. Bob Harris adds that Brooks is likely the better pass protector of the two and should see third-down work, possibly with some fullback-ish usage mixed in. Both frame him as a name to monitor on the waiver wire depending on how the timeshare plays out early in the season.
- 2026-08-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-31]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris ranks Brooks behind Lloyd (10th-11th round) among the Packers backs left after the Jacobs suspension. He calls Brooks a power back and potential goal-line option, a good pass protector, but with little wiggle or big-play ability -- a Chris Rodriguez type, and reminiscent of what Emmanuel Wilson did for Green Bay last year, which Harris says the Packers must regret not paying $1.6M to retain. Brooks is the most proven of the group with 41 NFL games (Miami and Green Bay) and 80-plus career carries, plus some receiving work, though he's not a dynamic playmaker. Harris says it 'wouldn't be a shock' if Brooks winds up the lead back by Week 1 even though that's not his expectation.
- 2026-09-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-03]]) [Best Ball]: called Brooks an interesting very-late-round dart throw in Green Bay, arguing the team may not be truthful that [[Marshawn Lloyd]] is the clear-cut starter and that Brooks could end up being 'the one you want' if Lloyd is banged up, benched, or simply doesn't hold the job.
- 2026-09-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris's #1 Week 1 waiver-wire add (2% rostered in Yahoo) is [[Chris Brooks]] of the Packers. His reasoning mirrors Scott Fish's case for [[Caleb Johnson]] -- a muddled Green Bay backfield behind an injury-prone [[Marshawn Lloyd]] -- but Harris prefers Brooks specifically because 'I know Brooks can play,' whereas he has 'no idea if Caleb Johnson can play.' Harris expects Brooks to rank ahead of Johnson in his Week 1 rankings, calling him a usable 'default answer' as a proven pass-blocker who played last year, even while admitting 'Brooks probably stinks' in an absolute sense.
- 2026-09-09 — According to co-host Andy (not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-09]]): Brooks is a real threat to [[Marshawn Lloyd]]'s snaps because he's good in pass protection, and with [[Josh Jacobs]] out the Packers likely need reliable protection for [[Jordan Love]]. Andy calls Brooks 'a perfectly fine, not flashy, not exciting, perfectly serviceable back' who could see the field even if Lloyd is the more talented runner. [[Chris Harris]] agrees these are 'all true statements' but notes Lloyd can still have a great game even if Brooks handles 30% of snaps.
