- 2026-06-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman firmly feels it that Chris Brooks can be an effective starter in Green Bay if needed, pushing back on the notion that his burst is lacking -- he called Brooks' change of direction, decision-making and blocking/receiving all strong. He projects Brooks as the clear #2 back behind an unsettled [[Marshawn Lloyd]] (who isn't well-loved internally and needs more proof of concept) and a [[Josh Jacobs]] situation complicated by a domestic-violence charge that was filed and released, still under investigation. If Jacobs plays a full season, Waldman still sees Brooks capable of 150 carries and 600 rushing yards (near 800 total yards from scrimmage); if Brooks became the lead back, Waldman doesn't expect a true bell-cow workload but projects 240-260 carries for 1,200-1,300 yards if the offensive line holds up -- drawing a name-recognition comp to how [[Emmanuel Wilson]] (whom Green Bay released) once broke out unexpectedly.
- 2026-08-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-10]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman currently prefers Brooks over the more physically talented [[Marshawn Lloyd]] for Green Bay's early-down work because Brooks 'has his game together,' though he acknowledges the roster's desired outcome is still Lloyd taking over once he proves he can stay healthy and hold onto the ball.
