# Jonnu Smith

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-11-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-21]]): Miami is actively designing plays for Smith and it is working — Harmon says that by down-and-distance success rate on his targets he has been one of the most efficient tight ends in the league over the last month, averaging seven yards after the catch per reception on a diet of out-breakers, in-breakers and designed touches behind the line of scrimmage. The structural reason is defensive attention: even with [[Tyreek Hill]] and [[Jaylen Waddle]] under-producing, teams still respect their speed and adjust coverages, leaving Smith unprioritised with room to operate — Harmon cites the wide-open seam route against the Raiders. He presents this as the main reason Waddle's opportunity share has shrunk.
- 2024-11-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-25]]): Smith is the guy in Miami's passing game, not a complementary piece. Waldman noted that in seven of the roughly ten games played, Smith finished first or second on the team in receiving yardage, which means [[Jaylen Waddle]] or [[Tyreek Hill]] most often projected as the third, fourth or fifth option that week. He credited the scheme for leveraging his yards-after-catch — a powerful runner given the ball with a runway, functioning as an extension of the ground and play-action game — after three touchdowns in two games. The structural read: Waddle and Hill are now competing for the co-star role behind him, either producing big enough plays to be the week's number one or disappointing as the number two.
- 2024-11-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-26]]): Smith has been the third pitch Miami has needed for years alongside [[Tyreek Hill]] and [[Jaylen Waddle]], and Harmon sees no reason his role should change — he leads the Dolphins in targets and is the only player on the team above 20% of first-read targets per Fantasy Points data. Harmon calls him awesome and fantastic this season and says Miami should prioritize the role rather than let him go; he notes the contract runs into 2025 with an out, and that at age 30 the team probably ought to extend him instead of taking that out. The caveat is that Smith's target primacy is exactly what caps Waddle's raw volume.
- 2024-12-17 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-12-17]]): the Jonnu Smith plays have genuinely been good and he has been a positive presence — Miami needed a third option to take middle-of-the-field work, so Harmon declines to criticize Mike McDaniel for featuring him. James Koh (not a tracked expert) pushed back with the sharper version: the problem is not that Jonnu is the third option, it is that he is too often the *first* read, alongside [[De'Von Achane]], which pushes [[Tyreek Hill]] and especially [[Jaylen Waddle]] down to third and fourth in the pecking order. Harmon partly conceded, agreeing many plays are designed with Jonnu as the number-one read, but argued the design still works because the receivers function as decoys. Both agreed the deeper issue is a stale base offense rather than Jonnu's usage.
- 2025-02-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-10]]): Smith is a tight end Waldman likes an awful lot, placed in the conversation at the tail end of his group above [[Travis Kelce]], assuming nothing changes materially in Miami. The case is that the offense refocused around him once somebody figured out what he is — namely faster than most of the players asked to cover him — with Waldman crediting Mike McDaniel for the design. His caveat, shared with Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked), is that Miami's offense has repeatedly been solved by defenses in subsequent offseasons, so whether the usage carries over is the thing to watch.
- 2025-02-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-17]]) [Best Ball]: 'feel' Jonnu Smith over [[Tyler Warren]] at Underdog TE7 — Waldman is 'riding or dying' with him right now because he fits that offense really well, is such a good runner after the catch, and is a central part of the scheme.
- 2025-02-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-28]]): Smith was the most efficient pass catcher on the Dolphins, period — team leader in successful target percentage, EPA per target, yards per route run and first downs per route — and the Jonnu Smith plays were genuinely some of the best in Miami's bag, exactly what the position needed after years of tight end window dressing. The tension both hosts raised is whether his individual success came at the expense of the offense: it is 'a little weird that you prioritized a journeyman tight end' over [[Jaylen Waddle]], whom they had just paid. Harmon is more open to that critique regarding Waddle than regarding [[Tyreek Hill]], while maintaining the plays themselves worked and should not simply be cut back.
- 2025-05-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-19]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Smith finishing 2024 as TE4 and being drafted as TE17 on Underdog makes no sense to Waldman or to Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked), who opened the segment with 'what the hell.' Waldman's case is scheme fit rather than a fluke: Miami's route concepts specifically want a tight end with run-after-catch ability, and the offense squeezed production out of [[Mike Gesicki]], who is not a YAC player — so what they got from Smith should continue. The market's discount, in Waldman's read, is part of a wider write-off of the Miami offense that also depresses [[Tua Tagovailoa]].
- 2025-06-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-02]]): on trade rumors sending Smith to Pittsburgh, Waldman says he prefers Miami for Smith's fantasy value but can see the Steelers' logic. Pittsburgh only feels clearly good about [[DK Metcalf]], okay about [[Calvin Austin III]], and is hoping on [[Roman Wilson]] — adding a second tight end like Smith means they never have to rely on Wilson and can cut down on three-receiver sets, and it helps the run game for [[Kaleb Johnson]]. He notes the move would effectively end the [[Darnell Washington]] experiment and would 'demolish a lot of best ball shares' he holds of both Smith and [[Pat Freiermuth]], though he thinks they would wash each other out. [Best Ball] Waldman says he is drafting Smith a fair amount on the assumption Mike McDaniel still views him as the player he was last year, while cautioning that last year's role felt like a necessity and may not repeat now that McDaniel has learned what mismatches Smith can create.
- 2025-06-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-30]]): reacting live to the trade sending Smith and Jalen Ramsey to Pittsburgh for Minkah Fitzpatrick, Waldman frames the fantasy outcome through Arthur Smith's 2023 Atlanta offense, where Smith and [[Kyle Pitts]] combined for 158 targets, 103 catches, roughly 1,240-1,250 yards and six touchdowns, split nearly evenly in receptions with Smith the more underneath option. Extrapolating that template, he projects Jonnu Smith at 70-80 targets and [[Pat Freiermuth]] at 60-70, each around 600-700 yards with three to four scores. Caveat: Atlanta's quarterbacks were Desmond Ridder and Taylor Heinicke, not [[Aaron Rodgers]], and Waldman expects Rodgers to have real say in how the offense is run — he speculates the Rodgers-Arthur Smith negotiation over offensive control may explain the late signing. He projects [[DK Metcalf]], Freiermuth and Smith as Pittsburgh's three leading target-getters.
- 2025-07-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the 'Arthur Smith hate' narrative around the trade is wrong. In Atlanta, Smith and [[Kyle Pitts]] split production by role and it worked — Pitts as the contested-catch, up-the-seam, boundary option and Jonnu as the underneath, red zone, flat and crossing-route guy who catches on the move and runs through people. His Miami career year came with receivers and quarterbacks injured and a young passer favoring the tight end. Waldman expects Pittsburgh to use him as the split-out yards-after-catch piece in two-tight-end sets, reducing their need for an extra receiver, and projects 50-70 targets. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) disagreed on the fit: Jonnu is a chess piece who needs a mismatch creator like Mike McDaniel, and Arthur Smith did nothing with him in Atlanta, where his best year was around TE22.
- 2025-11-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-03]]): Waldman likes Smith fine enough but wants his snaps in Pittsburgh — 'get Jonnu Smith's ass off the field' — arguing he is good after the catch but is not the route runner or the tight-coverage player [[Pat Freiermuth]] is, and joking that Arthur Smith's loyalty to a fellow Smith is driving the deployment. He would rather see the [[Darnell Washington]] and Freiermuth pairing.
- 2025-12-09 — According to Jeff Bell (Football Guys, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-09]]): Bell references the earlier report that Smith and Aaron Rodgers weren't exactly on the same page as part of why he'd expect the Steelers to lean on Pat Freiermuth over Smith if Darnell Washington is out in Week 15.
- 2026-01-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-15]]): Arthur Smith's trade for Jonnu Smith was a bust in Pittsburgh -- Smith averaged just 5.8 yards per reception on the season and had two catches for -1 yards in the wild-card blowout loss to Houston. Harmon joked 'the grass ain't always greener,' noting Smith left a good situation in Miami for a bigger contract that didn't pay off on the field.
