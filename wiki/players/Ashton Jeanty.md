- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Dynasty]: Naming his top two draft-eye-catchers from recent college tape study, Waldman singled out Boise State's Ashton Jeanty (5'8', 209) as an excellent receiver out of the backfield and an explosive, efficient runner who uses moves well both between the tackles and in the open field. With backfield mate [[George Holani]] (a UDFA trying to make the Seahawks' roster) gone, Jeanty is set to be the clear lead back this season, and Waldman said he has a real shot to be the top running back drafted out of this college class.
