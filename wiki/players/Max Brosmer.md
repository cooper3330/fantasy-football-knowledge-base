# Max Brosmer

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-31 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-31]]) [Dynasty]: Brosmer has real decision-making skill — he works sideline to sideline with timely reads off play action, correctly takes the check-down, holds defenders to one side before pivoting to throw the middle of the field off a backside break, and delivers layered throws over linebackers up the seam with pinpoint accuracy to vertical routes. His inverse of [[Kyle McCord]]'s problem is that where McCord fails to recognize unfavorable leverage, Brosmer sometimes fails to recognize favorable leverage and throws the ball away when a completion was there. He must improve at accounting for the peripheral defender on crossers — the flat defender under an over route who peels off the back to undercut the throw — though he handles it better when that defender is playing high or low of the target. He and his receivers also need to be on the same page on middle-of-field routes about settling versus continuing; he has thrown interceptions expecting the opposite of what the receiver did. Accuracy dips a little under pressure, but he makes enough layered throws with defenders in his face that Waldman thinks it premature to write him off. Verdict: not likely to transcend a backup role, but a potentially good long-term backup without the lows of higher-profile options.
- 2025-08-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-25]]) [Dynasty]: a name to know for the future, especially in two-quarterback leagues. Waldman describes him as fundamentally sound with his drops, play-action and field reads, with decent placement at times and reasonable pocket movement. His comp is deliberately hedged: maybe the same zip code as [[Brock Purdy]], but 'not as nice of a neighborhood'. He has impressed in Minnesota and could get a deeper look if [[J.J. McCarthy]] flops and Carson Wentz does not hold up, though Waldman expects the Vikings to try to stash him on the practice squad at minimum. Bob Harris (Footballguys, NOT tracked) noted the surrounding moves — [[Sam Howell]] traded, Brett Rypien released, Wentz added — and said he is interested in any quarterback who gets on the field for Kevin O'Connell.
