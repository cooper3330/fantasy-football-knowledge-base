# Kurtis Rourke

## Expert Takes

- 2025-02-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-27]]): [[Kurtis Rourke]] of Indiana is his big off-consensus call — he has him higher than roughly two-thirds of the class, in the tier of guys who could become starters if it all comes together. At 6-5, 223, Rourke is not fast but has an excellent pocket clock, knowing when to make a one-step move to avoid pressure and when to abandon the pocket; Waldman compares that feel to [[Brock Purdy]] and calls it very difficult to acquire. He has a quick, compact release, is pinpoint in the intermediate range, and showed opposite-hash accuracy out to 40-45 yards, so Waldman grades it as an NFL arm without calling it special; the flaw is underestimating trail coverage, the same lapse he saw from [[C.J. Stroud]]. The enormous 'if' is the knee: Rourke played the entire 2024 season on a re-torn ACL (originally torn in 2022 at Ohio) and had revision surgery. Waldman consulted a doctor friend who said a twice-torn ACL alone need not end a career, but meniscus or additional ligament involvement changes everything; [[Brandon Angelo]] says the recovery timeline will reveal which it was.
- 2025-03-31 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-31]]) [Dynasty]: Rourke is the prospect Waldman sounds most enthusiastic about in this class, calling him compelling. He moves well in the pocket, has clean footwork and a compact, efficient release, and is an accurate timing passer with good downfield placement — Waldman puts the upper range of his 2024 accuracy at 40 to 45 yards and says he saw deeper range at Ohio. He holds middle-of-field defenders to one side to open the backside route, sells the out with a long look to the corner to hit the corner route behind it in cover two, and sets up screens with a look to the wide or swing route before coming back the other way. He throws into tight windows with confidence and placement, accounts for peripheral defenders peeling off to help, works multiple reads high-to-low across the field, sidesteps and resets, and has the pocket clock to slide outside and hit the check-down on the move or throw it away. Not a top-end athlete as a scrambler but he feels pursuit, buys time and moves the chains. Toughness note: he had thumb surgery to miss only one game, and it emerged in early January that he played the entire 2024 season on an ACL tear suffered in August, in the same knee he hurt in 2022.
- 2025-04-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-28 (Feel It or F-It Post-Draft)]]): Rourke was his QB4 in the pre-draft RSP, and San Francisco taking him in the seventh is a smart use of the late-round-quarterback approach while they negotiate [[Brock Purdy]]'s extension. Profile: a well-built passer with a good-enough arm much like Purdy's, accurate to the opposite boundary, good pocket feel and movement but not a runner — Tom Brady / Matt Ryan style, where ten yards downfield is a big run. Key context: he played most of last season on a partially torn ACL, the same knee he hurt before. Waldman argues drafting him rather than signing him as a UDFA means the 49ers saw the medicals and planned a redshirt year, and cites Gene Bramble that an isolated ACL tear with no meniscus or other ligament damage returns the knee to its pre-injury state. He names Rourke and [[Jalen Milroe]] as the only two quarterbacks in this weak class he would draft as a GM.
- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: an interesting long-term name but not a rookie-draft add. Waldman takes validation from Kyle Shanahan and John Lynch liking him, notes Rourke played through a partially torn ACL and that San Francisco intends to redshirt him a full year before letting him compete, and reads him as a potential second-contract journeyman starter or bridge quarterback rather than a threat to [[Brock Purdy]], who will get his extension. Practically: you are only adding Rourke in something like a 50-team league with seven multi-year practice-squad spots.
