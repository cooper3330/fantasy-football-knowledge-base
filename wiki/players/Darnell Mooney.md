# Darnell Mooney

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-14]]): signs
  with Atlanta for $39M total value, with the fully-guaranteed portion
  matching [[Saquon Barkley]]'s Eagles deal ($37.8M) — used at length by the
  hosts as the marquee example of how far the wide-receiver replacement-level
  price has risen relative to running backs. Koh calls it borderline
  collusion against running backs; Harmon's framing of the market: "you hit
  free agency, and you're a guy that should be on the field in 11
  personnel... you're going to get $11, $12, $13 million a year," while a
  running back has to be a Saquon Barkley-level talent to command the same
  money. On Mooney himself: "I actually kind of like the signing by the
  Falcons" — a "nice player" who fits alongside [[Drake London]].
