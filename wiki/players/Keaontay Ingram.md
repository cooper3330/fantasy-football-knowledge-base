# Keaontay Ingram

## Expert Takes

- 2024-09-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-19]]): Waldman likes Ingram's profile but reads the Chiefs' treatment of him as a bad sign — if he had shown anything he would have been on the active roster in the first place, and he did not get the preseason opportunities that would have told the team what it has. Stylistically Waldman compared him to Kareem Hunt with fresher legs. He offered two explanations: either Kansas City views him as a special-teamer picked off the end of the Cardinals' roster, or they saw enough to doubt his dedication. He connected the second possibility to a broader recruiting-and-scouting concern he has heard repeatedly — that a five-star back (Ingram was one at Texas before injury and [[Bijan Robinson]] pushed him down the depth chart) can carry a sense of having already arrived, and that many players who do not make it fail because they do not realize there is another level up.
