# Chimere Dike

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): Waldman agrees the Florida receiver is underrated. At 6-1, 196 pounds with a 4.34 forty, Dike catches the ball pretty well and is a very good, very athletic runner after the catch. Waldman's comparison is to what people expected of [[Puka Nacua]] heading into his draft year — a player who works over the middle and turns that into yards after the catch — and he thinks Dike could develop into a good outside threat as well. The caveat is route running: he needs more work there before the outside projection is real.
- 2025-05-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-05-09]]): a late addition to the Rookie Roundup (bringing the class to 27 charted prospects) whom Harmon did not expect to go first pick of the fourth round, but a useful piece for Tennessee. Measurables cited by James Koh (not tracked): 6-1, 195, 4.34 forty, 38.5-inch vertical, fifth-best three-cone at the combine. Harmon says his overall success rates do not stand out (61.9% versus man) and that the film is middling while the athletic traits are interesting; his best work is truly running away from man coverage and stretching the field horizontally, and he is a much better man beater than zone beater — his zone routes are uninspiring and he overruns them, needing work on tempo. Harmon likes his arm length and that he plucks the ball away from his frame, which he calls a requirement for a vertical threat. He projects Dike as the motion/horizontal-movement player Tennessee lacks, since [[Calvin Ridley]] is a real route runner best left off the line rather than motioned around, and frames him as a possible [[Tyler Lockett]] replacement. Koh, who was higher on the vertical flashes, thinks Dike can actually get downfield and simply was used horizontally at Florida.
- 2025-08-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-01]]): Harmon sees Dike as a slot move-around player who stretches the field horizontally rather than vertically, a little bit gadgety, and wants him getting work in space with the ball in his hands after the catch — a different function from [[Tyler Lockett]], whom Harmon describes as more of a downfield in-breaker player at this stage. James Koh says Dike is the Titans receiver he is most excited about out of the group of [[Tyler Lockett]], [[Van Jefferson]], Dike and [[Elic Ayomanor]], while noting all of them come with significant flaws. Harmon covered Dike previously in the rookie roundup and here emphasizes vertical work from the slot as part of his profile.
- 2025-10-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-20]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is fully bought into Dike as a buy-low Dynasty target, comparing his rugged, high-pace, tackle-breaking play style to [[Puka Nacua]] -- 'as long as he can position himself well to catch the ball, he does good work' -- and he flashed that with several nice catches replacing the injured Calvin Ridley. Waldman doesn't expect the target share to hold once Ridley returns, so he frames Dike specifically as a cheap long-term dynasty stash worth trying to pry loose in a combo trade package, not a short-term starter.
- 2025-10-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-28]]): a fourth-round Tennessee rookie the show had barely discussed all season, Dike broke out in Week 8 with a team-leading line of 8 targets, 7 catches, 93 yards, following a 4-catch, 70-yard, 1-TD game the week before -- 11 completions on his last 12 targets combined. His route participation jumped to 93% (second on the team behind Elic Ayomanor) after never topping 17 receiving yards in any earlier game. He's running 71.2% of his snaps from the slot as Tennessee's clear speed-slot receiver while Van Jefferson absorbs the 'sacrificial X' role and Ayomanor plays an X/flanker hybrid with Calvin Ridley banged up. Harmon had Dike and Ayomanor in the same pre-draft tier (late Day 2/early Day 3) and ranked Ayomanor slightly higher for perimeter pop, but now says he could see Dike outproducing him -- 10 of Dike's 30 season targets have come at or behind the line of scrimmage, giving him yards-after-catch value as almost an extension of the run game. Harmon: 'leave the light on' for him as the more intriguing Titans rookie right now. [Dynasty]
- 2025-11-21 — According to Chris Harris on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-21]]: Dike was back at practice for Tennessee. Harris said he'd 'strongly prefer not to use any Titans players,' but if forced into a Titans receiver, Dike 'feels like the correct option.'
- 2025-11-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-25]]): an honorable-mention Week 13 waiver add at 14% rostered after scoring a punt-return touchdown in a big week, but Harris tempers the recommendation -- 'I don't want a lot of Titans' right now.
- 2025-12-23 — According to Matt Harmon ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-23]]): Dike (5 targets, 3 catches, 40 yards, TD vs. Kansas City) has 4 total touchdowns in his last five games (one a punt return) and 6 receiving/return touchdowns on the season, tied for second-most among all rookie wide receivers -- a result Harmon called surprising given how few touchdowns Tennessee's offense has scored overall. Per the Reception Perception rookie report, Dike doesn't yet look like a full-field X or Z receiver, but profiles well as a gadget/speed slot option who wins as a returner and after the catch; Harmon projects him as a solid wide receiver three long-term, while cautioning Tennessee's incoming new coaching staff to treat this production as a bonus and still attack the receiver room hard in the offseason rather than pencil Dike in as a locked-in starter.
- 2026-01-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-05]]) [Dynasty]: asked whether Dike and [[Elic Ayomanor]] form a viable starting tandem for [[Cam Ward]], Waldman says yes and leans toward Dike as the one he likes most. He calls Dike a pretty good route runner and overall pass catcher who plays with high intensity, a strong tackle-breaker with good speed who profiles more as a yards-after-catch weapon -- comping him to a developmental version of [[Luther Burden III]], though Waldman isn't sure Dike ever reaches the ceiling he projects for Burden.
- 2026-03-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-03-03]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon is higher on Chimere Dike than fellow Titans rookie Elic Ayomanor, projecting him as more of a No. 3 receiver at best but with real ability beyond his special-teams role -- he liked his capacity to stretch the field horizontally as both a rookie and a prospect, and thinks he could fit well if new OC Brian Dable brings more pre-snap motion and layups to what was, per Harmon's own 'static score' metric, the NFL's least dynamic offense in 2025 (32nd of 32 teams). Harmon wants him to get a full runway at the starting job rather than the Titans bringing in a Dable-connected free agent like [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] to compete with him.
