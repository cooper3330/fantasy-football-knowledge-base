# Brian Robinson Jr.

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Cliff Kingsbury's move from Raiders OC to Washington OC "doesn't hurt [Robinson's value] as much as people think." His precedent: Kingsbury had James Connor in Arizona, and Connor "looked pretty darn good in that offense" — a capable fantasy producer even though the offense overall wasn't great. Caps the redraft ceiling at touchdown/red-zone-volume dependent — "probably looking at him more as a low-end RB1, high-end RB2" — but explicitly not a death knell, crediting Robinson's receiving work as proof he "can pretty much do it all." Context: both hosts assume incumbent committee-mate Antonio Gibson leaves in free agency, opening more of the workload to Robinson.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: reiterates Robinson "looked so good last year" and reads the low cost of Washington's [[Austin Ekeler]] signing as evidence the team doesn't see Ekeler as a real threat to Robinson's role — still expects a disappointing timeshare for both unless one gets hurt.
- 2024-07-01 — According to Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Redraft]: In a feel-it-or-f-it pairing against [[Austin Ekeler]] (RB40), the hosts split. Bob takes Robinson (RB32), expecting a significant workload and noting he already holds 'enough shares' of Robinson and may add more. Waldman goes the other way with Ekeler 'against my better judgment,' saying he's not sure Robinson's decline last year fully reflects where Ekeler truly is, and that Ekeler may never have fully recovered from an earlier ankle issue that depressed his play.
- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman went 'all in' calling Brian Robinson Jr. the underrated side of the Washington backfield over [[Austin Ekeler]], saying Robinson 'needs to be a starter' and blocks better than Ekeler; Bob Harris (untracked) disagreed and preferred Ekeler. Waldman noted the fantasy price gap between the two has closed significantly since Robinson signed -- Ekeler opened well above him and has fallen as drafters remembered Robinson was already there -- and floated that a run-heavy 'Brian Robinson show' under Arthur Smith is plausible, comparing it to how Kliff Kingsbury leaned hard on Kenyan Drake as his clear lead back in Arizona once he ran out of better options. Both hosts agreed the crowded backfield was a bad outcome for both players' fantasy value even though each is individually talented.
- 2024-09-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman is 'totally feeling' that Robinson is underrated as a mere flex value, and says he has lots of shares. The reasons are scheme and skill fit: Washington runs a lot of gap/power plays that ask a back to hit one crease, which is exactly what Robinson does well; he is 'such a good receiver out of the backfield for a big man'; and he can pass protect, so [[Austin Ekeler]] will not vacuum up all the passing-down work despite being a really good route runner. Waldman expects Washington to generate big gains with Robinson in the passing game and put him closer to top-15-to-20 among backs than the top-25-to-30 range where he has often been ranked. Untracked co-host Bob Harris noted the staff's language points to Robinson as the lead back.
- 2025-01-28 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-01-28]]: Robinson is 'fine, but they need more than fine' — Washington should spend an early pick in what he calls a heavy running back draft on a special back who elevates the whole offense, the way Philadelphia's did. [[Matt Harmon]] does not hate the idea but pushes it down the priority list: Washington's clearer needs are offensive line, pass catcher, cornerback and edge rusher, they have [[Austin Ekeler]] under contract for another year and only three picks in the first three rounds, so running back reads to him as a phase-three, year-three move.
- 2025-07-07 — According to Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-07]] [Redraft (Standard)]: he is getting keener on Robinson, whose price has dropped precipitously to RB30 in about the seventh round, and he considers the role locked in. Harris prefers him to [[Tony Pollard]], whose role he thinks is less certain, would probably still take [[Isiah Pacheco]] two picks earlier despite the risk, and considers Robinson a safer play than [[Tyrone Tracy Jr.]] in what he calls an ambiguous Giants backfield. He also notes Robinson goes ahead of [[Travis Etienne Jr.]], whom both hosts think may be underpriced. [[Matt Waldman]] agreed, calling him a cheap handcuff and one of the few backs in that range you actually know will start.
- 2025-08-18 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Washington's leak that it had shopped Robinson strikes Harris as strange and probably not actionable. He is skeptical that 'the bathtub on wheels' is a player NFL teams would surrender anything meaningful for — a fifth-round pick at most, since league-average running backs do not fetch big prices — and notes Robinson makes little on his rookie deal, so cost is not the motive. His guess is that this is a negotiating message aimed at Robinson's representatives ahead of free agency next offseason. He is also skeptical a trade happens because Washington has no obvious replacement: [[Austin Ekeler]] is not taking over lead duties, and turning it over to seventh-round rookie [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] would be a surprise — though if the trade did happen, Harris says that would say a lot about JCM.
- 2025-08-20 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the Commanders now look dead set on essentially cutting Robinson, with national media moles told he will not play for Washington again and any trade returning only a late-round pick. Harris calls the frugality baffling given Robinson is on roughly $3 million in the last year of his rookie deal, and pairs it with the [[Terry McLaurin]] standoff as a bad message to [[Jayden Daniels]] — though he stresses he does not rate the two players similarly, describing McLaurin as an absolute stud and Robinson as 'the bathtub on wheels'. He is not claiming Robinson takes over wherever he lands, but he is a thumper, a north-south, potential touchdown back, and the landing spot matters: Cowboys, Texans (which would signal [[Joe Mixon]]'s ankle), Bears, Browns (if the [[Quinshon Judkins]] situation drags) or Titans (if [[Tyjae Spears]]'s ankle is bad).
- 2025-08-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-21]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: he dropped Robinson well down the rankings as the back sits in limbo waiting to be cut or traded, with his money on the former — and he argued a trade barely matters, since getting dealt with a sixth-rounder for a fifth-rounder is just being cut with the middleman removed. Harris said Robinson's landing spot is basically all that determines whether he is worth a bench fantasy roster spot, and he does not know that yet, so the ranking move is cautious and reactionary rather than news-driven. He also tied the ejection to Washington being delighted with seventh-round pick [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] and believing the remaining backs can replace Robinson.
- 2025-08-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-25]]): Washington essentially dumped Robinson, trading him to San Francisco for only a sixth-round pick and sending over a million dollars along to cover part of his contract. Kyle Shanahan then immediately announced Robinson as the number two back behind [[Christian McCaffrey]], which Harris says tells you everything about how the 49ers feel about [[Isaac Guerendo]] and rookie [[Jordan James]]. Harris keeps Robinson as a top-100 overall pick and calls him a must-handcuff for anyone taking the McCaffrey plunge. On whether Robinson vultures touchdowns, Harris labels it 'pure category two' — he does not know the coach's plans — but given Shanahan's past usage of McCaffrey he does not expect much restraint.
- 2025-08-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-27]]): Harris read his updated Almanac profile aloud and reverted Robinson's nickname to 'the bathtub on wheels', retracting the 'better [[James Conner]]' label after watching both men in 2024 — Robinson came close to 'only okay if he scores a touchdown' territory, the zone Harris names after Damien Harris. Robinson scored in four of Washington's first five games, was the team's first 100-yard rusher in 24 games in week two, then from week 11 forward scored just twice and averaged only 67 scrimmage yards. Harris allows the knee and hamstring issues may explain it. Washington benched him for an exhibition, leaked that he would not play for them again, and traded him to San Francisco for a 2026 sixth-rounder while eating salary. Kyle Shanahan immediately named him RB2 behind [[Christian McCaffrey]], putting [[Isaac Guerendo]] on the outs and giving Robinson the keys to what Harris calls Jordan Mason Kingdom. Harris says he should go inside the top 100 in Labor Day drafts and that if you take McCaffrey, Robinson is a mandatory top-100 handcuff pick.
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Christian McCaffrey]] missing Thursday practice with a calf strain, Robinson is 'the handcuff of all handcuffs until further notice' — if he is somehow available in your league, 'you gotta go get Brian Robinson.'
- 2025-11-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-10]]): once San Francisco fell behind 21-0 and Kyle Shanahan Jr. 'got out the running shoes,' the backfield was McCaffrey and Robinson over and over. Robinson took four straight carries to move them into the red zone and then punched in the score from the one with a nice tackle-breaking run, with [[Christian McCaffrey]] on the field for only one snap in between. Harris says there is nothing actionable yet, but acknowledges anyone panicking about [[Tyler Allgeier]] should apply the same logic here.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-11]]): guest **Brandon Funston** (The Athletic, not tracked) made Robinson his number three Week 11 add, similar to [[Blake Corum]] except San Francisco uses him more at the goal line, and there are signs they may lean on him down the stretch to preserve [[Christian McCaffrey]], whose usage is 'insane' - Funston likes his upside a little more than Corum's. Harris agrees neither belongs in a lineup right now, but is emphatic on the handcuff logic: 'if you have McCaffrey and you don't have Brian Robinson, do what you can do - trade for him, for heaven's sake'. He notes McCaffrey still came in for one play to get to the two before Robinson scored, so 'McCaffrey's still the one you want'.
