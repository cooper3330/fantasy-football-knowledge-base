# Kendre Miller

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]]): would rather stash Miller than [[Tank Bigsby]] on a dynasty bench, on the belief that "if they get an opportunity, they're going to produce" whether or not the underlying talent grade fully supports it.
- 2024-07-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Redraft]: Waldman calls Miller intriguing at his cheap ADP (around RB50) but says there are backs with more near-term upside available in the same range, naming Jaleel McLaughlin (untracked, RSP-favorite value pick) as one he'd rather grab a few spots later at a similar or cheaper price. He notes Sean Payton is reportedly high on Miller ('the apple of his coach's eye') and that he has real speed and receiving upside as [[Alvin Kamara]]'s backup, but questions whether Miller has proven himself conceptually reliable as an NFL player yet -- 'a lot to like there, but people don't get carried away.'
- 2024-10-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-28]]): 'fuck it' on the premise that Miller's fantasy future depends on Dennis Allen being fired. Waldman argued the opposite — Allen's tough love is working, Miller is starting to play well and something is emerging that Waldman does not think was always there, even if the talent was; media criticism of Allen as an unfair old curmudgeon is something he now begs to differ with. The real concern is availability: Miller left this game with a hamstring injury, and Waldman counted roughly 15 games missed in his career (nine last year, six this year). Allen has made no bones about it — 'you don't make the team in the tub' — and Waldman's worry is that Allen eventually sours on him over durability rather than over talent.
- 2024-12-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-09]]): Miller looks better than [[Isaiah Davis]], but not by as much as you would expect. Waldman calls him a quick-twitch back, promising as a receiver though not as good as Davis there, a better pure runner — especially in a zone scheme — a good tackle breaker with great top-end speed, and a player who could eventually succeed [[Alvin Kamara]]. Both hosts flagged the availability problem: Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) recalled the summer 'you can't make the team in the tub' cliche being used out loud about him, and Waldman noted whatever Miller did to rub the previous staff the wrong way seemed specific to Dennis Allen, who is now gone — the rest of the staff remains. [Dynasty]
- 2025-06-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-16]]): the Cam Akers signing matters precisely because New Orleans cannot trust what it has behind Alvin Kamara. On Miller specifically, you do not know what you are going to get from week to week, at least as the coaching staff portrays it — but Waldman is clear that Miller has the talent, which [[Devin Neal]] may not. He frames Akers as a stopgap in case Miller gets hurt, Neal does not work out, and Clyde Edwards-Helaire's bounce-back never comes.
