# Kendre Miller

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]]): would rather stash Miller than [[Tank Bigsby]] on a dynasty bench, on the belief that "if they get an opportunity, they're going to produce" whether or not the underlying talent grade fully supports it.
- 2024-07-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Redraft]: Waldman calls Miller intriguing at his cheap ADP (around RB50) but says there are backs with more near-term upside available in the same range, naming Jaleel McLaughlin (untracked, RSP-favorite value pick) as one he'd rather grab a few spots later at a similar or cheaper price. He notes Sean Payton is reportedly high on Miller ('the apple of his coach's eye') and that he has real speed and receiving upside as [[Alvin Kamara]]'s backup, but questions whether Miller has proven himself conceptually reliable as an NFL player yet -- 'a lot to like there, but people don't get carried away.'
- 2024-10-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-28]]): 'fuck it' on the premise that Miller's fantasy future depends on Dennis Allen being fired. Waldman argued the opposite — Allen's tough love is working, Miller is starting to play well and something is emerging that Waldman does not think was always there, even if the talent was; media criticism of Allen as an unfair old curmudgeon is something he now begs to differ with. The real concern is availability: Miller left this game with a hamstring injury, and Waldman counted roughly 15 games missed in his career (nine last year, six this year). Allen has made no bones about it — 'you don't make the team in the tub' — and Waldman's worry is that Allen eventually sours on him over durability rather than over talent.
- 2024-12-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-09]]): Miller looks better than [[Isaiah Davis]], but not by as much as you would expect. Waldman calls him a quick-twitch back, promising as a receiver though not as good as Davis there, a better pure runner — especially in a zone scheme — a good tackle breaker with great top-end speed, and a player who could eventually succeed [[Alvin Kamara]]. Both hosts flagged the availability problem: Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) recalled the summer 'you can't make the team in the tub' cliche being used out loud about him, and Waldman noted whatever Miller did to rub the previous staff the wrong way seemed specific to Dennis Allen, who is now gone — the rest of the staff remains. [Dynasty]
- 2025-06-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-16]]): the Cam Akers signing matters precisely because New Orleans cannot trust what it has behind Alvin Kamara. On Miller specifically, you do not know what you are going to get from week to week, at least as the coaching staff portrays it — but Waldman is clear that Miller has the talent, which [[Devin Neal]] may not. He frames Akers as a stopgap in case Miller gets hurt, Neal does not work out, and Clyde Edwards-Helaire's bounce-back never comes.
- 2025-09-30 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-30]]): the hosts openly disagree. Miller played well against Buffalo — a hard-to-tackle power back who withstood a big red zone hit and scored, on 21 snaps to [[Alvin Kamara]]'s 47 — and Harris grants he looks like a strong player who would get volume if Kamara were gone, but you are not adding him to use him right away; he is a bye-week bench body in a deeper league. Guest Jeff Erickson (RotoWire, not tracked) goes further, making Miller his number-two waiver add at 5% rostered, calling him a top-five handcuff and noting he was getting opening-series carries and red zone looks behind a Saints line that can actually run block, and that he was mishandled last year (IR against his and the OC's wishes, strong Dennis Allen comments). Harris refuses that tier — he cannot see Miller reaching [[Tyler Allgeier]]/[[Blake Corum]] territory on this team — and both note Miller had zero targets, capping PPR upside.
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris' No. 4 Week 6 waiver add at 31% rostered, No. 3 for guest Brandon Funston of The Athletic (not tracked). Harris explicitly rejects the takeover story — 'I did not emerge from this thinking that Kendre Miller had somehow Wally Pipped [[Alvin Kamara]]' — with snaps 39 to 26, and 36 to 17 before the Saints were up 12 and running clock. But Miller had pop runs in the second half and got much of a third-quarter series and three straight carries on a fourth-quarter drive. Funston, who didn't think much of him as a rookie, says the game has slowed down for him and usage has climbed on 'a subtle straight line' each week, with the pie-in-the-sky scenario being a Kamara trade at the deadline. Harris' framing: a good handcuff and a good power back who'd be useful, not a superstar, in a pure starting role.
- 2025-10-17 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and co-host Josh (not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-17]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Discussing the Saints as a surprisingly supportive offense, Harris cites a real home-league example -- a 5-1 manager trading for Miller purely on the offensive-environment thesis, betting that if [[Alvin Kamara]] is traded or loses touches late in the season, Miller becomes usable simply by being the lead back in a functional offense. Josh agreed Miller is worth a speculative waiver stash on that logic in shallower or less competitive leagues.
- 2026-08-20 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Miller returned from a prolonged absence to practice in the same Saints joint practice where [[Alvin Kamara]] suffered his MCL sprain, putting him in the mix for early-season backfield touches. Harris says it's hard to know yet whether Miller or [[Devin Neal]] would be the clear number-two back, and it may not shake out as just one guy.
- 2026-08-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-28]]) [Dynasty]: Miller (nicknamed 'Chinese Democracy' for how long he's been promised) makes the Super Deep Sleeper list in his third pro year having done little but get hurt -- knee surgery, a knee sprain, an ankle injury, two hamstring pulls, a concussion, and a torn ACL partway through last season. He was the number-72 running back, non-draftable, in the first Almanac edition. Harris's case: before the ACL tear, Miller flashed real power and enough wiggle that he compares the archetype (with the caveat 'clearly more injury prone') to [[Nick Chubb]] and [[Josh Jacobs]] in their primes, and of the non-Etienne, non-Kamara Saints backs, Miller is the only one whose film has actually looked good to him. The catch: it's pure guesswork what post-ACL Miller looks like, he's not confident Miller is even the clear backup over Devin Neal with Zamir White also in the mix, and since Kamara will eventually return healthy, Harris says that's exactly why he is NOT drafting Miller himself.
