# Sam Roush

## Expert Takes

- 2026-04-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-06]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman fully feels it that Sam Roush could be the most productive rookie tight end in 2026 if he lands in the right spot. He's a big in-line tight end with strong hands who can go up and win contested balls, has after-the-catch skill for his size (not Gronkowski-level, but comparable to how [[Tucker Kraft]] or [[Tyler Warren]] get deployed in space), and enough speed/quickness to win up the seam. Waldman floats a specific fit: a run-heavy, two-tight-end Atlanta look, with Roush as the in-line blocker/detached-option complement, potentially unlocking more from [[Kyle Pitts]] and getting more juice out of 12 personnel.
- 2026-04-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman calls the Bears' draft underrated, including taking Roush after already having [[Colston Loveland]] and re-signing [[Cole Kmet]] -- Roush was his top-ranked in-line tight end in the class, a better blocker than Kmet, and both Roush and Loveland project as good red-zone go-up-and-get-it threats in what could be a real three-tight-end package. Waldman notes Kmet's restructured contract created cap flexibility and eased a possible trade, framing the pick as insurance and complementary rather than a signal Kmet or Loveland are being phased out.
- 2026-05-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-04]]) [Dynasty]: sees the addition of center-caliber [[Tyler Lindenbaum|Tyler Lindenbaum-type]] play at center and tight end Sam Roush as part of the Bears prioritizing offense over adding a defensive lineman this offseason. Waldman jokingly frames Roush and existing pass-catcher as a 'more civilized' Aaron Hernandez/Rob Gronkowski-style tandem, and argues that adding two offensive difference-makers can sustain execution better than one marginal defensive add, especially if a better offense lets Chicago build bigger leads and reduce reliance on late-game pass rush.
- 2026-07-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Jeff Erickson (Rotowire, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-28]]): the Bears used a third-round pick on rookie tight end Roush, and both hosts flag his 12/13-personnel role as a real question for how many of the extra post-DJ-Moore-trade targets actually land on [[Rome Odunze]] and [[Luther Burden III]] versus going to Roush and the run game under Ben Johnson -- something neither expects joint practices or preseason snaps to definitively answer.
