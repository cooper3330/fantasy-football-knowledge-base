- 2024-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and guest Jagger May (Football Guys, untracked) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-20]]) [Dynasty]: both go "fuck it" on Michael Mayer's dynasty value after the Raiders drafted [[Brock Bowers]] in round one. May, who had Mayer as his TE4 in the 2023 class, still viewed him as a serviceable Dalton Schultz-level producer but doesn't see enough target volume for Las Vegas to feed Mayer, Bowers, Davante Adams and Jakobi Meyers under Gardner Minshew. Waldman compared Mayer's outlook to Harrison Bryant's on this same roster — projecting him as a short-yardage, schemed option good for 2-3 targets a game, with occasional spike weeks only off matchups or injuries to those ahead of him, and real value only as an injury-driven, Isaiah Likely-style handcuff in a two-TE look.
- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman said reports that new Raiders TE coach Luke Steckel (son of former hard-nosed Vikings OC Les Steckel) called tight ends 'blockers first' point toward Michael Mayer, not [[Brock Bowers]], as the primary in-line run blocker, which under 'rational coaching' should make Mayer fantasy's TE2 in the offense while Bowers stays the room's nominal TE1. He projected Mayer for only around 500-600 receiving yards with occasional red-zone value, since fully deploying Bowers' wide-receiver-caliber route skills would require the coaching staff to make what Waldman considers the smarter, less likely call.
