# Kendall Milton

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: one of Angelo's mid-to-late-pick names he thinks could become a viable NFL committee contributor. Calls him a genuinely unique size-speed player at roughly 6'1", 220-225 lbs, doing interesting things laterally for a back his size, and says the class hasn't seen his best tape yet. Flags the combine/pro day circuit as the real swing factor for whether his draft stock rises toward an earlier pick or falls to a Day 3/UDFA outcome. [[Matt Waldman]], same episode: had hoped for an Eddie George-caliber talent based on Milton's high-school recruiting profile entering Georgia, and says that hasn't fully manifested on tape at that level — while allowing he understands where Angelo's optimism comes from.
- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast [Dynasty]: elaborates beyond the earlier Eddie George-ceiling disappointment — praises Milton's power-with-base combination, efficient hip-opening for changes of direction behind the line and in the open field, and pacing/stride control to set up cutbacks and bounces in tight spaces, generally keeping his feet under his pads even through dynamic hits. Calls it "some really compelling aspects" but withholds judgment on whether it integrates into a complete NFL game.

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-11 (Going Deep - Fave Evals, Coach-Killer Candidates, and High-End Career Outcomes)]]), discussing [[Daijun Edwards]]'s Georgia backfield: revisit the earlier Eddie George-ceiling framing — the flashes of that five-star talent ("a slower Eddie George") came, in their view, too late in his college career to fully cash in, though he did show glimpses late. Both agree that pairing Milton's physical tools with Edwards's processing and approach to the game would produce a top-five running back prospect in this class.
