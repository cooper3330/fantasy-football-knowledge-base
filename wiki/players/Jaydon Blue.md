# Jaydon Blue

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-13]]) [Dynasty]: one of the players he is most excited about in the class — young, with raw tools. Angelo puts him in a similar vein to [[Brashard Smith]] but less impactful overall as a receiver: a really good accelerator who can run away from people, throttle down to run option and angle routes, beat linebackers with ease and take screens 60-70 yards. The comp is [[Justice Hill]] coming out of Oklahoma State, with Angelo grading Blue a touch above Hill on total talent because so much is untapped. The caveat is developmental: he needs a system that keeps him on a roster and feeds him gradually over two or three years, at which point he could be a secondary option rather than a lead back.
- 2025-04-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-07]]) [Dynasty]: at a listed 201 pounds, Blue has one of the highest ceilings of any back in the class — terrific speed, a very good pass receiver, and a smart between-the-tackles runner with good footwork who sees things well. The single gating issue is ball security: he cannot hold onto the football and has to fix it. If he does, Waldman puts the ceiling at starter level and compares him to a young [[Austin Ekeler]]. If he does not, Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) quips he will find a comfortable spot on special teams, the bench, or starring in the CFL — and Waldman agrees that works too.
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21 (Film & Data with Dwain McFarland)]]): Blue grades as a competent starter — good at dragging defenders and pushing for yards after being wrapped up, but committee-level absorbing both direct and indirect contact, which Waldman calls the bare minimum for a starter. He confirms the speed on tape (elite at top gear, will pull away from and challenge cornerbacks) but not the acceleration: starter-to-high-end-starter getting there, needing a runway like [[Omarion Hampton]]. As a receiver Blue is one of Waldman's five best route runners at the position and one of his four or five best pass catchers in the class. The reason he isn't in Waldman's top ten is ball security: Blue fumbles once every 45.5 touches against Waldman's baseline of one per 60 — 'if he had better ball handling he'd probably be in my top ten.' Guest Dwain McFarland (Fantasy Life, NOT tracked) is skeptical, calling Blue almost entirely carried by draft capital (now projected pick 143, up from undrafted) and speed score (81, a 106.5 speed score), with a 66 production rating from a shared Texas backfield and a merely okay 73 film score; McFarland added that watching Texas games in season he never saw the speed his testing implied.
- 2025-05-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-05]]) [Dynasty]: Blue is the big threat in the Dallas backfield and the reason Waldman says 'fuck it' to [[Javonte Williams]] finishing as the lead back. His case: Blue offers more big-play ability than Williams, is a really good pass catcher, and is a smart runner. The single stated risk is ball security — 'he just has to hold on to the ball.' Waldman also referenced Blue running past Williams as something Williams was not happy about. He views the room as a genuine committee also containing [[Phil Mafah]] and [[Miles Sanders]], but with Blue the most likely to end the season as the most productive back.
- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: 'hands down the most talented back in this room, like not even close', and a value at his rookie-draft cost. He dismisses the competition with numbers — [[Javonte Williams]] averaged 2.8 yards per carry and 14 yards per game over his last five games of 2024 and has not rushed for 90 yards in a game since 2021, while [[Miles Sanders]] has been under four yards per carry for two straight seasons. Blue profiles as the best pass catcher of the group by a long shot on a Cowboys team that will have to throw, so Angelo expects him to win a significant role by midseason, allowing for the fact that Texas did not use him in a traditional back's role. [[Matt Waldman]] likes the receiving and pure-running traits but makes ball security the gate: if the fumbling is correctable, the sky's the limit.
- 2025-05-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-19]]) [Best Ball]: Blue is his preferred Dallas back — the best combination of ability, upside and value at his round (ADP ~141, round 12-13, versus [[Javonte Williams]] at RB38 in the tenth) — and the player he expects to take the biggest bite out of the backfield. Projection: 120 attempts, 517 yards (4.3 YPC), 35 targets and 25 catches, and Waldman says Blue's role is the one most likely to grow through the summer and the projection he is most likely to raise. Caveats: his ball security is worse than [[Bhayshul Tuten]]'s, though Waldman calls that correctable, and the Dallas line is poor. Against Tuten he leans Blue on receiving work and blocking upside — Blue is a physical talent who already shows nuance, Tuten is a physical talent still learning it. Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked) is taking a lot of Blue and notes his sub-4.4 speed fits Ryan Schottenheimer's stated balance and new line coaching.
- 2025-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: guest JJ Zachariason (not a tracked expert) called Blue the low-hanging-fruit landing-spot pick — a good analytical receiving profile with strong burst and acceleration into a Dallas backfield of [[Miles Sanders]], a post-injury [[Javonte Williams]] who he thought had lost something, and the one-dimensional [[Phil Mafah]]. Size is the concern, but he argues Dallas may be willing to give a smaller back 100-120 carries plus real receiving work because they lack a true dual-threat who can do damage in space, and he could see Blue eventually reaching a 150-carry, loosely Jahmyr Gibbs-style role at some point in his career (explicitly not saying he is that player). Waldman agreed the fit works and comped him to the [[Austin Ekeler]] archetype, which supports the long-term projection.
- 2025-08-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-04]]): asked whether Blue will be Dallas' lead back by the end of the year, Waldman says he feels it — Blue has the best skills on the roster for the position, is a good pass catcher, and is an [[Austin Ekeler]]-ish talent who is young and still has to grow into his paws, which may be difficult in Dallas. He does not trust [[Miles Sanders]] at all and only 'could get talked into' [[Javonte Williams]], and expects a committee split early that resolves to Blue emerging late, contingent on the interior line staying healthy. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) says Blue sits in a tier of dart throws with Williams, [[Travis Etienne Jr.]] and [[Tank Bigsby]] where he holds shares of all of them, but likes Blue best: after early leaked reports calling him lazy, the motivation appears to have worked and he has drawn nothing but rave reviews from Brian Schottenheimer since.
- 2025-08-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-14]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris is negative on Blue from watching him at Texas — Blue was the hyped one but 'something stupid was always lurking,' and Harris found himself wanting Tre Wisner to get the ball instead, granting Blue is undeniably fast and made big plays. He also notes Blue missed the beginning of minicamp and camp and drew criticism for not knowing what it takes to be a pro, and only suddenly looks good now. Guest Sigmund Bloom (Footballguys, not tracked) is more neutral: Blue has the straight-line acceleration and burst to break long plays if defenses stay in light boxes, and the Dallas backfield deserves a head-on-a-swivel approach. Harris allows that college kids can change.
- 2025-08-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-19]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: absolutely fine with Blue as a double-digit-round redraft flier, projecting him as the Cowboys' second-leading rusher and second-leading overall producer out of the backfield this year. He likes Blue's vision enough to think that if he curbs a few things he could grow into an [[Austin Ekeler]] type. On the reports that Blue frustrated coaches early in the offseason program before flipping a switch, Waldman is encouraged rather than alarmed and draws an [[Alvin Kamara]] parallel — a player Waldman admits he missed on because Kamara fumbled a lot and bounced everything outside playing hero ball, until Sean Payton got in his face for running scared and something switched. Waldman does flag ball security as a real worry, joking that Blue and [[Bhayshul Tuten]] should race carrying a greased watermelon. [[Chris Harris]], who watched Blue's Texas games, says he was frustrated that Blue would not run where the play was designed.
- 2025-08-25 — According to guest JJ Zachariason (NOT a tracked expert) on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-25]]: Blue is his number three, an upside shot around the 11th round. He has serious concerns about Blue's size and archetype — one of his prospect comps was [[Justice Hill]], and Hill stuck largely on pass protection, which you cannot rely on from a rookie. The case is the offense and the competition: Dallas projects pass-heavy given personnel and Brian Schottenheimer's history, [[Javonte Williams]] has not been the same since the knee, [[Miles Sanders]] is Miles Sanders, and [[Phil Mafah]] is a camp body. JJ sees a possible 10-11% target share and 100-120 carries rather than a 200-carry bell cow, which in PPR is enough. Harris, who watched him at Texas, calls him explosively fast but a knucklehead and a fumble machine who ran the wrong way repeatedly; he likes the shot anyway because speed kills and nobody has a handle on that backfield. Blue also sprained a low ankle in the last exhibition.
- 2025-09-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Blue was ineligible for the waiver list at 38% rostered on Yahoo, which Harris finds odd given [[Javonte Williams]] only went about a round ahead of him on average. Blue got some helium in Dallas camp before missing a couple of weeks with a bad ankle. Guest BRANDON FUNSTON (NOT tracked) says that between Blue and [[Ollie Gordon II]] he leans Blue, because he sees a clearer path to a bigger workload regardless of the back ahead of him; Harris declines to take a strong take either way and says only the actual games will settle it.
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: 'the rookie sensation' was a healthy scratch for Week 1 and was not listed on the Dallas injury report at all. Harris allows that the sprained ankle may have factored in and that teams play fast and loose with injury reports, but leans toward the simpler explanation: Blue is third on the depth chart and 'the training camp hype was generic — I love rookies hype.' He is not in the Cowboys' early-season backfield plans, and Harris says if you were hoping to sneak Blue into a Week 1 lineup, 'Dallas probably did you a favor by making him inactive.'
- 2026-01-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-26]]) [Dynasty]: fuck it -- Waldman says Blue has not proven he learned any lessons and is now one of the more expendable/dispensable names on a dynasty roster. His framing is that the open question is entirely about commitment and professionalism (practice habits, conduct in the facility) rather than talent, and that if the answer to whether Blue can show that commitment is 'no' right now, he can be cut.
- 2026-06-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-22 (Feel It or Fk It)]]) [Dynasty]: agrees Blue and [[Phil Mafah]] are 'for real' as reserves behind starter [[Javonte Williams]], and rates Blue as having the most upside of the two to become a starter, with fantasy-relevant production as high as top-15 if given the opportunity. Waldman's caveats: Blue still has real developmental work to do beyond speed, notably ball security, which needs to improve. If he plays well subbing for an injured Williams this year, Waldman thinks he could lock down the starting role entering next season.
- 2026-07-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-06]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman feels Blue turns a corner in 2026, pointing to Blue's own admission that he 'didn't have the maturity or understanding' of pro requirements as a rookie -- footwear/blister issues that cost him practice time last year were 'indicative of the bigger issue.' Waldman comps his ceiling to an 'aspiring Austin Ekeler,' projecting Blue could push into lead-back range by 2028-2029 given [[Javonte Williams]]' new three-year extension isn't expected to run its full length. Bob Harris (untracked) agreed Blue is the shiftier, speedier receiving-down outlier in this backfield ('one of these things is not like the other'), but cautioned it isn't zero-sum: Williams finished last season as an RB12 and remains 'super dangerous' if he keeps his red-zone role, so Blue's rise depends on carving out touches rather than an outright takeover.
