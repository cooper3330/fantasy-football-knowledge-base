- 2026-06-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-22 (Feel It or Fk It)]]) [Dynasty]: sees Dulcich as the first read up the seam or on sail routes off play-action in Miami's offense, alongside [[Malik Washington]] as the top two fantasy-relevant weapons in a thin receiving corps. Waldman graded him a mid-30s tight end and calls him and Washington 'true free square plays' -- late-round dart throws worth a shot for the boom-bust variance in Best Ball, noting 800 receiving yards from either would count as a win given the low expectations.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]): Waldman checked the numbers mid-show and said Dulcich has been 'faring reasonably well' though quietly, in an offense built around play-action off Malik Willis rollouts that typically limits the quarterback to two reads -- a structural cap on Dulcich's target opportunities even as his underlying play has been solid.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]): Waldman lists Dulcich as one of the few likely later-read targets for [[Malik Willis]] in Miami, alongside [[Caleb Douglas]] and [[Malik Washington]], if Willis ever progresses past his first look.
