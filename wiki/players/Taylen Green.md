# Taylen Green

## Expert Takes

- 2026-02-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-16]]) [Dynasty]: Green is raw but has excellent physical tools -- 6'6', 230, long-striding speed that plays faster than it looks -- drawing early Colin Kaepernick and (lesser) Anthony Richardson comps. Waldman likes his play-action detail (selling the exchange, pad level to buy pocket time or scramble lanes), his ability to layer throws over linebackers on the move, and pocket maneuvering beyond just spin-and-roll -- he can sidestep pressure, climb, and throw accurately in the middle of the field while cycling through progressions. The flaw: he doesn't account well for coverage adjacent to the route, especially in the middle of the field or against zone in the flats, and his athleticism tempts him into 'hero ball' throws from his own territory instead of easy checkdowns. Waldman's projected range: a Kaepernick-type ceiling in a run-heavy, strong-O-line system with playmakers, versus a Terrell Pryor-type bust who gets pushed off quarterback if the mistakes don't clean up.
- 2026-03-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-02]]) [Dynasty]: pushed back a bit on a comparison to Randall Cunningham he'd heard floated (attributed to commentary possibly from Nate Tice), saying Green's movement reminds him more of Colin Kaepernick -- 'Kaepernick and Cunningham... move the same way' -- and that Green had a fantastic athletic showing and can throw the ball well. Waldman believes Green will develop into a startable NFL quarterback, but the open question is conceptual development. He relayed a theory from Drew Brees (heard on the Kelce brothers' podcast, not a tracked-expert source) that a quarterback needs roughly 50 combined college-plus-pro starts before the game 'clicks' conceptually, citing Brock Purdy's Iowa State starts as an example of a QB who got there faster; Waldman's expectation is that Green benefits from sitting behind a veteran early rather than starting right away, though he noted that's rarely how rookie QB situations actually play out.
- 2026-04-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-06]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman feels it that Arkansas QB Taylen Green has a shot at becoming a future NFL starter, but rates it at the weakest tier of 'feel it.' He comps Green to a Colin Kaepernick-type: capable of strong stats and even Pro Bowl-caliber production if surrounded by a good offensive line, run game and after-catch receivers, but questions whether Green can carry a lesser situation as its 'thermostat.' He likes Green's play-action game and arm talent but says accuracy and mechanical consistency need work. Athletically he places Green in Anthony Richardson's sphere, though without Richardson's bulk or arm talent, and says Green needs more experience, more time, and a patient development plan. Co-host Bob Harris flags hero-ball tendencies; Waldman agrees, saying Green needs to improve retreating from pressure and choosing to maneuver rather than run, noting he has the ability to be more efficient in the pocket but doesn't always take it.
- 2026-04-16 — According to Dalton Wasserman (guest draft analyst, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-16]]) [Dynasty]: [[Taylen Green]] 'crushed the combine,' pairing one of the two strongest arms in the class (with Drew Allar) with dangerous scrambling ability, built on a winding college path -- Boise State starter, then benched, then Arkansas under Bobby Petrino. At 23 he's barely younger than Anthony Richardson was at draft time. Wasserman's two flags on Green are the same ones that made him low on Richardson pre-draft: accuracy and decision-making, comparing Green's tools-over-fundamentals profile to Jalen Milroe's. He projects as a late-round athletic developmental pick only, not a near-term fantasy factor. Chris Harris added that three years removed from Richardson's bust, the league appears to have soured on the rushing-QB-first archetype, meaning Green likely won't be drafted or developed as aggressively as that profile once was.
- 2026-04-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is intrigued by Cleveland's 6th-round flier on Green (Arkansas), comparing him to a more athletic version of Colin Kaepernick -- raw and needing more experience and accuracy development, but a plausible scheme fit for OC Todd Monken. Waldman doesn't expect Green to reach Lamar Jackson-level conceptual mastery, but thinks Cleveland will try to build strong enough surrounding talent that Green can play a Kaepernick-style role early and grow toward a Jalen Hurts-level ceiling rather than staying as limited as Kaepernick was in San Francisco.
- 2026-04-28 — According to James Koh (untracked co-host) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-28]]): questioned Cleveland's late-round pick of Taylen Green, arguing a QB who 'needs at least a full year of development' has been dropped into a room with [[Deshaun Watson]], [[Shedeur Sanders]] and [[Dylan Gabriel]] all fighting for their careers -- 'that's not the move,' even while acknowledging Green's traits are interesting and that Todd Monken has a strong track record developing quarterbacks.
