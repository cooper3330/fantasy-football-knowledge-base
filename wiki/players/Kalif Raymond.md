# Kalif Raymond

## Expert Takes

- 2026-09-29 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-09-29]]): Raymond, 32 and in his 10th-plus NFL season, scored a 45-yard touchdown on what Harris called a purely blown coverage, plus a long gain on a Case Keenum moon-shot throw -- Harris floats whether Raymond is secretly the best fantasy receiver in the world, answers no, but admits he's wavering. Guest [[Jake Seeley]] (not tracked) made Raymond his #5 Week 4 waiver add, citing that Raymond stays on the field in two-tight-end sets (unlike [[Luther Burden III]]), runs 75%-plus of routes every week regardless of who's at quarterback, and is Chicago's designed short/intermediate safety valve when the offensive line breaks down. Harris has him as only a fringe WR3 and his own #8/honorable mention, surprised he's still just 10% rostered on Yahoo, but cautions the touchdown was a blown-coverage fluke and he's wary of betting into a winning streak.
- 2026-10-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-10-01]]) charting Kalif Raymond's Week 3 tape against Philadelphia: he has unexpectedly emerged as the leading receiver in Ben Johnson's Bears offense, a 32-year-old speed-based veteran who knows the system from a prior stint under Johnson. He played 64 snaps off-ball (30 slot, 11 backfield), ran a go/nine route on over 20% of his charted routes, and was successful on 14-of-20 routes against zone and 3-of-4 against man coverage, including the well-designed 41-yard touchdown off a Luther Burden III motion clear-out that drew two defenders away. He is at a 90.5% catch rate and leads the team in yards per route run and YAC yardage, though his average depth of target is low (6.19), so Harmon expects efficiency to regress. He doesn't expect Raymond to lead the team in receiving yards wire-to-wire and thinks the role could fade late-season as Chicago's younger receivers develop, but calls the production 'real,' not a fluke, noting Raymond is also an excellent run blocker who has taken valuable designed touches from Colston Loveland, Luther Burden III and Rome Odunze.
