# Hakeem Butler

- 2026-04-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-20]]) [Dynasty]: Asked if there's an NFL-caliber receiver hiding in the UFL, Waldman said yes, pointing to Hakeem Butler, who has had NFL flirtations for years off strong UFL/St. Louis Battle Hawks tape without a roster spot sticking. Waldman argued the realistic path in isn't as a standalone outside receiver -- teams rarely hand a lower-league receiver real competition for that role -- but through a specialist wrinkle, comping the path to KaVontae Turpin, who leveraged return work into slot reps. Waldman acknowledged Butler's known drop issues but said there was real talent evident on tape, and that continuing to play at a high level in the UFL still justifies the belief he could contribute in the NFL, even if it's unlikely to actually happen. He also referenced the Arizona Cardinals' history of being slow to use talented receivers, noting it took them four years to feature [[Michael Wilson]] and joked it might take similarly long for [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]].
