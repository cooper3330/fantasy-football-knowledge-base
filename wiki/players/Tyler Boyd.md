# Tyler Boyd

> ⚠️ Takes below are from April 2024 — treat as historical context until
> superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) on *Reception Perception: The Show* ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-11]]), buy/sell on a reported link to Kansas City: Harmon says the "Tyler Boyd experiment outside" doesn't work, especially at this stage of his career, and doesn't love him as a fourth Chiefs slot option given the room is already getting "too slotty." Both hosts call him a career slot receiver, somewhat overrated historically, "a fine number three," and a player they view as declining. Harmon is more receptive to a reported Steelers fit, since Boyd could play off the ball there without being asked to command a large target share behind [[George Pickens]].
- 2024-06-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-20]]): signed in Tennessee with familiarity to new playcaller Brian Callahan from Cincinnati, projected to play primarily slot; Harmon calls him 'definitely a declining player' but still 'a reasonable slot receiver' in this offense.
- 2024-07-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-11]]): Boyd is a capable slot receiver whose best days are 'probably behind him' but not 'a needle mover' for Tennessee's WR room, sitting clearly behind [[Calvin Ridley]] and [[DeAndre Hopkins]] on the depth chart, with Kyle Phillips as the backup slot option.
- 2024-10-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-29]]): Boyd cannot be counted on to absorb the vacated Tennessee targets — Harmon says he 'wasn't a needle mover in his prime, and now he's a declined version of that player.' He notes the Titans are down to five active receivers ([[Calvin Ridley]], Boyd, [[Nick Westbrook-Ikhine]] and two unknowns) with [[Treylon Burks]] unavailable, and criticizes the team for having no young developmental prospect on the roster worth evaluating over the back half of a lost season.
