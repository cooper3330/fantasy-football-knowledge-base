# Jonathan Taylor

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-07-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Best Ball]: Waldman says Taylor 'absolutely could' regain his RB1-overall form because [[Anthony Richardson]]'s rushing threat should force defenses to spread attention, opening creases that could make Taylor 'the most efficient running back in football' and produce big games. The caveat: red-zone touchdown equity could be at risk if Indianapolis starts vulturing goal-line carries with Richardson the way Philadelphia has with Jalen Hurts and Buffalo has with Josh Allen, which would cap Taylor's ceiling even in an efficient season. Waldman ranks him ahead of [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] but behind [[Breece Hall]] and [[Bijan Robinson]] in this group of RB1-overall candidates.
- 2024-09-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-30]]): co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) reported Taylor suffered another high ankle sprain, described as mild, ahead of a Jaguars matchup, and voiced doubt that high ankle sprains are ever mild. Waldman did not dispute the injury framing and moved straight to the replacement question, endorsing [[Trey Sermon]] and [[Tyler Goodson]] as low-end stopgaps in the Jacksonville matchup rather than arguing for a quick Taylor return.
- 2024-12-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-23]]): he 'feels it' that Taylor is as talented as [[Saquon Barkley]] — just as fast, more powerful, better at playing both in and out of structure, a very good receiver, and a better processor of information coming into the league (which Waldman thinks is still true). Barkley is the more creative runner and the better downfield receiving option, but Waldman notes downfield receiving for a back only shows up once every several games. The difference in output, he argues, is line play: Taylor simply hasn't had as good a line, and give him a crease and he is the equal. He concedes that if you argue purely from box-score stats, Barkley is the easy answer — and pre-empts the 'you're an idiot' replies. Format-neutral talent take, relevant to [[Dynasty]] valuation gaps created by team context.
- 2025-06-13 — According to co-host James Koh ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-06-13]]; Koh is not a tracked expert): Taylor is the forgotten man in every Colts conversation — the discussion is always the receivers, the quarterback situation and the head coach — and 'on a legitimate team could legitimately run for 2,000 yards'. [[Matt Harmon]] agreed the Colts will likely be run-heavy, expecting a lot of RPOs and quarterback run game with [[Daniel Jones]] under center, much as they would have been with [[Anthony Richardson]].
- 2025-08-29 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-29]]): guest Jeff Bell of Football Guys (not tracked) rates Taylor a 1 out of 10 at ADP 19 — a back who could rush for 2,000 yards, and the [[Daniel Jones]] situation cuts both ways: 'don't let Daniel Jones throw the ball, just give it to Jonathan Taylor', the same logic as Philadelphia's. Harris rates him a 4 on availability alone, noting Taylor seems to miss time every year and gets hurt more consistently than most, and countering Bell's QB argument with the years [[Saquon Barkley]] spent below his peak with Jones. Harris still allows Bell's case, since Taylor was the overall 1.01 and the number one fantasy player two years prior.
