# Sterling Shepard

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-10-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-15]]): Shepard has a legitimate role in Tampa Bay, having supplanted rookie third-round pick [[Jalen McMillan]] as the flanker — notable to Harmon because Shepard gets profiled as a small slot guy but he always thought Shepard could play outside. He will never be a big target-getter, and he made a couple of avoidable mistakes against the Saints, but he is a real third receiver used in motion to create leverage for [[Chris Godwin]] and [[Mike Evans]]. Harmon frames it as a story of survival after a string of injuries that could easily have ended his career, reunited with his college quarterback [[Baker Mayfield]]; Koh (not tracked) echoes the storyline angle. No fantasy endorsement was attached beyond the role note.
- 2024-10-24 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-24]]): with [[Chris Godwin]] out and [[Mike Evans]] hurt, Harmon agreed with co-host James Koh (not tracked) that Shepard is the best receiver left on Tampa Bay's roster and should be the most-targeted of the group. Koh pushed the take hardest, saying Shepard has 'straight up outplayed' [[Jalen McMillan]] and that he still sees the shake and juice when he watches him. Koh dug out Shepard's 2019 Reception Perception scores — 75.7% success rate versus man, an 88th-percentile mark at the time, plus a 94th-percentile score versus press — with Harmon noting those are pre-COVID numbers but that you do not post them without real skill. Shepard ran 56%, 57.7% and 72.5% of dropbacks in weeks four to six before the job briefly went back to McMillan. The caveats: he is old, chronically injured and cannot be counted on to stay healthy, and he is not an outside X at this point, which leaves Tampa without an X entirely. Harmon noted his [[Baker Mayfield]] chemistry dating to Oklahoma.
- 2025-09-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-23]]): with [[Mike Evans]] hurt, it is 'Sterling Shepard time' in Tampa Bay — which Harmon joked probably nobody except him, [[Baker Mayfield]] and Shepard himself finds exciting. He then took a victory lap on it: ten years into a career full of injuries, at 32 years old, Shepard was making clutch plays late in a game for a 3-0 team, and Harmon said he is taking the W on his long-running Shepard advocacy. Harmon sees him as a real part of why the Buccaneers still have usable depth until [[Chris Godwin]] returns, though he framed the role as a complementary slot piece alongside [[Emeka Egbuka]] rather than a volume play.
- 2025-09-23 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-23]]): Shepard had some important plays against the Jets and stands to get a little more spotlight with [[Mike Evans]]'s hamstring and [[Chris Godwin]] still out. In the waiver segment Harris listed him as an honorable mention with an explicit one-week frame: 'if you get no Godwin and no Evans, Sterling Shepard, I mean, for one week, I would use Sterling Shepard.' Not a durable add in his view — a contingency play.
- 2025-10-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-16]]): with Tampa Bay's receiver room decimated (Egbuka hurt, Godwin re-injured, Evans still working back), Harmon pointed to [[Sterling Shepard]] as a reliable slot option who 'has played well this year' and 'makes clutch plays,' getting first downs winning both breaking inside and outside.
- 2025-10-20 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: if [[Emeka Egbuka]] is ruled out for Monday Night Football, Harris named Shepard as his preferred fallback option in Tampa's receiving corps, while acknowledging it's 'real tough to be definitive' at that depth of the lineup.
