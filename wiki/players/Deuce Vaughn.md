# Deuce Vaughn

## Expert Takes

- 2024-05-20 — According to guest Jagger May (Football Guys, untracked) and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-20]]) [Dynasty]: "fuck it hard" on Deuce Vaughn as a 2024 fantasy factor. May argued he had all of last season to carve out a role and didn't, and Dallas's only backfield change is swapping Tony Pollard for an older Ezekiel Elliott — who May expects to vulture around 10 touchdowns — with Rico Dowdle handling early-down work behind him; Vaughn only sees the field in blowouts either direction, neither a stable path to volume. Waldman agreed, projecting Vaughn as a 3-5 touch player per game that fantasy managers will hold too long, valuable only if there's an injury ahead of him. He noted Vaughn was a genuinely strong pure runner at Kansas State, but speculated the team wants to see more from him in pass protection and route-running before trusting him with bigger snaps.
