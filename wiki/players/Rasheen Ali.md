# Rasheen Ali

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his
> name as "Rasheena Lee"; normalized here to the real name of the Marshall
> running back, Rasheen Ali.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo's second mid-to-late-pick, viable-committee-contributor name. Was a genuinely explosive, impactful freshman at Marshall, tore his ACL, and came back playing at largely the same level. Graded as the best running back at the 2024 Senior Bowl for the short time he was on the field there, alongside [[Dylan Laube]]. Archetype comp: a faster, lighter version of Salvon Ahmed (not tracked) if he stays healthy — real play speed paired with genuine positional instincts. [[Matt Waldman]]'s stylistic comp, same conversation: a smaller Jauan Jennings (not tracked) — tackle-breaking and contact balance on glancing shots that looks like it belongs on a bigger frame than his likely sub-210-lb listed weight. Both hosts flag ball security as the swing risk on him, treating it as a genuinely unpredictable trait to project rather than a fixed one — see [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]] for the fuller discussion.
- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast: reaffirms Ali's good leg protection (high knees/feet against low shots) and his stride/pacing control to set up blocks or take away pursuit angles, but flags a specific technical flaw — he leans too much on jump stops when opening his hips into 90-degree turns, and loses his balance and falls before completing the cutback or bounce when he tries to combine a jump stop with a line-of-scrimmage bounce.

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-11 (Going Deep - Fave Evals, Coach-Killer Candidates, and High-End Career Outcomes)]]): the ruptured bicep tendon injury will cost him real draft capital — "half a round plus" — despite still being "a really good back." Same episode, [[Matt Waldman]] names Ali one of his two favorite off-radar prospects in the class: his freshman-year tape is "some of the best in this class," and he's a shifty, explosive mover in the mold of a same-school comp the transcript audio doesn't render clearly enough to identify with confidence.
- 2024-07-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-22]]): of the backs behind [[Derrick Henry]], Ali has the most upside to be the short-term load carrier if Henry is unavailable. On tape Waldman sees a hard, determined runner with strong contact balance who is powerful and frenetic enough that, if you did not know he was only 200 pounds, there are moments he resembles Adrian Peterson stylistically; he plays faster than he tested and has produced breakaway runs, catches the ball and makes people miss. The risks are a tricep or bicep tear at the Senior Bowl — from which Waldman thinks he is essentially ready — and real fumbling problems, which he flags as less predictable to fix. Keaton Mitchell has no timetable off his ACL. Waldman wants to see more of Ali in camp.
