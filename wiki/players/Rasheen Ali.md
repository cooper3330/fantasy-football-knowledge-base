# Rasheen Ali

> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context

> running back, Rasheen Ali.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo's second mid-to-late-pick, viable-committee-contributor name. Was a genuinely explosive, impactful freshman at Marshall, tore his ACL, and came back playing at largely the same level. Graded as the best running back at the 2024 Senior Bowl for the short time he was on the field there, alongside [[Dylan Laube]]. Archetype comp: a faster, lighter version of Salvon Ahmed (not tracked) if he stays healthy — real play speed paired with genuine positional instincts. [[Matt Waldman]]'s stylistic comp, same conversation: a smaller Jauan Jennings (not tracked) — tackle-breaking and contact balance on glancing shots that looks like it belongs on a bigger frame than his likely sub-210-lb listed weight. Both hosts flag ball security as the swing risk on him, treating it as a genuinely unpredictable trait to project rather than a fixed one — see [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]] for the fuller discussion.
- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast: reaffirms Ali's good leg protection (high knees/feet against low shots) and his stride/pacing control to set up blocks or take away pursuit angles, but flags a specific technical flaw — he leans too much on jump stops when opening his hips into 90-degree turns, and loses his balance and falls before completing the cutback or bounce when he tries to combine a jump stop with a line-of-scrimmage bounce.
