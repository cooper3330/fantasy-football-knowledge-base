# Dalton Schultz

> ⚠️ Takes below are from April 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-04-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-08]]) [Best Ball]: expects Schultz's best-ball ADP to get driven down alongside [[Nico Collins]] and [[Tank Dell]] as [[C.J. Stroud]]'s price rises after the [[Stefon Diggs]] trade — but says that price drop "should not" happen, since Schultz still has a solid enough role in the Houston passing game to be fine. Uncertain whether he's ever been a great best-ball play (unclear if he offers real spike weeks), but comfortable with him in redraft.
- 2024-10-10 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-10]]): a hidden beneficiary of the [[Nico Collins]] absence — roughly five weeks without Collins, with [[Joe Mixon]] back from his high ankle in about two of those. Angelo expects Schultz to take a bigger target share, citing the precedent in Dallas when [[Amari Cooper]] got hurt and Schultz stepped up in production alongside [[CeeDee Lamb]]. The supporting argument is that Schultz is a steady veteran presence [[C.J. Stroud]] can rely on while Houston leans more on the run game and ball-control football, since the defense and pass rush are among the best in the league.
- 2025-02-04 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-04]]: given Nick Caley's history as a tight end coach with the Patriots and Rams, and given how well paid Schultz is, Koh expects Schultz to be more heavily utilized in the Houston passing game in 2025. [[Matt Harmon]] adds the structural reason a tight end coach matters here: the tight end has hands in both departments — an active participant in building the run identity alongside the offensive line coach, and the safety-blanket answer to pressure in the pass game — and Houston had no answer to pressure last season. Neither host puts a ranking or target projection on Schultz.
