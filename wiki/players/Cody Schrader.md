## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo's pick for a back who can make an NFL roster as a late-round selection and develop into a reliable reserve. Leans on Senior Bowl week plus SEC production — roughly 1,800 total yards and 14 touchdowns, a total Angelo says very few backs in the country matched. Explicit grade: probably not a star, but someone who can be a long-term contributor and a reliable asset to a run game given his style. [[Matt Waldman]], same episode, agrees — calls him a smart, determined runner who profiles well strictly as an NFL role player.
- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast: reaffirms the role-player grade — promising blocker, reliable underneath-zone receiver/route runner, strong ball-security track record. Consistency plus special-teams value could make him "a valued part of an NFL roster"; better-than-estimated explosion could push him to "more than good enough" for the league, but he's likely too limited to start outside of an injury-created opportunity.
