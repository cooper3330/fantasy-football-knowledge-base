# Leonard Fournette

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Dismissing a report (relayed via Adam Schefter, sourced to Fournette's agents rather than Fournette himself) that Leonard Fournette is ready to contribute, Waldman said to monitor him as a potential 'next Latavius Murray' -- an injury-replacement option who could become startable -- contingent on his conditioning, which has historically been inconsistent for him. He credited peak Fournette (roughly LSU through his early Jaguars years) with a rare old-school trait of accelerating through and dropping his pads into contact, but said durability, mostly foot-related, has been the recurring issue, and he's not investing until there's tangible proof of a role.
- 2025-10-09 — According to Adam Harstad (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman]]'s show ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-09]]) [Dynasty]: Fournette is his favourite illustration of holding an undervalued asset through a rebuild. When Jacksonville cut him and he signed in Tampa, the market treated him as finished; Harstad judged him slightly too old to hold through a rebuild but saw that nobody would give anything at all for him, that he was a former first-round pick who had finished top-12 in each of his first three seasons, and that the underlying fundamentals were intact. He held, Fournette posted another top-12 finish with the Buccaneers and became 'playoff Lenny,' and Harstad calls it a very good decision — the point being that a rebuild gains value only by stockpiling players the market has mispriced, whether or not they fit the timeline.
