# Tanner McKee

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-01-13 — According to Matt Harmon ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-13]]): flagged as a name Philadelphia could look to trade this offseason given the thin market of available starting quarterbacks league-wide, or alternatively keep as [[Jalen Hurts]]'s backup if the Eagles want more competition behind him.
- 2026-10-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-16]]): McKee will start for Philadelphia with [[Jalen Hurts]] out due to a blood clot, stepping into an Eagles offense that 'has been just awful, very very very very bad to watch' even with Hurts under center. Harris says he is not excited about the 'Tanner McKee experience,' though he allows one game could change his view.
- 2026-10-19 — According to Bob Harris (untracked) and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-10-19]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Jalen Hurts]] out for the season, McKee takes over as Eagles starter. Bob argued McKee may be better than people assume given how close the camp competition with Andy Dalton (who signed with the Falcons that same day) reportedly was, suggesting the staff 'know more than we do.' Waldman's verdict was 'fuck it' for fantasy purposes -- not a knock on McKee specifically but on the supporting cast, saying he doesn't trust the receiver corps, the offensive line or the running backs, calling it 'too banged up of a situation.' He still sees promise in McKee's game to develop into a competent 'Andy Dalton-like' or '[[Tyler Huntley]]-type' backup capable of holding down a lineup if called on.
- 2026-10-19 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-19]]): in his first start with [[Jalen Hurts]] on IR, McKee 'very much did not look ready for his close-up.' His first drop-back saw two unblocked defenders free, resulting in an 11-yard sack; his first actual throw was overthrown in front of [[Makai Lemon]] and deflected into an easy linebacker interception that was dropped. Harris said McKee was 'predetermining some of his reads and just throwing it heedless of coverage,' calling him outright 'a flag player' in this one. Ross Tucker, calling the game, noted Carolina blitzed McKee on every snap because they simply didn't fear him. Harris's one caveat: 'he's a kid, he can get better because sometimes they do,' but the final numbers flattered a rough performance.
- 2026-10-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-10-20]]): with [[Jalen Hurts]] out for the season, [[Tanner McKee]] takes over as Philadelphia's starter. Harmon called him 'a totally qualified NFL backup quarterback' who is 'functional' and capable of pushing the ball downfield, noting [[DeVonta Smith]] immediately became his clear top target (41% targets-per-route in his first start). Whether McKee proves to be more than a replacement-level stopgap is, in Harmon's words, 'worth asking' and something to track the rest of the season given how banged-up the Eagles' receiving corps already is.
- 2026-10-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-10-22 (Going Deep Eagles & 2027 QB Class)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Angelo is lukewarm on McKee filling in for Hurts, calling him merely 'fine' -- adequate but without the game-changing qualities Hurts brought -- and says he'd rather see the Eagles bring in veteran journeyman arms like [[Joe Milton III]] for pure entertainment value down the stretch.
- 2026-10-27 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-27]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: McKee strung together two long scoring drives against a penalty-prone, poor Dallas defense and briefly gave Philly a 21-20 third-quarter lead, but Harris describes him as 'a bad combination of doesn't really want to take chances and then when he does, kind of doesn't know what he's doing.' After the go-ahead score the offense went punt, interception, turnover on downs. With the Eagles facing the Commanders next and the receiving corps battered ([[Saquon Barkley]], [[Dameon Pierce]] both hurt), Harris says he is 'hard pressed to imagine running out there to start any Philly players' going into Week 8.
- 2026-11-03 — According to James Koh (untracked co-host) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-11-03]]) [Dynasty]: Koh argued McKee's unusually long windup, combined with slow pre-throw processing, is throwing off the timing of Eagles pass-catchers -- receivers get their hands up early expecting the ball and it arrives late, contributing to drops from [[Makai Lemon]], Darius Cooper, Dameon Pierce and Elijah Mitchell since McKee's Week 6 first start. [[Matt Harmon]] agreed there's 'something to' the theory, noting McKee's time-to-throw is relatively quick but everything before the release is slow. Both hosts said McKee's checkdown-heavy quick game has denied Lemon the big-breaking corner/out/comeback routes that were his best trait as a prospect, and that backup Joe Milton -- while not a real answer -- would at least push the ball downfield more aggressively if forced into action.
