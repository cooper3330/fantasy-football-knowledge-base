# T.J. Harden

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-02-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-16]]) [Dynasty]: Harden is built like Arian Foster or Eddie George, and Waldman likes him as a receiving weapon out of the backfield -- a decent route runner who can leak out on screens, get up the seam and up the numbers off play-action/misdirection, and track the ball well downfield. He's also big and strong enough to carry a real workload as a between-the-tackles 'B-back' without needing elite timed speed, breaks a lot of tackles, and has good footwork. He had success at UCLA before transferring to an ascending SMU program. The open question for Waldman is translatable explosiveness -- he's intrigued but wants to see how it shows up against NFL-level competition.
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman praised Harden as an agile, ball-protecting 'bulldozer' with dynamic jump cuts and spins who decelerates efficiently and often turns losses into gains -- with only two fumbles on nearly 600 career touches. He contrasted him with poor-technique power backs, saying 'this was not like rookie year Adrian Peterson' in terms of ball security in traffic.
