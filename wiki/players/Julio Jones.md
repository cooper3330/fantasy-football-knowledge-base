# Julio Jones

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-04-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-04-08]]): on Jones's retirement, a Hall of Famer and one of the best to ever do it — 13 seasons, seven Pro Bowls, sixth overall in 2011. Harmon calls the 2016 season his apex rather than the gaudier 2015 (136 catches, 1,871 yards, third-most in NFL history at the time): in 2016 he was the focal point of the league's best offense (8.2 net yards per attempt, a huge gap over Washington at 7.6) as the true X in Kyle Shanahan's route tree, posting a career-best 86.2% success rate vs zone — above the 95th percentile, in the same bucket as Stefon Diggs (86.8%) and Davante Adams (86.8%) — plus 76.3% vs man. His 2016 route tree was almost all big-boy in-breakers: 0.8% screens, 9.3% slants (82% success), 14.9% digs (78.4%), 10.8% posts (74%). James Koh (co-host, not a tracked expert) noted the 2014-2019 prime averaged 104 catches, 1,565 yards and 9.7 yards per target but only about six touchdowns a season, faulting Matt Ryan and the coordinators for not force-feeding him near the goal line. Harmon adds that injuries later dulled his play, and that landing next to Roddy White and Tony Gonzalez meant he never had to be the man from day one — unlike [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] in 2024.
