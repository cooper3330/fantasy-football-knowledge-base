# Jacoby Brissette

- 2025-10-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-14]]): Harmon argued Arizona's passing offense actually looked better statistically with Brissette under center than with [[Kyler Murray]], citing an Establish the Run (Pat Thorman) stat that Brissette produced more passing yards in this game than Murray has averaged since 2022. Brissette pushed the ball downfield more consistently than Murray has, including the deep in-breaker to [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] before Harrison's concussion. The caveat: Brissette collapses completely under pressure behind a banged-up offensive line, whereas Murray can extend plays with his legs even under duress -- so Harmon isn't calling Brissette clearly superior overall, just noting the sample raises real questions about whether Murray unlocks these receivers any better than an average game manager would.
- 2026-10-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-08]]): Discussing Week 5 streamers with guest Scott Pianowski (Yahoo Fantasy, not a tracked expert), Harris called the Lions matchup for Jacoby Brissette 'divine' - every Detroit game turns into a shootout given the Lions' injury-riddled, scandal-hit secondary, so streaming the Cardinals QB against them is close to a layup even though Arizona loses almost every game Brissette starts. Harris said he would play Brissette over Jordan Love without hesitation given how bad the Packers offense looks, and framed the decision as a 'heuristics' question - which would you regret more, benching a struggling Joe Burrow for Brissette, or sticking with Burrow while Brissette goes off? Pianowski had Brissette at QB6 earlier in the week but had dropped him to QB8 by show time, still ahead of Jordan Love and Drake Maye, with only Burrow and Brock Purdy jumping ahead of him. Both agreed Arizona will still score 20+ points regardless of who starts at QB.
