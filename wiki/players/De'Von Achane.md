## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and guest **Daniel Harms** (not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-15]]) [Dynasty]: both flag him as a real overrated-in-2024 risk despite a spectacular rookie efficiency profile — "we haven't seen enough from him as a full-time player" to trust the per-touch numbers holding at volume. Harms's mechanism: he's excellent getting to the edge in space in Mike McDaniel's Shanahan-tree scheme, but is a merely good (not great) between-the-tackles runner, and defenses that take away the edge (as Kansas City's run defense did in the frigid Wild Card game, after doing the same to other backs in Germany earlier in the season) neutralize him; comps him to a boom/bust, breakaway-speed-plus-checkdown-volume Travis Etienne 2023 profile rather than a true three-down bell cow. Already battled three separate injuries as a rookie, a durability flag layered on top of the touch-competition question if Miami's other backs (Raheem Mostert, Jeff Wilson) stay involved. Verdict: fine value in the 4th–5th round range, dangerous priced as a top-3-to-top-5 dynasty running back, which both say they've already seen happen in early market chatter. Christopher Brooks flagged as a depth name worth a late-round dynasty look as Miami's Gus Edwards-style contingency if Mostert/Wilson age out.
