# Laquan Treadwell

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2026-09-21 — According to Bob Harris (not tracked) and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-09-21 (Feel It or Fk It)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Alec Pierce]] re-aggravating the injury that cost him the offseason, the hosts discussed whether Treadwell could fill the void. Waldman said he's a fine desperation streamer in a 20-to-25-roster-spot deep league -- he got open on a deep post and made the catch, ran a solid slant, and offers some physicality and after-catch ability as a long-tenured veteran -- but flagged a history of drops and said in anything shallower than a very deep league the answer is 'fuck it, no.'
- 2026-10-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-10-15]]): Harmon called it 'pretty depressing' watching a struggling Daniel Jones keep forcing the ball to Treadwell, who has run the most routes on the team over the last two weeks (111, ahead of Josh Downs' 77 and Tyler Warren's 70) while converting only 19% of those routes into targets -- the volume isn't translating into production, and Harmon framed him as the team's sacrificial X. Harmon doesn't think Jones is playing well enough for another receiver addition to meaningfully help Indianapolis, even with a healthy [[Alec Pierce]] ('a baller'), and expects GM Chris Ballard -- on the hot seat -- to be tempted into a desperation move that ownership may resist given last year's since-regretted Sauce Gardner/Ad Mitchell trade that cost two first-round picks.
