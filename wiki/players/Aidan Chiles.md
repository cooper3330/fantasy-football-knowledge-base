# Aidan Chiles

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-10-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-23]]) [Dynasty]: Aidan Chiles is a very good dual-threat quarterback paired with a very good offensive play caller in Jonathan Smith, who coached him at Oregon State before both moved to Michigan State (TE Jack Velling followed too). Waldman had moved Chiles DOWN in his rankings preseason — not on the player, but on the situation: Michigan State's receiving corps looked dire and he could not answer who Chiles would throw to. The emergence of true freshman boundary receiver [[Nick Marsh]] as his No. 1 for the foreseeable future materially improves that outlook. Waldman and co-host Felix Sharpe also flag Chiles as another example of a lower-rated recruit who outgrew his ranking, alongside [[Ashton Jeanty]] and [[Quinshon Judkins]]. Caveat: this is still a rebuilding program.
