# Emari Demercado

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-09-26 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-26]]): Demercado injected life into Arizona's late drive and scored the tying touchdown while [[Trey Benson]] sat out the entire final possession. The game established a baseline pecking order in which you are mostly going to get Demercado on third downs — he played 12 third-down snaps to Benson's two and [[Michael Carter]]'s one — and Harris doesn't expect to see much Benson in the two-minute drill either.
- 2025-09-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-29]]): Demercado was a good preemptive add and can keep the third-down work — he was the back on the field in the fourth quarter with the game on the line, not [[Trey Benson]], and Arizona relied on him as a receiver. Waldman says Benson has the higher ceiling on athletic ability and the staff likes what he can do, but Benson still has ups and downs and is not as patient setting up blocks as he should be, while the coaches feel better about Demercado in the passing game and especially in pass protection and as an outlet receiver. Demercado is consistent at getting the yards as designed with good enough speed, and coaches like knowing what they will get, even if he is less creative than Benson. Waldman's advice: if he is on your waiver wire and you roster Benson or have a hurt back, grab him — he will get enough touches to warrant a start. Bob Harris (not tracked) started him as an emergency [[James Conner]] replacement and won because of it.
- 2025-10-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Trey Benson]] on IR, anyone claiming to know whether Demercado, [[Michael Carter]] or Zonovan Knight is the Arizona starter is 'reading tea leaves and goat entrails' — Harris expects the Cardinals themselves to be guessing and to open with a mixture against the Titans before adjusting. He ranked Demercado and Carter side by side in 'pure emergency territory,' saying adding both to all rosters makes sense purely because the outcome is unknown, not because either has earned it. Demercado, in his third year, started a couple of games as a rookie without the staff loving him, and Harris has mostly seen a third-down back and okay pass catcher; a Carter-early-downs, Demercado-third-downs split is plausible, though Carter has also been a third-down back.
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the third-down back stayed the third-down back. His first touch was a give-up run with 30 seconds left in the first half; his second was the third-and-one on Arizona's own 28 where the seas parted for what was going to be a long touchdown, and he slowed down to celebrate and had the ball knocked away from behind by a hustling Titans defender before crossing the goal line — a play Harris says cost him a fantasy game he had started Demercado in for lack of better options. He played more snaps afterwards, but 'certainly neither you nor I want to use him.' Harris uses it to argue nobody can legislate usage in advance: pre-game reporting had Demercado keeping his role and [[Michael Carter]] noncommittal, and the split still wasn't close.
- 2025-10-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-14 (Week 7 Waiver Wire & More W6 Film)]]): Demercado got the first goal-line carry of the Colts game before leaving with an ankle injury on a special-teams play, which turned the Cardinals' three-way backfield split into a two-way [[Michael Carter]]/[[Bam Knight]] timeshare. Harris notes Demercado is a bigger back than Carter but runs smaller than his size suggests.
- 2025-11-04 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-04]]): Demercado is Jeff Erickson's number three waiver add (Erickson of RotoWire is not a tracked expert) and Erickson calls the pick gross, hunting Mr. Right Now rather than Mr. Right in a bye-heavy week. Harris's film review found no pattern at all in Arizona's backfield: Demercado took the first back touch and had a 16-yard gain into the red zone plus a carry inside the 10, and led on third downs 8 to 5 and in touches 15 to 11 with a clock-killing series padding his line, but Zonovan Knight out-snapped him 37 to 26 and owned the goal line 3 to 0 inside the five. Erickson thinks he is better than Knight and hopes for ten carries and five catches, which Harris calls aggressive. Both flag the Week 10 trip to Seattle and one of the league's best run defenses; Harris vows the Cardinals backs will not look startable in his ranks and Erickson calls it an emergency break-glass add only.
- 2025-11-06 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-06]]): Harris ranks [[Zonovan Knight]] slightly higher but says he has little conviction, and agrees qualitatively that Demercado was the more 'dangerous'-looking back on Monday night. Harris re-watched the Arizona possessions and found Demercado's role expanding beyond the old third-down niche he held behind [[James Conner]] — he saw first-down snaps too — but the two-minute drill before halftime was all Knight, and inside the five it was three carries to zero in Knight's favor. Guest Ben Gretch (Stealing Signals, not tracked) prefers Demercado as the more explosive back, citing roughly a 60/40 snap split favoring Knight (about 57% to 40%) but a touch lead for Demercado around 15 to 11, plus prior long touchdown runs and a pass-leaning [[Jacoby Brissett]] offense that may leave green grass. Both flag Seattle's run defense as a bad matchup, and both warn against drawing conclusions from single plays. [[Trey Benson]] is expected out again.
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]): [[Emari Demercado]] played only about 10 snaps last week coming off a high-ankle sprain, with the game largely belonging to [[Michael Carter]]. Harris expects Demercado to work back into more of a two-man split with Carter in Arizona's backfield, which he said is manageable to roster together, unlike the prior three-man committee.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]): Demercado is a solid journeyman backup who can produce in a change-of-pace, pass-catching role, but isn't projected as the every-down back ahead of rookie [[Emmitt Johnson]] or [[Kenneth Walker III]].
