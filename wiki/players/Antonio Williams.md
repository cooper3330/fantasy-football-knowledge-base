# Antonio Williams

- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: one of his top two receivers for 2026 alongside [[Jordyn Tyson]], and 'really really interesting'. He tempers the production expectation because Clemson's receiver room is loaded — Angelo puts him nearer 900-1,000 yards than 1,200-1,300 — and reads him as primarily a slot receiver who can play outside, with great suddenness out of his breaks, real after-catch ability and value as a crafty punt returner on special teams. He also lists Williams among the day-two-or-better NFL receivers catching passes from [[Cade Klubnik]].
