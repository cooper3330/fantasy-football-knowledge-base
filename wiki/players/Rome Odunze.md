# Rome Odunze

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024, pre-draft. Team/role are as of that
> date and are almost certainly stale — treat as historical pre-draft
> scouting context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]): an early (3-game, explicitly non-final) Reception Perception look, and the biggest surprise of the trio charted alongside [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] and [[Malik Nabers]] — "I don't think I have a weakness for him through two, three games." Estimated 6'3"/215 lb. Elite off the line against heavy press coverage, a "smooth separator," and — despite a reputation for winning a lot of contested catches — no real separation concerns on tape, which Harmon attributes to quarterback Michael Penix Jr. trusting him in contested spots rather than to a lack of true separation. Plays all three receiver alignments (X/flanker/slot) plus full-speed pre-snap motion. NFL Draft analyst Daniel Jeremiah (not a tracked expert) independently gave Odunze the same grade as Harrison Jr., which Harmon says he "can see why" after charting him himself. Not yet ranked among the three prospects by either host — "I don't know if I will have him ranked number one... but I see why he would be" — but all three are treated as tier-one, top-10-worthy prospects if the early numbers hold.
