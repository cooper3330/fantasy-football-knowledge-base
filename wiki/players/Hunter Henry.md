# Hunter Henry

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-10-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-03]]): Henry is guest Josh Van Blank's number four trade-away (Van Blank is Harris's cousin and NOT a tracked expert), flagged as 'found money' at TE9 and 96th overall on FantasyPros — above [[Jordan Addison]] and [[Zach Charbonnet]]. Van Blank's ask is modest and explicit: he would take [[Bhayshul Tuten]] or [[Matthew Golden]] (Golden currently on a bye and yet to do much) over Henry, and says a national TV game might let you wait a week for another end-zone trip to 'really fool someone.' Harris is more skeptical that a buyer exists at all — 'I can't imagine who's paying for Hunter Henry' — and says it's only reasonable if you find someone who hasn't caught up with the tight end times. Both hosts say they care about roughly four tight ends and joke about petitioning to eliminate the position.
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-06]]): Henry 'played good enough to be in your lineup' against Buffalo, catching two deep seam balls including one on the first play of the fourth quarter where he got popped and held on. Harris frames it as tight end being 'a stupid position' where Henry can be 'a nominee to be your guy,' and explicitly ranks him ahead of [[Dalton Kincaid]] right now because Kincaid's production is so week-to-week unpredictable — though both belong in starting consideration.
- 2025-10-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-09]]): he would still start Henry over [[Mason Taylor]] this week purely on quality of targets -- Henry is 'that kind of Mason-Dixon line between good and not so good,' the model of a competent NFL starting tight end and a security blanket. The qualifier is his read on [[Drake Maye]]: good, but not unbelievable, and 'I just don't think that Drake Maye is seeing the field quite as well as people want to say,' which is also why he prefers [[Luther Burden III]] to [[Kayshon Boutte]], describing Boutte as the guy who is wide open and not seen while Maye focuses on Henry, [[Stefon Diggs]] and the run game. [[Chris Harris]] countered that within a month we may be asking why anyone starts Henry over Taylor.
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-10-27]]) [Redraft]: scored on a bootleg red-zone touchdown as New England's second-half comeback against Cleveland got going.
- 2025-11-20 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-20]]) [Best Ball]: Harris rostered Henry at $3,900 in DFS against Cincinnati, arguing the Bengals defense regularly gives up production to tight ends and there's a real chance Henry pops -- while noting Austin Hooper's potential return from a concussion for Cincinnati is a mild concern, and joking he can barely remember the last time Henry did anything, expecting to eat those words after this game. Guest Jake Trowbridge (untracked) countered by spending up on Mark Andrews instead at only $100 cheaper ($3,800), saying he prefers to 'pay for' his tight ends.
- 2025-11-24 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris admitted he had 'egg on his face' for recently saying Henry hasn't been good enough to start, after Henry topped 100 receiving yards against Cincinnati with a second touchdown wiped out by a questionable offensive pass interference call. The catch: Cincinnati simply wasn't covering New England's tight ends at all (Henry and Austin Hooper combined for more than half of Drake Maye's yardage), so Harris was skeptical this specific exploit repeats against other defenses going forward, even though he 'can't prove it won't.'
- 2026-07-17 — According to Patrick Doherty (Rotoworld, not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-17]]): Doherty says he won't buy any hype on Henry as a Drake Maye pass-catching riser -- 'he is what he is,' a fine, probably startable tight end in deeper leagues, but not an exciting summer storyline.
- 2026-08-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-28]]): Henry just signed a two-year extension with very little of the money guaranteed -- a pattern Harris notes Patriots fans have seen before. He remains the trusted, established [[Drake Maye]] weapon for now, leaving no near-term fantasy room for rookie [[Eli Raridon]], though Harris flags Henry's age (mid-30s) as a reason that could change if he starts to slip.
