# Jimmy Horn Jr.

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-06-05 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-05]]): Angelo's number one pick from the minicamp-buzz list — his quickness jumps off the page and there is no other receiver like him in the Carolina room ([[Hunter Renfrow]] is a good mentor but, in Angelo's view, no longer a starting-caliber receiver). Angelo projects a slot or Z role by season's end, with [[Tetairoa McMillan]] outside and [[Jalen Coker]] in the slot at times, calling that the scariest three the Panthers can field, and says Horn taking vertical pressure off McMillan would also help [[Bryce Young]]. Waldman makes him his third guy: the things that lowered Horn's RSP grade were a want of patience and suddenness in routes, but he allows that a receiver adjusting to a quarterback who did not throw timing routes well may have abandoned that on purpose. Waldman also notes some clap-attacking at the catch point that can be straightened out.
- 2025-07-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: asked whether Horn is getting too much early buzz, Waldman said no — keep an eye on him, mainly because Carolina has nobody else like him: a potential big-play slot who could also play flanker and win outside. The open questions are whether he can read coverage, get on the same page with [[Bryce Young]], and beat top corners in man, which is what an impactful role requires. His hamstring injury from the spring appears healed and he is practicing with Young. Waldman was skeptical of Thomas Davis comparing Horn to a [[Tyreek Hill]]-type factor — he would feel better about that if Davis had been a cornerback — but noted the competition is soft: [[Xavier Legette]] 'who can't catch' and [[Jalen Coker]], who can catch but may not get deep, with Bob Harris (not tracked) asking whether Horn can even beat out [[Hunter Renfrow]].
- 2025-10-16 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-16]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Angelo said he's liked watching Horn and thinks Carolina should play him more in 11 personnel, envisioning a package with Coker bumping inside to the slot and Horn playing Z and motioning to keep defenses off balance.
- 2026-01-13 — According to Matt Harmon ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-13]]): flashed as a pre-snap motion piece for Carolina against the Rams, but Harmon noted he dropped a late pass that, had he caught it, might have changed the outcome of the game.
- 2026-05-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (untracked) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-05-05]]) [Dynasty]: while discussing Zachariah Branch's projected Atlanta role, the hosts pointed to Horn (sub-170-180 lbs, Panthers) as the model -- he lines up in the slot at the snap but cuts across the formation behind [[Tetairoa McMillan]] to create leverage for McMillan's in-breaking routes, a real, valuable NFL role even if it rarely shows up as Horn's own fantasy production.
