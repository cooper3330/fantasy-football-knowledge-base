# David Montgomery

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-21 — [[Brandon Angelo]] (co-host of *Going Deep*) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21 (Pacing and Control)]]: the comparison is with [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] and it is about range of outcomes, not quality. Detroit is "much more successful with Jahmyr Gibbs on the field — not because Gibbs is so much better a player than David Montgomery, but because defenses have to think a lot more when Gibbs is out there." His specific limit on Montgomery: he "very rarely will break a 25-plus-yard run," so the distribution of his carries is narrow, where Gibbs forces a defense to defend the whole range dynamically. Explicitly framed as a scheme-leverage point rather than a talent gap.
- 2023-12-21 — **Angelo**, same episode, the positive: names Montgomery among the players who define the Detroit locker room's culture — with [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], Jared Goff and [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] — an "unselfish brand of football" he credits for the Lions outperforming their talent. See [[Player Development and Coachability]].

**Fantasy read as of Dec 2023:** neither host makes a start/sit or ranking call
on Montgomery; the take is that he is the lower-variance half of a committee
whose upside sits with the other back.

- 2024-01-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-29]]): named, without a ranking split, as one half of a "dynamic running back duo" that "gives you all the pieces" — part of a broader case that Detroit is built for continued success beyond its 2023 coordinators, alongside [[Jahmyr Gibbs]], [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] and [[Sam LaPorta]].
- 2024-05-20 — According to guest Jagger May (Football Guys, untracked) and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-20]]) [Dynasty] [Redraft (Standard)]: May said he's "arguably still overdrafting" David Montgomery but defends it — the "data bros" dismiss him on spreadsheet explosive-run numbers, yet he smashed expectations after leaving Chicago for Detroit, ranking alongside [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] near the top of the league in explosive plays. Waldman built the touchdown-equity case: with a statue-in-the-pocket QB like [[Jared Goff]] who adds no rushing value, offenses without a running quarterback tend to lean on a two-back split — one big-play/receiving back, one goal-line/first-down grinder — and Montgomery fills the latter role. He added that Montgomery's value isn't speed, which he called the trait that makes NFL front offices and analytics evaluators "lose their mind and stop thinking," but rather quickness, processing, vision and getting exactly the needed yardage without chasing a home run. Both project him as a fringe top-20 running back for 2024 even as Gibbs's role grows.
- 2024-07-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Best Ball]: Discussing the Lions backfield split, Waldman defends Montgomery against fans who 'overvalue speed' and write him off as merely slow, arguing he's still good enough to hold a high floor as a top-3-to-5 back even as [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] earns more touches, though his ceiling remains lower than Gibbs's given the receiving-work gap between the two.
- 2024-11-14 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-14]]): Montgomery is Detroit's designated momentum-changer. Waldman says Dan Campbell's resiliency system depends on identifying who you go to when things are not going right — the player who gets the hard yards, shows the effort, excites teammates and does not give up — and on offense that is Montgomery, with [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] as the other name. This is a role-and-usage read rather than a volume projection: it explains why Montgomery keeps meaningful work in a backfield he shares with [[Jahmyr Gibbs]], because the offense deliberately returns to him in adverse game states.
- 2025-02-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-17]]) [Best Ball]: 'feel' Montgomery over [[Kaleb Johnson]] at Underdog RB20 — he is the better overall running back, nicknamed 'Knuckles', and Waldman argues Detroit specifically wants him as the one hitting the tree trunk and splintering it rather than [[Jahmyr Gibbs]]. Waldman also said he would take Montgomery over [[Omarion Hampton]]. Untracked co-host Bob Harris framed the pairing as recency bias: the last thing everyone saw was Gibbs running rampant while Montgomery was out five games with a knee injury, and Harris thinks the market is forgetting that Montgomery 'is there and isn't going anywhere'.
- 2025-05-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-26]]): Waldman is 'feeling that all day long' on [[David Montgomery]] at an RB24 price and calls him definitively overlooked, saying people are discounting him on age when he is still in the prime of his career, in an offense still in its prime with the line, quarterback, receivers and tight end all intact. He was banged up last year but Waldman does not treat that as predictive. Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked) supplies the numbers: 404 carries, 1,290 yards and 25 touchdowns plus 52 receptions for 458 yards over the last two seasons; 16 fantasy points per game last year, 14th in the league on a per-game basis in the games he played despite missing five with a knee injury. Waldman adds that Montgomery being available as an RB3 later is precisely the reason he might pass on [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] very early.
