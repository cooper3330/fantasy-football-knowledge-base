## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-08]]): has the arm strength and fundamentals to earn an NFL roster spot — surveys the field well, finds secondary options, and targets tight downfield coverage with good placement. The limiter is reading certain coverage types, specifically trail coverage, and diagnosing different forms of pressure; his overall accuracy is also mostly reliable only in the short-to-intermediate ranges of the field. Grade: a shot at earning playing time in a professional league, but not likely a long-term NFL roster spot right away — needs stints developing elsewhere first.
