- 2026-02-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-09]]) [Dynasty]: an off-the-radar name Waldman flags as worth monitoring -- 6'0', 215 pounds, with 'a little Le'Veon Bell going on' in his patient, press-and-cut style. He reads box counts and edge penetration well, sets up defenders with layered footwork, and gets his pads/feet high to avoid leg shots, which Waldman credits for excellent ball security (one fumble on 505 career touches). The concern is durability: seven games missed in 2023, a season-opener absence in 2024, and multiple leg injuries in 2025. If he stays healthy, Waldman thinks he 'could have a bearing on an NFL team's ground game.'
