# Kevin Coleman

## Expert Takes

- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Coleman's hands and ball security are his calling card -- technically sound, few lapses, no bad habits -- and after the catch he's a fast processor who dictates the chain of movement against defenders rather than reacting to them. Waldman projects him as a likely slot-first player who needs a good offensive pairing to produce weekly, but calls him underrated with real potential to develop into an every-down flanker if his route game keeps improving.
- 2026-04-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-02]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman described Kevin Coleman (Mississippi State to Missouri transfer) as a possible bargain, slot-heavy version of [[KC Concepcion]] -- very quick with good hands and some flanker upside. He framed Coleman's range as Jalen Nailor's floor (his pre-Las Vegas usage with the Vikings) to Jayden Reed's ceiling, saying Coleman is likely to land closer to the Reed outcome than the Nailor floor, making him a name worth stashing as a late-round dynasty dart throw.
- 2026-04-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-30 (Round 3 WRs)]]): Miami added Coleman on day three, and Harmon says he'll compete with Malik Washington for the slot role (Harmon still likes Washington too). Coleman's success rate vs. man was a modest 66%, but his best work came on quick flat routes and out routes from the slot, and he won 7 of his 10 contested targets. His standout trait is after-the-catch play: in space on 69% of his sampled catches, going down on first contact just 47% of the time and breaking multiple tackles on 18% of receptions -- Harmon calls him 'a very slippery guy in the open field.' He frames Miami's three third-day/round-three WR additions as complementary archetypes: Caleb Douglas as a sacrificial X, Chris Bell as the highest-upside big body who can move around, and Coleman as the traditional slot option.
- 2026-05-07 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-07 (Going Deep with Brandon Angelo)]]) [Dynasty]: Angelo has Coleman rated in the low 80s overall, well above where he's being drafted, and thinks his ceiling in a good ecosystem is a peak [[Zay Flowers]]-level player -- but he called Miami's current landing spot 'terrible,' noting [[Tutu Atwell]] is the most accomplished receiver on the roster ahead of him. He expects [[Malik Washington]] to finally get every-down starter usage with Coleman backing him up initially, with more over-routes and crossers if Miami leans into 12 personnel. Both hosts were critical of the incoming offensive staff's history of misusing skill talent, citing Malik Washington being miscast as primarily a screen-game piece, and of Mike McDaniel's rigid, role-based scheme philosophy.
- 2026-06-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman flagged Kevin Coleman as a name to watch off waivers behind [[Malik Washington]] in Miami's receiver room, calling him 'a good slot receiver' worth a look if [[Tutu Atwell]] isn't available.
