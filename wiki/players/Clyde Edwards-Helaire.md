# Clyde Edwards-Helaire

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-07-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-22]]): now cleared from the non-football injury list, Edwards-Helaire is worth taking at his near-free price and could be the [[Isiah Pacheco]] handcuff — he is a very good receiver out of the backfield and a smart runner — but Waldman sees him more in a Jerick McKinnon role than as a bell cow, because he is not a physical runner. He ties it to work he is doing at Footballguys on contact balance via 'process-driven analytics': he has seen only two or three backs perform like 1,000-yard rushers while unable to defeat indirect collisions and bounce out, and Edwards-Helaire was never that exception. He suggests Keaontay Ingram or Deneric Prince, neither being drafted, might be the better bet if Pacheco is hurt. Both hosts use him as the cautionary tale for hype: he once climbed to about pick six in ADP on Brian Westbrook comps, and Waldman says his RSP work on the Texas game showed the production hid a bad process — draft capital and scheme were great, 'but is he?'
- 2024-09-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Edwards-Helaire opened the season on the non-football injury list and will miss the first four games, reportedly while working through PTSD stemming from a self-defense incident dating back to 2018. Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris treated the news mainly for its knock-on effect in the Kansas City backfield — it elevates the meaning of [[Samaje Perine]]'s arrival rather than opening a lane for Edwards-Helaire himself. Neither host offered any path back to fantasy relevance for him this season; the segment moved immediately to whether Perine caps [[Isiah Pacheco]].
