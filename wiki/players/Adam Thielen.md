# Adam Thielen

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-14 — **Adam Harstad** (guest — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-14]]: argues Thielen's reputation is badly miscalibrated — widely read as a "wily veteran" possession receiver when he is in fact "low-key one of the most athletic receivers in the NFL," and that his continued production late in his career owed to having athleticism in reserve, not to guile. [[Matt Waldman]] agreed, noting that going undrafted out of a Division II program and still reaching the league at all required an outlier athletic profile.

- 2024-03-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-14]]): now with Carolina, described as able to get open on short
  routes, quick hitters and slants vs. zone, but not a guy who separates
  downfield anymore. Floats him as a potential trade candidate given his age
  and Carolina's newly added [[Diontae Johnson]], but also notes it would be
  odd to move your only proven, competent receiver; whether he keeps his role
  may hinge on whether the coaching staff tries to develop [[Jonathan Mingo]]
  into the "power slot" role Thielen currently occupies.
