# Adam Thielen

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-14 — **Adam Harstad** (guest — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-14]]: argues Thielen's reputation is badly miscalibrated — widely read as a "wily veteran" possession receiver when he is in fact "low-key one of the most athletic receivers in the NFL," and that his continued production late in his career owed to having athleticism in reserve, not to guile. [[Matt Waldman]] agreed, noting that going undrafted out of a Division II program and still reaching the league at all required an outlier athletic profile.

- 2024-03-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-14]]): now with Carolina, described as able to get open on short
  routes, quick hitters and slants vs. zone, but not a guy who separates
  downfield anymore. Floats him as a potential trade candidate given his age
  and Carolina's newly added [[Diontae Johnson]], but also notes it would be
  odd to move your only proven, competent receiver; whether he keeps his role
  may hinge on whether the coaching staff tries to develop [[Jonathan Mingo]]
  into the "power slot" role Thielen currently occupies.

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): a more
  skeptical read than a straight endorsement — much of his 2023 production is
  characterized as "empty calorie production," and age is flagged as a factor
  working against him. Even so, Harmon argues that of Carolina's supporting
  cast Thielen is still "the best number two" ahead of [[Jonathan Mingo]], and
  that pairing him with newly acquired [[Diontae Johnson]] is what turns
  Carolina's receiver room from an outright disaster into "an actual
  functional NFL unit." Floated as a fringe trade candidate the team likely
  couldn't get more than a sixth-round pick for.

- 2024-04-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]] [Best Ball]: says he personally drafted Thielen at the end of drafts last year and it worked out — Thielen was a fantasy WR1 through Weeks 12-13 despite [[Bryce Young]] posting some of the worst rookie-QB numbers in league history, before tailing off. Cites it as evidence of upside value in both Thielen and [[Diontae Johnson]] at their current ADPs if Young takes a step forward.
- 2024-06-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-20]]): projected to start as Carolina's Z receiver in two-WR sets and kick inside to the slot in 11 personnel once Xavier Legette is on the field. Harmon calls him a 'reliable veteran slot receiver' who'll serve as Bryce Young's checkdown answer against zone coverage when the primary read doesn't come open — everybody's 'kind of bored' by Thielen, per Harmon, but he's a stabilizing presence for a young QB.
- 2024-07-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-25]]): Thielen's 103 catches on 137 targets for 1,014 yards, four touchdowns and a 75% catch rate say less about him than about a room where he was the only game in town — the alternatives were [[Jonathan Mingo]] miscast outside as a day-one rookie and [[D.J. Chark]], whom Harmon calls the worst 2022 performer in success rate vs man, zone and press. He credits Thielen for playing well on short option routes that let [[Bryce Young]] get the ball out fast, and sees his old Minnesota flanker-slot role fitting in 2024. With [[Diontae Johnson]] added, Harmon frames him as a comfortable number two who no longer has to carry the offense; Koh expects the counting stats to take a huge dip while the catch rate stays high.
- 2024-10-31 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-31]]): Thielen returning from injury is a negative for Carolina's young receivers — a 'progress stopper' to [[Jalen Coker]] who moves him out of position, since Harmon says you are not playing Thielen outside at this point in his career and he is effectively a slot-only player. Harmon argued the Panthers should simply trade him for whatever a 2028 seventh-round pick is worth, and said if he were Thielen he would want out to a contender, though he could not name a room Thielen actually improves. He half-joked that Dallas is exactly the team that would trade for him to look like it is 'making moves' — despite the Cowboys not even getting [[CeeDee Lamb]] enough slot reps, with Lamb's slot rate this year the lowest of the last three seasons.
- 2024-12-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-12-03]]): mirage on Adam Thielen as a top-25 wide receiver — 'if I sat down to rank my top 25 receivers right now I don't think Adam Thielen is making the list.' What Harmon will credit is the profile: a veteran professional, not explosive, but 'he's where he's supposed to be and he still has great hands', which showed on a one-handed grab late plus a 10-target, eight-catch, 99-yard, one-touchdown game (Harmon and co-host James Koh, not a tracked expert, both thought a second touchdown was wrongly overturned). Harmon redirected the segment toward [[Bryce Young]], saying the throw itself — working through a muddy pocket with eyes downfield to find Thielen on an out-breaking route — was the real story.
- 2024-12-31 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-12-31]]): an explicit reversal. Harmon previously dismissed Thielen's 2023 as empty-calorie production on a terrible offense — a Frank Reich quick-game role at 7.7 air yards per target and 3.3 yards after catch per reception, on the offense that 'stunk out loud' harder than any other. This year he is 'absolutely' changed his opinion: same 3.3 YAC per reception but 11.4 air yards per target, his highest since 2020 with Minnesota, running vertical routes from a near-pure slot role at a 74% slot rate since Week 15 while [[Jalen Coker]] splits at 41.6%. At 34 and undrafted in fantasy, Thielen leads Carolina in receiving yards by over 100 despite an IR stint, and Harmon notes [[Bryce Young]] clearly trusts him. Koh emphasizes how hard he plays and how he has stiff-armed every younger option — [[Xavier Legette]], Coker, [[Diontae Johnson]].
- 2025-02-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with Thielen undecided on his playing future but seemingly with football left in him, Waldman endorsed what he called the Bob Harris football-diehard approach to Carolina's receivers — take the lowest-ADP guy — and said that is likely Thielen. His reasoning: Thielen remains the most reliable of the group at getting open in zones, making plays in tight coverage and running the route where he is supposed to be, all things [[Bryce Young]] will value as Young shows signs of becoming the player Carolina drafted. The contrast is with [[Jalen Coker]], the exciting name who will get overdrafted.
- 2025-02-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-10]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked) reports Thielen going around round 16 in a SiriusXM fantasy host draft and expected back in Carolina, possibly on an extension. Waldman feels it emphatically — he would feel great taking Thielen anywhere after round 12, because [[Bryce Young]] trusts him as the veteran who reads the field better than any other receiver on the roster, and he is sure-handed, reliable and good on scramble drills, effectively an assistant coach on the field. Waldman is careful about the ceiling: he is not being used like [[Davante Adams]], so the realistic output is roughly 700-900 yards — which is why the price, not the projection, is the point.
- 2025-02-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-21]]): Thielen's late-2024 stretch was materially different from his 2023 volume-only production because he was actually winning downfield — from week 11 on he ranked 12th among ALL wide receivers in yards per route run against man coverage, at 15 air yards per target versus man, while still lining up in the slot on 75.3% of snaps for a confident [[Bryce Young]]. Harmon frames Carolina's whole offseason around whether Thielen is on the roster, since the pre-snap picture for [[Jalen Coker]] and [[Xavier Legette]] only resolves after that. Co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) argued the cut-candidate talk is overblown: an ~$8M cap hit and similar cash is 'so dang affordable' in today's market, and the quarterback chemistry plus veteran presence make a release surprising. Harmon's counter-risk is age (entering his age-35 season) and whether Thielen himself wants to stay through a rebuild.
- 2025-04-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception - 2025-04-28]]): Thielen is a bigger slot/flanker player occupying the same alignment as [[Jalen Coker]] now that Carolina has added [[Tetairoa McMillan]]. Harmon says he would not be totally shocked if Thielen is traded to a contender before the season just to get a pick, given his age, his short-term deal and his own comments that these are his last years — though he likes playing in Carolina, likes playing with [[Bryce Young]], and had a good thing going with Dave Canales, so Harmon frames it as a conversation the team may have with the player rather than a decision already made.
- 2025-05-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-12]]): Thielen at WR68 in the 14th round is too cheap — Waldman said he could be moved up to about WR48, but he will happily take the discount. His evidence is that Thielen has been dismissed too easily two years running regardless of quarterback play or situation: he finished as the WR12 over Weeks 12-18 last year as [[Bryce Young]] improved, and the year before he finished WR18 and spent much of the season at WR12 or better; he got hurt in 2024, came back and still finished strong. The counterweight Waldman raised himself is opportunity cost — the realistic outcome is 800-900 yards and four to six touchdowns, a low-end WR2 or high-end WR3, and at that point in a draft you may prefer a running back one injury away from a [[Jordan Mason]]-style top-five run. He also credited Thielen with refining [[Stefon Diggs]]'s route running in Minnesota.
- 2025-05-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-05-21]]): Thielen is the blocker in Carolina's receiver room — Harmon says the group makes more theoretical sense on paper without him, since that would leave [[Tetairoa McMillan]] at X, [[Xavier Legette]] at flanker and [[Jalen Coker]] in the slot. But Thielen ran a 75.2% slot rate last year (against Coker's 55.9% and Legette's 25.3%), has built a genuinely good two-year connection with [[Bryce Young]], and with Young still not fully operational Harmon values the extra option and depth. He raises a midseason trade as possible but concludes Thielen is worth more to Carolina than to anyone else and will likely play out the season. Koh (untracked) jokes about his age; Harmon corrects the record — Thielen was born in August 1990.
- 2025-06-25 — According to guest Cory Kinnan (Reception Perception QB charting, NOT a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-06-25]]): Kinnan says that while charting [[Bryce Young]] he kept marveling that Thielen 'turned back the clock' last year and was still cooking defensive backs at his age, and slots him as the crafty veteran in Dave Canales' attempt to recreate his Tampa personnel structure — the Chris Godwin analogue alongside rookie [[Tetairoa McMillan]]. He notes Thielen holding down the big slot is what blocks [[Jalen Coker]]'s clearest path to a ceiling. [[Matt Harmon]] counters that Thielen not falling off a cliff at 35 may be the most difficult ask of everything Carolina's receiver room needs to go right this season.
- 2025-07-30 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-07-30]]): he still believes Carolina could move on from Thielen if a trade partner emerges, given how stocked the team is at flanker/slot. [[Matt Harmon]] is less sure there is a market or a motive — Thielen is 34 or 35, but he has a very good rapport with [[Bryce Young]] and is still winning on downfield routes rather than only catching pop-gun stuff underneath, so Harmon does not see the Panthers cutting him loose just to do it. If it did happen, Harmon says [[Hunter Renfrow]] could be the answer in the slot, which then squeezes [[Jalen Coker]].
- 2025-08-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-27]]): Thielen was traded from Carolina back to Minnesota mid-recording — effectively a salary dump, a seventh-rounder next year and a fifth the year after, 'please take this money' rather than a cut. Harris says the move is about [[Jordan Addison]]'s suspension, not [[Justin Jefferson]]'s hamstring. He is dropping Thielen in his ranks: whatever appeal Thielen had as the slot-only, short-distance target from [[Bryce Young]] in Carolina is crushed by the move, and in Minnesota Addison eventually comes back. Guest Chris Raybon of the Action Network (not a tracked expert) cited the 'desperation trade for Thielen' as evidence of how thin the Vikings' receiving corps is, part of his case to fade Minnesota's playoff odds.
- 2025-08-27 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-27]]): a Minnesota reunion is rumored and reportedly wanted by both sides. Asked by Koh (not tracked) to rate how badly he wants the trade on a one-to-ten scale as a [[Jalen Coker]] backer, Harmon says only six and a half, because he is worried about removing the veteran from the room around [[Bryce Young]] — Thielen played good football in Carolina and is their only veteran receiver. Harmon is otherwise optimistic on the Panthers offense: Young was fifth in scramble yards from Week 10 on, the unit was a top-12 EPA per play offense over that stretch, and he likes the run game, offensive line and [[Tetairoa McMillan]] walking into the X job. He notes Minnesota is very light behind [[Justin Jefferson]] with [[Jordan Addison]] suspended, so the interest makes sense on all three sides.
