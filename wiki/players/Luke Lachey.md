# Luke Lachey

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman describes Lachey as a pentagon or octagon entering a league whose two prototypical tight end roles are circles and squares. He has build-up speed but not the acceleration or quickness to earn the runway to reach top gear unless the route develops slowly; he has a lot of effective blocking skills but is not the size to handle NFL edge defenders alone. Teams want a tight end with a full set of skills from one column — inline, or receiving-heavy — and hope for bonus skills from the other; Lachey has some of each and not enough of either, which makes him hard to fit. Waldman explicitly flags this as a case where his own grade for the player is likely higher than how the NFL will actually assess him.
