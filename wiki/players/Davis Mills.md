# Davis Mills

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-11-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-10]]): Mills is better than people think, though 'not much better' — and Waldman flags the confound that the Jacksonville defense he faced is also worse than people think. Mills has bright spots: a penchant for downfield accuracy, he can move, and he can do the things you need from a spot starter, with no special characteristic beyond that. His read is that Mills profiles as a nice journeyman starter over the next several years once he hits free agency; being drafted where he was meant he had to show it faster than he did, so Houston drafted [[C.J. Stroud]] instead. Waldman's speculative fit: San Francisco adding Mills behind [[Brock Purdy]] once they move on from [[Mac Jones]], if [[Kurtis Rourke]] does not work out — with Bob Harris (not tracked) joking that Jones will be marketed as having been 'Shanahan-ed' into a future the way [[Sam Darnold]] was.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-11]]): Mills led a 26-unanswered comeback from 29-10 down over Jacksonville and posted 292 passing yards, but Harris warns the box score is a trap - 'realize it was really' three ugly quarters, with a first-series interception on a stop route the DB jumped, a near-second pick, a delay of game deep in his own territory, a sack, and a blown corner-route throw that cost [[Nico Collins]] a touchdown ([[Dalton Schultz]] scored instead). He scrambled for the winning score. Harris's conclusion: 'you don't really want this offense, at least not the ancillary pieces of this offense, unless and until [[C.J. Stroud]] can come back', which Houston is hopeful about for Week 11 against Tennessee.
