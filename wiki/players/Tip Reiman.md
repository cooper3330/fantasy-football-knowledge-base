> to the real name, Tip Reiman.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: the athletic ability of a good NFL in-line starter — can threaten the seam with speed/acceleration, prototypical size, and sudden moves off the line, but "no craft in setting it up." Needs to develop the patience-vs-suddenness contrast and general route "guile" to become a viable multi-purpose in-line threat; also has lapses tracking the ball, leaving his feet unnecessarily. Overall a competent catcher who wins against contact and adjusts well. Could handle extended in-line work but likely at a cost to receiving upside.

- 2024-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on *Feel It or F@#k It* ([[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-01]]): does have NFL starter ceiling, but that's not automatically fantasy-starter ceiling — he needs to become as good a route runner as he is a blocker for the fantasy upside to show up. Calls him a terrific athlete who blocks well and has pretty good hands, "not far away even if he looks far away on the rankings."
