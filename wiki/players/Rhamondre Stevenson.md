- 2024-07-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Redraft]: Bob notes Stevenson just signed an extension making him the seventh-highest-paid running back in the NFL and calls him 'the only draftable New England Patriot for redraft purposes' on a team that tied Carolina for fewest points scored last year. Waldman feels great about him at RB20 ADP, expecting New England to lean on a ball-control offense with Jacoby Brissett under center. He praises Stevenson's decision-making, ability to set up blocks, patient outside-zone running (comping him to Mike Anderson and Terrell Davis as backs who didn't need elite speed to excel in that scheme, which fits incoming OC Alex Van Pelt's wide-zone install built around Nick Chubb-style film) and his receiving chops -- capable of 50-60 catches if New England chooses to target him that heavily, though usage could be capped by a committee approach.
