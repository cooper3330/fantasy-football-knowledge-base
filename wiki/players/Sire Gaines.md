- 2024-09-11 — According to guest co-host Felix Sharpe of Campus to Canton (NOT a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman]]'s show ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-11]]) [Dynasty]: Gaines is the backup-to-know, sitting behind [[Ashton Jeanty]] with Jeanty expected to leave for the NFL after the season. A California product and the 821st-ranked player in the 2024 class per 247Sports, he was a high-school tweener listed as an athlete who went over 800 rushing and 800 receiving as a junior playing both ways, then ran for over 1,600 yards as a senior after moving to running back full time. He is 6-0, 209 as a true freshman with a 100-yard rushing game already and is the team's second-leading rusher. Sharpe applies a size rule his group likes — a freshman back over 200 pounds when the season starts usually gets to about 215 — and projects Gaines to 217-218 with receiving chops. He is bigger than Jeanty and Sharpe expects the 2025 backfield to be his all day.
