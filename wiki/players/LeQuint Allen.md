# LeQuint Allen

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-13]]) [Dynasty]: one of his three best receiving backs in the class and a likely day-three pick. Angelo describes him as a tougher version of [[Rachaad White]] but not as good an athlete, citing the ease with which he catches the football, genuinely good pass protection that keeps the quarterback clean, and effectiveness in the low red zone. The projected role is a reliable Kenneth Gainwell-type contributor rather than a lead back.
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): asked whether Allen is a future starter or the next Bilal Powell / Kenny Gainwell, Waldman put him squarely between Gainwell and Powell — a permanent backup rather than a future starter. He credits Allen as a good receiver with smarts about working between the tackles, the kind of back who on a running-back-needy team hit by injuries could lead the way on draw plays, screens and check-downs and produce by volume. What he does not see is dynamism: the high-end production on a given play or across a stretch of games is small, so Waldman calls him explicitly a high-floor, low-ceiling player who will need precise circumstances to get a role beyond special teams. He notes Allen scores similarly in the RSP to backs like [[Phil Mafah]], but with the floor and ceiling distributed differently.
- 2025-06-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-09]]) [Dynasty]: Allen splits the verdict — 'fuck it' for fantasy, 'feel it' for real football. Waldman sees a Kenneth Gainwell-type who is a little sturdier, or a Bilal Powell type: capable of giving you some starting weeks as an emergency fill-in, but more likely a situational piece. He found it interesting that Liam Coen has talked about using Allen in the slot and as a jack-of-all-trades, and speculated the appeal may be his blocking — though Waldman countered that [[Parker Washington]] is also a nice blocker in bunch sets. Bottom line for 2025: too cloudy to project anything good from him.
- 2025-07-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-10]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Allen is Waldman's favorite example of the training-camp 'key role in the passing game, detached from the formation' narrative. He argues that phrase, judged by coaching history, almost never means Marshall Faulk or Brian Westbrook — more often one play a game and maybe a target or two, i.e. you are on the roster and playing about 5% of snaps. [[Brandon Angelo]] agrees emphatically, pointing to a Jacksonville backfield that is four deep with [[Bhayshul Tuten]], [[Travis Etienne Jr.]] and [[Tank Bigsby]] all capable, so no one gets a large touch share; Waldman adds [[Dyami Brown]] (a three-year camp darling) and [[Parker Washington]] compete for the same detached slot work, with Washington having real rapport with [[Trevor Lawrence]] and winning after the catch.
- 2025-09-09 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-09]]): Allen is his number five waiver add at 1% rostered. On the Carolina film Allen played nine snaps, appeared on third downs, and touched the ball before [[Bhayshul Tuten]] did, catching a pass on an important third down without converting it. Harris is candid that he does not know him from Adam and would not strain to roster him in any league — this is a last-roster-spot stash in a 12-team league to see whether Jacksonville's third-down back role is real now that [[Travis Etienne Jr.]] is clearly the main guy and [[Tank Bigsby]] has been traded, and especially if you own Etienne and not Tuten. Guest Jake Ciely of The Athletic (not a tracked expert) says he drew a pre-draft comp of Allen to [[Rachaad White]].
- 2026-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-19]]): Allen projects as a strong third-down back in Liam Coen's Jacksonville offense -- Waldman compared his likely role to Rashaad White's in Tampa -- which further complicates the touch outlook for [[Bhayshul Tuten]] alongside goal-line work Waldman expects to go to [[Chris Rodriguez Jr.]].
