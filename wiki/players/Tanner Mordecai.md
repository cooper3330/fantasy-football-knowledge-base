# Tanner Mordecai

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): a former four-star recruit who started at Oklahoma before transferring to SMU (where his tape was "pretty nice") and finally Wisconsin for a graduate year in a new offense with new receivers — a rocky transition year with communication issues, a lot of sacks, poor protection, and hesitant decision-making. Rebounded late in the season against LSU after returning from a hand injury, looking more like the SMU-caliber player. Waldman's grade: not a future starter, but "a future backup of value," with "some starter-level skills" worth double-checking further.

- 2024-03-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-08]]), solo cast on the full QB class: a good-not-great arm, with a release process that could use more work, though Waldman isn't sure fixing it should be the top development priority. What he loves: Mordecai trusts his receivers, especially contested-catch guys — he "really trusted" [[Rashee Rice]] at SMU and "he's going to throw them open" into tight zone windows, an aggressive mentality overall. At Wisconsin (a graduate transfer year with a new offense and less-talented receivers) that comfort level and rapport were missing, leaving him a beat or two slower with his reads, including reading the middle of the field against more complex coverage looks.
