# Gavin Bartholomew

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Bartholomew is a good route runner who sets up coverage well against zone and man. Waldman highlights his stair-step technique — stemming one direction, flattening as if challenging the vertical seam, then breaking another way — and a subtle inset against off coverage playing outside him, where he sells the inside break at the top of the stem with his eyes, shoulders and hips before breaking outside. Waldman's coaching point is that these fakes work when you attack the defender's leverage in the direction of the fake at high speed, because speed lets the movements stay subtle and believable, and Bartholomew does it the right way. The question is whether he can rein in sloppiness attacking the ball away from his frame: on extreme high or low adjustments his hands widen instead of extending together. If he fixes that, Waldman says he could be a very intriguing prospect with more upside than people think.
