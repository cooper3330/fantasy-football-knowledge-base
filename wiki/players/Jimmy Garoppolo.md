- 2025-08-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-18]]): as the Rams' next man up behind [[Matthew Stafford]] (with Stetson Bennett after him), Garoppolo throws a beautiful ball and does throw with anticipation, and could be good enough for this offense and for fantasy — but when pressure gets amped up a notch he self-destructs, the same problem Waldman ascribes to [[Derek Carr]]. Bob Harris (not tracked) added he is still drafting [[Davante Adams]] with reckless abandon regardless, since Adams saw 20 targets in a game with Garoppolo despite disliking him.
