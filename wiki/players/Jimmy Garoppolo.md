# Jimmy Garoppolo

## Expert Takes

- 2025-08-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-18]]): as the Rams' next man up behind [[Matthew Stafford]] (with Stetson Bennett after him), Garoppolo throws a beautiful ball and does throw with anticipation, and could be good enough for this offense and for fantasy — but when pressure gets amped up a notch he self-destructs, the same problem Waldman ascribes to [[Derek Carr]]. Garoppolo gives the Rams a chance in the division but is 'a bigger problem' in the playoffs, which is why Waldman would be fine swapping him for [[Kirk Cousins]] if Stafford's back proves serious. Bob Harris (not tracked) added he is still drafting [[Davante Adams]] with reckless abandon regardless, since Adams saw 20 targets in a game with Garoppolo despite disliking him.
- 2026-11-02 — According to Bob Harris ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-11-02 (Feel It or Fk It)]]), not a tracked expert, on whether Garoppolo could be Baltimore's emergency replacement for the injured [[Lamar Jackson]]: 'I feel this.' His reasoning: Garoppolo has been largely inactive recently, sat behind Tom Brady for much of his career and more recently behind [[Matthew Stafford]], so he should be fresh; he's a precision passer who can run a strong play-action game, which would help the Ravens' scheme, even though he isn't great under pressure. Given the thin free-agent QB market at this point in the season, Harris says Garoppolo is probably the best available option to keep Baltimore's playoff hunt alive and offers strong value relative to cost.
