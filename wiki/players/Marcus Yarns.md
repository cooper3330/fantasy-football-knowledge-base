# Marcus Yarns

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24 (Feel It or Not with Bob Harris)]]) [Dynasty]: asked whether overlooking the small-school 4.45 back is a horrible mistake, Waldman says no — more like jaywalking on a slow-traffic street, it could get you in trouble but probably will not. He initially thought Yarns looked like DeSean Jackson playing running back, but notes he added about 15 pounds for the combine and is now a solid 193. Waldman's case is optionality: kick return duty, a gadget back/receiver getting perimeter rushing yardage, a change-of-pace who at best becomes a [[Bucky Irving]] type (less sturdy, definitely faster), or a slot receiver, because he likes how Yarns catches the ball and runs routes. Worth it only in deep leagues; in a three-way call with [[Kimani Vidal]] and [[Dylan Laube]] he says ignore Yarns.
- 2025-04-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-07]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman names the Delaware back the MOST INTRIGUING rookie in this running back class. When he first watched him he looked closer in size to DeSean Jackson and Waldman assumed the path was a receiving role — a late-career Eric Metcalf type, the precursor to a Julian Edelman. Weighing in at the combine at over 190 pounds changed that: he is an electric player, skilled in the open field, and there is at least enough between-the-tackles potential to see him grow into a change-of-pace complement for an NFL team. If he can reach the 200-pound range, Waldman thinks that may actually happen.
