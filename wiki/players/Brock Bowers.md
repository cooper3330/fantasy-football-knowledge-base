# Brock Bowers

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-05]]) [Dynasty]: leaning "feel it" on Bowers becoming the next big fantasy-producing rookie tight end, though states he's genuinely on the fence. Profile: a "move tight end" in the [[Sam LaPorta]] mold who needs the right scheme fit rather than a plug-and-play talent — strong after the catch, can run through contact, but has an underrated technique flaw on one specific target type (high-and-away throws, where he "claps" the ball with both hands and drops it), and is not yet a technically sound blocker. Floats a Jim Harbaugh-to-the-Chargers landing spot as a positive scheme fit if Harbaugh installs more two-tight-end sets. Explicit ceiling calibration: probably won't top LaPorta's or Kyle Pitts's rookie-year production, but should still have real fantasy value — closer to a [[Dalton Kincaid]] outcome (underused on some route types as a rookie, but still finished as a top-12 fantasy tight end).
- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]) [Dynasty]: the Chargers landing spot gets a firmer scheme endorsement now that the pick is real rather than speculative (LA holds the 5th overall selection) — not too early to take him there at all "if you have a cogent idea of how you're going to use him," pointing to how aggressively new OC Greg Roman used Mark Andrews in Baltimore as a direct positional/archetype comp for how Bowers would be deployed, with the explicit caveat that Bowers is "not quite as rugged in the open field" as Andrews. Illustrated with a live anecdote: co-host **Bob Harris** (not a tracked expert) drafted Bowers 11th round in an early best-ball mock and had him sniped away, underscoring how little draft capital the market is currently pricing him at.

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: "you gotta view Bowers through a similar lens as Sam LaPorta" — a move tight end with competency in specific blocking roles whose primary job is wreaking havoc as a receiver and runner after the catch, ranked TE3 in this RSP class. LaPorta comped as "Evan Ingram without the technical flaws," while Bowers's game is comped to Vernon Davis mixed with Aaron Hernandez's versatility. Named, alongside [[Cade Stover]], as the class's most pro-ready tight end if his scheme uses him as a receiver/H-back rather than an in-line blocker.

- 2024-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on *Feel It or F@#k It* ([[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-01]]): "feeling" the possibility that Bowers breaks Mike Ditka's 1961 rookie tight end receiving-yardage record (1,076 yards) — but that's contingent on landing spot. In a 1.5-PPR league specifically, says Bowers should be a top-three pick, "if not the top pick," given how many ways he produces: efficient and dynamic after the catch, able to re-accelerate to beat corners on the same side of the field, useful in the screen game, and sets up his own blocks well — praised as making a good athlete like [[David Njoku]] "look like a lumbering guy with cement in his shoes." His one real flaw as a receiver: in contested catch situations he sometimes "clap attacks" the football rather than securing it cleanly; if that doesn't improve he risks becoming a [[Courtland Sutton]]-type good-not-great fantasy option who drops one or two a game, but Waldman expects him to work through it. With the right landing spot, has a chance to be a top-five fantasy tight end as a rookie and the talent equivalent of prime Vernon Davis mixed with Aaron Hernandez — "a pretty strong statement with those two guys."
- 2024-04-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-22]]) [Dynasty]: names Kansas City as his ideal landing spot for Bowers so he can develop behind [[Patrick Mahomes]] and [[Travis Kelce]] rather than start immediately; co-host Bob Harris relays reported buzz (via NJ.com's Andy Vasquez, untracked) that the Jets could realistically take Bowers if he's available.
