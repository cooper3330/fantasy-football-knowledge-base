# JuJu Smith-Schuster

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-08-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-26]]): Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) opened by calling Smith-Schuster washed on his return to Kansas City, citing 20 targets, 12 catches and 143 yards. Waldman's read is more functional than dismissive: JuJu is essentially a replacement for Justyn Ross, who had a good end to the preseason after the receiver coaches told him to be a contested-catch marvel and box people out. Waldman sees Smith-Schuster as the coach on the field — he knows the scheme, they can plug him in when others fail, and he will give you 10-11 yards per catch as a middle-of-the-field presence. He notes rumors [[Kadarius Toney]] holds the spot above him and doubts Mecole Hardman is a major factor, and concludes that if JuJu gets 40-50 targets in this offense, it means multiple people got hurt.
- 2024-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-29]]): Smith-Schuster was cut by New England — Harmon says it was apparent from the word go that he would not make a Patriots roster not exactly loaded with proven receiver talent — and re-signed with Kansas City. Harmon calls him another player who needs role catering, and agrees he is 'samey samey' with [[Rashee Rice]]; co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) had already noted that Smith-Schuster's last KC season and Rice's rookie year look nearly identical in route concepts, average depth of target, target location and route success rates. Both wonder aloud whether the signing implies Kansas City expects a Rice suspension, while noting there was no public indication one was coming for 2024. Harmon's conclusion: absent a suspension it would be 'nutso' to play Smith-Schuster over a healthy Rice at any point.
