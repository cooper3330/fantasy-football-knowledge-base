# Kendrick Bourne

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-07-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Kendrick Bourne, coming off an ACL tear and set to be New England's presumptive Week 1 X receiver, actually charted as a solid man-coverage beater in Harmon's mid-2023-season sample before the injury -- but Harmon cautioned this is 'a total projection' since Bourne has never posted a truly great X-receiver season, with his best year (2021, his first with the Patriots) merely 'nice.' Co-host James Coe (not tracked) questioned whether Bourne is even a top-25 receiver in the league. Patriots coach Jerod Mayo's minicamp comments implying the rookies would get run without Bourne around suggested to Harmon that the team views a healthy Bourne as their established starter.
- 2025-02-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-28]]): Bourne is the Patriots' highest-paid receiver at 7.9M entering his age-30 season, which co-host James Koh (not tracked) used to illustrate how bare the room is. Koh said Bourne did not look great coming back from the 2023 knee injury — 305 receiving yards — but sees room for further growth and grades him as 'a really nice three, a low-end number two.' Harmon's framing of the whole room is that New England has a bunch of threes and fours with things to prove and no one who can consistently win on the perimeter, and that the smarter offseason path is fixing the offensive line rather than chasing receivers. Both agreed the Patriots still need to add a veteran starting-level X plus a draft pick at the position.
- 2025-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-21]]): Harmon says Bourne is not really a needle mover at this point in his career and could get cut, especially with New England adding bodies at receiver. James Koh (co-host, not tracked) thought Bourne came back from injury nicely and finished the 2024 season strong after a weak start, but notes he is north of 30 and that his value now may be as veteran leadership for a team reportedly short on maturity. Both agree that whatever New England adds — [[Stefon Diggs]], a draft pick, or both — squeezes either Bourne or [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] off the depth chart.
- 2025-10-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with [[Brock Purdy]], [[George Kittle]], [[Ricky Pearsall]] and [[Jauan Jennings]] all out on Thursday night, Bourne is the volume-chase play — Harris ranked him WR43 standard, WR41 PPR; Jeff Bell of Football Guys (not tracked) had him WR48. Harris sarcastically dismissed the Mac Jones reunion narrative as a 2021-2023 Patriots throwback that 'worked so perfectly,' and warned the 49ers will play other receivers who could be the ones to fall into production. His stated philosophy: when good players sit and less good players play, that tends to be worse for fantasy, and he does not want to chase in what feels like a hopeless operation — you can go all-in on an inside straight and hit it, but it is not the mathematically correct way to play the hand. Both agreed they could not live with starting Bourne over [[A.J. Brown]] and having him do nothing.
- 2025-10-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-03]]): Bourne's 10-catch, 142-yard Thursday night line came almost entirely underneath — seven of the 10 catches travelled 10 air yards or fewer — and was a product of the Rams playing two-high and conceding the short game with the 49ers missing their main receiving weapons. Harris has 'always had a soft spot' for him as a savvy player who instinctively finds space even if he is not a good route runner and will not run by anybody. But he is 'pretty skeptical that a ton of regular value is coming' once the 49ers are healthy: Bourne is clearly the number three receiver then, and San Francisco runs far fewer three-wide sets than most teams because of their fullback usage and two-tight-end looks when [[George Kittle]] is healthy. 'He'll always have week five.'
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-06]]): as long as [[Mac Jones]] is out there, Waldman thinks Bourne is the answer in San Francisco's passing game, and he thought Bourne looked great in the Thursday game. His read on why: the Shanahans call basically the same play until you prove you can stop it, and Kyle Shanahan dressed it up — the final drive included three targets that were all variations of a slant to Bourne from different alignments with different window dressing. Waldman expects the role to be limited once [[Juwan Jennings]] and [[Ricky Pearsall]] return this week, but believes Bourne deserves to stay in a three-man rotation at least while [[Brandon Aiyuk]] is out, and Bob Harris (not tracked) noted the latest reporting has Aiyuk still many weeks away.
- 2025-10-13 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-13]]): Bourne delivered his second straight huge game for San Francisco, and Harris admitted he ranked Bourne ahead of Jauan Jennings for the week 'purely guessing' on which returning/healthy piece would get the volume. Jennings, despite playing 51 snaps in his return, mostly showed up drawing a penalty for a big hit on a defenseless defender rather than through target volume -- Harris noted that outcome (Bourne over the bigger name) doesn't always hold and is worth watching as Jennings gets fully healthy.
- 2025-10-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-14 (Week 7 Waiver Wire & More W6 Film)]]): Bourne (35% rostered, so outside the show's normal top-5 cutoff) has saved Harris's lineups in a couple of leagues the past two weeks connecting with [[Mac Jones]]. Harris frames him as roughly a 'Jauan Jennings with [[Ricky Pearsall]]' floor -- a low-end WR3, maybe WR4 if [[George Kittle]] also returns -- and recommends an aggressive 15-20% FAAB bid for two-to-three weeks of top-25 value, while noting this is a short-term, win-now pickup rather than a long-term stash. Guest Jake Seeley (The Athletic, not a tracked expert) had ranked Bourne significantly behind [[Jauan Jennings]] the prior week and issued a mea culpa after Jennings turned out to be playing through undisclosed injuries.
- 2025-10-16 — According to guest Dave Kluge of FootballGuys.com (not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-16 (Week 7 Flex Decisions)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Kluge said Kendrick Bourne would be inside his top-24 receivers this week if both [[Ricky Pearsall]] and Juwan Jennings are out, putting him in the same value neighborhood as [[George Kittle]] -- a matchup- and injury-contingent flex, not a standing recommendation.
