- 2024-07-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Kendrick Bourne, coming off an ACL tear and set to be New England's presumptive Week 1 X receiver, actually charted as a solid man-coverage beater in Harmon's mid-2023-season sample before the injury -- but Harmon cautioned this is 'a total projection' since Bourne has never posted a truly great X-receiver season, with his best year (2021, his first with the Patriots) merely 'nice.' Co-host James Coe (not tracked) questioned whether Bourne is even a top-25 receiver in the league. Patriots coach Jerod Mayo's minicamp comments implying the rookies would get run without Bourne around suggested to Harmon that the team views a healthy Bourne as their established starter.
