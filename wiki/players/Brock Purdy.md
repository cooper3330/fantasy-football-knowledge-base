# Brock Purdy

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21]]): defends Purdy against the consensus "system quarterback" reading. Asked to rank Dak Prescott, [[Lamar Jackson]], [[C.J. Stroud]] and Purdy by scheme dependence, a Footballguys roundtable unanimously named Purdy; Waldman named Jackson instead. His case for Purdy: he may well be worse off-structure than Prescott or Jackson and has made mistakes there, but he is "very good at processing in structure against tight coverage, making really quick reads that are very difficult to do" — and that skill "fits in any system," which is precisely what a system-*dependent* player's skills do not do.
- 2023-12-21 — **Waldman**, same episode, the supporting comparison: Jimmy Garoppolo played six years in the same San Francisco offense and, while he posted some gaudy yards-per-attempt numbers, "they weren't anywhere near the 9.88 that Purdy was doing." Same scheme, same play-caller, different output — Waldman's argument that the delta belongs to the quarterback.
