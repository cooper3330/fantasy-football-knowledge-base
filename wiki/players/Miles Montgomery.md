- 2026-02-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-09]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman is 'totally feeling' Montgomery as a sleeper who took over UCF's backfield after [[R.J. Harvey|RJ Harvey]], calling that backfield one of the most fun to watch this year. At around 205 pounds, Montgomery runs with balance, plays bigger than his listed size, and projects as a plug-in contributor rather than a liability if drafted late. Waldman floats a Rams-style outcome -- a fifth/sixth-round pick who could work into a [[Kyren Williams]]/[[Blake Corum]]-type role, potentially becoming the eventual Corum replacement in LA if [[Jarquez Hunter]] doesn't develop.
- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman called Montgomery 'one of the most underrated movers for his position in this class,' citing his information processing -- he sees developments early and accurately, letting him avoid penetration that would normally foil most plays and still produce as a short-yardage runner. Waldman noted occasional lapses where Montgomery overthinks and errs toward the safe play instead of creating, but said what sets him apart is combining that processing with efficient, well-paced movement.
- 2026-04-09 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-09]]) [Dynasty]: a deep sleeper at 5'10'/198 lbs, placed at the top of Angelo's third tier of the RB class -- close to his second tier -- largely on pass protection: diagnosing inside/outside blitzers, rarely fooled, good footwork. He's also a fast processor who reads pre-snap keys, adjusts when the blocking scheme breaks down, and gets downhill quickly without unnecessary bounces. Angelo comped him to [[Kenneth Gainwell]] for well-rounded, coach-endearing play, said he told a pro scout Montgomery was his top 'might not get drafted but I have an early grade on him' sleeper, and projected he could add roughly 12 lbs to around 210 with a small explosiveness gain. Angelo called him possibly the biggest sleeper of the class if he adds a tick more speed/explosion. [[Matt Waldman]] agreed on the film after a partial viewing, noting Montgomery's processing edge shows up as a leverage advantage against bigger defenders, especially valuable as a likely UDFA/late-round type.
- 2026-05-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-04]]) [Dynasty]: believes New England releasing [[Elijah Mitchell]] this week is a real, if early, step forward for Miles Montgomery, who moves up from RB6 to roughly RB5 on the depth chart with one less healthy body to beat out. Waldman reads the move as evidence the Patriots feel comfortable with what they already have and want to develop from within rather than lean on Mitchell's larger body of work; he cautions Montgomery could still be in the same spot if New England adds another back in the next week.
- 2026-05-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-18]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said feel it, emphatically, on undrafted rookie Montgomery -- there's already beat-writer expectation he could be the Patriots' RB3, putting him one injury away from playing time. Waldman had him as a top-five running back in his own pre-draft grade, calling him the best pass protector and run blocker in this class, and noted Montgomery got the most lucrative UDFA contract of any player New England signed this cycle, a signal of organizational belief comparable to how [[Sean Tucker]] was signed as an undrafted priority two years earlier. Montgomery reportedly chose New England for its coaching and open competition. Waldman's takeaway: an end-of-draft dynasty stash worth watching closely this summer.
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-17]]) [Dynasty]: Montgomery, who signed the largest guaranteed UDFA deal in this year's class (reportedly $270K+), was injured and landed on IR rather than being released outright, which barring an injury settlement ends his 2026 season. Waldman called the IR designation (rather than a cut) a good sign the Patriots value the investment and want to keep developing him. He still projects Montgomery could be the team's RB3 by year two and grow from there, comping the path to [[George Holani]] in Seattle, who didn't make the roster as a rookie behind Kenny McIntosh but is now drawing buzz while McIntosh looks for work.
