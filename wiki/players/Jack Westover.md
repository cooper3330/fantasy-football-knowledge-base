# Jack Westover

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]] [Dynasty]: a walk-on prospect (6'3", 243 lbs) name-checked as a runner-up to [[Ben Sinnott]] at tight end in this class — "catches just about everything" and makes some hard plays, but projects more as a zone-blocking or fullback-type role than an every-down receiving tight end.
- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: expands on the profile — an H-back/fullback type likely relegated to special teams if he makes a roster, but Waldman rates his pass-catching skills among the best in this tight end class: good back-shoulder adjustments, tracks the ball over his inside shoulder, high-points well with a tight overhand attack, and makes full-layout extensions. Mobility to decelerate and separate on underneath routes is another plus. Comps him, if he sticks and sees playing time, to undersized-but-productive "quarterback security blanket" types like Todd Christensen and Frank Wycheck.
