# Kalel Mullings

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-25 — According to guest Felix Sharpe of Campus2Canton (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman]]'s show ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-25 (Campus2Canton with Felix Sharpe)]]) [Dynasty]: Mullings is the biggest riser in the Michigan backfield, going for 153 and 159 rushing yards in back-to-back games, the latter against USC in a game where Michigan threw for only 32 yards with new starter Alex Orji. A 6'2', 233-pound fifth-year who spent most of three seasons at linebacker, he has 17 missed tackles forced to [[Donovan Edwards]]' five, averages 8.4 yards per attempt on A-gap runs, 9.3 on B-gap runs, 5.4 yards after contact, and has seven runs of 10-plus yards at 8.2 per carry overall. Sharpe credits decision-making and patience to set up blocks, and notes Mullings runs smaller than his height by shrinking his tackling surface, exploding through defenders to fall forward — bringing Michigan back to its Bo Schembechler downhill roots. [[Matt Waldman]] pushed back on whether the rise is real for NFL purposes or just optics, offering Hassan Haskins, Jordan Howard and [[Tyler Allgeier]] as comps; Sharpe admitted he does not know, worrying about age and a likely lack of elite straight-line speed and agility testing, and settled on Mullings landing between Howard and Haskins with more upside than Haskins — a day-three pick, possibly a special-teams roster type given the linebacker background.
