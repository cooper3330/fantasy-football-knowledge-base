# Tyler Neville

- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]): Neville, a former Harvard player now at Virginia, is someone Waldman calls a badass — pointing listeners to his story and medical history — while being clear that badassery is not the same as fantasy value. He expects Neville to show it most often on special teams rather than as an offensive weapon, but would not put it past him to develop into a competent backup and stopgap H-back. He catches the ball well, wins targets requiring athletic adjustments, earns position against tight coverage, takes contact to win plays, and is patient, determined and rugged after the catch — a chain-mover and a smart decision maker who creates against unblocked defenders within the realm of his athletic gifts.
