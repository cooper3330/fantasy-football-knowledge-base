# Sam LaPorta

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-21 (Pacing and Control)]]): a self-graded miss, and an unusually specific one. Waldman "wasn't extremely high on him because I thought he needed the perfect fit — and he found the perfect fit." He raises it because a former scout and biomechanics/analytics consultant who has worked with roughly 30 teams (a longtime RSP subscriber, unnamed on air) wrote to tell him he had "really nailed the LaPorta evaluation" — meaning the *floor* scenario rather than the ranking.
- 2023-12-21 — **Waldman**, same episode, what that floor scenario actually said: LaPorta "winds up on the bench getting fattened up and put on special teams because the coach and the GM are in a pissing match over whether he should have been drafted" — the coach wanting an inline tight end who can block, the GM having drafted a weapon, the coach telling him to gain 20 pounds and block, and then telling the GM the pick was wrong. The consultant's reply, per Waldman: "that's literally the RIP for so many NFL players." Recorded as an evaluation-method point — a tight end's floor is frequently organizational rather than physical. See [[Player Development and Coachability]].
- 2023-12-21 — [[Brandon Angelo]], same episode, on the fit that materialized: names LaPorta's Detroit as the league's best current example of an organization that recruits for cultural fit and gets players to accept coaching, alongside [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], [[David Montgomery]], Jared Goff and [[Jahmyr Gibbs]].
- 2024-01-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]]): notes plainly that LaPorta's rookie-year results have outproduced [[Dalton Kincaid]]'s, and calls LaPorta "underrated" in his own pre/post-draft tiers (11th overall, in his top tight-end tier alongside Kincaid at 5th) — but says he remains a "Kincaid over LaPorta guy" long-term regardless, on the belief that a tight end's rookie season is usually not his final form. Not a downgrade of LaPorta, who Waldman still rates a top-5 dynasty tight end; recorded as a case where Waldman's stated ranking preference and the season's actual production diverge.
- 2024-01-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-29]]): the strongest single-line praise yet — "one of the best young tight ends, if not the best young tight end in the game" — cited among the reasons Detroit's core is set up for continued success beyond its 2023 coaching staff.
