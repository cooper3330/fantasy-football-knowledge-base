# Lan Larison

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-04-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-07]]) [Dynasty]: after a long running joke with Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) that the name sounds like a pen name for Cecil Lammey, Waldman gives a real evaluation — the rodeo-champion back can catch a football, including Brandon Lloyd-esque one-handed grabs over the middle, and runs pretty good routes. He needs to improve as a decision maker and is a better gap-scheme player than zone player at this point, but he could have a role on a team, could probably start eventually, and most likely works his way up from special teams.
- 2025-04-24 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-24]]): Larison is his favorite player who may go undrafted, for the same reasons he liked [[Dylan Laube]] a year earlier 'plus' the special teams value — he can play everywhere on special teams, projecting as a kick returner and a gunner. Angelo rates him a very good receiver out of the backfield who can align outside at a higher level than most running backs, with contributor-level qualities, and says his best position is honestly a Taysom Hill type role. [[Matt Waldman]] endorses the profile, joking about the wrestling and rodeo background showing up on the tape.
- 2025-08-06 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-06]], flagged as his deep sleeper: Larison, out of UC Davis (Koh guessed Sacramento State on air), 'can absolutely catch the football' — full-extension one-handed grabs you rarely see from a running back — and parts of the internet think he could make a position switch or fill a hybrid pass-catching role in the mold of Danny Woodhead. Koh notes pre-draft chatter had him fitting an [[Alvin Kamara]]-style role in New Orleans or Detroit behind [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] before he landed in New England. [[Matt Harmon]] did not offer an evaluation of his own.
