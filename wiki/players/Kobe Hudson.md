# Kobe Hudson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], solo cast on the 2024 WR class A-Z ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-16]]): can contribute in four-receiver sets as a deep threat against mismatched defenders. If he elevates his man-to-man game from the stem through the break point, Waldman thinks he can go from a talented open-field/vertical threat to a well-rounded option capable of starting outside.
- 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24]]): the UCF receiver's speed sits right on the borderline between starter and contributor, so to thrive he has to optimize his route-running precision to compensate. He has a few setup techniques at the top of his stems that turn defenders around before the break, but uses them mainly on vertical routes — Waldman wants them applied to routes breaking back to the quarterback too, because a decent college vertical player may find that harder in the pros. He needs breaks that are more precise and delivered at full speed, and is currently missing refined speed breaks and hard breaks.
