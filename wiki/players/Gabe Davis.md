# Gabe Davis

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]: pushes back on his own reputation for bashing Davis — "he's a good football player, he's a good wide receiver," but his role isn't consistent enough to fantasy-start reliably. The spiked, boom-or-bust performances are the design of his role, not a sign of an emerging larger one: "don't draft him on one game, draft him on the full body of work."
- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], same episode: expects Davis to leave Buffalo in free agency, with [[Khalil Shakir]] taking over the role he vacates.
- 2024-01-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: firms up the free-agency call from two weeks earlier — "he is playing somewhere else next year for sure" — and floats the Atlanta Falcons as a plausible fit (their historical type of receiver) while doubting the front office will actually spend on him.
- 2024-01-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-23]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: independent corroboration from a different analyst — "not a true number two receiver," a "just a guy" rotational piece, with zero receiving yards in five of his last nine games played, closing a stretch where he hasn't played since the Miami game. Notes he's only 24 and has said he's open to returning to Buffalo, but frames Buffalo's receiver room as needing an outside upgrade regardless of what happens with him. Separately floated as a plausible, if unspectacular, veteran add for Houston's thin receiver room — "a good blocker," a clear WR3 who wouldn't threaten [[Nico Collins]] or [[Tank Dell]].

- 2024-03-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-14]]): signs a 3-year, $39M deal with Jacksonville (only $11M
  guaranteed). Confirms the down 2023 charting grade — 7th percentile success
  rate vs. man coverage, "not good." Doesn't see him as capable of being one
  of a team's top-two receivers, pointing to Buffalo's struggles when they
  leaned on him that way. His skill set is narrow but real — nine routes,
  posts, out routes, stretching the field vertically — which would have made
  more sense paired with a retained [[Calvin Ridley]] (Davis on low-value X
  reps, Ridley freed to move around). Instead paired with [[Christian Kirk]]
  and a limited in-line TE (Evan Ingram, not a tracked page), which Harmon
  calls a "hyper-specific," bottom-of-the-league receiver room — in the
  conversation for bottom five — despite the money spent; expects Jacksonville
  now has to draft a true WR1.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: on the finalized Jacksonville deal (3 years, $39M) — calls him roughly what [[Calvin Ridley]] was "at his absolute worst," just taller, stronger and a better blocker: a third option who can win downfield when the quarterback scrambles but only "catches the ball one out of every three times" when schemed open. Flags the contract as a market-inefficiency red flag rather than praise — Davis making more than [[Saquon Barkley]] "tells you how institutionally stupid things have gotten." Doubts Jacksonville is done addressing receiver.

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): reaffirms
  he's "a passable outside guy" who "can be a starting outside receiver in the
  league... not a guy you want being your second most targeted player, but he
  could be your three." Grouped with [[Zay Jones]] as functional-but-limited
  outside options who can't by themselves lift Jacksonville out of its
  bottom-tier receiver room.
- 2024-05-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-07]]): after signing with Jacksonville, Harmon says he's 'kicked Gabe Davis to the curb' in his own evaluation, viewing him as a one-dimensional vertical-only receiver he'd rather keep off the field in 2-WR sets behind [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] and [[Christian Kirk]]. Co-host James Koh (untracked) disagrees, arguing Davis 'is not like freaking [MVS] out there' -- he's not worthless, brings more size and physicality to the run game than Kirk, and should see real snaps, with Koh projecting something closer to a 50/50 X/flanker rotation between Davis and Thomas rather than Davis being phased out.
- 2024-07-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-11]]) [Dynasty]: projects Gabe Davis to play almost exclusively X for Jacksonville, which frees [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] to work more as a flanker. Co-host James Koh (untracked) said he is 'not a huge fan' of the 3-year, $39M signing and does not view Davis as one of the top two outside receivers in the room -- a factor in the hosts ranking Jacksonville's WR trio behind Houston, Indianapolis and Tennessee.
- 2024-10-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harmon jokes that Davis is 'the most sacrificial of the sacrificial X's' — if there were a sacrificial X Zoom call, Davis would moderate it. That role is a positive for [[Brian Thomas Jr.]], who gets freed to play flanker and run underneath and intermediate routes because Davis absorbs the clear-out work. On Davis himself Harmon is dismissive: a Week 5 sequence of an offensive pass interference followed by a fumble, and a game log where every line is 'Gabe Davis did this' and none of it is good. He is explicitly not the picture of reliability in a Jacksonville receiver room Harmon does not consider reliable overall.
- 2024-10-24 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-24]], relayed alongside [[Matt Harmon]]: the Gabe Davis contract in Jacksonville is 'still horrendous' — but because the Jaguars paid him, they feel obliged to play him as the outside X, and that has accidentally freed [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] from being anchored to the boundary. Koh argued Doug Pederson's staff would otherwise have had Thomas playing X on 90% of snaps; instead Thomas moves between X, flanker and slot. Harmon did not dispute the framing, and separately made the same point about [[Calvin Ridley]] having been the 'sacrificial Jaguars receiver' in 2023 whose poor X usage taught the staff what not to do with Thomas.
- 2024-10-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-29]]): with [[Christian Kirk]] out for the season and [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] hurt, Jacksonville will ask Davis to play a bigger role, but Harmon expects 'the Gabe Davis experience' — high volatility, up-and-down production and up-and-down individual play — attached to an offense that is itself inconsistent. He is explicit that Davis cannot do what Thomas does, so this is not a like-for-like promotion. He also stacks schedule against it: Eagles, Vikings, Lions and then Texans after the bye, with the Eagles defense improving and the Vikings secondary having 'put better quarterbacks than [[Trevor Lawrence]] into the blender.' Net: Harmon does not feel good about the Jacksonville passing game with Davis as the top outside receiver.
- 2025-03-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-07]]): a 'pretty forgettable freaking season' — 10 games, 20 receptions, 239 yards, two touchdowns, a 47.6% catch rate and just 12.0 yards per reception, so he was not even hitting big plays. Harmon says everyone except the Jaguars front office saw it coming and calls the three-year, $39M contract a front-office 'special'; Koh suggests he might be a game-day inactive in 2025. Harmon does allow the original logic: Jacksonville was likely envisioning [[Calvin Ridley]] returning, and their 2023 problem was using Ridley as too static an X receiver, so they went and signed 'the number one sacrificial X receiver on the market'. Ridley never came back, but the one benefit was that Davis absorbing the X role let [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] play flanker off the line of scrimmage in creative ways early in his rookie year.
- 2025-05-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-05-28]]): released by Jacksonville, and Harmon jokes he never once thought of Davis as a Jaguar this offseason — the front office evidently agreed once they drafted [[Travis Hunter]] to pair with [[Brian Thomas Jr.]]. Both hosts agree Davis is no longer an every-down starter; Koh (not tracked) frames him as a bench/depth X only. Harmon likes Las Vegas as a fit specifically as a sacrificial X — a body to clear space so [[Jakobi Meyers]], [[Jack Bech]] and [[Brock Bowers]] can work underneath — and Koh raised the Giants for the [[Brian Daboll]] familiarity, New England, Tennessee and Kansas City. San Francisco worked him out around May 12-13 and has a first-six-weeks hole with [[Brandon Aiyuk]] rehabbing and [[DeMarcus Robinson]] facing a likely three-game suspension, but Davis is himself coming back from a season-ending injury.
- 2025-09-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Davis signing to Buffalo's practice squad is 'probably a nothing burger' — he does not move the needle for Harmon at all anymore and is still coming back from a torn meniscus. What he does bring is special teams versatility and a willingness to play the sacrificial X role better than [[Elijah Moore]] or [[Curtis Samuel]], which could in theory let Buffalo move [[Keon Coleman]] around more; a Davis promotion could also cost one of those two a roster spot. James Koh (not tracked) filed it under 'the year of reunions' alongside the Raiders and Amari Cooper, Adam Thielen and Minnesota, and Brandin Cooks and New Orleans.
- 2025-11-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-17]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman is 'kind of feeling it' on Davis having fantasy value the rest of the way. On tape he was 'the same old Gabe' -- caught one tight slant, dropped another on the very next play, but also worked his way open for [[Josh Allen]] on a nice dig route downfield and had another route where he eventually got open. Waldman said he'll be a preemptive add-column candidate this week, essentially the inverse of [[Keon Coleman]], who's trending toward being droppable.
- 2025-12-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-12-30]]): Davis was a healthy scratch the prior week, came back hoping to provide a spark, and instead ran 16 routes for 1 target and 0 catches -- Harmon treats this as further confirmation he isn't part of any answer in Buffalo's receiver room.
- 2026-01-15 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-01-15]]): Davis tore his ACL in the same game as [[Tyrell Shavers]] and is out for the season. Harmon said 'we know Gabe Davis is what he is' talent-wise, but the loss -- combined with Shavers' injury and Josh Palmer already on IR -- leaves Buffalo with what was already the worst outside receiver room in the league now even thinner.
- 2026-06-16 — According to Matt Bove ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-06-16]]): Bove expects Gabe Davis to be back on the Bills roster at some point, currently sidelined recovering from a late injury. He cited Davis' up-and-down Bills tenure -- left, struggled to do anything elsewhere, then came back and was immediately targeted heavily again -- as the clearest example of how much Josh Allen's trust in a receiver (rather than raw talent or separation ability) drives his usage.
