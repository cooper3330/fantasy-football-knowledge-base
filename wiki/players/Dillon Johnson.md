## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-22]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman's fourth name in the class's early-round-talent-caliber group (after [[Blake Corum]], [[Dylan Laube]], and [[Jonathan Brooks]]). Played through a foot fracture and, per Waldman, a quad strain and a knee injury sustained during the CFP semifinal, then played through it into the national championship game. Waldman treats that as directly contradicting an unverified rumor — a leaked/disputed video reportedly of former Mississippi State coach Mike Leach questioning his toughness — since what Waldman actually saw on tape was a hard-nosed, tight-crease runner with some of the best contact balance in the class on both direct and indirect shots. His real reservation is explosiveness: good initial acceleration but not enough top-end speed to consistently turn a good run into a 40-plus-yard gain, meaning his path onto an NFL field depends on out-competing faster backs who lack his instincts and physicality rather than on any big-play trait of his own. Both hosts group him with [[Trey Benson]] as the "need a little nasty" archetype NFL running back rooms value, and Angelo notes how easy it is to overlook Johnson's per-play impact on Washington tape dominated by [[Rome Odunze]]'s much higher profile.
