# Jordan Addison

> ⚠️ Takes below are from 2023. Team/role are as of that date and are almost
> certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2023-12-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2023-12-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: **feeling it** with Nick Mullens under center. Describes Addison as a promising young receiver whose reliability as a route runner is what makes a backup quarterback workable. Ruling: knock him one tier off his top-15 WR ceiling, but keep starting him.
- 2023-12-18 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert), same episode: a reversal on his own position. He had "dialed back" on Addison after a lengthy stretch of poor fantasy output, and says the error was looking only at box scores — he'd missed that Addison had climbed out of the No. 3 role in the rotation, and that "he's very good, eyeball test." A clean example of the evaluation failure mode in [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]].
