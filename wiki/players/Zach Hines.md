## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: has to stop overextending against Division I defensive ends to hold position on cutoff blocks — when he gets his hands right he's strong enough to maintain position and move his feet, and has an easier time on back-side cutoff blocks than on the front side, where he still needs work. Not an elite athlete but a bigger frame; Waldman's bottom line is he has to become a very good in-line blocker to make the NFL.
