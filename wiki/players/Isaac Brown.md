# Isaac Brown

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-10-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Felix Sharpe of Campus2Canton (not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-23]]) [Dynasty]: Louisville true freshman Isaac Brown (5'9\", 195 lbs, Homestead FL, No. 25) is a must-add in Campus to Canton leagues. Sharpe's numbers: 68 carries for 564 yards (8.3 per carry) and 20 receptions for 115 yards, leading the team in carries in each of the last four games and leading ALL true freshmen in rushing yards, despite upperclassmen Maurice Turner (hurt) and Miami transfer Don Chaney in the backfield; an early-season rotation has become essentially exclusively Brown. Waldman saw the Miami game in person and describes a dynamic one-step speedster winning on outside (and what looks like inside) zone, used out of the backfield on wheel routes in Brian Brohm's prolific offense. Caveats: a three-star recruit the legacy services did not rate, undersized at 195 (two-three years to reach the 200-pound threshold), and he can be taken to the ground by the first secondary player who reaches him. Comps offered: [[Bucky Irving]] (a bit more slippery), [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] (a different animal), and [[Sean Tucker]] as a true-freshman-flash precedent.
- 2025-08-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-15]]) [Dynasty]: discussing the 2026 college class, Waldman says that if the comparison is [[Bucky Irving]], he would like Brown more than Irving right now, as good as Irving looked. He calls him a very good receiver with possibly a little Clinton Portis to the real skill sets he shows, and is very impressed with his ability to catch and track the ball. He describes a creative open-field runner who nonetheless sees things unfolding well and is disciplined about making decisions within the scheme, with the ability to get his feet up over people, quick-twitch and sudden with very good stop-start movement and efficiency. Caveat on eligibility: Waldman has not charted him yet because Brown was a true freshman last year, and although some sites list him as draft-eligible, Waldman did not have him pinned as eligible or worth consideration at this stage.
