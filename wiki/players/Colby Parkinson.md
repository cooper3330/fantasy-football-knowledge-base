- 2025-12-09 — According to Jeff Bell (Football Guys, not tracked) and Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Bell's #3 Week 15 waiver add (3% rostered) -- Parkinson has been tied for the Rams' team target lead since Week 10 with Tyler Higbee out, in what both hosts call one of the best red-zone-throwing offenses in the league. Both agree the role is somewhat touchdown-dependent and fluky (roughly a 2-3 catch/game floor), but Harris says when he finalizes his tight end rankings, Parkinson will likely land close behind Mike Gesicki, just above Pat Freiermuth.
- 2025-12-15 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Patrick Doherty of Rotoworld (untracked guest, on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-15]]): Parkinson keeps scoring touchdowns and even had a deeper completion this week rather than his usual five-yard catch-and-fall-down plays. Harris says he's resisted trusting Parkinson as more than a touchdown-dependent player but 'he keeps catching touchdowns' -- both hosts theorize it's a 'Devante effect,' where defenses double- and triple-teaming [[Davante Adams]] at the goal line left Parkinson repeatedly open; Doherty calls him a classic 'Tier 2 touchdown desperado' benefiting from attention on the true Tier 1 target. With Adams likely out, they wonder whether defenses will now account for Parkinson directly, but conclude that amid a deep, mediocre tight end field ('no real difference between Hunter Henry at TE14 and A.J. Barner at TE22'), he's simply worth starting while hot.
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and guest Dave Kluge of FootballGuys.com (untracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]): Kluge made the case for [[Colby Parkinson]] (4% rostered) as a Week 16 TE streamer -- averaging a touchdown per game since Week 10, with at least one red-zone target in seven straight games (about two per game). With [[Davante Adams]] out for a couple of weeks, Kluge said Parkinson becomes the primary red-zone target for a Matthew Stafford offense that wants to throw to a big body near the goal line, putting him in the back-end TE1 streamer conversation despite being touchdown-dependent. Harris resisted recommending Parkinson for weeks but relented, comparing him to Robert Tonyan-style touchdown vulture in a good offense (the show's 'Tunyon' archetype) and noting he's paid real FAAB to roster him in a deep league. Parkinson was Kluge's No. 4 waiver add and Harris's No. 5.
- 2026-06-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-29]]) [Dynasty]: Likes Colby Parkinson in the crowded Rams TE room, noting he led targets, receptions and touchdowns among the group last year, including a stretch starting around Week 10 with touchdowns in three straight games, a quiet Week 13 against Carolina that cooled bandwagon sentiment, then a touchdown the next week, two more the week after, and another in Week 18. Calls him the 'sneaky guy' in the room even as [[Terrance Ferguson]] carries the bigger-play reputation heading into year two.
