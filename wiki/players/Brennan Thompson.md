- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Thompson has speed and route nuance -- pace and directional changes at the top of his stem -- but he stumbles out of his breaks, which costs him balance, timing on snapping his breaks, and separation he's already earned. Waldman likes the long speed and deep-ball ability but flags the stumbling as a real problem to fix.
- 2026-04-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-16]]) [Dynasty]: Mississippi State transfer (by way of Texas and Oklahoma) Brennan Thompson is a tiny (5'9, 164 -- first percentile weight) but explosive prospect who ran a 4.26 40 (98th percentile) and broke out only in his final season (57 rec, 1,054 yards, 6 TD, 81 yards/game) after three seasons under 250 yards. Seven of his 10 career TDs came on plays of 42+ yards. RP charting: a 70.2% success rate on nine routes (18.5% route participation) that Harmon compares favorably to [[Xavier Worthy]]'s sub-63% mark, plus 82% on posts; overall 71% vs. man, 76% vs. zone (both flattered by his heavy vertical route mix), and a strong 68% vs. press (51st percentile for his size). Major red flag: he is a skewed-alignment receiver, running just under 82% of snaps at right outside receiver, and he ran curls on 39% of routes with little else -- 'all gas no brakes,' struggling to settle against zone over the middle. Harmon questions his after-the-catch ability at his size (went down on first contact on 7 of 12 in-space touches) and flags focus drops. Comps: mock-draft comp DeSean Jackson, plus Harmon's preferred archetypes [[Tank Dell]] and [[John Brown]] over the [[Xavier Worthy]]-style horizontal-stretch outcome. Harmon has him ranked in his 'tier two,' projecting late third to top of the fourth round.
