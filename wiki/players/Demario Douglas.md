## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Adam Harstad** (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: scores almost exactly average (97.5) on Adam Harstad's [[Post-Rookie-Year Receiver Model]] — a solid, if unspectacular, raw yards-per-route-run number (106.1) dragged all the way to league-average by zero receiving touchdowns on the year. Both hosts read him as more valuable than the score implies: on a barren New England offense, a throw to Douglas "wasn't just the best option, it was pretty much the only option to make something positive happen" for the Patriots — Waldman felt he should have scored higher given the degree to which he outclassed his own supporting cast. Verdict: a pause on paying a third-round rookie-draft price, but a clear buy in the fourth or fifth round.
