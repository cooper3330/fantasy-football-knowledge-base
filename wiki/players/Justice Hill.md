# Justice Hill

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-08-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-05]]): asked whether Hill is the handcuff to own behind [[Derrick Henry]], Waldman answered that Hill is the reserve behind Henry but not the one to have. He likes Hill's game — quick in space, overcame an Achilles injury and still looks fairly explosive, can catch, and is a tough runner for his size — but says John Harbaugh's long-standing M.O. is a volume back, so if Henry goes down Baltimore signs a veteran like Dalvin Cook or Kareem Hunt, or finds someone on waivers, to be the do-it-all main back rather than handing the job to Hill.
- 2024-10-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-07]]): Baltimore's use of both [[Derrick Henry]] and Hill is working, and Hill can be plugged straight in as a flex-level play heading into bye weeks and league-wide attrition. Waldman likes that Hill is a shifty, speedy back who nonetheless does not get cute — 'I get the ball and there's a downhill crease and I'm hitting that sucker as hard as possible.' Co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) asked whether a returning Keaton Mitchell upsets the balance; Waldman conceded it is potentially the case but there is no timetable, and his advice was to ride Hill for as long as you can. Context: the Ravens have out-rushed their opponent by at least 100 yards in all five games, though John Harbaugh noted at halftime that blitz and run fronts were making it tough.
- 2025-05-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-19]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Hill is the handcuff Waldman is pairing with [[Derrick Henry]] in most drafts where he lands Henry, getting him at a reasonable late price and noting he carries a little standalone touchdown value. He is candid that Hill would not produce Henry-like numbers but expects good-enough value to keep a roster afloat until a better option emerges; Keaton Mitchell was named as an even later alternative. Waldman's caveat is that Baltimore has a habit of signing veteran backs and could bring in [[Nick Chubb]] or [[J.K. Dobbins]] — either of whom he thinks would deliver RB2 value — if Henry is hurt.
- 2026-07-31 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-31]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris characterizes Justice Hill as strictly a passing-downs role player who would not become the lead back even if [[Derrick Henry]] were to miss time -- he'd remain the change-of-pace piece rather than inherit the job, which is part of why Harris is watching rookie [[Adam Randall]] for any sign of a true complementary role opening up behind Henry.
