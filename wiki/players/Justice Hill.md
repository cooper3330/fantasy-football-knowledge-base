# Justice Hill

## Expert Takes

- 2024-08-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-05]]): asked whether Hill is the handcuff to own behind [[Derrick Henry]], Waldman answered that Hill is the reserve behind Henry but not the one to have. He likes Hill's game — quick in space, overcame an Achilles injury and still looks fairly explosive, can catch, and is a tough runner for his size — but says John Harbaugh's long-standing M.O. is a volume back, so if Henry goes down Baltimore signs a veteran like Dalvin Cook or Kareem Hunt, or finds someone on waivers, to be the do-it-all main back rather than handing the job to Hill.
