# Justyn Ross

## Expert Takes

- 2025-07-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-21]]): after Ross asked the Chiefs for his release, Waldman says 'feel it, in the right circumstance' — but no more than as a WR3 or WR4 right now. He never took the next step in Kansas City and is not supremely fast, just fast enough, so he needs a team that specifically wants a timing route runner who can win the ball, and he has to prove he can develop rapport as an outside receiver. Waldman's named best fit is Pittsburgh: as a big flanker opposite [[DK Metcalf]], winning targets on well-developed timing routes with [[Aaron Rodgers]], he could be a higher-end version of [[Josh Reynolds]] — and the Steelers need depth and could afford to take that shot.
