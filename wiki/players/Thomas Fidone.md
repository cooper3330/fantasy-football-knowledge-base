# Thomas Fidone

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-10]]): Fidone is the most interesting evaluation in the 2025 tight end class. A former four-star recruit at Nebraska who looks 250-260 pounds on tape, he is a good inline blocker with real quickness, wins up the seam and over the middle, catches the ball at difficult angles through contact, and creates after the catch with footwork against three or four unblocked defenders. He then cut about 15 pounds to weigh 241 at the Combine and tested well, which Waldman reads two ways: he may have made weight for the test intending to regain it and play inline, or his knees — he has had two ACL tears, which is why he is under the radar — may simply feel better lighter, pushing him toward H-back. At 255-260 he is more an NFL value than a fantasy one; at 240-245 he could be a low-end fantasy starter at peak. Waldman's comp for that either/or body is [[Keenan Allen]], via Ryan Riddle's old description of a player who could slim down to a quick slot or muscle up to a contested-catch boundary receiver.
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Fidone's tape is full of catches at every angle and window, but Waldman was more impressed by the routes than the hands. He does not have a packed toolbox of ways to sell a stem and set up a break, yet he executes the handful he owns at a high level and they are interchangeable across his route tree: his setups carry the right amount of emphasis, his breaks are clean and crisp, he drops his weight well and makes snapped flat turns — a trait Waldman says most NFL prospects do not display until their third or fourth pro year, if ever. Waldman adds that Fidone could probably handle extended inline work, unlike most of the class, but warns that using him that way would deflate his receiving upside.
