- 2026-05-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-18]]) [Dynasty]: asked whether Blue and [[Phil Mafah]] are worthwhile dynasty stashes, Waldman said fuck it -- beat reporter Nick Harris has already floated [[Malik Davis]] as the presumptive Dallas RB2, which Waldman reads as a sign neither rookie has performed to expectation this offseason. He views Davis as a longtime career backup ('second banana to [[Rico Dowdle]] for years') and doesn't think Davis has improved much, but still calls Blue and Mafah 'wait and see' guys worth cutting now if needed -- if holding Blue specifically for his speed and talent, Waldman says wait until at least August, watching for reports of coaches getting on him about focus.
- 2026-08-03 — According to Ian Hartitz (untracked co-host) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-03 (Feel It or Fk It with Ian Hartitz)]]) [Best Ball]: he'd rather take a late flier on Blue than any of Denver's crowded backfield names, citing a strong offseason, his speed and receiving ability, an Austin Ekeler-style skill set, Blue's own admission he didn't have his 'head on straight' as a rookie, and a good Dallas offensive line behind an uninspiring depth chart (with Phil Mafah as the presumed 'bigger back' default).
- 2026-08-31 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and Bob Harris ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-31]]) [Dynasty]: Blue was cut along with Phil Mafah, surprising both hosts mainly because both backs were let go rather than just one. Bob Harris says positive training-camp reporting had suggested Blue matured and 'turned the corner.' Waldman compares Blue's talent profile to an Austin Ekeler-type back, but speculates the final cuts came down to fit off the field -- how a player gels with the team and contributes in ways beyond snaps -- rather than pure talent, and that the team may have preferred to take chances on other players they scouted in the preseason. Both frame this as a cautionary example of how loudly-praised preseason performances (X/Twitter hype reels against backup competition) can evaporate by final cuts, comparing it to the pattern with Ty Simpson-style preseason overreactions.
