# Austin Reed

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-08]]): a West Florida transfer who led the team to the Division II national championship in 2019, with a style Waldman compares to "David throwing a slingshot at Goliath" — aggressive, urgent throwing motion and velocity, squeezing the ball into windows that don't look open and usually finding a way to complete it. Comps: shares the tight-window, tight-pocket urgency of [[Matthew Stafford]] and Kurt Warner, but isn't as mobile as Stafford or as coverage-savvy as Warner; also carries some of Tony Romo's risk-taking flair. The flip side of the aggression: rushed, off-platform, unnecessary inaccurate throws. Projects him as likely a journeyman — a practice-squad or bottom-of-roster arm, with a shot at a second-contract start if his pre/post-snap coverage recognition develops — "a good quarterback prospect if you're not a fantasy guy," with real fantasy value, if any, three to four years out.
