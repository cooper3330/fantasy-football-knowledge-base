## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-01 — **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-01]] [Redraft (Standard)]: makes the host case for Lamb as the 2024 #1-overall pick over [[Christian McCaffrey]] and [[Tyreek Hill]]. Argues Lamb's usage down the stretch of 2023 "felt more like target totals" than reception totals, and downplays how much the supporting cast (Brandon Cooks, others) matters to his role.
- 2024-01-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]], same episode: ranks Lamb #2 overall for 2024, behind McCaffrey and ahead of Hill — "it's between McCaffrey and Lamb" as the real question at the top of the board, with no specific film mechanism given beyond the ranking itself.
