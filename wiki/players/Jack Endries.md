# Jack Endries

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-11]]) [Dynasty]: Texas's Endries (a Cal transfer who played with QB Fernando Mendoza) is a versatile tight end whose value is mostly as a receiver -- creating openings in zone, gaining yards after the catch, stretching the seam and winning some man coverage in the middle of the field. Waldman comps him as an 'aspiring version of [[Zach Ertz]]' if he lands in a spread system that stays spread; he could add 5-10 pounds to become a competent situational blocker around 250-255, but Waldman groups him with [[Max Klare]] as a player who could get 'lost in the NFL's trends' toward heavier personnel and end up only a situational contributor if the league shifts toward in-line tight ends.
- 2026-04-16 — According to Dalton Wasserman (guest draft analyst, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-16]]) [Dynasty]: [[Jack Endries]] tests as a B/B-plus athlete -- clearly behind the A-level tools of [[Kenyon Sadiq]], [[Eli Stowers]] and [[Max Klare]] -- but Wasserman calls him possibly the best pure hands in the class, a solid blocker with multiple years of production including a strong 2024 season at Cal, plus some slot ability. He projects as a late-day-three (round 3-4) pick with a Dalton Schultz-style outcome as the ceiling: not a game-breaking talent like Travis Kelce, but a reliable every-down tight end. Chris Harris corroborated from personal viewing of Texas games this season, saying Endries was the one Longhorns pass-catcher he trusted to make the catch amid an otherwise messy passing offense.
