# Josh Simon

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Simon is a speedy H-back who is skilled after the catch and does his best work as an outlet player in the flats and around the numbers — off play action, screens and checkdowns — because he is a patient, decisive runner who gets his feet and knees high enough to work over low shots from pursuit. Waldman thinks he could become a competent route runner against both zone and man, but his breaks are where things break down, and while he can catch the ball at a variety of angles his catch technique has too many leaks to call him reliable doing so.
