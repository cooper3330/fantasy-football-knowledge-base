# Mike Evans

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: coming off an 11th straight 1,000-yard season, Evans's 2024 outlook is entirely a situation question rather than a talent one. If he re-signs with Tampa and keeps Baker Mayfield, Waldman would draft him around his 2023 ADP (third round) or "a little above," expecting "going rate will be cheap again." If he leaves via free agency, Waldman turns notably more cautious, noting "these late-career moves don't always work out for the best" for aging receivers changing situations.
- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: "an absolute smash spot" for the Divisional Round at Detroit — the Lions allowed the most receiving yards, second-most receptions, and six touchdowns to outside wide receivers between Weeks 10-17 (bottom-12 in completion percentage allowed). Expects a bounce-back after a quiet Wild Card game, on the reasoning that Evans "rarely almost never has two quiet games" in a row.
- 2024-01-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-23]]): explicit free-agency mechanics after Tampa Bay's Divisional Round exit — Harmon says Tampa "cannot allow him to test the open market," expecting a bidding war (floats a minimum 3-year, $50M deal) from receiver-needy teams if he gets there — the Jets, Kansas City, and especially New England ("going way over the top") are named as plausible bidders, with Detroit's $58.6M in cap space and a stated Dan Campbell-culture fit (never a diva, all business) singled out as the best non-Tampa landing spot, a DeAndre-Hopkins-to-Detroit comparison made and rejected on culture-fit grounds. Overall verdict: Tampa likely retains him given its own $47M in cap space, but it's not close to guaranteed.
- 2024-01-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-29]]): independently floated (alongside [[Tee Higgins]] and Michael Pittman Jr., the latter not a tracked page) as one of the top free-agent receiver names Kansas City could pursue to pair with [[Rashee Rice]] — corroborating Harmon's read from six days earlier that Evans will draw serious outside interest if he reaches the market, now with a specific Chiefs connection attached.
- 2024-01-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-30]]): a new complication on top of the free-agency question — **Tampa Bay OC Dave Canales is leaving to become Carolina's head coach.** Harmon credits Canales's 2023 scheme changes directly for "unleashing" Evans on more big in-breaking and traditional routes rather than the "bunch of go routes" he ran in the prior Byron Leftwich offense, meaning Evans's 2024 outlook is now uncertain on scheme as well as free-agency grounds, even if he re-signs with Tampa.
- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]): reports a "soft deadline" for a new Tampa Bay deal has reportedly passed (per Jordan Schultz, Bleacher Report) and Evans now looks headed to the open market as an unrestricted free agent — the clearest signal yet, in this wiki, that he actually reaches free agency rather than re-signing before hitting it. Logged mainly for its downstream effect: [[Baker Mayfield]]'s own 2024 outlook is explicitly conditioned on Evans staying in Tampa.
- 2024-02-27 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-27]]): fresh 2023 Reception Perception charting shows one of his best seasons — 73.1% success rate vs. man coverage (career best), 75.4% vs. press (second-best of his career, trailing only his 79.1% in 2021), and third-best success rate vs. zone. Credits new OC Dave Canales's route-tree redesign: go routes fell from 27% of his routes in 2022 to 16.5% in 2023, slants rose from 12.6% to 18.5%, and simple outs rose from 8% to 15% — and per Next Gen Stats he actually produced more targets/catches/yards/touchdowns on 20+ air-yard routes in 2023 than in a heavier-go-route 2022, despite running fewer deep routes overall. Calls him an underrated, technically excellent route runner miscast as "just a big jump-ball receiver." On free-agency value: projects a deal north of $18M/year, citing Brad Spielberger's (PFF, not tracked) Kenny Golladay 2021 comp as a floor and Davante Adams's Raiders deal as a ceiling comp.

- 2024-03-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-04]]) [Best Ball]: with his new two-year, $52M Tampa Bay deal now official (a 10-year stay, 10 straight 1,000-yard seasons), projects his 2024 ceiling depends entirely on who throws him the football — a rookie starter likely caps him near 1,000 yards and reads as "a down season"; a veteran addition like [[Kirk Cousins]] could sustain his usual production; [[Baker Mayfield]] returning "could do it again." Notes he's currently going in the fourth round of early best ball drafts and expects the price to keep rising now that the signing is confirmed.
- 2024-03-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-05]]): calls the finalized two-year, $52M deal ($35M guaranteed) "an absolute W for both sides." Fresh full-season 2023 charting confirms one of his best years: 73.1% success rate vs. man (career best), 75.4% vs. press and 77% vs. zone (both second-best of his career), 83% on slants (career high), 86.8% on out routes. Frames the 2022 dip (72.5% vs. zone) as route-tree misallocation under Byron Leftwich rather than decline — the same shape of dip that preceded real cliffs for Allen Robinson (2021) and Andre Johnson (2014) — but credits OC Dave Canales's redesign (slant rate up from 12.5% to 18.5%, out-route rate up to 15%) for the 2023 rebound. Calls him a "stereotyped," underrated technician rather than just a "nine-route specialist," citing a self-driven climb from ~67-69% success his first four seasons to consistently 70%+ since. Also frames the deal as protecting [[Baker Mayfield]]'s outlook, since losing Evans would have left Tampa with only [[Chris Godwin]] — "a really good two, but not a one" — at receiver.
- 2024-06-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-20]]), discussed as a case study for what Dave Canales does with X receivers: Evans' 2022 role under Byron Leftwich/Tom Brady's quick-passing offense pushed his go/nine-route rate to a career-high 27.7% of routes, which Harmon calls a poor scheme fit. Under Canales in 2023, that dropped to 16.5% (still Evans' second-highest, but the lowest since), while his slant rate jumped from 12.6% to 18.5% and his flat-route rate hit a career-high 7.1%, with post, corner and out routes also up. Harmon reads this as evidence Canales and new Carolina WR coach Brad Idzik (both from that staff lineage) know how to diversify a receiver's route tree to maximize quarterback success — the same treatment he expects for Diontae Johnson in Carolina.
- 2024-07-25 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-25]]): Evans is still the best receiver in the NFC South, ahead of [[Chris Olave]], off 79 catches on 136 targets for 1,255 yards and 13 touchdowns — 12-plus scores in three of his last four seasons. The key is Dave Canales' route-tree redesign around [[Baker Mayfield]]: the nine route fell from 27.7% in 2022 (usage Harmon says he has railed against for years) to a tree led, relative to NFL average, by the out route at 15% and the corner at 8.7%, plus more slants to get the ball out fast. Harmon's warning is that if Liam Cohen changes an ounce of that passing structure 'these guys are idiots' — the run game is where tweaks belong. Koh counters with age-31 and post-Canales efficiency risk, comparing it to [[Geno Smith]]'s 2023 slide in Seattle.
- 2024-09-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-19]]): asked to buy or sell [[Chris Godwin]] having a better fantasy season than Evans, Harmon sells — he expects Evans to keep hitting the touchdowns and for production to come back his way, and still calls him the most productive receiver on the team, even while praising Godwin's designed slot out-breaking role.
- 2025-01-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-01-07]]): Tampa Bay declined to kneel and threw Evans one more target to secure his 1,000-yard season, and Harmon pushed back hard on the online dismissal of the streak on 17-game-schedule grounds, noting Evans missed time this year anyway. He stressed that Evans is still playing at a high level at his age while playing the position as a downfield receiver, which he called very unique, and that 11 seasons with one team is remarkable in an era when receivers change teams constantly — contrasting [[Tyreek Hill]] heading to a fourth team and [[Diontae Johnson]] on his fourth in a calendar year. Harmon was less confident in the team, calling the Tampa Bay defense an eyesore, though he saw the Washington wild-card matchup as fairly even and preferable to drawing Philadelphia.
- 2025-01-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-01-09]]): Evans is an honorable mention both against man coverage and as a ball winner — Harmon jokes the ball-winner category could be called the Mike Evans Invitational. He was third in the NFL in first downs per route run against man coverage. Harmon's evaluation is about craft plus retained traits at this stage of his career: he has quietly worked himself into one of the better technicians in the league, while still being pretty damn fast with freaky long strides and freaky long arms. Koh jokes that Evans is so fast his hamstrings will not admit his age; Harmon says he is not sure he agrees with that framing.
- 2025-01-14 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-01-14]]): Evans answered the pre-game hand-wringing about the Marshon Lattimore matchup by going 7 for 7 for 92 yards with a clutch touchdown in the wild card loss. Koh's caveat is age — Evans is 'getting a little bit longer in the tooth' — and with [[Chris Godwin]] most likely moving on, he frames Tampa Bay as potentially a team in transition offensively. Harmon pushes back that this is said about Tampa Bay every year and they just keep winning the division, crediting Jason Licht's drafting ([[Jalen McMillan]], [[Bucky Irving]], Graham Barton) with restocking the roster.
- 2025-02-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-17]]) [Best Ball]: 'absolutely' Evans over [[Xavier Worthy]] at Underdog WR19 — Waldman is taking him 'until the wheels fall off and we see it'. Untracked co-host Bob Harris, whose favorite this pairing was built around, ranks Evans WR10 in his initial Footballguys rankings and told listeners he will not last to WR19 in any draft Harris is in — 'you're officially notified'.
- 2025-02-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-21]]): Evans is the locked-in X in Harmon's projected Tampa alignment stack alongside [[Chris Godwin]] in the power slot and [[Jalen McMillan]] at flanker, a trio he thinks could be a top-five receiver room. He stresses Evans is not alignment-locked — he can play anywhere and has actually been more maximized from the slot in recent seasons than earlier in his career, with both Evans and McMillan under 70% of snaps out wide in 2024. The caveat both hosts raise is age plus injury: James Koh (not tracked) noted Evans and Godwin are both older players, and Harmon agreed that another injury-ruined season from either is where trouble starts for that room.
- 2025-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-07-30]]): Harmon sees no specific reason to call Evans declining — he was very good last year — but he is a 30-plus-year-old receiver on the last year of his deal, and Harmon repeats that the hardest thing in evaluation is forecasting when a receiver hits the age cliff. Evans also missed time with injury and always seems to have a hamstring issue going on. That uncertainty is Harmon's core justification for the [[Emeka Egbuka]] pick: good organizations zero out uncertainties rather than wait to be caught out, as Tampa was last year when losing Evans and [[Chris Godwin]] forced them to run the offense through [[Cade Otton]] for three weeks. Harmon also recalls that Dave Canales unlocked Evans in 2023 by feeding him out routes at the highest rate of his career, because [[Baker Mayfield]] loves that throw.
- 2025-08-18 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: a genuine reversal — Evans is Harris' No. 4 bust after a decade on his sleeper, flag and positive lists, and after Harris says he has made more money on Evans than on any player in the show's history. Evans is outside his top 24 receivers, at WR25, precisely as the rest of the market has come back in and pushed Evans into the third round, the highest Harris can remember. His reasons: late last season the speed looked gone and Evans struggled to separate downfield, with the iconic arm-raise-against-man move now often ending with a defender still draped on him; and Week 18, when Tampa Bay seemed to prioritize getting Evans to 1,000 yards over making the playoffs, felt patronizing, as if the team knew it was the last one. He still concedes 12-touchdown red-zone potential with [[Baker Mayfield]]. Guest Patrick Doherty of Rotoworld (not tracked) agrees the slow stretches — usually injury-driven — get harder to recover from at 32 and that Evans is now needing every trick in a famously savvy bag, but says he has been scarred too often to fade him himself.
- 2025-08-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-20]]): mid-way through charting Evans's Reception Perception sample, Harmon says he is 'still so good' with not a lot of evidence on last year's film that a decline is imminent, while allowing that with a 32-year-old receiver it 'could happen at any moment'. His actual concern is availability — Evans misses time or plays through injuries basically every year. Harmon also frames Evans as one of the best downfield threats the game has seen, which is the backdrop for his view that [[Chris Godwin]] has at times been the more dangerous weapon of the two, and for expecting defenses to pay Evans veteran deference in a way that opens space for [[Emeka Egbuka]].
- 2025-08-29 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-29]]): guest Jeff Bell of Football Guys (not tracked) rates Evans an 8 out of 10 and puts him on his bust list — the mirror of the [[George Kittle]] argument, in that nothing changed about Evans but the price moved from the seventh round to the third. Bell says this is the first year he actually sees the decline people have claimed to see on Evans's film for six years, and that he has been buying every previous year and is not buying this one. Harris rates him a 7 and agrees: when [[Chris Godwin]] was healthy Evans simply was not part of the offense, and after Godwin's injury there was a very concentrated team effort to drag Evans to 1,000 yards — a streak-preservation number Harris will not pay a third-round price for when [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] might quietly do 1,300.
- 2025-09-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Evans's 2024 Reception Perception profile may have been one of his best charting seasons, but Harmon cautioned that a great profile is 'not really an indicator of anything' going forward — he cited saying the same about [[Tyler Lockett]] before 2023, after which Lockett fell off. Evans is a 32-year-old big perimeter receiver, so decline is within the range of outcomes, though Harmon explicitly said he is not predicting a fall-off and thinks Evans can still have a nice year alongside a breakout from [[Emeka Egbuka]]. James Koh (not tracked) noted Evans posted 74 receptions, exactly 1,004 yards and 11 touchdowns on 110 targets in 2024.
