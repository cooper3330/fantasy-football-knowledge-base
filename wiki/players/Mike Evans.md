# Mike Evans

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-01]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: coming off an 11th straight 1,000-yard season, Evans's 2024 outlook is entirely a situation question rather than a talent one. If he re-signs with Tampa and keeps Baker Mayfield, Waldman would draft him around his 2023 ADP (third round) or "a little above," expecting "going rate will be cheap again." If he leaves via free agency, Waldman turns notably more cautious, noting "these late-career moves don't always work out for the best" for aging receivers changing situations.
- 2024-01-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-18]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: "an absolute smash spot" for the Divisional Round at Detroit — the Lions allowed the most receiving yards, second-most receptions, and six touchdowns to outside wide receivers between Weeks 10-17 (bottom-12 in completion percentage allowed). Expects a bounce-back after a quiet Wild Card game, on the reasoning that Evans "rarely almost never has two quiet games" in a row.
- 2024-01-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-01-23]]): explicit free-agency mechanics after Tampa Bay's Divisional Round exit — Harmon says Tampa "cannot allow him to test the open market," expecting a bidding war (floats a minimum 3-year, $50M deal) from receiver-needy teams if he gets there — the Jets, Kansas City, and especially New England ("going way over the top") are named as plausible bidders, with Detroit's $58.6M in cap space and a stated Dan Campbell-culture fit (never a diva, all business) singled out as the best non-Tampa landing spot, a DeAndre-Hopkins-to-Detroit comparison made and rejected on culture-fit grounds. Overall verdict: Tampa likely retains him given its own $47M in cap space, but it's not close to guaranteed.
- 2024-01-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-29]]): independently floated (alongside [[Tee Higgins]] and Michael Pittman Jr., the latter not a tracked page) as one of the top free-agent receiver names Kansas City could pursue to pair with [[Rashee Rice]] — corroborating Harmon's read from six days earlier that Evans will draw serious outside interest if he reaches the market, now with a specific Chiefs connection attached.
