# Cyrus Allen

## Expert Takes

- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Allen is a shifty slot receiver with big-play ability after the catch and vertical skill against zone, best used as an extension of the run game or working across the middle. Waldman thinks he could develop into a viable boundary receiver but has plenty of release/stem/setup details to clean up first, and rates his odds of escaping late-round/UDFA space-player status as small but worth monitoring.
- 2026-04-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-23]]): Allen is his favorite receiver from this year's record 31-WR rookie roundup and he is 'still confused' why Allen was snubbed from the NFL Scouting Combine invite list. Measurables: 5'11', 180 lbs, 4.47 40, 36-inch vertical, 11-foot broad jump. Senior year at Cincinnati: 51 receptions, 674 yards, 13 touchdowns. In a three-game charted sample he posted a 75% success rate versus man coverage and just under 84% versus zone -- both very strong, though Harmon caveats the small sample. He played 90.7% of his snaps in the slot and Harmon expects him to stay mostly slot-based in the NFL. On tape Harmon called him 'dangerous in space,' decisive with good vision after the catch, though not likely to be a high-rate tackle breaker (broken down on first contact on 75% of in-space attempts, breaking multiple tackles on the other 25%). He drew a partial comp to [[Ladd McConkey]] as a yards-after-catch threat via spatial awareness against zone, while noting Allen isn't as fast and McConkey is the better tackle breaker. Harmon grades him as a legitimate day-two prospect, projecting him as high as late round three despite the missed combine invite. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) agreed the combine snub 'must have been an oversight.'
- 2026-06-04 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-06-04]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon liked the fifth-round pick of Allen ('the GOAT') and called him Kansas City's best man-coverage-beating receiver on the roster, though he's still a small, twitchy, explosive archetype -- not the size profile Harmon thinks the league is trending toward. Harmon says Allen could play a real role for the Chiefs in 2026 but is unsure how large.
- 2026-07-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman takes Allen's dynasty potential seriously after a strong camp practice with Patrick Mahomes looking his way, comparing him to a Tank Dell-esque intriguing mode, but says 'fuck that' on 2026 redraft fantasy relevance barring injuries -- Xavier Worthy remains heavily invested in, Travis Kelce still has 'a near telepathic bond' with Mahomes, Jalen Royals and Tyquan Thornton are also ahead of him. Waldman likes that Allen has talked about locking in on Mahomes's exact timing/precision ('this isn't college anymore') and calls it a real dynasty stash, but not a 2026 draft target.
- 2026-07-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-07-30]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon flags a report from Charles Goldman (formerly of Chiefs Wire) quoting Andy Reid saying 'I think Pat's got some trust' in rookie fifth-round WR Cyrus Allen, who is getting first-team reps. Harmon argues the Chiefs' receiver room has a specific hole -- nobody who consistently beats man coverage, not [[Xavier Worthy]] (even accounting for last year's injuries) and not [[Rashee Rice]] (a good zone-beater, weak versus man) -- and Allen graded a 75% success rate versus man coverage in Harmon's charting, which he now states outright (dropping his usual joking 'two beers in me' qualifier) is the best man-coverage mark on the Chiefs roster. He discloses that in his newly updated dynasty rankings, Allen (5'11', 180 lbs, a relatively slow 4.49 40) is the single player he is furthest ahead of the market on, while cautioning it's still just one July 29th first-team-reps report on a Day 3 rookie, and comping the risk to a past crush who never developed, [[Skyy Moore]].
- 2026-08-03 — According to Ian Hartitz (untracked co-host) and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-03 (Feel It or Fk It with Ian Hartitz)]]) [Best Ball]: both went 'fuck it' on Allen being a real fantasy factor even if healthy, citing how rarely Day 3 Chiefs skill picks (Justyn Ross, Carson Steele, Darwin Thompson) have hit; Waldman, who hadn't studied Allen's film closely, still called him at best a large-format dynasty stash worth no more than a dollar or three in FAAB, comping him to an 'aspirational Tank Dell' pre-injury and noting he'd sooner hold [[Jalen Royals]] than drop him for Allen.
- 2026-08-06 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-06]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Angelo is buying the Allen hype out of Cincinnati -- a quick winner who can play inside or out, which matters with [[Patrick Mahomes]] coming off an ACL and needing to get the ball out fast, and with [[Rashee Rice]]'s health uncertain after legal issues and another surgery. Beyond camp buzz, Angelo cites Albert Breer (untracked national reporter) relaying that Chiefs brass called Allen their best camp receiver and compared him to [[Stefon Diggs]], which Angelo weighs more heavily than routine coach-speak. He's uncertain Allen gets day-one opportunity but sees him as the short-to-intermediate answer Kansas City's offense -- abysmal last year -- badly needs to keep Mahomes clean in the pocket.
- 2026-08-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-08-11]]) [Dynasty]: Allen is back at Chiefs practice and reportedly getting first-team reps alongside [[Rashee Rice]], easing an earlier worry that he'd be pigeonholed purely as a slot replacement for Rice. Harmon says he's 'very in' on Allen and wants to see him play more than just the slot given that he grades as Kansas City's best man-coverage-beating receiver.
- 2026-08-20 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-20]]) [Dynasty] [Redraft (Standard)]: Angelo says Allen (out of Cincinnati) looks 'not even day different' at the catch point than in college, with the biggest jump coming from new WR coach Chad O'Shea -- clear separation from KC's other camp receivers in one-on-ones, plus route creativity and a rookie confidence that's 'pretty palpable.' He should win quickly for Patrick Mahomes (coming off an ACL) early this year and could start as WR3/4 before climbing into a second-target role given Xavier Worthy is still working through an 11-month-old shoulder issue and Rashee Rice doesn't look like himself off a second knee surgery in two years. Angelo projects Allen as more of a 2027 focal-point piece for KC, since Travis Kelce may retire and Rice's roster spot is uncertain; Waldman adds nothing to dispute the read and agrees the offensive infrastructure (tier-one Andy Reid system) makes him worth rostering now.
- 2026-08-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Allen is Harmon's guy in Kansas City -- someone he was genuinely excited about as a prospect and believes the Chiefs desperately need for his particular skill set. He jokes that if [[Patrick Mahomes]] returns to peak form, 'Cyrus Allen truthers' will be vindicated and going to the moon.
- 2026-08-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-27]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris flagged Chiefs rookie WR Cyrus Allen -- an unknown name a few months ago in his own rookie draft who has since drawn buzz and praise from Patrick Mahomes in camp -- as a super-deep sleeper he isn't drafting, but would add off waivers if he's on the field a lot in September. He framed the pick against broader skepticism of the Chiefs passing game, noting the team has repeatedly hyped a 'number two' behind [[Rashee Rice]] that never panned out, so he's fading the offense generally while acknowledging Allen would be one of the hottest Week 1-2 pickups if he wins a real role sending targets more than 10-15 yards downfield.
