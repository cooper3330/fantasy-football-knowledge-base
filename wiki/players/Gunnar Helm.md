# Gunnar Helm

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Helm has enough acceleration and short-area quickness to separate on man routes and can challenge zone defenders up the seam, and Waldman groups him with [[Harold Fannin Jr.]], [[Elijah Arroyo]] and [[Tyler Warren]] as prospects a team could maximize as receivers while minimizing blocking to get rookie production. The limitation is top-end explosion — Waldman has seen him flip the field, but more often than not he will not break away from defensive backs or even many linebackers. His technical gap is pacing: Helm has learned stem techniques to manipulate zone defenders facing him, but without artistry in pacing they become too predictable. Waldman's model of good route running is establishing a rhythm, baiting the defender into it, then altering it to make him uncomfortable and second-guess; if Helm learns that, his routes go to another level.
- 2025-09-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-01]]): Waldman thinks it is entirely possible Helm is Tennessee's long-term starting tight end by season's end if [[Chigoziem Okonkwo]] cannot hold the job and be the player the Titans thought he was after his rookie year — but nothing is chiselled in stone, and Okonkwo still has a say. He grades Helm as a Hunter Henry-esque player: fine to draft and start in a given year if it falls that way, but not someone he would trade for in dynasty and not an anchor for a roster. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) framed this season as make-or-break for Okonkwo's extension, with Helm's development feeding directly into that decision, and said Tennessee seems to be gaining confidence in Helm. [Dynasty]
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]): [[Gunnar Helm]] caught a long touchdown from Cam Ward against San Francisco on what Harris called a good throw.
- 2026-01-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-01-26]]) [Dynasty]: feel it -- Helm rose toward becoming Tennessee's TE1 over Chigoziem Okonkwo (who was still making plays) by the end of the season, and Waldman calls him talent- and developmental-wise a future Hunter Henry who can provide real fantasy value.
- 2026-02-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-02]]) [Dynasty]: in discussing the Titans' weapons for [[Cam Ward]], Waldman called Helm 'a good young tight end' as part of the existing skill-position pieces Tennessee has to work with heading into the new coaching regime.
- 2026-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-19]]): Waldman is 'much higher' on Helm than the long-term consensus -- he vaulted Helm up in his end-of-year projections once it became clear Chigoziem Okonkwo wouldn't hold down the Titans' starting TE job, and Helm showed good moments as a rookie. Waldman compares him to a Hunter Henry-type player, meaning he could be replaceable or paired with a complementary tight end rather than a true bell-cow. He currently has Helm in the range of starters in his pre-draft, pre-free-agency winter projections but notes the ranking could move once free agency settles.
