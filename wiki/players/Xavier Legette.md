# Xavier Legette

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-29]]), on Senior Bowl prospects: draws a DK Metcalf comp from most of the industry, which Waldman thinks is defensible — big, physical, fast enough to make plays. His own range puts the outcome "somewhere between Alshon Jeffery and DK Metcalf," which he calls plenty of athletic goods to play in the league barring off-field issues. Names him specifically as part of why he considers this a genuinely strong receiver draft class, independent of what analytics-model-based evaluators might say about him.
- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): a real tempering after live Senior Bowl reps, not a reversal — Angelo: "I was hoping to see more... on tape sometimes it's a great play if you're a guy of that stature, but just everything looked a little too sloppy, a little too forced," and expected more dominance given his size than he actually saw this week. Both hosts keep the DK Metcalf/Alshon Jeffery archetype framing from the prior episode, but now flag real questions about whether he lands at the Metcalf end of that range or slides toward the middle/lower tier.
