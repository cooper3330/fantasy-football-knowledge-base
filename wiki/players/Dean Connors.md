# Dean Connors

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-05 (Going Deep)]]): a small-school back he has been posting about, placed in the [[Austin Ekeler]]/[[DeAndre Swift]] lineage — Ekeler at the apex, Swift below that lacking some of what Ekeler has, and Connors below both. Waldman praises the footwork, hips and acceleration, and how he runs to open space; the gating item is learning to stay with the play design. He is a very good pass receiver, which Waldman says could earn him situational work, and he thinks Connors could stick as a punt returner if he can learn to field punts, because the movement quality is there. Waldman's floor comp is a Myles Gaskin-style situational player, and he is explicit that Connors sits well below the headline names in this class.
- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: a 2026 name he watched and liked without going overboard — at 6-0, 205 he fits an [[Austin Ekeler]]/[[D'Andre Swift]] archetype (Ekeler at the top of that range, Swift in the middle). Waldman praises his running style, quickness, footwork and punt-return aptitude, says he knows when to drop his pads and take what is in front of him but can also freeze and split defenders, and credits a good feel for pursuit. His stated ceiling here is a player who might be able to make a roster.
