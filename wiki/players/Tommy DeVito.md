# Tommy DeVito

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-11-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-18]]): with [[Daniel Jones]] benched — which Waldman said should surprise nobody — the real surprise was DeVito getting the ball over [[Drew Lock]], and Waldman was only mildly surprised. He rates DeVito a good anticipatory thrower who moves well in the pocket, and noted the Giants liked what he showed mentally and conceptually last year. He will not give the vertical opportunities Lock does; Waldman's comp is a Rich Gannon-esque player who can still get it downfield. He read the decision as game-plan-driven, with Lock as the emergency option, and allowed that tanking for a higher pick plus ticket sales to Giants fans excited about a local player may also be in the mix. Aside: Waldman said DeVito is 'way better' than [[Desmond Ridder]].
