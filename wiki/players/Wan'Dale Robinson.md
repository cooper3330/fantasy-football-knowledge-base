## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Dynasty]: cautions that New York's total lack of a passing game inflates how good any Giants receiver can look, but still credits Robinson with real after-the-catch value, solid contested-catch ability, and adequate speed. Projects him as a likely second- or third-most-productive option in the offense if the Giants land a quarterback who can support three or four weapons — his outlook is entirely a quarterback-situation question, not a talent question.
