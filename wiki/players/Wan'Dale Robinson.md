# Wan'Dale Robinson

> ⚠️ Takes below are from early January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-08]]) [Dynasty]: cautions that New York's total lack of a passing game inflates how good any Giants receiver can look, but still credits Robinson with real after-the-catch value, solid contested-catch ability, and adequate speed. Projects him as a likely second- or third-most-productive option in the offense if the Giants land a quarterback who can support three or four weapons — his outlook is entirely a quarterback-situation question, not a talent question.

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): "might be
  the best player" on paper of the Giants' disaster-level receiver room, and
  Harmon says he has "a pretty good understanding" of what he offers — but
  caps the outlook there: "I don't think Wan'Dale Robinson can [beat man
  coverage in the intermediate area] on a consistent route-by-route basis,"
  rating [[Demario Douglas]] ahead of him as a slot option for that reason.
- 2024-07-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-16]]): Harmon said he's 'not that big of a fan' of Wan'Dale Robinson, calling him 'not a needle mover' but 'all right in the slot' -- a complementary short-area piece behind [[Malik Nabers]] rather than a difference-maker.
