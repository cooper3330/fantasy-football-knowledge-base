- 2025-03-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-13]]): Watson, now in Houston, is 'the ultimate sacrificial X receiver'. Koh notes he played the second-most wide receiver snaps in Kansas City last year — only [[Xavier Worthy]] and [[Travis Kelce]] ran more among pass catchers — so his departure opens a meaningful snap share. Harmon's numbers: Watson ran a route on 58.4% of dropbacks and was targeted on just 8% of those routes, against roughly 10% for [[JuJu Smith-Schuster]] and near 24% for [[DeAndre Hopkins]]. Harmon calls it an intense amount of cardio for no footballs, a genuinely strange player type, and wonders half-seriously what Andy Reid does without him. He speculates Watson could let [[Nico Collins]] get out of the extra-receiver role in Houston.
