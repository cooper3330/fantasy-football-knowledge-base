# Garrett Wilson

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-26]]): discussed in the context of the Jets signing [[Mike Williams]] to play X. Wilson has told Harmon directly that he prefers working the slot, where he gets freedom to run post and corner routes as option routes; had big rookie-season plays as a slot receiver but was pushed into more perimeter/X-type usage in 2023 because the Jets had no other real outside receiver behind him (not Randall Cobb or [[Xavier Gipson]], neither a proven perimeter option). Harmon reads the Williams signing as freeing Wilson to move around the formation again, though he'd still want the Jets to add more receiver help even after it — "wouldn't blink" at the Jets taking a receiver 10th overall.
- 2024-03-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]], same episode, discussing [[Malik Nabers]]'s prospect comps: compares Nabers to Wilson as a fellow "walking explosive play," but notes Wilson was the better zone-coverage separator of the two as a college prospect. Recalls having similar "wild horse"/refinement concerns about Wilson's own pre-draft profile, yet Wilson still finished as the top-ranked receiver of his 2022 draft class (ahead of [[Drake London]] and Chris Olave, neither a tracked page) by the end of his rookie season — floated as a hopeful precedent for how Nabers's career could unfold.
- 2024-06-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-13]]): Garrett Wilson's charted numbers are elite-tier despite ugly box-score efficiency caused by Jets quarterback play, not talent -- 95 catches for just over 1,000 yards but only 3 touchdowns and a 56.5% catch rate in 2023, which Harmon attributes to Zach Wilson's pressure issues and the offensive design, not Wilson's game. His success rates are 78.6% vs. man, 82% vs. zone, 81.1% vs. press (93rd-96th percentile). As a rookie he hit 81.2% success rate vs. press, joining CeeDee Lamb, Michael Thomas, Tyreek Hill, Ja'Marr Chase, Justin Jefferson and Odell Beckham Jr. as the only rookies in RP history to clear 80%. In year two he cleared the 95th percentile success rate vs. man, a group that includes Stefon Diggs, Antonio Brown, Davante Adams, A.J. Brown, Justin Jefferson, Tyler Lockett and Terry McLaurin at their peaks. He also posted 91% on curls and 90% on slants (up from 84% as a rookie). Harmon flags a real weakness: contested-catch rate under 70% both seasons on a 27.2%-then-29.4% contested-target rate, and directly comps this to Justin Jefferson's year-two contested-catch dip before Jefferson's year-three breakout under a new coordinator -- floating that Wilson could take a similar leap if he gets even league-average-plus quarterback play (hopefully a full season of Aaron Rodgers). Projects medium 2024 numbers of 86 catches/1,337 yards/9 TD with a 1,700-yard high-side ceiling, the 7th-highest high-side projection at receiver leaguewide behind Ja'Marr Chase, CeeDee Lamb, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Tyreek Hill, Justin Jefferson and Puka Nacua. Not yet placed in Harmon's elite tier -- he wants to see a full statistically dominant season materialize first. [Best Ball] [Dynasty]
- 2024-06-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-18]]): Waldman is buying into Garrett Wilson delivering Davante Adams-in-Green-Bay-era value if Aaron Rodgers returns healthy, projecting top-10 and possibly top-5 fantasy WR numbers even accounting for Rodgers' Achilles recovery, since Wilson has already produced with Zach Wilson, Mike White and Joe Flacco throwing to him. The caveats are big ones - Rodgers staying healthy and an offensive line (with Tyron Smith added) that has to prove it can keep him upright.
- 2024-07-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-09]]) [Best Ball]: Harmon called Garrett Wilson 'the most obvious guy that could break into the elite tier this year,' with man/press/zone success rates of 96th/93rd/82nd percentile that 'check every box' for the profile RP looks for, and put him in the same 2,000-yard-upside conversation as [[CeeDee Lamb]], [[Tyreek Hill]] and [[Justin Jefferson]] alongside [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[A.J. Brown]] and [[Nico Collins]]. His medium projection is 1,337 yards with a high end of 1,700 and a touchdown high-side of 12.3; he noted 1,500 yards wouldn't surprise him even though that's roughly 500 yards above Wilson's 2023 total (~1,050). He thinks Wilson could be further maximized with more slot/flanker usage (more digs, posts, curls vs. his current heavy diet of nines/corners/outs) once [[Mike Williams]] is healthy enough to soak up boundary X routes.
