# Garrett Wilson

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-26]]): discussed in the context of the Jets signing [[Mike Williams]] to play X. Wilson has told Harmon directly that he prefers working the slot, where he gets freedom to run post and corner routes as option routes; had big rookie-season plays as a slot receiver but was pushed into more perimeter/X-type usage in 2023 because the Jets had no other real outside receiver behind him (not Randall Cobb or [[Xavier Gipson]], neither a proven perimeter option). Harmon reads the Williams signing as freeing Wilson to move around the formation again, though he'd still want the Jets to add more receiver help even after it — "wouldn't blink" at the Jets taking a receiver 10th overall.
- 2024-03-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]], same episode, discussing [[Malik Nabers]]'s prospect comps: compares Nabers to Wilson as a fellow "walking explosive play," but notes Wilson was the better zone-coverage separator of the two as a college prospect. Recalls having similar "wild horse"/refinement concerns about Wilson's own pre-draft profile, yet Wilson still finished as the top-ranked receiver of his 2022 draft class (ahead of [[Drake London]] and Chris Olave, neither a tracked page) by the end of his rookie season — floated as a hopeful precedent for how Nabers's career could unfold.
