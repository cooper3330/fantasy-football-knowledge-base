# Jaylin Noel

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24]]): the Iowa State receiver has a lot of skills that could let him start early, and the one thing Waldman flags as likely to hold him back is release work. He already has a variety of moves, but has to learn more of them and add a contrast of patience and suddenness to his footwork so both the new and existing maneuvers carry more craft.
- 2025-04-04 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-04-04]]): Harmon is mid-charting Noel — through game six of the sample at recording time — with a full Reception Perception profile still to come. Context given here is that Noel formed a 'really formidable one-two combo' with [[Jayden Higgins]] in the Iowa State receiver corps, and actually led the team in receiving yards with 1,194 and eight touchdowns of his own while Higgins posted 87 catches for 1,183 and nine scores. No success-rate numbers or tier grade were shared for Noel on this episode.
- 2025-04-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-04-08]]) [Dynasty]: full Reception Perception prospect chart, and Harmon is emphatic — a fringe top-five wide receiver in this class for him, versus 76th on The Athletic's consensus big board, which he calls hilariously too low. Profile: 5'10", 195, 4.39 forty, 41.5-inch vertical, 11'2" broad, and 23 bench reps, which James Koh (co-host, not tracked) notes led all receivers by a wide margin and tied [[Donovan Edwards]] for the most among running backs. Charting: 74.1% vs man, 81.8% vs zone, 68% vs press on a real 12.6% sample; 94% success on the dig, 83.7% on the post (digs and posts together about 20% of his tree), 77.3% on the out, 58% on the nine (prospect average, good for his size), slightly below average 73% on the curl; 19.2% contested target rate with an 80% contested catch rate. Harmon grades him in the same tier as [[Ladd McConkey]] (74.6% vs man) and [[Ricky Pearsall]] (75.8%) were as prospects, a true vertical slot rather than a bunny-hop slot, playable as a flanker and in two-receiver sets. Caveats: wasted steps off the line slow his release, and he is a slightly weaker zone beater than McConkey or Pearsall. Koh agrees he is the better Iowa State prospect but concedes he leans sizeist. Career comp: [[Josh Downs]]; a listener comp to Emmanuel Sanders is judged athletically light but a fine career outcome.
- 2025-04-14 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-14]]): asked how much the three-hundredths-of-a-second speed gap to [[Arian Smith]] should matter, Waldman says not at all — 'Jaylin Noel is excellent' and is 'definitely not Mecole Hardman part two.' His framing is that Noel is a discount version of [[Matthew Golden]]: you can get him a round or two later and feel like you got the better deal overall. He reserves the Hardman comparison for [[Jaylin Lane]] instead.
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): Bob Harris (co-host, not a tracked expert) put it to Waldman that he is lower on Noel than the field. Waldman clarified that he likes Noel and has him at a borderline starter grade — the speed is nice, the hands are good — but small things keep him below other receivers, specifically a few corrections needed in his route running against zone. He expects Noel to be a good player and one who could rise on his board with landing spot and draft capital, in the same way he described [[Cam Skattebo]]. The reason Noel sits where he does is methodological: Waldman deliberately ranks on current and projected on-field skill without accounting for draft capital, so a listener can spot a sixth-rounder graded on par with players taken two or three rounds earlier and target that value late.
- 2025-04-22 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-04-22]]): Noel is his best overall route runner in the 2025 class, narrowly over [[Jack Bech]]. Both separate largely with route running and are close versus man, but Noel has the edge versus zone — roughly 74% success vs man and almost 82% vs zone — which Harmon credits to an unusually good understanding of when to settle, throttle down and tempo routes to widen the throwing window on posts and digs. The deciding tradeoff went the other way on releases: Bech was the better press beater at 79.4%, almost ten percentage points clear of Noel, and Harmon thinks Bech has the better set of press release moves; Noel's pitter-patter off the line at 5-10, 195 is the fixable flaw, with Harmon comping the possible improvement to how [[Tyler Lockett]] became a better press beater in the NFL than he was in college. James Koh (not tracked) said it is 'clearly' Noel and noted he put up 23 bench reps despite short arms, so he is stronger than his size suggests.
- 2025-04-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception - 2025-04-28]]) [Dynasty]: Noel going 79th overall to Houston, reuniting him with Iowa State teammate [[Jayden Higgins]], is Harmon's favourite part of an A-plus Houston draft. He notes 79th is the exact slot [[Josh Downs]] went in two years earlier — Downs being Harmon's stated comp — and treats it as the league repeating a size-ism error, since some defensive coaches said Noel, not Higgins, was the receiver they worried about. His charting: roughly 74.1–74.5% success rate versus man coverage, right up there with [[Ladd McConkey]] last year, so he can win down the field and does not have to be a slot-only player. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) argues Noel was simply the more important receiver to Iowa State than Higgins, floats the [[Tank Dell]] role, and thinks he can play outside. Harmon goes further: he expects Noel to be the most consistent and perhaps most explosive slot player [[C.J. Stroud]] has worked with, no disrespect to [[Stefon Diggs]], and says there is a chance Noel is just better than Higgins.
- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: a rookie-draft value being forgotten in the third round — Waldman took him at the equivalent of pick 22-23 in a 32-team league, versus an ADP he saw as low as 309 in one draft. The bull case is Houston's vacated volume: with [[Tank Dell]] confirmed out for 2025 and, in the hosts' view, unlikely to return to form, [[Brandon Angelo]] counts roughly 145 vacant targets in an offense that should again throw 500-plus times. Waldman expects Noel to beat out [[Christian Kirk]] for the slot role by midseason at the latest, and Angelo thinks Houston prefers feeding Noel over an aging [[Dalton Schultz]]. Both note Noel also opens things up for [[Nico Collins]] and [[Jayden Higgins]] rather than merely competing with them.
- 2025-06-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-06-02]]): Noel is behind [[Jayden Higgins]] in both dynasty and redraft because Higgins fits the outside role opposite [[Nico Collins]] and is more versatile for Houston's current needs. But Waldman is explicit that the gap is about opportunity — if [[Christian Kirk]] goes down, Noel 'could wind up being as big of a producer' as Higgins. He frames Noel as good enough to be an interesting waiver-wire play in-season, or a late pick worth making in deeper drafts, and groups him with Kirk and Higgins as Texans pass-catchers who are 'all great values off the radar.' [Dynasty]
- 2025-08-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-01]]): Harmon compares Noel to [[Josh Downs]] a couple of years ago — same size profile, taken at effectively the same draft slot (both around 79th overall) — and says Downs has been a steal and Noel will be one too. Charting shows an 84th-percentile success rate versus man and strength on the corners, outs and comebacks that gave [[Tank Dell]] his verticality, and Harmon insists he is not a slot-only player. The caveat is opportunity: with [[Christian Kirk]] likely starting in the slot if healthy and [[Jayden Higgins]] the other outside starter opposite [[Nico Collins]], Harmon says he does not see how Noel gets on the field right now, but 'talent finds a way' — if not this year then in time. Koh argues Noel may simply be the better player than Higgins, pointing to more receiving yards at Iowa State.
- 2025-08-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-29]]): Harmon likes Noel 'a lot more as a prospect' than teammate [[Jayden Higgins]] and now puts them at closer to even odds to be the more productive rookie — a deliberate correction of priors that had bowed to draft capital. He does not see Noel as slot-only and expects him to work into the flanker mix; he is the player who best replaces what Houston lost in [[Tank Dell]], and both hosts called him twitchy and explosive. Koh (not tracked) added that Noel led Iowa State in receiving yards over Higgins, who missed time injured, and that coaches said they were more scared of Noel. Near-term caveat: Harmon expects veterans [[Nico Collins]], [[Christian Kirk]] and [[Xavier Hutchinson]] to open the season as the three-receiver set.
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-05]]): Noel is a really good prospect who went too late in the NFL draft, and Harmon said it is very live that he is simply better than teammate [[Jayden Higgins]] despite the draft capital gap — 'the draft capital zombies and zealots will not love this,' but capital is an indicator, not a linear good-to-bad ordering. He cited an 84th percentile success rate versus man coverage and said parts of the Reception Perception profile reminded him of [[Ladd McConkey]], though McConkey was more pro-ready; he sits on that McConkey / [[Josh Downs]] axis of smaller receivers typecast as slot-only despite strong intermediate and downfield work. Harmon argued Noel is the one in the room who could replace what [[Tank Dell]] offered as a vertical outside-the-numbers threat from the slot and flanker. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) added that Noel is exceptionally twitchy in the 10-to-15-yard range, has real play strength, and out-produced Higgins in yards and touchdowns on the same college team.
- 2025-09-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-09]]): Noel ran just 11 routes in the opener, tied with fellow Iowa State rookie [[Jayden Higgins]] and well behind Nico Collins (27), Xavier Hutchinson (21) and Justin Watson (17), even with Christian Kirk out. Harmon read it as Houston simply not trusting its rookie receivers yet and said he did not see Watson playing ahead of the rookies coming; he was confused by the usage and wondered whether it related to wanting more run-game help to alleviate pressure.
- 2025-10-09 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception - 2025-10-09]]): Noel set a season high at 32% snaps (21% at halftime of a blowout), and the encouraging part was intent rather than volume — Houston designed a goal-line play for him and he slipped into the end zone for his first touchdown. He is the slot focus alongside [[Christian Kirk]], where his fellow Iowa State teammate [[Jayden Higgins]] is the boundary player. Harmon expects a rotation rather than a takeover in year one, with [[Xavier Hutchinson]] and Kirk still holding roles, but thinks both rookies matter long term for this club and named the post-bye return as the thing to watch.
