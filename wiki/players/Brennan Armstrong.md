## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-08]]): a former Virginia quarterback who transferred to NC State for 2023 and was benched for part of that season. Grades him as a developmental option for a league other than the NFL at this stage — a lefty with an elongated throwing motion and a stance that needs to narrow to generate more acceptable velocity and accuracy. His anticipation as a thrower is good in short ranges of the field, "but that's pretty much all." Needs to unlock more arm talent before he can develop elsewhere and become a viable passer.
