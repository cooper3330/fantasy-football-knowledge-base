# Darren Waller

## Expert Takes

- 2025-09-30 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-30]]): Waller played his first game for Miami on Monday night and scored twice — a fourth-and-goal from the five where [[Tua Tagovailoa]] launched one up to the crossbar and Waller went up, got it and got his feet down, and a second on the drive where [[Tyreek Hill]] was injured. Harris ranks him fourth on his Week 5 waiver list; guest Jeff Erickson (RotoWire, not tracked) has him fifth at 7% rostered, saying he wears 'the cone of shame' for it but that Waller will at least be a red zone target and upside is hard to find at tight end. Harris's framing: tight end is fantasy's dumbest position, so Waller can be your 'Tanya' — the guy you add because he just did good, probably won't repeat, but might latch on.
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-06]]): Waldman feels the player but not the deployment. Waller looked good working backside slants, seam routes and out routes at a variety of levels, and his snaps doubled from 16 in Week 1 to 32 as Miami said they would, but he drew zero targets in the entire second half with all of the production coming in the first half — which Waldman called pretty incredible. He warned this may stay a slow burn where you get the highlight scoring plays rather than a stable weekly floor, and that the constraint is Mike McDaniel. He suspects McDaniel saw the mismatch value [[Jonnu Smith]] gave him last year and hopes he figures out the different mismatches Waller offers. Bob Harris (not tracked) said he blew his entire waiver budget in one league on Waller because his team was on life support, in a 1.5 PPR league.
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: it was shaping up to be the Waller game — first pass of the day to him, a 34-yard seam stretcher, a fourth-down conversion at the end of the first quarter, a 34-yard double-move catch in the second and a red-zone touchdown off it — and then he never caught, or was even thrown, another ball after roughly the 10-minute mark of the second quarter despite playing 16 more snaps. Harris guesses Carolina clamped down on him and notes the broadcast's report that Waller is on a pitch count and will not play every down. With [[Tyreek Hill]] done for the year, Harris positions Waller as the Dolphins' clear No. 2 pass catcher behind [[Jaylen Waddle]], and 'seemed to be planned number one' in the first half — he wants to see whether that becomes a full-game thing. Second touchdown in two weeks.
- 2025-10-13 — According to Chris Raybon (guest on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast]], not a tracked expert; [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-13]]): Waller was quiet for most of the game but scored a touchdown, and Raybon flagged a real trend -- Miami is now consistently using Waller in the red zone, a role he says Waller rarely got on his prior, worse offenses. Raybon compared it to a 'Jonnu Smith-type' role and said he expects it to continue as De'Von Achane's ground game (a career-high rushing day in this contest) takes pressure off the passing concentration, positioning Waller as the tight end beneficiary in the scoring zone even as Jaylen Waddle's target share may dip slightly going forward.
- 2025-10-21 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-21 (Week 8 Waivers & W7 Reviews)]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waller left the game right before halftime with a pectoral injury, never even targeted once beforehand, and did not return. Harris said it's reportedly not season-ending but he probably won't play next week, and framed the injury as potentially ending his run as a productive fantasy tight end this season given how unreliable Miami's offense has become around him.
- 2025-10-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-28]]): Waller is on IR with a chest/pectoral injury (severity, including whether it's torn, unclear to Harmon and Koh), out at minimum the next three games. Harmon reiterated a stance from last year that Miami's offense is more efficient when tight ends are de-emphasized -- with Waller out and his replacement barely used, Miami leaned harder into wide receivers and, per Harmon, looked notably better for it in Week 8.
- 2025-12-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-01 (Feel It or Fk It)]]) [Redraft]: Waldman is fully bought in on Darren Waller as a potential league-winner, projecting him as Miami's second-most-impactful receiver -- possibly even a '1B' -- now that he's back, noting Waller already put up 47 yards on two catches in his return game after missing several weeks. He said [[Tua Tagovailoa]] looks comfortable throwing to him and credited Mike McDaniel as a coach who is 'way more smart when he has all the pieces,' using Waller as a chess piece the way he used [[De'Von Achane]] last year. Waldman added he'd heard from Dolphins reporter Travis Wingfield that McDaniel, who seemed likely to be fired, may now stick around, adding stability to the passing-game plan built around Waller.
- 2025-12-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waller had two long gains against the Saints on blown coverage and was left wide open for what should have been a touchdown late, only for [[Tua Tagovailoa]] to overthrow him. Two catches for 47 yards isn't eye-popping, but Harris said 'you can easily tell me a story' that Waller can serve as an emergency streaming tight end off this tape.
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]): [[Darren Waller]] caught two garbage-time red-zone touchdowns against Pittsburgh as the Dolphins' offense stalled clock management down big. Harris said Waller will probably be a top-10 TE for him in Week 16 against a Bengals defense that's 'very gettable' via tight end in warm weather -- the difference between the No. 10 and No. 24 TE is basically random, so he'd take the slight scale-tip toward Waller and recommends starting him unless there's a clearly better option.
