# Darren Waller

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-09-30 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-30]]): Waller played his first game for Miami on Monday night and scored twice — a fourth-and-goal from the five where [[Tua Tagovailoa]] launched one up to the crossbar and Waller went up, got it and got his feet down, and a second on the drive where [[Tyreek Hill]] was injured. Harris ranks him fourth on his Week 5 waiver list; guest Jeff Erickson (RotoWire, not tracked) has him fifth at 7% rostered, saying he wears 'the cone of shame' for it but that Waller will at least be a red zone target and upside is hard to find at tight end. Harris's framing: tight end is fantasy's dumbest position, so Waller can be your 'Tanya' — the guy you add because he just did good, probably won't repeat, but might latch on.
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-06]]): Waldman feels the player but not the deployment. Waller looked good working backside slants, seam routes and out routes at a variety of levels, and his snaps doubled from 16 in Week 1 to 32 as Miami said they would, but he drew zero targets in the entire second half with all of the production coming in the first half — which Waldman called pretty incredible. He warned this may stay a slow burn where you get the highlight scoring plays rather than a stable weekly floor, and that the constraint is Mike McDaniel. He suspects McDaniel saw the mismatch value [[Jonnu Smith]] gave him last year and hopes he figures out the different mismatches Waller offers. Bob Harris (not tracked) said he blew his entire waiver budget in one league on Waller because his team was on life support, in a 1.5 PPR league.
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-07]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: it was shaping up to be the Waller game — first pass of the day to him, a 34-yard seam stretcher, a fourth-down conversion at the end of the first quarter, a 34-yard double-move catch in the second and a red-zone touchdown off it — and then he never caught, or was even thrown, another ball after roughly the 10-minute mark of the second quarter despite playing 16 more snaps. Harris guesses Carolina clamped down on him and notes the broadcast's report that Waller is on a pitch count and will not play every down. With [[Tyreek Hill]] done for the year, Harris positions Waller as the Dolphins' clear No. 2 pass catcher behind [[Jaylen Waddle]], and 'seemed to be planned number one' in the first half — he wants to see whether that becomes a full-game thing. Second touchdown in two weeks.
