# DeVonta Smith

## Expert Takes

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-11 (Going Deep - Fave Evals, Coach-Killer Candidates, and High-End Career Outcomes)]]), part of a discussion on how few "WR2s" are actually capable of being a true WR1 (see [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]] and [[Tee Higgins]]): names Smith as one of the very few who can — "he can be awesome anywhere in the league" — pointing to his 900+-yard rookie season with Jalen Hurts and a thin supporting cast as proof of concept. [[Matt Waldman]], same episode, pushes back with real reservations: doesn't think Smith is physical enough to consistently win tight coverage at the boundary, questioning whether he can separate and earn position there. Still calls him arguably "the best two in the league," while noting the field hasn't actually answered whether he can be a true WR1, since he's spent his whole career playing next to [[A.J. Brown]] in Philadelphia and "might be in Philly forever."

- 2024-04-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15]]: on his new 3-year, $75M extension ($51M guaranteed) — reads it as Smith making "the smart" choice to commit long-term to a great situation rather than force a trade the way [[Brandon Aiyuk]] and [[Tee Higgins]] have been doing elsewhere. Views the perception that Philadelphia's passing game "wasn't great" in 2023 as more about inflated preseason expectations than an actual decline, with Smith playing a valuable, if secondary, role to [[A.J. Brown]] rather than being miscast as a true WR1.
