# DeVonta Smith

## Expert Takes

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] on *Going Deep* ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-11 (Going Deep - Fave Evals, Coach-Killer Candidates, and High-End Career Outcomes)]]), part of a discussion on how few "WR2s" are actually capable of being a true WR1 (see [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]] and [[Tee Higgins]]): names Smith as one of the very few who can — "he can be awesome anywhere in the league" — pointing to his 900+-yard rookie season with Jalen Hurts and a thin supporting cast as proof of concept. [[Matt Waldman]], same episode, pushes back with real reservations: doesn't think Smith is physical enough to consistently win tight coverage at the boundary, questioning whether he can separate and earn position there. Still calls him arguably "the best two in the league," while noting the field hasn't actually answered whether he can be a true WR1, since he's spent his whole career playing next to [[A.J. Brown]] in Philadelphia and "might be in Philly forever."

- 2024-07-02 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-02]]) [Dynasty]: DeVonta Smith's 2023 season showed career-low success rates versus man, zone and press coverage, though each mark stayed within 2.5% of his career best and his press success rate landed at the 82nd percentile -- still elite -- so Harmon isn't 'raising alarm bells.' Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) expect new OC Kellen Moore to reinstall Smith as the boundary/X press-beater -- 78%+ success versus press in all three seasons, 90% success on slants -- while [[A.J. Brown]] handles the pre-snap motion/slot role, since Moore's past five offenses have each produced a top-20 fantasy WR.

Harmon's biggest concern is fit around him -- Philadelphia's receiver room (Smith, Makai Lemon, Dontavian Wicks) has no one over 205 lbs and only Wicks over six feet, raising questions about whether it matches Jalen Hurts's tighter accuracy window; co-host James Koh (not tracked) goes further, arguing the roster construction suggests the front office either misjudged what Hurts likes or doesn't view him as a true franchise quarterback. Harmon names Smith, alongside Ladd McConkey, as one of the two best 'this year's JSN' breakout candidates in the league.
- 2026-08-06 — According to JJ Zachariason (not a tracked expert; guest) and [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-06]]) [Redraft]: Both ranked Smith a top-12 receiver (Zachariason WR10, Harris WR12) with AJ Brown gone from Philadelphia and the passing offense being rebuilt rather than simply redistributing Brown's old work. Harris said re-watching Eagles tape this summer, defenses rolled coverage toward Smith far more often than toward Brown last season, arguing that reflects who defenses actually consider more dangerous regardless of which player is faster; he called Smith one of the best route runners in the league, an efficient speed-changer and strong ball-tracker who doesn't tense up over the middle despite his small frame, adding he's been a personal draft-guide target for years. Zachariason agreed, calling Smith a baller who generates YAC through big plays rather than broken tackles, while acknowledging his size and durability remain a real, unresolved risk that makes him a high-variance asset either way.
- 2026-08-10 — According to Bob Harris and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-10]]) [Dynasty] [Best Ball]: both hosts think Philadelphia's passing offense will only be marginally better than last year, not the leap ADP expects. Waldman's core concern is Smith's size -- 'he's a shrimp' who plays bigger than his frame but still gets pressed and pushed around in key physical moments -- and that no one on the roster replaces the way AJ Brown used to take defensive heat off him. Waldman likes [[Makai Lemon]] as a good young player with moments and sees Eli Stowers as a future factor, but views the rest of the receiving corps (including Marquise Brown) as matchup-dependent options who can disappear for stretches. When Smith gets schemed away, Waldman expects to see 'the worst of Jalen Hurts.' As a result, neither host is buying much of anyone on this offense at current ADP except [[Saquon Barkley]] and [[Tank Bigsby]], and even Barkley is 'a little high' for Waldman right now.
- 2026-08-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Cousin Josh (untracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-14]]): Harris gave Smith a 3, Josh a 2, viewing him as the Eagles' now-clear WR1. The main risk flagged is whether the Eagles' passing game already peaked; Josh actually views a lingering [[Makai Lemon]] injury as a mild positive for Smith's target share and notes people forget Smith won a Heisman.
