# Jake Ferguson

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-09-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-23]]): Ferguson is back and ready to roll after his injury, and stands to be a real factor because Dallas projects to throw the ball a great deal this year rather than run it. Waldman's evidence was tape: back-shoulder play after back-shoulder play, including a matchup against Roquan Smith in which Smith 'looked overmatched.' He said Ferguson looked good, full stop.
- 2025-05-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-19]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Ferguson is the cheap tight end Waldman is comfortable investing in, projecting 94 targets — third most on the team — with 65 catches (four more than [[George Pickens]]), 623 yards and five touchdowns, since he is not stretching the field the way Pickens will. That is a low-end TE1, not high-end TE1 value, and Waldman says there is no other non-rookie tight end he would make the same argument for. Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked) framed the price: TE9 in 2023, knee injuries in 2024, but fifth-most targets among all tight ends and second-most on Dallas in the six games he and [[Dak Prescott]] shared the field; projected TE13 and drafted TE15.
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-05]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Ferguson was more involved in the Week 1 opener than [[George Pickens]], but Harris frames the volume as misallocation rather than a green light — he counted three fairly deep seam shots to Ferguson where the ball was well thrown but a defender was 'on Ferguson like a blanket,' so 'it probably shouldn't have been thrown.' One of those was a post to the end zone on which Ferguson took a headshot and set Dallas up at the one. Harris' summary: 'I sure wish Jake Ferguson was fast,' and he would rather see [[Dak Prescott]] pushing contested balls down the field to Pickens. Real target share, but earned on throws Harris thinks were mistakes.
- 2025-09-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-23]]): Harmon is candid that he is 'still not quite sure how good Jake Ferguson is' as a player, but the volume case with [[CeeDee Lamb]] out on a high ankle sprain is not in doubt — Ferguson 'was already getting pummeled with targets' and is 'definitely going to get pummeled with targets now.' Harmon's framing separates the projection from the evaluation: Dallas is very light at receiver behind [[George Pickens]], [[Jalen Tolbert]] and [[KaVontae Turpin]], and the quick in-breaking work that Lamb and [[Dak Prescott]] used to run automatically has to go somewhere.
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-06]]): Ferguson scored his second little dump-off flat touchdown of the day and now has 41 catches on the season — of which Harris 'dare say zero of them impressive.' The take is volume-as-floor at a bad position: 'that's a lot of catches, and Jake Ferguson certainly can be your tight end.'
- 2025-10-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and Bob Harris (untracked co-host) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-20]]): Ferguson beat Jaguars safety Andrew Wingard deep for a touchdown after Wingard again bit on a play fake, echoing the same coverage bust that gave up 63 yards to [[A.J. Barner]] the week before -- another data point that any tight end facing Jacksonville with even modest snaps is a streaming start while Wingard remains on the field.
- 2025-12-12 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-12]]): Harris said flatly he hopes fantasy managers are no longer excited by Ferguson, arguing his earlier-career production numbers overstated how good a player he actually is; Ferguson was limited Wednesday and then didn't practice Thursday with a calf issue, and Harris flagged a midweek downgrade like that as 'never great' for availability.
