## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Adam Harstad** (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: scores in the bottom tier of Adam Harstad's [[Post-Rookie-Year Receiver Model]] (a historically bleak range for future production), but both hosts single him out as one of the two names from that tier worth owning cheaply. Waldman calls him "a good all-around route runner" who is presently the "Amari Cooper-esque hopeful" behind the actual Amari Cooper in Cleveland's receiver room — the framework being that a young receiver waiting two-to-three years to force his way past an established veteran usually doesn't pan out, because the front office tends to draft his eventual replacement before he breaks through. Waldman personally rosters him in a deep dynasty league and would add him for free off waivers, but isn't paying up.
