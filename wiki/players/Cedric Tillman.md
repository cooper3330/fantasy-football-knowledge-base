# Cedric Tillman

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Adam Harstad** (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: scores in the bottom tier of Adam Harstad's [[Post-Rookie-Year Receiver Model]] (a historically bleak range for future production), but both hosts single him out as one of the two names from that tier worth owning cheaply. Waldman calls him "a good all-around route runner" who is presently the "Amari Cooper-esque hopeful" behind the actual Amari Cooper in Cleveland's receiver room — the framework being that a young receiver waiting two-to-three years to force his way past an established veteran usually doesn't pan out, because the front office tends to draft his eventual replacement before he breaks through. Waldman personally rosters him in a deep dynasty league and would add him for free off waivers, but isn't paying up.

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: names Tillman (with [[Elijah Moore]]) as one of the two in-house options who could push [[Jerry Jeudy]] down to a clear third receiving option in Cleveland.

- 2024-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-21]]): a brief passing mention as Cleveland "drafted Cedric Tillman last year in the third round" and calls him "an interesting player with some interesting upside," grouped with [[Elijah Moore]] and [[Jerry Jeudy]] as pieces the Browns are throwing at an unsettled passing game rather than a set hierarchy.
- 2024-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-23]]): Tillman gained buzz starting in place of the holding-out [[Amari Cooper]] during OTAs and minicamp, and Harmon thinks that fits because he was a decent pure X prospect — a one-side-of-the-field player who took 90.7% of his charted 2021 snaps on the right outside and lined up on the line of scrimmage 86.7% of the time. Charting: 68.5% success rate vs man and 78.7% vs zone (both okay rather than special), but a standout 90.9% contested catch rate on a good volume of contested targets. That makes him the odd man out stylistically in a room where Cooper is a separation-speed X and [[Jerry Jeudy]] and [[Elijah Moore]] are interchangeable flanker/slot types — Harmon says a strong camp could push Tillman ahead of those two and free Cleveland to do more with Cooper. James Koh (not tracked) cited 6'3', 215 lbs, a 4.50 forty and a 37-inch vertical as more than enough size and athleticism to play outside, calling him a good depth piece for the room.
- 2024-08-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-12]]) [Dynasty]: he liked Tillman coming out of Tennessee and is more curious about him than about newly acquired [[Jerry Jeudy]]. Waldman sees Tillman as the receiver who could eventually take over for [[Amari Cooper]] in Cleveland — maybe not as good a route runner as Cooper, but at the very least a better version of [[Josh Palmer]]. He hedges the whole projection on environment, calling Cleveland a weird place for football at this point.
