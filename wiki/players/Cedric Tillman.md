# Cedric Tillman

> ⚠️ Takes below are from January 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-01-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Adam Harstad** (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Post-Rookie Model)]]) [Dynasty]: scores in the bottom tier of Adam Harstad's [[Post-Rookie-Year Receiver Model]] (a historically bleak range for future production), but both hosts single him out as one of the two names from that tier worth owning cheaply. Waldman calls him "a good all-around route runner" who is presently the "Amari Cooper-esque hopeful" behind the actual Amari Cooper in Cleveland's receiver room — the framework being that a young receiver waiting two-to-three years to force his way past an established veteran usually doesn't pan out, because the front office tends to draft his eventual replacement before he breaks through. Waldman personally rosters him in a deep dynasty league and would add him for free off waivers, but isn't paying up.

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: names Tillman (with [[Elijah Moore]]) as one of the two in-house options who could push [[Jerry Jeudy]] down to a clear third receiving option in Cleveland.

- 2024-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-21]]): a brief passing mention as Cleveland "drafted Cedric Tillman last year in the third round" and calls him "an interesting player with some interesting upside," grouped with [[Elijah Moore]] and [[Jerry Jeudy]] as pieces the Browns are throwing at an unsettled passing game rather than a set hierarchy.
- 2024-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-23]]): Tillman gained buzz starting in place of the holding-out [[Amari Cooper]] during OTAs and minicamp, and Harmon thinks that fits because he was a decent pure X prospect — a one-side-of-the-field player who took 90.7% of his charted 2021 snaps on the right outside and lined up on the line of scrimmage 86.7% of the time. Charting: 68.5% success rate vs man and 78.7% vs zone (both okay rather than special), but a standout 90.9% contested catch rate on a good volume of contested targets. That makes him the odd man out stylistically in a room where Cooper is a separation-speed X and [[Jerry Jeudy]] and [[Elijah Moore]] are interchangeable flanker/slot types — Harmon says a strong camp could push Tillman ahead of those two and free Cleveland to do more with Cooper. James Koh (not tracked) cited 6'3', 215 lbs, a 4.50 forty and a 37-inch vertical as more than enough size and athleticism to play outside, calling him a good depth piece for the room.
- 2024-08-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-12]]) [Dynasty]: he liked Tillman coming out of Tennessee and is more curious about him than about newly acquired [[Jerry Jeudy]]. Waldman sees Tillman as the receiver who could eventually take over for [[Amari Cooper]] in Cleveland — maybe not as good a route runner as Cooper, but at the very least a better version of [[Josh Palmer]]. He hedges the whole projection on environment, calling Cleveland a weird place for football at this point.
- 2024-10-17 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-17]]): Tillman is the most interesting Browns receiver post-[[Amari Cooper]] trade and the only genuine X candidate in the room. His college RP profile drew a [[Courtland Sutton]] comparison — a run-of-the-mill NFL X — and Harmon says you could squint and see a poor-man's [[George Pickens]] or a really poor-man's [[Tee Higgins]]. At 6-3, 215 he is comfortably the biggest receiver in the room with low-4.5 speed, which Harmon calls 'fine.' The charting highlight is an 80.6% success rate on the curl route, and Harmon wants exactly that combination: threaten vertically as the boundary X, snap back on the curl, which is 'where healthy offenses live' in the modern NFL. His current 55.9% outside / 44% slot split is not the right deployment; he needs to be a vertical downfield ball winner. Standing caveat: [[Deshaun Watson]] makes any of this hard to bank on.
- 2024-10-22 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-22]]): asked whether Tillman is already better than [[Jerry Jeudy]], Harmon says it's 'well within the range of outcomes' and wouldn't be surprised if Tillman ends up the best receiver on the team — partly because his opinion of Jeudy is low. He had flagged Tillman as the obvious X replacement after the [[Amari Cooper]] trade because nobody else on the roster ([[Jerry Jeudy]], [[Elijah Moore]], Jalen Darden, Jamari Thrash) profiles as an outside X. Week 7: 12 targets, 8 catches, 81 yards, 76.6% of snaps out wide and 21.9% in the slot per PFF. Harmon sees him fitting the [[Tee Higgins]] / [[Courtland Sutton]] X archetype but wants to see him stack weeks before committing; James Koh (not tracked) called it only his first game of true involvement.
- 2024-10-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-28]]): among the Cleveland pass catchers now that [[Amari Cooper]] is gone, Tillman has the highest ceiling but roughly the second lowest floor, because he is still an inexperienced pro who will have pronounced ups and downs. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) supplied the usage: target shares of 25% and 22% in the two games without Cooper, totalling 47 fantasy points over that span — against the 43.6 points Cooper scored across his previous five Browns games (a stat Harris credited to JJ Zachariason, also not tracked). Waldman warned the schedule matters more than the target share here: [[Chris Olave]]'s Saints and the Chargers are vulnerable, but Cleveland then runs into Pittsburgh, Denver, Pittsburgh again and Kansas City before a Cincinnati/Miami reprieve to close.
- 2024-10-29 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-29]]): Tillman's emergence is a role unlock, not a sudden development — he is primarily an X receiver, which was [[Amari Cooper]]'s job, so he was never going to play over [[Elijah Moore]], who wins differently; Harmon says beat-writer talk about Tillman as the third receiver misread inter-receiver positions. With Cooper gone, Harmon charted him and stands by his pre-draft comp of [[Courtland Sutton]] — specifically pre-ACL Sutton — because Tillman beats man coverage, releases against press and is excellent on in-breaking routes, the same slant/post/over-route profile Sean Payton wants from a big-body X. He pushes back on people comping him to [[Nico Collins]]: Collins is a top-five NFL receiver with explosive ability Tillman may not have. Fantasy read: likely the lead receiver for Cleveland rest of season, still cheap on waivers this week.
- 2024-10-31 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-31]]): Tillman is quickly becoming [[Jameis Winston]]'s primary target and is now Cleveland's starting X. Angelo calls him 'a dang good player' whose college injuries robbed everyone of seeing his ceiling at Tennessee, and notes the Browns moved him around in the Ravens game and he made plays from the slot. Waldman agrees Tillman is the top receiver of the two over [[Elijah Moore]], and explains the mechanism: Stefanski's under-center play action gets Cleveland into cover-one and cover-three looks where a safety-rotation read produces one-on-ones, and Tillman ran a lot of slants into those openings. Both frame him as a real piece of a Browns offense that should create explosive plays down the stretch.
- 2024-11-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-05]]): Harmon says the Tillman breakout is real — 6-of-11 for 75 yards and a touchdown, and he considers Tillman a legitimately good starting X receiver who can hold that job down. He repeats his pre-draft comparison of [[Courtland Sutton]], noting people gnash their teeth at it but that he thinks it sticks; he is unsure Tillman ascends beyond that into a future star. Caveats he flags himself: the touchdown came in pure garbage time, which counts in fantasy but not in real football. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) agrees on the player but calls part of the production a mirage, citing start-stop issues in Tillman's game and a brutal remaining schedule for an outside X — after the Week 10 bye it is New Orleans, then Pittsburgh, Denver, Pittsburgh and Kansas City.
- 2024-11-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-21]]): with [[Jameis Winston]] under center the Browns passing game finally has a 'real quarterback', and Tillman looks like a real deal X receiver — Harmon says every time he watches him he feels better and better about the [[Courtland Sutton]] comparison. He works the perimeter well, can high-point the football, and Harmon thinks he also looks good on in-breaking routes. Tillman is the X who mostly stays on the outside while [[Jerry Jeudy]] plays flanker and moves inside and [[Elijah Moore]] works primarily from the slot. Harmon's caveat is that Winston is a particular type of quarterback who over-inflates the statistics of an entire passing game — maybe not the success, but the counting stats — so he frames the whole Cleveland breakout as a 'Winston scam' he is nonetheless happy to take, comping it to [[Chris Godwin]]'s 2019 season.
- 2025-03-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-05]]): Harmon 'really, really liked' what he saw from Tillman in 2024 — 50 catches, and he looked like a true X receiver who could beat man coverage, win contested situations and make plays after the [[Amari Cooper]] trade. He noted he had a [[Courtland Sutton]] comp on Tillman during his prospect season and thinks he played a bit like that in his 2024 stint, and would pencil him in as one of Cleveland's starting outside receivers. Two caveats: the sample was cut short by a scary week 12 concussion after which Tillman never played again, and Koh characterizes him more as a 'hope guy' in a room short on high-end talent and cap resources.
- 2025-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-21]]): Harmon says he really likes Tillman, and that is the reason he put Cleveland in the softer bucket of teams who should only take a receiver if the right one falls rather than the must-draft-one-early group. He would not want to walk into 2025 with [[Jerry Jeudy]] penciled in as the number one and calls Jeudy a viable starter rather than an answer, but with Tillman and [[David Njoku]] already there, and with the quarterback situation completely unresolved, he argues Cleveland has bigger problems than receiver — comparing it to people saying the Colts need a tight end when they already have plenty of players not getting the ball. James Koh (co-host, not tracked) disagrees, saying the Browns belong in the must-take-one-early bucket given their roster construction.
- 2025-04-18 — According to guest Cory Kinnan (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Harmon]]'s show ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-04-18]]): Tillman 'is very much a sacrificial X' in Harmon's terms — he was coming along at the position once Jeudy's Achilles-injured teammate went down and [[Jameis Winston]] started letting it rip, but in a projected room with [[Travis Hunter]] and [[Jerry Jeudy]] he is the piece who absorbs the hard outside alignment rather than the one who gets fed. Harmon accepts the label as a fit for his own 'sacrificial X' concept.
- 2025-05-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-05-07]]): Tillman is one of the most interesting players to consider this offseason, and Harmon thinks the market is forgetting his mid-season stretch precisely because it happened mid-season — invoking Rich Hribar's line that people only remember how a player started or finished. He charted well in Reception Perception during that run and put it in the box score, and Harmon would deploy him as the true backside X running in-breakers, with [[Diontae Johnson]] in the slot and [[Jerry Jeudy]] on vertical routes. Co-host James Koh (not tracked) supplied the athletic and statistical case: 6-3, 215 pounds, a 4.54 forty and a 37-inch vertical, and a four-game stretch from Week 7 through 11 averaging 10 targets, six receptions and 75.5 yards per game with three touchdowns. The concern both raised is health — the concussion that effectively ended his season from Week 12 on — and Harmon cautioned that the Browns' 2024 receiving numbers were partly a product of the Jameis pump-and-dump.
- 2025-05-30 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-05-30]]) [Dynasty]: Harmon is 'really, really interested' in Tillman, the Browns receiver he is confident will actually be on the roster, and has him in tier six. He thought Tillman showed well in his first few games in an expanded role and that his Reception Perception profile looked like that of a starting X receiver. Tillman is on the year-three charting list dropping June 9, but Harmon flags the profile will be weird and truncated: Tillman barely played early in the season, only got the target bump after [[Amari Cooper]] was traded, and then lost the back half of the year to a scary week-12 concussion. There will be enough charted routes for a full profile, but Harmon says the open question is how much to take away from it.
- 2025-07-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-07-16]]): Tillman is the forgotten man of 2024 because his good stretch came in the middle of the season rather than at the start or end. Through weeks one to six he was the fourth receiver behind [[Amari Cooper]], [[Jerry Jeudy]] and [[Elijah Moore]], oddly taking 43% of snaps in the slot; after the Cooper trade he took only 19% slot and became the X, ending at 73.4% outside and 63.7% on the line. Harmon charts a 70.2% success rate versus man, 76.6% versus zone, 76.9% on the dig (16.4% participation) and 83.6% on the slant (28.2% participation) — a big X who wins on in-breakers with fluid route setup — plus fewer than 2% drops and four of five contested targets caught. He offers [[Courtland Sutton]] as a skill-set comparison rather than a projection. Koh (not tracked) highlights weeks 7-11: 40 targets over four games, 24 catches, 302 yards, three touchdowns, a 17-game pace near 1,300 yards, before a concussion ended his season. Harmon notes Tillman's production nearly matched Jeudy's over weeks 7-12 yet he is going outside the top 60 receivers, and that the four-way Browns quarterback battle is the risk.
- 2025-08-13 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-13]]): Harmon feels good about Tillman as a starting-level X receiver in the [[Michael Gallup]] mold, with a ceiling comp of [[Courtland Sutton]] if things break right; neither host claims to know his true ceiling given the injury-shortened sample and concussions. Charting: 70.2% versus man (acceptable for an X), a below-77% zone number Harmon calls fine but improvable, a 16.4% dig rate at 76.9%, and standout slant work — 28.2% of routes at 83.6% success. Alignment settled after the [[Amari Cooper]] trade: 43% slot from weeks one to six, just 19.7% from week seven on, 63.7% on the line and 73.4% outside. In that weeks 7-12 stretch he out-targeted [[Jerry Jeudy]] 44 to 42, with 330 yards to 397 but more receptions and two more touchdowns, a 17-game pace of roughly 1,100 yards and 10 scores. Harmon is not eager to invest in the 2025 Browns at all, but if forced he takes Tillman, undrafted in Yahoo leagues, over Jeudy's top-100 price.
- 2025-09-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Tillman is the number one waiver name for both Harris and guest BRANDON FUNSTON of The Athletic (NOT a tracked expert) — the one place they fully converge. The July storyline was that the Browns quarterback competition mattered mostly because [[Joe Flacco]] winning would bump the receivers, and Harris says fair play, Tillman did get a bit of a bump. Funston points to Tillman's hot stretch with [[Jameis Winston]] last year producing huge numbers, calls him an interesting downfield threat still working toward his upside, likes his opportunity as the clear number two alongside [[Jerry Jeudy]], and says he much prefers Flacco to [[Dillon Gabriel]] or [[Shedeur Sanders]] out of the gate even if he does not love the overall situation. Harris agrees, saying not every team in a 12-team league can own a Tillman but at least one should — a good IR-spot add to see how Week 1 goes, not a Week 1 starter.
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-05]]): Harmon is buying Tillman as a starting X receiver and calls him an interesting sleeper, but selling the broader idea most analysts have that Cleveland's pass catchers get a [[Joe Flacco]] bump. He noted the Week 1 Bengals matchup is the softest defense on the schedule until Miami in Week 7, but the run after it is brutal — Baltimore, Green Bay, Detroit, Minnesota, Pittsburgh — and the Browns offensive line has serious questions at tackle. Koh (not tracked) argued that if any Browns receiver benefits from Flacco throwing bombs under center it is Tillman, liked him in DFS for Week 1, and thought Tillman is the one who could break off a single big play even against Minnesota's cover-zero blitzing. Harmon agreed on the skill-set fit and said he hopes he is wrong on the offense.
- 2025-09-09 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-09]]): Tillman played well up to the ending — a third-quarter red-zone touchdown as the primary read, another crossing catch downfield — before dropping a short out that popped into the air for a game-ending interception, which Harris says he either catches or bats down. He put Tillman third on his waiver list. Guest Jake Ciely of The Athletic (not a tracked expert) had him second, arguing his role is '100% locked in' as one of the top two Cleveland options and possibly the top one all year, and that if [[Joe Flacco]] were guaranteed 17 starts he would argue hard for Tillman over [[Quentin Johnston]]; Ciely also asked whether anyone would truly be shocked if Tillman out-scored [[Jerry Jeudy]] on the season. The stated risk is Cleveland falling out of it and turning to [[Dillon Gabriel]]. Ciely adds [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] does not threaten Tillman's role.
- 2025-09-11 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-11]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Guest Jake Trowbridge (not a tracked expert) flexes Tillman at 4,300 dollars and says the price is crazy — it went down after a week in which Tillman looked pretty darn good against Cincinnati, and Cleveland should be throwing a lot against Baltimore. Trowbridge does not care whether Tillman or [[Jerry Jeudy]] is the 1A; he nearly used Tillman in Week 1 and pivoted to Jeudy at the last moment.
- 2025-09-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-18]]): mildly bullish. Cleveland is using Tillman as the flanker with [[Jerry Jeudy]] at X, and Harmon thinks that is backwards on fit — Tillman is the big-bodied receiver he would call an X — but the flanker role is working for him because it moves him around, buys favorable matchups and frees him from press so he can run big in-breakers. Tillman has a higher slot rate than Jeudy, leads the team in touchdowns (one on a deflection) and has seven catches for 74 yards through two weeks. Harmon's caveat is explicit: 'I'm not going crazy about the Tillman stuff' — it is the deployment that is interesting, not yet the production.
- 2025-09-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-29]]): Tillman is 'not a bad player at all,' but he came from a Tennessee scheme very different from NFL reality and still had more to learn, which is why Waldman gives [[Isaiah Bond]] the upside edge now. A hamstring issue may open the door for Bond to take the job. Bob Harris (not tracked) adds that he has liked Tillman's upside only in a very narrow band and that 'availability is still the best ability' — something Tillman has not demonstrated.
- 2025-09-30 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-30]]): Tillman caught a pass down to the one on Cleveland's opening touchdown drive against Detroit but hurt his hamstring, and Harris relays that it sounds like he is out multiple weeks.
