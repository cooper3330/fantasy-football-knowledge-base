- 2025-01-23 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-01-23]]): Golden is the safest player in the 2025 receiver class and Angelo's favorite route runner in it — good at all three levels, with a [[Chris Olave]] archetype comp in the sense that a healthy Olave could start for essentially any NFL team and produce, though Golden is a little more abrupt than Olave. He praises the bevy of releases, the ability to keep defensive backs guessing by throwing a lot at them, and the quality of his stem. [[Matt Waldman]] independently puts Golden in his top tier of route runners in the class and agrees he is on the short list, naming him alongside [[Travis Hunter]] and [[Luther Burden III]] as the players in the top-12-of-2024 conversation. Both frame him as the pure-value pick over [[Tetairoa McMillan]].
- 2025-02-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-24]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman has Golden as probably a top-five receiver on his board and one of the safer ones in terms of skills that translate to the NFL, preferring him to teammate [[Isaiah Bond]]. Guest Dwain McFarland (Fantasy Life, NOT tracked) agrees and explains why the model likes him: Golden played with [[Tank Dell]] as a Houston freshman, transferred to Texas, and had to beat out Bond and Helm for targets, so his adjusted production is credited for target competition; he did very well with limited opportunities, and the model's program-quality component rewards being recruited to Alabama-tier and title-contending programs. McFarland notes this 'did great with his chances despite modest raw production' profile is what historically caught [[George Pickens]] and [[Terry McLaurin]], two hits the market missed.

- 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24]]): the Texas receiver attacks the ball at its earliest window when it arrives at the numbers, can turn behind the break path and catch with overhand position away from his frame, and transitions downhill obeying the ball in one smooth motion. Waldman singles out his tracking — good at following the ball over one shoulder and rotating his body to the opposite side as it arrives — and credits good hand-eye coordination. In a class where Waldman flagged clap attacks and hand position as the dominant flaw across dozens of prospects, Golden's capsule is pure praise at the catch point. See [[Catch Technique and Ball Tracking]].
- 2025-03-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-26]]) [Dynasty]: Golden (5'11″, 191 lbs, 4.29 forty; 58-987-9 final year at Texas) is a 'very useful NFL player' whose best trait is the contested-catch game — 83.3% contested catch rate on 22.6% of sample targets — despite his size, with a 7.4% drop rate Harmon attributes to focus drops on layups. He clears deep and intermediate route averages (80.6% on digs) but only 68.5% vs man and sub-70% vs press, a weakness he says a team must scheme around. Comps: rookie-year [[Jordan Addison]] on route profile, and vs-man rates matching [[Tank Dell]] (68.7%) and [[Xavier Worthy]] (68.4%) — better ball tracking than Worthy, less separation than Dell.
