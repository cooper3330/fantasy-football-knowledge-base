# Calvin Austin III

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-06-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-10]]): named Austin as what people thought Wan'Dale Robinson would be -- he called Austin the better of the two -- saying Austin has impressed every camp and could be the next Doug Baldwin for Russell Wilson: a small, explosive route-runner who wins contested catches and is the fastest player in Pittsburgh's receiving corps. If Wilson builds rapport with him, Waldman sees Austin as a cheap bet to lock down real playing time as Pittsburgh sorts out its WR2 spot behind [[Denzel Mims]] and Van Jefferson.
- 2024-07-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-04]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said Austin was the same physical archetype as [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] at Memphis but the better player -- more capable against man coverage, able to win on the outside, and slightly faster -- though injuries and a carousel of poor quarterback play limited him to flashes as a rookie and last season. Angelo compared Austin's size/speed/contested-catch profile to Tutu Atwell and argued the swing factor is scheme: if Arthur Smith borrows Sean McVay's motion-heavy usage of similar players, Austin could become a dynamic movement-Z threat, but Smith's run-first, low-creativity tendencies (cited: minimal passing-game use of Bijan Robinson) instead favor [[Roman Wilson]], a blocking-first 'dirty work' receiver, as the better scheme fit for now. Both flagged Austin's usage as a real swing factor for his 2024 outlook.
- 2024-07-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-08]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Asked to revisit a question from the Fantasy Football Expo, Waldman said he's 'feeling it' on [[Calvin Austin III]] at his current price, calling him a quicker, faster version of Wan'Dale Robinson who wins more consistently at the boundary in man coverage. He noted Austin has been hampered by injuries and poor QB play in Pittsburgh, and that new OC Arthur Smith rarely motioned receivers in Atlanta only because Desmond Ridder struggled to read the field -- with [[Russell Wilson]] under center, Waldman thinks Austin could be used more like a 'wannabe Doug Baldwin.' He flagged this as a change from his prior, more skeptical Expo answer, and noted the WR2 job behind Austin (Van Jefferson vs. others) is still unresolved.
- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Reacting to a report (Mark Kaboly, The Athletic) that Calvin Austin III had an impressive camp, Waldman agreed it's legitimate and not just a small-pads mirage -- his one-on-one outside ability for a smaller receiver has shown up on film in pads before, both in Pittsburgh camps the past couple seasons when healthy and back at Memphis. He expects the staff to find him a role and believes [[Russell Wilson]] will like him enough to target him all over the field.
- 2024-07-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-29]]) [Best Ball]: Austin is Waldman's late-round bet in Pittsburgh. He argues Austin and CFL standout Khalil Pimpleton were always better than [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] at the same physical profile — Austin more explosive, and unlike Robinson able to win one-on-one outside in college, beat press, go up for the ball and run vertical routes. With [[Van Jefferson]] unreliable at the catch point and [[Roman Wilson]] unready, Waldman thinks Pittsburgh may end up leaning on the guy they discount for being short and small; Russell Wilson himself compared Austin to [[Tyler Lockett]], and Waldman's own comp is Doug Baldwin. Arthur Smith's heavy motion usage, McFarland notes, is exactly the design that would unlock him. McFarland has Austin projected for essentially nothing and isn't recommending him, but says he may sprinkle him into last-round best ball picks.
