# Hunter Renfrow

> ⚠️ Takes below are from April 2024, while he was an unsigned free agent —
> treat as historical context until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-04-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) on *Reception Perception: The Show* ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-04-11]]), buy/sell on a reported link to Kansas City: both buy, cautiously. Harmon says he likes Renfrow and thinks he can play for a lot of teams, and believes he got iced out over his last couple of seasons under the Raiders' prior coaching staff. Notes his best season included some flanker-type snaps, not purely slot, and thinks Andy Reid could get the best out of him — but both hosts worry Kansas City is already getting "too slotty" as a receiver room with [[Rashee Rice]] and [[Marquise Brown]] and would rather see the team add a true outside X receiver who can beat man coverage.
