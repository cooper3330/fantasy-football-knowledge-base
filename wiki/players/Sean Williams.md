# Sean Williams

## Expert Takes

- 2026-04-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-13]]) [Dynasty]: he considers the Kansas back underrated -- a 5'9', 218-lb 'bowling ball' with legit speed, acceleration, vision and receiving ability who could already contribute off a weaker bench right now. Says Williams' blocking is closer to committee-tier than people realize, and that situational deployment playing to his strengths could make him effective immediately with room to keep improving -- ranks him ahead of [[Jonah Coleman]] in that same range of the class. The bigger question is makeup, not conduct: per his trainer, Williams arrived at Kansas from Iowa out of shape and 'uninspired' before quickly regaining fitness and love of the game. Waldman frames the open question as whether NFL teams read that as a maturity rebound worth trusting or as a red flag that he'll disengage again once football stops being fun -- he did not get a combine invite.
