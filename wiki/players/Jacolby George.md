# Jacolby George

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): asked whether the Miami receiver is an aspiring [[Jordan Addison]], Waldman said he could be, with a long way to go. George can work the middle of the field and is quick and fast enough to work downfield as either a slot or a flanker. The open question is how good a route runner he really is — Waldman thinks Miami's offense limited the evidence, with heavy RPO and four-verts usage, though the same offense gave the Hurricanes receivers a lot of practice working off-structure with their quarterback, so he is reasonably decent there. Waldman's range runs from a Travis Benjamin type who gets downfield from the slot, gives big plays and contributes in the return game, up to an Addison-like outcome who could even be a WR2 for a team. He later used George as his worked example of a grade that needs depth-chart circumstances to convert into fantasy value.
