# Davante Adams

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024. Team/role are as of that date and
> are almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded
> by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his name as
> "Devontae Adams"; normalized here to his real name, Davante Adams. The
> same garble appears — as an unrelated joke, not an evaluative take — on
> the [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-05]] source page.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-12]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: "feeling it" on Adams staying with the Raiders now that Luke Getsy has been hired as offensive coordinator, on the strength of their prior Green Bay relationship — "Getsy and Adams know each other. Adams is going to feel a little more comfortable." States he'd feel good about Adams's fantasy value "regardless of quarterback," with a specific ADP value call: currently going in the late second round, and Waldman says he is "chasing it ever downward every chance I get" as the price keeps dropping — an explicit buy signal on a falling ADP.
- 2024-05-14 — According to Adam Rank ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-14]]), not a tracked expert: ranks [[Davante Adams]] as his No. 6 receiver overall, behind Tyreek Hill, Justin Jefferson, Ja'Marr Chase, [[DJ Moore]] and [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], stressing he isn't calling Adams a 'bum' — just that he's 'looking forward, not looking behind' given Adams' age. Rank says he wouldn't push back hard against someone ranking Adams as high as No. 4.
- 2024-08-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-01]]): Adams is clearly the best receiver in the AFC West and still a top-three NFL receiver — Harmon's order is [[Justin Jefferson]] first, [[Tyreek Hill]] second on the back of a career year and field-flipping ability, Adams third. The 2023 charting: 85th percentile vs man (his lowest in recent years), 91st vs press, 90th vs zone, with man success at 79.6% in 2021, 78.1% in 2022 and 76.1% in 2023 and press still 80.3%. Route rates remain elite — 95.7% on curls, 91.7% on outs, 89.8% on slants, 87% on posts, 81.8% on digs with digs at 20.4% of his routes — while comebacks, corners and nines are down, hinting at lost vertical juice. Harmon says aging-curve analysis is the work he is least confident in. Both hosts note the Netflix 'Receiver' fallout over Jimmy Garoppolo, and Harmon says he now better understands why Adams preferred [[Aidan O'Connell]], who will stand in the pocket and push it downfield. Koh has Adams over [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] in fantasy on 175 targets; Harmon disagrees.
- 2024-10-03 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-03]]): Adams stating a trade 'preference' out of Las Vegas is unsurprising at 32 on a team going nowhere, but Harmon doubts the Raiders get the second-round pick plus they are asking for. He is still a very good receiver and a coverage dictator — 2023 Reception Perception showed 76.1% success rate vs man and 80.3% vs press, a tick below his 99th-percentile career bests, with 2024 marks at the 85th percentile vs man, 90th vs zone and 91st vs press, which Harmon calls 'grade-A alpha dog' but a half-step below the very top tier. Vegas has used him on a narrow vertical X tree: over 30% corner/nine/post, 20.4% digs, 22% slants, with a 34% first-read target share. Harmon's favorite landing spot is the Rams (an X next to [[Puka Nacua]] and a slot [[Cooper Kupp]], with [[Matthew Stafford]] able to push it downfield); Koh ranks the Rams first too but likes a Jets reunion and the Chargers next. Both note the deadline is late this year and the trade may never happen.
- 2024-10-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-07]]): with Adams wanting out of Las Vegas, the hosts treat the Jets as the obvious destination — the joke being whether the on-field general manager has as much control over the front office as everyone suspects. Waldman still places Adams in the conversation for the best receiver in football, at least on the short term, alongside [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Justin Jefferson]], [[Malik Nabers]] and [[Tyreek Hill]]. On the trade landing spot, his view is that Adams would be a net positive for [[Garrett Wilson]]'s development. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) added, half-joking, that Adams came off as a jerk on the Netflix receiver show, with both hosts noting that diva behavior largely comes with the position.
- 2024-10-22 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-10-22]]): Adams' first big showcase with the Jets on Sunday night was 'objectively better' than having [[Mike Williams]] out there, mostly for the space he created rather than his own line. Harmon says you could feel it on the broadcast — Adams running boundary routes drew two defenders that way, which bought [[Garrett Wilson]] more one-on-one looks, gave [[Allen Lazard]] room over the middle (four catches on four targets, 58 yards) and let [[Breece Hall]] work underneath against a backed-off defense. The caveat Harmon repeats: he does not think this Jets offense is 'one Davante Adams away' from being absolved of its sins; the structural problems — no intermediate middle-of-field attack, 28th in rushing success rate — all still showed up in the same game.
- 2024-11-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-12]]): the supposedly airtight [[Aaron Rodgers]] reunion has been the most baffling thing about the Jets — Koh (not a tracked expert) says the two 'look like they've never played together' and Harmon agrees nobody saw that coming. Harmon's explanation is that the industry, himself included, fell into the trap of assuming no time had passed: the pair last played together in 2021, and both players have declined since. He is careful to say the broken chemistry is not the Jets' biggest problem — the defense and the surrounding roster are — but treats it as a caution against assuming a receiver-quarterback connection survives a three-year gap intact.
- 2024-12-17 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-12-17]]): Adams went 9-of-12 for 198 yards and two touchdowns against Jacksonville, with 135 receiving yards in the final four minutes of regulation — per Rich Cimini the most by any player in a game this century. Harmon says people wanted to put Adams on 'wash watch' this year and that was wrong: he can very much still play. His explanation for the timing is that expecting instant results from Adams and [[Aaron Rodgers]] was unfair given the years apart, and the chemistry has only now returned to vintage form. The caveat is strong — Jacksonville's defense is horrible, at points putting linebackers on receivers, and the Jets create no matchup binds schematically the way Miami does; they simply line up and feed Adams and [[Garrett Wilson]].
- 2025-02-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-10]]): with Adams unlikely to return to the Jets after [[Aaron Rodgers]] was told he is done there, Waldman splits the question — the capability to be an NFL WR1 is still there, but for fantasy valuation it is 'f**k it', a WR2 whose outcome swings on landing spot. He still rates Adams among the better route runners in the league and blames the couple of years without top-level production on his quarterbacks rather than decline. On the age cliff he uses a stairs metaphor: for Adams it is probably a step or two rather than a whole flight, which keeps him a bargain. Preferred landings: Waldman would most like Adams back in Green Bay to finish his career as their WR1 (over signing [[Tee Higgins]]); Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked) floats the Chargers alongside [[Ladd McConkey]].
- 2025-02-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-19]]): Harmon expects Adams to be cut by the Jets and reads [[Josh Jacobs]] repeatedly saying on Super Bowl radio row that Green Bay needs a true number one as a calculated push for a reunion — Jacobs's best statistical seasons came in Las Vegas when Adams was a coverage dictator forcing two-high looks and light boxes. Harmon concedes Adams might not be that player anymore but calls him the best perimeter option likely to hit free agency, and says a three-receiver set of Adams at X or flanker with [[Romeo Doubs]] and [[Jayden Reed]] in the slot would work and would actually help the young Packers by forcing zone looks. His prediction, though, is that Green Bay does not sign him because it seems too easy, and instead drafts a big-bodied receiver around round three. Alternatives raised: the Rams, which Harmon says is genuinely on the table, and the Chargers; both hosts agree [[Aaron Rodgers]] to the Rams will not happen because Sean McVay would not want to run Rodgers's stuff.
- 2025-02-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-02-28]]): Adams is expected to be released rather than traded — he is owed roughly 38M across 2025-26 and Harmon does not believe a team pays a pick plus that contract — but he remains 'definitely the best available X on the market' in a league with a genuine dearth of X receivers. Harmon was about halfway through his 2024 Reception Perception sample and had him hovering near a 75 percent success rate vs man and 74 percent vs press: well below his all-time RP seasons but still a really good starting X. He compared the decline shape to [[Keenan Allen]]'s gradual slide rather than a Dez Bryant-style cliff. Note the Jets rotated him and [[Garrett Wilson]] at X, which Harmon thought smart; he floated the Chargers (with [[Ladd McConkey]] flexing inside-out) and, later, Buffalo as fits. Adams reportedly prefers a contender and the west coast; Harmon would not tie himself to [[Aaron Rodgers]] at this stage.
- 2025-03-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-05]]): after the Jets released him, Harmon's in-progress charting shows Adams is clearly past peak — success rate vs man and vs press are on the downturn — but still 'very much high-end starting outside receiver territory'. He rates Adams one of the very few true X receivers available in a free-agent class otherwise dominated by slot types like [[Chris Godwin]], with the other perimeter options being veterans ([[DeAndre Hopkins]], [[Amari Cooper]]) who did not hit the ground running elsewhere. Adams wins in tight coverage, runs great routes, is quarterback-friendly and can play inside-out. Koh highlighted Adams' final six games of 2024: 7 receptions and 96 yards per game, 14.0 per catch and six touchdowns, a 116-catch/1,600-yard pace. Harmon's landing-spot tour: Chargers is his favorite fit with [[Ladd McConkey]] and [[Justin Herbert]]; New England is where the mega-bag lives, since they have to overpay and could go three years/$90M against a two-year/$50M market; Detroit is a fit as a true X for [[Jared Goff]] but he worries about too many mouths with [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], [[Jameson Williams]] and [[Sam LaPorta]] under a first-time play caller in John Morton; the Rams work as a pivot off [[Cooper Kupp]]; Green Bay is possible but not likely; the Giants only if [[Aaron Rodgers]] lands there. He says he would rather have Adams than [[Tyreek Hill]] right now.
- 2025-03-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-10]]): Adams signing with the Rams (roughly $26 million guaranteed, $40-plus million over two years, having wanted $20 million a year and a West Coast home) is a positive, not a subtraction. Waldman said 'fuck it' to the idea that Adams limits [[Puka Nacua]], pointing to Adams' 11 games with [[Garrett Wilson]], where Wilson still finished as the WR22 in a mediocre Jets offense with [[Aaron Rodgers]] off-point. He argued Adams is more additive than cannibalizing, is a professional who knows how to stay healthy and train, and can carry the load if needed. The bigger touchdown-share hit, he suggested, may land on [[Tyron Williams]] given Adams' red-zone capability. Co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) expected Adams to move up from a late-WR2 ADP toward the front of that group.
- 2025-03-12 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-03-12]]): Adams signs with the Rams for two years and $46M, and Harmon calls it a home-run fit and one of the landing spots he most wanted. He argues Adams is the best charted receiver of the Reception Perception era (2014-present): three seasons in the all-time top 10 for success rate vs man, two seasons above the 95th percentile vs zone, and a 2021 campaign Harmon calls probably the best season he has ever charted, including the third-best press score all time. The decline is real but graceful — success rate vs zone fell from 84.4% in his last full Raiders season to 77% in 2024, yet he still sat at the 80th percentile vs man and 89th percentile vs press and was at or above NFL average on every route in the tree. Harmon's stated caveat is the [[Allen Robinson]] precedent he wrote up before the signing: Robinson's final Chicago zone drop preceded a cliff in that same Rams X role, and age cliffs remain the hardest thing to forecast even with RP data. He also notes Adams' clean injury history matters because older players recover worse. Alignment is the other reason he likes it: Adams took 42.4% of sampled snaps in the slot and 52% on the line, giving Sean McVay a multi-positional X who frees [[Puka Nacua]] to move.
- 2025-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: guest JJ Zachariason (not a tracked expert) made Adams his headline wide receiver value, arguing the market is scared of the age cliff at 32 despite him not slowing down last year and winning titles down the stretch. His case is structural: the Rams have been bottom three in running back targets each of the last six seasons and run heavy 11 personnel under Sean McVay, so nobody should be shocked if Adams and [[Puka Nacua]] combine for half the team's targets. He projects Adams as WR10-11 against a late-teens WR ADP, plus contingent upside — with Nacua out, Adams would project for roughly a 30% target share with [[Matthew Stafford]]. Waldman agreed, calling Adams still one of the league's best route runners paired with a veteran who anticipates and throws with timing, and who retains 'see it, throw it' velocity, while Adams remains excellent adjusting to the football.
- 2025-06-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-06-11]]): Harmon says the Adams–[[Puka Nacua]] pairing in Los Angeles is one of the things he is most excited to watch in 2025, and expects Adams to absorb enough outside-receiver work that Nacua's off-the-line and slot snap rates climb past the 60% and 27.1% figures in Nacua's year-three profile — meaning more layups for Nacua. James Koh (untracked co-host) agrees and adds that Sean McVay should be salivating at using Adams inside as well, since even at his peak Adams comfortably played around 30% of his snaps in the slot and can do everything from any alignment; Koh expects McVay to move both receivers around the formation to create alignment problems for defensive coordinators.
- 2025-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception - 2025-07-23]]): Adams is a declining but still-viable X receiver for the [[Los Angeles Rams]] whose Reception Perception profile shows the decline in one specific place — success rate versus zone fell from 84.4% in 2023 to 77% in 2024, a 37th-percentile mark and his first season below roughly 81-82% versus zone since his rookie year. His man (76% to 74.6%, 80th percentile) and press (80.3% to 79.2%, 89th percentile) numbers held. Harmon refuses to 'yada yada' the zone drop because he did exactly that with Allen Robinson, who fell from 79.1% to 75% versus zone before being finished in his first Rams season, and calls it worth acknowledging real risk with a 32-year-old. He still loves the signing: Adams ran a slant on 25.7% of routes at an 80.6% success rate and a dig on only 10.7% at 85.7%, and Harmon expects the Rams to raise his snaps on the line from 52.1% and cut his 42% slot rate, arguing he is really replacing [[DeMarcus Robinson]] as the X rather than [[Cooper Kupp]]. James Koh (not a tracked expert) is more bullish on the McVay fit — 'peanut butter and jelly' for an in-breaking technician — and pushes back that Adams may not play as much X as Harmon assumes, noting Adams's 2024 situation in New York was 'downright bad'.
- 2025-08-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-14]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris ranks Adams inside his top 15 but flags that the ranking assumes [[Matthew Stafford]] plays, and guest Sigmund Bloom (Footballguys, not tracked) observed Adams already slipping toward the third round, asking what you do if he falls to the early fourth. Both noted Adams publicly downplaying the Jimmy Garoppolo scenario — 'when Jimmy and I were together, everything was great' — with Harris relaying the counter-read that a player insisting the backup is fine is itself a signal the starter's back is badly compromised, and adding that the Raiders version of Garoppolo did not resemble the Super Bowl one.
- 2025-08-18 — According to Bob Harris (co-host, not a tracked expert) on ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-18]]): he is still drafting Adams 'with reckless abandon' despite the uncertainty around [[Matthew Stafford]]'s back, because he went back to the numbers Adams posted with [[Jimmy Garoppolo]] — Adams does not like Garoppolo, yet there were games he was heavily targeted, including one 20-target game, so he is not being overlooked in that pairing. Harris added that Sean McVay can cobble something together whoever is under center, and that the injury uncertainty is precisely where he hopes to gain a bit of draft value. [[Matt Waldman]] noted he took [[Puka Nacua]] 14th overall in a draft this week on the same logic.
- 2025-08-22 — According to Cousin Josh, a recurring guest on the [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast]] and not a tracked expert ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Adams is his 2025 Least Valuable Player — a reversal, since he says he was in on Adams as a target as recently as a few weeks ago. The stack of risks: a new team, a number-two role behind a number one who will absorb targets, [[Matthew Stafford]]'s back injury meaning the quarterback has barely taken reps, an age-33 season nobody is discussing, and a price of roughly WR15-16 at the end of round three. He grants Adams still commands targets and is a great player, and says Adams has not shown the same dip he thinks [[Mike Evans]] has, but the last two seasons of roughly 1,100/8 and 1,060/8 are good rather than sensational. His fear is the [[Andre Johnson]]-style cliff — receivers you have adored your whole career hitting the wall or landing in a bad situation and being finished quickly. [[Chris Harris]] says he hears it, and adds credit: Adams's ADP is lower than [[Rachaad White]]'s was when White was Josh's successful LVP call last year, so this is a bolder call than the bigger name suggests.
- 2025-08-22 — According to guest Dr. Deepak Chona of SportsMedAnalytics (not a tracked expert) on [[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-22]] (ASR: 'Devontae Adams'): Chona has downgraded Adams on his board alongside [[Puka Nacua]] because of [[Matthew Stafford]]'s suspected herniated disc, arguing that at the ADP cost of the Rams' receivers there are many other options without that quarterback-health flag. Koh (not tracked) framed the same point as the whole Rams plan collapsing if Jimmy Garoppolo has to play.
