# Davante Adams

> by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his name as

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-12 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-12]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: "feeling it" on Adams staying with the Raiders now that Luke Getsy has been hired as offensive coordinator, on the strength of their prior Green Bay relationship — "Getsy and Adams know each other. Adams is going to feel a little more comfortable." States he'd feel good about Adams's fantasy value "regardless of quarterback," with a specific ADP value call: currently going in the late second round, and Waldman says he is "chasing it ever downward every chance I get" as the price keeps dropping — an explicit buy signal on a falling ADP.
- 2024-05-14 — According to Adam Rank ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-05-14]]), not a tracked expert: ranks [[Davante Adams]] as his No. 6 receiver overall, behind Tyreek Hill, Justin Jefferson, Ja'Marr Chase, [[DJ Moore]] and [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], stressing he isn't calling Adams a 'bum' — just that he's 'looking forward, not looking behind' given Adams' age. Rank says he wouldn't push back hard against someone ranking Adams as high as No. 4.
- 2024-08-01 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-08-01]]): Adams is clearly the best receiver in the AFC West and still a top-three NFL receiver — Harmon's order is [[Justin Jefferson]] first, [[Tyreek Hill]] second on the back of a career year and field-flipping ability, Adams third. The 2023 charting: 85th percentile vs man (his lowest in recent years), 91st vs press, 90th vs zone, with man success at 79.6% in 2021, 78.1% in 2022 and 76.1% in 2023 and press still 80.3%. Route rates remain elite — 95.7% on curls, 91.7% on outs, 89.8% on slants, 87% on posts, 81.8% on digs with digs at 20.4% of his routes — while comebacks, corners and nines are down, hinting at lost vertical juice. Harmon says aging-curve analysis is the work he is least confident in. Both hosts note the Netflix 'Receiver' fallout over Jimmy Garoppolo, and Harmon says he now better understands why Adams preferred [[Aidan O'Connell]], who will stand in the pocket and push it downfield. Koh has Adams over [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] in fantasy on 175 targets; Harmon disagrees.
