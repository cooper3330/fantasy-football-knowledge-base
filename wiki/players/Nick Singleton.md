# Nick Singleton

## Expert Takes

- 2026-06-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-25]]) [Dynasty]: Tennessee drafted Singleton in part because [[Tony Pollard]] is 29 and [[Tajay Spears|Tajae Spears]]'s two bad knees (no ACL involved) are enough of a red flag that 'the Titans are just... shaking their head' on giving him more than situational touches. Waldman sees Singleton potentially working his way into a rotational role as a rookie and growing into lead-back opportunity as the year progresses, with a real shot to be a starter in 2027 if Pollard and Spears are gone. [[Brandon Angelo]] agreed on the pedigree case -- as a Penn State freshman Singleton was neck-and-neck with [[Quinshon Judkins]] and looked like a first-round talent, with disappointing production largely explained by a predictable, over-schemed Penn State offense built around [[Kaytron Allen]].
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman thinks Singleton was unfairly dinged by evaluators for Penn State's coaching-staff dysfunction and a struggling quarterback situation (Drew Allar) that forced the offense into static, predictable roles for its skill talent. Waldman considers Singleton a better back than his college tape suggests and believes he's in a good backfield long-term, though he won't get much time as a rookie this year. Waldman recommends holding him in dynasty as a waiver-wire dart if injuries mount ahead of him.
