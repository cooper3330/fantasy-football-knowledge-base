# Mac Jones

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-10-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-03]]): Jones played probably the best game of his NFL career in the 49ers' overtime win over the Rams, but Harris credits the Rams' defensive plan more than Jones — 42 of Jones's 49 attempts came against two safeties high, including all six in overtime, and the Rams sat back and left short receivers open all night. Harris counted 38 of 49 attempts and 28 of 33 completions travelling 10 air yards or fewer, and 29 of 49 attempts / 21 of 33 completions under five air yards. He credits Jones for taking what was there and keeping it safe (one near-interception deflected away in the fourth quarter), but calls him 'legitimately one of the softest players' he can remember, notes he acted shot in the calf and was dry heaving by the end, and says he is skeptical of the 'Mac Jones is better than [[Brock Purdy]]' talk. Verdict: not a quarterback Harris wants as a regular fantasy starter.
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-06]]): asked whether San Francisco has a quarterback controversy with [[Brock Purdy]] hurt, Waldman said 'fuck it — the cheese has slipped off somebody's cracker' if they think that is going on. He accepts Jones is making himself a lot of money as the 49ers' backup, the way [[Sam Darnold]] once did, and that someone will pay him, but he does not think Jones is the big-armed profile teams actually buy. Bob Harris (not tracked) expects Purdy to need another week or two, so Jones likely starts again.
- 2025-11-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-10]]): Harris pre-empts the defenders of a 32-for-38 line — after going down 21-0 and never having a realistic chance to tie, Jones was the best version of himself with 'a thousand screens and check downs,' and he eventually threw the back-breaking interception 'because it's what he does.' The mini-Mac throws kept [[Christian McCaffrey]], [[George Kittle]] and [[Jauan Jennings]] fantasy-viable, but Harris says people who watch the games know this has played out as far as it can go: '[[Brock Purdy]] needs to come back' so the offense can look like its non-checkdown self. He jokes that Cleveland will sign Jones next year and we will do this all over again.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not tracked) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-11]]): Jones has played well enough in San Francisco to look like at least a bridge starting quarterback, and on a cheap two-year deal at a sub-$5 million level he is an unusually attractive asset — Harmon notes a suspicious number of teams (Vikings, Panthers, Dolphins) would be improved by him, which he calls a bad sign for the quarterback state of the NFL. He credits the ecosystem, and explains the breadth of fit by the spread of the Shanahan and McVay systems across the league, naming Kevin O'Connell and Mike McDaniel. Harmon also says San Francisco could not realistically expect a second-round pick back for him. Koh floats the wild scenario of keeping Jones and trading [[Brock Purdy]] for a first; Harmon is skeptical, citing how loved Purdy is in that locker room, and says it will never happen.
