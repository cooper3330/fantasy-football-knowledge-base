# Mac Jones

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-10-03 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-03]]): Jones played probably the best game of his NFL career in the 49ers' overtime win over the Rams, but Harris credits the Rams' defensive plan more than Jones — 42 of Jones's 49 attempts came against two safeties high, including all six in overtime, and the Rams sat back and left short receivers open all night. Harris counted 38 of 49 attempts and 28 of 33 completions travelling 10 air yards or fewer, and 29 of 49 attempts / 21 of 33 completions under five air yards. He credits Jones for taking what was there and keeping it safe (one near-interception deflected away in the fourth quarter), but calls him 'legitimately one of the softest players' he can remember, notes he acted shot in the calf and was dry heaving by the end, and says he is skeptical of the 'Mac Jones is better than [[Brock Purdy]]' talk. Verdict: not a quarterback Harris wants as a regular fantasy starter.
- 2025-10-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-06]]): asked whether San Francisco has a quarterback controversy with [[Brock Purdy]] hurt, Waldman said 'fuck it — the cheese has slipped off somebody's cracker' if they think that is going on. He accepts Jones is making himself a lot of money as the 49ers' backup, the way [[Sam Darnold]] once did, and that someone will pay him, but he does not think Jones is the big-armed profile teams actually buy. Bob Harris (not tracked) expects Purdy to need another week or two, so Jones likely starts again.
- 2025-10-13 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-13]]): filling in for the injured Brock Purdy, Jones threw for over 300 yards against Tampa Bay but Harris remains 'fairly skeptical he's a winning starting quarterback long term.' He's effective on clear, in-breaking reads -- deep skinny posts and over routes -- doing a passable but inferior imitation of what Harris considers Jared Goff's game, and much of his yardage came on a huge Kendrick Bourne day. The concerning part is his pocket presence: Harris said Jones is 'so soft in the pocket' that contact reliably produces a fumble, which happened twice (one recovered by his own line, one overturned but likely a legitimate soft fumble on a Lavonte David sack). Harris credited Jones by comparison, noting Cooper Rush 'would not have performed this well' as Purdy's fill-in and wouldn't have posted a 300-yard game.
- 2025-10-27 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2025-10-27]]) [Redraft]: didn't get much help from drops (Jauan Jennings, Jordan Watkins) but 'wasn't too bad' for a backup, finding [[George Kittle]] through heavy coverage for a touchdown. Harris says he'd personally 'like to see' Brock Purdy return once healthy, implying Jones is a fill-in stopgap.
- 2025-11-10 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-10]]): Harris pre-empts the defenders of a 32-for-38 line — after going down 21-0 and never having a realistic chance to tie, Jones was the best version of himself with 'a thousand screens and check downs,' and he eventually threw the back-breaking interception 'because it's what he does.' The mini-Mac throws kept [[Christian McCaffrey]], [[George Kittle]] and [[Jauan Jennings]] fantasy-viable, but Harris says people who watch the games know this has played out as far as it can go: '[[Brock Purdy]] needs to come back' so the offense can look like its non-checkdown self. He jokes that Cleveland will sign Jones next year and we will do this all over again.
- 2025-11-11 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not tracked) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-11-11]]): Jones has played well enough in San Francisco to look like at least a bridge starting quarterback, and on a cheap two-year deal at a sub-$5 million level he is an unusually attractive asset — Harmon notes a suspicious number of teams (Vikings, Panthers, Dolphins) would be improved by him, which he calls a bad sign for the quarterback state of the NFL. He credits the ecosystem, and explains the breadth of fit by the spread of the Shanahan and McVay systems across the league, naming Kevin O'Connell and Mike McDaniel. Harmon also says San Francisco could not realistically expect a second-round pick back for him. Koh floats the wild scenario of keeping Jones and trading [[Brock Purdy]] for a first; Harmon is skeptical, citing how loved Purdy is in that locker room, and says it will never happen.
- 2025-11-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-14]]): Jones went 33-of-39 for three touchdowns filling in for Brock Purdy against the Rams, but Harris cautions that judging it by raw stats alone is misleading -- Jones took too many checkdowns in a game the defense couldn't win and needed to be more aggressive. Still, Harris admits Jones 'filled in better than I thought he would, admirably' and 'probably really did earn himself a look as somebody's potential starter next year,' while stressing the Niners still need Purdy back now.
- 2025-11-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-17]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman disagreed that San Francisco should have stuck with Jones over [[Brock Purdy]]. He said Jones played well and will likely draw real trade interest next offseason, and is a borderline starter-caliber talent, but lacks Purdy's arm, anticipation and mobility. He also argued waiting longer to switch back risked Jones getting 'found out' more by opposing defenses, and that going with the hot hand instead of getting Purdy reps for a playoff push didn't make sense given the 49ers' contention window.
- 2026-02-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-02-19]]): Harmon is skeptical Minnesota should surrender significant capital for Jones, arguing the 49ers won't move him within the NFC for less than a second-round pick (he doubts a third gets it done). He questions whether Jones is even a real starter outside Kyle Shanahan's scheme -- comparing him to Jimmy Garoppolo and Nick Mullens, who also posted good numbers in that system -- and cites Jones ranking 31st of 40 qualified QBs in EPA+CPOE composite, 30th in EPA/play and 28th in success rate on 250-plus 2024 plays. He also disputes the 'fantasy bro' narrative crediting Jones (not Trevor Lawrence) for Brian Thomas Jr.'s production, noting Jacksonville still struggled with Jones starting. Harmon projects Jones could get Minnesota's offense (27th in efficiency in 2025) to roughly 18th, not into the top ten, and argues a cheaper veteran like Geno Smith could accomplish the same. Co-host James Koh (untracked) strongly disagreed, calling Jones a 'huge upgrade' who could drag the Vikings into the playoffs given his draft pedigree, starting experience, and fit with Kevin O'Connell.
