- 2026-04-16 — According to Dalton Wasserman (guest draft analyst, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-16]]) [Dynasty]: asked which of three once-hyped-then-faded college quarterbacks -- [[Cade Klubnick]], [[Drew Allar]] and [[Carson Beck]] -- is most likely to become an above-average NFL starter, Wasserman picked Klubnick. He's the best athlete of the three and a vertical passer who layers the ball well down the seams, comped to a lesser-built Jaxson Dart. His 2024 Clemson playoff run was genuinely good tape, but Wasserman still files him as a developmental prospect rather than a near-term starter.
