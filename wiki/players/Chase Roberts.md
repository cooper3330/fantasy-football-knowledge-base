# Chase Roberts

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-02-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-02-16]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman comps Roberts (6'3', 216) to Eric Decker -- a technically sound route runner who wins one-on-one matchups against NFL-caliber corners more than he wins contested 50/50 or double-coverage balls. He has good feel for cutback lanes after the catch, sets up his breaks well, and is a good red-zone/in-line blocker who can be moved tight to the formation like a mini tight end. Waldman sees a floor around [[Mack Hollins]]-level contributor for a long time, with a ceiling more like what [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] is doing currently (not quite as good, but in that archetype).
- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Roberts has a good feel for cutback lanes and blocked/unblocked pursuit angles after the catch, and knows how to veer to force a chasing corner into a long pursuit angle he can't win. Waldman projects him as an intermediate, working-underneath vertical threat in the Tim Patrick/Eric Decker mold rather than a true field-stretching X.
- 2026-04-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-23]]): Roberts is a 6'3', 209-lb BYU receiver who turned 25 in January and was 24 years old during his charted final season -- Harmon calls that age 'without a doubt a red flag' and a real factor in evaluating his tape, drawing a parallel to how he under-weighted age on Andrew Armstrong last year. Final season: 52 receptions, 854 yards, 4 touchdowns. Charted success rates: 74% versus man, 79% versus zone, with standout route splits of 100% on the post, 82% on the dig, and 88% on the slant -- but 'nothing working against press.' He tested poorly at the combine (4.64 40, slowest among WRs there) before improving to roughly 4.54 at his pro day; 37-inch vertical and 10'6' broad. Harmon praised his catch radius, sticky hands, and fearlessness in traffic (only one drop across three charted games), but questioned whether his movement translates given he looked 'smooth' rather than 'snappy' and lacks top-end long speed or the ability to work back to the quarterback. Harmon's projected player comp is a power-slot/Mack Collins-type role (his closest mockdraftable.com athletic comp is teammate-of-the-week [[Pat Bryant]]), and co-host James Koh (not tracked) called him 'the poor man's Jayden Higgins.' Harmon's grade: priority free agent or seventh-round pick, better than that range on tape but discounted for age and long speed.
