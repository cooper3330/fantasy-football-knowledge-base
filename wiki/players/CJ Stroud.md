# CJ Stroud

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-10-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-23]]): Harmon admits one of his most wrong preseason calls was expecting new OC Nick Caley to be an upgrade in offensive design and feel -- he says Caley has actually been a significant downgrade, with nothing in the scheme complementing anything else (deep routes across the board, no quick game to bail Stroud out) behind a shaky offensive line. Stroud is now taking hits from constant pressure that a more cohesive scheme and quicker-hitting route concepts would mitigate, a stark contrast to early in Stroud's rookie year when Houston hit [[Nico Collins]] quickly off the top of the drop.
- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-08]]): Stroud made 'absolutely heroic throws' in a game Houston should have won by 30 against a depleted Chiefs offensive line, but Harmon noted the Texans' own line has only improved from dead-last (30th, effectively unplayable) to roughly 25th in the league this year, helped by shifting Trent Brown to right tackle and kicking Titus Howard inside. Spagnuolo's Chiefs defense exploited that in the second half with a heavy blitz package Houston couldn't identify, producing three-and-outs. Harmon framed this as a 'growing season' for Stroud, learning to operate more from the line of scrimmage under a tougher scheme while still working behind bad protection - he still doesn't feel great about Houston's offense beyond [[Nico Collins]] and a low-end [[Woody Marks]].
- 2026-01-29 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-01-29]]): Harris pushes back preemptively on what he expects will be a popular 2026 offseason narrative that 'CJ Stroud sucks' stemming from his poor playoff performance against the Patriots. He argues that game can't be separated from a genuinely terrible Houston offensive line -- calling the O-line the only glaring weakness on the entire Texans roster heading into the offseason -- and says it's where he'd be spending draft capital and money, not on another skill-position running back.
