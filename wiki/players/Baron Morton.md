# Baron Morton

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-30]]): asked whether Baron Morton is a top-five quarterback, Waldman says 'fuck it' -- he is not. Waldman notes analyst Chris Simms reportedly has Morton as his QB2 (and once had Kellen Mond similarly high), a preference Waldman ties to an affinity for air-raid quarterbacks. He credits Morton as an accurate shallow-range thrower who distributes well in quick-game/spread concepts, shows manipulation skills and patience in compressed pockets, and is tough absorbing hits, but says accuracy craters beyond the shallow range, there are technical gaps in how he sees the field, and he has clear lapses against edge pressure -- overreacting to the flash of color -- that will be exposed harder and faster in the NFL. Waldman's bottom line: making an active NFL roster 'would be quite an accomplishment' for Morton this year.
