Harris has Moore as a 4th-round pick, a round ahead of Kluge, and argues Moore's eight-year track record of only one truly elite season (2023, with Justin Fields in a broken Bears offense) reflects scheme fit, not talent -- comparing Moore's improvisational, 'bebop and jazz' style unfavorably to Chicago's route-precision offense but favorably to Buffalo's. Both think Moore slots into almost any build (paired with an early TE if RB-heavy, or as a true WR1 if WR-heavy) and attribute the discount to fans still feeling burned by his down 2025 season rather than to logic.
