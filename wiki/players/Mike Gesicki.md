# Mike Gesicki

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-10]]): 'feel it, much to my chagrin' on Gesicki as a sneaky TE1 in his second Cincinnati season, his first with the same coordinator twice. Waldman's evaluation is unflattering — he pictures a ballet dancer with his feet cemented into the ground, graceful anywhere as long as he does not have to move his feet quickly — but he fits what [[Joe Burrow]] likes to do, and the seam routes, corner routes and routes to the flat where he runs free for five to ten yards in space are enough volume to matter. He would not over-invest. The market disagreement: Gesicki sits at TE27 on Underdog ADP behind names Waldman does not think need to go ahead of him, largely because his big games came in the five contests without [[Tee Higgins]], and people distrust the profile with both [[Ja'Marr Chase]] and Higgins on the field. Waldman called that a crazy value. [Best Ball]
- 2025-11-24 — According to Chris Harris ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-24]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: In a revenge game against New England (one of his many former teams), Gesicki caught three passes on the opening drive coming back from a pectoral injury, then had exactly one more catch the rest of the game - a hot-start-then-vanish usage pattern Harris flagged as a caution against chasing him off one big early drive.
- 2025-12-09 — According to Chris Harris and Jeff Bell (Football Guys, not tracked) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-09]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Gesicki has moved into a slot-type role over the last three weeks and has been tied for the Bengals' team target lead in that span. Harris ranks him above Mitchell Tinsley and Andrei Iosivas among Bengals options given the opportunity, but cautions Gesicki has a long history in Harris's experience of looking like 'the obvious logical conclusion' before disappearing after one catch. Bell lists him as an honorable mention/#7 (2% rostered) with Tee Higgins likely out Week 15.
