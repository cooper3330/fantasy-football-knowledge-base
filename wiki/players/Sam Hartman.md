# Sam Hartman

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-08]]): aggressive, with technically sound drops, an excellent play-action game from center and pistol, and effective pump fakes. The limiter is arm strength — his vertical accuracy is real but capped around 35-40 yards from the catch point, "just enough in a lot of schemes" but not what teams want from a starter. Likes that he's willing to climb the pocket to get the ball out, but sees little room for his velocity to improve. Ceiling: a distributor in a quick-game offense that sets up vertical shots for him to attack immediately on favorable matchups, with diminishing returns the longer he's forced to hold the ball.
