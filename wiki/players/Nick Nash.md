# Nick Nash

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-12-16 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-16]]): Waldman 'feels the argument' that Nash, not [[Travis Hunter]], should have won the Biletnikoff — the statistical case is better. Measurables and profile: about 6'3', 195 pounds, and not as explosive as Hunter, with the central question being how explosive he really is. What Waldman likes is technique — he plays big, wins the ball in the air with a level of technique a lot of NFL players do not have, is tough at the catch point, can make people miss and is a very good route runner. His comp is [[Jauan Jennings]], another Waldman favorite, with the speed a question mark but the skills absolutely there. He says Nash might end up as one of the top five receivers in the class by RSP grade.
- 2025-01-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-01-23]]): Nash, measured at 6'3, is an interesting player Waldman groups with [[Andrew Armstrong]] and [[Konata Mumpfield]] in the tier defined by one question — can they run fast enough to be more than what they are? He places Nash at the lower end of that group and is not in love with him, but sees the type that improves over a few years into something useful.
- 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24]]): the San Jose State quarterback-turned-receiver is a good route runner with really effective short-area acceleration and change-of-direction quickness, though his long speed sits at the bottom range of starter value as a flanker. The most promising part of his game is release work at the line: a wide variety of maneuvers combined with the right balance of patience and suddenness to bait defenders out of position. Once into the route he understands the value of setups and needs only tweaks to become adept at all of them.
