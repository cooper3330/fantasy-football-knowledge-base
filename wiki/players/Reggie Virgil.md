# Reggie Virgil

## Expert Takes

     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2026-04-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-23]]): Virgil is a fourth-year Texas Tech transfer (three seasons at Miami of Ohio with just two total career receptions there) whose lone breakout year produced 57 receptions, 705 yards, 6 receiving touchdowns (8 total). He's 6'2', 187 lbs and ran a slower-than-ideal 4.57 40 for his size, with a 36-inch vertical and a strong 10'7' broad jump. Charted profile: 64% success rate versus man (below the 70% threshold Harmon looks for) but 81% versus zone, 80% success rate on the dig and 87% on the slant -- indicating a short/intermediate, zone-beating profile rather than a vertical threat. He posted a 23% contested target rate and an 83.3% contested catch rate, with 'really good ball tracking skills' and strong hands in contested situations, but struggled badly to separate long against press/man (ran a nine route on 26% of sample snaps at only 50% success). He lined up at left WR on 90.4% of snaps, making him one of roughly ten 'skewed alignment' receivers Harmon flagged in this class. Harmon compared his style to Robbie Anderson in his 'Z' years in Carolina -- best snapping off in-breaking routes rather than as a true X go-route runner -- and projects him as a day-three pick who could carve out a role as a Z/flanker/big slot contributor, presenting a decent target at 6'2' and helping his QB draw pass-interference flags with his ball tracking.
