- 2025-02-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-24]]) [Dynasty]: guest Dwain McFarland (Fantasy Life, NOT tracked) says 'f that' to the too-small knock — at a listed 238 pounds Fannin is within four or five pounds of [[Brock Bowers]] (245), [[Trey McBride]] (246), [[Dalton Kincaid]] and [[Sam LaPorta]], though weighing in at 230 would change the calculus. Fannin posted a 100 grade, the best adjusted production score the model has ever produced for a tight end even after being dinged for the MAC, plus a 125 quarterback rating when targeted (79th percentile) and a 6.23 Zierlein grade; McFarland notes athleticism matters more for tight ends than any other position in his model, so combine testing matters here. Waldman loves the footwork, lateral agility, vision on smash screens and ability to block as a detached option or from the wing, but charted six tight-coverage opportunities in which Fannin caught none of them — 'he went Jared Cook' — and attributes drops to clap-catching. He thinks Fannin is quick enough to threaten a seam off play action but sees a James Casey or [[Chigoziem Okonkwo]] hybrid-role risk; McFarland's version of that risk is the Juwan Johnson pattern of disappearing whenever the team leads and runs. McFarland expects Fannin to end up his TE3 after the draft.
- 2025-03-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-10]]): Waldman has moved UP on Fannin post-Combine, reversing his earlier position — he had been lower than Dwain McFarland (not a tracked expert) when McFarland pushed Fannin on the show weeks earlier, and had doubted Fannin was a top-three tight end on his board, but now thinks he might be. The reason is contrarian: the bandwagon jumped off because Fannin ran roughly 4.71 rather than the expected 4.5-4.6, which Waldman calls borderline starter speed, but he weights the 20-yard shuttle and three-cone far more, and those times are nearly identical to [[Sam LaPorta]], whom Fannin also resembles in style — good route runner, catches well, runs well after the catch, creative footwork, good vision. His caveat is fit: as long as the landing spot is not idiotic, and Detroit is his example of a team that used LaPorta correctly, Fannin could be one of the three most productive tight ends from a good class.
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman spends the episode's longest segment defending Fannin against the 40-time backlash. Fannin and [[Sam LaPorta]] are both converted wide receivers at essentially the same height and weight, and their agility numbers sit in the same tiers: Fannin's 4.39 20-yard shuttle to LaPorta's 4.25, a 6.97 three-cone to LaPorta's 6.91, and a vertical one inch below LaPorta's 35 inches. The gap is the 40 — LaPorta's 4.59 is a hundredth inside Waldman's elite tier, Fannin's 4.71 a hundredth outside his starter tier, which Waldman says effectively places him in it. On-field GPS backs it: Fannin's career max speed at Bowling Green was 20.3 mph with fastest-moment averages of 19 to 19.5, versus LaPorta's 21.1 max and 19.6 to 20. Waldman's conclusion is that Fannin's measured speed meets the NFL starter standard while his initial acceleration and change-of-direction quickness exceed it, and that comparing the two players' actual route trees shows the 40 barely matters — most of LaPorta's rookie vertical wins came on long-developing play action against zone and broken zone, routes Fannin could have won too. He is one of the four Waldman would want maximized as a receiver with blocking responsibility minimized.
- 2025-04-24 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-24]]): of the three tight ends he would want in the Jets offense, he personally prefers Fannin — metrically and in height and weight he is the closest of the group to [[Sam LaPorta]], and in Angelo's opinion the best blocker of the three. - 2025-04-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-28 (Feel It or F-It Post-Draft)]]) [Dynasty]: an underrated asset, explicitly against Alfredo Brown (not tracked), who argued on their draft party that the Cleveland landing spot sinks Fannin's value. - 2025-05-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-05]]) [Dynasty]: underrated relative to where he is going — TE35 in Best Ball, a free-square flyer, and going around 4.02 in rookie drafts behind [[Mason Taylor]] (3.03) and just ahead of [[Elijah Arroyo]] (4.06), a range Waldman calls a very good spot and hopes he falls that far to him. - 2025-05-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: aggressively higher than the market — he would take Fannin at the end of round one in a 1.5-PPR rookie draft, has him third on his tight end board ahead of [[Tyler Warren]] and [[Colston Loveland]], and is stunned people draft [[Terrance Ferguson]] over him. - 2025-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-20]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman called Fannin an absolute value whose market cratered after the Combine, and said he is a player 'you can't lose with' in dynasty even if he gives you nothing in 2025 redraft.
