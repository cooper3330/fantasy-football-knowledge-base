# Rahsul Faison

- 2026-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-01]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman called Faison a decisive gap runner with good vision who identifies both interior and, more effectively, edge penetration and finds solutions to avoid defenders, sometimes a step later than ideal. He reads leverage well on duo, bends away from penetration into the crease, sets up linebackers into the wrong gap to split double teams, and rides zone blocks patiently before cutting -- overall, Waldman called him 'an effective tight-crease runner' who gets skinny through contact to the second level.
