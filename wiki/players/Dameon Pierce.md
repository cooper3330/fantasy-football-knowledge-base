# Dameon Pierce

## Expert Takes

- 2024-05-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-05-06]]): Waldman says Dameon Pierce's run as a true NFL starter is over, pointing to a misuse-of-assets pattern -- Pierce was an effective receiver out of the backfield at Florida, but Houston hasn't used him that way, stripping out a large chunk of what made him valuable. He frames the pre-draft hype two years ago as somewhat overrated to begin with: Pierce grades as a tough, capable complementary runner and solid receiver rather than a true lead-back creator, and Waldman doesn't expect Houston to lean on him as a starter going forward. Co-host Bob Harris (untracked) adds that Pierce also isn't even the Texans' best kick-return option anymore.
- 2025-08-26 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-26]]): the [[Joe Mixon]] NFI news forces Pierce's rank up too — he now at least cracks Harris's top 160 and is 'draftable in a very late round if you like'. Harris ranks rookie [[Woody Marks]] level with him in standard and ahead of him in PPR, and prefers the unknown to the known here. [Redraft (Standard)]
- 2026-10-12 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-12]]): with Will Shipley hurt, Harris flags Pierce as next up but is skeptical: he calls Pierce a 'rat trap' runner who 'squirms and struggles and will chew his own leg off to get an extra yard' but is simply slow, and says given Philadelphia's poor offensive line and non-running quarterback, the backfield 'almost doesn't matter' because Pierce isn't the creative, explosive runner that offensive environment would need.
- 2026-10-13 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-13]]), his #1 overall waiver add: with Saquon Barkley expected to again miss time with a hamstring injury and Philadelphia's other backs (Will Shipley, Tank Bigsby) out long-term, Pierce is 'the running back to start this week' by default despite being 'pretty mid.' Harris calls him 'the rat trap... runs like a rat stuck in glue' and warns against spending real FAAB -- four or five dollars of a $100 budget at most, even for someone desperate -- citing a bad offensive environment (a 'disastrous offensive line,' a quarterback who struggles without structure) and no faith the Eagles are about to turn things around. Pierce did play okay with some big throws against Jacksonville in a game Harris says they 'should have won.'
- 2026-10-15 — According to Dave Kluge (Football Guys, guest, not tracked) and [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-15]]) [Saquon Barkley's status unconfirmed as of recording]: Kluge ranks Pierce ahead of [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] and [[Marshawn Lloyd]] this week purely on projected volume into a bad Carolina run defense, but says plainly he doesn't believe in Pierce as a talent -- he wanted people to sell after Pierce's rookie year on what he called 'junk volume,' and Pierce was 'awful' in year two. Harris compares him to [[A.J. Dillon]]: a guy who can grind out three yards in a cloud of dust with no breakaway speed and zero passing-game value. Both flag real risk the touches don't funnel cleanly to Pierce given Philadelphia's offensive line losses (Lane Johnson retired, Cam Jurgens traded), and Kluge thinks Jaydon Blue and Carson Steele could siphon work even if Pierce nominally starts.
