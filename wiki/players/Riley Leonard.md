# Riley Leonard

## Expert Takes

- 2025-04-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not tracked) with guest Cory Kinnan (not tracked) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-04-18]]): Koh argues Leonard has the most upside of any mid-round quarterback and says he feels like 'one of one' in believing the 6'4", 220 former higher-rated basketball recruit can actually throw — he just was never asked to at Notre Dame. Kinnan is torn: there are no big throws on the tape, Duke spammed mesh and Notre Dame turned him into a battering ram, and he wonders how much can be installed with him week to week, though he notes a beautiful slot fade for a touchdown against Ohio State. Kinnan's comp is Josh McCown — a freak athlete (99th percentile shuttle, 97th percentile vertical) who hangs around as a journeyman and finds spot starts; Harmon's best case is what people expected [[Bo Nix]] to be. Three-game charting: 86% curls, 82% slants, encouraging even if pigeonholed as a short-area option. Both float Denver behind Nix as an ideal landing spot.
- 2025-06-06 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-06-06]]): the sixth-round rookie is a live dark horse to get into the mix if [[Anthony Richardson]]'s shoulder lingers and [[Daniel Jones]] falters — 'Riley Leonard could get in the mix here too, why not?' Harmon calls him toolsy and super athletic, cut from the same cloth as the other two, and says that while Notre Dame did not ask him to make many throws, he was pretty good when asked. He cites a Reception Perception mini-sample charted by Corey Kinnan: 82% success rate on slants, nearly 86% on curls, 50% on the nine (collegiate average) and a 50% success rate on the dig, which Harmon calls 'horrible, not what you want to see.' Koh (not tracked) noted the slant/curl/nine profile lines up with what an outside X receiver runs, so Leonard's success would be north-south rather than on in-breakers.
