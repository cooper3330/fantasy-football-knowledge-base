# DeAndre Swift

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: reacting to his three-year free-agent deal with Chicago ($24M, $8M/year) — calls him "the most hyped running back, period" but rates backfield-mate [[Khalil Herbert]] as simply "the better running back." Expects Swift to benefit situationally from [[Caleb Williams]]'s scrambling ability creating 2-on-1/3-on-1 run advantages, but projects him as a part-time player who won't have a career year — "more in the mid-range of what he's been" at every previous stop. Separately notes Philadelphia's investment in him last offseason means little now that the Eagles moved on to [[Saquon Barkley]], rating Swift "a good three tiers below Barkley in ability."
