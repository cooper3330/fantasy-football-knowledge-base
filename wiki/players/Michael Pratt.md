# Michael Pratt

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-08]]): a developmental prospect with a high floor thanks to good fundamentals — a clean drop and release, quality pump and play fakes, and decent size/arm strength that NFL teams desire. Potential to become a better pocket manager, but waits too long to throw to the middle of the field and out-breaking routes at all ranges, and waits too long to throw the ball away under pressure rather than cutting his losses — a maturity Waldman doesn't think he's developed yet.
