# Chigoziem Okonkwo

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2024-06-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-20]]): expects Okonkwo to line up as more of a regular in-line Y tight end in 2024 rather than the slot/gadget role Tennessee tried him in previously, per Okonkwo's own comments that he wants to just play tight end. Harmon notes that won't help him much as a run-blocker but should favor him as a pass-game safety valve/leak-out target, which Harmon says was where Okonkwo was most effective in college (Maryland drew up tight end screens for him). Calls him 'a pretty talented player' who simply hasn't had a defined role in this offense, and says it 'would be very interesting' if Tennessee's new staff can unlock him.
