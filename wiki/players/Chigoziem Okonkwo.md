# Chigoziem Okonkwo

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-06-20 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-06-20]]): expects Okonkwo to line up as more of a regular in-line Y tight end in 2024 rather than the slot/gadget role Tennessee tried him in previously, per Okonkwo's own comments that he wants to just play tight end. Harmon notes that won't help him much as a run-blocker but should favor him as a pass-game safety valve/leak-out target, which Harmon says was where Okonkwo was most effective in college (Maryland drew up tight end screens for him). Calls him 'a pretty talented player' who simply hasn't had a defined role in this offense, and says it 'would be very interesting' if Tennessee's new staff can unlock him.
- 2024-09-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-09-05]]): Okonkwo's rookie season was awesome — Harmon calls him one of the most efficient pass catchers in the NFL on a per-route basis, not merely in college — but the previous Tennessee regime was not interested in throwing him the ball at all. Koh (not tracked) points to his Maryland tape catching passes all over the field, including deep. Harmon is curious whether the Callahan staff explores that ceiling, while cautioning that in projected 12-personnel looks neither Tennessee tight end profiles as a big-time target getter.
- 2025-12-16 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-12-16]]): [[Chigoziem Okonkwo]] made two consecutive impressive, physical catch-and-run plays in the second quarter against San Francisco and then simply wasn't targeted again the rest of the game. Harris said he assumes it's a lack of coachability or attention to detail that's kept Okonkwo from ever getting a real toehold, noting he's a pending free agent and Harris will 'at least watch where he goes in 2026.'
- 2026-03-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-09]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman doesn't like the idea of Washington pursuing Okonkwo in free agency, arguing it would functionally be a vote of no confidence in incumbent [[Ben Sinnott]] since the two are similar player types -- Okonkwo is a somewhat faster, better route-running, after-the-catch version of Sinnott. Waldman would rather Washington 'unwrap' and commit reps to Sinnott instead, using him as the receiving tight end in two-TE sets under Dan Quinn's run-heavy scheme, with a blocking specialist handling the in-line role rather than adding Okonkwo.
- 2026-03-12 — According to [[Chris Harris]] and Dave Kluge (Footballguys.com, untracked guest) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-03-12]]): Okonkwo, a long-time tease in Tennessee who Harris always believed had more ability than the Titans got out of him, signs with Washington as [[Zach Ertz]]'s replacement. Harris floats that Okonkwo could have a real role there with [[Jaden Daniels]] and a new offensive coordinator, but Kluge talks him out of much optimism -- Ertz's value came from being a 'pro's pro' who reads zone coverage and breaks with the quarterback, a skill Okonkwo has never shown, and it would be a mistake to assume any new Washington tight end inherits Ertz's security-blanket role by default. Kluge pegs Okonkwo as more likely a tight end 20 dart-throw who's fast but unrefined -- a big-play best-ball option rather than a weekly starter, per Harris's 'cop-out' framing.
- 2026-07-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and Bob Harris ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-13]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: with Harris already holding shares of Okonkwo, both hosts like him as a value at TE21 current ADP, walking into the kind of role [[Zach Ertz]] previously thrived in for Washington under a new [[Jayden Daniels]]-led offense. Waldman argues Okonkwo is more explosive than Ertz and a more dangerous after-the-catch runner -- ideally deployed underneath, working the seams and flats, or as a play-action H-back running wheel/bootleg-adjacent routes given the new under-center scheme -- but flags that his undersized frame means fit matters, noting Washington's coaching lineage has historically valued undersized tight ends (Fred Davis, Chris Cooley, [[Ben Sinnott]]) as a reason for optimism about usage. He calls Okonkwo, along with Daniels and [[Terry McLaurin]], one of the 'most certain roles' on the team worth investing in without much risk, especially at TE2-range ADP.
- 2026-07-14 — According to Jake Seeley (guest, The Athletic, not a tracked expert) ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-07-14]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the most aggressive he'll get on any other Washington pass-catcher this preseason is Okonkwo, and only if the passing offense stays at roughly the same overall volume as 2025 - if his target range climbs from about 70 to 90-plus on a similar catch rate, that production puts him in the back-end TE1 conversation. Short of that, both Seeley and Chris Harris say no amount of camp praise will move Okonkwo's rank; Harris calls him strictly a September waiver-wire watch, not a draftable piece now.
