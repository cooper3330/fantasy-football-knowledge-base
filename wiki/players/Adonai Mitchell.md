## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-29 — According to guest **Eric Froton** (NBC Sports college football analyst — not a tracked expert) and **James Koh** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on *Reception Perception: The Show*, hosted solo by Koh with [[Matt Harmon]] absent ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-29]]) [Dynasty]: both are "torn" on him. Elite ball-tracking and hand-eye coordination on deep balls at an estimated 6'4"/190 — Froton says he wins late via hand usage, timing and tracking at the catch point — but a thin, largely linear release without much route manipulation, and real short-to-intermediate separation questions: 1.8 yards per route, only three targets behind the line of scrimmage all season, and 3.4 yards after the catch. Froton grades him behind [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] and [[Keon Coleman]] in this tier of big receivers because he hasn't shown the "complete package" needed to profile as more than a deep-threat specialist at the next level, and separately flags Texas teammate [[Xavier Worthy]] as a similar size/speed archetype who tests faster.
