# Ollie Gordon II

## Expert Takes

- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said he came away unimpressed watching Ollie Gordon II's tape relative to his hype as a projected top prospect -- he didn't think Gordon looked as fast as advertised, and said Oklahoma State's scheme and Gordon's decision-making within it remind him of [[Chuba Hubbard]], projecting he may need a couple of years to learn to read an NFL running game and make mature decisions. Waldman does not view him as a true top-of-class prospect and said he may not even be the third-best back in this incoming class, despite plenty of outside analysts ranking him top three.
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-13]]) [Dynasty]: more promise than present substance. The 2023 season (over 2,200 total yards) was a big one and he looked the part at the Senior Bowl, where he won running back of the week for his team, but the swing factor is down-to-down consistency — improve it and he is [[Najee Harris]], fail to and he is [[A.J. Dillon]] or 'just a guy'. Angelo likes his cognitive skill set at the second level and in open space, while the decision-making behind the line of scrimmage is where he struggles. [[Matt Waldman]] added a Miles Sanders comp: a back who looks the part physically and is natural in open space, but runs to open grass rather than to how the scheme develops. He is a volume-based back, currently a fringe third-to-fourth-round prospect; Angelo says a good combine pushing him to round two would be the sweet spot, since he needs opportunity.
- 2025-05-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-19]]) [Dynasty]: Gordon is a free-square, back-of-roster add in deeper and dynasty leagues purely on fit — he is big, fast and strong, and Miami's scheme essentially says 'here's the hole, hit it,' which suits him. Waldman does not see a superstar: he expects Gordon is the kind of back who has one huge year off an injury ahead of him and gets viewed the way [[Elijah Mitchell]] once was, after which he has to grow into a more nuanced player. If he does not, Waldman's comps are Tevin Coleman and Mitchell — a reserve who bounces around the league offering hope rather than an annual top-15 to top-20 back. Bob Harris (Footballguys, not tracked) noted he is free at ADP and asked whether to take some; Waldman said yes, worth it that late partly for the tradeable eyeballs.
- 2025-08-21 — According to Jake Ciely of The Athletic (guest, NOT a tracked expert) on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-21]] [Redraft (Standard)]: Gordon was his No. 3 super-deep sleeper, on the argument that [[Jaylen Wright]] has done everything possible to lay down as a doormat and let Gordon take the job. Ciely said he was laughed at two months ago for arguing Gordon fits the Raheem Mostert role in this offense better than Wright does — people answer with Mostert's 25 mph explosiveness, which misses the point about the role itself, which carried real touchdown equity inside the 10. He noted [[De'Von Achane]]'s calf injury widens the door further, and that if the switch happens in Week 1 or 2 everyone will be clamoring for Gordon. He would still take a flyer on Wright first right now. [[Chris Harris]] agreed Wright is the right play a week out, called Gordon a great super-deep sleeper you should not draft, and both flagged how hard the in-season switch will be to time.
- 2025-08-22 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Gordon had been nominated as a super-deep sleeper by Jake Seeley (not a tracked expert) on the previous day's show, and Harris says the [[Jaylen Wright]] practice injury is one news update away from invalidating that listing — if Wright misses significant time, Gordon becomes the clear number two behind [[De'Von Achane]] (himself nursing a calf injury), with only Mike Boone otherwise in the room, and would be 'draftable in a lot of standard-sized fantasy leagues' rather than a deep stash. Harris's caveat: if the Wright injury is serious enough the Dolphins might sign an outside veteran such as [[Brian Robinson Jr.]], in which case Gordon returns to the super-deep tier.
- 2025-08-22 — According to [[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-08-22]]: host James Koh (not a tracked expert) said that with [[De'Von Achane]] out for a few weeks with a calf strain and backup [[Jaylen Wright]] also helped off the field with an undisclosed injury, it is looking more and more like the rookie Gordon could have a big role in Miami — though Koh framed the whole Dolphins situation as bad vibes (Mike McDaniel hot seat, [[Tyreek Hill]] reportedly not wanting to be there).
- 2025-08-25 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-25]]): with [[Jaylen Wright]] week to week and [[De'Von Achane]] out for the entire second half of August, Harris raised Gordon to sit side by side with Wright as a deep flyer, noting the rookie is the only fully healthy Miami back you would seriously consider giving carries to, unless you are thrilled with Mike Boone or Aaron Shampklin. He treats this as opportunity-driven rather than an upgraded evaluation, and says he will watch cutdown day and the signings immediately after: if Miami makes no transaction at running back, that probably means Achane is fine and maybe Wright is fine too, which would collapse the opening again.
- 2025-09-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-09-01]]): with [[De'Von Achane]] not yet practicing and [[Jaylen Wright]] out into September or October after a procedure, Gordon is the only healthy back on Miami's active roster. Waldman says the return of Jeff Wilson Jr. does not kill the excitement but is a healthy hedge — it pressures Gordon to perform early, because if he fumbles or cannot find the hole and 'needs directions' like Darren McFadden, as he occasionally did at Oklahoma State, Mike McDaniel is a play-it-safe coach who will lean on Wilson. He recalls Gordon leading college football in rushing two years ago before looking lost last year. Bob Harris (not tracked) cited Travis Wingfield of the team site calling Gordon a grab-and-stash who will be a big part down the stretch, and said he looked the part in the final exhibition; Waldman conceded a bias, describing him as an athletic centrefold, 'Lou Ferrigno with a football', and has drafted him in round 16 of his last four drafts.
- 2025-09-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: The Dolphins backfield behind [[De'Von Achane]] was a July storyline that fully delivered — [[Alexander Mattison]] is out for the year with a neck injury and [[Jaylen Wright]] played poorly then needed a minor leg procedure and is expected to miss multiple weeks, leaving Achane and rookie Gordon as the only healthy backs on the active roster, with Jeff Wilson on the practice squad and possibly activated. Harris calls Gordon's Week 1 workload a big story. Gordon rose about five percentage points over the weekend to 35% rostered, which pushed him off guest BRANDON FUNSTON's waiver list; Funston narrowly prefers [[Jaydon Blue]] as a future bet, while Harris has no strong take and notes that if Miami has already decided Gordon gets the goal line, the ranking flips.
