- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman said he came away unimpressed watching Ollie Gordon II's tape relative to his hype as a projected top prospect -- he didn't think Gordon looked as fast as advertised, and said Oklahoma State's scheme and Gordon's decision-making within it remind him of [[Chuba Hubbard]], projecting he may need a couple of years to learn to read an NFL running game and make mature decisions. Waldman does not view him as a true top-of-class prospect and said he may not even be the third-best back in this incoming class, despite plenty of outside analysts ranking him top three.
