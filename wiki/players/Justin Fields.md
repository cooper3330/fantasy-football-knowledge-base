# Justin Fields

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-19]]): with Chicago holding the 1st-overall pick and [[Caleb Williams]] available, frames Fields as a near-certain trade/release candidate this offseason and picks Atlanta or Pittsburgh as his best landing spots — "he needs to get out of Chicago," citing a real fan-base/organizational split that makes it hard for him to trust the building going forward, independent of talent. Prefers Atlanta specifically: a ready-made weapon set (Drake London, Kyle Pitts, Bijan Robinson), a strong offensive line, and a real running game, plus a new coaching staff (Raheem Morris) without Chicago's baggage. Draws an explicit processing-speed comparison to fellow Atlanta-adjacent quarterback [[Desmond Ridder]]: Fields "may be a little tick slow sometimes" processing the field but still understands when to throw and when not to throw against certain coverages better than Ridder did, projecting a more sophisticated decision-maker than what Atlanta had in 2023 under then-HC Arthur Smith (coach — out of scope for this wiki, since departed). Pittsburgh (Mike Tomlin) is graded as a solid second option — good and improving offensive line, but a receiver room "not quite as strong" as Atlanta's.

- 2024-02-26 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-26]]): despite the destination uncertainty, keeps Fields as a locked-in fantasy top-10 quarterback no matter where he lands, and says he'll draft Fields as his QB1 in a lot of best ball drafts this cycle given the discount his uncertain landing spot creates. Floats a value-stack idea: pairing Fields with [[Anthony Richardson]] in the mid-to-late rounds, arguing that if Richardson's rookie-year flashes (before his injury) hold up over a full season, he could end up rostering two of fantasy's top-five quarterbacks — likening both to a [[Jalen Hurts]]-style profile that scores at a high rate without needing to lead the league in passing yardage. Relays sourcing from Pro Football Weekly's Hub Arkush (not tracked): Fields is universally well-regarded as a person and teammate, with the only real question being whether he's proven himself an elite passer yet. Also relays that Atlanta remains the most-discussed speculated destination, with [[Kirk Cousins]] also in the mix there and most quarterback-seeking teams likely weighing both options.

- 2024-02-29 — According to **Kevin Knight** (Falcons reporter, SB Nation's Falcoholic — guest, not a tracked expert) on *Reception Perception: The Show*, with [[Matt Harmon]] absent ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-02-29]]): reports Falcons buzz has shifted toward pouncing on a Fields trade if Atlanta can't move into the top three of the draft, likely for a modest package (a 2024 day-two pick plus a conditional 2025 day-two pick) — low financial/draft risk given Fields is only guaranteed for two years even if his fifth-year option is exercised. Says the front office wants a quarterback with real long-term-starter upside rather than a short-term bridge, and views Fields as having that chance even if it isn't a certainty.

- 2024-03-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-04]]): weighing Pittsburgh's veteran quarterback options (Fields, [[Russell Wilson]], Kenny Pickett, Mason Rudolph), picks Fields as the "ideal" fit — his athletic ability offsets the Steelers' still-unresolved offensive-line problems, and he "fits very well within that culture" as "a worker" and "a young player to grow with," with Pickett kept as a developmental backup. Separately, on Atlanta: still rates Fields the best long-term-upside option there over [[Kirk Cousins]] and [[Baker Mayfield]] — comparing his ceiling to what Sean McVay (coach — out of scope) extracted from Jared Goff — while conceding Cousins offers a more immediate path to contention.


- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (co-host — not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-19]]: reacting to the finalized trade (Chicago to Pittsburgh for a conditional 2025 sixth-round pick), both "feel it" — Waldman calls Pittsburgh's return "far better" value than what other teams paid at quarterback this cycle (e.g. Minnesota/[[Sam Darnold]]), and expects either a full sit-and-develop year behind [[Russell Wilson]] or an "intermittent benching" competition rather than a clean QB1 handoff. Reframes the "hasn't proven himself as a passer" critique relayed from Hub Arkush (Pro Football Weekly, not tracked) on 2024-02-26: attributes much of Fields's Chicago struggles to a poor offensive coordinator and an offensive line that didn't protect him, not talent — repeats the "needed a Josh Allen-style reset" framing, adding that Chicago still "did right by him" at the end by prioritizing his long-term development situation even at the cost of immediate playing time.

- 2024-03-19 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-19]]), reacting to the finalized Bears-to-Steelers trade: the hosts disagree on the return. One host calls trading Fields for only a conditional sixth-round pick (upgrading to a fourth if he plays 51%+ of Pittsburgh's snaps) "a flat-out bomb" given his rookie-scale contract and said he would rather have kept Fields as a cheap, high-quality backup than take that little back. The other argues Chicago had essentially no leverage — the trade was common knowledge across the league for months — and that keeping Fields in the building behind Caleb Williams was untenable given how much the roster loves him, framing Chicago as having "genuinely wanted to do right by him" by sending him somewhere (Pittsburgh) he can compete to start amid uncertainty over [[Russell Wilson]]'s recent play.

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]]: on the same Pittsburgh pole-position report, frames Fields as being given real time to win the job rather than facing an all-or-nothing competition — losing the competition early wouldn't mean the team is "jettisoning" him, since he's competing against a proven, quality NFL quarterback in [[Russell Wilson]] regardless of Wilson's recent performances.
