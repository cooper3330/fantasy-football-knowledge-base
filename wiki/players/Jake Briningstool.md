- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman calls Briningstool a fascinating player and the highest-variance evaluation in his class. Because of the highs he has seen from him as a pass catcher, it would not shock Waldman one bit if Briningstool emerges as the best ball winner in the entire 2025 tight end class. But there is enough holding him back that he could fail to emerge outright and get stuck in his own head when facing difficult targets. Waldman's baseline read is that his floor skills are good enough to make a team and contribute, while the variance in his pass catching may cap him as a tight end two or a playable tight end three in a rotation.
