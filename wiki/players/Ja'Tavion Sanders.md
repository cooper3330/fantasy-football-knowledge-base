# Ja'Tavion Sanders

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes. (ASR note: the raw transcript renders his
> name as "Getavian Sanders"; normalized here to the real name of the Texas
> tight end, Ja'Tavion Sanders.)

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-25]] [Best Ball]: "kind of feeling him" as a late-draft dynasty/best-ball flyer — an "aspiring [[David Njoku]] starter kit," with hope he blocks better and catches more reliably early in his career than Njoku did. Views tight ends at this tier as good targets around the 20th round of deep best-ball drafts, since several in this class carry athletic upside that could let them emerge as starters within a year or two.

- 2024-03-30 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-30]] [Dynasty]: a lot of potential as a matchup receiver who can use size, quickness and technique to create mismatches, even when pressed at the line — but has to improve his footwork ("great hand counters, but you win with your feet first"), a flaw again compared to [[David Njoku]] (and Jonnu Smith, not tracked). Has the athletic ability to earn playing time, but the technical gaps could delay when he reaches his ceiling. Named among the class's more pro-ready group alongside [[Ben Sinnott]], just behind [[Brock Bowers]] and [[Cade Stover]].
- 2024-04-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-22]]) [Dynasty]: doesn't fully buy the 'George Kittle' blocking comp for Sanders but sees him as a plus version of Jonnu Smith as a receiving threat; would be most excited about Kansas City drafting him to develop behind [[Travis Kelce]], and also likes a Miami fit alongside Jonnu Smith.
- 2024-04-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-29]]) [Dynasty]: liked Carolina as a landing spot because the Panthers have 'never really had' a true receiving tight end, with incumbent Tommy Tremble profiling more as a blocker/special-teamer. He referenced Cliff Kingsbury's tight end usage in Arizona as a ceiling data point — Zach Ertz's 588-yard year was the best output there, with most other seasons in the 200-300 range — projecting 500-600 yards as reasonable if that offensive approach holds in Carolina. Waldman noted this outlook initially dropped Sanders a few spots in his post-draft rankings, though he flagged he may revisit that with further research.
- 2024-08-05 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-05]]): with Tommy Tremble dealing with a calf injury and Ian Thomas out possibly into the season, Waldman expects Sanders to have meaningful games as a rookie but not to be a weekly starter you feel good about. His distinction is that reports of 'more meaningful opportunities' translate to snaps, and more snaps do not automatically become production — the honest question is whether Sanders becomes a bye-week fill-in or an every-week starter, and Waldman does not see every-week starter material yet. He is more positive for [Dynasty], calling Sanders promising. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) said he has not invested this year, noted Carolina added Jacob Hollister, and joked that the same caution could be said of any Carolina tight end going back to Greg Olsen and Wesley Walls.
- 2024-11-26 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-11-26]]): Sanders suffered a scary neck injury and was carted off after looking like he was on his way to a big performance; Dave Canales said the same day it appears he avoided a serious injury, which Harmon treats cautiously since a neck injury sounds serious regardless. Both hosts are high on him as a player — a high-upside rookie tight end who is only 21 years old, an age at which rookie tight ends almost never make an impact — and he is part of the late-round drafting Harmon credits Carolina for, alongside [[Jalen Coker]]. Koh calls him such an interesting player and offers prayers up; Harmon says he is really intrigued and hopes he gets back on the field to keep developing.
- 2025-07-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-07]]) [Best Ball]: Sanders is 'totally' underrated at TE31. He was an early day-two/day-three pick and one of the top prospects at the position, a good runner after the catch who tracks the ball well; extrapolating the roughly seven-game stretch before he landed on his neck in the Kansas City game gets you close to 700 receiving yards over a full season. Add three to five red zone touchdowns from an improved [[Bryce Young]] and Waldman says that is a fantasy starter who is not being drafted anywhere near like one. He also likes the Panthers receiving room as an open 'casting call' beyond [[Tetairoa McMillan]]. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) agreed and paired him with [[Theo Johnson]] as the two tight ends he grabs if he misses the tight end train, especially in tight end premium.
- 2025-07-30 — According to co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-07-30]]): Sanders is his Carolina sleeper tight end — a very young second-year player out of Texas with a real opportunity to establish himself as the starter now that Tommy Tremble has had back surgery, having not been especially mobile to begin with. Koh calls Sanders a really good athlete who would add another athletic body to the Panthers' pass-catching group if he pops. [[Matt Harmon]] did not give his own evaluation of Sanders in this segment.
