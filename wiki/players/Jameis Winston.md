- 2024-06-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-06-18]]): Waldman thinks Jameis Winston could be this year's 'Joe Flacco' - a cheap, safe insurance option who fits Kevin Stefanski's downfield, play-action, run-heavy scheme better than Deshaun Watson currently does while Watson recovers from shoulder surgery. Waldman reads Cleveland's decision to bring in both Winston and Tyler Huntley, rather than hand the backup job to Dorian Thompson-Robinson, as a sign the organization wants a real contingency plan in case Watson struggles again, calling Winston 'not great, but cheap and safe enough.'
- 2024-09-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman is 'totally feeling' that Winston starts for Cleveland by midseason. His reasoning is financial and behavioural rather than schematic — the Browns have already restructured to free up as much cap space as they could off [[Deshaun Watson]], leaving them with as much room as anyone in the NFL, which he reads as preparation for moving on. If Watson does not come around, Waldman says Winston 'is more than serviceable.' Untracked co-host Bob Harris agreed, called it 'a very Browns thing to happen,' and added that Winston is the ultimate rah-rah guy who will fire a locker room up.
