# Aaron Anderson

## Expert Takes

- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Anderson does a good job selling a 'stair-step' release -- diving inside then straightening out to threaten vertical before breaking inside -- but he's missing a step: he should run into the toes of the defender and win with his hands at the top of the stem instead of rounding off his break early. Rounding off opens the angle for defenders to cut off the route, which Waldman says will matter most on the intermediate timing routes (curls, digs) that top receivers get targeted on in critical spots.
