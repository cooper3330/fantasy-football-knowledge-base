- 2024-08-22 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-08-22]]): Engram is the signal flare at tight end — he is not truly one of the system-safe group, but he is good enough, and once he is the sixth or seventh tight end off your board everyone after him becomes a big question mark on system or ability. Waldman's illustrations of the drop-off: [[David Njoku]]'s huge share of production came with [[Joe Flacco]] rather than with the quarterback now tied to a large contract who Waldman suspects would rather disappear than play, and Dallas Goedert is talented in a possibly better system but may be third in the pecking order regardless.
