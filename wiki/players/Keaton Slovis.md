# Keaton Slovis

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-08]]): once considered a top prospect at USC, now graded as a pure developmental player. Has arm talent, pocket toughness and athletic ability that will intrigue an NFL team, but "plays a lot slower than his timed speed" and is much better attacking the boundary vertically than targeting the middle of the field. Struggles to recognize what's open early in a route, stares down targets past the point of patience (a tell for defensive backs who peel off or cut off the throwing lane), and shows a real lack of peripheral vision and pressure-origin recognition — "it's almost like he's playing without a seat belt."
