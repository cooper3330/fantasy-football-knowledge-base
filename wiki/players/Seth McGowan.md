# Seth McGowan

## Expert Takes

- 2026-04-09 — According to Paul Pertichese, guest analyst on the [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-04-09]] (not a tracked expert) [Dynasty]: Seth McGowan is Pertichese's RB8, boosted by a strong combine (223 lbs, sub-4.5 40, strong jumps) that Pertichese used specifically as a tiebreaker over the very similar [[Kaytron Allen]] -- he sees the two playing almost identically on tape as power, inside-the-numbers early-down backs, but gives McGowan the edge for 'untapped' athleticism the testing revealed. Two flags keep him behind Allen in likely NFL draft capital: he's considerably older (turning 25) and had a publicized off-field incident earlier in his college career. Pertichese was careful to note this isn't him 'taking the cheese' on combine numbers -- he had McGowan and Allen close on film before the testing even happened.
- 2026-05-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-05-28]]): McGowan (6'0/223, 4.40 forty, 42.5-inch vertical, 10'11 broad) has a serious off-field background -- a 2021 arrest for robbery, conspiracy to commit robbery and assault and battery with a dangerous weapon, pled guilty to larceny, 3 months in prison plus a year of probation -- and turns 25 in October, making him one of the oldest prospects in the league (older than Bijan Robinson, same age as Breece Hall and De'Von Achane). On tape, Harmon charted him at 71% success rate on outside zone and roughly 78% on man/gap runs that bounced outside, but only 41% on inside man/gap runs -- his bounce-outside instinct is both his best and worst trait. Harmon flags a scheme mismatch concern: the Colts are one of only two teams in the NFL running man/gap at a higher rate than zone, and rank bottom-10 in outside rush rate, which cuts against McGowan's natural profile. Still, Harmon calls him the most talented backup running back on the Colts roster behind [[Jonathan Taylor]] and [[DJ Giddens]], capable of being a consistent contributor if he makes the 53-man roster (drafted 237th overall).
- 2026-07-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-07-13]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Waldman likes McGowan over fellow Colts rookie RB [[DJ Giddens]] for the RB2 job, calling him 'the hammer' -- a straightforward downhill runner with good size and speed who fits Indianapolis's gap-scheme run game and can catch passes in the short area. He expects McGowan to win the number-two job and get early chances to show he can build on it; if he does, Waldman thinks the Colts could eventually give [[Jonathan Taylor]] more rest, though he doubts Taylor -- who Bob Harris says wants to play every down -- will willingly give up snaps.
- 2026-08-10 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-10]]) [Dynasty]: the rookie out of Oklahoma is a power runner with some speed -- a 'straight-line banger' who can produce chunk yardage between the tackles -- but Waldman doesn't view him as the passing-down/third-down option compared to [[Ulysses Bentley]]. Local beat reporting (James Boyd, The Athletic) instead has McGowan looking like the back best suited to a between-the-tackles role in Indianapolis's currently wide-open RB3 competition behind [[Jonathan Taylor]].
- 2026-08-17 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Harris Football Podcast - 2026-08-17]]): A little bird told Harris that McGowan, a very late-round pick, impressed in Indianapolis's joint practice with the Colts, to the point [[DJ Giddens]] could be in trouble for the backup job behind [[Jonathan Taylor]]. Waldman isn't surprised -- he calls McGowan a hard runner who fits a gap-scheme offense well with good speed. If Taylor were to miss extended time, Waldman projects McGowan as the two-down runner alongside [[Ulysses Bentley]] handling passing downs as the scat-back complement.
