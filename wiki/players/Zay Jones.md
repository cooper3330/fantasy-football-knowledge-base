## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host — not a
  tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-28]]): graded as
  a passable, functional outside receiver — "better than" Van Jefferson (not a
  tracked page), "better than [[Marvin Mims Jr.]]," "better than" Josh Reynolds
  (not a tracked page) "and [[Gabe Davis]]"
  — but not someone who gives you many big plays, and "getting pretty long in
  the tooth." Cited as a real injury/aging risk for Jacksonville's already-thin
  receiver room: when Jones missed time in 2023, the passing game around
  [[Christian Kirk]] and [[Calvin Ridley]] "couldn't produce anything." Part of
  why the hosts debate placing Jacksonville in the "disaster-adjacent" tier
  alongside Pittsburgh and Denver.
