# Ray Davis

> ⚠️ Takes below are from February 2024, pre-draft. He was not yet on an NFL
> roster at the time of this take — treat as historical scouting context
> until superseded by newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]): 5'8", 217 lbs, a "Frank Gore-esque" build that plays more fluidly than the frame would suggest. Competent across all three phases — runner, receiver, and pass protector — and graded as someone who "can come in and make an impact on the NFL roster right away," though not expected to go higher than the fourth or fifth round.
- 2024-02-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-02-01]]), relaying secondhand Senior Bowl reports rather than his own tape: names Davis as one of the prospects he specifically needs to revisit based on what he's hearing.
- 2024-03-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23]]), solo RB-class cast: singles Davis out as the player who "epitomizes" how narrow the margin is among the class's top-15 backs — small early improvements could make any of them a rookie's most productive option. His between-the-tackles running is the strength: decisive, determined, punishing, bouncing off multiple tackle types for a 211-pounder with good pad use in tight quarters. Pass protection needs real work on setting angles, foot positioning, and developing a punch.

- 2024-04-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] on *Feel It or F@#k It* ([[Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-01]]), with co-host **Bob Harris** (not a tracked expert): 5'8", 211 lbs, but "runs like he's 225" — the fun of watching him is that he'll run over and through defenders, with enough footwork to also go around them; comped to an undersized Travis Henry (not tracked). Usable as a dump-off receiver too — "makes mincemeat out of secondary defenders" in that role. Pass protection "needs a lot of work right now," which is the specific thing that could keep him out of a rotation or major role early. Floats him as a fourth-or-fifth-round redraft best-ball target — a low-investment, high-reward add at running back if still on the board that late.
- 2024-04-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-29]]): pushed back on the reaction that Ray Davis threatens James Cook's value, arguing Josh Allen's own tendency to keep goal-line carries himself already caps any Bills back's touchdown equity more than Davis would. He projected Davis as a short-yardage/closeout-carry role player who is an okay-not-great receiver and a weak pass blocker, with room to improve in both, noting Buffalo is historically cautious deploying rookie backs early to protect Allen. His expectation: Davis flashes promise as a runner in 2024 without threatening full-time work absent an injury to Cook.
