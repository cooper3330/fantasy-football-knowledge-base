# Ja'Corey Brooks

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-24]]): the Louisville (formerly Alabama) receiver is a Jekyll-and-Hyde evaluation. Watching him run routes is exciting — he releases well against press, has extra burst to the ball late in the vertical route, and shows real man-to-man route skills in the short and intermediate game, enough that Waldman calls him a future starter as a route runner. Then the ball reaches his hands: late reactions, fading away on jump-up-and-throughs, suboptimal attack positions, clap attacks and focus drops. Waldman's summary is that until Brooks exorcises the demons from his pass catching, all you can do is hope and pray, and that as things stand he is a smaller [[Marquez Valdes-Scantling]] with more upside and better routes. See [[Catch Technique and Ball Tracking]].
- 2025-04-21 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-21]]): asked whether the ex-Alabama, now Louisville receiver has starter potential, Waldman gave a striking split verdict — from the release all the way up to the catch point the tape looks really good, and then at the catch point he plays like he is possessed by a spirit that is scared of footballs. The hands work is the whole gap: Waldman says if the hands matched the rest of the game, Brooks would be a top-five-to-seven receiver on the RSP board. His template for a fix is Quincy Enunwa, who addressed the same problem at Nebraska before his neck injury; if Brooks can do that, we may hear from him.
