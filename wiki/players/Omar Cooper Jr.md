- 2026-10-22 — According to Jeff Bell of Football Guys with [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-10-22]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: both see [[Omar Cooper Jr]] as a high-upside flier opposite Garrett Wilson with the Jets, built almost entirely on one data point -- a garbage-time long touchdown against the Patriots in what became a one-score game. Bell ranks him outside the top 36 at receiver but just behind that cutoff, arguing there's little real difference between WR24 and WR40 given the uncertainty; Harris has him WR38, ahead of [[Jaylen Noel]] at WR43, and admits a bias toward Indiana University alumni as 'glue guys' who 'figure it out.' Both flag that Adonai Mitchell's return (practicing this week, timetable unclear) threatens Cooper's role, though Harris contrasts the two as different archetypes: Mitchell is the boom/bust deep-shot receiver while Cooper profiles as a possession target capable of 8 catches in any given week, with added versatility to play outside in multi-tight-end sets.
- 2026-10-27 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-10-27]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: Cooper went for 5/5/115 and his second straight 100-yard game (218 yards over two games), with Harmon's confidence meter 'very, very high' that it continues -- he called Cooper one of the best tackle-breaking prospects he's ever charted and said the Jets haven't even seen his best yards-after-catch stuff yet. Harmon noted the Jets are using Cooper as a true mismatch piece out of the backfield (his touchdown came on a backfield-alignment route the Dolphins lost him on), and said he's winning on post/corner routes -- more of a downfield profile than the pure YAC guy Harmon originally projected. Harmon floated Cooper as possibly the best complementary receiver [[Garrett Wilson]] has had aside from a half-season of prime Davante Adams, and said he could lead all rookie receivers in yardage from here on. Both hosts noted Cooper, not Wilson, is the one who 'needs role catering' given his skill level, yet it's Wilson stuck running X.
