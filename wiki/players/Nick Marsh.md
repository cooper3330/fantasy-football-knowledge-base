- 2024-10-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Felix Sharpe of Campus2Canton (not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-23]]) [Dynasty]: Michigan State true freshman Nick Marsh (6'3\", 205, No. 6, River Rouge HS, Detroit) has emerged as the clear No. 1 in Jonathan Smith's first year and should be picked up off the waiver wire in any Campus to Canton league rather than left in next year's freshman pool. Waldman had downgraded QB [[Aidan Chiles]] preseason precisely because Michigan State's skill group looked dire; Marsh has answered that. Production: 24 receptions, 433 yards, one touchdown, including 8-113 in the upset of Iowa and 8-194 with the score against Maryland, where he got behind the defense twice on posts. He is third among all freshmen in receiving yards, behind only [[Jeremiah Smith]] and [[Ryan Williams]]. Profile: a four-star prospect, a bigger boundary receiver rather than a speed guy, living on slants, comebacks, hitches, posts and outs — a simple route tree run reliably. Waldman's comp is [[Keon Coleman]]'s emergence at Michigan State in a sluggish offense: the kind of break you just go scoop up.
