# Trevor Etienne

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2024-07-15 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-15]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman flagged Trevor Etienne, [[Travis Etienne Jr.]]'s younger brother and a Florida-to-Georgia transfer, as a good receiver who works well in space and a solid inside runner with good hips and functional NFL power, but said pass protection is a real weakness -- he needs to learn to punch and stop dropping his head into blocks, which lets defenders work around him easily. Waldman also noted a recent DUI will likely cost him some early-season college availability.
- 2025-02-13 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-02-13]]) [Dynasty]: his second 'lunch pail' back — literally the opposite of his brother [[Travis Etienne Jr.]], who Angelo says was a cannonball accelerator who never added a second conceptual layer to his game. With Trevor, everything is adequate, nothing far above or far below: a spot starter and probably a tertiary option in most offenses, though he could be a secondary piece in the right backfield. [[Matt Waldman]] agreed and added that Etienne was merely fine at Georgia but sizzled at Florida, sees explosive plays rather than breakaway plays, and calls [[Woody Marks]] the low end of his outcome range — with Etienne currently the higher-end version of Marks in open-field footwork and pass catching.
