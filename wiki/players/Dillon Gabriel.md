# Dillon Gabriel

## Expert Takes

- 2025-03-31 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-31]]) [Dynasty]: Gabriel finds the hole in cover three, layers the ball over shallow and intermediate coverage, and places throws so receivers can make back-shoulder catches rather than take safety hits to the chest. He feels blindside edge pressure well and rolls past it to the open flat, works through two to three reads, and can buy time outside the pocket. The problem is pressure sequencing: he is reactive to hot, fast edge pressure from a safety, corner or linebacker, and with two points of pressure he can roll to the side of the field where nothing came open while cutting off the side where receivers were running free. His first instinct is to retreat or bolt outside rather than climb, costing him check-downs and drawing sacks in the black zone, and he sometimes drifts too close to his own blocker or toward defenders and generates pressure that was not there. He also lacks the confidence to pull the trigger on favorable zone windows while anticipating unfavorable ones, so his leverage reads must improve. Waldman calls him a fascinating, game-competitive, creative player who could become a compelling backup and could transcend his current grade with work.
- 2025-04-18 — According to guest Cory Kinnan (not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Harmon]]'s show ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-04-18]]): a genuinely anticipatory thrower out of necessity — at 5'10" without the arm to drive threaded windows, he releases rails, fades and posts before the receiver turns his head, which deserves credit. The discount is context: Josh Heupel's UCF offense was 'the most juvenile college offense you've ever seen' (nines, curls, slants into green grass), Oklahoma was similar, and even Oregon's charting profile is 'diluted with stick routes' the same way Kinnan felt about [[Bo Nix]]. He calls the combine one of the worst scripted on-air throwing sessions he has seen. Verdict: a backup who can give you the bare minimum if you hammer home a routine and game plan, with real worry about the tools as the hashes narrow and the middle of the field condenses. Harmon likes him a little as a backup prospect on that anticipation.
- 2025-07-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-07-28]]): 'fuck it' on Gabriel as 'the [[Brock Purdy]] of the Browns'. Waldman calls him a mispick in the early half of the draft and a candidate to be a developmental practice-squad body or a QB3 with a good preseason. He grants Gabriel is aggressive downfield, has some movement, competes and tries to prolong plays, but says he is not the organized pocket mover with the timing, placement and speed of field reads Purdy showed at Iowa State — more of a system quarterback than Cleveland is portraying, the kind draft nicks praise for 'moxie' and whiteboard work. Waldman ties it to his broader camp caution: highlight clips from practice do not show the high-end conceptual adjustments that separate a good college starter from an actual pro producer.
- 2025-08-04 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-04]]): asked if Gabriel is a future starter prospect, Waldman says 'fuck that — come on, man,' allowing only 'maybe in a couple years.' He is skeptical of Cleveland's praise for Gabriel's processing, arguing teams routinely confuse whiteboard processing with on-field processing — his example is [[Desmond Ridder]], who aced the whiteboard but on the field misjudged defender trajectory and closing distance so badly Waldman jokes he would have needed his hand held crossing the street. Gabriel is not that extreme, he says, but has real issues of that kind that will take time. He cites ESPN reporting on a Sunday-night practice in which the defense disguised looks and switched coverages pre-snap and Gabriel had an awful session — not proof he will always be that way, but proof he is that way now, and 'not a starting-caliber candidate right now.' Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) would rather have [[Joe Flacco]] early, and notes Jimmy Haslam has said he wants to see [[Shedeur Sanders]] play this year.
