# Caden Prieskorn

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]): Prieskorn is a fun watch because of his one-handed plucks on a variety of targets — if the rest of his game matched that, Waldman says he would be a really compelling NFL prospect. As it stands his releases, routes and blocking all need a lot of work, and he is a linear mover who does well in a straight line but struggles when asked to move outside his frame, make sharp changes of direction and bend deeply. Waldman warns explicitly that anyone touting Prieskorn has probably been watching the highlight-reel catches rather than the full body of work.
