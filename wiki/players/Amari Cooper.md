# Amari Cooper

> ⚠️ Takes below are from March 2024. Team/role are as of that date and are
> almost certainly stale — treat as historical context until superseded by
> newer takes.

## Expert Takes

- 2024-03-11 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-11]]): reads him as Cleveland's secure top target regardless of the [[Jerry Jeudy]] trade — "Amari Cooper ain't going anywhere" — and grades him "certainly an upgrade" over [[Courtland Sutton]] as [[Deshaun Watson]]'s top receiver, with Jeudy more likely to slot in as the offense's third option behind Cooper and [[David Njoku]].

- 2024-03-21 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-03-21]]): names Cooper (with [[David Njoku]]) as the one part of Cleveland's offense whose identity is settled — "I think the identity can be Amari Cooper and David Njoku" — with [[Jerry Jeudy]], [[Elijah Moore]] and [[Cedric Tillman]] as supporting pieces around them rather than a settled receiver-room hierarchy of their own. Points to the play-action passing success Cooper had with [[Joe Flacco]] late last season as a template the offense can lean back on.
- 2024-07-01 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-07-01]]) [Redraft]: Waldman, a self-described Browns and Cooper fan, says Cooper's WR23 ADP might be slightly high because he doubts Deshaun Watson returns to his pre-injury form unless Cleveland tailors the offense specifically to Watson's strengths -- which would waste some of Cooper's talent. He notes Cooper hasn't attended voluntary offseason work amid a contract dispute (wants to be paid market rate, with a strong case based on recent production) but hasn't formally held out. Digging into last year's splits, Waldman found Cooper was roughly a WR18 during Watson's healthy stretch and played to a WR1 level in the two games Watson looked good on return, which pushes him toward calling Cooper fairly priced with outperformance upside that is 'highly dependent' on Watson's health and play.
- 2024-07-23 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2024-07-23]]): Cooper has played the best football of his career in Cleveland and is now 'a true number one receiver' — something Harmon says he never believed about the Raiders/Cowboys version, whom he saw as a genuinely boom-bust, half-speed-at-times player rather than just volatile in the box score. 2023: 72 catches on 128 targets for 1,250 yards, a career-high 17.4 yards per reception against a career-third-lowest 56.3% catch rate. Reception Perception: 71% success rate vs man, 78% vs zone — right at the 70/80 benchmark — with an exceptional intermediate profile of 87.1% on digs, 91% on outs (never cracked 80% before Cleveland) and 78.7% on slants, routes making up about half his tree. Harmon credits the pure perimeter X role plus play-action off the [[Nick Chubb]] run game, and speculates the way Dallas shipped him out for nothing lit a fire under him. Risks: age-30 season, holding out over his $20M final year (fined over $100k for skipping minicamp), and possible scheme change under new OC Ken Dorsey. Harmon still called him the best player in the division's four rooms when ranking them; James Koh (not tracked) leaned younger, preferring Baltimore's room to Cleveland's partly on Cooper's age.
