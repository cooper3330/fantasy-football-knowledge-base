# Sincere McCormick

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.
     Don't overwrite old ones — see CLAUDE.md rule 4. -->
- 2024-12-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-02]]): feeling him as a pure speculative playoff-roster stash after 12 carries against Kansas City paced the Raiders backfield with [[Zamir White]] out and [[Alexander Mattison]] questionable and closer to returning. Waldman likes him as a tight-crease runner who trusts the scheme and hits the hole where he is supposed to, breaks tackles, bounces off hits and shows enough burst to get the corner; a touted Texas-collegiate producer at a smaller program who could never stick on an active roster and is now making the most of the chance. He projects him as a complement to Mattison down the stretch. Co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) cautioned this is a 2-10 Raiders team needing a lot of bench room, and to feel him as nothing more than a speculative play.
- 2024-12-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-09]]): 'fuck it' on McCormick as a future starter. Waldman likes what he sees — decisive like [[Isaiah Davis]], good contact balance, decent burst — but points to another loaded incoming running back class and argues the opportunity could leave him as fast as it arrived. Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) was more pointed, saying he would feel it only for the next four games because this is Antonio Pierce's pattern: he finds a guy late in the season and pumps him up, exactly as happened with [[Zamir White]] a year earlier, so be careful with McCormick.
- 2024-12-12 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-12]]) [Dynasty]: both think the UTSA UDFA is here to stay. Angelo says he 'looks every bit of a starter in this league' — through-contact work and early-acceleration burst to the second level — and notes he was the only back since last season to go over 60 yards at over five yards a carry against Steve Spagnuolo's Kansas City front with Chris Jones inside. Pass protection and receiving still need cleaning up and never were his thing. Waldman liked him coming out as a gap-scheme fit over zone (he'd press but not deep enough to look like an advanced zone decision-maker), and Las Vegas runs a lot of gap: decisive, tight-crease, strong contact balance, gets around the short corner when it's blocked. Both rate him clearly above [[Alexander Mattison]] and [[Zamir White]] for the same work; the caveat is a deep 2025 RB class and a possible staff change.
- 2026-08-24 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-24 (Feel It or Fk It)]]): Sincere McCormick, now with the 49ers, 'should have been on a roster last year' in Waldman's view -- he believes teams wrote McCormick off simply because he was undersized and didn't stick with the Raiders after a coaching change, not because of his actual tape. Waldman called him a smart, instinctive runner in contrast to the 'fast but dumb' runners who dominate many NFL depth charts, capable of plugging in for reasonable regular-season production, and suggested San Francisco may have brought him in partly as a training-camp evaluation body.
