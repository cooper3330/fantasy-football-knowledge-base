# CJ Dippre

## Expert Takes
<!-- Append new dated, attributed bullets here as sources are ingested.

- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]): Dippre, at Alabama after transferring from Maryland, does not beat defensive backs downfield in man coverage but can win up the seam against linebackers and some safeties. Waldman wants to see him develop better positioning and a more assertive hand attack when he does, and specifically a pullback after high-pointing in contested situations so the ball is not stripped away — he has seen Dippre drop accurate targets he would have caught had he protected the ball more quickly.
