[Redraft (Standard)]
- 2026-08-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2026-08-28]]) [Dynasty]: Sanders ('Rocket' to teammates), a 220-230 lb back who went undrafted in 2025 after an injury-plagued college career, carries scouting concerns that he might be 'stiff' and not a real football-position athlete -- good north-south top-end speed but not great in traffic or accelerating out of cuts. Harris pointedly compares the skepticism to how analysts fawn over other big-fast backs (Omarion Hampton, Bijan Robinson types get the 'underwear Olympics' bump) while Sanders doesn't. He notes that in December, after Quinshon Judkins got hurt, Sanders got real work in a 'stinky' Browns offense and looked like 'an absolute moose to tackle' with good power and patience between the tackles. With Judkins now healthy and Harris drafting Judkins heavily, and Dylan Sampson entrenched as the third-down back, there's no near-term path for Sanders -- but Harris says he'd be very interested to see Sanders get the early-down nod if Judkins gets hurt again.
