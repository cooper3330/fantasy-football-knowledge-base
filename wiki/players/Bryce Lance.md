# Bryce Lance

## Expert Takes

- 2025-12-08 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-12-08]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman compared Trey Lance's younger brother Bryce Lance -- like [[Christian Watson]], a tall, skinny North Dakota State product with real deep speed and YAC skills who can win the ball downfield -- favorably to Watson's early-career profile, while noting Bryce still needs to clean up his hands and route-running and could stand to add weight. He projected Bryce as a multi-year dynasty stash, not someone to expect production from in the next year or two, but a potential riser in three to four years in the mold of [[Alec Pierce]] or [[Jauan Jennings]] if he stays healthy.
- 2026-03-02 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-02]]) [Dynasty]: Trey Lance's brother (North Dakota State) is 'absolutely a combine winner' after running 4.34. Waldman thinks he's capable of becoming a good NFL receiver but 'isn't there yet' -- the workout mainly buys him earlier developmental opportunity and confirms the belief of scouts who were touting him as a strong athlete who can still grow into his frame.
- 2026-03-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-03-28]]) [Dynasty]: Lance (Trey Lance's brother) positions himself well to the ball with late hands and a smooth, timely high-point near the boundary, but ball tracking could be his biggest obstacle in the same way it was for [[Marvin Harrison Jr]] -- a flaw Waldman says he flagged early on Harrison, and one that has indeed shown up at both Ohio State and in Arizona. Lance leaves his feet unnecessarily on chest-level targets and is a habitual 'clap attacker,' catching with his palms instead of fingertips, which creates a violent recoil. Waldman expects him to go late Day 2/early Day 3 as a field-stretcher, with risk of Harrison-Jr.-style inconsistency if the catch-point technique isn't corrected.
- 2026-04-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-16]]) [Dynasty]: North Dakota State's Bryce Lance (6'3, 204) tested as a 'complete freak show' -- 4.34 40 (94th percentile), 41.5' vertical (96th), 11'1 broad (95th) -- and produced back-to-back 1,000-yard seasons averaging 16.8 yards per reception with 25 TDs over his last two years, though he lacked a breakout season until year four per JJ Zachariason's model. RP charting: 67.4% success vs. man, 73.6% vs. zone, 65% vs. press, with elite results on nines (61%), corners (80%) and posts (73%) but an 'atrocious' 41% on digs -- Harmon calls him a pure vertical/perimeter X who needs a deeper release package and more intermediate/short-area game to become a high-volume X. Contested catch rate was ~82% but he underhand-cradles the ball instead of fully extending, a technical flaw Harmon flags as similar to what he underweighted with [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]'s profile. Comps: [[Christian Watson]] and [[Marquez Valdes-Scantling]] (athletic profile), and [[Darius Slayton]]/early-career [[D.J. Chark]] (role/outcome range). Harmon views him as a late-third/early-fourth round arbitrage play on pricier vertical SEC receivers like [[Chris Brazzell]], whose success rates (67.5% man, 73.2% zone, 61% press) are nearly identical despite tougher competition. Projected role: [[Sacrificial X Receiver]] early, growing into more.
- 2026-04-28 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2026-04-28]]): projected as a sacrificial vertical X early in his rookie year for New Orleans, a field-stretcher who will make occasional splash plays down the sideline against single-high looks as defenses adjust; his presence is also read as evidence the Saints don't see [[Jordan Tyson]] as an X, freeing Tyson to move around the formation instead.
- 2026-06-29 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-06-29]]) [Dynasty]: Feels Bryce Lance (younger brother of Trey Lance) is closer to a rookie-year breakout than the public realizes, comping him to a slightly smaller, less powerful version of [[Christian Watson]] out of the same North Dakota State program -- Waldman flags the shared-school comp as 'logo scouting' but says the tape similarities (long, athletic frames, some ball-fighting issues, less severe than Watson's) hold up. Wouldn't be surprised if Lance is New Orleans' Week 1 third receiver or a frequent 3-WR-set piece, developing into a starter by year three, with downfield ability separating him from more blocking-oriented [[Devaughn Vele]].
- 2026-08-03 — According to Ian Hartitz (untracked co-host) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-08-03 (Feel It or Fk It with Ian Hartitz)]]): he's 'fuck it' on Lance becoming a fantasy starter by end of 2027, seeing a best case as a boom/bust field-stretcher in the Alec Pierce 2024 mold, given New Orleans' offense projects as bottom-10 (Vegas implied win totals had them 23rd) and Lance sits behind Chris Olave and [[Jordan Tyson]] on the depth chart. [[Matt Waldman]] put roughly a 30% chance he 'feels it,' contingent on Jordan Tyson staying healthy; if Tyson's issues open a bigger role, Waldman sees Lance as more of a Marquise Valdes-Scantling-type field-stretcher than a true starter, while flagging the Saints' offensive line and a possible running back committee as further headwinds for the whole passing game.
