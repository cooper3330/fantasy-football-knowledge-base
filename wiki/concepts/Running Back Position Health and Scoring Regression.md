## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-05-20 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: guest JJ Zachariason (Late Round Draft Guide, not a tracked expert) argued the top-24 running backs in 2024 were the healthiest in roughly 15 years, and that 90% or more of the position's scoring bump came from that health rather than talent. The gains were concentrated in low-end RB1 and RB2 range, not the top five or six. His roll call: [[J.K. Dobbins]] was the least healthy at RB24 with 13 games; [[Rico Dowdle]] 16, [[Tony Pollard]] 16, [[Najee Harris]] 17, [[Rachaad White]] 16, [[D'Andre Swift]] 17, [[David Montgomery]] and [[Joe Mixon]] 14, [[Breece Hall]] 16, [[Chuba Hubbard]] 15, [[Bucky Irving]] 17, [[Jonathan Taylor]] 14, with [[Alvin Kamara]] the only top-12 back under 16 games. Because backups did not have to step in, the usual Zero RB payoff never materialized and ADP closely matched finish. He expects a market overcorrection into inflated 2025 running back costs, and will again run a hero/anchor RB build — lock in an [[Bijan Robinson]]-type RB1, play volatility at RB2.
