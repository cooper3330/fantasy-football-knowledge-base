## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-10-02 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-02]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: guest Jeff Bell of Football Guys (not a tracked expert) coined 'hot dog quarterback' on this episode for a passer whose points you do not want to watch being made — 'I don't want to see a documentary on how hot dogs are made, I just want to enjoy one at a ball game.' You log in at the end of the week and the 30 points are there. Bell's archetype is Blake Bortles; his current example is [[Justin Fields]], whom he ranked QB2 on matchup while feeling 'pretty uncomfortable' about it. Harris, a vegetarian, joked that knowing how hot dogs are made is exactly the problem, but ranked Fields QB8 on the same logic — ahead of quarterbacks who are better than him. The concept's built-in risk is the running-quarterback dud game: five carries, 17 yards, no touchdown, and you benched [[Patrick Mahomes]] for it.
