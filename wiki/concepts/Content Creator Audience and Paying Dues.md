## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2024-12-23 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-23]]) with untracked co-host Bob Harris: know your audience, know that they think they know as much as you do and are probably right, be honest, and talk to people the way you like to be talked to — you are not fooling a knowledgeable audience, and it is harder to unring a bell than to ring one. On niche vs broad, Waldman frames the trade-off as Matthew Berry's life (TV, money, a company employing people like Dwain McFarland) versus dropping content into a well and never hearing it hit bottom — but the broader you go, the more people call you an idiot, while a granular niche means fewer readers who are far more devoted, and 'the better you better be at it.' On dues: Bob Harris says it cost him a lot of money and he might break even in another ten years; Waldman reframes paying dues as ongoing rather than a phase, with equity that must be re-earned every year. Both note AI is a useful tool — Waldman finds AI scouting reports helpful now — but that AI rewriting his prose changes it without improving it.
