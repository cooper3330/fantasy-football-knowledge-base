## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-10-07 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-10-07]]): Koh argues the dig route and the deep over are the two routes he hyper-focuses on because beating tight cover two requires attacking the seams — if a receiver cannot run a dig, the remaining ways to win get very small. Harmon's charting backs the point with who is producing: the league's most productive receivers right now are [[Puka Nacua]], [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], [[Emeka Egbuka]] and [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], all middle-of-field dominators, and all paired with a confident veteran quarterback willing to throw into zone. Egbuka's charted 89.5% success rate on digs (20.7% usage) and 83.3% on posts is offered as the current example. Harmon frames it as an emerging archetype: a receiver who can play all three alignments and win over the middle is how you 'unlock something special' in the modern NFL.
