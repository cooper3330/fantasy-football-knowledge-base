# Players Not Positions

## Definition


## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-08-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-14]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: prompted by an overwhelming volume of panicky Reddit AMA questions about draft-slot permutations — 'I pick sixth, if I take a running back am I ruined at receiver in rounds two and four, should I wait on tight end until the fifth' — Harris restates his abiding principle: players, not positions. Do not draft positions in the first few rounds; build one blended list of the players you want in the order you want them and simply take them off the list. Break your hump making the list, not war-gaming permutations beforehand. His argument is that you cannot mess a team up badly by picking good players — no more than anyone else, since drafters trying to game the system positionally end up with injured players just as often, and there is always in-season time to fix a roster.
- 2025-08-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-28]]): heading into Labor Day draft weekend he repeats his standing instruction to draft players, not positions, and pairs it with a plea not to treat drafting as an equation that can be solved. His reasoning is that we are horrendous at predicting which situations will be great and which will be horrible — nobody had the Commanders' offense on the good list or the Dolphins' on the bad list a year in advance — so his own tendency is simply to ask whether the players are good. He also proposes a rules change that follows from the same instinct: abolish the separate tight end slot in favour of a combined wide receiver/tight end position, a view guest DENNY CARTER of Rotoworld (NOT a tracked expert) shares, adding that he would also throw out both defense and kicker since defense scoring hinges on largely unpredictable on-field events.
- 2025-08-29 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-29]]): closing the 2025 draft season, Harris makes 'players not positions' his final pep talk. He warns that by late August a feeling settles over the market — the experts have decided the right order, so any deviation feels like a huge mistake, and therefore 'there's a dead zone in the fifth round' or 'a huge surplus in the third'. Harris promises that is all going to be wrong, because this is predicting sports and one week into the season everyone will already have been caught out on workloads, offense quality and the old chestnut that a bad defense guarantees a pass-heavy offense. His prescription is to build your own list, allow a small bend for roster construction — reaching a little for a running back after going receiver-receiver is fine — but otherwise take as many of the guys you actually like as you can.
