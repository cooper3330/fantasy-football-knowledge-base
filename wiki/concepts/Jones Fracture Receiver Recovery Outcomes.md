## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-05]]): a Jones fracture — a break in the fifth metatarsal on the outside of the foot — should not be read like the foot injuries receivers routinely play through. When a player describes it as a 'pain issue', as [[Jayden Reed]] did, Harmon said to take that with a pound of salt, because a plantar fascia problem can be managed with a plate in the shoe and a broken bone cannot. Koh's precedent list: [[Deebo Samuel]] 2020 (seven games, later strong seasons), Sammy Watkins 2016 (missed eight, then one 600-yard season in eight remaining years after 990 and 1,050-yard starts), [[Julio Jones]] 2013 (missed 11, recovered), Dez Bryant 2015 (missed seven, never a 900-yard season again after three straight 1,200-plus), and Julian Edelman 2015 as the clean slot-receiver bounce-back (98 catches, 1,100 yards, three touchdowns the next year). The framework both hosts landed on: the downside of playing through it — bad film, re-injury, a worse break — usually outweighs the upside, and getting fully healthy for the following contract year is the better path.
