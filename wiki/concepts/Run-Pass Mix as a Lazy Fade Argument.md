# Run-Pass Mix as a Lazy Fade Argument

## Definition


## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-09-08 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-09-08]]): when the argument against a player is run-pass mix, depth chart or coaching, it can be real, but Harris says it is usually just a lazy argument made by people who do not want to like the player. His Week 1 evidence is the Chargers: the constant offseason chatter about Greg Roman and Jim Harbaugh being run-focused felt real silly once [[Justin Herbert]] threw, and Harris generalizes it as there being no such thing in the current NFL as a run-pass mix that cannot sustain a fantasy passing game, unless the components of that passing game stink. He is careful to caveat that it is one week against a Chiefs front that is stout against the run, and that the real test comes in Week 2.
