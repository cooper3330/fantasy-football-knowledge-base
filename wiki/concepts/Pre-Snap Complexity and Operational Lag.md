## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-09-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-16]]): the fashionable pre-snap-motion play callers — he names Liam Coen and Ben Johnson — buy their scheme advantage with operational complexity, and a young or unfamiliar roster needs time to execute it cleanly. Evidence from Week 2: Chicago leads the league with six false starts, with Darnell Wright alone at four; Jacksonville was flagged for five illegal shifts in one game, all declined, including on the play [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] alligator-armed an in-breaker for an interception. Harmon says his mistake was expecting instant Jacksonville results because Coen inherited a functioning Tampa Bay offense in 2024 stocked with by-the-book veterans — [[Mike Evans]], [[Chris Godwin]], [[Baker Mayfield]] — who had played with Tom Brady, versus a Jacksonville group with far less experience and [[Travis Hunter]] playing two ways. Detroit's offense also took time to coalesce under Johnson. Implication for panic meters: give the operation weeks, not games.
