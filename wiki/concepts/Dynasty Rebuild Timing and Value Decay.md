# Dynasty Rebuild Timing and Value Decay

## Definition


## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-10-09 — According to Adam Harstad (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert) with [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-09]]) [Dynasty]: in ~20 years of dynasty (since 2007) he has done exactly one true strip-to-studs rebuild — turning over 19-20 of a 24-man roster — and won the title the very next year and set league scoring records. He rebuilds rarely because he respects randomness: the best team in a league only wins 25-33% of the time and a No. 6 seed still has 5-10%, so cashing in a middling season is expensive. His trigger is not 'worst team' but 'not good enough to win, old enough to be hemorrhaging value.' Two errors he sees: people rebuild too early, then rebuild too slowly, valuing youth for its own sake with no exit strategy — itself a metric-becoming-a-target failure. A rebuild should last one to two years, three max. The underlying math: roster value decays roughly 20% per year because each incoming rookie class claims ~20% of the pool, so a roster that merely holds value is gaining ground. Mantra: 'don't lose value.' Waldman described blowing up his own team in 2024 after his core ([[Nick Chubb]], [[Rhamondre Stevenson]], [[Bryce Young]], [[Russell Wilson]]) aged out, banking a pile of picks, and being about another year from contending.
