## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-08-20 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-20]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: the value of a 'safe' pick is as a counterpoint to what you have already done in a draft — if you took a shot on [[Christian McCaffrey]] early and do not feel great at running back, rounds six through eight become the place to settle things down with a high-floor player. Harris considers six-seven-eight a crucial stretch precisely because it forces the question of whether to press for upside or stabilize your starters. Guest Scott Pianowski (Yahoo Fantasy, not a tracked expert) frames it in golf terms — he does not hit driver off the tee, he hits a three wood or five wood — and adds the honest caveat that safety can be an illusion: [[Isiah Pacheco]] was a safe pick on his sheets last year. Both lean on betting on talent in trusted offenses, and Harris notes he tries to let offensive situation matter as little as possible, granting benefit of the doubt to only a handful of offenses per year.
