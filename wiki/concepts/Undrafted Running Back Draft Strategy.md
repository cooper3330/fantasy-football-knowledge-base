## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2026-04-27 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-04-27]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman says he's increasingly leaning toward prioritizing undrafted or late-round running backs over mid-tier wide receivers, quarterbacks and tight ends in rookie drafts, and that this shows up as his default approach in the preliminary Top 52 cheat sheet. His reasoning: many mid-tier WR prospects grade well but have a realistic ceiling of only ever becoming a 'Chris Conley or Demarcus Robinson' type -- decent but replaceable, clogging a bench spot behind 3-4 players they never fully overtake -- while a UDFA or late-round RB can post immediate rookie-year value that's tradeable at a profit even if he ultimately proves too small or slow to stick long-term. He cites players like [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] as the kind of back he'd rather roster for a short-term rental-and-flip than a fourth or fifth receiver on a depth chart.
- 2026-05-07 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-05-07 (Going Deep with Brandon Angelo)]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman argues 2026 running back is actually underrated relative to its class reputation, precisely because rookie-draft managers can't easily trade a second-round pick for future capital this year, making it a good range to instead trade down (e.g. a third-round pick for extra fourth/fifth/sixth-round capital) and take multiple dart throws rather than one 'safe' committee back. His targets in that range: Kalen Black as insurance if [[Christian McCaffrey]] gets hurt, Robert Henry as a training-camp threat to Kaytron Allen if Jacory Croskey-Merritt doesn't ascend, and a wave of undrafted names -- Jamari Taylor, Le'Veon Moss, LaShawn Williams, Roman Hemby -- where you'll know by the end of camp whether to keep or cut, letting managers stay flexible and even flip a hot camp performer to a win-now team for more draft capital later. He called this preferable to spending a mid-round pick on a receiver he thinks is roster-clogging, pointing to [[Chris Bell]] as an example of that risk.
