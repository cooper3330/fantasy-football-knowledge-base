## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-05-15 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-05-15]]) [Dynasty]: the organizing principle of their rookie-draft value list is to buy rookies whose veteran competition is likely gone or diminished the following season, because the market prices year one and you collect year two at a discount. Angelo: 'if you're not contending, you want to take the guys that are going to be contributing next year, because you're going to get a massive discount on those guys'. Worked examples from the episode — [[Kyle Williams]] with [[Stefon Diggs]] aging post-ACL in New England; [[Pat Bryant]] with [[Courtland Sutton]] 30 and in a contract year; [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] if [[David Njoku]] prices himself out of Cleveland; [[Jack Bech]] once [[Jakobi Meyers]] leaves Las Vegas; [[Jaylin Noel]] against an aging [[Christian Kirk]]; [[Savion Williams]] with [[Christian Watson]] and [[Romeo Doubs]] departing; [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] with Washington's backs leaving. The test they apply is whether the rookie can plausibly reach a 125-plus target or lead-back role in year two.
