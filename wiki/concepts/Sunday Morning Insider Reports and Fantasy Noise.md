## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-10-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-10-13]]): a case study in letting a Sunday-morning insider 'splash report' override your own evaluation. Waldman's instinct was to rank [[Kimani Vidal]] ahead of [[Hassan Haskins]] because Vidal is the better receiving asset and that is his tiebreaker under uncertainty, but a Mike Garafolo report that Haskins would take the lead role and bulk of the workload moved him off it — and Vidal went for 18-124 with a receiving touchdown. His conclusion, in his own words: fuck the news, feel your hunches when you have them. He allowed that people are genuinely being told things, but insisted those reports are not the be-all end-all, citing an Adam Schefter report that Bam Knight would start when the actual usage was a fairly even split. Bob Harris (not tracked) added that on Sunday mornings people are often looking for attention more than to give you good information. Waldman paired this with a separate warning that camp reports on Vidal contradicted each other outright.
- 2025-10-14 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-10-14 (Week 7 Waiver Wire & More W6 Film)]]): Harris says mysterious Sunday-morning backfield reports are 'starting to feel less and less reliable,' citing a bad pre-game report that pegged Hassan Haskins as the lead Cardinals/Chargers-adjacent back before both [[Bam Knight]]'s and [[Kimani Vidal]]'s backfields played out differently on film. Harris admits he took both reports at face value and rendered both backfields unusable pre-game, which cost him the chance to roster Vidal early -- a cautionary example for weighting beat-reporter chatter over actual film.
- 2025-11-04 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-04]]): the recurring problem is knowing when to believe a leak and make a big ranking change. He lists the Week 9 crop — reports on [[Tyjae Spears]], [[Bhayshul Tuten]], [[Brashard Smith]] and [[Michael Carter]] — and notes Carter was said to have taken reps with the ones in practice and then did not play a single offensive snap. Against that, the [[Rico Dowdle]] takeover report was 100% true and Harris was wrong not to act on it. His defense of the process is that it is usage and it is really hard to know in advance, but he is explicit that being right about the reasoning does not excuse the ranking miss. Jeff Erickson of RotoWire (not a tracked expert) adds that a team will never state outright that a back is the workhorse, so the absence of confirmation is not evidence either way.
- 2025-11-21 — According to Chris Harris on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-21]] [Redraft (Standard)]: Harris flagged Tampa broadcaster Ronde Barber's on-air report that [[Bucky Irving]] will not play against the Rams as unconfirmed but credible enough to act on, noting it would shift work to [[Rashad White]] and [[Sean Tucker]].
