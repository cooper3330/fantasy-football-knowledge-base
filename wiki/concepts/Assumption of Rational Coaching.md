## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-11-06 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-11-06]]): borrowing a phrase from Sigmund Bloom, Waldman warns against assuming a staff will keep doing the obviously good thing it just did. The case in point is Atlanta pairing [[Michael Penix Jr.]]'s best trait — fades and jump balls at every depth — with [[Drake London]]'s ability to go up and take the ball, producing three touchdowns against New England, while never having used [[Kyle Pitts]] the same way despite his Florida rebounding profile. Waldman's test for whether a discovery is real rather than a happy accident is repetition and extension: if it shows up over the next two games and spreads to Pitts, it is genuine; if it appears only occasionally with London, the staff is not taking it seriously. Angelo's supporting evidence for the pessimistic read is that Zach Robinson still runs 'the whole heck out of pistol', and Waldman applies the same skepticism to Chip Kelly's use of [[Ashton Jeanty]] and John Morton's of [[Jahmyr Gibbs]].
