## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-07-16 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-07-16]]): essentially the entire 2025 second round held out of camp seeking fully guaranteed deals after Houston gave 34th pick [[Jayden Higgins]] one, forcing Cleveland to match at pick 33; Seattle had not done so for Nick Emmanwori at 35, and [[Quinshon Judkins]] at 36 was complicated by a domestic violence arrest. Both hosts put the impetus on teams: the cap impact is trivial next to the on-field cost of missed reps, and the rookie wage scale already hands clubs a bargain the veterans negotiated for them. Harmon's fantasy-relevant point is that the damage falls on rookie receivers who need camp to learn an offense and build quarterback chemistry — [[Tre Harris]] and [[Luther Burden III]] most directly.
- 2025-08-25 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-08-25]]): the [[Quinshon Judkins]] standoff shows the mechanism working in both directions. Waldman reads Cleveland calling other teams about running backs as deliberate pressure on the agent, with the club's leverage supplied by an ongoing NFL investigation that gives it a stated reason to seek a discount after Judkins declined to move off the offer. Bob Harris (Footballguys, NOT tracked) framed the other side: slotted money for the round was reset when a player picked ahead of Judkins secured a better guarantee, so everyone behind him is now negotiating guarantees, and this may simply be Cleveland refusing to pay that much fully guaranteed. Fantasy implication both agreed on — the discount is real and temporary, making the holdout player a late-round value if you can wait out the resolution.
