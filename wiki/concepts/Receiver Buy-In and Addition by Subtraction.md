## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-09-18 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-18]]): a talented receiver who stops believing in his quarterback can be a net negative, and removing him can improve the offense. The template is [[Diontae Johnson]] in Carolina — Koh argues [[Bryce Young]] would not be a functional quarterback if Johnson were still in that room, and that trading him was addition by subtraction. They apply the same read to [[D.J. Moore]] with [[Caleb Williams]] ('like 0%' bought in, from body language and public comments, which Koh calls fair given Moore's success with [[Justin Fields]]) and to [[Tyreek Hill]] in Miami, recalling Hill's early praise of Tua Tagovailoa as the most accurate quarterback in the league. Harmon's practical conclusion: move the player before the cheese curdles, because that is when you get maximum value, and the acquiring team can still get real production out of him in the right role.
