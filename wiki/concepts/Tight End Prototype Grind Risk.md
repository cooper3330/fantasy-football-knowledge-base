## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-03-18 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-03-18]]) [Dynasty]: Waldman argues the tight end 'prototype' barely exists — he says [[Rob Gronkowski]] and George Kittle are close to figments of our imagination, because the role stretches the bounds of reasonable athletic ability and human beings are not normally built to handle defensive ends one-on-one as blockers and also beat corners in press on intermediate routes, and those who try do not stay healthy long (he cites Kellen Winslow II as the closest lost example). Referencing a past conversation with [[Adam Harstad]] (co-host, not a tracked expert) on Film and Theory, he holds that receiver-first tight ends have the most sustainable NFL careers. The 2025 risk: seven tight ends are worth serious fantasy consideration, but five of them offer enough promise as future 'prototypes' that their teams may demand prototype usage and grind them to dust. Waldman's hope is that teams adopt a division of labor and multiple-tight-end alignments instead — if they do, the class has a much better chance of hitting its fantasy potential.
