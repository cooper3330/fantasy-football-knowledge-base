## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2024-12-09 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-12-09]]): a 'fantasy black hole' quarterback is the football analogue of James Harden in the NBA — the ball goes in and never comes back out. Waldman notes the tiering exercise was credited on air to Alex Hanowitz. Elite: [[Josh Allen]] — scores enormously but still supports teammates. Good version: [[Jalen Hurts]] — QB3 production, but he vultures goal-line touchdowns and leaves an open [[A.J. Brown]] unfed. Bad version: [[Kyler Murray]] — YOLO balls, poor pre-snap reads, tucks and runs, and in Waldman's view caps [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] outright. Low grade: [[Marcus Mariota]] — puts up starter fantasy points while none of his real weapons produce. Waldman's caveat is that the NBA analogy is not one-to-one: the bad-version quarterbacks accumulate points via rushing and garbage-time throwing to players who will not matter the following week.
