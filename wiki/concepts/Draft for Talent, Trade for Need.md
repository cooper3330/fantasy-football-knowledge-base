## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2024-09-05 — According to Adam Harstad (Footballguys co-host, not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-05 (Film and Theory)]] [Dynasty]: his two oldest dynasty rules are 'draft for talent, trade for need' and its corollary, 'nobody has needs in June' — if you are not submitting a lineup, you do not have a need, and your only need is maximizing talent. The best way to get a good running back is to draft a stack of good players and trade one for him: he has never spent a top-30 rookie pick on a running back in a league running since 2013, yet acquired [[Austin Ekeler]] and [[Jonathan Taylor]] by trade, and turned [[Michael Thomas]] into [[Alvin Kamara]]. He also warns that a June trade for need is usually undone by camp — the acquired back loses his job — and that if you think you are set at a position you are probably wrong (his own Ekeler season). [[Matt Waldman]] agreed and extended it: stockpile a strength when you lack the capital to fix a weakness, then convert the surplus. He cited hoarding four of the top six quarterbacks in a one-QB league until leaguemates had to come to him, and trading for [[Nick Chubb]] and Taylor rather than drafting them. Waldman prefers not to trade before the season at all unless the deal is absurd, arguing midseason is the truest reveal of value because managers stop fooling themselves; Harstad is comfortable with high trade volume year-round.
