## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-11-06 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-11-06]]): a receiver traded at the deadline rarely hits the ground running — Harris struggles to name one who did, landing only on [[Amari Cooper]] — because a player traded Tuesday reaches the new team Wednesday and gets a couple of practice days before playing. Two things reduce the risk. First, scheme continuity: [[Rashid Shaheed]] moved to Seattle under Klint Kubiak, the same coordinator he played for in New Orleans, so terminology and play calls carry over. Second, scoring profile: guest Ben Gretch of Stealing Signals (not a tracked expert) notes a chunk-play receiver like Shaheed can post a usable score on two targets, whereas a volume receiver like [[Jakobi Meyers]] needs the offense to trust him with a full route tree first. Harris's counterweight is that some skills are universal — a curl, a post, a shallow corner — so a technician like Meyers should be as plug-and-play as receivers get, and the real question is whether a banged-up team can even afford to bring him along slowly.
