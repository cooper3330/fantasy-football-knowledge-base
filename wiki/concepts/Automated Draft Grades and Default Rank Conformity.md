## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-08-28 — According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-28]]): the grade a fantasy platform assigns your draft deserves not a scrap of attention, because all it gauges is how close you stuck to the platform's default ranks — draft in accordance with those ranks and you get an A-plus, and Harris's view is that those default ranks are crap and will not wind up being right. DENNY CARTER of Rotoworld (guest, NOT a tracked expert) added that the machines have hated him his entire fantasy playing life, and that anyone who even dabbles with anything approaching [[Running Back Dead Zone]]-adjacent Zero RB construction will be destroyed by them: the grader is really just projections, so early-round running backs project well while a bench full of receivers does not. Both treat automated grades as evidence that the tooling rewards conformity rather than edge. Harris's broader framing is that a draft is a set of educated guesses whose quality is unknowable until October, so managers should have fun rather than treat it as a final exam with an answer key.
