## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-04-28 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-04-28 (Feel It or F-It Post-Draft)]]): a rarely-stated but real component of NFL draft boards is the player's family. Teams fear a parent who plays little-league dad at the pro level — parents who turn up at the facility bugging operations staff who only handle travel, ticket sales or marketing, who create office drama, or whose crises force a player to take time off. None of it reaches the media. Waldman's escalated version is the celebrity parent: Deion Sanders can call a press conference at any moment, command every major outlet and lean on ownership relationships, which he ties to the Tim Tebow media circus — if you invite the clown car in, the driver has to be really good. His rule: teams tolerate the circus only when the player clears a high enough talent bar (Antonio Brown, temporarily), and he applies it directly to [[Shedeur Sanders]] falling past round three.
