## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2026-10-22 — According to [[Brandon Angelo]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2026-10-22 (Going Deep Eagles & 2027 QB Class)]]): Angelo frames an 'all third wheel team' as a recurring evaluation exercise -- naming backup-or-complementary skill players who offer a real, specific, projectable NFL skill (burst, run blocking, contested catching, decision-making as a runner) that distinguishes them from the rest of the depth chart, even while buried behind an established starter or a bigger-name rookie. He and Waldman applied the framework this episode to [[Jaylin Noel]], [[Kayshon Boutte]], [[Michael Mayer]], [[Josh Cameron]], [[Savion Williams]], [[Emmitt Johnson]], [[Isaiah Davis]] and [[Charlie Jones]], while explicitly excluding players like Corey Kiner and Bam Knight, whom they view as reliable, replaceable career depth rather than true sleepers with a buildable, featurable skill.
