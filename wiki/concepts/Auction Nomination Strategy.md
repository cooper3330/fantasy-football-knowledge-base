## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-08-28 — Asked for their sneakiest auction nomination tactics, DENNY CARTER of Rotoworld (guest, NOT a tracked expert) said on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-28]] that tricks do not work in a room full of sharps, but in a homer league they work beautifully: in an old friends-and-family league full of Washington fans he nominated every Washington player first and watched their WR3 go for more than a top-15 receiver overall, draining opponents' budgets before he started spending. According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-28]]): he plays in two auctions containing both industry sharps and civilians and has no trick play, but his rule is that he almost never nominates a player he actually wants — he will happily buy an early-nominated player he likes, just not one he put up himself — and he waits a very long time to nominate his own targets. The result is that he is nearly always the manager holding the most money at the point in the middle-to-late auction where the room tips and running backs start going for ten dollars, which is when he can take anybody.
