## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-08-28 — Asked whether to take two elite players from the same team early — the listener took [[Saquon Barkley]] first and passed on [[A.J. Brown]] at the 2-3 turn for [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] and [[Kyren Williams]] — DENNY CARTER of Rotoworld (guest, NOT a tracked expert) said on [[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-28]] that the objection holds mainly in a bad offense; in a good one like Philadelphia you just do it, because on a single 80-yard touchdown drive Brown and Barkley can both get theirs. He called the swap a huge downgrade. According to [[Chris Harris]] ([[Harris Fantasy Football Podcast - 2025-08-28]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: he agrees it is a downgrade but not a huge one, defends the question as a good one because a fantasy roster is a portfolio that wants more than one team's worth of pie, and says you cannot be completely situation-agnostic when an offense looks as safe as the Eagles — while conceding the Dolphins looked that safe last year. He would take Barkley plus Brown, but respects taking JSN, whom he calls a flag player. He offers a further case: whether you can start both [[Brock Bowers]] and [[Jakobi Meyers]] in a deep 12- or 14-teamer, where one can eat but maybe not both.
