## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-01-13 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2025-01-13]]): the episode is structured around a rookie receiver model built by Adam Harstad of Football Guys (NOT a tracked expert), which scores rookie yards per route run with bonuses for touchdowns and sorts results into tiers — superstars at 118 points or more, strong starters 108-117, then good bets, average rookies (roughly 97-103), bad bets (93-97) and terrible bets below 93. Waldman notes Ryan Heath at Fantasy Points runs a comparable YPRR model. Harstad was reportedly shocked how high the 2024 class scored: [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] 125.5, [[Ladd McConkey]] 124.1, [[Malik Nabers]] 120.9 (Waldman calls that Odell-like), [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] 110.4, [[Keon Coleman]] 105.2, [[Jalen Coker]] 102.5, with [[Ricky Pearsall]], [[Xavier Worthy]] and [[Devaughn Vele]] in the average band and [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] recording the worst rookie season in the model's history. [[Matt Harmon]] repeatedly uses the model as a sanity check against his own Reception Perception film work, saying it sorts Harrison and Odunze 'perfectly,' while cautioning that context — offense quality, alignment, target competition — is what the raw score cannot see.
