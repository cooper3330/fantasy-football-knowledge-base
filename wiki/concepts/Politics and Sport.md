- 2024-11-07 — According to co-host Adam Harstad (not a tracked expert) ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-11-07]]): responding to the 'stick to sports' demand, Harstad declined to make a normative claim about whether politics belongs in sport, but as an amateur football historian said there has never been a football free of politics, so nobody can return to an era that did not exist. His examples: the forward pass was legalized only because Teddy Roosevelt summoned football's leaders to the White House and threatened to outlaw the game after players died in scrums and drowned in mud; amateurism rules were invented by British elite colleges so aristocrats would not have to compete against day laborers, and cost Jim Thorpe his Olympic records for a $100 baseball game, records not restored for a century; NIL exists because courts imposed it after the NCAA refused to recognize its workers; the Rams integrated because Los Angeles made it a condition of using the Coliseum (Kenny Washington and Woody Strode) and Washington integrated last, under federal pressure over its taxpayer-funded stadium, trading for Bobby Mitchell; Vince Lombardi, shaped by anti-Italian prejudice and a gay brother, refused hotels that excluded his black players; the on-field national anthem displays predate Kaepernick by only about four years and were paid for by the government as military recruiting. Harstad's summary: sport is not an escape from society but a microcosm of it, and the battles are usually fought in miniature there first. He noted 'stick to sports' in practice usually means politics one already agrees with, since nobody objected to the paid patriotic displays.
