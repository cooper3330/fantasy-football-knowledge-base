## Expert Takes
<!-- Attributed, dated bullets on how specific experts apply/discuss this concept -->
- 2025-09-05 — According to [[Matt Harmon]] ([[Reception Perception The Show - 2025-09-05]]): Harmon repeatedly pushed back on what he called the 'draft capital zombies and zealots' — analysts who treat draft position as a linear ordering from good to bad. His position is that capital is a genuinely good indicator but not a ranking, so exceptions are live: he argued third-rounder [[Jaylin Noel]] may simply be better than second-rounder [[Jayden Higgins]] on the same roster, citing Noel's 84th percentile success rate versus man coverage and a profile reminiscent of [[Ladd McConkey]]. The other half of the idea is what capital does tell you: with [[Xavier Legette]], Harmon said first-round capital does not guarantee the player will be good, but it does mean the team badly wants him to be — Legette was Dan Morgan and Dave Canales's first draft pick together and a consensus choice, which shapes how a staff builds his role. Koh (not tracked) framed the same point from the opposite side, saying he is not a draft-capital guy but that Legette's first-round status is why the silence around him is strange.
