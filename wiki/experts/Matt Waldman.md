# Matt Waldman

Independent analyst, best known for the annual Rookie Scouting Portfolio
(RSP) — deep film-based scouting of rookie skill-position prospects. Also
writes the Gut Check column at Footballguys. Particularly relevant to
[[Dynasty]] given the prospect-evaluation focus.

## Background

Publishes the RSP pre-draft and post-draft guides (available April 1 and one
week post-draft) plus a projections/dynasty-rankings service. Co-hosts several
recurring formats: *Feel It or F**k It* with Bob Harris (rapid-fire hot takes),
*RSP Film and Theory* with Adam Harstad (conceptual/analytical), and *Going
Deep* with Brandon Angelo.

## Philosophy / Known Biases

- **Film first, reactionary news second.** Explicitly prefers his post-draft
  rankings because they're "rooted in film study and long-term thought and not
  reactionary thinking."
- **Values processing and technique over testable athleticism.** Repeatedly
  frames his interest as "smarts, footwork, and contact balance" rather than
  highlight-reel athletic traits — self-describes as uninterested in
  "fetishizing the athleticism."
- **Skeptical of archetype labels**, including how they track player appearance
  rather than measured traits — see [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]].
- **Matchup tinkerer** in lineup decisions, in contrast to strict
  best-players-play discipline ([[Start Your Best Players]]).
- **Evaluates by route running, not speed.** Sorts receivers by whether they
  beat man, zone, or both, and treats inside/outside versatility as the premium
  trait — see [[Zone vs Man Route Running]]. Explicitly: "you don't always have
  to have blazing speed to be a top wide receiver."
- **Separates the player from the price.** Repeatedly makes valuation calls that
  run opposite to his own talent calls — he pushed [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] over
  [[Bijan Robinson]] in 2023 and then, on being proven right, immediately flipped
  to Robinson for 2024 because the market premium had moved. Reading his takes
  as pure player rankings will misread him.
- **Separates short-term start/sit from long-term asset value**, often in the
  same sentence ("feel it this week, f\*\*k it long-term").
- **Comps to specific NFL players** as his shorthand for a prospect's ceiling
  and skill set (Sermon as Jordan Howard / Jamaal Williams; Goodson as an
  "aspiring Austin Ekler"; Tre Tucker as an "aspiring Jaylen Waddle").

## Track Record Notes

- **[[Jahmyr Gibbs]] over [[Bijan Robinson]], 2023 — correct, and he says so.**
  On 2023-12-18 he took an explicit victory lap: "everything I said about Jahmyr
  Gibbs this summer is 100% correct... I took a lot of abuse for this." Gibbs
  finished the segment ahead of Robinson (RB9 vs RB11). The underlying argument
  was about consistent pricing of draft capital, not about talent.
