# Matt Harmon

Fantasy football analyst at Yahoo Sports, known for the "Reception Perception"
receiver-charting project. Co-hosts *Reception Perception: The Show* with
**James Koh** (not a tracked expert). Previously appeared in this wiki only
as a name Matt Waldman cited approvingly on his own show — this is Harmon's
first source ingested in his own right.

## Background

Charts every route of every receiver he covers from all-22 tape and grades
them on success rate versus coverage type (press/man/zone), route type, and
alignment usage — the [[Reception Perception Methodology]], published at
ReceptionPerception.com. Runs a cross-class "stacked board" of graded
prospects; a **tier-one** grade means a player is worth a top-10 pick as a
"ready-made star" — as of January 2024 only five prospects across the
2021-2023 classes had earned one (Ja'Marr Chase, Chris Olave, DeVonta Smith,
Drake London, Garrett Wilson — Chase #1 overall on the board). Normally
starts charting rookie prospects in spring; made an exception in January 2024
to get early (explicitly caveated, 3-game-sample) reads on the 2024 receiver
class given how hyped it already was. *Reception Perception: The Show* itself
is a general NFL analysis show, not fantasy-first — Harmon has said outright
"we don't talk about fantasy a lot on this show" — so fantasy-relevant
material has to be extracted from broader player/game evaluation rather than
pulled from dedicated rankings segments.

## Philosophy / Known Biases

- **Data-first, but not data-only.** Leans on his own charting numbers as
  evidence for a claim (e.g. Puka Nacua's man-coverage separation now
  exceeding Cooper Kupp's) rather than as a replacement for a qualitative
  read — he pairs stats with film language ("uncoverable," "crispiest routes
  of all time") in the same breath.
- **Explicitly caveats small samples.** Repeatedly warns not to be "held to
  the fire" on 3-game prospect reads before a full ~8-game sample is charted,
  even while being willing to give strong, specific opinions on that partial
  data.
- **Comfortable with strong, immediate declarations** on veteran players too
  — "straight up bad" on a Tua Tagovailoa game, "the baton has been passed"
  on Cooper Kupp to Puka Nacua — rather than hedging toward consensus.
- **Willing to publicly self-correct or heavily caveat takes in real time**
  with co-host James Koh, e.g. immediately following an "I don't need a new
  generational prospect at quarterback every year" line with an explicit
  carve-out for players he considers genuinely generational (Ja'Marr Chase).
