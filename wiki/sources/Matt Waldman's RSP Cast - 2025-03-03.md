## Summary
A post-Combine 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-fire episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Bob Harris is NOT a tracked expert, and is a different person from tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman is 29 days from the release of the Rookie Scouting Portfolio and finalizing his tight end rankings.

Topics: the Combine's real value (interviews, medicals, position-specific drills) versus its role as an NFL marketing event; [[Cam Ward]] vs [[Shedeur Sanders]] as immediate starters and their best landing spots among the Giants, Raiders and Saints; the 2025 running back class as deep-but-not-elite; sleepers [[Tahj Brooks]] and [[Andrew Armstrong]] as the two players Waldman predicts will most outplay draft position; a bearish read on consensus favorites [[Tetairoa McMillan]] and [[Tyler Warren]]; [[Mason Taylor]] atop Waldman's tight end board over [[Colston Loveland]] and [[Elijah Arroyo]]; [[Matthew Stafford]] staying with the Rams; [[Aaron Rodgers]] to the Giants; [[Deebo Samuel]] traded to Washington; skepticism on [[Jayden Daniels]] as QB1 with [[Josh Allen]] preferred; and [[Travis Kelce]]'s return as a price question.
