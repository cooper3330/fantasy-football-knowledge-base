## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on NFL cut day and a wave of receiver news. Koh opens with a note that the episode was recorded before final cuts, so Robbie Chosen, Russell Gage and Isaiah Hodgins were released after taping.
