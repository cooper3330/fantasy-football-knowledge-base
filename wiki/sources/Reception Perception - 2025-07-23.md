## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) open their 2025 division-preview series with the NFC West, going room by room through the Rams, Cardinals, 49ers and Seahawks and grading each offense for fragility.

Rams: [[Davante Adams]] replaces [[Cooper Kupp]] alongside [[Puka Nacua]]. Harmon argues Adams is genuinely declining — his Reception Perception success rate versus zone fell from 84.4% (2023) to 77% (2024), a 37th-percentile mark and his first sub-81% zone season since his rookie year — but that his press (79.2%) and man (74.6%) numbers held, and that a primary X role in Sean McVay's offense should lean into slants and digs. Harmon invokes the Allen Robinson precedent as the cautionary comp. Koh is more bullish on the Adams-McVay fit and more skeptical Adams plays as much X as Harmon projects. They rate the Rams offense a 7.5/10 for combustibility ([[Matthew Stafford]] back injury, Coleman Shelton at center, [[Kyren Williams]] injury history).

Cardinals: the deployment of [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] is the whole question — 80.4% of rookie snaps on the line of scrimmage, a group that otherwise contains only [[Quentin Johnston]], [[Nico Collins]] and [[George Pickens]]. Harmon likes the player in isolation but flags [[Kyler Murray]]'s aversion to the dig.

49ers: [[Brandon Aiyuk]] has no timetable, [[Jauan Jennings]] wants a new deal, [[Ricky Pearsall]] is the breakout bet if healthy. Koh rates the 49ers a 9/10 for fragility; Harmon says 6/10 on faith in [[Brock Purdy]].

Seahawks: [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] as the straw that stirs the drink, a severely declined [[Cooper Kupp]], [[Marquez Valdes-Scantling]] as sacrificial X, and a long enthusiastic segment on fifth-rounder [[Tory Horton]]. Both hosts reject the idea of [[Elijah Arroyo]] playing X receiver.
