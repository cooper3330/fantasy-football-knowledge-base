## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts a Thursday Week 6 episode with two guests. First, [[Matt Waldman]] (Footballguys, Rookie Scouting Portfolio) brings five players he found genuinely hard to rank for Week 6 -- [[Derrick Henry]], [[Luther Burden III]], [[Mason Taylor]], [[DeAndre Hopkins]] and [[Michael Penix Jr.]] -- with Harris pressure-testing each against alternatives from his own weekly ranks. The two then preview Thursday Night Football, Eagles at Giants, covering the [[A.J. Brown]] conundrum and Kevin Patullo's play calling, plus Waldman's charting-based read on [[Jackson Dart]] and the [[Cam Skattebo]] / [[Tyrone Tracy Jr.]] backfield. Second segment is a DraftKings Week 6 lineup walkthrough with Jake Trowbridge (not a tracked expert), which is DFS pricing and game-script talk rather than player evaluation. Episode opens with a listener snark bag.
