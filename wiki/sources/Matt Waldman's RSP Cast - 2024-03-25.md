# Feel It Or F@#k It: 3.25.24 — An RSP Cast with Bob Harris and Matt Waldman

*Feel It or F**k It* rapid-fire episode, [[Matt Waldman]] with co-host **Bob
Harris** (not a tracked expert). Recorded the week of the NFL owners meetings,
one week before the RSP's April 1 pre-draft release (final tight-end chapter
being finished, 150th and final prospect watched: Austin Stogner).

## Covered

- **Late-round tight end strategy**: [[Ja'Tavion Sanders]] (ASR: "Getavian
  Sanders") as a deep-best-ball 20th-round flyer; Bob Harris separately floats
  Jonnu Smith and Zach Ertz as late-draft safety-valve tight ends (not tracked
  content, no pages created).
- **2024 QB draft class fits**: Washington at No. 2 ([[Michael Penix Jr.]] vs.
  [[Drake Maye]], with Marcus Mariota discussed as a non-viable bridge for
  fans), New England at No. 3 ([[Jacoby Brissett]] as bridge, [[Drake Maye]]
  vs. a [[Jayden Daniels]] trade-back scenario), and a direct
  Penix-over-[[J.J. McCarthy]] preference from Waldman.
- **Veteran QB competitions**: Las Vegas ([[Aidan O'Connell]] vs. [[Gardner
  Minshew]]), Seattle ([[Geno Smith]] vs. [[Sam Howell]]), Denver-to-Pittsburgh
  framing revisited for [[Russell Wilson]] vs. [[Justin Fields]], New York
  Giants ([[Daniel Jones]], with a [[Saquon Barkley]] paid-the-wrong-guy
  argument), Dallas ([[Dak Prescott]]'s contract not being reworked).
- **Los Angeles Chargers backfield**: [[Gus Edwards]]'s Greg Roman history as
  an underrated best-ball value, possible committee with [[Braelon Allen]] or
  [[Jaylen Wright]].
- **Rookie "off the radar" quick-hitters**, one per position: QB [[Jordan
  Travis]], RB [[Kimani Vidal]] (extended [[DeAndre Swift]] comp), WR [[Ainias
  Smith]] (reaffirmed), TE [[Ben Sinnott]] (ASR: "Ben Sanat") with [[Jack
  Westover]] as a runner-up.
- **[[Xavier Worthy]]** post-pro-day Miami fit and [[Tyreek Hill]] mentorship
  angle (plus Hill's own retirement-timeline comments).
- **[[Zach Wilson]]** trade/redemption-path discussion as the Jets shop him.
- Non-fantasy closing segment: the NFL's new hip-drop-tackle ban and Guardian
  Cap expansion (player-safety policy discussion, no player-evaluation content,
  not logged to any page).
