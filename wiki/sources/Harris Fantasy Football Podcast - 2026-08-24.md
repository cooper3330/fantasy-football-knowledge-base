## Summary
Chris Harris, recording from Seattle mid-way through his 'Pound Foolish' book tour, opens with breaking news that Ashton Jeanty went down hard and couldn't put weight on his leg in Sunday (Aug 23) Raiders practice; the team called it an ankle sprain, but Harris is skeptical of the messaging and lays out the Raiders' thin depth behind him, centering on rookie Mike Washington. He also notes Nick Chubb's retirement. The bulk of the episode is a 'Five Busts for 2026' countdown with returning guest Patrick Doherty (Roto-World, not a tracked expert), each ranking five overdrafted players for 2026 with reasoning, and reacting to the other's picks. Doherty's busts (5-to-1): Carnell Tate, Javonte Williams, Omarion Hampton, Bhayshul Tuten, Luther Burden III. Harris's busts (4-to-1, tight end pick folded into #5 discussion): Dalton Kincaid, Josh Downs, Malik Nabers, Jeremiah Love. The episode closes with sponsor reads and tour/book promo.
