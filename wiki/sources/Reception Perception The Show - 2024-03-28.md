# The Worst Wide Receiver Depth Charts Out There

[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) rank the NFL's
worst wide receiver rooms after the bulk of 2024 free agency, then pivot to
top-10 landing-spot scenarios for [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Rome Odunze]] and
[[Malik Nabers]] ahead of the draft.

## Disaster-level rooms
Four teams named outright: Giants, Chargers, Patriots, Cardinals. Debated
order settles on Giants (best of a bad group, because [[Wan'Dale Robinson]],
[[Jalen Hyatt]] and [[Darius Slayton]] at least give a knowable floor) >
Patriots (tenuous #2, hurt by Kendrick Bourne's ACL) > Cardinals (some hope in
[[Michael Wilson]] and [[Greg Dortch]]) > [[Quentin Johnston|Chargers]] (named
the single worst room in the NFL — nothing behind [[Josh Palmer]] and
Johnston).

## Disaster-adjacent (one good starter, nothing behind him)
Steelers ([[George Pickens]]), Broncos ([[Courtland Sutton]], [[Marvin Mims Jr.]]),
Jaguars ([[Christian Kirk]], [[Zay Jones]], [[Gabe Davis]]). Ranked Pickens >
Kirk > Sutton, with extended discussion of why running an offense through a
slot receiver (Kirk) is much harder than through an outside X, using
Jacksonville's 2023 Kirk/[[Calvin Ridley]] split-attention as the example.

## Fragile group (good WR1, shaky depth)
Ravens ([[Zay Flowers]], [[Rashad Bateman]]), Lions (Amon-Ra St. Brown),
Panthers ([[Diontae Johnson]], [[Adam Thielen]], [[Jonathan Mingo]]), Falcons
([[Drake London]], [[Darnell Mooney]], [[Rondale Moore]]), with Saints ([[Chris Olave]],
[[Rashid Shaheed]], A.T. Perry) and Jets ([[Garrett Wilson]], [[Mike Williams]],
[[Xavier Gipson]], [[Allen Lazard]]) proposed as additions. Ranked Lions >
Ravens > Falcons > Panthers.

## Draft prospect scenarios
Extended discussion of where [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Rome Odunze]] and
[[Malik Nabers]] should land in the top 10-12, including a possible Julio
Jones-style trade-up, Chargers/Bears/Falcons as trade-down or trade-up
candidates, and a detailed Bears fit case ([[D.J. Moore]] vs. [[Keenan Allen]]
target-share debate for a Caleb Williams-led offense). Extended Odunze-vs-Drake
London long-term prospect comparison, with a Devante Adams comp for Odunze
backed by PFF explosive-play-rate stats.

## Not given pages (passing mentions only)
Isaiah Hodgins (Giants — "shown a little something something," no full
evaluation), Van Jefferson (Broncos WR4 projection), Josh Reynolds (Broncos
signing questioned), Jason Brownlee (Jets UDFA, "intrigued" but no depth),
Lil'Jordan Humphrey (Broncos blocking mention), A.T. Perry (Saints, "that's
like projection"), Simi Fehoko and Derius Davis (Chargers depth, named only
to illustrate the room's thinness), Kalif Raymond and Jameson Williams (Lions
depth trio, no new evaluation), Rondale Moore and Darnell Mooney (Falcons
depth, brief role tags only), Nelson Agholor (Ravens WR3, name-check only),
Trey McBride (brief Kyler Murray/Cardinals passing-game aside — see his own
page for the substantive part of this take), Caleb Williams (brief "I don't
have concerns about his processing" aside).
