## Summary
Day two of the draft breakdown, recorded in person by [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert). They walk the rounds 2-3 pass catchers landing spot by landing spot, pairing Reception Perception charting data (success rate vs man/zone/press, route participation, in-space and contested-catch numbers) with film impressions.

Main segments: [[Luther Burden III]] to Chicago and what Ben Johnson's staff — especially WR coach Antoine Randle El — means for his effort and consistency questions, plus the knock-on for [[D.J. Moore]] and the crowded Bears pass-catching room; [[Tre Harris]] as the Chargers' needed X receiver and what that unlocks for [[Ladd McConkey]] and [[Quentin Johnston]]; [[Jack Bech]] to the Raiders as a Chip Kelly power slot alongside [[Jakobi Meyers]], with [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]] as the size-speed project; [[Kyle Williams]] to New England as a young-[[Stefon Diggs]] separator and Diontae Johnson-with-a-plan comp; Detroit trading up for [[Isaac TeSlaa]] and the hosts' half-serious 'Detroit's Taysom Hill' theory; [[Pat Bryant]] to Denver on a three-game mini sample; [[Savion Williams]] to Green Bay as a gadget weapon and Harmon's plea to stop using [[Jayden Reed]] that way; [[Tai Felton]] to Minnesota as a speed slot with catch-point problems.
