# Hot Start in Free Agency: Kirk Cousins, Michael Pittman, Saquon Barkley & Josh Jacobs

Day-one-of-free-agency reaction show. Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not a
tracked expert) rapid-fire through the day's biggest signings.

**[[Kirk Cousins]] to Atlanta** (4yr/$180M, $100M guaranteed) — dubbed the "bag
king," with real injury/age risk flagged (turns 36 coming off an Achilles
tear), but framed as Atlanta's only real option at quarterback this offseason
and a "grown-up offense" upgrade. Big knock-on boost projected for
**[[Drake London]]**, whose talent the hosts view as unlimited and simply
buried by a bad-QB carousel (grouped with [[Terry McLaurin]]/[[D.J. Moore]]/
Allen Robinson). Falcons' receiver depth beyond London flagged as a real,
still-unaddressed need.

**Minnesota post-Cousins** — [[Justin Jefferson]] "will be fine" regardless of
QB; [[Jordan Addison]] is the one expected to suffer. Hosts "buy the smoke" on
a [[J.J. McCarthy]] trade-up target, and separately debate veteran bridge
options, landing on **[[Jacoby Brissett]]** (new page) as the best bet ahead of
[[Joe Flacco]], [[Gardner Minshew]], Jameis Winston (not tracked) and
[[Ryan Tannehill]].

**[[Michael Pittman Jr.]]** signs a team-friendly 3yr/~$70M Colts deal. Harmon
calls him a true, underrated No. 1 receiver — citing three years of charted
success-rate data and comparing his profile to a "Bud Light" peak-Michael-
Thomas (not tracked) — and predicts a possible top-10 WR breakout in 2024.

**[[Saquon Barkley]]** signs with Philadelphia (3yr/$46.75M). The
[[Christian McCaffrey]]/[[Jahmyr Gibbs]] signings are cited as having
challenged Harmon's prior that ecosystem alone (not premium RB talent) drives
value; Barkley's pass-game upside is downplayed relative to hype.
[[Jalen Hurts]]'s outlook flagged as more dependent on new OC Kellen Moore
than people are crediting.

**[[Josh Jacobs]] to Green Bay**, and Green Bay releases **[[Aaron Jones]]**
in response. Jacobs's youth over Jones is cited as the deciding factor; Jones
still praised as clearly good when healthy, with health/age (30) as the real
concern.
