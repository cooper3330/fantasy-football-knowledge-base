## Summary
Chris Harris solo-reviews the five Week 4 games not covered on Monday's show -- Cowboys-Texans, Chiefs-Raiders, Cardinals-Giants, Titans-Ravens, Packers-Buccaneers -- plus opens with Monday Night Football (Falcons blowout of the Saints). He then hosts guest Peter Overzet (In a Vacuum podcast / Fantasy Life newsletter; NOT a tracked expert) for a Week 5 waiver-wire top-five countdown limited to players rostered in a third or fewer of Yahoo leagues, plus streaming QB and defense picks.
