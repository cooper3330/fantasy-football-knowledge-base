## Summary
Week 15 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-fire segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]). A brutal injury Sunday framed the show: [[Nick Chubb]] broke a foot, [[David Montgomery]] went down with an apparent MCL injury, [[Jaylen Waddle]] left hurt, and [[Saquon Barkley]] took a knee shot from Minkah Fitzpatrick.

Topics: whether [[James Cook]] is a Chris Johnson comp; [[Khalil Shakir]] as an overlooked YAC engine; [[Malik Nabers]] as proof that talent and volume beat quarterback worry; [[Rashad Bateman]] finally being used downfield in Todd Monken's offense; [[Rico Dowdle]] as a 2025 Cowboys starter; the [[Travis Etienne Jr.]] / [[Tank Bigsby]] Jacksonville split; whether [[Tee Higgins]] should stay in Cincinnati and Bob Harris's provocative hypothetical of the Bengals trading [[Ja'Marr Chase]] instead; [[Joe Burrow]] as the NFL's best mobile passer (as distinct from running quarterback); the Cleveland backfield after Chubb's injury; [[Malik Washington]] as a Waddle replacement; a victory lap on [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] and why contested-catch details matter; [[Sione Vaki]] as a cheap Detroit contingency behind [[Jahmyr Gibbs]]; [[Ladd McConkey]] as the most impressive rookie receiver after Nabers, with [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] and [[Xavier Worthy]] discussed alongside; [[Christian Watson]] as the Packers' highest-ceiling receiver; [[Calvin Austin III]] as Pittsburgh's 2025 leading receiver with [[Roman Wilson]] doubted; the workload risk of chasing Barkley's 2,105-yard record (via Adam Harstad's research, NOT tracked); and Nick Nash of San Jose State versus [[Travis Hunter]] for the Biletnikoff.

Also a long plug for the Rookie Scouting Portfolio pre-order and the show's donations to Darkness to Light (over $65,000 to date).
