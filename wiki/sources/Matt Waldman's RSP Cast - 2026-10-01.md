## Summary
Matt Waldman hosts his regular 'Going Deep' segment with tracked co-host [[Brandon Angelo]]. Three main topics: (1) the 2026 'quarterback roller coaster' -- [[Drake May]], [[Justin Herbert]] and [[C.J. Stroud]] all diagnosed with the same processing problem (fine on a clean picture, no answer when coverage is disguised), New England's thin receiver room, the [[J.J. McCarthy]] trade to the Giants as insurance/runway for [[Jackson Dart]], and [[Malik Nabers]]'s slow recovery; (2) a deep film breakdown of Saints QB [[Tyler Shough]] built from Waldman's Gut Check column (touchdowns, sacks, interceptions all charted) crediting OC Kellen Moore's scheme and crediting/blaming [[Noah Fant]], [[Devaughn Vele]], [[Chris Olave]] and [[Juwan Johnson]]; (3) rookie breakout picks ([[Jeremy Bernard]], [[Kenyon Sadiq]], [[Malachi Fields]], [[Emmett Johnson]], [[Antonio Williams]]) and a long closing take on [[DeVonta Smith]] as a top five-six receiver limited by [[Jalen Hurts]]'s arm talent, plus [[Matthew Golden]] and [[Christian Watson]] in Green Bay and [[Jordan Love]]'s lack of an 'escape hatch.' Waldman also revisits his old pre-draft Patrick Mahomes scouting report to lay out his 'robo-quarterback' evaluation framework, comparing Mahomes and Johnny Manziel as unconventional, intuitive players penalized relative to technically-sound 'assembly line' prospects.
