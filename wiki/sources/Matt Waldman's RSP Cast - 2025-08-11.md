## Summary
A 'Feel It or F**k It' episode with [[Matt Waldman]] hosting and guest co-host Dave Kluge of Footballguys (NOT a tracked expert) filling in for regular co-host Bob Harris (also not tracked). Preseason-week-one reactions: Waldman opens by warning that preseason is the most misleading part of the analysis calendar.

Topics: the four-headed Cleveland QB room and [[Shedeur Sanders]]; [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] being deployed as a slot receiver rather than an in-line tight end, contrasted with [[David Njoku]]; why preseason box-score stats and untouched runs are worthless for grading backs ([[Tahj Brooks]] vs [[Braelon Allen]]); contingent-value backs ([[Sean Tucker]] behind [[Rachaad White]] and [[Bucky Irving]]); [[Trey Lance]] and [[Joe Milton III]] as reclamation projects; [[J.J. McCarthy]]'s effect on [[T.J. Hockenson]] and conflicting Minnesota camp reporting; [[Kyler Murray]]'s field-reading limits and the knock-on for [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] and [[Trey McBride]]; the Saints' receivers [[Chris Olave]] and [[Rashid Shaheed]] with [[Tyler Shough]]/[[Spencer Rattler]]; a long segment on whether quarterback-turned-analysts are the definitive authority on film study; [[Keenan Allen]]'s value with [[Justin Herbert]]; the Chargers backfield after [[Omarion Hampton]]'s eye injury and [[Najee Harris]]'s fall in drafts; [[Kyle Monangai]] over [[Roschon Johnson]] in Chicago; and when [[Jackson Dart]] replaces [[Russell Wilson]].
