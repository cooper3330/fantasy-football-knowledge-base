## Summary
A *Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked) on 2025 rookie-draft values — players going below where the hosts grade them — followed by a first look at 2026 college prospects.

The organizing idea for the values half: target rookies whose veteran competition is likely gone or diminished in 2026, so you buy the year-two breakout at a year-one discount ([[Kyle Williams]], [[Pat Bryant]], [[Harold Fannin Jr.]], [[Jack Bech]], [[Jalen Milroe]]). Angelo led on ADP and landing-spot vacancy; Waldman led on tape grades and where each player sits on his RSP board.

Long segments on [[Luther Burden III]] as Waldman's top rookie value and the Ben Johnson/[[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] role in Chicago, on [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] over [[Terrance Ferguson]] and the linear-athlete tight end trap, and on [[Rashee Rice]]'s multi-ligament knee recovery — Angelo's concern is not whether Rice looks good in September but whether Kansas City affords him the bandwidth to survive volume, contact and fatigue over time, with [[Kirk Cousins]] in 2024 as the cautionary case.

2026 peek: Angelo on quarterbacks ([[LaNorris Sellers]], [[Cade Klubnik]], [[John Mateer]]); Waldman on [[Nicholas Singleton]], [[Dean Connors]], [[Eli Stowers]], [[KC Concepcion]], and a stated do-not-draft list of [[Evan Stewart]] and [[Nyck Harbor]].
