## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) break down Week 5 of the 2024 season, the longest Sunday in NFL history (15 hours 27 minutes).

Four long segments: (1) the [[George Pickens]] situation in Pittsburgh — a 59% snap rate, Mike Tomlin's 'less is more' explanation, [[Pat Freiermuth]]'s pointed postgame quote, and Harmon's suggestion the Steelers should consider trading him before the deadline; (2) [[Dontayvion Wicks]] and a 34% season catch rate despite elite Reception Perception separation scores, with Harmon arguing the problem is confidence, ball tracking and contested-catch technique rather than true bad hands; (3) the Buffalo passing game — [[Keon Coleman]] as a pure X with almost no slot usage, [[Mack Hollins]] running far too many routes, the absence of [[Khalil Shakir]], the underuse of [[Curtis Samuel]], and [[Dalton Kincaid]] capped at 65% of snaps under Joe Brady; (4) [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] exceeding his pre-draft projection with charted route and separation data.
