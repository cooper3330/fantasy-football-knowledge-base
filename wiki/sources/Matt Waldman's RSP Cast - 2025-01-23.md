## Summary
A *Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked) covering two topics. First, the 2024 rookie WR class one year on: which rookies they are writing off ([[Xavier Legette]], [[Jermaine Burton]], [[Javon Baker]]), the New England receiver room's chronic miscasting of slot players at X ([[Ja'Lynn Polk]], [[Kayshon Boutte]], [[Demario Douglas]]), and which 2024 receivers gain 500-plus yards in 2025 ([[Malik Washington]], [[Rome Odunze]], [[Ricky Pearsall]], with [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] contingent on [[Arizona Cardinals]] moving on from [[Kyler Murray]]).

Second, an early scouting pass on the 2025 WR class, which both describe as far thinner than 2024: Waldman scored 20 players at 85-plus in 2024 versus roughly six within striking range of 85 this year. [[Travis Hunter]] is Angelo's clear WR1 prospect if he plays receiver full-time; [[Tetairoa McMillan]] and [[Luther Burden III]] are the two they think are over-bought; [[Matthew Golden]] and [[Jalen Royals]] the two they think are under-bought; [[Andrew Armstrong]] and [[Konata Mumpfield]] are Waldman's deep-sleeper favorites.
