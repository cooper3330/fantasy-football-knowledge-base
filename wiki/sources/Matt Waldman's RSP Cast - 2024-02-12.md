## Summary

*Feel It or F**k It* with **Bob Harris** (not tracked; not [[Chris Harris]]).
The first half is a Super Bowl LVIII recap — extensive halftime-show and
prop-bet color that is not wiki content, plus real fantasy-relevant material
on the Chiefs' and 49ers' key players. The back half is a rookie-RB
segment (three underrated 2024 names) and a closing "feel it or f\*\*k it"
run through veteran quarterback/receiver free-agency destinations.

Four existing pages updated off the Super Bowl itself: [[Isiah Pacheco]]
(both hosts read his two fumbles as a buying opportunity, not a red flag, on
the strength of Kansas City never benching him), [[Travis Kelce]] (reframes
his diminished role as a *supporting-cast* problem, not decline),
[[Rashee Rice]] (a real ADP downgrade — an explicit "Gabe Davis effect,"
undrafted deep into Waldman's own current mock), and [[Patrick Mahomes]]
(Waldman's fullest statement yet on his playing style — "a wiser Brett
Favre"). The [[Matt Waldman]] expert page also picks up a new Track Record
Notes entry: Waldman cites a Jed York report that Kyle Shanahan privately
called Brock Purdy the 49ers' best quarterback pre-2022-season as
independent validation of his own pre-draft [[Brock Purdy]] grade, supplied
directly to Purdy's quarterback coach at the time.

Three new rookie running back pages from Waldman's "underrated" segment:
[[Daijun Edwards]] and [[George Holani]], plus a substantial tape-based
follow-up on the already-created [[Dylan Laube]]. Five new veteran pages
from the closing quick-hitter segment: [[Davante Adams]] (staying in Vegas,
Luke Getsy reunion, falling ADP framed as a buy), [[Kirk Cousins]] (staying
in Minnesota, "double-digit rounds" value), [[Daniel Jones]] ("not the
answer," a two-step Giants succession plan), [[Ryan Tannehill]] (a
speculative Pittsburgh/Arthur-Smith veteran-room reunion, Joe-Flacco-style
non-starter role), and [[Aaron Rodgers]] (both hosts bullish on an
Achilles-recovery return to form).
