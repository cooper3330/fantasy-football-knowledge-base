## Summary
A Memorial Day edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman opens with an RSP status report — projections done by the end of the week, rankings over the following weekend, publication no later than June 10 and likely sooner.

Topics: the tush push ban debate and what it means for [[Jalen Hurts]]; [[Aaron Rodgers]] to Pittsburgh as a foregone conclusion; Detroit's coordinator change from Ben Johnson to John Morton and whether [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], [[Jahmyr Gibbs]], [[David Montgomery]], [[Jameson Williams]] and [[Sam LaPorta]] are play-caller proof; [[Isaac TeSlaa]] as a rookie value question; [[Jayden Daniels]] vs [[Jalen Hurts]] at the QB3 price; [[Deebo Samuel]] in Washington and the knock-on for [[Terry McLaurin]]; [[Nick Chubb]] and [[J.K. Dobbins]] as late free-agent signings; [[Tetairoa McMillan]] and the Adam Thielen 'that guy' quote; [[Jermaine Burton]] and [[Marshawn Lloyd]] second-year outlooks; and Patriots UDFA [[Efton Chism III]] as the launch point for a broader warning about spring practice reports.
