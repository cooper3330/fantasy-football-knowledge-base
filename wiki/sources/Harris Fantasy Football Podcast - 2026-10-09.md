## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a recap of Tampa Bay's win over Dallas, praising Bucky Irving's 72-yard touchdown run and using his wild game-log swings as a case against spreadsheet-only analysis; he also flags a shoulder injury scare for Irving. He runs through a large week 5 injury board (Lamar Jackson, Jaden Daniels, Caleb Williams, Saquon Barkley, Ashton Jeanty, Breece Hall, Jeremiah Love, Rhamondre Stevenson, Kyle Monongai, Rashad White, Rico Dowdle, Chris Brooks, Jalen Wright, Zach Charbonnet, Justin Jefferson, Chris Olave, Ja'Marr Chase, Tee Higgins, Mike Evans, Ladd McConkey, DJ Moore, DeVonta Smith, Dontavian Wicks, Dallas Goedert, Carnell Tate, Stefon Diggs, Terry McLaurin, Keenan Allen, Michael Pittman Jr.). The main segment is a 'players to trade away' countdown with recurring untracked guest Cousin Josh (Instagram: detective_fisch), mirroring a similar list untracked analyst Andy Barron ran on the subscriber Yacht Club show. Harris and Josh each name five players they'd sell at the right price with suggested trade returns: Harris picks Trevor Lawrence, Jaylen Waddle, Omarion Hampton, Aaron Jones and Christian Watson; Josh picks Malik Nabers, Emmanuel Wilson, Jeremiah Love, TreVeyon Henderson (implicitly) and Puka Nacua, with both converging on Jeremiah Love. Dalton Kincaid, DeVonta Smith, Chase Brown and Carnell Tate come up as comparison points within those trade pitches.
