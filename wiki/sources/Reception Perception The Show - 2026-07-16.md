## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) run three segments. First, a full Reception Perception profile of Jordan Addison's 2025 season, noting his route tree is almost entirely downfield/intermediate with almost no slant, screen or flat 'layup' routes, tying it to Kevin O'Connell's boom-bust vertical scheme and J.J. McCarthy's rocky rookie transition. Second, a new 'take shopping' segment floats the scenario that Eagles rookie Makai Lemon could out-produce DeVonta Smith in 2026, discussed alongside the Luther Burden/Rome Odunze Bears framing, Dontayvion Wicks' Packers WR3 outlook, and the AJ Brown-to-New-England move; Koh pushes back hard (rates it 1-in-10). Third, a quarterback-to-receiver matching segment on the Cleveland Browns pairs Shedeur Sanders' (and Deshaun Watson's) charting tendencies with rookies Casey Concepcion and Denzel Boston plus veteran Jerry Jeudy, projecting roles under new OC Todd Monken.
