## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a recap of Baltimore's 28-6 Thursday-night win over Miami (De'Von Achane, Jalen Waddle, Malik Washington's two fumbles, Ollie Gordon II's apparent high ankle sprain, Derrick Henry's slow-building 119-yard game, Mark Andrews' two touchdowns). Cousin Josh (co-host, not tracked) then joins for a five-minute Week 9 injury roundup covering Kyler Murray, Brock Purdy, Ricky Pearsall, Joe Flacco, DeAndre Swift, Rhamondre Stevenson, Woody Marks, Nick Chubb, Isaiah Pacheco, Terry McLaurin, Nico Collins, Ladd McConkey, Brian Thomas Jr., Travis Hunter and Rashid Shaheed. The main segment is a head-to-head 'five biggest surprises of the 2025 season so far' countdown between Harris and Josh: Matthew Stafford's resurgence, D.J. Moore's collapse under Ben Johnson, Jameson Williams' quiet season (where the hosts disagree on the turnaround outlook), Kaleb Johnson's fall into Pittsburgh's doghouse behind Jaylen Warren and Kenneth Gainwell, Jackson Dart's bust-proof QB1 floor, George Pickens' WR2 emergence, Drake May lifting a thin Patriots offense, the Daniel Jones-led Colts' 7-1 start, and the persistent Kenneth Walker III/Zach Charbonnet even timeshare in Seattle. The episode closes with a DraftKings Pick 6 read and Cousin Josh's against-the-spread picks.
