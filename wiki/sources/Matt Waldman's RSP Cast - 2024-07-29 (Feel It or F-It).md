## Summary
Second full week of training camp. [[Matt Waldman]] with co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]) run the rapid-fire 'feel it or f-it' format across camp reports. Waldman opens by framing camp season as 'feeling it as a fan, f-it as an analyst' — beat-writer hype as a 'molly dispenser' that fantasy players consume like a new relationship they refuse to see flaws in.

Ground covered: [[Keon Coleman]]'s realistic year-one ceiling in Buffalo, [[Dalton Kincaid]] vs. Dawson Knox target concerns, [[Chase Brown]] overtaking [[Zach Moss]] in Cincinnati, [[Ladd McConkey]] atop the Chargers depth chart (and the scouting-process-over-production argument behind it), [[Malik Nabers]] as the class's best rookie WR and an ADP arbitrage vs. [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], late-round RB lottery tickets [[Dylan Laube]] and [[Deneric Prince]] plus [[Zamir White]], [[Andrei Iosivas]] vs. [[Jermaine Burton]] as the [[Tee Higgins]] succession plan, the [[Aaron Rodgers]]–[[Garrett Wilson]] sideline argument and Waldman's ball-tracking research, [[Kirk Cousins]]'s Achilles and [[Michael Penix Jr.]] as a dynasty stash, a dismissal of [[Jordan Whittington]] comps, [[Tre Tucker]]'s path around [[Brock Bowers]], [[Hendon Hooker]]'s fading outlook, [[Eric All]], [[DeMarcus Robinson]] as a best-ball flyer, and a long segment on [[Nick Chubb]]'s knee.
