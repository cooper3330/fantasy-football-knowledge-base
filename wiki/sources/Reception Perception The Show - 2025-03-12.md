## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) react to the opening days of 2025 NFL free agency, with heavy focus on quarterback and receiver movement and what it means for passing games. Harmon opens by recounting his 20-minute presentation on Reception Perception at the MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference — 12 years of charting work, a packed room, and a Q&A where multiple attendees asked whether he would automate the charting with AI (his answer: he likes doing the work, and it is a million-dollar project he has no idea how to build).

Main segments: [[DK Metcalf]] traded to Pittsburgh for a second-round pick and signed to a five-year, $150M deal — Harmon delivers a full freshly-charted 2024 Reception Perception profile and calls it Metcalf's worst NFL season. [[Geno Smith]] traded to the Raiders for a third and seeking $45M/yr; Koh calls it a classic Raiders overpay while Harmon defends it. [[Sam Darnold]] to Seattle on a three-year, $100M deal — both agree it is a modest downgrade from Smith. [[Justin Fields]] to the Jets on two years, $40M and the [[Garrett Wilson]] reunion. [[Davante Adams]] to the Rams on two years, $46M, with an extended alignment discussion involving [[Puka Nacua]] and [[Cooper Kupp]]. Closes with the roughly $10M 'playable wide receiver' free agency tier.
