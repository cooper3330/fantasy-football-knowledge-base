## Summary
A 'Feel It or F**k It' ADP-reaction episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Bob Harris supplied ADP and box-score context; Waldman gave evaluations plus in-progress 2025 projections (he noted he was doing a couple of teams a day, with Footballguys rankings and Dynasty rankings due early June).

Segments: the [[Brock Purdy]] five-year, $265M extension and whether his lack of elite physical traits explains his depressed quarterback ADP; the San Francisco pass-catcher cluster ([[Jauan Jennings]], [[Ricky Pearsall]], [[Brandon Aiyuk]]) and how to price an Aiyuk knee recovery; [[George Kittle]] as the value tight end of the elite tier; a Dallas backfield 'running back roulette' ([[Javonte Williams]] vs [[Jaydon Blue]]) plus a Blue-vs-[[Bhayshul Tuten]] coin flip; [[CeeDee Lamb]] and [[George Pickens]] target-share math; [[Dak Prescott]] as a QB16 value; [[Jake Ferguson]]; the [[Derrick Henry]] extension and aging; [[De'Von Achane]], [[Jonnu Smith]], [[Tua Tagovailoa]] and [[Ollie Gordon II]] in a Miami offense Waldman thinks the market has written off.
