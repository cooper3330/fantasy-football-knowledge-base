## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a full film review of the Monday night Cowboys-Cardinals game, then works through four more Week 9 games he re-watched in full: Colts at Steelers, Panthers at Packers, Texans at Broncos and Giants at 49ers. The second half is the weekly waiver-wire segment with guest Jeff Erickson of RotoWire (NOT a tracked expert), who counts down his top five adds at 33% rostered or fewer alongside Harris's own five.

Key threads: Dallas's offense laid an egg (seven offensive points) but Harris refuses to downgrade the Cowboys off one night; the Cardinals backfield is a genuine mess with no discernible pattern between Zonovan Knight and [[Emari Demercado]] and a brutal Seattle matchup ahead; the Colts offensive line collapsed and [[Daniel Jones]] reverted under pressure; [[Alec Pierce]] led Indianapolis in targets on intermediate routes rather than bombs; [[Rico Dowdle]] is unambiguously Carolina's lead back despite an in-game groin issue; [[Tucker Kraft]] tore his ACL and is out for the year, elevating [[Luke Musgrave]]; [[C.J. Stroud]] left with a concussion; [[Bo Nix]] went 2-for-11 on throws of 20+ air yards.

Erickson's list: 5) [[Devin Singletary]], 4) [[Theo Johnson]], 3) [[Emari Demercado]], 2) [[Tory Horton]], 1) [[Parker Washington]]. Harris's list: 5) [[Colston Loveland]], 4) [[Luke Musgrave]], 3) [[Devin Singletary]], 2) [[Christian Watson]], 1) [[Tory Horton]].
