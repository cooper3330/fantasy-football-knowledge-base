## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh recap both conference championship games (Patriots 20-17 over Broncos in a snow game; Seahawks 31-27 over Rams) and preview Seahawks-Patriots in the Super Bowl. Football content: New England's offense was inefficient but Rhamondre Stevenson dominated the backfield snaps over TreVeyon Henderson; Denver's skill-position depth (J.K. Dobbins, Troy Franklin out; Marvin Mims Jr. and Evan Engram unproductive; Adam Trautman a nonfactor) was cited as costing them more than Bo Nix's absence; Courtland Sutton and Pat Bryant discussed as Denver's offseason WR needs; R.J. Harvey praised but not yet a bell-cow. On Seattle, Jaxon Smith-Njigba's emergence as the alpha receiver, Cooper Kupp's complementary role, and the Rashid Shaheed trade (after Tory Horton's injury) are credited to GM John Schneider's full offseason rebuild. The back half covers coaching news: Pittsburgh hiring Mike McCarthy over Mike Tomlin, the Cleveland Browns' head-coaching search (Nate Sheilhouse as presumed target), and a long discussion of Josh Allen sitting in on Buffalo's head-coaching interviews (including the revelation that Allen, not Buffalo's coaching staff, pushed to draft Keon Coleman), contrasted with how Baltimore is handling Lamar Jackson's input on its own coaching search.
