## Summary
Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) and Matt Waldman run their 'Feel It or F**k It' combine-reaction format, grading 2026 draft prospects off NFL Scouting Combine workouts across WR, RB, TE and QB. General theme: skepticism of the 40-yard dash as a predictor (gamed by pre-draft speed training, inflated by Lucas Oil Stadium's new turf), with the 20-shuttle and 3-cone weighted more heavily by Waldman. RSP progress update (150-170 players studied, book out April 1). Discussed: Carnell Tate, Makai Lemon, Mike Washington, Emmett Johnson, KC Concepcion (gauntlet drill/clap-attacking), Malachi Fields, Chase Roberts, Antonio Williams, Ja'Kobi Lane, Brendan Thompson, Bryce Lance, Carson Ryan, Matthew Hibner, Oscar Delt, RJ Maryland, Taylen Green. Closed with a quick take on David Montgomery being traded to the Houston Texans and the related Titus Howard/offensive line moves.
