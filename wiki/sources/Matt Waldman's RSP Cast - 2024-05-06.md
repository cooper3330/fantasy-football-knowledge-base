## Summary
Weekly "Feel It or F**k It" segment: Matt Waldman with untracked co-host Bob Harris, running through quick-hit dynasty/redraft/best-ball verdicts post-NFL Draft. Waldman promotes the post-draft RSP release, then the two work through Ben Sinnott's Ertz-shadowed TE outlook, the Chargers' WR hedge signings around Quentin Johnston, the Giants' RB committee (Eric Gray vs. Tyrone Tracy Jr.), rookie QB1 debate (Jayden Daniels vs. Caleb Williams), 2024 best-ball QB ADP strategy, Jalen McMillan/Trey Palmer in Tampa, Odell Beckham Jr. in Miami, Jared Wiley behind Noah Gray in KC, Kirk Cousins' leg injury (citing Brandon Angelo's prior analysis), Michael Penix Jr.'s landing spot, Chase Claypool in Buffalo, Kadarius Toney's collapse, Najee Harris's option decline, Jaylen Warren, Ricky Pearsall/Brandon Aiyuk in SF, Dameon Pierce and Joe Mixon in Houston, Nick Chubb's injury recovery odds, Justin Fields as a non-returner, and Drew Lock projected to open the season as Giants starter over an ACL-recovering Daniel Jones.
