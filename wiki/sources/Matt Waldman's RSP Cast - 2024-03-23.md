# Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-23

## Summary

Solo cast (no co-host) — Matt Waldman's alphabetical A-W scouting rundown of
the 2024 running back class ahead of the RSP's April 1 pre-draft release,
mirroring the format of his 2024-03-08 QB and 2024-03-16 WR solo casts.

Opens with a historical framework rather than a class grade: reviewing his
own pre-draft assessments against actual NFL outcomes for the 2016-2023 RB
classes, Waldman finds the number of prospects per class who become
multi-year successful starters is almost always just one to three — 2017
(headlined by [[Christian McCaffrey]] and [[Alvin Kamara]]) is "a complete
outlier" at six. His conclusion: most running back classes are "bad classes"
by this standard, including 2024, which he grades as wider (24 plausible
contributors) but shallower at the very top (only two grades near immediate
starter-level, versus five or six in 2023). Explicit dynasty rookie-draft
recommendation: prioritize receivers and quarterbacks over running backs,
then trade for established veteran backs once a talent surplus is built —
logged on [[Dynasty]].

The bulk of the episode is then a fast, alphabetical run through 53
prospects with one or two scouting notes each — praise, criticism, lingering
questions, or broader thoughts, explicitly framed as condensed previews of
full reports available in the April 1 Rookie Scouting Portfolio. Several
touch on the RSP's own charted contact-balance/broken-tackle metrics (e.g.
[[George Holani]]), on 40-yard-dash skepticism relative to tape
(Notre Dame's [[Audric Estime]]), and on toughness narratives that Waldman
pushes back on with tape evidence ([[Dillon Johnson]] and the Mike Leach
rumor). A DeMarco Murray anecdote about learning to play within a team after
being a high-school star, relayed re: Monmouth's Jaden Sheridan, was logged
to [[Player Development and Coachability]] rather than a standalone page for
Sheridan, who was not otherwise given a substantive evaluative take.

Nineteen previously-created prospect pages received new dated bullets:
[[Bucky Irving]], [[Blake Corum]], [[Blake Watson]], [[Braelon Allen]],
[[Cody Schrader]], [[Deshaun Fenwick]], [[Dillon Johnson]],
