## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) run through Week 3 of the 2025 season: the Browns thrashing the Packers on Thursday night, Tampa Bay's receiver room collapsing after [[Mike Evans]]' hamstring injury, [[CeeDee Lamb]]'s high ankle sprain and what it means for [[George Pickens]] as a forced-into-the-role number one, the Eagles finally letting [[A.J. Brown]] and [[DeVonta Smith]] eat, the Giants' handling of [[Malik Nabers]], [[Russell Wilson]] and [[Jackson Dart]], the Raiders' offensive-line and play-tipping problems around [[Ashton Jeanty]] and the [[Tre Tucker]] explosion, and a long closing segment on [[Quentin Johnston]]'s third-year leap alongside [[Ladd McConkey]] and [[Keenan Allen]] in the Chargers' three-receiver set.

Harmon brought fresh Reception Perception charting on [[Tre Tucker]] (vs. Washington) and [[Quentin Johnston]] (vs. Denver); Koh brought his own run-blocking charting on [[Ashton Jeanty]] from Week 2.
