## Summary
Chris Harris solo-reviews Monday night's Bears-Eagles game (Case Keenum's system-driven win, Jalen Hurts's anticipation-throw limitations, the Andy Dalton blue-tent chaos), then breaks down five more Week 3 games on film: Seahawks-Commanders, Bengals-Steelers, Panthers-Browns, Cardinals-49ers, and Titans-Giants. Guest Jake Seeley (The Athletic, not a tracked expert) then joins for the Week 4 waiver wire segment, running through a ranked list of adds under 33% Yahoo-rostered plus notable Dolphins/Saints/Bucs backfield situations created by injuries to De'Von Achane, Travis Etienne Jr. and Bucky Irving.
