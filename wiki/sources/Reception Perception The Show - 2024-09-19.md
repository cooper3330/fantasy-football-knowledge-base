## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) record on the Wednesday before Week 3 of the 2024 season. Part one is a 'GM/coach hat' segment on fixing the league's worst passing offenses: Tennessee (6th fewest passing yards), New England (4th fewest) and Pittsburgh (3rd fewest). Part two is a buy-or-sell round on surprising receiver production, good and bad: [[Rashid Shaheed]], [[Alec Pierce]], [[Michael Pittman Jr.]], [[Jameson Williams]], [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], [[Chris Godwin]] vs [[Mike Evans]], [[Courtland Sutton]], [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Amari Cooper]] and [[Tank Dell]]. Harmon leans on Reception Perception charting (route-level success rates vs man, zone and press), fantasy points first-read target share and PFF pressure data throughout.
