# Matt Waldman's RSP Cast — 2024-01-08

## Summary

*Feel It or F**k It*, the Week 18 wrap-up with **Bob Harris** (not a tracked
expert; not [[Chris Harris]]). Opens with news that Harris is joining
Football Guys after Football Diehards' founder stepped back for health
reasons — industry color, not wiki content. A wide, fast-moving sweep of
2024 redraft and dynasty value across four team situations in particular:

1. **Buffalo's three-way passing-game split.** [[Gabe Davis]] (expected to
   leave in free agency), [[Khalil Shakir]] (inheriting his role),
   [[Dalton Kincaid]] (getting more intermediate volume), and [[James Cook]]
   (fully unleashed in year two) — Waldman's stance is the offense has room
   for three fantasy-relevant starters at once, not a strict hierarchy.
2. **Arizona's crowded backfield and receiver room.** [[James Connor]],
   [[Michael Carter]], and [[Michael Wilson]], the latter now upgraded from a
   passing mention in [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]] to a full
   evaluative take.
3. **San Francisco's McCaffrey-contingency depth chart.** [[Jordan Mason]]
   preferred over [[Elijah Mitchell]], plus [[Ronnie Bell]] as an
   injury-contingent dynasty stash.
4. **Green Bay's wide receiver room.** [[Dontayvion Wicks]] threatening
   [[Romeo Doubs]]'s and [[Christian Watson]]'s roles, with [[Jayden Reed]]
   unaffected; [[Tucker Kraft]] reaffirmed as the cost-efficient pick over
   [[Luke Musgrave]] at tight end.

Also covered: [[Drake London]] and [[Bijan Robinson]] (with
[[Tyler Allgeier]]) now that Atlanta has fired Arthur Smith; [[Tony Pollard]]'s
rebound case, built on a new concept —
[[Healthy Enough to Play vs. Healthy Enough to Perform]], a framework
attributed to [[Brandon Angelo]] though he isn't a co-host on this episode;
[[Derrick Henry]]'s free-agency market (Dallas floated alongside the
established Ravens preference); [[Josh Jacobs]] vs. [[Zamir White]] as a
direct comparison; and [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] and [[Austin Ekeler]].

An extended continuation of the Bill Belichick GM-legacy debate from
[[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-04]] recurs here — **not** ingested as
wiki content, since no fantasy-relevant player claim is at stake.
