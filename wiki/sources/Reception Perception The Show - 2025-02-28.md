## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] (broadcasting from the NFL Combine) and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) went team-by-team through the AFC East wide receiver rooms ahead of 2025 free agency.

Jets: [[Davante Adams]] is expected to be cut and is 'definitely the best available X on the market' despite a partial Reception Perception sample showing ~75 percent success rate vs man and 74 percent vs press — down from his all-time RP seasons but still a quality starting X. The Jets lost four of their top six route runners; [[Garrett Wilson]] is the lone foundation piece, with [[Malachi Corley]] and [[Xavier Gipson]] behind him.

Patriots: a young, unaccomplished room. [[Ja'Lynn Polk]]'s 0.35 yards per route run rookie year was, in Harmon's view, part player failure and part coaching-staff failure via a misfit X-receiver deployment. [[Javon Baker]] ran ~40 routes and will not be charted; his prospect profile (70.7 percent of snaps at left outside, 35.6 percent nine routes) always screamed developmental. [[Demario Douglas]] and [[Kendrick Bourne]] are complementary pieces; [[Drake Maye]] may be better served by fixing the offensive line first.

Bills: [[Khalil Shakir]] earned his extension as a man-beating, YAC-heavy slot (7.9 yards after catch per reception) but plays only ~66 percent of routes; [[Keon Coleman]] was targeted on 17.6 percent of routes and profiles as a 'sacrificial X'; [[Mack Hollins]] led the room in routes (408) and touchdowns (5); [[Amari Cooper]] flopped after the trade; [[Dalton Kincaid]] and Dawson Knox fit awkwardly in Joe Brady's run-heavy build.

Dolphins: [[Tyreek Hill]] is now more likely than not to stay; his efficiency collapsed across the board (1.79 YPRR from 3.85). [[Jaylen Waddle]] also cratered to 1.56 YPRR and was deprioritized behind [[Jonnu Smith]]. [[Malik Washington]] deserves more work.
