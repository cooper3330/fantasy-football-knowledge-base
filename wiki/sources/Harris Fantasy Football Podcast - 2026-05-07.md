## Summary
The final ad-supported show before Harris's summer hiatus (back in July; Yacht Club subscriber show continues on Mondays). Chris Harris walks his 2026 wide receiver and quarterback ranks with untracked co-host 'cousin Josh' (a recurring, non-tracked guest), who pushes back and compares Harris's numbers to consensus/ADP across multiple services. Covers the WR1-3 super tier, the WR4-9 group, WR10-20 (including flag players Terry McLaurin and Rashee Rice), WR21-30 (DJ Moore, Ladd McConkey, Garrett Wilson, Christian Watson, Alec Pierce), WR31-43 (Jacksonville's three receivers, Marvin Harrison Jr. vs Michael Wilson, Mike Evans, Ricky Pearsall) and late-round names (Romeo Doubs, Jayden Reed, Travis Hunter). Then covers 1QB and Superflex quarterback ranks 1-16 plus sleepers Jackson Dart and Kyler Murray. Recurring theme: Harris calls 2026 wide receiver 'an absolute poop show' -- flatter than ever, with tiny per-week point gaps translating into large rank swings -- and argues quarterback is similarly flat/replaceable in single-QB formats.
