## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) reviewed Week 4 of the 2024 season. Main threads: Green Bay's receiver room clarifying after [[Christian Watson]]'s high ankle sprain, with [[Dontayvion Wicks]]' 13-target, two-touchdown, three-drop game and [[Jayden Reed]]'s emergence as a possible top-12 receiver; a long 'tier one' elite-receiver framing in which Harmon named [[Justin Jefferson]], [[Tyreek Hill]], [[Davante Adams]], [[A.J. Brown]] and [[CeeDee Lamb]] as elite, placed [[Ja'Marr Chase]] and [[Brandon Aiyuk]] at 'tier one and a half', and argued [[Nico Collins]] has earned entry; skepticism that [[Garrett Wilson]] is making the elite leap. Then a rookie roundup: [[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[Ladd McConkey]], [[Xavier Worthy]], [[Xavier Legette]] and [[Jordan Whittington]], plus [[Rashee Rice]]'s injury and its effect on Kansas City, and [[Diontae Johnson]]'s career arc under [[Dave Canales]] and Andy Dalton.
