## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) recap all four Divisional Round games. Buffalo: Josh Allen's four-turnover meltdown, Sean McDermott's firing, the Brandon Cooks non-catch controversy, and the barren receiver room after the Keon Coleman bust. Houston: CJ Stroud's historically bad game (420th of 429 postseason QB performances by EPA/dropback since 2007), his regression from a special rookie year, and the team's failure to develop Jayden Higgins and Jaylen Noel while Nico Collins and Tank Dell were out. Chicago: DJ Moore's low-effort route that led to Caleb Williams' overtime pick, Caleb's uneven-but-promising Year 2, and rookie flashes/mistakes from Rome Odunze, Colson Loveland and Luther Burden III. San Francisco: the championship window judged closed, Christian McCaffrey's fading burst despite a huge 480 combined-touch season, George Kittle's injury outlook, Ricky Pearsall's usage, and a thin receiver room with Juwan Jennings and Brandon Aiyuk likely gone.
