## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] reviews Week 10 all-22 style, game by game, deliberately avoiding box-score talk. Solo segments cover Colts-Falcons in Germany (an overtime [[Jonathan Taylor]] takeover), Patriots-Buccaneers, Bills-Dolphins, Rams-49ers and Saints-Panthers, plus a Monday night preview of Eagles at Packers. Guest **Jamie Eisenberg** of CBS Fantasy (a guest, NOT a tracked expert) joins for Giants-Bears, Ravens-Vikings and Steelers-Chargers. Two running threads: a data-backed debunking of the goal-line 'vulture' panic around [[Bijan Robinson]] and [[Christian McCaffrey]], and a role-change flag on [[Alec Pierce]] as the Colts' primary read on third-and-long. Harris cites earlier show appearances by co-host Pat Doherty and 'Paywall Andy' (neither tracked).
