## Summary
Chris Harris solo-hosts, opening with a full breakdown of the Monday night Panthers-49ers game (Purdy's three first-half interceptions, Shanahan muzzling him after halftime, CMC's 31-touch workload, Bryce Young's red-zone meltdown, Pearsall staying benched, Dowdle cooling off and splitting snaps with Hubbard, McMillan's lone bright spot). He then reviews the four remaining Week 12 Sunday games: Steelers-Bears (Swift benched down the stretch for Monangai, Caleb Williams' erratic-but-productive day, Odunze as the lone startable Bear WR, Rudolph's conservative screen-heavy debut, Metcalf's touchdown-via-screen), Jaguars-Cardinals OT thriller (Lawrence's 3 TD/3 INT line compared to Bortles, Etienne's clear lead role over Tuten, Strange's strong IR return, Brissett's gutsy 50-attempt outing, Wilson's clock blunder), and Jets-Ravens plus Browns-Raiders (Breece Hall's workhorse role and goal-line fumble, Tyrod Taylor's conservative debut, Lamar Jackson's second sluggish half in a row, Derrick Henry's two scores, Shedeur Sanders' rough debut, Judkins' two Wildcat TDs, Jeanty's ankle flag, Bowers' must-start status, Geno Smith's implosion, Jeudy's fumble, Fannin's flash of promise). Guest Scott Fish of Fantasy Cares (untracked) joins for the Week 13 waiver wire, and he and Harris rank their top-5 adds -- Iosivas, Taysom Hill, Strange, Higgins, Rodriguez, and Neal headline -- plus honorable mentions Kirk, Dike, McLaughlin and Metchie. Episode closes with a DraftKings point-spread segment (Cousin Josh's picks) and Fantasy Cares charity plugs.
