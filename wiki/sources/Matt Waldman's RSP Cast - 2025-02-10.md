## Summary
Post-Super Bowl LIX edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman opens with a plug for the 20th edition of the Rookie Scouting Portfolio (pre-draft and post-draft, $21.95; projections/dynasty rankings a separate $24.95 service), then the pair work through the Eagles' Super Bowl win and a long list of offseason quarterback, running back and receiver questions, using early mock-draft ADP that Waldman and Bob Harris have both seen as the reference point for value.

Main threads: whether [[Jalen Hurts]] is good/very good/great and how the Chiefs' plan against [[Saquon Barkley]] (57 rushing yards) still lost; Barkley as the locked-in RB1 with hypothetical challengers; [[A.J. Brown]] falling to the middle of round two; [[Travis Kelce]]'s value if he returns and Waldman's tight end pecking order; the Jets moving on from [[Aaron Rodgers]] and likely [[Davante Adams]]; the Vikings' [[J.J. McCarthy]] / [[Sam Darnold]] situation and Darnold's fit with the Raiders and Giants; quarterback carousel takes on [[Justin Fields]], [[Russell Wilson]], Kirk Cousins and [[Daniel Jones]]; [[Nick Chubb]]'s free agency and the Giants' backfield; [[Rome Odunze]] without Keenan Allen; [[Deebo Samuel]]'s trade market; [[Cooper Kupp]] and the Rams' WR2/TE openings; [[Tee Higgins]] vs [[Terry McLaurin]] as a 'baker vs chef' role framing; [[Trey McBride]] and [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] in Arizona; [[Adam Thielen]] as a very late-round value; and a price check on [[Tyreek Hill]].
