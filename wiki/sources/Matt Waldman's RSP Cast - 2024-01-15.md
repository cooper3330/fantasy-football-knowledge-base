## Summary

*Feel It or F**k It*, with guest **Daniel Harms** (Football Guys / NFL33.com,
"InHarmsWay19" — not a tracked expert; filling in for regular co-host Bob
Harris) covering both current-NFL and 2024 draft-prospect ground in rapid
question-and-answer format. A fast-moving episode with a genuinely high
hit rate of substantive individual takes, several of which materially move
existing pages:

**Current NFL players:** [[Aaron Jones]] (reaffirmed 2024 hold), 
[[Michael Gallup]] (new page — Harms is out on a rebound), [[Tony Pollard]]
(a real reversal — both hosts flip to skeptical after the January 8
rebound-odds framing, tied back to
[[Healthy Enough to Play vs. Healthy Enough to Perform]]), [[Rashee Rice]]
(Harms gives the most detailed positive role breakdown yet, "100% feeling
this"), [[De'Von Achane]] (new page — flagged as a real 2024 overrated risk
despite rookie efficiency), [[Tua Tagovailoa]] (new page — good QB, not an
elite one, per both hosts), and [[Travis Kelce]] (Harms pushes back hard on
Waldman's own January 1 decline read — recorded as a live disagreement, not
resolved).

**2024 draft prospects (pre-draft scouting, entirely [[Dynasty]]):**
[[Drake Maye]] (new page — ASR-garbled "Drake May," normalized), [[Bo Nix]]
(new page — a skeptical outlier take), [[Keon Coleman]] (independent
third-source agreement with the [[Brandon Angelo]]/Waldman take from
[[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-01-11 (Going Deep - Draft Prospects)]]),
[[Brian Thomas Jr.]] (new page), [[Ricky Pearsall]] (new page — Harms's
favorite sleeper, ASR-garbled "Ricky Purcell," normalized), [[Braelon Allen]]
(new page — ASR-garbled "Braylon Allen," normalized; Harms's most overrated
running back in the class), [[Troy Franklin]] (new page — real talent, real
floor risk against physical coverage), and [[Caleb Williams]] (new page —
still the top QB prospect, but "the most boom-bust top prospect... I've
graded in a few years").
