# Reception Perception: The Show — 2024-04-02

[[Matt Harmon]], with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert), drops finished
full-sample Reception Perception profiles on two 2024 receiver prospects:
[[Brian Thomas Jr.]] (LSU) and [[Ladd McConkey]] (Georgia). Both were
previously covered only in Harmon's early 3-game-sample charting
(2024-02-01); this episode supersedes those with complete-profile numbers and
final class-rank/verdict calls.

## Covered
- [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] — full profile: elite size/speed backed up on tape, a
  heavily concentrated route tree (mostly a function of LSU's scheme
  alongside [[Malik Nabers]]), elite nine-route and press-coverage success
  rates, surprisingly few double teams seen, strong contested-catch and
  after-catch ability. Graded a clear tier-two prospect (4th in Harmon's
  class rankings, behind the top three), landing-spot speculation (Colts,
  Cowboys). Median-outcome projection: a very good vertical WR2, not a lock
  true WR1.
- [[Ladd McConkey]] — full profile: elite athletic testing (RAS 9.33/10),
  properly an outside flanker rather than a true slot receiver despite his
  size, elite zone-coverage and out-route success rates, a real press-
  coverage weakness, zero drops, strong after-catch/tackle-breaking numbers.
  Comped to Tyler Lockett; co-host James Koh also floats an Emmanuel Sanders
  comp. Harmon views him as roughly equal in value to Brian Thomas Jr. on his
  board despite very different archetypes.
