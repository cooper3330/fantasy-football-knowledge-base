## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) run a Week 11 news-and-notes show. Harmon flags the four-game Rookie Report going live on receptionperception.com and calls 2025 a strange rookie year because few rookies, even day-two picks, walked into full-time roles.

Segments: [[Travis Hunter]] out for the season after LCL surgery and what that does to Jacksonville's pass-catcher picture ([[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[Parker Washington]], [[Brenton Strange]], [[Trevor Lawrence]]); Green Bay's banged-up receiving room and the absence of [[Jayden Reed]] plus confusion over [[Matthew Golden]]'s alignment; [[Jameis Winston]] named the Giants' starter and whether the 'pump and dump' is worth chasing with [[Darius Slayton]], [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] and [[Theo Johnson]]; a heated argument over whether Minnesota should play [[J.J. McCarthy]] out; Arizona's receiver injuries ([[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] appendicitis, Zay Jones Achilles) and the [[Trey McBride]] / [[Michael Wilson]] fallout; [[A.J. Brown]]'s unhappiness and 83% outside alignment versus [[DeVonta Smith]]'s ascent; Denver's target shift to [[Troy Franklin]] over [[Courtland Sutton]] with [[Pat Bryant]] taking slot snaps; and [[Jaylen Waddle]]'s post-Tyreek-Hill breakout. Picks segment: Waddle over 78.5 receiving yards, [[Rashid Shaheed]] over 2.5 catches.
