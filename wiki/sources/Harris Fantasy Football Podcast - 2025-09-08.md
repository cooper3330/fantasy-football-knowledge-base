## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens the 2025 regular season with his Monday film-review format: he watches every game rather than reading box scores, and here covers nine of them. Solo segments on Ravens-Bills, Steelers-Jets, Buccaneers-Falcons, Chiefs-Chargers, Texans-Rams and Raiders-Patriots. Guest **Sam Gold** of Sam's Film Room (NOT a tracked expert) joins for Giants-Commanders, Lions-Packers and 49ers-Seahawks.

The running theme is snap counts and roles shattering preseason assumptions: Kenneth Gainwell out-snapping Jaylen Warren early in Pittsburgh, Kareem Hunt owning third down and short yardage in Kansas City, Zach Charbonnet out-snapping Kenneth Walker III in Seattle, and Bucky Irving's dominant touch share vindicating the bull case Harris had questioned in August. Harris also uses the Chargers' pass volume to attack run-pass-mix arguments as a lazy way to fade a player, and both hosts caution against selling Detroit off one data point.
