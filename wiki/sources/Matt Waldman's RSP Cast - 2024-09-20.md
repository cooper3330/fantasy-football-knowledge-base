## Summary
A solo [[Matt Waldman]] episode — [[Brandon Angelo]] was traveling for a wedding, so there was no *Going Deep* segment. Waldman laid out his framework for evaluating quarterbacks once they begin their NFL careers, then applied it as a week-three progress report on the 2023 and 2024 rookie QB classes.

The framework: the first two to six starts are a *continuation* of the rookie evaluation, not a re-evaluation. Per scouts and QB coaches he has spoken with, it takes roughly 18-30 games to know what a quarterback can and cannot do. He splits the runway into three windows — games 6-8 (does the scheme allow a fair evaluation, and does the player look like his college self, pluses and minuses), games 6-20 (defenses have four to six weeks of pro tape and start building a book, so early hype fades and regression shows), and games 15-30 (has he rebounded and grown against his negatives — the guys who do earn second contracts). He stresses process over results and box-score stats.

Progress reports on [[Caleb Williams]], [[Anthony Richardson]], [[Bryce Young]], [[Will Levis]], [[Jayden Daniels]], [[Bo Nix]] and [[Skylar Thompson]], plus a look back at [[Baker Mayfield]]. He cited [[Adam Harstad]]'s point from that day's *Film and Theory* episode (Harstad is not a tracked expert) that a late-round QB who sticks on a roster three seasons is a signal worth monitoring. He also pushed back on Greg Cosell (not a tracked expert) on Bryce Young's height and archetype.
