## Summary
Season-opening show back from a two-month hiatus. Chris Harris previews the new almanac ($22, out August 1) and his novel Pound Foolish, then kicks off eight training-camp preview shows (one per division) with untracked guest Jim McCormick covering the NFC South: Panthers, Buccaneers, Falcons, Saints. Reads an almanac profile excerpt for Tyler Shough. Extended back-and-forth with McCormick on backfield and receiver pecking orders, an injury-heavy rookie WR (Jordan Tyson), the Falcons QB competition (Penix vs. Tua), Kyle Pitts, and whether Alvin Kamara survives Saints camp.
