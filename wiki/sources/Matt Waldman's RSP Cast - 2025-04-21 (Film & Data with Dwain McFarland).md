## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] hosts Dwain McFarland of Fantasy Life (guest, NOT a tracked expert) to cross-check RSP film grades against McFarland's 'supermodel', which blends production rating, film rating (converted from Lance Zierlein's grades), program quality/strength of schedule and draft capital on a 50-100 scale. McFarland repeatedly contrasts the supermodel against a version with draft capital removed, treating a large gap between the two as an overvaluation flag (he cites Jahan Dotson, Jalen Reagor and Kadarius Toney as roughly 40-spot flags that busted; Zay Flowers, D.J. Moore and Rashod Bateman at -27).

Players discussed in depth: [[Tyler Warren]] (both low), [[Luther Burden III]] (both high, Brandon Aiyuk comp), [[Tetairoa McMillan]] (model's WR1, Waldman's WR5), [[Jack Bech]], [[Isaiah Bond]], [[Kyle Williams]] and [[Jaydon Blue]]. Waldman also detours into technique vs craft, catch-technique lapses vs bad habits, and fumble-rate research.
