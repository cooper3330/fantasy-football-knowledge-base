## Summary
A *Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked). Five segments: (1) the [[Anthony Richardson]] benching for Joe Flacco in Indianapolis — the pair defend Richardson's play against Houston, blast the Colts' lack of organizational transparency, argue Jim Irsay drove the decision, and reframe Richardson through an archetype lens (Elway, Bradshaw, Cam Newton, Josh Allen, Lamar Jackson) rather than statistical comp lists; a long digression on the 'tap out', warrior-mentality rhetoric and why every ex-player pundit said he should have lied. (2) Cleveland with [[Jameis Winston]] under center — fit with Kevin Stefanski's under-center play action, [[Cedric Tillman]] as the emerging primary target, [[Elijah Moore]] outside, and [[David Njoku]] finally being thrown contested balls. (3) [[Russell Wilson]] over Justin Fields in Pittsburgh — moon balls, 12 personnel, [[Najee Harris]]'s best stretch, [[Calvin Austin III]]'s emergence. (4) The [[Diontae Johnson]] trade to Baltimore for a fifth, plus [[Jalen Coker]] as the Panthers' 2025 riser. (5) [[Bo Nix]] as QB3 in October and what the Broncos still lack around him.
