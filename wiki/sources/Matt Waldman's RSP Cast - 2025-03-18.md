## Summary
Solo cast by [[Matt Waldman]] previewing the 23 tight ends featured in the 2025 pre-draft Rookie Scouting Portfolio, delivered alphabetically (B through T) as one or two notable observations per prospect rather than full scouting reports or rankings. Framing takes: 2025 is the third straight 'promising, not great' tight end class; outside 1.5 PPR tight-end-premium leagues, no tight end in the class belongs in the first five picks of a rookie draft, though the top five RSP talents are worth top-15 rookie picks and the next four the bottom third of the top 45 in 1.5 PPR. Waldman argues the tight end prototype (Rob Gronkowski, George Kittle) is close to a myth — receiver-first tight ends have the most sustainable careers — and that the biggest risk to this class is teams demanding prototype usage and grinding five of the seven fantasy-relevant prospects to dust. He references a past [[Adam Harstad]] (not a tracked expert) conversation on that theory. He also uses the class to argue against 40-yard-dash-driven evaluation ([[Harold Fannin Jr.]] vs [[Sam LaPorta]] workout tiers and GPS speed data) and against linear athletes at the position.
