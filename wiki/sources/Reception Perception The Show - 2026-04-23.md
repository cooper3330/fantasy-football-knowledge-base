## Summary
Recorded hours before the 2026 NFL Draft. Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) preview the final Reception Perception rookie roundup, which hit a record 31 charted wide receiver prospects this cycle. They dig into small-sample (three-game) charting profiles on four Group of Five/small-school WRs: [[Cyrus Allen]] (Cincinnati), [[Reggie Virgil]] (Texas Tech, Miami of Ohio transfer), Jeff Caldwell (Cincinnati, Allen's teammate) and [[Chase Roberts]] (BYU). They also spend a long stretch on a broader trend: an increasing number of prospects who play almost exclusively on one side of the formation ('skewed alignment' receivers) coming out of stripped-down, one-read college spread systems (Big 12/SEC 'Art Briles coaching tree' offenses), and what that means for NFL translation. Closing segment covers combine/pro-day testing culture -- the near-disappearance of the three-cone and shuttle drills, and a riff on paying prospects to test.
