## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) react to Week 9. Topics: the Jayden Daniels elbow injury while playing down 31 on Sunday Night Football and whether Washington should have pulled him; [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]]'s league-leading receiving production and his year-three 'graduation'; [[Sam Darnold]] (21-of-24, 330 yards, 4 TD) and Klint Kubiak elevating each other alongside JSN; [[Tory Horton]]'s two-touchdown breakout in Cooper Kupp's absence and Harmon's charting of the game; [[Cooper Kupp]]'s diminished on-field role versus his value as a mentor and run-game blocker; the Jacksonville receiver room after [[Travis Hunter]]'s non-contact knee injury/IR and [[Brian Thomas Jr.]]'s possible high ankle sprain, with [[Parker Washington]] picking up slack; [[Drake London]]'s 14-target, 118-yard, 3-TD game and the correlation between his target volume and [[Michael Penix Jr.]]'s efficiency; [[Kyle Pitts]] and [[Darnell Mooney]] as role players in Atlanta; [[Demario Douglas]]'s first career 100-yard game despite a collapsed snap share; [[Kayshon Boutte]]'s hamstring sprain opening the door for rookie [[Kyle Williams]]; and [[Rome Odunze]]'s zero-catch game in a 600-yard Bears offense against a Bengals defense that gets beaten by tight ends, backs and slot receivers.
