## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) open with two free-agency landings: [[Russell Wilson]] signing a one-year deal with the Giants (10.5M guaranteed, escalators Harmon and Koh dismiss as 'funny money') and [[Stefon Diggs]] signing a three-year deal worth up to 69M with New England (26M guaranteed). Harmon argues the Wilson signing caps [[Malik Nabers]] because Wilson's short-middle passing has cratered, while Nabers' best charted routes are digs and slants; Koh mourns the loss of the Jameis Winston 'pump and dump' scenario and reads the Giants' quarterback-draft talk as gamesmanship to inflate trade-up value for [[Shedeur Sanders]].

The second half is a guest segment with Cory Kinnan, Reception Perception's quarterback charter (NOT a tracked expert), walking through his charting profiles on [[Jalen Milroe]], [[Cam Ward]], [[Shedeur Sanders]] and [[Jackson Dart]], with deep/intermediate/middle-of-field success rates for each and comps to Josh Allen, Jalen Hurts, Jared Goff, Geno Smith and Joe Burrow.
