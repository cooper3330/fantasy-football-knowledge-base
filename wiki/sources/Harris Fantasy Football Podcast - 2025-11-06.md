## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts a Thursday Week 10 episode built around flex decisions in the wake of the NFL trade deadline, with two guests, neither of whom is a tracked expert. Ben Gretch of the Stealing Signals newsletter (not tracked) brings five flex-adjacent names — [[Devin Singletary]], [[Jakobi Meyers]], [[TreVeyon Henderson]], [[Rashid Shaheed]] and [[Emari Demercado]] — and he and Harris then preview the Thursday night Raiders-Broncos game, covering [[Ashton Jeanty]], [[Brock Bowers]] and [[Troy Franklin]]. Jake Trowbridge (not tracked) joins for the weekly DraftKings DFS segment, where the two build lineups and argue over chalk, tinking and the cardinal rule against paying up for a defense.

Recurring themes: whether a receiver traded at the deadline can hit the ground running (Shaheed's scheme continuity under Klint Kubiak vs Meyers learning a new Jacksonville offense), the irrelevance of the slot/outside label in the modern NFL given motion, and the danger of drawing conclusions from a single blown-up run.
