## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) continue the second-year receiver series, working through the rookie-season Reception Perception profiles of three 2024 draftees: [[Ladd McConkey]] (crushed it), [[Xavier Legette]] (a disappointment) and [[Ricky Pearsall]] (incomplete but interesting).

Harmon says however high you are on McConkey you are not high enough — a 98th-percentile 84.4% success rate vs press, 75.8% vs man (best of the rookies charted so far), 81.8% vs zone, a nearly all-green route tree, and outside yards per route run (2.85) higher than his slot number (2.24), which rebuts the slot-only label. His only caution is contact absorption; he repeats his [[Tyler Lockett]] comp and says McConkey has the higher ceiling.

On Legette, the diagnosis is deployment plus hands: his role flipped from off-the-line/flanker/backfield usage before the week 10 bye to 63.5% on the line at X after the [[Diontae Johnson]] trade, and the press and man numbers fell with it. Contested-catch rate collapsed from 81.8% as a prospect to 60% with a 13.5% drop rate. Harmon wants [[Tetairoa McMillan]] to absorb the X job and Legette scheme-shielded from press, the way Kyle Shanahan built around [[Deebo Samuel]].

On Pearsall, the season splits are the story: 57.1% vs press in the first four games charted, 75% across the full sample, with the final four games driving nearly every gain. Harmon flags his outbreaking/option-route usage as [[Cooper Kupp]]-like and something the 49ers have never had, and both hosts call him a round-11 redraft bargain with [[Brandon Aiyuk]] rehabbing.
