## Summary
A guest-hosted episode: [[Matt Harmon]] was travelling and did not appear. James Koh (co-host, NOT a tracked expert) ran the show solo with two guests, neither of whom is a tracked expert.

Segment 1 — Ted Nguyen (senior writer, The Athletic; ASR rendered him 'Ted Wynn'), a film analyst with a coaching background, on three offenses: Minnesota under Kevin O'Connell with [[J.J. McCarthy]] replacing Sam Darnold (expects a more run-oriented, play-action/bootleg 'training wheels' offense in the Shanahan-McVay mold, fewer deep shots); the Las Vegas receiver room (a slot logjam behind [[Jakobi Meyers]], [[Brock Bowers]] and [[Ashton Jeanty]], with speed needed outside from [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]] and [[Tre Tucker]], and [[Jack Bech]] squeezed out as a rookie); and the injury-ravaged San Francisco room ([[Ricky Pearsall]] rising, [[Jauan Jennings]] absent, [[Brandon Aiyuk]] jogging).

Segment 2 — Dr. Deepak Chona (orthopedic sports surgeon, SportsMedAnalytics; ASR rendered him 'Deepak Jonah'), an injury analyst, walked through his model's projections for [[Matthew Stafford]], [[Brandon Aiyuk]], [[Christian McCaffrey]], [[Chris Godwin]], [[Malik Nabers]], [[De'Von Achane]] and [[Stefon Diggs]], including ramp-up windows and percentage-of-pre-injury-production estimates.
