## Summary
Matt Harmon and James Koh break down two new 2023 Reception Perception profiles: [[Jaylen Waddle]] and [[Demario Douglas]]. Harmon calls Waddle's 2023 profile his best yet despite a down statistical season, driven by major growth versus press coverage and confirmation that Waddle -- not [[Tyreek Hill]] -- plays the harder boundary/X role in Miami. They debate whether Waddle has ascended to true WR1 tier (Diggs/Ayuk-level) or remains a tier below. They then chart Douglas, a rookie slot/motion piece with an unusually inverted man/zone split, and discuss his murky 2024 role behind incoming rookies [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] and Javon Baker plus veterans Kendrick Bourne and JuJu Smith-Schuster. Koh (untracked co-host) offers his own scouting view that Polk projects as the more traditional line/boundary complement to Douglas and could lead the Patriots in targets as a rookie.
