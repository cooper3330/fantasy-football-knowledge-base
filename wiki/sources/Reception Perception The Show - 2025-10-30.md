## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) open by breaking down DeVonta Smith's under-the-radar breakout with A.J. Brown out (hamstring), crediting Eagles scheme changes (more under-center play action, a sixth-lineman run-game wrinkle replacing blocking-liability Grant Calcaterra) for unlocking both Saquon Barkley and Smith. They discuss Jahan Dotson's low-volume-but-splashy role. The bulk of the show is a speculative, self-described 'for fun' trade-deadline exercise centered on A.J. Brown: a hypothetical AJ Brown-for-Brian Thomas Jr. swap (Harmon says Jacksonville says no, breaks down BTJ's alligator-arming issue on in-breaking routes and cost-control value), then a tour of hypothetical destinations -- Raiders, Falcons (with detailed Drake London target-share/Michael Penix Jr. and Darnell Mooney context), Patriots (Stefon Diggs and Kayshon Boutte usage breakdown), Titans, and Bills (Keon Coleman critique). They also cover the Vikings QB chaos (Carson Wentz injury, J.J. McCarthy, Kirk Cousins/Aaron Rodgers speculation), then run FanDuel player props on Rashee Rice, Khalil Shakir, Zay Flowers and an under on Justin Jefferson, closing with a Troy Franklin/Pat Bryant/Courtland Sutton zone-vs-man breakdown for Denver's receiver room.
