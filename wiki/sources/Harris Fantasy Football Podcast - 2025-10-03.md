## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a film-style review of the Thursday night 49ers-Rams overtime game, arguing the Rams' two-high zone plan was the real story: 42 of Mac Jones's 49 attempts came against two safeties high, including all six in overtime, and 38 of 49 attempts / 28 of 33 completions travelled 10 air yards or fewer. He then runs a Week 5 injury rundown, and is joined by his cousin Josh Van Blank (NOT a tracked expert) for the recurring 'five players to trade away' segment, counted down 5-to-1 by each host and benchmarked against FantasyPros rest-of-season ranks. Both hosts push the same underlying thesis: running back is the only genuinely scarce position, so quarterbacks, tight ends and even hot receivers should be cashed in for backs.
