## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) run a Week 12 'temperature check' on three passing games that have evolved during the 2024 season — Cleveland, the Rams and Denver — then work through six non-injury disappointing receiver seasons and rank them by disappointment relative to expectation.

Cleveland: [[Jameis Winston]] under center has made all three Browns receivers relevant, which Harmon calls a scam he is happy to take (comping it to how Winston fueled [[Chris Godwin]]'s 2019 breakout). [[Cedric Tillman]] looks like a real X with a [[Courtland Sutton]] comparison; [[Elijah Moore]] is being let loose downfield; [[Jerry Jeudy]] is producing despite Harmon being lower than consensus on him as a route runner, and Koh admits Jeudy is a personal blind spot.

Rams: Harmon sees another level jump from [[Puka Nacua]] — ~68% out wide / ~28% slot, the intentional first read on concepts via motion usage that used to belong to [[Cooper Kupp]], and visible improvement on catching the ball away from his frame after Nacua told Harmon he wanted to fix his drops.

Denver: [[Bo Nix]] praised for command of the offense and week-over-week growth after a poor September; [[Courtland Sutton]] has 30% of targets since Week 8 and fits Sean Payton's X role on in-breakers; [[Devaughn Vele]] (90 routes since Week 8) intrigues Harmon; [[Marvin Mims Jr.]] has been converted into a gadget piece; [[Troy Franklin]] is on the field but shows little chemistry with Nix.

Disappointments, ranked least to most: [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Calvin Ridley]], [[Jordan Addison]], [[Tank Dell]], [[Jaylen Waddle]], [[DJ Moore]].
