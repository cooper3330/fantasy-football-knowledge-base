## Summary
A *Film and Theory* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Adam Harstad (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert), recorded ahead of Week 3 of the 2024 season.

Three segments:

1. **Fran Tarkenton vs. Patrick Mahomes.** Waldman reacted to an article quoting the 85-year-old Tarkenton saying Mahomes does nothing he could not do, and to the mockery it drew online. Waldman rewatched Tarkenton film and defended him; Harstad delivered an extended historical case (longest-held all-time passing yardage record, dead-ball-era 1970s context, league-average adjusted yards per attempt as a 21-year-old rookie on an expansion team, all-time record for sack yardage lost). Both agreed the real gap is structure: Harstad said Mahomes can run an offense in structure and bail out of it, while Tarkenton essentially only played off-script. Comps offered for Tarkenton: [[Kyler Murray]], early-career John Elway, Russell Wilson. Harstad places Tarkenton in the bottom half of his all-time top 10 (previously top five). Waldman noted his own earlier [[Anthony Richardson]]/Otto Graham comp.

2. **Harstad's *Dynasty in Theory* model — the value of one fantasy week.** Harstad had finally modeled what he previously estimated by reasoning from first principles: one guaranteed regular-season win raises title odds from roughly 8% to about 10% in a 12-team league, at the high end of his earlier 1-2% estimate, mostly through bye odds. Practical use: games missed early cost far less than 25% of value even if they are 25% of games, while missing the final four weeks costs much more; and how good your team already is changes the math (a 90%-win-rate team barely cares, a 30% team can be knocked out by one absence). He also referenced the draft reach-vs-fall finding: reaches underperform their pick, falls do not signal anything.

3. **Taking a swing vs. rostering a 'meh' player.** Anchored on [[Skylar Thompson]], thrust into the Dolphins' starting job after the Tua Tagovailoa injury, versus high-floor/low-ceiling alternatives — Andy Dalton, the non-[[Samaje Perine]] Chiefs backs, Rams receivers. Also covered [[Isiah Pacheco]], [[Carson Steele]], [[Bryce Young]], [[Malik Willis]] and the [[Caleb Williams]]/C.J. Stroud postgame discourse.
