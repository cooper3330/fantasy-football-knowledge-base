# Marquise Brown to KC, Jerry Jeudy Charting & New Falcons WR Room

Weekly free-agency reaction show, [[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not
a tracked expert). Covers a busy week of NFL transactions filtered for
fantasy/receiver relevance, plus a first look ahead to the 2024 draft class.

## Covered

- **[[Marquise Brown]] signs with Kansas City** (1yr, $7M, up to $11M) — full
  discussion of his zone-beating strength, declining man-coverage numbers by
  year, and projected flanker/slot role fit for the Chiefs' offense.
- **[[Jerry Jeudy]] Reception Perception profile officially drops** —
  confirms the man/zone charting numbers previewed on the 2024-03-14 episode
  and expands on the flanker-vs-slot debate, Cleveland's "distressed asset"
  receiver-room strategy, and route-running technique critique.
- **Cleveland Browns WR/TE room** — [[Elijah Moore]], [[Cedric Tillman]],
  [[Amari Cooper]], [[David Njoku]] and [[Deshaun Watson]] all discussed as
  pieces of an unsettled receiver room riding on Watson's play.
- **Minnesota Vikings trade** 42nd pick + 2025 2nd-rounder to Houston for the
  23rd overall pick — general discussion of a likely further trade-up for a
  rookie QB; hosts explicitly dismiss Justin Jefferson-to-Cincinnati trade
  rumors as unserious offseason noise, not evaluated as a real scenario.
- **[[Desmond Ridder]] traded to Arizona for [[Rondale Moore]]** — straight
  player-for-player swap after Atlanta signed Kirk Cousins.
- **Atlanta Falcons WR room rebuild** — [[Drake London]]'s route-tree
  usage, [[Darnell Mooney]]'s signing and projected role, and [[Rondale
  Moore]]'s gadget-player evaluation, plus the case Atlanta still needs
  another true X-receiver.
- **2024 WR draft class preview** — Reception Perception charting to begin
  dropping March 25th starting with [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Malik
  Nabers]] and [[Rome Odunze]]; Harmon calls this class "so stacked."

## Not given pages (passing mentions only)

- **Justin Jefferson** — trade rumors dismissed as unserious March noise; a
  Vikings extension is expected but not detailed.
- **Jordan Addison, T.J. Hockenson, Brian Flores** — named as pieces already
  in place for Minnesota's rookie-QB timeline, no evaluative detail.
- **Sam Darnold, J.J. McCarthy** — floated only as part of Minnesota QB
  speculation (Darnold as bridge starter, McCarthy as trade-up target), no
  evaluative claim made.
- **Bijan Robinson** (ASR: "B. John Robinson") — one-line mention as a
  "special player out of the backfield" who will draw targets.
- **Kyle Pitts** — brief note that he can move around the formation and
  could become more of a full-time pass-catcher if Atlanta adds another
  tight end.
- **Nick Chubb** — mentioned only as a run-game piece Cleveland lost.
- **Ray-Ray McCloud** (ASR: "Ray McLeod") — one-clause mention as another
  "gadgety, speedy" Falcons addition; no further evaluation.
- **Kirk Cousins** — mentioned only as context for why Desmond Ridder had to
  be traded.
- Quarterback trade carousel banter (Kenny Pickett, Justin Fields, Mac
  Jones, Sam Howell, Drew Lock) — general news roundup, no player-evaluation
  content.
