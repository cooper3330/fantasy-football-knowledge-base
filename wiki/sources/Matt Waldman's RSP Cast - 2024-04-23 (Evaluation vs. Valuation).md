## Summary
Matt Waldman hosts NFL.com draft analyst Chad Reuter (guest, not a tracked expert) days before the 2024 NFL Draft. First half is a framework discussion distinguishing scouting evaluation from draft valuation, using the Jalen Reagor/Justin Jefferson hindsight trap, the Patriots' history of reaching above consensus tiers, and the 2023 Lions' Jahmyr Gibbs/Jack Campbell picks as examples; also covers overvaluing coachability (citing Kyle Shanahan on reduced in-season teaching) and combine-number skepticism (Kingsley Suamataia's footwork, Chop Robinson's sack total). Second half runs through Waldman's pre-draft favorites and risk cases: Ben Sinnott, Brock Bowers, Byron Murphy, Blake Corum, Cooper Beebe, Dadrian Taylor-Demerson, Olu Fashanu, Chop Robinson, Michael Penix Jr., Adonai Mitchell and Kingsley Suamataia, plus a QB-backup-as-template theory touching Drake Maye, J.J. McCarthy, Bo Nix, Zach Wilson, Gardner Minshew, Aidan O'Connell and the Giants' QB situation.
