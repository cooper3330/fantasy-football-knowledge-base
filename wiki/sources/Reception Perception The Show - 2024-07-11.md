## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) break down all four AFC South wide receiver rooms using Reception Perception charting data, ranking them Houston > Indianapolis > Tennessee > Jacksonville. They cover Jacksonville's Brian Thomas Jr./Gabe Davis/Christian Kirk trio around Trevor Lawrence, Tennessee's Calvin Ridley/DeAndre Hopkins/Tyler Boyd (plus a skeptical read on Treylon Burks), Houston's Nico Collins/Tank Dell/Stefon Diggs group (with an extended discussion of Diggs' age-cliff risk), and Indianapolis' Michael Pittman Jr./Josh Downs/Adonai Mitchell/Alec Pierce room around Anthony Richardson.
