## Summary
Recorded the day before the 2024 NFL Draft, Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) run a superlatives exercise across the 2024 WR class, grading prospects on Harmon's Reception Perception charting data (success rate vs. man/zone/press, contested-catch rate, alignment splits) alongside film. Categories covered: most likely to exceed draft expectations (Jermaine Burton), most likely to disappoint (Xavier Legette, with Adonai Mitchell discussed and passed over), best slot-only receiver (Malik Washington), best lunch-pail receiver (Ja'Lynn Polk), deep-ball specialist (Troy Franklin), best contested-catch winner (Rome Odunze, plus Xavier Legette/Keon Coleman/Javon Baker as the next tier), most intriguing day-three sleeper (Johnny Wilson and Ainias Smith), and best overall route runner both including the 'big three' (Marvin Harrison Jr.) and excluding them (Ricky Pearsall over Ladd McConkey). Also discussed an Adam Schefter/ESPN stat on first-round WR bust rates (27% hit rate via second-contract signings) and Harmon's contested-target charting methodology.
