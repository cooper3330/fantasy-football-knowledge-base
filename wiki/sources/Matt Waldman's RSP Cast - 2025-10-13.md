## Summary
Week 6 reaction episode of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]). The pair worked through a rapid-fire list of Week 6 developments and voted 'feel it' or 'fuck it' on each: [[Drake Maye]]'s leap, [[Kayshon Boutte]]'s two-touchdown day, [[Rico Dowdle]]'s 239 scrimmage yards against his former team, [[Kimani Vidal]]'s breakout versus a bad Miami defense, [[Rachaad White]] being underrated, [[Ladd McConkey]]'s bounce-back, [[Xavier Legette]] as a bust, [[Demario Douglas]] as a real play, [[Spencer Rattler]]'s promise, [[Matthew Golden]]'s readiness, [[Justin Fields]]' historically bad passing game, [[Tucker Kraft]] and [[Romeo Doubs]] as weekly starters, [[Cam Skattebo]] as a league winner, [[Jackson Dart]] being good, and [[Quinshon Judkins]] not being an RB1 this year. Waldman also railed against Sunday-morning insider 'splash reports' after one moved him off his own [[Kimani Vidal]] hunch.
