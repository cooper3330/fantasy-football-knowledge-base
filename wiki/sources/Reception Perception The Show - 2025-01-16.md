## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) previewed the four 2024-season divisional round games. The opening segment was an extended post-mortem on [[Diontae Johnson]] being cut by Houston after two games — his fourth team in a calendar year — and what it does to his 2025 free agency, which Harmon framed against a weak WR free-agent class ([[Tee Higgins]], [[DeAndre Hopkins]], [[Amari Cooper]], [[Davante Adams]], [[Marquise Brown]], [[Keenan Allen]], [[Elijah Moore]], [[Chris Godwin]], [[Brandon Cooks]], [[Mike Williams]], [[Stefon Diggs]]).

Game breakdowns: Houston at Kansas City (Harmon on the Chiefs' rebuilt interior OL and [[Nico Collins]] as the only reason Houston's offense functions), Baltimore at Buffalo (Buffalo's committee pass-catching corps, [[Derrick Henry]] against a Bills run defense that gave up 199 yards to him earlier in the year), Washington at Detroit ([[Jahmyr Gibbs]], [[Terry McLaurin]] shadow coverage, [[Jayden Daniels]] and scar tissue), and the Rams at Philadelphia (Rams' surprise cover-1 usage, [[Matthew Stafford]] versus the blitz, [[Cooper Kupp]]'s decline).

A Discord question drove a long segment on whether you would rather have one alpha receiver or a committee of three or four good pass catchers — see [[Alpha Receiver vs Committee Pass Catchers]]. Harmon closed with player props.
