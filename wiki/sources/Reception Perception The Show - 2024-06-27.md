## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) run a 'choose your fighter' exercise, pairing 2023 Reception Perception success-rate data for four current WRs against historical charted profiles from Harmon's database, debating which player they would rather roster based on route-success rates vs. man/zone/press and after-catch data. Comps discussed: [[Nico Collins]] (2023) vs. [[Brandon Aiyuk]] (2022); [[Zay Flowers]] (2023) vs. 2015 Eric Decker (retired, untracked historical figure); [[Puka Nacua]] (2023) vs. 2016 Emmanuel Sanders (retired, untracked historical figure); [[George Pickens]] (2023) vs. 2015 DeSean Jackson (retired, untracked historical figure).
