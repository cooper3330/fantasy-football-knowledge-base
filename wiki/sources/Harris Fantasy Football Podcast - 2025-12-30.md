## Summary
Season wrap-up (Week 17 recap, fantasy finals results) followed by the show's annual 'players we love on film' countdown with guest Chris Raybon (Action Network, not a tracked expert). Both Harris and Raybon each named five players purely on film evaluation (not necessarily fantasy projection): Raybon had Parker Washington, Harold Fannin Jr., Tucker Kraft, Jackson Dart and (tied at No. 1) Luther Burden III; Harris had Chase Brown, Jayden Higgins, J.K. Dobbins and also Luther Burden III at No. 1, with Tory Horton and Marvin Mims Jr. mentioned as 'just missed'/callback picks. The two also directly compared Luther Burden III to Rome Odunze for 2026 rankings, preferring Burden.
