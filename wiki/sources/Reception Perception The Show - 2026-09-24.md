## Summary
Matt Harmon (tracked) and co-host James Koh (untracked) run through Week 3 injury fallout and trade speculation. Indianapolis: Alec Pierce placed on IR (heel/ankle), elevating Darius Slayton as the 'sacrificial X' and cementing Josh Downs's huge role expansion (93% routes/86% snaps); Tyler Warren and Keenan Allen also discussed. New York Giants: Jackson Dart out for the season (multi-ligament knee), Malik Nabers playing through a long-running shoulder injury, Jameis Winston looking poor in relief, Malachi Fields praised as a blocker/complementary piece. Rams: Puka Nacua's possible sports hernia sparks a long trade-candidate discussion (Terry McLaurin, Calvin Ridley, Brian Thomas Jr., Marvin Harrison Jr., Jerry Jeudy) alongside praise for Davante Adams's Week 2 outburst as a route-running case study. Green Bay: Jayden Reed done for the season, Christian Watson/Matthew Golden discussed as the offense's best remaining pieces amid thin WR depth. Matchup previews cover Garrett Wilson/Adonai Mitchell vs. Detroit, Rashee Rice/Xavier Worthy/Travis Kelce vs. Miami, Carnell Tate/Cam Ward vs. the Giants, Terry McLaurin vs. Seattle, Jaxon Smith-Njigba entering the 'best receiver in football' conversation, and Ja'Marr Chase/Tee Higgins vs. a strong Pittsburgh pass defense.
