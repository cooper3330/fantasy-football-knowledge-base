## Summary
Week 11 recap edition of the 'Feel It or F--k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Bob Harris poses the prompts and supplies the fantasy-points context; Waldman gives the film reads.

Run of play: the Chargers-Bengals Sunday nighter ([[Ladd McConkey]], [[Quentin Johnston]], [[J.K. Dobbins]], [[Joe Burrow]], [[Tee Higgins]]); then a young-quarterback block — [[Anthony Richardson]] post-benching, [[Caleb Williams]] under Thomas Brown after the Shane Waldron firing (with [[D.J. Moore]]'s route-concept inconsistency), [[Bo Nix]] as the class's best rookie QB and why the industry missed, [[Jayden Daniels]]'s decline in the splits, [[Michael Penix Jr.]] as a dynasty buy-low, and [[Tommy DeVito]] over [[Drew Lock]] in New York. Closing block: [[Saquon Barkley]] versus his Penn State self, [[Christopher Brooks]] versus [[Marshawn Lloyd]] and draft-capital bias in Green Bay, [[Desmond Ridder]], [[Matthew Stafford]] as a Mahomes prequel, and [[Aaron Rodgers]].

Concepts touched: [[Quarterback Game Management and Bandwidth]], [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]], [[NFL Combine and Pro Day Skepticism]], [[Quarterback Processing and Confidence]], [[Dynasty]].
