## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) open with a long segment on [[Ja'Marr Chase]]'s triple-crown pace, arguing his career-high slot rate and expanded route tree — not just Cincinnati's bad defense — are behind the leap. Harmon frames it against a league-wide down year for elite receiver production: only two receivers had 1,000 yards through 14 weeks versus 11 last year. The second block dissects the Philadelphia offense — [[A.J. Brown]]'s career-best efficiency on collapsed volume, [[Jalen Hurts]] as a hyper-specific thrower, [[Saquon Barkley]] masking design problems, and Koh's argument that the running-back-doesn't-matter case has gone too far.

Guest Patrick Peterson (former NFL CB, not a tracked expert) joins for a cornerback master class: how divisions dictate offseason coverage-roster building, the [[Nico Collins]] vs Jalen Ramsey size matchup, Ramsey's technique slippage against [[Garrett Wilson]], the Detroit secondary under Aaron Glenn, and the moment in year nine — facing a rookie [[DK Metcalf]] — when he knew he had to become a technician. Harmon closes on why man-heavy defenses are the best player-isolating charting environments.
