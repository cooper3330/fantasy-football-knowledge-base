## Summary
A 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-reaction episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Bob Harris reads out current Best Ball ADPs and Waldman gives a feel-it / fuck-it verdict on each.

Ground covered: the rebuilt Chicago offense under new play caller Ben Johnson ([[Caleb Williams]] at QB8, [[D.J. Moore]] at WR18, the [[DeAndre Swift]] / [[Kyle Monangai]] / [[Roschon Johnson]] backfield); [[Christian McCaffrey]] back at RB5 off a four-game season; the [[Brock Purdy]] contract and the $50M quarterback club; [[Dak Prescott]] as QB18 and [[CeeDee Lamb]] in the first round; Minnesota's quarterback situation and [[Sam Howell]]; the Dallas backfield with [[Javonte Williams]] and [[Jaydon Blue]]; [[Kyle Pitts]] with [[Michael Penix Jr.]]; rookie evaluations of [[Kyle Williams]], [[Kaleb Johnson]], [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] and [[Damien Martinez]]; [[David Njoku]] at TE13; [[Shedeur Sanders]]'s odds of starting in Cleveland; [[Jordan Mason]] behind [[Aaron Jones]]; and a dynasty strategy segment on trading out of the final rounds of rookie drafts. Waldman also plugged the post-draft Rookie Scouting Portfolio.
