## Summary
Chris Harris opens noting Bijan Robinson signed a $51M-guaranteed deal, resolving the holdout watch, with Jahmyr Gibbs expected to follow. After listener emails, Harris walks through 2026 Football Almanac film-grade changes: Drake Maye's accuracy grade bumped to an A (QB4); running back speed/elusiveness grades for Jahmyr Gibbs, De'Von Achane and Christian McCaffrey held; Travis Etienne Jr.'s speed grade bumped after his move to New Orleans; Javonte Williams's power grade bumped to A-; Rome Odunze's elusiveness grade bumped to A- (with a Luther Burden III comparison and 'footgate' pushback); Jaxon Smith-Njigba's hands grade bumped to an A (Harris's WR2 overall); and Travis Kelce's grades landing better than Harris expected walking in (TE11). Guest Ross Tucker (Ross Tucker Football Podcast, CBS analyst, former NFL offensive lineman) then reviews six 2026 offensive lines with wide range of outcomes: Eagles, Chargers, Panthers, Ravens, Titans and Browns, tying line health/continuity to fantasy-relevant backs and quarterbacks.
