## Summary
After a coaching-carousel rundown (McDermott fired, Harbaugh to the Giants, Stefanski to the Falcons, McDaniel likely Chargers OC, Petzing to the Lions) and a lengthy snarkbag reading listener email, [[Chris Harris]] is joined by [[Matt Harmon]] of Reception Perception for their annual 'five level jumpers for 2026' list, revisiting last year's picks (Drake May, Rome Odunze, Tyrone Tracy, Tank Bigsby, Jalen Coker) for accuracy before naming new ones. Harris and Harmon go player by player through running backs, receivers, tight ends and a quarterback they each expect to take a step forward in 2026, frequently disagreeing on ceiling and usage.
