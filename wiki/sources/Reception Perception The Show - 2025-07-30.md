## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) preview the NFC South receiver rooms, plus housekeeping on a new batch of Reception Perception profiles (Tyreek Hill, [[D.J. Moore]], [[Terry McLaurin]], [[Calvin Ridley]], [[Jauan Jennings]], [[Jerry Jeudy]], [[Courtland Sutton]], [[Jaylen Waddle]], [[Nico Collins]], [[Chris Olave]]) and the site's first-ever running back charting of the top 12 incoming NFL backs, split by man/gap vs zone, inside vs outside, and pass protection.

Saints: an extended defense of [[Chris Olave]] as an underrated near-tier-two receiver despite the concussion-shortened 2024, plus concerns about the [[Tyler Shough]] fit on digs and posts, [[Rashid Shaheed]]'s camp, and a thin depth chart behind them. Panthers: [[Tetairoa McMillan]] as the potential unlock, [[Xavier Legette]], [[Jalen Coker]], [[Adam Thielen]] trade chatter and [[Hunter Renfrow]]'s comeback. Falcons: [[Drake London]] as a possible tier-one graduate off his weaponized slot usage, and the [[Darnell Mooney]] shoulder injury leaving no second answer. Buccaneers: [[Emeka Egbuka]] as a possible top rookie receiver with an [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] comp, alongside [[Mike Evans]], [[Chris Godwin]] and [[Jalen McMillan]].
