## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) reviewing Week 5 of the 2025 season. Segments: [[Travis Hunter]]'s most encouraging NFL game as a receiver on Monday night and the parallel plan to rebuild [[Brian Thomas Jr.]]'s confidence on perimeter routes; [[Deebo Samuel]]'s resurgence in Washington driven by a jump to 64% slot rate under Kliff Kingsbury; [[Jaylen Waddle]] as the focal point of Miami's passing game without Tyreek Hill; and a five-rookie snap-share segment covering [[Jayden Higgins]], [[Jaylin Noel]], [[Isaiah Bond]], [[Pat Bryant]], [[Jack Bech]] and [[Isaac TeSlaa]]. Harmon also made a broader argument that the [[Las Vegas Raiders]] misused Jack Bech by chasing a traditional size-speed X receiver, tying it to his shifting preference for the [[Power Slot Receiver Archetype]]. FanDuel pick segment: [[DeVonta Smith]] receiving yards over and [[Troy Franklin]] receptions over.
