## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] hosts the 'Feel It or F**k It' format with [[Matt Harmon]] filling in for regular co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]). The episode is a post-season review of the 2024 rookie wide receiver class, structured around listener questions and around a rookie yards-per-route-run model built by Adam Harstad of Football Guys (NOT a tracked expert), which scores rookies into tiers — superstar 118+, strong starter 108-117, then good bets, average, bad bets, terrible bets (below 93).

Model scores quoted on air: [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] 125.5, [[Ladd McConkey]] 124.1, [[Malik Nabers]] 120.9, [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] 110.4, [[Keon Coleman]] 105.2, [[Jalen Coker]] 102.5, with [[Ricky Pearsall]], [[Xavier Worthy]] and [[Devaughn Vele]] noted in the average band and [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] flagged as the worst rookie season in the model's history.

Main threads: whether the 2024 class can match 2014 (Harmon says no, Waldman says yes); which under-the-radar receivers emerge in 2025; contested-catch technique as a hard ceiling on elite outcomes; role misassignment in New England and Chicago; and a closing appreciation of [[Chris Godwin]] as one of the era's most versatile and underrated receivers.
