## Summary
Immediate post-2025-NFL-Draft reaction episode of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment, with [[Matt Waldman]] (tracked) and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Bob Harris pitches names and Waldman rules 'feel it' or 'f**k it' on each landing spot. Waldman notes the RSP post-draft edition ships that Saturday, with a preliminary top 55 already out and roughly 250 players headed to the cheat sheet.

Ground covered: San Francisco's late-round hauls (UDFA [[Isaiah Neyor]], fifth-rounder [[Jordan James]], seventh-round QB [[Kurtis Rourke]]), Kansas City's backfield and receiver adds ([[Brashard Smith]], [[Jalen Royals]], plus [[Rashee Rice]]'s recovery outlook), Chicago's offense under Ben Johnson ([[Luther Burden III]], [[Kyle Monangai]], [[D.J. Moore]]), Denver's [[RJ Harvey]], the Chargers' [[Omarion Hampton]] and [[Najee Harris]], Cleveland's [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] vs [[David Njoku]] and the [[Shedeur Sanders]] slide, New Orleans' [[Tyler Shough]], New England's [[Kyle Williams]], the Rams' [[Mario Anderson]], and free agents [[J.K. Dobbins]] and [[Nick Chubb]] as Bears fits.
