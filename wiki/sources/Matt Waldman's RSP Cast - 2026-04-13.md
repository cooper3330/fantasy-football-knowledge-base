## Summary
Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) runs Matt Waldman through a rapid-fire pre-draft 'Feel It or F**k It' segment covering Day 2/3 running backs, receivers, quarterbacks and tight ends ahead of the 2026 NFL Draft. Waldman gives quick verdicts with reasoning on DeMond Claiborne, Nicholas Singleton, Ja'Kobi Lane, Ted Hurst, Diego Pavia, Jonah Coleman, a Kansas running back named Sean Williams, Caleb Black, Cade Klubnik, Jamori Macklin, Camden Brown, Oscar Delt, Eli Raridon, Eli Stowers and Desmond Reed.
