## Summary
Matt Waldman and Going Deep co-host [[Brandon Angelo]] (tracked) break down 2024 rookie landing spots now that draft capital is known: how much weight to give draft slot by position (least at QB, most at RB), day-three gambles, favorite UDFAs, and overrated/underrated players by fit. Deep dives on Malik Washington, Audric Estime, Kimani Vidal, Cade Stover, Blake Watson, Jalen Coker, Dylan Laube, Dylan Johnson, Michael Penix Jr., Brian Thomas Jr., Ricky Pearsall, Roman Wilson, Malachi Corley, Bo Nix, Ja'Lynn Polk, Jermaine Burton, plus shorter infrastructure-focused hits on Marshawn Lloyd, Adonai Mitchell, Jonathan Brooks, Caleb Williams, Drake Maye, Miles Sanders, Javon Baker and Quentin Johnston.
