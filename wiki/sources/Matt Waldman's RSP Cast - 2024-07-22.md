## Summary
A training-camp-open edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (news analyst, NOT a tracked expert — and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Bob Harris frames each news item and asks Waldman whether he is 'feeling it'; Waldman gives the evaluation.

Topics: the [[Brandon Aiyuk]] trade request and why San Francisco is the best landing spot for him (with [[Deebo Samuel]] as the player they should actually move); the [[CeeDee Lamb]] holdout and buying his and Aiyuk's discounted shares while negotiations get loud; [[Brandin Cooks]] as a WR4/WR5 value; [[Joe Mixon]] and the Houston volume signal; the wide-open Denver backfield ([[Javonte Williams]] on the roster bubble, [[Jaleel McLaughlin]] as the buy, [[Audric Estime]], [[Blake Watson]]); [[Daniel Jones]] vs [[Drew Lock]]; [[Jordan Love]] as underpriced at QB10; [[Isaiah Likely]]'s two paths to value; [[Rasheen Ali]] behind Derrick Henry; [[Sam Darnold]] and whether [[Justin Jefferson]] is QB-proof; [[Rashid Shaheed]]'s extension; [[Marshawn Lloyd]]'s possible early role in Green Bay; and [[Clyde Edwards-Helaire]] as a [[Isiah Pacheco]] handcuff, which becomes a retrospective on process vs. production.
