## Summary
Matt Harmon (tracked) with co-host James Koh (not tracked) close out their division-preview series with the NFC North.

Green Bay: a deep but banged-up room (Jayden Reed in a boot, Dontayvion Wicks and Savion Williams dinged, Christian Watson likely PUP, Jordan Love with a non-throwing thumb injury). Harmon charts rookie Matthew Golden as a strong zone beater with average man/press marks and real drop history, projecting him as a very good number two who can function as Green Bay's de-facto number one, and calls him and Emeka Egbuka his two favorite ADP values. Harmon also pushes back on the fantasy community over-indexing on Green Bay's 32nd-ranked neutral pass rate.

Chicago: Harmon is bullish on Rome Odunze settling in as a full-time X after a scattered rookie alignment, citing an 87th-percentile press success rate and a weaker zone number. He details D.J. Moore's mismatched 2024 usage (Andre Johnson alignment, Kadarius Toney route tree) and warns against copy-pasting Ben Johnson's Detroit offense onto Caleb Williams. Luther Burden III is a slot-heavy project who needs to be pushed on details, with 12-personnel usage capping early snaps.

Minnesota: Justin Jefferson plus very little, with Jordan Addison suspended three games, Tai Felton an underneath/YAC dart throw and T.J. Hockenson a PPR target-volume candidate.

Detroit: Harmon just finished charting Amon-Ra St. Brown and walks through four years of steadily improving man/zone/press success rates plus his shift to majority outside alignment; he and Koh hammer the over on his 1,075.5-yard line. Jameson Williams is a good but volatile number two; rookie Isaac TeSlaa is a high-athleticism, low-production project pushing Tim Patrick for X snaps.
