## Summary
Matt Harmon (just back from the NFL Combine) and co-host James Koh (untracked) break down combine takeaways, then run a bottom-to-top AFC South preview (Titans, Colts, Texans, Jaguars). Combine section: praise for Deion Burks' testing (26 bench reps, 4.34 40), skepticism of Jordan Tyson's viral bench press reps, KC Concepcion's strong gauntlet performance and 196 lb weigh-in, Malachi Fields' disappointing 4.6 40, Makai Lemon's solid 192 lb measurement, and an extended defense of Carnell Tate's 4.53 40 using a data study of top-21 deep-ball receivers' 40 times. AFC South section covers Calvin Ridley's contract/roster fit in Tennessee alongside Elic Ayomanor and Chimere Dike; the Michael Pittman Jr. vs. Alec Pierce cut/tag debate plus Daniel Jones's Achilles recovery timeline in Indianapolis; the David Montgomery trade, Nico Collins, C.J. Stroud, Jayden Higgins and Jaylen Noel in Houston; and Brian Thomas Jr., Jakobi Meyers, Parker Washington and Travis Hunter in Jacksonville. Also announces Max Toscano joining Reception Perception as a dedicated tight end charter.
