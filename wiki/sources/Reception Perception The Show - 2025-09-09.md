## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recorded Monday afternoon of Week 1, before Monday Night Football, and played a game of 'fluke or for real' across six passing-game storylines. Harmon opened with a pep talk on week-to-week receiver volatility: the Week 1 half-PPR top five was Zay Flowers, Quentin Johnston, Emeka Egbuka, Keon Coleman and Deebo Samuel, while Ja'Marr Chase (26 yards), Nico Collins (25), Amon-Ra St. Brown (45), Malik Nabers (71), Drake London (55), Brian Thomas Jr. (11 receiving), A.J. Brown (8), Tee Higgins (33), Mike Evans (51) and Terry McLaurin (27) all disappointed. He noted last year's Week 1 top five was Jayden Reed, Cooper Kupp, Allen Lazard, Tyreek Hill and Jameson Williams as evidence that how it starts is not how it finishes.

Segments: the Chargers as a genuine pass-first team under Jim Harbaugh and Greg Roman; the Jets unlocking Justin Fields under new OC Tanner Engstrand and the resulting alignment overhaul for Garrett Wilson; A.J. Brown's hamstring and whether he reaches 1,100 yards; Keon Coleman's Week 1 charting versus his rookie profile; Houston's offensive line and Nico Collins; and Jacksonville, Brian Thomas Jr. and Trevor Lawrence's accuracy. Harmon brought fresh Reception Perception charting from the Week 1 games for Garrett Wilson, A.J. Brown and Keon Coleman.
