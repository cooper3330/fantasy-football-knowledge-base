## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh preview the AFC West's wide receiver and pass-catching rooms ahead of a separate free-agency reaction episode. They cover all four teams: Raiders (new OC Clint Kubiak/Andrew Janocko, rookie QB Fernando Mendoza, Brock Bowers, Tre Tucker, Jack Bech, Dont'e Thornton Jr.), Chiefs (Travis Kelce, Rashee Rice's off-field issues, Xavier Worthy, Jalen Royals, returning OC Eric Bieniemi, Mike Evans free-agency speculation), Chargers (new OC Mike McDaniel, Ladd McConkey's sophomore dip and 2026 outlook, Quentin Johnston's trade candidacy, Trey Harris, Oronde Gadsden II, Keenan Allen's departure), and Broncos (new playcalling OC Davis Webb, Courtland Sutton's age-31 outlook, Pat Bryant, Troy Franklin, Marvin Mims, RJ Harvey, Bo Nix).
