# Matt Waldman's RSP Cast — 2024-03-04

*Feel It or F**k It*, [[Matt Waldman]] with co-host **Bob Harris** (Football
Diehard — not a tracked expert). Two segments: NFL Combine fallout/skepticism,
and an extended sweep of 2024 free-agent and rookie-QB landing-spot fits ahead
of the March 13 legal tampering/signing period.

## Summary

- **Combine skepticism.** Both hosts endorsed [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] skipping
  all combine testing, and framed [[Caleb Williams]] withholding his medicals
  from non-drafting teams as rational leverage rather than evasion. See
  [[NFL Combine and Pro Day Skepticism]].
- **Xavier Worthy's record 40 time** — Waldman flagged a real ball-security/
  hand-technique flaw the speed obscures, projecting him as a speed-complement
  receiver rather than a true No. 1. See [[Xavier Worthy]].
- **RB combine speed methodology** — [[Ray Davis]], [[Trey Benson]], [[Kimani
  Vidal]] and Isaac Guerendo (not given a page — mentioned only as a workout-time
  surprise, no tape-based evaluation offered) used to walk through Waldman's
  process for handling an outlier 40 time. See [[NFL Combine and Pro Day
  Skepticism]].
- **QB draft/free-agency fit sweep** ahead of the legal tampering period:
  [[Caleb Williams]] to the Bears; [[Bo Nix]] and [[J.J. McCarthy]] as
  Washington/late-first options; the Patriots' rookie-QB fit called weak
  regardless of player due to a thin receiver room; [[Justin Fields]],
  [[Kirk Cousins]] and Baker Mayfield as Atlanta's veteran options;
  [[Michael Penix Jr.]] to Minnesota; Justin Fields, Russell Wilson (not given
  a page — only Bob Harris's personal, non-evaluative preference offered) and
  new page **Kenny Pickett** as Pittsburgh's options.
- **Mike Evans's new two-year, $52M Tampa Bay deal** — production outlook tied
  to who plays quarterback. See [[Mike Evans]].
- **Baker Mayfield / Michael Pittman Jr. free agency** — Mayfield likely
  staying in Tampa under new OC Liam Coen; new page **Michael Pittman Jr.**
  discussed as the top free-agent WR value, tied to [[Anthony Richardson]]'s
  development. See [[Baker Mayfield]], [[Michael Pittman Jr.]].
- **Scouting-bias aside** on the "next Patrick Mahomes" media label applied to
  Caleb Williams. See [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]].
