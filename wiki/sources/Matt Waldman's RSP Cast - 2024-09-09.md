## Summary
Week 1 reaction episode in the 'Feel It or F**k It' format, hosted by [[Matt Waldman]] with co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys / SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman wrote the prompts; Bob Harris read them and pushed back throughout. The framing throughline was anti-overreaction: Bob Harris cited Tom Brady's mid-2020 deep-ball 'slump' before finishing as the league's most efficient deep thrower, and both hosts closed on 'relax, it's week one' and divorcing emotion from process.

Segments covered: [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] as an overpriced redraft commodity and a bad team fit; a long [[Kyler Murray]] critique (pocket, target distribution, only one top-12 receiver in three fantasy-caliber seasons) with Bob Harris defending the 4,000/500 path; [[Anthony Richardson]] at 60 percent of his potential; the Packers receiver pecking order; [[Kirk Cousins]]'s job security and the [[Michael Penix Jr.]] timeline; [[Saquon Barkley]] and [[Joe Mixon]] as top-five backs; [[Alec Pierce]] as a must-add; [[Lamar Jackson]]'s offensive line; [[Isaiah Likely]] and whether [[Mark Andrews]] is washed; [[J.K. Dobbins]] vs [[Gus Edwards]]; [[Justin Fields]] keeping the Steelers job; week-one utilization data and Waldman's [[Zamir White]] loss; [[Keon Coleman]] vs [[Dalton Kincaid]]; [[Drew Lock]] as a preemptive add; [[Quentin Johnston]]'s usage; [[Chris Godwin]]; [[Baker Mayfield]]; [[Jayden Daniels]].
