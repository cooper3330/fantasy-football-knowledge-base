## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) run an August training-camp episode devoted to fringe and depth wide receivers — the 'gutters' of NFL receiver rooms — arguing that a team's WR3 often reshapes the entire offensive ecosystem.

Segments: DeAndre Hopkins' knee injury (out 4-6 weeks) and what it does to Tennessee's receiver alignment, with Calvin Ridley pushed toward X and Treylon Burks' limits; Will Levis' route-chart profile and the Ridley fit; the Giants' Darius Slayton roster bubble as Jalen Hyatt runs ahead of him, and what a field-stretcher does for Malik Nabers' deployment; Adonai Mitchell splitting first-team reps with Alec Pierce in Indianapolis and Harmon's 'sacrificial X' framing; Jonathan Mingo's camp buzz versus Xavier Legette's foot injury in Carolina; Johnny Wilson's emergence as a potential outside starter in Philadelphia; Andrei Iosivas as a Bengals camp standout and possible full-time slot player alongside Jermaine Burton; and Jalen Tolbert's third-year-leap case in a very thin Dallas receiver room.
