## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh review Week 2 of the 2026 NFL season. Topics: Denzel Boston's dominant rookie start for Cleveland (and a bad Tampa Bay defense inflating Deshaun Watson's best game since 2020); a rundown of other rookie WR route-participation shares; confusing Chicago Bears pass-catcher usage after a 59-point Week 1 crashed into a 3-point Week 2 (Caleb Williams hamstring injury, Colson Loveland's near-invisible usage, Rome Odunze's bounce-back to a full workload, Luther Burden III's concerning heavy-personnel snap share); the Raiders' clean, scheme-driven 2-0 start behind Kirk Cousins, Tre Tucker and Ashton Jeanty, with a note of caution on Brock Bowers's recurring lower-body injuries; and an extended diagnosis of the struggling Chargers offense (Justin Herbert's play-action/sack numbers, Ladd McConkey's rib injury and on/off-field EPA splits, Omarion Hampton's lack of explosive runs vs. Derrick Henry as a preseason comp).
