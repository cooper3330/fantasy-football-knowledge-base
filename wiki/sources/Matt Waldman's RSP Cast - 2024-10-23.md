## Summary
A Campus to Canton (C2C) edition of the RSP Cast with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Felix Sharpe (Campus2Canton; NOT a tracked expert). Four segments: Louisville true-freshman RB Isaac Brown, Michigan State true-freshman WR Nick Marsh, Georgia QB Carson Beck after his three-interception game against Texas, and a preview of Waldman's Footballguys Gut Check column on Anthony Richardson.

Recurring theme: legacy recruiting star ratings badly miss on production — Waldman and Sharpe cite Ashton Jeanty (three-star, RB80-something by services, RB16 on Campus2Canton's board), Quinshon Judkins and Aidan Chiles as low-rated prospects who became household names, and argue early breakout age is a real talent signal in positions with touch competition. Waldman also attacks film-based hand-wringing narratives about Richardson, arguing some critics are chasing clicks and lack a long-term development track record.
