## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recap Week 2 of the 2025 season, recorded Monday afternoon before the two Monday night games. They open on the quarterback carnage — [[Joe Burrow]]'s minimum three-month absence, [[Jayden Daniels]] missing a game, [[Justin Fields]] in concussion protocol, [[J.J. McCarthy]]'s ankle — then spend a long segment on the state of the Kansas City offense ([[Rashee Rice]]'s suspension, [[Xavier Worthy]]'s injury, [[Isaiah Pacheco]]'s lost athleticism, [[Brashard Smith]]'s unused speed, [[Patrick Mahomes]]'s lost aura).

The body of the show is a buy-or-sell game on Week 2 breakouts ([[Troy Franklin]], [[Hunter Renfrow]], [[Wan'Dale Robinson]], [[Elic Ayomanor]]) followed by a panic meter on struggling stars ([[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[A.J. Brown]], [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]). Harmon brings fresh Reception Perception charting from the Franklin and Thomas games plus Ayomanor's Week 1 chart, and revisits prospect-profile numbers throughout.
