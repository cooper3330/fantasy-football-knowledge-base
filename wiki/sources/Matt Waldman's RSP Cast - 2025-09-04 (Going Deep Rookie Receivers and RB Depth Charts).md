## Summary
A Week 1 eve edition of *Going Deep* with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked), recorded the day before the 2025 season opener. The pair worked through rookie receivers whose situations had shifted in camp and several running back depth charts they considered mispriced.

Main threads: (1) [[Tetairoa McMillan]] as an unprepared-for-alpha in Carolina — both hosts kept their pre-draft reservations about his release package and early-phase route creativity, with Waldman explicitly betting against Mike Clay's 1,100-yard projection; (2) [[Matthew Golden]] as the preferred rookie WR at cost in a chaotic Green Bay passing game hollowed out by injuries to [[Jayden Reed]] and [[Christian Watson]]; (3) [[Travis Hunter]] slotting into the Chris Godwin role under Liam Coen and being treated as one of the safer rookie receivers; (4) a long Chicago segment on [[Caleb Williams]], [[Ben Johnson]]'s development plan, [[Rome Odunze]] as the likely target-share winner, [[D.J. Moore]] recast as a Deebo-type, and [[Luther Burden III]] stuck behind [[Olamide Zaccheaus]] on an intentional on-ramp; (5) Pittsburgh's backfield after the [[Jaylen Warren]] extension, with [[Kaleb Johnson]] framed as a low-red-zone specialist who cannot yet handle an Aaron Rodgers operation; (6) Washington's backfield, where the hosts expect [[Austin Ekeler]] to lead work, [[Chris Rodriguez Jr.]] to take short yardage, and [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] to need injuries to justify his ADP.
