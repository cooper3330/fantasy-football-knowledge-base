## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) break down Week 9 of the 2024 season. They open with the scary [[Chris Olave]] concussion — his fourth known NFL concussion — and the Michael Thomas / [[Derek Carr]] 'hospital ball' spat, then cover the Saints firing Dennis Allen and the Raiders firing OC Luke Getsy. A long positive segment on [[Terry McLaurin]]'s career-best season with [[Jayden Daniels]]. The back half is a 'Real or Mirage' game covering [[DJ Moore]] and the dysfunction of the Chicago offense (with [[Caleb Williams]], [[Keenan Allen]] and [[Rome Odunze]]), [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]]'s 180-yard slot explosion, [[Quentin Johnston]]'s bounce-back year, and [[Cedric Tillman]]'s two-game breakout in Cleveland.
