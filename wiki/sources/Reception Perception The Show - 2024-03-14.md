# Free Agency Continued: Breaking Calvin Ridley News, Diontae, Russ, Jeudy & Gabe Davis

A frantic mid-free-agency reaction show, recorded Wednesday afternoon and
partly rewritten live when the Calvin Ridley news broke mid-taping. Matt
Harmon and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) work through the day's
biggest receiver-market moves.

**[[Diontae Johnson]]** traded from Pittsburgh to Carolina for only a Day 3
pick swap plus CB Dante Jackson — both hosts call it a fleecing given
Harmon's charted evidence of Johnson's elite, all-three-levels separation
ability. Frames [[George Pickens]] and Steelers WR unit as run-first around
[[Russell Wilson]] and [[Najee Harris]]/[[Jaylen Warren]]. Also touches
Pittsburgh's uneven wide-receiver draft history (Antonio Brown/Chase Claypool
both cited).

**[[Russell Wilson]]** signs a vet-minimum deal with Pittsburgh. Harmon is
skeptical it materially fixes the offense despite Wilson being a clear
upgrade over Kenny Pickett, citing his declining ability to see/work the
middle of the field (charted from [[Jerry Jeudy]]'s frustrated 2023 Denver
tape) and a Warren Sharp stat on plays thrown behind the line of scrimmage.

**[[Jerry Jeudy]]** traded to Cleveland for a 5th/6th-round pick swap. Harmon
reaffirms years of below-consensus skepticism with fresh zone-coverage
charting data, while noting real fit upside in Cleveland's play-action scheme
and flagging [[Amari Cooper]]/[[Elijah Moore]]/Jeudy as Cleveland's run of
cheap "distressed asset" WR trades — all caveated hard on [[Deshaun Watson]]'s
play.

**[[Calvin Ridley]]** signs 4yr/$92M with Tennessee (breaking mid-show) after
Jacksonville failed to retain him. Defended on 2023 charting data despite an
"out of market" price tag; good scheme fit next to [[DeAndre Hopkins]] and a
justifiable overpay given [[Will Levis]]'s rookie-contract window and a
barren receiver room ([[Treylon Burks]], Chris Moore). Knock-on 2024 Draft
note: Tennessee now unlikely to take a receiver at pick 7, loosely freeing up
[[Rome Odunze]]/[[Malik Nabers]] for other landing spots.

**[[Gabe Davis]]** signs 3yr/$39M with Jacksonville. Harmon confirms a poor
2023 charting grade and calls the pairing with [[Christian Kirk]] and TE Evan
Ingram (not tracked) one of the league's worst, most "hyper-specific" skill
groups — bottom-five caliber — despite the spend; Jacksonville now needs a
true WR1 in the draft.

**Market inefficiency discussion:** an extended, largely Koh-driven riff
("collusion-y") on the 2024 guard/TE market explosion (Robert Hunt, Gerald
Everett, Colby Parkinson) against continued running back devaluation, using
[[Darnell Mooney]]'s $39M Falcons deal (fully-guaranteed portion matching
[[Saquon Barkley]]'s) as the marquee example. See [[League Trend Cycles and
Market Inefficiency]].
