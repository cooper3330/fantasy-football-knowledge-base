## Summary
Chris Harris, joined by recurring untracked co-host 'Cousin Josh,' reveals his initial 2026 running back and tight end rankings (standard/half-PPR, pre-film, pre-Almanac). They work through Harris's full RB list from RB1 down through the mid-40s and his TE list from TE1 through roughly TE20+, with Josh pushing back or sanity-checking picks throughout. Recurring themes: skepticism of best-ball-driven ADP, the RB dead zone (picks ~23-30), a growing distrust of beat-reporter camp narratives, handcuff value rising across the board, and Harris's usual refusal to draft a top-five tight end early. Wide receivers and quarterbacks are reserved for the following week's episode.
