## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) do deep RP-charting breakdowns of two 2026 WR draft prospects: [[KC Concepcion]] (NC State/Texas A&M) and [[Jordan Tyson]] (Arizona State). Harmon calls Concepcion his personal favorite of the class and a likely tier-two prospect held back only by inconsistent catch technique, citing extensive RP success-rate data versus man and zone coverage plus elite return-game production. On Tyson, Harmon and Koh dig into major injury red flags (torn ACL/MCL/PCL in 2022, broken clavicle in 2025, lingering November 2025 hamstring injury that prevented all pre-draft testing), strong RP zone-coverage numbers offset by weak man/press numbers, short-arm concerns, and a debate over comps (Harmon: Amari Cooper; an unnamed evaluator/co-host floated Terry McLaurin, which Harmon rejects). Both segments cite JJ Zachariason (not tracked) as a bullish outside voice on Tyson. The episode closes with a brief, non-evaluative aside about Elijah Moore signing with the Eagles.
