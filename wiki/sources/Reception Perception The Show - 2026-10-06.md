## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) break down week 4 2026 wide receiver performances. Deep charting dives on [[Zay Flowers]] (alignment/efficiency breakout), [[Tetairoa McMillan]] (slot usage explosion, 41% target share), and [[Rome Odunze]] (bounce-back game, pushback on the 'sacrificial X' framing, fan comp to [[Luther Burden III]] vs. [[Brandon Aiyuk]]/[[Deebo Samuel]]). Both [[CeeDee Lamb]] (21 targets, 17 catches) and [[Nico Collins]] (7-for-8, 118 yards playing hurt) had huge games discussed together as a 'elite alpha receiver solves problems' thesis. [[Jaylin Noel]]'s continued underuse in Houston despite strong efficiency is a recurring frustration. [[Bryce Young]]'s processing improvement under new Panthers play-caller Brad Idzik is covered at length, referencing an offseason conversation with Jeff Bell (untracked colleague). Chiefs wide receiver room churn (Tyquan Thornton ankle injury, Rashee Rice hobbled) drives speculation on a [[Tyreek Hill]] reunion, a possible [[Jerry Jeudy]] trade (new WR coach Chad O'Shea's Cleveland ties cited), [[Chris Godwin]], and Odell Beckham Jr. [[Terry McLaurin]] is flagged for a possible multi-week hamstring injury. [[Kyle Monangai]] took over the Bears backfield after a [[DeAndre Swift]] fumble. [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]'s lone catch (a touchdown) and Adam Schefter trade chatter are briefly discussed.
