## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (*Going Deep*) run a Week 4/5 2025 tour of broken offenses and backfield splits. Segments: Tennessee's collapse around [[Cam Ward]] and Brian Callahan's lack of a drop plan; the Eagles' neutered offense under first-time play caller Kevin Patullo and what it means for [[A.J. Brown]], [[Saquon Barkley]] and [[Jalen Hurts]]; Cleveland's [[Dillon Gabriel]] start, with [[Isaiah Bond]], [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] and [[Quinshon Judkins]] as the reasons it might briefly work; Houston's [[Woody Marks]] / [[Nick Chubb]] tandem and [[C.J. Stroud]]'s processing; [[Jackson Dart]] and the [[Cam Skattebo]] enabling problem in New York; Jacksonville's mismatch between [[Trevor Lawrence]] and [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] plus the misuse of [[Travis Hunter]] and the case for [[Parker Washington]] and [[Brenton Strange]]; and [[Kenneth Gainwell]]'s big game against Minnesota versus [[Jaylen Warren]].
