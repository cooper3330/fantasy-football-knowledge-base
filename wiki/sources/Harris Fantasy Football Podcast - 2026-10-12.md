## Summary
Chris Harris runs a marathon Week 5 'Monday film review,' covering Jaguars-Eagles, Bears-Packers, Ravens-Falcons, Lions-Cardinals, Patriots-Raiders, and previewing the Bills-Rams Monday nighter, then brings on guest Sam Gold (not tracked, formerly of NFL Breakdowns) to break down Giants-Commanders, Broncos-Chargers, and 49ers-Seahawks. Heavy focus on game-flow observations, offensive-environment quality, and injury notes rather than rankings. Harris revisits his August skepticism on Ashton Jeanty and Jeremiah Love's ankle injuries and the Ravens' Mark Andrews-over-Isaiah Likely contract decision.
