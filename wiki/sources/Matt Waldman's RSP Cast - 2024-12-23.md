## Summary
A Week 16 'Feel It or F**k It' episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (co-host of the RSP Cast's 'Feel It or F**k It' segment; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]).

The first half is a long conversation about fantasy content creation: whether the barrier to entry is higher than ever, whether writing ability still matters, paying dues, niche vs broad audiences, and where AI fits. Bob Harris has been in the industry since 1993; Waldman started the Rookie Scouting Portfolio in summer 2006. Both argue writing remains foundational even for video creators, and Waldman says he currently finds AI most useful for scouting-report support but not as a replacement for his own writing.

The second half is rapid-fire player evaluation: [[Michael Penix Jr.]]'s first start, [[Kyle Pitts]]'s continued disappointment, [[Jaylen Warren]] out-touching [[Najee Harris]], [[Chuba Hubbard]]'s year-over-year technical improvement, [[Bryce Young]]'s pre-snap growth under Dave Canales, [[Saquon Barkley]]'s pursuit of the single-season rushing record, a [[Jonathan Taylor]] vs Barkley talent comparison, [[Sam Darnold]]'s future in Minnesota, [[Rhamondre Stevenson]]'s ceiling with a real offensive line, [[Caleb Williams]] and [[Keenan Allen]] under play caller Thomas Brown, and a sobering [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] take.
