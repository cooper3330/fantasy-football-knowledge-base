## Summary
Post-2024-NFL-Draft rapid reaction episode. Matt Waldman (tracked, host of the RSP Cast) and Bob Harris (untracked co-host of Feel It or F**k It) run through nearly every notable rookie landing spot from night one through day three, giving fantasy-relevant evaluations of the pick, scheme fit, and depth chart implications for each. Waldman also plugs the post-draft Rookie Scouting Portfolio release. A recurring theme is separating 'football evaluation' from 'fantasy value' when a landing spot is crowded or uncertain.
