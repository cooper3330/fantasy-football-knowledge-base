## Summary
A one-off free (non-paywalled) Wednesday episode ahead of Week 1, hosted by [[Chris Harris]] with regular Yacht Club co-host Andy ('Paywall Andy', not tracked). After a lengthy movie-draft update and promotion of Harris's new book 'Pound Foolish', the two run through roughly 20 players where their Week 1 ranks diverged from their season-long/draft ranks, explaining why (mostly injury uncertainty, unproven backfield/receiver competition, or a lack of trust in coach-speak).
