## Summary
Chris Harris recaps the Wednesday-night Week 1 opener (Seahawks 13, Patriots 10), covering Sam Darnold's hip injury and Drew Lock's uneven relief effort, Jaxon Smith-Njigba's game-winning fourth-down touchdown, and the Jadarian Price/George Holani/Emmanuel Wilson backfield split. On the Patriots side he blames the loss on Drake Maye's turnovers, covers Romeo Doubs's drop, A.J. Brown's ankle injury, Mack Hollins's role, Eli Raridon's touchdown and Rhamondre Stevenson's workload with TreVeyon Henderson out. He previews the Thursday Rams-49ers game from Australia (Puka Nacua, Christian McCaffrey tightness; George Kittle matchup; Brock Purdy, Deebo Samuel, De'Zhaun Stribling) and notes Brock Bowers needed a knee scope and is out longer than initially reported. The back half is a 'Flexual Healing' listener-question segment (J.K. Dobbins, DJ Moore, Jeremiah Love, Tyler Allgeier, Ashton Jeanty, Marshawn Lloyd, Rome Odunze, Alec Pierce, Keaton Mitchell, Kimani Vidal, Omarion Hampton) followed by a DraftKings DFS lineup-building segment with guest Andy Behrens (untracked, filling in for regular co-host Jake), which touches Trey McBride, Malik Willis, Chris Olave, Juwan Johnson, and others.
