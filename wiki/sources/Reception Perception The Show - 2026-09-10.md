## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh preview Week 1 matchups, focusing on wide receiver rooms and secondaries. Topics: Mike Evans/De'Zhaun Stribling vs. the rebuilt Rams secondary; Bills WRs/James Cook/Dalton Kincaid vs. Houston under new HC Joe Brady; a leaguewide 'haves and have-nots' gap on defense (Dallas singled out as a potential riser); Terry McLaurin/Stefon Diggs role split in Washington vs. Philadelphia; Alec Pierce/Josh Downs/Keenan Allen/Tyler Warren target-share modeling in Indianapolis vs. Jesse Minter's Baltimore defense; the Jaguars WR room (Brian Thomas Jr., Parker Washington, Jakobi Meyers) with a 159-snap efficiency breakdown; Darius Slayton's release by the Giants and its effect on Malik Nabers/Malachi Fields; landing-spot speculation for Slayton (Kansas City, Buffalo, Houston) tied to Patrick Mahomes skepticism; the Dolphins' rookie WR trio (Chris Bell, Caleb Douglas, Malik Washington); and predicted Week 1 overreactions on Chris Olave (positive) and Drake London (negative).
