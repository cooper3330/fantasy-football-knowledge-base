## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recap Week 17 of the 2024 season. Three segments: (1) a long autopsy of the Green Bay receiver room after the loss to Minnesota — Harmon's verdict is the Packers simply do not have a true No. 1 receiver, and Koh's counter is that the coaching staff has never given [[Jayden Reed]] the snap share to become one; (2) two players whose 2023 evaluations Harmon has revised upward — [[Marvin Mims Jr.]] on deployment change in Denver and [[Adam Thielen]] on a real jump in air yards per target in Carolina; (3) a 'good player or good stats on a bad team' lightning round on [[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[Jerry Jeudy]], [[Malik Nabers]] and [[Calvin Ridley]], plus a tangent on the Colts' organizational mediocrity and [[Anthony Richardson]]'s work-ethic questions.
