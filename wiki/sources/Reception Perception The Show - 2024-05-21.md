## Summary
Matt Harmon (with co-host James Koh, not tracked) returns from vacation to break down the year-two Reception Perception profiles for [[Tank Dell]] and [[Dontayvion Wicks]], the first two of four second-year drops on the site (Josh Downs and Quentin Johnston to follow in a later episode). Deep dive into route-by-route success rates versus man/zone/press coverage, combine measurables, and each player's fit/outlook within a crowded Houston and Green Bay receiver room, respectively. Harmon also previews that he is mid-charting on [[Nico Collins]]'s year-three profile and draws comparisons across all three Texans/Packers receivers, plus [[Jayden Reed]] and [[Christian Watson]] as roster context for Wicks.
