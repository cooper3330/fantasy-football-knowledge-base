## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh chart the year-two profiles of [[Josh Downs]] and [[Quentin Johnston]] using Reception Perception data. Downs' rookie profile is praised as elite versus man coverage and unusually strong at winning off-script/second-read targets despite Gardner Minshew at QB, with discussion of his fit alongside [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] and [[Anthony Richardson]] and Adonai Mitchell in Indianapolis. Johnston's rookie profile is described as one of the worst charted in RP history, with a near-all-red route tree; Harmon frames him as set up to fail as a rookie X receiver by the Chargers' prior regime and discusses his outlook under new HC Jim Harbaugh alongside Ladd McConkey, Josh Palmer, DJ Chark and Mike Williams.
