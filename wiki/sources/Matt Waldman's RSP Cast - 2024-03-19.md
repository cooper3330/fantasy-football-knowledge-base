# Feel It Or F@#k It: 3.19.24: An RSP Cast with Bob Harris and Matt Waldman

## Summary

[[Matt Waldman]] and co-host **Bob Harris** (*Feel It or F**k It* — not a
tracked expert) run a rapid-fire "feel it or f**k it" reaction to a full week
of NFL free-agency movement, then close with a short Aaron Donald retirement
tribute.

**Quarterbacks:** [[Justin Fields]] traded Chicago to Pittsburgh for a
conditional 2025 sixth-round pick — both hosts feel it, expecting either a
sit-and-develop year behind [[Russell Wilson]] or an "intermittent benching"
competition. [[Kenny Pickett]] dealt to Philadelphia as a locked-in backup to
Jalen Hurts. [[Kirk Cousins]] to Atlanta — cures the offense's surrounding
weaknesses per Waldman, boosting [[Drake London]], [[Kyle Pitts]] and
[[Bijan Robinson]]; Waldman flags irritation on [[Justin Jefferson]]'s behalf.
Minnesota's post-Cousins QB options ([[J.J. McCarthy]], [[Michael Penix
Jr.]], [[Jayden Daniels]], [[Drake Maye]]) discussed for fit with
[[Justin Jefferson]]/[[T.J. Hockenson]]. [[Sam Howell]] to Seattle, competing
with incumbent Geno Smith. [[Sam Darnold]] to Minnesota — no Baker-Mayfield
redemption-story framing from Waldman.

**Running backs:** [[Derrick Henry]] to Baltimore — Waldman's happiest move of
the cycle, citing 2023 speed/breakaway data. [[Saquon Barkley]] to
Philadelphia (not the Houston fit Waldman predicted 2024-03-11) — both hosts
feel it, "vengeance tour" framing floated. [[Josh Jacobs]] to Green Bay
(with [[Aaron Jones]] released) — both feel it. [[Zamir White]] as Las
Vegas's lead back with new addition Alexander Mattison as backup;
[[Khalil Herbert]]/[[Roschon Johnson]] Chicago tandem re-priced after
[[DeAndre Swift]] signs there. [[Joe Mixon]] traded to Houston; Zach Moss and
[[Chase Brown]] the new Cincinnati backfield. [[Austin Ekeler]] to Washington
— Waldman hates it for [[Brian Robinson Jr.]]'s value. [[Tony Pollard]] to
Tennessee, dropping [[Tyjae Spears]]'s redraft price.

**Wide receivers:** [[Calvin Ridley]] to Tennessee for a contract Waldman
calls "a joke" relative to his No.-2/No.-3 caliber; [[Treylon Burks]] and
[[DeAndre Hopkins]] discussed as receiving-corps leadership context.
[[Jerry Jeudy]]'s Cleveland fit revisited, with [[Elijah Moore]] and
[[Cedric Tillman]] as the in-house competition for the No. 2 role.
[[Gabe Davis]] to Jacksonville for $39M/3yr, called a market-inefficiency red
flag. [[Mike Williams]] named the top remaining free-agent name; Michael
Thomas floated as a sneaky-good Miami fit if healthy.

**Other:** Aaron Donald's retirement — Waldman, formerly of Football
Outsiders, called him one of the two "safest" evaluations in his draft class
(with Sammy Watkins), a dominant, disruptive interior lineman despite modest
size.
