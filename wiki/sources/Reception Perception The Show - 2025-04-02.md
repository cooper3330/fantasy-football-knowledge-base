## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] (tracked) with co-host James Koh (not tracked) delivered full Reception Perception prospect profiles on two 2025 receivers: [[Travis Hunter]] and [[Luther Burden III]].

On Hunter, Harmon called him one of the best prospect profiles he has ever charted — a 'generational' two-way player who was above the collegiate average on every route on his chart, 92nd percentile success rate vs man, 97th vs zone, 97th vs press, while lining up outside on 94% of sampled snaps and on the line on ~90%. Harmon's comp is prime [[Odell Beckham Jr.]]; he rebutted the Big 12 competition objection, noted the BYU game he charted was one of Hunter's best, and stressed nobody knows whether Hunter will be a full-time NFL receiver. Koh pushed the Shohei Ohtani analogy and raised durability at 188 pounds.

On Burden, Harmon called him the most confusing evaluation in the class — a 'grade the flashes' player with the highest ceiling after Hunter but a much lower floor, an 82.9% slot rate, a poor feel for zone coverage, and reported character/work-ethic concerns. He endorsed JJ Zachariason's (not tracked) [[Brandon Aiyuk]] comp as the developmental template.

The pair also went on an extended tangent about combine weigh-in obsession being a moving target.
