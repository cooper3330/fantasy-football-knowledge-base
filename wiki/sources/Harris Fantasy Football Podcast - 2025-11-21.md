## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a recap of the Thursday-night Bills-Texans game (Houston won behind a dominant Will Anderson-led pass rush; Josh Allen was sacked a career-high 8 times, threw a late pick to Josh Palmer, and scored zero touchdowns; Keon Coleman was a healthy scratch again; Woody Marks flashed for Houston).
