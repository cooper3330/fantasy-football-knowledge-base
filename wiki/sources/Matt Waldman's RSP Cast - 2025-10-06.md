## Summary
Week 5 reaction edition of the rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys / SiriusXM Fantasy, NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman gives film-based verdicts on a long list of Week 5 storylines: [[Stefon Diggs]] fully back from the ACL in New England, [[Dalton Kincaid]] finally being used over the middle instead of in the flat, a firm fade on [[TreVeyon Henderson]], [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]'s improving attack on the ball and the ceiling [[Kyler Murray]] puts on him, the Arizona backfield, [[Cam Ward]]'s cleaner mechanics, [[Jackson Dart]]'s deep-ball accuracy problem, the Houston backfield split, [[Ja'Marr Chase]] vs [[Puka Nacua]] as receiver evaluations, [[Matthew Stafford]] as the Rams' engine, [[George Pickens]], the Carolina backfield, [[Bryce Young]]'s resilience, Miami's target distribution, [[Dillon Gabriel]], and [[Kendrick Bourne]]'s role in San Francisco.
