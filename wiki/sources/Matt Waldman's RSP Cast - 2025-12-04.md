## Summary
Going Deep segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] covering: Philadelphia's offensive collapse under Kevin Patullo/Nick Sirianni despite elite personnel (scheme-vs-talent framing); Chicago's offense maturing around [[Caleb Williams]] under Ben Johnson, with a Jared Goff development-timeline comp and a call to lean into a true number-one target among Rome Odunze/Luther Burden III/Colston Loveland; a deep dive on Patriots WR [[Kyle Williams]] as a dynasty buy-low given pending free agency for Stefon Diggs, Hunter Henry and Mack Hollins, with George Pickens/Alec Pierce/Calvin Ridley/Romeo Doubs discussed as the free-agent replacement tier; [[Adonai Mitchell]]'s big target-share game for the Jets and whether new Jets brass can replicate Detroit's Jameson Williams turnaround; and an extensive [[Shedeur Sanders]] evaluation against Cleveland's very weak receiving corps, with Sanders comped to [[Brock Purdy]] and Kirk Cousins as a 'system operator' quarterback archetype who needs elite playmakers to thrive, versus 'system elevators' like Patrick Mahomes and Josh Allen.
