## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a philosophical reminder from his Reddit AMA — 'players, not positions' — arguing that drafters should build one blended ranking list and take the best player rather than pre-planning positional permutations. He then runs his joint-practice review for Wednesday's sessions (Browns-Eagles, Patriots-Vikings, Jets-Giants, Lions-Dolphins), noting [[A.J. Brown]]'s hamstring absence, encouraging [[Stefon Diggs]] reports, [[J.J. McCarthy]] missing high, [[Justin Fields]] zone-read keepers, and a disastrous Miami showing (0-for-9 in the red zone).

Main event: flag players 10 through 6 for 2025 — his self-limited list of ten players he is meaningfully higher on than ADP. Numbers 10 through 6: [[Jaylen Wright]] (15th round), [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] (10th round), [[Justin Herbert]] (10th round), [[Tank Bigsby]] (11th round) and [[Joe Mixon]] (a record fifth career flag).

Second half: guest Sigmund Bloom of Footballguys (NOT a tracked expert) joins for 'offenses we might be wrong about' — each host names five situations where the assumptions baked into ADP could be badly wrong. Covered: Philadelphia, Indianapolis, Dallas passing game, Dallas backfield, the Rams and [[Matthew Stafford]]'s back, Chicago under Ben Johnson, Jacksonville under Liam Coen, Minnesota's run-balance shift, San Francisco's possible collapse, and Miami getting even worse.
