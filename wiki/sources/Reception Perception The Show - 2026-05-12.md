## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh run Harmon's 'Year 2' Reception Perception charting profiles for three of the top 2025 rookie receivers: Emeka Egbuka (TB), Tetairoa McMillan (CAR) and Luther Burden III (CHI), plus discussion of Rome Odunze, Xavier Legette, Bryce Young, Chris Brazell, Chris Godwin, Jalen McMillan, Tez Johnson and Ted Hurst. Egbuka's rookie season is split into a strong first half and an injury-hampered collapse; McMillan is broken down as a true X with elite dig/comeback success but underused in the quick game; Burden's hot finish and disagreement over his ceiling (Koh bullish, Harmon more measured) anchors a wider Bears WR-room discussion including a Burden-vs-Odunze debate Harmon calls close to a toss-up.
