## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a recap of the Thursday-night Falcons-over-Buccaneers game: Kyle Pitts's historic tight end performance with Drake London out, Mike Evans's return diversifying Tampa's WR corps alongside Godwin and Egbuka, and a goal-line committee concern for Bucky Irving with Sean Tucker vulturing all inside-the-10 work. Harris then runs through Week 15 injury designations across the league (QB battles in Indy/Vegas/NY Jets, plus Jacobs, Adams, Lamb, Higgins, Harrison Jr., Odunze, Bryant, Ferguson). Guest co-host Jake Trowbridge (untracked) joins for the weekly DraftKings DFS picks segment, discussing Watson, Likely, Mariota/McLaurin, Nacua, Burrow/Chase and more. Second guest 'cousin Josh' (untracked, recurring) joins for the annual exercise of drafting a hypothetical 2026 fantasy first round one through twelve, generating extensive back-and-forth on Bijan Robinson vs Jahmyr Gibbs at 1-2, Jonathan Taylor at 3, a split on Saquon Barkley and Christian McCaffrey, James Cook, De'Von Achane vs Josh Jacobs, the JSN/Nacua/Chase receiver tier, and a lengthy debate over the 12th pick (Ashton Jeanty, Kyren Williams, Breece Hall, Justin Jefferson, Quinshon Judkins, Omarion Hampton, Derrick Henry all discussed).
