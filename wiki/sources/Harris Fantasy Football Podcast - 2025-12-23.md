## Summary
Chris Harris runs a deliberately abbreviated week 16 film review of all 15 Sunday/Monday games (skipping the Thursday game covered previously), explicitly avoiding fine-toothed-comb minutiae with the fantasy championship approaching -- 'let's mostly dance with who brung ya.' He covers injuries and role changes across the slate: George Kittle's ankle, Lamar Jackson's contusion, TreVeyon Henderson's concussion, Jordan Love's concussion, Quinshon Judkins's gruesome leg injury, JJ McCarthy's hand/fumble injury, Pat Bryant getting carted off, and DK Metcalf's suspension for punching a fan. He then hosts guest Jake Seeley (The Athletic's waiver columnist, host of the All In podcast -- not a tracked expert) for a five-round championship-week waiver countdown, with both giving independent, sometimes-diverging rankings on Luther Burden III, Taysom Hill, Raheim Sanders/Dylan Sampson, Jawhar Jordan, Parker Washington, Chris Rodriguez Jr. and Samaje Perine.
