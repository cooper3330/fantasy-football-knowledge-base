## Summary
Matt Harmon is on vacation this week; co-host James Koh runs a team-focused deep dive instead of the usual charting show, bringing on two guests. Segment 1: Adam Rank (Chicago Bears superfan, NFL Network fantasy personality, not a tracked expert) breaks down why Justin Fields' Bears tenure failed (organizational mismanagement, not talent), why he believes the DJ Moore/Keenan Allen/Rome Odunze receiver room is a top-3 unit, ranks his top-6 WRs league-wide, projects Caleb Williams' rookie passing yardage and praises the DeAndre Swift signing, and projects double-digit Bears wins. Segment 2: Chris Long (former Eagles/Patriots/Rams DE, Green Light Podcast host, not a tracked expert) discusses Nick Sirianni's coaching outlook, the Eagles' offensive/defensive line losses (Jason Kelce, Fletcher Cox), the Saquon Barkley signing as insurance for Jalen Hurts' workload, and gives an extended take on how running back/defensive lineman aging and decline actually work.
