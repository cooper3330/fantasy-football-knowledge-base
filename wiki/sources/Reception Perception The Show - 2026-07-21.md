## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) cover fresh Reception Perception profiles. They debunk the Brock Bowers-to-Jaxon Smith-Njigba comparison using inline-TE target-share charting and instead favor a Scott Turner-sourced Bowers-to-Drake London comp, with London's own route-tree shift (dig down, curl up) discussed. They run their preseason QB/WR matching game on Drake May and A.J. Brown, diving deep into May's route charting and a lengthy, somewhat divided discussion of A.J. Brown's 2025 decline, his ideal role (Z vs X) in New England, and comps to Vrabel-era Titans Brown. They close with new RB profiles: Cam Skattebo (scheme fit questions under new Giants OC Matt Nagy vs. Greg Roman's influence), Woody Marks and David Montgomery in Houston (outside-zone fit debate, Harmon and Koh disagreeing on Montgomery's outlook), Jawhar Jordan's rising Texans RB3 role, and a teased-but-undiscussed Quinshon Judkins profile.
