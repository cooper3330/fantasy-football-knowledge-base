## Summary
Third of three 2026 NFL Draft skill-position preview shows (after Matt Waldman on WRs two weeks prior and Paul Pertichese on RBs the week before). Chris Harris hosts guest Dalton Wasserman (formerly of Pro Football Focus, now co-host of the Preferred Walk-On podcast, not a tracked expert) for a full pass through the 2026 quarterback and tight end draft classes ahead of dynasty rookie drafts, one week before the actual NFL Draft. Opens with an extended non-fantasy media-criticism segment about Mike Vrabel/Dianna Russini and NFL insiders, which is not fantasy-relevant and is not summarized further here.
