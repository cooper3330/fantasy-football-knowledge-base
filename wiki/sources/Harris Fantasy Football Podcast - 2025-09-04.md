## Summary
Final pre-football show of 2025 for [[Chris Harris]]. Three segments: (1) news on [[Jauan Jennings]]' resolved calf/hold-in after the 49ers added $3M to his 2025 deal, plus a standing warning not to believe pre-Week-1 depth charts or coach-speak; (2) the 'Preseason Huggy Awards', Harris's end-of-draft-season summary awards covering [[Terry McLaurin]], [[Javonte Williams]], [[Ashton Jeanty]], [[Garrett Wilson]], [[De'Von Achane]] and season MVP pick [[Josh Jacobs]]; (3) a Cowboys-at-Eagles Thursday night preview centered on whether Dallas has enough defense, the Eagles' heavy defensive turnover, and heavy skepticism of the [[George Pickens]]/Brian Schottenheimer breakout narrative. The show closes with the weekly DraftKings DFS segment with guest Jake Trowbridge (not a tracked expert), who builds a Week 1 lineup around mispriced rookies.
