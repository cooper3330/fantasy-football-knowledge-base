## Summary
Solo episode: Matt Waldman runs alphabetically through 45 of the 65 wide receivers he scouted for the 2026 Rookie Scouting Portfolio (out April 1). He opens with a class-wide framework -- his Depth of Talent Score system versus 'rankings suck,' tier breakdowns, and a sample-based baseline for how many receivers from any class become starters -- then argues NFL offenses' shift back to gap-run schemes and two/three-tight-end sets could shrink demand for pure slot receivers. He also gives an extended defense against 'logo scouting' using Tennessee's mixed recent receiver track record, and a long segment on RAS/Combine skepticism anchored on Malachi Fields. He closes with a detailed framework on 'clap attacking' catch technique, applied to projecting Skyler Bell.
