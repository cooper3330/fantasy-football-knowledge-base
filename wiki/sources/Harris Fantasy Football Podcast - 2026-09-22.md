## Summary
Chris Harris opens with Monday Night Football film review (Giants-Rams): Jackson Dart's knee injury, Jameis Winston's rough relief outing, Malik Nabers' shoulder scare, Davante Adams' huge game with Puka Nacua out, and praise for Terrance Ferguson's dynasty upside. He then reviews five Sunday games not covered on Monday's show: Packers-Jets, Commanders-Cowboys (including Jayden Daniels' dislocated elbow), Browns-Buccaneers, 49ers-Dolphins, and Panthers-Falcons. The back half is a Week 3 waiver wire segment with guest John Daigle of Establish the Run (not a tracked expert), counting down both hosts' top-5 adds -- Denzel Boston, Jonah Coleman, Tank Bigsby, Emmanuel Wilson, Oronde Gadsden II -- plus FAAB bid guidance and a long list of honorable mentions.
