## Summary
Solo Week 8 game-film review episode -- Chris Harris hosting alone due to co-host Andy Barron being unavailable. Harris works through essentially the entire Sunday/Monday slate: Packers-Steelers, Bears-Ravens, Texans-49ers, Broncos-Cowboys, Jets-Bengals, a Monday-night Commanders-Chiefs preview, Buccaneers-Saints, and Patriots-Browns. For each game he calls out usage/snap splits, scoring plays, and fantasy-relevant role notes rather than doing full recaps. He references a prior Wednesday Yacht Club show (Andy Barron, untracked co-host) on TreVeyon Henderson, and a Friday show discussion of Lamar Jackson and Jacory Croskey-Merritt with 'cousin Josh' (untracked).
