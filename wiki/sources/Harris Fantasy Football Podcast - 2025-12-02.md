## Summary
Chris Harris solo-hosts, opening with schedule notes and condolences to producer Dave Piper before recapping Monday Night Football (Patriots 27, Giants 7) in depth -- the TreVeyon Henderson/Rhamondre Stevenson snap battle, Devin Singletary's takeover, Tyrone Tracy Jr.'s probable concussion, and continued skepticism of Jackson Dart. He then reviews four remaining Week 13 games not covered on Monday's show: Falcons-Jets (Bijan Robinson, Tyler Allgeier, Kirk Cousins, Darnell Mooney, Kyle Pitts, Adonai Mitchell), Vikings-Seahawks (Max Brosmer's 4-INT shutout, Justin Jefferson's floor/ceiling debate, Aaron Jones's shoulder injury, Kenneth Walker III vs Zach Charbonnet), Saints-Dolphins (Tua Tagovailoa, Darren Waller, De'Von Achane, Taysom Hill mea culpa, Chris Olave), and Jaguars-Titans (Brian Thomas Jr. vs Jakobi Meyers, Brenton Strange, Travis Etienne Jr./Bhayshul Tuten, Trevor Lawrence, Cam Ward, Tony Pollard). The back half is a long Week 14 waiver-wire segment with guest John Daigle of Establish the Run (not tracked), ranking top-five adds (Isaac TeSlaa, Bam Knight, Adonai Mitchell, Brenton Strange, Chris Rodriguez Jr.) plus honorable mentions (Marvin Harrison Jr./Greg Dortch, Pat Bryant, streaming QBs, streaming defenses) and a shallow-league debate between Trey Benson and Omarion Hampton.
