## Summary
A *Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked). Four segments: (1) [[George Pickens]] traded to Dallas and what it means for him, [[Dak Prescott]] and the Cowboys' pass game given the Tyron Smith-to-guard offensive line shuffle; (2) [[Breece Hall]] 'rotting on the vine' in a New York Jets two-back system under new OC Tanner Engstrand, with [[Justin Fields]] siphoning red-zone carries — plus a hypothetical Hall-for-[[D.J. Moore]] dynasty trade with Chicago; (3) [[Justin Herbert]] as a talented quarterback capped by a Jim Harbaugh/Greg Roman run-first scheme, with Waldman's projection numbers, and the resulting outlook for [[Tre Harris]], [[Quentin Johnston]] and [[Ladd McConkey]]; (4) five under-the-radar receivers making early minicamp impressions — [[Jimmy Horn Jr.]], [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]], [[Jordan Watkins]], [[Isaiah Neyor]] and [[Konata Mumpfield]] — and whether the buzz is substance or shorts-and-t-shirts speed. Waldman noted his Dynasty Rankings and Projections were due no later than June 10.
