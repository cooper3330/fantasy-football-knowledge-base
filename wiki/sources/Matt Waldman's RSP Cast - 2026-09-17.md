## Summary
Week 1 recap episode of the 'Going Deep' segment with Matt Waldman and tracked co-host Brandon Angelo. They trade Week 1 surprises (Antonio Williams, Braelon Allen, Bryce Lance, Kendrick Bourne, Pat Freiermuth) and disappointments (Justin Herbert and Drake Maye, both diagnosed as processing-limited 'throwers not quarterbacks'), then dig into three offenses: New England's dysfunction (receiver purge, Hunter Henry/Rhamondre Stevenson as the best remaining options), the Giants' Greg Roman-powered offense (Cam Skattebo, Jackson Dart, a still-recovering Malik Nabers, rookie Malachi Fields), and the Colts (Daniel Jones not ready post-Achilles, Tyler Warren's blocking/receiving tradeoff, Josh Downs, Alec Pierce, Anthony Richardson as the eventual fallback), with a Deshaun Watson/49ers tangent on mismanaging injured quarterbacks.
