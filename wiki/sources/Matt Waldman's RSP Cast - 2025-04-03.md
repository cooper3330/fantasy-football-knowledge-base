## Summary
A listener-question episode of *Going Deep* with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked), recorded a few weeks before the 2025 NFL Draft. Topics: whether [[Travis Hunter]] is the dynasty 1.01 if he is drafted as a receiver, [[Isaiah Bond]]'s split evaluation and off-field noise, [[Damien Martinez]] and [[Tahj Brooks]] as late-round value against [[Omarion Hampton]]'s first-round cost, players each host feels too bullish or too bearish on ([[Tre Harris]], Kelly Akharaiyi, Bhayshul Tuten, [[Isaiah Neyor]], [[Mario Anderson]], [[Jalen Milroe]]), a run of less-heralded prospects, [[Luther Burden III]]'s 2023-to-2024 drop-off, whether [[Jayden Higgins]] is an outside receiver, and a 1.02/1.03/1.04 comparison of [[Quinshon Judkins]], [[TreVeyon Henderson]] and Hampton. Angelo also gave per-round fantasy points-per-game data and Waldman cited his six-year RSP ball-security study.

Non-tracked people referenced: Sigmund Bloom (Waldman relayed his money-value and Mike Vrabel arguments on Hunter), Dan Hatman and Matt Bowen (cited as liking Martinez), Brandon Thorn and Lance Zierlein (named as offensive-line authorities).
