## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) reunite after roughly three weeks apart and walk through two freshly charted Reception Perception profiles: [[George Pickens]] on his move from Pittsburgh to Dallas, and [[Garrett Wilson]] entering year four with the Jets. A planned [[Jameson Williams]] segment was cut for time after technical issues.

On Pickens, Harmon calls 2024 the best year of his career on the charting despite worse box score numbers than 2023, citing career-best success rates versus man (72.8%, 74th percentile) and press (77.6%, 83rd percentile), an 82.4% success rate on slants, and near-identical 80/80 outside and on-line alignment rates. Koh emphasizes the rarity of a five-point man-coverage jump. Both flag the 19th-percentile zone score (73.9%), below-average contested catch, and loafing on non-primary reads as the real concerns. Harmon's comp: a [[Tee Higgins]] role in Dallas alongside [[CeeDee Lamb]], with [[Michael Gallup]]'s 2020 season as the floor baseline.

On Wilson, Harmon puts his talent in the same bucket as the 2024 rookie class the dynasty community is fawning over, citing 94th-percentile man coverage and 86th-percentile press marks plus a first-time 70% contested catch rate. The complaint is scheme: a nine-and-slant route tree (21% nine, 26% slant), a dig rate slashed from 17.8% as a rookie to 7.5%, and slot rate down from 33% to 20.5%. Harmon and Koh both argue new OC Tanner Engstrand may matter more than [[Justin Fields]] for Wilson's year-four production.
