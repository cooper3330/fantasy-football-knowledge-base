## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with news: the Commanders appear set to move on from [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] (leaked to national media, likely a trade for a late pick), which he frames as a strange frugality alongside the [[Terry McLaurin]] situation, and which bumps [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] and [[Austin Ekeler]]. He also reacts to the Colts naming [[Daniel Jones]] over [[Anthony Richardson]], using it as an object lesson that rushing ability alone does not justify an expensive quarterback pick. He then reads his 2025 Almanac profile of [[DeVonta Smith]].

Main segment: guest Scott Pianowski (Yahoo Fantasy, NOT a tracked expert) joins for a round-by-round countdown of high-floor, 'safe' players to draft, rounds six through two. Pianowski nominates [[Tony Pollard]] and [[Calvin Ridley]] (round 6), [[DeVonta Smith]] (round 5), [[Chuba Hubbard]] (round 4), [[Ladd McConkey]] and [[Omarion Hampton]] (round 3), and [[Derrick Henry]], [[Josh Jacobs]] and [[Drake London]] (round 2). Harris counters with [[Zay Flowers]] (round 5), [[Joe Burrow]] (round 4), [[Kyren Williams]] (round 3) and [[A.J. Brown]] (round 2). Concepts touched: [[High Floor Picks and Draft Risk Balancing]], [[Fantasy Quarterback Value vs NFL Quarterback Quality]].
