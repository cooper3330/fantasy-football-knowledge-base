## Summary
Week 12 aftermath edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman broke down [[Nick Chubb]] in the snow against Pittsburgh and argued Chubb is still an excellent NFL back trapped behind a broken Cleveland offensive line, revisiting his 2018 RSP ranking of Chubb as RB1 over [[Saquon Barkley]] and asking whether Barkley is simply 'Nick Chubb in a good offense.' Other segments: [[Calvin Austin III]] as an end-of-roster deep-target bet over [[Van Jefferson]]; [[Russell Wilson]] versus [[Justin Fields]] and the Steelers' scoring problem; a watershed game for [[Bryce Young]] against the Chiefs and what it means for Carolina's 2025 draft plans; [[David Moore]] as a surprise Panthers contributor; the [[Isiah Pacheco]] / [[Kareem Hunt]] fantasy-playoff split; [[DeAndre Hopkins]] in Kansas City; [[Jonnu Smith]] leading Miami's pass game over [[Jaylen Waddle]] and [[Tyreek Hill]]; another 'better than the box score' game for [[Anthony Richardson]]; [[Caleb Williams]] and the Thomas Brown offense; and rookie receiver rankings comparing [[Rome Odunze]] to [[Malik Nabers]], [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] and [[Ladd McConkey]], plus [[Jordan Addison]] versus [[DeVonta Smith]]. Closes with a non-football Thanksgiving segment.
