## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) continue their pre-free-agency 'alignment bros' team-needs series with the NFC South: Carolina, New Orleans, Tampa Bay and Atlanta. The framing throughout is that a team's receiver need is never just 'a receiver' but a specific pre-snap role — X, flanker, big slot, vertical option.

Carolina: whether [[Adam Thielen]] stays, why [[Jalen Coker]] graded out as a legitimate late-round find, and Harmon's uncertainty on [[Xavier Legette]] after the Panthers moved him to X post-bye. Both hosts land on Carolina needing a true X and a vertical threat.

New Orleans: a thin room where [[Chris Olave]] (four known concussions in three years) and [[Rashid Shaheed]] (meniscus tear, four-to-six-month timeline) are the only real assets; Harmon likes the Kellen Moore fit for Olave but flags availability. Quarterback uncertainty around [[Derek Carr]].

Tampa Bay: Harmon predicts [[Chris Godwin]] is re-signed, breaks down [[Jalen McMillan]]'s split rookie season, praises [[Baker Mayfield]] as a genuine talent elevator, and both hosts weigh the coordinator brain drain risk.

Atlanta: [[Drake London]] as a near-tier-one alpha, [[Darnell Mooney]] as a strong running mate, no cap room to add, and Harmon calling [[Kyle Pitts]] a trade candidate.
