## Summary
Bonus midweek episode with [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert), recorded the day after the Amari Cooper-to-Buffalo and Davante Adams-to-New York trades. Three segments: (1) what is left of the Cleveland and Las Vegas receiver rooms after the trades, (2) a temperature check on [[Romeo Doubs]] and [[Darius Slayton]] following Week 6, and (3) a quick look at the Rams room with [[Cooper Kupp]] nearing a return.

Harmon leaned on Reception Perception charting benchmarks (70% vs man, 80% vs zone) plus fantasy points and success-rate data. Recurring themes: alignment fit matters more than opportunity, receivers who cannot beat zone cannot survive in the slot, and a No. 1 receiver drawing more targets than his team scores points is an offense-wide failure, not a fantasy gift.
