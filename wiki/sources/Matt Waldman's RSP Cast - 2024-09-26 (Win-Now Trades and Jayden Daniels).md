## Summary
An RSP *Film and Theory* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Adam Harstad of Football Guys (NOT a tracked expert). Two segments. First, Harstad walks through his 'Dynasty in Theory' modeling of win-now trades: he defines a win-now trade as one where you knowingly give up more value than you get in order to chase a title, and his model says the playoff bye is so valuable that guaranteed wins have *increasing* marginal value up to about three or four, and that a high playoff-week score is nearly as valuable as an extra regular-season win even for a 70% win-rate team. His conclusion: mostly don't make win-now trades, and if you do, make them when chasing a bye (three-to-five seed), not when fighting for the last playoff spot. He shared a real trade of his own — [[Jahan Dotson]], [[Andrei Iosivas]], a 2025 1st and a 2025 2nd for [[Derrick Henry]], Keaton Mitchell, [[DeAndre Hopkins]] and a 2nd — off a roster with [[Lamar Jackson]], [[Dak Prescott]], [[Tyreek Hill]] and [[Mike Evans]]. Second, Waldman's film read on [[Jayden Daniels]] after the Bengals game, contrasted with Harstad's outcome-and-draft-capital framing, plus a detour on [[Kyler Murray]] and Cliff Kingsbury's offense.
