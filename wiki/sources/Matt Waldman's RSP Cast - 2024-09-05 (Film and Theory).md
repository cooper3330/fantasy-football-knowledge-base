## Summary
Season-opening 'Film and Theory' episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and returning co-host Adam Harstad (Footballguys; 2024 Fantasy Writer of the Year; NOT a tracked expert). Four blocks: (1) common offseason dynasty mistakes — Harstad's two oldest rules, 'draft for talent, trade for need' and 'nobody has needs in June', plus his argument that leagues are won by the most strengths rather than the fewest weaknesses; (2) September roster management — FAAB philosophy (spend the budget early on your best option rather than optimizing bids), treating the last bench spots as information-buying options and the waiver wire as a practice squad, and Harstad's four-week rule for when in-season production overtakes preseason ADP; (3) the 2024 kickoff rule change, its history from 2004 through the 2023 fair-catch rule, the XFL-derived alignment, the 30- vs 35-yard-line touchback fight, and what a projected 50-60% return rate means for return-scoring leagues; (4) players on their radar — Waldman's bearish case on [[Kyler Murray]] supporting multiple receivers and the knock-on effect for [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], plus [[Quentin Johnston]] and coach-speak skepticism.
