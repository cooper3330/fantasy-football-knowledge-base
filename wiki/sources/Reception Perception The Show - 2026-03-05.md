## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) preview the NFC West going into 2026 free agency, working bottom-to-top through Cardinals, 49ers, Rams and Seahawks. Cardinals: new HC Mike LaFleur hired Nathaniel Hackett as OC; the QB room is Jacoby Brissett with heavy buzz around signing free agent Malik Willis after cutting Kyler Murray, which would mean pass-volume regression for the receiving corps; Michael Wilson's monster second-half breakout and Marvin Harrison Jr.'s disappointing Year 2 are broken down in depth. 49ers: wide receiver room called an 'abject disaster' post-Aiyuk/Samuel/Jennings departures, with Ricky Pearsall as the lone man-beater and a Romeo Doubs-type addition floated. Rams: Puka Nacua confirmed elite, Davante Adams in decline and 'full green' on the route chart, plus the Trent McDuffie trade context. Seahawks: Kenneth Walker III let walk in free agency despite a monster finish, Zach Charbonnet's ACL timeline, new OC hire questioned, Jaxon Smith-Njigba's huge season and extension outlook, Rashid Shaheed's likely departure, and Tory Horton flagged as a cheap dynasty buy-low target.
