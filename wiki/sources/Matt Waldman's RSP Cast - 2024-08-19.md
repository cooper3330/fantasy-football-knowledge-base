## Summary
A 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-reaction episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Guys / Football Diehard; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]), recorded the Monday after the second week of 2024 preseason games. The running thesis of the episode: preseason football is closer to college football than NFL football — vanilla coverages, no disguises, constant personnel churn — so nearly every hot preseason name belongs on a waiver-wire watch list rather than in a draft. Waldman repeatedly separates the football evaluation from the fantasy verdict (most explicitly on [[Xavier Worthy]]: 'from a football point of view, totally feel it; from a fantasy point of view, fuck it').

Segments covered [[Michael Penix Jr.]]'s lack of preseason snaps, [[Caleb Williams]] as a top-12 QB at ADP, [[Rome Odunze]] versus [[Malik Nabers]] and [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], Nabers' quarterback situation, backfield hype names ([[Dillon Johnson]], [[Kimani Vidal]], [[George Holani]], [[Deneric Prince]], [[Ray Davis]], [[Christopher Brooks]], [[Sione Vaki]]), the Raiders QB decision ([[Gardner Minshew]] over [[Aidan O'Connell]]), [[Drake Maye]]'s readiness, [[Jayden Daniels]] as an RG3 comp alongside [[Anthony Richardson]], and camp risers [[Jalen Nailor]], [[Olamide Zaccheaus]], [[Xavier Weaver]] and [[Jordan Whittington]].
