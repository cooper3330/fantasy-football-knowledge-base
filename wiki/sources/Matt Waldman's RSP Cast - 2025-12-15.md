## Summary
Week 15 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with Matt Waldman and Bob Harris (untracked RSP Cast co-host). Opens with RSP pre-sale promo and Waldman's charity note (Darkness to Light), then rapid-fire hot-take rounds on players and situations across the league. Major thread: Patrick Mahomes' season-ending ACL injury and how the Chiefs passing offense (Rice, Kelce, Worthy) shifts under Gardner Minshew. Other threads: Shedeur Sanders vs Gardner Minshew development arcs; Quinshon Judkins' Browns backfield reassessed after early-season overreaction; Isaiah Bond's talent-vs-maturity risk; Kyle Pitts' role emergence in Atlanta; the Rams' Corum/Kyren Williams committee persisting into 2026; Saints outlook (Tyler Shough, Devin Neal); Panthers WR pecking order (Jalen Coker vs Tetairoa McMillan); Ricky Pearsall as next summer's overhyped breakout; Broncos backfield (RJ Harvey/JK Dobbins); Ben Sinnott as a cheap Commanders TE add; a Texans waiver-wire RB (Jawhar Jordan) emerging with Nick Chubb and Woody Marks out; Mike Evans' injury-driven redraft discount; Chris Godwin as a bargain behind Emeka Egbuka's up-and-down rookie year; and Joe Burrow trade speculation dismissed as normal season frustration.
