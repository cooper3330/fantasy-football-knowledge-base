## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens Labor Day draft weekend with a plea to treat drafts as educated guesses rather than solvable equations, and to ignore automated platform draft grades, which he says only measure how closely you hugged the platform's default ranks. He runs a Wednesday news block: [[Rashee Rice]] drawing a six-game suspension served from the start of the season, [[Marquise Brown]] back at practice in Kansas City, [[De'Von Achane]] cleared as precautionary, [[Jayden Reed]] uncertain for Week 1, [[Jauan Jennings]] not being traded but calf-questionable, [[Marvin Mims Jr.]] leaving practice with a groin issue, [[Isaiah Likely]] and [[Cade Otten]] both vague injury timelines, and [[Jaylen Wright]] out for Week 1 with Jeff Wilson returning to Miami. He then reads his new 2025 Almanac profile of [[Keenan Allen]].

The main body is a listener-question show with guest DENNY CARTER of Rotoworld (NOT a tracked expert), covering recurring ADP mistakes and 'legendary upside', stacking two elite teammates, quarterback strategy in 14- and 16-team leagues, auction nomination tactics, [[Bucky Irving]] as Carter's lone early-round running back, [[Kyle Williams]] in New England, [[Jackson Dart]] as a deep-league stash, and a [[Ricky Pearsall]] vs [[Emeka Egbuka]] breakout question on which the two hosts disagree. Much of the episode is non-football (music, movies, Arby's, hair).
