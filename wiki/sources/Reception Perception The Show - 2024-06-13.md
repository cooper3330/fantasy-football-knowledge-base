## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) run full reception-perception profiles on [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] and [[Garrett Wilson]], then cover the rookie mini sample for [[Brendan Rice]]. St. Brown's profile shows continual year-over-year gains against man and press coverage; Harmon places him in a 'superstar' tier just below his 5-7 man elite WR group. Wilson's success-rate numbers rank among the best charted rookie/sophomore seasons in RP history despite poor box-score efficiency in a broken Jets offense; Harmon is bullish for 2024 with better QB play. Brendan Rice's mini sample shows a strong in-breaking route runner who struggles badly on vertical routes; hosts comp him to Muhsin Muhammad and discuss the wide-open Chargers WR depth chart (Ladd McConkey, Quentin Johnston, Josh Palmer, D.J. Chark).
