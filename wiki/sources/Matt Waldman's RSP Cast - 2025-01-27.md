## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys / SiriusXM, NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]) run the weekly 'Feel It or F--k It' rapid-reaction format the morning after the conference championship games. Topics: whether officiating/'script writers' aided Kansas City and [[Patrick Mahomes]]; the Eagles roster and [[Saquon Barkley]]'s workload reshaping [[Jalen Hurts]]'s passing volume; whether Buffalo should fire Sean McDermott (both say no) and what [[Josh Allen]] actually needs around him ([[Keon Coleman]], [[Dalton Kincaid]], [[Khalil Shakir]], [[Amari Cooper]]); Washington's playoff repeat odds, Kliff Kingsbury's scheme staleness and [[Terry McLaurin]]'s alignment against Quinyon Mitchell; 2025 bounce-back verdicts on [[Christian McCaffrey]], [[Tyreek Hill]], [[Anthony Richardson]] and [[Kirk Cousins]]; whether [[Chuba Hubbard]] is a one-year wonder; whether [[Bucky Irving]] and [[Chase Brown]] are RB1s; Ben Johnson's arrival and [[Caleb Williams]]; Liam Coen, the removal of Trent Baalke and [[Trevor Lawrence]]; [[Baker Mayfield]]; the Brian Schottenheimer hire in Dallas; Pete Carroll to Las Vegas and a possible [[Russell Wilson]] reunion; and whether the Jets should retain [[Aaron Rodgers]]. Also an extended plug for the 20th edition of the Rookie Scouting Portfolio and an RSP giveaway contest.
