## Summary
Week 9 'Feel It or F**k It' with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Guys / SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]).

The spine of the episode is Pittsburgh's dismantling of the Colts: whether the Steelers handed the league a blueprint against [[Daniel Jones]] and [[Tyler Warren]], and whether other defenses have the personnel to copy it. From there Waldman worked through [[Alec Pierce]] vs [[Michael Pittman Jr.]], Pittsburgh's tight end deployment ([[Darnell Washington]], [[Pat Freiermuth]], [[Jonnu Smith]]), [[Kaleb Johnson]], Baltimore's tight ends, the [[Tucker Kraft]] ACL tear and [[Luke Musgrave]]'s opening, Atlanta's downfield trust throws ([[Michael Penix Jr.]], [[Drake London]], [[Kyle Pitts]], [[Bijan Robinson]]), New England ([[Demario Douglas]], [[Stefon Diggs]], [[TreVeyon Henderson]], [[Rhamondre Stevenson]]), [[J.J. McCarthy]]'s return, Detroit ([[Jameson Williams]], [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] as a pass protector), Chicago ([[Caleb Williams]], [[Colston Loveland]], [[Kyle Monangai]]) and Cincinnati ([[Chase Brown]], [[Samaje Perine]], [[Tahj Brooks]]), closing on [[Joe Flacco]].
