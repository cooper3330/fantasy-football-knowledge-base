## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a five-minute critique of broadcast analysis, using Kirk Herbstreit's in-game claim that [[Kyler Murray]] fails to 'get his guys to believe' as the example of retrofitting personality explanations onto results. He then reviews Thursday night's Seahawks-Cardinals game ([[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], [[Kenneth Walker III]], [[Zach Charbonnet]], [[Trey Benson]], [[Emari Demercado]]) and runs a short Friday-morning injury rundown, noting NFL teams are being cagier than ever about status.

The main segment is a trade show with recurring guest Cousin Josh (Instagram: detective fisch), who is NOT a tracked expert. Each names five players they would trade for and constructs concrete offers, with reference to a separate paywalled show where Andy Behrens (also not tracked) made different calls. Josh's five: [[TreVeyon Henderson]], [[George Kittle]], [[Zay Flowers]], [[Tetairoa McMillan]], [[Breece Hall]]. Chris's five: [[Ashton Jeanty]], [[Bucky Irving]], [[Jordan Addison]], [[Chase Brown]], [[Brian Thomas Jr.]]. The through-line is that running backs are automatically priced far above receivers in the current trade market, backed by Chris's target-concentration data.
