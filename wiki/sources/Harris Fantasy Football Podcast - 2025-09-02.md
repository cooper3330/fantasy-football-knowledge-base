## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts a three-part post-draft, pre-Week-1 show. First, a Week 1 news roundup on injury/holdout unknowns ([[Najee Harris]], [[Darnell Mooney]], [[Jauan Jennings]], [[Isaiah Likely]], [[Cade Otten]]) with a running theme that teams have no incentive to tell the truth in September and that 'practicing' means little once nobody is being tackled. Second, a look back at the July training-camp storylines the show said it would track, checking which resolved and whether the market moved — [[TreVeyon Henderson]] over [[Rhamondre Stevenson]], the Steelers split, the Jaguars three-headed backfield, [[Isiah Pacheco]] winning the Chiefs job, [[Terry McLaurin]]'s contract, the Cowboys backfield, [[Colston Loveland]]'s ADP climb, Tampa's receiver injuries, the Seahawks' [[Cooper Kupp]] skepticism, and the 49ers receiver chaos around [[Ricky Pearsall]]. Third, Harris walks through his own most-drafted players of 2025 across roughly seven or eight leagues. Finally, a Week 1 waiver segment with guest BRANDON FUNSTON of The Athletic (NOT a tracked expert), counting down five names rostered in 33% of Yahoo leagues or fewer, which turns into an explicit talent-vs-volume argument between the two.
