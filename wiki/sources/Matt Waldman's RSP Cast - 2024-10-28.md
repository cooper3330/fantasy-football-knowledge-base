## Summary
Week 8 in-season 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-take episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Diehard / Football Guys; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Topics: the Bears staff failing [[Caleb Williams]] (including the goal-line handoff to a guard that was fumbled away), [[Jayden Daniels]]' Hail Mary win and the apples-in-a-glass-jar vs apples-in-a-mallet-factory contrast between the two rookie QBs, a long defense of [[Anthony Richardson]] after a 10-of-32 game (Waldman charted five of the eight disputed passes as catchable drops), the Browns' quarterback situation after [[Jameis Winston]]'s 334-yard game against Baltimore, how to rank the Cleveland pass catchers, and a run of quick calls on [[Adam Trautman]], [[Christopher Brooks]], [[Jalen Coker]], [[Parker Washington]], [[Nick Westbrook-Ikhine]], [[DeAndre Hopkins]], [[Cade Otten]] vs [[Kyle Pitts]], [[Rakim Jarrett]], [[Sean Tucker]], [[Alvin Kamara]], [[Jake Haener]] vs [[Spencer Rattler]], [[Kendre Miller]] and [[Kimani Vidal]]. Closes with Waldman declaring running back by committee dead for 2024.
