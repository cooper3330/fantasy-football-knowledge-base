## Summary
Week 1 reaction edition of 'Feel It or F**k It' with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris of Footballguys (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]). Rapid-fire feel-it/fuck-it verdicts on Week 1 performances: Buffalo's tight end and receiver usage under Joe Brady, Atlanta's quarterback and passing game, Tampa Bay's rookie receiver, three contested backfields (LAC, SEA, NE), Cleveland's skill players and quarterback, New England's receiver room, Pittsburgh's passing game, and quarterback job security in New York and Cleveland. Recurring theme: Week 1 volume has no context, and a long list of stars who missed most of August practice may need weeks to get up to speed.
