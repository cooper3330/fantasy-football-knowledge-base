## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a full film review of the Monday night Eagles-Packers 10-7 game (which he defends as a good game despite the low score), then finishes the Week 10 film slate: Texans-Jaguars, Lions-Commanders, Browns-Jets and Cardinals-Seahawks. He then runs a Week 11 waiver-wire top five with guest **Brandon Funston** of The Athletic (NOT a tracked expert; every view attributed to him is his own, given inline). Recurring themes: box scores mislead when garbage time or special-teams touchdowns inflate them; November waivers are about handcuffs and insurance rather than league-winning plug-and-plays.
