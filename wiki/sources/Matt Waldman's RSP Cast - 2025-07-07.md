## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT tracked; not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]) run a 'Feel It or F**k It' segment during the dead period between minicamp and training camp, working through late-round redraft and best ball ADP values. Waldman opens with RSP business: a free look at the June RSP Dynasty Rankings and Projections update, a preliminary scouting report on Vanderbilt TE [[Eli Stowers]] (whom he expects to be one of the top tight ends in the 2026 class), and an evergreen piece on how to read training camp and spring practice reports with perspective. Both hosts warn against 'looking down the drinking straw' and moving ADP off a single practice rep.

Segments covered: [[Tyler Lockett]] vs [[Calvin Ridley]] for the Titans WR1; the Rams backfield ([[Jarquez Hunter]] vs [[Blake Corum]]); [[Brashard Smith]] in Kansas City behind [[Isiah Pacheco]]; [[Raheem Mostert]] and whether to believe Pete Carroll on a split with [[Ashton Jeanty]]; [[Ja'Tavion Sanders]] and [[Theo Johnson]] as late tight end values; the 49ers backfield ([[Isaac Guerendo]] vs [[Jordan James]]) behind [[Christian McCaffrey]]; [[Jalen Royals]] as the [[Rashee Rice]] handcuff; [[Will Shipley]] vs [[A.J. Dillon]] behind [[Saquon Barkley]]; [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]]; [[Brian Robinson Jr.]]; [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] vs [[David Njoku]]; [[Sean Tucker]] vs [[Rachaad White]] and [[Bucky Irving]]; [[Jermaine Burton]]; [[Jimmy Horn Jr.]]; [[Jalen Milroe]]; and the Steelers pass-catchers after the [[Jonnu Smith]] trade ([[Pat Freiermuth]], [[Calvin Austin III]]).
