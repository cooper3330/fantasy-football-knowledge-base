## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a late-August news block ahead of NFL cutdown day: he lowered [[Terry McLaurin]] to WR18 / late fourth round over the hold-in, lowered [[Jauan Jennings]] about a round over his hold-in 'calf' injury, absorbed the [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] trade from Washington to San Francisco (a sixth-round pick plus over a million dollars of salary sent along, with Kyle Shanahan immediately naming Robinson the RB2 behind [[Christian McCaffrey]]), dropped [[Isaac Guerendo]] to deep-flyer territory, and shuffled the injured Miami backfield ([[Jaylen Wright]] week to week, [[De'Von Achane]] out for the rest of August, [[Ollie Gordon II]] raised). He also flags the new mishmash Washington backfield of [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]], [[Austin Ekeler]] and [[Chris Rodriguez Jr.]].

The main segment is the annual late-round draft targets countdown with guest JJ Zachariason of LateRound.com (NOT a tracked expert), who used Footballguys aggregate ADP and confined himself roughly to picks 110-plus in 12-team leagues. JJ's five: [[Rashid Shaheed]], the [[C.J. Stroud]] / [[Jordan Love]] late-QB pair, [[Drake Maye]], [[Jaydon Blue]], [[Keon Coleman]] and [[Bhayshul Tuten]]. Harris's: [[Ray Davis]], [[Keenan Allen]], [[Brandon Aiyuk]] and [[Austin Ekeler]] at number one. Side discussion covered [[Rachaad White]], [[Sean Tucker]], [[Tank Bigsby]], [[Josh Palmer]], [[Ricky Pearsall]] and [[Luther Burden III]]. Framework talk centered on [[Ambiguous Backfields and Market Risk Aversion]] and [[Late-Round Quarterback and Positional Flattening]].
