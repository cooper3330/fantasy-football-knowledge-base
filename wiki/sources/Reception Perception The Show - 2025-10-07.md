## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recap Week 5 of the 2025 season, built around Harmon's Reception Perception charting notebook. Four segments: (1) Tampa Bay vs Seattle as a duel between the 2018 reclamation quarterbacks [[Baker Mayfield]] and [[Sam Darnold]] and their Ohio State receivers, with [[Emeka Egbuka]] charted over three games and [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] declared a top-10 NFL receiver; (2) [[Stefon Diggs]] in his revenge game against Buffalo, and what his post-ACL usage curve means; (3) Denver-Philadelphia, covering [[Courtland Sutton]] beating Quinyon Mitchell and a long, skeptical segment on [[A.J. Brown]] covering deployment, man-coverage success-rate decline, lower-body injury history and trade/dynasty implications; (4) [[Chris Olave]] as a 'PPR scam' in Kellen Moore's Saints offense, plus a defense of [[Spencer Rattler]].
