## Summary
Solo cast. Matt Waldman previews the 2026 tight end class for the Rookie Scouting Portfolio pre-draft publication, going alphabetically through roughly 25 prospects with one or two prevailing thoughts on each. Before the player-by-player breakdown he lays out a theory from receptionperception.com writer Max Toscano (untracked): NFL offenses may be shifting from 11-personnel spread systems toward condensed 12/13/21/22-personnel sets to counter the nickel/dime 'umbrella' coverages that shut down spread passing after 2023, citing the Ravens, Lions, 49ers and Rams as early adopters and [[A.J. Barner]], [[Tucker Kraft]], [[Tyler Warren]] and [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] as early tight-end beneficiaries. In response, Waldman introduces a second grading system for the 2026 RSP -- an 'inline depth of talent' ('high depth') score that weights blocking at 30% (up from 15%) alongside his existing spread-oriented default score -- and reframes what a fantasy-relevant 2026 TE class looks like under each scenario.
