## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recap Week 10 of the 2025 season. Segments: the firing of Giants head coach Brian Daboll and what it means for [[Jackson Dart]]'s development and the Giants' next coaching hire; Dan Campbell taking over play calling in Detroit and the immediate effect on [[Jameson Williams]], [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] and [[Sam LaPorta]]; the New Orleans passing game after the Rashid Shaheed trade, with [[Tyler Shough]]'s first extended film review plus [[Chris Olave]], [[Juwan Johnson]], [[Devaughn Vele]] and [[Brandin Cooks]]; a mea culpa on [[Jerry Jeudy]] after a 12-target game against the Jets; [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]'s inefficient 3-33-1 line on 12 targets against Seattle; and a long look at [[Justin Jefferson]]'s worst game in 14 outings and what [[J.J. McCarthy]]'s scattershot play is doing to Minnesota's receivers. Closes with [[Mac Jones]] as a league-wide bridge-quarterback solution.
