## Summary
Chris Harris's Monday game-film review of Week 6. He covers the Texans-Jaguars London shootout, Panthers-Eagles, Bears-Falcons, Rams-Cardinals, Bills-Raiders, and previews Commanders-49ers, then brings on untracked guest Patrick Doherty (Rotoworld, co-host of the Roto World Football Show with Denny Carter) to review three more games Doherty picked out: Chargers-Chiefs, Jets-Patriots, and Cowboys-Packers.
