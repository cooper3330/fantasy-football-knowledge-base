## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) break down Matt Harmon's newly published Reception Perception charting data on five of the top 2026 collegiate WR prospects: Carnell Tate, Makai Lemon, Denzel Boston, Jordan Tyson and KC Concepcion.
