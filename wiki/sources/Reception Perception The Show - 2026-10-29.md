## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (both fighting illness) open on the Colts trading Quenton Nelson to Seattle for a 3rd and 5th; both think Indianapolis got fair value but question why they moved now rather than at the deadline, framing it as the start of a full teardown. They cover Josh Downs formally requesting a trade from Indy and debate landing spots, then flag rookie Deion Burks as a dynasty stash if Downs leaves. The back half breaks down three struggling offenses: Kansas City (Rashee Rice injury, Xavier Worthy critique, rookie WRs Cyrus Allen and Jalen Royals, Mahomes efficiency splits), Minnesota (Kyler Murray/Kevin O'Connell scheme mismatch, Justin Jefferson's X-only usage, JJ McCarthy's turnaround with the Giants), and Houston (Nico Collins snap-share limits and shutdown by Deontay Banks, Nick Caley's historically high jumbo-personnel rate, Tank Dell/Xavier Hutchinson/Jaylin Noel/Marvin Harrison Jr.).
