## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys / SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]) run the 'Feel It or F**k It' format ten days before the 2025 NFL Draft, mixing veteran-situation calls with rookie evaluation.

Veteran segments: [[Aaron Rodgers]] to Pittsburgh (Waldman feels it, notes reported front-office rancor over the delay), [[Derek Carr]] shoulder surgery likely ending his time in New Orleans, the Browns QB room ([[Kenny Pickett]] wins the job, [[Joe Flacco]] takes it back later), the Jets passing on [[Amari Cooper]] / [[Tyler Lockett]] / [[Keenan Allen]], [[Andrei Iosivas]] as a third-year breakout candidate, [[Kyler Murray]] running more, and [[Najee Harris]] vs [[Omarion Hampton]].

Rookie segments: [[Jayden Higgins]] as one of the few true X receivers in the class, Kyle Williams (Washington State) as a combine-speed riser, [[Elic Ayomanor]] as overrated at the catch point, [[Cam Skattebo]] as a top-three back off the board, [[Arian Smith]], [[Jaylin Noel]] vs [[Jaylin Lane]], [[Isaac TeSlaa]] as the next Galladay/Claypool trap, and [[Luther Burden III]] — where Waldman responds to [[Matt Harmon]]'s 'flashes' framing of his WR1 grade.

Also: Waldman on pre-draft misinformation season, and on how often GM/owner sizzle-reel enthusiasm overrides scouting work.
