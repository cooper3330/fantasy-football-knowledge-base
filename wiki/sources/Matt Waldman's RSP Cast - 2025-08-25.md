## Summary
A 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-reaction episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]), recorded roughly 11 days before the 2025 season opener. The pair opened on the [[Terry McLaurin]] three-year extension worth up to $96M and what a missed camp does to soft-tissue risk, then ran a yes/no gauntlet on late-round and post-draft-season values: [[Keon Coleman]], [[Dalton Kincaid]], [[Malik Washington]], [[Harold Fannin Jr.]], [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]], [[RJ Harvey]], [[Kaleb Johnson]], [[Omarion Hampton]], [[Marvin Mims Jr.]], the Chiefs' stated vertical shift, [[Caleb Williams]], [[Olamide Zaccheaus]] and [[Luther Burden III]], [[Tez Johnson]], the [[Quinshon Judkins]] contract standoff, [[Skylar Thompson]], [[Max Brosmer]], [[Isaac TeSlaa]], [[Chris Rodriguez Jr.]] and [[Mason Taylor]]. Recurring themes: Joe Brady's 'whack-a-mole' Buffalo passing game suppressing any primary target, preseason buzz versus separation ability, and split backfields being priced as though they were not splits.
