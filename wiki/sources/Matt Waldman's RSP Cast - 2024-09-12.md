## Summary
A *Film and Theory* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Adam Harstad (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert), recorded after Week 1 of the 2024 season. Three segments:

1. **Week 1 developments** — the Saints passing game and whether [[Derek Carr]] has re-entered his aggressive Gruden-era form after the 59-yard opening-quarter bomb to [[Rashid Shaheed]]; [[Baker Mayfield]] climbing dirty pockets and getting audible freedom under Liam Coen versus Dave Canales; the Buccaneers adding zone runs to help [[Rachaad White]]; Jim Harbaugh's Chargers and the re-tooled usage of [[Quentin Johnston]]; [[Jayden Daniels]] as a rushing-driven fantasy QB with 2022 Justin Fields vibes; [[Tank Bigsby]] as an unchanged-but-clean version of his Auburn self; [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] running nuanced routes vs man and zone contrasted with [[Gabriel Davis]] as a scheme-dependent zone beater; [[Trevor Lawrence]] as a fine, non-binary quarterback; and Waldman's growing affection for [[Kenneth Walker III]].
2. **Win-win Dynasty trades** — Harstad's Dynasty in Theory column on searching for *agreement* rather than disagreement, trading around consensus value tools, and counting a freed roster spot as trade value. Filed to [[Win-Win Trade Construction]] and [[Dynasty]].
3. **Ed Reed's all-time greatness** — a non-fantasy argument (aesthetic preference, interception return yardage, positional bias toward pass rushers). No wiki pages created; not fantasy-actionable.
