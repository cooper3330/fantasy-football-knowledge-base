## Summary
A *Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked), recorded in Week 10 of the 2025 season. Four segments plus a buy list.

1. **Bears** — [[Caleb Williams]]'s year-two jump inside Ben Johnson's timing-and-structure offense, with a [[Jared Goff]] air-raid-to-West-Coast comp; [[Rome Odunze]] shadowed and the drop discourse around him; the emerging [[Kyle Monangai]] / [[DeAndre Swift]] committee.
2. **Jaguars** — [[Parker Washington]] as the steadiest option and a buy, [[Trevor Lawrence]]'s third-level limitation, and why that has capped [[Brian Thomas Jr.]]; the [[Jakobi Meyers]] trade and Liam Coen's stated plan to play him on the boundary; [[Travis Hunter]] and [[Brenton Strange]] returns pending.
3. **Steelers-Colts** — Pittsburgh's gap-discipline blueprint against [[Jonathan Taylor]], how it stripped [[Tyler Warren]]'s schemed 'gimme' throws, and three interceptions plus four tipped passes from [[Daniel Jones]].
4. **Falcons** — whether Zach Robinson deliberately unlocked the [[Michael Penix Jr.]]-to-[[Drake London]] fade game against New England, or whether that is the 'assumption of rational coaching'; [[Kyle Pitts]] as the unused version of the same idea.
5. **Buys** — [[Jalen Coker]], [[Greg Dulcich]], [[Jahmyr Gibbs]], [[Jameson Williams]], [[Devaughn Vele]], [[Michael Wilson]], [[Mark Andrews]].
6. **[[Ashton Jeanty]]** — a long defense of the Raiders' sixth-overall pick, built on Waldman's 20-year study of running back and receiver longevity by draft round, and Angelo's top-five vs bottom-five offensive line yards-per-carry split.
