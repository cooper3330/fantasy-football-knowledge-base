## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) preview the AFC East wide receiver rooms, opening with the [[Terry McLaurin]] trade demand in Washington and whether New England is a logical landing spot. Patriots: the crowded post-[[Stefon Diggs]] room, [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] as a likely miss now fighting for a roster spot, [[Demario Douglas]], [[Kyle Williams]], [[Kayshon Boutte]] and camp risers. Bills: [[Keon Coleman]] locked in at X despite a third-percentile man-coverage score, [[Khalil Shakir]] hurt, [[Josh Palmer]] and [[Elijah Moore]] as role players, and Harmon and Koh debating whether Buffalo has a third-and-long one-on-one winner. Dolphins: Reception Perception charting on [[Tyreek Hill]]'s across-the-board 2024 decline and the argument that [[Jaylen Waddle]] is the one who deserves role catering, plus [[Nick Westbrook-Ikhine]] as a possible sacrificial X. Jets: [[Garrett Wilson]]'s target dominance versus a barren supporting cast and [[Justin Fields]]' shaky camp.
