## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a Monday-night film review of Chiefs-Jaguars, then works through the remaining Week 5 games (Titans-Cardinals, Dolphins-Panthers, Giants-Saints, Raiders-Colts) before a Week 6 waiver segment with guest Brandon Funston of The Athletic (NOT a tracked expert). Harris argues the Chiefs are not 'cooked' but that outside [[Patrick Mahomes]] and [[Travis Kelce]] there is no must-start Chief, and walks back his own top-10 ranking of [[Xavier Worthy]] from Week 4. He is openly unimpressed by [[Trevor Lawrence]] despite Jacksonville sitting at 4-1. The waiver list (players rostered in a third of Yahoo leagues or fewer) produced the same five names from both hosts: [[Kimani Vidal]] and Hassan Haskins in the wake of the [[Omarion Hampton]] IR placement, [[Kendre Miller]], [[Tyler Allgeier]] and [[Tyquan Thornton]], plus non-eligible adds [[Rico Dowdle]], [[Michael Carter]] and [[Kenneth Gainwell]]. [[Sam Darnold]] was the streaming quarterback for both.
