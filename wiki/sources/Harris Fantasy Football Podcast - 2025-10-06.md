## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts his Monday film-review show, walking through eight Week 5 games he watched all the way through: Patriots-Bills (Sunday night), Vikings-Browns from London, Lions-Bengals, Cowboys-Jets, Texans-Ravens, plus a Monday night Chiefs-Jaguars preview. Guest **Sam Gold** (Sam's Film Room on YouTube; NOT a tracked expert) joins for the second half and picks three games: Broncos-Eagles, Commanders-Chargers and Buccaneers-Seahawks.

Recurring themes: whether to bench skill players when an offensive line is decimated (Cowboys down four starters, Chargers down to backup-to-the-backup tackles); tight end as fantasy's 'dumbest position' and streaming it; and the ethical discomfort of praising [[Quinshon Judkins]], which Harris ties to how the audience has treated [[Joe Mixon]].
