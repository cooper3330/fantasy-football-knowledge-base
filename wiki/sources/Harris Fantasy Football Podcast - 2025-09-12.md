## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with film review of the Thursday night Packers-Commanders game: Green Bay's defense (Micah Parsons added to an already strong front) has now smothered two good offenses, which he says recontextualizes both Washington's and Detroit's struggles. He is scathing about Washington's game plan for [[Jayden Daniels]] — zero designed runs, no read option, no tempo — and calls it a wake-up call for anyone who wanted to make Daniels into Lamar Jackson immediately. He covers the [[Austin Ekeler]] non-contact Achilles injury and the resulting Washington backfield question, the Green Bay offense ([[Josh Jacobs]], [[Tucker Kraft]], [[Jayden Reed]]'s broken collarbone, [[Matthew Golden]]'s non-role), and runs a Friday injury rundown.

The back half is 'Overreaction Theater' with recurring guest cousin Josh (untracked; also known as Detective Fisch), a five-name countdown each of players they have already changed their minds on after Week 1. They agreed to leave [[Kenneth Walker III]] off as the 'Kenneth Walker memorial list' since he had been discussed all week. Josh's five: [[Kyler Murray]] (up), [[Jaylen Waddle]] (down), [[Jordan Mason]] (up), [[DeVonta Smith]] (down), [[Zay Flowers]] (up). Chris's five: [[Jaylen Waddle]] (down), [[Javonte Williams]] (up), [[Keenan Allen]] (up), [[Travis Etienne Jr.]] (up), [[Isaiah Pacheco]] (down). Josh also gave a Week 2 spread pick (Vikings -3.5 over Atlanta).
