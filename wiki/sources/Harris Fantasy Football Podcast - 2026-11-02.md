## Summary
Chris Harris's Monday game-film review of Week 8, covering 15 games from film rather than box scores. Harris solo-covers Cardinals-Cowboys (a wild Arizona comeback with a quarterback benching and multiple injuries), Chiefs-Broncos (two rookie running backs), Patriots-Dolphins, Eagles-Commanders, Falcons-Buccaneers (a rain-soaked disaster for Atlanta's QB), and previews the Bears-Seahawks Monday night game. Guest Jeff Erickson of RotoWire (not a tracked expert) joins for the back half to break down Ravens-Bills (dominated by a Lamar Jackson hand injury), Vikings-Lions, and Titans-Bengals.
