## Summary
Matt Harmon and James Koh (co-host, not tracked) run through a wave of camp news. Stefon Diggs officially signed with Washington, prompting a full reception-perception breakdown of his 2025 New England season and fit with Terry McLaurin, complicated by LT Laremy Tunsil's season-threatening triceps tear. They then cover a rash of camp WR injuries (Luther Burden III out ~a month; Rome Odunze getting no ADP credit by comparison; Zay Flowers and Elijah Surratt day-to-day/less than 100%), a long riff on Rashod Bateman reportedly moving around the formation plus rookie Jaylin Lane's camp buzz, and quick hits on Cyrus Allen, Andrew Armstrong, Caleb Douglas, Malik Washington, Chris Bell and Parker Washington. The back half is a non-fantasy retrospective on the defunct NFL Fantasy Live podcast, where the two first met.
