## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) open with veteran receivers who could be cut or traded into the 2025 free agent pool — [[Deebo Samuel]], [[Cooper Kupp]], [[Tyler Lockett]] and [[Christian Kirk]] — then run their division-by-division 2025 preview on the NFC North (Bears, Packers, Vikings, Lions).

Main threads: Deebo's four-year decline in yards after catch and rushing efficiency and why Washington is the cleanest schematic fit; whether Lockett retires rather than take an Allen Robinson-style roster-bottom role; Christian Kirk and [[Evan Engram]] as Jacksonville's two biggest cap charges; Chicago's [[D.J. Moore]] / [[Rome Odunze]] alignment puzzle under Ben Johnson; Green Bay's 'do we need a true number one' narrative flip and a possible [[Davante Adams]] reunion; Minnesota's need for a run-game-willing slot behind [[Justin Jefferson]] and [[Jordan Addison]]; Detroit's thin depth behind [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] and [[Jameson Williams]].
