## Summary
A devy/Campus-to-Canton crossover episode. [[Matt Waldman]] hosts alongside Felix Sharpe of Campus to Canton (a guest co-host, NOT a tracked expert). Sharpe brings college risers and backups-to-know: [[Cam Ward]] as the season's biggest quarterback riser, true freshman receivers [[Jeremiah Smith]] and [[Ryan Williams]], [[Ashton Jeanty]]'s backup [[Sire Gaines]], and Tulane passer [[Darian Mensah]]. Waldman plays skeptic on the two-game sample driving Ward's draft-stock jump and presses on what actually changed versus what surrounding talent explains. The show closes with Waldman's own film breakdown of Texas Tech RB [[Tahj Brooks]] in a small/medium/large running back game, including his summer RSP study on college ball security and a note on high-workload college backs not wearing down.
