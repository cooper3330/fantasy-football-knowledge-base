## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] devote the whole 'Going Deep' episode to the 2025 NFL quarterback draft class: how it compares to 2024, whether there is a gap between the top prospect and the rest, and how NFL teams still evaluate the position. Both land on [[Cam Ward]] as QB1, with Waldman adding [[Jalen Milroe]] as the one prospect who might close the gap. They work through [[Shedeur Sanders]], [[Tyler Shough]], [[Jackson Dart]], [[Will Howard]], [[Kyle McCord]] and Waldman's off-consensus pick [[Kurtis Rourke]], then close on a long exchange about neurologic recovery from ligament injury sparked by Rourke playing a full season on a torn ACL.

A large middle section covers the 'robo quarterback' bias — Waldman relays that a league contact told him roughly half the NFL still evaluates on size, arm and recruiting pedigree — plus developmental infrastructure ([[Patrick Mahomes]] behind Alex Smith vs. [[Anthony Richardson]] rushed by Chris Ballard, and [[Malik Willis]] rescued by Green Bay).
