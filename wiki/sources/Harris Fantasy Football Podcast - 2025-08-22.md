## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts solo for the first half, then is joined by recurring guest 'Cousin Josh' (not a tracked expert) for the annual preseason Baggy Awards.

Harris opens by reading the [[Christian McCaffrey]] profile from his 2025 Almanac — a mea culpa on having made CMC the no-doubt 1.01 in 2024, plus his long-running workload/weight argument. News segment covers the [[Jaylen Wright]] practice-field injury and its knock-on effect on [[Ollie Gordon II]] and on [[Joe Mixon]]-style 'flag player' risk.

He then finishes his Super Deep Sleeper list (players outside the top 170 that he explicitly says NOT to draft in normal leagues — the point is dynasty stashes and being first to the waiver wire): [[Keaton Mitchell]], [[Konata Mumpfield]], [[Josh Palmer]], [[Sean Tucker]], [[Casey Washington]].

Moore]]; stealth trash bag [[Garrett Wilson]]; Chipotle award [[Jaylen Waddle]]; Steve Slaton irrational-hate award [[Rashee Rice]]; Carlos Hyde out-of-the-NFL award [[Javonte Williams]]; LVP [[Davante Adams]].
