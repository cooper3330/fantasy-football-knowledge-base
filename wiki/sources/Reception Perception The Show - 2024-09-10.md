## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recap Week 1 of the 2024 NFL season the Monday after the slate. Lead item is the [[Puka Nacua]] PCL injury and short-term IR designation, and what it does to a Rams pass game that already looks like a house of cards up front — with [[Cooper Kupp]] at 21 targets, [[DeMarcus Robinson]] as the only real vertical/outside option and [[Tyler Johnson]] the surprise contributor nobody wrote a camp story about. Long segment on [[Jameson Williams]] first 100-yard game and Ben Johnson's motion-heavy deployment to keep him away from press man, and what that means for [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]]. Also [[Sam Darnold]]'s Vikings debut, rocky rookie QB debuts from [[Caleb Williams]] and [[Jayden Daniels]] (with a sustained critique of Cliff Kingsbury's usage of [[Terry McLaurin]]), and strong rookie WR debuts from [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] and [[Ladd McConkey]]. Closes on the Colts' receiver rotation — [[Adonai Mitchell]], [[Alec Pierce]], [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] — and [[Anthony Richardson]]'s accuracy variance.
