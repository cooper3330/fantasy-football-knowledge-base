## Summary
Chris Harris opens with quick-hit film notes on the four Thanksgiving/national games (Packers-Lions, Chiefs-Cowboys, Ravens-Bengals, Bears-Eagles), then slows down for detailed breakdowns of Sunday's games: Rams-Panthers, Cardinals-Buccaneers, Bills-Steelers and 49ers-Browns, plus a Monday-night Patriots-Giants preview. Guest Andy Behrens (untracked, of The Deep Shot and the Yacht Club podcast) joins for the back half to break down Raiders-Chargers, Texans-Colts and the Broncos-Commanders overtime thriller. Recurring themes: several backfields (Panthers, Chiefs, Texans, Broncos) reverting to messier committees than fantasy managers had priced in; injuries reshaping receiver rooms (Amon-Ra St. Brown, Tee Higgins, Marvin Harrison Jr., Justin Herbert, Woody Marks); and the Philadelphia offense's four-game collapse discussed as a case study in shifting offensive environments.
