## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] finishes his Week 1 film review of the 2025 season, covering Monday night's Bears-Vikings game plus Titans-Broncos, Bengals-Browns, Panthers-Jaguars, Cardinals-Saints and Dolphins-Colts. He says he has now watched every minute of every Week 1 game; games covered on the previous Monday show are not repeated here.

The back half is the Week 2 waiver-wire segment with guest Jake Ciely of The Athletic (ASR rendered his name as 'Jake Seeley'; he is NOT a tracked expert and all of his views are attributed to him inline). They count down five waiver adds each, restricted to players rostered in a third of Yahoo leagues or fewer, plus shallow-league running back options and streaming defenses.

Recurring themes: quarterback play was broadly bad in Week 1 (Caleb Williams, Bo Nix, Cam Ward, Bryce Young, Tua Tagovailoa all criticized on film); running back waiver options are thin league-wide; and Harris repeatedly warns against over-reading one game against a possibly-good defense.
