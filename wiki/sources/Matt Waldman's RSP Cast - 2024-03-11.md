# Feel It Or F@#k It: 3.11.24: An RSP Cast with Bob Harris and Matt Waldman

## Summary

Recorded the morning of the 2024 legal-tampering period (day before free
agency officially opened). [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host **Bob Harris** (*Feel
It or F**k It* — not a tracked expert) run through breaking free-agency news
and Waldman's landing-spot predictions, then close with a rookie-scouting
segment previewing the 2024 Rookie Scouting Portfolio ahead of its April 1
release.

**Free agency / current NFL:** [[Russell Wilson]] signs with Pittsburgh
(Denver eats nearly all of the remaining money); Waldman sees real upside for
Wilson given a better receiving room than Denver's ([[George Pickens]] over
[[Courtland Sutton]]), and reads Pittsburgh's public "not a lost cause"
framing of [[Kenny Pickett]] as protecting trade value rather than a real
quarterback competition. [[Diontae Johnson]] trade rumors (Arizona). [[Mike
Evans]]/[[Baker Mayfield]] reunion in Tampa reaffirmed, with new OC Liam
Coen; [[Rachaad White]] evaluated as a volume-driven, still-inefficient
runner. [[Michael Pittman Jr.]]'s new three-year extension announced live on
air. [[Tee Higgins]] requests a trade from Cincinnati; Waldman grades him a
"very good WR2," not a true WR1, and comps him to [[Mike Williams]]. [[Jerry
Jeudy]] traded from Denver to Cleveland — mixed outlook, paired with
[[Amari Cooper]] and [[Deshaun Watson]]; [[David Njoku]] expected to keep his
role. Free-agent landing-spot predictions (Waldman's own, framed as
predictions rather than reporting): [[Kirk Cousins]] to Atlanta, [[Calvin
Ridley]] to Carolina (Waldman pushes back on the fit), [[Saquon Barkley]] to
Houston, [[Derrick Henry]] to Baltimore (wish-cast), [[Josh Jacobs]] staying
in Las Vegas, [[Tony Pollard]] to Philadelphia. General discussion of the
2024 free-agent RB class's depth, tied to [[League Trend Cycles and Market
Inefficiency]].

**2024 draft class:** Waldman previews a historically strong WR class ahead
of RSP release — top-five call on [[Xavier Worthy]] (elite but "fights the
football"), underrated reads on [[Keon Coleman]] (fit-dependent) and
[[Ainias Smith]] (Curtis Samuel comp), a new deep-sleeper page for [[Tyler
Harrell]], and an overrated/skeptical read on [[Roman Wilson]].
