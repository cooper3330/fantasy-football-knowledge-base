## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a brief tribute to the late Doug Martin before running a Week 8 six-team-bye lineup-decisions segment with guest Scott Pianowski (Yahoo Fantasy, untracked), who brought five start/sit names: [[Troy Franklin]], [[Kayshon Boutte]], [[Tyler Allgeier]], [[Alec Pierce]] and [[Cade Otten]]. They also previewed the Thursday night Vikings-Chargers game, covering [[Justin Herbert]], [[Ladd McConkey]], [[Keenan Allen]], [[Quentin Johnston]], [[Oronde Gadsden II]], [[Jordan Mason]] and [[Aaron Jones]], plus a DraftKings Pick Six prop discussion on [[Jordan Addison]], Gadsden and Herbert. Harris and Pianowski also discussed [[Daniel Jones]] and [[Christian McCaffrey]]. Harris then answered a Patriots-fan tangent on [[TreVeyon Henderson]]. The back half is a DFS lineup-reveal segment with Jake Trowbridge (untracked, recurring 'tinking' co-host bit) covering [[Bo Nix]]/[[Courtland Sutton]], [[A.J. Brown]]/[[DeVonta Smith]], [[Zay Flowers]], [[Wan'Dale Robinson]]/[[Jackson Dart]], and honorable mentions [[Breece Hall]], [[DeAndre Swift]], [[Bhayshul Tuten]] and [[Josh Downs]].
