## Summary
Pre-NFL-Draft "Feel It or F**k It" segment. Matt Waldman (tracked) and untracked co-host Bob Harris trade rapid-fire draft-landing-spot speculation and current best-ball/dynasty ADP takes ahead of the 2024 NFL Draft. Topics: ideal landing spots by position (QB/RB/WR/TE), Josh Allen's makeup, the Buffalo and Denver QB situations, New England/Washington as tough rookie-QB spots, and a run of quick-hitter ADP value calls.
