## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) record live in-studio for an AFC West receiver-room breakdown, working team by team through the Chargers, Chiefs, Broncos and Raiders before ranking the four rooms.

Chargers: heavy segment on [[Ladd McConkey]] as a full-field separator (85th percentile vs man, 81st vs zone, weak vs press) whom both hosts expect to lead the team in targets, receptions and yards; [[Quentin Johnston]] graded near the very bottom of the rookie Reception Perception class; [[Josh Palmer]] framed as an ideal No. 3; backfield skepticism on [[J.K. Dobbins]] and [[Gus Edwards]] with [[Kimani Vidal]] dismissed as a threat.

Chiefs: Koh pegs [[Marquise Brown]] to lead the team in targets and yards, Harmon leans toward [[Rashee Rice]] as the chain-mover with 100-plus catch upside; [[Xavier Worthy]] profiled as the vertical/horizontal stretch piece; [[Travis Kelce]] discussed as the likely actual target leader at age 35; [[Skyy Moore]] written off.

Broncos: called the clear worst room in the division — [[Courtland Sutton]] a limited good-not-great X, [[Marvin Mims Jr.]] among the worst-charting rookies of 2023, [[Troy Franklin]] a talented but undersized deep threat, [[Josh Reynolds]] Harmon's bet for second-most productive receiver.

Raiders: [[Davante Adams]] still a top-three NFL receiver on the charting, [[Jakobi Meyers]] underrated, but Harmon projects Las Vegas last in pass attempts with [[Aidan O'Connell]] under center and [[Brock Bowers]] and [[Michael Mayer]] also needing targets — leading to a Harmon-vs-Koh disagreement over [[Davante Adams]] vs [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] in fantasy.

Final room ranking (Harmon): Raiders, Chiefs, Chargers, Broncos.
