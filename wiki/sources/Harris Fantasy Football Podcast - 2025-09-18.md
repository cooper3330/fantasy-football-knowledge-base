## Summary
Week 3 Thursday show hosted by [[Chris Harris]] (tracked). Opens with a quarterback injury sweep: Joe Burrow needing toe surgery (Jake Browning takes over), Jayden Daniels at risk with Marcus Mariota as the downgrade path, Justin Fields out in Tampa with Tyrod Taylor starting, Brock Purdy likely still out with Mac Jones starting, and J.J. McCarthy out up to a month with a high ankle sprain leaving Carson Wentz to keep Justin Jefferson usable. Harris also reports no ramp-up news on Joe Mixon (NFI, not eligible until week 5, possibly week 7 given the Houston bye) and flags Woody Marks as a speculative deep add over Nick Chubb and Dare Ogunbowale.

Segment one: guest Scott Pianowski of Yahoo Fantasy (NOT a tracked expert) nominates five hardest-to-rank players for week 3 — A.J. Brown, TreVeyon Henderson, Calvin Ridley, Jauan Jennings and Wan'Dale Robinson — plus a Dolphins-at-Bills Thursday preview covering Keon Coleman, Jaylen Waddle, Tyreek Hill, Khalil Shakir and Dalton Kincaid. Pianowski moves Coleman above Waddle live on air.

Segment two: guest Jake Trowbridge (NOT tracked) and Harris build DraftKings week 3 lineups, discussing Bijan Robinson as the consensus 1.01 redraft re-pick, Rome Odunze as the week's biggest misprice, Javonte Williams, Chase Brown, Tony Pollard, Justin Herbert, Keenan Allen, Caleb Williams, CeeDee Lamb, DK Metcalf, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Ashton Jeanty, David Njoku and Tyler Warren.
