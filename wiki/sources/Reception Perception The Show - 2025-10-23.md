## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh cover Week 8 news and fantasy takes. Topics: Mike Evans' season-ending clavicle injury and Tampa Bay's WR depth; Jacoby Meyers as the top trade-deadline candidate; Nico Collins' concussion and a broader critique of Houston's offense under new OC Nick Caley, including the Jaylin Noel-over-Jayden Higgins snap/role controversy and Woody Marks/Nick Chubb's reversed roles; an extended 'sacrificial X receiver' deep dive on Alec Pierce and Romeo Doubs graduating from that archetype, with free-agency contract projections; over/underperformer temperature checks on Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Keenan Allen, Zay Flowers, Jameson Williams (reverting to sacrificial X under new deployment), Brian Thomas Jr./Travis Hunter, and Jerry Jeudy; and a FanDuel pick segment on Matthew Golden, CeeDee Lamb, George Pickens and Rashee Rice.
