## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] works through the remaining Week 4 game film — Bengals-Broncos, Jets-Dolphins, Commanders-Falcons, Saints-Bills, Browns-Lions and Texans-Titans — then is joined by Jeff Erickson of RotoWire (guest, NOT a tracked expert) for a Week 5 waiver-wire countdown restricted to players rostered in a third of Yahoo leagues or fewer.

Headline items: [[Tyreek Hill]] dislocated his knee on Monday night and is out for the season, which reshapes the Miami passing game around [[Jaylen Waddle]], [[Darren Waller]] and waiver target [[Malik Washington]]. Harris counsels against panic-selling [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Tee Higgins]] and [[Chase Brown]] during the [[Joe Burrow]] absence. [[Woody Marks]] is the clear number-one add of the week over [[Nick Chubb]], with [[Joe Mixon]] reported as 'not close' to returning. Harris pushes back hard on the idea that [[Quinshon Judkins]] is a top-10 rest-of-season back, and on the idea that [[Michael Penix Jr.]] is close to being benched. Harris and Erickson disagree openly on [[Kendre Miller]] versus [[Tyjae Spears]] and on how much FAAB the Marks bid is worth.
