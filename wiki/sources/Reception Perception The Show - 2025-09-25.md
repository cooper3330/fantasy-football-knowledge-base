## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) work through Week 3 charting. First segment is the Las Vegas Raiders: Harmon charted [[Tre Tucker]] for the first time and found a full-time (95% route participation), pure-outside speed player who beats zone but not press; both hosts argue the Raiders may be 'galaxy braining' the offense by pairing Tucker with [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]] as lid-lifters while lacking a chain-mover, and both want more [[Jack Bech]]. Koh charted the [[Ashton Jeanty]] run game and pushes back hard on Ryan Clark and Chris Sims (neither tracked), citing a historically bad offensive line, ~40% loaded-box rate, a record 32% unblocked defender rate and 101% of yards after contact. Second segment: Week 3 breakouts — [[Tory Horton]] as a legitimate starter in Seattle alongside a leaping [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] and a role-reduced [[Cooper Kupp]]; [[Luther Burden III]] splash plays on tiny snap volume; [[Travis Hunter]]'s two-way workload capped by Liam Coen's comments, with [[Parker Washington]] absorbing outside snaps. Closes on [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] re-establishing himself in Indianapolis with [[Daniel Jones]], and Harmon partially walking back his [[Josh Downs]] preference.
