## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]) run the 'Feel It or F**k It' format over the May news cycle: schedule-release week, rookie minicamps, the [[Derek Carr]] retirement and the resulting Saints quarterback competition ([[Tyler Shough]], Jake Haener, [[Spencer Rattler]]), the [[Aaron Rodgers]]-to-Pittsburgh waiting game, the [[George Pickens]] trade to Dallas and its knock-on effects for [[CeeDee Lamb]], [[Dak Prescott]] and [[KaVontae Turpin]], Pittsburgh's May hype around [[Roman Wilson]], Cleveland's quarterback room and [[Jerry Jeudy]], [[Stefon Diggs]] in New England, the [[Tre Harris]] vs [[Quentin Johnston]] Chargers question, [[Adam Thielen]]'s final season, [[Matthew Golden]] and the end of the [[Christian Watson]] era in Green Bay, and [[Woody Marks]] behind [[Joe Mixon]] in Houston. Waldman also plugged the RSP dynasty ranks/projections shipping in early June.
