## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (Going Deep) open with which training-camp buzz deserves skepticism — rookie minicamp reps, vertical-shot highlights, and the perennial 'the back will play in the slot' narrative. They then work through health concerns ([[Joe Mixon]] still in a boot, [[Nick Chubb]] reportedly clocking 23 mph in spring workouts), top-30 ADP players they will not pay for ([[Tyreek Hill]], [[Bucky Irving]]), and a run of either/or segments: [[Roschon Johnson]] vs [[Kyle Monangai]] in Ben Johnson's Chicago backfield, [[Colston Loveland]] vs [[Mason Taylor]] at rookie tight end, two of three among [[DeAndre Hopkins]] / [[Isaiah Likely]] / [[Rashad Bateman]] in Baltimore, [[Damien Martinez]]'s roster path in Seattle, [[Sean Tucker]] vs [[Rachaad White]] in Tampa, and [[Tyler Lockett]] vs [[Calvin Ridley]] (plus [[Xavier Restrepo]]) in Tennessee. Angelo supplies the medical framing throughout; Waldman argues the contrarian side on Hopkins and Lockett.
