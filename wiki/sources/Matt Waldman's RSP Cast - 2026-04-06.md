## Summary
Bob Harris and Matt Waldman run their 'Feel It or F**k It' format on the newly released Rookie Scouting Portfolio (RSP) ahead of the 2026 NFL Draft. Waldman first pitches the RSP product itself, then works through a run of rapid prospect Feel-It-or-F**k-It calls: QB Fernando Mendoza (comped to a Luck/Herbert blend, landing with the Raiders behind newly signed Kirk Cousins), the Rams' 2026 QB draft plans, RB Emmett Johnson (poor combine testing defended via a Dalvin Cook curvilinear-movement comp), WR Skylar/Skyler Bell (Ja'Marr Chase comp pushed back on, comped instead to Laveranues Coles), WR Carnell Tate (Justin Jefferson 'Hindu theory' comp), TE Kenyon Sadiq (elite speed, raw processing), WR De'Zhaun Stribling (projection-pick upside vs. inconsistent tape), TE Isaiah Likely with the Giants, QB Taylen Green (Colin Kaepernick / Anthony Richardson comps), WR Ted Hurst (A.J. Green / Christian Watson / Tee Higgins range of outcomes), TE Justin Joly (Mark Andrews / Harold Fannin Jr. comps), TE Sam Roush (best-fit projection to Atlanta), and RB Le'Veon Moss (Zach Moss / Ray Davis comps). Waldman speaks throughout as the tracked expert; Bob Harris frames each question and editorializes lightly but is not tracked.
