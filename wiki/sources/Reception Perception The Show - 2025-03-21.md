## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) react to free agency news and then run Harmon's list of teams that still need wide receiver help. The show opens with a long Reception Perception breakdown of [[Stefon Diggs]] after his Patriots visit — his 2024 Houston charting, the 50/50 slot usage, the decline in his vertical and dig-route production, and the age-31 ACL question. They then cover [[Michael Gallup]] un-retiring with Washington, the state of the New England receiver room ([[Ja'Lynn Polk]], [[Kendrick Bourne]], [[Javon Baker]], [[Mack Hollins]]), and then two buckets of teams: those who must take a receiver in rounds one to three (Patriots, Raiders, Jets, Chargers, Cowboys, Texans, Titans) and those who should only pounce if the right player falls (Panthers, Broncos, Packers, Cardinals, Saints, Browns). A recurring theme is the league-wide scarcity of true X receivers and the resulting glut of power slots.
