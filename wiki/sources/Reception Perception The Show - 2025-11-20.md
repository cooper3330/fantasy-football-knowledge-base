## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) open on George Pickens's big Monday-night game before doing a full retrospective on the 2022 wide receiver draft class, then close with betting picks. Ground covered: Pickens's leap in Dallas and revisiting Harmon's early skepticism on him; a comparison to DK Metcalf, whom Pittsburgh traded for; the 'unlucky trio' of Drake London, Garrett Wilson and Chris Olave all suffering injuries/lacking QB support; the class's second tier of developing players (Alec Pierce, Romeo Doubs, Christian Watson); the first-round busts (Jahan Dotson, Treylon Burks) and Jameson Williams's role-dependent bounce-back; round 2-5 value (Khalil Shakir); New Orleans surprisingly releasing Brandon Cooks and who inherits his 'for the love of the game' clear-out role (Mason Tipton); Buffalo's Tyrell Shavers snap-share breakdown and Keon Coleman's healthy scratch; and player-prop picks on CeeDee Lamb and Stefon Diggs.
