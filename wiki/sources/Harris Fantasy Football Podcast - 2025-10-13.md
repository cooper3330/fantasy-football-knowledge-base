## Summary
Chris Harris does a solo game-film review of Sunday night's Chiefs-Lions game, then reviews Niners-Buccaneers, Bengals-Packers, Rams-Ravens and the Broncos-Jets London game before a Monday-night preview. Guest Chris Raybon (Action Network, not a tracked expert) then joins to review three more games he watched in full: Patriots-Saints, Chargers-Dolphins and Seahawks-Jaguars. The throughline across the episode is running-back committees resolving (Pacheco over Hunt, Vidal over Haskins, Charbonnet over Walker), receiver value tied to erratic quarterback play (Trevor Lawrence/Brian Thomas Jr., Spencer Rattler/Chris Olave), and injury tracking (Egbuka, Puka Nacua, Garrett Wilson, Deebo Samuel, Dalton Kincaid).
