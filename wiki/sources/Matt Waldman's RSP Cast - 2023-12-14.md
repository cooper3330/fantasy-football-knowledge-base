## Summary

RSP Film and Theory episode. [[Matt Waldman]] with **Adam Harstad**
(Footballguys — *not* a tracked expert in this wiki; his analysis is attributed
to him by name throughout).

Almost entirely conceptual rather than player-specific. Harstad presents data
showing a sharp, unexplained collapse in productive seasons by older players
across most positions since roughly 2017, and the two work through competing
explanations without landing on one. Directly relevant to [[Dynasty]] age-curve
decisions.

Closing segment covers playoff lineup philosophy — Harstad argues for starting
your best players regardless of matchup rather than chasing matchups.
