## Summary
Matt Waldman hosts JJ Zachariason (LateRoundQB, Late Round Draft Guide) for a long, game-theory-heavy 2024 draft-prep conversation. Topics: JJ's philosophy on outlier-hunting and market inefficiency (Puka Nacua, Kyron Williams as recent examples); Ladd McConkey's variance profile in a new Greg Roman/Jim Harbaugh Chargers offense; the 'pocket passer trap' (CJ Stroud vs. Brock Purdy/Tua Tagovailoa); the running back dead zone and why rookie RBs outperform in it (Jonathan Brooks as the marquee example); Kyren Williams vs. Blake Corum in the Rams backfield; De'Von Achane's efficiency-driven upside in Miami; Josh Jacobs vs. Marshawn Lloyd in Green Bay; Chase Brown's receiving development under trainer Drew Lieberman; Tyjae Spears vs. Tony Pollard in Tennessee's timeshare; Dalton Kincaid, Evan Engram, Luke Musgrave and Tucker Kraft at tight end; DeAndre Hopkins' aging-curve case; Malik Nabers vs. Marvin Harrison Jr.; and rookie-QB drag on Jayden Daniels, plus a brief note on Justin Fields in Pittsburgh.
