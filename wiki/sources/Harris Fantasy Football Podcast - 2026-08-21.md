## Summary
Chris Harris recaps his LA auction league results and joint-practice reports (Dolphins looking bad on offense, Puka Nacua still out with a groin injury, Devante Adams rampaging, Saints backfield murky) before finishing his 2026 flag players list, ranking #5 through #1: Chris Rodriguez Jr., Kyren Williams/DeVonta Smith, Jameson Williams, Bucky Irving, and Zay Flowers at #1. He also announces he is lowering De'Von Achane after two ugly Dolphins joint practices. In the second half, cousin Josh (untracked recurring guest) counts down his 'frag' (fade) players for 2026: Jeremiah Love (#5), Jaxson Dart (#4), Harold Fannin Jr. (#3), Bucky Irving (#2 -- directly contradicting Harris's #2 flag ranking), and Rashee Rice (#1). Tucker Kraft comes up as a shared ranking drop for both hosts.
