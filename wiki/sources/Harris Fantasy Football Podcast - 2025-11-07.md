## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a full recap of the Thursday night Raiders-Broncos game (10-7 Denver), then a Week 10 injury watch list, then the main segment: a countdown of the five most 'pivotal' players for the rest of the 2025 season with guest Jim McCormick (fourth-grade teacher, formerly of the fantasy industry; filling in for Cousin Josh). Jim McCormick is NOT a tracked expert and his views are attributed to him inline.

The framing for the segment: not simply high-variance players, but wild cards whose outcomes make or break rosters down the stretch. Jim McCormick's five: [[Chris Olave]], [[Chase Brown]], [[Dalton Kincaid]], [[Ricky Pearsall]], [[TreVeyon Henderson]]. Chris Harris's five: [[Breece Hall]], [[Joe Flacco]], [[Bucky Irving]], [[Justin Herbert]], [[A.J. Brown]]. Both also gave against-the-spread picks for Week 10 (McCormick: Bills -9.5 at Miami; Harris: Buccaneers -2.5 vs Patriots).
