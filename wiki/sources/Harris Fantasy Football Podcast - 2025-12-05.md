## Summary
Chris Harris opens with Thursday night fallout from the Lions-Cowboys 74-point shootout: Amon-Ra St. Brown played through an ankle injury and delivered a clutch late catch, George Pickens had a self-described 'old Pickens' meltdown game (penalties, a fumble, quitting on routes), CeeDee Lamb suffered a concussion, and Ryan Flournoy saw expanded second-half work. Jake Trowbridge joins for a DraftKings DFS picks segment (Christian Watson, Dontayvion Wicks, Colston Loveland, James Cook, Chase Brown, Davante Adams, Justin Jefferson as a GPP dart throw, Michael Wilson contingent on Marvin Harrison Jr.'s injury status). Cousin Josh joins for the fantasy playoff preview: a 'panic list' of ice-cold players heading into the fantasy playoffs (Justin Jefferson, Saquon Barkley, Lamar Jackson, DK Metcalf, Ashton Jeanty, Michael Pittman Jr.), a running back handcuff priority ranking (Blake Corum, Tyler Allgeier, Brian Robinson Jr., Emmanuel Wilson, Kenneth Gainwell, Ray Davis, Tank Bigsby, Ollie Gordon II, Sean Tucker, Bhayshul Tuten, Devin Singletary, Jaleel McLaughlin, Isaiah Davis), and streaming quarterback/defense recommendations for weeks 15-17.
