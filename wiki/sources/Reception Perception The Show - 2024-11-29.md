## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) record a post-Thanksgiving Week 13 episode in two halves. First, a Rookie Report deep dive on [[Adonai Mitchell]], covering his in-season Reception Perception charting, his misuse as an off-ball slot early in the year after [[Josh Downs]]'s high-ankle sprain, and his long-term projection as an X receiver. Second, a run through the surprising names near the top of the 2024 receiving-yards leaderboard through 12 weeks — [[Terry McLaurin]] (4th), [[Zay Flowers]] (5th), [[George Pickens]] (6th), [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] (7th), [[Courtland Sutton]] (10th) and [[Darnell Mooney]] (14th) — with Harmon ranking them least-to-most surprising and arguing that 2024 is a down year for league-wide receiver production because of injuries.
