## Summary
Solo episode: Matt Waldman runs an alphabetical A-Z breakdown of roughly 45 of the 53+ running backs slated for the 2026 Rookie Scouting Portfolio (available April 1 at mattwaldman.com). He opens with class-level context -- 2025's rookie RBs who broke out despite low draft capital (Omarion Hampton, Kyle Monangai, Jacory Croskey-Merritt, Woody Marks, Quinshon Judkins, TreVeyon Henderson, RJ Harvey, Ashton Jeanty), the tight talent margins in this year's Tier 2/3, and strategic advice to trade current rookie picks for future capital or prioritize receivers/QBs, with Jeremiah Love as the lone exception worth an early pick. He then works through individual prospects with brief scouting notes (strengths, weaknesses, comps), including an extended defense of Emmett Johnson against RAS/three-cone drill criticism.
