## Summary
A Film and Theory episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Adam Harstad (Footballguys, Dynasty in Theory — NOT a tracked expert). Three segments: (1) where [[Brock Bowers]] ranks against the best rookie tight end seasons in history, with Harstad arguing Mike Ditka's 1961 is untouchable and Bowers is trending toward third-best behind Ditka and Jeremy Shockey, while Waldman does the film compare-and-contrast against Aaron Hernandez, Rob Gronkowski, Keith Jackson, Jordan Reed, [[Sam LaPorta]] and [[Kyle Pitts]]; (2) Harstad's 'many leagues, one team' cross-league dynasty portfolio framework, and Waldman's disagreement about concentration vs. diversification; (3) Waldman's 'enjoy the panic on [[Anthony Richardson]]' argument against alarmist micro-analysis of quarterback footwork, plus a broader Harstad riff that NFL careers are random walks rather than orderly curves.
