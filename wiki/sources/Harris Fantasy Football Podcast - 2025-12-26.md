## Summary
Chris Harris opens solo with a run of championship-round injury news: Lamar Jackson doubtful (Tyler Huntley starting, dragging Zay Flowers down the WR ranks), Jordan Love (concussion) and Malik Willis (shoulder) both questionable for Green Bay, Josh Jacobs's diminished role behind Emmanuel Wilson, TreVeyon Henderson vs Rhamondre Stevenson, Kimani Vidal out, the Dylan Sampson/Raheem Sanders Browns platoon with Quinshon Judkins out, Davante Adams likely out for Monday night, Christian Watson (illness), Rome Odunze unlikely to play with Luther Burden a sneaky start, Brock Bowers placed on IR, and George Kittle/Dalton Kincaid/Dawson Knox as games to monitor. Jake Trowbridge (untracked DFS guest) then joins for a Week 17 DraftKings lineup breakdown -- mostly DFS roster construction, not evaluative fantasy takes. The back half is a 2026 'undervalued players' countdown between Harris and untracked co-host Cousin Josh, alternating picks: Josh has Garrett Wilson, Colston Loveland, Chuba Hubbard, Ladd McConkey, and Blake Corum; Harris has Brenton Strange, Tony Pollard, Alec Pierce, Travis Etienne, and Ricky Pearsall, with reactions from each other throughout.
