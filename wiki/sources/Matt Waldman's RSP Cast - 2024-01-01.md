# Matt Waldman's RSP Cast — 2024-01-01

**Raw transcript:** `raw/ingested/rsp-cast/2024-01-01-rsp-feel-it-or-fk-it-1124-an-rsp-cast-with-bob-harris-and-matt-waldman.md`

## Summary

*Feel It or F**k It*, the recurring rapid-fire format co-hosted by
[[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (veteran fantasy analyst — *not* a tracked
expert in this wiki, and **not** the same person as [[Chris Harris]]; his
views are attributed to him by name throughout). New Year's week episode,
recorded during the NFL Week 18 finale — a looser, more free-associating
conversation than the Week 15 show, with both hosts trading verdicts on 2024
redraft value across roughly 20 players rather than one host driving strict
questions.

Three threads carry the most weight:

1. **The 2024 #1-overall debate.** [[Christian McCaffrey]], [[CeeDee Lamb]],
   and [[Tyreek Hill]] are debated as the top of the 2024 board. Waldman ranks
   McCaffrey 1st, Lamb 2nd, Hill 3rd; Bob Harris takes Lamb over McCaffrey and
   would slot Hill above Lamb. Recorded as an explicit disagreement rather than
   flattened into a consensus.
2. **Scheme-driven backs.** [[Kyren Williams]] and [[Kenneth Walker III]] get
   extended treatment as backs whose 2024 fantasy value depends more on
   coaching staff and scheme than on individual talent — Williams graded
   explicitly as sub-top-10 talent producing top-10 output because of the
   Rams' line and receivers. See [[Scheme vs Talent]], updated with both this
   and a parallel Cleveland Browns case built around [[Joe Flacco]].
3. **Draft-risk philosophy.** A cluster of aging or injury-flagged backs
   ([[Raheem Mostert]], [[Aaron Jones]], [[James Connor]]) prompts a shared
   "injury agnostic, not stupid" framing — draft assuming everyone eventually
   gets hurt, price and plan for the loss rather than avoiding the player. New
   concept page: [[Injury-Agnostic Roster Construction]].

Also covered, each with a real if brief evaluative take: [[Mike Evans]],
[[Stefon Diggs]], [[Cooper Kupp]], [[Zamir White]], [[Anthony Richardson]],
[[Travis Kelce]], [[Lamar Jackson]], and a continuation of the
[[Brock Purdy]] vs [[C.J. Stroud]] mid-round-quarterback comparison from
2023-12-18.

The episode closes with a non-fantasy segment: tribute and a GoFundMe mention
for Emil "the Cadillac" Kadlubowski, founder of Football Diehards / Fantasy
Sports Publications, who stepped back from the industry after a cancer
diagnosis. Not wiki content — no player or strategy claims — noted here only
for completeness.
