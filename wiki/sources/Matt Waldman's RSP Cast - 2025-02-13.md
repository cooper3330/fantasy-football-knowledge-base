## Summary
A two-hour *Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] devoted entirely to the 2025 NFL running back draft class. They open on the state of the position — Angelo argues running backs are 'back' as a core part of offensive identity, citing [[Bijan Robinson]], [[Jahmyr Gibbs]], [[De'Von Achane]], [[Saquon Barkley]], [[Derrick Henry]], [[James Cook]] and [[Bucky Irving]], and counts the Bears, Cowboys, Commanders, Chiefs, Raiders, Chargers, Steelers (and possibly Browns) as backfields with a genuine 17-18+ touch job open. Angelo says he has eight backs in this class at a 70th-percentile-or-better film grade; Waldman says 23-25 players who could become at least [[Rachaad White]]-level contributors.

Angelo presents charting data on rookie touches per game by draft round since 2019 (1st: ~18, 2nd: 12, 3rd: 10, 4th: 7), and Waldman questions whether those numbers are about to drift upward as offenses re-commit to the run.

The format is a series of prompts: who needs no specific landing spot, who needs a perfect scheme fit, who is overrated, underrated, and who is a 'lunch pail' what-you-see-is-what-you-get back. The show closes with an extended segment on [[Ashton Jeanty]] and on contact mitigation as the separating trait for elite backs.
