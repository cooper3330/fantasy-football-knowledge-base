## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) recap a blowout-heavy Week 8. Segments include Christian Watson's tape-charted return from a second ACL tear, a mea culpa on Roman Wilson, a deep dive on Emeka Egbuka's two-game cooldown and Baker Mayfield's man-vs-zone splits, Miami's Tua Tagovailoa eye injury and benching scare with Jalen Waddle carrying the WR1 role, Darren Waller's IR pec injury, a long Tennessee segment comparing rookies Chimere Dike and Elic Ayomanor after Tyler Lockett's release, CJ Stroud's efficient game against a banged-up 49ers defense, and an extended Houston passing-game debate over Xavier Hutchinson, Jayden Higgins, Jaylin Noel and Braxton Berrios snap-share allocation, plus notes on Nico Collins's stationary-X usage and Drake London's importance to Atlanta.
