## Summary
Chris Harris runs through Week 7 injury notes (Terry McLaurin, Chuba Hubbard, Vikings/Cardinals QB uncertainty, Mike Evans, Puka Nacua, Garrett Wilson, Ricky Pearsall, Quentin Johnston, DJ Moore) before a long flex-decisions segment with guest Dave Kluge of FootballGuys.com (not a tracked expert). They debate five 'rough decision' players -- Kimani Vidal/Hassan Haskins at Chargers RB, Keenan Allen, Tajay Spears vs. Tony Pollard, Jameson Williams, and Tre Tucker -- comparing rankings and reasoning stat-by-stat (targets per game, loaded-box rate, yards before/after contact). A short Steelers-Bengals Thursday-night preview covers Jalen Warren vs. Chase Brown, Samaje Perine's role, and Tee Higgins vs. Ja'Marr Chase volume. The back half is a DFS segment with guest Jake Trowbridge (not tracked) reviewing his winning Week 6 DraftKings $5-contest lineup and building his Week 7 lineup (Rashee Rice, Justin Jefferson, Ja'Cory Croskey-Merritt, Quinshon Judkins, JK Dobbins, Courtland Sutton, Keenan Allen, Darren Waller/Mason Taylor, defense picks), mostly game-theory/pricing commentary rather than deep evaluative fantasy takes.
