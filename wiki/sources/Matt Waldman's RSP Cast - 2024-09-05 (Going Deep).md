## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and co-host [[Brandon Angelo]] record the *Going Deep* segment on the eve of the 2024 season. Three blocks: (1) rookies likely to start hot and finish cold — Angelo names [[Xavier Worthy]] and [[Bucky Irving]], Waldman names [[Jayden Daniels]] and, most emphatically, [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], whom he projects at roughly 900-950 yards and WR35-36 rather than his WR15 ADP; (2) post-waiver veteran additions expected to matter — [[Tim Patrick]] to Detroit, [[Samaje Perine]] to Kansas City, [[Christopher Brooks]] to Green Bay, plus [[Adonai Mitchell]] sliding into the Indianapolis slot with [[Josh Downs]] hurt; (3) 2025 draft prospects each host has been studying — Angelo on [[Cam Ward]] and Oregon slot receiver [[Tez Johnson]], Waldman on [[Nicholas Singleton]], [[Kyle Monangai]] and [[Dean Connors]], with a long detour into ball-security grading. Waldman also narrates a live 17th-round keeper-league pick, weighing [[Kimani Vidal]], [[Dylan Laube]], [[Andrei Iosivas]] and [[Ricky Pearsall]].
