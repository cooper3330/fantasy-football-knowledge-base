## Summary
A Black Monday edition of the Feel It or F**k It segment: [[Matt Waldman]] with co-host Bob Harris (not a tracked expert) working through the NFL coaching carousel and what each situation means for the quarterbacks and skill players attached to it. Topics: whether a new Jacksonville coach can succeed with Trent Baalke retained, what [[Trevor Lawrence]] actually is; whether [[Caleb Williams]] makes the Chicago job attractive; Jerod Mayo's firing and [[Drake Maye]]; New Orleans' cap situation; the Jets' ownership problem and Rex Ryan; whether [[Aaron Rodgers]] is finished; Indianapolis keeping Shane Steichen and Chris Ballard around [[Anthony Richardson]]; the Giants keeping Daboll and Schoen after the [[Daniel Jones]] era; Miami keeping Mike McDaniel amid [[Tyreek Hill]] trade talk and the argument that McDaniel only trusts [[Tua Tagovailoa]]; Antonio Pierce in Las Vegas and credit for [[Brock Bowers]]; and Ben Johnson's and Aaron Glenn's head-coaching candidacies. Recurring theme: the coach–GM pairing, and continuity, matter more than the individual hire.
