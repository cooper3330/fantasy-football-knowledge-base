## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) react to the first wave of 2026 NFL free agency, focused heavily on wide receiver movement plus one quarterback deal. Covered: Malik Willis to Miami and the ripple effect on Jaylen Waddle; Romeo Doubs's four-year deal with New England and how he could fit alongside Stefon Diggs's departure, Kayshawn Boutte and a possible AJ Brown trade; Alec Pierce's re-signing in Indianapolis paired with the Michael Pittman trade to Pittsburgh (and Pittman's extension there alongside DK Metcalf); Daniel Jones's new Colts deal and the team's decision-making around it; DJ Moore's trade from Chicago to Buffalo for a second-round pick, including Luther Burden III as his eventual replacement; and Mike Evans signing with San Francisco, including the fit with the Shanahan X-receiver scheme and the effect on Ricky Pearsall and Juwan Jennings. Brief teaser for next week on Jahan Dotson to Atlanta, Jalen Nailor to the Raiders, Rashid Shaheed and Wan'Dale Robinson.
