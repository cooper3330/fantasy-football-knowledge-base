## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] returns from a personal absence (his mother-in-law's death after a 20-year cancer battle) and is joined by co-host James Koh (NOT a tracked expert) for a cut-day reaction show walking through several NFL wide receiver rooms. Harmon opens with a broadside at insiders who report a player is 'having a really strong camp' days before he is released — naming [[Tim Patrick]] and Terrace Marshall — arguing camp reports that contradict roster decisions do not add up. The two then break down Denver (which Harmon calls a bottom-five receiver room), Detroit's late depth additions, Kansas City's seven-receiver roster and the [[Kadarius Toney]] release, Houston's crowded top and bottom, Washington's room after the [[Jahan Dotson]] trade, Minnesota post-J.J. McCarthy injury, and New England's X-receiver competition. The episode closes on a debate about how loosely 'drop' is defined, using a [[Javon Baker]] preseason play.
