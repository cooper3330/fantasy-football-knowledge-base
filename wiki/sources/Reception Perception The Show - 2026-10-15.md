## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) tour the NFL's wide-receiver trade rumor mill ahead of the deadline. They work through named trade candidates from an ESPN report (Graziano/Fowler) -- Brian Thomas Jr, Jerry Jeudy, Quentin Johnston, Rashad Bateman, Juwan Jennings -- debating fit, likely return, and which teams should buy (49ers, Chiefs, Bills, Raiders, Colts, Giants) versus sell (Chargers, Titans, Dolphins, Cardinals, Saints, Bucs, Washington). Along the way they cover Travis Hunter's CB/WR snap split, Malik Nabers' shoulder issue and the Giants' extreme Nabers/Isaiah Likely target concentration (including Likely's Baltimore backstory), Devon Vele and the Saints, Tampa's spread target tree (Godwin, Egbuka, Otten, Irving, Hurst, Gainwell, Johnson), Laquan Treadwell's high-volume low-conversion role in Indianapolis, Dalton Kincaid's disappearance from Buffalo's 13-personnel package tied to DJ Moore's injury and Keon Coleman's forced return to X, and a broader take on league-wide X-receiver scarcity. They close with an extended, enthusiastic breakdown of Christian Watson's and Matthew Golden's breakout seasons in Green Bay.
