## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]) run the rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' format shortly after the release of the 2025 Rookie Scouting Portfolio. Waldman works through the 2025 rookie class: the quarterback pecking order ([[Cam Ward]], [[Shedeur Sanders]], [[Jalen Milroe]], [[Will Howard]], [[Hunter Dekkers]], [[Tyler Shough]]), a long run of small-school and sub-200-pound running backs ([[Kyle Monangai]], [[Jaydon Blue]], [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]], [[Brashard Smith]], [[Marcus Yarns]], [[Lan Larison]]), undersized and slot receivers ([[Tez Johnson]], [[Daniel Jackson]], [[Xavier Restrepo]], [[Bru McCoy]]), the tight end class ([[Mason Taylor]] vs [[Tyler Warren]]), and his view that [[Luther Burden III]] is already a better prospect than [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] was. Also a Giants roster-construction segment arguing for [[Ashton Jeanty]] over an early quarterback.
