## Summary
A *Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked). Three segments: (1) [[Bryce Young]]'s in-season resurgence after his benching, framed through what Angelo calls a quarterback's growing 'inventory of answers' — with [[Anthony Richardson]] and [[Will Levis]] as parallel cases; (2) [[Chase Brown]]'s ceiling in Cincinnati and whether [[Sincere McCormick]] has locked down the Raiders' backfield; (3) the best pure technicians each host has watched in the 2025 draft class — [[Travis Hunter]], [[Cam Skattebo]] and [[Savion Williams]] — closing with Waldman's view that the 2025 receiver class is thin at the top.
