## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) preview the upcoming RP Year 2 receiver profiles, opening with a defense of Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 'disappointing' rookie season. They then work through eight teams that were pegged as wide-receiver-needy pre-draft but didn't add a receiver early -- Falcons, Ravens, Cowboys, Lions, Rams, Saints, Eagles, and Titans -- reading each team's draft inaction as a signal of internal confidence in (or lingering doubt about) their current receiver room. Extensive discussion of Drake London/Kyle Pitts target-share math, Rashad Bateman's contract situation and route-running growth, Jameson Williams's structural role in Detroit's offense, Cooper Kupp's injury/age risk, Chris Olave's fit with Derek Carr, Philadelphia's crowded WR3 competition, and Treylon Burks's uncertain fit under Tennessee's new staff.
