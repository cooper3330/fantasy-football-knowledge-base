## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) record two days before the 2024 season opener. They open with news: [[Ricky Pearsall]] was shot in the chest in San Francisco and left the hospital after one day with no surgery, and [[Ja'Marr Chase]] remains a holdout who has not practiced with the Bengals at all. The bulk of the show is their annual hot takes episode, all wide receiver focused. Harmon's takes: [[Zay Flowers]] clears 1,200 receiving yards; [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] finishes second among rookies in receiving yards behind [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]; [[Nico Collins]] leads the NFL in receiving touchdowns. Off-the-record extras from his Yahoo show: [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] leads Seattle in catches and yards, [[Rome Odunze]] is Chicago's fantasy WR1, [[Diontae Johnson]] finishes as a fantasy WR1. Koh's takes: [[Jordan Whittington]] outgains [[Adonai Mitchell]] and [[Xavier Worthy]]; [[Sam Darnold]] leads Minnesota to the playoffs; [[Javon Baker]] cracks 1,000 yards. Harmon counters the Darnold take with EPA/success-rate data and calls the Rams a fragile house of cards, tweeting that neither [[Cooper Kupp]] nor [[Puka Nacua]] finishes as a top-12 fantasy receiver.
