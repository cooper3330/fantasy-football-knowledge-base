# Feel It Or F@#k It - 2024-04-15

## Summary

*Feel It or F**k It* episode co-hosted by [[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris**
(co-host, not a tracked expert), recorded April 15, 2024, ~11 days before the
NFL Draft. Rapid-fire "feel it or f\*\*k it" takes across current-NFL and
2024-offseason storylines: Tony Pollard/Tyjae Spears backfield-share news in
Tennessee, C.J. Stroud's post-Stefon Diggs-trade dynasty QB1 hype, Joe Burrow's
best-ball ADP value, Tee Higgins dropping his Bengals trade request, DeVonta
Smith's Eagles extension and what it implies for A.J. Brown, Brandon Aiyuk's
trade/extension drama and possible landing spots, CeeDee Lamb's contract
standoff and Dallas's Dak Prescott cap situation (with a Trey Lance aside),
Tua Tagovailoa's outlook under HC Mike McDaniel, the Seattle QB competition
(Geno Smith vs. Sam Howell) and Las Vegas QB competition (Michael Penix Jr.
vs. Aidan O'Connell vs. Gardner Minshew), and Bryce Young's Superflex/Carolina
outlook alongside Diontae Johnson and Adam Thielen. Closes with a media-criticism
segment on NFL-draft analytics/expected-value data and a plug for the 2024 RSP
pre-draft (April 1) and post-draft editions.
