## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) walk through two newly published receptionperception.com profiles: [[Jayden Reed]] (the 'underused' receiver) and [[Josh Downs]] (the 'underrated' receiver).

On Reed: 75 targets / 55 receptions / 857 yards / 6 TD in 2024 on a 73% catch rate. Harmon's charting shows Reed is emphatically not a gadget player — 67% success rate on the nine, 81.6% on the post, ~73% on the corner, 79% on digs and 79% on slants, with those three deep routes making up about a third of his route tree. His 22% post-route participation would be the second-highest mark in Reception Perception history (up from 16.2% as a rookie); screen rate also rose 8.1% to 11.7%. He was second among all receivers in yards on post routes (fantasy points data) behind [[Justin Jefferson]], ahead of [[Alec Pierce]], [[Nico Collins]] and [[Tyreek Hill]]. Negatives: a 12.8% drop rate (vs 5.3% as a prospect and 3.6% as a rookie) and near-identical year-one to year-two success rates — no development. Offsetting that, an 88.9% contested catch rate, a big jump from a poor rookie mark. Per Rich Rebar of Sharp Football, Reed ran only seven routes from one- and two-receiver sets over two seasons (2.6% of team dropbacks in those sets). Koh went on an extended tirade about Green Bay's deployment; Harmon defended the line in his profile that the Packers 'know more than me' and invoked [[Elijah Moore]] and [[Curtis Samuel]] as players whose man-coverage ability never got a real chance.

On Downs: 107 targets / 72 receptions / 803 yards / 5 TD in 14 games, 67% catch rate catching [[Anthony Richardson]] passes. 76.7% vs man (90th pct), 86% vs zone (92nd pct, top-30 all time), 77% vs press (82nd pct) at 5-9, 171 lbs. Harmon calls him possibly the most underrated receiver in the NFL and comps him to [[Tyler Lockett]], with historical database neighbors of 2018 Antonio Brown, 2016 Tyreek Hill, 2021 [[Deebo Samuel]] and 2016 Odell Beckham. 94.4% on flat routes; slant success 76% is the soft spot; nine route regressed. 10th in first downs per route run overall, 8th when lined up wide — near [[Ladd McConkey]] (6th), [[Mike Evans]], [[Nico Collins]], [[Stefon Diggs]]. Only real criticism: went down on first contact on ~68% of plays where he was in space (~55% of his sample catches).

The back half covers Colts alignment: [[Alec Pierce]] as the sacrificial X, [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] as flanker, Downs in the slot, [[Adonai Mitchell]] rotating at X as a 2026 breakout candidate; more 11 personnel expected with [[Tyler Warren]] drafted, and more RPOs with [[Daniel Jones]] expected to start.
