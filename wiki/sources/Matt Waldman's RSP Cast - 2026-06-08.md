## Summary
Matt Waldman (host, tracked expert, Rookie Scouting Portfolio) and Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) run their rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' format across a wide swath of June offseason news: OTAs, mandatory mini-camp timing, and Waldman's dynasty rankings/projections nearing release. Major threads: A.J. Brown's trade to New England and the fallout for Kayshaun Boutte, Demario Douglas, Kyle Williams and rookie TE Eli Raridon; the TreVeyon Henderson vs. Rhamondre Stevenson Patriots backfield; a Jaguars backfield value call on Bhayshul Tuten over Chris Rodriguez Jr.; Chris Godwin's bargain price behind Emeka Egbuka with Ted Hurst/Jalen McMillan/Tez Johnson as the depth chart's back end; Rashee Rice's legal situation and Jalen Royals as a contingency; Saquon Barkley's rebound outlook under new Eagles play-caller Sean Mannion, modeled on Todd Gurley's 2017-18 seasons, plus a skeptical read on DeVonta Smith's post-Brown hype; the Jalen Waddle/Courtland Sutton Broncos WR1 debate; patience on Xavier Worthy behind Rashee Rice and Travis Kelce; Tyler Warren's route-tree ceiling and Daniel Jones sustainability question (Waldman skeptical, Harris more bullish -- explicit disagreement); Brian Thomas Jr.'s Trevor Lawrence-dependent outlook; Kyler Murray/Vikings target distribution risk for Justin Jefferson, T.J. Hockenson and Jordan Addison; Justin Herbert's scheme-driven footwork changes under Mike McDaniel; and the Carolina backfield/receiver picture (Chuba Hubbard's aging-curve concern per a Brandon Angelo framework, Jonathan Brooks and Trevor Etienne as buy targets, Tetairoa McMillan sliding into a Drake London-style role behind Chris Brazell).
