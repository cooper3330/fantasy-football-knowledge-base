## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh (recording just before the [[A.J. Brown]] trade to New England became official) break down the trade compensation (2028 first, 2027 fifth) and Harmon's charting-based case that Brown's decline started at 29, contrasting his hoped-for graceful aging (Stefon Diggs comp) with the cliff-decline risk (Dez Bryant, Allen Robinson comps). They then cover [[Drake London]]'s new contract extension and Harmon's tier-two ranking of him among NFL receivers, and close with a skeptical [[Xavier Worthy]] profile covering his size limitations, 2025 shoulder injury, usage shift, and the Chiefs' broader passing-game concerns involving [[Rashee Rice]] and [[Cyrus Allen]].
