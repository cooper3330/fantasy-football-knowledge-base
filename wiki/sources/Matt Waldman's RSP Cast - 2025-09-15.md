## Summary
Week 2 reaction edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys / SiriusXM Fantasy Sports Radio; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Rapid-fire prompts covering the Joe Burrow turf-toe injury and its knock-on effects in Cincinnati, the Seattle backfield split, Pittsburgh's viability and target tree, the Giants' quarterback and backfield situation, Baltimore's receiver pecking order, Cleveland's backfield, Green Bay's tight ends and receivers, Washington's offense, Dallas' receivers, Atlanta's tight end, Minnesota's passing game, and the New England backfield.

Waldman leaned heavily on film specifics: box-count advantages on Seattle's run designs, route pacing on George Pickens' sluggo versus KaVontae Turpin's slant, Rodgers' lost burst out of the pocket, and Rhamondre Stevenson's contact balance and footwork. Bob Harris supplied target counts and beat-reporter context (Bob Condotta of the Seattle Times on Seattle's backfield; Mark Kaboly on Freiermuth as a potential heartbeat of the Steelers offense).
