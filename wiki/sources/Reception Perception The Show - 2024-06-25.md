## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) continue a two-part breakdown of 2024 offensive coordinator changes and what they mean for pass-catchers. Teams covered: Saints (Clint Kubiak replacing Pete Carmichael, with A.T. Perry, Chris Olave, Rashid Shaheed and Bub Means discussed), Eagles (Kellen Moore replacing Brian Johnson, with heavy focus on whether A.J. Brown or DeVonta Smith plays more slot, plus rookies Aeneas Smith and Johnny Wilson), Steelers (Arthur Smith replacing Matt Canada, covering George Pickens' scheme fit, Roman Wilson's rookie profile, and a Brandon Aiyuk trade-rumor tangent), Commanders (Kliff Kingsbury replacing Eric Bieniemi, covering Terry McLaurin, Jahan Dotson, Zach Ertz and rookie Ben Sinnott), and a faster pass over Bears (Shane Waldron/Caleb Williams), Chargers (Greg Roman/Justin Herbert) and Patriots (Alex Van Pelt). Closes with a broader point that coaching deployment (cited: Michael Pittman Jr. under Shane Steichen) often outweighs individual receiver talent in determining fantasy outcomes.
