## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) run Vegas over/under betting lines against Koh's own rookie WR season projections for the 2024 class, going market by market: Marvin Harrison Jr. (ARI), Malik Nabers (NYG), Rome Odunze (CHI), Brian Thomas Jr. (JAX), Xavier Worthy (KC), Xavier Legette (CAR), Keon Coleman (BUF) and Ladd McConkey (LAC). For each, Koh gives median and high receiving-yard/reception/TD projections and both hosts call over or under against the Vegas (or DraftKings) line, debating target-share ceilings, offensive-coordinator trust, and depth-chart competition. Along the way they rank the talent tiers on the Chiefs' non-Kelce receiver corps (Marquise Brown vs. Rashee Rice vs. Worthy), debate Gabe Davis's role in Jacksonville, and note Diontae Johnson's new Panthers role as CAR's X receiver ahead of Adam Thielen and Legette.
