## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on the Week 1 injury wave in San Francisco, a rookie receiver report card, and FanDuel player props.

The 49ers segment covered George Kittle to IR, Jauan Jennings' shoulder MRI, [[Brock Purdy]]'s non-throwing shoulder plus toe, the reported pursuit of another quarterback and a possible Mac Jones start, whether Kyle Shanahan is overworking [[Christian McCaffrey]], and why Harmon thinks [[Ricky Pearsall]] still deserves ten targets a week regardless of quarterback. Harmon announced the Reception Perception Notebook replacing his rest-of-season rankings, with in-season charting notes on [[Garrett Wilson]], [[A.J. Brown]], [[Keon Coleman]], [[Kayshon Boutte]] and [[Rome Odunze]].

The rookie report card ran through [[Travis Hunter]], [[Tetairoa McMillan]], [[Emeka Egbuka]] and [[Matthew Golden]], with alignment data on each and a long detour into Green Bay's receiver rotation. Props segment: [[Jayden Reed]], [[Romeo Doubs]] receptions and [[Jared Goff]] passing yards.
