## Summary
Week 18 wrap and end-of-regular-season review with [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert), both recording while sick. Opens on the Lions' shutdown of Minnesota on Sunday Night Football — Aaron Glenn playing man coverage on roughly 67% of dropbacks per NextGen and having Amik Robertson shadow [[Justin Jefferson]] on over 80% of his routes — and a poor game from [[Sam Darnold]], including a debated end-zone incompletion to Jefferson.

The bulk of the episode is [[Tyreek Hill]]'s 'I'm out bro' exit interview and a long landing-spot tour (Washington, Rams, Denver, San Francisco, Cincinnati, Minnesota, Chargers, Detroit, Dallas), including a speculative [[Deebo Samuel]]-for-Hill swap. Then [[Drake London]]'s 18-target, 187-yard, two-touchdown finale and what [[Michael Penix Jr.]]'s arm unlocks; [[Quentin Johnston]]'s 13-catch, 186-yard breakout game and full-season turnaround; [[Mike Evans]] reaching 1,000 yards for the 11th straight season; [[Jalen McMillan]]'s late-season surge; and [[Jahan Dotson]]'s 11-target Week 18. Harmon also explained his deliberate 'silver linings' analytical posture versus Koh's fast-cut instinct.
