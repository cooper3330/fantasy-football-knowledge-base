## Summary
Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris run a rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' episode as training camps open. Topics: Seattle's crowded backfield ([[Jadarian Price]], [[George Holani]], [[Zach Charbonnet]], [[Emmanuel Wilson]]) and receiving pecking order ([[Rashid Shaheed]] vs [[Cooper Kupp]] vs [[Tory Horton]]); the Jets' quarterback bridge plan with rookie [[Cade Klubnik]] and its parallel to Arizona's [[Jacoby Brissett]]/[[Carson Beck]] situation; a long, skeptical take on the [[Jackson Dart]]-to-Lamar-Jackson comparison plus concussion concerns, and the unsettled Giants receiver room around an injured [[Malik Nabers]] and [[Darnell Mooney]]; the Raiders' [[Kirk Cousins]]/[[Fernando Mendoza]] QB timeline and what it means for [[Brock Bowers]]; Baltimore's thin receiver room behind [[Rashad Bateman]] ([[Elijah Surratt]], [[Jacoby Lane]]); New Orleans' [[Alvin Kamara]]/[[Travis Etienne Jr.]] committee risk; a preference for [[DeAndre Swift]] over similarly-priced backs; skepticism on [[Jalen Tolbert]]'s Dolphins OTA buzz; dismissal of [[Roman Wilson]] coach-speak; rising [[Stefon Diggs]] ADP and Commanders speculation; and late-round Buffalo receiver value in [[Skylar Bell]] and [[Keon Coleman]].
