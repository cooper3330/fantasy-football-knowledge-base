## Summary
Chris Harris previews the opening of 2026 NFL free agency with fill-in guest Andy Behrens (Yahoo/paywall analyst, not a tracked expert, subbing for the usual guest Patrick Daugherty). They run through the wave of cap-driven releases and tags: Kyler Murray released by Arizona, Daniel Jones transition-tagged by Indianapolis, Tua Tagovailoa expected to be cut, Kirk Cousins cut, Tyreek Hill released, Stefon Diggs released, TJ Hockenson expected cut. They discuss the running back market re-opening (Kenneth Walker III, Breece Hall's franchise tag standoff, Travis Etienne's release, David Montgomery traded to Houston pushing Woody Marks into a timeshare, Bucky Irving comps, Rico Dowdle, Rachaad White, Tyler Allgeier signing in Denver, RJ Harvey, J.K. Dobbins, Kenneth Gainwell, Aaron Jones, Alvin Kamara, James Conner) and the wide receiver market (AJ Brown trade rumors, George Pickens' tag, Alec Pierce as the top receiver available, Michael Pittman Jr., Mike Evans, Jauan Jennings, Rashid Shaheed, Wan'Dale Robinson, Romeo Doubs, DJ Moore/Luther Burden III, Calvin Ridley, Brandon Aiyuk) plus tight ends Kyle Pitts (franchise tagged) and TJ Hockenson. Javonte Williams' re-signing with Dallas is treated as the one settled outcome discussed. Malik Willis is flagged as the likely top free-agent QB contract.
