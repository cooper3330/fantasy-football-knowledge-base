## Summary
Matt Harmon and James Koh (co-host, not tracked) cover three main topics: Tua Tagovailoa's benching in Miami for Quinn Ewers and what it says about teams paying quarterbacks; Tampa Bay's wide receiver room returning to full health in week 15 (Mike Evans, Chris Godwin, Jalen McMillan, Emeka Egbuka) plus Kirk Cousins' bizarre career dominance of the Bucs; and a full re-rank/retrospective of the 2024 wide receiver draft class in year two, discussing why the class is on pace for zero 1,000-yard receivers. Closes with a FanDuel pick segment (Mike Evans catches prop, DK Metcalf anytime TD prop).
