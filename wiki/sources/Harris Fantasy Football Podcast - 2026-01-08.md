## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a non-football reflection on current events, then is joined by untracked guest Jim McCormick (ESPN NBA writer, longtime fantasy football friend) to review roughly a dozen 2025 fantasy 'outlier' seasons and debate which are believable going forward. Topics: [[Trevor Lawrence]]'s late-season rushing-TD surge, [[Derrick Henry]]'s age-32 season and running back aging curves, the second-half collapses of [[Jonathan Taylor]] and [[Jahmyr Gibbs]], goal-line usage anomalies for [[Saquon Barkley]], [[Bijan Robinson]], [[Breece Hall]], [[Chase Brown]] and [[Kenneth Walker III]]/[[Zach Charbonnet]], [[Rhamondre Stevenson]] and [[TreVeyon Henderson]]'s Patriots backfield, [[Davante Adams]]'s touchdown-driven age-32 season, [[Jameson Williams]]'s boom/bust profile, [[Emeka Egbuka]]'s rookie fade, and tight ends [[Trey McBride]] and [[Kyle Pitts]] posting outlier point totals against Harris's standing anti-early-TE value-based-drafting stance.
