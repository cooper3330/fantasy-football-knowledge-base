## Summary
Chris Harris hosts guest Paul Pertichese (SaturdayToSundayFootball.com, not a tracked expert) for a deep dive on the 2026 NFL Draft running back class ahead of the draft, part of Harris's annual pre-draft coverage series (WRs covered with Matt Waldman the prior week; QBs/TEs next week). Pertichese frames the class as very top-heavy: Jeremiah Love stands alone as a true blue-chip prospect, comped to a blend of Todd Gurley and Bijan Robinson, while the rest of the top-11 or so backs are bunched closely with modest, similar ceilings. They work through Jadarian Price, Mike Washington, Emmett Johnson, Nicholas Singleton, Jonah Coleman, DeMond Claiborne, Seth McGowan, Kaytron Allen, Adam Randall, Jayden Ott and Eli Heidenreich, plus Alabama's Jam Miller, with running comparisons to veteran and recent-draft comps. Harris also opens with an extended non-football aside on doomscrolling and media negativity before getting into football.
