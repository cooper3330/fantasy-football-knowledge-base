## Summary
Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris open with training-camp calendar talk, then work through contract-holdout risk (CeeDee Lamb, Brandon Aiyuk, Alvin Kamara) and a series of ADP value/committee-backfield debates: Kyren Williams vs. Blake Corum in LA, James Conner/Trey Benson/Michael Carter in Arizona, Kenneth Walker III/Zach Charbonnet in Seattle under new OC Ryan Grubb, and Zamir White/Dylan Laube in Las Vegas. They cover TE value plays (Isaiah Likely, Taysom Hill) and run the 'feel it or f**k it' segment on several of Waldman's '15 fantasy prospects under the ADP radar' (Braelon Allen, Isaac Guerendo, Javon Baker, Ja'Lynn Polk, Calvin Austin III, Noah Brown, Rashad Bateman). Waldman closes with mattwaldman.com/RSP pricing info.
