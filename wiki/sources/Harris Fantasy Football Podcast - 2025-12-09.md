## Summary
Chris Harris recaps Week 14 game film not yet covered (Chargers-Eagles MNF, Dolphins-Jets, Vikings-Commanders, Broncos-Raiders, Titans-Browns) and then runs a Week 15 waiver wire countdown with guest Jeff Bell (Football Guys, not tracked). Harris breaks down the Chargers' OT win over the Eagles (Herbert playing hurt but unsustainable; Hurts turnover-prone but still a top-10 QB start; Barkley limited to five second-half carries), the Dolphins-Jets blowout (Achane's rib injury, Jalen Wright emerging over Ollie Gordon II, Tyrod Taylor's groin injury gutting the Jets passing game), the Vikings-Commanders game (Jayden Daniels re-injured, Zach Ertz's season-ending ACL tear off a Mariota throw, Jefferson doubled into a quiet day, Addison's garbage-time TDs), the Broncos-Raiders game (Troy Franklin buried by Pat Bryant, RJ Harvey's best tape of the season, Ashton Jeanty's continued bust arc), and the Titans-Browns 'weather game' (Judkins, Shedeur Sanders' Bortles-esque meltdown, Tony Pollard's long TDs). Harris and Jeff Bell then each rank their top-5 Week 15 waiver adds, touching tight ends (Freiermuth, Gesicki, Parkinson, Tinsley, Iosivas, Likely), Pat Bryant, Devin Singletary, Jalen Wright, Kayshon Boutte, and Blake Corum (both hosts' #1 pick), plus honorable mentions (Brian Robinson Jr., Emmanuel Wilson, Devaughn Vele, Tyrone Tracy Jr.) and Trey Benson's activation off IR.
