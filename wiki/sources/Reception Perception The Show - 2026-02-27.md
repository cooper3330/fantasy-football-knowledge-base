## Summary
Matt Harmon (at the NFL Combine) and untracked co-host James Koh preview the NFC South team by team, from worst 2025 record to best: Saints, Falcons, Buccaneers, Panthers. Heavy focus on personnel-grouping trends (11/12 personnel shifts for New Orleans and Atlanta), the Chris Olave vs AJ Brown tier debate, New Orleans' offseason receiver additions (Ja'Lynn Polk, Devaughn Vele) and Alvin Kamara's declining workhorse role; Atlanta's new Kevin Stefanski/Tommy Rees staff, Michael Penix's injury history, Kirk Cousins' expected cap cut, and Tyson Bagent trade buzz; Tampa Bay's new OC Zach Robinson, Mike Evans' likely free-agent departure, and Emeka Egbuka's rookie-season home/road split analysis; Carolina's Tetairoa McMillan/Jalen Coker/Xavier Legette receiver trio and Bryce Young's non-extension verdict.
