## Summary
Going Deep segment hosted by [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]]. First half: the non-physical/conceptual traits they look for at each position -- quarterback game management and 'black zone' composure, running back down-and-distance discipline and pre-snap box counts, and receiver/tight end catch technique plus college-program pro-readiness. Second half: each host names the prospect who surprised him most this cycle -- Waldman picks QB [[Fernando Mendoza]] (Indiana), Angelo picks RB [[Robert Henry Jr.]] (UTSA). Closing segment stress-tests a fantasy-community model claiming low college slot-snap percentage predicts NFL bust risk for 2026 receiver prospects, testing it against [[Malik Nabers]], [[Drake London]], [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Makai Lemon]] and [[Carnell Tate]]. Show closes by teasing Emmett Johnson and the Matt Nagy/Jackson Dart pairing for a future episode.
