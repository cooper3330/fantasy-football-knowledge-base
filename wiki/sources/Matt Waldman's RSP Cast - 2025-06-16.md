## Summary
A post-minicamp 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-fire episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Bob Harris supplies the ADP prompts and Waldman answers with projections.

Main threads: the Raiders offense under new OC Chip Kelly — Waldman calls [[Ashton Jeanty]]'s top-10 ADP premature (a Marshawn Lynch/Reggie Bush blend, comped for market risk to Clyde Edwards-Helaire 2020), sees a real [[Raheem Mostert]] timeshare, likes [[Geno Smith]] as a QB22 value, buys high on [[Brock Bowers]], and calls [[Jakobi Meyers]] an overlooked eighth-round pick. Dynasty darts graded: [[Tre Tucker]] and [[Jack Bech]] down, [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]] up.

Backfields: Denver's [[J.K. Dobbins]] 1A / [[RJ Harvey]] 1B split (Waldman projects Dobbins ~1,100 scrimmage yards, Harvey ~900), Houston's [[Nick Chubb]]–[[Joe Mixon]] ankle uncertainty, New Orleans' [[Kendre Miller]]/[[Devin Neal]] depth behind Alvin Kamara, Chicago's [[DeAndre Swift]] vs rookie [[Kyle Monangai]], and [[Bijan Robinson]] over [[Saquon Barkley]] at the 2/3 turn ([[A.J. Dillon]] as a volume-shaving signal in Philadelphia). Closes on [[Christian McCaffrey]] as Waldman's overall RB1 and San Francisco's receiver hierarchy — [[Jauan Jennings]] as a steal, [[Ricky Pearsall]] as a small-sample enthusiasm risk.

Concepts touched: [[Ambiguous Backfields and Market Risk Aversion]], [[Fantasy Quarterback Value vs NFL Quarterback Quality]], [[Coaching Talent Elevation]], [[Preseason ADP vs In-Season Production]], [[Dynasty]].
