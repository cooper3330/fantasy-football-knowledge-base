## Summary
Bob Harris was absent, so Matt Waldman ran the 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-fire debate segment with untracked RotoWire analyst Ian Hartitz, covering roughly 30 players across a wide range of teams. Format was quick takes: a proposition read, each host says 'feel it' or 'fuck it,' then elaborates. Topics included Charlie Kolar's Chargers TE outlook, Luther Burden III's year-two breakout case, DJ Moore's fit in Buffalo, Bryce Lance and Jordan Tyson in New Orleans (plus Alvin Kamara's workload debate), Josh Downs and the shaky Colts passing game (with Daniel Jones's Achilles recovery discussed via a Kirk Cousins/Dan Marino comp), Jayden Reed vs. Matthew Golden in Green Bay, the San Francisco WR shuffle after Ricky Pearsall's injury (De'Zhaun Stribling, Deebo Samuel, Mike Evans, Jauan Jennings), Jeremiah Love's Cardinals outlook, Parker Washington's breakout case and Travis Hunter's dart-throw value in Jacksonville (plus Tank Bigsby and Quinshon Judkins comps), Rashee Rice, Stefon Diggs's free-agent fit in Washington, the crowded Denver backfield (Jonah Coleman, JK Dobbins, RJ Harvey), Jayden Blue in Dallas, Tetairoa McMillan's slot usage question, Bryce Young skepticism, Chris Brazzell, Cyrus Allen and Jalen Royals in Kansas City, Malik Benson in Las Vegas, and a closing New England touch-split note on TreVeyon Henderson and Rhamondre Stevenson.
