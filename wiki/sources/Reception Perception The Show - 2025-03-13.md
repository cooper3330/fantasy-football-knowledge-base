## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) record on a Wednesday afternoon in mid-March 2025 to react to the second wave of NFL free agency. Harmon brings fresh Reception Perception charting to several of the players discussed.

Main segments: [[Cooper Kupp]]'s release by the Rams and whether he is a declining slot-only player; [[DeAndre Hopkins]] to Baltimore on a one-year, $6M deal and how he fits behind [[Zay Flowers]] and [[Rashad Bateman]]; [[Chris Godwin]]'s three-year, $55M re-signing with Tampa Bay and the continuity of the Bucs offense including [[Mike Evans]] and [[Jalen McMillan]]; [[Daniel Jones]] to Indianapolis and what it means for [[Anthony Richardson]], [[Michael Pittman Jr.]], [[Josh Downs]], [[Adonai Mitchell]] and [[Alec Pierce]]; [[Marquise Brown]] re-signing in Kansas City alongside [[Rashee Rice]] and [[Xavier Worthy]]; Houston trading for [[Christian Kirk]] and away from Laramie Tunsil, with concerns for [[C.J. Stroud]] and [[Nico Collins]]; and a run of minor deals — [[Darius Slayton]], [[Josh Palmer]], [[Dyami Brown]], [[Mack Hollins]], [[Nick Westbrook-Ikhine]] and [[Marquez Valdes-Scantling]].

Concept threads: [[Sacrificial X Receiver]], [[Aging Curves and Career Longevity]] and [[Wide Receiver Free Agency Contract Tiers]].
