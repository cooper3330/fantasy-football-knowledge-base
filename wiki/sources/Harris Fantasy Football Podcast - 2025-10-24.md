## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a Thursday Night Football recap (Chargers 27, Vikings 14) touching on Justin Herbert, Oronde Gadsden II, Kimani Vidal, Quentin Johnston, Ladd McConkey, and the Vikings' J.J. McCarthy/Carson Wentz/Aaron Jones-Jordan Mason situation, plus a quick Week 8 injury roundup. The bulk of the show is the annual midseason awards. Chris solo-hosts the six 'Huggy' awards (Fantasy MVP, Fantasy LVP, Likeliest to Fall Off a Cliff, Rookie of the Year, Best Buy-Low Candidate, and a Bold Midseason Call), naming Javonte Williams, Nico Collins (with Brian Thomas Jr. as runner-up), Quinshon Judkins, Cam Skattebo, Zay Flowers, and a Rams-to-the-Super-Bowl prediction respectively. Cousin Josh (untracked co-host) then joins for the football and non-football 'Baggy' awards, covering DeAndre Swift (cliff), Zay Flowers (redemption), Kenneth Walker III (won't bounce back, with Zach Charbonnet's emergence discussed via Seahawks observer Sam Gold), a coach hot-seat segment (Mike McDaniel, Aaron Glenn -- not wiki-tracked entities), Derrick Henry (bold call), and the marquee Fantasy LVP Rest of the Way award going to J.K. Dobbins over Chris's objection, with Kyren Williams raised as an alternate.
