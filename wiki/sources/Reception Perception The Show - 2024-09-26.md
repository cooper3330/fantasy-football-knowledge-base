## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) review Week 3 film ahead of Week 4 of the 2024 season. Harmon walks back his own Week 1 concern on [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] after charting Weeks 2-3, diagnoses the Texans' seven-point dud against Minnesota (offensive line, 11 penalties, Brian Flores disguising coverage, the 11-personnel identity change) and its knock-on effects for [[Nico Collins]], [[Tank Dell]] and [[Stefon Diggs]]. He covers the Saints' offensive line injuries after the Eagles loss, the [[DeVonta Smith]] concussion and whether [[Jahan Dotson]] can fill in, a long Chiefs segment on [[Xavier Worthy]], [[Rashee Rice]] and [[Travis Kelce]], the [[Brandon Aiyuk]] slow start versus [[Jauan Jennings]] breakout, and two off-radar receivers in [[Luke McCaffrey]] and [[Jalen Tolbert]]. Recurring frame: the modern NFL prioritizes yards-after-catch and zone-beating reliability over small route-runners who separate but cannot finish.
