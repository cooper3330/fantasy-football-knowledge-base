## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with news: [[Terry McLaurin]] signs his Commanders extension after a four-week holdout; [[Joe Mixon]] starts the year on NFI (at least four games missed), pushing Harris to raise [[Nick Chubb]], [[Dameon Pierce]] and rookie [[Woody Marks]]; the Raiders sign [[Amari Cooper]] amid a [[Jakobi Meyers]] trade request; [[Tyjae Spears]] may head to IR; [[Kenny Pickett]] is traded to Las Vegas.

The main segment is a counting-down conversation with guest Ben Gretch of the Stealing Signals newsletter (NOT a tracked expert) on 'players we might be wrong about' — i.e. takes each host holds but least trusts. Gretch: [[Xavier Worthy]], [[Brock Bowers]], [[Calvin Ridley]], [[Ricky Pearsall]], [[Matthew Golden]]. Harris: [[Mark Andrews]], the Dallas offense ([[Dak Prescott]], [[George Pickens]], [[CeeDee Lamb]]), [[TreVeyon Henderson]], [[Alvin Kamara]], [[Breece Hall]]. The two are explicitly on opposite sides on Worthy, Bowers and Pearsall.
