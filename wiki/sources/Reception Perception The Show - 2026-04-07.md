## Summary
Matt Harmon hosts solo (co-host James Koh on vacation) with guest Matt Waldman previewing Waldman's newly-released 2026 Rookie Scouting Portfolio. They open with a class-level overview, then debate a slate of 2026 draft WR prospects one by one: Carnell Tate (consensus WR1), KC Concepcion (catch-technique/drop debate), Jordan Tyson (ceiling vs. readiness/injury debate), Chris Brazzell (Tennessee 'Looney Tunes' stigma), Malachi Fields (RAS skepticism vs. Waldman's top-five grade, Keon Coleman conversion comp), then rapid-fire rounds covering Antonio Williams vs. Germie Bernard, a flag-plant sleeper (Skylar Bell), a dynasty stash (Deion Burks), and players each host is scared to/not to draft (Chris Bell, De'Zhaun Stribling). Comparisons are drawn throughout to established NFL players including Tetairoa McMillan, Trey Harris, Khalil Shakir, Cedric Tillman, Jalen Hyatt and Keon Coleman.
