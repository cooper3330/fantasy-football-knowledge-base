## Summary
Chris Harris opens solo running through a wave of 49ers camp news (last day of July, almanac launching Monday): the CMC backup race (Isaac Guerendo limited by shoulder surgery, Jordan James out 2-4 weeks with a fractured rib, Khalil Herbert a camp body only, third-round rookie [[Kalen Black]] getting the leg up), Ricky Pearsall's knee swelling and possible surgery cratering his rank, a resulting bump for Mike Evans, an increased but modest rank for rookie [[De'Zhaun Stribling]], and the Deebo Samuel reunion on a short one-year deal. He also runs down non-Niners camp notes: Jahmyr Gibbs and Bijan Robinson both holding in for new deals, Malik Nabers avoiding the PUP list amid conflicting NY beat reports, Rome Odunze declaring his surgically repaired foot fine, and brief injury/role notes on Chris Rodriguez Jr. and Jaylen Noel. He also pitches pre-orders of his novel Pound Foolish (releasing Sept 1) and a four-city bar-tour meetup in late August.

Matt Harmon (Yahoo Fantasy, Reception Perception) then joins for the last of the July AFC North training-camp preview shows, walking division-by-division through what's worth tracking versus noise: Steelers (Aaron Rodgers's high floor, the Jalen Warren/Rico Dowdle RB timeshare, skepticism on any Caleb Johnson buzz, Michael Pittman Jr./DK Metcalf as settled options, Germie Bernard as a dynasty speed-dial glue guy); Ravens (Zay Flowers as a rising favorite, the Mark Andrews/Isaiah Likely contract-extension oddity, rookies Jacoby Lane and Elijah Surratt, skepticism on Adam Randall as a name-only handcuff behind Justice Hill and Derrick Henry); Bengals (settled Burrow/Chase/Higgins outlooks, Joe Burrow's under-center scheme evolution as a wildcard, Chase Brown's ADP/workload risk and Tahj Brooks as an unproven black box, speculation the Bengals could trade for a complementary back like James Connor); and Browns (Quinshon Judkins and Dylan Sampson's passing-down split, Harold Fannin Jr.'s groin-surgery recovery, the Shedeur Sanders/Deshaun Watson battle Harmon says is not really close, and the Casey Concepcion/Denzel Boston rookie WRs against Jerry Jeudy and Cedric Tillman, with Isaiah Bond also pushing for reps).
