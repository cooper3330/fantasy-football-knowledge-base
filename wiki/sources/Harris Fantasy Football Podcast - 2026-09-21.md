## Summary
Chris Harris runs solo film review of nine Week 2 games (Chiefs-Colts OT thriller, Saints-Ravens, Jaguars-Broncos, Bengals-Texans, Steelers-Patriots, Seahawks-Cardinals, plus a Giants-Rams MNF preview), then brings on Patrick Doherty of Roto World (untracked) to cover Vikings-Bears, Eagles-Titans and Raiders-Chargers. Heavy focus on backfield committees (Chiefs/Broncos/Cardinals/Chargers/Steelers/Patriots), receiver target distribution after injuries (Colts, Texans, Cardinals), and several in-game injuries (Alec Pierce, Ronnie Stanley, Kelvin Banks, J.K. Dobbins, Jadarian Price, Rico Dowdle, Caleb Williams, Saquon Barkley, David Njoku, Puka Nacua questionable for MNF).
