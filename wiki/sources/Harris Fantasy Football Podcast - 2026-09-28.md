## Summary
Chris Harris's Monday game-film review of Week 3, watched start to finish. Solo for the first half covering Bills-Chargers, Raiders-Saints, Jets-Lions, Texans-Colts, Patriots-Jaguars, and Chiefs-Dolphins, then a Monday-night Bears-Eagles preview, then a guest segment with Jamie Eisenberg (CBS Fantasy, not tracked) covering Ravens-Cowboys, Vikings-Buccaneers, and Rams-Broncos. Heavy running back injury theme: Travis Etienne (hamstring), Breece Hall (thigh), De'Von Achane (knee, pessimistic), Bucky Irving (back, undiagnosed), Baker Mayfield (dislocated thumb), Caleb Williams (hamstring, ~month), plus Justin Jefferson's absence and RJ Harvey banged up. Also a notably scathing take on Quentin Johnston and a full teardown of Drake May's performance.
