## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) run through Week 15, which Harmon framed as an 'Empire Strikes Back' bounce-back week for the wide receiver position after a season he attributes largely to bad injury luck at the top of the position — a conclusion he says he reached in a 2,000-word Yahoo piece. Segments: [[Nico Collins]] shut down by Jalen Ramsey (17 yards on six targets), which validated prior guest Patrick Peterson's prediction and opened a discussion of separator styles (Collins as a long-strider vs [[Garrett Wilson]] as a short-area shifty winner) and the Richard Sherman comp for big physical corners; a long Miami segment on [[Tua Tagovailoa]], Mike McDaniel, motion volume, pure-progression offenses vs line-of-scrimmage quarterbacking, and the pecking order of [[Jonnu Smith]], [[De'Von Achane]], [[Tyreek Hill]] and [[Jaylen Waddle]]; Seattle's pass protection collapse and what it means for [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], [[Geno Smith]] and [[Sam Howell]]; the [[A.J. Brown]] target-share controversy resolving into an 11-target game alongside [[DeVonta Smith]], plus a [[Jalen Hurts]]-as-Russell-Wilson framing; the Jets' late-season surge with [[Davante Adams]] and [[Aaron Rodgers]], including a Rodgers-to-Cleveland scheme-fit thought experiment and the [[Jerry Jeudy]] 'Jameis Winston effect'; and a closing deep dive on [[Jalen Coker]], [[Xavier Legette]]'s misuse at X, and where [[Bryce Young]] actually stands.
