## Summary
Chris Harris opens with news that Josh Jacobs was placed on the commissioner's exempt list for a domestic violence matter, walking through suspension precedent (Adrian Peterson, Greg Hardy, Kareem Hunt) and how the Packers' backfield (Marshawn Lloyd, Chris Brooks, Caleb Johnson, who was just traded in from Pittsburgh) should now be ranked. He also covers a Keenan Allen DWI arrest with no rank change yet. The main segment is a countdown with guest Scott Pianowski (Yahoo Fantasy, untracked) of five late-round players each of them loves drafting: Harris picks Jakobi Meyers, Khalil Shakir, Woody Marks (shared with Pianowski), Denzel Boston, and Tyler Allgeier as his number one; Pianowski picks Emmett Johnson, Jordan Love, Adonai Mitchell, Tre Tucker, and Juwan Johnson (also Harris's number one). Along the way they discuss Jalen Nailor, KC Concepcion, Jordan Tyson, Brenton Strange, and the Cardinals backfield situation (Jeremiah Love, James Conner on IR, Trey Benson out for the year). Episode closes with honorable mentions and sponsor reads.
