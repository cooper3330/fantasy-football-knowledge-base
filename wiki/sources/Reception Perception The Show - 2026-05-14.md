## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) debut Reception Perception's new player-comparison tool (built by Zach Miller), which returns the ten most comparable charted seasons for any WR since 2014. They run it on several year-two receivers -- Tory Horton, Romeo Doubs and Luther Burden III -- then move into three fresh year-two rookie profiles: Jack Bech (Raiders), Travis Hunter (Jaguars) and Jaylin Noel (Texans), with substantial side discussion of Brian Thomas Jr., Jayden Higgins, Christian Kirk, Xavier Hutchinson, Tank Dell, Nico Collins and the Jaguars' shift toward 12-personnel (compared to the Packers). The show closes with a tangent on C.J. Stroud's disastrous playoff performance versus his strong regular season, and skepticism about Houston fixing its run game by signing David Montgomery instead of drafting a back; Harmon also credits his own prospect work on Woody Marks and notes Mike Washington's profile went live.
