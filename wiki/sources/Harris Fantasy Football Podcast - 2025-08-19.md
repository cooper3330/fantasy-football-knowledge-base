## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts a full-episode rookie preview with tracked expert [[Matt Waldman]] (Football Guys / Rookie Scouting Portfolio), recorded mid-August 2025 with drafts a couple of weeks away. The frame Harris sets up front: he is broadly skeptical of rookies in [[Redraft (Standard)]] and says he will never take one in the first round, so each name is checked against its current ADP rather than its long-term profile. Waldman supplies statistical projections for nearly every rookie discussed.

Two segments go to running backs — Harris calls it a deep back class and a thinner receiver class. Waldman projects [[Ashton Jeanty]] at 1,100-1,200 rushing yards with limited receiving work in Pete Carroll's offense; sees [[Omarion Hampton]] in a near-even split with a healthy [[Najee Harris]]; likes [[Kaleb Johnson]] as a late-sixth/early-seventh value while criticizing Steelers fans and media rather than the team; and calls [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] an 'Aaron Jones starter kit'. Waldman also gives a detailed non-doctor explanation of Najee Harris's eye injury (hyphema) and why the five-to-six-week timeline is credible.

On receivers, Waldman goes against the grain on [[Tetairoa McMillan]] (under 800 yards; a zone-beater who will struggle vs press man), is measured on [[Travis Hunter]] and [[Emeka Egbuka]], and is bullish on [[Luther Burden III]] despite a rough August. He projects a near three-way split between Burden, [[Rome Odunze]] and [[DJ Moore]]. At tight end he prefers [[Colston Loveland]] to [[Tyler Warren]] and rates [[Mason Taylor]] above Warren as well.

Waldman also disagrees explicitly with [[Matt Harmon]]'s charting-based read on McMillan, and Harris notes that his cousin and Pat Doherty of Rotoworld (both untracked) have called Kaleb Johnson a bust.
