## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) open with brief non-fantasy discussion of John Harbaugh's firing in Baltimore before running their annual season-end wide receiver superlative awards, using Reception Perception charting data, NextGenStats and Fantasy Points tracking. Categories covered: best underneath WR (1-8 air yards), best deep threat WR (20+ air yards), best vs. man coverage, best vs. zone coverage, best contested-catch/ball-winner, best with the ball in his hands (YAC), biggest surprise, and best overall WR. The episode closes with a three-pick wild-card weekend segment (AJ Brown, Christian Watson, Jalen Coker props) and listener reviews.
