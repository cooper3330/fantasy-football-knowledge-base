## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] answer listener questions two weeks into the 2025 season. The longest segment is Chicago: [[Caleb Williams]] playing on schedule in [[Ben Johnson]]'s system, [[Rome Odunze]] taking over the meaningful snaps from [[DJ Moore]] (first-read share up from 19% to 33%, two-WR-set rate up from 31% to 90%), the coming openings for [[Luther Burden III]] and [[Colston Loveland]], and a broken run game behind [[DeAndre Swift]]. Waldman also spends time critiquing an anonymously sourced Tyler Dunne article about Williams and Ryan Poles's height-weight-speed roster-building.

Other segments: whether [[Daniel Jones]]'s Colts start is scheme or player, with a long breakdown of [[Tyler Warren]] and [[Tucker Kraft]] as schemed first-read tight ends rather than [[George Kittle]]-style matchup players; [[Troy Franklin]]'s breakout and [[Matthew Golden]]'s buy-low case in a Green Bay offense limited by [[Jordan Love]]; a listener question on which running back metrics are sticky (yards before contact vs efficiency delta, missed tackles forced); [[Tyreek Hill]]'s likely exit from Miami and what it opens for [[Malik Washington]]; and closing takes on [[Rhamondre Stevenson]] as a dynasty buy and [[Michael Penix Jr.]] as the standout second-year quarterback.
