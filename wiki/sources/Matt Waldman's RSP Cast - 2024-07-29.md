## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] is joined by Dwain McFarland of Fantasy Life (guest, NOT a tracked expert) for an early-training-camp, unscripted 2024 redraft/best ball conversation. The pair open with 'players whose profile says no but whose situation says yes' ([[Joe Mixon]]) and 'talent I like in a bad environment' ([[Josh Downs]]), then work through [[De'Von Achane]]'s projected workload, Arthur Smith's arrival in Pittsburgh, the Titans' passing game, the Giants, the Patriots and the Panthers.

Waldman previews new RSP charting work on catch technique — across six years of top-75 fantasy finishes, elite (top-3) receivers graded strong on attacking the football 94% of the time, WR1s 82%, WR2s and WR3s 58%, with serial hand flaws at 0% for WR1s, 8% for WR2s and 17% for WR3s. McFarland counters with play-caller 'cheat code' data: a play-action route is worth 26% more PPR per route for a WR, motion at the snap 55% more, and two-WR sets 29% more.

Disagreements recorded: Waldman projects [[De'Von Achane]] at 6.0 yards per carry, McFarland at 5.0; McFarland is higher on [[Will Levis]] (3,700 yards, 60% pass rate) than Waldman (3,500 yards, ~18 TDs).
