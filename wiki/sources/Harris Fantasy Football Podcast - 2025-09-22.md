## Summary
Monday Week 3 film-review show. [[Chris Harris]] reviewed nine games himself (Eagles-Rams, Broncos-Chargers, Cardinals-49ers, Packers-Browns, Steelers-Patriots, Chiefs-Giants) and previewed Lions-Ravens, then brought on guest Patrick Doherty of Rotoworld (NOT a tracked expert) for Bengals-Vikings, Cowboys-Bears and Colts-Titans.

The dominant theme was running back attrition: season-ending Achilles rupture for [[Najee Harris]], a broken ankle for [[James Conner]], and a shoulder stinger for [[Tyrone Tracy Jr.]] — each handing a role to a younger back ([[Omarion Hampton]], [[Trey Benson]], [[Cam Skattebo]]). A second theme was fumbling as a job-loss mechanism in New England, where both [[Rhamondre Stevenson]] and [[Antonio Gibson]] put the ball on the ground and disappeared, leaving [[TreVeyon Henderson]] the rest of the game. Harris also repeatedly criticized NFL officiating and the replay-assist system.
