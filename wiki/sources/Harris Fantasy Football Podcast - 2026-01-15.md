## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a rundown of the 2026 NFL coaching carousel (nine openings, John Harbaugh to the Giants pending, Matt LaFleur's Green Bay future in doubt) before turning the bulk of the show over to guest Ben Gretsch of the Stealing Signals newsletter (NOT a tracked expert) for a position-by-position 'salary cap casualties' breakdown -- really a cash-owed breakdown, since Harris argues the cap itself is largely fictional ('the cap is crap'). They go name-by-name through veteran running backs, wide receivers and one tight end who are on expiring or unguaranteed money and project who actually gets cut, traded, renegotiated or retained.
