## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] (tracked) and co-host James Koh (NOT tracked) hand out eight all-positive, Reception-Perception-flavored wide receiver awards for the 2024 season, recorded the week of the January 2025 Los Angeles wildfires. Harmon opens with an explicit disclaimer that he has NOT charted every player named — the awards lean on Next Gen Stats, Fantasy Points and PFF-style splits (all cited on a 250+ routes qualifying threshold unless stated), plus film work and prior-season RP profiles.

The eight awards: Best Underneath — [[Khalil Shakir]] (honorable mentions [[Puka Nacua]], [[Josh Downs]], and Harmon's stated 'real answer' [[Chris Godwin]] had he stayed healthy). Best Deep Ball — [[Justin Jefferson]] (HMs [[Nico Collins]], [[Terry McLaurin]], [[George Pickens]], [[Jayden Reed]]; [[Alec Pierce]] added by Koh). Best vs Man — [[A.J. Brown]] (HMs [[Puka Nacua]], [[Mike Evans]], [[Nico Collins]]; Koh argues hard for [[CeeDee Lamb]]). Best vs Zone — [[Puka Nacua]] (HMs [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], [[Jayden Reed]]). Best Ball Winner — [[Tee Higgins]], explicitly 'pure vibes, no stats' (HMs [[Mike Evans]], [[Terry McLaurin]], [[Drake London]], [[Jauan Jennings]]). Best Rookie — [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] (HMs [[Ladd McConkey]], [[Malik Nabers]], [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], vanity mention [[Jalen Coker]]; extended [[Rome Odunze]] defense). Most Improved — [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] (HMs [[Jerry Jeudy]], [[Jauan Jennings]], [[Drake London]]). Best Overall — [[Ja'Marr Chase]], the triple crown winner.

Two on-air self-corrections: Harmon walks back calling [[Alec Pierce]] something worse than a [[Sacrificial X Receiver]], and Koh walks back his worry that [[Puka Nacua]]'s rookie year was his ceiling.
