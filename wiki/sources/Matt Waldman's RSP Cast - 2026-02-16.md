## Summary
Bob Harris (co-host, untracked) runs the recurring 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-fire format with Matt Waldman, who is midway through the 2026 rookie scouting portfolio (at quarterback, tight ends to follow). Heavy pre-draft QB/RB/WR prospect coverage (Garrett Nussmeier, Taylen Green, Ty Simpson, Robert Henry, T.J. Harden, KC Concepcion, Chase Roberts) followed by a run through veteran free agency/roster news across many teams: Cowboys (Javonte Williams, George Pickens, Tyler Linderbaum as a Ravens FA target), Panthers (Xavier Legette vs Jalen Coker), Texans (Jaylen Noel vs Christian Kirk, Travis Hunter's CB/WR future, Brian Thomas Jr. trade buzz, Jacoby Meyers, Parker Washington), Vikings (Jordan Addison trade speculation, Jalen Nailor, Tai Felton), Derek Carr un-retirement talk, a QB-vs-roster-build philosophy debate centered on Sam Darnold, Bills tight ends (Jackson Hawes, Dawson Knox), and a closing breakdown of Drake Maye's Super Bowl performance and MVP case.
