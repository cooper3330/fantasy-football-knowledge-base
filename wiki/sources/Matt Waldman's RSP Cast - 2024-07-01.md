## Summary
A rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' style ADP review co-hosted by Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris ahead of the 2024 redraft/best ball season. They run through QB1-overall value (Josh Allen), value QBs (Anthony Richardson, Kyler Murray, Trevor Lawrence), the RB1-overall tier (Christian McCaffrey, Breece Hall, Bijan Robinson, Jahmyr Gibbs, David Montgomery, Jonathan Taylor, Saquon Barkley, Derrick Henry), mid-round RB values (Rhamondre Stevenson, Alvin Kamara, Kendre Miller), paired feel-it-or-f-it duos (Zach Moss/Chase Brown, Brian Robinson Jr/Austin Ekeler, Najee Harris/Jaylen Warren), an injury-recovery bet (Nick Chubb), and two receivers (DK Metcalf, Amari Cooper). Waldman also touches on Marvin Harrison Jr's rookie rapport with Murray and Calvin Ridley's inconsistency as a primary receiver.
