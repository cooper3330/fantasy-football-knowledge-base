## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) break down four first-time NFL offensive coordinators and what their scheme history projects for pass-catchers: Zach Robinson (Atlanta, ex-McVay assistant), Dave Canales/Brad Idzik (Carolina, ex-Seattle/Tampa Bay), Ryan Grubb (Seattle, ex-Washington Huskies OC), and Nick Holtz (Tennessee, paired with playcaller Brian Callahan). Heavy use of Reception Perception charting data and route-percentage splits to project role changes for Drake London, Diontae Johnson, Xavier Legette, Bryce Young, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, DK Metcalf, Tyler Lockett, Calvin Ridley, DeAndre Hopkins, Tyler Boyd, Adam Thielen, Michael Penix Jr., Will Levis and Chigoziem Okonkwo, plus a Bijan Robinson run-scheme breakdown and a Mike Evans/Dave Canales case study.
