## Summary
Bob Harris and Matt Waldman run their weekly 'Feel It or F**k It' format, pitching Waldman quick-hit verdicts on players and storylines from Week 16-17. Also a plug for the RSP guide's early-bird deadline. Waldman is bullish on [[Konata Mumpfield]] (Adams-comp traits), skeptical the Judkins injury changes Cleveland's plan, bullish on [[Ashton Jeanty]] long-term if the Raiders invest in the line, and spends significant time on quarterbacks heading into the playoffs: high on [[Caleb Williams]] and [[Trevor Lawrence]], cautiously bullish on [[Aaron Rodgers]] and [[Drake May]] as dark horses, skeptical of [[Sam Darnold]] and [[Jordan Love]], notes [[Malik Willis]] as a likely bridge/backup elsewhere (floats Arizona), and expects [[Jacoby Brissett]] to hold the Cardinals job through most of 2026. Also covers [[Jackson Dart]] (regressed hype), [[Adonai Mitchell]] (stalled step forward), [[RJ Harvey]] (committee back, not a true bell cow), and a joint take on [[Parker Washington]] and [[Michael Wilson]] as catch-point/YAC receivers. Closes with an early 2026 receiver-class overview: [[Carnell Tate]] near the top, [[Makai Lemon]] a tier below, and a strong feel on Ja'Kobi Lane (USC) as one of the most intriguing names in the class. A general point on quarterback completion percentage and running back yards-per-carry as team stats misapplied to individuals, illustrated with Lamar Jackson, Caleb Williams and TreVeyon Henderson.
