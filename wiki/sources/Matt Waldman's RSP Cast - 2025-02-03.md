## Summary
A 'Feel It or F**k It' offseason news-reaction episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]). Recorded on the first football-free Sunday after the Pro Bowl, ahead of the Super Bowl.

Waldman opened with a Rookie Scouting Portfolio 2025 progress report: nearly 70 running backs watched ('a lot of good ones, not great ones' — a class where good backs can post great seasons), roughly 60-65 receivers, with quarterbacks and tight ends filling February. He used [[Ladd McConkey]] as a retrospective example of the RSP's fit-and-price analysis paying off, and flagged via [[Kimani Vidal]] that many good backs contribute little as rookies.

Rapid-fire segments: [[Ja'Marr Chase]]'s contract year and whether to leverage the angst; franchise-tagging [[Tee Higgins]] and Waldman's long-standing view that Higgins is a WR2 archetype; Duke Tobin's public shot at [[Jermaine Burton]] and Waldman's broader skepticism of off-field character intel; Myles Garrett's trade request; Chip Kelly to the Raiders as OC; Josh Grizzard promoted in Tampa and what continuity means for [[Baker Mayfield]]; Tanner Engstrand to the Jets; Kellen Moore to the Saints; Liam Coen praising [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] in the slot; time-in-pocket numbers for [[Jalen Hurts]] (2.7s, league high) and [[Sam Darnold]] (2.6s); [[Patrick Mahomes]] and 98 RPOs; Detroit's brain drain and [[Jared Goff]]; [[Geno Smith]]'s contract and Klint Kubiak's arrival for [[Kenneth Walker III]]; [[Matthew Stafford]] staying with the Rams and [[Puka Nacua]]; [[Will Levis]] working with Jordan Palmer; and how to price [[Adam Thielen]] vs [[Xavier Legette]] vs [[Jalen Coker]] in Carolina.

The show closed with the Kirk Cousins nickname contest (winners: 'the linguine launcher' and 'the bologna sandwich').
