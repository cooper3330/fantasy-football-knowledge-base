## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) work through the three notable wide receivers still unsigned in late May — [[Amari Cooper]], [[Keenan Allen]] and [[Gabe Davis]] — proposing landing spots by alignment fit rather than by raw talent, then sweep the league for teams that need a true X receiver and teams that need a slot, flagging in-house sleepers at each. The through-line is Harmon's alignment framework: which players can actually survive on the line of scrimmage against press, which are flanker/slot-only, and which teams are simply running last year's personnel back. Notable data cited from Reception Perception charting: [[Rashad Bateman]] at 7.7% slot rate on 444 routes (43rd in the NFL in routes), [[Diontae Johnson]] at 7.9% slot in 2021 and a career-high 21.3% in 2023, [[Marquise Brown]] at a 7th-percentile success rate vs press, [[Malik Nabers]] at just under 30% slot with 5.3% posts and 13.3% digs, [[Tre Tucker]] at 639 routes (most of any third-year receiver) with only 23 slot snaps, Amon-Ra St. Brown at 44% slot and [[Jameson Williams]] at 30%, [[Jerry Jeudy]] at 34.6% slot in 2024 after 75%/49%/54% in prior years, [[Terry McLaurin]] at 18%, and [[Zay Flowers]] at 41.7%.
