## Summary
Week 3 reaction episode of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment, hosted by [[Matt Waldman]] with co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). The pair ran through roughly a dozen 'is this real or temporary' verdicts on Week 3 performances: [[Dallas Goedert]]'s 10-catch game with [[A.J. Brown]] out, [[Calvin Austin III]] as a permanent riser in Pittsburgh, the [[Drake London]] / [[Kyle Pitts]] stall under [[Kirk Cousins]], the Dallas passing volume ([[KaVontae Turpin]], [[Jake Ferguson]], [[Rico Dowdle]]), the Kansas City short-passing problem ([[Patrick Mahomes]], [[Travis Kelce]], [[Xavier Worthy]]), [[Saquon Barkley]]'s revenge tour, [[Malik Willis]] as a possible real starter, the [[Sam Darnold]] / [[Baker Mayfield]] coaching-credit debate, the [[Rachaad White]] / [[Bucky Irving]] backfield, [[Jauan Jennings]]'s three-touchdown explosion, [[Jordan Mason]] and [[Isaac Guerendo]] in San Francisco, [[Kyren Williams]] in 12 personnel, [[Diontae Johnson]]'s career game, and a closing argument on patience with young quarterbacks built off a [[Bryce Young]] / [[Trevor Lawrence]] first-18-starts comparison.

Roster-management thread throughout: Waldman argued for staying the course through the first five to six weeks even at 0-4, and that tight end is the one position where replacement production is findable enough to justify moving on early.
