## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh run through 2025 Reception Perception profiles for three 'interesting' year-two receivers with murky outlooks: [[Isaac TeSlaa]] (DET), [[Tory Horton]] (SEA) and [[Kyle Williams]] (NE). For each they walk through man/zone/press success rates, alignment splits, route-participation-by-route data, and player-comparison-tool comps, then discuss 2026 target-share outlook given roster additions (Detroit's WR room stability, Seattle's Rashid Shaheed extension plus JSN/Kupp, New England's likely AJ Brown/Romeo Doubs additions). Closing segment covers listener comments and site updates (running back/TE/QB profiles and dynasty ranking tool coming), no fantasy content.
