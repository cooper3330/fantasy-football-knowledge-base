## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) break down Week 2 of the 2024 season, focused on quarterback play and its downstream effect on receiver value. Segments: the [[Bryce Young]] benching in Carolina and what Andy Dalton means for the Panthers pass catchers; the Chicago Bears offensive meltdown ([[Caleb Williams]], Shane Waldron, protection, [[Rome Odunze]]'s MCL, the misuse of [[D.J. Moore]]); Houston's disjointed passing game and [[Nico Collins]] creating out of structure; the [[Quentin Johnston]] revival and Harmon partially walking back his 'zero percent chance' take; the New Orleans offensive transformation under Clint Kubiak ([[Derek Carr]], [[Rashid Shaheed]], [[Chris Olave]], [[Alvin Kamara]]); and the Indianapolis receiver room ([[Alec Pierce]], [[Josh Downs]], [[Michael Pittman Jr.]], [[Adonai Mitchell]], [[Anthony Richardson]]).
