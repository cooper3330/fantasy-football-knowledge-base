# Matt Waldman's RSP Cast — 2024-01-04

**Raw transcript:** `raw/ingested/rsp-cast/2024-01-04-rsp-favorite-2023-nfl-storylines-and-2023-rookie-review-rsp-film-and-theor.md`

## Summary

*RSP Film and Theory* with co-host **Adam Harstad** (Footballguys — not a
tracked expert in this wiki; his views are attributed to him by name
throughout). Two-part offseason show: favorite storylines from the 2023
season, then a full review of the 2023 rookie class.

**Storylines segment** (lighter wiki content): Harstad's favorite story is the
Houston Texans turnaround under DeMeco Ryans and [[C.J. Stroud]]; both hosts
dig into [[Jordan Love]] as a case study in how fans overreact to young
quarterbacks with small samples, and the Packers' pattern of giving young
passers real development runway (see [[Player Development and Coachability]]).
[[Dak Prescott]] and Mike McCarthy's "second chances" fit get a similar read.
[[Brock Purdy]] gets an extended, durable forecast — Waldman compares his
career shape to early Tom Brady/Kurt Warner/Russell Wilson/Ben Roethlisberger
and predicts he'll stay just as effective once San Francisco leans on him more.
[[Lamar Jackson]] and [[Joe Flacco]] both get updated, largely reinforcing
takes. A long Bill Belichick/Bill Walsh coaching-legacy debate and a Mike
McCarthy "wins above .500" statistical detour are **not** wiki content — no
player fantasy value is at stake — and are noted here only for completeness.
[[Christian McCaffrey]] and Myles Garrett (a defensive end, out of this wiki's
skill-position scope) are named as the season's two most outstanding players;
not logged as a new McCaffrey take since it adds no new mechanism beyond his
existing #1-overall status.

**Rookie review segment** (the bulk of the wiki content): Waldman and Harstad
work through the 2023 rookie class post-mortem, position by position. Waldman
gives an explicit personal wide receiver tier —
1) [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], 2) [[Jordan Addison]], 3) [[Puka Nacua]],
4) [[Jayden Reed]], 5) [[Zay Flowers]], with [[Tank Dell]] just outside — and
both hosts work through [[Quentin Johnston]] as the class's clearest bust risk
so far. Harstad introduces a durable evaluation heuristic, "prefer the guy
doing the harder thing" (new concept:
[[Role Difficulty and Replaceability]]), applied to [[Nico Collins]] vs.
[[Tank Dell]] and to [[Rashee Rice]]'s Chiefs role. Also covered:
[[Marvin Mims Jr.]], [[Jalen Hyatt]], [[Josh Downs]] (each a brief but real
evaluative take), the rookie tight end class ([[Dalton Kincaid]] vs.
[[Sam LaPorta]], and [[Luke Musgrave]] vs. [[Tucker Kraft]] within Green Bay),
and three rookie running backs ([[Tank Bigsby]], [[Kendre Miller]],
[[Chase Brown]]).
