## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] hosts solo (James Koh is traveling in Asia) with returning guest Cory Kinnan (quarterback charting for Reception Perception; also runs Daft on Draft — NOT a tracked expert). The episode maps quarterback charting onto Reception Perception receiver profiles across four situations: [[Aaron Rodgers]] in Pittsburgh with [[DK Metcalf]]; [[Justin Fields]] and [[Garrett Wilson]] with the Jets under Tanner Engstrand; the Colts battle between [[Anthony Richardson]] and [[Daniel Jones]] under Shane Steichen; and [[Bryce Young]] in year two of Dave Canales with a rebuilt Carolina receiver room.

Recurring threads: Rodgers requires timing and rapport that Metcalf's route tree may not supply; Harmon's charting shows the Jets' 2024 offense was route-tree impoverished (Wilson ran a go or slant on 47.9% of charted routes) and that a more dynamic Engstrand design may matter more than Fields himself; Indianapolis is chasing the 'Minshew minimum'; and Bryce Young's ceiling is a receiver-room and anticipation question.
