## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on three topics. First, Daniel Jones being named the Colts' week one starter: Harmon is openly pessimistic, expects Jones, [[Anthony Richardson]] and even [[Riley Leonard]] all to start games, and says he cannot see the Indianapolis pass catchers gaining a fantasy ceiling from the change. Koh offers 'hopium' via the Kevin O'Connell and Shane Steichen coaching backgrounds and the Gardner Minshew 'Minshew minimum' framing; Harmon counters with Jones's Reception Perception QB profile (77% success rate on the post on 5.7% route participation, roughly league average on slants, below average on digs, comebacks and curls, ~50% success on digs) and his loss of arm strength driving the ball over the middle. Second, injury news: [[Jalen McMillan]] to IR possibly through week nine, [[Chris Godwin]] not yet jogging on a side field, [[Brandon Aiyuk]] running routes pre-game but Shanahan not counting on him until ~week six. Harmon is 'full on buying' [[Emeka Egbuka]] and hammers his 825.5 receiving-yard over. Third, the main segment: ranking the seven best clear number-two receivers by what they would look like as a number one, 7 to 1 — [[Jordan Addison]], [[Jameson Williams]], [[George Pickens]], [[Jaylen Waddle]], [[Tee Higgins]], [[Chris Godwin]], [[DeVonta Smith]].
