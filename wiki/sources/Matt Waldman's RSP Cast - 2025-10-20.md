## Summary
Week 7 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-fire segment with Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris covering Sunday's games. Topics: Jackson Dart's divisive rookie tape vs. Denver; why coaches lean on prevent defense; Troy Franklin as a tertiary Broncos target; Cam Ward as a dynasty buy-low trapped in a shaky Titans organization; the Bears' run game clicking as the offensive line's scheme timing improves (DeAndre Swift, Kyle Monangai) with credit to Ben Johnson's culture-building; Jordan Whittington and Konata Mumpfield as marginal Rams pass-catchers; Andrew Wingard's coverage busts making any Jaguars opponent tight end a stream (A.J. Barner, Jake Ferguson); Chimere Dike as a Titans dynasty buy-low; Pat Freiermuth's touchdowns credited to Aaron Rodgers' reads rather than role change, with Roman Wilson's non-production as context; Joe Flacco's Bengals stats vs. Cleveland's used to argue game-management value is undercounted by metrics; a Ja'Marr Chase wide receiver tier framework (gadget vs. Puka Nacua-tier vs. true alpha tier with CeeDee Lamb and Davante Adams); Jordan Addison compared to DeVonta Smith with real trade offers discussed; Jayden Daniels' receiver-continuity problems; Justin Fields and Anthony Richardson as cheap-only rushing QB investments; Spencer Rattler's Ryan Fitzpatrick-esque outlook and the Saints' plan for Tyler Shough; Tyjae Spears capped as a committee back by ACL history; Colston Loveland defended against dynasty-disappointment framing; and Daniel Jones/Tyler Warren's Colts outlook tied to Jonathan Taylor and an easy schedule, plus a note on Michael Pittman Jr.'s value return.
