## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and untracked co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys) run the 'Feel It or F**k It' format against the first 2025 Underdog best ball ADPs, going position by position and pairing players who are being drafted within a pick or two of each other. Waldman is finishing quarterbacks for the 2025 Rookie Scouting Portfolio (out April 1) and notes he has not yet charted Quinn Ewers or Seth Henigan. Bob Harris references his own initial Footballguys rankings and Jason Wood's projections throughout.

Major threads: quarterback pricing (Waldman feels [[Patrick Mahomes]] over [[Bo Nix]] while Harris fades both as overpriced), a shared preference for [[Caleb Williams]] over [[Jordan Love]], a lean to [[C.J. Stroud]] over [[Dak Prescott]], [[Michael Penix Jr.]] over [[Tua Tagovailoa]], and a split on [[Anthony Richardson]] vs [[Bryce Young]]. At running back both hosts fade pre-draft rookie ADP hard — [[Omarion Hampton]], [[TreVeyon Henderson]] and [[Quinshon Judkins]] are all called overpriced before landing spots are known, with only [[Ashton Jeanty]] exempted — and Waldman calls [[Kenneth Walker III]] criminally underpriced in a Klint Kubiak offense. Receiver and tight end pairs close the show.
