## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recorded days before the 2025 NFL Draft and handed out superlatives for the incoming wide receiver class: best sleeper, best deep sleeper, riskiest receiver, best X, best slot-only, most misunderstood, and best route runner, closing with a Travis Hunter rookie-ceiling projection.

Harmon's framing of the class: no true top-end star power outside [[Travis Hunter]] — he says he would not have put [[Tetairoa McMillan]] in the same tier as [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] or [[Malik Nabers]] in prior classes — but the class is unusually rich in day-two role players, with more players in his tier four (priority second-rounders) than in any year he has charted. He counters his own pessimism by noting that of his current top-12 dynasty receivers, only four ([[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Malik Nabers]], [[Drake London]], [[Garrett Wilson]]) were top-10 picks, so a good player in the right role can still become elite. Harmon said 19 full prospect profiles were published this year with 25-plus players planned.

Awards: best sleeper (3rd round or later) — [[Tory Horton]]; best deep sleeper — [[Andrew Armstrong]]; riskiest — [[Jayden Higgins]]; best X — [[Tetairoa McMillan]]; best slot-mostly — [[Emeka Egbuka]]; most misunderstood — [[Luther Burden III]]; best route runner — [[Jaylin Noel]] over [[Jack Bech]].
