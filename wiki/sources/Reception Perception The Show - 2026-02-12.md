## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) run their annual early-offseason NFC East preview, team by team, covering coaching changes, cap situations and receiver-room outlooks. Giants: new HC John Harbaugh paired with OC Matt Nagy and QB coach Brian Callahan draws skepticism from Harmon on scheme fit; Malik Nabers' knee injury is confirmed more severe than known (meniscus repair plus ACL), with a likely PUP start; Wan'Dale Robinson is credited with a real route-running breakout season and is a pending free agent; Darius Slayton is locked in by contract structure; Theo Johnson and Daniel Bellinger discussed as a possible two-TE answer. Washington: barren WR room behind Terry McLaurin; extended discussion of Brandon Aiyuk's injury timeline, voided 49ers contract, and rumored fit with Jayden Daniels, with Harmon and Koh disagreeing on buy-low risk versus bust risk. Cowboys: George Pickens expected to be franchise-tagged (tag-and-trade speculation from Ian Rapoport noted but downplayed), CeeDee Lamb's down counting stats explained by injury and near-even target split with Pickens, Ryan Flournoy praised as WR3. Eagles: new OC Sean Mannion/passing coordinator Josh Grizzard and new run-game coordinator Ryan Mahaffey (ex-Packers) discussed as scheme shifts that could help Jalen Hurts and Saquon Barkley; extensive AJ Brown trade speculation centered on New England, with Harmon downgrading Brown's tier; Jahan Dotson flagged as an under-the-radar Eagles free agent. Brief comparative notes on New England's Mack Hollins and Kayshawn Boutte as the team weighs adding outside receiver help.
