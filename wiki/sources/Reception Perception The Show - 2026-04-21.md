## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh break down three big-bodied X-alignment WR prospects ahead of the 2026 NFL Draft using Reception Perception charting data: [[Jacoby Lane]] (USC), [[De'Zhaun Stribling]] (Ole Miss), and [[Eric McAllister]] (TCU). Harmon also references a new RP study on receivers below the 35th percentile in man/zone/press success rate and how those profiles translate (or don't) to the NFL, sorting past prospects into buckets (rookie-contract cuts, slot-based hits, volatile deep threats, low-role 'depth', and 'looking bad'). Discussion leans heavily on measurables, snap alignment splits, route-tree success rates, and after-catch/contested-catch marks for each prospect, with historical comps (Rashee Rice, Jonathan Mingo, Alec Pierce, Marquez Valdes-Scantling, Quentin Johnston, Jermaine Burton) used to project outcomes.
