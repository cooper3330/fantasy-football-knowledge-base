## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert), recorded the Tuesday after the NFL Scouting Combine. Three news blocks then an AFC North wide receiver room tour.

News: [[Tee Higgins]] franchise-tagged a second time (~$26M, 120% of the prior tag); Harmon puts 85-90% odds Higgins is on the Bengals roster in 2025 but flags a tag-and-trade risk, and says Cincinnati's real constraint is ownership cash, not cap — Zac Taylor and Duke Tobin both said publicly [[Ja'Marr Chase]] will be the highest-paid non-QB in football, yet the sides are reported far apart. [[Deebo Samuel]] traded to Washington for a fifth-round pick; Harmon called the landing spot in advance and shares early Reception Perception charting on Samuel's 2024 season. [[Matthew Stafford]] reworked his Rams deal after Giants/Raiders interest, which Harmon reads as Stafford capitulating on money. [[Davante Adams]] released by the Jets; Harmon walks six landing spots (Chargers, Rams, Giants, Patriots, Packers, Lions) and says the film still supports a high-end outside starter.

AFC North: Pittsburgh ([[George Pickens]] and nobody else, with [[Justin Fields]] likely back at QB), Baltimore ([[Zay Flowers]] / [[Rashad Bateman]] 'good enough' but desperate for role-player depth), Cleveland ([[Jerry Jeudy]] and [[Cedric Tillman]] in a resource-poor, cap-strapped situation), Cincinnati ([[Andrei Iosivas]] as the third option after [[Jermaine Burton]] flamed out).
