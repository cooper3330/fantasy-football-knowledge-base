## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) preview the AFC West wide receiver rooms, team by team, using Reception Perception charting data.

Kansas City: [[Rashee Rice]]'s pending suspension frames the room; Harmon calls him a specialized 'route shrub' horizontal winner rather than a true number one, and argues [[Xavier Worthy]] must become the vertical piece despite a poor slant profile and 16th-percentile press mark. [[Jalen Royals]] is floated as Rice insurance on a JJ Zachariason data-guide comp.

Los Angeles Chargers: Harmon pushes back on the 'not enough around [[Justin Herbert]]' narrative, calling [[Ladd McConkey]] a fringe top-10 NFL receiver with the sixth-best press success rate in Reception Perception history. He plays [[Quentin Johnston]] apologist with a [[Jerry Jeudy]] usage comp (get him off the line), and breaks down [[Tre Harris]] and camp riser [[KeAndre Lambert-Smith]].

Denver: [[Courtland Sutton]] plus orbiting parts; Harmon picks [[Pat Bryant]] over [[Devaughn Vele]], [[Troy Franklin]] and [[Marvin Mims Jr.]] to emerge as the number two, despite historically bad slant and nine-route marks, on a [[Tim Patrick]] comp and power-slot deployment. Koh disagrees, backing Vele as the every-down option.

Las Vegas: Harmon likes the revamped room — [[Jack Bech]] (Puka Nacua-style condensed-formation player), [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]] as a raw vertical X with an MVS athletic comp, [[Jakobi Meyers]] as more than a slot, and [[Brock Bowers]]/[[Michael Mayer]] driving 12 personnel under Chip Kelly. [[Tre Tucker]] is expected to be squeezed out.
