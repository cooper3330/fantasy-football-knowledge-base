## Summary
A Week 7-into-Week-8 rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Guys; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Bob Harris poses flag-planting propositions and Waldman rules on each.

Ground covered: whether Denver already rosters its 2025 starting RB (Waldman: no); the Jacksonville backfield after [[Tank Bigsby]]'s 26-118-2 game and whether this is the last sell-high week; a long segment on [[Russell Wilson]]'s Pittsburgh debut against the Jets, why he fits the Steelers' defense-first script better than [[Justin Fields]], and the media drama around Mike Tomlin's decision; downstream Steelers value in [[George Pickens]], [[Calvin Austin III]] and [[Darnell Washington]]; a soapbox on good fantasy QBs vs good NFL QBs using [[Drake Maye]], [[Patrick Mahomes]] and [[Anthony Richardson]]; New England's big-play hierarchy ([[Kayshon Boutte]] over [[Ja'Lynn Polk]]); [[Deshaun Watson]]'s season-ending Achilles injury and what it does for the Browns' locker room, plus [[Jameis Winston]] as the likely starter and [[Nick Chubb]]'s return; the 49ers' red-zone problems and [[Christian McCaffrey]]'s ramp-up risk; [[Kareem Hunt]] vs [[Isiah Pacheco]] in Kansas City; and three one-answer questions on [[Brock Bowers]], [[Breece Hall]] and [[Jared Goff]], closing with Marcus Mariota's spot start for Washington.
