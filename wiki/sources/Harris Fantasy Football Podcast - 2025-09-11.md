## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts a Thursday Week 2 start/sit show with two guests, neither tracked: Dave Kluge of Football Guys (co-host of the Football Guys Dynasty Show with Jeff Bell), who counts down five players creating start/sit questions, and Jake Trowbridge, who builds the weekly DraftKings DFS lineup with Harris.

Harris opens with a long solo monologue on the collapsing San Francisco 49ers offense: [[Brock Purdy]] almost certainly out Sunday in New Orleans with turf toe, reportedly two to five weeks; [[George Kittle]] on IR a month with a pulled hamstring; [[Jauan Jennings]] questionable with a chest injury; [[Brandon Aiyuk]] out until roughly Week 6 off a torn ACL; [[Christian McCaffrey]] getting midweek rest days over calf concern; Trent Williams charged with 10 pressures at left tackle; and Mac Jones taking over at quarterback. He lowers [[Ricky Pearsall]] to about WR30 and frames the injury cluster as a buy-low window rather than a sell window ([[Dented Cans]]).

Kluge's countdown: 5) [[Tyreek Hill]], 4) [[DJ Moore]] (with [[Rome Odunze]]), 3) [[Keon Coleman]], 2) [[Zach Charbonnet]] and [[Kenneth Walker III]], 1) [[Bo Nix]]. Then a Thursday night Commanders-Packers preview covering [[Terry McLaurin]], [[Deebo Samuel]], [[Josh Jacobs]], [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]], [[Austin Ekeler]] and [[Tucker Kraft]]. Trowbridge and Harris then walk DFS pricing on [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], [[A.J. Brown]], [[Cedric Tillman]], [[Justin Fields]], [[Breece Hall]], [[Travis Etienne Jr.]], [[TreVeyon Henderson]], [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], [[Tetairoa McMillan]], [[Marquise Brown]], [[Tyler Warren]], [[Travis Kelce]] and [[Harold Fannin Jr.]].
