## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert). Three segments: (1) a deep dive on [[Jerry Jeudy]]'s 9-235-1 revenge game against Denver on Monday night — the largest receiving day against a former team in NFL history — and why [[Kevin Stefanski]]'s usage of him as a flanker rather than a vertical slot unlocked it; (2) a rapid-fire WR/CB matchup segment for week 14 ([[Garrett Wilson]] vs Jalen Ramsey, [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] vs Tariq Woolen/Devon Witherspoon, [[Justin Jefferson]] vs A.J. Terrell, [[Ja'Marr Chase]] vs Trevon Diggs, [[George Pickens]] vs Denzel Ward); (3) a long look at impending 2025 free agent receivers — [[Tee Higgins]], [[Amari Cooper]], [[Chris Godwin]], [[Diontae Johnson]], [[Marquise Brown]], [[Keenan Allen]], [[Mike Williams]], [[Elijah Moore]] — and what each has to prove down the stretch. Harmon repeatedly admitted a blind spot on Jeudy and apologized on air for the start/sit miss.
