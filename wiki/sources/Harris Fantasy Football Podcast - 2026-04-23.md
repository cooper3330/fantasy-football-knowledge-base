## Summary
Chris Harris is joined by untracked co-host/guest Andy Behrens for the show's annual tradition, done on the day the 2026 NFL Draft begins: a full re-draft of the top 20 2025 rookie skill-position players (RB/WR/TE/QB), ranking them 1-20 with the benefit of one NFL season, and comparing that order to how each was regarded pre-draft/pre-rookie-season. Both hosts build independent 20-man lists and compare pick by pick, agreeing at the very top (Ashton Jeanty #1, Tetairoa McMillan #2) and diverging steadily further down as the pool of 'proven good players' runs out. The framing throughout is explicitly anti-certainty: Harris's stated goal is to show how foolish it is to think you know what a rookie will become on draft weekend, since names that were consensus top-5 a year ago (Travis Hunter, Caleb Johnson) don't even crack this list. First half of the show is a long snarkbag/listener-email segment (new book 'Pound Foolish' under Harris's new Cumulative Press imprint, meetup planning, no fantasy content) before the redraft begins.
