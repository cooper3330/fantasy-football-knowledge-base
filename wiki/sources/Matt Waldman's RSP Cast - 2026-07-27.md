## Summary
Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris run their rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' training-camp format, giving quick verdicts and projections on roughly two dozen players as camps open. Waldman plugs the RSP projections/rankings and post-draft guide; Harris plugs the FootballGuys Draft Guide 2026 and notes he'll be off the podcast for a couple weeks.
