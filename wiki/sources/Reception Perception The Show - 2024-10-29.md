## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) break down Week 8 wide receiver play. Segments: the Jacksonville receiver room gutted by injuries ([[Christian Kirk]] out for the season with a broken collarbone, [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] out 2-4 weeks with a rib/chest injury) and what that leaves for [[Gabe Davis]] and [[Parker Washington]]; a long tangent on public sentiment around [[Trevor Lawrence]] and the 'generational' label; [[Calvin Ridley]]'s 10-143 outlier game in a 52-14 Titans loss and whether he has declined post-suspension; [[DeAndre Hopkins]]'s first game in Kansas City and where he actually lines up versus what Andy Reid said; charting-driven praise for [[Ladd McConkey]] and [[Josh Downs]] as slot receivers who beat man coverage; the Cleveland passing game unlocked by [[Jameis Winston]] under center, with [[Cedric Tillman]], [[Elijah Moore]] and [[Jerry Jeudy]]; and undrafted Panthers rookie [[Jalen Coker]] as a possible power-slot archetype.
