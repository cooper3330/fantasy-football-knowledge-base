## Summary
Going Deep segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]]. Opens with an early look at the 2027 and 2028 college running back/QB/WR classes (largely speculative freshmen/sophomores, not yet NFL-relevant), then pivots to three fantasy-actionable threads: [[Jonathan Brooks]]'s return from two ACL tears and what to expect timeline-wise, [[Cade Klubnik]]'s disappointing final college season reframed through a possible ankle/wrist injury, and a deep dive into the Green Bay backfield contingency plan if [[Josh Jacobs]] misses time due to an active legal investigation. Along the way the hosts use [[Malik Nabers]] and [[J.K. Dobbins|JK Dobbins]] as case studies for how ACL recovery timelines and redefined archetypes actually work.
