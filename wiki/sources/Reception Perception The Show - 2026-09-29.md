## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) cover the Monday trade of [[JJ McCarthy]] from Minnesota to the Giants for a fifth-round pick, and its ripple effects: [[Jameis Winston]]'s shaky play in New York, [[Jaxson Dart]]'s uncertain long-term standing, [[Malik Nabers]]'s recurring shoulder injury and diminished usage, and on the Vikings side [[Kyler Murray]]'s rocky first extended start, [[Justin Jefferson]]'s injury, and [[Jordan Addison]]'s resulting boom-bust role. They then break down Las Vegas's 3-0 start under [[Clint Kubiak]], crediting [[Kirk Cousins]]'s system familiarity and Kubiak's 'get the ball to your best player' philosophy exemplified by [[Brock Bowers]]'s huge target shares. A long Atlanta segment covers [[Drake London]]'s breakout with [[Michael Penix Jr.]] back at QB, including a historical target-share/production sample together. The back half is a deep dive on Jacksonville: [[Parker Washington]]'s continued ascent as a power-slot receiver in [[Liam Cohen]]'s scheme (with a dynasty ranking jump), [[Jakobi Meyers]]'s complementary outside role, [[Josh Cameron]]'s emergence as a blocking/dirty-work rookie, and [[Brian Thomas Jr.]]'s reduced role as a 'sacrificial X receiver' and trade-value discussion.
