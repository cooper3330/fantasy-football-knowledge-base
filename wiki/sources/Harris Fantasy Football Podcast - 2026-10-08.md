## Summary
Chris Harris opens with NFL news (Lane Johnson retiring, Cam Jurgens traded to Ravens, Tyreek Hill's civil trial, Joe Mixon not signing with Seattle) and listener emails before two guest segments. First, Scott Pianowski of Yahoo Fantasy (not a tracked expert) joins to work through five Week 5 'hard to rank' players: Jacoby Brissette, Bucky Irving, TreVeyon Henderson (with Rhamondre Stevenson comparison), Alvin Kamara, and Trey Harris (with a brief Keon Coleman aside), plus a Thursday-night Buccaneers-Cowboys preview covering George Pickens' target share and the Tampa Bay receiver outlook with Jalen Daniels at QB.
