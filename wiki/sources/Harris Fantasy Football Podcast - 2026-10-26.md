## Summary
Chris Harris's Monday game-film recap for Week 7, covering San Francisco-Atlanta, Giants-Texans, Broncos-Cardinals, Steelers-Saints, and Buccaneers-Panthers himself, then joined by guest Jamie Eisenberg (CBS Fantasy, not a tracked expert) to break down three more games he watched: Bengals-Ravens, Packers-Lions, and Chiefs-Seahawks. Heavy focus on backfield committees (Denver's Dobbins/Harvey/Coleman, Seattle's Charbonnet/Wilson, Green Bay's Jacobs/Lloyd), quarterback evaluations (JJ McCarthy's strong Giants debut, Bo Nix's rushing touchdown, Bryce Young's regression, Patrick Mahomes' weapon-starved floor), and several injury notes (Jalen Waddle, Tyrone Tracy Jr., Isaiah Likely, DeVonta Smith, Saquon Barkley, Christian McCaffrey's brief scare).
