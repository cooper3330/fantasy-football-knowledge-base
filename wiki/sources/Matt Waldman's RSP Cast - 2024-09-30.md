## Summary
Week 4 reaction episode of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment, with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Diehard/SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Rapid-fire format: Bob poses a proposition, Waldman either 'feels it' or rejects it.

Ground covered: the Kansas City receiving corps after the reported Rashee Rice ACL tear (Waldman is skeptical of [[Xavier Worthy]] as the simple next man up and expects [[Travis Kelce]] plus Noah Gray to absorb the work); the KC backfield reshuffle after Carson Steele's fumble, with Kareem Hunt taking over; Green Bay's receiver roles with [[Christian Watson]] possibly headed to IR; Colts stopgap backs behind [[Jonathan Taylor]]'s high ankle sprain; a long segment on [[Jayden Daniels]] versus [[Caleb Williams]] built on Waldman's pass-depth splits; [[Malik Nabers]] and a 208-target dynasty projection; [[Keon Coleman]] and Josh Allen's trust; Atlanta's pass-game ceiling under Kirk Cousins; San Francisco ([[Jauan Jennings]], [[Brock Purdy]]); backfield-committee questions in Tampa Bay, Cincinnati and Carolina; [[Breece Hall]] versus [[Braelon Allen]]; and a closing anti-[[Kyler Murray]] rant.
