## Summary
Chris Harris runs his Monday film-review show solo through the first block (Bills-Patriots, Broncos-Packers, Chargers-Chiefs, Colts-Seahawks, Ravens-Bengals, Vikings-Cowboys, plus a brief Dolphins-Steelers preview), grading players purely on what should change lineup usage for the fantasy playoffs. He then brings on Patrick Doherty of Rotoworld (untracked guest, 11th straight season on the show) to review three more games he watched for Harris: Raiders-Eagles, Jets-Jaguars and Lions-Rams. Big storylines: Patrick Mahomes tore his ACL late in the Chiefs' loss to the Chargers; Christian Watson appears to have suffered a serious shoulder/collarbone injury; Davante Adams left the Rams-Lions game with what looked like an Achilles injury (officially a hamstring) and is unlikely to play in three days, raising questions about Blake Corum's goal-line role. Chris opens with a note on embracing playoff variance rather than overmanaging lineups.
