## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) break down the 2025 NFL trade deadline from a receiver-usage perspective, leaning on Harmon's week-nine Reception Perception charting notebook in which he charted all three traded wide receivers. Both hosts open by admitting they publicly predicted a 'nothing burger' deadline and took a loss on it.

Four segments: (1) [[Jakobi Meyers]] to Jacksonville and the vacuum he leaves in Las Vegas, where Harmon argues [[Jack Bech]] should inherit the role and Koh predicts veteran deference to [[Tyler Lockett]]; (2) [[Rashid Shaheed]] to Seattle, reuniting with OC Klint Kubiak, and what it means for [[Tory Horton]], [[Cooper Kupp]] and [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] on a team running the league's second-lowest 11-personnel rate; (3) New Orleans post-Shaheed, with [[Brandin Cooks]], [[Devaughn Vele]] and [[Chris Olave]]; (4) [[Adonai Mitchell]] to the Jets as a throw-in on the Sauce Gardner deal, and how Harmon would deploy him at pure X alongside [[Garrett Wilson]]. Closes with [[Kyler Murray]] going on IR, [[Jacoby Brissett]]'s scheme fit and [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]'s activation, plus a FanDuel picks segment on [[Brock Bowers]], [[Alec Pierce]] and [[Drake London]].
