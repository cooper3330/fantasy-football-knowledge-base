## Summary
Chris Harris opens with Week 1 injury notes ahead of the Wednesday Patriots-Seahawks and Thursday Niners-Rams (Australia) games -- [[TreVeyon Henderson]] very likely out, [[Rhamondre Stevenson]] to get the load, and [[George Kittle]] making the trip to Australia but with uncertain workload. Pound Foolish grand-prize winner Greg Berthiume (a listener, not tracked) does a silly Q&A segment with Harris -- no fantasy content. Harris then runs his annual survey of the players he personally drafted most across ~10 real leagues this summer, surfacing [[Sam LaPorta]] as his top overall pick plus notes on [[Brenton Strange]], [[Courtland Sutton]], [[Drake London]] and several others. Scott Fish (Fantasy Cares / Scott Fishbowl, not a tracked expert) joins for a Week 1 waiver-wire countdown (players rostered in 33% or fewer Yahoo leagues), with both hosts giving their own top-5 lists: [[Jacoby Lane]], [[Keenan Allen]], [[Chris Bell]], [[Cyrus Allen]], [[Kalen Black]], [[Tre Tucker]], [[Terrance Ferguson]], [[Caleb Johnson]] and [[Chris Brooks]], plus drop-candidate notes on [[Trey Benson]], [[Alvin Kamara]] and [[Isaiah Pacheco]].
