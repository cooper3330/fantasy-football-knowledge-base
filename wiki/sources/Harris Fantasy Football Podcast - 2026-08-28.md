## Summary
Chris Harris, back from a book tour, opens with a Josh Jacobs legal update (misdemeanor charges filed) and joint-practice notes on Colston Loveland, Caleb Williams, Luther Burden III, Wan'Dale Robinson and Carnell Tate. He finishes the alphabetical Super Deep Sleepers dynasty-bench list (started Thursday with Jake Seeley) covering Kendre Miller, Konata Mumpfield (plus the Tutu Atwell trade and Jordan Whittington), Terrance Ferguson (bumped off the list as too hyped), Eli Raridon, Hunter Henry, Raheem Sanders, and Nicholas Singleton (whose view shifted after a conversation with Matt Waldman) alongside a comparison to Jonah Coleman. Cousin Josh (untracked co-host) then runs his Warp Zone segment: five players he says will return at least two rounds better than ADP -- Jared Goff, Romeo Doubs, Juwan Johnson, Jameson Williams, and David Montgomery -- with Harris largely co-signing each. A DraftKings prop segment has Josh taking the over on Jalen Waddle's receiving yards.
