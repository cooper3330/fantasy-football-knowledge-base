## Summary
James Koh is out; Matt Harmon hosts solo with guest Brandon Thorn (Trench Warfare Substack, Establish the Run OL rankings) -- an untracked OL specialist, not a show co-host. They break down six offensive lines -- Washington, Green Bay, LA Chargers, LA Rams, Buffalo, New Orleans, Tennessee -- and cross-reference each unit's quality/scheme against the attached receiver room. Thorn gives OL grades/context (Washington bottom-five; Chargers 7th; Rams retooled/heaviest interior in NFL; Bills 13th and ascending; Saints below-average and reliant on Ryan Ramczyk's health; Titans young/rebuilding after drafting J.C. Latham). Harmon layers in Reception Perception route-charting data and separation grades for the relevant receivers.
