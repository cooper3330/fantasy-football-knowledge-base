## Summary
Rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' news-reaction episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Feel It or F**k It; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Recorded as training camps opened. Waldman opens by noting he is already studying 2026 prospects — Nicholas Singleton, Jeremiah Love (Notre Dame) and Makai Hughes (Tulane) — and that he is running roughly ten simultaneous drafts as part of a portfolio approach.

Topics: the Quinshon Judkins arrest and the resulting ADP cascade to Jerome Ford and Dylan Sampson; Noah Fant's release and Elijah Arroyo's ceiling in Seattle; the Chargers receiver room after Mike Williams' retirement (Tre Harris, Quentin Johnston, Ladd McConkey); Stefon Diggs' PUP status and long-rehab risk; Ricky Pearsall's hamstring; Terry McLaurin trade chatter and its effect on Jayden Daniels; Savion Williams as a dynasty sleeper; Terrance Ferguson in Los Angeles; Omarion Hampton vs Najee Harris after Harris' eye injury; Rashee Rice's sentencing and likely suspension; Patrick Mahomes and a return to downfield passing with Xavier Worthy; Justyn Ross' release request; Braelon Allen and Breece Hall in New York; Jahmyr Gibbs lining up wide; the Xavier Legette drop-rate narrative in Carolina; and Garrett Wilson's extension.
