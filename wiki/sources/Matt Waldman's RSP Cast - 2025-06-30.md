## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]) record their 'Feel It or F**k It' segment the morning of the Dolphins-Steelers trade that sent [[Jonnu Smith]] and Jalen Ramsey to Pittsburgh for Minkah Fitzpatrick. They open on the fantasy fallout in Pittsburgh and Miami, then run through a rapid-fire agenda: [[Bucky Irving]] red-zone touchdown regression and [[Sean Tucker]] as the value, [[Dyami Brown]] as a supposed slam-dunk WR3 in Jacksonville, [[Jalen Hyatt]] with Russell Wilson, [[Travis Etienne Jr.]] holding the Jaguars' RB1 job, George Kittle's shout-out to [[Isaiah Neyor]], [[Dylan Sampson]] in the slot, [[Marquez Valdes-Scantling]] as Seattle's X, rookie [[Mason Taylor]], [[Zay Jones]] and [[Kyler Murray]]'s ability to support more than one pass catcher, [[Dallas Goedert]], [[Saquon Barkley]] volume regression, [[A.J. Dillon]] as the sleeper Eagles backup, [[J.J. McCarthy]] as a top-12 QB, and whether [[Patrick Mahomes]]' demise is exaggerated. Waldman also plugs the Rookie Scouting Portfolio and his monthly-updated dynasty rankings and projections.
