## Summary
Thursday pre-Labor-Day Total Request episode. Chris Harris opens with recent ADP-mover news from his Yacht Club appearance with Andy Behrens -- [[Alec Pierce]] rising after practicing and looking cleared for Week 1, and [[George Kittle]] moving up his TE tiers after apparently making the trip to Australia for the Week 1 game. He also runs down injury/roster limbo notes ([[Isaiah Pacheco]] to short-term IR, no known [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] backup) and previews his second Almanac edition dropping the next morning, plus a long non-football segment on Pound Foolish hitting bestseller status, book tour recaps, and social-media advice. RotoWorld's Denny Carter (untracked co-host) joins for the bulk of the show to field listener questions covering aging wide receivers who could follow [[Larry Fitzgerald]]'s late-career path (Mike Evans, Chase, Higgins, Waddle, Godwin, Keenan Allen), Denny's evolving stance on undersized 'tiny bones' backs like [[De'Von Achane]], candidates for this year's 'James White PPR scam' ([[Kenneth Gainwell]], [[Keaton Mitchell]]), how much weight to put on offensive-coordinator scheme-fit narratives, Kevin O'Connell's 'quarterback whisperer' reputation in Minnesota, why running backs aren't fully top-heavy in Harris's value-based ranks (David Montgomery as the cautionary example), and Green Bay backfield/receiver value questions ([[Tucker Kraft]], [[Christian Watson]], [[Marshawn Lloyd]], [[Chris Brooks]]). Show closes with pop-culture and personal listener questions and a giveaway plug.
