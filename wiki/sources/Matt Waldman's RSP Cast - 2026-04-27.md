## Summary
Post-2026 NFL Draft 'Feel It or F**k It' roundtable between Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris, running quickly through rookie landing spots across the league. Waldman previews the RSP post-draft cheat sheet and confirms Skyler Bell was his pre-draft WR5. Major threads: Jeremiah Love to Arizona (Waldman contrarian-bullish vs. industry consensus), Kenyon Sadiq to the Jets (bust risk flagged), Cleveland's WR/QB haul (KC Concepcion, Denzel Boston, Taylen Green) tied to a Deshaun Watson-starts scenario, Buffalo's Skyler Bell pick and its implications for Keon Coleman, Malachi Fields defended as a good Giants pick alongside Malik Nabers, Jonah Coleman to Denver graded skeptically (LenDale White comp) with a debate on whether it's an indictment of RJ Harvey, a broad discussion of a deep-but-murky rookie tight end class (Eli Stowers as clear TE1, Max Klare, Oscar Delp, Justin Joly, undrafted Michael Trigg), Antonio Williams to Washington and Zachariah Branch to Atlanta, Chris Brazell's impact on Tetairoa McMillan's projected role in Carolina, and the Bears' tight end/interior-line draft (Sam Roush, Xavier Thomas). Waldman also lays out a broader rookie-draft strategy: prioritize undrafted/late-round running backs over mid-tier wide receivers, quarterbacks and tight ends.
