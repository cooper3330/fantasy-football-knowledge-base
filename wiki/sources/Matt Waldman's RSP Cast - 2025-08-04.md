## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]) run the 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-reaction format against early August training-camp reports. The through-line of the episode is Waldman's repeated warning against judging players — especially running back burst — off a single practice rep or a single beat-writer report, illustrated with [[Omarion Hampton]]'s Hall of Fame Game red-zone carry and the noise around [[Nick Chubb]] in Houston. Long segments on the Ben Johnson Bears offense ([[Caleb Williams]], [[Kyle Monangai]], [[Luther Burden III]], [[Rome Odunze]], [[Colston Loveland]], [[D.J. Moore]]), the Arizona backfield split ([[James Conner]] / [[Trey Benson]]), Seattle's backfield where the two hosts openly disagree ([[Kenneth Walker III]] vs [[Zach Charbonnet]]), tight ends ([[Noah Fant]] cut, [[Pat Freiermuth]] as Waldman's favorite value), plus [[Michael Pittman Jr.]], [[Khalil Shakir]]'s high ankle sprain, the [[Terry McLaurin]] holdout and its knock-on for [[Jayden Daniels]], [[Jaydon Blue]], [[Dillon Gabriel]], [[Cam Skattebo]] and [[KeAndre Lambert-Smith]] vs [[Tre Harris]].
