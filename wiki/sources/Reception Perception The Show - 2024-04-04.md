# Stefon Diggs is a Texan & Keon Coleman Breakdown

Emergency two-part episode from [[Matt Harmon]] and James Koh (co-host, not a
tracked expert). Part one was recorded same-day in reaction to Buffalo trading
[[Stefon Diggs]] to Houston for a future 2nd-round pick, eating a ~$31M dead-cap
hit; part two is a pre-recorded, previously-scheduled Reception Perception
prospect breakdown of FSU's [[Keon Coleman]].

## What was covered

- **Stefon Diggs trade reaction:** Harmon's still-in-progress full-season 2023
  Reception Perception charting shows a real (not cliff-level) decline,
  concentrated in downfield separation — Next Gen Stats passer-rating-when-
  targeted-20+-yards fell from 3rd-best in the NFL in 2022 to 66th of 70 in
  2023. Rules out an unreported second-half injury as the explanation. Frames
  the Buffalo side as a bad team-building move and the Houston side as an
  outright steal, projecting Diggs into a diminished, more underneath/flanker
  route runner role behind [[Nico Collins]] and [[Tank Dell]] in target share.
  Retrospective on the [[Josh Allen]]/Diggs on-field relationship. Speculation
  on Buffalo aggressively trading up in the 2024 draft for a receiver.
- **Keon Coleman full-sample Reception Perception breakdown:** detailed
  charting data (man/zone/press splits, route-type splits, alignment splits),
  athletic testing (4.61 40, 38" vertical, GPS gauntlet-drill speed vs. slow
  stopwatch time), and a new historical study of 32 prospects who charted
  poorly against college coverage, used to argue Coleman fits a "must become a
  big slot receiver" archetype and profiles as a Day 2, not Round 1, prospect.
  Archetype comps to [[Treylon Burks]], [[Jonathan Mingo]], [[Amon-Ra St.
  Brown]], [[Cooper Kupp]] and [[Rashee Rice]]. Landing-spot speculation
  (Houston, Cincinnati).
