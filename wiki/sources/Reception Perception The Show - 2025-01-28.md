## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recap the 2024 conference championship round: Kansas City over Buffalo, and Philadelphia's 55-23 rout of Washington.

The Buffalo half is a long argument about roster construction. Harmon's thesis is that the Bills have a deep, egoless collection of complementary pass catchers ([[Khalil Shakir]], [[Mack Hollins]], [[Curtis Samuel]], [[Amari Cooper]], [[Dalton Kincaid]]) and a genuinely good gap-scheme run game with [[James Cook]], but no blue-chip perimeter man-beater to dictate coverage the way [[Stefon Diggs]] once did. Koh pushes back, arguing this year's un-defendable, spread-the-ball offense was better precisely because there is no No. 1 to take away. Both agree [[Keon Coleman]] is not that X receiver. Also covered: whether Sean McDermott is a talent elevator on defense, and the Kincaid first-round trade-up as a miss.

The NFC half covers [[Saquon Barkley]] and [[Jalen Hurts]] shredding Washington, [[A.J. Brown]]'s 4.27 yards per route run leading the league, and Washington's offseason: Harmon wants a [[Chris Godwin]]-type complementary receiver next to [[Terry McLaurin]], while Koh wants an early-round running back. Harmon delivers a detailed alignment critique of Cliff Kingsbury's usage of McLaurin against Quinyon Mitchell.
