## Summary
Matt Waldman and Going Deep co-host Brandon Angelo (both tracked) open by relaying analyst Max Toscano's theory (via Sigmund Blum) that heavier 12/13-personnel offenses are shifting tight end value away from man-beating 'move' tight ends toward zone-blocking, shallow/red-zone pass catchers. They then run through their riskiest, contrarian and safest 2026 draft prospects: Michael Trigg and Kenyon Sadiq as riskiest tight ends (with Eli Stowers as the TE Waldman is most willing to bet on), Jordan Tyson and Mike Washington Jr. as players both hosts are furthest below consensus on, Emmett Johnson as a liked-but-draft-capital-risk running back, Skyler Bell and Antonio Williams as safer Day 2-plus receivers, KC Concepcion with a specific catch-technique flaw flagged, and Carson Ryan as a BYU tight end Waldman rates far above consensus and was shocked was not invited to the Combine.
