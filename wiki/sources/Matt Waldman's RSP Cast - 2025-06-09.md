## Summary
Minicamp-week edition of 'Feel It or F**k It' with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Diehard; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman opened by noting his June Dynasty rankings and projections update was hours from release.

Three main blocks: (1) Pittsburgh after [[Aaron Rodgers]] officially signed — Waldman's projections for [[DK Metcalf]], [[Calvin Austin III]] and [[Pat Freiermuth]], with Bob Harris noting Freiermuth is one of his most-rostered best ball players; (2) Jacksonville under Liam Coen and Grant Udinski — [[Trevor Lawrence]]'s offensive line dependency, [[Brian Thomas Jr.]]'s WR1 range of outcomes, [[Travis Hunter]]'s two-way usage uncertainty, the [[Travis Etienne Jr.]] / [[Bhayshul Tuten]] / [[Tank Bigsby]] ADP cluster, [[LeQuint Allen]] and Waldman's sleeper interest in [[Parker Washington]] and [[Dyami Brown]]; (3) Kansas City — [[Patrick Mahomes]]'s deep-ball comments, a strong [[Xavier Worthy]] breakout projection, a health-based fade of [[Rashee Rice]] citing [[Brandon Angelo]], plus [[Marquise Brown]], [[Travis Kelce]] and [[Isiah Pacheco]]. Closed with [[Sam Darnold]] in Seattle and [[Tetairoa McMillan]]'s redraft price.
