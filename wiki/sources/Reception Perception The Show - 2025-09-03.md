## Summary
Annual bold predictions show with [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert), recorded days before the 2025 season opener.

News items first: [[Jalen Coker]] landing on short-term IR a day after Adam Thielen was traded out of Carolina (Harmon had called it 'national Jalen Coker day'), and [[Gabe Davis]] signing to the Buffalo practice squad amid what Koh called 'the year of reunions'.

Harmon's five bold predictions: [[Emeka Egbuka]] catches 100 passes and leads Tampa Bay in receptions; [[Rashad Bateman]] outproduces [[Zay Flowers]]; [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] finishes top five in receiving yards; [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] outproduces [[Trey McBride]]; [[Michael Penix Jr.]] leads the NFL in passing yards.

Koh's five: [[Trevor Lawrence]] finishes as a top-five fantasy QB; [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] top five in receiving yards and touchdowns; [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] sets a career high with 1,200+ receiving yards; [[Kenneth Walker III]] top five in scrimmage yards; [[Rome Odunze]] breaks out for 1,200+ yards and beats [[DJ Moore]].

Side segments: the Jacksonville backfield (Harmon has charted every back and lands on [[Travis Etienne Jr.]]), the Indianapolis target hierarchy including [[Tyler Warren]] and [[Josh Downs]], and a FanDuel over on Smith-Njigba's 975.5 receiving yards.
