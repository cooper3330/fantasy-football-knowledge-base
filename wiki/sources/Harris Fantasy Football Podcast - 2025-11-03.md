## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] breaks down eight Week 9 games he watched every snap of: Patriots-Falcons, Bears-Bengals, Seahawks-Commanders (Sunday night), Jaguars-Raiders, Saints-Rams, plus a Monday night preview of Cardinals-Cowboys. Guest Patrick Doherty of Rotoworld (NOT a tracked expert) joins for Chargers-Titans, Vikings-Lions and Chiefs-Bills.

Major threads: [[TreVeyon Henderson]] finally getting the full workload with [[Rhamondre Stevenson]] out but not blowing up; a [[Drake London]] three-touchdown explosion; [[Jayden Daniels]] suffering an apparent broken arm on a scramble, ending his season for fantasy purposes; the Bengals defense as a fantasy cheat code; [[Aaron Jones]] leaving with an AC joint injury Harris does not trust; and a Chiefs offense Doherty calls 'a copy of a copy'.
