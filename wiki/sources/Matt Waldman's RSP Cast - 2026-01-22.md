## Summary
Going Deep segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] covering four teams. Bears: Caleb Williams' year-two leap under new coach Ben Johnson, framed as a structure/accountability story rather than pure talent, plus detailed breakdown of why DJ Moore is a scheme misfit for Ben Johnson's F position (including a technical dagger-route breakdown of the divisional-round interception) and both hosts' willingness to trade him. Eagles: why a Matt Nagy hire would fail (refuses under-center/12-13 personnel, Andy Reid-lineage two-high issues) versus Jim Bob Cooter as the continuity fit for Jalen Hurts, Saquon Barkley, A.J. Brown and DeVonta Smith, plus RB2 outlook (Tank Bigsby, Will Shipley, a floated David Montgomery scenario). Commanders: Cliff Kingsbury's predictable year-two offense, Deebo Samuel and Terry McLaurin evaluated as incomplete weapons, Brian Robinson Jr.'s exit to San Francisco, Rhamondre Stevenson vs. TreVeyon Henderson framing, Jacory Croskey-Merritt's runway, Zach Ertz's ACL tear, and 2026 draft receiver targets at pick 7 (Makai Lemon preferred). Jaguars: Trevor Lawrence's uneven chemistry with Brian Thomas Jr. versus Jakobi Meyers, Travis Etienne Jr.'s past trade-bait scare, Brenton Strange, Travis Hunter's two-way future clouded by injury and a possible DC departure, and an extended, story-driven case for Parker Washington as a 2026 1,000-yard breakout. A closing aside questions Garrett Wilson's decision to sign long-term with the Jets rather than pursue a trade.
