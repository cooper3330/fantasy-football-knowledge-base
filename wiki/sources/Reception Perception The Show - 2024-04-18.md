## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) break down two new Reception Perception profiles ahead of the 2024 NFL Draft: [[Xavier Legette]] and [[Troy Franklin]]. They dig into RP success-rate charts vs. man/zone/press coverage, contested-catch data, and route-by-route breakdowns, and compare both prospects to [[Keon Coleman]] and [[Jordan Addison]] as touchstones. Harmon calls Legette the higher-ceiling/lower-floor player and Franklin the more NFL-ready, lower-ceiling/higher-floor player, and grades both as Round 2 talents.
