# Feel It Or F@#k It: 4.8.24: An RSP Cast with Bob Harris and Matt Waldman

*Feel It or F**k It* rapid-fire episode, [[Matt Waldman]] with co-host **Bob
Harris** (not a tracked expert). Recorded the week after the RSP's release,
roughly two weeks before the 2024 NFL Draft.

## Covered

- **2024 QB prospects:** [[Drake Maye]] vs. [[J.J. McCarthy]] (Waldman prefers
  McCarthy as a Day 1 starter), Meryl Hodge's (not tracked, ESPN) "coach
  killer" comment on Maye discussed as a systemic NFL-development critique
  rather than a Maye pan, and a detailed [[Caleb Williams]] vs. Maye pocket-
  movement breakdown ([[Quarterback Processing and Confidence]]).
- **2024 WR prospects:** [[Rome Odunze]] visiting Arizona; Waldman comps Odunze
  to a complete Davante-Adams-type receiver vs. [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] as more
  of a DeAndre Hopkins/Larry Fitzgerald archetype.
- **Stefon Diggs traded to Houston** — a "Diggs edition" feeler-bucket segment:
  [[C.J. Stroud]] value rising on three startable WRs, [[Nico Collins]]/[[Tank
  Dell]]/[[Stefon Diggs]] best-ball ADP calls, [[Dalton Schultz]] as a
  price-down beneficiary.
- **Buffalo Bills roster:** [[Josh Allen]] redraft/best-ball caution amid the
  Diggs fallout; rookie-WR fit archetypes discussed for the Bills' presumed
  first-round pick ([[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[Keon Coleman]], [[Xavier
  Legette]], [[Johnny Wilson]], [[Javon Baker]] as hypothetical fits — no
  actual pick made yet); [[Dalton Kincaid]] role/target-share upside;
  [[Khalil Shakir]] bargain; [[James Cook]] steady outlook.
- **Green Bay:** [[Josh Jacobs]] fit, [[Jordan Love]] as a QB9 best-ball
  bargain, cheap shares on [[Jayden Reed]]/[[Christian Watson]]/[[Romeo
  Doubs]]/[[Dontayvion Wicks]].
- **Jacksonville:** [[Travis Etienne Jr.]] near his ceiling in ADP but still
  preferred over [[Tank Bigsby]]; Devin Duvernay (not a tracked page) floated
  as a kickoff-rule-driven backfield hedge.
- **AFC South/other value calls:** [[Trevor Lawrence]] as a bargain QB1;
  [[Aaron Jones]] (Minnesota) boom/bust volume risk; [[Joe Mixon]] and
  [[Raheem Mostert]] value calls; [[De'Von Achane]] "fuck it" on cost/health;
  [[Christopher Brooks]] sleeper in Miami's backfield.
- **RSP Q&A closer:** [[Keon Coleman]] as the player Waldman is afraid he's too
  high on (landing-spot dependent); [[Marshawn Lloyd]] as the player he's
  afraid he's too low on, contingent entirely on fixing ball security.
- RSP product plug (pre-draft/post-draft guides, dynasty rankings/projections
  service).
