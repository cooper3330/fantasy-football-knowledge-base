## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] (recording from New York) and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) preview the NFC East wide receiver rooms.

Dallas: [[George Pickens]]'s 2024 Reception Perception breakout versus man coverage (72.8%, 83rd percentile, a career best after never clearing 70%) plus his long-standing press-coverage strength (77.6% in 2024), his role as a primary X with [[CeeDee Lamb]] moved around the formation, stack/bunch alignments to free both from press, and the thin third-receiver picture ([[Jalen Tolbert]], [[Jonathan Mingo]], [[Jake Ferguson]], [[Jaydon Blue]]) under new play caller Brian Schottenheimer.

Washington: [[Deebo Samuel]]'s 39.7% success rate versus man in 2024 — third lowest Harmon has ever charted, behind Justin Hunter and Terrace Marshall — and his zone-beating decline from the mid-80s to 76.3%. [[Terry McLaurin]]'s contract standoff and his alignment problem under Kliff Kingsbury (72.3% of sampled snaps at left outside receiver, one of only four such seasons in RP history alongside two [[DeAndre Hopkins]] Arizona seasons and Nelson Agholor's rookie year). Also [[Luke McCaffrey]], [[Jaylin Lane]], [[Michael Gallup]], [[Noah Brown]].

Philadelphia: an extended [[Jahan Dotson]] segment, plus [[DeVonta Smith]]'s career-year efficiency and slot usage spike and an in-progress [[A.J. Brown]] charting update.

New York: [[Malik Nabers]]'s toe and shoulder injuries, his route tree and after-catch profile, the [[Russell Wilson]] check-down/go-route pairing, and a bleak supporting cast ([[Wan'Dale Robinson]], [[Darius Slayton]], [[Jalen Hyatt]], [[Theo Johnson]]).
