## Summary
Week 3 edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Guys / SiriusXM, NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Rapid-fire verdicts on in-season narratives: [[Cam Skattebo]] taking the Giants backfield, whether [[Caleb Williams]] had his emerging moment, [[Luther Burden III]]'s snap ramp, the Kansas City backfield and [[Travis Kelce]]'s elite status, [[Tyreek Hill]] and quarterback arm strength, [[Harold Fannin Jr.]]'s role in Cleveland, [[Daniel Jones]]' hot start, the Rams backfield split, [[Michael Penix Jr.]] after a rough game, whether Buffalo produces a 1,000-yard receiver, [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] and [[Tetairoa McMillan]] evaluations, the Dallas backfield and [[KaVontae Turpin]] with [[CeeDee Lamb]] hurt, [[Nick Chubb]], Tampa Bay's returning pass catchers, and the [[Justin Fields]] question in New York. Waldman also delivered an extended rebuttal to Ryan Clark's Trent Richardson comparison for [[Ashton Jeanty]] and a critique of insider incentives.
