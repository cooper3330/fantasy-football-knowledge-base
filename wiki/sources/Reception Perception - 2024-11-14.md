## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) walk through the fourth annual in-season Reception Perception rookie report, four charted games per player, released to receptionperception.com subscribers. Harmon notes he only got the data 30 minutes before recording, so both hosts are in discovery mode; Koh's stated role is putting the numbers in historical context. Four receivers get full segments: [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Malik Nabers]], [[Xavier Worthy]] and [[Ladd McConkey]]. Harmon repeats the RP benchmarks throughout: 70% success rate vs man and 80% vs zone marks a straight-up good NFL player, 72%/82% marks a great one. The episode closes on a tangent about [[Will Dissly]]'s first-read share for the Chargers and Jesse Minter's Chargers defense.
