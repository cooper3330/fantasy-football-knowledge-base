## Summary
Matt Harmon (in San Francisco for Super Bowl radio row) and untracked co-host James Koh run through the wave of NFL head coach and coordinator hires from the prior week and their fantasy fallout. Covered: Kwesi Adofo-Mensah fired as Vikings GM with no replacement named; Clint Kubiak to the Raiders as head coach; Seahawks franchise sale rumors; Todd Monken to the Browns as head coach and the Shedeur Sanders/Deshaun Watson QB competition; Mike LaFleur to the Cardinals as OC and fit with Marvin Harrison Jr and Kyler Murray's expected exit; Joe Brady promoted to Bills head coach with Pete Carmichael as OC, and fit questions around Keon Coleman; Jim Leonard hired as Bills defensive coordinator; Brian Dayball to the Titans as OC under Robert Saleh, fit with Cam Ward; Bobby Slowik to the Dolphins as OC under Jeff Halfley; Drew Petzing to the Lions as OC and fit with Jared Goff; Sean Mannion to the Eagles as OC. Hosts also map how much of the league now runs Shanahan-tree or Sean Payton-tree systems.
