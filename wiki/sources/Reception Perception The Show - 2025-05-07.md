## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) work through the flurry of post-draft veteran wide receiver signings. Harmon notes he has already moved on to year-two charting, with year-two WR profiles due on the site May 12.

Segments: [[Diontae Johnson]] signing with Cleveland on a $1.17M veteran minimum with no guaranteed money, and how he, [[Cedric Tillman]] and [[Jerry Jeudy]] could be aligned around one another; [[Elijah Moore]] to Buffalo and whether he or [[Curtis Samuel]] survives the crowded Bills room; [[Amari Cooper]] rumors of a Dallas reunion and his collapsed charting profile; [[Hunter Renfrow]] to Carolina after a year away with ulcerative colitis; [[Robert Woods]] to Pittsburgh as the veteran presence around [[DK Metcalf]] and [[George Pickens]]; and a closing rant on the report that rookie tight end [[Elijah Arroyo]] may take X receiver snaps in Seattle, with [[Kyle Pitts]] and [[Dalton Kincaid]] as cautionary precedents.
