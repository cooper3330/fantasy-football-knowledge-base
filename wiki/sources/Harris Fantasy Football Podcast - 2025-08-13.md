## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with news and joint-practice reports (James Cook's Bills extension, Terry McLaurin still holding in, Jordan Love's non-throwing-hand surgery, Falcons-Titans and Jets-Giants joint practices), then reads his Footballguys-style Almanac profile of [[Brock Bowers]] — a 'don't draft him' case built on value-based drafting rather than any doubt about the player. He then welcomes Dave Kluge of Footballguys (guest, NOT a tracked expert; co-host of the Footballguys Dynasty Show with Jeff Bell) for a countdown of five ADP surprises each for 2025: quarterback ADP inflation, [[Alvin Kamara]], [[De'Von Achane]] and [[Bucky Irving]], [[Jameson Williams]], the second-year receiver market ([[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Xavier Worthy]], [[Rome Odunze]], [[Keon Coleman]]), [[Travis Hunter]], [[Ashton Jeanty]], [[Demario Douglas]] and [[Omarion Hampton]]. Recurring theme: ADP is now fractured across platforms and the edge is in hunting platform-specific outliers.
