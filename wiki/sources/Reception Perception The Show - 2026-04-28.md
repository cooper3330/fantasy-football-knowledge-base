## Summary
Matt Harmon and James Koh (untracked co-host) react to the 2026 NFL Draft's wide receiver landing spots. They open with a tangent on the 'consensus draft board' discourse before working through the first round: [[Carnell Tate]] to Tennessee at 4, [[Jordan Tyson]] to New Orleans at 8, [[Makai Lemon]] to Philadelphia at 20 (Eagles jumping the Steelers), and [[KC Concepcion]] plus [[Denzel Boston]] both to Cleveland at 24 and in round two. Each pick is broken down via Harmon's Reception Perception charting data (success rates vs. man/press/zone, route-by-route marks, contested catch rates) and projected scheme fit. They also cover secondary pieces: [[Wan'Dale Robinson]]'s free agent deal and the receiver room around [[Calvin Ridley]] in Tennessee, [[Chris Olave]] and [[Bryce Lance]] and [[Oscar Delp]] in New Orleans, [[Jerry Jeudy]] and [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] and Cleveland's crowded quarterback room ([[Deshaun Watson]], [[Shedeur Sanders]], [[Taylen Green]], [[Dylan Gabriel]]), and the confirmed [[A.J. Brown]] trade out of Philadelphia and [[DeVonta Smith]]'s standing as Eagles WR1.
