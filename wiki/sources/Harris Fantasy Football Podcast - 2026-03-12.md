## Summary
Chris Harris opens with an extended riff on why he believes the NFL salary cap is a myth (a recurring bit), then is joined by Dave Kluge of Footballguys.com (untracked guest) to review the first wave of 2026 free agency moves, position by position. They cover quarterback movement (Daniel Jones transition-tagged by Indy, Malik Willis to Miami, Tua Tagovailoa to Atlanta, Geno Smith to the Jets, Kyler Murray and Kirk Cousins cut), then spend most of the show on running backs (Kenneth Walker III to KC, Travis Etienne Jr. to New Orleans, J.K. Dobbins back to Denver over R.J. Harvey, Rico Dowdle to Pittsburgh alongside Jaylen Warren, Kenneth Gainwell to Tampa Bay, the muddled Cardinals backfield of Tyler Allgeier/James Conner/Trey Benson, Isaiah Pacheco to Detroit, Aaron Jones re-signing in Minnesota, Chris Rodriguez Jr. joining the Jacksonville mix with Bhayshul Tuten, and Keaton Mitchell to the Chargers) and wide receivers (Alec Pierce re-signing in Indy as Michael Pittman Jr. is traded to Pittsburgh, DK Metcalf staying in Pittsburgh, DJ Moore traded to Buffalo, the knock-on effect on Khalil Shakir, Romeo Doubs to New England, Mike Evans to San Francisco, Wan'Dale Robinson to Tennessee, Rashid Shaheed re-signing in Seattle). They close on tight ends: Isaiah Likely to the Giants (and what it means for Jackson Dart's rushing role), Mark Andrews's surprising new Ravens deal, Theo Johnson's blocking-heavy role, and Chigoziem Okonkwo to Washington.
