# Matt Waldman's RSP Cast — 2024-03-01

*RSP Film and Theory*, [[Matt Waldman]] with co-host **Adam Harstad**
(Footballguys — not a tracked expert). Two segments: an extended methodology
discussion of NFL Combine 40-yard-dash measurement, and a "Big Four" 2024
free-agent running back roundtable (Austin Ekeler, Josh Jacobs, Tony Pollard,
Derrick Henry).

## Summary

**40-time measurement validity.** Waldman opens by relaying [[Brandon Angelo]]'s
explanation (from an earlier *Going Deep* conversation) of how the Combine
actually times the 40: a partially-electronic "Zybek" system (electronic
start, hand-timed finish) with an estimated 0.24–0.3 second standard
deviation — enough that a "4.45" runner could really be running a 4.69, which
Waldman notes is the practical difference between a first-round and a
Day 3 wide receiver grade. Waldman uses [[Quentin Johnston]] as the worked
example of a player whose agent likely saved him money by not running.
Harstad reframes the whole question through four types of measurement
validity (construct, face, content, criterion): he agrees construct validity
(does the 40 accurately measure 40-yard speed) is weak, but argues face,
content and criterion validity are all solid — the fastest-40 leaderboard
matches observed NFL speed, and combine testing as a whole has ~60 years of
established predictive value. Waldman's rejoinder is that even acknowledging
the statistical argument, real damage is done from the fifth round downward,
where executives and coaches who don't understand the noise treat a bad
workout as validating a talent doubt and simply give a player fewer reps.
Feeds [[NFL Combine and Pro Day Skepticism]].

**Aging curves.** Harstad presents his research on career shape: the
population-level "age curve" (ramp-up, peak at 26 for RBs, decline) is an
ecological-fallacy artifact — individual careers rarely follow a smooth
curve. At receiver, 50% of players were actually better in their final
relevant season than their second-to-last; at running back it's closer to
33–40%, evidence RBs do show some real decline before falling off the "age
cliff," which he attributes to RB production being more role-dependent than
receiver production (talent-dependent). Feeds
[[Aging Curves and Career Longevity]] and [[DeAndre Hopkins]].

**Big Four 2024 free-agent RBs.** Waldman and Harstad each rank Austin
Ekeler, Josh Jacobs, Tony Pollard and Derrick Henry as bounce-back bets for
dynasty rosters not in a rebuild. [[Dynasty]] framing throughout. Both land
on [[Josh Jacobs]] as the best bet; disagree on Henry's risk/reward and on
how to read [[Tony Pollard]]'s 2023 excuse-making.
