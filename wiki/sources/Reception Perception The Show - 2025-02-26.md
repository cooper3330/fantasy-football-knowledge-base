## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] (recording from the NFL Scouting Combine in Indianapolis) and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) previewed every NFC West wide receiver room ahead of 2025 free agency.

Opening segment: both hosts lamented that the combine is 'basically canceled' for headliners — Shedeur Sanders not throwing, [[Ashton Jeanty]] not working out, [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] having skipped drills the prior year. Harmon argued a placement-accuracy quarterback like Sanders had something to gain the way [[C.J. Stroud]] did, and that he'd want to see Jeanty in pass-catching drills; both conceded there is little downside for a prospect with nothing to prove and framed it as player empowerment versus a lost information opportunity for analysts.

San Francisco: the room hinges entirely on [[Brandon Aiyuk]]'s multi-ligament knee recovery. Los Angeles: [[Cooper Kupp]] is gone, [[Puka Nacua]] led the NFL in first downs per route run, and the hosts debated whether Nacua can hold up without a credible number two, plus the Aaron Rodgers / Sam Darnold / [[Matthew Stafford]] quarterback branches. Arizona: alignment, not talent, is the problem with [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]. Seattle: Clint Kubiak's arrival, whether [[DK Metcalf]] has plateaued, and Harmon's claim that [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] is the best receiver on the roster. Both hosts picked the 49ers to win the division.
