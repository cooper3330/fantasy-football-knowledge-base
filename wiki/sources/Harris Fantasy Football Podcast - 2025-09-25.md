## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts a Thursday Week 4 show with two guests, neither tracked. First, Ben Gretch of the Stealing Signals newsletter (not a tracked expert; no longer produces weekly ranks and works mostly from film) nominates his five hardest players to evaluate for Week 4: [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]], [[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[Cade Otten]], [[TreVeyon Henderson]] and [[Cam Skattebo]]. Harris supplies his own weekly rank numbers alongside Gretch's qualitative read, so most segments contain two views. The pair then preview Thursday night Seahawks at Cardinals, centered on [[Trey Benson]] as the [[James Conner]] replacement and on [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]'s continued struggles, plus a one-word verdict on [[Kenneth Walker III]] over [[Zach Charbonnet]].

The back half is a DFS lineup segment with Jake Trowbridge (not tracked), where Harris and Trowbridge reveal DraftKings Week 4 rosters and explain the reasoning behind price-based plays: [[Puka Nacua]], [[Nico Collins]], [[A.J. Brown]], [[Keenan Allen]], [[Ashton Jeanty]], [[Chris Rodriguez Jr.]] and a Drake Maye triple stack.
