## Summary
A *Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked), built around organizational competence and quarterback development rather than weekly fantasy calls.

Four segments: (1) Chicago's handling of [[Caleb Williams]] — Angelo argues the failure is organizational, from Ryan Poles retaining Matt Eberflus through the Shane Waldron hire and firing, with Thomas Brown now expected to bring accountability; (2) the [[Daniel Jones]] to [[Drew Lock]] switch in New York, including Waldman's insider backstory on Lock's Missouri career, the Peyton Manning phone call he never followed up on, and what Lock's Seattle start showed; (3) [[Trey Lance]] and whether Dallas should play him behind Cooper Rush in a lost season; (4) Detroit — Dan Campbell, Brad Holmes, and resiliency as a roster-building principle, with a long digression on [[Jared Goff]] as a development-timeline case study.

Waldman also cautions against anointing [[Jayden Daniels]], noting his rookie data resembles Robert Griffin III's through nine games down to an 8.59 adjusted yards per attempt.
