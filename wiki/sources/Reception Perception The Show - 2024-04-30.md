## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked), recording together in person after the 2024 NFL Draft, break down every first-round wide receiver's landing spot in draft order: Rome Odunze (CHI), Marvin Harrison Jr. (ARI), Malik Nabers (NYG), Brian Thomas Jr. (JAX), Xavier Worthy (KC), Ricky Pearsall (SF), and Xavier Legette (CAR). For each they cite team receiving splits from 2023, RSP-charted man/zone/press success rates, and projected target share/roles, debating fantasy ceiling and scheme fit. Notable disagreement: Koh is initially down on the Pearsall pick before Harmon talks him into it; both are skeptical of the Worthy pick for Kansas City despite liking him as a prospect.
