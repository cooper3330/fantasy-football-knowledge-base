## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] hosts solo (co-host James Koh, not tracked, is traveling) with guest [[Matt Waldman]] — both tracked experts — for a room-by-room walk through four young wide receiver groups: Chicago, Denver, the Los Angeles Chargers and Green Bay. Harmon brings Reception Perception charting data (success rates vs man and press, route charts, alignment percentages) and Waldman brings RSP film evaluation plus target/yardage projections for nearly every receiver discussed.

Main threads: whether [[Luther Burden III]] is a gadget/manufactured-touch player or a real man-coverage winner, and how the Chicago target tree splits with [[D.J. Moore]] and [[Rome Odunze]] under Ben Johnson; Denver's unsettled group behind [[Courtland Sutton]], including Sean Payton's habit of speaking in archetypes about rookies like [[Pat Bryant]]; a [[Ladd McConkey]] appreciation segment with the one injury-mechanics caveat raised by [[Brandon Angelo]]; the Chargers' X-receiver vacancy between [[Mike Williams]], [[Tre Harris]] and [[Quentin Johnston]]; and Green Bay's flat, role-ambiguous room. The show closes on a would-you-rather between a dynamic-after-catch player with bad hands ([[Quentin Johnston]]) and a promising route runner with bad hands ([[Dontayvion Wicks]]), and on Waldman's observation that the baseline technical fluency of incoming receivers has risen.
