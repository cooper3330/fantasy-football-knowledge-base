## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] (tracked) walks through his monthly ReceptionPerception.com dynasty wide receiver rankings tier by tier with co-host James Koh (NOT a tracked expert), who plays devil's advocate throughout. Harmon explains the rankings are built as tiers rather than a straight list — he will not quibble about swapping one tier-two name for another — and that the only real driver of movement is his charting cycle: the May 16 update followed the year-two charting, and the year-three charting drop lands June 9, after which Puka Nacua, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Jordan Addison and Cedric Tillman may all move.

Tier 1 is [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Justin Jefferson]], [[CeeDee Lamb]]. Tier 2 ('knocking on the door of tier one') includes [[Puka Nacua]], [[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[Ladd McConkey]], [[Malik Nabers]], [[Nico Collins]]. Tier 3 has [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], [[Garrett Wilson]], [[A.J. Brown]], [[Travis Hunter]]. Tier 4 spans [[Emeka Egbuka]], [[Chris Olave]], [[Zay Flowers]], [[Josh Downs]], [[Matthew Golden]], [[Jayden Reed]]. Tier 5 runs from roughly WR32 to WR53 and includes [[Tyreek Hill]], [[Ricky Pearsall]], [[Jameson Williams]], [[Jordan Addison]], [[Luther Burden III]], [[Michael Pittman Jr.]], [[Jack Bech]]. Tier 6 has [[Jalen Coker]], [[Rashod Bateman]] (page as [[Rashad Bateman]]), [[Xavier Legette]], [[Diontae Johnson]], [[Cedric Tillman]]. Tier 7 is Koh's 'sleeper king' territory — [[Chimere Dike]] and [[Jordan Whittington]].

Methodology thread: Harmon argues NFL formations have condensed to the point that the slot/outside line is blurring — the [[Los Angeles Rams]] had the league's lowest average formation width in 2024 — but he will not change ten years of alignment charting because of it.
