## Summary
A 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-reaction episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys / SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Recorded in the first week of August 2024 training camp, the episode reacts to a [[Puka Nacua]] knee tweak reported as week-to-week, a healthy-looking [[Cooper Kupp]] in Rams camp, the [[Aaron Rodgers]]–[[Garrett Wilson]] timing questions in Jets camp, and a long run of camp-report calls: [[Caleb Williams]] vs [[Jayden Daniels]] as a top-10 rookie QB, [[J.K. Dobbins]] vs [[Gus Edwards]] in the Chargers backfield, [[Justin Herbert]]'s pass volume under Jim Harbaugh and Greg Roman, the Pittsburgh [[Russell Wilson]]/[[Justin Fields]] competition, [[Bo Nix]] being started immediately, [[Chris Olave]]'s dependence on [[Derek Carr]] and the Saints line, [[Parker Washington]] as a late dart throw, [[Ja'Tavion Sanders]]' rookie opportunity, [[Drake Maye]] vs [[Joe Milton III]] in New England, [[Isiah Pacheco]]'s workload ceiling under Andy Reid, [[Brock Bowers]] and [[Jakobi Meyers]] in Las Vegas, the [[Gardner Minshew]]/[[Aidan O'Connell]] job, [[De'Von Achane]]'s touch share, [[Justice Hill]] behind [[Derrick Henry]], and a closing [[Breece Hall]]-over-[[Bijan Robinson]] question. Two framework threads run through it: healthy enough to play vs. healthy enough to perform, and how much to trust early-camp reporting and teammate hype.
