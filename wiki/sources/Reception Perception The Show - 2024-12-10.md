## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) break down Week 14 with the fantasy playoffs starting. Opening segment on injury chaos ([[George Pickens]] and [[Ladd McConkey]] late scratches). Closes with a 'true or false' round on surprising team number ones in San Francisco, Atlanta, Buffalo and Seattle.
