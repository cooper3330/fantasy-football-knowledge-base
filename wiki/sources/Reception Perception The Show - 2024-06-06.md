## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not tracked) break down the 2023 Reception Perception charting for [[Nico Collins]] and [[George Pickens]]. On Collins, Harmon details a historic year-three jump in success rate vs. man (68% to 71% to 77.6%, 94th percentile) and vs. press (71.9% to 74.5% to 82.5%, 96th percentile), compares him to Allen Robinson's late breakout in Chicago, notes he beat Puka Nacua's in-breaking success rate, and argues RP metrics are sticky year-to-year so the profile should hold. He places Collins in a 'tier two' receiver bucket with Mike Evans, DeVonta Smith and Terry McLaurin, and flags his 2024 ADP/consensus rank (outside top 35 on CBS/ESPN, top 20 on Underdog) as too low. On Pickens, Harmon and Koh debate route allocation and target-share ceiling: Pickens improved press (72.4% to 73.9%) and man (64.9% to 68.7%) success but zone coverage success remains weak (17th percentile). Both note his inconsistent effort (per Steelers coaching staff comments) and emotional volatility, his 78.6% contested-catch rate in back-to-back years, and disagree mildly on whether his heavy deep-route usage (42% of routes) should be trimmed toward NFL-average volume.
