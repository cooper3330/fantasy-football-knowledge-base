## Summary
Recorded the Monday after the divisional round, with Sean McDermott just fired in Buffalo and ten head-coach openings league-wide (two filled: John Harbaugh to the Giants, Kevin Stefanski to Atlanta). Matt Waldman gives an RSP progress update (~110 players scouted toward 150+, ~60 WRs and ~45 RBs done, QBs/TEs next; 2026 RSP ships April 1 at $21.95; a dynasty rankings-and-commentary update for 2025 subscribers lands within days, no projections until June). The bulk of the episode is a rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' format co-hosted by Bob Harris (not a tracked expert), running through dynasty fringe/bench players from the 2024-2025 rookie classes -- mostly quick hold/cut verdicts with Waldman doing almost all of the evaluative talking, Bob Harris asking and occasionally reacting.
