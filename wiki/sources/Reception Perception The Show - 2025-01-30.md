## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) use the pre-Super-Bowl dead period to cover the 2025 coaching carousel and announce the site's charting calendar. Harmon lays out the Reception Perception release schedule: March 3 free agents and offseason-impacted receivers, March 24 first NFL draft prospect profiles, May 12 the 2024 rookie class, June 9 year-three receivers, July 1 year-four receivers, July 28 priority veterans, August 25 last call, targeting 60-70 total players charted by Week 1. He will attend the combine for the first time, and quarterback charting joins the site via new hire Cory Kinnan.

Football content: the Bobby Slowik firing in Houston and what it means for [[C.J. Stroud]]; whether every quarterback needs a Shanahan-style incubator offense; Kellen Moore to New Orleans and his receiver-deployment history; Pete Carroll and a possible Darrell Bevell staff in Las Vegas; the [[Matthew Stafford]] trade rumors; and Klint Kubiak to Seattle with the downstream effects on [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], [[Kenneth Walker III]], [[Geno Smith]], [[DK Metcalf]] and [[Tyler Lockett]]. Harmon also gives his first extended read on [[Travis Hunter]]'s NFL position.
