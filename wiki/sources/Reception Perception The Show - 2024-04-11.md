# Reception Perception: The Show — Adonai Mitchell & Free Agent WR's Remaining (2024-04-11)

Hosted by [[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert).

## Covered

- **[[Adonai Mitchell]] finished full-sample Reception Perception profile.**
  Charted back-to-back with Texas teammate [[Xavier Worthy]] in an offense
  Harmon dislikes (shaky QB play, layered RPOs, jogged-through routes on
  half-field reads). Extensive pushback on "lazy"/loafing narratives around
  Mitchell as scheme/coaching artifacts common to the whole Texas receiver
  corps, not a personal effort issue — though some teams reportedly have
  separate personality concerns. Strong man/press success rates (84th/83rd
  percentile), weak vs. zone (31st percentile). True X-receiver alignment
  profile (82.8% of snaps on the line, highest of any prospect charted this
  cycle). Heavy downfield route usage (35% corner/post/nine) but also a
  strong, underrated short-to-intermediate in-breaking profile (52% of
  routes; 82.4%/89.5% success on digs/outs) that Harmon says most excites him
  about the ceiling. Player comps: aggressive comp [[DeAndre Hopkins]] at his
  peak, cautious comp [[George Pickens]]. Sees a Round 1 (late) to Round 2
  draft range, not the best X receiver in the class but still a clear Day 2+
  prospect. See [[Reception Perception Methodology]], [[Scouting Bias and
  Player Archetypes]], [[Scheme vs Talent]].
- **[[Xavier Worthy]]** — contrasting note: expected to chart as more of a
  "layup receiver," not skewed downfield like Mitchell's profile.
- **[[Rome Odunze]]** — brief aside pushing back on a "Pac-12 discount"
  narrative, distinct from Harmon's genuine Big 12-offense skepticism.
- **Free-agent wide receiver buy/sell segment** (all pre-signing, recorded
  before any of these players signed): [[Odell Beckham Jr.]] to Buffalo
  (buy) or Miami (sell); [[Hunter Renfrow]] to Kansas City (cautious buy);
  [[Marquise Brown]] flanker/slot role concern amid a "too slotty" Chiefs
  room; [[Rashee Rice]] outside-alignment growth speculation and pushback on
  premature suspension-risk fantasy takes; [[Michael Gallup]] to Dallas/
  Steelers (sell) or Detroit (buy, replacing [[Josh Reynolds]]); [[D.J.
  Chark]] to Cowboys (sell); [[Tyler Boyd]] to Kansas City (sell) or
  Pittsburgh (more open); [[Marquez Valdes-Scantling]] to the Chargers
  (sell as a fit, alongside [[Quentin Johnston]]).
