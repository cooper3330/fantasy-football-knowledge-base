## Summary
Rapid-fire 'feel it or f**k it' Q&A between [[Matt Waldman]] and untracked co-host Bob Harris ('Feel It or F**k It' segment of the RSP Cast). Opens with Waldman detailing five games of film charting on supplemental-draft QB prospect Brendan Soresby, then works through a long list of dynasty/redraft questions: a superflex trade scenario involving [[Fernando Mendoza]] and [[Cam Ward]], rookie WR pecking orders (Carnell Tate, Makai Lemon), Quentin Johnston's trajectory in the Chargers offense, overblown minicamp practice-video panic (C.J. Daniels, Drew Allar), Cade Klubnik's injury-driven mechanical issues, the crowded Seattle backfield (George Holani, Jadarian Price, Emmanuel Wilson, Zach Charbonnet), Washington's receiver depth (Antonio Williams, Dyami Brown), a Cincinnati RB3 debate (Jamal Haynes vs. Tahj Brooks), a Steelers RB conversion prospect (Xavier Thomas), Baltimore's QB room (Skylar Thompson, Tyler Huntley), Tank Dell's recovery outlook and Jaylin Noel as his successor, and the Vikings QB competition between J.J. McCarthy and Kyler Murray and its effect on Juwan Jennings.
