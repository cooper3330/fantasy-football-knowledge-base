## Summary
Chris Harris reviews the remaining Week 11 Sunday games (Bears-Vikings, Packers-Giants, 49ers-Cardinals, Panthers-Falcons, Ravens-Browns) plus Monday night's Cowboys-Raiders blowout, then hosts guest Jake Seeley (The Athletic, All-In Speedrun podcast, not a tracked expert) for a Week 12 waiver countdown of players rostered under one-third of Yahoo leagues.

## Not given pages
CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens were benched for the Cowboys' first series (no reason given, presumed discipline) then both produced -- Lamb a wide-open touchdown, Pickens a strong three-target start plus his own score. Dak Prescott bounced back after two rough weeks; Jake Ferguson and Javonte Williams also scored/produced well in the blowout. Nick Chubb and Jacory Croskey-Merritt were called flatly droppable in 10-team leagues. Zay Flowers had two long catches, Lamar Jackson had a rough game (four sacks, near-picks). Harold Fannin had one catch then a drop; Dylan Gabriel was concussed and Jerry Jeudy was targeted late. Rico Dowdle and Chuba Hubbard split Panthers goal-line work in one series; Tyler Allgeier vultured a short touchdown; Xavier Legette and Jalen Coker had ancillary roles. Isaiah Hodgins had a strong game for the Giants. Christian McCaffrey scored three touchdowns (garbage-time-heavy) and George Kittle scored twice off Brock Purdy's return; Bhayshul Tuten, Brian Robinson Jr. and Blake Corum were named as handcuffs to grab (to Ashton Jeanty, Christian McCaffrey and Kyren Williams respectively). Tyrod Taylor was named the top streaming QB option (rejoined as Jets starter); Jacksonville's defense vs. Arizona and the Saints' defense vs. Falcons were named streaming DST options.
