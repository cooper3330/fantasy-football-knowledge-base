## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts, with guest Andy Behrens (co-host of the paywalled Wednesday 'Yacht Club' show; NOT a tracked expert) brought over to the free feed for the first week of the season. After a long listener-email 'snarkbag' segment and business housekeeping (the Wednesday show moving behind a $5/month paywall, plus thedeepshot.com), the two walk through selected Week 1 ranks and where they diverge from season-long ranks.

Core football content: why [[Ja'Marr Chase]] going 1.01 on every platform is, in Harris's and Behrens's shared view, a value-based-drafting error in 10- and 12-team redraft (positional scarcity, best-ball stacking habits leaking into redraft, herd behavior among analysts); Behrens's Week 1 bump of [[Trevor Lawrence]] from QB13 to QB8 versus Carolina, and his broader view that the QB position is squishy past the top six; both hosts holding [[Kenneth Walker III]] well ahead of [[Zach Charbonnet]] despite camp beat reports; [[TreVeyon Henderson]] as Behrens's flag-plant pick with Patriots-offense risk; [[Rachaad White]] as an ignored late-round value; the Jacksonville backfield ([[Travis Etienne Jr.]], [[Tank Bigsby]], [[Bhayshul Tuten]]); [[Xavier Worthy]] as the Chiefs receiver to own during [[Rashee Rice]]'s suspension; and deep skepticism about [[Stefon Diggs]] returning from a midseason torn ACL at 33. Harris also disclosed a $72 auction buy on [[Ashton Jeanty]]. The last ten minutes is a non-football four-round 'movies draft' scored by Metacritic.
