## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] (back from New Orleans) and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recap the Eagles' 40-22 Super Bowl LIX win over the Chiefs. Main threads: whether [[Xavier Worthy]]'s 8-catch, 157-yard, 2-TD line was real or garbage-time noise (he had one receiving yard at halftime, nine yards when it was 34-0 late in the third); the collapse of the Chiefs' interior offensive line and Philadelphia's 0% blitz rate with Patrick Mahomes pressured on 50% of dropbacks; Vic Fangio's 95% zone coverage rate (up from 71% in the regular season) and why it neutralized Kansas City's man-beating quick game; a long debate over where [[Jalen Hurts]] ranks among NFL quarterbacks (Koh: top five; Harmon: top 10-12); [[DeVonta Smith]] and [[Jahan Dotson]]'s slot usage; [[Saquon Barkley]] being bottled up on the ground but leading the team in first downs per route run; and Kansas City's long offseason needs list at receiver, running back, tight end and offensive line.
