## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) previewed every AFC North wide receiver room: Cincinnati, Cleveland, Pittsburgh and Baltimore. Themes were alignment and route-tree fit rather than raw projection. Cincinnati was framed as an old-school vertical attack that schemes very little for its receivers but does not need to, with [[Ja'Marr Chase]] finally being moved around (career-high 32.3% slot rate, pre-snap motion routes doubling every year) and [[Tee Higgins]] the size/press winner on the line of scrimmage. Cleveland drew heavy volume-regression warnings — [[Jerry Jeudy]] led all WRs in routes run and Harmon expects that not to repeat — with [[Cedric Tillman]] preferred as the cheaper investment. Pittsburgh was called the worst supporting cast in the NFL, with [[DK Metcalf]] coming off his worst charted season and an Arthur Smith / [[Aaron Rodgers]] route-tree mismatch. Baltimore closed the show with [[Zay Flowers]] as a high-end number two who must cut mistakes, [[Rashad Bateman]] as Harmon's favorite bet in the division, and [[DeAndre Hopkins]] as a situational X. The episode also debuted a FanDuel-sponsored props segment.
