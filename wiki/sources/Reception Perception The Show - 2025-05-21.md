## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) open on the report that Pittsburgh has called New Orleans about [[Chris Olave]]'s availability, then continue the Reception Perception second-year receiver series with charted rookie-season profiles for [[Keon Coleman]], [[Xavier Worthy]] and [[Jalen Coker]].

Harmon argues Olave is underrated (12th in yards per route run since 2022, 10th in first downs per route run among 500-plus route runners, sandwiched between [[Drake London]] and [[Ja'Marr Chase]]) and that New Orleans should keep him for [[Tyler Shough]] despite the concussion history, citing [[Brandin Cooks]] as a player who outran concussion problems. On Coleman, Harmon is openly skeptical: an 88.8% outside snap rate, 76.3% on the line of scrimmage, 50% success rate vs man, 47.9% vs press, 74.7% vs zone, a 60% contested-catch rate and a route tree that is 70% nine/curl/slant. He floats a big-slot conversion as a theory, not a projection, and offers [[Romeo Doubs]] and Devante Parker as realistic outcomes. Worthy is framed as a horizontal-stretch weapon rather than a vertical X — 60.2% vs man (20th percentile), 53.8% vs press (16th percentile), 80.1% vs zone (64th percentile) — with Harmon still preferring [[Rashee Rice]] as Kansas City's most productive receiver. Coker draws the most enthusiasm: 70.5% vs man and 70.4% vs press (63rd percentile each) as an undrafted rookie, an 88.1% slant success rate, a 78.6% contested-catch rate, and a [[Jakobi Meyers]] comparison.
