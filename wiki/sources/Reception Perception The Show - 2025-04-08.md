## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) across three segments. First, a career retrospective on [[Julio Jones]]'s retirement, leaning on the Reception Perception historic database and centering his 2016 season as the apex — 76.3% success rate vs man, 86.2% vs zone (career best, above the 95th percentile) as the true X in Kyle Shanahan's in-breaking route tree. Second, a reaction to Sean McVay saying he had not done a good job utilizing [[Tutu Atwell]], which became a broader argument about what teams pay for WR3/WR4 types versus what first-round rookie contracts cost. Third, the full Reception Perception prospect chart for [[Jaylin Noel]], whom Harmon grades in the same tier as [[Ladd McConkey]] and [[Ricky Pearsall]] were as prospects, with a career comp to [[Josh Downs]].
