## Summary
Week 8 rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' recap co-hosted by Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris. Topics: Jackson Dart's pocket-climbing deficiency exposed by the Eagles (contrasted with Justin Herbert); TreVeyon Henderson vs Rhamondre Stevenson in New England; Atlanta's offense (Bijan Robinson, Kirk Cousins, Kyle Pitts, Charlie Woerner) graded overrated but not on Cousins; Caleb Williams defended on advanced-situation QB rating; Houston's three-way WR timeshare (Jayden Higgins, Jaylen Noel, Xavier Hutchinson); Jets passing game (Arian Smith, Tyler Johnson, Mason Taylor vs Tyler Warren vs Oronde Gadsden II at rookie TE, Breece Hall trade speculation with Isaiah Davis and a hypothetical D.J. Moore swap); Chargers backfield (Kimani Vidal, Najee Harris) and passing game (Justin Herbert, Ladd McConkey); Chase Brown's resurgence; Quinshon Judkins volatility and a possible Shedeur Sanders switch; dropping Jerry Jeudy; adding Kayshon Boutte; skepticism on Roman Wilson's spike and the Steelers passing game beyond DK Metcalf (Jalen Warren); Christian Watson and Tucker Kraft in Green Bay; and a rough Tyler Shough debut.
