## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh recap Week 14. Topics: the AFC playoff picture destabilizing (Chiefs, Ravens, Colts all fading); Daniel Jones' season-ending torn Achilles vs Jacksonville and what it means for the Colts and his free agency; rookie Riley Leonard stepping in at QB and the fit with Michael Pittman Jr and Alec Pierce; a deep dive on Brian Thomas Jr's injury-marred, disappointing 2025 and Jacoby Meyers emerging as Jacksonville's lead receiver with Trevor Lawrence playing one of his best games; an extended charting-based feature on Christian Watson's post-ACL breakout as Green Bay's clear WR1 alongside notes on the crowded Packers WR room (Romeo Doubs, Jayden Reed, Dontayvion Wicks, Matthew Golden, Bo Melton); and the Chicago Bears passing game struggling without an injured Rome Odunze (revealed to be playing through a foot stress fracture), with Luther Burden III's role, DJ Moore's continued disconnection from the offense, and Caleb Williams' up-and-down game discussed.
