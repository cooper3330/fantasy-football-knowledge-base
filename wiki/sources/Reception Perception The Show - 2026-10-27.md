## Summary
Matt Harmon and James Koh (co-host, not tracked) review Week 7, built around two threads: a wave of rookie wide receiver breakouts (Casey Concepcion, Omar Cooper Jr., Malachi Fields, Caleb Douglas, Carnell Tate) and a deep dive on Malik Nabers' post-injury resurgence under J.J.
