## Summary
An RSP *Film and Theory* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Adam Harstad (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert). The whole episode is built around Atlanta benching [[Kirk Cousins]] for [[Michael Penix Jr.]] after Cousins threw eight interceptions and zero touchdowns over four weeks.

Harstad argues the near-universal criticism of Atlanta — that signing Cousins to a big deal AND drafting Penix at 1.08 was 'dumb' — is flatly wrong, and that more teams should split their quarterback bet across two players rather than concentrating it on one. Waldman agrees, adds his own view of intermittent starting/benching as a development tool, and criticizes Cousins for publicly saying the pick was a mistake. Waldman closes with a detailed film-based scouting report on Penix and the fantasy fallout for Atlanta's pass catchers over the closing schedule (Giants, Commanders, Panthers). The two also discuss breakout age skepticism, Kurt Warner's complaint about progression reads dumbing down quarterback development, and the principal-agent problem between coaches' job security and a young quarterback's long-term growth.
