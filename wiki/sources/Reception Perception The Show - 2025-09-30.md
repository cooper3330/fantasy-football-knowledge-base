## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) review Week 4 of the 2025 season. Lead segments: [[Puka Nacua]] on a 2,100-yard pace and whether he should be the No. 1 dynasty WR; the confirmed season-ending ACL tear for [[Malik Nabers]] and what is left of the Giants receiver room; [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]]'s leap to a full-time X receiver in Seattle with weeks 2-3 Reception Perception charting; a long, ambivalent segment on [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] and the oil-and-water fit with Kyler Murray and Drew Petzing; [[George Pickens]] shredding Green Bay from 96.7% outside alignment; and a 'more or less' rest-of-season game covering [[A.J. Brown]] vs [[Elic Ayomanor]], [[Ladd McConkey]] vs [[Romeo Doubs]] and [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] vs [[Cooper Kupp]]. Closes on Liam Coen's fiery sideline persona in Jacksonville.
