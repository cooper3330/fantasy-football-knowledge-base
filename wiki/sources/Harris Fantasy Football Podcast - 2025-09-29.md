## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] reviews nine Week 4 games he watched all the way through, going beyond the box score on usage, injuries and film impressions. Solo segments cover Packers-Cowboys (the 40-40 tie), Vikings-Steelers, Colts-Rams, Chargers-Giants, Bears-Raiders and Panthers-Patriots, plus a quick Monday night lineup survey (Jets-Dolphins, Bengals-Broncos).

Guest for the back half is Jamie Eisenberg of CBS Fantasy Football Today (NOT a tracked expert; all of his views are attributed to him inline). Harris and Eisenberg co-watched and discussed Eagles-Buccaneers, Jaguars-49ers and Ravens-Chiefs, including a trade debate on A.J. Brown vs Emeka Egbuka and buy-low cases on Chris Godwin and Brian Thomas Jr.

Major injury news: [[Malik Nabers]] knee (grabbed it in the air, feared season-ending), [[Lamar Jackson]] hamstring, [[Josh Jacobs]] shin/knee scare, Joe Alt high ankle sprain gutting the Chargers line.
