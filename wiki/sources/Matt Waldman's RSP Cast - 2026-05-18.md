## Summary
Bob Harris (untracked co-host) and Matt Waldman run a rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' segment reacting to takes and ADP with OTAs underway across eight teams. Topics: Aaron Rodgers' fit with Mike McCarthy in Pittsburgh (and DK Metcalf/Michael Pittman Jr. as his preferred receiver types); Pat Freiermuth's outlook now that Arthur Smith is gone; Jackson Dart's outlook in a new Matt Nagy offense minus Wan'Dale Robinson and with a diminished Malik Nabers, plus Malachi Fields' fit; Deshaun Watson's grip on the Browns' job and Shedeur Sanders/Deion Sanders speculation; a movement-coach theory about Denzel Boston's stride and injury risk (compared to Kenneth Walker III, Christian McCaffrey, Ashton Jeanty, and contrasted with Casey Concepcion); the Buccaneers' RB3 picture (Sean Tucker vs. Bucky Irving vs. Kenneth Gainwell) and Emeka Egbuka's ceiling relative to Jaxon Smith-Njigba; late-round RB flyers Nicholas Singleton and Kaytron Allen; Patriots UDFA Miles Montgomery and the Henderson-vs-Stevenson backfield; Dallas's Jayden Blue/Phil Mafah/Malik Davis picture; Ashton Jeanty's stride fix and the Raiders' offensive line investment, with a Geno Smith comp; Breece Hall's 2026 outlook behind Geno Smith; and San Francisco's Jacoby Lane/Christian Kirk/De'Zhaun Stribling/Rashad Bateman WR3 competition behind Zay Flowers, with George Kittle's Achilles recovery in the mix.
