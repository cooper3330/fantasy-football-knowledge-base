## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh recap Week 1 of the 2026 season. Main threads: Kansas City's run game transformed by Kenneth Walker III under new play-caller Eric Bienemi while the receiver corps (Rashee Rice, Xavier Worthy, Cyrus Allen) posted historically bad target/route numbers, with disagreement over how much is talent vs. Mahomes' league-fastest 2.27s time-to-throw protecting his surgically repaired knee. Denver's Sean Payton-to-Davis Webb play-calling handoff went poorly, with Jaylen Waddle badly underused (3 targets) despite fine tape -- Harmon calls it a buy-low. Green Bay's Matthew Golden debuted a clean X-outside role after a mixed rookie year. Buffalo's DJ Moore and Dalton Kincaid both impressed in the new Joe Brady offense, with Harmon actually more excited about Kincaid. Houston: Nico Collins confirmed as clear WR1, David Montgomery scored three TDs on inefficient volume (Koh unmoved on his skepticism), Woody Marks looked improved. Las Vegas's Jack Bech flashed but is buried in snap share behind Tre Tucker/Michael Mayer/Jalen Naylor. Marvin Harrison Jr.'s one-catch game is reframed through a 'sacrificial receiver' lens -- he still drew safety attention that opened the field.
