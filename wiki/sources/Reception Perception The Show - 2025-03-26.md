## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) open on free agency — [[Aaron Rodgers]] to Pittsburgh under Arthur Smith, [[Jameis Winston]] signing a two-year, $8M deal with the Giants and what that means for [[Malik Nabers]], and [[Brandin Cooks]] returning to New Orleans. The back half is the first batch of 2025 Reception Perception rookie profiles: [[Tetairoa McMillan]], [[Matthew Golden]] and [[Emeka Egbuka]], each with charted man/zone/press success rates, route-tree splits and historical prospect comps. Harmon notes [[Travis Hunter]]'s profile drops the following Monday and that he considers Hunter clearly the best receiver in the class.
