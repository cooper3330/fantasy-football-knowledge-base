## Summary
Pre-NFL-Draft 'Feel It or F**k It' episode with Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris, recorded days before the 2026 NFL Draft. Waldman recaps his own picks in a pre-draft superflex dynasty startup (Ty Simpson, Michael Trigg, DeMond Claiborne, Josh Cameron) and the two work through rapid-fire prospect and news takes: Jordan Tyson's viral workout tape and injury-mechanism concerns, Oscar Delp in a compressed tight end class, whether KC Concepcion is the WR the Chiefs need (with a skeptical dive into Andy Reid's WR-drafting track record and Rashee Rice), the meaning of top-30 pre-draft visits, Zachariah Branch's pre-draft arrest, C.J. Allen as an IDP dynasty target, UFL standout Hakeem Butler's NFL prospects, Connecticut QB Joe Fagnano as a developmental sleeper, underrated Penn State RB Nicholas Singleton, Drew Allar's likely NFL overdraft, Matthew Golden's stalled rookie year, and a Makai Lemon vs. KC Concepcion yards-after-catch comparison.
