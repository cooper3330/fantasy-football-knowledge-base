## Summary
Chris Harris opens with Stefan Diggs signing with the Commanders and gives his ranking/analysis of the move, plus a note that it does not downgrade Terry McLaurin. Housekeeping follows (Pound Foolish book pre-order bribes, almanac, schedule). The main segment is a 'Film Room Heroes' countdown with guest JJ Zachariason (Lateround.com, not a tracked expert), each naming five players they enjoy on film regardless of current fantasy price: Zachariason picked Nico Collins, Josh Downs, Brenton Strange, Chase Brown, Khalil Shakir, DeVonta Smith and Jalen Warren (final list order 5-1 mixed with Harris's own five); Harris picked Nico Collins, Alec Pierce, Bucky Irving, DeVonta Smith and De'Von Achane. They discuss and sometimes disagree on player evaluations throughout.
