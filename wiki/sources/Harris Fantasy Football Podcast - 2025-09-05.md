## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a film-and-eyeball review of the Thursday night Cowboys-Eagles opener, heavily caveated by two variables: Jalen Carter being ejected before the first offensive snap for spitting on [[Dak Prescott]], and an hour-plus thunderstorm delay after which neither team scored again (24-20 at the delay, 24-20 final). He covers the Dallas run game and backfield, [[CeeDee Lamb]]'s three-drop night, [[George Pickens]] and [[Jake Ferguson]] usage, then the Philadelphia side: [[Saquon Barkley]] as the best player on the field, the backup-back picture, [[Jalen Hurts]]' scramble-first habits and the invisible outside receivers.

A Friday injury roundup follows: the [[Christian McCaffrey]] calf-strain practice report, [[Christian Kirk]], [[Jayden Reed]], [[Najee Harris]], [[Darnell Mooney]] and [[Amari Cooper]]'s retirement.

Guest for the back half is JEFF ERICKSON of RotoWire (NOT a tracked expert), filling in for Cousin Josh, for a countdown of the five things freaking each of them out before Week 1 — Erickson: not owning [[Puka Nacua]]/[[Ladd McConkey]]/[[A.J. Brown]]/[[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]], too much Atlanta exposure, forgetting a league among ~20, McCaffrey, and Bengals fandom; [[Chris Harris]]: [[Malik Nabers]]/[[Brian Thomas Jr.]] regression risk, the Texans offense, [[Tyreek Hill]], McCaffrey, [[J.J. McCarthy]].
