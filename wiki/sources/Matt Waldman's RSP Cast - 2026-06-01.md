## Summary
Matt Waldman and co-host Bob Harris (untracked, Feel It or F**k It) run their rapid-fire 'feel it or fuck it' format across roughly 20 players and situations during June 1 OTAs, covering the Bills receiving room after the DJ Moore trade, the Giants' post-Nabers-recovery pecking order, Miami's thin receiving corps and QB plan, Pittsburgh's Pat Freiermuth and Darnell Washington, Philadelphia's Saquon Barkley scheme fit, Jacksonville's three-man receiver race, Arizona's Devin Duvernay, Chicago's Luther Burden III/Colston Loveland/Rome Odunze mix versus Amon-Ra St. Brown, Green Bay's backfield behind an under-investigation Josh Jacobs, Detroit's Sam LaPorta injury outlook versus Mark Andrews and Trey McBride, Houston's Tank Dell recovery, the Chargers' Ladd McConkey/Quentin Johnston/Trey Harris group, and Las Vegas's Ashton Jeanty workload projection. Waldman also announces his 2026 RSP dynasty rankings/projections package releasing by June 10 and his upcoming SiriusXM Fantasy Sports Radio slot starting July 6.
