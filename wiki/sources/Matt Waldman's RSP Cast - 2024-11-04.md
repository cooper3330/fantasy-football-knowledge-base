## Summary
Week 9 reaction show. [[Matt Waldman]] with co-host Bob Harris (Feel It or F**k It; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Topics: the Saints firing Dennis Allen and the collapse from 45 points per game under Klint Kubiak; the Raiders firing OC Luke Getsy, QB coach Rich Scangarello and OL coach James Cregg, and what NFL position coaches actually do; Michael Thomas's public tirade at Derek Carr; the Colts benching [[Anthony Richardson]] for [[Joe Flacco]] and what Jim Irsay's impatience means for the franchise; [[Sam Darnold]]'s Vikings play and a hypothetical [[Matthew Stafford]] trade; [[C.J. Stroud]] as a fade until [[Nico Collins]] returns; [[Jayden Reed]] as Green Bay's go-to receiver; [[Jordan Love]]'s 'Utah State quicksand' decisions; whether the Packers miss [[Aaron Jones]] with [[Josh Jacobs]] in the backfield; [[Jared Goff]] in bad weather on the road; [[Saquon Barkley]]'s reverse hurdle; the Jaguars leaving [[Brenton Strange]] out of the game plan and the [[Tank Bigsby]] usage warning; [[James Conner]]'s longevity; [[DeMarcus Robinson]] as a matchup-dependent Rams option; and [[De'Von Achane]] vs [[Raheem Mostert]] rest-of-season value.
