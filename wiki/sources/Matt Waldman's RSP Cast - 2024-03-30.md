# Matt Waldman's 2024 RSP TE A-Z Solo Cast

## Summary

Solo cast (no co-host), the last of Waldman's four positional A-Z sweeps ahead
of the RSP's April 1 pre-draft release, covering all 22 tight ends in the 2024
RSP class alphabetically. Opens with historical framing: tight end is the
hardest rookie position to produce in immediately, "opportunity precedes
talent," and rookie TE production is a two-decade small sample — [[Sam
LaPorta]]'s 2023 season (235.3 fantasy points) came within 0.3 points of Mike
Ditka's 1961 all-time rookie-TE record (235.6), with [[Dalton Kincaid]] and
[[Kyle Pitts]] also inside the historical top 15. Names [[Brock Bowers]] as
the class's obvious "can he bust Ditka's door down" question. Identifies
Bowers and Cade Stover as this year's most pro-ready prospects if used as
receivers/H-backs rather than in-line blockers, with Ja'Tavion Sanders and Ben
Sinnott close behind; Tip Reiman and Theo Johnson could handle extended
in-line work but at a receiving-upside cost. Closes with the usual RSP
pre-order pitch (April 1 release, $21.95, 150+ prospects).
