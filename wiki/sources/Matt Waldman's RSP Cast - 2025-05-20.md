## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] hosts JJ Zachariason (Late Round Draft Guide / lateround.com, NOT a tracked expert) for a ~90-minute conversation about 2025 redraft strategy, draft philosophy and specific player values. Zachariason lays out his core framework — embracing variance, being 'directionally right' rather than precise, and buying ambiguity where the market buys safety — then argues that 2024 was the healthiest top-24 running back season in roughly 15 years, which inflated RB2 scoring and will cause a market overcorrection in 2025 RB ADP. He restates his 'pocket passer trap' rule (immobile top-six QBs by ADP have historically finished worse than mobile high-end QB2s), naming [[Jared Goff]] as the quintessential overpriced pocket passer and [[Brock Purdy]] as a discount alternative. The back half is player-by-player: [[RJ Harvey]] (Sean Payton RB target share history vs. Waldman's film concerns on pass pro, decision-making and ball security), [[Kenneth Walker III]], [[Davante Adams]], [[Jaylen Waddle]], [[Evan Engram]], [[Harold Fannin Jr.]], [[Luther Burden III]] and [[Rome Odunze]] (including Zachariason's much-discussed dynasty trade of Odunze for the 1.08), [[Kaleb Johnson]], [[Matthew Golden]], [[Jaydon Blue]], [[Jarquez Hunter]], [[Emeka Egbuka]] and [[Tyler Warren]]. Waldman shares his own in-progress 2025 projections for Denver, Chicago and Green Bay. He also relays an injury-risk note from co-host [[Brandon Angelo]], an athletic trainer, about [[Puka Nacua]].
