## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a rankings-update segment driven by the Washington Commanders drama and other late-August news: [[Terry McLaurin]] dropped a full round, [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] pushed down the board while awaiting a cut or trade, [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] moved into the top 100, [[Quinshon Judkins]] left untouched pending a contract, [[Najee Harris]] cratered on a report he will not play Week 1, [[Omarion Hampton]] bumped up near the top of the fourth round, and the [[Zach Charbonnet]] / [[Kenneth Walker III]] Seattle noise explicitly not acted on.

The main segment is the annual Super-Deep Sleepers list — players Harris explicitly says NOT to draft in a 12-team league, meant as dynasty and in-season waiver-wire names outside the top 200. Guest Jake Ciely of The Athletic (co-host of the All In Show; NOT a tracked expert) brings five names ([[Cam Ward]], [[DJ Giddens]], [[Ollie Gordon II]], [[Elic Ayomanor]], [[Roman Wilson]]) and Harris brings five ([[Tahj Brooks]], [[Efton Chism III]], [[Jalen Coker]], [[Jordan James]], [[Adonai Mitchell]]).
