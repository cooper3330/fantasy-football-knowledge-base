## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] with co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]) for the Week 13 'Feel It or F-It' segment. Framed by the [[Christian McCaffrey]] season-ending PCL injury, a likely-done [[Taysom Hill]] and six teams on bye in Week 14, the theme was speculative 'just in case' playoff-roster adds: bench stashes who could win a week outright. Players covered: Sincere McCormick, [[KaVontae Turpin]], [[Noah Gray]], [[Michael Penix Jr.]], [[Kareem Hunt]] / [[Isiah Pacheco]], [[Christopher Brooks]], [[Calvin Austin III]], [[Alec Pierce]], [[Parker Washington]] and [[Nick Westbrook-Ikhine]].

A second segment ran underrated/overrated: [[Jayden Reed]], [[Ladd McConkey]] (knee) and his possible replacements, [[James Cook]], [[C.J. Stroud]], [[Aaron Rodgers]], [[Kyler Murray]], [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] and [[Michael Wilson]]. A recurring thread was recovery timelines — [[Kirk Cousins]], Rodgers, [[Tony Pollard]] and Pacheco — and whether nine weeks out differs in kind from nine months.
