## Summary
Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris run their weekly 'Feel It or F**k It' segment reacting to preseason Week 1 news. Waldman opens with a position-by-position framework for how much stock to put in camp/preseason reports (QB, WR, RB, TE each treated differently), then the two work through a long list of players: Christian McCaffrey's non-practice status and its possible ties to Bijan Robinson/Jahmyr Gibbs getting paid; Chuba Hubbard's hamstring injury opening a window for Jonathan Brooks; a minor Breece Hall groin issue; Caleb Douglas's role and ceiling behind Malik Washington; Greg Dulcich's quiet efficiency; the Commanders rookie RB battle between Robert Henry and Kaytron Allen; Camden Brown's roster chances in Dallas; the Denzel Boston vs. Casey Concepcion timeshare in Cleveland alongside Quinshon Judkins and Harold Fannin Jr.; Davon Booth as a Judkins handcuff; Emmett Johnson's Chiefs debut; a long Ty Simpson dynasty-QB discussion; a detailed Jeremiah Love value/ADP take; the Malik Benson/Jack Bech/Dont'e Thornton Jr. Raiders WR competition; Miles Montgomery's IR designation in New England and the George Holani comp; Jordan Tyson's injury risk and technique concerns; pushback on starting Drew Allar in Pittsburgh; and the Patriots' Lan Larison/Jam Miller handcuff roles.
