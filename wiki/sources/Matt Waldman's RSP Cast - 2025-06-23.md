## Summary
A solo episode — Bob Harris (co-host of 'Feel It or F**k It', NOT a tracked expert) was off, so [[Matt Waldman]] ran his own version of the segment alone, reacting to offseason news items and closing with IDP rookie recommendations.

News-point takes covered: Mike Williams reuniting with Justin Herbert as the Chargers' 'X' and contested-catch answer; Cole Kmet's future in Chicago as a check-down/blocking tight end rather than a one-on-one matchup threat; R.J. Harvey's receiving progress under Sean Payton and why the J.K. Dobbins signing pushed Harvey back down to Waldman's pre-draft placement; a long critique of Drew Petzing's stated plan to lean further into Kyler Murray's off-schedule play creation, built on a Baker Mayfield case study (the Rose Bowl vs. Georgia, the Cleveland years, and learning to climb the pocket); Ladd McConkey bulking up and Brandon Angelo's observation about how McConkey lands; Garrett Wilson's extension talks with the Jets; the Giants' Russell Wilson / Jaxson Dart timeline and what it means for Brian Daboll and Joe Schoen; Travis Kelce's weight loss and a bounce-back projection; Michael Wilson being capped by his quarterback; Jayden Higgins' alignment versatility opposite Nico Collins; Malachi Corley's route-tree limitations; Tyjae Spears vs. Brian Callahan's stated three-way split; Dont'e Thornton Jr.'s 'top target' reports; Roman Wilson's opportunity in a crowded Pittsburgh room; DK Metcalf and slants; DeAndre Hopkins' red-zone fit with Lamar Jackson; and Joe Mixon's availability opening a window for Nick Chubb.

The show closed with an IDP segment sourced partly from Dan Hatman and Alex Hanowitz at the Scouting Academy (neither tracked), recommending four defensive front players by draft range, and a read of a subscriber email about RSP-driven dynasty win-rate gains.
