## Summary
A training-camp-opening edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman notes RSP dynasty rankings and projections subscribers should expect their first update no later than August 5.

The format is a rapid-fire yes/no on training camp claims. Topics: the Buffalo receiver pecking order ([[Josh Palmer]] vs [[Keon Coleman]] behind [[Khalil Shakir]] and [[Dalton Kincaid]]); Dan Orlovsky's claim that [[Caleb Williams]] needs years to learn Ben Johnson's timing game; [[Anthony Richardson]] as a free-square last-round best ball quarterback; the difference between [[Adonai Mitchell]]'s technique-based drops and [[Keon Coleman]]'s focus-based drops; [[Dillon Gabriel]] as 'the Brock Purdy of the Browns'; [[Kyle Monangai]] versus [[Roschon Johnson]] for the Chicago RB2 job; [[Isaiah Neyor]]'s 49ers roster odds; [[Jalen Milroe]] versus [[Sam Darnold]] in Seattle; [[Nick Chubb]] and [[Stefon Diggs]] as comeback player candidates; [[Oronde Gadsden II]]'s camp buzz as the rookie Waldman might have whiffed on; the Jacksonville backfield ([[Tank Bigsby]] vs [[Travis Etienne Jr.]]); what first-team camp reps actually signal; [[Travis Hunter]]'s risk of being a gadget/scheme player in year one; [[Parker Washington]] over [[Dyami Brown]]; and whether [[Chase Brown]] is a true feature back.
