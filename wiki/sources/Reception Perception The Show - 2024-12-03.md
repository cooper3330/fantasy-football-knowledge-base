## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on Week 13 takeaways plus a full re-rank of the 2024 rookie receiver class.

Opening segment: fantasy playoff teams, and a long autopsy on [[C.J. Stroud]] and the Houston offense — Harmon argues the offensive line is a bottom-five unit, the weapons collapsed around him (Nico Collins injury, Stefon Diggs to IR, Tank Dell below replacement level) and Stroud is developing bad habits; Koh notes Stroud is QB26 in fantasy points per game and is the most negatively impactful fantasy QB because he is healthy enough to keep starting.

Real or Mirage: Kirk Cousins cooked (both say real — six INTs and zero TDs over three straight losses, no velocity, suspected leg/health issue); D.J. Moore rejuvenated (real-ish, with the Matt Eberflus firing and Thomas Brown's interim offense); Parker Washington as a viable starter (viable receiver, not a clear-cut starter); Adam Thielen as a top-25 WR (mirage on the ranking, real on the hands), which turned into praise for [[Bryce Young]]'s in-season development under Dave Canales.

Main segment: 2024 rookie WR class re-rank — Harmon's current order is Nabers, Harrison, McConkey, Odunze, Brian Thomas Jr., then a large drop to Coleman/Legette/Pearsall and an incomplete grade for Xavier Worthy.
