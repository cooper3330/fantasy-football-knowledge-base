## Summary
Week 14 'Feel It or F**k It' episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys / SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Opens with RSP publication housekeeping (2025 RSP pre-draft/post-draft pre-order at $19.95 through Dec 21, then $21.95; Dynasty Rankings and Projections at $24.95, updated June through June).

Main segments: whether [[Saquon Barkley]] breaks Eric Dickerson's single-season rushing record; an extended 'fantasy black hole quarterback' tier exercise (credited on air to Alex Hanowitz) covering [[Jalen Hurts]], [[Kyler Murray]], [[Marcus Mariota]] and [[Josh Allen]]; [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]'s disappointing rookie season and the technique flaws Waldman charted pre-draft; Bob Harris's 'don't pay a premium in drafts' rule and the Gibbs-over-Bijan / Nabers-over-Harrison precedent; [[Jameis Winston]] in Cleveland; [[Sam Darnold]]'s five-TD game in Minnesota; Jets and Saints backfield questions ([[Isaiah Davis]], [[Kendre Miller]]); [[Sincere McCormick]] and Antonio Pierce's late-season hype pattern; [[Michael Mayer]] stuck behind [[Brock Bowers]]; [[Kirk Cousins]] vs [[Michael Penix Jr.]] in Atlanta; and [[Bryce Young]]'s post-benching revival and how much credit Dave Canales deserves.
