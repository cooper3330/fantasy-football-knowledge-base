## Summary
Chris Harris opens the show back from an off-season break and reacts to Jaylen Waddle being traded from the Dolphins to the Broncos, debating with Yacht Club co-host Andy Behrens (not tracked) whether the crowded Denver room (Courtland Sutton, Troy Franklin, Pat Bryant, Marvin Mims Jr.) helps or hurts Waddle and what it does to the fantasy value of the incumbents. The bulk of the episode is a guest interview with tracked expert Matt Waldman covering the newly-released 2026 Rookie Scouting Portfolio wide receiver rankings: Carnell Tate (WR1), Makai Lemon (WR2), Malachi Fields (WR3), KC Concepcion (WR4), Skylar Bell (WR5), Jordyn Tyson (WR6), Chris Brazzell (WR7), Omar Cooper Jr. (WR8), plus deeper dives on Denzel Boston (WR14 vs. consensus top-5), Chris Bell, and late-round names Kevin Coleman, Junior Vandeross III, Eric McAllister and Daniel Sopkiewicz (Waldman's WR36). Waldman also expands on his Combine/RAS skepticism and his 'clap attacking' catch-technique framework.
