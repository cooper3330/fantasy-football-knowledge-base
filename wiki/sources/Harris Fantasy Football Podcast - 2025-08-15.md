## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] closes out his 2025 'flag player' list (players he believes the market undervalues) with numbers 5 through 1, opening with a news block from Thursday's joint practices and then handing the second half of the show to Cousin Josh (untracked co-host) for his annual 'frag player' list — players he thinks will blow up your fantasy team at their price.

News and ranks moves: [[Josh Downs]] pulled a hamstring in the Packers joint practice; [[Chris Godwin]] is expected to open on PUP and drops outside Harris's top 40 WRs; [[Rashee Rice]] falls to WR32 as a suspension looks likely but is now expected mid-season rather than Week 1; [[Quinshon Judkins]] had his domestic battery charge dismissed and [[Isaiah Bond]]'s was thrown out; [[TreVeyon Henderson]] was raised to RB28 on joint-practice speed reports.

Flag players 5-1: [[Jordan Addison]] (5), [[Chuba Hubbard]] (4), [[Ricky Pearsall]] (3), [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] (2), [[Josh Jacobs]] (1). Harris also ran a [[Best Ball]] DraftKings entry built entirely on stacks.

Cousin Josh's frag players 5-1: [[DeAndre Swift]] (5), [[Xavier Worthy]] (4), [[Kaleb Johnson]] (3), [[Baker Mayfield]] (2), [[Brock Bowers]] (1), with an extended value-based-drafting argument about paying up for tight ends.
