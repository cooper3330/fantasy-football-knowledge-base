## Summary
Chris Harris recaps the Thursday night Rams-49ers game from Australia (49ers won convincingly), covering the Rams' disappointing defense (Miles Garrett neutralized), Matthew Stafford's misses to Davante Adams, the Kyren Williams/Blake Corum timeshare, and the 49ers' dink-and-dunk passing attack featuring Mike Evans, a suspected Achilles injury to De'Zhaun Stribling, Deebo Samuel's expanded role, a Christian McCaffrey/Kalen Black snap split with a cramping explanation Harris is skeptical of, and George Kittle's limited return from injury.
