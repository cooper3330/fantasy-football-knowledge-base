## Summary
Week 1 edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys / SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Rapid-fire verdicts on news and draft-season debates the morning of Week 1.

Topics: [[Clyde Edwards-Helaire]] placed on the non-football injury list (four games) and what [[Samaje Perine]]'s arrival means for [[Isiah Pacheco]]; whether [[Carson Steele]] is real; [[Josh Palmer]] vs [[Ladd McConkey]] for Chargers targets; [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] as an underrated flex; [[Dyami Brown]] and [[D.J. Chark]] as last-round dart throws in Washington; [[Michael Thomas]] as a waiver-wire watch; [[Josh Jacobs]] and his stated dislike of the Chiefs; the futility of drafting a 49ers reserve running back behind [[Christian McCaffrey]] ([[Jordan Mason]], [[Isaac Guerendo]]); the Cowboys committee ([[Rico Dowdle]], [[Ezekiel Elliott]], [[Dalvin Cook]]); [[Xavier Worthy]] as the rookie receiver people will wish they drafted; [[Caleb Williams]] as the QB people will wish they drafted; [[Jonathan Mingo]] as the second-year breakout and what that means for [[Xavier Legette]]; [[Tim Patrick]] to Detroit and skepticism about [[Jameson Williams]]; and [[Jameis Winston]] starting in Cleveland by midseason over [[Deshaun Watson]]. Strategy segments on FAAB allocation and preseason trades.
