## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) covered the NFL coaching carousel and previewed both conference championship games, then took Discord questions on receiver tiering.

Coaching segment: Aaron Glenn to the Jets (Harmon loves the hire for the Jets, Koh 'hates it for Aaron Glenn' given Woody Johnson and no quarterback), the Detroit brain drain (Mark Brunell, Hank Fraley, Tanner Engstrand), Josh McDaniels back as New England OC — Harmon calls him a solid 'B-level' coordinator who will never be a head coach again and therefore a stable long-term fit for [[Drake Maye]] — Trent Baalke's firing in Jacksonville (Harmon: too late, a toxic building; he would chase Joe Brady), and Liam Coen returning to Tampa Bay, which Harmon called the key retention for a young Bucs offense.

Game previews: Bills at Chiefs (Harmon sees a [[Khalil Shakir]] matchup vs. Kansas City's interior coverage weakness; both hosts note the total failure of the [[DeAndre Hopkins]] and [[Amari Cooper]] deadline trades) and Commanders at Eagles ([[Saquon Barkley]] vs. a Washington run defense Harmon considers unable to stop him; Quinyon Mitchell's shoulder and [[Terry McLaurin]]'s alignment).

Discord segment: Harmon tiered the 'complementary number two' receivers — [[DeVonta Smith]] 1, [[Tee Higgins]] 2, [[Jaylen Waddle]] 3, [[Jordan Addison]] 4 — then added [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] (possibly number one of the group), [[DK Metcalf]] and [[Jameson Williams]] (bottom). He also revealed he is rebuilding his dynasty rankings and cut [[Odell Beckham Jr.]] from the list.
