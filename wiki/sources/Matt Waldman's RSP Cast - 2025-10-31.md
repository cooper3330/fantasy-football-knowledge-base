## Summary
Film and Theory segment co-hosted by Adam Harstad (untracked) and Matt Waldman. First half: Harstad extends his 'platonic idealism' framework from the prior week to player value -- distinguishing real-world lowercase-v value (what charts like Dan Hendry's Football Guys work, FantasyCalc and KeepTradeCut actually track) from an unreachable capital-V platonic ideal value that would have perfect foreknowledge of career outcomes. Harstad's key insight: platonic ideal value should decline after every big game (one fewer big game left in the tank), but real-world value often rises after a breakout because it teaches the market something new -- except for already-fully-scouted stars, whose value should stay flat because a big game tells us nothing new. He illustrates the 'rises after production' pattern with Oronde Gadsden II vs Colston Loveland, and the 'flat value = free production' inefficiency with Patrick Mahomes and Dak Prescott, extending the idea to Justin Jefferson, Brock Bowers, Matthew Stafford, Justin Herbert and C.J. Stroud. Second half: the two go position-by-position through the NFL's 2026 Hall of Fame semi-finalist list (QBs, RBs, WRs) -- all retired-player HOF debate, not fantasy-relevant, summarized below but not built out as wiki pages.
