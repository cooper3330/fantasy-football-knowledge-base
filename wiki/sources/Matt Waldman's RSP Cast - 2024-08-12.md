## Summary
A solo cast — [[Matt Waldman]] notes Bob Harris (co-host of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment; not a tracked expert) was traveling for the Fantasy Expo, so there is no co-host and no normal segment format. Waldman covers four things: (1) why preseason film clips get overrated, since analysts reviewing them are often seeing a player for the first time and the flashy plays are usually things the player already did in college; (2) coach-speak as a blind-spot detector, using Jim Harbaugh's praise of [[Quentin Johnston]]'s ball tracking and the Packers' claim that [[Marshawn Lloyd]]'s ball security is 'an easy fix' as examples of coaches speaking outside their real expertise; (3) a six-year study he ran on RSP ball-security grades versus top-60 fantasy running back finishes; and (4) three fantasy lists — players he is changing his mind on, players he is holding firm on, and dynasty players he is simply curious about.
