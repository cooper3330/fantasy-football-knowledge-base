## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] on the *Going Deep* segment of the RSP Cast. Angelo, who works in the injury/rehab field, opened with a long breakdown of [[Rashee Rice]]'s surgery, arguing the hamstring tendon rupture is far more severe than the widely-reported LCL and that recovery to full performance is a 16-18 month proposition. From there: the Houston passing game without [[Nico Collins]] ([[Tank Dell]], [[Xavier Hutchinson]], [[Dalton Schultz]], [[Joe Mixon]]), the Jacksonville backfield ([[Tank Bigsby]] vs [[Travis Etienne Jr.]]), [[Josh Allen]]'s 9-for-30 game against Houston and the misuse of [[Dalton Kincaid]] and [[Keon Coleman]], early 2025 draft-class notes ([[Ashton Jeanty]], Travis Hunter, [[Nicholas Singleton]], [[Quinshon Judkins]], [[Mario Anderson]], [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]]), and a shared 'young tight ends' thread ([[Eric All]], [[Charlie Kolar]], [[Brenton Strange]], [[Mark Andrews]]).
