## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) walk through the Reception Perception year-two charting profiles for the top of the 2024 rookie wide receiver class — [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Malik Nabers]], [[Rome Odunze]] and [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] — arguing the 'big three' at the top of that draft ended up being a big four. Harmon says he is now convinced the 2024 class will live up to its billing, and that several members are being underrated in isolation because a few were so productive right away.

Harrison gets the longest segment: Harmon splits the blame for his disappointing counting stats three ways (the player, [[Kyler Murray]]'s quarterback play, and Arizona's deployment), and rejects the idea that Harrison cannot separate. Nabers is framed as the highest-ceiling player of the group. Odunze is defended as a victim of a poisoned Chicago offensive environment and a crowded, ego-heavy receiver room, with Harmon arguing he should be the full-time X. Brian Thomas Jr.'s 93rd-percentile press-coverage mark puts him in company with Chase, Lamb, Hill, Jefferson and Beckham as rookies. Harmon also pushes back on 'generational prospect' inflation, saying only [[Travis Hunter]] earned that label in his view.
