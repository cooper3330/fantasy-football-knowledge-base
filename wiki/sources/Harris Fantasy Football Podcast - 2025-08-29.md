## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] closes the 2025 draft season with two segments. First, the final Almanac update: he reads the rewritten [[Joe Mixon]] profile in full (Mixon placed on the NFI list, guaranteed to miss the first four games, dropped to a round-eight target but with the 'flag' kept planted), and notes the Micah Parsons trade from Dallas to Green Bay as a hit to the Cowboys and a boost to the Packers defense. He also recaps a DraftKings $15 million best ball 'Millie Maker' draft from the seven slot.

The main segment is a risk-quotient exercise with guest Jeff Bell of Football Guys (host of the Dynasty Show with Dave Kluge; NOT a tracked expert), rating the top 36 Football Guys ADP players 1 (least risky) to 10 (most risky). Harris and Bell give separate numbers for every player and frequently disagree — most sharply on [[Christian McCaffrey]] (Harris 8, Bell 3), [[Brock Bowers]] (Harris 8, Bell 2), [[Ashton Jeanty]] (Harris 7, Bell 4), [[Lamar Jackson]] (Harris 3, Bell 6) and [[Trey McBride]] (Harris 5, Bell 2). Harris closes with a 'players not positions' pep talk for draft weekend.
