## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] (tracked) and co-host James Koh (NOT a tracked expert) walk through two freshly published Reception Perception charting profiles: [[Puka Nacua]] and [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]]. Harmon says six profiles are live on receptionperception.com (Nacua, Smith-Njigba, [[Jordan Addison]], [[Jayden Reed]], [[Josh Downs]], [[Rashee Rice]]) with [[Cedric Tillman]], [[Dontayvion Wicks]], [[Zay Flowers]], [[Marvin Mims Jr.]] and possibly [[Quentin Johnston]] to follow; he notes he charts full samples of at least eight games rather than partial roundups, with [[Rashee Rice]] an exception at ~3.1 games.

The Nacua segment is Harmon formally promoting him into what he calls the six-man tier one of NFL receivers. The Smith-Njigba segment covers his 2024 breakout, his unusually balanced route tree, his history as a man-coverage beater, and whether he can move outside now that [[Cooper Kupp]] is in Seattle. The episode closes on a broader argument that condensed formations in the Shanahan/McVay tree are blurring the slot-vs-outside distinction fantasy analysis still leans on.
