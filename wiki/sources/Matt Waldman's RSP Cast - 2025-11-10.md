## Summary
Week 10 edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys/SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Rapid-fire prompts across rookie quarterbacks, the Colts' skill players, tight end class debates and buy-low backs.

Main threads: [[Jackson Dart]]'s improved downfield accuracy and the self-preservation problem behind his design-run volume; [[Matthew Stafford]] as a case study in survival-as-greatness; [[Kyle Pitts]] and whether [[Michael Penix Jr.]] is finally targeting him downfield; [[Jonathan Taylor]]'s 244-yard, three-touchdown game and Waldman's pass-protection nitpick; an extended [[Tyler Warren]] vs [[Colston Loveland]] comparison in which Waldman holds his April tight-end-one ranking on Loveland; [[Luther Burden III]]'s imminent role expansion in Chicago; a defense of [[Bo Nix]] against film-community criticism; [[J.K. Dobbins]] as a what-could-have-been; [[Blake Corum]] as a hold; [[Breece Hall]] trade fits; [[Davis Mills]] as a journeyman starter; the [[Rashid Shaheed]] effect on [[Cooper Kupp]] and [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]]; [[Justin Fields]] as Kordell Stewart; [[Sean Tucker]]; [[Kyle Williams]]'s speed and drops; [[Charlie Kolar]] vs [[Isaiah Likely]] in Baltimore; [[Tetairoa McMillan]] and Carolina's lack of a true number one; and [[Ladd McConkey]]'s WR7 run since Week 4.
