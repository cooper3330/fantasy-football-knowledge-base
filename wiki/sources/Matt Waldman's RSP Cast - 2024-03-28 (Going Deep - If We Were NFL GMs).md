# Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-03-28 (Going Deep - If We Were NFL GMs)

*Going Deep*, with co-host [[Brandon Angelo]]. A structured "if we were NFL
GMs" exercise about a month before the 2024 draft (RSP pre-draft guide due
April 1). Both hosts go through four categories in turn: players they would
put on a "do not draft" list, high-floor round-four-plus picks, "problem
solvers," and a closing "plant your flag" pick each. Ends with both hosts
previewing their respective subscription products (Waldman's RSP guide,
Angelo's site).

## Do Not Draft
- [[Adonai Mitchell]] (Angelo) — elite athlete, but inconsistent route intent/detail; devastating in the low red zone but a different player between the 20s.
- [[Devontez Walker]] (Angelo) — speed/size present, but erratic route execution; struggled to separate/track late vs. good corners at the Senior Bowl.
- [[Braelon Allen]] (Waldman) — a volume/rhythm back as the NFL trends away from RB-centric offenses; "fragmented" game with a hip-turn power-leak flaw.
- [[Drake Maye]] (Waldman, with a qualifier — only if a team needs him to start immediately) — the "robo quarterback": pedigree/physical boxes checked, but pinpoint accuracy, coverage processing, and pocket management aren't there yet.

## High Floor (Round 4+)
- [[Malik Washington]] (Angelo's flag pick) — day-one slot starter, Jarvis Landry/Santana Moss comp.
- [[Jermaine Burton]] (Angelo) — better-NFL-than-college-career candidate, Robert Woods comp.
- [[Dylan Laube]] (Angelo) — best receiving back in the class, poor man's [[James Cook]].
- [[Blake Watson]] (Angelo) — best pure pass-catcher of the RB tier behind Laube/Will Shipley.
- [[Luke McCaffrey]] (Angelo) — technically sound, efficient, no elite trait but a long-career floor.
- [[Dillon Johnson]] (Waldman) — top-5 RB for Waldman regardless of Mike Leach toughness rumor; Roschon Johnson comp.
- [[Ben Sinnott]] (Angelo, Waldman agrees) — Kansas State walk-on TE, T.J. Hockenson workout comp.

## Plant Your Flag
- [[Javon Baker]] (Angelo) — his 4th-highest-graded receiver of the cycle; three-level X skill set.
- [[Ricky Pearsall]] (both hosts, Angelo's "door two" pick) — safe, top-20-overall-caliber prospect; Chris Olave/Chad Johnson comp.
- [[Keon Coleman]] (Waldman) — problem-solving receiver; ideal Bengals fit if Tee Higgins leaves; LeGarrette Blount run-after-catch comp.

## Problem Solvers
- [[Blake Corum]] (Angelo) — dictates rather than reacts; safest RB in the class.
- [[Jonathan Brooks]] (Waldman's RB1) — best pass protector in the class, dictates the chain of movements.
- [[Caleb Williams]] (Angelo) — plays "on script even off script"; occasional over-reliance on "Superman moments."

## Also discussed (brief, not page-headline-changing)
- [[Trey Benson]] — contrast case to Corum: hard-running, not a "problem solver."
- [[Rome Odunze]] / [[Malik Nabers]] — Chargers fit discussion; Angelo has Nabers as his top overall grade but Odunze as the better [[Justin Herbert]] scheme fit.
- [[Marshawn Lloyd]] — more dynamic than [[Kimani Vidal]] today, but likely to be drafted much higher on hype vs. tape.
- [[Isaac Guerendo]] — an unproven flyer Waldman would rather pass on for a proven back like Dillon Johnson.
- [[DeAndre Swift]] / [[Kimani Vidal]] — Swift again used as the cautionary "production vs. complete player" comp for Vidal.
- [[Michael Penix Jr.]] — passing reaffirmation that Angelo remains "a big Penix fan."
