## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] with co-host Bob Harris (untracked; NOT [[Chris Harris]]) run the 'Feel It or F-It' format over week one of 2025 NFL free agency, largely reacting to a winners-and-losers article by Sigmund Bloom of Football Guys (untracked). Topics: the [[Ja'Marr Chase]] and [[Tee Higgins]] extensions in Cincinnati; [[Justin Fields]] to the Jets reuniting with [[Garrett Wilson]]; the Colts adding [[Daniel Jones]] to 'compete' with [[Anthony Richardson]]; Pittsburgh's backfield after [[Najee Harris]] left for the Chargers; Dallas signing [[Javonte Williams]] and [[Miles Sanders]]; Chicago's line rebuild and [[Caleb Williams]] / [[DeAndre Swift]]; Seattle's [[Sam Darnold]] and [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]]; [[Jordan Mason]] traded to Minnesota behind [[Aaron Jones]]; [[C.J. Stroud]] losing his line; [[Chris Godwin]] returning to Tampa and what it costs [[Jalen McMillan]]. Waldman then takes rookie questions ahead of the April 1 Rookie Scouting Portfolio release: [[Ashton Jeanty]] to the Raiders at 6, [[Luther Burden III]], [[Daniel Jackson]], [[Tai Felton]], [[Matthew Golden]] and [[Isaiah Bond]].
