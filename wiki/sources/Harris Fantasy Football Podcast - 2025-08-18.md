## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with news reaction, then runs a countdown of five 2025 busts each with guest Patrick Doherty of Rotoworld (NOT a tracked expert; also referenced as 'Roto Pat').

News block: Harris walks back his own flag-player call on [[Joe Mixon]] after a Friday Aaron Wilson (Houston Chronicle/TV) report that Mixon is 'not considered a lock' for the opener, dropping him from RB16 in the Almanac to RB25 standard / RB27 PPR. He flags [[Terry McLaurin]] (activated from PUP, still holding out) as needing a rank cut this week, is skeptical the reported shopping of [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] leads anywhere, and treats the [[De'Von Achane]] soft-tissue injury as a red flag against the 'draft the young back' argument. He also reads the Almanac profile of [[Elijah Mitchell]].

Bust countdowns — Doherty: 5. [[James Cook]], 4. [[Tyreek Hill]], 3. [[Baker Mayfield]], 2. [[Garrett Wilson]], 1. [[Kaleb Johnson]]. Harris: 5. [[Bo Nix]], 4. [[Mike Evans]], 3. [[RJ Harvey]], 2. [[Brock Bowers]], 1. [[Xavier Worthy]]. Harris nearly picked [[Christian McCaffrey]] but judged the injury case too obvious to be interesting.
