## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] hosts a Week 5 start/sit episode built around three blocks. First, a news-reaction monologue: [[Trey Benson]] landed on IR needing a meniscus scope (four to six weeks), blowing up the waiver advice Harris gave on Tuesday and leaving the Arizona backfield to [[Michael Carter]] and [[Emari Demercado]]; [[Bucky Irving]] was in a walking boot Wednesday seeking second opinions on a foot injury, so Harris removed him from his running back ranks and raised [[Rachaad White]] to RB22; and the 49ers ruled out [[Brock Purdy]], [[George Kittle]], [[Ricky Pearsall]] and [[Jauan Jennings]] for the Thursday night game against the Rams, leaving [[Christian McCaffrey]] and [[Kendrick Bourne]] as the only meaningful pieces.

Second, a guest segment with Jeff Bell of Football Guys (NOT a tracked expert), who brought five players he found hardest to rank: [[Justin Fields]], [[Chase Brown]], [[Woody Marks]], [[Jameson Williams]] and [[Wan'Dale Robinson]]. Harris and Bell compared exact weekly ranks on each and previewed the 49ers-Rams game ([[Matthew Stafford]], [[Kyren Williams]], [[Blake Corum]]).

Third, a DraftKings DFS segment with Jake Trowbridge (NOT tracked), whose deliberately chalk-heavy lineup prompted takes on [[Ashton Jeanty]], [[Javonte Williams]], [[Quentin Johnston]] and others.
