## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) continue their division-by-division preview with the NFC South receiver rooms. New Orleans gets the longest segment: whether [[Chris Olave]] makes a year-three jump toward a Stefon Diggs tier or settles as a Terry McLaurin / Tyler Lockett type, [[Rashid Shaheed]] as a more complete route runner than his 'Zoolander' route chart suggests, and [[A.T. Perry]] as a possible X-receiver answer, with [[Bub Means]] as a depth athlete. Tampa Bay: [[Mike Evans]] as still the best receiver in the division and a plea not to let Liam Cohen alter the Dave Canales route-tree design built for [[Baker Mayfield]]; [[Chris Godwin]] moving back to the slot; [[Jalen McMillan]] as a man-beating slot whose data hints at flanker work. Carolina: [[Diontae Johnson]] as one of the best separators in RP history finally getting non-X deployment under Canales, [[Adam Thielen]] demoted to a comfortable No. 2, and [[Xavier Legette]] as a raw, contested-catch athlete. Atlanta is dismissed as a bottom-10 room outside [[Drake London]]. Harmon ranks the rooms Bucs > Saints > Panthers > Falcons; Koh ranks Saints > Bucs > Panthers > Falcons.
