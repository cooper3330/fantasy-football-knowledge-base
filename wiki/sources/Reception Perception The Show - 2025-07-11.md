## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) devote a full episode to three Reception Perception charting profiles: [[Jameson Williams]], [[Drake London]] and [[Dontayvion Wicks]].

On Williams, Harmon walks through the year-over-year charting jump (57.5% vs man in 2023 to 65.7% in 2024, 79.5% vs zone, 61.9% vs press) and argues he is a clear, very good number two rather than a number one — comparing his tier to [[Jordan Addison]] and setting both below [[Tee Higgins]]-, [[DeVonta Smith]]- and [[Chris Godwin]]-class twos. Koh calls Williams one of the biggest regression candidates in the league with Ben Johnson gone; Harmon backs it with the 2016-17 Falcons play-caller-change comp and John Morton's 2017 Jets ranking dead last in success rate.

On London, both hosts call him a top-10 receiver at the position off a genuine third-year leap: 74.1% vs man, 78.8% vs press (87th percentile), 89.5% on out routes, and a record 27% dig-route participation at an 83.1% success rate. The Zac Robinson scheme moved him off the line (52.7% of snaps off the LOS, 38.9% slot), and Koh notes Next Gen Stats had London in the slot more often than [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]].

On Wicks, Harmon publicly reverses a prior bullish call: every charting split fell in year two, contested catch rate collapsed from 71.4% to 25%, and his 15.4% RP drop rate is the second highest in RP history behind [[Romeo Doubs]]' rookie year. He now profiles Wicks as an ideal WR3/WR4 rather than a breakout.
