## Summary
James Koh is out this week, so [[Matt Harmon]] hosts solo with guest Jeff Bell, FantasyPros' top-accuracy WR ranker for four straight years and a rookie-guide/rankings contributor at Football Guys (not a tracked expert; attributed by name throughout). Bell explains his projection-first, target-share ('pie') methodology, then he and Harmon run through Bell's five biggest disagreements with current Yahoo redraft ADP (Jameson Williams, Wan'Dale Robinson, Jayden Higgins, the Panthers duo of Jalen Coker/Tetairoa McMillan, and Jakobi Meyers) and then Bell's five biggest disagreements with Harmon's own rankings (Rome Odunze vs. Colson Loveland/Luther Burden, Tee Higgins, Courtland Sutton vs. Jalen Waddle/Pat Bryant, and the Packers trio of Christian Watson/Jayden Reed/Matthew Golden vs. Tucker Kraft). Closes with both naming Ryan Flournoy as an underrated Cowboys WR3.
