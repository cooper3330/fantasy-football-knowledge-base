## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) open a division-by-division offseason series with the NFC East, previewing every wide receiver room ahead of 2025 free agency. Harmon notes he has begun charting draft prospects and has two games of [[Tetairoa McMillan]] done ahead of his first combine trip; Koh calls McMillan the best receiver in the class while Harmon withholds a firm take.

Giants: [[Malik Nabers]] is the centerpiece, with Harmon calling him a potential top-10 NFL receiver and the best after-catch prospect he has charted since 2021; the room behind him ([[Wan'Dale Robinson]], [[Jalen Hyatt]], pending free agent [[Darius Slayton]]) is thin, and both hosts want a lid lifter added. Cowboys: negative cap space, fourth-most cap spent at WR because of [[CeeDee Lamb]], with [[Jalen Tolbert]], [[Jonathan Mingo]] and departing [[Brandin Cooks]] behind him; Harmon floats a [[Stefon Diggs]] Dallas reunion with brother Trevon. Commanders: the [[Terry McLaurin]] extension market, skepticism of the [[Luke McCaffrey]] draft pick, [[Dyami Brown]]'s late-season run, and fits for [[Chris Godwin]] and [[Christian Kirk]]. Eagles: room is set with [[A.J. Brown]] and [[DeVonta Smith]], with upside interest in [[Jahan Dotson]], [[Johnny Wilson]] and [[Ainias Smith]], plus a long detour on Eagles cap management and the lesson of paying [[Jalen Hurts]] and still winning a title.
