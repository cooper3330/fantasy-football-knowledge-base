## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a recap of Thursday night's Bears-Patriots game (Caleb Williams, Luther Burden III, Rome Odunze, DeAndre Swift, Colston Loveland, Cole Kmet, Drake May, A.J. Brown, DeMario Douglas, Rhamondre Stevenson, TreVeyon Henderson), then runs a Friday injury canvas covering most of the league (Lamar Jackson, Baker Mayfield, Saquon Barkley, Breece Hall, Braylon Allen, Quinshon Judkins, Zach Charbonnet, Cam Skattebo, Jordan Mason, Puka Nacua, Davante Adams, Mike Evans, Christian Kirk, Rashee Rice, Tyler Warren, Travis Kelce, Emeka Egbuka and others). Guest Cousin Josh (DetectiveFish on Instagram, NOT a tracked expert) joins for a six-weeks-in exercise sorting every NFL offense into five trust tiers: true wagons, partly-trustable wagons, good surprises, surprisingly bad (not disasters), and 'wheels fell off.' Along the way they debate George Pickens (sell vs. hold) and CJ Stroud (Josh reverses his long-standing skepticism).
