## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) run through a wave of WR contract/roster news before Harmon delivers a full Reception Perception breakdown of 2024 draft prospect Xavier Worthy. News covered: Brandon Aiyuk trade rumors and Harmon's dynasty ranking of him; Justin Jefferson's stalled extension; CeeDee Lamb waiting on Jefferson's number; Rashee Rice's legal situation and its effect on Kansas City's draft plans; DeVonta Smith's new extension and Harmon's case that his rookie tape already proved him a true WR1; Tee Higgins playing on the franchise tag. The hosts also fantasy-book a hypothetical Bills trade-up scenario for Rome Odunze. The back half is a deep-dive on Xavier Worthy's RP profile (success rates vs. man/zone/press, contested catches, drops) and where he projects best.
