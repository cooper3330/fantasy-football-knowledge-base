## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a Saints update -- Alvin Kamara re-signed at a discount rather than leaving, which changes his outlook on the Etienne/Kamara backfield split and prompted a rank drop for Travis Etienne Jr. He then reads an excerpt of his Almanac's Drake Maye profile, framing Maye's 2026 outlook through the lens of last year's one-year-wonder anxiety about Jayden Daniels. The main segment is an AFC East training-camp preview with guest Patrick Doherty (Rotoworld, not a tracked expert), going division by division: New England (TreVeyon Henderson vs Rhamondre Stevenson, AJ Brown, Hunter Henry, Romeo Doubs), Buffalo (DJ Moore, James Cook, Ray Davis), Miami (De'Von Achane, Malik Washington, and the crowded rookie/veteran WR room), and the Jets (Garrett Wilson, Geno Smith, Adonai Mitchell, Breece Hall, Braelon Allen). Doherty and Harris repeatedly debate which camp reports are signal versus noise.
