## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) recap NFL Week 7. Brown]], [[DeVonta Smith]] and [[Jalen Hurts]]; [[Travis Hunter]]'s breakout usage in Jacksonville's London loss to the Rams; [[Rashee Rice]]'s explosive return for Kansas City; the Raiders' and Dolphins' apparent season give-ups (Geno Smith, [[Tua Tagovailoa]] benched for Quinn Ewers, [[De'Von Achane]] as a checkdown machine); the winless Jets, [[Justin Fields]] benched, [[Tyrod Taylor]] likely starting, and [[Xavier Legette]]'s big game off a Jets defensive trend of surrendering plays to the number-two receiver.
