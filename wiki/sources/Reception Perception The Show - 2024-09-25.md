## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) run through Week 3 of the 2024 season. Segments: Jacksonville's broken offense (isolation route concepts, [[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[Trevor Lawrence]], coaching accountability for Doug Pederson and Press Taylor); Washington's breakout with [[Jayden Daniels]] and the [[Terry McLaurin]] alignment story, including McLaurin asking Cliff Kingsbury on the sideline to line up on the right side and Harmon and Koh partially walking back their Kingsbury skepticism; Chicago's [[Caleb Williams]] progression plus charting praise for [[Rome Odunze]] and the first-read data on [[DJ Moore]]; Indianapolis usage after [[Josh Downs]] returned, with [[Adonai Mitchell]] nearly benched behind [[Alec Pierce]] and [[Anthony Richardson]] struggling; a long charting segment on [[Malik Nabers]] versus Cleveland with a [[Garrett Wilson]] rookie-year comp; Carolina, where [[Andy Dalton]] revived [[Diontae Johnson]] and [[Xavier Legette]] gets a runway with Adam Thielen on IR; and a closing meta discussion on two-high safety shells, veteran quarterbacks and cyclical coverage trends.
