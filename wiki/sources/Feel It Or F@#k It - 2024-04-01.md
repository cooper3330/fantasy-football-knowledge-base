# Feel It Or F@#k It: 4.1.24

RSP release-day episode of *Feel It or F**k It*, [[Matt Waldman]]'s rapid-fire
hot-take format with co-host **Bob Harris** (not a tracked expert — distinct
from tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Opens with the annual promo for the
Rookie Scouting Portfolio's 19th edition (1,153 pages, $21.95 via
mattwaldman.com), a nod to a rocky PayPal-related customer email, and thanks
to Waldman's editing team and longtime supporter Mike McGregor. Closes with
the show's recurring darkness2light.org charity plug (up to $5,000 of RSP
proceeds donated; Waldman has personally given ~$65,000 since 2011).

Content is a quick run through 2024 draft prospects ahead of the RSP's
official release, each framed as "feel it" (buy the take) or "f**k it"
(fade it):

- [[Marshawn Lloyd]] — boom-bust RB, fumbling the deciding flaw
- [[Blake Corum]] — safest RB in the class, versatile scheme fit
- [[J.J. McCarthy]] — hardest player to evaluate, red-zone/backed-up decision-making concerns
- [[Bo Nix]] — mentioned only in passing (rising public buzz), no new evaluative content this episode
- [[Brock Bowers]] — chance to break Mike Ditka's 1961 rookie TE receiving record; 1.5-PPR top-3 pick
- [[Ricky Pearsall]] vs. [[Ladd McConkey]] — close, Pearsall graded slightly ahead on proof of concept
- [[Ray Davis]] — undersized power runner, pass pro the limiter
- [[Bucky Irving]] — situational player, some overrated relative to hype
- [[Blake Watson]] — underrated Memphis RB, best pass-catcher in the class
- [[Michael Penix Jr.]] — top-tier talent if given a redshirt runway; injury history the risk
- [[Tip Reiman]] — starter ceiling, route running the swing skill
- [[Cade Stover]] — could start now in a move-TE role, elite hands
- [[Caleb Williams]] — Bears' offseason moves (Keenan Allen, Gerald Everett, Shane Waldron) read as learning from Justin Fields mishandling
