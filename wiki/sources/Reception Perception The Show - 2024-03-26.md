# Breaking Down the Top 3 Wide Receiver Prospects

[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host **James Koh** (not a tracked expert) open with
free-agency catch-up — the Jets signing [[Mike Williams]] to pair with
[[Garrett Wilson]], and speculation about where [[Odell Beckham Jr.]] lands
(Miami had offered him a contract as of recording) — before spending the
bulk of the show on Harmon's just-published, full 8-game Reception
Perception charting profiles for the top three 2024 receiver prospects:
[[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Rome Odunze]] and [[Malik Nabers]].

Harmon's headline point: all three grade as tier-one, top-10-worthy
prospects, and he explicitly declines to fight over 1-2-3 ordering among
them for viewers not making actual draft picks. That said, he reveals his
own thinking has shifted toward Odunze as possibly his WR1 of the class
over Harrison — a real break from the public consensus that has Harrison
and Nabers in a clear top tier with Odunze a tier below at three. Nabers
remains third of the trio for Harmon, mainly on a flagged zone-coverage
weakness.
