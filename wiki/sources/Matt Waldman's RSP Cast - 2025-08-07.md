## Summary
A *Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] (both tracked) built around three segments. First, the 'age of the weapon back' — Angelo's thesis that backs built like [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] and [[De'Von Achane]] now take real slot and out-wide snaps, and that their fantasy value may not rise with that usage because much of the job is creating spacing and neutering blitz packages rather than absorbing targets. The hosts then ran a rapid up/neutral/down exercise across most starting backfields. Second, rookie risers and fallers in camp — [[Tory Horton]], [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]], [[Isaac TeSlaa]], [[Kyle Monangai]] and [[Tahj Brooks]] up; [[Tyler Shough]] down. Third, year-two and year-three players falling into bigger roles: [[Adonai Mitchell]], [[Roschon Johnson]], [[Xavier Worthy]] and [[Jayden Reed]]. Long tangents on [[Rashee Rice]]'s LCL-plus-hamstring recovery and what contact tolerance reveals that camp video cannot, and a critical detour on Cleveland's roster construction.
