## Summary
Week 9 news reaction show with [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert), plus guest Patrick Peterson, the retired All-Pro cornerback (not a tracked expert).

Three news segments and one interview:

1. **Diontae Johnson traded from Carolina to Baltimore.** Harmon framed the league-wide problem as a 'dearth of X receiver play' — when a player can win at X it is transformative for a franchise. He liked the move for Baltimore (the Ravens face Cover 1 at the eighth-highest rate in the NFL, so more man-beaters is logical) but was skeptical of the fit, since [[Diontae Johnson]], [[Zay Flowers]] and [[Mark Andrews]] all want the 10-15 yard range. Both hosts blasted Carolina's return (sixth-round pick upgraded to a fifth) and wondered why contenders like Dallas did not bid. Downstream: [[Rashad Bateman]], [[Jalen Coker]], [[Xavier Legette]], [[Adam Thielen]].
2. **Anthony Richardson benched for [[Joe Flacco]] in Indianapolis.** Both hosts called it defensible; Harmon said he changed his mind after hearing Dominique Foxworth on Mina Kimes's show and concluded the helmet tap-out was 'a symptom, not a disease.' Knock-on takes on [[Josh Downs]], [[Michael Pittman Jr.]], [[Adonai Mitchell]], [[Alec Pierce]].
3. **Stefon Diggs out for the season (knee).** Houston pass-catcher fallout: [[Xavier Hutchinson]], [[Tank Dell]], [[Nico Collins]], [[Cade Stover]].
4. **Patrick Peterson interview** on why shutdown corners no longer travel, and why the slot is uniquely hard to cover — the basis of the new [[Shutdown Corner Travel and Slot Coverage Difficulty]] page. He named [[Ladd McConkey]] as the most underrated receiver in the game right now.
