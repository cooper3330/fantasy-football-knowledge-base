## Summary
Rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' format with untracked co-host Bob Harris as training camps open across the league. Waldman gives quick-hit but detailed verdicts on a large slate of players: Kyle Pitts' role in Atlanta's new offense, the Bijan Robinson/Breece Hall/Christian McCaffrey RB1 pecking order, Travis Kelce's aging curve, Rachaad White's scheme-driven efficiency, Tyrone Tracy Jr. vs the Jahmyr Gibbs comp, the Patriots' unsettled WR room (Ja'Lynn Polk, K.J. Osborne, Javon Baker), Russell Wilson's Pittsburgh outlook (with Cecil Lammey's counter-take), the Michael Mayer/Brock Bowers Raiders TE split, Ladd McConkey's fit with Justin Herbert under Greg Roman, Khalil Herbert as a waiver stash, the Austin Ekeler/Brian Robinson Jr. Washington debate, Steelers depth pieces (Calvin Austin III, Van Jefferson, Cordarrelle Patterson), Leonard Fournette, the Saints' Spencer Rattler/Jake Haener backup battle, Zamir White, Tank Bigsby, and closes with Waldman's college scouting notes on Ashton Jeanty, Trevor Etienne and Ollie Gordon II.
