## Summary
Week 10 reaction episode of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Rapid-fire format where Bob poses a proposition and Matt says whether he 'feels it' or not.

Ground covered: the Lions' Sunday night win over Houston and why Waldman thinks resilience and the Dan Campbell / Brad Holmes coach-GM pairing is a real, underrated input; how sustained pressure degrades even good quarterbacks ([[C.J. Stroud]], [[Caleb Williams]], the David Carr comparison); the Steelers' receiver room after [[Mike Williams]]' touchdown and [[Calvin Austin III]]'s injury, plus a Steelers Super Bowl dark-horse case built on [[Russell Wilson]]; [[Joe Burrow]] as the best quarterback in the NFL right now; [[Ja'Marr Chase]] vs [[Justin Jefferson]] for WR1; the [[Mark Andrews]] drop-or-hold debate revisited; [[Jayden Daniels]]' three-week dip; [[Bo Nix]] as possibly the most productive rookie QB; [[Trey Lance]]'s runway in Dallas; [[Ricky Pearsall]] as a matchup play; the [[Christian McCaffrey]] / [[Jordan Mason]] backfield; [[Marquez Valdes-Scantling]]'s spike week; [[Dalton Kincaid]]'s fit with [[Josh Allen]]; [[Joe Flacco]] over [[Anthony Richardson]]; the [[Chuba Hubbard]] extension and what it means for [[Jonathan Brooks]]; [[Tyrone Tracy Jr.]]'s hands; and a Bob Harris rant on [[Daniel Jones]].
