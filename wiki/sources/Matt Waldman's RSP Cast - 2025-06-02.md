## Summary
Weekly 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]), recorded at the close of the voluntary OTA period. Bob Harris opens with a caution that OTA attendance is voluntary and that the mandatory minicamps the following week are the real signal, since skipping those costs a player roughly 100k dollars. Waldman notes the RSP dynasty rankings and projections are due no later than June 10.

The bulk of the show is a Houston Texans deep dive — [[C.J. Stroud]]'s rebound case after a 52-sack season, [[Nico Collins]] as a legitimate WR1, [[Christian Kirk]] as a double-digit-round value, [[Jayden Higgins]] over [[Jaylin Noel]], [[Joe Mixon]] at his RB16 price, and rookie [[Woody Marks]]. Then a run of Waldman's favorite players and news reactions: [[Kyler Murray]], [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Jayden Daniels]], [[Kyren Williams]], a dynasty sell call on [[Treylon Burks]], trade rumors on [[Jonnu Smith]] and [[Kyle Pitts]], the [[Stefon Diggs]] off-field noise, and [[Aaron Rodgers]] versus [[Kirk Cousins]] in Pittsburgh.
