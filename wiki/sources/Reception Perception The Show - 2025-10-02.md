## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on a Week 5 check-in episode with two long segments plus a FanDuel picks segment.

Segment one: [[Xavier Worthy]]'s return in Week 4 against Baltimore, which Harmon charted. Harmon's read is that Kansas City has settled on Worthy as a vertical and quick-out player rather than a middle-of-the-field target earner, and that his presence alone is opening space for [[JuJu Smith-Schuster]], [[Marquise Brown]] and [[Tyquan Thornton]]. Both hosts flagged ramp-up risk for [[Rashee Rice]] after more than a year away, using the [[Tank Dell]] 2024 comp, and Harmon noted the Chiefs' historically glowing camp reports (the [[Skyy Moore]] year-two-leap hype) as a reason for skepticism.

Segment two: Ryan Heath's (Fantasy Points, not tracked) finding that receptions by top-30 fantasy wide receivers through four weeks have fallen to 635 in 2025 from 651 in 2024 and 700+ in 2021-2023, despite a higher league-wide pass rate. Harmon's theories: unprecedented positional depth and rotation-heavy receiver rooms, the rise of 12 personnel (22.4% the last two years vs 18.9% in 2016-2023 and 13.8% in 2011-2015), the new kickoff rules shortening drives, and a rushed-to-the-field quarterback crop. He extends the [[Elijah Moore Rule]] to [[Josh Downs]] in Indianapolis, and floats an untested theory that big-name receivers are now absorbing running-back-like injury risk.

Picks segment: [[Davante Adams]] props vs San Francisco, plus Denver/Philadelphia, Detroit/Cincinnati and Kansas City/Jacksonville spreads, where the hosts disagreed on all three.
