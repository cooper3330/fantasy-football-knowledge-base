## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] (live from Super Bowl LIX radio row in New Orleans) and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) react to the news that the Rams told [[Cooper Kupp]] they will seek a trade immediately, then work through every plausible landing spot for him, before previewing Chiefs-Eagles from a matchup and prop-betting angle.

Kupp segment: Harmon's Reception Perception history on Kupp (91.4% success rate vs zone in 2021, the second-best mark he has charted; 87.3% in 2022; 80%+ vs zone every year 2017-2022; man-coverage score collapsing to 65.2% in 2023 with slot/off-line snaps spiking to a career-high 81.9%), his conclusion that Kupp must now be a 70-80% interior player, and a tour of Minnesota, Houston, Denver, Tampa Bay, Jacksonville, Cincinnati, New England and Washington as fits. Harmon notes the Rams eat $22.2M in dead cap and expects a release rather than a trade.

Super Bowl segment: Eagles as the more talented 53 with Mahomes and Andy Reid as the counterweight; Chiefs coverage tendencies (8th-highest man rate, but 66.7% two-high in the postseason per Fantasy Points); Trent McDuffie travelling with No. 1 receivers, opening a big [[DeVonta Smith]] game; Chiefs run-defense splits (40.4% success rate allowed on zone-concept runs, third worst; 3.79 YPC to RBs, second best); the Chiefs' four-back committee; and postseason route participation for the Chiefs pass catchers. Both picked Kansas City -1.5.
