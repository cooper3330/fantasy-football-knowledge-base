## Summary
A Week 2 reaction edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Diehard / Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman notes the RSP is entering its 20th year.

Rapid-fire verdicts on Week 2 fallout: the Kansas City backfield after [[Isiah Pacheco]]'s fractured fibula, [[Will Levis]]'s ball security, a defense of [[Anthony Richardson]] after a 9.9-point disaster, [[Breece Hall]] as the Jets' best weapon, [[Dalton Kincaid]]'s 7.3 PPG versus [[Brock Bowers]]'s TE3 start, [[Gardner Minshew]]'s processing, [[Justin Fields]]'s hold on the Pittsburgh job, [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]] finally getting fed versus [[Malik Nabers]] as the class's best receiver, [[Caleb Williams]] behind a bad line, [[Eric All]] as a closing dynasty buy window, [[Treylon Burks]] as an indictment of big-and-fast scouting, the Miami offense under Skylar Thompson, [[Sam Darnold]]'s hot start, the Klint Kubiak Saints offense, and [[Bryce Young]].
