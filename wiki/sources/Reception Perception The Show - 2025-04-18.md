## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) open with a full Reception Perception charting profile of Miami slot receiver [[Xavier Restrepo]], centered on his 4.80 pro-day forty, his split route chart, and Harmon's 'pop gun slot receiver' archetype. Guest Cory Kinnan (Reception Perception QB charter, not a tracked expert) then walks through three-game charting samples on three quarterback prospects — [[Tyler Shough]], [[Dillon Gabriel]] and [[Riley Leonard]] — plus a detour on whether quarterback height still matters, and closes on the Browns' near-certain selection of [[Travis Hunter]] and how he would fit alongside [[Jerry Jeudy]], [[Cedric Tillman]] and [[David Njoku]].
