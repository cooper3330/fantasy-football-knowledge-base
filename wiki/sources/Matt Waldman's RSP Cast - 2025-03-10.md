## Summary
A 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-reaction episode recorded on the morning the 2025 legal tampering period opened, with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Guys / Sirius; NOT a tracked expert, and not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). Bob Harris frames each move as a yes/no question and Waldman answers.

Free agency and trade reactions: [[DK Metcalf]] to Pittsburgh for the 52nd pick plus a $132M deal, [[George Pickens]] trade odds, [[Najee Harris]] hitting free agency, [[Geno Smith]] traded to Las Vegas and its effect on [[Brock Bowers]] and [[Jakobi Meyers]], [[Sam Darnold]] as Seattle's target under Klint Kubiak, [[Kenneth Walker III]] vs [[Zach Charbonnet]], [[Davante Adams]] to the Rams and the knock-on for [[Puka Nacua]], [[Matthew Stafford]] and [[Kyren Williams]], [[Aaron Jones]] back to Minnesota, [[Marquise Brown]] back to Kansas City, [[Zach Ertz]] re-signing in Washington over [[Ben Sinnott]], [[Christian Kirk]] to Houston with [[Tank Dell]] rehabbing, [[Parker Washington]] and [[Brenton Strange]] in Jacksonville, [[Mike Gesicki]] re-signing in Cincinnati, [[Chris Godwin]] back to Tampa with [[Bucky Irving]] and [[Sean Tucker]], and [[J.K. Dobbins]].

RSP/rookie segment: [[Savion Williams]] as a possible running back convert, [[Andrew Armstrong]] as an underrated receiver, [[Thomas Fidone]] as the most interesting tight end evaluation, and [[Harold Fannin Jr.]] rehabilitated post-Combine on agility rather than 40 time. Waldman also previewed RSP publication timing (April 1, post-draft edition a week after the NFL draft) and ran a listener contest.

Concepts touched: [[NFL Combine and Pro Day Skepticism]], [[Preseason ADP vs In-Season Production]], [[Offensive Line Investment and Skill Player Value]]. Format: [[Best Ball]].
