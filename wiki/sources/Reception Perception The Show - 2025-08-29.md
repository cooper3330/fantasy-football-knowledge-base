## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) react to a wave of late-August roster moves and what they mean for Reception Perception favorites and non-first-round rookie playing time. Segments: Carolina's receiver room after the Adam Thielen trade to Minnesota ([[Jalen Coker]], [[Xavier Legette]], [[Tetairoa McMillan]]); Detroit trading Tim Patrick to Jacksonville and elevating [[Isaac TeSlaa]], with Koh pushing back that Detroit's top three are all zone beaters; Miami's third receiver job going to [[Malik Washington]] and what that means for [[Jaylen Waddle]]'s alignment; Houston's veteran-first rotation with [[Xavier Hutchinson]] and [[Christian Kirk]] ahead of rookies [[Jayden Higgins]] and [[Jaylin Noel]]; Denver trading [[Devaughn Vele]] to New Orleans, opening power-slot work for [[Pat Bryant]], plus Harmon's insistence that a move tight end like [[Evan Engram]] is not a slot receiver; a Bill Barnwell three-way trade proposal involving [[Quentin Johnston]] and its knock-on for [[Tre Harris]] and [[KeAndre Lambert-Smith]]; Charles Davis's Chris Godwin comp for [[Elic Ayomanor]] in Tennessee; [[Jaylin Lane]] in Washington; and a FanDuel pick segment on [[Matthew Golden]] over 700.5 receiving yards after news of [[Jayden Reed]]'s Jones fracture.
