## Summary
Chris Harris reviews Week 12 game film across many games: Cowboys-Eagles (Saquon Barkley's diminished inside-zone role, George Pickens' turnaround, CeeDee Lamb's meltdown), Giants-Lions OT (Jameis Winston, Wan'Dale Robinson, Jahmyr Gibbs, Jared Goff), Patriots-Bengals (Hunter Henry, Austin Hooper, Rhamondre Stevenson/TreVeyon Henderson committee, Chase Brown, Joe Flacco, Tahj Brooks, Mike Gesicki), Falcons-Saints (Kirk Cousins, Kyle Pitts, Bijan Robinson, Darnell Mooney, Alvin Kamara injury, Devin Neal, Taysom Hill, Tyler Shough), and previews Panthers-49ers Monday Night (Christian McCaffrey, Tetairoa McMillan, Ricky Pearsall, Rico Dowdle). Harris also flags Vikings-Packers (Justin Jefferson, J.J. McCarthy, Green Bay backfield). Guest Sam Gold (Sam's Film Room, not a tracked expert) joins to break down Seahawks-Titans (Rashid Shaheed, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Kenneth Walker III, Zach Charbonnet), Buccaneers-Rams (Baker Mayfield injury, Teddy Bridgewater, Emeka Egbuka, Sean Tucker, Rashad White, Bucky Irving, Kyren Williams, Puka Nacua), and Colts-Chiefs (Daniel Jones, Michael Pittman Jr., Alec Pierce, Patrick Mahomes, Xavier Worthy, Rashee Rice).
