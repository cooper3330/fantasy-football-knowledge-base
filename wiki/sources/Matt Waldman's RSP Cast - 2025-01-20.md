## Summary
A post-divisional-round 'Feel It or F--It' rapid-fire episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]). Waldman opens with an RSP production note — roughly 120 players watched, wide receiver film nearly finished, 20th edition targeted for an April 1 release.

The segment format is Bob Harris posing a proposition and Waldman answering 'feel it' or otherwise. Topics: which 2024 rookie quarterback you would start a franchise with (Waldman takes [[Caleb Williams]] over [[Jayden Daniels]]); [[Bo Nix]] and [[C.J. Stroud]] as lessons in year-one context; the 2024 rookie receiver class ([[Malik Nabers]], [[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[Ladd McConkey]]); [[Brock Bowers]] vs [[Trey McBride]]; coaching-carousel situations in New England ([[Drake Maye]]), Chicago, Dallas ([[Dak Prescott]]), Jacksonville ([[Trevor Lawrence]]), Las Vegas, New Orleans ([[Derek Carr]], [[Chris Olave]]), the Jets ([[Aaron Rodgers]]), Detroit and Pittsburgh ([[Justin Fields]], [[Russell Wilson]], [[George Pickens]], [[Roman Wilson]]); and 2025 draft-value questions on [[Joe Mixon]], [[Kyren Williams]], [[Blake Corum]], [[Cooper Kupp]] and [[Travis Kelce]]. Waldman also relays [[Matt Harmon]]'s [[Sacrificial X Receiver]] framing of [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]].

A running theme: offensive line play, not scheme or coordinator hires, is the dominant variable for nearly every quarterback discussed.
