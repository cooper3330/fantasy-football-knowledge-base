## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] run a six-topic preseason Going Deep episode: buzzy players they are NOT sold on, buzzy players they ARE sold on, deep-cut players to monitor from afar, favorite college/devy watches of the summer, which rookie makes the first big splash, and the first sleeper to hit.

Angelo is out on [[Jameson Williams]] as a startable asset (target competition from [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]], [[Sam LaPorta]] and a slot-shifted [[Jahmyr Gibbs]]), and Waldman is out on [[Jayden Daniels]], comping him to Marcus Mariota on processing speed and durability and preferring [[Bo Nix]] on games played. Angelo is sold on [[Ray Davis]]; Waldman on [[Michael Penix Jr.]], reading Atlanta's decision to rest him in the preseason as a hedge against [[Kirk Cousins]]' Achilles recovery. Deep monitors: [[Jalen McMillan]] (Angelo), [[Dylan Johnson]] and [[Christopher Brooks]] (Waldman), plus a streaming case for [[Cordarrelle Patterson]]. Devy segment covers [[Cam Ward]], [[Jordan Lyle]], [[Kyle Monangai]] and [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]]. First-splash rookies: [[Malik Nabers]] (Angelo), [[Brock Bowers]] (both), [[Xavier Worthy]] (Waldman). Sleepers: [[Khalil Shakir]] and [[Dylan Laube]] (Angelo), [[Taysom Hill]], [[J.K. Dobbins]] and [[Kyle Pitts]] (Waldman), with Waldman laying out a condensed-formation framework for finding safe tight ends.
