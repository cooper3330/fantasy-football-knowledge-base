## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) preview the AFC South wide receiver rooms ahead of 2025 free agency. Koh opens with a note that the [[Christian Kirk]] trade happened immediately after recording. Jacksonville: [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] is a possible NFL target leader in a Liam Coen offense, with [[Gabe Davis]] a failed 'sacrificial X' signing and [[Parker Washington]] the likely slot. Houston: [[Tank Dell]] is out for 2025 after a multi-surgery knee injury (ACL/MCL/LCL/meniscus), [[Stefon Diggs]] is a 31-year-old free agent coming off an ACL tear, and Harmon wants a dirty-work addition like [[Emeka Egbuka]] or a cheap [[Elijah Moore]] rather than a top-of-market swing. Tennessee: possibly the worst room in the NFL behind a 31-year-old [[Calvin Ridley]] and [[Treylon Burks]]; Harmon would bring back [[Nick Westbrook-Ikhine]] and thinks [[Travis Hunter]] ends up playing receiver there. Indianapolis: a glut — [[Josh Downs]] called the best receiver on the roster, [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] an ideal number two, [[Alec Pierce]] a straight-line X whose presence stunts [[Adonai Mitchell]]. Also draft-class talk on [[Matthew Golden]] and [[Tetairoa McMillan]].
