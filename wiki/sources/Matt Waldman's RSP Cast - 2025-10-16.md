## Summary
Going Deep segment with tracked co-host [[Brandon Angelo]] and [[Matt Waldman]]. Topics: whether [[Luther Burden III]]'s stock is rising in Chicago with [[DJ Moore]] hospitalized (groin); which receiver Buffalo should trade for ([[Rashid Shaheed]] favored over [[Chris Olave]], Brandon Cooks, a DJ Moore reunion, Jakobi Meyers or Treylon Burks), with [[Keon Coleman]] and Buffalo's pass-catching corps (Dalton Kincaid, Khalil Shakir, James Cook, Josh Palmer) discussed as context; whether [[Jalen Coker]] elevates Carolina's passing game alongside [[Jimmy Horn Jr.]], with [[Tetairoa McMillan]] and [[Xavier Legette]] also assessed; whether [[Rico Dowdle]] is 'here to stay' over [[Chuba Hubbard]]; a Greg Olson-inspired theory on running back workload and in-game adaptation (Ashton Jeanty cited) applied to Detroit's [[Jahmyr Gibbs]]/[[David Montgomery]] split, with Jameson Williams' down year and OL injuries (Ragnow, Decker) as context; a deep dive on whether the Colts stay hot -- [[Daniel Jones]] and [[Tyler Warren]] -- with [[Michael Pittman Jr.]], Alec Pierce and Adonai Mitchell as supporting context; Cleveland's [[Quinshon Judkins]] and [[Dillon Gabriel]] struggling behind a bad scheme, with Harold Fannin Jr. praised and Shedeur Sanders' benching explained politically; a detailed [[Rachaad White]] evaluation versus [[Bucky Irving]]; and a closing wishlist of trade-deadline running back targets for the Bears, topped by [[Sean Tucker]] and including [[Tank Bigsby]], Tyler Allgeier, TyJae Spears, Tahj Brooks, Jacory Croskey-Merritt, Jaylen Wright, Keaton Mitchell, Tyler Badie, Rasheed Ali, Blake Watson, Blake Corum, and Jaleel McLaughlin.
