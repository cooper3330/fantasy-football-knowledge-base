## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) release Reception Perception charts for three 2025 draft prospects roughly a week before the NFL draft: [[Kyle Williams]] (Washington State), [[Isaiah Bond]] (Texas) and [[Jalen Royals]] (Utah State). Harmon notes he will have more profiles on the site than any prior year — 23 players in 2024 across full profiles and the rookie roundup, 24-25 expected for 2025 — despite the class being widely seen as weak.

Kyle Williams gets the most enthusiastic charting review: freakish after-catch ability, natural separation, real release packages, with deep-route stacking and catching low balls as the flagged weaknesses. Isaiah Bond is framed as a speed-based, incomplete route runner with serious character and legal concerns. Jalen Royals is the coldest take — Harmon has him 13th of 14 charted receivers, worried about one-side-of-the-field deployment and a stripped-down Utah State route tree, with a [[Rashee Rice]] athletic comp raised by JJ Zachariason (not tracked) as the counterweight.
