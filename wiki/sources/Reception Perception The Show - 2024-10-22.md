## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) break down Week 7 of the 2024 season. They open on the volatility of the receiver position — seven notable WRs ([[Deebo Samuel]], [[DeVonta Smith]], [[DeAndre Hopkins]], [[Jameson Williams]], [[Josh Downs]], [[Tank Dell]], [[Courtland Sutton]]) combined for negative five receiving yards — as an argument against reading week-to-week production as signal.

Segments: (1) Jets vs Steelers on Sunday Night Football — what [[Davante Adams]] actually changed for [[Garrett Wilson]], [[Allen Lazard]] and [[Breece Hall]], and Harmon's structural critique of [[Aaron Rodgers]] and the Jets' refusal to attack the intermediate middle of the field; (2) [[Russell Wilson]] vs [[Justin Fields]] in Pittsburgh, and why [[George Pickens]] fits Wilson's play-action shot game; (3) San Francisco after the [[Brandon Aiyuk]] knee injury — Harmon's charting of [[Ricky Pearsall]]'s first real game, plus [[Jauan Jennings]], [[Deebo Samuel]], [[George Kittle]] and [[Brock Purdy]] against man coverage; (4) [[Xavier Worthy]]'s Reception Perception numbers from the Chiefs-49ers game; (5) a buy-or-sell round on [[Keon Coleman]], [[Cedric Tillman]] vs [[Jerry Jeudy]], and [[Romeo Doubs]] as Green Bay's best receiver.
