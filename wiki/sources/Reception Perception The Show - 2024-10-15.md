## Summary
Week 6 reaction show with [[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert). Three segments: (1) veteran receivers outproducing the young breakout candidates — [[Chris Godwin]], [[Stefon Diggs]] and [[Terry McLaurin]] all top-12 fantasy WRs, each winning in a different ecosystem; (2) the Baltimore passing game under Todd Monken, with offensive line stabilization, [[Zay Flowers]] finally being used on real intermediate and outbreaking routes, [[Rashad Bateman]] efficiency and whether this is the best passing offense of the [[Lamar Jackson]] era; (3) [[Drake Maye]]'s first start and a New England receiver room Harmon calls a room of WR3s — [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] drops, [[Demario Douglas]] as the layup beneficiary, [[Kayshon Boutte]]'s surprise involvement. Closing segments on a 'constipated' Philadelphia offense ([[DeVonta Smith]] deployment) and the [[Calvin Ridley]] / [[Will Levis]] disaster in Tennessee.
