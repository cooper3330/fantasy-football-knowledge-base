## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert, returning from vacation) continue their divisional receiver-room previews with the AFC North. Opening chatter covers padless-camp hype for [[Xavier Worthy]] and [[Tank Dell]], with Harmon invoking the late Chris Wesseling's line that OTAs and minicamps are when small speedy receivers light it up (the 'Parris Campbell honorary award'), while noting Dell is at least moving at full speed after his injury and offseason shooting.

Cleveland gets the longest segment: [[Amari Cooper]] as a genuine No. 1 X receiver playing the best ball of his career, with a contract holdout and age-30 season looming; [[Elijah Moore]] as a misused vertical/mid-range player capped as a No. 2; [[Jerry Jeudy]] as an inconsistent No. 3 with an 8th-percentile zone success rate; and [[Cedric Tillman]] as a ball-winning depth X. Harmon and Koh discuss the [[Deshaun Watson]] / Ken Dorsey / Kevin Stefanski scheme tension.

Pittsburgh: [[George Pickens]] as the lone target hog in a thin room, [[Roman Wilson]] as a middle-of-field utility rookie fit for Arthur Smith. Baltimore: a strong [[Rashad Bateman]] bounce-back case, [[Zay Flowers]] as a high-end No. 2 with press and catch-point questions, [[Devontez Walker]] as a project athlete. Cincinnati: [[Ja'Marr Chase]] and his expanding slot usage, [[Tee Higgins]] and Harmon's below-consensus WR13-among-under-25s ranking, and [[Jermaine Burton]] as first-round talent with character questions.

They close by ranking the four rooms: Harmon has Cincinnati 1, Cleveland 2, Baltimore 3, Pittsburgh 4; Koh flips Baltimore ahead of Cleveland.
