## Summary
Chris Harris opens with meetup recap, book promo (Pound Foolish) and sponsor reads, then runs through Monday's news: Ashton Jeanty's ankle injury drops him out of round one on Harris's board; Kayshawn Boutte is traded from New England to Houston, which Harris reads as reinforcing a deep-round bet on Romeo Doubs; the Cardinals cut Trey Benson, leaving a muddled backfield between James Conner and Jeremiah Love (who is separately dealing with a high ankle sprain); and Deshaun Watson is named the Browns' Week 1 starter, which Harris calls indefensible but says doesn't move his ranks on Quinshon Judkins, Casey Concepcion or Denzel Boston. The main segment is a guest interview with untracked co-host Jeff Erickson (RotoWire), where each host counts down five 'safe' players for 2026 roughly round-by-round: Harris goes Derrick Henry, Saquon Barkley, DeVonta Smith, AJ Brown, DeAndre Swift, Ladd McConkey, Dak Prescott and Jalen Warren; Erickson goes Travis Etienne Jr., Harold Fannin Jr. and others, with both comparing Fannin against the Sam LaPorta / Tyler Warren / Colston Loveland tight end tier. They close discussing DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. as players they're avoiding due to Aaron Rodgers's checkdown tendencies.
