## Summary
Week 4 reaction edition of the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Diehard / SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). Rapid-fire verdicts on Week 4 breakouts, injuries and role changes: [[Ashton Jeanty]]'s 21-138-1 rushing plus two receiving scores against Chicago, [[Quinshon Judkins]]' workload, [[Jackson Dart]]'s debut, the Cleveland receiver picture, Seattle's passing game under Klint Kubiak, the tight end landscape ([[Kyle Pitts]], [[Brock Bowers]], [[Elijah Arroyo]], [[Oronde Gadsden II]]), the Chargers receiver hierarchy panic around [[Ladd McConkey]], Arizona's backfield and [[Michael Wilson]], Washington replacement receivers, [[Omarion Hampton]] (Waldman revisits and partially softens his pre-season stance), [[Brian Thomas Jr.]]'s wrist, a [[Bijan Robinson]] vs [[Christian McCaffrey]] redraft/dynasty split, and the Jacksonville backfield. Closes with the Robert Saleh / Liam Coen sign-stealing spat, which both hosts dismiss as league-wide normal practice.
