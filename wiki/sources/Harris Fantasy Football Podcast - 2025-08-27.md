## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens the day after NFL cutdown day with roster bookkeeping — PUP and IR designations, surprise cuts, and the depth-chart consequences — then reads the updated [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] profile from his 2025 Almanac (an update shipping Friday with Dynasty and Superflex ranks). The bulk of the episode is his annual NFL handicapping segment with guest Chris Raybon of the Action Network (NOT a tracked expert), covering team win totals, playoff and conference futures, statistical league-leader bets, and six over/under player props. Harris closes with the in-recording news that [[Adam Thielen]] was traded from Carolina back to Minnesota, and works through the ranks fallout for [[Jordan Addison]], [[Jalen Coker]] and [[Jalen Nailor]].
