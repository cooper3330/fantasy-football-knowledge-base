## Summary
Bob Harris is out this week; Matt Waldman runs the 'Feel It or F**k It' segment with guest Jagger May (Football Guys video editor/Fantasy Blue Chip, untracked co-host). They rapid-fire dynasty/redraft takes on the Michael Mayer/Brock Bowers TE glut in Las Vegas, Trey Benson vs. retiring David Johnson, Deuce Vaughn's stuck-behind-Zeke outlook in Dallas, Matthew Stafford's extension and durability, Malik Willis's roster-bubble status in Tennessee, Antonio Gibson and Tyrone Tracy Jr. as gap-scheme lottery tickets, Quentin Johnston's Chargers outlook (Waldman sold his own shares), Brendan Rice as a cheap Johnston-adjacent add, Bo Nix/Sean Payton skepticism in Denver, undrafted Colts RB Justin Strong vs. Tyler Goodson, the Jahmyr Gibbs/David Montgomery timeshare, an extended Jared Goff/Sean McVay career-arc retrospective, and the Bengals tight end competition (Eric All vs. Tanner McLaughlin vs. Mike Gesicki).
