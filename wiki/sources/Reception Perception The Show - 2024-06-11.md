## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh break down three new Reception Perception profiles. On [[Ja'Marr Chase]], Harmon details his year-over-year man/press/zone success rate consistency, his likely expanded slot role in 2024 with Tyler Boyd gone and Jermaine Burton drafted, and a debate over whether Chase or [[Garrett Wilson]] is the more 'skilled' vs 'talented' receiver. On [[Christian Watson]], Harmon argues the touchdown production masks a below-30th-percentile route runner who needs 'role catering' rather than a full route tree, with a long breakdown of his dig/out/curl route splits and contested catch woes. The rookie roundup segment covers Jets third-rounder [[Malachi Corley]], his gadget-heavy Western Kentucky usage, the Deebo Samuel comp Harmon rejects, and Aaron Rodgers' public enthusiasm for him.
