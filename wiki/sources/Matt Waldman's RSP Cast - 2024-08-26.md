## Summary
A late-August 'Feel It or F**k It' rapid-reaction episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as [[Chris Harris]]). The organizing theme is preseason skepticism: Waldman repeatedly calls the exhibition slate 'a great time for false positives and false negatives' and warns that both practice-report panic and highlight-clip hype mislead drafters 11 days from kickoff.

Segments: the Pittsburgh QB decision ([[Russell Wilson]] over [[Justin Fields]]); [[Nick Chubb]]'s second complicated knee reconstruction; a defense of [[Anthony Richardson]] against overwrought joint-practice reporting; a long, contested exchange on [[Jayden Daniels]] where Waldman is the bear and Bob Harris argues the rushing floor; [[Bo Nix]] and [[Drake Maye]] as year-one starters; the Philadelphia pecking order ([[Jahan Dotson]] vs [[Dallas Goedert]]); Washington's WR3 competition ([[Olamide Zaccheaus]] vs [[Luke McCaffrey]]); holdout/hold-in risk for [[CeeDee Lamb]], [[Ja'Marr Chase]] and [[Brandon Aiyuk]] with [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] as the safe alternative; a 'quickies' round on [[Trey Lance]], [[Spencer Rattler]] and [[Deshaun Watson]]; Kansas City's backfield and [[JuJu Smith-Schuster]]; and a closing [[Breece Hall]] vs [[Bijan Robinson]] comparison decided largely on offensive line.
