## Summary
Matt Waldman and [[Brandon Angelo]] (Going Deep co-host, tracked) review preseason camp buzz and reframe fantasy expectations for a batch of rookies and role battles ahead of the 2026 season. They open comparing three rookie WRs across tiers of offensive infrastructure -- [[De'Zhaun Stribling]] (SF, tier-one system), [[Cyrus Allen]] (KC), and [[Caleb Douglas]] (MIA, worst infrastructure) -- for both redraft and dynasty. They spend significant time on [[Jordyn Tyson]]'s recurring hamstring/knee injury history and argue the Saints should shut him down for the year, using it to riff on 'the Great Divide' between college tape and NFL acclimation (with Ben Watson and Frank Gore anecdotes) and on the NFL's poor daily-load management. They cover [[Eric All]] as a cheap dynasty TE stash, a Washington backfield battle between [[Robert Henry]] and [[Kaytron Allen]], a Houston WR room reshuffled by injuries to Jayden Higgins and uncertainty around [[Tank Dell]] that benefits [[Jaylen Noel]] and especially [[Nico Collins]], a Detroit backfield note on [[David Montgomery]] and dark-horse [[Daniel Sopkiewicz]], a bearish/bullish split on [[Drake London]] under new Atlanta OC Kevin Stefanski (with [[Kyle Pitts]] and Bijan Robinson as the offense's other trusted weapons and Tua Tagovailoa flagged as a major risk), and closes on [[Omarion Hampton]] landing on Waldman's redraft Do-Not-Draft list over a scheme-fit concern with [[Keaton Mitchell]] and [[Kamani Vidal]] as complicating factors.
