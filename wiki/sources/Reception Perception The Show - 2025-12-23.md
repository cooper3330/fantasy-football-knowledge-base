## Summary
Matt Harmon and James Koh (co-host, not tracked) run a Week 16-into-Christmas veteran-vs-rookie check-in. Vets covered: DJ Moore's late-season eruption for Chicago despite being written off after Rome Odunze's injury, Courtland Sutton's quietly elite five-game stretch for Denver, Troy Franklin's role, George Pickens' X-receiver profile in Dallas, Bo Nix's criticized late-game 'hospital ball' to Pat Bryant (who suffered a concussion on the play), Stefon Diggs' huge game and part-time-but-elite-efficiency role in New England alongside Drake Maye's strong second year, and the state of Buffalo's receiver room. Rookies covered: Tetairoa McMillan's touchdown-leading season for Carolina, Isaac TeSlaa's snap-share spike and alignment shift for Detroit (with a Jameson Williams alignment tangent), Chimere Dike and Elic Ayomanor for Tennessee, Kyle Williams' breakout flashes for New England, and Trey Harris overtaking Keenan Allen's snaps for the Chargers. Also a brief note that DK Metcalf may be suspended after an incident with a fan. Show closes with a FanDuel Christmas prop segment (CeeDee Lamb, George Pickens, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Troy Franklin).
