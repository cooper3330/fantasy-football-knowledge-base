## Summary
Season-ending awards recap. Chris Harris hands out his own 'Huggy Awards' (fantasy MVP/LVP, good performer he still doesn't believe in, bad performer he still does believe in, worst/best call of 2025) solo with robot-bit banter, then untracked co-host/guest Cousin Josh (Detective Fish) joins for the 'Baggy Awards,' a parallel set of categories (MVP/LVP, believer/non-believer picks, worst/best call, irrational hatred, coach who needs to be cutting limes, player who'll be out of the league by midseason, plus non-fantasy bits). Both close with Super Bowl predictions before the show plays its 2025 season supercut.
