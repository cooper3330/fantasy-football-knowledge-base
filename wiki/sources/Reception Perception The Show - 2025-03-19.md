## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) spend nearly the whole episode on three players plus a Travis Hunter preamble. Segment one: the back-to-back Cincinnati extensions for [[Ja'Marr Chase]] (4 years, $161M, $40.25M AAV, highest-paid non-QB in NFL history) and [[Tee Higgins]] (4 years, $115M, ~$29M AAV, first two years reportedly guaranteed), with Harmon arguing Higgins left roughly $15M on the table given league-wide cap space and that a shared agent leveraged the two deals together. Segment two: a scathing charting review of [[Cooper Kupp]] after his three-year, $45M Seattle signing, plus what it implies for [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] and [[Marquez Valdes-Scantling]] in Klint Kubiak's offense. Harmon also previewed that he charted three [[Travis Hunter]] games as a receiver and is holding the profile back, and flagged upcoming prospect profiles on [[Emeka Egbuka]] and [[Tetairoa McMillan]].
