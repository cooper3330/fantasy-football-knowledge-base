## Summary
Chris Harris solo-reviews film from eight Sunday Week 11 games (Broncos-Chiefs, Bills-Buccaneers, Lions-Eagles, Commanders-Dolphins in Madrid, Bengals-Steelers) and previews Cowboys-Raiders, then brings on Chris Rabon (Action Network, untracked guest) to review three more games he watched together with Rabon (Texans-Titans, Chargers-Jaguars, Seahawks-Rams). Heavy focus on backfield committees (Denver, Tampa, Miami, Washington, Pittsburgh), receiver pecking orders (Denver, Philadelphia, Houston, Jacksonville, Seattle), and several in-game injuries (JK Dobbins out for the season, Calvin Ridley broken leg, Dalton Kincaid hamstring, Jalen Warren ankle, Aaron Rodgers wrist).
