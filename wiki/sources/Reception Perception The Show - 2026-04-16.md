## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh cover three new Reception Perception mid-round WR prospect profiles ahead of the 2026 NFL Draft: [[Bryce Lance]] (North Dakota State), [[Brennan Thompson]] (Mississippi State), and [[Ted Hurst]] (Georgia State). They open by discussing Harmon's new subscriber article on 'skewed alignment' receivers -- players who take over 70% of their charted snaps on one side of the field -- pulling from 535 historical RP player seasons to show how rare and historically unproductive that profile is, with Drake London, DJ Moore, Rashee Rice and JuJu Smith-Schuster cited as the only players to break the mold. For each prospect they walk through Reception Perception success-rate charting (by man/zone/press coverage and by route), combine/pro-day measurables, comps to past prospects, and a rough draft-round projection.
