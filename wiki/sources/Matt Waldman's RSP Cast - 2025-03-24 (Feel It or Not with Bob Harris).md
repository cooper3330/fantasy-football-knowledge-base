## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] (tracked) with co-host Bob Harris (NOT tracked; not the same person as [[Chris Harris]]) run the rapid-fire 'feel it or f**k it' format across the 2025 rookie classes and early Underdog best ball ADP. Waldman opens with the imminent April 1 Rookie Scouting Portfolio release (~162-165 scouted players, 900+ pages).

Main threads: he pushes back on the 2025 running back class being a 2017 repeat (2017 produced ~10-11 multi-year starters plus undrafted Austin Ekeler; he expects 4-6, maybe 7, and expects most backs drafted day three); he reframes the 2025 receiver class as short on true primary/X receivers but deep in future WR2-WR4 career types; and he argues the rookie tight end pool should be attacked around round 12, not round 9.

Player segments: [[Malik Nabers]] with [[Jameis Winston]], the [[Aaron Rodgers]] vs [[Russell Wilson]] Steelers question and what it means for [[DK Metcalf]], [[Mark Andrews]] at TE6, [[Brandin Cooks]] to New Orleans alongside [[Chris Olave]] and [[Rashid Shaheed]], [[Michael Pittman Jr.]] at WR53, [[Rome Odunze]], [[Courtland Sutton]], [[Chris Godwin]], and rookies [[Ashton Jeanty]], [[Omarion Hampton]], [[TreVeyon Henderson]], [[Marcus Yarns]], [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]], [[Kimani Vidal]], [[Dylan Laube]], [[Tetairoa McMillan]], [[Tyler Warren]] and [[Colston Loveland]].
