## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) react to the [[George Pickens]] trade from Pittsburgh to Dallas, then break down Day 3 rookie receivers and UDFAs from Harmon's Rookie Roundup charting (27 prospects charted this year).

Pickens segment: Harmon says he had been on record since before the combine that Pickens would be moved, frames the trade from the Steelers' side first (a season-long roller coaster: elite first four weeks, loafed routes from Week 5 on, drops and an OPI in the playoff loss), and notes Pittsburgh went outside the building to pay an older player with the same strengths and weaknesses in [[DK Metcalf]]. Both like the fit in Dallas — [[Dak Prescott]] as by far the best QB Pickens has played with, a pure boundary X freeing [[CeeDee Lamb]] to keep working from the slot — while flagging conduct and target-volume risk.

Day 3 / UDFA segment: [[Chimere Dike]] and [[Elic Ayomanor]] to Tennessee, [[Xavier Restrepo]] as a UDFA reuniting with [[Cam Ward]], [[Dont'e Thornton Jr.]] to Las Vegas, [[Jalen Royals]] to Kansas City, [[KeAndre Lambert-Smith]] to the Chargers, [[Tory Horton]] to Seattle, and UDFAs [[Elijah Badger]] (KC) and [[Andrew Armstrong]] (Miami).
