## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opened with a 'Film Futures' film study of [[Ashton Jeanty]], having watched coach's-tape end-zone view of carries from September plus the last three games (Chiefs, Jaguars, Broncos) and the Week 4 Bears game. Harris concluded Jeanty looks like a good player but not yet a first-round fantasy ADP player, offering Ray Rice and D'Angelo Williams as on-field comps and pushing back on the Mark Ingram comp.

[[Matt Waldman]] (footballguys.com, Matt Waldman's RSP) joined for five Week 11 flex-range names: [[Michael Wilson]], [[Woody Marks]], [[Jameson Williams]], [[Parker Washington]] and [[Luther Burden III]], plus a Thursday-night Patriots-Jets preview covering [[TreVeyon Henderson]], [[Breece Hall]], [[Stefon Diggs]] and [[Mason Taylor]], and two Pick 6 over/unders. Waldman relayed a [[Brandon Angelo]] idea from their 'Going Deep' show about [[Trevor Lawrence]] and [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] scheme fit.

Jake Trowbridge (NOT a tracked expert) closed the show with the weekly DraftKings DFS lineup contest.
