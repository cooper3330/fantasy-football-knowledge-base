## Summary
The 'Film and Theory' segment of the RSP Cast, hosted by [[Matt Waldman]] with untracked co-host Adam Harstad. Mostly theory/philosophy: Harstad uses Newtonian inertia as a metaphor for how player market values (ADP, KeepTradeCut, FantasyCalc) behave irrationally -- risers keep rising, fallers keep falling -- beyond what a rational market would predict, using [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]]'s ADP run-up as the example. Waldman then leads a discussion of Plato's theory of forms applied to dynasty 'value,' distinguishing an unreachable ideal Capital-V value from the practical, market-facing lowercase-v value that trade charts approximate, using [[Patrick Mahomes]] as the running example of a player whose lowercase-v value is essentially free to hold in dynasty. The two also debate league scoring/roster design (flex rules, vampire/guillotine leagues, interception and sack penalties, salary-cap leagues) as creativity constraints, and debate whether analysts should publish full ROI records, using injury analyst Dr. Jene Bramel and Waldman's own 20-years-ago-vs-now ROI as examples. Brief practical asides: [[Jackson Dart]]'s evaluation timeline and [[Malik Washington]]'s streaming value in return-yardage-heavy scoring.
