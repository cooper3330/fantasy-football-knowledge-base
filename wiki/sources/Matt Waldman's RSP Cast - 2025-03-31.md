## Summary
A solo cast by [[Matt Waldman]] previewing the 12 quarterbacks charted for the 2025 pre-draft Rookie Scouting Portfolio (plus an unofficial 13th, [[Hunter Dekkers]]), delivered alphabetically by first name with one or two notable observations per prospect. No co-host — Bob Harris was away, so 'Feel It or F**k It' did not air that week.

Waldman frames the class as deeper but weaker at the top than 2024's group ([[Caleb Williams]], [[Michael Penix Jr.]], [[Bo Nix]], [[Jayden Daniels]], [[Drake Maye]], [[J.J. McCarthy]]), with eight prospects carrying realistic paths to contributor status and roughly one to two expected to hit. Long methodology preamble on RSP quarterback charting, the limits of completion percentage, confidence as a trackable trait, and why prospect age is a sloppy evaluation variable.
