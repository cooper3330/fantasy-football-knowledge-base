## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) walk through two 2025 Reception Perception receiver profiles — Ole Miss X receiver [[Tre Harris]] and TCU's [[Jack Bech]] — then bring on guest Cory Kinnan of Daft on Draft (not a tracked expert) to discuss the quarterback charting profiles of [[Will Howard]] and [[Quinn Ewers]].

Harmon frames Tre Harris as the archetypal straight-line X, with a spectrum running from [[DK Metcalf]] at the top through [[Mike Williams]] and [[Alec Pierce]] down to Terrace Marshall — whose Reception Perception numbers Harmon calls 'frighteningly similar' to Harris's. Jack Bech is one of Harmon's favorite prospects in the class despite a single-side alignment red flag, with a nearly all-green route tree, elite contact balance and the best contested-catch rate in the class. Kinnan rejects the John Gruden [[Josh Allen]] comp on Will Howard in favor of a Mason Rudolph comp, and is deeply negative on Quinn Ewers, whose best case he grudgingly frames as Jimmy Garoppolo or a poor man's [[Derek Carr]].
