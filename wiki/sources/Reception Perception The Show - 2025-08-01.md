## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) preview the AFC South receiver rooms: Texans, Colts, Jaguars and Titans, using freshly published Reception Perception charting profiles.

Houston: Harmon declares [[Nico Collins]] has 'graduated' into tier one, a top-five NFL receiver behind/alongside Ja'Marr Chase, Justin Jefferson, CeeDee Lamb and A.J. Brown, with [[Puka Nacua]] sixth. He walks through Collins's 93rd-percentile man success rate (77.5%), 82.1% zone, 82.3% press (96th percentile), 66.7% on nines, ~86% on both digs and outs, and a contested catch rate that rose from 75% to 81.3% while contested target rate doubled to 22.2%. He flags Collins's extreme line-of-scrimmage usage (89.1% then 83.4%, never more than 17% slot) as an unusual role for a genuine number one, and expects [[Jayden Higgins]] taking X snaps to free Collins up the way Justin Jefferson was freed in 2024. Koh praises Nick Caserio for overloading the room with Higgins, [[Jaylin Noel]] and [[Christian Kirk]] after [[Tank Dell]] injuries.

Indianapolis: Koh calls it arguably a top-five pass-catching group; Harmon agrees on the personnel but says the quarterback situation ([[Anthony Richardson]] vs [[Daniel Jones]], possibly [[Riley Leonard]] in week 18) undercuts it. Takes on [[Michael Pittman Jr.]], [[Josh Downs]], [[Alec Pierce]] and [[Adonai Mitchell]].

Jacksonville: [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] and [[Travis Hunter]] as a screen-and-alignment nightmare under Liam Coben, with [[Dyami Brown]] as the sacrificial X and [[Parker Washington]] as the slot.

Tennessee: [[Calvin Ridley]] charted well again and is Harmon's favorite quarterback-receiver pairing story with rookie [[Cam Ward]]; the rest of the room ([[Tyler Lockett]], [[Van Jefferson]], [[Chimere Dike]], [[Elic Ayomanor]], [[Xavier Restrepo]]) is flawed.
