## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) on the two Week 16 quarterback benchings and their receiver fallout, then a run of high-profile WR/CB matchups.

First segment: Atlanta benching [[Kirk Cousins]] for [[Michael Penix Jr.]]. Koh's data had Cousins dead last in the NFL in EPA on intermediate throws (10-19 air yards) since Week 11 — a league-worst seven interceptions there, six of them to the intermediate middle, 40% completion rate (second worst), minus 20.6 total EPA, minus 18% completion rate over expected to the intermediate middle. Season line of 10 TD to 15 INT, sub-80 passer rating, 5.5 net yards per attempt, with eight of his touchdowns and one interception coming in two games against Tampa Bay (509 yards/4 TD in Week 5; 276/4 TD in the rematch). Harmon: the benching 'had to be done', and unlike Cleveland the Falcons are still alive in the NFC South. Cousins' intermediate collapse neutralizes the whole reason [[Drake London]] worked as a [[Puka Nacua]]-style middle-of-field usage player rather than the perimeter X he was under Arthur Smith. On Penix, Reception Perception's charting (Derrik Klassen's profile on receptionperception.com) had him below prospect average on dig, curl and slant, but 88% success on out routes and 63% on corners; 15% of his college attempts were nine routes and only 8.5% slants — Klassen called him a classic Bruce Arians quarterback. Harmon expects deeper out-breaking work for London and [[Darnell Mooney]]. Aside: [[Aaron Rodgers]] is second-worst in the same intermediate bucket at 29% completion and minus 6.5 EPA despite [[Garrett Wilson]] and [[Davante Adams]].

Second segment: Cleveland benching [[Jameis Winston]] for Dorian Thompson-Robinson, and what it does to [[Jerry Jeudy]].

Third segment: Week 16 matchups — [[Terry McLaurin]] vs Quinyon Mitchell/Darius Slay and the Eagles' coverage mix; [[Puka Nacua]] vs Sauce Gardner/DJ Reed; [[Nico Collins]] vs Trent McDuffie; [[Tyreek Hill]] and [[Jaylen Waddle]] vs San Francisco; [[Deebo Samuel]] and [[Jauan Jennings]] vs Jalen Ramsey; plus [[Isaac Guerendo]]'s hamstring.
