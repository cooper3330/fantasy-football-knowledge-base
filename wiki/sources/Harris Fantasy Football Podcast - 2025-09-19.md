## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a full film review of the Thursday night Dolphins-Bills game (Buffalo won by 10), arguing Miami's offense is no longer the 2023 juggernaut but is 'good enough to support fantasy weapons' — [[De'Von Achane]] and [[Tyreek Hill]] stay in all lineups, [[Jaylen Waddle]] is a flex consideration, [[Tua Tagovailoa]] is superflex-only. He flags [[Ollie Gordon II]] as the short-yardage/goal-line answer and writes off [[Jaylen Wright]]. On the Buffalo side he pushes back on Kirk Herbstreit's 'look at all these toys' line, comparing [[Josh Allen]] to prime Cam Newton and concluding Buffalo's skill group is 'kind of just a bunch of guys' — [[James Cook]] is a weekly starter, [[Dalton Kincaid]] a top-10 TE by attachment, and no Bills wideout is startable.

A midweek injury sweep follows, then the main segment: Harris and returning guest 'cousin Josh' (Josh Fisch, @DetectiveFisch on Instagram — NOT a tracked expert) each count down five things blowing their minds two-plus weeks into 2025. Josh: beat-writer misinformation, [[Daniel Jones]] and the Colts, the [[A.J. Brown]]/Eagles passing game, the Chiefs run game, [[Baker Mayfield]]. Harris: the [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] panic, [[Tetairoa McMillan]], the Jaguars run game ([[Travis Etienne Jr.]] over [[Bhayshul Tuten]]), the [[Caleb Williams]]/Ben Johnson Bears offense, and Daniel Jones at number one.
