## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recap the 2025 wild card round, deliberately focusing on the losing teams. They open with the Mike McCarthy/Dallas coaching-search news (Deion Sanders at plus-100, Jason Witten reportedly in the mix, Ben Johnson and Joe Brady as the names Harmon would actually want to interview), then work through four games: Chargers-Texans, Steelers-Ravens, Broncos-Bills, Packers-Eagles and Bucs-Commanders. Central theme Harmon states outright: the story of wild card weekend was the talent gap between the winners and losers, most visible at receiver and on the offensive interior. Big segments on [[Ladd McConkey]] carrying 81.4% of the Chargers' passing yards, [[Quentin Johnston]] as a volatile splash player rather than a functional one, [[George Pickens]] as a tradeable but unreliable lone playmaker, Green Bay's over-rotated receiver room, and Tampa Bay's game management under Todd Bowles.
