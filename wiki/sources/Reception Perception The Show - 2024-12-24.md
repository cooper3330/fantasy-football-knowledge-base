## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) recap Week 16 the day before Christmas. Main segments: (1) [[Tank Dell]]'s second straight major leg injury (dislocated kneecap, possible torn ACL and multi-ligament damage) and what it means for him, [[Nico Collins]] and the Houston offense; (2) why Houston's 2024 offense regressed after adding [[Stefon Diggs]] — Harmon's 'sometimes having too many good players can be a problem' argument and doubts about whether Bobby Slowik can evolve past distilled Shanahan concepts; (3) [[Diontae Johnson]]'s waiver situation, with Harmon arguing Houston is more desperate for him than Kansas City; (4) [[Michael Penix Jr.]]'s debut for Atlanta and knock-on effects for [[Drake London]], [[Darnell Mooney]] and [[Bijan Robinson]]; (5) why the [[Amari Cooper]] and [[DeAndre Hopkins]] midseason trades underwhelmed, plus [[Xavier Worthy]]'s manufactured-touch breakout and [[Marquise Brown]]'s return; (6) a 'football Santa Claus' Christmas-wish segment covering [[Brian Thomas Jr.]], [[D.J. Moore]], [[Josh Downs]] and the Jets/[[Garrett Wilson]] situation.
