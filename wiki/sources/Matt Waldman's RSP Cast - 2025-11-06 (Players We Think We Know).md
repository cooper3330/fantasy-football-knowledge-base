## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] (tracked) and Adam Harstad (Footballguys; co-host of *Film and Theory*, NOT a tracked expert) ran two segments. First, 'players we think we know' — settled evaluations whose dynasty value should stay flat while production accrues free, versus players the consensus has wrong. Harstad named [[Patrick Mahomes]], [[Dak Prescott]], [[Lamar Jackson]], [[Josh Allen]], [[Justin Jefferson]], [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[Bijan Robinson]], [[Jonathan Taylor]] and [[Brock Bowers]] as known quantities; Waldman added [[Ashton Jeanty]], [[Caleb Williams]], [[Rome Odunze]], [[Daniel Jones]], [[Bo Nix]], [[C.J. Stroud]], [[Woody Marks]], [[Javonte Williams]], [[Nico Collins]] and [[Michael Pittman Jr.]]. The hosts disagreed directly on [[Tyler Warren]] and [[Trey McBride]]. Waldman also presented a hand-built study of seven-year careers since the mid-2000s comparing running back and receiver draft round to sustained fantasy-starter production. Second segment: what each would do as an NFL owner — culture, explicit coach runways, scouting infrastructure, a private minor-league pipeline, and Waldman's plan to hand the franchise to the city after 15 years.
