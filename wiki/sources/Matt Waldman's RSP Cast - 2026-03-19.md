## Summary
Rapid-fire listener Q&A episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host [[Brandon Angelo]] (Going Deep) covering dozens of 2026 rookie prospects ahead of the NFL draft, dynasty rookie-draft strategy, and several veteran buy-low/role questions. Major threads: tiering the 2026 WR/RB/QB classes (Jeremiah Love as the lone true blue-chip prospect; Carnell Tate vs Makai Lemon at the top of the WR class; Fernando Mendoza as the safe QB1); deep dives on Jordan Tyson's injury profile, Chris Bell and Skyler Bell as boom/bust WR bets, Denzel Boston and Chris Brazzell comps; a veteran dynasty buy-low segment naming Jalen Coker, Kayshon Boutte, Malik Washington, and a possible AJ Brown-to-Patriots trade reshaping New England's receiver room (Boutte, Kyle Williams, Demario Douglas, Romeo Doubs, Drake Maye); Kenneth Gainwell over an injury-flagged Bucky Irving in Tampa; Jacksonville backfield touch-share questions (Bhayshul Tuten, LeQuint Allen, Chris Rodriguez Jr.); Cam Ward's year-two outlook and WR1 need; Gunnar Helm's vaulted TE grade; a Steelers Aaron Rodgers/Michael Pittman Jr./DK Metcalf role discussion; day-three depth receivers (Konata Mumpfield, Isaac TeSlaa, Jaylin Lane, Devaughn Vele); dynasty pick-trading strategy (2027 vs 2028 capital); NIL as a driver of players returning to school; and a detailed discussion of Relative Athletic Score (RAS) as a flawed evaluation tool, using Devin Singletary, Dalvin Cook, Emmett Johnson and Malachi Fields as case studies.
