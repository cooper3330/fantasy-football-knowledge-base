## Summary
Going Deep segment: [[Matt Waldman]] and [[Brandon Angelo]] run through their 2024 rookie dynasty drafts a few weeks post-draft. Topics: players they least want to draft but keep taking anyway (Bo Nix, Ben Sinnott), players they're most likely to reach for a round-plus early (Jermaine Burton, Javon Baker, Michael Penix Jr., Blake Corum, Jaylen Wright), rookies likely to sizzle in camp but fizzle as rookies (Adonai Mitchell, Jayden Daniels, Trey Benson, Ricky Pearsall), rookies likely to sizzle quietly in games despite a quiet camp (Luke McCaffrey, Ja'Lynn Polk, Ainias Smith, Brendan Rice, Cade Stover, Jared Wiley), players where landing spot makes them more willing to project a positive outcome than their pre-draft grade (Johnny Wilson, Trey Benson, Marshawn Lloyd, Drake Maye, Troy Franklin, Jordan Travis, Aidan O'Connell), and their picks for the biggest running back and receiver risers by next year (Jermaine Burton, Tyrone Tracy Jr., Marshawn Lloyd, Blake Watson).
