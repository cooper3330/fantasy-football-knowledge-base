## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) close their divisional preview series with the NFC West, recorded on a Monday afternoon amid live [[Brandon Aiyuk]] trade rumors. The bulk of the episode is Harmon's Reception Perception charting data: Aiyuk's 2023 profile as one of the nine season-long instances in RP history of clearing the 90th percentile versus man, zone and press; [[Deebo Samuel]] as a zone-beater who has never beaten man coverage; [[Puka Nacua]] as a very good but nowhere-elite tier-two receiver; [[Cooper Kupp]] showing man-coverage and in-breaking-route decline that Koh reads more harshly than Harmon; the Cardinals room ([[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Michael Wilson]], [[Greg Dortch]]) which both hosts think is underrated; and Seattle, where Harmon says [[DK Metcalf]] may have leveled off, [[Tyler Lockett]] has likely started his decline, and [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]] could lead the team in catches in year two. Rookie [[Jordan Whittington]] gets a camp-hype segment as a McVay-archetype slot receiver. The hosts close by ranking the four receiver rooms and disagreeing about where San Francisco lands without Aiyuk.
