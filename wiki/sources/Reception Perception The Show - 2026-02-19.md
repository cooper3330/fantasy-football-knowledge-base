## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh preview the NFC North team by team, going in reverse order of 2025 standings: Lions, Vikings, Packers, Bears. Lions: new OC Drew Petzing's fit with Jared Goff, Jameson Williams and Amon-Ra St. Brown, plus rookie Isaac TeSlaa's uncertain role. Vikings: Justin Jefferson's 'down' year explained by offensive context, Jordan Addison's tier, Tai Felton's quiet rookie year, the JJ McCarthy limitations report (Alec Lewis, The Athletic), and extended debate on trading for Mac Jones vs. signing a veteran like Geno Smith. Packers: Romeo Doubs's likely free-agent departure, Christian Watson's bounce-back, Jayden Reed's injury-marred year, and Matthew Golden's rocky rookie role. Bears: whether Chicago trades DJ Moore, Rome Odunze's sophomore dip, Luther Burden III's misleading rookie efficiency numbers, and Colston Loveland's emergence as the offense's most trusted target.
