# Mike Evans Stays in Tampa & Levels of Wide Receiver Need

Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) cover two topics.

**Part 1 — Mike Evans's new deal.** Tampa Bay re-signed Evans to a two-year,
$52M contract ($35M guaranteed) rather than let him test free agency. Both
hosts call it a win for both sides. Fresh full-season 2023 [[Reception
Perception Methodology]] charting shows one of his best years by success rate
vs. man/press/zone, credited to OC Dave Canales's route-tree redesign (more
slants/outs, fewer go routes) rather than a rebound from decline. See
[[Mike Evans]]. The signing's downstream effects are also logged on
[[Baker Mayfield]] (removes his incentive to test the market) and
[[Chris Godwin]] (Tampa's fallback WR1 had Evans left).

**Part 2 — Levels of wide receiver need.** Harmon sorts ~20 teams into three
buckets — **critical need** (a team fails at wide receiver without adding a
verified starter), **substantial need** (would benefit from a possible
starter, but isn't embarrassed without one), and **justifiable pursuit**
(a smart add, not a true hole). Critical need: Chiefs, Bills, Cardinals,
Bears, Titans, Giants, Patriots, Panthers. Substantial need: Ravens, Broncos,
Jaguars, Chargers (debated up to critical), Jets, Commanders, Colts (Koh
disagrees, thinks the Colts are fine). Justifiable pursuit: Bengals, Eagles,
Lions (Koh instead grades Detroit substantial), Browns, Texans, Rams (with
Miami and Minnesota also floated as similar-tier teams). Player-level takes
extracted from this breakdown are logged on the individual player pages:
[[Stefon Diggs]], [[Khalil Shakir]], [[Rashee Rice]], [[Malik Nabers]],
[[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Rome Odunze]], [[Marquise Brown]], [[D.J. Moore]],
[[Caleb Williams]], [[Quentin Johnston]], [[Keenan Allen]], [[Jameson
Williams]], [[Puka Nacua]], [[Cooper Kupp]], [[Michael Pittman Jr.]].
