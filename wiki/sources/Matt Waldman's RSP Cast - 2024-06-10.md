## Summary
Rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' format episode with Bob Harris (untracked co-host, not Chris Harris) running Matt Waldman through 2024 season-long projections and take/leave calls on a long list of players. Waldman gives specific touch/yardage/TD projections for most names discussed, drawing on his RSP projections and rankings product (out same week). Covers the CMC/Bijan/CeeDee Lamb top-of-draft tier, the Breece Hall vs. Saquon Barkley RB1-usurper conversation, Jahmyr Gibbs receiving-role skepticism, Josh Jacobs in Green Bay, DeAndre Swift's crowded Chicago backfield, the Malik Nabers vs. Marvin Harrison Jr. rookie-WR opportunity-cost debate (with a long soapbox riff on drafting receivers paired with shaky quarterbacks), Devin Singletary as a cheap Giants handcuff, a Giants tight end segment (Theo Johnson/Daniel Bellinger both faded), a long Steelers segment (Pat Freiermuth, Calvin Austin III, Denzel Mims, Van Jefferson, Roman Wilson, Deebo Samuel trade chatter, Najee Harris vs. Jaylen Warren), and closes on the Rams WR room (Puka Nacua, Cooper Kupp, DeMarcus Robinson, with a nod to A.J. Brown).
