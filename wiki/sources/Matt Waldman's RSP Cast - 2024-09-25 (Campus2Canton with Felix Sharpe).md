## Summary
A Campus2Canton crossover episode of the RSP Cast. [[Matt Waldman]] hosts Felix Sharpe (Campus2Canton / @SharpReview, a guest — NOT a tracked expert), who profiles two college risers: Michigan RB [[Kalel Mullings]] and NC State TE [[Justin Joly]]. The conversation covers Mullings overtaking [[Donovan Edwards]] as Michigan's interior runner, Joly as a flex/mismatch chess piece in Robert Anae's system, and then previews Georgia–Alabama devy names ([[Nate Frazier]], [[Carson Beck]], [[Jalen Milroe]], [[Ryan Williams]], Jam Miller, Justice Haynes, Germie Bernard). Waldman closes with NFL notes on [[Jordan Mason]], [[Jayden Daniels]], [[Bo Nix]] and [[Braelon Allen]] from tape he had just finished for the RSP and Football Guys.
