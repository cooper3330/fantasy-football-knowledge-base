## Summary
Chris Harris opens with injury news -- Jordan Tyson's hamstring (out ~2 months), the ripple effects on Chris Olave and Devaughn Vele, Kyle Monangai's hyperextended knee and the DeAndre Swift implications, Emeka Egbuka's lingering toe injury, Breece Hall's groin strain, and Keenan Allen signing with the Colts (with knock-on effects for Alec Pierce). The main segment is a countdown of 'ADP surprises' with guest Dave Kluge of Football Guys (NOT a tracked expert), each naming five ADP-market surprises and reacting to the other's: quarterback depth pushing elite QBs down draft boards; running backs going unusually early this year ('2006 again') versus Kluge's WR preference; Colston Loveland vs. Tyler Warren's ADP gap; Blake Corum being too cheap behind Kyron Williams; Kyron Williams being too cheap in general; Omarion Hampton being overpriced on thin tape; DJ Moore being badly underpriced as Josh Allen's new WR1; and a muddled Jacksonville WR picture (Parker Washington, Brian Thomas Jr., Jakobi Meyers, Brenton Strange, Travis Hunter) that Harris and Kluge think signals Trevor Lawrence is undervalued. Harris closes with his own #1 surprise: Matthew Stafford being drafted as a back-of-pack QB1 despite an MVP season. Non-fantasy content: meetup tour announcement, book (Pound Foolish) promo, sponsor reads (Wild Grain, Leesa, Fantasy Cares), listener giveaway winner.
