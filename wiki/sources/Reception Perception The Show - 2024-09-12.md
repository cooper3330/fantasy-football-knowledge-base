## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) review Week 1 charting data and preview Week 2. The bulk of the episode (roughly 30 minutes) is an autopsy of the Atlanta offense: [[Kirk Cousins]] taking 55% of snaps from pistol despite never exceeding 10 pistol snaps in a game in his career, zero play-action snaps, one under-center snap, 81% of runs from pistol and zero runs from shotgun, and 81-82% of runs going outside the tackles. Harmon separates [[Drake London]]'s deployment (which he liked — over 60% off the line, more slot and motion, 38% of routes post/dig/slant) from the surrounding quarterback play. They also cover [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]]'s one-catch debut and his curl-heavy route chart, [[Tyler Lockett]] as a declining but not washed player in a scaled-back Seattle role, the Carolina offense around [[Bryce Young]] and [[Diontae Johnson]], the Houston trio of [[Nico Collins]], [[Stefon Diggs]] and [[Tank Dell]] (the one room Harmon says looked exactly as he predicted), and a conservative Cincinnati passing game with [[Joe Burrow]] and [[Ja'Marr Chase]].
