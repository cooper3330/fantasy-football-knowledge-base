## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh run through Week 6 NFL news with a fantasy/real-football lens: Brian Callahan's firing in Tennessee and the state of Cam Ward's receiver corps; the Eagles' locker-room 'bad vibes' storyline; Puka Nacua's high-ankle injury and how the Rams offense adjusts (Davante Adams, Jordan Whittington); George Pickens's huge stretch filling in for CeeDee Lamb and his looming free agency; Brian Thomas Jr.'s continued struggles on in-breaking routes and the Jaguars' scheme workaround; Travis Hunter's route mix against Seattle; Jauan Jennings's injury-and-contract-driven sideline outburst in San Francisco; the Jets' offensive collapse in London with Justin Fields and Garrett Wilson; and Arizona's passing offense with Jacoby Brissette in relief of Kyler Murray, plus Marvin Harrison Jr.'s concussion and the debate over whether to move on from Murray.
