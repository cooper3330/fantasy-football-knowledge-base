## Summary
Matt Harmon is out this episode; co-host James Koh fills in with guest Alfredo Brown, who does the site's quarterback charting profiles, to talk through several 2026 quarterback situations using Reception Perception charting data. Topics: the Browns' Deshaun Watson vs. Shedeur Sanders competition and Cleveland's brutal early schedule; Tua Tagovailoa's rough start with his new team (Atlanta) and whether his short-game reliance is exposed without Miami's scheme; Cam Ward's ugly preseason attributed to a new coaching staff/playbook/receivers; Geno Smith's fit with the Jets and skepticism of OC Frank Reich; a deep dive on Kyler Murray's charting profile and his scheme mismatch with Kevin O'Connell's Vikings offense (with a J.J. McCarthy comparison); and a deep dive on Daniel Jones's charting profile with the Colts, including how much of his 2025 success depended on a soft early schedule, Shane Steichen's scheme, and a strong supporting cast (Jonathan Taylor, Michael Pittman Jr., Josh Downs, Alec Pierce, Tyler Warren), plus how that receiver room looks now that Pittman is out and Keenan Allen is in.
