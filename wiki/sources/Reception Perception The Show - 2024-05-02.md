## Summary
Matt Harmon and untracked co-host James Koh record in-person, working through Day 2 and Day 3 wide receiver picks from the 2024 NFL Draft and grading each landing spot against Harmon's Reception Perception charting data. Covered in depth: [[Keon Coleman]] to Buffalo, [[Ladd McConkey]] to the Chargers, [[Ja'Lynn Polk]] and [[Javon Baker]] to New England, [[Adonai Mitchell]] to Indianapolis, [[Jermaine Burton]] to Cincinnati, [[Troy Franklin]] to Denver, and [[Malik Washington]] to Miami. Harmon and Koh disagree on several picks (Coleman vs. McConkey/Mitchell for Buffalo, Polk's draft slot for New England, Burton's immediate role in Cincinnati) and Harmon repeatedly cites specific man/zone/press success-rate percentiles from his charting to support projected roles.
