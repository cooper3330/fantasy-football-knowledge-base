## Summary
Season-opening 'Almanac Day' show. Chris Harris opens with Almanac/Pound Foolish/FFT Open housekeeping, then confirms Ricky Pearsall needs knee surgery and is out for 2026, bumping Mike Evans, De'Zhaun Stribling and Deebo Samuel in San Francisco's receiving corps. The main segment is a training-camp capstone countdown with guest Dave Richard (CBS Fantasy, not a tracked expert), each host ranking their top five non-injury camp storylines that could move ranks: Houston's offensive line (Nico Collins, David Montgomery, Tank Dell, Jayden Higgins), Makai Lemon's Eagles role, TreVeyon Henderson vs Rhamondre Stevenson, Washington's backfield (Jacory Croskey-Merritt vs Rashad White), Jadarian Price vs Zach Charbonnet, the Raiders WR3 competition (Tre Tucker, Jalen Nailor, Jack Bech) behind Brock Bowers, the Vikings QB competition (Kyler Murray) and its effect on Justin Jefferson/Jordan Addison, the crowded Cardinals backfield around rookie Jeremiah Love, and the Jahmyr Gibbs/Bijan Robinson contract holdouts (with Terry McLaurin's 2025 holdout cited as precedent).
