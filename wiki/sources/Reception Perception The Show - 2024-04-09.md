# Reception Perception: The Show — 2024-04-09

[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host **James Koh** (not a tracked expert) discuss the
depth of the 2024 receiver class in general — many good WR2-caliber prospects,
few clear "number ones" outside the top three — before breaking down two
freshly finished full-sample Reception Perception profiles.

## Roman Wilson
Harmon is markedly below consensus. Below-20th-percentile success rates versus
both man and zone coverage, a one-note/linear route runner with little tempo
deception, and a strong dig-route number (93.3%) that doesn't carry to the
slant. Full take on [[Roman Wilson]].

## Ricky Pearsall
Harmon is markedly above consensus — one of his favorite non-top-3 prospects
to chart, with genuine alignment versatility, strong man-coverage separation,
excellent hands, and standout in-breaking route success. Full take on
[[Ricky Pearsall]].
