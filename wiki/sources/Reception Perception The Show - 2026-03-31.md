## Summary
Matt Harmon (with co-host James Koh, untracked) runs through three new Reception Perception prospect profiles released on the site: Indiana WRs Omar Cooper Jr. and Elijah Surratt, and Tennessee WR Chris Brazell. Two more profiles (Jeremy Bernard and Chris Bell of Louisville) are mentioned as posted but not discussed in this episode. Harmon leans heavily on RP charting numbers -- success rates vs. man/zone/press, route participation, after-catch data -- and repeatedly invokes his 'skewed alignment receiver' methodology (guys who take 70%+ of snaps on one side of the field as an outside receiver), citing a decade-long study (2015-2025, 35 charted prospects) where only four became high-impact starters (JuJu Smith-Schuster, DJ Moore, Drake London, Rasheed Rice) and many others (Javon Baker, Corey Coleman, Laquan Treadwell) busted. Alec Pierce is used repeatedly as the model for a developmental 'sacrificial X receiver' who can survive early NFL snaps on a limited route tree while growing into the position.
