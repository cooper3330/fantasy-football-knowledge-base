## Summary
Week 5 'Feel It or F--It' episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (Football Guys / SiriusXM; NOT a tracked expert, and NOT the same person as tracked expert [[Chris Harris]]). The pair open on the Ravens-Bengals overtime game, then run a rapid-fire flag-planting format across Baltimore's backfield committee, the Bengals passing game, Atlanta's Thursday-night win over Tampa Bay, the Rachaad White / Bucky Irving 'referendum', San Francisco after the Arizona loss, the Rams backfield, the Davante Adams trade rumor and its effect on Garrett Wilson, Romeo Doubs' benching, the Jets offense, Jacksonville's backfield, Indianapolis' receivers, Bo Nix's rookie development curve, and Brock Bowers.

Recurring themes: Waldman repeatedly rejects binary 'who is better' framings of backfield committees in favor of asking which play designs and blocking are working; and he publicly credits [[Brock Bowers]] with teaching him a lesson about letting a bad landing spot cloud a talent evaluation.
