# Matt Waldman's RSP Cast — 2023-12-18

**Raw transcript:** `raw/ingested/rsp-cast/2023-12-18-rsp-feel-it-or-fk-it-121823-an-rsp-cast-with-bob-harris-and-matt-waldman.md`

## Summary

*Feel It or F**k It* — the recurring rapid-fire format co-hosted by
[[Matt Waldman]] and **Bob Harris** (veteran fantasy analyst, SiriusXM /
Footballguys — *not* a tracked expert in this wiki, and **not** the same person
as [[Chris Harris]]; his views are attributed to him by name throughout). Bob
Harris drives the questions; Waldman gives the verdict and the film reasoning.

Week 15 reaction show, recorded during fantasy playoffs. The stated premise:
"feel it or f**k it" is an instant verdict on a player's **fantasy value**, not
on his ability, effort, or character. Most calls are one- to two-week
start/sit rulings on injury-created openings, but three threads run longer:

1. **Receiver play propping up quarterback play.** Bob Harris, relaying a point
   from Jeff Mans, argues route-running and catching have improved enough
   league-wide that journeyman quarterbacks stay fantasy-viable — and therefore
   that a bad quarterback situation shouldn't scare you off a receiver. Both
   hosts cite it as a mistake they made in 2023. See
   [[Weak Quarterback Play and Receiver Value]].
2. **Route running beats speed.** Waldman's framework for why
   [[Keenan Allen]] and [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] are top-5 receivers without
   top-end speed, and the man/zone split that separates them from someone like
   Deebo Samuel. See [[Zone vs Man Route Running]].
3. **The 2024 running back market.** Waldman takes a victory lap on
   [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] over [[Bijan Robinson]] in 2023 and then flips the trade —
   he plans to pound the table for Bijan in 2024 precisely because the market
   will now overpay for Gibbs. [[Christian McCaffrey]] is an unqualified
   RB1-overall for 2024 despite turning 28.

Also touched: whether Houston's offense is [[C.J. Stroud]] or the
Shanahan-tree system around him ([[Scheme vs Talent]]).
