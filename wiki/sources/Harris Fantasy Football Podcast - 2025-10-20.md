## Summary
Chris Harris runs his Monday game-film review, having watched all eight Week 7 games. Solo segment covers Eagles-Vikings (Hurts/Brown/Smith huge, Barkley scheme-capped, Bigsby now active as handcuff over A.J. Dillon), Giants-Broncos (Bo Nix conservative-but-productive vs Jackson Dart outplaying him despite the loss, Skattebo/Tracy split, Wan'Dale Robinson), Colts-Chargers blowout (Daniel Jones redemption, Jonathan Taylor, Tyler Warren, Herbert hurt by losing his 3rd-string LT, Gadsden breakout), Patriots-Titans (Drake May concussion scare, Stevenson, Henderson still light-usage, Pollard), and Jags-Rams London game (Kyren Williams hard luck, Davante Adams stat-padding TDs, Trevor Lawrence rough outing, Brian Thomas Jr. defended, Travis Hunter garbage-time). Also covers Monday-night previews: Buccaneers-Lions WR situation (Egbuka hamstring, Godwin out, Shepard as fallback) and Texans-Seahawks (Woody Marks/Chubb split, Kirk out, Higgins upside, Walker/Charbonnet in Seattle). Guest segment brings on Matt Harmon (Yahoo Fantasy, receptionperception.com) to break down three additional games he picked: Commanders-Cowboys (Jayden Daniels hamstring/Mariota in relief, CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens usage), Saints-Bears (Caleb Williams, Rome Odunze down week, Kyle Monangai hype-check, DeAndre Swift scheme fit, Spencer Rattler and Chris Olave, Tyler Shough benched), and Falcons-49ers (George Kittle no catches but still start him, McCaffrey running room, Michael Penix Jr. flashes and flaws, Drake London).
