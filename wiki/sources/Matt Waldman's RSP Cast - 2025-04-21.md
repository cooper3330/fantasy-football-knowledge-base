## Summary
Draft-week edition of 'Feel It or F**k It' with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host Bob Harris (NOT a tracked expert; not to be confused with [[Chris Harris]]), recorded the Monday before the 2025 NFL Draft. The format is Bob Harris posing a proposition and Waldman answering 'feel it' or 'f**k it', mostly on Day 3 and undrafted-range prospects Waldman thinks the draft media has skipped.

Running back segment: [[Phil Mafah]] as an overlooked between-the-tackles banger, [[LeQuint Allen]] as a high-floor/low-ceiling permanent backup, [[Montrell Johnson]] as an underrated post-draft waiver name, and [[Jacory Croskey-Merritt]] alongside an undrafted comp Waldman likes. Receivers: [[Jacolby George]], [[Xavier Guillory]], [[Jordan Moore]], [[Ja'Corey Brooks]], [[Chimere Dike]], [[Tez Johnson]] and a [[Xavier Restrepo]] vs [[Parker Washington]] head-to-head. Quarterback: [[Tyler Shough]]'s pocket play. Waldman also named [[Luther Burden III]] and [[Travis Hunter]] as the only two rookie receivers likely to clear 1,000 yards, said he expects to be higher on [[Cam Skattebo]] post-draft (possible top-five RB) but lower than the field on [[Terrance Ferguson]] and [[Jaylin Lane]], and argued for trading out of the 2025 rookie draft in [[Dynasty]] absent a top-five pick.

Concept content: existing depth-chart talent as the most overlooked input to a scouting grade (a change Waldman says he will build into next year's RSP grading key), and pre-draft misinformation season — 'everyone is lying until they start spending the draft capital'.
