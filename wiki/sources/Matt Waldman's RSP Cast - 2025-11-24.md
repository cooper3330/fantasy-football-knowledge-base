## Summary
Weekly 'Feel It or F**k It' segment hosted by Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris, running through week-12 NFL takes and college prospect evaluations. Topics: the Houston backfield (Woody Marks, Joe Mixon, Nick Chubb), Davis Mills's slow-start pattern, Nico Collins's WR-hierarchy placement, the Bills' broken passing offense around Josh Allen, extended prospect breakdowns of Ohio State WR Carnell Tate, Arizona State WR Jordyn Tyson and USC WR Makai Lemon, the Jets' John Metchie III/Adonai Mitchell WR rotation, Caleb Williams's accuracy perception vs reality, Kyle Monangai's role growth behind Caleb Williams and the Bears backfield, Colston Loveland as the top rookie TE with a Brock Bowers long-term comp, Jacoby Brissett's mentorship of Michael Wilson (implicitly indicting Kyler Murray), Shedeur Sanders's rookie evaluation versus Dylan Gabriel, Harold Fannin Jr.'s after-catch playmaking for Cleveland, and J.J. McCarthy's improved accuracy offset by red-zone hero-ball sacks.
