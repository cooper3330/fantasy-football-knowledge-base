## Summary
Chris Harris, joined by guest Dave Kluge of footballguys.com (untracked), previews AFC South training camps (Jaguars, Texans, Colts, Titans), framing the whole episode around what camp/beat noise is worth reacting to versus ignoring. Harris also reads a 2026 Almanac profile of Wan'Dale Robinson (including a quote from untracked Almanac contributor 'Cousin Josh') and updates on Almanac progress (pre-order at harrisfootball.com, delivery August 3). Jaguars: Bhayshul Tuten vs Chris Rodriguez Jr. backfield debate after Travis Etienne Jr.'s departure, plus how much Travis Hunter will play offense versus defense and its impact on Brian Thomas Jr., Jakobi Meyers and Parker Washington. Texans: Jayden Higgins outlook, Tank Dell's injury recovery, and David Montgomery vs Woody Marks. Colts: Daniel Jones's health as the swing factor for Jonathan Taylor, Alec Pierce, Josh Downs and Tyler Warren. Titans: Carnell Tate price discipline (with a Matt Waldman comp mentioned), a look back at Matthew Golden/Tetairoa McMillan camp hype as a cautionary tale, a brief note on Jordan Tyson, and the Tony Pollard/Tajay Spears/Nicholas Singleton backfield contingent on Singleton's foot recovery.
