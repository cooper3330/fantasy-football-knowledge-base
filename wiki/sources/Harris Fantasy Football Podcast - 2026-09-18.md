## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a recap of Buffalo's Thursday night blowout of Detroit: [[Josh Allen]] in MVP form, [[James Cook]] and [[Dalton Kincaid]] both huge, [[D.J. Moore]] hurt (sprained AC joint) opening a role for [[Keon Coleman]], and a detailed film breakdown of Coleman's tape. On the Lions side: [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] and [[Jahmyr Gibbs]] praised as environment-proof, but [[Jared Goff]] and [[Jameson Williams]] flagged as shakier given the offensive line and a target-tree squeeze versus [[Sam LaPorta]]. Harris runs his weekly Friday injury report covering many players across the league. The back half is a guest segment with Jeff Erickson (RotoWire, not a tracked expert) where Harris and Erickson each count down five players (plus honorable mentions) they've already changed their opinion on after Week 1: [[Bo Nix]], [[Jackson Dart]], [[Bucky Irving]], [[Chuba Hubbard]] (with [[Jonathan Brooks]] cratering), [[Quinshon Judkins]], [[Rashee Rice]], [[Devaughn Vele]], [[Parker Washington]], [[Isaiah Likely]] (with [[Brenton Strange]]), and [[Kenneth Walker III]] (with a [[Derrick Henry]] comparison), plus honorable mentions [[De'Von Achane]] and [[Justin Herbert]].
