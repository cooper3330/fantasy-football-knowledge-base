## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] guests on Matt Waldman's own show (a role reversal from their usual arrangement) to discuss his new football-noir novel 'Pound Foolish,' then the two run through 2026 season storylines. No clean speaker separation is possible in this transcript for most of the football segment -- both hosts trade off building on each other's points -- so player/team takes below are attributed jointly to [[Chris Harris]] and [[Matt Waldman]] except where one voice is clearly distinguishable (the DJ Moore scheme-fit take, and the Mike Evans/49ers injury litany, both read as a single continuous turn attributed to Harris).
