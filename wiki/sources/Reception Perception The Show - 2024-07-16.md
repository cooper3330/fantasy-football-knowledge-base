## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) work through all four NFC East wide receiver/pass-catcher rooms as part of their division-by-division series, then rank the units. Giants: deep dive on [[Malik Nabers]]'s RP charting and projected deployment, plus [[Darius Slayton]], [[Jalen Hyatt]] and [[Wan'Dale Robinson]] battling for snaps behind him. Eagles: brief hit on [[A.J. Brown]]'s ceiling under new OC Kellen Moore and rookie [[Ainias Smith]]'s slot role. Cowboys: extensive discussion of Dallas's lack of offseason WR investment, [[CeeDee Lamb]]'s target-share ceiling, [[Brandon Cooks]] and [[Jalen Tolbert]]'s RP chart. Washington: coaching change (Eric Bieniemy out, Kliff Kingsbury in), [[Terry McLaurin]] and [[Jahan Dotson]]'s per-route metrics, rookie [[Luke McCaffrey]], and speculation about heavier 12-personnel usage with [[Ben Sinnott]] and [[Zach Ertz]], plus a note on [[Austin Ekeler]]'s signing. They close by ranking the four rooms: Eagles 1, Dallas 2 (narrowly over Washington), Washington 3, Giants 4, with both hosts noting the Giants room could outperform its ranking if Nabers is as good as advertised.
