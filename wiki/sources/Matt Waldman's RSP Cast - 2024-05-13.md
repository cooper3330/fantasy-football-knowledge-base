## Summary
Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris run a rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' segment on 2024 rookie QBs and a wide slate of dynasty/best-ball/redraft players in mid-May, pre-training-camp. Rookie QB coverage: Caleb Williams (CHI starter, Peyton Manning comp), Jayden Daniels (WAS, expected Week 1 starter over Marcus Mariota), Drake Maye (NE, fading as Year 1 starter behind Jacoby Brissett), J.J. McCarthy (MIN, 60/40 to start over Sam Darnold), Bo Nix (DEN, floor-over-ceiling Sean Payton fit), Michael Penix Jr. (ATL, contingent on Kirk Cousins's Achilles). Also covered: Terry McLaurin and Jahan Dotson (WAS roles under Daniels), Ja'Lynn Polk and Javon Baker (NE rookie WRs), Jaheim Bell (NE fullback, no fantasy value), Xavier Legette (CAR route-running concerns), Zay Jones and Marvin Harrison Jr. (ARI), Aidan O'Connell vs. Gardner Minshew (LV camp battle), Will Levis (TEN), Treylon Burks (TEN, bust risk), George Pickens (PIT alpha-role odds), Xavier Worthy and Rashee Rice (KC best ball), Kyren Williams and Blake Corum (LAR), Rachaad White and Bucky Irving (TB), and Kimani Vidal (LAC).
