## Summary
Matt Waldman and untracked co-host Bob Harris run their 'Feel It or F**k It' format on pre-draft prospects ahead of the April 1 RSP release, previewing the guide's methodology (depth-of-talent score over raw rankings, draft-capital-agnostic pure talent grades, post-draft ADP-vs-rank 'X value'). They work through quarterbacks (Cole Payton, Baron Morton, Ty Simpson, Cade Klubnik), a run of running backs (Chip Trainum, Adam Randall, Jaden Nixon, Dominic Richardson, Coleman Bennett, Caleb Black, Jamal Haynes), and receivers (Makai Lemon vs Carnell Tate, Daniel Sopkiewicz, Jordan Tyson, Denzel Boston), plus a note that Najee Harris is visiting Seattle while recovering from his Achilles injury.
