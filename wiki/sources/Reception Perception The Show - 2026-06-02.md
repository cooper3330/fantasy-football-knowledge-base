## Summary
Matt Harmon and James Koh (untracked co-host) cover five new Reception Perception year-three player profiles from the 2024 WR class (Marvin Harrison Jr., Malik Nabers, Rome Odunze, Brian Thomas Jr., Xavier Worthy), noting every first-rounder in that class took a statistical step back in 2025 while dealing with significant injuries. They react live to Odell Beckham Jr. signing with the Giants under John Harbaugh, discuss Jalen Royals getting starting Chiefs OTA reps with Rashee Rice in jail/rehabbing, react live to the Myles Garrett trade to the Rams (for a 2027 first plus Jared Verse) and question the Rams' Ty Simpson pick given the Stafford extension and win-now moves, then do deep-dive breakdowns of Brian Thomas Jr.'s (Jaguars) mystifying 2025 regression -- including the Jakobi Meyers trade and Parker Washington's emergence -- and Ladd McConkey's (Chargers) bounce-back case under new OC Mike McDaniel. They close with a rundown of the increasingly crowded, chaotic Giants WR room (Beckham, JuJu Smith-Schuster, Malik Nabers' health, Darius Slayton, Darnell Mooney, Calvin Austin III, Braxton Berrios, Malachi Fields).
