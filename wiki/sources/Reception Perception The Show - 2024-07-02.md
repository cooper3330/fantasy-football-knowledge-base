## Summary
Matt Harmon and co-host James Koh (untracked) break down two newly-charted Reception Perception profiles. First, [[DeVonta Smith]]'s 2023 season, where his man/zone/press success rates all dipped slightly to career lows, his after-catch and nine/dig route numbers fell, and Harmon now grades him a tier-three/bottom-tier-two receiver rather than 1A/1B -- while projecting new OC Kellen Moore will use Smith as the boundary/X press-beater and [[A.J. Brown]] (not yet profiled) as the motion/big-play threat. Second, [[Rashee Rice]]'s rookie season, contrasted against his poor SMU prospect profile, showing Andy Reid built a completely different slot/screen-heavy role around him; Harmon comps Rice to Juju Smith-Schuster and Jarvis Landry (both untracked/non-tracked comps, no pages), with [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] as the upside outcome. They also cover how [[Marquise Brown]] and [[Xavier Worthy]] fit into the Chiefs' receiver room to clear space for Rice underneath.
