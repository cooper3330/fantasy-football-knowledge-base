## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) record in person for a division-by-division walk through the NFC North receiver rooms, using Reception Perception charting data throughout.

Green Bay gets the longest segment: Harmon calls all four of [[Jayden Reed]], [[Romeo Doubs]], [[Dontayvion Wicks]] and [[Christian Watson]] 'good to some degree' and argues the room's problem is snap allocation rather than talent. Reed is his pick as top target earner; Wicks is his bold pick for team receiving yards leader; Doubs his pick for most touchdowns; Watson he frames as a less-is-more shot player who needs deep overs, digs and posts rather than boundary X duty. Harmon also raises a caution flag on [[Jordan Love]] regressing after a soft late-season schedule.

Detroit: a 'buy-ish, 75 cents on the dollar' stance on [[Jameson Williams]] built on the split between his first three charted games and his final five, plus a long appreciation of [[Amon-Ra St. Brown]] as the top of tier two with two named routes to tier one (the [[CeeDee Lamb]] path and the [[Cooper Kupp]] path).

Minnesota: [[Justin Jefferson]] is unambiguously the best receiver in the league and a candidate for a 200-target season behind a fragile depth chart; [[Jordan Addison]] is capped as a strong number two with real off-field concern.

Chicago: Koh calls the trio the second best in the NFL behind Houston; Harmon promotes [[D.J. Moore]] from tier three to tier two, is 'sky high' on [[Rome Odunze]], and predicts [[Keenan Allen]] leads the team early. Both rank the four rooms and disagree on the order.
