## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] opens with a Thursday-night recap of the Giants' upset of the Eagles, focusing on rookie QB [[Jackson Dart]] and rookie RB [[Cam Skattebo]], and on what looks wrong with the Philadelphia offense ([[Jalen Hurts]], [[Saquon Barkley]], new coordinator Kevin Patullo). He runs a Week 6 injury rundown, then is joined by regular guest Cousin Josh (Detective Fish, NOT a tracked expert) for the episode's main segment: five hot starts they would not sell high on and five cold starts they would not buy low on. Andy Behrens (Yahoo, NOT tracked) is cited from Harris's paywall show on [[Emeka Egbuka]] and [[Javonte Williams]]. [[Matt Waldman]] is referenced twice from the prior day's episode (skepticism that Dart is ready to start; approval of the Eagles' new coordinator; willingness to start [[Luther Burden III]] out of the bye) but did not appear on this show.
