## Summary
Matt Harmon (with co-host James Koh) unveils Reception Perception's 2026 running back charting profiles, covering the three backs drafted earliest at the position: Jeremiah Love (ARI, 3rd overall), Jadarian Price (SEA, last pick of round 1), and Kalen Black (SF, 3rd round, out of Indiana). Harmon walks through his charting methodology and benchmarks (55% overall success rate, 28% broken-tackle rate, ~17.5-20% explosive play rate), then breaks down each prospect's scheme splits (man gap vs zone, inside vs outside), broken-tackle and explosive-play numbers, and landing-spot context. Extended tangent on Christian McCaffrey's 2026 outlook (workload, age, Super Bowl interview quote) tied into the Kalen Black discussion, plus a segment on Zach Charbonnet's ACL recovery timeline, George Holani's pass-protection trust, and De'Zhaun Stribling's draft slot relative to consensus.
