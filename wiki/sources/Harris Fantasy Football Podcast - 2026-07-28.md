## Summary
Chris Harris, joined by guest Jeff Erickson (Rotowire, not tracked), runs the seventh of eight July training-camp preview shows, covering all four NFC North teams. Harris opens by reading his Almanac profile of [[Jahmyr Gibbs]], naming him his #1 overall running back. He and Erickson then work through the Bears ([[Rome Odunze]] vs [[Luther Burden III]], [[Sam Roush]], [[Xavier Thomas]]), Packers ([[Josh Jacobs]]'s legal situation, [[Marshawn Lloyd]], [[Matthew Golden]], [[Tucker Kraft]], [[Luke Musgrave]]), Vikings ([[Kyler Murray]] taking the QB job, [[Justin Jefferson]], [[Jordan Addison]] vs [[Juwan Jennings]], [[Aaron Jones]], [[Jordan Mason]], [[DeMond Claiborne]]) and Lions ([[Jahmyr Gibbs]], [[Isaiah Pacheco]], [[Jameson Williams]], [[Sam LaPorta]]). A recurring theme is skepticism of training-camp/joint-practice noise and 'nonsense fantasy risers,' plus a discussion of ADP unreliability and rank-hedging ('pretzel logic').
