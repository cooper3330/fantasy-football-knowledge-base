## Summary
Chris Harris hosts a Thursday Week 9 lineup-decisions show with two untracked guests. Scott Pianowski (Yahoo Fantasy, not tracked) brings five hard-to-rank names -- Adonai Mitchell/Omar Cooper Jr. (Jets WR room amid Garrett Wilson's concussion protocol), Dylan Sampson (Browns PPR scam back), Emeka Egbuka (Buccaneers, thumb injury, run-heavy game plan), Jalen Coker (Panthers vs. Denver), and Jaden Daniels (Commanders QB who has lost his rookie-year magic) -- and the two compare full-PPR rankings on each, often disagreeing by a tier. They also preview the Thursday-night Jags-Ravens game: Jacoby Brissett was traded to Baltimore but the deal won't clear in time, so emergency QB Joe Fagnano starts, raising start/sit questions on Zay Flowers (both keep using him) while agreeing Derrick Henry is unsittable regardless, and discussing Chris Rodriguez Jr. as a must-play flex if Bhayshul Tuten sits for Jacksonville. Jake Trowbridge (DraftKings co-host segment, not tracked) joins for Week 9 DFS lineup construction with Harris, covering Drew Lock/Jaxson Smith-Njigba stacking logic, Christian McCaffrey, Chuba Hubbard, Matthew Golden, George Kittle, Jonah Coleman, Quinshon Judkins, Greg Dulcich, Mac Hollins vs. Demario Douglas, and Malik Nabers/Malachi Fields -- Harris explicitly reverses an earlier-season skeptical take on Nabers.
