## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a recap of Thursday Night Football, Broncos over Seahawks: [[Bo Nix]] was brutal and checkdown-dependent against a defense Denver clearly feared letting him throw against, while [[Sam Darnold]] got pressured at a far higher rate than usual and melted down late and in overtime. Harris runs his Friday pre-Week 6 injury notes covering a long list of names led by [[Jalen Hurts]]' blood clot (out, [[Tanner McKee]] starts), [[Lamar Jackson]]'s ankle, and status checks on skill players across the league. Guest Cousin Josh (not a tracked expert) joins for the main segment: a 'hot starts we won't sell, cold starts we won't buy' draft, five picks each, covering [[Brock Purdy]], [[Kenneth Walker III]], [[Terry McLaurin]], [[Emeka Egbuka]], [[Sam LaPorta]], [[Kyren Williams]], [[Matthew Golden]], [[Cam Skattebo]], [[Saquon Barkley]] and [[Zay Flowers]].
