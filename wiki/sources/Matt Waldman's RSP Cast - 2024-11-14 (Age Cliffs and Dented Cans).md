## Summary
A *Film and Theory* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and Adam Harstad (Footballguys; NOT a tracked expert). Two segments.

First, Harstad re-argues his 'age cliff' model against the conventional dynasty 'age curve.' His claim: individual careers almost never trace a smooth bell curve — players improve for a year or two, then fluctuate around a fixed peak until they abruptly fall off a cliff. The population-level curve is an artifact of ecological fallacy: if 32-year-old receivers average 50% of prime production, that is often half of them at full production and half at zero, not everyone at half. He cites Braylon Edwards (No. 3 overall pick, 512 yards at 22, ~900 at 23, 1,300 yards and 16 TDs at 24, then never above ~900 again), Josh Gordon, David Boston, Javon Walker, Patrick Jeffers and Dez Bryant (first-team All-Pro at 28, then irrelevant) as cliff cases, and notes ~50% of top backs and receivers over the last 30-40 years were BETTER in their final fantasy-relevant season than in the one before — inconsistent with steady decline. He frames it as a weighted coin flip each season, which also explains a Jerry Rice: unlikely for any one player, near-certain across a large enough population. 2024 evidence: four backs averaging 20+ PPR points/game are 30-year-old [[Derrick Henry]], 29-year-old [[Alvin Kamara]], 27-year-old [[Saquon Barkley]] and 28-year-old [[Joe Mixon]], all coming off down years.

Second, the annual 'dented cans' segment — buy-low rebound candidates. Plus a long detour on the best receiver in the NFL, where the two hosts have swapped positions over two years.
