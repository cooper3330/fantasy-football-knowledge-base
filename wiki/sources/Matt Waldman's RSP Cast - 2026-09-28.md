## Summary
Rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' Week 3 recap co-hosted by untracked RSP Cast regular Bob Harris and [[Matt Waldman]]. They run through injuries and reactions from Sunday's games across many teams: De'Von Achane's season-ending ACL tear and the resulting Miami backfield committee, Kenyon Sadiq's breakout usage for the Jets, Alvin Kamara's bye, Kendre Miller's speculative appeal, Rashad White outplaying Ja'Cory Croskey-Merritt in Washington, Marcus Mariota's hot start, Parker Washington's ceiling in Jacksonville, a hard fade on Deshaun Watson, the Judkins/Raheim Sanders Browns committee, Michael Penix Jr.'s strong game and its knock-on effect for Drake London and Kyle Pitts, a Brock Bowers 'best skill player' debate, Keaton Mitchell vs. Omarion Hampton in the Chargers backfield, a detailed defense of Drake Maye against a fraud narrative (with a direct rebuttal of a Dan Orlovsky take), Breece Hall's talent ceiling despite a new thigh injury, a hypothetical on Christian McCaffrey's value elsewhere, Bhayshul Tuten's trade price, Jordan Addison's ceiling if Justin Jefferson misses time, Jordan Watkins' lack of current value, Brock Purdy's case as more than a scheme product, and a long Justin Herbert breakdown arguing his struggles predate Mike McDaniel's scheme tweaks.
