## Summary
[[Chris Harris]] reviews nine Week 2 games solo, then is joined by tracked expert [[Matt Harmon]] (Yahoo Fantasy / Reception Perception) for three more. Harris covers Colts-Broncos, Seahawks-Steelers, Patriots-Dolphins, 49ers-Saints, Rams-Titans and Panthers-Cardinals, plus a short Monday-night preview. Harmon picked Bills-Jets, Falcons-Vikings and Eagles-Chiefs, and opens by describing his new in-season charting notebook at receptionperception.com (film clips and observation-led notes early in the year, color-coded charts once he has three or four games of sample; Week 2 notebook heavy on [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] by subscriber request; free profiles for [[Ja'Marr Chase]], [[CeeDee Lamb]] and [[Justin Jefferson]]).

Biggest movers: [[TreVeyon Henderson]] brakes pumped, [[Kaleb Johnson]] effectively out of the [[Jaylen Warren]] backfield after a kick-return blunder, [[Kenneth Walker III]] reasserted over [[Zach Charbonnet]], [[Troy Franklin]] preferred to [[Marvin Mims Jr.]], [[Tetairoa McMillan]] climbing Harris's ranks, [[Xavier Legette]] declared cuttable, and Harmon very concerned about the [[Patrick Mahomes]] supporting cast.
