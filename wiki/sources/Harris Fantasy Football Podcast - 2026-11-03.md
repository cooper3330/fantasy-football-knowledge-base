## Summary
Chris Harris opens with Monday night game film: Seattle's two returning injured running backs (Jadarian Price, Zach Charbonnet) splitting work almost evenly while Emmanuel Wilson is phased out entirely, Sam Darnold struggling under pressure from Jeffrey Simmons and Chicago's pass rush, and Caleb Williams leading Chicago behind a huge Luther Burden III first half and a Rome Odunze second-half surge, with DeAndre Swift getting all the goal-line work. Harris then rewinds through Sunday's slate: Adonai Mitchell's breakout game for the Jets (with Garrett Wilson and a cold Omar Cooper Jr.) and the Braylon Allen/Breece Hall timeshare question; Ashton Jeanty's shocking benching for Mike Washington in Las Vegas; a Chargers-Rams game dominated by Justin Herbert turnovers and a Kyren Williams/Blake Corum committee; Harold Fannin Jr.'s big game for Cleveland against a dominant Pittsburgh run game (Jalen Warren, Rico Dowdle) and a blown goal-line fumble by Quinshon Judkins; and a Colts collapse against Jacksonville with Jonathan Taylor's trade appearing imminent and Seth McGowan positioned as the likely new starter. Guest Jake Seeley (The Athletic, not tracked) joins for a full Week 9 waiver wire: a shallow-league debate over Jordan Tyson, Deebo Samuel and Makai Lemon, then a main board topped by Seth McGowan, Antonio Williams, Adonai Mitchell, Caleb Douglas, Mike Washington and Kalen Black, plus FAAB budget guidance and streaming QB/DST notes.
