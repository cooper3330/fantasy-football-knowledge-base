## Summary

*Feel It or F**k It* with **Bob Harris** (not tracked; not [[Chris Harris]]).

(Cliff Kingsbury to Washington as OC, Bobby Slowik and Ben Johnson both

noting the picks), a rapid-fire "feel it or f\*\*k it" rookie-QB/TE segment,
and a closing personal-history/philosophy segment that produced one durable
general framework.

Seven new player pages, more than any episode logged so far: [[Brian Robinson Jr.]] and [[Tyjae Spears]] (RB, off coaching/personnel-change fantasy
value calls), four tight ends — [[Brycen Hopkins]], [[Pat Freiermuth]],
[[Kyle Pitts]], [[Brock Bowers]] (2024 prospect) — and [[Desmond Ridder]],
created because his play is the explicit mechanism Waldman uses to explain
Atlanta's 2023 passing-game struggles. Four existing pages updated:
[[Caleb Williams]] (a Cliff-Kingsbury-to-Washington landing-spot risk),
[[Derrick Henry]] (a Best Ball ADP value call plus a repeated Ravens
wish-cast), [[Drake Maye]] and [[Bo Nix]] (both reinforcing/expanding
existing rookie-QB reads with new detail rather than reversing them).

Two concept pages were updated: [[Scheme vs Talent]] picks up the specific
quarterback-processing mechanism (Ridder's inability to read the field
dynamically) Waldman uses to explain the whole Arthur-Smith-in-Atlanta case,
and [[Pace Control and Movement Intellect]] picks up a reinforcing "teams
overrate speed" restatement of its existing compensatory-skill framework.
One new concept page was created — [[Quarterback Processing and Confidence]]
— from Waldman's extended answer to "why do NFL teams miss on quarterbacks,"
a durable evaluation framework (processing as a confidence/intuition skill,
not an academic one) independent of any single prospect, worth tracking as
this wiki accumulates more rookie-QB coverage.
