# Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-04-11 (Going Deep - Fave Evals, Coach-Killer Candidates, and High-End Career Outcomes)

*Going Deep* episode with [[Matt Waldman]] and co-host [[Brandon Angelo]],
recorded just after the 2024 Rookie Scouting Portfolio's release. Three
segments: (1) each host's favorite write-up of the 2024 class, plus a broader
discussion of why this class's middle tier is unusually deep and hard to
separate; (2) the "coach killer" quarterback framework and named 2024
candidates; (3) high-end career-outcome NFL comps for three polarizing
receivers.

## Covered

- **Class-depth framing** — both hosts open by noting 2024 has a clear top
  five ([[Caleb Williams]], [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Malik Nabers]], [[Rome
  Odunze]], [[Brock Bowers]]) but an unusually deep, hard-to-separate middle
  tier (picks ~6-25), which raises the importance of landing spot/opportunity
  over pure talent gap. See [[Prospect Pro-Readiness vs Ceiling]].
- **Favorite write-ups** — Angelo: [[Javon Baker]] and [[Marshawn Lloyd]].
  Waldman: [[Javon Baker]] again, plus [[Keon Coleman]] (including a
  self-described "illogical" Anquan Boldin comp).
- **Running back class depth/parity** — small-school-vs-big-school draft
  capital bias discussed via [[Kimani Vidal]] vs [[Marshawn Lloyd]]. See
  [[League Trend Cycles and Market Inefficiency]].
- **[[Jonathan Brooks]]** — extended praise for sequential processing and
  down-to-down control, comped to [[Christian McCaffrey]] and [[Nick Chubb]];
  post-ACL decel/re-accel question flagged, with [[Saquon Barkley]] as the
  cautionary comp; early-second-round/Dallas projection.
- **Off-radar sleeper picks** — [[Rasheen Ali]], [[George Holani]], [[Daijun
  Edwards]] (plus [[Kendall Milton]] discussed alongside Edwards); a
  not-fully-identifiable comp raised for Ali.
- **Coach killer framework** — new concept page [[Coach Killer Prospects]].
  Candidates named: [[Drake Maye]], [[J.J. McCarthy]], [[Jayden Daniels]].
  Historical example: [[Justin Fields]] under Matt Nagy in Chicago.
- **High-end career-outcome comps** — [[Troy Franklin]] (DJ Chark, not
  tracked), [[Xavier Worthy]] (DeSean Jackson/Isaac Bruce, not tracked),
  [[Keon Coleman]] (Tee Higgins/Michael Thomas/Anquan Boldin, not tracked).
- **"Robin, not Batman" WR framing** — applied to [[Tee Higgins]] and
  [[DeVonta Smith]] (new page), tied to the NFL's shift toward two-alpha
  receiver rooms. See [[Scouting Bias and Player Archetypes]].
