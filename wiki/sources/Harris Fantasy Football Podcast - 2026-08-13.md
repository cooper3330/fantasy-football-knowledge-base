## Summary
Chris Harris opens with joint-practice intel gleaned from beat reporters covering Colts-Patriots, 49ers-Titans, Jets-Buccaneers, Cowboys-Rams and Dolphins-Commanders sessions, making two real ranking moves (Daniel Jones up to QB19; TreVeyon Henderson and Rhamondre Stevenson pulled closer together) while treating most other reports (Tyler Warren, Cam Ward, Geno Smith, Caleb Douglas, Chris Bell) as corroborating notes. The main segment is a countdown of five post-hype sleepers for 2026 apiece from Harris and returning guest Jamie Eisenberg of CBS Fantasy (not a tracked expert): Eisenberg picks Matthew Golden, Jordan Mason, Jalen Coker, Jayden Higgins and (unstated fifth, overlap-adjusted); Harris picks Keaton Mitchell, Jordan Addison, Marshawn Lloyd and Isaiah Likely as his headliner, with both agreeing enthusiastically on Mason and largely aligned on Golden vs. Higgins. Marshawn Lloyd's case leans heavily on Josh Jacobs's groin injury and Adam Schefter's report naming Jacobs (with Puka Nacua) among players facing possible discipline.
