## Summary
Going Deep segment with hosts Matt Waldman and Brandon Angelo defining sleeper archetypes and running through 2024 WR/TE sleeper candidates across several teams. Topics: Javon Baker's reported draft-board fall (Greg Bedard report) and comparison with Ja'Lynn Polk in New England; Brandin Cooks as a perennial afterthought target hog in Dallas; Noah Brown as a Houston depth sleeper; the Cincinnati WR room behind Ja'Marr Chase (Andrei Iosivas, Jermaine Burton, Charlie Jones) in light of a likely Tee Higgins departure, plus a tangent on trainer Drew Lieberman's work with Iosivas and Chase Brown; a body-type/injury-risk discussion (Tee Higgins, Mike Williams, DK Metcalf, DeVonta Smith, Saquon Barkley/Jahmyr Gibbs weight and movement); Calvin Austin III vs Roman Wilson in Pittsburgh under Arthur Smith, plus speculation on where free agent Michael Thomas signs (Jets, Bengals, Steelers, Falcons) and a wish-cast pairing with Russell Wilson and George Pickens; Darnell Mooney and Casey Washington in Atlanta; and a closing look at three second-year Jaguars/Raiders/Falcons receivers -- Tre Tucker, Parker Washington, and Casey Washington -- including Gabriel Davis and Brian Thomas Jr. as context in Jacksonville, and Evan Engram's target volume.
