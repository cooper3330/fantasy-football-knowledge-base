## Summary
[[Matt Waldman]] hosts the second 'Feel It or F**k It' edition with guest Dwain McFarland of Fantasy Life (NOT a tracked expert), standing in for regular co-host Bob Harris (also not tracked). The format is rapid-fire agree/disagree on 20 statements about the 2025 rookie class, filmed pre-combine. McFarland walks through his 'rookie supermodel' — a pre-draft model built on adjusted production (strength of schedule, program quality, age, target competition), quarterback rating when targeted, PFF grades and Lance Zierlein's free NFL.com grades, with draft capital layered on afterward. He cites correlations to future NFL success of 0.58 for draft pick alone, 0.70 for the model without draft capital and 0.75 combined. Waldman counters mostly from film. They cover the 2025 wide receiver, running back, quarterback and tight end classes, with Waldman repeatedly warning listeners that his own picks get sniped by listeners in dynasty leagues.
