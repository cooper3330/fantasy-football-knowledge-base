## Summary
Bob Harris and Matt Waldman open with brief Super Bowl reaction (Seahawks over Patriots, Sam Darnold, Kenneth Walker III MVP) then pivot to Waldman's 2026 Rookie Scouting Portfolio prep (120+ players watched, April 1 release). The bulk of the episode is a rapid-fire 'Feel It or F**k It' rookie evaluation segment covering quarterbacks (Fernando Mendoza, Garrett Nussmeier, Carson Beck), running backs (Kaytron Allen, Nicholas Singleton, Jeremiah Love vs. Ashton Jeanty, Demond Claiborne, Emmett Johnson, Jonah Coleman, Davon Booth, Miles Montgomery, Mike Washington Jr., Darius Taylor) and wide receivers (Jalen Walthall, Germie Bernard, and a Carnell Tate vs. Makai Lemon slot-usage debate). Waldman gives detailed film-based evaluations with numbers, comps and stated caveats throughout; Bob Harris co-hosts and offers occasional reactions/questions but defers scouting judgments to Waldman.
