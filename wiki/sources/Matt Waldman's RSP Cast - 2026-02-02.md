## Summary
Bob Harris (co-host, not tracked) and Matt Waldman run their 'Feel It or F**k It' segment on the wave of 2026 NFL coaching hires and the fallout for fantasy assets, then close with quick rookie/draft-prospect calls as Waldman heads into full-time film study for the RSP. Waldman is mid-way through the Rookie Scouting Portfolio (about 120 players watched) and plugs the April 1 pre-order. Coaching threads covered: John Harbaugh to the Giants (implications for [[Jackson Dart]]); Kevin Stefanski to Atlanta (implications for [[Bijan Robinson]], [[Drake London]], [[Kyle Pitts]], [[Michael Penix Jr.]]); Mike LaFleur to Arizona (implications for [[Michael Wilson]], [[Marvin Harrison Jr.]], [[Kyler Murray]], [[Jacoby Brissett]]); Clint Kubiak to the Raiders (implications for [[Ashton Jeanty]]); Todd Monken to Cleveland and [[Shedeur Sanders]]'s outlook; Mike McCarthy to the Steelers and a possible [[Aaron Rodgers]] return; Robert Saleh to the Titans with a new OC for [[Cam Ward]] and [[Gunnar Helm]]; Joe Brady to Buffalo and continuity questions around [[Josh Allen]], [[James Cook]], [[Khalil Shakir]] and [[Keon Coleman]]; Jesse Minter/Declan Doyle to Baltimore and a discounted buy on [[Lamar Jackson]]. Injury/recovery discussion covers [[Cam Skattebo]] (foot) and [[Malik Nabers]] (return timeline). Miami segment covers [[De'Von Achane]]'s 2026 RB1 chances tied to a potential [[Malik Willis]] signing. Closing rapid-fire rookie takes cover QB prospect [[Fernando Mendoza]], WR prospects [[Malachi Fields]] and [[Skyler Bell]], and a general assessment of the 2026 running back class.
