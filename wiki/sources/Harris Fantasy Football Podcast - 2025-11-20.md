## Summary
Chris Harris opens with listener snark/mixtape segments (no fantasy content), then hosts two guest segments. First, Dave Kluge of Football Guys (untracked) joins to debate five potential flex plays for Week 12: the Rhamondre Stevenson/TreVeyon Henderson Patriots timeshare, the Zonovan Knight/Michael Carter Cardinals committee (plus Trey Benson's injury status), Brian Thomas Jr.'s return from a high-ankle sprain, DK Metcalf's declining role vs. a big-play-prone Bears defense, and Darnell Mooney as a Kirk Cousins-offense dart with Drake London/Michael Penix Jr. out. They also briefly preview Thursday Night Football (Bills at Texans), highlighting Woody Marks as a green-light start and noting the muddled Bills WR corps (Terrell Shavers, Gabe Davis, Josh Palmer) behind Josh Allen/James Cook, plus Nico Collins on the Texans side. Second, Jake Trowbridge (untracked) joins for a DraftKings DFS lineup build: stacks discussed include Dak Prescott/CeeDee Lamb, Jalen Hurts/A.J. Brown, Jahmyr Gibbs and Jaxon Smith-Njigba (citing untracked analyst Chris Raybon's 'WR1 in Seattle' take) as studs, Kenneth Walker III/Zach Charbonnet in a Seattle stack, cheap RBs Kenny Gainwell/Emmanuel Wilson as Jalen Warren/Josh Jacobs pivots, Andrei Iosivas as a chalky value play, Alec Pierce vs. Jameson Williams as the third-receiver debate, and Mark Andrews vs. Hunter Henry at tight end, with Tee Higgins, Quinshon Judkins, Zay Flowers and Juwan Johnson as honorable mentions.
