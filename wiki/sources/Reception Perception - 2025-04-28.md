## Summary
Post-draft landing-spot breakdown of the top wide receivers taken in the 2025 NFL Draft, recorded in person by [[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert). Harmon works through five landing spots using Reception Perception charting splits: [[Travis Hunter]] to Jacksonville alongside [[Brian Thomas Jr.]]; [[Tetairoa McMillan]] to Carolina at 8th overall and what it does for [[Xavier Legette]], [[Jalen Coker]] and [[Adam Thielen]]; [[Emeka Egbuka]] to Tampa Bay at 19 despite the [[Chris Godwin]] extension, and the knock-on for [[Jalen McMillan]]; [[Matthew Golden]] to Green Bay at 23 as, in Harmon's framing, a replacement for [[Christian Watson]]'s vertical/in-breaker role; and Houston taking Iowa State teammates [[Jayden Higgins]] (34th) and [[Jaylin Noel]] (79th) to rebuild the room around [[Nico Collins]] and [[C.J. Stroud]]. Recurring themes: alignment over raw label, the difference between an elite separator and an elite route runner, and man/press success-rate splits driving fit.
