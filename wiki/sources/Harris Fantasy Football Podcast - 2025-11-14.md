## Summary
Chris Harris opens with a recap of New England's Thursday-night win over the Jets, praising Drake May's playmaking and TreVeyon Henderson's three-touchdown game (with a nod to Matt Waldman's prior-show comments on Henderson's scheme fit), plus notes on Adonai Mitchell's drops, Justin Fields, Mack Hollins and Stefon Diggs. He then runs the Week 11 injury report (Brock Purdy, Mac Jones, C.J. Stroud/Davis Mills, J.K. Dobbins, Isaiah Pacheco, Bucky Irving, Chris Godwin, Chris Rodriguez, Bam Knight, Drake London, Davante Adams, Brian Thomas Jr., Parker Washington, Ladd McConkey, Quentin Johnston, Ricky Pearsall, Xavier Worthy, Calvin Ridley, Dalton Kincaid, Oronde Gadsden II, Harold Fannin Jr.). The back half is a trade-deadline special with recurring co-host Cousin Josh (not a tracked expert): each proposes five trades to pursue before deadlines close, covering Jaylen Warren, Deebo Samuel, Michael Pittman Jr./Alec Pierce, Tetairoa McMillan, Patrick Mahomes/Jalen Waddle, Saquon Barkley/Quinshon Judkins, Kimani Vidal/Omarion Hampton, Rico Dowdle/James Cook/George Pickens/Chuba Hubbard, Nico Collins, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba/Derek Henry.
