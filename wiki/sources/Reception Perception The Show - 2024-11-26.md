## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] and co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) play 'real or mirage' on Week 12 breakouts and busts. Harmon calls Week 12 humbling and warns against overreacting to small samples, using Seattle's post-bye defense as the example of a matchup read that flipped. Segments: San Francisco's collapse and [[Deebo Samuel]]; [[Jaylen Waddle]]'s 144-yard game and the [[Jonnu Smith]] target hierarchy in Miami, plus [[Tua Tagovailoa]]'s cold-weather record ahead of Thursday in Green Bay; [[Jordan Addison]]'s 162-yard game against Chicago's bracket coverage on [[Justin Jefferson]]; a long segment on [[Jayden Reed]]'s snap share and the crowded Green Bay room ([[Romeo Doubs]], [[Christian Watson]], [[Dontayvion Wicks]]) with Harmon walking back his early-season dynasty enthusiasm; Carolina's receiver room ([[David Moore]], [[Xavier Legette]], [[Jalen Coker]], [[Adam Thielen]]) framed as a reflection of [[Bryce Young]]'s best game; [[Nick Westbrook-Ikhine]]'s touchdown streak in Tennessee's 32-27 win over Houston; new Reception Perception charting on [[Devaughn Vele]]; and [[Jaxon Smith-Njigba]]'s usage change in Seattle.
