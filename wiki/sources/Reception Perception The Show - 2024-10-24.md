## Summary
[[Matt Harmon]] with co-host James Koh (not a tracked expert) react to a busy 48 hours in Week 8 of the 2024 season. Four segments: (1) the [[DeAndre Hopkins]] trade to Kansas City, with fresh Reception Perception charting on three Titans games and what Hopkins unlocks for [[Xavier Worthy]] and [[Travis Kelce]]; (2) the [[Chris Godwin]] season-ending ankle dislocation and where Tampa Bay's targets go — Harmon and Koh both argue [[Sterling Shepard]] over [[Jalen McMillan]], with [[Cade Otten]] the likeliest volume winner; (3) whether [[Brian Thomas Jr.]] is now the best rookie receiver in the 2024 class, using his three-game charting sample; (4) the [[Jameson Williams]] two-game suspension and [[Tim Patrick]]'s role in Detroit, then a ranking of the NFL's worst pass-catching rooms (Browns least bad, then Raiders, Patriots, and Titans worst).
