# Best Ball

No in-season lineup management — the highest-scoring eligible players at
each position each week count automatically. This is the wiki owner's
highest-priority format.

## What matters here
- Weekly ceiling/variance over floor/safety
- Bye week and injury-window stacking across the whole roster (no waivers to
  patch holes)
- Upside picks in the middle/late rounds over "safe" low-ceiling starters
