# Redraft (Standard)

A traditional single-season league with a new draft each year and active
in-season lineup/waiver management. Lowest priority format for the wiki
owner, but still tracked.

## What matters here
- Current-year role, floor, and matchup-driven starts/sits
- Waiver wire and in-season roster management
- Standard scoring nuances (as opposed to best ball or dynasty-specific
  scoring/roster rules)

- 2024-09-05 — According to Adam Harstad (Footballguys co-host, not a tracked expert) on [[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-09-05 (Film and Theory)]] [Redraft (Standard)]: redraft only requires knowing the what — will this player produce — whereas dynasty requires the why, and that difference drives his in-season behavior. - 2024-10-03 — According to [[Matt Waldman]] ([[Matt Waldman's RSP Cast - 2024-10-03]]) [Redraft (Standard)]: an in-season diagnostic rather than a draft tool — four to six weeks in, bucket quarterbacks by share of total yardage gained rushing (0-4%, 5-9%, 15-19%, 20-24%, 25%+). Once a quarterback is running for 20% or more of his yardage, Waldman says stop waiting on a hoped-for receiver breakout behind him and consider making a move, since such offenses typically support one clear go-to pass catcher and leave the rest as WR3/WR4 plays. Co-host Adam Harstad (not a tracked expert) would run the same check on rush attempts rather than rush yards, because efficiency doesn't matter to a receiver — only the snaps consumed. Waldman also restated his in-season read that spread formations and lighter boxes favor volume runners like [[Derrick Henry]] and [[Saquon Barkley]].
